## TITLE (at most 6 words)
Does Anyone Even Care?

## WHO / TAKEAWAY / CURIOSITY GAP (one line each)
- WHO: a worn-out person who keeps everything running and feels nobody notices or helps.
- TAKEAWAY: Jesus does not bring you close by pushing someone else away; He kept Mary near and called tired Martha near too.
- CURIOSITY GAP: Martha asks Jesus to send her sister away from Him. Will He do it, and does He even care?

## WHAT A NEWCOMER TAKES HOME (one sentence, in a newcomer's own plain words)
Martha wanted Jesus to send her sister off to help, but He would not take that place from anyone; He kept her sister close and invited worried Martha close too, because He loved them both.

## NARRATION (speaker tag before each part: NARRATOR: or JESUS: or MARTHA: ; then the word count)
NARRATOR: You do everything for everyone. Does anyone care? Martha asked Jesus that. She had opened her home to Him. Her sister Mary sat at His feet.

MARTHA: Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore that she help me.

NARRATOR: She wanted Mary taken from His feet. His first words? Her name.

JESUS: Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her.

NARRATOR: "Careful" means full of worry. He never called her work wrong. He named her worry. The one thing needed was Him. He kept Mary close, and called Martha close too. Did He care? "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister."

## WHY THE ENDING IS EARNED AND INSIGHTFUL (2 lines, name the concrete thing from the passage it pays off)
It pays off "taken away" and the place "at His feet": Martha asks Jesus to take Mary from that place, Jesus says it "shall not be taken away," and the ending shows the twist: He kept Mary there and called Martha to that same one thing.
The small discovery: with Jesus, one person is never moved away so another can come close. Then John 11:5 ("Martha, and her sister") answers "does anyone care?" by naming BOTH sisters as loved.

## WHAT YOU CUT AND WHY (2 lines)
Cut "Twice," "pulled in every direction," "Mary chose to stay close... no one would take that from her" and "Jesus was right there, in her own house": each one repeated what a quote or picture already says.
Cut the slow bridge "Later, the Bible says it plainly, about this family"; the question "Did He care?" now leads straight into the verse, and the opening was tightened to one stake and one setup.

## RISKS (2 lines: what could be misread, by a newcomer or by a Bible reader)
A Bible reader may say "called Martha close too" goes past the text; it rests on Jesus saying "one thing is needful" TO Martha, but the film must not imply she sat down (s15 shows her sitting, so keep that picture as mood, not a claim).
A newcomer might hear "He kept Mary close" as Jesus taking sides; "He never called her work wrong" and "loved Martha, and her sister" are there to stop that, and the John 11:5 quote is cut short before "and Lazarus" (still word-for-word, but partial).
