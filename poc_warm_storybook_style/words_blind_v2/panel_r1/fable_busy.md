## TITLE (at most 6 words)
Her Name Comes First

## WHO / TAKEAWAY / CURIOSITY GAP (one line each)
WHO: A worn-out person who does everything for everyone and feels nobody sees it.
TAKEAWAY: Jesus sees the tired one by name, and what He offers her is Himself, not one more job.
CURIOSITY GAP: She asked Jesus to His face, "Do You not care?" What did He say back, and did He care?

## WHAT A NEWCOMER TAKES HOME (one sentence, in a newcomer's own plain words)
"A tired woman asked Jesus if He even cared, and He said her name twice, and later the Bible says He loved her and puts her name first."

## NARRATION (speaker tag before each part: NARRATOR: or JESUS: or MARTHA: ; then the word count)
NARRATOR: You do everything for everyone. Does anyone even care? A woman named Martha asked Jesus that. She had welcomed Him into her home. While she served, her sister Mary sat at His feet, listening.

MARTHA: Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone?

NARRATOR: He answers with her name. Twice.

JESUS: Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: But one thing is needful.

NARRATOR: "Careful" means worried. He never called her work wrong. He named her worry. The one thing needed was not another job. It was Him: to sit close and listen, like Mary. Did He care? Later the Bible says: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus." She asked if He cared. Her name comes first.

**Word count: 125 spoken words** (about 58-62 s of voice + 3 s silent hold)

## WHY THE ENDING IS EARNED AND INSIGHTFUL (2 lines, name the concrete thing from the passage it pays off)
The concrete thing is HER NAME: Jesus's first words back to her are "Martha, Martha" (Luke 10:41), and the film's last beat finds that same name standing first in "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus" (John 11:5).
It is a small discovery the viewer can check and say back: the woman who asked "dost thou not care" is the first one named in the list of people Jesus loved, so her own question is answered by the Bible's own words, not by a slogan.

## WHAT YOU CUT AND WHY (2 lines)
Cut "bid her therefore that she help me", the rest of verse 42 ("Mary hath chosen that good part..."), the "pulled in every direction" bridge and the "right there, in her own house" close: each repeated a point another line already makes, and Mary's choice is now carried by "to sit close and listen, like Mary".
Cut the second closing idea (alone / in her house) so the film has ONE thread, care answered by her name, from the first line to the last.

## RISKS (2 lines: what could be misread, by a newcomer or by a Bible reader)
Newcomer: "The one thing needed was not another job" could be heard as "stop working"; the line "He never called her work wrong" sits right before it to block that.
Bible reader: "Her name comes first" must stay a plain observation of the word order in John 11:5, not a claim that Jesus loved Martha more than Mary; also "It was Him" is the film's plain reading of "one thing is needful" (the text shows it as sitting at His feet and hearing His word), and s13/s15 show Martha sitting, which Luke does not report.
