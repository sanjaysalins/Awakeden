# One narration for a short vertical film (YouTube Shorts) on Luke 10:38-42

Channel: short films that find Jesus across the Bible, for a worldwide audience. Warm hand-drawn storybook pictures. NARRATOR voice, JESUS voice, MARTHA voice.
Intended viewer: a worn-out person who does everything for everyone and feels nobody helps.
House rules: KJV word for word; grace not pressure; no guilt; Martha is not the villain; the ending must be earned from the passage; plain global English.

## The film
NARRATOR: You do everything for everyone. Does anyone even care? Martha asked Jesus that. She had opened her home to Him. While she served, her sister Mary sat at His feet and listened.
MARTHA: Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore that she help me.
NARRATOR: His answer? Her name. Twice.
JESUS: Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her.
NARRATOR: "Careful" means full of worry. The one thing needed? To hear Him, like Mary. He would not take that from her. So, did He care? Later, the Bible says: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus." Look again at His answer. He never called Martha's work wrong. He looked past all her work, and saw her worry.

## KJV TEXT
Luke 10:38 Now it came to pass, as they went, that he entered into a certain village: and a certain woman named Martha received him into her house.
Luke 10:39 And she had a sister called Mary, which also sat at Jesus' feet, and heard his word.
Luke 10:40 But Martha was cumbered about much serving, and came to him, and said, Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore that she help me.
Luke 10:41 And Jesus answered and said unto her, Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things:
Luke 10:42 But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her.
