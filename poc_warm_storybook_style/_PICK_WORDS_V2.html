Whole G (147 words, ~70 s)

You do everything for everyone. Does anyone even care? Martha asked Jesus that. She had opened her home to Him. While she served, her sister Mary sat at His feet and listened.

MARTHA: Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore that she help me.

His answer? Her name. Twice.

JESUS: Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her.

"Careful" means full of worry. The one thing needed was to hear Him, as Mary did, and He would not take that from Mary. So, did He care? Later, the Bible says: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus." He never called Martha's work wrong. Under all her work, He saw her worry. And He loved her.
