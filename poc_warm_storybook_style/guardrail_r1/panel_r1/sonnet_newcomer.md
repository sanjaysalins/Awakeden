## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Amina, 24, a phone user in Nairobi who only knows the name Jesus. She stops at the hook (line 1: "You do everything for everyone. Does anyone even care?") because it is about feeling unseen. She finishes if the warm pictures hold her. She shares it with a tired friend. Not served: someone wanting teaching or detail, or anyone who needs to know why these people matter.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus loves you even when you are tired and worried from working so hard." Line 7 carries it ("Under all her work, He saw her worry. And He loved her."). Mostly clear. But line 5 ("The one thing needed was to hear Him") muddies it: I think I am told listening is better than working, which clashes with line 7 saying her work was not wrong.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Martha, a busy woman. Mary, her sister. Jesus, a guest. What: Martha is angry that Mary sits and she works. Jesus says Mary chose well. Why: He cares about Martha.
Lost or misled:
- Line 1: "Martha asked Jesus that" comes before I know who Martha is.
- Line 2: "dost thou", "hath", "bid her therefore" are very hard in translation.
- Line 4: "careful and troubled", "that good part", "shall not be taken away". "Careful" sounds like "cautious" to me. Line 5 fixes it, but late.
- Line 5: "He would not take that from Mary" is unclear. Take what?
- Line 6: "Later, the Bible says" - what is the Bible? Who are Lazarus and "her sister"? "Did He care?" - the question comes back, good, but "Now Jesus loved Martha..." is odd English.
- Line 3: "His answer? Her name. Twice." - I don't see why a name is an answer.
- Picture 13: a brother appears and I never learn who he is.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. What I was TOLD but could not follow: who Lazarus is (6); what "the Bible" is and why a later line answers an earlier scene (6); why saying her name twice is an answer (3); "that good part" (4); "not be taken away" (4, 5); what "careful" meant until line 5 (4); how a "later" line fits the same story (6).

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would put the answer to "does anyone care" up front in plain words, and use modern English for the long quote, so a second-language viewer follows without a dictionary. The visuals (lamp being lit, hands putting down the cloth) are strong, but the words do not match their calm. The ONE highest-value change: make lines 4 to 6 plain and connected, saying clearly that Jesus was not scolding her, he was naming her worry because he loved her. Score: 6/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1 ("Does anyone even care?"), pays off at line 6-7 ("And He loved her."). Score: 7/10. Worth the wait, yes, but line 6 is a jump: a later event, not this scene. The final picture and the last on-screen line pay it off emotionally even if I do not follow the book detail.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 1: "Martha asked Jesus that." -> "A woman named Martha asked Jesus that."
2. Line 5: "He would not take that from Mary." -> "He would not make Mary stop."
3. Line 6: "Later, the Bible says:" -> "In another story, the Bible says:" and add "Lazarus, their brother."
