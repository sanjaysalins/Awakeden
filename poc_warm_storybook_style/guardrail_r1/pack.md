# EVIDENCE PACK: one vertical short film (YouTube Shorts / Reels), about 72 seconds

What the viewer gets: a narrator speaks; Martha's own words are in a woman's voice; Jesus's own words are in a second, separate voice. Soft felt-piano music underneath that turns warm near the end. The pictures are warm hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache storybook scenes (honey, terracotta, cream), gently animated: a first-century stone house, Martha (black hair) and her sister Mary (red hair), Jesus. Over the pictures, hand-drawn doodles grow on the cream paper: little five-petal flowers on vines in every scene, small butterflies, pencil steam curls, a pencil oil lamp in the kitchen whose flame is painted gold, the gold flames of the clay lamps over Mary and over Martha being coloured in, gold olive leaves. Six soft transitions (a curtain wipe, page slides, watercolour blooms).

## THE SPOKEN LINES (numbered)
1. NARRATOR: You do everything for everyone. Does anyone even care? Martha asked Jesus that. She had opened her home to Him. While she served, her sister Mary sat at His feet and listened.
2. MARTHA: Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore that she help me.
3. NARRATOR: His answer? Her name. Twice.
4. JESUS: Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her.
5. NARRATOR: "Careful" means full of worry. The one thing needed was to hear Him, as Mary did, and He would not take that from Mary.
6. NARRATOR: So, did He care? Later, the Bible says: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus."
7. NARRATOR: He never called Martha's work wrong. Under all her work, He saw her worry. And He loved her.

## WORDS WRITTEN ON SCREEN (in order)
"Does anyone care?" (hook, frame 1) · "Martha" (name label) · "Mary" (name label) · "Martha, Martha," (in Jesus's colour, as He says it) · "one thing" (under the newly lit kitchen lamp) · "careful" means full of worry · "And He loved her." (last frame, held 3 s)

## THE PICTURES IN ORDER (one line each)
1 Martha alone at the hearth, tired, hand in her hair; pot steaming. 2 Close: Martha's tired face. 3 Martha draws back a cloth curtain; Jesus at the door, smiling. 4 Mary kneels at Jesus's feet, listening; He talks with an open hand. 5 Martha standing with a work cloth, hand out toward Mary, upset; Jesus seated; Mary on the floor. 6 Close: Jesus looking up, kind. 7 Close: Martha's face softening, eyes wet. 8 The kitchen corner full of pots, jars, bread, baskets, nobody in it. 9 Close: Mary listening, hands on her heart. 10 Martha's hands putting down the work cloth. 11 Wide: one room, Jesus on a stool, Mary on the mat, Martha standing near. 12 Martha kneeling on a mat for the first time, eyes closed; a small lamp above her lights up. 13 Jesus with Martha, Mary and their brother Lazarus, all close, eyes closed. 14 Jesus holds Martha's hand; she smiles through tears. 15 Last picture: Martha sits beside Jesus in the evening doorway light, holding a steaming clay cup, His hand on her shoulder, at peace.
