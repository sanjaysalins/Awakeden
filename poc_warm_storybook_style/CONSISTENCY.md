# Consistency sheet: Mary and Martha (Luke 10:38-42)

Every still prompt pastes the STYLE block (STYLE.md) + the people and place blocks below, word for word.
Refs on every still (in this order): test/t2_mary_at_feet.png (north star: Jesus + Mary), test/t1_martha_busy.png
(Martha + kitchen), refs/ref1_feet.jpg (owner style). Nothing else, so nothing drifts.

## People (exactly these 3, nobody else unless the beat says so)
- **Jesus:** man about 30, warm brown wavy shoulder-length hair, short full brown beard, kind brown eyes, warm
  smile. Oatmeal woven tunic, rust-terracotta mantle over one shoulder, brown cloth belt, simple leather sandals.
  Sits on a low wooden stool.
- **Mary:** young woman, very long flowing red-orange hair, freckles, rosy cheeks. Yellow-ochre coarse-weave
  tunic, brown cloth belt, barefoot. Sits on a woven reed mat on the floor near His feet.
- **Martha:** young woman (older sister), long wavy black hair loosely tied back, freckles, flushed cheeks.
  Mustard-ochre coarse-weave tunic, sleeves rolled up, brown cloth belt, barefoot, a grey-brown work cloth.

- **Lazarus (s14 only):** young man, their brother, short dark curly hair, short dark beard, oatmeal tunic,
  brown belt, kind smile.

## Place (one house, 1st-century Judea; John 11:1 places it in Bethany)
- Small stone house: limestone and mud-plaster walls, dark wooden ceiling beams, one small high window with a
  rolled woven reed blind, an open wooden doorway.
- Main room: wall shelf / niche with clay jars and a clay oil lamp (small flame), woven reed floor mats,
  a low wooden stool.
- Kitchen corner: low stone hearth with wood fire, round clay cooking pot, flat round breads, woven baskets,
  clay water jar, small HANDLELESS clay cups and bowls.
- Light: soft warm daylight from the window + lamp glow.

## Never draw (check every still)
- No modern things: no light switches, sockets, pictures on walls, glass, metal pots, mugs with handles,
  baguettes or risen loaves, forks, chairs with backs, tables at chair height.
- No faint, ghost or sketch-only people: every person is fully painted.
- No text, letters or captions in the picture.
- Count the people. Five fingers, five toes. Same face, hair and clothes in every still.
