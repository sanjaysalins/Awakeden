## What the style is
- Modern children's-storybook illustration: soft pencil line + gouache/watercolour fill, visible paper and cloth texture.
- Palette: warm honey, ochre, mustard, terracotta, cream. Almost no cool colour.
- Background: pale cream paper; props (jars, oil lamps, shelves, blinds) drawn as light sepia pencil outline only,
  barely filled. Or a soft warm wash wall with a bright doorway.
- Faces: rounded, gentle, rosy cheeks, freckles, eyes closed or downcast, small content smile.
- Hair: long, flowing, painterly strands.
- Clothes: loose coarse-weave tunics (yellow / ochre / oatmeal), cloth belt, hatched weave texture.
- Mood: quiet, cozy, tender. Steam curls from cups / bowls. Small lamp flames.
- Jesus: warm brown wavy hair, short beard, kind closed-eye or downcast smile, oatmeal tunic + rust mantle.
