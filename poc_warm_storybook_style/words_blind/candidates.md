## A  "He Said Her Name Twice"  (fable spine + claude "seat")
NARRATOR: You do everything for everyone. And nobody helps. A woman named Martha knew that feeling. Jesus came to her village, and she opened her house to Him. Then came the work: so much serving, it pulled her in every direction. Her sister Mary? She sat at Jesus' feet and listened. So Martha went straight to Him.
MARTHA: Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone?
NARRATOR: Don't you care? She said it to His face. And He answered with her name. Twice.
JESUS: Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things:
NARRATOR: "Careful" here means full of worry. He saw all of it.
JESUS: But one thing is needful:
NARRATOR: Not one more job. Him. Mary chose to sit near Him, and that place was open to Martha too. He never called her work wrong. He named her worry. And her question, don't you care? Later, John wrote: "Now Jesus loved Martha." Her name comes first. She said she was alone. Look who was sitting in her house.
