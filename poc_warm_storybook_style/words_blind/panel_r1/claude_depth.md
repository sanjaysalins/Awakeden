## TITLE
She Asked If He Cared

## WHO / TAKEAWAY / CURIOSITY GAP / WHY IT EARNS ITS PLACE
**Who:** The worn-out person who keeps everything running and feels nobody notices.
**Takeaway:** Jesus answers the tired, hurt question "do You care?" with your name, said gently, and with Himself as the one thing you need.
**Curiosity gap:** Martha asked Jesus to His face, "Do You not care?" How did He answer? And did He care?
**Why it earns its place:** Most people know this as "Mary good, Martha busy." This film follows Martha's own question all the way to John 11:5, where Scripture names her first.

## NARRATION
NARRATOR: She asked Jesus the question every tired person asks.

MARTHA: "Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone?"

NARRATOR: It was Martha's house. She had opened the door to Him. Luke says she was "cumbered about much serving", which means pulled in every direction. Her sister Mary sat at Jesus' feet, listening. Martha did not go to Mary. She came to Jesus.

NARRATOR: Listen to how He answers. He says her name. Twice.

JESUS: "Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things:"

NARRATOR: Here, "careful" means full of worry. He saw all of it. Then:

JESUS: "But one thing is needful:"

NARRATOR: Not one more job. Him. Sitting near Him, hearing His word. Meals get eaten. Days get used up. But what Mary chose "shall not be taken away from her." And that seat was never closed to Martha.

NARRATOR: So, did He care? Later, John wrote: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus."

NARRATOR: Martha, first.

NARRATOR: She asked if He cared. He answered with her name: said twice, then written first. He knows yours too.

**Word count: 175** (about 70 seconds)

## WHY THE OPENING HOLDS A WORN-OUT VIEWER
The first words are the viewer's own late-night thought, "Do You not care?", spoken out loud to Jesus by someone with flour on her hands.
They stay to find out whether He got angry, ignored her, or answered, and how.

## WHY THE ENDING IS EARNED
It pays off Martha's exact question ("dost thou not care") and the doubled name ("Martha, Martha"), both set up in the first 40 seconds.
The answer comes from Scripture itself: John 11:5 names Martha before her sister. The closing line to the viewer, "He knows yours too," rests on that rather than on pressure.

## RISKS
**Newcomer:** "Careful" and "cumbered" could mislead, so both are explained in plain words right beside the quote. "That seat was never closed to Martha" is the narrator's reading, not a quote, and should not be shown on screen as if it were Scripture.
**Bible reader:** Someone may say "Martha first" in John 11:5 is reading too much into word order. The film only states what the text shows (she is named first) and claims no hidden meaning. "Him" for "one thing" is paired with v39's "heard his word" so it stays grounded.
