**Witness:** Noah  ·  **Core text:** Genesis 6–7  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** the ark's one door, and the LORD who shut him in — a shadow of Christ the only door of salvation, who bore the flood of judgment so all inside Him come through alive

## Beat 1 — I was there

I am Noah. I hammered a box of timber on dry ground for a hundred years while my neighbours laughed — and mine was the only house still standing when the laughing stopped. I stood inside one door, and the LORD shut me in — what I never did with my own hands.

## Beat 3 — The act

God gave me one instruction I turned over more than any other: **"the door of the ark shalt thou set in the side thereof"**. One door — not a row, one. And it troubled me: why only one? And was I righteous enough to deserve the dry side of it? Then the sky darkened, my house was inside, and a thing I did not do: **"and the LORD shut him in"**. I built the whole ark, but never closed that door — the sealing was His, so I could never say I saved myself.

## Beat 6 — The reveal

I see it now, by a light that came long after I was laid in the ground. One door. I never closed it. The LORD shut me in. That door was a shadow of a Man to come — Jesus, who said:

**[Jesus]** **"I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved"**

The ark was a refuge: it sheltered me through the judgment, the wood bearing me up. But Christ did more than shelter me — He took the flood. The judgment my sin had earned broke over Him, the just for the unjust, that everyone shut inside Him comes through alive.

## Beat 7 — The invitation

Be still a moment. Feel the cost — not my hammer and years, but the wrath that fell on Him so it would not fall on you. There is still one door, thrown wide open; will you come in? Do not trust the man who swung the hammer — come in through the one door, Jesus Himself, and be carried clean through.
