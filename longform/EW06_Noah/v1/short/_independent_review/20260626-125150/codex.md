# Independent review — codex (OK, 22s)

Findings:

- **EW-INV-4:** `**"I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved"**` is attributed correctly to Jesus, but it is an unmarked fragment of John 10:9. KJV continues: “and shall go in and out, and find pasture.” The missing comma after `saved` also makes the quoted punctuation non-verbatim. Mark it as a fragment or quote the full verse exactly.
- **EW-G8:** The wrestling is thin. `So I could never say I saved myself` is a good insight, but Noah’s strongest internal objection is barely voiced. The piece never really makes him wrestle with the scandal of judgment, the silence of God during years of warning, or the terror of being shut in while others perish.
- **EW-G7:** The reveal is mostly earned through `That ark had one door` and `the LORD shut me in`, but `He is the one door — my ark, only its shadow` is still partly asserted. The type-to-Christ turn would be stronger if the lived detail of being shut in by God carried more weight before the doctrinal explanation arrives.
- **EW-G9:** Substitution is named well: `The wrath my sin had earned did not pass over Him; it broke over Him, the just for the unjust`. That is clear and sound. No supersession issue.
- **EW-G10:** Voice is mostly first-person and characterful, but `Long after I was laid in the ground, a light fell that I never saw` briefly becomes mystical essay narration rather than Noah’s plain eyewitness voice. It is pretty, but vague.
- **CTA-on-Jesus:** The close names Jesus and gives a real response: `Come in to Jesus`. It is grace-anchored: `nothing to build, nothing to bring`. This passes, and avoids the tired trust-template.
- **First-hearing clarity:** Generally clear, but `shut inside Him` may confuse a zero-Bible listener. It is theologically intelligible, but the image is abstract on first hearing.
- **Short form:** Tight enough. One witness, one thread, one moment. No major sag.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the KJV quotation so it is exact, including punctuation, or clearly mark it as a fragment.
2. Strengthen Noah’s real internal wrestling instead of only giving the clean conclusion.
3. Make the ark-to-Christ reveal more shown through Noah’s lived door/judgment experience before asserting the doctrine.
