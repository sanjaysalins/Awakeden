## Independent adversarial review — *The One Door* (Noah, eyewitness-short)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution: **PASS (narrow)**

The only `**"..."**` quote is exact KJV John 10:9:

**`"I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved"`**

Speaker is set up in prose (**`A Man named Jesus said:`**) and tagged **`[Jesus]`**. No altered word inside the quote.

**Narrow caveat (not a quote fail, but a grounding gap):** The spine’s other binding line — Gen 7:16 **`"and the LORD shut him in"`** — is paraphrased as **`the LORD shut me in`** without a KJV quote or `**[the LORD]**` tag. EW-INV-4 doesn’t flag paraphrase, but the *most load-bearing OT moment* never gets Scripture’s exact wording or divine-speaker routing. A zero-Bible listener hears Noah’s claim, not the text’s seal.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling: **FAIL (absent)**

There is **no wrestling beat**. No honest doubt, no steel-man objection in Noah’s voice.

The format’s compressed short spine still expects wrestling to be *touched* (spec §2: SHORT may compress beats but the doubt is part of the DNA; EW-G8 is a named gate). This piece jumps from **`So I could never say I saved myself`** (monergism conclusion) straight to **`Long after I was laid in the ground…`**

For Noah, the obvious steel-mans are missing:

- **Election / theodicy:** neighbors laughed, then drowned — **`mine was the only house left`** raises *why me, why not them?* but Noah never voices it.
- **Merit vs. grace:** Gen 7:1 names him righteous; the piece never wrestles *was I righteous enough to deserve the dry side?* or *did a hundred years of hammering buy my seat?*
- **One door for the world:** **`one door — not a row, one`** invites *why only one way in?* — never asked.

What’s left is a **conclusion without a fought objection**. The CTA (**`nothing to build, nothing to bring`**) answers a doubt the witness never stated.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned: **FAIL / weak**

**Earned (door thread — good):**

- **`That ark had one door — not a row, one`** → **`"I am the door"`** — clean type→fulfillment from lived design.
- **`I built the whole ark, but never closed that door`** / **`the LORD shut me in`** → **`So I could never say I saved myself`** → **`do not come to me — I could not even shut my own door`** — monergistic sealing is *shown* from the witnessed moment before the NT turn.

**Asserted (substitution thread — weak):**

- **`my ark, only its shadow`** — typology label, not walked out from what Noah saw at the door.
- **`But Christ did more`** — announced contrast; the witnessed moment showed *refuge inside while judgment fell outside*, not Christ doing “more” in the penal sense.
- **`The wrath my sin had earned did not pass over Him; it broke over Him, the just for the unjust`** — substitution is named (EW-G9 help), but this is **1 Peter 3:18 shorthand**, not demonstrated from the door/shutting thread. The ark moment never displayed *my sin earning wrath*; Gen 7:1 frames Noah as righteous. The piece leaps from *divine sealing + refuge* to *penal substitution* in one sentence.
- **`all shut inside Him come through alive`** — clever echo of **`shut me in`**, but it compresses typology faster than the text earned.

**Cross-time device:** **`Long after I was laid in the ground, a light fell that I never saw`** avoids the banned *“at last I understood”* template, but it’s still an **announced posthumous reveal frame**, not memory from the lived moment.

Verdict: **half-earned** — John 10:9 hinge works; ark-as-shadow + wrath-on-Christ is grafted, not shown.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine: **PASS with clarity risks**

**Passes:** one door of salvation; Noah as recipient not savior; substitution named (**`wrath`**, **`broke over Him`**, **`the just for the unjust`**); Christ explicit; no supersession; **`Come in to Jesus`** aligns with door theology.

**Risks:**

- **Two typologies in one short beat:** **door** (John 10) and **ark-as-refuge-through-judgment** (**`wood carried me safe through the judgment`**) plus **wrath-bearer** — three Christ-images stacked in ~46 words. Not heresy, but crowded for a single witnessed moment.
- **`my sin had earned`** vs. Gen 7:1’s righteous Noah — evangelical humility can justify it, but without wrestling it can sound like the text’s own verdict on Noah’s merit.
- **`the just for the unjust`** — unexplained for a zero-Bible listener.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus: **PASS**

**`Come in to Jesus, and be carried clean through`** names Jesus and a named response (**come in**). **`nothing to build, nothing to bring`** is grace-anchored. No fear/gain-loss, no bare *“will you trust Him?”* template. **`So do not come to me`** correctly redirects from the witness to Christ.

Minor: **`Come in to Jesus`** is slightly stiff vs. **`Come to Jesus`**, but the door metaphor supports *in*.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character: **FAIL / mixed**

**Strong (Beats 1–2, 4):** **`I built a boat on dry land for a hundred years while everyone laughed`**, **`never closed that door`**, **`I could not even shut my own door`** — consistent first-person Noah, not costume essay.

**Broken (Beat 3):** Noah did not hear Jesus, did not see posthumous light, and did not eyewitness the cross. From **`Long after I was laid in the ground`** through **`the just for the unjust`**, the voice shifts to **omniscient dead narrator + sermon**. That’s the detached essay narrator in a Noah mask for the doctrinal payload — same failure pattern flagged on Jonah/EW shorts when Beat 3 becomes NT commentary.

### First-hearing clarity

- **`the just for the unjust`** — opaque theological shorthand.
- **`only its shadow`** — needs typology literacy; not explained from the flood/door facts alone.
- **`all shut inside Him`** — lands only if **`the LORD shut me in`** registered; fragile for new listeners.
- Hook and flood context (**`only house left when the laughing stopped`**) — clear and strong.

---

### Hook, spine, SHORT tightness

- **Hook:** Grips immediately; vivid, one image, one stakes line. **Pass.**
- **Spine:** ONE witness, ONE thread (one door / divine sealing → Jesus the door). No swap. **Pass.**
- **Length:** ~**180 spoken words** — **below** the binding spec’s **220–320** SHORT band (~75–110s). Undershoot correlates with absent wrestling and an asserted substitution turn stuffed into Beat 3.
- **Single moment:** Door + LORD’s sealing is the right single moment, but Beat 3’s substitution paragraph **crowds** it with a second doctrinal thread the moment didn’t carry.

---

### What genuinely works (surgical fixes, not rewrite)

- **`I built the whole ark, but never closed that door`** — strong monergism in character voice.
- John 10:9 as NT hinge is the right anchor for this episode.
- **`do not come to me`** redirect is crisp and memorable.
- CTA lands on Jesus with a named invitation.
