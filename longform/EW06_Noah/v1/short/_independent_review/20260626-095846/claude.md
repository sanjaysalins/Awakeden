# Independent review — claude (OK, 85s)

Reviewed adversarially against the eyewitness lens. The KJV work is clean, but the close has a real thread/metaphor problem.

## KJV verbatim + attribution (EW-INV-4) — PASS
All three quotes are exact and properly attributed:
- **"the door of the ark shalt thou set in the side thereof"** — Gen 6:16, verbatim, attributed ("God gave me one instruction"). ✓
- **"and the LORD shut him in"** — Gen 7:16, verbatim, framed as scripture about Noah. ✓
- **"I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved"** — John 10:9, verbatim (correctly stops before "and shall go in and out"), attributed to Jesus by name + `[Jesus]` tag. ✓

No altered words, no misattribution. This is the strongest part of the piece.

## Thread cohesion — the core problem (REVISE)
The title and the quoted type are **the door** (John 10:9). But the emotional/doctrinal payload of the close rides a *different* type — **the ark/flood**. The substitution is built entirely on the ark image ("He took the flood… the judgment my sin had earned broke over Him"), not the door. The door metaphor is quoted and then dropped.

Worse, the two types actively collide:
- Beat 6: *"But Christ did **more than** shelter me — He took the flood."* (Christ ≠ the ark; Christ exceeds it.)
- Beat 7: *"trust **the Ark Himself**."* (Christ = the ark.)

You can't tell me in Beat 6 that Christ is more than the ark and then in Beat 7 call Jesus "the Ark Himself." That's a swap, and it muddies the single witnessed thread the short is supposed to hold. Pick one spine: either Christ is **the door** (then the substitution language needs to flow from door/access/being-shut-in, and the CTA should be "come *through the door*"), or Christ is **the ark/refuge that took the flood** (then don't headline and quote "the door"). Right now it's two types crowding one 90s moment.

## CTA-on-Jesus (EW-INV-2) — borderline PASS
- Jesus named, response named ("Come to Jesus"), grace-anchored framing ("not my hammer and years"). Good.
- **"will you come in?"** is one step off the banned bare-question template. It's redeemed by the explicit "Come to Jesus, and be carried clean through," but the bare interrogative is doing the heavy lift and is weaker than the rest of the beat.
- **"the wrath that fell on Him so it would not fall on you"** — borderline on the gain/loss / fear bar. It's defensible because EW-G9 *requires* the penalty be named, and it's stated contemplatively ("Be still… Feel the cost"). I'd let it stand, but it's the closest the piece comes to self-interest framing — watch it.

## REVEAL earned (EW-G7) — PASS, with a tell
The mapping is genuinely shown: *one door → I never closed it → the LORD shut me in* lands on "I am the door" + sovereign grace. That's earned from lived facts. But **"I see it now, by a light that came long after I was laid in the ground"** is the announced-understanding device the gate warns against. The earning carries it, so it survives — but it's announced more than it needs to be.

## Wrestling (EW-G8) — PASS, minor text tension
*"was I righteous enough to deserve the dry side of it?"* is a real objection, well-resolved by "the sealing was His, so I could never say I saved myself." One snag: Gen 7:1 has God *declare* Noah righteous ("thee have I seen righteous before me"), so "was I righteous enough" lightly rubs against the text. It's defensible as a grace-vs-merit doubt (Heb 11:7), but a sharp listener may catch it.

## Doctrine (EW-G9) — PASS
Substitution explicitly named: penalty ("the judgment my sin had earned") + what Christ bore ("broke over Him, the just for the unjust" — apt 1 Pet 3:18 echo, on-theme for the flood). No supersession, no contrarian reading.
