## Independent adversarial review — *The One Door* (Noah, eyewitness short)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim:** All three bold quotes are exact KJV substrings of the episode passage (`Gen 6:16`, `Gen 7:16`, `John 10:9`). No altered words inside the quotes themselves.

**Attribution failures (two):**

1. **`God gave me one instruction I turned over more than any other: **"the door of the ark shalt thou set in the side thereof"**`** — Prose says “God,” but there is no `**[God]**` / `**[the LORD]**` speaker tag before a divine line, and this is direct speech from God, not narrator prose. Per the format spec, the speaker must be tagged before the quote is routed to the right voice.

2. **`a thing I did not do: **"and the LORD shut him in"**`** — This is **third-person Moses-narrator text about Noah** (`shut *him* in`), not words Noah or the LORD spoke. Noah is suddenly quoting Scripture *about himself* with no frame (“Scripture says,” “Moses wrote,” “the record says”). That breaks EW-INV-4 and EW-G10: a first-person witness should not mouth third-person narration as if it were his own speech. For a zero-Bible listener, **“him”** is also confusing while Noah is saying **“I.”**

The Jesus line passes: **`**[Jesus]** **"I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved"**`**.

---

### EW-INV-11 / EW-G10 — Invented detail asserted as eyewitness fact

**`I stood just inside one door and watched a hand not mine pull it shut.`** (Beat 1)

Genesis 7:16 only gives **`"and the LORD shut him in."`** — no visible hand, no latch, no Noah at the threshold watching the close. This image is **not in the binding text**; it is presented as lived memory and drives the hook plus the whole “not my hand / not my sealing” spine. That violates the witness contract (EW-INV-11b): invented detail asserted as historical fact, not signposted as interpretation. The piece’s strangest sensory beat is **fabrication**, not Scripture.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal partly earned, partly asserted

**Earned (good):**
- One door → **`"I am the door"`** is a clean type→fulfillment line from Beats 1 and 3.
- **`I never closed it. The LORD shut me in.`** → monergistic sealing before the NT turn is shown from Noah’s lived moment.
- Cross-time device is signposted: **`by a light that came long after I was laid in the ground`** — not the banned *“at last I understood”* template.

**Asserted (weak):**
- **`But Christ did more than shelter me — He took the flood.`** — The witnessed moment showed refuge *inside* while judgment fell *outside*. It did not show Christ absorbing flood-wrath; that is **theological overlay**, not demonstrated from the door/shutting thread.
- **`The judgment my sin had earned broke over Him, the just for the unjust`** — Substitution is named (good for EW-G9), but this is **Peter’s formula** (`1 Pet 3:18`) dropped into Noah’s voice without being **shown** from the ark moment. The reveal jumps from “door + divine sealing” to penal substitution in one sentence.
- **`everyone shut inside Him comes through alive`** — Clever echo of **`shut him in`**, but it compresses typology faster than the text earned.

Verdict on G7: **half-earned** — the John 10:9 hinge works; the flood-on-Christ substitution is announced, not walked out from what Noah saw.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling present but not the steel-man

**`And was I righteous enough to deserve the dry side of it?`** (Beat 3)

Works-righteousness doubt is real and internalized, and it pairs with **`so I could never say I saved myself`**. But for Noah’s story the **stronger** objection is theodicy (a whole world drowned) — which the long piece apparently wrestled with and this short dropped. What remains is brief (two questions in Beat 3, no dedicated wrestling beat) and partly undercut by the text itself (`Gen 7:1`: the LORD already saw Noah righteous).

**`dry side of it`** is also muddy for a first-time listener — dry land? inside the door? the “safe” side? Ambiguity weakens the steel-man.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Passes on core claims:** one door of salvation, Noah saved *inside* not as savior, substitution named (**`wrath` / `judgment`** + **`broke over Him`**), no supersession, Christ explicitly named.

**Minor clarity risk:** **`He took the flood`** could be heard as literal flood-waters hitting Christ rather than judgment typology — not heresy, but loose for a zero-Bible listener.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Passes.** **`Come to Jesus, and be carried clean through`** names Jesus and a named response (**come**). **`will you come in?`** is specific, not the banned bare *“will you trust Him?”* template. **`trust the Ark Himself`** ties trust to Christ typologically.

**Borderline:** **`Feel the cost`** + **`so it would not fall on you`** nudges toward emotional pressure, but it is grace-anchored substitution framing, not fear/gain-loss manipulation. Not a fail, but watch the tone.

### First-hearing clarity

- **`shut him in`** while Noah says **“I”** — pronoun confusion.
- **`dry side of it`** — unclear referent.
- **`the just for the unjust`** — unexplained theological shorthand.
- **`trust the Ark Himself`** — requires the prior typology line to land; otherwise “Ark Himself” sounds like trusting a boat.

---

### Hook, spine, tightness

- **Hook:** **`I am Noah`** + hundred-year hammering + neighbours laughing is strong; undermined by the **fabricated hand** as the payoff image.
- **Spine:** ONE witness, ONE thread (one door / divine sealing → Jesus the door). No swap. Good.
- **Length:** ~265–275 spoken words — inside the 220–320 SHORT budget. Tight enough; Beat 6 reveal runs a little doctrinally dense for ~90s but no second thread crowding.

---

### What is genuinely working (so the fixes are surgical, not a rewrite)

- **`I built the whole ark, but never closed that door — the sealing was His`** — strong monergism in character voice.
- John 10:9 as the NT hinge is the right anchor.
- Cross-time reveal is properly signposted.
- CTA lands on Jesus with a named invitation.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Remove or reframe the invented Beat 1 image** — **`watched a hand not mine pull it shut`** — and build the hook from **`"and the LORD shut him in"`** as written; do not treat fabricated threshold detail as binding eyewitness steel (EW-INV-11 / EW-G10).
2. **Fix `"and the LORD shut him in"` attribution** — frame as Scripture/record (**`Scripture says`**, **`Moses wrote`**, or first-person witness paraphrase *He shut me in*), not Noah quoting third-person narration about himself; add **`**[the LORD]**`** (or **`**[God]**`**) before the Gen 6:16 command quote (EW-INV-4).
3. **Earn the substitution turn from the door/shutting thread** — cut or rework **`He took the flood`** / **`the just for the unjust`** so penal substitution is **shown** from one-door + divine sealing + refuge-through-judgment, not asserted in Pauline shorthand the ark moment never displayed (EW-G7).
