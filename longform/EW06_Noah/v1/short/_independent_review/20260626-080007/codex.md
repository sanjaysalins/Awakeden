# Independent review — codex (OK, 35s)

Concrete findings:

- **EW-INV-4 attribution gap:** `**"and the LORD shut him in"**` is KJV-exact, but it is narrative, not direct speech. The line before it says only `a thing happened that I did not do:`. Attribute it as Genesis/Moses/narration before quoting.
- **Possible KJV fragment weakness:** `**"the door of the ark shalt thou set in the side thereof"**` is KJV-exact as a fragment, and `God gave me one instruction` attributes it. This passes, though a fuller attribution/reference would reduce risk.
- **Wrestling is underpowered:** Noah never really steel-mans the strongest internal objection. `while my neighbours laughed` is external pressure, but the piece does not make Noah ask the harder question: why judgment must fall at all, why one door only, why the LORD shuts it, or why those outside perish.
- **Reveal mostly earned, but slightly over-asserted:** `The ark was Him` jumps from door/refuge to total identification. The shown logic is strongest in `one door`, `the LORD shut him in`, and `wrath...broke over the wood`; but `The ark was Him` is broader than the developed thread and risks sounding like assertion.
- **Doctrine mostly sound, substitution named:** `the judgment my sin had earned broke over Him, the just for the unjust` clearly names penalty and substitution. No supersession issue detected.
- **Voice is decent but intermittently essay-like:** `by a light that came long after I was laid in the ground` and `So it was with Christ` sound like a devotional narrator more than Noah’s lived first-person witness.
- **First-hearing clarity is mostly strong:** `one door`, `refuge`, `judgment`, `come to Jesus` are clear. But `the just for the unjust` may be opaque to a zero-Bible listener unless grounded in plainer language.
- **CTA passes but could be fresher:** `Come to Jesus...and let Him be your refuge` names Jesus and the response. It is grace-anchored, not fear-driven. `There is still one door` adds urgency but does not become manipulative.
- **Short form is tight:** The piece is within range and mostly holds one thread. No major sag.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add a real internal wrestling line from Noah that faces the hardest objection, not just neighbors laughing.
2. Attribute the narrative KJV quote `**"and the LORD shut him in"**` before quoting.
3. Make the reveal less asserted by showing the door/refuge/judgment logic before saying Christ fulfilled it.
