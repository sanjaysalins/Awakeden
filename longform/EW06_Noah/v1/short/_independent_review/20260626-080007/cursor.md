# Independent adversarial review — *The One Door* (Noah, eyewitness short)

## EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Quoted words:** All three fragments appear **verbatim KJV** against the episode pericope:
- `"the door of the ark shalt thou set in the side thereof"` (Gen 6:16) ✓  
- `"and the LORD shut him in"` (Gen 7:16) ✓  
- `"I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved"` (John 10:9, excerpt) ✓  

**Attribution problems:**

1. **Beat 3, first quote** — `God gave me one instruction I turned over more than any other:` then the quote. The setup is *paraphrase*, not a clean speaker tag. Sibling shorts use `**[God]**` / `**[the LORD]**` before divine lines. Here God’s command is embedded in Noah’s prose with no dialogue tag; a listener may not know *who* is being quoted until the words land.

2. **Beat 3, second quote — the real breach** — `a thing happened that I did not do: **"and the LORD shut him in"**`. That is **Genesis narrator text** about Noah in the **third person** (`shut *him* in`), not words Noah or the LORD spoke. Noah is suddenly quoting Moses about himself, with no frame like “Scripture records” or “Moses wrote.” Same class of failure flagged on Abraham’s knife quote and Joseph’s `"sold Joseph to the Ishmeelites…"`.

3. **Beat 6** — `There came a Man, Jesus, who said:` + `**[Jesus]**` before John 10:9 ✓ — this one is clean.

## EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man) — **absent**

SHORT may compress to B1→B3→B6→B7, but the panel lens still requires **internalized steel-man doubt**. This piece has **zero** voiced objection.

The closest tease is Beat 3: **`God gave me one instruction I turned over more than any other`** — but the piece **never states what Noah turned over**. No line of honest doubt follows. The strongest live tension in *this* text — *I hammered for a century; did my hands save me, or did His hand shut me in?* / *Why one door for the whole world?* / *What about everyone still outside when that hand moved?* — is **resolved without being wrestled**:

> `The sealing was His, not mine — so I could never say I saved myself.`

That’s the **answer**, not the struggle. EW-G8 fails: no strawman, but also **no steel-man**.

## EW-G7 — Reveal earned — **mixed / partly asserted**

**What works:** The spine’s strange detail — **one door**, **I did not close it**, **`the LORD shut him in`** — genuinely sets up John 10:9. Jesus naming Himself **the door** is the right NT anchor (same pattern that saved Bronze Serpent / Jonah when *Jesus* names the sign).

**What doesn’t:**

- **`I see it now, by a light that came long after I was laid in the ground.`** — Signposted cross-time is allowed, but this is still an **announced** turn (“I see it now”) before the lived moment completes the work. Same template flagged on Abraham, Joseph, Two Goats.

- **`There came a Man, Jesus, who said:`** — The pivot leans on a **time-jump introduction** rather than Noah walking the door logic in his own voice *first*, then Jesus confirming it.

- **`The ark was Him:`** — Typology **labeled**, not **shown** step-by-step from what Noah witnessed at the threshold.

- **`The wrath that should have drowned me broke over the wood, and it bore me up.`** — This is the weakest link. In Genesis, the flood **surrounds** the ark; the timber is **not** a penal substitute absorbing wrath *for* Noah (contrast Christ: “the just for the unjust”). The next line (`So it was with Christ…`) tries to fix the slippage, but the **ark-as-wrath-bearer** image blurs type and substitution before Christ is named. A zero-Bible listener may think the *boat* took the punishment.

The reveal is **half-earned** (door + divine sealing → Jesus “I am the door”) and **half-asserted** (ark = Him, wrath on wood, “I see it now”).

## EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Strengths:**
- Substitution is **named** in Beat 6: **`the judgment my sin had earned broke over Him, the just for the unjust`** (1 Pet 3:18 language; in `passage.txt`).
- Grace over works in Beat 3/7: Noah didn’t shut the door; **`Do not trust the man who swung the hammer; trust the Ark Himself.`**
- No supersession; one door / enter to be saved is orthodox.

**Flags:**
- **`The wrath that should have drowned me broke over the wood`** — conflates **refuge through judgment** (ark) with **penal substitution** (cross). The ark floated *through* floodwaters; Christ **bore** sin. That distinction matters for EW-G9.
- **`everyone shut inside Him comes through alive`** — Clever echo of **`the LORD shut him in`**, but “shut inside Him” is **theological compression** that may outrun what Noah’s moment actually showed.

Overall: **sound direction**, but one line risks **invented typological physics** (wrath breaking *over the wood*).

## EW-G10 — Voice / character
