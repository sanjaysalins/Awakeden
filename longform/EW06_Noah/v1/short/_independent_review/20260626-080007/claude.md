## THE PROBLEMS

**1. EW-G8 (the wrestling) is essentially absent — the strongest finding.** There is no internalized honest doubt or steel-man objection in the witness's own voice. The closest the text comes is "one instruction I turned over more than any other" — but that's curiosity about the *placement* of the door, not a genuine wrestling. Noah never voices the real, hard question a man in his position would carry: *why only one door and the rest drown? was I righteous enough to deserve the inside? why me?* The line "so I could never say I saved myself" is a **resolution**, but there's no wrestling that earns it. The format's whole spine needs the witness's strongest objection internalized; here it's skipped.

**2. Factual/textual tension: "I am the only man still standing."** Eight souls survived the flood (Noah, his wife, three sons, three daughters-in-law). Beat 1 says "I am the only man still standing when the laughing stopped," but Beat 3 says "when my house was inside." Those contradict, and a Bible-literate listener will catch "only man" as wrong. Easy fix: "the only house still standing" / "the last men left alive" — keep it accurate to the eight.

**3. The reveal is half-announced (EW-G7).** "I see it now, by a light that came long after I was laid in the ground" leans on *announcing* the turn before showing it. It mostly recovers — the door-in-the-side, the wrath breaking over the wood and bearing him up, are genuinely shown — but the opening of Beat 6 tells before it shows. Tighten so the type does the turning, not the narrator's "I see it now."

Minor: voice (EW-G10) is strong and consistent ("I built a box of timber on dry ground, hammering a hundred years while my neighbours laughed"); length looks in-band (~290 words); first-hearing clarity is fine ("the just for the unjust" is the only churchy phrase). One thread, one witness, no swap. The beats are labeled 1/3/6/7 (2/4/5 dropped for short form) — presumably by design, but confirm that's intended.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add the genuine wrestling (EW-G8): give Noah one honest, hard question in his own voice — *why only one door, and the rest left to drown? was I good enough to be inside?* — so "I could never say I saved myself" is earned, not just stated.
2. Fix the "only man still standing" inaccuracy — eight souls survived and Beat 3 itself says "my house was inside"; make Beat 1 accurate to the household.
3. Stop announcing the reveal — cut/reshape "I see it now, by a light…" so the type→Christ turn is shown from the door-in-the-side and the wrath-borne-on-the-wood, not declared up front.
