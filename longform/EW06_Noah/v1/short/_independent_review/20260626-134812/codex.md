# Independent review — codex (OK, 24s)

Findings:

- **EW-INV-4 / KJV quote:** Mostly clean. `**[Jesus]**` attributes the speaker before the quote, and `**"I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved"**` matches the KJV wording of John 10:9 up to that point. It is a truncated quote, but the quoted words themselves are not altered.

- **EW-G8 / Wrestling is weak:** Noah never really wrestles. `“So I could never say I saved myself.”` is a conclusion, not a lived objection. The strongest internal question would be something like: why only one door, why judgment at all, why my house and not theirs, or whether wood and pitch could really stand between him and death. As written, the doubt is mostly absent.

- **EW-G7 / Reveal partly earned, but too announced:** `“Long after I was laid in the ground, a light fell that I never saw.”` is atmospheric but vague, and it distances Noah from the reveal. The turn gets stronger afterward with `“He is the one door — my ark, only its shadow,”` but the first movement into Christ feels mystical rather than text-earned.

- **EW-G9 / Doctrine sound:** `“The wrath my sin had earned did not pass over Him; it broke over Him, the just for the unjust”` clearly names substitution: deserved wrath, Christ bearing it, just for unjust. No supersession issue.

- **Voice / character:** The first two beats sound like Noah. `“Long after I was laid in the ground, a light fell that I never saw”` is less eyewitness and more detached theological narrator. It risks breaking the “moment I lived” frame.

- **First-hearing clarity:** Mostly clear, but `“shut inside Him”` may confuse a zero-Bible listener. The ark image explains it, but the phrase could sound physically odd without a clearer relational term.

- **Hook / spine:** Strong hook: `“I built a boat on dry land for a hundred years while everyone laughed”`. One witness, one thread, one image. No major spine break.

- **Short form:** Tight, maybe slightly underdeveloped because the wrestling is missing. The compression helps the piece, but it skips the hardest emotional beat.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add a real Noah-voiced objection/wrestling inside the ark-door moment.
2. Make the Christ reveal less mystical and more directly earned from “one door / LORD shut him in / judgment outside.”
3. Clarify the final invitation so “come in to Jesus” means named faith in Him, not just entering an image.
