## Independent adversarial review — *The One Door* (Noah, eyewitness-short)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution: **PASS (narrow)**

The only `**"..."**` block is exact KJV John 10:9 through **`"he shall be saved"`** — no word altered. Speaker is named before the quote: **`A Man named Jesus said:`** plus the **`[Jesus]`** tag. Clean on the letter of the rule.

**Narrow grounding gap (not a quote fail):** The hinge OT moment — Gen 7:16 **`"and the LORD shut him in"`** — appears only as paraphrase **`the LORD shut me in`**, with no KJV quote and no **`[the LORD]`** tag. EW-INV-4 doesn’t require it, but the piece’s load-bearing seal never gets Scripture’s exact wording. A zero-Bible listener hears Noah’s claim, not the text’s.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling: **FAIL (absent)**

There is **no wrestling beat at all**. No honest doubt, no steel-man objection in Noah’s voice.

The short spine compresses beats, but EW-G8 is still a named panel gate — and this piece has zero internalized doubt. It jumps from **`So I could never say I saved myself`** (already a resolved conclusion) straight to **`Long after I was laid in the ground…`**

The obvious steel-mans are never voiced:

- **Election / theodicy:** **`mine was the only house left when the laughing stopped`** raises *why my house alone? why not them?* — but Noah never asks it.
- **Merit vs. grace:** a hundred years of hammering invites *did my work buy my seat?* — never asked. Gen 7:1’s “righteous before me” is never wrestled with.
- **Scandal of one door:** **`one door — not a row, one`** invites *why only one way in?* — never asked.

What remains is **reflection without struggle**. The CTA’s **`nothing to build, nothing to bring`** answers a doubt the witness never stated.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned: **FAIL / half-earned**

**Earned (door thread):**

- **`That ark had one door — not a row, one`** → **`"I am the door"`** — type→fulfillment from lived design. Good.
- **`I built the whole ark, but never closed that door`** / **`the LORD shut me in`** → **`So I could never say I saved myself`** → **`do not come to me — I could not even shut my own door`** — divine sealing is *shown* from the witnessed moment.

**Asserted (substitution + cross-time turn):**

- **`Long after I was laid in the ground, a light fell that I never saw`** — avoids the banned *“at last I understood”* template, but it’s still an **announced posthumous reveal frame**, not memory from the lived moment. Noah did not see this light; the turn is imported.
- **`He is the one door — my ark, only its shadow`** — typology label, not walked out from what Noah saw at the door.
- **`But Christ did more`** — announced pivot; the witnessed moment showed refuge inside while judgment fell outside, not penal substitution.
- **`The wrath my sin had earned did not pass over Him; it broke over Him, the just for the unjust`** — substitution is *named* (EW-G9 help), but **grafted**: the door/shutting thread never displayed *my sin earning wrath*; Gen 7:1 frames Noah as righteous. One sentence leaps from divine sealing to penal substitution.
- **`all shut inside Him come through alive`** — echo of **`shut me in`**, but compresses typology faster than the text earned.

Verdict: **half-earned** — John 10:9 hinge works; ark-as-shadow + wrath-on-Christ is sermon, not shown memory.

### EW-G9 — Doctrine: **PASS with clarity risks**

**Passes:** one door of salvation; Noah as recipient not savior; substitution named (**`wrath`**, **`broke over Him`**, **`the just for the unjust`**); Christ explicit; no supersession; **`Come in to Jesus`** aligns with door theology.

**Risks:**

- **Three Christ-images stacked in one beat:** **door** (John 10), **ark-through-judgment** (**`wood carried me safe through the judgment`**), and **wrath-bearer** — not heresy, but crowded for a single witnessed moment.
- **`my sin had earned`** vs. Gen 7:1’s righteous Noah — evangelical humility can justify it, but without wrestling it can sound like the text’s own verdict on Noah’s merit.
- **`did not pass over Him`** — Passover language in a flood piece; may confuse a zero-Bible listener about what “pass over” means here.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus: **PASS**

**`Come in to Jesus, and be carried clean through`** names Jesus and a named response (**come in**). **`nothing to build, nothing to bring`** is grace-anchored. No fear/gain-loss lexicon, no bare *“will you trust Him?”* template. **`So do not come to me`** correctly redirects from witness to Christ.

### EW-G10 — Voice / character: **FAIL / mixed**

**Strong (Beats 1–2, 4):** **`I built a boat on dry land for a hundred years while everyone laughed`**, **`never closed that door`**, **`I could not even shut my own door`** — consistent first-person Noah.

**Broken (Beat 3):** From **`Long after I was laid in the ground`** through **`the just for the unjust`**, the voice shifts to **omniscient dead narrator + sermon**. Noah did not hear Jesus, did not see posthumous light, and did not eyewitness the cross. **`the just for the unjust`** is Pauline/Petrine formula, not how a hammer-worn patriarch would speak.
