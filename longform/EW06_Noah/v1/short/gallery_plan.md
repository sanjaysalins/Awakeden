# Gallery Plan — EW06 Noah · "The One Door" (Awakeden eyewitness SHORT)

**Witness:** Noah · **Core text:** Genesis 6–7 → John 10:9 · **Runtime:** ~70s, 9:16 vertical
**Engine:** LOCKED gallery hard-cut — one rich Baroque painting per beat; eye sees the WHOLE canvas, then HARD-CUTS to the named elements (the Kling gallery-tour targets). LANDS ON THE RISEN CHRIST.
**Doctrine spine:** the ark's ONE door / "the LORD shut him in" (Gen 7:16) → Jesus the one door (John 10:9). Refuge vs substitution kept DISTINCT: the ark only LIFTED Noah above the wrath (P2/P6); Christ BORE the wrath in our place (P7, the just for the unjust). Noah saved INSIDE, never the savior — he never shut his own door (no human hand on the seal, ever). Lands on the risen Christ's open door (P8).

8 paintings · 7 NEW · 1 REUSE.

## Painting 1 — Hook
**[beat: Hook] [0:00–0:08] [NEW]**
Spoken: "I built a boat on dry land for a hundred years while everyone laughed…"

**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. A colossal half-built wooden ark looms on cracked dry desert ground under a hard blue cloudless sky — utterly absurd, no water anywhere. Old Noah, weathered and resolute, grips a mallet on the high scaffolding; below in warm shadow a small knot of robed townsfolk point up and laugh, faces twisted with mockery. Dust haze, vast negative-space sky, single shaft of light catching the ark's ribbed timbers.

**Named tour elements (cut order):**
1. Noah's weathered, resolute face turned to the work
2. Noah's hand gripping the mallet against a timber rib
3. The mocking laughing faces of the townsfolk below
4. The towering ribbed hull of the ark against the dry empty sky

*Lands on Christ:* opens the witness — the lone obedience the world mocked sets up the rescue only God provides.

## Painting 3 — The one door
**[beat: strange thing] [0:16–0:24] [NEW]**
Spoken: "That ark had one door — not a row, one."

**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. Close, low-angle view of the ark's vast tarred-timber hull filling the frame — and set into it, one single massive wooden door, the only opening in an otherwise unbroken wall of wood. Warm lamplight glows from the gap within; the rest is deep pitch-dark grain. One door, no windows, no second way in. Strong negative space of dark hull around the single lit doorway.

**Named tour elements (cut order):**
1. The single great wooden door in the unbroken hull
2. The warm lamplight glowing from the gap within
3. The heavy pitch-sealed timber grain around it
4. The dark blank wood where no other door exists

*Lands on Christ:* "not a row, one" — the single way of entry is the visual seed of John 10:9, the one door.

## Painting 4 — The LORD shut me in
**[beat: strange thing] [0:24–0:34] [NEW]**
Spoken: "And when my whole house was safe inside, the LORD shut me in. I built the whole ark, but never closed that door. So I could never say I saved myself."

**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. Interior of the ark in warm lamp-gloom: the great door swings shut from the OUTSIDE, sealed by a brilliant shaft of divine light pouring down from above — NO human hand touches it. Old Noah stands just inside, hands open and empty at his sides, face lifted in awe at the closing door he did not close. Animals dim in the deep background shadow. The seal is God's; the man only watches.

**Named tour elements (cut order):**
1. Noah's awed upturned face in the lamplight
2. Noah's open, empty hands at his sides (he did not close it)
3. The closing door sealed by the shaft of divine light from above
4. The thin shrinking line of stormlight at the door's closing edge

*Lands on Christ:* Gen 7:16 — God shuts the door. The saved one cannot save himself; entry and security are wholly God's gift.

**[beat: turn] [0:43–0:53] [NEW — own-world living Christ]**
Spoken: "A Man named Jesus said: 'I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved.' He is the one door — my ark, only its shadow."

Warm gold light pours from around Christ into the dark foreground. One dominant figure, deep negative space.

**Named tour elements (cut order):**
1. Christ's calm, kind face in the doorway light
2. Christ's open welcoming hands
3. The doorway of pure light around him
4. The faint ghostly ark-and-door silhouette behind him (the shadow)

*Lands on Christ:* John 10:9 spoken — Christ IS the one door; the ark was only its shadow.

## Painting 7 — The wrath broke over Him
**[beat: turn] [0:53–1:02] [NEW]**
Spoken: "The flood that drowned the world only lifted my ark; the wood carried me safe through the judgment. But Christ did more. The wrath my sin had earned did not pass over Him; it broke over Him, the just for the unjust, so all shut inside Him come through alive."

**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. A towering black wall of storm-water curls and BREAKS down directly over a single upright figure of Christ, who stands firm and unbowed beneath the crushing wave, head bowed, taking the full blow that the ark only floated above. A high upright wooden cross stands in silhouette against the breaking storm behind him. Solemn, reverent, no gore; one figure absorbing the wrath, dark crushing negative space above.

**Named tour elements (cut order):**
1. Christ's bowed, firm face beneath the breaking wave
2. The black wall of storm-water curling down over Him
3. Christ's steady shoulders taking the full blow
4. The upright cross in silhouette against the storm

*Lands on Christ:* the substitution made DISTINCT from refuge — the wrath that the ark escaped breaks on Christ, the just for the unjust, so all in Him come through alive.
