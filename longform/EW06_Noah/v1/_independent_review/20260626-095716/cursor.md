## EW-INV-11 / EW-G10 — invented “I was there” detail

**`"watched a hand that was not my hand reach in and pull it shut"`** (Beat 1) — not in Genesis. The text only gives **`"and the LORD shut him in."`** No visible hand, no latch, no Noah-at-the-threshold eyewitness POV. This is presented as lived memory that drives the hook and the whole “His hand, not mine” spine. That breaks the witness contract: the piece builds its strangest detail on fabrication, not the binding text.

(v1.1 removed the “fists on the walls” / “beam” inventions — good — but the **hand** remains the same class of problem.)

## EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**What improved:** Beat 5 now names a real objection — **`"how can it be right that a whole world should drown at all?"`** — not only survivor guilt. That is the harder theodicy question, and it’s in Noah’s voice.

**What still fails:**

1. **The answer retreats.** After the theodicy question, the piece pivots to **`"I belonged among the guilty"`** and **`"mercy had cut a single door"`** — grace-for-Noah, not a sustained answer to *global* drowning. **`"judged a real and willing evil, not an innocent one"`** does not engage infants, the scope of “all flesh,” or why judgment had to look like this. The steel-man is **asked**, then **softened**.

2. **Stronger in-text tension is quoted then dropped.** Beat 3 quotes **`"for thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation"`** while Beat 2 insists **`"I was no better clay than any of them"`**. That is Noah’s sharpest internal clash — *Why did God call me righteous and shut me in?* Beat 5 never wrestles that line; it routes weight through theodicy + “inside not outside” instead.

3. **Hearability clash:** Beat 2 quotes Hebrews **`"moved with fear, prepared an ark"`** while Beat 5 says **`"not because I feared the ark would fail"`**. Theologically distinct “fears,” but a zero-Bible listener may hear contradiction without a one-line bridge.

## EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**What works:** Cross-time device is signposted — **`"by the light that came long after I was laid in the ground"`** — not the banned *“at last I understood”* template. John 10:9 is the right hinge: Jesus names **`"I am the door"`**, which maps to Beats 3–4’s one-door spine. Beat 4’s **`"I did not shut the door"`** / **`"The LORD shut me in"`** genuinely earns *monergistic sealing* before the NT turn.

**What fails:**

1. **Beat 6 opens with announcement:** **`"I see it now"`** — then imports 1 Peter almost entirely from later-written word, not from what Noah lived in Beats 3–5.

2. **Typology asserted, not shown from the flood text:**
   - **`"The waters that should have drowned me broke over that wood instead, and the wood bore me up"`** (Beat 6)
   - Repeated in Beat 7: **`"The waters that should have drowned me broke over the timber instead"`**

   In Genesis the flood **lifts** the ark (7:17–18: *“bare up the ark”*); it does not fall **on** the hull as penal wrath. The ark is **refuge-from-judgment**; Christ is **judgment-bearer**. Collapsing them weakens the reveal’s textual spine and blurs the type.

3. **`"And the ark was Him"`** — door = Christ and ark = Christ in the same breath. A first-hearing listener loses the map: is Christ the vessel, the entry, or both? The metaphor stacks before any one image lands.

4. **Lived material does too little lifting before John 10:9.** Beats 3–4 earn *door + divine shut-in*; they do **not** earn *wrath broke over the wood like it broke over Christ*. That move is pasted on in Beat 6, then repeated verbatim in Beat 7.

## EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Sound:**
- Substitution on **Christ** is named: **`"the wrath that my sin had earned did not pass Him by"`**, **`"the just for the unjust"`**, **`"everyone shut inside Him might come through untouched"`**.
- Noah as passenger, not savior: **`"Do not come to me. I was only a passenger"`** / **`"I could not close my own door"`** — guards against Noah-as-savior.
- 1 Pet 3:21 now includes the qualifier — good fix; prose afterward (**`"never the water that saved… but the answer of a heart turned to God"`**) steers away from baptismal regeneration.

**Friction:**
- **Ark-as-penal-substitute** language (above) is typologically sloppy under evangelical reading — wrath on timber ≠ wrath on Christ in the same way.
- Beat 7 **`"God hung His bow over a washed world — pure mercy, holding the door open to you now"`** paraphrases Gen 9:13 without quoting it, and fuses **rainbow covenant** with **door metaphor** in one breath. `passage.txt` includes **`"I do set my bow in the cloud"`** — omitting the verbatim line loses a free, text-grounded mercy beat and makes the close feel preached rather than witnessed.

No supersession detected.

## Hook, spine, first-hearing clarity, long-form runtime

**Hook:** Grips — **`"one door"`** + **`"a hand that was not my hand"`** — but the hand is invented (see above).

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (one door → Christ the door). No swap.

**First-hearing:** Mostly followable; weak spots are stacked metaphors (ark = Him + door = Him), unwarranted wrath-on-timber, and the righteousness/theodicy questions raised but not fully carried.

**Long-form earn:** Beats 1–5 mostly earn runtime (world → build → strange detail → wrestling). **Sag in the back half:** Beats 4, 5, 6, and 7 restate the same turns — inside not outside, His hand not mine, one door, wrath on wood/Christ, come in — with diminishing new material. Beat 7’s **`"Weigh what it cost"`** / timber-wrath block is nearly duplicate of Beat 6. Trim or add a late turn only Christ supplies (e.g. engage **`"righteous before me"`** vs Gen 8:21 **`"imagination of man's heart is evil from his youth"`** post-flood) instead of re-looping door imagery.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Remove or reframe the invented threshold detail** — **`"watched a hand that was not my hand reach in and pull it shut"`** — and build the hook/spine from **`"and the LORD shut him in"`** alone; do not treat fabricated eyewitness steel as binding text (EW-INV-11 / EW-G10).
2. **Stop making the ark a penal substitute** — cut or rework **`"waters… broke over that wood instead"`** / **`"broke over the timber"`**; keep ark-as-refuge and Christ-as-wrath-bearer distinct, and earn the reveal from door + divine shut-in before John 10:9 (EW-G7 / EW-G9).
3. **Tag every KJV quote before the bold span** (`**[the LORD]**` / scripture voice for Heb 11:7, Gen 6:14/6:16/7:16, 1 Pet 3:20–21, Gen 7:23), fix Gen 7:16 **`And`**, and deepen Beat 5 wrestling around **`"righteous before me in this generation"`** vs **`"no better clay"`** instead of only partially answering the theodicy question.
