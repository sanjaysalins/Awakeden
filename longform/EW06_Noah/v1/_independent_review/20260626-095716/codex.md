# Independent review — codex (OK, 90s)

**Findings**

- EW-INV-4 attribution is not clean. `**"By faith Noah..."**` is introduced as `men who knew the whole story wrote down`, not attributed to Hebrews / the writer of Hebrews. Same problem with the 1 Peter quotes introduced by `The men who walked with Him wrote it down`. That is too vague for quoted Scripture.
- Possible KJV punctuation issue: `**"The like figure... by the resurrection of Jesus Christ"**` omits the KJV terminal colon after `Christ:`. If the standard is strict verbatim, that fails.
- `I am the only man still standing when the laughing stopped` is factually loose. Noah’s sons also survived, and the piece later says `eight souls`. The hook creates a contradiction.
- `I was no purer than the lost whose cries I heard` overcorrects grace and risks contradicting `for thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation.` Better: Noah was not sinless or self-saving, but Genesis still distinguishes him as righteous.
- The reveal is partly earned by the one-door motif, but `And the ark was Him` overstates the typology. The text supports ark-as-picture/refuge pointing to Christ; it does not support flattening the ark into Christ without qualification.
- The first-hearing listener may stumble at `saved by water` followed by `It was never the water that saved, then or now`. That needs clearer handling, or it sounds like the narration quotes a verse and immediately contradicts it.
- Voice slips into essay mode: `A figure — a likeness, not the saving thing itself`, `My ark was the shadow; He is the substance that cast it`, and `the word written once the One my ark had always pointed to had come` sound like a theological lecturer, not Noah.
- The long form sags through repetition. Beat 7 repeats Beat 6 almost verbatim: `The waters that should have drowned me broke over that wood instead... So it was with Him` becomes `The waters that should have drowned me broke over the timber instead... So it was with Jesus`.
- CTA mostly names the response, but `Before you turn away, be still` leans toward manufactured pressure. The stronger close is already there: `Come to Jesus — the Ark, the open door`.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix Scripture attribution and strict KJV punctuation.
2. Correct the grace/righteousness tension around Noah so it does not contradict Genesis.
3. Tighten the reveal and final beat by removing repeated ark/waters language and qualifying the typology.
