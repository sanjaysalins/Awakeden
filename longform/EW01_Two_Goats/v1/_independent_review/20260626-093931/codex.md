# Independent review — codex (OK, 157s)

Findings:

- Factual contradiction: “I carried them out myself.” Leviticus 10 has Mishael and Elzaphan carry Nadab and Abihu out, while Aaron is told not to leave the tabernacle door. That is not a small dramatic liberty; it breaks the eyewitness claim. Source: Leviticus 10:4-7 KJV. ([biblegateway.com](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus+10%3A4-7&version=KJV))

- Possible location error: “died in that holy place” comes right after the Holy-of-Holies setup, so it can sound like Nadab and Abihu died behind the veil. The text says they died “before the LORD,” and were carried “from before the sanctuary,” not that they died inside the Most Holy Place. ([biblegateway.com](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus+10%3A4-7&version=KJV))

- Ritual/doctrinal imprecision: “year after year I carried the people’s sin in past that veil, and year after year I carried it back out again” is wrong. Aaron carried blood within the veil; the live goat bore iniquities away. The line also contradicts the earlier, better sentence: “I did not bear their guilt… I only carried the blood in.”

- KJV quotation handling is not clean enough for EW-INV-4. I did not find obvious altered words in the checked quotes, but several quoted fragments change KJV punctuation or make a partial quote look complete: “two kids of the goats for a sin offering.” stops before the rest of Leviticus 16:5; “into the wilderness.” and “unto a land not inhabited.” replace KJV colons in Leviticus 16:21-22. ([biblegateway.com](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus+16&version=KJV)) ([biblegateway.com](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus+16&version=KJV))

- Attribution is inconsistent. “That later word says plainly” before Hebrews quotes is vague; “the Psalm had promised” is better; “[the LORD]” before Leviticus 16:2 is best. If the standard requires speaker/source attribution before every `**"..."**`, this needs systematic labels.

- The wrestling is too tidy: “A cleansing you must make again every year is true, but it is not done” is already the sermon’s answer. Aaron’s strongest objection should be harder: if the LORD Himself said Israel was clean, how can later Scripture say goat blood cannot take away sins? The piece gestures at this but does not steel-man it.

- The reveal is mostly earned, but overstates: “That is why there were two. No single creature could show both halves at once” is asserted as if Scripture says the reason. The textual bridge should be shown more tightly from Leviticus 16 itself, especially the live goat’s atoning role, not just from later synthesis.

- Voice weakens in Beat 6. “It was a shadow — and a shadow waits for the body that casts it. The body came.” sounds like a doctrinal essay using Aaron as a mask. Earlier lines have Aaron’s fear, hands, sons, linen, blood; the reveal needs more of that embodied witness.

- The close is basically compliant: it names Jesus, names the response (“Come,” “Trust”), and stays grace-anchored. But “will you come in?” is close to a familiar CTA template; the stronger line is already there: “Come to Jesus — the High Priest who finished it.”

- Long-form sag: Beat 7 repeats Beat 6 nearly verbatim: “the veil I trembled before… was torn” then “The curtain I trembled before… was torn.” That second pass adds no new turn and should be compressed or made to do fresh work.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Correct the Aaron/Nadab-Abihu factual error and the inaccurate “carried sin in/back out” ritual language.
2. Tighten KJV quote discipline: preserve punctuation/truncation honestly and attribute every quoted source before the quote.
3. Rework the wrestling/reveal so Aaron’s strongest objection is real and the two-goat fulfillment is shown from Leviticus before being declared.
