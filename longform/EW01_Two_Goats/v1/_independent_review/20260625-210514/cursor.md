## Independent adversarial review — *The Two Goats* (eyewitness long)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim:** All bold quotes appear to be exact KJV substrings against `passage.txt`. No altered words spotted.

**Attribution gaps (review lens, not just span-check):** Only one quote is speaker-tagged:

- `**[the LORD]** **"Speak unto Aaron thy brother…"**` — acceptable, though the quoted span is Moses’s relay (“Speak unto Aaron”), not direct divine speech; the full KJV verse begins “And the LORD said unto Moses.”

Every other quote is dropped into witness prose with no attribution:

- `**"it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul."**` — no “the LORD said” / “Moses wrote” / “as it is written” frame.
- Same for Lev 16 ritual quotes in Beat 3, Hebrews/Isaiah/Matthew/Psalm quotes in Beats 5–7.

For a listener, unattributed Leviticus law fragments (`**"And Aaron shall cast lots…"**`) sound like the priest reciting statute, not Scripture being read — muddy for zero-Bible clarity and for EW-INV-4’s “attribute before quoting.”

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned?

**What works:** Beat 4’s riddle (“why two? why one killed and one sent away alive?”) is the piece’s best turn. Beat 6’s `That is why there were two. One could never show it all.` pays that off.

**What is asserted, not shown from what *he lived*:**

- `And He was both my goats.` — announced mapping; the witness never walks the listener through *how* one death fulfills both halves without the 1:1 sleight-of-hand. The essay long-form already flagged this asymmetry (slain goat ≠ live goat sent away).
- `There came a Man who was led outside the city wall to die — outside, just as the offering was always carried outside the camp.` — **wrong anchor.** He watched the **live** goat go to the **wilderness** (Beat 3: `watched it go… into a land where no one would ever find it`). “Outside the camp/gate” belongs to the **slain** offering’s body (Lev 16:27 / Heb 13:11), not the scapegoat’s route. He is importing a NT echo onto the wrong goat from his own memory.
- `He was the living goat, the One who carried the guilt away` — Christ was not released alive into uninhabited land; the parallel is typological but stated as lived experience, not earned step-by-step.

Beat 6 opens with a proper signpost (`I did not see the answer in my own day`), but much of the reveal is still NT quote-stacking glued with typological assertions the witness’s hands never demonstrated.

### EW-G8 — Wrestling: steel-man or strawman?

**The doubt is real theology — but it is not the priest’s voice in his own era.**

Beat 5 climaxes with:

- `**"there is a remembrance again made of sins every year."**`
- `**"For it is not possible that the blood of bulls and of goats should take away sins."**`

Those are **Hebrews 10:3–4** — written centuries after this witness died. They appear **before** Beat 6’s cross-time signpost, embedded as *his* old-age thought (`until I could no longer pretend not to think it:`). That breaks the historical frame and puts the epistle’s argument in the priest’s mouth instead of internalizing an OT-era steel-man.

A faithful high priest’s *strongest* honest objection is not “Hebrews is right about goats” — it is something like:

- *God commanded this; Lev 16:30 says we **are** cleansed this day — am I impious to suspect it isn’t enough?*
- *Nadab and Abihu died for one mistake — what if I fail at the veil?*
- *The nation is cleansed today; why must I return next year if God’s mercy already worked?*

Instead: `A cure you must take again every year is not truly a cure` — that’s the **post-NT** verdict, and it **over-denies** what Lev 16 actually claims (same-day cleansing was real; the issue is “not final,” not “not truly a cure”). The essay long-form fixed this exact line to “real, but never final”; this draft regressed.

`I began to fear that I was only drawing a picture` — the “picture/shadow” frame is essay voice, not a man who believes he stands in the gap between a holy God and a guilty people.

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Substitution:** Named adequately — `He paid the price in His own blood, and He carried your sin away` plus Isa 53:6 / Heb 9:12. Penalty + removal both present.

**Problems:**

1. **Scapegoat / “outside the camp” conflation** (Beat 6, cited above) — geographic and typological error carried over from an earlier draft the essay narration explicitly decoupled.
2. **“Both my goats” 1:1 typology** — risks inventing a symmetry the text doesn’t support (live release vs crucifixion).
3. **Diminishing the Day of Atonement’s real efficacy** — `not truly a cure` vs Lev 16:30’s “ye may be clean from all your sins.” Shadow ≠ sham.
4. **No supersession smell, but close:** `Do not come to me. Do not come to a goat, or a yearly altar` — grace-anchored, but could sound like dismissive replacement rather than shadow fulfilled (borderline, not a FAIL by itself).

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Passes the bar:** `Come to Jesus — the true and final High Priest`; `Come to Him yourself, and trust the One who finished what I only ever began`; `Come — and be carried clean.` Jesus named; response verbs named (come, trust); not the bare “will you trust Him?” template; grace-anchored overall.

**Minor friction:** Beat 7 opens `you who are still afraid of the dark behind the curtain` — fear-flavored address at the invitation. Not manufactured pressure, but it leans on anxiety the piece itself cultivated (`heart in my throat`, `not certain I would come out alive`) rather than grace alone.

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strengths:** Beat 1 is vivid and in-character (`I laid aside my garments of gold and glory`, `heart in my throat`, `curtain fell shut behind me`). Beat 3’s hand-work (`for I did it forty times in my life and I could do it in the dark`) feels lived.

**Costume slips:**

- `You must understand what it was to carry this office` — docent mode.
- Beat 5–6 read like a Hebrews sermon in first-person past tense, especially the wrestling quotes pre-signpost.
- `forty times in my life` — unnamed composite witness; invented biographical precision with no anchor (Aaron? a historical name?).

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Move Hebrews 10:3–4 out of the priest’s pre-reveal interior monologue** — reframe Beat 5 as an OT-era steel-man (annual return + “cleansed today, yet back again” + fear at the veil), and reserve NT quotes for Beat 6’s signposted “far side of my life” reveal.
2. **Fix the outside-the-camp error** — decouple the scapegoat’s wilderness departure (what he watched) from Heb 13:12’s “without the gate” (slain offering / cross); do not say `just as the offering was always carried outside the camp` off the live goat’s trail.
3. **Replace `He was both my goats` with “both halves in one Priest / one sacrifice”** — pay off the two-goat riddle without 1:1 live-goat mapping; qualify Lev 16’s same-day cleansing as **real but not final**, not “not truly a cure.”
