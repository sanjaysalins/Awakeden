## Real weaknesses (polish, not blocking)
1. **Beat 6 is a citation cascade.** Seven KJV quotes stacked (Heb 10:3 → 10:4 → 9:12 → Isa 53:6 → Heb 13:12 → 10:12 → Matt 27:51). This is where Aaron's first-person voice thins toward a proof-text reader (EW-G10). It holds, but it's the one spot that risks sagging.
2. **Temple-veil vs tabernacle-veil conflation.** Aaron trembled before the *tabernacle* veil; Matt 27:51 is the *temple* veil centuries later. The line "The veil I trembled before is torn" treats them as one. Theologically defensible (same symbol/function), but a careful listener will notice the seam.
3. **Frame names "the letter to the Hebrews" outright, twice** — slightly breaks the from-beyond immersion. "A word written long after me" would carry it more obliquely.
