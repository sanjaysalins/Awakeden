## Independent adversarial review — *The Two Goats* (eyewitness long)

Reviewing the artifact as pasted (matches locked `longform/EW01_Two_Goats/v1/narration.md` v1.2). Prior fixes are real (blood-not-sin through the veil in Beat 7; outside-the-camp decoupled from the scapegoat). Remaining problems below.

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim failures / near-failures**

- **`"it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul."`** — KJV Leviticus 17:11 reads **`for it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul.`** The leading **`for`** is dropped. That is an altered word inside the quote marks, not a clean mid-verse pull.

- **`"there is a remembrance again made of sins every year."`** — KJV Hebrews 10:3 is **`But in those sacrifices there is a remembrance again made of sins every year.`** The quoted substring is exact, but the lead-in **`That later word says plainly what I had only feared`** does not name Hebrews, Paul, or any speaker before the quote.

- **Lev 16:21–22 fragments** end with periods where KJV uses colons before the next clauses (`…into the wilderness:` / `…not inhabited:`). Minor, but under strict EW-INV-4 discipline this reads like complete verses when it is not.

**Attribution: systematic failure.** Only one block is speaker-tagged:

- **`[the LORD]`** before **`"Speak unto Aaron thy brother…"`**

Every other KJV block is dropped without a named source — e.g. Beat 3’s **`"And Aaron shall cast lots upon the two goats…"`**, Beat 6’s **`"and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all."`**, Beat 7’s **`"Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus."`**

Per EW-INV-4, the speaker/source must be identified **before** the quote. A zero-Bible listener cannot tell whether Aaron is reciting law, Moses, Paul, Isaiah, Matthew, or David.

**Attribution oddity on the one tagged quote:** The tag is **`[the LORD]`**, but the quoted span is Moses’s relay (**`"Speak unto Aaron thy brother…"`**), not the full divine frame (**`"And the LORD said unto Moses, Speak unto Aaron…"`**).

### EW-G9 — Doctrine (most serious flag)

**Eyewitness factual break — Nadab and Abihu (Beat 2)**

- **`I buried two of my own sons.`**
- **`I carried them out myself.`**

Leviticus 10:4–7 has **Mishael and Elzaphan** carry the bodies out; Aaron is **forbidden to leave the tabernacle door** and forbidden the normal mourning rites. An Aaron who says he carried them out himself contradicts the text he claims to have lived. That is not a small dramatic liberty; it breaks the eyewitness contract.

**Location imprecision (Beat 2)**

- **`died in that holy place`**

This follows Beat 1’s Holy-of-Holies setup (`Behind that heavy curtain was the mercy seat`). A first-time listener can hear “inside the veil.” Leviticus 10 has them die **before the LORD** at the sanctuary — not inside the Most Holy Place. The line needs tightening or the veil beat needs separation.

**Diminishing Lev 16’s real efficacy (Beat 5)**

You honor **`That cleansing was real.`** — good. But then:

- **`the stain came back with the new year, as faithful as the feast.`**

Leviticus 16:30-style cleansing is **real for that year**; what returns is **new sin / the need for another annual rite**, not the prior atonement failing and “stain” reappearing. Hebrews’ point is *not final*, not *sham*. The stronger line **`A cleansing you must make again every year is true, but it is not done`** is undercut by **`the stain came back`**, which sounds like God’s declared cleansing didn’t hold.

**Substitution:** Mostly named — **`He was the blood that paid the price`**, **`the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all`**, **`carried clean away`**. Penalty could be sharper (wrath/judgment **due** is implied by **`They led Him outside the city to die`** but never named as *our* due). EW-G9 bar is **mostly** met except the Nadab/Abihu error and “stain came back” imprecision.

**Missed textual anchor:** Leviticus 16:10 says the live goat is presented **`to make an atonement with him`**. The piece never quotes or uses that line — yet it would strengthen the two-halves reveal from Aaron’s own rite instead of leaning on later synthesis.

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned?

**What earns it:** Beat 4 is the spine’s best turn:

- **`It was one sin offering — yet it took two goats.`**
- **`One goat died to pay; the other lived to carry it off — as though God meant to show us two things at once that no single creature could hold.`**

Beat 6 pays off with **`That is why there were two.`** and **`both halves were finished in one Priest`** — better than mapping “both my goats” onto Christ.

**What is still asserted more than shown:** After the signpost **`I tell you now from the far side of my life — by the light that came long after I laid down my office, and by the word that was written after me`**, Beat 6 becomes a **NT quote stack** glued with thesis lines:

- **`It was a shadow — and a shadow waits for the body that casts it. The body came.`**
- **`He was the blood that paid the price — and He was the One upon whom the guilt was laid and carried clean away`**

The cross-time device is allowed, but EW-G7 wants the **two-goat logic Aaron lived** to carry more of the Christ-turn **before** Hebrews/Isaiah/Matthew do the heavy lifting. **`That is why there were two`** lands as announcement, not a stepwise walk through how **one** Priest completes **both** halves from Leviticus alone (especially the live goat’s atoning role in 16:10).

### EW-G8 — Wrestling: steel-man or strawman?

**Improved** vs earlier drafts: Hebrews 10:3–4 sit under Beat 6’s **`That later word says plainly what I had only feared`**, not inside pre-signpost interior monologue.

**Still not the fullest steel-man in Aaron’s own era.** Beat 2 gives Nadab/Abihu and **`that he die not`** — the priest’s **strongest** lived dread is failing at the veil and being consumed. Beat 5 barely uses that; it pivots to annual repetition and **`I was only ever pointing`**.

A faithful Aaron wrestling Lev 16:30 would also voice the hardest tension:

*God said we are cleansed this day — am I impious to feel it is never finished?*

You gesture (**`The people went home clean`**) but don’t **wrestle** it — you resolve quickly into shadow/pointer language (**`I was a sign. I did not yet know of what.`**). That is honest, but closer to essay foreshadowing than the hardest objection internalized in Aaron’s voice.

Also: Beat 5’s **`quiet fear`** is about pointing-at-greater-atonement — which is already the sermon’s answer. The steel-man should hurt more before it resolves.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Mostly passes:** **`Come to Jesus — the High Priest who finished it.`** / **`will you come in?`** / **`Trust the One who finished what I only ever foreshadowed. Walk in`** — Jesus named; response verbs named (come, come in, trust, walk in); grace-anchored **`Not when you are clean enough, not next year, not by your own blood — but now, on His.`** Not the bare banned **`will you trust Him?`** template.

**Friction:** Beat 7 opens **`this is the thing I waited my whole life to understand`** — adjacent to banned “at last I understood” stinger energy even if not a literal hit.

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strengths:** Beat 1–3 are vivid — **`I laid aside my garments of gold and glory`**, **`heart in my throat`**, hand-work on the goats.

**Costume slips:** Beat 6–7 drift sermonic — **`It was a shadow — and a shadow waits for the body that casts it`**, **`Sit with that a moment`**, repeated veil-torn oratory. The Aaron who **`could have done it in the dark`** yields to a Hebrews expositor wearing his name.

**Broken character claim:** **`I carried them out myself`** is not Aaron’s lived memory; it is invented pathos that contradicts Leviticus 10.

### LONG form — runtime / sag

Prose is roughly **~1,250–1,300 spoken words** — at or **below** the long-form floor; may run short of 9–11 min without slow visual fill.

**Repetition without new turns:**

- Veil torn: Beat 6 **`was torn from the top the very hour He died`** → Beat 7 again **`was torn from top to bottom the hour He died, and no hand will ever sew it shut again`**.

Beat 5 (~130 words of fresh wrestling) is the thinnest long-form earn relative to runtime.
