# Independent review — grok (OK, 99s)

**Findings (adversarial, line-specific):**

- EW-INV-4 violation on attribution: In Beat 6 the two Hebrews quotes are dropped bare after a vague pointer:  
  "That later word says plainly what I had only feared: that the blood I carried could never finish it.  
  **"there is a remembrance again made of sins every year."**  
  **"For it is not possible that the blood of bulls and of goats should take away sins."**"  
  No book or speaker is attributed immediately before the **" "** the way Beat 2 does with **[the LORD]**. Same pattern recurs for the Isaiah quote ("exactly as I had laid the people's sin upon the head of the goat I sent into the wild: **"and the LORD hath laid on him..."**") and the later Hebrews/Matthew quotes.

- EW-INV-4 violation on verbatim text:  
  "**"but by his own blood he entered in once into the holy place, having obtained eternal redemption for us."**"  
  The quote begins with lowercase "but". The actual KJV (Heb 9:12) has it as a continuation after "Neither by the blood of goats and calves, but...". The word sequence is substring-correct, but the presented text inside the required **" "** markers is not the exact KJV casing/start.

- EW-INV-4 / source problem: The blood principle quote in Beat 2 ("it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul") is introduced as "the law the LORD gave was plain, and it ran red through everything I did" right after the Lev 16 veil warning. The exact phrasing is Lev 17:11, not the Day of Atonement statute in ch. 16. The witness attributes it to the immediate ritual law without distinction.

- EW-INV-2 failure on CTA form: The closing beats end with a direct personal question after the facts:  
  "So I ask you the question I asked myself behind that veil... will you come in? Not when you are clean enough, not next year, not by your own blood — but now, on His. Trust the One who finished what I only ever foreshadowed. Walk in..."  
  This is structurally the bare appeal template the spec prohibits. "Come to Jesus" and "Trust the One" appear, but the rhetorical close reduces to the listener being asked the question in Aaron's voice.

- EW-G7 (reveal earned): The piece repeatedly announces the later knowledge rather than letting it emerge strictly from what the witness lived:  
  "I did not see the answer in my own day. I tell you now from the far side of my life — by the light that came long after..."  
  and "by the word that was written after me". The "why two goats" question in Beat 4 is the strongest earned thread; after that the piece shifts to listing seven NT quotes with explanatory glue ("He was the blood that paid... exactly as I had...").

- EW-G8 (wrestling): Beat 5 is one summary paragraph of general reflection ("a quiet fear pressed on me... I was only ever pointing"). There is no dramatized, specific memory of a particular year, a particular confession over the goat, or a repeated thought that Aaron could not shake while performing the rite with his own hands. The objection is close to the biblical one (annual repetition), but it stays at the level of essay summary.

- EW-G10 (voice/character): The first-person frame is inconsistent. Strong concrete lines ("I buried two of my own sons... I carried them out myself") sit next to modern reflective idioms and meta-commentary that do not belong in Aaron's mouth:  
  "the whole nation stood outside, holding its breath", "with my heart in my throat", "the riddle of it followed me home every time", "And I will be honest with you, for an honest man must be honest before God."  
  This is the detached narrator in costume.

- Long-form sagging: After the strong concrete ritual description in Beat 3 and the "why two" plant in Beat 4, Beat 6 becomes a sequence of quote + mapping sentence + quote. The repetition of "year after year" and "veil" motifs across beats becomes padding once the point is made. The interior wrestling does not expand to justify the added runtime; the length is carried by expository proof-texting instead of additional lived turns, remembered moments, or extended doubt.

- First-hearing clarity gaps for zero-knowledge listener: "mercy seat", "the cloud", "sprinkled it before the mercy seat", "strange fire", "fit man", "scapegoat" (in the quote) arrive before the meaning is anchored. The piece explains the two actions but relies on the listener already sensing the weight of those terms.

- Minor but real: "the sin offering's body was always carried outside the camp and burned" is applied to Jesus' death location. This conflates the Lord's goat (killed, blood in, body burned outside) with the live goat (sent away intact). The typology mapping blurs the two halves the piece itself insists must be kept distinct.

The thread does not swap, the hook is immediate, substitution (price + guilt carried) is named, and the two-goats structure is the right spine. These are not surface issues.

VERDICT: REVISE  
TOP FIXES:  
1. Restore strict EW-INV-4: every **"..."** quote after Beat 2 must have explicit speaker/book attribution immediately before it; make the quoted text inside the markers match KJV exactly (case, wording, no mid-sentence lowercase starts).  
2. Remove the final "will you come in?" direct question and the "I ask you the question..." framing. Rewrite the close to name the named response (come / receive / turn) in grace terms only, without interrogating the listener.  
3. Rewrite Beat 5 and the reveal to earn long-form length: expand Aaron's specific, repeated, first-person memories and unresolved thoughts while doing the rite with his hands; stop announcing "from the far side of my life" and let the type-to-Christ connections arise from the two-goats actions themselves rather than post-event quote exposition. Remove modern idioms and meta lines from the voice.
