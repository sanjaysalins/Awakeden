## Independent adversarial review — EW01_Two_Goats biblical fact sheet

### CITATION TRUTH (highest weight)

**1. “Mature he-goats, not baby kids” overrules the cited KJV (SPECIFIED — wrong)**

- **Claim:** World fact + Scene 7: “show TWO goats together (**mature he-goats, not baby kids**).”
- **Cited text:** Leviticus 16:5 — “two **kids** of the goats for a sin offering.”
- **Problem:** The sheet puts in **specified** a visual rule that **denies** what the citation literally says. KJV “kids” means young goats; the text does **not** say “mature” or “he-goats.” That reading may be defensible in Hebrew, but it is **not** what these quoted verses state. The negative (“not baby kids”) is an active overreach in the wrong bucket.

**2. “Seven times” for the goat’s blood is inferred, not quoted (Scene 8 SPECIFIED — soft overreach)**

- **Claim:** “sprinkled … **seven times** (as with the bullock’s blood).”
- **Cited for goat:** Leviticus 16:15 — “do with that blood **as he did with the blood of the bullock**.”
- **Cited for seven:** Leviticus 16:14 — seven times for the **bullock**.
- **Problem:** Seven sprinklings for the goat are a fair inference via 16:15, but 16:15 does **not** say “seven.” For a **specified** visual-must, the claim should either cite 16:14+16:15 together explicitly or drop to **constrained** (“as with the bullock” → seven times implied).

**3. “Two blood-entries” packed into a once-a-year SPECIFIED fact (World fact — scope creep)**

- **Claim:** “the bullock's blood and the goat's blood are each brought in on that one day — **two blood-entries**.”
- **Citations given:** Leviticus 16:2, 16:34 only.
- **Problem:** 16:2/16:34 support **annual** entry and **once-a-year** atonement. They do **not** mention bullock blood, goat blood, or “two entries.” That detail belongs to 16:14–15 and should not ride on 16:2/16:34 inside a **specified** line.

**4. Scene 8 title vs. Scripture order (sequence error)**

- **Scene title:** “**The first goat** I killed — blood within the vail.”
- **Text order:** Leviticus 16:11–14 — Aaron kills the **bullock** and sprinkles **its** blood within the veil **before** 16:15 kills the people’s goat.
- **Problem:** “First goat” is misleading. The **first** blood inside the veil is the **bullock**, not the goat. A painter following the title can get the typological sequence wrong (mediator’s offering before the people’s).

**5. Leviticus 16:17 scope stated correctly, but easy to misread against Scene 9**

- **Claim (Scene 3 SPECIFIED):** “no man is in the **TABERNACLE** … this does not govern the outer court.”
- **Verse:** Leviticus 16:17 — supports empty **tabernacle** during inner atonement.
- **Nit:** Scene 9 shows Aaron + “a fit man” with **no other priests** — fine if outside the tent, but the sheet never flags that 16:17’s solitude rule **ends** once he is out doing the scapegoat (16:20–21). Minor; not a citation fail, but sequence context is thin.
