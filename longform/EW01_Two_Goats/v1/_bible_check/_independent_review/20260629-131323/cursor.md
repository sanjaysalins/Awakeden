## CITATION TRUTH — concrete failures

### 1. **“Mature he-goats, not baby kids” contradicts the quoted KJV (SPECIFIED)**

**Fact (world + Scene 7):** “show TWO goats together (**mature he-goats, not baby kids**).”  
**Cited KJV Lev 16:5:** “two **kids** of the goats for a sin offering.”

The cited English text says *kids*, not mature he-goats. The visual line **over-reaches beyond what the quoted verse states**. If the intent is Hebrew *se’irei izzim* (male goats), the sheet must cite that honestly — not quote “kids” and then forbid kid-goats in **specified**.

Same problem in Scene 7: “Two **he-goats** stood together” — Lev 16:7 says “the two goats,” not “he-goats.” Gender/age is not in the cited verses.

---

### 2. **“Two blood-entries” is composited inference, not a cited statement (SPECIFIED)**

**Fact:** “…the bullock's blood and the goat's blood are each brought in on that one day — **two blood-entries**…”  
**Citations:** Lev 16:2, Lev 16:34 only.

16:34 says once a year; 16:2 says not at all times. Neither mentions two blood entries. That detail is stitched from 16:14 + 16:15. Valid inference, **wrong bucket** as part of a **specified** claim.

---

### 3. **Exodus 28:2 does not support the negative gold-within-veil constraint**

**Fact (CONSTRAINED):** “…inner Day-of-Atonement actions are done in linen, so **no gold is worn**…”  
**Cited:** Exodus 28:2 (“for glory and for beauty”), Exodus 28:4, Leviticus 16:4.

28:2 describes ordinary vestments; it does **not** say gold is absent within the veil on Yom Kippur. The binding negative comes from **Lev 16:4** (and context), not Exodus 28:2. Weak/misapplied citation on the “must NOT show gold” half of the claim.

---

### 4. **Scene 8 “seven times” for goat blood — citation chain is indirect**

**Fact (SPECIFIED):** goat blood sprinkled “**seven times** (as with the bullock's blood).”  
**Primary citation:** Lev 16:15 — says “do with that blood **as he did** with the blood of the bullock,” not “seven times.”

Seven is explicit only in **16:14** (bullock). The fact does cite 16:14 as well, so this is **defensible but not standalone-specified from 16:15 alone**. Borderline; flag as inference bundled into specified.

---

### 5. **Scene 3 — Lev 16:17 misapplied to a pre-entry threshold beat**

**Fact (SPECIFIED):** “When the high priest goes in to make atonement… **no man is in the TABERNACLE**… Visual: **Aaron alone inside the tent during the atonement**.”  
**Scene 3 depicts:** Aaron “**arrested at the veil, not walking**” at the threshold.

Lev 16:17 governs the period **when he goeth in to make an atonement in the holy place** until he come out. A frozen threshold pause is not clearly that moment. Applying 16:17 here over-specifies solitude for a beat that may be **before** formal entry — and the scene description itself says he is not walking in.

## CHRIST-LENS — gaps that could distort the two-goat type

### 9. **Missing Lev 16:27 — slain sin-offering bodies burned outside the camp**

Narration explicitly ties **the slain offering’s body** (not the scapegoat) to Christ “without the gate” (Lev 16:27 / Heb 13:11). The fact sheet:

- Guards the **live** scapegoat not being killed (good).
- Has **no fact** guarding that the **slain bullock/goat carcasses** are carried **outside the camp** and burned.

That is a checkable biblical detail and a core typological half. Omitting it invites the common error: conflating “sent to wilderness” (scapegoat) with “burned outside the camp” (slain sin offerings).

---

### 10. **Slain-goat “payment” half is mostly inner-veil blood, not death-at-altar**

Scene 8 covers blood within the veil (good). There is **no constrained guard** that the LORD’s goat is **killed** (16:15) as a distinct visual beat — only that the scapegoat must not be shown slain at lot-casting. For a two-goat Christ-type episode, under-guarding the **death** of the LORD’s goat (vs. blood-sprinkling only) skews the set toward “carry-away” over “price paid.”
