# Independent review — codex (OK, 94s)

Findings:

1. **Invented / unsupported goat age and sex.**  
   Exact line: **“show TWO goats together (mature he-goats, not baby kids)”** under World facts, and **“Two he-goats stood together”** under Scene 7.  
   The cited KJV says **“two kids of the goats”** and **“the two goats”** (Lev. 16:5, 16:7). The quoted text does not state “mature,” and “not baby kids” is especially risky because the KJV wording itself says “kids.” If using “he-goats” from Hebrew, that needs handling as translation/lexical note, not as a KJV-cited visual fact. This should not be in the specified bucket as written.

2. **The ram constraint overreaches the lot-casting scene.**  
   Exact line: **“If sacrificial animals are shown at the door/altar, do not omit or substitute the ram.”**  
   Lev. 16:5 and 16:3 do specify rams for the day, but they do not say the ram stood with the two goats at the door during lot-casting. Lev. 16:7 specifically singles out the two goats at the door. A painting of the lot scene with only the two goats is not contradicting the cited text.

3. **Mercy seat fact cites the wrong verses for “gold lid.”**  
   Exact line: **“The mercy seat was a gold lid bearing two cherubim of beaten gold…”**  
   Ex. 25:18 and 25:20 support the two golden cherubim, wings, and facing posture. They do not quote the mercy seat itself being pure gold; that is Ex. 25:17. “Lid” is also interpretive rather than KJV wording. Add Ex. 25:17 or soften the claim.

4. **“Censer cloud” is asserted under an ark-location fact without the supporting citation.**  
   Exact line: **“The censer cloud covering the mercy seat is correct…”** under the ark-within-the-veil fact.  
   The quoted verses there support the ark/mercy seat being within the veil, but not the censer cloud. Lev. 16:13 supports that and is cited later, but this individual fact should either cite it or remove the parenthetical.

5. **“Seven times” is slightly imprecise in Scene 8.**  
   Exact line: **“sprinkled with the finger upon and before the mercy seat, seven times.”**  
   Lev. 16:14 explicitly says blood is put **upon** the mercy seat eastward, and **before** the mercy seat seven times. Lev. 16:15 says the goat blood is treated as the bullock blood. The current wording can sound like seven times both upon and before the mercy seat. Tighten to “upon the mercy seat and seven times before it.”

6. **“Seven-branched” is not supported by the quoted lampstand verses.**  
   Exact line: **“Light from the seven-branched golden lampstand.”**  
   Ex. 25:31 supports a pure gold candlestick/lampstand, but the quoted text does not give the branch count. Add Ex. 25:32 or avoid making “seven-branched” part of the citation-backed claim.

7. **Scene 1’s “first light” remains free, but the title may imply more than Scripture says.**  
   Exact line: **“Once a year, only once”** plus the depiction **“at first light.”**  
   “Once a year” is biblical from Lev. 16:34. “First light” is correctly treated as free elsewhere, but the scene framing should not imply Scripture gives the time of day.

8. **Coverage gap: the altar reconciliation step is mostly missing.**  
   Lev. 16:18-19 specifies Aaron going out to the altar, putting blood on its horns, and sprinkling blood seven times. The sheet only alludes to this through Lev. 16:20 before the live goat. If the sequence from slain goat to scapegoat is being guarded, this is an obvious specified step a painting set could skip or misplace.

9. **Coverage gap: the exact annual date is absent.**  
   Lev. 16:29 specifies the tenth day of the seventh month. This may not matter visually unless captions, calendars, season cues, or festival context appear, but it is a checkable biblical fact for “once a year.”

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove or qualify “mature he-goats, not baby kids”; the cited KJV does not support it.
2. Fix the ram constraint so it does not require a ram in the two-goat lot scene.
3. Add missing/accurate citations for “gold mercy seat,” “seven-branched lampstand,” and the altar reconciliation step.
