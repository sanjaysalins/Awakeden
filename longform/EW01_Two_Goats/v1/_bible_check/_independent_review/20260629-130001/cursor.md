## Independent adversarial review — EW01_Two_Goats biblical fact sheet

Reviewed against Leviticus 16, Exodus 26–28, and the episode’s scene plan. Several items improved since earlier drafts (annual rite via **16:34**, ark placement, veil colours in Scene 3). Remaining problems below, most serious first.

---

### CITATION TRUTH — Scene 1 gold vestments (central defect)

**Scene 1 [CONSTRAINED]:** *“Here in the open court before the service, Aaron may wear his ordinary gold high-priestly vestments”* — cited to **Exodus 28:2**, **Leviticus 16:4**, **Leviticus 16:23**.

- **Exodus 28:2** only commands that glorious garments be *made*. It does not say Aaron wore them on the Day of Atonement morning, or in the open court that day.
- **Leviticus 16:4** says wash, then put on **linen** — it says nothing about gold, an “outer-court phase,” or vestments worn *before* the change. Using **16:4** to *license* gold is a misapplication of the very verse that prescribes linen.
- **Leviticus 16:23** describes removing linen **after** the inner rite, not gold worn **before** it.

**Leviticus 16:24** (*“put on his garments”* after removing linen) supports resuming non-linen garments **after** the scapegoat work — not a gold phase at Scene 1’s “first light, before service” moment. The world-fact `historical_note` correctly flags multiple gold/linen changes as **Mishnaic (Yoma)**, not Scripture — but Scene 1 still presents gold-on-Atonement-morning as **constrained-by-citation**, and the **Depicts** line (*“small in golden vestments”*) teaches a visual the chapter never authorizes at that beat.

Narration Beat 1 (*“I laid aside my garments of gold”*) implies gold existed earlier that morning; that is **narrative inference**, not a cited fact. If Scene 2 is the gold-stripping moment, Scene 1 in gold is **tradition/narrative**, not **Exodus 28:2**.

---

### CITATION TRUTH — linen colour dressed as **specified**

Repeated claims lock **white / undyed / plain** linen as biblical:

- World fact visual: *“simple plain linen (the colour of the linen is not stated by Scripture; undyed is the natural reading)”* — then **Must NOT show: coloured/embroidered priestly robes for the inner atonement service**
- Scene 2 **Must NOT show: the atonement linen shown as dyed/coloured cloth**
- Scene titles/depictions: *“plain white linen”* (Scenes 3, 7, 9)

**Leviticus 16:4** names **“holy linen”** garment types (coat, breeches, girdle, mitre). It does **not** say white, undyed, plain, or uncoloured. Banning dyed linen from the **specified** bucket has **no cited verse**. That is inference presented as Scripture — exactly the failure mode the sheet warns against.

The honest handling is already half-written (*“colour … not stated”*); the **Must NOT** dyed-colour bans and **specified** whiteness should be **free**, not binding.

---

### BUCKET ERROR — **Leviticus 16:4** over-scoped to specific acts

**Scene 7 [SPECIFIED]:** *“He performed this in holy linen.”*  
**Scene 9 [SPECIFIED]:** same, citing **16:4** alone.

**16:4** governs the **initial** washing and donning at the day’s start. That linen continues through the rite until **16:23**, but the verse itself does not say “while casting lots” or “while laying hands on the scapegoat.” Sequence inference → **constrained**, not **specified** on **16:4** alone.

Same issue on world fact line 16: *“not his gold vestments (which he removes for the service)”* — **16:4** states what to put **on**, not what is taken **off**. Gold-vs-linen belongs under **16:23–24** + Exodus 28, at **constrained** tier.

---

### CITATION TRUTH — **Leviticus 16:17** still stretched in Scene 7 depiction

Scene 3 fact correctly narrows **16:17** to the inner atonement (*“governs the inner sanctum, not the outer court”*). Good.

But **Scene 7 Depicts:** *“Aaron alone, no other priests”* at lot-casting — with **no scene fact** correcting that. **Leviticus 16:17** empties the **tabernacle interior** during holy-place atonement; it does **not** require Aaron alone in the **court** at the door (**16:7**). **Leviticus 16:5** has goats taken *“of the congregation”* — others may be present. The depiction imports **16:17** scope into a court scene the verse does not govern.

### CITATION TRUTH — annual entry **[SPECIFIED]** (minor over-tightening)

**World [SPECIFIED]:** *“entered the most holy place only once a year — on the Day of Atonement”* — **16:34** supports annual atonement; **16:2** supports *“not at all times.”* Fair overall.

Caveat: **16:34** says atonement is made **once a year**, not literally “one physical entry per year.” Leviticus 16 has bullock blood (**16:14**) and goat blood (**16:15**) brought within the veil — likely multiple entries **on that day**. The fact should guard **annual Day of Atonement**, not “single entry per calendar year.”
