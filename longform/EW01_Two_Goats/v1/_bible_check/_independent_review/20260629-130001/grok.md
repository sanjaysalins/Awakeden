## Independent adversarial review — EW01_Two_Goats biblical fact sheet

---

### 1. INTERNAL CONTRADICTIONS (facts vs their own scene descriptions)

**Scene 2 — stone bench**
- *Depicts* line: garments on a **“worn stone bench.”**
- Scene 2 **[CONSTRAINED]**: **“Must NOT show: a carved stone/masonry bench implying a permanent temple.”**
- Those two lines fight each other. A painter following the depiction will violate the guard. The bench is also nowhere in Exodus 26:1 (the only citation for that constraint).

**Scene 3 — colonnade**
- *Depicts* line: **“dim colonnaded passage.”**
- Scene 3 **[SPECIFIED]** guard: **“Must NOT show: … stone colonnade / masonry hall (it is a tent).”**
- Same problem: the canonical scene text asks for exactly what the facts forbid.

**Scene 3 — hanging oil lamps**
- *Depicts*: **“hanging oil lamps.”**
- No cited verse supports wall-hung oil lamps in the holy place. Exodus 25:31–40 / 27:20–21 specify the **golden lampstand** (`menorah`), not pendant lamps. This is unbucketed tradition/artistic detail that can mislead painters.

---

### 2. CITATION TRUTH — claims the KJV does not state

**Gold removal is not explicit in cited verses**
- World **[SPECIFIED]**: *“not his gold vestments (which he **removes** for the service).”*
- Leviticus 16:4 only says wash and **put on** linen. It never says he takes off gold first.
- Lev 16:23–24 implies a change back to “his garments,” but **removal of gold at the start** is inference, not a stated visual fact. Bucket should be **constrained**, not **specified**.

**“Plain white” linen is not biblical**
- World fact admits: *“the colour of the linen is not stated.”*
- Yet Scene 2 visual: **“plain folded white linen”**; Scene 3 depiction: **“plain white linen”**; Scene 7/9 **[SPECIFIED]** visuals assume white.
- KJV says **“holy linen”** — not white. Calling it white (repeated across scenes) is licence dressed as **specified**.

**Leviticus 16:2 does not say “once a year”**
- World **[SPECIFIED]**: *“entered the most holy place only once a year.”*
- Lev 16:2 says **“come not at all times”** — frequency, not calendar.
- **“Once a year”** comes from Lev 16:34 (atonement statute). The claim is supportable only as a **pair** of verses; citing 16:2 alone over-reaches.

**Lev 16:17 scope is narrower than some guards imply**
- Scene 3 **[SPECIFIED]**: solitude *“when he goeth in to make an atonement in the holy place.”*
- That matches the verse.
- Scene 7: **“Aaron alone, no other priests”** during lot-casting at the door — Lev 16:17 triggers on entry **into the holy place for atonement**, which is **after** lots (16:7–8 precede veil entry in 16:12+). Requiring solitude at the lot scene is **not citation-backed**; at most **free/constrained** artistic choice.

**Scene 2 [CONSTRAINED] — weak citation**
- *“Furnishings should read as portable tabernacle, not permanent stone temple furniture.”*
- Cited: Exodus 26:1 (curtain colours). That verse says nothing about bench material. The constraint is reasonable; the citation does not earn it.

**Scene 1 gold vestments “before the linen change”**
- Scene 1 **[CONSTRAINED]**: gold acceptable in open court **before service**.
- Citations: Ex 28:2 + Lev 16:4 + 16:23. None describe what Aaron wears in the court **before** v.4. Inference from 16:23–24, not a stated fact. Acceptable as **constrained**, but overstated as if proven by those three verses alone.

### 4. CHRIST-LENS / TYPOLOGY DISTORTION

**Leviticus 16:10 is missing and mis-framed**
- KJV 16:10: the scapegoat is **“presented alive before the LORD, to make an atonement with him”** before release.
- World **[CONSTRAINED]** frames the rite as slain-goat = **“price paid”** vs live-goat = **carries sin away** only.
- Scripture assigns **“atonement”** language to **both** goats (16:9–10, 16:15–16, 16:21). Silencing 16:10 flattens the type toward “payment vs exile” only and undercuts the live goat’s own atonement role **before the LORD**.

**Blood / incense / mercy-seat work nearly absent**
- Lev 16:12–16: censer, incense cloud on mercy seat, bullock blood seven times, then goat blood — the **substitution-and-access** half the Christ-type rests on.
- World fact mentions sprinkling in one **[CONSTRAINED]** bullet but **no scene facts** guard incense, censer, cloud, or blood on mercy seat. A painter could omit the entire inner rite and still pass scene-level checks.

**Bullock for Aaron omitted**
- Lev 16:3, 6, 11: Aaron’s **young bullock** precedes and parallels the people’s goat (16:15: blood handled **“as he did with the blood of the bullock”**).
- No fact guards this. Omitting the bullock skews the substitution pattern (priest + people).

### 6. WHAT IS SOLID (brief — not the job to praise, but fair to note)

- Two goats together (16:5, 7) — citations match.
- Lot-casting, not pre-slain goat (16:8) — correct.
- Veil colours + cherubim (Ex 26:31) — correct; guard against plain white **veil** is good (ironic given white **linen** is assumed elsewhere).
- Ark behind veil, not in court (Ex 26:33–34; Lev 16:2) — correct.
- Scapegoat: both hands, live, confess, fit man, wilderness (16:21–22) — correct.
- Scapegoat **after** inner reconciliation (16:20) — correct sequencing.
- Goats at **door**, not mandatorily at bronze altar (16:7) — honest **[CONSTRAINED]** correction.
- Historical note on Mishnaic multiple changes — appropriately secondary; does not override Scripture.

---

### 7. SUMMARY

The sheet is **directionally strong** on tabernacle-as-tent, linen vs gold timing, two goats, lots, veil, and scapegoat gestures. But it has **hard internal contradictions** (stone bench, colonnade), **white linen and gold-removal promoted to specified without verse support**, **Lev 16:10 and inner blood/incense/bullock work under-covered** (Christ-type risk), and **16:17 misapplied** to the lot-casting scene.
