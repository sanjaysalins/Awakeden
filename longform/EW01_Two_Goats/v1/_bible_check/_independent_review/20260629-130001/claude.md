# Independent review — claude (OK, 124s)

I checked every cited KJV verse against its claim and every bucket assignment. The citation work is genuinely clean — no fabricated quotes, no misapplied verses, and the honest notes (linen colour not stated, Yoma multi-change as Mishnaic not Scripture) are good. But there are real problems, mostly around coverage and the scene depictions the sheet is guarding.

## Findings

**1. (MOST IMPORTANT — typology) The slain-goat / blood-on-the-mercy-seat half has no fact-guarded scene.**
World-fact #8 explicitly warns: *"do not depict only the live scapegoat and silently drop the slain goat / blood within the veil (the substitution the Christ-type rests on)."* Yet the scenes given facts here (1, 2, 3, 7, 9) show only: establishment → laying aside gold → at the veil → casting lots → **sending the live goat away**. Not one depicts the goat for the LORD killed (Lev 16:9, 16:15) or its blood sprinkled on the mercy seat (Lev 16:15–16). The sheet states the principle but the actual coverage commits the exact omission it forbids — the "price paid" half that carries the cross typology is visually absent. Either a slain-goat/blood scene exists in scenes 4–6/8 and needs its own fact entry, or it's missing and must be added. This is the Christ-lens risk for the whole episode.

**2. The fact sheet is forced to forbid its own scenes' described architecture — a sign the scene plan is wrong, not just at-risk.**
- Scene 2 *depicts* "a worn **stone** bench" — then the fact must say *"Must NOT show: a carved stone/masonry bench implying a permanent temple."*
- Scene 3 *depicts* "a dim **colonnaded passage**" and "**colonnade**" — then the fact must say *"Must NOT show: ... stone colonnade / masonry hall (it is a tent)."*
The guards are correct (it was a curtained tent — Ex 26:1, 27:9), but a sheet that has to negate the elements its own scene descriptions specify means the scene plan should be revised to drop "stone bench" / "colonnaded passage," not merely guarded against them.

**3. Scene 1's gold-before-the-people rests on tradition more than the scene-level citation admits.**
Scene 1 shows Aaron "in golden vestments" before the multitude at first light, bucketed CONSTRAINED with Ex 28:2 / Lev 16:4 / 16:23. None of those verses place Aaron in gold in the court before the rite — Lev 16 opens with him washing and putting on *linen* (16:4); the gold-then-linen sequence on the Day itself is Mishnaic (Yoma) reconstruction. World-fact #3's historical note is honest about this, but the Scene 1 entry should say plainly that Aaron-in-gold during this day's rite is tradition/licence, not a specified biblical fact, so the painting isn't read as Scripture asserting it.

**4. Minor — Scene 2 "metal crown" warning is slightly off-target.**
The laid-aside gold set legitimately *included* a gold plate on the mitre (the "holy crown," Ex 28:36–37, 39:30). "Must NOT show: a metal crown in place of the linen mitre" reads oddly in the one scene that is showing the gold attire. Reword to target a *European/royal* crown, since a gold frontlet/plate is actually correct here.
