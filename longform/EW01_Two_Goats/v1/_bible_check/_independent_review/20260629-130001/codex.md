# Independent review — codex (OK, 154s)

**Findings**

- **Invented linen colour.** The sheet admits “the colour of the linen is not stated,” but Scene 2 says “plain folded white linen” and “Must NOT show: the atonement linen shown as dyed/coloured cloth”; Scene 3 is titled “Plain white linen.” Leviticus 16:4 specifies **linen**, not white/undyed/plain-colour. Material is specified; colour is licence.

- **Gold-vestment claims are over-cited.** “The high priest’s ordinary garments ... were the gold vestments described in Exodus 28” cites Exodus 28:2 and 28:4, but those quoted verses do not say “gold.” Exodus 28:4 lists garments; 28:2 says “glory and beauty.” The gold detail needs better citations, and “worn before” the linen service is inferred, not directly stated by Leviticus 16.

- **Scene 1’s “first light” and pre-service gold are not biblical facts.** “Gold ordinary vestments acceptable in the open court at first light, before the linen change” is possible staging, but Exodus 28:2 + Leviticus 16:4/23 do not state first light or explicitly narrate Aaron wearing gold immediately before the rite.

- **Scene 2 contradicts the Tabernacle constraint.** The scene depicts garments on “a worn stone bench,” but its own fact says furnishings should “read as portable/wood, not carved masonry” and “Must NOT show: a carved stone/masonry bench implying a permanent temple.” This is an internal contradiction and risks temple imagery.

- **Scene 3’s “dim colonnaded passage” is dangerous.** The fact correctly says the veil is a curtain and “Must NOT show ... stone colonnade / masonry hall,” but the scene description says “colonnaded passage.” If those are not clearly tent posts/pillars, this violates the Tabernacle setting.

- **Hanging lamps are unsupported and probably wrong.** Scene 3 says “hanging oil lamps.” The sheet gives no guard for this. The Tabernacle’s light source is the lampstand/candlestick, not unspecified hanging lamps. Add a constraint or remove that visual.

- **“Entered ... only once a year” is too imprecise.** Leviticus 16:2 says Aaron must not come “at all times”; Leviticus 16:34 says atonement is made “once a year.” Those support an annual rite, not necessarily one physical crossing of the veil. Leviticus 16:12-15 includes incense, bullock blood, and goat blood operations within the veil. Avoid wording like “only once” if it implies a single entry event.

- **Missing major specified visual: incense/censer.** For any veil/inner-sanctum scene, Leviticus 16:12-13 explicitly requires Aaron to take a censer of coals and incense within the veil so the cloud covers the mercy seat. Scene 3 shows him at the veil with no such constraint, leaving a major checkable fact unguarded.

- **Lots: “no readable text” is invented.** Scene 7 says the lot markings must be “illegible” and “Must NOT show: readable text on the lots.” Leviticus 16:8 only says one lot was for the LORD and the other for the scapegoat. Scripture does not forbid readable labels or markings.

- **“Aaron alone” is over-applied outside the tent.** Scene 7 says “Aaron alone, no other priests.” Leviticus 16:17 only excludes other men from the tabernacle while he makes atonement inside. It does not require the lot-casting scene in the court to be priestless.

- **Scene 9 adds posture details as if specified.** “Living, standing goat” and “head bowed in confession” go beyond Leviticus 16:21. The verse specifies both hands, the live goat, and confession; standing posture and bowed head are licence.

- **The “price paid” wording is theological interpretation, not cited fact.** The two-goat fact is broadly right, but “the ‘price paid’ half” and “the substitution the Christ-type rests on” are not stated in Leviticus 16:9, 15-16. Keep that as a theological note, not a Scripture-cited visual fact.

- **Coverage gap: Aaron’s bullock for himself/house.** Leviticus 16:6, 11, and 14 specify Aaron’s bullock and blood before the people’s goat blood. If the set covers the rite sequence, the fact sheet should guard against implying the two goats are the whole atoning blood ritual.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove or demote invented specifics: white/undyed linen, illegible lots, first light, bowed head, standing goat.
2. Fix scene contradictions: stone bench, colonnaded passage, and hanging lamps must not imply a stone temple or non-tabernacle furnishings.
3. Add missing Leviticus 16 constraints for the incense/censer and Aaron’s bullock/self-atonement before the people’s goat.
