## Findings

**1. Scene 2 — "golden breastplate / ephod" risks a plain-gold-slab render (coverage gap).**
The depiction calls the items "a golden breastplate, ephod and priestly garments" and the narration beat is "I laid aside gold." But Exodus 28:15 (which you quote) says the breastplate was made "of gold, of blue, and of purple, and of scarlet, and of fine twined linen" — a coloured weave shot with gold and set with **twelve stones** (28:21), not a solid gold object. The ephod likewise (Ex 28:6). Your visual note says "a breastplate (twelve set stones)" — good — but the **Must NOT show** only guards against a European crown. Nothing stops the painter rendering a plain gold breastplate with no stones and no colour. Add a guard: the breastplate must read as twelve distinct set stones on a coloured (blue/purple/scarlet) field, not a blank gold plate.

**2. Scene 8 — the incense cloud must COVER (obscure) the mercy seat, not merely float nearby.**
You cite Lev 16:13 correctly: "that the cloud of the incense may cover the mercy seat... that he die not." The whole purpose is concealment. But the depiction shows "the faint golden glow of the mercy seat" and the constraint only says "incense cloud over/before the mercy seat." A painting that shows the mercy seat clearly visible with a wisp of smoke nearby technically contradicts 16:13's "cover... that he die not." Sharpen the Must-NOT to: the mercy seat must be substantially veiled/obscured by the cloud, not displayed in the open.

**3. Scene 8 — "seven times" is fine, but verify the reader doesn't mis-apply it.**
Minor: your phrasing "sprinkled... upon the mercy seat, and before the mercy seat seven times" is actually correct to Lev 16:14 (the seven-times attaches to *before* the mercy seat; the *upon... eastward* sprinkle is the single one). No change needed — flagging only so it isn't "corrected" into an error later. The SPECIFIED bucket here is defensible because the goat inherits the manner via 16:15 "as he did with the blood of the bullock."

**Non-issues I checked and cleared:** the killing of the LORD's goat is correctly assigned to Aaron (Lev 16:15 "he shall kill"), not over-claimed; the scapegoat is correctly sent "by the hand of a fit man" (16:21), not by Aaron — both match the kind of witness-over-claim error logged for this episode. Scarlet-thread correctly excluded as Mishnaic. Gold-before-rite correctly bucketed FREE. Veil colours and cherubim correctly SPECIFIED. Christ-lens intact.
