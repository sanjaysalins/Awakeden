## Independent adversarial review — EW01 biblical fact sheet

Overall: citation discipline is **much better than average** on linen/gold honesty, ark placement, two-goat mechanics, and Mishnaic scarlet-thread rejection. The sheet still has **coverage holes**, a few **citation stretches**, and **scene-plan mismatches** that leave high-risk paintings unguarded.

### CITATION TRUTH

**1. Scene 8 — “seven times” marked [SPECIFIED] over-reaches Lev 16:15**

Claim: *“sprinkled with the finger upon the mercy seat, and before the mercy seat **seven times**”* citing **16:15 + 16:14**.

**16:15** says sprinkle upon and before the mercy seat; it does **not** say seven times. Seven is explicit only for the **bullock** in **16:14** (“as he did with the blood of the bullock” imports it). That inference is reasonable, but it is **not** a direct KJV statement under 16:15. This belongs in **constrained** (“as with the bullock, seven times”) not **specified** as if 16:15 states it.

**2. Scene 7 — “linen continues through the rite” over-reads Lev 16:4**

Claim: *“He performed the rite in linen (donning stated at Lev 16:4; **it continues through the rite**).”*

**16:4** only commands putting **on** linen. **16:23–24** shows Aaron **removes** linen after the live-goat step and **puts on** ordinary garments before the burnt offerings. “Continues through the rite” is true for lot-casting / inner work / scapegoat, but **false** if “rite” means the whole chapter. The citation does not support the continuity claim by itself.

**3. World fact — linen “removed them again afterward” compresses sequence**

Claim: wore linen *“and removed them again afterward”* (**16:4 + 16:23**).

**16:23** is **after** scapegoat confession (**16:21–22**), not immediately after veil/blood work. The KJV supports removal, but the fact’s wording can mislead artists to show a linen→gold change right after exiting the veil—**before** the live-goat beat Scene 9 depicts. Sequence under-cited.

**4. Scene 3 — Lev 16:17 applied at the veil threshold is borderline**

Claim [SPECIFIED]: *“no man is in the TABERNACLE… when he goeth in to make an atonement.”*

**16:17** governs the period when he is **making atonement in the holy place**, not necessarily every pre-entry pause at the veil. Using it to require solitude at the threshold is **staging inference**, not an explicit verse about the threshold moment. The outer-court gloss (“does not govern the outer court”) is good; the **specified** bucket here is aggressive.

**5. Mostly solid (credit where due)**

- Two goats + lots (**16:5, 7, 8**): supported.
- Linen garments + washing (**16:4**): supported; colour caution is honest.
- Ark behind veil (**Ex 26:33–34, Lev 16:2**): supported; “never in open court” is valid constraint from placement texts.
- Live goat: both hands, fit man, wilderness (**16:21–22**): supported.
- Scapegoat after reconciliation (**16:20**): supported.
- Incense cloud before mercy seat (**16:12–13**): supported.
- Mercy-seat cherubim (**Ex 25:18, 20**): supported (“wings on high… faces one to another” matches KJV).

### NO INVENTED FACTS / BUCKET HONESTY

**6. Scene 2 — Exodus 28 garment inventory in [SPECIFIED] dresses up licence**

Facts describe ephod/breastplate/gold plate as **[SPECIFIED]** for a still-life of “laid-aside gold.” **Exodus 28** describes priestly garb; it does **not** say Aaron wore gold that morning and laid it on a bench. The sheet correctly marks the tableau as **[FREE]** below—but the **[SPECIFIED]** garment inventory still reads like Scripture mandates that **staging**. Safer bucket: **constrained** (“if gold vestments are shown, they must match Ex 28”), not **specified** (“the service included laying these aside”).

**7. Scene 2 — washing [SPECIFIED] but visual guidance drops the guard**

**16:4** requires wash **then** put on linen. Visual line: *“Plain folded linen waiting beside the laid-aside gold (the change follows washing).”* No **Must NOT show: linen donned without washing**; a painter can skip washing and still satisfy the written visual note.

**8. Gold-before-rite honesty is good — keep it**

Scene 1 **[FREE]** for gold vestments + Mishnaic note in world facts: correctly separates tradition from Leviticus 16. That part is well bucketed.

**9. Scarlet-thread rejection is excellent**

Scene 9 **[CONSTRAINED]**: *“Scripture names no marker on the scapegoat; a scarlet thread… is later tradition (Mishnah Yoma), not biblical.”* Correct; citations **16:21–22** appropriately support what is **not** there.

### HISTORY STAYS SECONDARY

**10. Theological notes are mostly labeled — one tension remains**

World fact historical note: slain goat as *“price paid”* alongside scapegoat *“sin carried away”* is marked secondary and supports Christ-typology without overriding Leviticus. Fine.

**11. Scene 6 (in plan, **no facts here**) is the biggest historical/biblical risk**

Narration/scene plan: *“I buried two of my own sons… Nadab and Abihu… cousins carried their bodies… forbidden to rend my clothes or leave my place.”*

That beat needs **Lev 10:1–7** and **Lev 16:1** (“after the death of the two sons of Aaron…”). With **zero scene facts**, painters can easily show Aaron carrying bodies, mourning openly, or deaths “inside the veil”—all contradicted by text. The fact sheet is silent where the episode is most fact-sensitive.

### CHRIST-LENS

**12. Principle stated, coverage still uneven**

World constraint: *“do not depict only the live scapegoat and silently drop the slain goat / blood within the veil.”* Scene 8 facts help. But scenes **4–5** (mercy seat / divine cloud) lack scene-level guards tying **blood + incense + divine cloud** together—the typology’s “both halves” can still collapse into mood-shot mysticism without the slain-goat/blood half visible anywhere else in the OT block.

**13. Missing Lev 16:10 weakens the two-goat witness**

**16:10**: the scapegoat is **“presented alive before the LORD”** while the LORD’s lot-goat is offered (**16:9**). No fact guards *alive presentation at the door* immediately after lots—easy to show the wrong goat dead at the wrong moment and break the dual-goat typology the episode is about.

### COVERAGE (most serious block)

**14. Scene facts stop at 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9 — plan has 25 scenes**

Unfactored OT beats include:

|---|---|

**15. Scene 5 — cloud confusion unguarded at the scene that needs it**

World fact correctly notes incense cloud (**16:13**) and divine cloud (**16:2**) are **distinct**. Scene 5 depicts *“a luminous cloud of divine presence above”* the mercy seat. Without a Scene 5 fact, artists can (a) show **only** Aaron’s incense and call it divine presence, or (b) omit incense entirely in inner-sanctum scenes that require it (**16:12–13**). The world-level “show incense cloud” visual line can even **fight** Scene 5’s divine-cloud intent if read globally.

**16. Missing Lev 16:18–19 (altar blood before live goat)**

**16:18–19**: blood on altar horns + altar sprinkled seven times **before** **16:20** brings the live goat. No fact anywhere. Scene 6’s memory tableau includes *“an altar with a basin of blood and rising flame”* with no sequence guard—can imply atonement finished at the altar alone, dropping inner-veil blood the sheet elsewhere insists on.

**17. Missing Lev 16:12 source — bronze altar**

Coals are taken *“from off the altar before the LORD”* (**16:12**; **Ex 27:1–2** bronze altar in court). Scene 8 shows a censer but not that coals come from the **court bronze altar**, not the mercy seat/ark.

**18. Scene plan vs fact sheet conflict on goat location (Scene 7)**

Fact sheet [CONSTRAINED]: goats at *“the door of the tabernacle… **not specifically at the bronze altar**.”*  
Scene plan `subject_block`: *“Two goats standing close together **before the bronze altar**.”*  
The fact sheet guards the text, but **does not flag that the approved scene plan already contradicts Lev 16:7**—production can render the wrong location while the fact sheet looks clean.

**19. Missing congregation / calendar guards for crowd scenes**

**16:29–31**: tenth day, afflict souls, Sabbath rest. Scene 1 **[FREE]** allows *“vast multitude”* and *“first light”* with no constraint that people should appear afflicting/resting, not festival-celebration chaos.

**20. Missing post-scapegoat garment change**

**16:23–24**: after sending the goat, Aaron removes linen and resumes glorious garments for burnt offerings. No guard—art can show gold vestments immediately exiting the veil (before Scene 9) or linen at the burnt-offering altar.

**21. Positive additions since earlier drafts (do not drop)**

Mercy-seat cherubim fact (**Ex 25:18–20**), veil colour/cherubim (**Ex 26:31**), double-ram distinction (**16:3** vs **16:5**), and no scarlet thread—these are real improvements.

### Summary judgment

The sheet is **strong on core Leviticus 16 mechanics** (linen, two goats, lots, ark, scapegoat sequence, anti-Mishnaic markers) and unusually honest about gold-before-rite licence. It **fails as a complete guard set** for the actual scene plan: critical scenes **4–6** are naked, **16:10** and **16:18–19** are absent, Scene 8 over-specifies “seven times,” and Scene 5’s divine/incense cloud distinction is not scene-anchored. Scene 6’s Nadab/Abihu memory is the highest-risk omission given the narration’s factual history.
