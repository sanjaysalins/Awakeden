## Independent adversarial review — EW01_Two_Goats biblical fact sheet

### CITATION TRUTH — over-reach / weak citations

**1. Exodus 28:36 alone does not prove the plate is on the mitre.**  
Scene 2 **[SPECIFIED]**: *"a gold plate (the holy crown) was on the mitre"* cites only Exodus 28:36 (*"And thou shalt make a plate of pure gold… HOLINESS TO THE LORD"*). Placement on the mitre is Exodus 28:37 (*"that it may be upon the mitre"*). The claim is true; the citation set is incomplete/misapplied.

**2. Leviticus 16:16 does not state blood-sprinkling.**  
Scene 8 **[SPECIFIED]** *"sprinkled upon and before the mercy seat"* adds Lev 16:16, which speaks of atonement for the holy place, not sprinkling mechanics. Lev 16:15 carries the visual claim; 16:16 is padding that could imply a verse says something it does not.

**3. Leviticus 16:17 gloss narrows the text.**  
Scene 3 **[SPECIFIED]**: *"(this governs the inner sanctum, not the outer court)"* — Lev 16:17 says *"no man in the tabernacle of the congregation"* while he is in *"the holy place"* making atonement. That is the whole tent, including the lampstand room, not only the inner sanctum behind the veil. The parenthetical misstates scope and could license other priests in the holy place during atonement.

**4. World fact [SPECIFIED] parenthetical synthesizes the chapter, not the cited verses alone.**  
*"several blood-entries occur on that one day"* — Lev 16:2 and 16:34 support *once a year* and *not at all times*; the multiple entries are a chapter-level inference. Fine as commentary; overstated as **[SPECIFIED]** from those two verses alone.

**5. Exodus 28:2 does not establish gold vestments in Scene 1.**  
Scene 1 **[CONSTRAINED]**: *"Aaron's gold vestments here in the open court… are his regular Exodus 28 garments"* — Exodus 28:2 says *"for glory and for beauty"*; it does not say Aaron wore those garments on the Day of Atonement before changing. Lev 16:4 is the chapter's opening garment instruction (linen). The historical note admits gold-in-court is *"narrative/traditional staging, not stated by Leviticus 16"* — but the bucket still treats gold as biblically licensed here.

### NO INVENTED FACTS / TRADITION IN WRONG BUCKET

**6. Scene 1 gold vestments are tradition presented as constraint, not licence.**  
The sheet honestly labels gold-before-rite as Mishnaic/traditional in Historical notes, yet Scene 1 and world facts mark gold in court as **[CONSTRAINED]** *"acceptable ONLY here."* That dresses staging tradition as text-safe depiction guidance. Should be **[FREE]** (or at minimum constrained *"do not contradict Lev 16:4 linen for the rite"*, not *"gold is acceptable"*).

**7. "Plain white linen" vs own color disclaimer.**  
World fact line 54: *"The COLOUR of the linen is not stated… do not treat whiteness as a doctrine."*  
Scene 3 title: *"Plain white linen, like a servant"* and multiple visuals say *"plain linen"* without guarding whiteness. The title asserts a colour Scripture never gives. Contradicts the sheet's best line on linen.

**8. Scene 8 "bronze basin" is unstated detail.**  
Depiction: *"shallow bronze basin of blood"* — no verse names the vessel. Not fatal if **[FREE]**; absent from buckets, so it reads as implied biblical furniture.

**9. Scene 1 "vast hushed multitude" / "first light"** — not in any fact.  
Leviticus 16 does not describe crowd scale or time of day for this beat. Scene prose treats them as settled; no **[FREE]** guard. Minor, but it is unmarked artistic licence.

### COVERAGE — missing guards painters will need

**20. Scenes 4, 5, 6 missing.**  
Sheet jumps Scene 3 → Scene 7. If those scenes exist in `scene_plan.json`, they have zero biblical guards.

**21. Leviticus 16:29–31 — seventh month, tenth day, rest, affliction of souls.**  
Nothing pins the calendar identity of Yom Kippur. Easy to depict as generic priestly day (partly mitigated by "once a year" facts).

**22. Finger sprinkling (Lev 16:14–15).**  
KJV: *"sprinkle it with his finger… seven times"* / goat blood *"as he did with the blood of the bullock."* Scene 8 depicts *"arm raised to sprinkle"* with no fact forbidding pouring or non-finger sprinkling.

**23. Incense cloud required but Scene 8 depiction omits it.**  
World + Scene 8 **[CONSTRAINED]**: mercy seat approach must show incense cloud (Lev 16:12–13). Scene 8 *Depicts* line: *"thick darkness toward the faint golden glow of the mercy seat"* — no censer/cloud. Internal contradiction between facts and scene brief.

**24. Altar reconciliation before scapegoat (Lev 16:20).**  
Fact correctly says scapegoat follows reconciling *"holy place, tabernacle, and altar"* — but only guards against showing live goat *inside* most holy place. No guard for showing hands-on-goat before altar reconciliation, or conflating altar-step with veil-step.

**25. Where the LORD's goat is killed.**  
Lev 16:9/15 — sin offering killed before blood enters veil (parallel to bullock). No fact prevents depicting slaughter inside the veil or skipping slaughter entirely (Scene 8 shows only intraveil blood).

**26. Lev 16:24 — revesting in ordinary garments after linen removal.**  
16:23 is covered; 16:24 (*wash, put on garments, come out*) is not, if any closing scene shows Aaron post-rite.

**27. Divine cloud on mercy seat (Lev 16:2).**  
*"I will appear in the cloud upon the mercy seat"* — distinct from incense cloud (16:13). Inner scenes could show only incense and miss the theophany cloud the text names.
