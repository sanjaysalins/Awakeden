# Independent review — claude (OK, 119s)

## Independent adversarial review — EW01_Two_Goats biblical fact sheet

I checked every cited verse against its claim and every bucket assignment. This is a careful, honest sheet — buckets are mostly right, tradition is flagged as tradition (the Mishnaic gold/linen staging note is exemplary), and the Christ-type is guarded well (the slain-goat-half guard at World fact 8 stops the painting set from collapsing the two-goat typology into "the live goat alone atoned"). No invented facts sit in the `specified` bucket, and no citation is misapplied. That said, here are the real problems.

### Findings

**1. "(young goats)" is an over-read of the cited verse — World fact 4 + Scene 7.**
The sheet glosses "two kids of the goats" (Lev 16:5) as "**young goats**" in a `SPECIFIED` fact and tells the painter to "show TWO goats together (young goats)." KJV "kids of the goats" (Heb. *se'irim* / *se'ir izzim*) is the standard idiom for **male/he-goats**, not baby kids. Telling the painter "young goats" risks tiny juvenile/baby goats being rendered. The specified part — *two goats together* — is correct; the "young" gloss is interpretation creeping into a `specified` line. Drop "(young goats)" or change to "two male goats."

**2. Coverage gap — the mercy seat's form is depicted but unguarded.** Scene 8 depicts "the faint golden glow of the mercy seat" and World fact 6 places the ark/mercy seat behind the veil, but nothing guards what the mercy seat actually *is*: a gold lid with **two cherubim of beaten gold, wings stretched upward, faces toward each other** (Ex 25:18–20). A painter could render a plain gold slab, a single cherub, or winged figures facing outward, and no fact catches it. Mitigated only by the scene deliberately keeping it dim/incense-veiled — but if it reads at all, it should read right. Add a `constrained` mercy-seat fact (Ex 25:18–20).

**3. Internal tension a careless painter will misread — incense over the ark vs. "ark not an altar."** World fact 6 forbids "the ark used as an altar with **fire/incense on it**," while World fact 10 *requires* "the cloud of the incense may cover the mercy seat" (Lev 16:13), and the mercy seat sits **on** the ark. So a correct image (censer-smoke cloud over the mercy seat) and a forbidden image (ark as fire-altar) look adjacent. Clarify in World fact 6 that the *censer cloud covering the mercy seat is correct*; what's forbidden is treating the ark itself as a burning altar.

**4. Minor citation imprecision — "several blood-entries" (World fact 7).** Lev 16 supports **two** blood sprinklings within the veil on the day (bullock, v14; goat, v15) plus a separate incense entry (v12–13). "Several blood-entries" loosely overstates; the text gives two blood entries. Tighten to "more than one" or "two blood-entries."

### Things that are correctly handled (so they don't get "fixed" wrongly)
- Veil = blue/purple/scarlet + cherubim, **not** plain white (Ex 26:31) — correctly distinguished from Aaron's plain linen garments despite Scene 3's "Plain white linen" title. Good catch built in.
- Linen colour is honestly left unspecified, not asserted as white doctrine.
- `gold-plate / frontlet`, not a European crown (Ex 28:36) — correct.
- "Both hands" on the live goat (Lev 16:21) and scapegoat **sent, not slain** — correctly guarded (this is exactly the kind of witness-claim that's gone wrong before in this corpus; here it's locked down).
- Bullock-for-himself-first (mediator, not substitute) — guarded. Christ-lens intact.

None of the findings are doctrinal errors, invented `specified` facts, or Christ-lens distortions — they are over-glossing, one genuine coverage gap, and a clarity tension. The sheet is sound enough to paint from, but #1 and #2 should be fixed before it's called done.

VERDICT: PASS
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the "(young goats)" gloss from the `SPECIFIED` two-goats fact (World fact 4 / Scene 7) — KJV "kids of the goats" = male he-goats, not baby kids; the gloss adds a claim the verse doesn't make.
2. Add a `constrained` mercy-seat fact (Ex 25:18–20: gold lid, two beaten-gold cherubim facing each other, wings upward) — Scene 8 depicts the mercy seat but nothing guards its form.
3. Resolve the incense/ark tension in World fact 6 — state that the censer cloud *over* the mercy seat is required (Lev 16:13) and only the ark-as-fire-altar is forbidden.
