# Independent review — cursor (OK, 115s)

## Independent adversarial review — EW01 biblical fact sheet

Overall: the Leviticus 16 core (two goats, lots, linen rite, blood within the veil, live goat sent away, bullock-for-Aaron-first) is mostly well anchored, and several historical caveats (gold before linen = tradition, linen colour unstated) show good self-awareness. The sheet still has citation over-reach, bucket slippage, Christ-typology gaps, and large coverage holes relative to the full scene plan.

---

### CITATION TRUTH — claims the cited KJV does not fully support

**1. World fact — “removed them again afterward” (linen garments)**  
Cited: Lev 16:4, 16:23.  
Lev 16:23 says Aaron puts off the linen **inside** the tabernacle and **leaves them there** — not that he “removed them again” in a general sense. Lev 16:24 adds washing **in the holy place** and putting on his (non-linen) garments. The composite claim is directionally right but slightly overstates 16:23 alone; “leave them there” is a specified visual the sheet never guards.

**2. Scene 2 — SPECIFIED: “For the service he put on holy linen garments, having first washed his flesh in water”**  
Lev 16:4 supports **wash → put on linen**. It does **not** support the scene’s central visual — gold breastplate/ephod **laid aside on a bench** — nor any prior wearing of gold that day. Lev 16 opens with the linen change; gold-first is acknowledged elsewhere as “narrative/traditional staging,” not Leviticus 16.

**3. Scene 2 — SPECIFIED: Exodus 28 garments on the bench**  
Exodus 28:4/15/21/36 describe what the high priest’s **ordinary** garments **are**, not that Lev 16 depicts him laying them on a bench. Citation truth is fine for garment identity; it is **misapplied** if read as “Scripture specifies this change scene.”

**4. Scene 3 — SPECIFIED: Lev 16:17 solitude at the veil threshold**  
Lev 16:17: “there shall be **no man in the tabernacle of the congregation** when he goeth in to make an atonement **in the holy place**…”  
That is a **during-the-inner-atonement** rule for the whole tent, not a general “Aaron alone at the threshold before entry” rule. Applying it to Scene 3 (he is arrested at the veil, not yet in the inner rite) **over-extends** the verse’s timing. Solitude is plausible staging; it is not **specified** for that exact moment.

**5. Scene 8 — SPECIFIED: blood sprinkled “upon and before the mercy seat”**  
Primary support is Lev 16:15 (good). Lev 16:16 is about **making atonement for** the holy place/tabernacle because of Israel’s uncleanness — not the act of sprinkling blood. Secondary citation is loose.

**6. Scene 8 depiction — “shallow bronze basin” / “arm raised to sprinkle”**  
Lev 16:15 says bring blood within the veil and sprinkle; it does **not** specify bronze, a basin, or arm motion. Lev 16:14–15 specify sprinkling **with his finger** (**seven times** eastward upon the mercy seat and before it; goat blood “as he did with the blood of the bullock”). A raised arm with a basin tends toward **pour/ladle**, which the text does not say and may contradict **finger** sprinkling.

**7. Scene 7 — Must NOT: “a sheep or bull in the goats’ place”**  
Correct for the **two-goat** moment. But Lev 16:5 explicitly requires **one ram for a burnt offering** — a sheep. The guard is right in context; the sheet never adds a positive fact that a **ram** belongs to the same day, so painters could wrongly treat all sheep as errors.

**8. World fact — “several blood-entries occur on that one day”**  
Supported by the chapter (bullock 16:14, goat 16:15, altar 16:18–19). Good — but the sheet never turns Lev 16:18–19 into an explicit constrained fact, so “several entries” is asserted without guarding the **altar-horns** phase that 16:20 makes prerequisite to the live goat.

### NO INVENTED FACTS / TRADITION IN THE WRONG BUCKET

**9. “Plain white linen” vs the sheet’s own guard**  
World fact correctly: “The COLOUR of the linen is not stated… do not treat whiteness as a doctrine.”  
Scene 3 is titled **“Plain white linen, like a servant”** and the scene header repeats “plain linen” while the narration/scene_plan push **white**. That is licence (or modern default) dressed as scene identity while the sheet forbids it elsewhere — internal contradiction painters will follow.

**10. Scene 1 — Aaron in golden vestments at first light with the multitude**  
Historical note honestly flags gold in court as **not stated by Leviticus 16**. Bucket is **CONSTRAINED**, not SPECIFIED — acceptable **if** artists treat it as staging. Risk: Scene 1 reads like the opening beat of the rite; Lev 16:4 begins with **linen after washing**, not a gold-to-linen parade in open court.

**11. Scene 2 still-life = implied biblical sequence**  
“I laid aside gold and glory” is **narrative staging**, not Lev 16. Washing + linen is biblical; **bench, laid-aside gold, hands releasing glory** are not. Those visuals belong in **FREE/constrained (do not contradict)** with an explicit “not in Lev 16” label, not under SPECIFIED facts fed by 16:4 alone.

**12. Bronze vessel for lots (Scene 7)**  
Correctly marked **FREE** for lot form. The scene description still fixes a **bronze vessel** — fine as licence, but don’t let it bleed into “bronze basin of blood” in Scene 8 as if Leviticus specifies temple metalware for blood.

### BUCKETS NOT HONEST

**13. Scene 2 Exodus 28 block in SPECIFIED**  
Should be **CONSTRAINED** (“if gold garments are shown laid aside, they must match Ex 28”) or split: SPECIFIED only for items the text names; **FREE** for the change tableau itself.

**14. Scene 3 Lev 16:17 in SPECIFIED**  
Should be **CONSTRAINED** for inner-atonement moments (Scenes 4–5, 8), not SPECIFIED for pre-entry threshold staging.

**15. World fact — slain-goat half “price paid”**  
The theological pairing is in **Historical (secondary)** — good. But it is not backed by Lev 16:9/15/16 alone as wording (“price paid”); that is typological language. Keep it secondary; do not let it migrate into SPECIFIED.

### HISTORY vs SCRIPTURE

**16. Gold/linen historical notes are good — but fight the scene plan**  
The sheet repeatedly warns gold-before-linen is Mishnaic/traditional. That is honest. It does **not** fully neutralize Scene 1/2 depict blocks that still **require** gold-first iconography. Historical notes should govern bucket placement, not only footnotes.

**17. No override detected**  
I do not see a historical_note flatly contradicting KJV text. The weakness is **under-correction**: tradition still drives key scenes while buckets claim Leviticus support.

### CHRIST-LENS / TYPOLOGY GAPS

**18. Missing Lev 16:27 — slain sin-offering bodies outside the camp**  
Lev 16:27 (bullock + **goat whose blood was brought within the veil**) must be carried **forth without the camp** and burned. The narration explicitly ties this to Hebrews 13 / “outside the gate,” and distinguishes it from the **live** scapegoat.  
**No fact guards:** (a) slain goat burned **outside camp**, (b) scapegoat **not** burned, (c) do not conflate “wilderness” (16:21–22) with “without the camp” (16:27). That is a major typology guard missing.

**19. Missing Lev 16:10 — scapegoat “presented alive before the LORD”**  
Before the bullock/goat blood sequence completes, the lot-chosen scapegoat is **presented alive before the LORD** (16:10). The sheet only treats the live goat at 16:20–21 (after altar reconciliation). Painters could show the scapegoat only at the wilderness edge and miss the **alive-before-LORD** beat — weakening the two-goats-in-one-offering logic.

**20. Altar reconciliation (16:18–19) omitted**  
16:20 explicitly waits until he has finished reconciling **holy place, tabernacle, and altar**. No fact prevents showing scapegoat confession **before** altar blood or skipping altar horns entirely — both would distort the chapter’s order and the “finished work” shape the Christ-type needs.

### COVERAGE — obvious checkable facts MISSING

**21. Only Scenes 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9 are covered** (~6 of 25). Unguarded high-risk scenes include:  
- **Scene 4–5** — entry within veil; **Lev 16:2 divine cloud** on mercy seat vs **16:13 incense cloud** (two different clouds; easy to merge wrong).  
- **Scene 5** — ark/mercy seat/cherubim (Ex 25 / 26:34).  
- **Scene 10** — goat in wilderness / land not inhabited (partially covered via Scene 9).  
- **Scene 11** — unified slain + live goat (needs 16:5 “one sin offering… two kids”).  
- **Scene 13** — Lev 16:30 same-day cleansing / 16:31 afflict souls (people should not look festive in violation of “afflict your souls”).  
- **Scene 14+** — repetition, burnt offerings (16:24–25), etc.

**22. Missing calendar fact — Lev 16:29**  
“**Tenth day of the seventh month**” + afflict souls / sabbath rest. Scene 1 “first light” + Scene 13 “relieved… unburdened” have no guard against wrong season or tone vs “afflict your souls.”

**23. Missing ram / burnt offering (16:3, 16:5, 16:24–25)**  
Two rams are in the chapter (Aaron’s and the people’s). No guard for a lawful burnt offering on the same day.

**24. Missing Lev 16:3 young bullock**  
Aaron enters with a **young** bullock — minor but checkable.

**25. Missing finger + seven sprinklings (16:14–15)**  
High-risk for Scene 8 if rendered as arm/ladle pour.

**26. Missing Lev 16:24 wash location**  
He washes **in the holy place** after removing linen — relevant if any post-rite scene shows washing.

**27. Missing bronze altar in court**  
Exodus 27 / Lev 16:12 coals “from off the altar before the LORD” — scenes with smoke/altar (7, 11, 14) lack “bronze altar in court” guards while forbidding wrong placements.

**28. Scene 3 vs its own lighting fact**  
Fact: holy place lit by **golden lampstand** (Ex 25:31, 27:20), must NOT use ad-hoc hanging lamps. Scene 3 header in the artifact says **“golden lampstand”** — good. (Scene plan still says colonnade + hanging lamps; the fact sheet partially fixes that in the artifact text but coverage is scene-local only.)
