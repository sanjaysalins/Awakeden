# Independent review — grok (OK, 66s)

Verifying key Scripture citations and the source scene plan against the fact sheet.
## Independent review — EW01_Two_Goats biblical fact sheet

### 1. CITATION TRUTH — over-reach and misapplied verses

**“Plain white linen” / “undyed white linen” (repeated SPECIFIED)**  
Leviticus 16:4 names a **holy linen coat, breeches, girdle, and mitre**. It never says *white*, *undyed*, or *plain*.  
- World fact visual: *“simple undyed white linen”*  
- Scenes 2, 3, 7, 9: *“plain white linen”* / *“Plain folded white linen”*  

That colour is tradition, not quoted text. The cited verse supports **linen holy garments**, not **white**.

---

**Scene 1 [CONSTRAINED] — gold vestments “acceptable here… before the linen change”**  
Only citation: Leviticus 16:4. That verse says what Aaron **puts on** for the service (linen). It does **not** say he wore gold first, that gold belongs to the outer court, or that gold is allowed in Scene 1.  
A gold-robed Aaron needs **Exodus 28** (ordinary priestly dress), cited elsewhere in Scene 2 — not Lev 16:4 alone. As written, this is reconstruction presented as citation-backed constraint.

---

**Leviticus 16:17 stretched beyond its scope (World fact + Scene 3)**  
KJV: *“no man in the tabernacle… when he goeth in to make an atonement **in the holy place**, until he come out.”*  

That is the **inner atonement entry**, not the whole day.  
- **Scene 7** (lots at the door): no fact should lean on 16:17 for “Aaron alone, no other priests.”  
- **Scene 9** (hands on live goat): Lev 16:20–21 — this is **after** inner atonement; a **fit man** is required (16:21).  
- **Scene 3** “Aaron alone at the veil” — he is at the threshold, not yet inside making atonement. 16:17 is a weak fit for “alone at the veil” as SPECIFIED.

---

**Scene 7 depiction: “before the bronze altar”**  
Leviticus 16:7: goats presented *“before the LORD at the **door of the tabernacle**.”*  
Door ≠ bronze altar. Altar may be nearby in the court, but the cited verses do **not** place the lots at the altar.

- Scene 1 gold vestments timing (needs Exodus 28, not Lev 16:4)

- Veil as hanging curtain (Ex 26:31, 26:33)  
- Breastplate + ephod among gold garments (Ex 28:4, 28:15)

---

### 4. COVERAGE GAPS — easy painting errors, no guard

**Scene 1 — “Once a year, only once”**  
No fact for annual timing. Leviticus 16:2 (*“come not at all times”*) and 16:34 (*“once a year”*) are central to the scene title but absent.

**Veil colour**  
Exodus 26:31 (already cited for Scene 3): veil is **blue, purple, scarlet, and fine twined linen** with cherubim. Visual only says *“heavy curtain.”* A plain white veil would pass this sheet but break Exodus.

**Tabernacle curtain colours**  
Exodus 26:1 is cited for “curtains,” but visual does not require **blue, purple, scarlet** — only generic fabric.

**Ark placement**  
No fact that the ark stays **behind the veil** in the most holy place (Ex 26:33–34; Lev 16:2). A prior image audit already flagged ark shown in the open court — this sheet would not catch it.

**Other Day-of-Atonement animals**  
Leviticus 16:3–6, 16:5: **young bullock** (Aaron) and **ram** (burnt offering) alongside the goats. No fact guards against wrong animals or a goats-only ceremony.

**Washing before linen**  
Leviticus 16:4: *“wash his flesh in water, and so put them on.”* Scene 2 is the garment-change moment; washing is unguarded.

**LORD’s goat vs scapegoat — sequence**  
Leviticus 16:9–10, 16:15: LORD’s lot-goat is **offered/slain**; blood goes **within the veil**. Scapegoat stays **alive** (16:10) until 16:20–21. Scene 7 bans a slain goat at lot-casting (good) but nothing guards the **slain LORD’s-goat / blood** thread that carries the substitution typology.

**Leviticus 16:20 timing for Scene 9**  
Hands on the live goat come **after** inner reconciliation (*“when he hath made an end…”*). Scene 9 should not read as simultaneous with veil/blood work — not stated.

**Linen mitre**  
Leviticus 16:4 lists a **linen mitre**; visuals only say “linen garment/sleeves” — head covering easy to miss.

---

### 5. CHRIST-LENS

No direct doctrinal distortion. But under-guarding the **slain LORD’s goat + blood within the veil** (Lev 16:15) while heavily guarding the **scapegoat** risks paintings that show only “sin carried away” and drop the **substitutionary death** half of the type — weak for a Christ-pointing piece.

---

### 6. INTERNAL CONFLICTS

- Scene 3 depiction: *“colonnaded passage”* vs Scene 3 [CONSTRAINED] ban on *“stone colonnade / masonry hall.”*  
- World [SPECIFIED] linen: *“not the gold vestments”* — Lev 16:4 does not name gold; contrast is inferred, not cited.

---

### Summary

Strong on **two goats, lot-casting, live scapegoat, both hands, fit man, tent-not-temple, veil-as-curtain**. Weak on **citation discipline** (white linen and gold timing dressed as SPECIFIED), **16:17 scope**, and **major missing guards** (once-a-year, veil/curtain colours, ark behind veil, bull/ram, LORD’s-goat slain, washing).

VERDICT: REVISE  
TOP FIXES:  
1. Demote all **“plain/plain white/undyed white”** linen claims from SPECIFIED to CONSTRAINED (“holy linen per Lev 16:4”) or FREE; cite colour only if a verse actually gives it.  
2. Add SPECIFIED facts for **Scene 1 annual timing** (Lev 16:2, 16:34), **coloured veil** (Ex 26:31), and **ark behind the veil** (Ex 26:33–34; Lev 16:2) — already a known render failure.  
3. Stop citing **Leviticus 16:17** for outer-court solitude (Scene 7) and veil-threshold scenes; add **Lev 16:20** sequencing for Scene 9 and a fact for the **LORD’s goat slain / blood within the veil** (Lev 16:9, 16:15) so the two-goat typology stays intact.
