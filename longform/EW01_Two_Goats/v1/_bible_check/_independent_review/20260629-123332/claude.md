# Independent review — claude (OK, 95s)

I reviewed the fact sheet against Leviticus 16 and the cited Exodus passages. Findings, most serious first:

## CITATION TRUTH / TRADITION-AS-BIBLICAL

**1. Scene 1's "gold vestments" rationale is not supported by its own citation (the central problem).**
The CONSTRAINED fact reads: *"Gold vestments belong to the ordinary approach OUTSIDE the veil; the atonement service within required plain linen, so gold is acceptable only in the outer court / before entering"* — cited to **Leviticus 16:4**. But Lev 16:4 says **nothing** about gold, an "ordinary approach," or an "outside the veil" phase. It says only: wash, and put on the linen garments. The entire Day of Atonement ritual in Leviticus 16 is performed in **linen** — there is no biblical text putting Aaron in gold at any point that day. The idea that the high priest alternates gold/white garments through the day's services is **Mishnaic tradition (Yoma)**, not Scripture. So:
   - The cited verse is **misapplied** — it is used to license the opposite of what it states (linen).
   - This should be demoted to a `historical_note`, clearly marked extra-biblical, **never** presented under a Lev 16:4 citation as a quasi-specified allowance.
   - It risks **contradicting** the passage it illustrates: the first thing a viewer sees (Scene 1: *"small in golden vestments"*) teaches a fact Leviticus 16 does not support. Safest fix is Aaron in linen in Scene 1 too; if the narration device ("I laid aside gold and glory," Scene 2) requires gold to exist, frame it explicitly as the *regular* high-priestly garments shown *before* the day's service, flagged as tradition.

## SCENE DEPICTION CONTRADICTS ITS OWN GUARD

**2. Scene 3 depiction says "colonnaded passage" — the guard then forbids exactly that.**
*Depicts:* "a dim **colonnaded** passage… hanging oil lamps." The CONSTRAINED guard correctly says *"Must NOT show: stone colonnade / masonry hall (it is a tent)."* Good that the guard exists — but the **scene's own description violates it**. The depiction text needs fixing, not just the guard. A colonnade implies stone/masonry the Tabernacle never had.

**3. Scene 3 "hanging oil lamps" — minor anachronism.** The holy place was lit by the single seven-branched golden lampstand (menorah, Exodus 25:31-37), not multiple hanging lamps. Not flagged. Low priority but it's a checkable detail.

## BUCKETS / INVENTED PROPS (acceptable but worth confirming)

**4. Scene 7 "bronze vessel" + "lot-stones."** Leviticus 16:8 says only "cast lots." The vessel and stone form are **artistic licence** — correctly NOT placed in the 'specified' bucket, and the markings are kept as "illegible scratches" (good, avoids hallucinated text). No change needed; noting it's licence, not text.

**5. Scene 2 "worn stone bench."** The world fact forbids masonry walls/columns; a stone bench is a smaller version of the same problem and is unguarded. Minor.

## WORDING NIT

**6. World fact 4 says "entered the **most holy place** alone"** but Lev 16:17 says "no man in the tabernacle… when he goeth in to make an atonement in the **holy place**." The prohibition is broader (whole tent) and the verse's phrase is "holy place." Claim is still true (he was alone in the most holy place), but the paraphrase doesn't match the quoted word. Tighten or note.

## WHAT IS SOLID
- Lev 16:4 linen for the service (specified) — correct, well-guarded across Scenes 2/3/7/9.
- Two goats together as one sin offering (Lev 16:5, 7) — correct.
- Casting lots, one for the LORD / one for scapegoat (Lev 16:8) — correct.
- **Both** hands on the **live** goat + confession (Lev 16:21) — correctly specified, the "both hands" detail is exactly right and important.
- Sent away by a fit man into the wilderness (Lev 16:21-22) — correct.
- Tent-not-stone, veil-not-door (Exodus 26:31, 33; 27:9) — correct buckets.
- Christ-lens (slain goat / scapegoat typology, kenosis framing) is not distorted by the facts.

The fact sheet is mostly faithful and well-guarded; the gold-vestment citation is the one genuine doctrinal/citation defect and it sits in the opening scene.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the Leviticus 16:4 citation from Scene 1's "gold vestments belong outside the veil" claim — Lev 16:4 prescribes linen and says nothing of gold or an outer-court phase. Demote it to an explicit `historical_note` (Mishnah Yoma tradition), or change Scene 1 to linen, so no extra-biblical tradition is presented as Scripture.
2. Fix Scene 3's own depiction text ("colonnaded passage") to match its guard — a tent has no colonnade/masonry.
3. Correct World-fact 4's paraphrase to the verse's actual word ("holy place," whole-tabernacle prohibition) per Lev 16:17, and treat the Scene 7 vessel/lot-stones and Scene 2 stone bench as flagged artistic licence, not implied period fact.
