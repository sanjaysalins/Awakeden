## Independent adversarial review — EW01 biblical fact sheet

Overall this sheet is unusually careful about Mishnaic tradition (gold-before-rite, scarlet thread), linen vs ephod, and the two-goat typology. That said, several claims overreach their citations, a few items sit in the wrong bucket, and there are checkable gaps a painter could still get wrong.

---

### CITATION TRUTH — over-reach / misapplied citations

**1. World [SPECIFIED] “once-a-year… two blood-entries” — cited verses do not say it**

Claim: *“the bullock's blood and the goat's blood are each brought in on that one day - two blood-entries”*  
Cited: Leviticus 16:2, 16:34 only.

- 16:2 forbids entry *at all times* except under prescribed conditions; it does not mention bullock blood, goat blood, or “two entries.”
- 16:34 says atonement *once a year*; it does not enumerate blood entries.

The “two blood-entries” detail requires **16:14–15** (bullock blood, then goat blood). As written, the **most important half of the claim is unsupported by the quoted KJV under it**.

**2. World [CONSTRAINED] “Aaron's ordinary high-priestly garments… no gold for the inner acts” — Exodus 28 citations do not prove the negation**

Exodus 28:2 and 28:4 describe glorious garments to *make*; they never say Aaron *removes* them on Yom Kippur or that gold is forbidden within the veil. The negative rule rests on **Leviticus 16:4** (linen put on). Bundling Ex 28 as co-proof overstates what those verses state.

**3. World [CONSTRAINED] “he mediates, he is not the substitute” — not a cited visual fact**

Leviticus 16:6/11/14 say Aaron atones *for himself and his house* first. They do **not** say “he is not the substitute.” That is theological commentary smuggled into a `[CONSTRAINED]` visual claim. Fine as a `historical_note`; wrong inline in the claim itself.

**4. Scene 8 [SPECIFIED] “seven times” for the goat — only by incorporation, not explicit quote**

Leviticus 16:15 says sprinkle goat blood *“as he did with the blood of the bullock”*; it does not repeat “seven times” in the goat verse. The sheet cites 16:14 + 16:15 and the inference is fair, but the fact line should say *“seven times per 16:14, applied to the goat by 16:15’s ‘as he did with the bullock’”* — otherwise it reads as if 16:15 itself states seven times.

**5. Scene 3 [SPECIFIED] Lev 16:17 + “this does not govern the outer court”**

16:17 says no man in *“the tabernacle of the congregation”* during atonement. It does **not** explicitly exempt the outer court. The exemption is reasonable exegesis, but it is **not** what the quoted verse states. Do not attach it to a `[SPECIFIED]` line as if Scripture said it.

### NO INVENTED FACTS — mostly good; a few soft spots

**6. Scene 2 still-life is honestly marked [FREE], but adjacent [SPECIFIED] lines blur the boundary**

Leviticus 16:4 states washing and **putting on** linen — not *laying aside* ephod/breastplate on a bench. The sheet correctly marks the tableau `[FREE]`, but the neighbouring `[SPECIFIED]` garment inventory reads like part of the Lev 16 rite rather than “if you show Ex 28 vestments, they must look like Ex 28.”

**7. Scene 9 scene description: “edge of the camp” — unbucketed licence**

Leviticus 16:21–22 specify a *fit man*, *wilderness*, and *a land not inhabited*; they do **not** place Aaron at the camp edge. That location is staging, not guarded.

**8. Good catches (credit where due, then stress-test)**

- Scarlet thread rejected as Mishnaic — correct.
- Gold-before-rite marked `[FREE]` / Yoma — correct.
- Linen colour not fixed as white — correct (world fact explicitly warns against doctrinizing whiteness).

### BUCKETS ARE HONEST — mis-bucketing

**9. Scene 2 [SPECIFIED] “The high priest's garments included an ephod, a breastplate… plate of pure gold upon the mitre”**

Exodus 28 supports the **objects**, but nothing in Lev 16 **specifies** those items appear in *this* scene’s action. This belongs in **`[CONSTRAINED]`** (“if depicted, match Ex 28”), not **`[SPECIFIED]`** (“the Bible states this visual fact for this beat”).

**10. Scene 2 [SPECIFIED] “having first washed his flesh in water”**

16:4 requires washing **before donning** linen. The scene shows folded linen beside laid-aside gold; it does **not** depict washing. The fact is biblical, but as a **scene guard** it is incomplete unless you also say washing may be off-frame or prior beat — otherwise `[CONSTRAINED]` + “do not show linen donned without prior washing” is cleaner.

**11. World [SPECIFIED] linen “removed them again afterward”**

16:23–24 support removal, but **after** the scapegoat (16:20–22), not “during the inner service.” Bundled under *“For the atonement service…”* slightly mis-times the removal relative to the cited rite.

**12. Mishnaic notes are labelled secondary — good**

Gold-before-rite, scarlet thread, multi-change Yoma sequence: properly quarantined.

**13. World [CONSTRAINED] slain-goat note: “price paid… substitution the Christ-type rests on”**

Theologically sound as **secondary** Christ-lens guidance, but it is not a visual fact. Keep it strictly under `historical_note`; the line *“do not depict only the live scapegoat”* is the actual guard painters need.

### CHRIST-LENS — intact, with one sequence risk

**14. Dual-goat theology is guarded well**

Slain LORD’s-goat blood within the veil + live scapegoat sent away: both are protected. That preserves the type without collapsing atonement into “carry-away only.”

**15. Scene 8 title/beat language: “The first goat I killed”**

Leviticus 16:11–14: **bullock blood enters the veil before the people’s goat blood** (16:15). Calling this beat “the first goat” invites painters/narration to skip or invert the **bullock-first** inner sequence already guarded elsewhere. The guard exists, but the scene header fights it.

**16. Missing Lev 16:10 weakens the Christ-lens guard at the lot-casting beat**

16:10: the scapegoat is **“presented alive before the LORD”** while the LORD’s lot-goat is offered (16:9). No fact guards *alive presentation at the door* immediately after lots — an easy way to accidentally show the wrong goat dead at the wrong moment.

### COVERAGE — obvious missing guards

**17. Partial scene coverage**

Facts exist for scenes **1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9** only. Unreviewed beats in the episode plan (e.g. inner entry, divine cloud on mercy seat, Nadab/Abihu echo) inherit only world facts. Scene-level guards for **incense cloud vs divine cloud (16:2 vs 16:13)** are in world facts but not tied to the scenes that actually depict the mercy seat.

**18. Incense **before** blood — sequence not guarded**

Leviticus 16:12–13 (censer within veil, cloud covering mercy seat) precede 16:14–15 blood sprinkling. Scene 8 depicts censer + blood together but no fact says: **incense cloud must precede blood application**, not merely coexist.

**19. Altar blood rite omitted (16:18–19)**

After inner sprinkling, Aaron puts blood on the **horns of the altar** and cleanses/consecrates it. No guard against depicting Yom Kippur as *only* inner-veil work with no altar atonement — a checkable omission.

**20. Lev 16:18–19 / 16:16 holy-place sprinkling outside the most holy**

16:16 extends atonement to the holy place/tabernacle because of uncleanness. No guard for blood in the **holy place** (not only most holy) if artists show multi-room sprinkling.

**21. Two rams, not one (16:3 + 16:5)**

Scene 7 correctly notes congregation ram “besides Aaron's own ram,” but there is no world-level guard that **Aaron’s ram (16:3)** and **the people’s ram (16:5)** are distinct offerings if multiple animals appear across the set.

**22. Day-of-Atonement congregation posture (16:29–31)**

*“Ye shall afflict your souls… a sabbath of rest.”* Scene 1 `[FREE]` allows a vast court multitude but never **`[CONSTRAINED]`**-guards against festive/normal-day crowd energy inconsistent with affliction/rest.

**23. Scene 2 garment inventory incomplete vs Exodus 28:4**

Exodus 28:4 also lists **robe** and **broidered coat** (and girdle). Scene 2 `[SPECIFIED]` guards breastplate, ephod, mitre plate but not the full Ex 28 set — a painter could omit the robe while claiming “laid aside gold and glory.”

**24. Coals “from off the altar before the LORD” (16:12)**

Inner-scene censer is guarded; **source** of coals (bronze altar in the court, not the mercy seat) is not — relevant given the sheet’s own warning against treating the ark as an altar.

**25. Scene 7 vs Lev 16:7 location tension (internal consistency)**

Scene 7 `[CONSTRAINED]` correctly says goats at **door of the tabernacle**, not specifically at the bronze altar. Good — but worth a hard `Must NOT show: goats presented at the bronze altar as if that were 16:7’s door` because that is a common painter default.
