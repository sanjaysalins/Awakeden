# Independent review — claude (OK, 117s)

Read the artifact closely against Leviticus 16 / Exodus 25–28. This is a notably disciplined sheet — it repeatedly flags tradition as non-biblical and guards the two-goat typology. My adversarial pass found no invented fact dressed as 'specified' and no wrong/misapplied citation. The findings below are real but minor (mostly conservative-direction bucketing), not blockers.

## Citation truth
- Every KJV quote actually supports its claim. Spot-checked the load-bearing ones:
  - World fact 4 "two goats … single sin offering" — Lev 16:5 reads "two kids of the goats for **a** sin offering" (singular). Correct.
  - Scene 8 "before the mercy seat **seven times**" — the seven-times in Lev 16:14 attaches to "before the mercy seat," and the goat inherits it via 16:15 "as he did with the blood of the bullock." Placement is right; the inference is fair. OK to mark specified.
  - Scene 3 veil "blue, purple, scarlet … with cherubim … not plain white" — Exod 26:31 supports all of it. Good.
  - Scene 9 both-hands / fit-man / land-not-inhabited — Lev 16:21–22 verbatim. Correct.
  - Scene 9 "sent AFTER reconciling the holy place" — Lev 16:20 supports the ordering. Correct, and it correctly keeps Scene 7→8→9 in Levitical sequence (lots → slay+blood → live-goat confession).

## No invented facts
- Tradition is consistently quarantined: gold-before-the-rite (World fact 3 + Scene 1) and the scarlet thread on the scapegoat (Scene 9) are both explicitly labelled Mishnaic/Yoma and **not** put in 'specified'. The European-crown guard in Scene 2 correctly points to "a flat gold plate/frontlet" (Exod 28:36). This is the right discipline.

## Bucket honesty (minor — all conservative direction)
- **World fact 8** ("goat for the LORD … killed … blood within the veil") is marked **[CONSTRAINED]**, but Lev 16:9 + 16:15 state it **explicitly** — and its own Visual note says "show the LORD's goat slain," which is a *must-show* (specified-grade) instruction. Same for **World fact 9** (Aaron's bullock first, Lev 16:6/11 — explicitly stated, marked constrained). These are under-claimed, not over-claimed, so they won't mislead a painter, but per the sheet's own definition ("specified = the Bible states this") they sit in the wrong bucket as set-level guards.

## Over-precise gloss
- **World fact 7** parenthetical "two blood-entries." The bullock blood (16:14) and goat blood (16:15) are two distinct sprinklings — true — but the censer/incense bring-in (16:12–13) is itself a separate entry within the veil, and the number of physical entries is rabbinic, not stated. The once-a-year claim is solid; soften the "two blood-entries" count so it doesn't read as a scriptural enumeration of entries.

## Coverage gap (small)
- Scene 9 depicts "a living goat" but carries **no** "must NOT show a sheep/ram in the scapegoat's place" guard, even though the species is specified (Lev 16:5/16:8) and Scene 7 has exactly that guard. Add the species guard to Scene 9 for symmetry.

## Christ-lens — intact
- The typology is actively protected: World fact 8's note forbids depicting only the live scapegoat and silently dropping the slain goat/blood, and World fact 9 guards "mediator, not substitute." Nothing distorts the two-goat witness to Christ.
