<speaker name="witness">Once a year I walked behind a curtain to meet God — and I was never sure I would walk back out. The law gave me two goats for one sin. I killed the first and carried its blood inside the veil. The second I did not kill — I laid my hands on its head, confessed every sin of the people over it, and drove it into the desert to be lost. One to pay. One to carry it away. For years I asked why it took two. I know now. No single creature could hold both halves. Long after me a Man named Jesus did what my two goats only pictured — He was the price paid, and the One the guilt was laid on:</speaker>
<speaker name="scripture">and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.</speaker>
<speaker name="witness">He carried it away Himself — once — and sat down. Finished. The curtain I trembled behind tore in two the hour He died. I was only the sign. He is the substance. Don't come to me, or a goat, or an altar. Come to Jesus — the way in is torn wide open. Walk in.</speaker>
