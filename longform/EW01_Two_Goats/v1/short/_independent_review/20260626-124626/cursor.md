## Independent adversarial review — *The Two Goats* (Aaron, short PUNCHY)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution: **FAIL (attribution); quote text OK**

The sole bold quote is **verbatim** KJV (Isaiah 53:6 fragment):

> **"and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all."**

But it is **unattributed**. Aaron drops straight from his prose into Scripture with no frame:

> *"He was the price paid, and the One the guilt was laid on:"* → quote.

A zero-Bible listener cannot tell this is **later prophecy**, not Aaron’s own Day-of-Atonement ledger. Sibling punchy shorts name the source first — e.g. Passover: *"As the Scripture written after me says:"* before the quote. This piece has nothing comparable.

**Secondary gap:** **Core text is Leviticus 16**, yet there is **not one verbatim Lev 16 quote**. The entire ritual is paraphrased (*"The law gave me two goats for one sin"*, *"confessed every sin of the people over it"*). For a format that treats `**"..."**` as binding Scripture voice, Aaron’s lived text never actually speaks.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling: **FAIL (strawman / absent)**

The only doubt is a **riddle**, not Aaron’s steel-man objection:

> *"For years I asked why it took two."*

That is curiosity about a puzzle, not the honest fear a high priest would carry. The locked long names what matters and this cut drops all of it:

- the rite was **real but never finished** (*"Every year I came back… the same blood, the same two goats"*)
- the fear that goat-blood **only pointed forward**, never finally took sin away (Hebrews 10:4 territory)
- Aaron’s **own sin-offering** before he could enter for the people (Lev 16:6, 11)

Without one compressed line of **lived** doubt, Beat 3’s turn is not owed — it is imported.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned: **WEAK / partly announced**

**Earned in Beat 2:** the two-goat split is shown in Aaron’s hands — *"One to pay. One to carry it away."* Good raw material.

**Unearned in Beat 3:**

- *"I know now."* — same family as the spec’s banned reveal stinger (*"at last I understood"*): the answer is **announced**, not walked out.
- *"No single creature could hold both halves."* — typology **stated**, not **demonstrated** from Lev 16’s own oddity (one **sin offering**, two goats — Lev 16:5). The long earns this; the short asserts it.
- Isaiah 53:6 lands as **imported proof** before the listener has been led from Aaron’s scapegoat (*"drove it into the desert to be lost"*) to Christ by logic inside the ritual.
- *"and sat down"* — Hebrews 10:12 theology with **no quote, no setup**. A first-time listener will not know why “sat down” is the punchline (no chair behind the veil; Aaron’s work never finished).
- *"The curtain I trembled behind tore in two the hour He died."* — **continuity break**: Beat 1 plants fear (*"never sure I would walk back out"*) but never **trembled**; Beat 3 borrows trembling from the long without planting it here.

**EW-INV-11 signposting:** *"Long after me a Man named Jesus"* is minimal compared to peer pieces (*"by a light that fell long after"*, *"Scripture written after me"*). Borderline — reads like Aaron knew the crucifixion rather than speaking by later light.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine: **mostly sound, two friction points**

**Passes minimally on substitution:** penalty (*"price paid"*, blood killed) + guilt borne (*"the One the guilt was laid on"*, *"carried it away Himself"*) are named. No supersessionism in the close.

**Friction:**

1. *"I was only the sign. He is the substance."* — acceptable **only if** the rite is first honored as a real shadow. This short never says the annual cleansing was **true but incomplete**; without that guardrail it can sound like Aaron’s God-given rite **failed**, not foreshadowed.
2. **Rite accuracy for newcomers:** Aaron enters *"inside the veil"* on **goat blood alone** — Lev 16 requires Aaron’s **own bullock** first. A listener may misread how atonement worked.

No contrarian reading detected.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character: **mixed**

**Strong:** Beat 1 is the best line in the piece — *"Once a year I walked behind a curtain to meet God — and I was never sure I would walk back out."* Immediate stakes, first-person, priest-specific.

**Weak:**

- Aaron is **never named in spoken text** (only the header). A listener may not know **who** is speaking.
- Beat 3 slips into **essay voice**: *"did what my two goats only pictured"*, *"No single creature could hold both halves"* — typology lecture, not a trembling priest remembering his hands on the goat’s head.
- No Aaron-specific steel (Nadab and Abihu, linen, mercy seat, own sin before the people’s) that makes this **Aaron** rather than Generic Priest Costume™.

---

### First-hearing clarity: **gaps**

- *"inside the veil"* — inner curtain / holy of holies never explained.
- *"and the LORD hath laid on him"* — **"him"** is Isaiah’s Servant; Jesus was just named, but the pronoun bridge is easy to miss.
- *"sat down"* — opaque without Hebrews context.
- *"tore in two the hour He died"* — no attribution; sounds like Aaron watched Golgotha.

---

### Hook / spine / SHORT form

**Hook:** grips. **Spine:** one witness, one thread (two goats → Christ). Holds.

**SHORT budget: FAIL.** Spoken text is **~185 words** — **~35 words below** the 220–320 floor (EW-INV-8). Tightness here is **under-feeding**, not discipline: the compression cost is the missing wrestling and Lev 16 grounding, not bloat.

Beat 3 also stacks **three** fulfillment moves (Isaiah guilt-bearing + “sat down” + veil torn) where one earned turn would suffice — slight crowding at the turn.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus: **PASS**

Names Jesus; invites with named responses — *"Come to Jesus"*, *"Walk in"*. Grace-anchored; no fear/gain-loss / bare *"will you trust Him?"* template. The punch lands.
