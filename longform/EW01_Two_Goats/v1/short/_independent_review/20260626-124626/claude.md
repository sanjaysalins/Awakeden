# Independent review — claude (OK, 63s)

Reviewed adversarially against the eyewitness lens. This is a strong, tight piece with a genuinely earned two-goats→Christ typology. But there's one real invariant breach and a couple of soft spots.

## Findings

**1. EW-INV-4 — the one Scripture quote is UNATTRIBUTED (real breach).**
Beat 3 drops `**"and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all."**` with no speaker frame. The line before it is `"He was the price paid, and the One the guilt was laid on:"` — that's narration, not an attribution. The invariant requires the speaker/source named *before* the quote. This is Isaiah 53:6, spoken by Aaron, centuries before Isaiah — so it specifically needs "as the prophet Isaiah would write" / "as it is written" / "the word God later gave." Right now a zero-Bible listener has no idea whose words those are or that they're Scripture at all. **The KJV text itself is exact and correct** (Isa 53:6 verbatim) — only the attribution is missing.

**2. The witness never quotes his OWN lived text (Leviticus 16).** Core text is declared as Leviticus 16, the entire moment is the Day of Atonement ritual Aaron performed — yet the only verbatim quote is from Isaiah. The scapegoat language ("laid my hands on its head, confessed every sin... drove it into the desert") is paraphrase, not quote. Not a hard fail, but the eyewitness anchoring his single quote in someone else's book is a thread-weakening choice worth a second look.

**3. EW-G8 — the wrestling is a puzzle, not the strongest objection.** "For years I asked why it took two" is curiosity, not steel-man doubt. The genuinely hard objection a high priest faces is the one Hebrews 10:4 names — *does any of this blood actually take sin away, or am I just repeating a thing that never finishes?* The narration gestures at it ("once... and sat down. Finished" vs his yearly return) but never lets Aaron *voice the doubt*. Internalizing that fear would make the "once / Finished" payoff hit far harder.

**4. Length is under target / leaves the doubt unexplored.** ~198 spoken words (~75–80s) against the ~220–320 / ~90s spec. It's "punchy" but at the low end — and the spare room is exactly where the EW-G8 wrestling above should live. Don't pad; deepen.

**5. First-hearing nit:** "behind a curtain" (Beat 1) → "inside the veil" (Beat 2) → "The curtain... tore" (Beat 3) uses *curtain* and *veil* for the same object. A no-Bible listener may not connect them. Pick one word, or make the equivalence explicit.

## What's working (so you don't break it)
- CTA (Beat 4) is clean: names Jesus, names the response ("Come to Jesus... Walk in"), grace-anchored, no fear/gain-loss, not the banned "will you trust Him?" template.
- Substitution is named on both halves (price paid + guilt laid on + "carried it away Himself"); sound, no supersession, no contrarian reading.
- Voice is consistently first-person Aaron and characterful ("the curtain I trembled behind").
- The torn-curtain close bookends the "behind a curtain" hook rather than crowding it — keep it.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Attribute the Isaiah 53:6 quote before it lands (e.g. "the word God would later give His prophet:") — EW-INV-4; the quote text is exact, only the frame is missing.
2. Replace the thin "why it took two" curiosity with Aaron's real doubt — *does this blood ever actually finish?* — so the "once / Finished / sat down" payoff is earned, and use the spare ~40–80 words to do it.
3. Unify "curtain"/"veil" (or make them explicitly the same thing) for first-hearing clarity.
