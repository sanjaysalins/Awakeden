# Independent review — codex (OK, 22s)

Findings:

- **EW-INV-4 violation:** `**"and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all."**` appears KJV-exact, but it is not attributed before quoting. The line before it says `a Man named Jesus...`, not `Isaiah said` or similar. That fails the attribution requirement.

- **EW-G7 partly unearned:** `No single creature could hold both halves` is asserted, not shown from Leviticus 16. The piece shows the two actions, but the Christ-turn jumps from ritual description to conclusion without explaining why Jesus fulfills both death and removal.

- **EW-G8 weak wrestling:** `For years I asked why it took two` is a real question, but not the strongest objection. Aaron’s deeper fear would be: how can blood and exile actually cleanse a guilty people before a holy God? The current doubt is tidy and technical.

- **EW-G9 substitution undernamed:** `He was the price paid, and the One the guilt was laid on` is close, but the required penalty is not clearly named. It needs explicit substitution: Christ bore the penalty/death our sin deserved in our place.

- **First-hearing clarity gap:** `inside the veil`, `sat down`, and `The curtain... tore in two` assume Bible knowledge. A first-time listener may not know why sitting down matters or what the torn curtain means.

- **Voice is mostly strong, but briefly sermonized:** `I was only the sign. He is the substance` sounds like theological essay language more than Aaron’s lived voice.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Attribute the KJV quotation before the quote.
2. Make the Christ reveal shown from the two-goat ritual, not merely announced.
3. Name substitution clearly: the penalty Christ bore in our place.
