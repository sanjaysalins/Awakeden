# The Two Goats — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Aaron, the first high priest of Israel  ·  **Core text:** Leviticus 16  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** the two goats — price paid + sin carried away — both halves finished in one Priest, Christ
---

## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Aaron, Israel's first high priest. Once a year the law asked a strange thing: two goats for one sin offering. Two — for one. I never stopped asking why.

## Beat 3 — The act
I cast lots over them:

**"And Aaron shall cast lots upon the two goats; one lot for the LORD, and the other lot for the scapegoat."**

The first I killed, its blood carried beyond the veil. On the second's head I confessed the people's sins, then sent it away where none could find it:

**"And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited."**

One died to pay; one carried it off. Yet I came back — same blood, same goats, same sins. A cleansing remade every year is real, but never finished.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
One offering, yet no creature could carry it — one to pay in blood, one to bear the guilt away. By the light that came long after me, I see what every Day of Atonement reached toward: a Man, Jesus, whose blood did what no goat could do alone:

**"and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all."**

Both halves, finished in one Priest who sat down.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
Come not to me, nor a goat, nor an altar. Be still; feel the cost — not a goat driven off, but God's own Son bearing your sin into a death none could undo. The curtain I trembled before is torn; the way stands open — for you, not one day a year. The word long after me says:

**"Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus."**

The question I lived and never could answer is answered now, in Him. Will you come in? Now, on His blood. Trust the One who finished what I only foreshadowed. Come — and be carried clean.
