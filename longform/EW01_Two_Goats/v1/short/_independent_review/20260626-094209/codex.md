# Independent review — codex (OK, 65s)

**Findings**
- **EW-INV-4 fails on attribution.** None of the Scripture quotes are attributed before quoting. “I cast lots over them:” is not attribution. “The word long after me says:” is too vague. Name the source/speaker before each quote.
- **KJV exactness issue:** `**"Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus."**` changes KJV punctuation; Hebrews 10:19 ends with a comma because the sentence continues.
- **Likely punctuation issue:** `**"And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited."**` also changes KJV punctuation; the verse continues after a colon.
- **Reveal is partly earned but still announced.** `“I see what every Day of Atonement reached toward”` tells us the conclusion instead of fully showing the move from two goats to one Christ. `“Both halves, finished in one Priest who sat down”` is strong theology, but “sat down” is insider shorthand and not explained.
- **The wrestling is thin.** `“I never stopped asking why”` is plausible, but it is not yet the strongest internal objection. Aaron’s sharper problem is not merely “why two goats?” but “why does this holy blood have to be brought again and again?”
- **Doctrine needs sharper substitution language.** `“feel the cost”` and `“bearing your sin into a death”` name cost and death, but not clearly the penalty/judgment Christ bore in the sinner’s place.
- **Possible doctrinal misfire:** `“a death none could undo”` risks sounding like death had the final word over Jesus. That is not what you mean, but the phrase is unsafe.
- **First-hearing clarity is mixed.** `“beyond the veil,” “the curtain,” “holiest,” “one Priest who sat down”` are meaningful to Bible-literate listeners but under-explained for zero Bible knowledge.
- **Voice sometimes slips into essay mode.** `“By the light that came long after me”` and `“what every Day of Atonement reached toward”` sound like theological narration more than Aaron recounting a lived moment.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add explicit KJV attribution before every quote and correct KJV punctuation.
2. Make the reveal shown, not announced: connect repeated yearly blood + the goat bearing sin away directly to Christ bearing the penalty once.
3. Replace vague/unsafe CTA language with grace-anchored invitation that names coming/faith and clearly names the penalty Christ bore.
