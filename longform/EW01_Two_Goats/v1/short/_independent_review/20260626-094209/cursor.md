## Independent adversarial review — *The Two Goats* (Aaron, eyewitness-short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim:** All four quoted spans match KJV substrings. No altered words found in:
- `**"And Aaron shall cast lots upon the two goats; one lot for the LORD, and the other lot for the scapegoat."**`
- `**"And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited."**`
- `**"and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all."**`
- `**"Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus."**`

**Attribution: fails on every quote.** EW-INV-4 requires the speaker to be named *before* the KJV block, for a dedicated scripture voice. None of these clear that bar:

| Quote | Frame before quote | Problem |
|-------|-------------------|---------|
| Lev 16:8 | `I cast lots over them:` | Aaron narrates his act; the quote is third-person statute. No *Moses wrote* / *the LORD commanded* / *as the law says*. A first-time listener hears statute text with no source tag. |
| Lev 16:22 | `…then sent it away where none could find it:` | Same — procedural lead-in, not attribution. |
| Isa 53:6 | `…whose blood did what no goat could do alone:` | **No attribution at all.** Isaiah is never named; the prophet’s line lands as Aaron’s own theological punch. |
| Heb 10:19 | `The word long after me says:` | Vague anachronism. No *Hebrews* / *the letter says* / author. Worse: `brethren` in the quote makes Aaron sound like he is addressing the church. |

This is not a polish issue — it breaks first-hearing clarity and the spec’s scripture-voice contract.

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (headline failure)

The hook plants a real question: **`I never stopped asking why.`**

The close promises it was live steel: **`The question I lived and never could answer is answered now, in Him.`**

**The middle never delivers.** There is no beat where Aaron voices the strongest objection in his own voice — e.g. *Why two goats for one sin? If blood pays, why also load guilt onto a live goat and drive it off? If this cleanses, why must I come back every year to the same sins?*

`Yet I came back — same blood, same goats, same sins. A cleansing remade every year is real, but never finished.` is sharp **observation**, not **wrestling**. Aaron never argues with the rite, never states the objection he “never could answer,” never lets the two-goat logic strain before the reveal resolves it.

Beats 2/4/5 are absent (1 → 3 → 6 → 7). That is exactly where this format puts the steel-man. Without it, EW-G8 fails outright — same pattern already flagged on sibling shorts (Isaiah, Boaz, Passover).

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned vs. announced

**What works:** Beat 3 earns the typological spine — `One died to pay; one carried it off` plus the annual loop — before Christ is named. That is real lived logic.

**What does not:**

- **`By the light that came long after me, I see what every Day of Atonement reached toward: a Man, Jesus`** — cross-time device is allowed (EW-INV-11), but this line **announces** fulfillment; it does not walk the listener from goat-one / goat-two to one Priest completing both halves.
- The Isaiah drop after that clause does the heavy lifting. Aaron’s lived moment does not **show** why the Suffering Servant completes the scapegoat half — the quote is imported.
- **`Both halves, finished in one Priest who sat down.`** — `sat down` is Hebrews 10:12 shorthand (finished work vs. Aaron’s yearly return). It is never quoted, never explained. A zero-Bible listener hears a triumphant tagline with no earned meaning.
- **`The question I lived and never could answer is answered now`** — resolution **asserted** after the NT quotes, not demonstrated stepwise from the two goats Aaron handled.

Partially earned; mostly announced. REVISE territory, not PASS.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

Mostly sound evangelical typology: penalty (`One died to pay` / `blood`) + bearing (`one carried it off` / `bearing your sin`) + both fulfilled in Christ. No supersessionism; `Come not to me, nor a goat, nor an altar` redirects without dismissing Israel’s story.

**Minor gap:** substitution on the **slain** goat is clearer than on the **scapegoat** half — `Come — and be carried clean` leans on removal language; the penal substitution half leans on Isaiah/Hebrews rather than Aaron’s own rite. Not heretical, but the witness-lived half of substitution is thinner than the carried-away half.

No invented doctrine or contrarian reading found.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Passes.** Jesus is named (`a Man, Jesus`; `blood of Jesus`). Invitation is named and specific: `Will you come in?` / `Come — and be carried clean` / `Trust the One who finished what I only foreshadowed` — not the banned bare *will you trust Him?* template. Grace-anchored; no fear/gain-loss framing.

**Nit:** `Be still; feel the cost` edges toward manufactured interior pressure, but it still points at Christ’s bearing, not at listener terror.

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

Beats 1 and 3 sound like Aaron at the tabernacle — good first-person witness.

Beat 7 slips into **essayist-preacher**: `Be still; feel the cost — … for you, not one day a year` is second-person pulpit address, not a priest recounting what he lived. The witness wears the costume in the opening, then lectures the audience at the close.

---

### First-hearing clarity

- **Clear:** `Two — for one`; pay vs. carry-off; annual repetition.
- **Opaque without Bible background:** `beyond the veil`; **`who sat down`**; **`brethren`** inside an Aaron-framed Hebrews quote; **`scapegoat`** (in the KJV quote only — never glossed).

### SHORT form — tightness / single moment

~300 spoken words — inside 220–320. One ritual, one thread, no swap. Pacing is lean.

The missing wrestling beat is not bloat — it is a **hole**. The piece is tight but **under-built** where the format requires steel.

---

### Hook / spine

Hook grips (`Two — for one. I never stopped asking why.`). ONE witness, ONE thread (two goats → one Priest). Holds.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Restore EW-G8 wrestling in Aaron’s voice** — stage the real objection the hook promises (*why two for one? why blood AND a bearer? why yearly if it cleanses?*) before Beat 6 resolves it; do not leave it as hook + closing bookend only.
2. **Attribute every KJV quote before it is spoken** — e.g. *as Moses wrote* / *as the prophet Isaiah wrote* / *as the letter to the Hebrews says* — so scripture voice, source, and `brethren` anachronism are intelligible to a first-time listener.
3. **Earn the reveal from Aaron’s two goats before Isaiah/Hebrews carry it** — walk pay + carry-off → one Priest completing both halves; quote or plainly unpack *sat down* (Heb 10:12) so `Both halves, finished in one Priest who sat down` is shown, not a unexplained payoff line.
