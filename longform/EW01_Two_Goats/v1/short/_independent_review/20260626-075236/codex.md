# Independent review — codex (OK, 35s)

Concrete findings:

- **EW-INV-4 attribution failure:** none of the KJV quotes are attributed before quotation. Example: `I cast lots over them:` is action setup, not attribution to Leviticus/Moses/God before `**"And Aaron shall cast lots..."**`.
- **EW-INV-4 punctuation/verbatim risk:** `**"And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited."**` changes KJV punctuation from a colon and omits the rest without signaling excerpt. Same issue with Hebrews 10:19 ending in a period instead of KJV comma.
- **EW-G8 weak wrestling:** `I never stopped asking why` and `Why would one need two?` are interesting, but not the strongest objection. Aaron’s deeper crisis should be: how can blood and banishment actually answer Israel’s guilt before a holy God?
- **EW-G9 substitution is close but undernamed:** `He was the price paid — and the One on whom the guilt was laid` names guilt-transfer, but not clearly `the penalty` or what Christ bore. “Price paid” is lighter than the lens asks for.
- **EW-G7 reveal partly asserted:** `By the light that came long after me, I tell you...` announces the turn. The type is strong, but the Christ connection depends on explanation rather than Aaron’s lived tension resolving from the ritual itself.
- **Voice weakens in reveal:** `both were finished in one Priest, who entered once and sat down` sounds like Hebrews commentary, not Aaron’s first-person witness. The costume drops into sermon shorthand.
- **First-hearing clarity gap:** `through the veil`, `scapegoat`, `High Priest`, `holiest`, and `sat down` are insider terms. A zero-Bible listener can follow the broad image, but some key stakes are unexplained.
- **CTA is mostly compliant but slightly tired:** `Trust the One who finished... Come — and be carried clean` names faith/coming and centers Jesus, but `be carried clean` is vague as a response/result and could be clearer about receiving mercy/grace.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add exact attribution before every KJV quote and fix punctuation/excerpt handling.
2. Deepen Aaron’s wrestling around how guilt can truly be removed before God, then let the two-goat ritual answer it in Christ.
3. Name substitution more explicitly: Christ bore the penalty for sin in the sinner’s place, then invite the listener to come to Jesus in faith for grace.
