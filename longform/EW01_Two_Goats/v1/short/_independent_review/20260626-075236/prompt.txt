# The Two Goats — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Aaron, the first high priest of Israel  ·  **Core text:** Leviticus 16  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** the two goats — price paid + sin carried away — both halves finished in one Priest, Christ
---

## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Aaron, Israel's first high priest. Once a year the law asked a strange thing of me: two goats for one sin offering. Two — for one. I never stopped asking why.

## Beat 3 — The act
I cast lots over them:

**"And Aaron shall cast lots upon the two goats; one lot for the LORD, and the other lot for the scapegoat."**

The first I killed, and carried its blood through the veil. The second I did not kill. I laid both my hands on its living head, confessed over it every sin of the people, and sent it away. I watched it go into a land where no one would find it:

**"And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited."**

One died to pay. The other lived to carry it off. Why would one need two?

## Beat 6 — The reveal
I did not see the answer in my own day. By the light that came long after me, I tell you what every Day of Atonement was reaching toward. There came a Man, Jesus, and with His blood He did what my two goats only pictured. He was the price paid — and the One on whom the guilt was laid and carried clean away:

**"and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all."**

That is why there were two: no single creature could hold both halves, and both were finished in one Priest, who entered once and sat down.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
So do not come to me, nor to a goat, nor to a yearly altar. Come to Jesus, the High Priest who finished it. The curtain I trembled before is torn; the way is open — not for one man one day a year, but for you:

**"Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus."**

Trust the One who finished what I only began. Come — and be carried clean.
