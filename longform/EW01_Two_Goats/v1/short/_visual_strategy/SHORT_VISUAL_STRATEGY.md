# Awakeden eyewitness SHORT — visual strategy decision

**Question for the panel:** how should Awakeden's eyewitness SHORTS (~75–110s, 9:16
vertical) be animated visually? Continue the current approach, or adapt it with what we
just validated on the long form? Red-team the options and tell us which you'd pick, why,
and what we're missing.

## Context

Awakeden is a Christian content channel. A biblical eyewitness tells their own story in
first person and points to Jesus. Each topic ships in two independent forms that share the
same script DNA:
- **LONG** — ~10 min, 16:9, contemplative deep-dive.
- **SHORT** — ~75–110s, 9:16 vertical, the viral/snackable entry that funnels to the long.

The pilot topic is **"The Two Goats"** (Aaron / Day of Atonement → Christ).

### What we just validated on the LONG form (a paid POC)
A **period-documentary** look: painterly **Baroque oil stills** (deep chiaroscuro, muted
earth tones, ancient biblical period) animated with **veo3 atmospheric, faithful camera
moves** — and a hard-won rule:

> **Translate or zoom the camera (push-in, pull-back, dolly, telephoto) → stays faithful.
> Rotate the point of view (orbit / 360 / pan / aerial) → the AI invents unseen geometry
> and the image morphs (faces warp, a second goat appears).**

So we built a **vetted camera palette**: GREEN = smooth-drift, dolly-in/push, crane
pull-back, telephoto zoom, forward dolly, tracking-drone (landscapes only). RED = orbit,
360, pan, aerial, top-down, worm's-eye, POV. Plus a "glitter-kill" negative (no sparkles/
motes/bloom) that removed the AI-reel tell. We also proved the painterly-oil look itself is
the main defense against "cheap AI slop," and that a face-forward **Christ renders reverent
on the cheaper image model** (no premium face-consistency model needed).

### What the SHORT does TODAY
The current Awakeden eyewitness short = **slideshow / boomerang stills**, sequenced to the
narration beats — deliberately **intimate and calmer** than the legacy "rapid-cut viral
gospel-short" (which packed many clips and sped them up for retention — the "always
punchier" doctrine). Boomerang fill is **$0** (no per-clip render). The legacy viral short
used generative Kling gallery-tour crop-cuts that sometimes morphed/"danced."

### The tension
- Shorts are the **first-class, highest-viewership product** — they must be PERFECT and
  win the first 3 seconds / retention.
- "Always punchier" (fast cuts, sped clips) historically drove short retention.
- BUT the eyewitness short was intentionally made **calm/intimate**, which fights "punchy."
- The long form just proved a **premium, anti-slop, faithful-motion** look that we could
  also apply to shorts — at the cost of paid veo clips instead of free boomerang, and the
  risk that slow cinematic moves hurt vertical-short retention.

## The options

**A — Continue as-is (slideshow / boomerang stills).**
Calm, intimate, $0 motion fill, lowest risk, brand-distinct from frantic competitors.
Risk: may read as static/under-produced next to motion-rich vertical feeds; boomerang can
feel repetitive; might under-perform on retention.

**B — Adapt fully to cinematic faithful motion.**
Bring the long form's GREEN-palette faithful camera moves (push-in, pull-back, drift) to
the short's painterly stills, 9:16, but **paced faster** (quicker moves, tighter cuts),
glitter-killed. Premium + visually consistent with the long + anti-slop. Cost: paid veo
clips per scene; risk of being too slow unless the pacing is genuinely punchy.

**C — Hybrid (recommended starting point).**
Painterly stills throughout (the anti-slop moat). **Boomerang/hold the calm middle beats
($0)**, and spend paid faithful cinematic moves ONLY on the **hook (first 3s)** and the
**Christ landing** — the two moments that carry retention and the CTA. Cheapest "premium"
option; keeps punch where it matters; visually consistent with the long.

## Our tentative recommendation
**C (hybrid)** for the pilot, measured against A on a real A/B, because it buys the premium
faithful-motion look on the two beats that decide a vertical short (hook + landing) while
keeping the rest cheap and calm. We are NOT confident this beats a genuinely *punchy*
fast-cut treatment on raw retention, which is why we want outside views.

## What we're asking
1. For a vertical Christian eyewitness short, which option wins on **retention + reverence +
   not-AI-slop**, and why?
2. Is "calm/intimate" a strategic moat or a retention liability for a 9:16 short?
3. Are we wrong that the painterly-oil + faithful-motion look transfers to shorts — does
   vertical demand a different visual grammar (faces bigger, cuts faster, text on screen)?
4. What are we not considering (e.g. hook design, motion-on-still tricks, captions as
   motion, first-frame thumbnail)?
