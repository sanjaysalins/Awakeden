# EW01 — The Two Goats · WORLD BIBLE (locked before any still)

> Every still is built from THIS shared world — same era, place, light, palette, and
> locked faces — so the episode reads as ONE film, never 25 separate prompts.

## ERA
Wilderness Tabernacle period, **c. 1400 BC** — the Exodus generation in the Sinai
wilderness. The **first Day of Atonement** (Leviticus 16).

## PLACE
The **portable Tabernacle** — a tent of skins and woven curtains in a desert camp,
**NOT a stone temple**. Linen court walls, bronze altar, the veil, the mercy seat.
Around it: bleached desert, cracked valley floor, scrub, distant hills.

## LIGHT (one consistent key)
A single warm key light — **dawn gold / oil-lamp flame from frame-left**, deep
Caravaggio shadow opposite. Incense/dust haze softens it. **Same light direction in
every scene.**

## PALETTE
Muted earth — sand, bone-white linen, ochre, dried-blood red, bronze, deep
shadow-brown. Warm gold highlights only. No bright or saturated modern colour.

## STYLE
Baroque oil, period-documentary (Rembrandt / Caravaggio), fine canvas texture, reverent.

## CAST — locked faces (same person every scene)
- **AARON** — the first high priest, aged ~80, Moses' brother. Weathered, sun-darkened
  **Hebrew** face; deep-set eyes; long grey-white beard; calm gravity. On the Day of
  Atonement he wears **plain white linen** (Lev 16:4) — coat, sash, breeches, mitre,
  **barefoot** — NOT the golden breastplate/ephod (he lays those aside, scenes 2–3).
  → locked to one reference portrait, attached to every Aaron scene.
- **CHRIST** — the living/risen Lord (the reveal). Semitic, robed, reverent, wounds in
  the hands. Same face in scenes 17, 18, 20, 22, 25. → locked to the approved
  risen-Christ reference.
- **THE PEOPLE** — Israelite congregation, ancient Near-Eastern robes / tunics /
  head-coverings; kept soft and in shadow.

## PERIOD GUARD — banned (kills the drift + the bearded-man bleed)
- No European / medieval / Renaissance dress, peasant caps, brimmed hats, bonnets.
- No stone-temple or cathedral architecture — **it is a TENT.**
- No modern metal, fabric, objects, chairs or furniture out of period.
- No Greco-Roman or Buddha idols.
- **No other elderly bearded men in side / crowd roles unless named** ← stops NBP
  defaulting its generic bearded-elder into half the scenes.
- No legible text or writing.

A scene is designed as part of one film, never in isolation. Same era, place, light
