# Two Goats — 16:9 scene plan (period-documentary)

25 scenes · 584.5s · hero #25 (Christ-close)


## Beat 1 — I was there
** 1. Once a year, only once**  (0–20s · wide · cam: pull_back)
   The high priest Aaron, small in golden vestments, stands alone before the towering curtained Tabernacle court at first light; behind and below him a vast hushed multitude of Israelites kept in shadow; immense pale sky; the sacred tent dominant and severe.
** 2. I laid aside gold and glory**  (20–39s · close · cam: zoomed_in · still/veil)
   Still-life: a golden breastplate, ephod and priestly garments laid upon a worn stone bench in a single shaft of light; two weathered hands releasing the gold; plain folded white linen waiting beside it. No face, hands only.
** 3. Plain white linen, like a servant**  (39–58s · medium-wide · cam: dolly_shot)
   Aaron, now in plain white linen, seen from behind, walking down a dim colonnaded passage toward a great heavy veil lit by hanging oil lamps; his back fully to us.
** 4. I went in alone**  (58–78s · wide · cam: dolly_in)
   Aaron's small linen-robed silhouette before an immense temple veil; through a narrow gap, thick darkness and a single faint golden glow of the mercy seat; the curtain towering and swallowing him.

## Beat 2 — The world
** 5. That he die not — the cloud upon the mercy seat**  (78–99s · wide · cam: smooth_cinematic · OT-ECHO)
   The Holy of Holies: the golden ark and mercy seat between two great cherubim, a luminous cloud of divine presence above filling the dark chamber; one tiny prostrate linen-robed form at the lower edge, awed and small.
** 6. I buried two of my own sons**  (99–121s · medium · cam: smooth_cinematic · UNIFIED)
   A unified memory tableau, soft-edged like remembrance. Foreground: Aaron's bowed grieving head and shoulders. Background memories dissolving softly into the dark: two fallen young priests amid strange-fire smoke; a shrouded body carried on a bier; an altar with a basin of blood and rising flame; the distant veil. Five soft vignettes, no panels or frames, all bleeding into one dim canvas.

## Beat 3 — The act
** 7. They brought me two goats, and I cast lots**  (121–144s · close · cam: zoomed_in · OT-ECHO, still/veil)
   Two goats standing close together before the altar; a priest's weathered hands casting two small marked lot-stones over a bronze vessel; the markings only illegible scratches, not readable text; tabernacle court behind in shadow.
** 8. The first goat I killed — blood within the vail**  (144–167s · medium · cam: dolly_in)
   Aaron in white linen carrying a shallow bronze basin of blood through thick darkness toward the faint golden glow of the mercy seat, his arm raised to sprinkle; seen from behind and side, the blood catching the light.
** 9. Both my hands upon the live goat**  (167–191s · medium · cam: smooth_cinematic)
   Aaron laying both hands upon the head of a living goat, his own head bowed low in confession; a robed 'fit man' waits at the edge; beyond the camp gate the open wilderness opens pale and vast. Aaron in profile, half-shadowed.
**10. I watched it go — a land not inhabited**  (191–214s · wide · cam: dolly_shot)
   A vast bleached desert wadi to a hazy horizon; a single small goat walking away down the cracked valley floor, reduced near to a dark speck; at the near edge, seen from behind, a lone robed man watches it go, dwarfed by the emptiness. (Reuse the approved test_hero.png.)

## Beat 4 — The strange detail
**11. One offering — yet it took two goats**  (214–240s · medium-wide · cam: smooth_cinematic · UNIFIED)
   One frame holding the riddle: on the left, the slain goat in blood-red altar light and smoke (the price); on the right, the live goat facing an open pale road into the waste (the guilt carried off); the altar standing dark between them. A single unified canvas, soft transition, no hard divider.
**12. Why two? — two things at once**  (240–265s · medium · cam: dolly_in)
   Aaron alone in the emptying court at dusk, turning the question over; behind him one altar still smoking and one empty road running out into the darkening waste; his face lined and contemplative, lit by a single low flame.

## Beat 5 — The wrestling
**13. The people went home clean**  (265–289s · wide · cam: pull_back)
   A great multitude of Israel departing at evening, faces lifted and unburdened, walking away from the tabernacle into golden dusk; the crowd kept soft and in shadow, a sense of relief over the whole field.
**14. Every year I came back and did it again**  (289–314s · medium · cam: smooth_cinematic · UNIFIED)
   A unified tableau of weary repetition: Aaron at the altar repeated as soft ghosted echoes receding into the dark, the same act year upon year; the same two goats faintly doubled; turning seasons hinted in the sky; the same basin of blood; the worn altar stone. Five soft vignettes melting into one dim recurring canvas.
**15. I was only ever pointing — a sign**  (314–338s · medium · cam: dolly_in)
   Aged Aaron, older and weary, alone before the veil at night, looking up toward a faint far light he cannot reach; deep shadow all around; his upturned face caught in a thin edge of light, full of unspoken longing.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
**16. A shadow waits for the body — the body came**  (338–361s · wide · cam: smooth_cinematic · JESUS)
   The long shadow of a cross falls across the old altar and the two goats, cast by an unseen figure of light off-frame; the shadow lengthens toward a luminous open doorway in the far dark; the old order dim, the coming light pale and growing. No gold cross, only shadow and a doorway of light.
**17. By his own blood he entered in once**  (361–383s · medium-wide · cam: dolly_in · JESUS, NBP-face)
   Christ as the true High Priest, robed, seen from behind and side, passing through a torn radiant veil into holy light; he bears his own wounds rather than a basin of blood; darkness behind him, glory opening ahead. The fulfillment of the lone priest at the veil.
**18. The LORD laid on him the iniquity of us all**  (383–406s · medium · cam: smooth_cinematic · UNIFIED, NBP-face)
   A unified fulfillment canvas: the figure of Christ central; soft-edged to the left, the slain goat and its blood (the price paid); soft-edged to the right, the scapegoat vanishing into the wilderness (the guilt carried off); the two dissolving and resolving into the one Priest. Five soft vignettes on one canvas: Christ-centre, slain-goat, scapegoat-road, the-two-becoming-one, a faint cross of light.
**19. He suffered without the gate**  (406–428s · wide · cam: dolly_shot · JESUS)
   Christ led outside the city gate: a lonely robed figure on a road beyond the walls, moving toward a barren hill, deliberately echoing the scapegoat's road into the waste; the city walls behind, the wilderness ahead; seen from a distance, his back to us.
**20. He sat down — the veil rent from the top**  (428–451s · medium-wide · cam: pull_back · JESUS, NBP-face, still/veil)
   Two truths in one frame: the seated High-Priest Christ at rest in glory at the right hand (the chair Aaron never had), and the great temple veil torn from the top downward by no human hand, light pouring through the painted rip. The tear is painted, still, not actively ripping.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
**21. Do not come to me, or a goat, or an altar**  (451–478s · medium · cam: pull_back)
   Aged Aaron in linen steps back and gestures away from himself and from the old altar and goats, turning the viewer's eye past him toward a distant growing light; he diminishes into shadow as the light beyond him grows.
**22. Come to Jesus — carried as far as east from west**  (478–504s · medium-wide · cam: dolly_in · JESUS, NBP-face)
   A lone everyman figure walks free toward the risen Christ, who waits with open hands in dawn light; behind the walking figure a dark burden is being carried off small to the far horizon, never to return; the road ahead bright and open.
**23. The way is thrown wide open**  (504–531s · wide · cam: dolly_in · still/veil)
   The torn temple veil now fully open, brilliant light flooding out into the once-dark holy place; the path through it clear and inviting; no guard, no priest barring the way; the great curtain hanging painted and still, the opening ablaze.
**24. Boldness to enter into the holiest**  (531–558s · medium-wide · cam: smooth_cinematic · UNIFIED)
   Ordinary people of every kind draw near and step through the opened veil into the light, unafraid, where once only one man one day a year could go; kept soft and in gentle shadow so no single face dominates; a quiet procession into glory.
**25. Will you come in? — be carried clean**  (558–584s · medium · cam: dolly_in · JESUS, NBP-face)
   HERO CLOSE: the risen Christ stands in the full open doorway of light, the torn veil framing him, one hand extended toward the viewer in welcome; the way wide open behind him; warm, reverent, the whole frame resolving on his face and open hand. The landing.
