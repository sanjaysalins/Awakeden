Once a year I walked behind a curtain to meet God, and I was never sure I would walk back out. The law gave me two goats for one sin. I killed the first and carried its blood inside the veil. The second I did not kill. I laid my hands on its head, confessed every sin of the people over it, and drove it into the desert to be lost. One to pay. One to carry it away. For years I asked why it took two.
