RETRO COMIC — THE DNA

What actually made old comics (~1938–1985) look retro — the print, the lettering, the panels, the pacing — shown on our own Bible art. Scroll to the bottom for a full mock page.

1 · The print: 4 inks, dots, cream paper

the single biggest "tell"

There was no colour in the file — just 4 inks (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, blacK) at fixed strengths, printed as dots on cream newsprint. Solid areas are flat; everything else is built from visible dots. Black is warm (#231F20), paper is cream (#E8D9B5), never pure.

2 · Ink & line — bold black "holding lines"

Every colour region is fenced by a heavy uniform black outline (it also hid the off-register printing). Bold, blocky, cel-shaded — never airbrushed.

3 · Lettering — the rules that sell it

ALL CAPS, always. Emphasis is bold-italic. Narration lives in a yellow caption box, top-left. Fonts: Kalam for dialogue/captions, Bangers for SFX & titles (both free).

Golgotha. The ninth hour.
Darkness over all the land…
Truly this was the Son of God.

5 · Sound effects — big, outlined, angled

SFX are drawn into the art, not boxed. Outlined + 3D-extruded + tilted. Colour = the sound (yellow impact, red heat, blue cold). Bigger = louder; repeated letters = longer.

K—KRAAK! RUMBLE

6 · Grids — tier-first: 6 or 9 panels

A page is a portrait 2:3 rectangle split into 3 tiers, each cut into 1–3 panels. Keep it strict, then break it once for the big beat. Gutters 2–4% (verticals thinner); black borders ~4–8px.

8 · Flow & pacing

Eye reads Z-path: left→right, top→bottom. Cuts between panels are mostly action-to-action and subject-to-subject. Retro = dense (say it fast, cram the page). The cliffhanger sits bottom-right for the page-turn.

Golden age = crammed & captioned. Silver age = the clean grid, electrified (Kirby) or disciplined (Ditko). Bronze age = the grid loosens, the camera moves, mood arrives.

This is our own art dropped into the retro machine — cream paper, dot screen, black-ruled tiers, yellow caption boxes, a burst balloon, an angled SFX, and the cliffhanger bottom-right. That's "what retro comic actually had."

Bottom line

To read as retro: constrain, don't brighten. Limited 4-colour palette + flat solids + visible dots on cream paper + heavy black lines + off-register + all-caps lettering + yellow caption boxes + big angled SFX + a strict tier grid you break once. Fonts: Kalam (dialogue), Bangers (SFX/titles) — both free, already usable.
