AWAKEDEN — RETRO COMIC DNA

v1.0 · MODERATE RETRO · model: SEEDREAM 4.5 · the "match this" sheet

Our locked series look: a moderate retro comic — bold ink + visible-but-not-blanket Ben-Day dots + rich muted comic colour + cinematic composition — always reverent. Not bland, not loud "holy-card." Full spec: v2/AWAKEDEN_COMIC_DNA.md.

1 · The recipe (how every frame is made)

  1. Model = Seedream 4.5 (won a 14-model bake-off: composes crowds + depth, holds the face, cheap).
  2. Chain the character reference (--image christ_pc_ref.png) into EVERY frame that character is in — this keeps the same Jesus. Skipping it = 3 different Jesuses.
  3. Prompt = moderate retro: bold black ink, flat muted 4-colour, subtle Ben-Day dots in sky/shadow, cream newsprint, warm directional light, composed with depth — no blazing halo, no baked text/panels (Remotion draws those).
  4. Light print-finish on top (dots stay in the shadows, faces clean).

2 · Character lock — every Christ is THIS man

Dark shoulder-length hair, full beard, calm reverent face, simple undyed robe. Chained reference = the same man across every scene (all Seedream 4.5):

3 · The "moderate" dial

Moderate = the middle. Warm directional light + a composed frame with depth (like the hero/welcome above) — NOT a flat centred figure (bland), NOT a blazing sunburst halo (loud / holy-card). Dots are felt, not blanket. Christ's face and robe stay clean.

5 · Composition & flow

Seedream's strength — compose with depth (rule-of-thirds, crowds receding, warm light). The reusable 5-beat arc, opening + closing on Christ:

8 · Do / Don't

DO

  • Render on Seedream 4.5 + the chained character ref, every frame.
  • Compose with depth — warm light, off-centre, crowds receding.
  • Give Scripture its gold treatment; open + close on Christ.
  • Keep the loud vocabulary (heavy dots, SFX) for occasional punch.

DON'T

  • No blazing halo / holy-card; no flat centred bland figure.
  • No bodybuilder Christ on the cross — marred, faint blood (Isaiah 53).
  • No dots on sacred faces; no SFX on the crucifixion / veil / resurrection.
  • No baked text/panels in the art; never a reference-free Christ (he drifts).
