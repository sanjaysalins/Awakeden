Once a year, I went in alone. I am the high priest. On the Day of Atonement I laid aside my gold, put on plain white linen, and carried blood behind the veil — into the thick dark where God dwelt. He had warned us: "that he die not: for I will appear in the cloud upon the mercy seat." They gave me two goats for one sin offering. The first I killed, and sprinkled its blood before the mercy seat. The second I kept alive — I laid both my hands on its head, confessed over it every sin of the people, and sent it away into the wilderness, to carry it all where it would never be found. Two goats. One died. One carried it away. I did this for forty years — and never knew Whose shadow I was casting. Then, far away, a Man died outside the city wall — and the veil of the temple tore in two, from top to bottom, by no human hand. "By his own blood he entered in once into the holy place, having obtained eternal redemption for us." And at last I understood. He was both goats — the blood that paid, and the One who carried it away. He was the true High Priest. And when His work was finished, He sat down. The veil I feared is torn. The way in is open. So do not come to me. Do not come to a goat, or a yearly altar. Come to Jesus — He carried your sin away, as far as the east is from the west. Come to Him, and be carried clean.
