# Independent review — codex (OK, 140s)

**Findings**

- Doctrinal overstatement: `"He died outside the city wall, where the sin offering was always taken."` False as written. Not every sin offering was taken outside the camp; the Day of Atonement sin offering was. Change “always” to this specific offering.

- Typology is too confidently stated: `"the New Testament points to the One in whom both goats meet"` and `"Both goats fulfilled in one Man"`. Hebrews clearly applies blood/sanctuary/outside-the-camp categories to Christ, but it does not explicitly say “both goats meet” in Him. Frame this as faithful typology, not a direct NT claim.

- Possible invented visual detail: `"that curtain ... was torn by a hand from above"` goes beyond Matthew’s wording. `"from the top to the bottom"` supports divine initiative, but “a hand” is theatrical inference, not text.

- Weak OT nuance: `"A cure you must repeat every year is not a cure."` Hebrews says animal blood cannot finally take away sins, but this line risks making God’s appointed ritual sound fake or useless. Say “not the final cure” or “not the finished atonement.”

- Application clarity problem: `"Your sin, if it is on Him"` is clever but unclear for a first-time listener. How does a listener know their sin is “on Him”? The CTA needs a clearer gospel response tied to Hebrews’ “draw near.”

- Potential universalizing close: `"The sin is gone. Come."` After “if it is on Him,” this lands as if removal is already true for every listener before faith/coming. Better: “Bring it to Him; by His blood, the way is open.”

- Hook is decent but not strongest: `"Once a year — only once — a whole nation held its breath for a single man."` It creates tension, but the artifact is titled “The Two Goats,” and the truly strange hook does not arrive until Movement 3. First five seconds would grip harder with “One goat died. One goat disappeared.”

- Thread mostly holds, but Movement 6 partly swaps images: `"He stands. He always stands"` shifts from two goats to priestly posture. It supports “finished work,” but it competes with the stated two-goat spine. Keep it shorter or tie it directly back to the two halves.

- KJV words appear exact in the checked Scripture quotations, but several quotes are silently truncated and repunctuated as complete sentences: `"And he shall take ... for a sin offering."`, `"Then shall he kill ... within the vail."`, and `"Having therefore ... by the blood of Jesus."` If “verbatim” includes punctuation/completeness, revise with ellipses or quote the full clause. VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove overclaims: especially “always taken” and “New Testament points to both goats meet.”
2. Clarify the invitation so the listener knows how the atonement is received.
3. Tighten the hook around the two goats instead of delaying the central strange detail.
