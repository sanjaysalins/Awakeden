## Weakest element: **HOOK**

The landing does the theological work. The hook spends its first 3 seconds explaining instead of landing a line that makes someone stop.

## One punchier rewrite (weakest = hook)

> **Psalm 22 ends: “that he hath done this.” A thousand years later, Jesus’ last words from the cross were: “It is finished.” Same song. Same finished work.**

Why this is better:
- Opens with the actual KJV phrases, not a paraphrase
- “Thousand years later” gives immediate scale
- No deferred payoff — the quote arrives in the hook itself
- Still earned; no invented symmetry
