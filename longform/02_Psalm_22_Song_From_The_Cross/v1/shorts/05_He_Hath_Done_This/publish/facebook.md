## TITLE
Psalm 22's Last Line and Jesus' Last Word Land on the Same Note

## DESCRIPTION
Psalm 22 closes with God's saving work declared to people not yet born:

Psalm 22:31 (KJV): "They shall come, and shall declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this."

A thousand years later, Jesus' last word from the cross was "It is finished." Different words, same note: a completed work. Nothing left to earn. Only someone to come home to.
