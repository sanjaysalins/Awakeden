## Weakest element: **HOOK**

The landing does real gospel work. The hook fails the strict 3-second scroll test: it opens on what *other people* do wrong instead of what the text *does* — and it takes four sentences to reach the turn.

## One punchier rewrite (hook only)

> **Psalm twenty-two starts forsaken — but it doesn't end at the cross. Before the psalm closes, that same voice is praising in the congregation.**

KJV-grounded (Ps 22:1 → 22:22), no invented facts, no “most people,” concrete textual pivot in the first breath.
