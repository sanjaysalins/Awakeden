## TITLE
Most People Stop Reading Psalm 22 at the Cross. Keep Going.

## DESCRIPTION
Most people stop reading Psalm 22 at the cross. But the psalm doesn't end there.

At verse 22, the same voice that cried "forsaken" turns to praise:

Psalm 22:22 (KJV): "I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee."

Who is that praising voice? Hebrews 2:12 takes that exact line and puts it in the mouth of the risen Christ. The psalm's turn is Jesus, alive on the far side of the cross.

And hear what He calls us: Hebrews says He is not ashamed to call us brethren. Brothers. Family.

He was forsaken so you could be family. The risen Christ is calling you in.
