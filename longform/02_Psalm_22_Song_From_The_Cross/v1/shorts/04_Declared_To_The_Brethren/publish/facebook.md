## TITLE
Most People Stop Reading Psalm 22 at the Cross. Keep Going.

## DESCRIPTION
Psalm 22 doesn't end at the crucifixion. At verse 22, the forsaken voice turns:

Psalm 22:22 (KJV): "I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee."

Hebrews 2:12 puts that exact line in the mouth of the risen Christ. And Hebrews 2:11 says He is not ashamed to call us brethren. Family. He was forsaken so you could be family.
