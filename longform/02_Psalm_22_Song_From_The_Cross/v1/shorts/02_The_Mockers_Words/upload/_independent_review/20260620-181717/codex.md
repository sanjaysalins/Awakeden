# Independent review — codex (OK, 90s)

**Findings**

- **KJV-strict failure, YouTube Short / Description:** `Psalm 22:7-8 (KJV): "they shake the head... let him deliver him."` is not Psalm 22:7-8 verbatim. It is a mid-verse excerpt, omits part of both verses, and changes the cut-off punctuation. Same issue appears in **YouTube Long / Description** and **Facebook / Description**.

- **KJV-strict failure, YouTube Long / Description:** `"He trusted in God — let him deliver him now."` is a truncated, re-punctuated Matthew 27:43 quote. If quoted, it must be exact KJV or clearly marked as a partial phrase.

- **Systemic overclaim across platforms:** **YouTube Short** says `the exact taunt` and `Same words`; **YouTube Long** says `the exact words` and `word for word`; **Facebook** says `word for word`; **TikTok/Instagram** say `the exact taunt` and `Same words`. This is not defensible. Psalm 22 and Matthew 27 strongly correspond, but they are not word-for-word identical in KJV.

- **Hooky but not honest enough:** **Title option 3** says `The Crowd at the Cross Was Quoting Psalm 22 Without Knowing It`. “Quoting” promises verbatim quotation. The safer claim is echoing, fulfilling, or repeating the taunt.

- **Doctrinal/history overreach, YouTube Long / Description:** `David wasn't writing a song about himself.` Psalm 22 is a Davidic psalm and can be read as both Davidic lament and messianic prophecy. This line invites an easy correction.

- **Platform fit issue:** **TikTok** and **Instagram** use `Subscribe to walk through...`; native CTA should be “Follow.” “Subscribe” reads copied from YouTube.

- **Kit integrity issue:** The artifact says `Copy each block straight into the platform's upload form` while its own gates show `FAILURES`. That is a bad operational instruction for known-failing metadata.

VERDICT: FAIL
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove every “exact,” “same words,” “word for word,” “quoting,” and “reciting” claim unless the KJV quotes are made truly verbatim.
2. Fix the Psalm 22 and Matthew 27 quotations: either quote full exact KJV or label them clearly as partial excerpts.
3. Replace `David wasn't writing a song about himself` with a Davidic-and-messianic framing.
