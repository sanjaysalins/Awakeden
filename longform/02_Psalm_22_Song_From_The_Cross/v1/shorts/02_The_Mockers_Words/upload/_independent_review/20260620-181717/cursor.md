## Independent adversarial review — Upload Kit: *The Mockers' Own Words*

### 1. KJV / precision — real failures, and the red-team contradicts itself

**UK-G2 is correctly failing**, but the in-engine red-team wrongly says *"KJV quotes are verbatim in all three platforms."* That is false.

**YouTube (Short) — `"Same words."`**  
The video itself was locked on *"the same gesture. The same taunt"* because the openings differ: Psalm 22:8 has **"on the LORD"**; Matthew 27:43 has **"in God"** (plus **"now"** and more). Calling them "Same words" is an overclaim a skeptic can clip in one screenshot.

**YouTube (Short) — `"the exact taunt — \"let him deliver him now\" (v.43)"`**  
That is not the exact KJV taunt. Matthew 27:43 KJV in full: *"He trusted in God; let him deliver him now, if he will have him: for he said, I am the Son of God."* Labeling a clipped fragment "exact" is a precision lie.

**YouTube (Long-form) — opening quoted line**  
`"He trusted in God — let him deliver him now."`  
Uses an em-dash, not KJV’s semicolon; presented in quote marks as if KJV. UK-G2 flags this block — correctly.

**YouTube (Long-form) — `"both appear at the cross, word for word"`**  
False on two counts: (1) wording differs LORD/God + *now*; (2) Matthew splits gesture (27:39 passers-by) from taunt (27:41–43 leaders). The locked narration explicitly unfused those groups. Metadata fuses them back into one "crowd."

**Facebook — `"Matthew 27 records both at Calvary, word for word"`**  
Same problem. "Word for word" is bait the text does not support.

**TikTok — `"Same words."`**  
Same overclaim.

**Instagram — `"Same words."`**  
Same overclaim.

**Psalm 22:7–8 block**  
The quoted span is an excerpt (fine), but it is labeled `(KJV)` while UK-G2 still fails — meaning the kit’s own gate engine rejects it as non-verbatim against the project’s KJV truth source. Do not ship with a `(KJV)` label the validator rejects.
