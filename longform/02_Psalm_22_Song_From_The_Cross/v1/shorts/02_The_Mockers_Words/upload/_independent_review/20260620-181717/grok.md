## Independent adversarial review — Upload Kit: *The Mockers' Own Words*

### 1. KJV / precision — real failures, and the in-engine red-team contradicts itself

**UK-G2 is correctly failing** (`youtube_short`, `youtube_long`), but the red-team block wrongly says *"KJV quotes are verbatim in all three platforms… ✓"* while the header still shows **❌ UK-G2**. That contradiction means the kit was not actually cleared.

**YouTube (Short) — partial Matthew quote flagged as KJV-overlap:**  
`the exact taunt — "let him deliver him now" (v.43)`  
That five-word fragment is **not** Psalm 22:7–8 KJV. It is a slice of **Matthew 27:43 KJV** (`He trusted in God; let him deliver him now, if he will have him: for he said, I am the Son of God.`). The upload gate treats it as a scripture span with ≥50% word overlap to the anchor verse, so it fails verbatim check. Calling it **"exact"** doubles the damage — a skeptic can clip "not KJV / not exact."

**YouTube (Long-form) — unmarked, non-verbatim "scripture" in quotes:**  
Opening line: `"He trusted in God — let him deliver him now."`  
Problems stacked:
- Uses an **em dash** where KJV has a **semicolon** after *God*.
- Truncates Matthew 27:43.
- Presented as crowd speech in quotes, **not** labeled `Matthew 27:43 (KJV)`.
- Wording is **Matthew** (*trusted in God*), not **Psalm 22:8** (*trusted on the LORD*). Fine as narration; bad as a implied KJV quote.

**Psalm 22:7–8 block (YouTube Short, YouTube Long, Facebook):**  
`"they shake the head, saying, He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him: let him deliver him."`  
Labeled `Psalm 22:7–8 (KJV)` but **drops** v.7 opening (*All they that see me laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip,*), v.8 tail (*seeing he delighted in him*), and uses no ellipsis. Excerpting is fine **only if marked**; as written it reads like the full labeled reference.

### 2. "Word for word" / "exact" / "Same words" — doctrine-adjacent overclaim (bait-and-switch risk)

The video’s real argument is **strong parallel** (gesture + taunt), not **identical wording** across OT and NT.

| Platform | Problem phrase |
|----------|----------------|
| **YouTube Long** | `the exact words` + `both appear at the cross, word for word` |
| **Facebook** | `Matthew 27 records both at Calvary, word for word` |
| **TikTok** | `sneered the exact taunt` + `Same words` |
| **Instagram** | `sneered the exact taunt` + `Same words` |
| **YouTube Short** | `the exact taunt` |

Psalm 22:8: *He trusted **on the LORD** that he would deliver him: let him deliver him*  
Matthew 27:43: *He trusted **in God**; let him deliver him **now**, if he will have him…*

That is **fulfillment by correspondence**, not "word for word." Apologetics-minded commenters will pounce; UK-G3 passed only because those phrases are not on the clickbait token list — they are still **SEO/doctrine precision lies**.

The red-team caught **"exact taunt"** on YouTube only and did **not** chase the same language on **TikTok, Facebook, Instagram**, or the **"word for word"** cluster.

### 3. YouTube Long — David line is a debatable negative claim

`David wasn't writing a song about himself.`  
Psalm 22 is headed *A Psalm of David* and is read as **both** Davidic lament **and** messianic. Flat denial invites "actually David was suffering" pushback and derails the thread. Safer: *David recorded a scene he could not have witnessed* — let the Matthew parallel carry the punch.
