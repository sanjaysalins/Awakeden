## Weakest element: **hook**

The landing does real gospel work and stays grace-clean. The hook fails the strict 3-second scroll test: it opens on *what the psalm does* instead of *what the psalm says*, and it spends its opening window on setup before the contrast lands.

---

## One punchier rewrite (hook)

> **One forsaken man, dying alone — and his own psalm ends with every nation on earth turning to the LORD.**

Same thread, same KJV truth (Ps 22:1 → 22:27), no hype, no invented facts — but the contrast hits in the first breath instead of after “Psalm twenty-two opens with…”

---

**VERDICT: REVISE** — Grace anchor passes; landing is adequate but bloated. Hook stop-power fails the scroll test as written. One surgical hook swap likely clears it.
