TITLE
The God Who Made Every Ocean Cried 'I Thirst' from the Cross
DESCRIPTION
The One who made every ocean gasped two words from the cross: I thirst.

Psalm 22:15 (KJV): "My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death."

Dried clay. Tongue stuck fast. A man sinking into the dust of death. David wrote this a thousand years before, in the first person, but about someone else.

Psalm 22 doesn't foretell that cry. It paints the body that said it, a thousand years early.

Think who is thirsting: the One who made every river, who offers the world living water, hanging there with nothing. He went dry in your place. He thirsted so you could drink and never thirst again. And that water is Himself.
