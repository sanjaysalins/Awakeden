## TITLE
The God Who Made Every Ocean Cried 'I Thirst' from the Cross

## DESCRIPTION
Psalm 22:15 (KJV): "My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death."

David painted this body a thousand years before Jesus hung on the cross. Dried clay. Tongue stuck fast. The body failing. Then from the cross, Jesus gasped: I thirst.

The One who made every river and offers living water went dry in your place. He thirsted so you could drink and never thirst again.
