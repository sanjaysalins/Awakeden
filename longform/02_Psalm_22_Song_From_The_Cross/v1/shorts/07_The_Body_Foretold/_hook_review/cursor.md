### Hook (strong)

**First ~3 seconds:** *"A king described a dying body so exactly it reads like an eyewitness account of a crucifixion he never saw."*

- **Stop power (4):** Specific, visual, and genuinely strange — a king describing a death he never witnessed. That earns the pause. Not a 5 because it opens like a mini-essay; the thumb stops on curiosity, not shock.
- **Earned (5):** Solid. Psalm 22 in the first person, David as king, bodily details that match crucifixion *descriptively* — all defensible. "Reads like an eyewitness account" is careful framing, not fake prophecy hype.
- **Grace (5):** Pure intrigue. No fear, no bargain, no pressure.

### Landing (weaker)

The close spends ~25 seconds **re-explaining** what the middle already showed: bones counted, Rome's timeline, "written early," then a doctrinal tag. By scroll-test standards, the last beat should **deliver**, not summarize.

- **Grace (3):** *"He was crushed in your place"* is true gospel substitution, but it lands as a **transaction closing** rather than an invitation. Paired with *"so you'd know none of it was chance,"* the final note is apologetics + atonement fact, not grace opening toward the viewer. Your own narration guard for this short originally pushed away from this cadence toward *willing bearing* language.
- **To Christ (3):** Jesus is named. *"Bring you home"* is warm. But the viewer is never personally invited **to Him** — the last line ends on what was done *for* them, not who Christ **is** and that He still receives sinners. Per your charter, that's a landing miss: strong hook, tired close.

## Weakest element: **landing**

### One punchier rewrite (KJV-truthful, no hype)

Replace the closing block from *"David never saw a crucifixion…"* through the end with:

> **David never saw a cross — Rome had not taken it up yet — yet his psalm names a body pulled apart and stared at. Jesus bore that song on the tree, willingly — and He is still the One who brings sinners home.**

Why this works:
- Keeps the thread image (joints loose, staring eyes — Ps 22:14, 17).
- Names **who Christ is** (the Man on the tree; the One who brings home).
- Ends on grace invitation, not evidence recap or debt language.
- No invented facts; no banned tired closer (*"Will you trust Him?"*).
