TITLE
A King Described a Crucifixion 1,000 Years Before Rome Invented It
DESCRIPTION
A king described a dying body so exactly it reads like an eyewitness account of a crucifixion he never saw.

Psalm 22:14 (KJV): "I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint." Psalm 22:17 (KJV): "I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me."

Every joint pulled loose, the way a body hangs when suspended by the arms. Every bone visible and countable, while onlookers stared.

David never saw a crucifixion. Rome would not make it its instrument for centuries. Yet his song described it exactly.

This is what Jesus bore to bring you home, every wrenched joint, every cold stare, recorded centuries early so you would know none of it was chance. He was crushed in your place.
