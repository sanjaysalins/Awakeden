## One rewrite (hook only — punchier, KJV-truthful)

**Current opener:**
> The loneliest sentence Jesus ever spoke wasn't His own line — He was quoting.

**Rewrite:**
> From the cross, Jesus didn't speak His own words first — He cried a psalm David wrote a thousand years before.

Why this works: cross + quote + David in one breath; drops the unverifiable “loneliest”; still earns the Ps 22:1 payoff that follows (“David's very first line:”).

**VERDICT: REVISE**

Doctrine and grace anchor are sound — this isn’t FAIL territory. But a strict scroll-test editor wouldn’t ship as-is: the hook under-delivers in the first 3 seconds, and the landing’s final beat is slightly generic where it should land on **who** this forsaken-yet-faithful Son is. Tighten the opener; optionally swap the last clause from “way home is open” to something thread-locked (e.g. the forsaken One who still calls God *my God* — and bore that forsaking so you never would).
