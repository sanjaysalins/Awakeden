TITLE
The Loneliest Sentence He Ever Spoke Was Not His Own Line
DESCRIPTION
The loneliest sentence Jesus ever spoke wasn't his own line. He was quoting.

Psalm 22:1 (KJV): "My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?"

David wrote it a thousand years before. At the ninth hour, Jesus cried those exact words from the cross. Not a coincidence, a deliberate quotation. He opened the psalm on purpose.

Even in that moment, he still called God his God. This was not a cry of lost faith. It was the sinless Son taking our place, bearing the forsaking our sin deserved.

He was forsaken so that you never will be. The way home is open. He opened it from the dark.
