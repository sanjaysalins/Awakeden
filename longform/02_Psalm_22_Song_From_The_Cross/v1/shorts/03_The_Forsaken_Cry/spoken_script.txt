The loneliest sentence Jesus ever spoke wasn't his own line. He was quoting, Psalm 22 opens in the voice of a forsaken man. David wrote it, Jesus made it his own. David's very first line, my God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? A thousand years later, at the ninth hour, Jesus cried it from the cross. My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? The very same words. This was no cry of lost faith. Even forsaken, he still calls God, my God. It was the sinless Son taking our place and bearing the forsaking that our sin deserved. He was forsaken so that you never will be. However far you've run, the way home is open, and he opened it from the dark.
