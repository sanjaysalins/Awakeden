*"They rolled dice for the clothes off His back."* Casting lots ≠ dice; it's a colloquial paraphrase outside quotes, so not a KJV violation, but on a short whose whole selling point is *"recorded exactly"* / *"to the letter,"* a precision slip in the climax line is a soft spot a hostile reader will poke.
