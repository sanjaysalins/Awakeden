TITLE
They Cast Lots for His Clothes, Written Down 1,000 Years Before
DESCRIPTION
Ten centuries before the cross, a psalm recorded exactly how a dying man's executioners would handle his clothes.

Psalm 22:18 (KJV): "They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture."

Two acts named: dividing the clothes, and casting lots for the seamless coat. A thousand years later, John watched both happen at Calvary, then cited this verse directly.

David was never executed. He died an old man. He was describing someone else.

That someone was Jesus. The soldiers rolled dice for the clothes off his back, never seeing what they were doing. God had it written down a thousand years early so when it happened, you would know the cross was no accident. It was the plan.
