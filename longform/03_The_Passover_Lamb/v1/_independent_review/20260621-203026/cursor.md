### KJV verbatim — FAIL on stated standard

The header claims: *"KJV quotes below are verbatim (verified vs cache)"* while also *"PENDING: KJV-strict lock."* Those cannot both be true. Against cache, at least one quoted string is altered:

**1 Corinthians 5:7 (twice)** — M4 and M6:
> **"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us."**

KJV (cache): **"For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us:"**

The lead-in **"For even"** is dropped inside the quotation marks both times. Your lens requires every word inside quotes to be exact KJV. That is not a boundary trim; it is a missing word inside the quote. Fix: either quote the full clause or put the words outside the marks (e.g., *Paul says Christ our passover is sacrificed for us*).

Other bracketed quotes (Ex 12:3, 5–6, 7, 13, 46; John 19:33, 36; 1 Pet fragment) match cache as partial citations. No other altered *words* spotted inside quotes — but the 1 Cor issue alone blocks a KJV PASS, especially with the false "verified" claim.

### Clarity on first hearing — serious problem in M7

Movement 7 opens with a sentence that fights the rest of the movement and the stated landing thesis:

> **"The blood on the doorpost did no good *outside* the house."**

Exodus 12 puts the blood **on** the doorposts — the **outside** of the house. That is where it belongs and where it works. Two sentences later you say salvation required blood **"on their own door."** Then you build the grace insight:

> **"Their whole security is outside of them."**

So the landing argues: objective blood **outside** the family (not inner feelings) **is** what saves. The opener says blood on the doorpost did no good **outside** the house. On a plain first hearing, that is self-contradictory.

A listener with zero Bible background will not supply the missing intent (*blood left unapplied in a basin*, or *mere belief inside*). They will hear: *outside = ineffective*, then *outside = where security rests*. That is a logic break at the most important beat.

Likely a word slip (**"inside"** for assent-without-application, or **"unapplied"** for blood still in the basin) — but as written it fails clarity.

### One thread spine — unredeemed clue

The metadata thread and M3 treat **"killed by the whole assembly"** as a core over-specified detail alongside blemish, inspection, doorframe, and bone-rule.

M3 gives it weight:
> **"the whole assembly of the congregation of Israel shall kill it in the evening."** … **"every family across the nation, each slaying its own lamb at the same appointed twilight. One people, one hour, one kind of death."**

M4–M5 recap the pattern for the objection rebuttal: spotless, inspected, doorframe, bone-rule, timing — **"whole assembly" never returns.** In a piece whose argument is *precise correspondence*, raising a flagged clue and not matching it (corporate death at twilight / all Israel / one hour) weakens the typology case. v1.2 fixed the factual gloss but not the payoff.

Either connect it (corporate guilt, one nation, one hour at the cross — if you can do so faithfully) or stop listing it as a spine clue.

### Doctrinal soundness — mostly solid; a few nits

**Strengths:** Substitution is clean (*"It died *instead* of the firstborn"*; *"God providing the substitute Himself"*). M2’s point that Israelite **address/bloodline does not auto-save** is a faithful, useful read of Ex 12:12–13. NT links (1 Cor 5:7, John 19, 1 Pet 1:18–19) are mainstream evangelical typology, not contrarian clickbait. M5’s objection handling is honest.

**Nits (not fails, but watch them):**

- **M3:** *"Two uprights, and a beam across the top"* nudges a crucifix shape from a Passover **doorframe** the text never identifies as cross-shaped. Typological hint, not text — a skeptical ear may hear overreach.
- **M4:** *"Jesus is crucified at Passover, the very feast where every family in the city is killing a lamb"* — chronology is debated (Johannine vs Synoptic timing). Common in evangelical preaching, but it is asserted as clean fact for typological punch.
- **M4 Pilate:** *"even Pilate… said he found no fault in Him at all"* — legal acquittal, not the four-day lamb inspection. You partially correct with *"across a public life no accusation could be made to stick to"* — still a stretch to fold Pilate into the inspection motif.

None of these invent doctrine; they are compression choices.
