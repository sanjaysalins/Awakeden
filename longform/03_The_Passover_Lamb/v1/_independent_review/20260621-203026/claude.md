## Real findings

**1. M1↔M2 self-contradiction on first hearing.** M1: "judgment is finally falling — **not on Israel**, but on the nation that enslaved them." M2, ~30s later: "God does not say the Israelites are automatically safe… **Death is coming to *their* doors too.**" To a listener with zero Bible knowledge these flatly contradict. The resolution (judgment targets Egypt, but the destroyer doesn't distinguish a home without blood) is true and is exactly the engine of the whole piece — but it's never stated, so the surface reads as a flat reversal. This is the one place the "follow it on first hearing" test fails. Fix M1's line to "falling on Egypt" *for a reason the next movement turns on*, or add one bridging clause.

**2. A foregrounded detail that never pays off.** M3's climactic recap list is: "A spotless male lamb, inspected and found faultless, **killed by the whole assembly**, its blood on a frame of wood, not one bone broken." Four of those five get an explicit Christ-match (spotless→without blemish; inspected→public life/Pilate; blood on wood→cross; no bone→John 19). "Killed by the whole assembly" is the only one left dangling — never mapped. If the omission is deliberate (wisely dodging the "the people killed Jesus" trope), then don't promote it into the payoff list; it sets up a parallel the script declines to honor and a sharp listener feels the gap.

**3. Doorframe-as-cross is the load-bearing stretch — keep it soft.** "Two uprights, and a beam across the top… its blood on a frame of wood" → cross. This is the typological move most exposed to the M5 objection ("reading the ending back in"). The draft is careful (says "a frame of wood," not "the door was cross-shaped"), and M5 honestly defends it — but the timing parallel and bone-rule are far stronger evidence than the door shape. Lead M5's defense with the bone-rule + timing (it currently buries them mid-list) so the weakest link isn't carrying weight.

Everything else holds: thread is one clean spine, conviction is grace-anchored (the "security is outside of them / not your blood — His" landing does genuinely new work and avoids the banned fear/loss leverage — confirmed the revision note's claim against the text), hook grips in the first sentence. These are polish seams on an otherwise sound draft.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Resolve the M1 "not on Israel" vs M2 "death is coming to their doors too" contradiction — add the one bridging clause (no blood = no distinction) so a first-time hearer isn't tripped.
2. Either pay off "killed by the whole assembly" with a Christ-match or drop it from the M3 recap list so no foregrounded detail dangles.
3. Reorder M5's defense to lead with the bone-rule + Passover-timing (strongest evidence) and treat the doorframe-shape as supporting, not central.
