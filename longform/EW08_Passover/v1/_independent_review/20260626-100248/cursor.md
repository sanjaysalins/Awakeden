## EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Inside the quote spans:** The bold fragments appear to be verbatim KJV substrings against a standard Exodus 12 / John 19 / 1 Peter 1 / 1 Cor 5 pericope. I did not find an altered word *inside* any `**"..."**` block.

**Attribution is still inconsistent under a strict reading:**

1. **Beat 3 command cluster** — After *"The command had come down through Moses from the LORD:"* only the first quote follows a clear source line. These four drop with no repeated tag or frame:
   - **`"Your lamb shall be without blemish, a male of the first year"`**
   - **`"the whole assembly of the congregation of Israel shall kill it in the evening"`**
   - **`"ye shall take a bunch of hyssop, and dip it in the blood that is in the bason, and strike the lintel and the two side posts"`**
   - **`"none of you shall go out at the door of his house until the morning"`**

2. **Beat 4 — both quotes unattributed** — *"Two things in that command I never understood"* then straight into:
   - **`"when he seeth the blood upon the lintel, and on the two side posts, the LORD will pass over the door, and will not suffer the destroyer to come in unto your houses to smite you"`**
   - **`"neither shall ye break a bone thereof"`**
   No *Moses said*, *the LORD commanded*, or **`[Moses]`** before either.

3. **Beat 6 — 1 Cor 5:7 weak frame** — *"just as the death that hung over my son had fallen on the lamb:"* then **`"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"`**. The colon is not a speaker/source; contrast Beat 6’s better frames (*"The word written of Him says"*, *"the Scripture says"*, *"it stands written"*) used for the other NT quotes.

4. **Beat 5 — improved but still thin** — *"The record of that night would say it plainly:"* before **`"there was not a house where there was not one dead"`** is better than raw drop-in, but *"the record"* is vague for a zero-Bible listener (Moses? Scripture? Egypt’s chronicle?).

**Mis-framing (not a word-change, but a meaning shift):**

5. Beat 6 setup for 1 Peter is now **better** than a *"He was the precious blood"* predicate — *"we were redeemed not with silver or gold, but"* → **`"with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot"`** aligns with KJV sense. Keep that; the remaining issue is still missing a named source tag before the quote block.

## EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Passes the bar:**
- Names **Jesus**: *"Come home to Jesus"*
- Names the response: *"will you make His blood your own"*, *"hide behind Him"*, *"Come home"*
- Grace-anchored: *"Not because you are good — I was not"*
- Avoids the banned bare *"will you trust Him?"* template

**Borderline:**

- *"will you make His blood your own"* — vivid for insiders; a first-hearing listener may not know what that means practically (faith? shelter? sacrament?). **Come** is named; **believe/receive** less so than the bone thread names John.

## EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Earned well (best work in the piece):**
- Beat 4’s bone riddle → Beat 6’s John 19:33/36 payoff: *"My lamb's unbroken bones had been pointing across the centuries to His"* is **shown** from a detail the witness obeyed without understanding.
- Spotless-lamb language (*"without blemish, without spot"*) is text-grounded and parallelled cleanly.

**Asserted, not shown:**
- *"There came another Lamb"* — pivot by announcement; the witness never saw a second lamb.
- *"Only now do I see that my lamb without blemish was His shadow — and a shadow is cast only by a body that comes after"* — typology label dropped in; the signpost (*"from the far side of my days, by a light that fell long after my night was over"*) satisfies EW-INV-11’s reflective device, but EW-G7 still asks whether the turn is **walked** from the lived night — here it is mostly **told**.
- The **blood-must-be-applied** strand is the piece’s sharpest lived mystery (*"It was not enough that the lamb had died. The blood had to be applied"*), but Beat 6 resolves it by assertion: *"was His blood, poured out and reckoned to everyone who comes to shelter beneath it"* — **no attributed NT verse** on sheltering/applying/faith (unlike the bone thread, which stacks John). The doctrinal center of Passover typology is preached, not quote-shown.
- *"This was the substitution my doorpost had been whispering all my life, and I never heard it"* — summary rhetoric. The doorpost whispered **firstborn-for-lamb**, not **Christ-for-the-world**, until Beat 6 asserts the extension.
- *"The death I deserved fell on Him instead"* — jumps from **firstborn under plague** to **personal desert** without a step for a zero-Bible listener. Beat 2’s *"If the judgment went by who deserved it, my door had no claim on mercy"* partially sets it up, but Beat 6 does not re-anchor before *"We all stand under sentence."*

## EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Genuine opening:**
*"How could the blood of a lamb stand in for the life of my son? What was a pen animal to the God who made heaven and earth, that a smear of its blood should turn aside His own judgment?"* — real, period-appropriate, internalized.

**Deflated before the reveal:**
- Same beat answers the steel-man empirically before the typology doubt deepens: *"A lamb had died in his place, and the blood of it was on my door"* / *"Something had to die, or my son would have."* Midnight **proves substitution worked**; the intellectual objection is overridden by outcome.
- What survives is softer: *"Was this small creature the end of it, or only a sign, pointing at something I could not yet see?"* — almost a **setup question for Beat 6**, not a steel-man the piece truly wrestles with. Beat 6 answers it immediately with NT montage.
- Beat 5 also **re-proves** Beat 3’s moral (*"I did not earn that difference"* / not saved by being good) — runtime spent restating what the act beat already landed.

Not a strawman; just **resolved too early**, leaving Beat 6 to deliver typology answers to a doubt the witness already half-accepts.

## EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Sound core:** Spotless lamb, blood turning away judgment, substitution (*"the lamb died in his place"* → *"the Lamb of God died in ours"*), **Christ our passover**, no supersession, CTA redirects to Jesus not annual lamb.

**Issues:**

1. **Universal atonement leap** — *"We all stand under sentence; the Lamb of God died in ours"* extends the **firstborn/destroyer** type to **sin-judgment** without showing the step. Orthodox, but **asserted** rather than earned from the type the way John 19 earns the bone.

2. **Penalty named thinly on the Christ side** — firstborn plague / destroyer is vivid; what Christ **bore** is named (*"death I deserved"*, *"judgment"*) but not anchored to a supporting verse (Isaiah 53, Romans, etc.) the way John 19 anchors the bone.

3. **Beat 2 moral framing slightly imprecise to Exodus** — *"I had no innocence to plead. I knew my own heart; I was no better than any Egyptian"* collapses **firstborn-under-same-plague** (blood-marker salvation) into **moral equivalence with Egypt**. Works as grace-not-works setup, but a careful listener may wonder why Israel needed blood if the issue were Egyptian guilt.

## EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strengths:** *"I painted my doorframe with blood"*, four days feeding the lamb, *"I obeyed it. I did not see why"* — hands-on, first-person, not essay-costume for long stretches.

**Weaknesses:**
- **Archetypal, not one man:** *"I will not give you my name — I withhold it on purpose, for I was not one man only"* / *"Let me stand for them all"* — justified composite per header, but the voice stays **Every Father** rather than one remembered man; less characterful than named witnesses (Abraham, Boaz, etc.).
- Beat 6–7 drift to **catechism voice:** *"a shadow is cast only by a body that comes after"* / *"That lamb was only the shadow"* — correct theology, less **I was there** than **let me explain the type**.
- *"Sit with that."* — breaks witness immediacy for a meditation prompt to the YouTube audience.

## First-hearing clarity

- Hook and plague stakes are clear: *"death would walk down our street, and would not pass our house unless I did a strange and bloody thing with my own two hands."*
- **Destroyer** appears in the Beat 4 quote (*"will not suffer the destroyer to come in"*) but is **never explained** for a listener with zero Bible background.
- Beat 6 assumes **Jesus + cross + soldiers breaking legs** without grounding who He is or what crucifixion is.
- Beat 1’s meta-composite framing (*"I was not one man only"*) may confuse before the story starts.

## Hook, spine, LONG runtime

**Hook:** Strong — immediate stakes, bloody act, firstborn son.

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (blood + unbroken bone + spotless lamb). No swap.

**LONG sag (~1,350 spoken words — low in the 1,300–1,650 band):**
- Beat 3 ritual walkthrough **earns** some runtime.
- Beat 5 **re-proves** righteousness-not-the-difference already in Beat 3 (*"We were not saved by being good. We were saved by being inside, under the blood"*).
- Beat 7 **re-teaches** shadow/blood/shelter already landed in Beats 4 and 6 without a fresh turn.
- *"Sit with that."* adds pause, not substance.
- The piece feels **complete around ~8–9 min**, not **deep at 10–11** — thin for the long-form band unless voice pace is very slow.

TOP FIXES:
1. **Earn the blood-applied reveal like the unbroken-bone thread** — add an attributed NT cross-ref for sheltering under Christ’s blood (faith/application), and walk Beat 6 from Beat 4’s *"It was not enough that the lamb had died. The blood had to be applied"* instead of asserting *"reckoned to everyone who comes to shelter beneath it."*

3. **Stop Beat 5 from fully resolving the steel-man before Beat 6** — let midnight prove judgment is real without answering *"How could a lamb’s blood stand in for my son?"* so completely; cut Beat 7 recapitulation (*shadow*, *blood shelters*, *I did not earn it*) and replace with ~150–200 words of **new** earned material so LONG hits runtime without repeating Beats 3–4.
