## Independent adversarial review — *The Passover Lamb* (eyewitness-long)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Quotes checked against KJV cache:** The Exodus/Johannine/Pauline substrings appear verbatim (including the corrected Ex 12:23 ending: *"come in unto your houses to smite you"*). Truncations (Ex 12:3 without the opening *"Speak ye unto all the congregation…"*, hyssop quote without *"with the blood that is in the bason"*) are valid substrings.

**Attribution gaps (strict read still fails):** Only two blocks carry speaker tags (`**[the LORD]**` in Beats 2 and 3). Under EW-INV-4’s “attribute before quoting,” these drop unattributed:

| Location | Quote | Issue |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Beat 3 | `"they shall take to them every man a lamb…"`, `"Your lamb shall be without blemish…"`, hyssop quote, `"none of you shall go out…"` | One Moses/LORD setup, then four quotes with no repeated tag |
| Beat 4 | Ex 12:23 destroyer quote, `"neither shall ye break a bone thereof"` | *"Two things in that command I never understood"* does not re-name Moses/LORD before either quote |
| Beat 6 | `"when they came to Jesus…"`, `"A bone of him shall not be broken"`, `"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"` | Prose frames (*"the Scripture says"*) help, but no `**[Scripture]**` / `**[Moses]**` tags on the bold blocks |

**Attribution framing nit:** Beat 5 — *"The record of that night would say it plainly:"* → **`"there was not a house where there was not one dead"`** — the witness is **inside** the night hearing the cry; citing “the record” (Exodus, written later) is composite-voice leakage, not how a man on the street would speak at midnight.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Passes the bar:** Beat 7 names **Jesus** (*"Come home to Jesus"*, *"the Lamb God provided, Jesus"*), grace-anchored (*"Not because you are good — I was not"*), no bare *"will you trust Him?"* template. The response is **named**: *"make His blood your own"*, *"hide behind Him"*, *"Come home."*

**Weak spots:**

- *"will you **make His blood your own**, as I made the lamb's"* — strong typology, but a zero-Bible listener may hear magic/ritual, not faith. **Come/hide** are clear; **believe/receive** are not.
- *"Sit with that."* — editorial pause, not witness speech; breaks character at the invitation hinge.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Earned (strong):** Beat 4’s bone riddle → Beat 6’s John 19:33/36 stack. *"My lamb's unbroken bones had been pointing across the centuries to His"* is **shown** from a detail the witness obeyed without understanding.

**Asserted / under-walked:**

- *"I did not learn the answer in my lifetime. I tell you it now from the far side of my days, by a light that fell long after my night was over"* — EW-INV-11 signpost is present, but the turn is still **announced**, not walked.
- *"a shadow is cast only by a body that comes after"* — typology lecture; less *I lived this* than *let me explain shadows*.
- The piece’s sharpest lived mystery — *"It was not enough that the lamb had died. The blood had to be applied"* — resolves by assertion: *"was His blood, poured out and **reckoned** to everyone who comes to shelter beneath it"* with **no attributed NT verse** on application/sheltering (unlike the bone thread, which stacks John). The doctrinal center is preached, not quote-shown.
- *"This was the substitution my doorpost had been whispering all my life"* — summary rhetoric. The doorpost whispered **firstborn-for-lamb**, not **Christ-for-the-world**, until Beat 6 asserts the extension.
- *"We all stand under sentence; the Lamb of God died in ours"* — legitimate theology, but **leaped** from firstborn/destroyer to universal sin-judgment without a shown step (Romans, Isaiah 53, etc.).

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Genuine opening:** *"How could the blood of a lamb stand in for the life of my son? What was a pen animal to the God who made heaven and earth, that a smear of its blood should turn aside His own judgment?"* — real, period-appropriate, internalized. Not a strawman.

**Deflated before Beat 6:**

- Midnight **answers the steel-man empirically**: *"Something had to die, or my son would have"* / *"A lamb had died in his place, and the blood of it was on my door."* The intellectual objection is largely overridden by outcome before the reveal earns it.
- What survives — *"Was this small creature the end of it, or only a sign, pointing at something I could not yet see?"* — reads as a **setup line for Beat 6**, not a doubt the piece truly sits in.

**Anachronism (voice + wrestling integrity):** *"a lamb dies, and still we keep this feast **year after year**"* — this is the **first** Passover night. The annual feast is **instituted** that night (Ex 12:24–26); they have not yet kept it “year after year.” That phrase smuggles post-Exodus knowledge into Beat 5’s in-the-moment doubt — especially awkward given v1.1 explicitly removed similar feast language from Beat 4 for over-claim.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Sound core:** Spotless lamb, blood turning away judgment, substitution named (*"the lamb died in his place"* → *"the Lamb of God died in ours"*), **Christ our passover**, no supersession, CTA redirects to Jesus not the annual lamb.

**Issues:**

1. **Universal atonement leap** — *"We all stand under sentence"* extends **firstborn/destroyer** to **sin-judgment** without a shown step or supporting verse.
2. **Christ-side penalty thin** — firstborn plague is vivid; what Christ **bore** is named (*"the death I deserved"*) but not anchored to a supporting verse (Isaiah 53, Romans, etc.).
3. Beat 2: *"I was no better than any Egyptian. If the judgment went by who deserved it, my door had no claim on mercy"* — collapses **blood-marker salvation under the same plague** into **moral equivalence with Egypt**. Works as grace-not-works setup, but a careful listener may wonder why Israel needed blood if the issue were Egyptian guilt alone.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strengths:** *"I painted my doorframe with blood"*, four days feeding the lamb, *"I obeyed it. I did not see why"* — hands-on, first-person, not essay-costume for long stretches.

**Weaknesses:**

- **Archetypal, not one man:** *"I will not give you my name"* / *"Let me stand for them all"* — justified composite, but the voice stays **Every Father** rather than one remembered man; less characterful than named witnesses (Peter, Abraham).
- Beat 6–7 drift to **catechism voice:** *"a shadow is cast only by a body that comes after"*, Beat 7’s full recap of blood/shadow/door — correct theology, less **I was there** than **let me explain the type**.
- Beat 5’s *"year after year"* and *"The record of that night would say"* — composite hindsight breaking the “night I lived” frame.

---

### First-hearing clarity

- Hook and stakes are clear: *"death would walk down our street, and would not pass our house unless I did a strange and bloody thing with my own two hands."*
- **Destroyer** appears in the Beat 4 quote (*"will not suffer the destroyer"*) but is **never explained** for a listener with zero Bible background.
- Beat 6 assumes **Jesus + crucifixion + soldiers breaking legs** without grounding who He is or what crucifixion is.
- Beat 1 composite framing (*"I was not one man only"*) may confuse before the story starts.

---

### Hook, spine, LONG runtime

**Hook:** Strong — immediate stakes, bloody act, firstborn son.

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (blood + unbroken bone + spotless lamb). No swap.

**LONG sag:** ~1,400+ spoken words (within budget), but Beat 5 **re-proves** Beat 3’s moral (*"I did not earn that difference"* vs *"We were not saved by being good"*), and Beat 7 **re-teaches** blood/shadow/door already landed in Beats 4 and 6. *"Sit with that."* adds dead air, not a new turn. The piece reads **complete around ~8–9 min** of substance; extra length is ritual walkthrough (Beat 3) and recap, not new earned beats for a 10–11 min band.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Remove Beat 5’s anachronism** — cut or rewrite *"still we keep this feast year after year"* (first Passover night; the witness cannot have kept it annually yet); replace with an in-the-moment doubt that Beat 6 must answer (e.g. the applied-blood riddle from Beat 4, not future feast-keeping).
2. **Earn the blood-applied reveal like the unbroken-bone thread** — add an attributed NT cross-ref for sheltering under/applied blood, and walk Beat 6 from Beat 4’s *"It was not enough that the lamb had died. The blood had to be applied"* instead of asserting *"reckoned to everyone who comes to shelter beneath it."*
3. **Fix EW-INV-4 attribution on every quote block and cut Beat 7 recap** — tag Beat 3’s command cluster, Beat 4’s mysteries, and Beat 6’s John/1 Cor blocks with explicit `**[Moses]**`/`**[the LORD]**`/`**[Scripture]**` frames; let midnight prove judgment is real without fully resolving *"How could a lamb’s blood stand in for my son?"*; replace Beat 7’s shadow/blood re-teach and *"Sit with that."* with one fresh earned turn so LONG runtime isn’t padding.
