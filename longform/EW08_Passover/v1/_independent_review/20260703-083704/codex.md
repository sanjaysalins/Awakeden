# Independent review — codex (OK, 146s)

**Findings**

- **Format failure:** the witness is not named. `I will not give you my name` and `let me be every father of Israel` violate the named eyewitness requirement and weaken the “one witness, one thread” spine.
- **KJV attribution problem:** the quoted wording appears KJV-exact as excerpts, but several quotes are not attributed before quoting: `The record of that night would say it plainly:`, `The word written of Him says`, `the Scripture says`, `it stands written`, and `Scripture written after me declares it outright`.
- **Voice slips into essay mode:** phrases like `my lamb without blemish was His shadow`, `this was the substitution my doorpost had been whispering`, and `Scripture written after me declares it outright` sound like a preacher’s exposition, not a father in Egypt.
- **Wrestling is underdeveloped:** the strongest line is `mothers weeping over sons as dear to them as mine`, but the piece moves too quickly to `A lamb had died in his place`. That moral pressure needs to be faced, not just used as backdrop.
- **CTA is close but not clean:** `will you make His blood your own... and hide behind Him` is metaphor-heavy. The close should name the response plainly: come to Christ, receive Him, trust His finished sacrifice.
- **Doctrinal precision issue:** `the very words of my own command` overstates the link. Exodus gives `without blemish`; `without spot` is from 1 Peter, not the Passover command.
- **Long-form sag:** Beat 7 repeats Beat 3 and Beat 6: `With these two hands...`, `Something spotless died`, `That lamb was only the shadow`, `The blood that truly shelters is His`. The close should add pressure-clearing gospel clarity, not recap.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Make the narrator a named, single biblical witness and remove the anonymous “every father” frame.
2. Attribute every KJV quote to the speaker/book before the quote and fix the “without spot” overclaim.
3. Rework the close to name faith/receiving/coming to Jesus plainly while deepening the Egyptian grief wrestling.
