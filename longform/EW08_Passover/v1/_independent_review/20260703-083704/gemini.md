# Independent review — gemini (OK, 122s)

This script has strong theological connections and a genuinely earned reveal, but it commits a fundamental violation of the series format, contains chronic quotation errors, and forces a flawed rhetorical transition at the end.

Here are the specific findings:

*   **Format Violation: Anonymous vs. Named Witness:** The lens explicitly demands a *"NAMED biblical witness"*. However, Beat 1 directly refuses this constraint, declaring: *"I will not give you my name — I withhold it on purpose, for I was not one man only... Let me stand for them all; let me be every father of Israel..."* This replaces a grounded, historical eyewitness with a composite literary trope. This breaks the entire premise of the series.
*   **KJV Verbatim Violations (EW-INV-4):** The script chronically drops leading conjunctions and surrounding context from the exact KJV text without indicating omissions via ellipses. "Exact" means exact.
    *   Beat 2: `**"I will pass through..."**` (Exodus 12:12 actually begins: *"**For** I will pass through..."*)
    *   Beat 3: `**"ye shall take a bunch of hyssop..."**` (Exodus 12:22 actually begins: *"**And** ye shall take..."*) It also cuts the verse short without an ellipsis, omitting *"with the blood that is in the bason;"*
    *   Beat 3: `**"none of you shall go out..."**` (Exodus 12:22 actually reads: *"**and** none of you shall go out..."*)
    *   Beat 4: `**"when he seeth the blood..."**` (Exodus 12:23 actually reads: *"**and** when he seeth the blood..."*)
    *   Beat 5: `**"there was not a house..."**` (Exodus 12:30 actually reads: *"**for** there was not a house..."*)
    *   Beat 6: `**"when they came to Jesus..."**` (John 19:33 actually reads: *"**But** when they came to Jesus..."*)
    *   Beat 6: `**"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**` (1 Corinthians 5:7 actually reads *"**For even** Christ our passover..."*)
*   **CTA Logic / Rhetorical Glitch (Beat 7):** The witness attempts to transition to the invitation by saying: *"So I ask you the question I carried through that whole long night... will you make His blood your own...?"* This is logically false based on his own narrative. In Beat 5, the actual questions he wrestled with were *"How could the blood of a lamb stand in for the life of my son?"* and *"Was this small creature the end of it, or only a sign...?"* He did not wrestle with an evangelistic appeal to the listener. The pivot rings hollow and breaks the grounded realism of his internal wrestling.
*   **Long Form Runtime:** The pacing does not sag and the beats are tight, but at roughly 1,100 words, this piece will likely clock in around 7–8 minutes rather than the targeted ~9–11 minutes. While not a fatal flaw given the lack of padding, it sits at the short end for the format.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Ground the script in a specific, named biblical eyewitness (e.g., an explicitly named elder of Israel or tribal head like Amminadab or Nahshon) instead of using an anonymous, composite "everyman."
2. Correct the KJV quotes to be truly verbatim. Restore all dropped leading conjunctions ("For", "And", "But") or properly use ellipses for cropped text. 
3. Fix the logic error in Beat 7's transition: connect the CTA to the actual theological questions he wrestled with in the dark (Beat 5), rather than pretending his wrestling was the final evangelistic question.
