# Independent review — grok (OK, 121s)

**INDEPENDENT REVIEW — *The Passover Lamb* (eyewitness-long)**

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Mostly clean on wording; attribution is uneven.**

Truncations are acceptable (no altered words spotted in the quoted spans). Two attribution problems:

1. **Beat 5 — witness cites “the record” for what he heard himself:**

   > *"The record of that night would say it plainly:"* → **"there was not a house where there was not one dead"**

   He is the eyewitness. He already said he heard the cry *"across the whole of Egypt."* Sending the line through *"the record"* weakens both attribution and voice. If quoted, attribute Scripture (Exodus) — or better, paraphrase in his own mouth without quote marks.

2. **Beat 6 — NT quotes lack named speakers.** OT lines get **[the LORD]**; NT lines do not:

   - *"The word written of Him says we were redeemed... with the precious blood of Christ..."* — no **Peter** / **1 Peter**
   - *"the Scripture says, they stopped:"* → John 19:33 — speaker not named before the quote
   - *"it stands written:"* → **"A bone of him shall not be broken"** — same

   EW-INV-4 asks for the speaker **before** the quote, not after or vaguely.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus

**Mostly passes.** Jesus is named; invitation is grace-anchored; response is named (*"Come home to Jesus"*, *"hide behind Him"*).

Minor friction:

- *"before you turn away, be still a moment"* — light urgency, not full fear-selling, but it nudges manufactured pressure.
- *"will you make His blood your own"* — tied to the door image, but still close to a template question. The stronger, specific close is *"Come home to Jesus"*; the question line could be sharper.

Not a fail, but worth tightening.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Mixed. The typology is earned; the frame announces it.**

**Earned (good):**
- Beat 4 plants two live riddles: blood on the **outside**; **unbroken bones**.
- Beat 5 deepens: *"Was this small creature the end of it, or only a sign, pointing at something I could not yet see?"*
- Beat 6 answers through those same details (John 19 bones, blood on wood, 1 Peter “without blemish”).

**Asserted (problem):**
- *"I did not learn the answer in my lifetime. I tell you it now from the far side of my days, by a light that fell long after my night was over."*
- *"There came another Lamb. Only now do I see that my lamb without blemish was His shadow"*

The turn is **told from omniscience**, not **shown from the night he lived**. He dies unknowing; then a post-death narrator delivers John, Peter, and Paul. The riddles earn the *logic*; the *frame* shortcuts with *"I know now"* and a stack of NT quotes he never heard. That is half announcement.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Strong.** This is real doubt in his voice, not a strawman:

- *"How could the blood of a lamb stand in for the life of my son?"*
- After relief: doubt **deepens**, not vanishes — *"Was this small creature the end of it, or only a sign..."*

The midnight cry (*"I did not earn that difference"*) lands the steel-man honestly. No major flag here.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Sound.** Substitution is named clearly:

- *"the death I deserved fell on Him instead"*
- *"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"*
- *"Something spotless died so my boy could live"* → typology, not confusion of Jesus as Egypt’s “firstborn”

No supersession, no contrarian reading spotted. *"deliverance that need never be done again"* fits once-for-all Christ vs annual feast.

Small overreach: *"No one who comes to His door is ever turned away"* — warm, but broader than the text strictly guarantees. Advisory, not fatal.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strong in Beats 1–5; breaks in 6–7.**

Good:
- Composite father (*"let me be every father of Israel on that night"*) holds one thread.
- Sensory, bodily voice: *"I caught his blood in a basin,"* *"red and wet in the torchlight."*

Breaks:
1. Posthumous NT theologian — see G7 quotes above.
2. Beat 5 *"The record of that night would say"* — essay voice, not a father behind the door.
3. Beat 7 recap — *"With these two hands I struck the blood on the wood..."* — slides from witness memory into sermon summary.

He stops **being** the man; he starts **explaining** the piece.

---

### First-hearing clarity

**Good.** Slave context, firstborn stakes, blood on the door, and “something had to die” are plain without Bible background. *Lintel*, *hyssop*, and *bason* are KJV-faithful; a zero-Bible listener may stumble slightly, but the narration carries them.

---

### Hook / spine

**Strong.** Opening grips:

- *"before the sun rose, death would walk down our street"*

One composite witness, one thread (lamb → blood → bones → Christ). No thread swap.

---

### Long-form runtime — sag / padding

**Some sag in the back half.**

- **Beat 3** — long procedural chain (lamb choice → four days → kill → hyssop → door → shut in). Each step is faithful, but four back-to-back block quotes plus full ritual may feel like **Exodus recitation** before the doubt in Beat 5. Not fatal; could trim one quoted step.
- **Beat 7** — **clear padding**: restates Beat 6 substitution (*lamb shadow, blood shelters, hide behind the door*) then *"Sit with that."* — a beat that adds no new turn before the CTA.
- Refrains of *blood on the wood* / *behind the door* across 5–7 — thematic, but Beat 7 repeats without a fresh hinge.

The middle **earns** runtime (Beat 4 riddles + Beat 5 wrestling). The close **does not** fully earn its minutes.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Re-anchor Beat 6 in the witness’s lived night** — answer the bone/blood riddles from what he *saw and obeyed*, not from a post-death voice that cites John/Peter/Paul; let the Christ turn rise from Beat 4–5, with NT support as brief confirmation and proper attribution, not as the main engine.
2. **Fix EW-INV-4 attribution** — name speakers before every NT quote (**Peter**, **John**, **Paul**); drop *"The record of that night would say"* in Beat 5 and speak from what he heard, or attribute Exodus explicitly.
3. **Cut Beat 7 recap** — remove the second summary (*"With these two hands..."* / *"Sit with that."*) and land the CTA on one fresh image from the door night so the long runtime doesn’t sag at the close.
