# Independent review — codex (OK, 129s)

Checked the KJV fragments against Bible Gateway. I do not see altered wording in the quoted fragments, but the attribution rule is not met consistently.

**Findings**
- **Named witness failure:** The genre asks for a named biblical witness, but the piece says, “I will not give you my name” and “Let me stand for them all.” That makes this an anonymous composite, not a named eyewitness.
- **EW-INV-4 attribution failure:** Several KJV quotes are not attributed before quotation: “Your lamb shall be without blemish…,” “the whole assembly… shall kill it…,” “there was not a house where there was not one dead,” “the precious blood of Christ…,” “when they came to Jesus…,” and both uses of “Christ our passover is sacrificed for us.” The words are KJV-exact, but the speakers/writers need explicit attribution.
- **Voice breaks in Beat 6:** “The command… was a shadow, and a shadow waits for the body that casts it” and “This is the substitution my doorpost had been whispering” sound like an essay narrator, not an Israelite father. The witness becomes a preacher explaining typology.
- **The reveal is partly earned, but over-announced:** The blood and unbroken bones are seeded well. But “I know now what my trembling hands were truly doing” announces the turn before the text shows it. The later connections do the work; this sentence weakens the earned reveal.
- **CTA leans fear-heavy:** “the judgment that is real — as real as the cry I heard at midnight — will pass over you” uses fear pressure at the close. The invitation does name Jesus and “trust,” but it needs more grace-anchored welcome and less gain-loss threat.
- **Possible doctrinal overstatement:** “the death my son deserved had fallen on the lamb” is theologically loaded beyond Exodus. The piece can say the son was under sentence and spared by the lamb’s death without making the child’s desert the main claim.
- **First-hearing clarity issue:** “from the far side of my days… in words written in an age I never saw” is unclear for a listener with no Bible knowledge. Is the witness dead? resurrected? narrating omnisciently? The frame needs cleaner rules.
- **Long-form sag:** The repeated thought “not because you are good / under the blood / pass over” appears in Beat 3, Beat 5, Beat 6, and Beat 7. The structure is strong, but this repetition pads instead of turning.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Replace the anonymous composite with a named biblical witness, or change the assignment.
2. Add explicit speaker/writer attribution before every KJV quote.
3. Rework the close so it invites to Jesus by grace without fear-pressure repetition.
