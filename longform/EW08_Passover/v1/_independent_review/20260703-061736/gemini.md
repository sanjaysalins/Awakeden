# Independent review — gemini (OK, 74s)

I have reviewed the artifact. While the core type-to-Christ reveal is structurally well-mapped, there are critical execution failures regarding quote attribution (including a bizarre logical error) and a direct violation of the CTA constraints.

Here are the specific findings:

**1. Absurd Misattribution / Logical Error (EW-INV-4)**
In Beat 5, the script reads:
> `...a cry such as I had never heard: **"there was not a house where there was not one dead"**`
This frames the narrative text of Exodus 12:30 as the literal screaming of the Egyptians. The Egyptians did not cry out the KJV text of Exodus. This is a severe misattribution and makes no sense in the world of the story.

**2. Missing / Orphaned Attributions (EW-INV-4)**
Quotes are repeatedly dropped in with a colon rather than being properly attributed to a speaker or text *before* the quote begins.
*   In Beat 6: `...exactly as the death my son deserved had fallen on the lamb: **"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**` (No speaker/writer attributed).
*   In Beat 7: `...not because you are good, but because He is your Lamb: **"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**` (Orphaned quote).
*   In Beat 3: `...and I killed him: **"the whole assembly of the congregation of Israel shall kill it in the evening"**` (Attributing a command from the Law to the physical action of the witness killing the lamb).

**3. Fear / Gain-Loss Framing in the CTA (EW-INV-2)**
In Beat 7, the script reads:
> `...shelter under it as I sheltered my children, and the judgment that is real — as real as the cry I heard at midnight — will pass over you...`
This directly violates the strict `NO fear / gain-loss / self-interest` constraint. You are using the threat of judgment and the terrifying "midnight cry" as the primary lever to pressure the listener into trusting Christ. The CTA must be grace-anchored and focused on the person of Jesus, not a transactional escape from terror.

**4. Pacing / Long-form Sag**
While the text is tight, it is quite short for a "long" form piece (9-11 minutes requires roughly 1100-1400 words; this is closer to 900). The "wrestling" in Beat 5 is good ("What was a pen animal to the God who made heaven and earth?"), but could be expanded to better earn the longer runtime. However, the attribution and CTA failures are the blocking issues.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the absurd misattribution in Beat 5; the Egyptians did not scream the text of Exodus 12:30.
2. Rewrite the Beat 7 CTA to remove the fear/gain-loss pressure ("judgment that is real... will pass over you") and anchor it entirely in grace and the person of Christ.
3. Properly attribute the 1 Corinthians 5:7 quotes in Beats 6 and 7, and the Exodus 12:6 quote in Beat 3, before opening the `**"..."**` tags.
