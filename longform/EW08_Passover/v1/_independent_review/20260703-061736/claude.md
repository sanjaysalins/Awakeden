# Independent review — claude (OK, 138s)

INDEPENDENT REVIEW — "The Passover Lamb" (eyewitness-long)

I checked every bolded quote word-for-word against the KJV and fact-checked the witness's narrative claims against Exodus 12, Numbers 9, and John 19.

**What holds up (verified):**
- All 14 quoted passages are exact KJV: Exodus 12:3, 12:5, 12:6, 12:12, 12:13, 12:22 (×2), 12:23, 12:30, 12:46; 1 Peter 1:19; John 19:33, 19:36; 1 Corinthians 5:7. Truncations are clean at quote edges, no internal elisions.
- The reveal is genuinely EARNED — the best I've seen this device done. Both strange details (blood must be *outside* where judgment meets it; no bone broken) are planted in Beat 4 as honest puzzlements and paid off precisely in Beat 6 with John 19:33/36 and 1 Peter 1:19. Not asserted — shown.
- The wrestling is the real steel-man: "What was a pen animal to the God who made heaven and earth, that a smear of its blood should turn aside His own judgment?" is exactly Hebrews 10:4's objection, in the witness's own voice, and the reveal answers it.
- Substitution is explicitly named twice: "the death I deserved fell on Him instead" / "We all stand under sentence; the Lamb of God died in ours."
- CTA names Jesus, names the response (come, take, shelter, trust), grace-anchored ("not because you are good, but because He is your Lamb"). Not the bare template.

**Findings (specific):**

1. **1 Peter 1:19 splice is ungrammatical and misframes the verse.** "the word written of Him says He was **'the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot'**" — this makes the sentence say *Jesus was blood*. The verse says we were redeemed *with* the precious blood of Christ. The quote is verbatim; the lead-in breaks it. Fix the frame: "…says we were redeemed with **'the precious blood of Christ…'**"

2. **Narrative over-claim (the exact recurring failure class): "The command stood, year upon year as we kept the feast."** A father of the Exodus generation kept Passover in Egypt and once at Sinai (Numbers 9:5) — then it lapsed until Gilgal (Joshua 5:5-10; the wilderness-born were uncircumcised and barred by Exodus 12:48). "Year upon year" didn't happen in his lifetime. Also, "When the LORD gave the law of this lamb, He guarded its very bones" implies the no-broken-bone command was part of the first-night instructions — Exodus 12:43-46 was given *after* the departure. Reword to something like "when the LORD gave the ordinance of the feast, after that night…" and drop "year upon year."

3. **The meal is missing.** The command wasn't only kill-and-paint — they had to *eat* the lamb, roast with fire, with bitter herbs, in haste, loins girded (Exodus 12:8-11). The witness describes every step *except eating*, which is both a narrative gap (a father telling this night would never skip the meal) and a lost typological payoff (feeding on the Lamb → "take, eat"). It also creates a quiet tension: his children are "asleep in the inner room" at midnight when the household was commanded to eat standing, ready to march.

4. **Advisory — the anonymous composite witness.** "I will not give you my name… Let me stand for them all" deviates from the named-witness format. It's defensible (Exodus 12 names no father) and honestly declared, but the three sentences of composite disclaimer sit right in the hook zone and slow the grip. Tighten Beat 1's opening so "a strange and bloody thing with my own two hands" arrives sooner.

5. **Advisory — CTA judgment reference.** "the judgment that is real — as real as the cry I heard at midnight" leans on the fear note inside the invitation. It's the text's own judgment, not manufactured, and the landing is grace — I'd let it stand, but it's at the line.

No sag: Beat 3's length is carried by quote payoffs, and every added long-form beat (the four days, the two riddles) earns its runtime. First-hearing clarity is good.

The doctrine is sound and the architecture is excellent, but #1 misframes Scripture and #2 is a checkable narrative-fact error — both are line-level fixes, not rebuilds.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Rewrite the 1 Peter 1:19 lead-in — "He was the precious blood" is a broken splice; frame it as "redeemed with the precious blood of Christ."
2. Fix the bone-command timeline: drop "year upon year as we kept the feast" and don't imply Exodus 12:46 was given on the first night (it came after the departure; wilderness Passovers lapsed until Gilgal).
3. Add the eating of the lamb (Exodus 12:8-11) — close the narrative gap, resolve the sleeping-children tension, and bank the feed-on-the-Lamb typology.
