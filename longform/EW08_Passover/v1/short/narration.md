## Beat 1 — I was there
I was a father in Israel, a slave, the night the LORD passed over Egypt. I give you no name — let me stand for every father that night, bent over his own slain lamb. My firstborn slept within, and death was coming before dawn, to pass my house only if I struck my door with blood.

## Beat 3 — The act
I took a lamb without spot, killed him at twilight, and caught his blood in a basin, and did as we were told:

**"strike it on the two side posts and on the upper door post"**

I painted my doorframe red and shut my family inside. We were saved not by being good but by the blood — for the LORD had promised:

**[the LORD]** **"when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you"**

At midnight a great cry rose across Egypt, and the judgment passed me by. A lamb had died in my son's place.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
I never learned it all in my lifetime. I tell you now from the far side of my days, by a light that fell after my night. There came another Lamb — a Man named Jesus — and now I see my spotless lamb was His shadow. The death I deserved fell on Him; His blood shelters all who hide beneath it. He is the true Passover:

**"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**

This was the substitution my doorpost foretold.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
So hear me, you who listen beyond my night. Do not look to a lamb, a painted door, or a father like me. Look to Jesus, the Lamb God has provided. Make His blood your own the way I made the lamb's — by trusting Him, hiding behind Him as my children hid behind that door. You were never running from the dark so much as coming home to Him. Come, and rest in Him.
