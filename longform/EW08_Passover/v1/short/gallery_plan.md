# Gallery Plan — EW08 Passover · "The Blood on the Door" (Awakeden eyewitness SHORT)

**Engine:** LOCKED gallery hard-cut. ~70s, 9:16 vertical. ONE rich Baroque oil painting per beat;
the eye sees the WHOLE painting, then hard-cuts to the named elements (the eye-tour targets). Each
painting is later animated as a full-res Kling "gallery tour" of those named elements only.
**Witness:** archetypal father of a household in Israel, Passover night (Exodus 12 → 1 Cor 5:7).
**Spine:** the spotless lamb's blood on the doorposts turns judgment away — fulfilled in Christ our
passover (1 Cor 5:7), the Lamb of God (John 1:29), no bone broken (John 19:36). **LANDS ON the
risen Christ** (CTA-on-Jesus is law).

Common subject_block stem (prepended to every painting): *"Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro,
Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern
elements, no text."* Animation-clean discipline: ≤3 faces, crowds in shadow, negative space, no tiny
repeated detail, no legible text.

## 1. [Beat 1 — Hook] · 0–8s · NEW
**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient
biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. ONE dominant hero
subject: a young firstborn boy asleep on a low mat inside a humble Hebrew mud-brick home, a single
warm oil lamp on him; beyond the doorway a vast cold blue-black night presses in, an unseen dread
filling the deep negative space above. Tender, fragile, ominous. Crowds none; one face only.
**Named eye-tour elements (in order):**
1. the sleeping boy's peaceful face lit by lamp-glow
2. the boy's small open hand resting on the mat
3. the single oil-lamp flame, the only warm light
4. the dark empty doorway opening onto the night
*Lands on Christ:* opens the death-threat that only blood can answer — the question Christ resolves.

## 5. [Beat 2 — The strange thing] · 36–44s · NEW
**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient

INSIDE (warm, lower), the family huddled close in lamplight behind the closed blood-marked door,

**Named eye-tour elements (in order):**
1. the mother's face shielding the firstborn child
2. the child held safe in the lamplit hollow
3. the blood-marked door seen from within
4. the dark passing shadow of death sweeping over, not stopping
*Lands on Christ:* "hiding under the blood" — the exact picture the punch fulfills in hiding under
Jesus.

## 6. [Beat 3 — The turn] · 44–53s · NEW
**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient

3. the pale shaft of heaven-light breaking the dark
4. the faint cross-shadow falling behind the lamb
*Lands on Christ:* the bridge — "there came another Lamb… spotless, His bones unbroken" — the
shadow steps into its substance, Jesus the Lamb of God.

### Doctrine notes (sound + Bible-grounded)
- Type→fulfillment is exact: spotless lamb (Ex 12:5) → Christ without blemish; blood on doorposts
  (Ex 12:7,13) → His blood; not a bone broken (Ex 12:46) → John 19:36; "Christ our passover" verbatim
  KJV (1 Cor 5:7). No supersession, no invented doctrine.
- Cross-time reveal is signposted (the father "sees by a light that fell long after") — he never
  claims he knew Christ in his moment. Painting 6's cross-shadow is the visual of that reflective
  device, not an anachronism inside the Exodus scene.
- CTA is grace-anchored: "not because you are good, but because He is the Lamb slain for you" — no
  fear/gain-loss framing.
- **Check:** painting 5's "winged shadow of death" is a visual personification of the destroyer
  (Ex 12:23); keep it an abstract dark sweeping shadow (NOT a literal angel figure / NOT a horror
  demon) to stay reverent and avoid inventing iconography.
