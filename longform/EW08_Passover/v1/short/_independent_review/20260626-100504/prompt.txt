# The Blood on the Door — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** A father of a household in Israel on the night of the Passover (representative/archetypal — every household, not one named man) · **Core text:** Exodus 12 · **Type→fulfillment:** the lamb's blood struck on the doorposts turns away judgment — fulfilled in Christ our passover (1 Cor 5:7)
---

## Beat 1 — I was there
I was a father in Israel, a slave, the night the LORD passed over Egypt. No name — I stand for every father bent over his slain lamb. Before dawn, death was coming for every firstborn. My boy slept within. Only blood on my door could turn that death away.

## Beat 3 — The act
I took a lamb without spot and caught his blood at twilight. But my hands shook — blood on a wooden door, can that turn death from my son? Yet I dipped the hyssop, as we were told:

**"strike it on the two side posts and on the upper door post"**

I struck it on the outside, seen only by the LORD. We broke not one of its bones. I shut my family in, for the LORD promised:

**[the LORD]** **"when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you"**

At midnight a cry rose across Egypt. A lamb had died in my son's place.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
For years the strangeness haunted me — a lamb without spot, blood God alone looked on, a bone never broken. I tell you from the far side of my days, by a light that fell after. There came another Lamb — a Man named Jesus — spotless, His bones unbroken, His blood seen by the Father. My lamb was His shadow. For the word that came after says:

**"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**

## Beat 7 — The invitation
Hear me. Be still a moment. A lamb died so my son could live — the innocent for the guilty. Do not look to a painted door, or to me. Look to Jesus, the Lamb God provided. Will you make His blood your own, and hide behind Him as my children hid behind that door? You were never fleeing the dark — you were coming home. Come, and rest in Him.
