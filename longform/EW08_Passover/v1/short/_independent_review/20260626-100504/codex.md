# Independent review — codex (OK, 77s)

**Findings**
- Core premise failure: `**Witness:** A father... (representative/archetypal — every household, not one named man)` violates the lens requirement for a named biblical witness. The narration doubles down with `No name — I stand for every father`, which is meta-commentary, not eyewitness voice.
- KJV accuracy: `**"strike it on the two side posts and on the upper door post"**` appears verbatim KJV, but `as we were told:` is not a speaker attribution. Attribute before the quote, likely the LORD through Moses.
- KJV attribution failure again: `**"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**` is verbatim KJV, but `For the word that came after says:` is too vague. Attribute it to Scripture/Paul before quoting.
- Reveal is partly earned but still announced. `There came another Lamb — a Man named Jesus` and `My lamb was His shadow` assert the turn; they do not yet show how judgment, blood, and substitution converge in Christ.
- The wrestling is too light. `blood on a wooden door, can that turn death from my son?` is a real question, but it is resolved too quickly and avoids the stronger objection: why must the innocent die, and why should blood answer judgment?
- Doctrine is close but undernamed. `A lamb had died in my son's place` and `the innocent for the guilty` are strong, but Christ’s substitution needs clearer gospel language: Jesus bore sin’s penalty/death/judgment in the sinner’s place.
- CTA has a vague phrase: `Will you make His blood your own` is not a clean named response. `Come, and rest in Him` is better; build the close around come/receive/rest by faith.
- Voice slips into essay mode: `I tell you from the far side of my days, by a light that fell after` and `For the word that came after says` sound like a theological narrator, not the father in the moment.
- First-hearing clarity is strained by `hyssop`, `passover`, `far side of my days`, and `light that fell after`. A zero-Bible listener can follow the main danger, but the reveal language gets misty.
- Short form is near the target length, but lines like `No name — I stand for every father` and `You were never fleeing the dark — you were coming home` add rhetorical fog instead of landing the single Passover moment.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Use a concrete named biblical witness or change the artifact type; remove the representative “No name” framing.
2. Fix attribution before every KJV quote, especially Exodus 12:7 and 1 Corinthians 5:7.
3. Make the reveal and CTA name Christ’s substitution plainly: Jesus bore sin’s penalty in our place, so come to Him and receive/rest in Him by faith.
