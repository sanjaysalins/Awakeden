## Independent adversarial review — *The Blood on the Door* (eyewitness short)

Reviewing the artifact you supplied (revised draft), not prior panel versions.

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution: **two failures**

**Exodus 12:7 quote — verbatim, unattributed**

> *"Yet I dipped the hyssop, as we were told:"* → **"strike it on the two side posts and on the upper door post"**

The words are a valid KJV substring (Ex 12:7). The speaker is not named. *“as we were told”* is not attribution under EW-INV-4 — Moses, the LORD, or Scripture must be named before the quote.

**1 Corinthians 5:7 quote — verbatim, unattributed**

> *"For the word that came after says:"* → **"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**

Again verbatim as a substring (KJV has *“For even Christ our passover…”*). Paul / Scripture is never named. *“the word that came after”* is vague for a zero-Bible listener and fails the attribution rule.

**Exodus 12:13 — pass**

> **[the LORD]** **"when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you"**

Correct speaker tag; wording matches KJV.

### EW-G8 — Wrestling: **present but thin; not the steel-man**

> *"But my hands shook — blood on a wooden door, can that turn death from my son?"*

This is real doubt, in his voice, and better than no wrestling. Problems:

1. **One beat, instantly closed** — *"Yet I dipped the hyssop"* answers it before midnight, before the cry, before any cost is felt. No tension carries through the act.
2. **Not the strongest objection** — the piece never wrestles *why an innocent lamb must die*, *why firstborn blood-guilt falls on Egypt*, or *what if the blood is wrong or the door missed*. Ritual-efficacy doubt is the softest honest objection available; the steel-man is barely touched.
3. **Beat 5 is absent** — acceptable in SHORT compression only if wrestling *lands*. Here it doesn’t land; it’s a speed bump.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal: **partly earned, still largely asserted**

Improvements: Beat 3 plants oddities (outside blood, unbroken bone, LORD-only sight); Beat 6 recycles them.

Still failing the “shown, not announced” bar:

- *"There came another Lamb — a Man named Jesus"* — the turn is **declared**, not walked out from what he lived that night.
- *"My lamb was His shadow"* — label, not demonstration. The listener is told the mapping; the witness does not reason through it.
- *"by a light that fell after"* / *"from the far side of my days"* — literary hand-wave; it substitutes for earning the Christ-link from Exodus detail.
- The **1 Cor 5:7** quote does the real typological work — but that is **Paul’s voice**, not the witness’s lived moment. The reveal leans on an unattributed NT punchline rather than on what his hands did at twilight.

The unbroken-bone → Christ’s bones parallel is typology-by-listing, not typology-by-text the witness internalized. Exodus 12 forbids breaking the lamb’s bones at the meal; it does not, in his moment, point him to a future crucifixion.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine: **mostly sound; substitution on Christ under-named**

**Sound:** No supersession (*"Do not look to… me. Look to Jesus"*); household substitution is clear (*"A lamb had died in my son's place"*, *"the innocent for the guilty"*); Christ as Passover lamb is orthodox.

**Gap:** What **penalty Christ bore** is never named in the witness’s mouth. The firstborn judgment is in the hook; the lamb dies **for the son**. The reveal jumps to Jesus as spotless Lamb + 1 Cor “sacrificed for us” without tying **judgment passed over us because His blood was seen** — the very hinge of Ex 12:13. Substitution on the **son** is named; substitution on **us before God’s judgment** is implied, not spoken. That’s thin for EW-INV-7 / EW-G9 at LOCK standard.

No heresy — but under-specified where the format demands precision.

---

### EW-G10 / EW-INV-1 — Voice: **archetype reads editorial, not eyewitness**

> *"No name — I stand for every father bent over his slain lamb."*

Meta-framing in the hook breaks first-person immersion. A father remembering Passover night does not normally explain he is a literary composite.

**Chronology slip in the same line:** *"bent over his **slain** lamb"* — in Beat 1 the lamb is not yet slain (that happens Beat 3). Signals narrator shaping language, not memory.

> *"by a light that fell after"* — abstract; not the register of a slave-father with basin, hyssop, and a sleeping boy.

The header justifies an archetypal witness; the **spoken voice** still wears the costume lightly.

---

### First-hearing clarity — **mixed**

**Better:** *"death was coming for every firstborn. My boy slept within"* — stakes are clearer than a generic “death before dawn.”

**Still muddy:**

- *"the night the LORD **passed over** Egypt"* — Passover idiom unexplained; a new listener may hear “passed over” as “visited” rather than “spared/skipped.”
- *"my boy"* is never explicitly **firstborn** — inference required.
- *"For the word that came after says"* — who spoke, when, and why should I trust it?
- **Internal contradiction:** Beat 3 — *"I struck it on the outside"* with hyssop; Beat 7 — *"Do not look to a **painted** door."* He did not paint. That will confuse listeners and breaks single-moment integrity.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA: **passes, with one soft spot**

**Pass:** Names **Jesus**; named response (*"make His blood your own"*, *"hide behind Him"*, *"Come, and rest in Him"*); grace-anchored (*"coming home"*, *"rest"*); no fear/gain-loss template; not the banned bare *“will you trust Him?”*

**Soft spot:** *"Will you make His blood your own"* without unpacking can sound mystical or transactional to a zero-Bible listener — borderline, not a fail on its own.

---

### SHORT form — **word budget OK; single moment slightly crowded at the close**

~295 spoken words — within 220–320. One thread, one night, no second plot.

Compression removed filler but also removed **depth** (wrestling that lasts, one strange detail carried into the turn). Beat 6’s *"For years the strangeness haunted me"* is acceptable as a signposted reflective device (EW-INV-11), but it adds post-hoc span to a “single moment” short. The **act** lands; the **type→Christ** landing is still mostly Beat 6 assertion.

---

### Hook / spine — **holds, underpowered between act and reveal**

Hook grips: slave-father, firstborn threat implied, blood as only shelter. ONE witness, ONE thread — no swap.

Spine sags between midnight (*"A lamb had died in my son's place"*) and Beat 6: no lingering doubt, no riddle beat, no detail that *forces* the Christ reading.

VERDICT: REVISE

TOP FIXES:
1. **Attribute every KJV quote before it is spoken** — name Moses/Scripture before Ex 12:7; name Paul/Scripture before 1 Cor 5:7 (not *“as we were told”* / *“the word that came after”*).
2. **Fix the painted-door contradiction and sharpen stakes** — he *struck* blood with hyssop; Beat 7 must match. Name the boy as **firstborn** so judgment’s target is explicit on first hearing.
3. **Earn the reveal and deepen wrestling** — let one lived oddity (outside blood / unbroken bone / LORD sees the blood) carry through midnight unresolved; name what **judgment Christ bore** (blood that turns away destroyer-judgment); cut or replace *“My lamb was His shadow”* / *“by a light that fell after”* with reasoning from what his hands did that night.
