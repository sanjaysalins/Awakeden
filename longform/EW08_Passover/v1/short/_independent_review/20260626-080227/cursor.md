## Independent adversarial review — EW08 Passover (short)

### EW-G8 / Wrestling — **missing (major)**

The eyewitness spine treats Beat 5 as the steel-man doubt, internalized in the witness’s voice. This short skips it entirely.

There is no honest objection, no unanswered “why,” no tension between obedience and comprehension. Beat 3 runs straight from ritual → promise → midnight cry → *“A lamb had died in my son's place”* with no pushback. Compare the Aaron short, which compresses wrestling into the act: *“Why would one need two?”* and Beat 1’s *“I never stopped asking why.”* This Passover short has nothing equivalent.

**Result:** EW-G8 fails. The piece reads like catechism delivered by a compliant narrator, not a father who lived the night.

---

### EW-G7 / Reveal — **asserted, not earned**

The turn to Christ is announced, not shown from the text the witness lived.

- *“I never learned it all in my lifetime”* — tells us the gap; doesn’t dramatize it.
- *“now I see my spotless lamb was His shadow”* — pure assertion. No strange detail from Exodus (blood on the **outside**, unbroken bone, hyssop/lintel, four days under the roof) is retained to **show** why the lamb points forward.
- *“This was the substitution my doorpost foretold”* — **foretold** is the problem word. Nothing in the short established foreshadowing; only substitution-in-the-moment (*lamb for son*). That’s typology-by-label, not typology-by-text.

The long-form version earns the reveal through Beat 4’s riddle and Beat 5’s doubt deepened at the midnight cry. This short deletes both and keeps the punchline.

### EW-G10 / Voice — **archetype reads like essay narrator**

The composite witness is spec-justified, but the voice is thin:

- Meta-framing: *“I give you no name — let me stand for every father that night”* — editorial, not lived memory.
- Beat 6: *“by a light that fell after my night”* — literary abstraction; a slave-father on Passover night would more likely carry the long-form’s concrete register (basin, hyssop, torchlight, a son’s breathing).
- Hook vs. act mismatch: hook — *“I struck my door with blood”*; act — *“I painted my doorframe red”*. Small, but it signals a narrator shaping language rather than one man remembering one act.

Aaron short works because *“I am Aaron”* + ritual specificity carry character. This father is a placeholder delivering theology.

---

### First-hearing clarity — **passover logic muddled in the hook**

Beat 1: *“death was coming before dawn, to pass my house only if I struck my door with blood.”*

A zero-Bible listener hears: death comes **if** you use blood. Passover idiom (“pass over” = spare/skip) is never unpacked. The firstborn threat is implied only late (*“A lamb had died in my son's place”*). Judgment’s **target** (firstborn) should land before the reveal, not after.

---

### EW-G9 / Doctrine — **mostly sound, one stretch**

**Sound:** Substitution is named (*lamb in son’s place* → *death I deserved fell on Him*); no supersession (*“Do not look to a lamb… Look to Jesus”*); blood-shelter logic matches Ex 12.

**Stretch:** *“The death I deserved fell on Him”* — the witness never voiced personal guilt or firstborn **sentence** on himself (long-form does: *“I had no innocence to plead”*). The OT moment is **firstborn judgment**, not a general “death you deserved” frame. The line imports NT imputation without the witness having earned it in his own mouth. Not heresy, but sloppy in **his** voice.

### SHORT form — **tight on words, thin on the single moment**

~300 spoken words — within 220–320 ✓. One thread, one night ✓. No sag ✓.

But compression cut the **steel** (wrestling + strange detail), not filler. The single moment **lands** as “blood saves” but not as “this night was always pointing to Christ” — that second landing is asserted in Beat 6, not witnessed.

---

### Hook / spine — **hook ok; spine holds but underpowered**

Hook grips: slave-father, firstborn inside, blood on the door. ONE witness, ONE thread — no swap.

Missing compressed hooks that other shorts use (Aaron’s persistent “why”) leave the spine functional but flat between act and reveal.

---

VERDICT: REVISE

TOP FIXES:
1. **Add compressed wrestling** — one steel-man line in the witness’s voice (e.g., *How can a lamb’s blood answer for my son’s life?* or doubt that **deepens** at the midnight cry), internalized, not answered until Beat 6.
2. **Earn the reveal from one lived oddity** — carry over a single Exodus strange detail (blood on the **outside**, unbroken bone, or lintel/posts shape) so *“shadow”* / *“foretold”* follows from what he **did**, not from Beat 6 assertion.
3. **Attribute the 1 Cor quote** before **"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"** (Paul / Scripture voice), and tighten Beat 1 so a first-time listener grasps **firstborn judgment** and **pass over = spared**, not “death comes if you paint blood.”
