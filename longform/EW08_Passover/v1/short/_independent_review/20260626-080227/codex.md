# Independent review — codex (OK, 76s)

**Findings**

- Hard artifact mismatch: `I give you no name — let me stand for every father that night` violates the “named biblical witness” requirement. This is representative narration, not a named eyewitness.

- EW-INV-4 attribution failure: `**"strike it on the two side posts and on the upper door post"**` is KJV wording from Exodus 12:7, but the speaker is not attributed. `did as we were told:` is too vague.

- EW-INV-4 attribution failure again: `**"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**` is KJV wording from 1 Corinthians 5:7, but no speaker/source is attributed before the quote. `He is the true Passover:` is not attribution.

- EW-G8 is missing. There is fear, but no real steel-man wrestling. `death was coming before dawn` creates stakes, but the father never asks the strongest objection: why blood should answer judgment, why an innocent lamb dies, or whether this can really protect his son.

- EW-G7 is partly announced rather than earned: `There came another Lamb — a Man named Jesus — and now I see my spotless lamb was His shadow.` That states the fulfillment instead of showing the lived details carrying him there.

- The substitution thread shifts imprecisely. `A lamb had died in my son's place` is the witnessed moment, but the reveal moves to `The death I deserved fell on Him`. That may be doctrinally true, but the narration has not established the father’s own deserved penalty from Exodus 12.

- Doctrine is mostly orthodox but under-specified. `The death I deserved fell on Him` names death, but the required penalty/what Christ bore would be clearer if it named judgment for sin rather than only “death.”

- First-hearing clarity weakens at `by a light that fell after my night` and `He is the true Passover`. These are poetic/theological phrases a zero-Bible listener may not follow.

- CTA mostly satisfies EW-INV-2: `Look to Jesus`, `by trusting Him`, and `Come, and rest in Him` name Jesus and a response. But `Make His blood your own` is awkward and may sound transactional or mystical without explanation.

VERDICT: FAIL
TOP FIXES:
1. Use a named biblical witness, not “every father.”
2. Add proper KJV attribution before every bold quote.
3. Build a real internal objection and show the Passover-to-Christ reveal from that lived moment.
