# Independent review — claude (OK, 75s)

I reviewed this against the eyewitness lens. The doctrinal spine is solid and the KJV is clean, but there is one structural gap that a gate is supposed to catch.

## KJV verbatim + attribution (EW-INV-4) — mostly clean
- **"strike it on the two side posts and on the upper door post"** — exact KJV (Ex 12:7). ✓
- **[the LORD]** **"when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you"** — exact KJV (Ex 12:13), correctly attributed to the LORD (He is the speaker there). ✓
- **"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"** — exact KJV (1 Cor 5:7, minus the lead-in "For even"). ✓ **But unattributed** — it drops in with no speaker frame ("as Paul/Scripture says"/"it is written"). The other two quotes get a frame; this one floats. Minor, but the lens asks for attribution before quoting.

## The WRESTLING is missing (EW-G8) — the real problem
There is **no genuine doubt or steel-man objection** anywhere. The father never voices the honest question the moment demands: *why must a lamb die? is blood on a door not superstition? what if it does not hold?* He goes straight from command → obedience → deliverance. "We were saved not by being good but by the blood" is a *conclusion*, not a wrestling. EW-G8 wants the strongest objection internalized in his own voice — it isn't here at all.

This is reinforced by the **beat numbering: 1 → 3 → 6 → 7**. Beats 2, 4, 5 are absent. On the 7-beat spine those middle beats are where the dread/doubt/turn live. Whatever happened in the export, the witnessed wrestling beat got dropped, and it shows.

## The reveal (EW-G7) — earned, with a caveat
The type→Christ turn is shown, not just asserted: spotless lamb → "His shadow," blood-on-door → "His blood shelters all who hide beneath it," and the substitution "the death I deserved fell on Him." That works. The one lean is the device "by a light that fell after my night" — the witness knows about Jesus by grant rather than discovery. It's a graceful anachronism dodge, but it slightly *announces* the knowledge.

## Doctrine (EW-G9) — sound
Substitution is named twice (penalty = death I deserved; Christ bore it; "a lamb had died in my son's place"). Grace-anchored ("not by being good but by the blood"). No supersession, no contrarian reading. ✓

## CTA (EW-INV-2) — strong
"Look to Jesus, the Lamb God has provided… by trusting Him, hiding behind Him… Come, and rest in Him." Response named (trust / come / rest), grace-anchored, no fear/gain-loss, no tired "will you trust Him?" template. ✓

## Voice (EW-G10) — mostly in character
Consistent first-person father. Slips toward poet-essayist in Beat 6/7 ("from the far side of my days," "you were never running from the dark") but stays a witness, not a lecturer. Acceptable.

## Length — over the cap
~330–340 spoken words, above the ~220–320 short-form ceiling. Beats 6 and 7 both carry abstraction that can be cut ("You were never running from the dark so much as coming home to Him" is pretty but soft).

## First-hearing clarity — fine
A zero-Bible listener can follow: slaves, death coming at night, blood on the door, firstborn spared. Clear.

## Minor notes
- "I painted my doorframe red" slightly embellishes the text (strike the posts/lintel, not the whole frame) — acceptable poetic license.
- Hook is good but not gripping until the second sentence; "death was coming before dawn… only if I struck my door with blood" is the real hook and could lead.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add the missing wrestling beat (EW-G8): give the father one honest, internalized doubt before he obeys — *blood on a door against the angel of death? is this enough?* — so the deliverance answers a real objection instead of none.
2. Attribute the 1 Cor 5:7 quote (a "Scripture says" / "it is written" / Paul frame) to satisfy EW-INV-4, and confirm the dropped beats (2/4/5) were intentional, not a truncated export.
3. Tighten to ≤320 spoken words by trimming the abstract lines in Beats 6–7 ("by a light that fell after my night," "running from the dark… coming home to Him").
