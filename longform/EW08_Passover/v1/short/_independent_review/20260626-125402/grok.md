# Independent review — grok (OK, 52s)

Verifying KJV wording and eyewitness rules against the artifact.
## Independent adversarial review — *The Blood on the Door* (eyewitness-short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Wording:** `**"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**` is an exact KJV substring (1 Cor 5:7). No altered words inside the quote.

**Attribution — weak:** It is introduced only by *"As the Scripture written after me says:"* — not Paul, not 1 Corinthians. *"Scripture written after me"* is a clever OT frame, but it is still vague for a zero-Bible listener and below what prior panels required on this same line. Name the writer/book before the bold span.

**Bigger gap:** Core text is Exodus 12, yet **no Exodus KJV is quoted at all**. The witness lived the LORD’s command and the blood token; the only verbatim Scripture is NT. That leaves the `scripture` voice carrying one late quote while the night’s proof-text never speaks in KJV.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (headline failure)

There is **no steel-man doubt in the father’s voice on the night**.

The only “wrestling” is:

> *"For years it haunted me — why a spotless lamb, why a bone never broken, why blood God alone looked on?"*

Those are **after-the-fact typology puzzles**, not the strongest in-the-moment objection. A father on that night would wrestle with things like: *Will blood on wood actually turn death away from my boy?* *Why must an innocent lamb die?* *What if I miss the doorpost?*

None of that appears. Beat 2 jumps straight from act to certainty: *"A lamb died so my son would not — I was saved not by being good, but by hiding under the blood."* Doubt is never internalized, never sustained, never paid off. Per EW-G8 this is **strawman-adjacent** — mystery labels swapped in for real fear.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal announced, not earned

The Christ turn is **mostly told**:

- *"I tell you now, by a light that fell long after"* — announces later understanding; does not walk the listener through how the night itself opened the door.
- *"My lamb was only His shadow"* — typology label **asserted**, not demonstrated step-by-step from what he saw that night.
- *"There came another Lamb — a Man named Jesus — spotless, His bones unbroken, His blood poured out"* — parallel list helps, but the unbroken-bone link to Christ is **imported** (John 19 territory), not lived in Exodus memory alone.

The piece earns **Passover substitution for the son**. It does **not** earn **Christ** from the witnessed moment without the Paul quote doing the heavy lift.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine (mostly sound, two slips)

**Passes:** No supersession. OT substitution is clear in *"A lamb died so my son would not."* Christ is named as the true Lamb. CTA does not send people back to annual blood-on-door ritual.

**Slip 1 — merit framing on a plague night:** *"I was saved not by being good, but by hiding under the blood"* (and Beat 4’s echo *"not because you are good"*) risks misframing Exodus 12. Death was coming on **firstborns** under plague judgment — not because the boy failed a moral test. The contrast works for grace-CTA, but it is **theologically sloppy in this witness’s mouth** if he never owned personal guilt.

**Slip 2 — Christ’s penalty not named in his words:** The witness says what the **lamb** bore for **his son**. He never says in his own voice that **death/judgment meant for us fell on Christ** — only *"Lamb slain for you"* plus the 1 Cor quote. Substitution on the Christ side stays **implied**, not named (penalty + what He bore).

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Archetype reads as costume + essayist:**

- *"I give you no name; I am every father in Israel"* — editorial framing, not one man remembering one night.
- *"by a light that fell long after"* — commentator voice, not an Israelite father at the door.
- *"Do not look to a painted door"* — he **struck** blood on doorposts in Beat 2; **painted** is the narrator’s metaphor, not the witness’s act. Voice inconsistency.

First-person is present throughout, but it often sounds like a **detached typology essay wearing a father mask**.

---

### First-hearing clarity

**Clear:** Death threatens the firstborn; blood on the door is the only shelter; a lamb dies so the son lives.

**Muddy for a cold listener:**

- **Why** death is coming (plague, destroyer, “pass over”) is never explained — only *"Death was coming for my firstborn son."*
- *"where only God would see"* — confusing; blood was struck on the doorposts, not hidden from the household.
- *"Christ our passover"* — “passover” is never unpacked before the quote.
- *"Make His blood your own"* — opaque response language.
- *"painted door"* — contradicts *"struck its blood"* and is unexplained.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus

**Passes**, with minor fatigue.

- Names **Jesus** ✓
- Named response: *"Come home"* (come) + *"hide behind Jesus"* + *"Make His blood your own"* ✓
- Grace-anchored; no fear, gain-loss, or manufactured pressure ✓
- Not the banned bare *"will you trust Him?"* template ✓

---

### Hook / spine / SHORT form

**Hook:** Strong — firstborn, grave, blood on the door in line one.

**Spine:** ONE witness, ONE night, ONE thread. No swap.

**SHORT form — fails budget:** Spoken body is **~194 words** — **below the 220–320 floor** (EW-INV-8 / EW-G3). At natural pace this is ~75–80s, not ~90s. Too compressed to carry wrestling, Exodus proof, and an earned Christ turn.

**Single moment:** The Passover night lands in Beats 1–2. *"For years it haunted me"* breaks immediacy and shifts to retrospective commentary before the Christ turn.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Add real steel-man wrestling in the father’s voice on the night** — one honest line that death might not turn at blood on wood (or that killing the lamb horrifies him), carried into the reveal; replace the post-hoc trivia list (*"why a spotless lamb, why a bone never broken…"*) as the sole “doubt.”
2. **Bring spoken word count into 220–320** — add ~30–50 words of earned material (Exodus KJV with proper `[the LORD]`/`[Moses]` attribution, midnight payoff, or Christ penalty named in the witness’s own words), not padding.
3. **Earn the Christ turn and fix attribution** — name Paul/1 Corinthians before `**"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**`; cut or rework *"by a light that fell long after"* / *"My lamb was only His shadow"*; have the witness say explicitly that **the death/judgment meant for us fell on Christ**; fix *"painted door"* to match *"struck"* or ground the metaphor.
