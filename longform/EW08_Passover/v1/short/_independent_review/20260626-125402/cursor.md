# Independent adversarial review — *The Blood on the Door* (Passover, eyewitness-short)

Default stance: skeptical. Findings below cite exact lines/phrases.

---

## EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution: **REVISE (narrow pass on words, fail on attribution)**

The only bracketed quote is **`"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"`** — that substring matches KJV 1 Corinthians 5:7 (`For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us:`). No altered word inside the quote marks.

**Attribution is too vague.** The lead-in is **`As the Scripture written after me says:`** — not Paul, not 1 Corinthians, not even “the apostle.” EW-INV-4 requires the speaker to be attributed *before* quoting. A zero-Bible listener hears anonymous “Scripture,” not a named voice routing to the `scripture` cast.

**Load-bearing Exodus language never gets KJV.** Beat 2 paraphrases Exodus 12 (`spotless lamb`, **`Not one of its bones we broke`**, blood on doorposts) without quotes. EW-INV-4 doesn’t flag paraphrase, but the *witnessed* moment never gets Scripture’s exact seal — only the later NT fragment does.

## EW-INV-8 / SHORT form — **FAIL (under budget)**

Spoken body ≈ **194 words** (spec: **220–320** for SHORT; ~75–110s). This is **26+ words short** of the floor. Beat 3–4 carry the whole typology turn + CTA in one breath; there’s no room for wrestling or earned reveal without blowing past the ceiling — the piece is thin, not tight.

---

## EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man): **FAIL**

The only “doubt” is retrospective curiosity:

> **`For years it haunted me — why a spotless lamb, why a bone never broken, why blood God alone looked on?`**

That is **symbol-hunting**, not the genuine strongest objection internalized in the father’s voice on that night. The steel-man this moment demands — and never gets voiced — is sharper:

- **Why must innocent blood stand between my son and death?**
- **Why does judgment require a death I didn’t die?**
- **How can smearing blood on wood turn away a plague?**

Those are the scandal of Passover substitution, not “why unbroken bones?” **`I was saved not by being good, but by hiding under the blood`** states the conclusion without letting the father *press* on it. Wonder is not wrestling; this is the same thin pattern flagged on Isaiah/Noah/Bronze Serpent shorts.

Also: **`For years it haunted me`** pulls a **second timeline** into a piece that should land on **one night** — the doubt feels post-hoc essay, not lived crisis.

---

## EW-G7 — Reveal earned: **FAIL**

The turn is announced, not shown:

> **`I tell you now, by a light that fell long after. There came another Lamb — a Man named Jesus`**

That is exactly the forbidden move: later enlightenment declared, not walked from the blood-on-the-door facts the witness lived. The parallels are **listed**, not **derived**:

> **`spotless, His bones unbroken, His blood poured out`**

A listener never sees *how* “lamb dies so son does not” becomes “Christ dies so I do not.” Instead:

> **`My lamb was only His shadow`**

— typology **asserted** in church shorthand. The 1 Cor quote **`"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"`** confirms doctrine **after** the assertion; it doesn’t earn the turn from Exodus 12’s logic (judgment passes over where blood is applied → another Lamb bears that judgment outside the camp). The reveal lands because the writer *says* shadow/lamb/Jesus, not because the night *shows* Christ.

---

## EW-G9 — Doctrine / substitution: **REVISE (direction OK, precision missing)**

**Passover-night substitution is named well:**

> **`A lamb died so my son would not`**

**Christ-side substitution is not.** Beat 3–4 give **`His blood poured out`**, **`the Lamb slain for you`**, and the 1 Cor tag — but never name **what penalty Christ bore in the sinner’s place** (judgment/death/wrath the household deserved under Egypt’s plague logic, fulfilled at the cross). EW-INV-7 wants penalty + what Christ bore; the OT half is clear, the NT half is metaphor-only.

**Supersession risk (borderline):** **`Do not look to a painted door`** can read as “the sign is empty; only Christ counts” — typological fulfillment is fine, but without one line grounding *why* the painted door mattered *then*, it can sound like dismissal of the institution, not fulfillment. Not heresy, but sloppy for “no supersession.”

Typology stack (unbroken bones → John 19:36) is **implied**, never tied — **`why a bone never broken`** asks the question then never pays it off in the Christ section beyond repeating **`His bones unbroken`**.

---

## EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus: **PASS (narrow)**

**Names Jesus:** **`hide behind Jesus`**, **`He is the Lamb slain for you`**.

**Grace-anchored:** **`not because you are good`**, **`No one at His door is turned away`** — no fear/gain-loss lexicon, no banned **`will you trust Him?`** template.

**Response named:** **`Come home`** uses an invitation verb from the spec set (`come`). Better than bare trust — but **`Make His blood your own`** is vague/mystical for a zero-Bible listener (faith? ritual? magic?) and weakens the named response.

---

## EW-G10 — Voice / character: **FAIL**

> **`I give you no name; I am every father in Israel`**

That is an **explicit refusal of eyewitness identity**. EW-INV-1 allows a *labelled* composite; this labels **archetype**, not a person. The voice reads as **detached catechist in first person**, not a father who killed a lamb that night. **`For years it haunted me`** + **`by a light that fell long after`** is posthumous sermon cadence (same failure mode as Noah/Jonah reviews), not a man inside the blood-marked doorway.

---

## First-hearing clarity: **FAIL (multiple friction points)**

- **`Do not look to a painted door`** — assumes Passover literacy; unexplained.
- **`My lamb was only His shadow`** — typology jargon; never defined.
- **`Make His blood your own`** — opaque action for someone with zero Bible background.
- **`Death was coming for my firstborn son`** — a listener may not know *why* unless Egypt/plague context is one line sharper (who sent death, why blood stops it).

Hook **`Death was coming for my firstborn son that night`** is strong and clear; spine holds on lamb/blood/door — but Beat 3’s **`long after`** / **`another Lamb`** jump breaks the single-moment contract.

---

## Hook / spine: **PASS (hook) / REVISE (moment discipline)**

Hook grips immediately. ONE thread (blood → lamb → Christ) holds. But **`For years it haunted me`** and **`There came another Lamb`** introduce a **second temporal frame** that crowds the witnessed night — sag is intellectual (unearned typology list), not a second plot thread, but still dilutes the “single moment” punch.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Steel-man the wrestling (EW-G8)** — in the father’s voice *that night*, voice the real scandal (innocent blood for my son’s life / judgment answered by a death I didn’t die); let Beat 3’s turn *answer that doubt*, not **`why a bone never broken`** curiosity alone.
2. **Earn the reveal from the doorpost facts (EW-G7)** — walk judgment-outside / blood-inside → another Lamb bears judgment for us; cut or re-voice **`by a light that fell long after`** / **`My lamb was only His shadow`** until the type→Christ chain is *shown*; then land 1 Cor 5:7 with **Paul / 1 Corinthians** attribution (EW-INV-4).
3. **Name Christ’s substitution explicitly and hit word budget (EW-G9 / EW-INV-8)** — state the penalty Christ bore in the sinner’s place; add ~30–80 words of earned wrestling/reveal so the piece reaches **220–320** spoken words without doctrinal compression in one breath.
