# The Blood on the Door — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** A father of a household in Israel on the night of the Passover (representative/archetypal — every household, not one named man) · **Core text:** Exodus 12 → 1 Cor 5:7 · **Type→fulfillment:** the spotless lamb's blood struck on the doorposts turns away judgment — fulfilled in Christ our passover (1 Cor 5:7)
---

## Beat 1 — Hook
Death was coming for my firstborn son that night — and the only thing between him and the grave was blood on my door. I give you no name; I am every father in Israel.

## Beat 2 — The strange thing
I took a spotless lamb, killed it, and struck its blood on the doorposts where only God would see. Not one of its bones we broke. A lamb died so my son would not — I was saved not by being good, but by hiding under the blood.

## Beat 3 — The turn
For years it haunted me — why a spotless lamb, why a bone never broken, why blood God alone looked on? I tell you now, by a light that fell long after. There came another Lamb — a Man named Jesus — spotless, His bones unbroken, His blood poured out. My lamb was only His shadow. As the Scripture written after me says:

**"Christ our passover is sacrificed for us"**

## Beat 4 — The punch
Do not look to a painted door. Make His blood your own; hide behind Jesus as my children hid behind that door — not because you are good, but because He is the Lamb slain for you. No one at His door is turned away. Come home.
