# Independent review — codex (OK, 22s)

Findings:

- **Artifact identity fails the form.** The lens requires “a named biblical witness,” but the artifact says: **“I give you no name; I am every father in Israel.”** That is explicitly not a named witness and weakens the eyewitness claim.

- **KJV quote attribution is too vague.** Before **“Christ our passover is sacrificed for us”**, the attribution is only **“As the Scripture written after me says:”**. EW-INV-4 asks for attribution before quoting. Better would identify Paul / 1 Corinthians 5:7. The quoted words themselves are KJV wording, though they are only a partial phrase from the verse.

- **Reveal is partly asserted, not fully earned.** The turn says: **“I tell you now, by a light that fell long after. There came another Lamb”**. That announces later understanding instead of showing how Exodus 12 itself drives him there. The connections are listed: **“spotless, His bones unbroken, His blood poured out”**, but not deeply shown from the moment.

- **Wrestling is present but too light.** **“why a spotless lamb, why a bone never broken, why blood God alone looked on?”** is a real question, but it does not steel-man the hardest objection: why an innocent lamb must die for the guilty household, and how blood can answer judgment.

- **Substitution is implied, not fully named.** **“A lamb died so my son would not”** is strong typology, but the Christ section only says **“the Lamb slain for you.”** It does not clearly name the penalty Christ bore, judgment/death in the sinner’s place, or substitution with enough doctrinal precision.

- **CTA is close but vague/metaphorical.** **“Make His blood your own; hide behind Jesus… Come home.”** names Jesus and avoids fear pressure, but the response is not clearly named as faith, coming to Christ, receiving Him, or turning to Him. “Make His blood your own” is also an awkward phrase that may confuse first-hearers.

- **First-hearing clarity issue.** **“Do not look to a painted door”** assumes the listener understands the Passover sign. Also **“My lamb was only His shadow”** is church shorthand; a zero-Bible listener may not know what “shadow” means here.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Use an actual named biblical witness or change the artifact type; “every father in Israel” fails the eyewitness requirement.
2. Strengthen the reveal by showing the innocent lamb bearing death/judgment in the household’s place, then explicitly name Christ’s substitution.
3. Replace the vague CTA with a grace-anchored invitation that names the response: come to Jesus / trust Christ / receive Him.
