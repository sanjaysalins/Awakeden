## TITLE
The Oldest Prophecy of Jesus -- Hidden in a Curse (Genesis 3:15)

## DESCRIPTION
Genesis 3:15 -- the first prophecy of Jesus in the whole Bible. In the moment everything breaks in Eden, God's first words aren't judgment on Adam and Eve. They're a promise, spoken to the serpent, hidden inside a curse:

"And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel." (Genesis 3:15)

This single verse -- the first messianic prophecy in the Bible -- names a coming deliverer before Adam and Eve are even sentenced. The woman's seed will crush the serpent's head; the serpent will only manage to bruise his heel. A costly victory, promised before the fall's consequences are even spoken.

This deep-dive traces that promise forward -- through its echoes in Galatians, Romans, Hebrews, Colossians, 1 John, and Revelation -- to the cross, where the enemy's defeat was finally accomplished, and the empty tomb, which put that victory beyond doubt.

The whole Bible is one story. It starts here, in a garden, with a promise spoken to a snake -- and it is still true today: the seed of the woman has crushed the head of the enemy, and His victory is not waiting to be won. It is already won. Step out from hiding, and be found by Him.
