## THE LOCKED SPINE (C-led)

> **"God's very first promise of rescue is spoken to the serpent — hidden inside a curse,
> before Adam or Eve are even sentenced. And it tells the whole story in advance: a deliverer
> is coming, the woman's seed, who will crush the enemy by taking the enemy's own deadly blow."**

ONE spine, hook → landing mirror intact:
- **HOOK (M3):** the first word of grace in the whole Bible is addressed **to the enemy** —
  airtight, rests on the *plain narrative order* of Gen 3 (unassailable; the panel's lowest-risk,
  freshest beat).
- **LANDING (M7):** the promise kept — the serpent's head crushed at the cross. The question the
  hook opens ("who is this promised crusher, and how does He win?") is paid off at the close.
- **"Her seed" (Candidate A)** is demoted to a *supporting clue* inside M4 — an unusual messianic
  hint, NOT a virgin-birth grammatical proof.
- **The wound that wins (Candidate B)** is the M6 exchange + the landing.

## 7-movement map (with panel fixes baked in)

1. **The Picture** — Eden, the fall, the LORD God walking in the garden in the cool of the day,
   the sentence begins. KJV Gen 3:8–13.
2. **The Problem** — guilt, hiding, exposure, the coming death. (Do NOT over-claim a settled
   "serpent's dominion" the Genesis text doesn't yet state — bondage-to-death is fine.)
3. **The Strange Detail (HOOK)** — God turns to the *serpent* first, and the first thing out of
   His mouth in the curse is a **promise of rescue**. Grace is preached to the enemy before the
   guilty couple are sentenced (3:16–19 come *after* 3:15).
4. **The Centuries-Early Match** — walk Gen 3:15 against the NT *honestly as trajectory/echo, not
   as a verbatim quote* (no NT writer quotes Gen 3:15 — unlike Bronze Serpent, where Jesus names
   the type himself). "Her seed" = an unusual messianic clue → Gal 4:4 "made of a woman", Luke 1:35.
   The serpent's later identification as Satan (Rev 12:9, 1 John 3:8) is **canonical theology**,
   stated as such — not read back into the Eden text as if Genesis names Satan.
5. **The Honest Objection (M5 — steel-man the STRONGEST real pushback):**
   - "No NT writer actually quotes Genesis 3:15 — you're reading Jesus in." (the killer)
   - "*Zera* is collective — humanity/Israel/the church, not one man." (Rom 16:20 "under **your**
     feet" = corporate.)
   - "'The woman' is just Eve; 'her seed' need not imply abnormal paternity." (other women get
     "seed" too — Gen 16:10 Hagar, Gen 24:60 Rebekah — VERIFIED.)
   - "It's only a snake curse; Satan isn't named here."
   **Answer honestly:** the singular individual emerges from the canonical trajectory + the NT's
   *application* (not citation); the head-wound/heel-wound asymmetry; the whole-canon arc that
   turns here.
6. **The Exchange (M6 — must name PENAL substitution, not victory-metaphor alone):** the heel-wound
   is real and costly (the cross — death, sin borne), and through that very death the head is
   crushed (Heb 2:14 "through death... destroy him that had the power of death"; Col 2:15). Guard
   with resurrection/consummation nuance — Rom 16:20 keeps a future/ongoing dimension ("shortly").
   Do NOT flatten it into "Satan struck and that was the win" — name what Christ *bore*.
7. **The Invitation (M7)** — come to the One whose bruised heel broke the serpent's head;
   grace-anchored, the Spirit convicts, the script invites specifically (no recycled "Will you
   trust Him?"; no fear/gain-loss).

## HARD CONSTRAINTS carried into the draft (panel-derived, fail-closed)

- **KJV-strict:** Gen 3:15 reads **"it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel."**
  Use "it" — never smuggle "he shall bruise" into the script unless explicitly marked as
  translation comparison. Per-verse attribution when quoting across 3:14/3:15. Mark every ellipsis.
- **No false uniqueness / no eisegesis:** strip "only/everywhere/always" from the "her seed" beat.
  Sinlessness is NOT a genetics workaround ("not of a fallen father's line" is OUT — Mary is also
  fallen). "Her seed" is a clue, not a proof of the virgin birth from Hebrew grammar.
- **Honest M4/M5:** Gen 3:15 is fulfilled by *trajectory + NT application*, NOT a verbatim NT quote.
  Never claim "the NT's own explicit application/citation" of this verse.
- **Multi-voice (INV-7):** Gen 3:14–15 is direct divine speech → tag **the_LORD** its own voice
  (as Bronze Serpent did for Num 21:8). Narrator + Scripture-reading voice + the_LORD.
- **Demoted to spinoff shorts (not the long spine):** Candidate D ("enmity as mercy") and the pure
  "her seed = virgin birth" angle. C is now the long-form spine, so the spinoff "first gospel in
  the curse" short overlaps the long's hook — fine; the distinct shorts are "her seed", "heel vs
  head", "serpent-crusher promised in Eden".
