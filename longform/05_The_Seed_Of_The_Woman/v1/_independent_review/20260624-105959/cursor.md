### KJV verbatim (FAIL-closed items)

**Colossians 2:15 — altered word.** M6 quotes:

> **"having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it."**

KJV opens with **"And having spoiled..."** The dropped **"And"** is not an ellipsis; it is a changed quotation. Fix or mark as partial with a labeled ellipsis.

**Partial quotes without labeled ellipsis** (your own lock brief requires this). Examples:
- M3 Gen 3:14 stops mid-verse with no `[...]`
- M2 **"till thou return unto the ground."** (from 3:19)
- M6 Heb 2:14 and Col 2:15 fragments
- M4 Rev 12:9 and 1 John 3:8 fragments

Gen 3:15 itself is correct (**"it shall bruise thy head"** — good; no smuggled "he").

### Chronology self-contradiction (clarity + fidelity to locked hook)

The locked spine is **sentencing order**: 3:15 before 3:16–19. M1 already has God speak to Adam and Eve first (**"Where art thou?"**, blame circle). M3 correctly narrows to **"When God begins to speak the sentence"** — but then overwrites that precision:

| Line | Problem |
|------|---------|
| M3: *"the very first words of consequence God speaks **after the fall** are not aimed at the guilty couple at all"* | False on plain hearing. Consequence-words to the guilty couple already happened in 3:9–13. |
| M3: *"**Grace gets the first word**"* | Same problem — listener just heard God question Adam. |
| M7: *"**the first thing God said after the fall** was that He would come for you"* | Direct contradiction of M1. A zero-Bible-knowledge listener will notice. |

This is not a small polish issue. It blurs the **one defensible hook** (protoevangelium before Adam/Eve's sentences) into a broader "God's first word was grace" claim the text does not support. Tighten every "first word / first response / first thing after the fall" line to **curse/sentence order only**.

### M5 — incomplete steel-man (locked brief gap)

The lock brief requires four real pushbacks; the draft delivers three. **Missing entirely:**

> *"'The woman' is just Eve; 'her seed' need not imply abnormal paternity."* (Hagar Gen 16:10, Rebekah Gen 24:60)

Without it, M5 feels stacked — you answer the easy objections and skip the one that most undercuts the "her seed" clue in M4. That weakens the "honest objection" movement.

---

### M5 — overstatement after promising honesty

M4 rightly says: *"No New Testament writer ever stops, quotes Genesis 3:15 word for word."* Then M5 claims:

> *"it is the New Testament writers **themselves who trace it**"*  
> *"They are the ones who **set the woman's seed at the center**"*

No NT writer explicitly links Gal 4:4's **"made of a woman"** to Gen 3:15 **"her seed."** That is trajectory inference — fine if labeled inference. Stated as NT-writer tracing, it reads like the **explicit application** the brief forbids and partially contradicts M4's honesty. Same risk in *"money in the bank"* (M4) — catchy, but it over-credits direct NT handling of 3:15.

---

### M4 — pinned corpus omission

Header pins **Luke 1:35** for the "her seed" trajectory alongside Gal 4:4. Luke never appears in the spoken draft. Not fatal, but the lock brief treated it as part of the honest M4 walk; skipping it leaves Gal 4:4 carrying a beat it was supposed to share.

---

### M7 landing — violates standing CTA constraint + eschatology slip

**Banned close recycled.** Lock brief: *"no recycled 'Will you trust Him?'"* Landing:

> *"You only have to come to the One whose heel was struck to break the serpent's hold over you — **and trust Him.**"*

That is the generic trust-close under new wording. The anti-works list before it is good; the final imperative collapses back to template.

**Eschatology flattened.** M6 carefully holds *"Already defeated at the cross; finished forever at the King's return"* and Rom 16:20's **"shortly."** M7 drops the nuance:

> *"the heel was bruised; **the head is crushed**"*

Present-tense total victory after M6's inaugurated/defeated-but-not-yet-finished frame. Doctrinally sloppy on first hearing.

**Landing mostly recapitulates, doesn't do new work.** M7 re-states M3 (rescue promised to serpent) + M6 (cross) + serpent's lie. The only fresh beats are the anti-works triad and *"Come, and find it kept"* — then undercut by *"trust Him."* The hook's question (*who is the crusher, and how does He win?*) is paid in M6, not M7; M7 doesn't advance beyond summary.

### Hook / cold open (first ~5 seconds)

M1 opens: *"Something has just gone terribly wrong in the garden."* Competent, not distinctive. The panel-locked hook — **grace preached to the serpent before the couple is sentenced** — lands ~90+ seconds in at M3. For a 6–8 min piece that's structurally acceptable, but on the **"grips in ~5 seconds"** lens this draft fails until a micro-tease of the spine is planted in M1's opening breath.

### Grace-anchored conviction

Mostly sound. M6's *"bearing what we had earned; the death that was our wages fell on Him"* describes **Christ's penal bearing**, not listener gain-loss motivation — acceptable.

M7's *"the serpent still hisses... that God is against you, that you had better hide"* names the accuser's lie without manufacturing fear-pressure on the listener — good.

No major works-salvation framing; the *"get clean first, or feel strong, or be sure your faith is large enough"* lines are properly grace-anchored.

### Doctrinal soundness

Minor eisegesis in M2: *"changed sides... joined the rebellion"* goes beyond what Genesis states at that moment (brief warned against over-claiming serpent dominion). Not heresy, but slightly ahead of the text.

Serpent = Satan handled correctly in M4 via Rev 12:9 as canonical identification, not Genesis naming.
