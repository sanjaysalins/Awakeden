## Independent adversarial review — *The Seed of the Woman* (DRAFT v1)

Reviewed against the locked C-led spine, panel constraints, and pinned KJV corpus. Skeptical pass; several fixable failures and one hard KJV breach.

---

### KJV verbatim

**FAIL — Colossians 2:15.** Quoted as:

> **"having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it."**

KJV opens with **"And"**: *"And having spoiled principalities and powers…"* Dropping the first word is not verbatim under your fail-closed rule.

**PASS elsewhere checked:** Gen 3:8, 3:9 ("Where art thou?"), 3:13, 3:14 (partial), 3:15 (both uses, correct **"it shall bruise"** — never smuggled "he"), Gal 4:4, Rom 16:20, Heb 2:14 (subset), 1 John 3:8 (subset), Rev 12:9 (subset).

**Gap, not breach:** Metadata pins **Luke 1:35** for M4, but it is never quoted or paraphrased in the spoken text. Not KJV failure, but a locked supporting clue left on the table.

---

### Doctrinal soundness

**Mostly sound.** Penal exchange in M6 is present and orthodox: *"bearing what we had earned"*, *"the death that was our wages fell on Him"*, Heb 2:14 on death destroying the devil. Resurrection + future *"shortly"* nuance is handled. Serpent = Satan is framed as canonical (Rev 12:9), not read back into Eden.

**Mild overreach — M2:** *"joined the rebellion"* / *"changed sides"* goes beyond what Genesis states at this moment. The brief warned against settled *serpent's dominion*; this edges toward allegiance language the text has not yet defined. Bondage-to-death framing is safer.

**Mild overreach — M4:** *"They are the ones who set the woman's seed at the center"* overstates the NT evidence. Gal 4:4 says *"made of a woman"* — one phrase, not a centring theme across writers. A hostile listener will hear triumph after M4's honesty about *no verbatim quote*.

**No virgin-birth grammar proof, no sinlessness genetics** — panel constraints respected. *"Her seed"* stays a *"clue, not a proof."* Good.

---

### Clarity on first hearing

**M5 incomplete steel-man (locked brief gap).** You handle three objections well (reading Jesus in; collective *zera* / Rom 16:20 corporate feet; serpent ≠ Satan named in Genesis). You **omit** the panel's third killer pushback:

> *"'The woman' is just Eve; 'her seed' need not imply abnormal paternity."*

(No Hagar Gen 16:10 / Rebekah Gen 24:60 parallel.) A Bible-literate skeptic gets a partial dodge; the answer sounds stronger than the objections you actually named.

**Hook/landing tension — M7:** M3 is precise: first words of consequence go **to the serpent**, before Adam/Eve are sentenced. M7 reframes: *"God's very first word **about you**"* — a zero-Bible-knowledge listener can hear that as contradicting M3 (was it to the snake or about me?). The mirror is blurred, not broken, but the distinctive hook is softened at the close.

**M4 logic hop:** *"Made of a woman"* → *"notice the deliverer is singular — it shall bruise"* — grammatically, KJV **"it"** can point to *seed* as collective. You assert singularity from trajectory; fair for orthodox reading, but the jump is fast for a novice.

---

### Grace-anchored conviction

**Mostly good.** M7 anti-works lines work: *"You do not have to get clean first, or feel strong, or be sure your faith is large enough."* Spirit convicts; script invites.

**Weak close — M7:** Ends with *"come to the One whose heel was struck… — **and trust Him**."* The locked brief explicitly bans a recycled *"Will you trust Him?"* close. This is that close with different upholstery. Gain/loss is avoided, but the landing does not do fresh invitation work.

---

### Freshness = faithful depth

**M3 delivers.** *"The very first sermon of the gospel in the Bible is preached to a snake"* — faithful, surprising about **narrative order**, not contrarian exegesis. Strongest freshness beat.

**M1 does not grip in ~5 seconds.** Opens *"Something has just gone terribly wrong in the garden"* — generic fall setup. Acceptable for 6–8 min long-form structure, but fails the standalone hook lens until M3 (~minute 2+). If anything is cut for a cold open, M3's serpent-first beat must lead.

---

### One thread spine (hook → middle → CTA)

**Mostly intact.** C-led spine runs: serpent-first promise → NT trajectory → objections → heel/head exchange → cross landing.

**Minor thread drift at landing:** Hook = grace **to the enemy**; close pivots to grace **for the hiding pair** (*"God's first response… was rescue"*). Related, not swapped — but the serpent-audience oddity (the panel's lowest-risk beat) is not mirrored back in M7.

---

### Landing does NEW work

**Partial fail.** M7 restates M3 (*oldest promise*, *first response was rescue*, *heel bruised / head crushed*) and lands on generic trust. What's new: *"You do not have to climb your own way back into the garden"* and serpent still hissing the lie. Not enough to pay off the hook's question (*who is the crusher, and how does He win?*) with a **specific** invitation tied to the serpent-addressed promise. The brief asked for more than a warmed-over *come and trust*.
