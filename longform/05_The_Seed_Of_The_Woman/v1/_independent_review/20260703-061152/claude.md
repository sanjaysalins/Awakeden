# Independent review — claude (OK, 75s)

I checked every quoted phrase against the KJV and read the piece against each lens. Findings below.

## KJV verbatim — CLEAN

All twelve quotations are exact KJV: Gen 3:8, 3:9 ("Where art thou?"), 3:13, 3:14, 3:15, Gal 4:4, Luke 1:35, Rev 12:9, 1 John 3:8, Rom 16:20, Heb 2:14, Col 2:15. No altered words found.

## Doctrine — sound, with two precision wobbles

- **Movement 6: "his final end, in God's word, is still coming 'shortly'"** — this is the weakest textual claim in the piece. Rom 16:20's "shortly" was spoken to the first-century Roman church; re-aiming that one word at the still-future return of Christ stretches it ~2,000 years. An eschatological reading of Rom 16:20 is within the orthodox range, but leaning the quoted word "shortly" on it invites the exact skeptic the piece elsewhere handles so well. Not heresy — but it's a proof-text seam in a narration whose whole brand is "honest about what kind of match this is."
- **Movement 4: "the angel's word to Mary names what kind of child she carried"** — tense overreach. At the annunciation Mary was not yet carrying; Luke 1:35 is prospective ("shall be born of thee"). This is exactly the witness-narrative-fact class of error the corpus re-audit flagged. One word fixes it: "would carry."
- Everything else holds: protoevangelium reading is standard and honestly qualified ("a clue, not a proof"); penal substitution in Movement 6 is clean; serpent-sentenced-first in Movement 3 is textually correct; "before a single drop of sacrificial blood" is accurate to the narrative order (3:15 precedes 3:21).

## Structure, thread, grace — strong

- **One spine, no swap**: hiding in the trees (hook) → promise spoken into the curse (middle) → "step out from behind the trees… be found by Him" (CTA). The bookend actually closes.
- **Landing does new work**: "Come out, and be found by Him" is not the generic "will you trust Him?" close — it resolves the piece's own opening image. Good.
- **Grace-anchored**: "you do not have to get clean first, feel strong, or be sure your faith is large enough — the dying do not heal themselves, and they were never asked to." No fear, no gain/loss framing anywhere.
- **Honest Objection movement is the piece's best asset** — it concedes the real weaknesses (no NT writer quotes Gen 3:15 as fulfilled; "under your feet" is the church's) and answers them without dodging. The freshness is about the text, the claim stays orthodox.

## Minor

- **Hook softness**: "Something has just gone terribly wrong in the garden" is serviceable but the flattest sentence in the piece; the second sentence ("hiding from Him") is where the grip actually is. First ~5 seconds could hit harder.
- "Beaten enemy" appears twice (Movements 6 and 7) — small echo.

VERDICT: PASS
TOP FIXES:
1. Movement 6 — drop or reframe the reuse of Rom 16:20's "shortly" for the final end at the King's return; either quote it only in its Movement 4/5 context or say plainly "his end is certain" without borrowing that word.
2. Movement 4 — change "names what kind of child she carried" to "would carry" (Luke 1:35 is spoken before conception).
3. Sharpen the opening line so the hiding image lands inside the first five seconds (e.g., open on the hiding itself, not "something has gone wrong").
