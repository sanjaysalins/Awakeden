# Independent review — codex (OK, 28s)

Findings:

- **KJV issue:** **"And they heard the voice of the LORD God walking in the garden in the cool of the day..."**  
  KJV has **"cool of the day"**, yes. This quote appears exact.

- **KJV issue:** **"Where art thou?"**, **"The serpent beguiled me, and I did eat."**, Genesis 3:14-15, Galatians 4:4, Luke 1:35, Revelation 12:9, 1 John 3:8, Romans 16:20, Hebrews 2:14, Colossians 2:15 all appear KJV-exact or excerpted without altered wording.

- **Doctrinal / exegetical overreach:** **"He is a beaten enemy already — though his final end, in God's word, is still coming 'shortly': defeated at the cross, finished forever at the King's return."**  
  The theology is broadly orthodox, but the handling of **"shortly"** is muddy. Romans 16:20 says God shall bruise Satan under the church’s feet shortly; tying that directly to “finished forever at the King’s return” imports a specific eschatological landing without proving it from the quoted verse.

- **Potentially contested framing:** **"The strike to the heel is Calvary"**  
  This is a standard Christian reading, but the narration presents it with more certainty than the earlier caveat allows. It first says Genesis 3:15 is **"a clue, not a proof"**, then later lands as if every detail maps directly. That creates an internal tension.

- **Clarity problem:** **"No New Testament writer ever stops, quotes Genesis 3:15 word for word, and declares it fulfilled in so many words."**  
  This is admirably honest, but for zero-Bible-knowledge listeners it may briefly destabilize the whole case. The narration recovers, but this line risks sounding like “the whole premise is unproven” before the listener has enough canon-level categories to follow the answer.

- **Thread-spine weakness:** The piece begins with **God seeking the hidden guilty**, then shifts into **canonical serpent-crushing prophecy**, then lands back on **"step out from behind the trees."** That mostly works, but the middle spends so much energy defending the prophecy reading that the “hiding / being found” thread goes quiet for too long.

- **Landing problem:** **"Come out, and be found by Him."**  
  This is stronger than a generic “trust Him” close, but it does not fully cash out the specific fresh insight of the narration: the promise was spoken to the enemy before sentence fell on the guilty. The CTA should make that do sharper work, not just return to the trees image.

- **Grace framing:** **"So you do not have to climb your own way back into the garden. You do not have to get clean first..."**  
  Strong grace-anchored invitation. No works-framing problem here.

- **Hook:** **"Something has just gone terribly wrong in the garden."**  
  Clear, immediate, and accessible. It grips well enough in the first five seconds, though it is familiar rather than startling.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Tighten the Romans 16:20 / “shortly” / final-return claim so it does not overstate what that verse proves.
2. Resolve the tension between “clue, not a proof” and the later direct claim that the heel wound is Calvary.
3. Make the landing do new work from the central insight: grace is announced to the enemy before judgment is spoken to the guilty.
