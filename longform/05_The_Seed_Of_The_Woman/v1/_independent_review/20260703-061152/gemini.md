# Independent review — gemini (OK, 219s)

As an independent, adversarial reviewer, I have scrutinized this script against the core lenses. While the structural thread is exceptionally tight and the CTA is a masterclass in grace-anchored framing ("the dying do not heal themselves"), there are specific flaws in clarity, quote formatting, and rhetorical strength that must be corrected.

### Findings

**1. Clarity / Insider Jargon (Movement 6)**
*Cite:* `"The strike to the heel is Calvary — and it was no glancing blow."`
*Critique:* The instructions demand that a listener with zero Bible knowledge must be able to follow. "Calvary" is insider Christianese; an unchurched listener does not know what or where Calvary is. Since you literally just wrote "That is the cross" in the preceding sentence, introducing a new proper noun here is both confusing and entirely unnecessary. Use plain language (e.g., "His crucifixion" or "the cross").

**2. KJV Verbatim & Unmarked Truncations (Movements 3, 4, & 6)**
*Cite:* `"above every beast of the field."` (Movement 3), `"called the Devil, and Satan."` (Movement 4), and `"that is, the devil."` (Movement 6)
*Critique:* While the words themselves are KJV, you truncated verses mid-sentence and silently altered the KJV's punctuation (swapping commas and semicolons for periods). For example, Genesis 3:14 continues `field; upon thy belly shalt thou go...` and Rev 12:9 continues `Satan, which deceiveth...`. If you cut off a quote before the sentence ends, you must use an ellipsis (`...`) to maintain the integrity of the verbatim text. 

**3. Tone / Conceding Too Much (Movement 5)**
*Cite:* `"So we do not rest the case on that one phrase."`
*Critique:* It is good to address the skeptic directly, but this phrasing concedes too much ground and sounds needlessly defensive. Telling the audience that you "do not rest the case" on the central verse of your entire script undermines your own premise. You can confidently point to the trajectory of the whole canon without verbally retreating from the weight of Genesis 3:15.

**4. The Landing does NEW work (Movement 7)**
*Cite:* `"So step out from behind the trees... Come out, and be found by Him."`
*Critique:* This is excellent. It perfectly closes the loop opened in Movement 1 (Adam and Eve hiding in the trees) and does not resort to a tired generic closing. No change needed here, but noted for successfully meeting the constraint.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the Christian jargon "Calvary" in Movement 6; replace it with terminology a zero-knowledge listener will instantly understand.
2. Add proper ellipses (`...`) to the ends of the KJV quotes in Movements 3, 4, and 6 where you truncated the verses and altered the original punctuation.
3. Reword the concession in Movement 5 ("we do not rest the case...") to address the skeptic confidently without undermining the foundational verse of the script.
