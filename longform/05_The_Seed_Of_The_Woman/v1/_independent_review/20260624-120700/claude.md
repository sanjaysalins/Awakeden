## Doctrine — sound, one real imprecision
- **Movement 6 — Col 2:15 attributed to the wrong event.** The line reads: *"And the empty tomb proved it: 'And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.'"* Col 2:15's "triumphing over them **in it**" points to the **cross** (the preceding clause is about the handwriting nailed to the cross), not the resurrection/empty tomb. Pinning a cross-triumph verse to "the empty tomb proved it" is a loose proof-text. Either swap to a resurrection text (e.g. keep Heb 2:14 logic) or reframe the lead-in to "the cross itself was the triumph." Not heresy, but it's the one place a careful panelist will catch a verse doing the wrong job.
- Everything else is orthodox: protoevangelium reading, already/not-yet handling of Rom 16:20's "shortly" ("defeated at the cross, finished forever at the King's return"), the seed/virgin-birth pairing (Gal 4:4 + Luke 1:35) is appropriately hedged ("a clue, not a proof").

## Freshness / honesty — handled unusually well
Movement 5 ("The Honest Objection") openly concedes "her seed" can just mean descendants and that no NT verse quotes Gen 3:15 — then rests the case on canon trajectory, not a single Hebrew word. This is the freshness-without-overclaim the contract wants. No contrarian/clickbait reading.

## Clarity on first hearing — one risk
- **Movement 5 is dense for the ear.** It stacks four objections in sequence ("First… Second… Third… and a fourth, sharper still…") before answering. A listener with zero Bible knowledge can lose the thread hearing four counter-arguments back-to-back with no breath. Consider compressing to the two strongest objections, or interleaving each objection with its answer rather than batching all four then all answers.
- **"Page three planted a promise" (Movement 4)** and **"on page three of the Bible"** framing is cute but anachronistic (the printed page vs. the scroll). Minor; clear enough, but it's a small tonal wobble in an otherwise reverent register.

## Thread spine, conviction, landing — all good
- One spine holds hook→middle→CTA: serpent / seed-who-crushes / come out of hiding. No thread-swap.
- Grace-anchored: Movement 7 ("You do not have to get clean first, feel strong… the dying do not heal themselves, and they were never asked to") — no fear, no gain/loss, no works. Clean.
- Landing does new work: "step out from behind the trees… Come out, and be found by Him" ties the close back to Movement 1's hiding. Not a generic "will you trust Him?"

Net: doctrinally sound, KJV-perfect, well-spined. The only substantive fix is the Col 2:15 / empty-tomb mismatch; the rest is polish.

VERDICT: PASS
TOP FIXES:
1. Movement 6 — re-anchor the Col 2:15 quote: it's a cross-triumph verse, so drop "the empty tomb proved it" lead-in (or replace with a genuine resurrection text), don't let it stand in as the resurrection's proof.
2. Movement 5 — thin the four-objection stack for by-ear clarity: keep the two strongest, or pair each objection with its answer instead of batching all four first.
3. Movement 4/landing — retire the "page three of the Bible" anachronism for something period-true (e.g. "in the opening pages," "at the very beginning").
