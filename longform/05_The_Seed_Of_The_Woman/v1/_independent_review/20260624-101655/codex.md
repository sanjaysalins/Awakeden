# Independent review — codex (OK, 173s)

**Findings**
- Lines 45-48 overstate the core hook: “Everywhere else… lineage is reckoned through the man… Only here is it ‘her seed’… points… to a birth with no human father.” That is too absolute. Scripture can speak of a woman’s seed/offspring, and genealogies are not as cleanly “fathers only” as claimed. Make this an unusual messianic/canonical clue, not a grammatical proof of the virgin birth.

- Lines 84-86 are doctrinally risky: “How God gets a sinless man into the world… not of a fallen father’s line.” This implies sinlessness comes from bypassing male biological descent. That is not airtight and can misstate orthodox Christology. Mary is also fallen; Christ’s sinlessness is not a genetics workaround.

- Lines 94-98 mishandle the KJV/exegetical argument: “the singular pronoun (‘it/he shall bruise’)” blurs KJV wording. KJV says “it shall bruise thy head” and “thou shalt bruise his heel.” Do not smuggle “he shall bruise” into a KJV-strict plan unless clearly marked as interpretive/translation comparison.

- Line 98 says “the NT’s own explicit application.” That is false as written. Galatians 4:4, Luke 1:35, Hebrews 2:14, Colossians 2:15, 1 John 3:8, and Revelation 12 support a canonical reading, but they do not explicitly quote Genesis 3:15 and say “this is fulfilled in Jesus.” Romans 16:20 is the strongest echo, but even there Satan is bruised “under your feet,” not simply Christ’s heel/head.

- Lines 80-81 and 99 over-compress the victory: “taking the serpent’s own deadly blow” and “the heel-wound is the head-crushing.” The cross-through-death reading is orthodox if guarded, but the plan needs resurrection/exaltation/consummation nuance. Otherwise Satan’s agency is overstated and Romans 16:20’s ongoing/future dimension is flattened.

- Lines 3-6 demand “ONE thread spine,” but lines 80-104 actually import A, B, C, and D: “first gospel” is C, “her seed” is A, “The Exchange” is B, and “enmity” remains in movement 2. The fused spine may work, but the current plan has not proven it is one spine rather than a bundle.

- Lines 25-38 label all NT cross-references “KJV, verbatim,” but several are excerpted with ellipses, especially Revelation 12:9-11. That can be acceptable, but the wording should say “KJV excerpts, ellipsized” and must be verified against the pinned strict corpus, not just “fetched.”

- Lines 8-10 assume “the panel” will answer the decision. In the actual episode folder, the only visible review output for this brief is a failed `gemini.md` run with no critique. The plan needs an acceptance rule: minimum healthy reviewers, red-team pass, and what happens when a provider fails.

- Lines 62-64 make Candidate C too absolute: “The first word of grace in the whole Bible.” God’s call to Adam before the curse can also be read as gracious pursuit. If C becomes a short, soften the claim.

- Lines 70-73 make Candidate D broader than Genesis 3:15: “hostility between us and evil is a gift God installed.” The verse speaks of enmity between serpent/woman and seed/seed; “regenerate hatred of sin” and James 4:4 are later theological applications, not direct matches.

VERDICT: REVISE

2. Downgrade “NT explicit application” to allusive/canonical support and add resurrection/consummation/Romans 16 nuance.
3. Run the real repo gates before lock: healthy multi-CLI panel/red-team, pinned KJV strict verification, and doctrine review.
