## Now the genuine critiques (polish-level, not blocking):

1. **The reveal leans on announcing (EW-G7).** Beat 6 does most of the mapping by *telling*: "the light that showed me what all my work had always been a shadow of" and the closing "I had no idea, in that gate, that I was sketching the gospel." The point-by-point parallel (right / wealth / willing-to-be-marred / bride / substitution) genuinely *shows* the type — but the meta-narration around it ("shadow of," "sketching the gospel") spoon-feeds what the parallels already prove. Trim one or two of those signposts and let the structural rhyme land on its own.

2. **Mild sag between Beats 4 and 5.** Both are reflective "let me be honest" beats orbiting the same figure — the nearer kinsman's refusal is invoked in Beat 3, quoted in Beat 4, and leaned on again in Beat 5. The turns *are* distinct (Beat 4 = cost/self-interest; Beat 5 = the foreigner/law tension), but the self-questioning cadence repeats: "I have asked myself a thousand times why I would" (end of 4) → "I wrestled before I went up to that gate" (start of 5). Tighten the seam so it doesn't read as the same beat twice.

3. **Bride applied to the individual.** "He… brings you home as His bride" and "be His own" map the corporate bride (the church, per the Eph 5 quote the piece itself cites) onto the single listener. Defensible as application, but slightly loose against the very verse quoted. A small reframe ("brings you into His bride, the church") would keep it precise without losing the warmth.

The wrestling (Beat 5) is a real steel-man — the Moab-reproach-vs-redemption tension is the genuine strongest objection, in Boaz's own voice, not a strawman. Voice is a consistent first-person Boaz throughout, not the essay narrator in costume. A zero-Bible listener can follow (famine, gleaning, the shoe custom, the nearer kinsman all explained in-line).

No real doctrinal or grounding error. The three items above are sharpening, not corrections.

VERDICT: PASS
TOP FIXES:
1. Trim the announced signposts in Beat 6 ("what all my work had always been a shadow of," "I was sketching the gospel") so the type→Christ turn is shown by the parallels, not declared.
2. Tighten the Beat 4→5 seam — the two reflective beats share a self-questioning cadence and both re-lean on the nearer kinsman's refusal; make each turn feel new.
3. Make the bride image precise against the Eph 5 verse cited — bring the listener *into* His bride (the church) rather than calling the individual His bride.
