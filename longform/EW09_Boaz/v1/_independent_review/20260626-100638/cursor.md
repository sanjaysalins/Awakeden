# Independent adversarial review — *The Kinsman-Redeemer* (Boaz, eyewitness-long)

Default skepticism. Concrete findings below, with exact lines cited.

---

## EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Most bold quotes are KJV substrings.** Ruth 2:8–10, 3:9/12, 4:9–10/17, Gal 3:13, Titus 2:14, Eph 5:25/27 check out against the project’s `kjv_cache.json`.

**Attribution failures (strict EW-INV-4):** only **[Ruth]** and **[the kinsman]** are tagged. Boaz’s own spoken KJV and several NT blocks are not.

1. **Beat 2 — `"under whose wings thou art come to trust,"`** — Framed as *“I blessed her, and prayed that the God of Israel… would repay her.”* In Ruth 2:12 this is **Boaz’s direct blessing to Ruth**, not a private prayer *about* God. Wrong speech-act; no **[Boaz]** tag.

2. **Beat 3 — three Boaz lines without speaker tags:**
   - **"Go not to glean in another field, neither go from hence, but abide here fast by my maidens."**
   - **"have I not charged the young men that they shall not touch thee?"** — Prose says *“And I charged my young men to leave her unharmed”* then quotes a **rhetorical question Boaz asks Ruth**. A zero-Bible listener may hear two different acts.
   - **"there is a kinsman nearer than I."**

3. **Beat 3 — `"a man plucked off his shoe, and gave it to his neighbour"`** — Ruth 4:7’s **legal narrator**, not Boaz’s first-person deed (4:8: *“So he drew off his shoe”*). The witness says he sealed it by the old custom, then quotes third-person statute language with no **[Scripture]** / book attribution.

4. **Beat 5 — `"how thou hast left thy father and thy mother, and the land of thy nativity, and art come unto a people which thou knewest not heretofore."`** — Boaz’s **field speech to Ruth** (Ruth 2:11), but placed under *“I only knew what I had seen”* as observed fact. Same speaker, wrong moment; no tag.

5. **Beat 6 — `"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."`** — Introduced only by *“as the Scripture says:”* — not Galatians / Paul / **[Scripture]** before the block. Titus and Eph get *“Paul wrote”* / *“Paul says”*; Gal 3:13 does not.

**Watch-item on Ruth 4:6:** The kinsman quote matches this repo’s cached KJV (*“redeem thou my right to thyself; for I cannot redeem it”*). Many printed KJV editions read *“to thee”* and repeat *“for myself”* at the end. If the bar is “standard Cambridge KJV,” this line is suspect; if the bar is this project’s bible-api cache, wording passes — attribution still passes only because **[the kinsman]** is tagged.

## EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Mostly passes.** Beat 7 names **Jesus** / **Christ** and names a response: *“Come to Jesus… Be redeemed”* / *“be His own”* — not the banned bare *“will you trust Him?”* template. Grace-anchored: *“The redeemed never pay.”* No fear/gain-loss stack.

**Weak spots:**

- **"Sit with that."** — Passive pause; the named invitation comes *after* the emotional landing, not through it.
- **"So I ask you what I once asked at that gate"** — At the gate Boaz asked **elders to witness a purchase** (*“Ye are witnesses this day…”*), not a stranger to accept redemption. The rhyme is clever but **story-false**; a listener who just heard Beat 3 will feel the mismatch.

---

## EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Partly earned, partly announced.**

**Earned well (Beat 4 is the steel):**
- *“lest I mar mine own inheritance”* → *“He was willing to be marred”* — wordplay grounded in the nearer kinsman’s refusal.
- Bride thread: threshing-floor redemption → *“He took the redeemed to be His own bride, the way I took Ruth.”*
- Outsider thread: Moabitess → messianic line.

**Not earned — asserted or imported:**

- **"I tell you now by the light of the word written long after me"** — Correct signpost (EW-INV-11), but the turn is **announced**, then illustrated.
- **"He is the true Kinsman-Redeemer, and He did what I only acted out in a barley field"** — Label **before** walk-through.
- **"That is the substitution my barley field could never show you"** — The piece **admits** the Ruth scene doesn’t carry substitution; Gal 3:13 then does the doctrinal work from outside Ruth. The gate economics show *cost* and *willingness to be marred*, not *curse borne in our place* — that hinge is **imported**, not shown from what Boaz lived.
- **"He stepped into the gate in our place"** — Application metaphor; not drawn from Boaz’s gate scene.
- **"I had no idea, in that gate, that I was sketching the gospel"** — Retrospective editorial; Ruth shows **goel law**, not “gospel” in Boaz’s mouth.

## EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Primary structural defect.**

Ruth’s **in-text** steel-man is the nearer kinsman (Beat 4): *“I cannot redeem it for myself, lest I mar mine own inheritance.”* That is the strongest objection **in the passage**.

Beat 5 pivots to **Moab / tenth-generation exclusion** — material **not voiced by Boaz in Ruth**, where he is immediately generous (field → threshing floor → gate).

**Chronology breaks character:**
- Beat 5: *“I wrestled **before I went up to that gate**.”*
- Beat 3 already has Boaz protecting Ruth in the field, saying at the threshing floor *“I would have done it that hour,”* then completing the gate redemption.

The listener hears the act **finished**, then hears doubt **retrofitted** as pre-gate. That makes the Moab objection feel **installed**, not lived — and it **sidesteps** the sharper steel-man the piece already has in Beat 4.

Beat 5 also **never wrestles Ruth’s actual tension**: if Moab was held at a distance, **how does a Moabitess enter David’s line?** It gestures (*“kindness to the outsider a breach of the law, or the very heart the law had been pointing at”*) but **doesn’t answer** the Deuteronomic problem it raises.

---

## EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Mostly sound evangelical typology.** Substitution is named: *“The curse that should have fallen on us fell on Him instead”* + Gal 3:13. Bride/redeemer mapping is standard. No supersession — *“A Moabitess in the line of the Messiah — the outsider brought all the way in”* is inclusion, not replacement.

**Soft spots:**

1. **Typology crack in Beat 4:** *“I gave and got back only an empty field and a foreigner's poverty”* vs Beat 3: *“the words I had waited my life to say”* when purchasing Ruth. Boaz **wanted** her; the “got nothing” arithmetic is **false to his own testimony** and weakens the Christ parallel (Christ **does** gain a bride — named later, but Beat 4 denies the redeemer receives anything).

2. **Two sermons stitched:** Jump from **goel land/levirate economics** to **“curse of the law”** is Pauline, not Ruth-native. *“We owed the law a debt we could not pay, and the penalty was a curse”* is **asserted**, not derived from what Boaz bought at the gate.

---

## EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strong in Beats 1, 3, 4, 7** — wealthy gate-elder with memory and cost.

**Essay bleed:**
- *“Redemption is costly, and only love will pay it — the price always falls on the redeemer”* — thesis sentence, not a man remembering a morning.
- *“Let me tell you the weight of it”* / *“Now let me tell you what I did with my own hands”* — presenter pivots.
- *“sketching the gospel”* — modern meta for an OT witness.

Beat 5’s **mis-timed wrestling** is the biggest character-consistency hit.

---

## First-hearing clarity

- Beat 1 **spoils the gate** (*“I bought it all… took a Moabitess to be my wife”*); Beat 3 **re-tells** the same gate at length. Zero-Bible listeners get the ending twice before the wrestling beat.
- **Mahlon** appears only inside a quote (*“the wife of Mahlon”*) with no setup.
- Threshing-floor gloss (*“spread thy skirt”* = redeem) — **good**.
- *“tenth generation”* Moab reproach is named but not anchored to a verse; listeners may not grasp the legal weight.

---

## Hook / spine

**Hook grips** (gate, shoe, Moabitess). **One witness, one thread** (goel → Christ). No swap. Spine holds.

---

## Long-form pacing (~9–11 min)

**Sags structurally, not just length.**

- Beats 2→3→4 repeat the same nouns (foreign widow / stranger / costly redeemer) without enough **new turns** between them.
- Beat 4 asks *“why I would”*; Beat 5 asks again in different clothing **after** the act is told.
- Beat 6’s Paul block adds **runtime without a new lived turn** from Boaz’s gate.
- Word count appears **~1,100–1,200** — likely **under** EW-INV-8’s 1300–1650 band; repetition makes it feel shorter than 9–11 minutes of earned material.

---

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Rebuild Beat 5’s chronology** — move wrestling **before** first kindness (field), or reframe it around Beat 4’s **inheritance-cost** objection already in Ruth; drop or compress the Moab/tenth-generation thread unless Boaz can wrestle Ruth’s **inclusion** without contradicting Beat 3’s *“I would have done it that hour.”*
2. **De-spoil Beat 1 / compress Beat 3** — tell the gate outcome once; cut duplicate *“why would I redeem?”* between Beats 4 and 5 so long-form runtime buys **new turns**, not recap.
3. **Earn substitution from gate economics** (*mar / inheritance / name-not-mine / bride-not-distance*) **before** Gal 3:13, and fix EW-INV-4 attribution on every bold quote block (**[Boaz]**, **[Scripture]**, Galatians/Paul before NT spans).
