# Independent review — claude (OK, 109s)

I read the whole piece and checked every KJV quote against the text. This is a strong artifact — but here are the concrete problems an adversarial pass surfaces.

## KJV verbatim — clean
I verified each quote. All are exact KJV:
- Ruth 2:10 ("Why have I found grace…stranger?"), 2:12 ("under whose wings thou art come to trust"), 2:8, 2:9, 3:9, 3:12, 4:9, 4:10, 4:7, 4:6, 2:11, 4:17 — all verbatim.
- Gal 3:13, Titus 2:14, Eph 5:25, Eph 5:27 — verbatim within the quote marks.

No altered words. Good.

## Attribution gap (EW-INV-4) — the real flag
The OT quotes and the kinsman's line are all attributed to a speaker. But the **NT proof-texts in Beat 6 are dropped with no speaker/source tag**:
- "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law…" (Gal 3:13)
- "Who gave himself for us…" (Titus 2:14)
- "Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it" (Eph 5:25)

Contrast Beat 6's own genealogy quote, which IS tagged — **"Scripture says of him,"** "he is the father of Jesse…". The blanket frame ("the word written long after me") loosely covers it, but the invariant wants each Scripture quote tagged, and you tagged one and not the other three. Inconsistent. Add a "Scripture says" / "Paul wrote" lead-in to the three.

## Eph 5:27 truncation — minor
**"…not having spot, or wrinkle."** closes with a period where KJV has a comma ("…or wrinkle, or any such thing"). The quoted words are exact, so it's an elision, not a corruption — but it's exactly the kind of mid-verse cut the panel flagged on Isaiah (Acts 8:35). Either close mid-verse cleanly or carry "…or any such thing."

## Factual imprecision — Beat 1
"a name that went back to **Elimelech**." Elimelech was Boaz's **kinsman of the same clan** (Ruth 2:1, "of the family of Elimelech"), not his ancestor. "a name that went back to Elimelech" reads as descent and overstates the link. Reword to kinship ("of Elimelech's own kindred") to stay accurate.

## Reveal (EW-G7) — earned, and well
The turn is genuinely shown, not announced. The pivot hinges on the kinsman's **"lest I mar mine own inheritance"** → Christ "willing to be marred." That is drawn straight from the text Boaz lived, not asserted. One of the better type→Christ landings I've reviewed.

## Wrestling (EW-G8) — genuine steel-man
The doubt is real: Moab's reproach / "tenth generation" (Deut 23:3) vs. the heart of the law. It's internalized in Boaz's own voice and not strawmanned. The resolution leans on emotional pull ("I could not turn her away") rather than reasoned argument, but that's honest and characterful, and the foreshadow line **"I did not yet understand why my heart would not count the cost"** earns it.

## Doctrine (EW-G9) — sound
Substitution explicitly named: "The curse that should have fallen on us fell on Him instead… He stepped into the gate in our place and bore it Himself." Penalty (curse) + what Christ bore, both present. No supersession — "the outsider brought all the way in" is fulfillment, not replacement.

## CTA (EW-INV-2) — clean
Names Jesus, grace-anchored ("You cannot earn it; the redeemed never pay"), response named (receive / trust / come), no fear or gain-loss, no tired "will you trust Him?" template. The "Do not come to me… Come to Jesus" beat is a strong, non-formulaic close.

## Voice / length
First-person voice is consistent and characterful throughout — not the essay narrator in costume. No thread swap; one witness, one spine. On **length**: my rough count is ~1,450 words → likely ~9–9.5 min at a measured eyewitness read. That's at the **floor** of the 9–11 min target, not the middle. It doesn't sag (Beat 4 = cost, Beat 5 = outsider-legitimacy are distinct turns), but there's no slack if the read comes in brisk — consider one more interior beat (e.g. the threshing-floor night from Boaz's hesitation) if you want margin.
