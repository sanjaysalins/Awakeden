# The Kinsman-Redeemer — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Boaz of Bethlehem, the mighty man of wealth  ·  **Core text:** Ruth 2–4  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** the goel — the near kinsman with the right and the wealth who, at his own cost, buys back the poor foreign widow and takes her as his bride — a shadow of Christ our Kinsman-Redeemer (Gal 3:13)
**Status:** v1 — Awakeden eyewitness SHORT (B1→B3→B6→B7), cut from the locked long. Beat 6 reveal is a SIGNPOSTED reflective device (EW-INV-11): Boaz speaks "by the light of the word written long after me," never claiming he knew Christ in his moment. Boaz is the shadow; Christ is the true Redeemer. Substitution named (Gal 3:13). No invented divine speech; no supersession.

## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Boaz of Bethlehem, the richest man in town. The land at my feet had belonged to Elimelech, my kinsman who died in Moab, his name and fields left to no one. At the gate, a poor widow's whole future hung on one man's word. I was that man.

## Beat 3 — The act
In our law the goel, the near kinsman, held the right to buy back a dead man's land and raise his name. Ruth asked it of me: **"I am Ruth thine handmaid: spread therefore thy skirt over thine handmaid; for thou art a near kinsman."** A nearer kinsman came first, and I will not hide the cost. To redeem meant land that would never bear my name, and a Moabitess for my wife — my inheritance thinned to raise another man's line. He weighed it and walked: **"I cannot redeem it for myself, lest I mar mine own inheritance."** I weighed the same, and paid it at the gate: **"Ye are witnesses this day, that I have bought all that was Elimelech’s."**

## Beat 6 — The reveal
I tell you now by the light of the word written long after me. From the son Ruth bore me came David's line, and a greater Kinsman who paid the cost no nearer kinsman would. He took our flesh, marred where that kinsman would not. He is Jesus, the Christ. Scripture says: **"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."** And He takes the redeemed outsider for His bride.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
So be still a moment. If you feel like that woman in my field — poor and far off — do not come to me. I am only the shadow. He paid not silver but His own blood, made a curse so you need never be. The redeemed never pay. Will you let the Redeemer pay what you cannot? Come to Jesus, the true Kinsman-Redeemer, and be His own.
