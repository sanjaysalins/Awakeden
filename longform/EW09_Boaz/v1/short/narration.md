# The Kinsman-Redeemer — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Boaz of Bethlehem, the mighty man of wealth  ·  **Core text:** Ruth 2–4  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** the goel — the near kinsman with the right and the wealth who, at his own cost, buys back the poor foreign widow and takes her as his bride — a shadow of Christ our Kinsman-Redeemer (Gal 3:13)
**Status:** v1 — Awakeden eyewitness SHORT (B1→B3→B6→B7), cut from the locked long. Beat 6 reveal is a SIGNPOSTED reflective device (EW-INV-11): Boaz speaks "by the light of the word written long after me," never claiming he knew Christ in his moment. Boaz is the shadow; Christ is the true Redeemer. Substitution named (Gal 3:13). No invented divine speech; no supersession.

## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Boaz of Bethlehem, the richest man in town. One morning at the city gate, a poor widow's last hope and a foreign woman's whole future hung on one man's word. I was that man. Before the elders I bought it all — the land, the name, a Moabitess for my wife.

## Beat 3 — The act
Ruth came to me in the night, a foreign widow with nothing, and asked the one thing only a kinsman could give: **"I am Ruth thine handmaid: spread therefore thy skirt over thine handmaid; for thou art a near kinsman."** A nearer kinsman could have redeemed her, but the cost drove him off: **"I cannot redeem it for myself, lest I mar mine own inheritance."** So I paid it all at the gate: **"Ye are witnesses this day, that I have bought all that was Elimelech’s."** It gained me nothing but a poor outsider. Only love pays such a price.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
I tell you now by the light of the word written long after me. From the son Ruth bore me came David's line, and from David's house came a Redeemer greater than I — Jesus, the Christ. He is the true Kinsman-Redeemer. He took our flesh to be our near kinsman, paid not silver but His own blood, and was willing to be marred where that nearer kinsman would not: **"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."** Then He took the redeemed outsider as His bride and gave her His name.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
So if you feel like that woman in my field — poor, far off, a stranger with nothing to bring — do not come to me. I am but the shadow. Come to Jesus, the true Redeemer, who has already paid the price with His own blood. You cannot earn it; the redeemed never pay. Come to Him, receive what He has done, and be His own.
