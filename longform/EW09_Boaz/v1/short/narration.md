# The Kinsman-Redeemer — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Boaz of Bethlehem, the mighty man of wealth  ·  **Core text:** Ruth 2–4 → Gal 3:13
**Status:** v1 — Awakeden eyewitness SHORT, PUNCHY hook-first structure (Hook → strange thing → turn → punch), cut from the locked long. Beat 3 reveal is a SIGNPOSTED reflective device (EW-INV-11): Boaz speaks "by the light of the word written long after me," never claiming he knew Christ in his moment. Boaz is the shadow; Christ is the true Redeemer. Substitution named (Gal 3:13). No invented divine speech; no supersession; the Moabitess grafted in is inclusion, not replacement.

## Beat 1 — Hook
A penniless foreign widow asked me to redeem her — to buy back all she had lost — though it would cost me my own inheritance.

## Beat 2 — The strange thing
In our law the goel — the near kinsman — could redeem a dead man's land and take his widow to raise his name. The land was Elimelech's, my kinsman, dead in Moab. One stood nearer than I; but when he heard redeeming meant a Moabitess and his money poured into another's name, he walked — it would mar his inheritance. So I paid the full price at the gate, and gained Ruth as my bride — joy worth every coin.

## Beat 3 — The turn
Long after, One came from David's line, born in my Bethlehem — a greater Kinsman than I. He took our flesh, paid not silver but His own blood; the curse we owed fell on Him. Scripture says: **"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."** He takes the poor outsider for His bride.

## Beat 4 — The punch
So if you feel poor, far off, a stranger with nothing to bring — good. The redeemed never pay. Let the Kinsman who bought you with His own blood take you home as His bride. His name is Jesus. Receive your Redeemer.
