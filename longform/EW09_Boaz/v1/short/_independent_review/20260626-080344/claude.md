# Independent review — claude (OK, 92s)

Reviewed the Boaz eyewitness short adversarially against the EW gates. KJV verbatim checks out across all four quotes (Ruth 3:9, 4:6, 4:9, Gal 3:13) and all are attributed before quoting — that's the strong part. The real problems are elsewhere.

**EW-G8 — WRESTLING is entirely missing (most serious).** Boaz never voices a single honest doubt or question. The whole piece is confident assertion: "I was that man… So I paid it all… Only love pays such a price." There is no internalized strongest objection — nothing like *"Why would I spend my name and inheritance on a foreign widow who could give me nothing?"* The steel-man beat the format requires simply isn't here. This is the signature device half-absent.

**Length — over budget for a ~90s short.** I count ~330 spoken words (Beat 1 ~57 + Beat 3 ~105 + Beat 6 ~100 + Beat 7 ~70). At a reverent narration pace that's ~2.2–2.4 min, well past 90s and above the 220–320 ceiling. Beat 6 especially sags ("I tell you now by the light of the word written long after me…") — it's the most essayistic, least first-person stretch.

**Beat numbering is broken / spine has holes.** Labels jump 1 → 3 → 6 → 7. Beats 2, 4, 5 are gone with no signal. That's not just cosmetic — the dropped beat(s) are exactly where the wrestling and the lived emotional turn should sit. It reads as a 7-beat spine with the middle deleted.

**First-hearing clarity — "Elimelech" is unexplained.** A zero-Bible listener hits *"that I have bought all that was Elimelech's"* with no idea who Elimelech is (the dead husband/father whose estate is redeemed). The "skirt over thine handmaid" archaism is partly rescued by the framing ("the one thing only a kinsman could give"), but Elimelech is a bare name.

**Voice (EW-G10) — mostly holds, weakest in the reveal.** Beats 1 and 3 are genuinely first-person and characterful. Beat 6 drifts toward the detached essay narrator ("From the son Ruth bore me came David's line, and from David's house came a Redeemer…") — it's Boaz reciting genealogy/theology rather than testifying.

**What's working (so I'm fair):**
- CTA (EW-INV-2) is strong and grace-anchored: "do not come to me. I am but the shadow. Come to Jesus… You cannot earn it; the redeemed never pay. Come to Him, receive what He has done, and be His own." Response is named (come/receive), no fear/gain-loss, not the tired "will you trust Him?" template.
- Reveal (EW-G7) is genuinely earned at one point: "willing to be marred where that nearer kinsman would not" cleanly pays off the quoted "lest I mar mine own inheritance." That's shown, not asserted.
- Doctrine (EW-G9) is sound: goel typology, substitution carried by Gal 3:13 ("the curse of the law… made a curse for us"), continuity Boaz→David→Christ with no supersession.

**Minor flags:** "paid not silver" — Ruth 4 records the shoe/sandal custom, no silver, so "silver" is figurative (defensible but loose); "the richest man in town" embellishes KJV's "mighty man of wealth"; curly apostrophe in "Elimelech’s" vs straight elsewhere (cosmetic).

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add the missing WRESTLING beat (EW-G8): give Boaz a real first-person doubt/objection in his own voice — e.g. the genuine cost to his own name/inheritance in redeeming a penniless foreign widow — and resolve it, so the steel-man is present, not skipped.
2. Cut to ~220–250 words for a true ~90s short: trim Beat 6's essayistic genealogy/recitation and tighten Beat 3 so the single witnessed moment lands without sag.
3. Fix the spine: renumber beats contiguously (no 1→3→6→7 holes) and give "Elimelech" a one-clause identifier ("my dead kinsman Elimelech") so a zero-Bible listener can follow.
