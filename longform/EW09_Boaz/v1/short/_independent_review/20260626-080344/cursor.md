## Independent adversarial review — *The Kinsman-Redeemer* (Boaz, eyewitness-short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim (inside quotes):** The four quoted spans match KJV substrings in `passage.txt`. No altered words inside the marks.

**Attribution failures:**

- **`"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."`** — Galatians 3:13, but nothing before the quote names Paul, Scripture, or “the word written long after me” *as the speaker of this line*. The prior sentence is Boaz’s own theology (*“He took our flesh… paid not silver…”*). A zero-Bible listener cannot tell this is Paul, not Boaz at the gate. Same pattern flagged on Passover and Bronze Serpent reviews.

- **`"I am Ruth thine handmaid: spread therefore thy skirt over thine handmaid; for thou art a near kinsman."`** — Ruth is implied by *“Ruth came to me… and asked”*, but the lens wants the speaker named *before* the quote (e.g. *Ruth said unto me*). Weak, not clean.

- **`"Ye are witnesses this day, that I have bought all that was Elimelech’s."`** — Boaz is implied by *“So I paid it all at the gate”*, but the KJV line is Boaz *to the elders and all the people* (Ruth 4:9–10). The clip also drops the marriage half of the redemption (*“Moreover Ruth the Moabitess… have I purchased to be my wife”*), so the gate moment is under-attributed and under-quoted for what the story actually is.

**Non-quote drift:** Beat 1 opens *“the richest man in town”* while Ruth 2:1 and the header use *“mighty man of wealth”* — minor, but it signals essay-polish over witness voice.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (headline failure)

There is **no steel-man doubt in Boaz’s own voice**. Beats 2/4/5 are compressed away (allowed for SHORT), but **the wrestling must survive inside the kept beats** — as Jonah does in Beat 1 (*“I would sooner drown than watch Him spare the city I hated”*).

What you get instead:

- *“It gained me nothing but a poor outsider. Only love pays such a price.”* — that is **resolution**, not struggle.

The real objections in Ruth are never voiced:

- Why should I **mar my inheritance** for a **Moabitess** when the nearer kinsman already refused?
- Can a **foreign widow** belong in Israel’s line / covenant?
- Why is **name-and-land redemption** worth more than my wealth?

Without one of those internalized, Beat 3’s cost and Beat 6’s “willing to be marred where that nearer kinsman would not” lands as clever wordplay, not lived wrestling answered by the reveal.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned vs. announced

**Announced:**

- *“I tell you now by the light of the word written long after me”* — generic cross-time explainer (same family as the banned *“at last I understood”* stinger).
- *“He is the true Kinsman-Redeemer”* — label before the type fully carries the turn.
- *“From the son Ruth bore me came David's line, and from David's house came a Redeemer greater than I — Jesus, the Christ”* — genealogy asserted; Boaz did not live to see David crowned, let alone Christ. The David bridge is doctrinally fine but **not shown from what he witnessed** in the kept beats.

**Partially earned:**

- Nearer kinsman’s *“lest I mar mine own inheritance”* vs. Christ’s willingness to pay — the best textual bridge in the piece.
- Bride/outsider echo (*“redeemed outsider as His bride”*) tracks Ruth.

**Weak bridge:**

- Gal 3:13’s **curse of the law** is imported without walking from *goel* (land, name, marriage) to *curse*. A first-hearing listener hears “kinsman bought land” then suddenly “curse of the law” with no shown step.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Sound core:** goel → Christ Kinsman-Redeemer; willing cost; blood not silver; grace not earning. No supersessionism in the text.

**Gaps:**

- **Substitution under-named.** Jonah short explicitly says *“The penalty I had earned, He took in my place.”* Here you have blood and curse language but never plainly name **what penalty** (sin’s curse/judgment under the law) Christ bore **for us**. *“Paid the price with His own blood”* is generic.

- **“Willing to be marred”** fuses Ruth 4:6’s *mar mine own inheritance* with Gal 3:13’s *being made a curse* — related themes, not the same word or act. Risks sounding clever rather than text-grounded.

- *“Then He took the redeemed outsider as His bride and gave her His name”* — familiar Eph 5 typology, but in 90s it can collapse Ruth → individual believer-as-bride without the **Gentiles-through-Abraham** thread Gal 3:14 actually carries. Not heresy; slightly over-tidy.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Mostly passes:** Names Jesus/Christ; *“Come to Him, receive what He has done”* names the response; *“You cannot earn it; the redeemed never pay”* is grace-anchored; no bare *“will you trust Him?”* template; no fear/gain-loss pressure.

**Soft note:** *“if you feel like that woman in my field”* leans on mood-matching more than on **substitution** as the reason to come — and see clarity issue below.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

Boaz is sometimes present (*“I was that man”*, gate payment), but Beat 6 slides into **systematic-theology narrator**:

- *“He took our flesh to be our near kinsman, paid not silver but His own blood…”*

That is catechism in first person, not a wealthy Bethlehem elder recounting a costly gate morning and a Moabite widow he chose. Contrast Jonah Beat 6, which stays in *his* fish/deep idiom while naming Christ.

---

### First-hearing clarity

- **Chronology whiplash:** Beat 1 — *“Before the elders I bought it all — the land, the name, a Moabitess for my wife”* — **spoils the ending** before Beat 3’s night scene and the nearer-kin refusal. A new listener will not know whether the story is gate-first or night-first.

- **Orphan CTA image:** Beat 7 — *“that woman in my field”* — **the field never appears** in Beats 1/3/6. Ruth 2 is in `passage.txt` but not in the spoken short. The invitation points at a scene the listener never heard.

- **Untranslated mechanics:** *“near kinsman,”* *“spread thy skirt,”* redemption of *“the name”* — a zero-Bible listener may not grasp **why a relative’s word at the gate decides a foreign widow’s future**. Hook stakes are vague (*“one man's word”*) until Beat 3.

---

### SHORT form — tightness / single moment

~280–310 spoken words — within budget. One thread (Ruth redemption → Christ) holds; no second episode crowding.

But it packs **two OT scenes** (night threshing floor + gate) **plus** genealogy **plus** Gal 3 Christology into ~90s, while **skipping the field** that the CTA still references. Tight on word count, loose on moment unity and listener orientation.

---

### Hook / spine

Hook identity works (*“I am Boaz… I was that man”*). Spine is one witness, one thread — **PASS**. The hook also **front-loads the outcome**, which weakens suspense and clarity rather than swapping threads.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Add Boaz’s steel-man wrestling in his own voice** (inheritance marred, Moabitess outsider, nearer kinsman’s refusal) inside Beat 1 or 3 — so cost and “willing where he would not” are earned, not asserted.
2. **Attribute Gal 3:13 before the quote** (Paul / Scripture voice) and **walk the goel→curse bridge in one shown step**; name substitution explicitly (sin’s curse/judgment borne in our place), not only “blood” and “price.”
3. **Fix listener clarity:** reorder Beat 1 so the gate payoff isn’t spoiled before the night scene; **either stage the field in Beat 1/3 or cut “in my field” from the CTA**; frame Ruth’s and Boaz’s quotes with explicit speaker tags.
