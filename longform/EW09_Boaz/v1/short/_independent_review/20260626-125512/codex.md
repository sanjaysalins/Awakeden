# Independent review — codex (OK, 24s)

Findings:

- **EW-INV-4 problem:** `Scripture says: **"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."**` is not clean KJV verbatim. KJV has a colon after `for us`, not a period. Attribution is also weak: `Scripture says` does not name Paul/Galatians before the quote.

- **EW-G7 reveal partly asserted:** `Long after, One came from David's line... a greater Kinsman than I` is mostly announced. The connection is plausible, but the narration does not show enough from Boaz’s lived moment why Christ is “greater” beyond parallels: kinsman, price, bride. The key leap `marred where the nearer kinsman would not be` is strong, but underdeveloped.

- **EW-G8 wrestling is thin:** The strongest internal objection is missing. Boaz never really wrestles with the scandal/cost of redeeming Ruth. `joy worth every coin` resolves too easily. The objection should live inside Boaz: the risk to his name, inheritance, reputation, and the Moabitess question.

- **EW-G9 doctrine mostly sound, but needs sharper substitution:** `the curse we owed fell on Him` is good, but the penalty is not fully named. “Curse” is biblical, but for first-hearing clarity, say what that curse means: judgment/condemnation/death borne by Christ in our place.

- **Voice issue:** `Long after, One came from David's line... Scripture says` shifts toward sermon narrator. Boaz’s first-person witness fades after Beat 2. The turn needs to stay in Boaz’s voice rather than become a compact theological essay.

- **First-hearing clarity risk:** `goel`, `mar his inheritance`, `redeemed never pay`, and `curse of the law` may not land for a listener with zero Bible knowledge without one plain explanatory sentence.

- **CTA is acceptable but could be less generic:** `Come to Jesus` names the response and names Jesus. It is grace-anchored. But `Let the Redeemer pay what you cannot` is slightly vague because Christ has already paid; better invite them to receive/rest in Him.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the KJV quote punctuation and attribute it specifically before quoting.
2. Add Boaz’s real internal wrestling over the cost/scandal of redeeming Ruth.
3. Strengthen the earned Christ reveal by naming the penalty Christ bore in our place while keeping Boaz’s first-person voice.
