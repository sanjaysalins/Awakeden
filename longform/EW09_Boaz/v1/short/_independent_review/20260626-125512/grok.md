# Independent review — grok (OK, 36s)

Checking the eyewitness spec for the exact gates and invariants before delivering the review.
## Independent adversarial review — *The Kinsman-Redeemer* (Boaz, eyewitness-short)

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling: **missing (headline failure)**

There is **no honest doubt in Boaz’s own voice**. He pays at once:

> *"So I paid the full price at the gate, and gained Ruth as my bride — joy worth every coin."*

The real steel-man — *this will thin my estate, marry a Moabitess, raise another man’s name, gain me nothing* — lives only in the nearer kinsman, externally:

> *"when he heard redeeming meant a Moabitess and his money poured into another's name, he walked — it would mar his inheritance."*

That is **his** objection, not Boaz **wrestling** with it. The eyewitness form needs the witness to voice the strongest cost/doubt, then resolve it. Here Boaz skips straight to payment with no hesitation, no question, no counted cost in first person. Signature beat absent.

---

### Beat 1 — **wrong witnessed moment**

> *"A penniless foreign widow knelt at my feet and asked me to buy back all she'd lost"*

That is **not** what happened in Ruth 2–4. At the field she asked to **glean** (Ruth 2:7). The redeem/marriage plea came later at the **threshing floor** (Ruth 3:9). The hook names the wrong scene and collapses two events into one.

For a short built on **ONE moment**, the opening mis-anchors the whole piece. A listener who knows Ruth will distrust it; a listener who doesn’t will get a fuzzy picture of what actually occurred.

---

### Hook — **inheritance cost misassigned**

> *"though it would cost me my inheritance"*

In the text, the nearer kinsman refused because redeeming **would mar his inheritance** (Ruth 4:6). Boaz **accepted** that cost. The hook makes it sound like Ruth’s plea threatened **Boaz’s** inheritance before he acted — which blurs who bore the objection and weakens the later parallel to Christ willingly being “marred.”

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution: **weak**

Only one quoted line:

> *Scripture says: **"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."***

- **Wording:** matches Galatians 3:13 KJV for the span quoted. ✓  
- **Attribution:** *"Scripture says"* is too vague — the rule asks for the **speaker/writer named before the quote** (e.g. *Paul wrote to the Galatians*). Not satisfied.  
- **Broader problem:** a Boaz eyewitness on Ruth 2–4 quotes **zero Ruth KJV**. The lived gate scene has verbatim material (*"I cannot redeem it for myself, lest I mar mine own inheritance"*, *"Ye are witnesses this day"*) — none used. The witness recount feels paraphrased, not text-grounded.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal: **partly shown, mostly announced**

What **works:** the rhyme from nearer kinsman → Boaz → greater Kinsman; *"paid not silver but His own blood"*; *"the curse we owed fell on Him"* — substitution is sketched.

What **fails:**

> *"Long after, One came from David's line, born in my Bethlehem — a greater Kinsman than I."*

That is **asserted foresight**, not earned from what Boaz saw at the gate. He did not witness David’s line or Christ’s birth. There is **no signpost** that he speaks *by light of Scripture written long after* (the allowed reflective device). Without that frame, the turn reads as omniscient essay, not eyewitness discovery.

> *"marred where the nearer kinsman would not be"*

Clever echo of *"mar mine own inheritance"* — but in Ruth that “mar” is **economic/social**, not bodily. Risk of **wordplay substituting for typology walked from the gate**, especially with no Ruth quote anchoring it.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine: **mostly sound, one soft spot**

- Substitution named: blood, curse, Gal 3:13. ✓  
- Inclusion, not supersession: *"He takes the poor outsider for His bride."* ✓  
- Soft spot: *"gained Ruth as my bride — joy worth every coin"* leans **transactional/self-reward** — closer to “good bargain” than goel duty (raise Elimelech’s name, costly kindness). Not heretical, but it dulls the shadow Christ casts.

Gal 3:13’s second clause (*"for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree"*) is omitted — a zero-Bible listener may not grasp **what curse** or **how** Christ bore it beyond a label.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus: **pass, borderline thin**

> *"Let the Redeemer pay what you cannot. Come to Jesus."*

- Names **Jesus** ✓  
- Grace-anchored (*"the redeemed never pay"*) ✓  
- Response **named** (*come*) — not vague “trust Him” ✓  
- Still quite **bare** after a strong setup; the punch could land harder with one more Christ-specific line tied to the bride/outsider thread. Acceptable, not strong.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character: **mixed**

Beats 1–2 mostly sound like Boaz. Beat 3 breaks character:

> *"Long after, One came from David's line… Scripture says:"*

Omniscient historian + generic Scripture tag — not a Bethlehem landowner recounting **his** gate. First-person costume slips off.

---

### First-hearing clarity: **gaps**

- *"goel"* is explained — good.  
- *"his money poured into another's name"* — dense; a new listener may not follow why that mattered.  
- No Ruth dialogue means the **gate drama** is told in summary, not shown.  
- *"curse of the law"* arrives without setup for listeners who don’t know Torah curse language.

---

### Hook / spine: **hook strong, spine strained**

Opening image grips. **One witness, one thread** mostly holds (kinsman-redeemer → Christ). But the hook’s factual slip and the missing wrestling beat weaken the spine between hook and turn.

---

### SHORT form: **too lean**

~**198 spoken words** — under the **~220–320** band. No fat to cut, but **missing weight** where it matters: wrestling beat, one Ruth KJV line from the gate, clearer INV-11 signpost. The single moment doesn’t fully **land** because Beat 1 names the wrong scene and Beat 2 rushes to payment without interior cost.

---

VERDICT: REVISE

TOP FIXES:
1. **Add Boaz’s own wrestling in his voice** — one or two honest lines on inheritance/name/Moabitess cost *before* *"So I paid the full price at the gate"*; don’t let only the nearer kinsman carry the steel-man (EW-G8).
2. **Fix Beat 1 to the actual witnessed moment** — gate or threshing-floor redeem plea, not “knelt at my feet… buy back all she'd lost” as if that were the first encounter; anchor the short on Ruth 3–4, not a Ruth 2 gloss.
3. **Ground the turn in text + attribution** — tag Gal 3:13 as *Paul wrote* (or equivalent) before the quote; add at least one verbatim Ruth gate line; signpost that Boaz speaks *by light of Scripture long after his day* so Beat 3 isn’t bare omniscience (EW-INV-4 / EW-G7).
