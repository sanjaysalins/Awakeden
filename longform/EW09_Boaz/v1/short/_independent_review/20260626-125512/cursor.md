## Independent adversarial review — *The Kinsman-Redeemer* (eyewitness short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution: **REVISE**

**Quote words: PASS.**  
**"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."** matches Galatians 3:13 KJV exactly (legitimate truncation before the colon).

**Attribution: FAIL (narrow).** The lead-in is only **`Scripture says:`** — not Paul, not Galatians, not even “the apostle.” EW-INV-4 requires the speaker to be identified *before* the quote. A zero-Bible listener gets anonymous “Scripture,” not a routed `scripture` voice with a named source.

**The Ruth moment never gets KJV.** The load-bearing scene (Ruth at the gate, the nearer kinsman’s refusal) is all paraphrase. Ruth 4:6’s exact line — **`"I cannot redeem it for myself, lest I mar mine own inheritance"`** — is the hinge the turn later wordplays on, but it never appears in quotes. The witnessed act is never sealed by Scripture’s own words.

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man): **FAIL**

There is **no wrestling beat at all**. SHORT compresses the spine (hook → act → reveal → invitation), but EW-G8 is still a named gate — and this piece has **zero** internalized doubt in Boaz’s voice.

Beat 2 reports that the nearer kinsman **`walked — it would mar his inheritance`**, but that is **his** objection, not Boaz’s steel-man. Boaz never voices the hardest honest question this story demands, e.g.:

- Why should a **Moabitess** have any claim on Israel’s inheritance?
- Why bind **my** house and name to a dead man’s debt when the law lets a nearer man refuse?
- Is mercy to the outsider a breach of the law, or its point?

The locked long-form Boaz *does* wrestle this (`"So which was it — was kindness to the outsider a breach of the law..."`). The short cut it entirely. Wonder-free exposition is not wrestling; this matches the same thin pattern flagged on Isaiah/Passover/Noah shorts.

### EW-G9 — Doctrine / grounding: **REVISE (direction OK, grounding weak + hook error)**

**Substitution is named, not fully landed.** **`the curse we owed fell on Him`** + Gal 3:13 names penalty and bearer — good. No supersession; **`He takes the poor outsider for His bride`** reads as inclusion typology, not replacement. Direction is sound.

**But the opening misstates Ruth.** Beat 1:

> **`A penniless foreign widow knelt at my feet and asked me to buy back all she'd lost`**

That is **not what Ruth 2–4 records.**

- Ruth **fell on her face** in the field (Ruth 2:10) and asked **`"Why have I found grace in thine eyes... seeing I am a stranger?"`** — gratitude, not a redemption plea.
- The goel request came **at the threshing floor** (Ruth 3:9): **`"spread therefore thy skirt over thine handmaid; for thou art a near kinsman."`**
- Land redemption happened **at the gate** (Ruth 4), with Naomi selling the parcel — not a widow kneeling at Boaz’s feet asking him to “buy back all she’d lost.”

This is not poetic compression; it **invents a scene** the witness did not live. For an eyewitness format whose hazard is fabricated history (EW-INV-11), that is a real defect.

**Economics slightly mis-told.** **`gained Ruth as my bride — joy worth every coin`** frames Boaz as a happy purchaser. Ruth’s law point is the opposite: redemption **cost the redeemer** and **did not enlarge his estate** — it raised **another man’s name**. The long form gets this right (`"gained no profit — nothing but her"`). The short flattens the type’s cost logic.

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned: **REVISE / weak**

Some machinery is genuinely shown:

- Nearer kinsman refuses **`lest... mar his inheritance`** → Christ **`marred where the nearer kinsman would not be`**
- Outsider bride → **`He takes the poor outsider for His bride`**
- Silver at the gate → **`paid not silver but His own blood`**

That parallel work is real.

But the **curse-of-the-law** turn is mostly **imported**, not walked from what Boaz lived. Ruth is about **land, name, levirate redemption, and a foreign widow grafted in** — not “curse of the law” on the page. Beat 3 jumps to **`the curse we owed fell on Him`** and then **asserts** the link via **`Scripture says:`** + Gal 3:13. The Gal tie is legitimate typology (and in the episode’s `passage.txt`), but the short **announces** it rather than **showing** the chain: debt you cannot pay → redeemer steps in at the gate → penalty falls on him instead of you. A zero-Bible listener hears “curse” with no Ruth-grounded explanation of *what* was owed.

**`marred where the nearer kinsman would not be`** is clever wordplay on Ruth 4:6’s **“mar mine own inheritance,”** but opaque on first hearing — “marred” is not Ruth’s word for Christ; it smuggles Isaiah 52:14 energy in through a pun. Earned for insiders; muddy for the stated audience.

**`Long after, One came from David's line`** is an acceptable signposted reflective device (EW-INV-11), not the banned **“at last I understood”** stinger — but it still leans on **time-skip assertion** more than demonstration.

### EW-G10 — Voice / character: **REVISE**

Boaz never names himself in the spoken text (only in the header). Beat 2 especially reads like a **Bible-study handout**, not a man remembering his gate:

> **`In our law the goel — the near kinsman — could redeem a dead man's land and take his widow to raise his name.`**

That is third-person legal exposition, not **`I sat down in the dust and called ten elders`** (the long form’s lived voice). Sprinkled **`I paid`** / **`my bride`** are not enough; the essay-narrator-in-costume pattern the gates are meant to catch.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus: **PASS (narrow)**

**`Come to Jesus.`** — names Jesus, names the response (**Come**), not the banned bare **“will you trust Him?”** template.

**`the redeemed never pay. Let the Redeemer pay what you cannot.`** — grace-anchored, no fear/gain-loss/manufactured pressure. **`So if you feel poor, far off, a stranger`** ties listener to Ruth without crass self-interest. Acceptable.

- **`goel`** is glossed — good.

### Hook / spine / SHORT form: **REVISE**

**Hook:** **`knelt at my feet and asked me to buy back all she'd lost`** — wrong scene, weak grip for anyone who knows Ruth; misleading for anyone who doesn’t.

**Spine:** ONE witness, ONE thread (goel → Christ) — holds. No mid-piece swap.

**Tightness:** Spoken body ≈ **200 words** — **below EW-INV-8’s 220–320 floor**. Pipeline meta for this short shows **~84s** natural runtime (~82s + pauses), not a ~90s piece. Too thin to carry wrestling + earned reveal; what remains is compressed typology, not tight witness testimony.

**Single moment:** Crowded across mis-hook + law lecture + gate + Christ turn; the **gate** should be the one moment — the hook should open *there*, not on a fabricated kneeling plea.

2. **Add real steel-man wrestling in Boaz’s voice** — Moab/inheritance/law cost vs mercy to the outsider — then let Beat 3’s Christ turn *answer that doubt*, not only the nearer kinsman’s arithmetic.
