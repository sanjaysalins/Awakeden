## Independent adversarial review — Boaz eyewitness (short)

### EW-G8 — Wrestling missing (FAIL-level)

There is **no wrestling beat** and **no doubt in Boaz’s own voice**. Beat 3 jumps from setup to payment:

- *"He weighed it and walked"* → *"I weighed the same, and paid it at the gate"*

The strongest objection lives only in the nearer kinsman’s mouth (*"I cannot redeem it for myself, lest I mar mine own inheritance"*). That is **external**, not **internalized**. Per EW-G8, Boaz must voice the same steel-man cost — inheritance thinned, Moabitess, raising another man’s line — and **wrestle** before he pays. Here he has no hesitation, no question, no “why should I?” Beat headers jump 1 → 3 → 6 → 7; wrestling is absent entirely.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal announced, not earned

The Christ turn is **signposted and asserted**, not drawn from the moment Boaz lived:

- *"I tell you now by the light of the word written long after me"* — meta frame; the witness steps out of the scene into later theology.
- *"From the son Ruth bore me came David's line, and a greater Kinsman who paid the cost no nearer kinsman would"* — genealogy + Christ link **told**, not **shown** from gate/redemption imagery the listener just heard.
- *"He took our flesh, marred where that kinsman would not"* — parallel is poetic, but “marred” does not match what the nearer kinsman feared (inheritance loss, not flesh). The turn leans on Gal 3:13 dropped in, not on the bride/redemption act just narrated.

The type→Christ bridge is thin because the **marriage half** of Boaz’s redemption (Ruth 4:10 — *“purchased to be my wife”*) never appears in Beat 3. The bride thread is only asserted at the end: *"And He takes the redeemed outsider for His bride."*

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution (mixed)

**Verbatim substrings checked** — the quoted fragments match KJV:

- Ruth 3:9 skirt line ✓  
- Ruth 4:6 inheritance line ✓ (truncated but exact substring)  
- Ruth 4:9 land-purchase line ✓  
- Gal 3:13 curse line ✓  

**Attribution weaknesses:**

- Ruth: *"Ruth asked it of me:"* — speaker implied, not named in KJV attribution form (*“Ruth said unto me”* / `**[Ruth]**`).
- Nearer kinsman: *"He weighed it and walked:"* — no *“the kinsman said”* before the quote.
- Boaz at gate: *"I weighed the same, and paid it at the gate:"* — does not say he spoke **to the elders and all the people** (Ruth 4:9 framing).
- Galatians: *"Scripture says:"* — no named speaker (Paul / the apostle); also breaks Boaz’s voice (see EW-G10).

**Doctrinal/story gap:** gate quote stops at land (*"Ye are witnesses this day, that I have bought all that was Elimelech’s"*) and omits the wife-purchase half that makes the kinsman-redeemer type land.

---

### SHORT form — two moments, one thread crowded

Beat 1 anchors **the gate** (*"At the gate, a poor widow's whole future hung on one man's word"*). Beat 3 then runs **Ruth 3** (skirt / threshing-floor request) **and** **Ruth 4** (gate redemption) in one beat. That is **two chapters, two scenes** — not a single witnessed moment.

Timeline wobble: gate in Beat 1 → law lecture → night request → gate payment. A zero-Bible listener will not know **when** or **where** they are.

---

### First-hearing clarity — broken callback

Beat 7: *"If you feel like that woman in my field — poor and far off"* — **the field was never narrated**. Ruth 2 gleaning is not in the piece. Listener gets a CTA image with no setup. Beat 1 only gives *"a poor widow"* (unnamed); Ruth is named only in Beat 3.

*"Moabitess"* is unexplained for a cold listener (foreigner? enemy? why that matters?).

---

### EW-G10 — Voice slips out of character

Boaz would not say:

- *"I tell you now by the light of the word written long after me"*
- *"Scripture says:"* (then Galatians)
- *"He is Jesus, the Christ"* — fine for reveal, but paired with the lines above it reads as **essay narrator in a Boaz costume**, not a gate elder recounting what he lived.

Stronger lines (*"I will not hide the cost"*, *"I am only the shadow"*) show character; the reveal block undercuts them.

---

### Hook — weak grip

*"I am Boaz of Bethlehem, the richest man in town"* — ID card, not tension. Compare metadata *"mighty man of wealth"* (Ruth 2:1): *"richest man in town"* inflates without adding hook. No cost, no stakes, no image in line one.

---

### EW-INV-2 / CTA — mostly passes (minor flags)

**Passes:** names Jesus (*"Come to Jesus, the true Kinsman-Redeemer"*); response named (*"Come"*); grace-anchored (*"The redeemed never pay"*, *"made a curse so you need never be"*); substitution named (*"His own blood"*, curse).

**Minor:** *"So be still a moment"* — slight manufactured pause. *"Will you let the Redeemer pay what you cannot?"* — close to a pay/trust template, but specific enough to survive.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine mostly sound (one stretch)

Substitution is named (curse of the law, blood, made a curse). No supersession. Kinsman-redeemer → Christ is standard.

**Stretch:** *"marred where that kinsman would not"* maps inheritance-fear onto incarnation/cross without grounding in what Boaz actually saw. Not heresy, but the parallel is **asserted**, not text-earned.

---

### EW-G6 (production) — no cast tags

No `**[Ruth]**` / `**[the kinsman]**` / `**[scripture]**` speaker tags on quote lines. Spec expects multi-voice cast with scripture voice on every KJV quote. All quotes are buried in witness prose — weak for production and attribution clarity.

---

### Word budget

~300 spoken words — inside 220–320. Length is fine; **density** is the problem (two scenes + no wrestling).
