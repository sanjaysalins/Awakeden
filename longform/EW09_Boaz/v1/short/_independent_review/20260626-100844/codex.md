# Independent review — codex (OK, 61s)

**Findings**

- **EW-INV-4:** `Scripture says: **"Christ hath redeemed us..."**` is too vague for the attribution rule. Attribute it directly, e.g. Paul/Galatians, before quoting.
- **EW-INV-4:** `**"Ye are witnesses this day, that I have bought all that was Elimelech’s."**` is KJV wording but presented as a complete sentence while truncating Ruth 4:9. That is risky in a verbatim standard; use an ellipsis or quote the fuller clause.
- **EW-G8:** The wrestling is weak. `I will not hide the cost` and `I weighed the same, and paid it` describe cost, but Boaz never actually wrestles with the strongest objection in his own voice.
- **EW-G9:** `the richest man in town` is an invented overstatement. KJV gives “a mighty man of wealth,” not necessarily the richest.
- **EW-G9:** `his name and fields left to no one` is inaccurate/overcompressed. Naomi is alive and Ruth 4:3 says Naomi is selling the parcel.
- **EW-G9:** `buys back the poor foreign widow` is legally/theologically sloppy. Boaz redeems land and takes Ruth as wife; he does not “buy back” Ruth as property.
- **EW-G7:** `He took our flesh, marred where that kinsman would not` leans on a clever “mar” echo, but Ruth’s “mar mine own inheritance” is not about bodily marring. This risks sounding like wordplay rather than an earned type.
- **CTA:** `Will you let the Redeemer pay what you cannot?` is the weakest line in the close. Christ has paid; the response should be come/receive/believe, not “let Him pay.”
- **Voice:** `I tell you now by the light of the word written long after me` sounds like an essay narrator stepping out of costume. It explains the device instead of staying in Boaz’s lived witness.
- **Hook:** `I am Boaz of Bethlehem, the richest man in town` is flat and partially invented. The stronger hook is already later: `a poor widow's whole future hung on one man's word`.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Give Boaz a real internal wrestle with the cost/shame/risk instead of merely stating he paid it.
2. Clean up biblical precision: “richest,” “left to no one,” “buys back the widow,” and the “marred” wordplay.
3. Strengthen the close so it names the response as coming/receiving/believing in Jesus, not “letting Him pay.”
