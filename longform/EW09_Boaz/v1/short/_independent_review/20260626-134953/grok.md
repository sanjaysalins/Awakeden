# Independent review — grok (OK, 48s)

## Findings (adversarial)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution
- The quoted clause is **KJV-exact** (Gal. 3:13): **"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."**
- Attribution is thin: **"Scripture says:"** names no speaker. A first-century Boaz voice would not naturally cite a Pauline epistle as undifferentiated “Scripture” with no “Paul later wrote” / “the apostle” frame. That strains both attribution and witness plausibility.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus
- Names Jesus and names the response: **"Come to Jesus."** Grace-anchored: **"the redeemed never pay"** / **"Let the Redeemer pay what you cannot."**
- Still borderline template: **"Come to Jesus."** is a bare two-word close after a direct-audience pivot. The invitation is named (“come”), but it does little work after the typology leap.

### EW-G7 — Reveal must be earned from what the witness lived
- The Ruth→Christ bridge is mostly **asserted**, not walked out from the gate scene Boaz actually lived.
- **"Long after, One came from David's line, born in my Bethlehem — a greater Kinsman than I."** — announced typology; Boaz did not witness David’s line or Bethlehem’s later birth in Ruth 2–4.
- **"marred where the nearer kinsman would not be"** — pun, not proof. In the lived scene the nearer kinsman feared **"it would mar his inheritance"** (financial/legal harm). Christ being physically marred is a different sense of the word, imported from elsewhere—not shown from Boaz’s transaction.
- **"the curse we owed fell on Him"** + the Galatians quote bolt on **curse-of-the-law** theology that the Ruth redemption Boaz describes never mentions. The turn lands on Paul, not on what Boaz paid at the gate.

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (major gap)
- **No honest doubt in Boaz’s voice.** No beat where Boaz questions cost, Moabite outsider status, law, shame, or whether redemption is worth it.
- The nearer kinsman’s refusal (**"he walked — it would mar his inheritance"**) is backstory, not Boaz’s steel-man struggle. Boaz moves straight from law-exposition to **"So I paid the full price at the gate"** — decisive, unscathed, unpersuasive as “wrestling.”

### EW-G9 — Doctrine
- Substitution is named (**"paid not silver but His own blood"**, **"being made a curse for us"**). No obvious supersession.
- Hook/witness fidelity problem: **"though it would cost me my own inheritance"** misassigns the nearer kinsman’s objection to Boaz. In Ruth 4 the nearer redeemer fears **"lest I mar mine own inheritance"**; Boaz pays willingly. The witness misstates his own stake.
- **"He takes the poor outsider for His bride"** is sound bride/church typology, but **"the redeemed never pay"** can sound like passive receipt with no call to faith—weak for evangelical clarity.

### EW-G10 — Voice / character
- Strong opening first person: **"A penniless foreign widow came to me"**, **"So I paid the full price at the gate."**
- Beat 3 slips into essay voice: **"Scripture says:"** + theological summary, not a man remembering threshing floor and gate.
- Beat 4 breaks character entirely: **"So if you feel poor, far off, a stranger"** — modern second-person pastoral address, not Boaz still witnessing.

### First-hearing clarity
- **"goel — the near kinsman"** — helpful.
- **"Elimelech's, my kinsman, dead in Moab"** — assumes backstory a novice won’t have.
- **"David's line"**, **"curse of the law"**, **"being made a curse for us"** — land without explanation for a zero-Bible listener. The single moment (gate redemption) gets crowded out by unexplained NT legal language.

### Hook / spine / short form
- Hook grips; one witness, one thread (kinsman-redeemer) holds.
- **~200 spoken words** — under the **~220–320** band; thin for ~90s, especially with **no wrestling beat**.
- Beat 2 does a lot of law + gate + bride in one pass; Beat 3 jumps to Christ + Galatians. The **single moment** (Boaz redeems at the gate) is told, but the **Christ reveal** is not earned from that moment—it’s a second theological move.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add a real EW-G8 wrestling beat in Boaz’s voice (cost, foreign widow, inheritance risk, “why should I pay for another man’s name?”) before **"So I paid the full price at the gate."**
2. Earn the Christ turn from the gate transaction Boaz lived—silver/price, nearer redeemer’s refusal, outsider bride—before Gal. 3:13; fix the hook so **"cost me my own inheritance"** matches Boaz’s actual stake, not the nearer kinsman’s fear.
3. Restore witness voice through the close: cut **"So if you feel poor, far off, a stranger"** sermon pivot; attribute the quote (**Paul** / **the apostle**) and tie **"curse"** to what the goel scene actually cost, or drop the pun on **"marred"**.
