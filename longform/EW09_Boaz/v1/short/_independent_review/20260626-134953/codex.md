# Independent review — codex (OK, 34s)

**Findings**

- **EW-INV-4 failure:** `Scripture says: **"Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us."**` is not clean KJV verbatim because Galatians 3:13 has a colon after `us`, not a period. Attribution is also too vague: `Scripture says` does not name Paul or Galatians 3:13 before quoting.

- **EW-G8 failure:** There is no real wrestling. `One stood nearer than I... he walked` gives the nearer kinsman’s objection, but Boaz never internalizes the strongest objection in his own voice. The piece never makes Boaz ask, “Should I spend my name, estate, and future for a Moabite widow?” Without that, the witness has no steel-man doubt.

- **EW-G7 partial failure:** The reveal is mostly asserted. `Long after, One came from David's line... a greater Kinsman than I` announces the type-to-Christ turn, but does not sufficiently show how Boaz’s lived moment points to Christ. `marred where the nearer kinsman would not be` is strong, but it needs to be anchored more visibly in the gate scene and inheritance risk.

- **Possible textual overstatement:** `A penniless foreign widow came to me and asked me to buy back all she'd lost` compresses Ruth 3–4 in a way that risks inaccuracy. Ruth asked Boaz to act as kinsman-redeemer; Boaz handled the land transaction at the gate. She did not directly ask him to “buy back all she'd lost.”

- **Doctrine mostly sound but thin:** `the curse we owed fell on Him` names substitution, and `paid not silver but His own blood` is faithful. But `He takes the poor outsider for His bride` risks sounding sentimental unless tied clearly to grace and union with Christ rather than mere outsider-status appeal.

- **CTA mostly acceptable but slightly generic:** `Come to Jesus` does name the response and names Jesus. `the redeemed never pay` is grace-anchored. But `if you feel poor, far off, a stranger` leans subjective; the invitation should be grounded more in Christ’s finished redeeming work than in the listener’s felt identity.

- **Short-form issue:** This is likely under the target range. It feels closer to a compressed summary than a 90-second narration. The missing wrestling is probably why it feels thin.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add Boaz’s real first-person wrestling over inheritance, cost, Ruth’s Moabite status, and raising another man’s name.
2. Fix the KJV quote punctuation and attribute it specifically before quoting.
3. Earn the Christ reveal from the gate transaction instead of mostly announcing “a greater Kinsman.”
