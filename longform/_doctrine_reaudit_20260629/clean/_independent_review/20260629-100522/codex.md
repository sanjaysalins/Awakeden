# Independent review — codex (OK, 25s)

**Findings**

- **EW-INV-4 attribution failure:** `**"and the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all."**` appears to be exact KJV from Isaiah 53:6, but it is not attributed before quoting. The line before it says `He was the price paid...`, not “Isaiah said...” or similar.

- **Witness identity is too vague:** `Once a year I walked behind a curtain...` implies the high priest, but he is never named. “Named biblical witness” is part of the form, and this gives us an office, not a witness.

- **Reveal partly asserted, not fully earned:** `No single creature could hold both halves` is rhetorically neat, but the text has not shown why “price paid” and “guilt carried away” must converge in Christ. The turn leans on explanation: `I know now... did what my two goats only pictured`.

- **Doctrine is close but substitution is under-named:** `He was the price paid, and the One the guilt was laid on` names guilt, but not clearly the penalty/wrath/judgment Christ bore in the sinner’s place. `price paid` can sound transactional without stating substitution.

- **CTA is too bare and imperative-only:** `Walk in.` names an action, but not clearly faith, coming to Christ, repentance, or receiving grace. It names Jesus nearby, but the invitation is vague and could sound like access by boldness rather than grace through Christ.

- **Possible clarity issue for first-hearing listeners:** `inside the veil`, `the holiest place`, `sat down`, and `curtain... tore in two` are powerful for Bible-literate hearers, but under-explained for zero Bible knowledge. The basic stakes of the curtain need one clearer phrase.

- **Wrestling is present but light:** `For years I asked why it took two` is a real question, but not the strongest internal objection. The deeper terror is already in the hook: `I was never sure I would walk back out`; the piece does not press that into the theological crisis.

- **Strong hook, but some repetition crowds the short form:** `The curtain I trembled behind` appears twice. In a short piece, that repeated phrase costs space that could clarify substitution or sharpen the CTA.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Attribute the Isaiah 53:6 KJV quote before quoting it.
2. Name the witness and strengthen the first-person wrestling from his actual fear before God.
3. Make the close explicitly invite faith/coming to Jesus by grace, with substitution clearly named.
