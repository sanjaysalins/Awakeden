# Independent review — codex (OK, 80s)

Findings:

1. **EW-INV-4 attribution breach:** `**"a man plucked off his shoe, and gave it to his neighbour"**` is KJV wording, but it is narrator text, not Boaz’s speech, and it is introduced only as `by the old custom`. Attribute it before quoting: “as the Scripture says…”

2. **EW-INV-4 attribution is too generic:** `as the Scripture says: **"Christ hath redeemed us..."**` should name Paul/Galatians before the quote, especially since the next quote says `for Paul wrote:`. The rule asks for attribution before quoting.

3. **Textual overstatement:** `First I gave her my field` is not what happened. Boaz let Ruth glean in his field and protected/provided for her; he did not give her the field. This matters because the eyewitness action gets inflated before the actual redemption scene.

4. **Possible doctrinal/textual wobble:** `was kindness to the outsider a breach of the law, or the very heart the law had been pointing at all along?` is a strong wrestling line, but it risks making Torah sound internally opposed to mercy. Ruth’s inclusion should be shown through covenant faith, widow/sojourner protection, and kinsman redemption, not by implying Boaz had to transcend “the cold letter.”

5. **Reveal mostly works, but one leap is announced:** `He is the true Kinsman-Redeemer, and He did what I only acted out in a barley field` is asserted before the textual parallels fully carry it. The later mapping is good: kinship, price, curse, bride. Move some of that evidence before the label lands.

6. **CTA is close, but the response is still a little passive:** `will you let the Redeemer pay what you cannot, and be His own? ... Be redeemed.` “Come to Jesus” helps, but “let” and “be redeemed” are vague/passive. Name the response more plainly: come, receive, trust, turn.

7. **Long-form sag risk:** Beat 4 and Beat 5 repeat the same cost logic: `Redemption was not free — it cost the redeemer everything`, `I spent myself and gained no profit`, `Redemption is costly`, `let mercy be someone else's risk`, `my heart would not count the cost.` These are all good, but too clustered. Beat 5 needs a sharper new turn beyond restating the nearer kinsman’s arithmetic.

8. **Voice is strong but occasionally essay-like:** `That is the substitution my barley field could never show you` sounds like a doctrinal lecturer more than Boaz. The doctrine is right, but the witness voice thins there.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix quote attribution, especially the shoe-custom narrator quote and the Galatians quote.
2. Correct the textual overstatement “I gave her my field” and sharpen the law/Ruth tension without making mercy oppose Torah.
3. Make the reveal and CTA less announced/passive: earn “true Kinsman-Redeemer” through the parallels, then invite the listener to come/receive/trust Jesus explicitly.
