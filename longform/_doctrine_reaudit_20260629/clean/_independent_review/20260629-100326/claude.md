## The strongest finding — a real narrative-fact error (Beat 1)
> "ten travel-worn men came begging me for grain… And when I could hold it in no longer… I said the words… **'I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt.'**"

This **telescopes two different visits into one**. The "ten men" is the **first** trip (Gen 42 — Benjamin stayed home). But the reveal where Joseph says "I am Joseph your brother" (Gen 45:1–4) happens on the **second** trip, with **Benjamin present** — **eleven** brothers in the room, not ten. The narration presents it as one continuous scene ("on that day… and when I could hold it in no longer"), which erases Benjamin — the one brother who did *not* sell him — from the moment Joseph weeps and reveals himself. A first-hearing listener walks away believing the reveal was to "the ten who sold me." It wasn't. This is exactly the verbatim-clean-but-factually-loose trap the EW01 Leviticus-10 lesson flagged.

## Doctrinal-precision slip (Beat 6)
> "He turned and **saved the very ones who sold and slew Him**."

Joseph genuinely saved *all* his brothers — but the Christ side overshoots. Judas (the "sold" parallel the narration itself draws) was **not** saved; the crucifiers were *offered* pardon and some received it at Pentecost, but "saved the very ones who slew Him" asserts applied, universal salvation. The surrounding framing ("stands with arms thrown wide," "rose holding pardon for the hands that nailed Him," "holds out bread, not a sword") is grace *offered* — which is right. Tighten the one overstated verb so it reads as pardon held out, not universally applied.

- **Substitution (EW-G9):** present — "dying in the place of the sinners who hated Him," "the price of our pardon," Rom 5:8. Could name the *penalty we owed* one notch more explicitly, but it's there.
- **Voice (EW-G10):** consistent, characterful first-person throughout. No essay-narrator leakage. No supersession (Acts 5:31's "repentance to Israel" actually affirms Israel).
- **Length:** ~1,300 words, no sagging beat; the "bread not sword / I could not, He can" refrain is deliberate, not padding.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the Beat 1 telescoping: the reveal ("I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold") happened with **Benjamin present — eleven brothers**, not the ten of the first visit. Reframe so the innocent youngest isn't erased (e.g. distinguish "the ten who sold me" who now bowed from the full company at the reveal).
2. Soften "saved the very ones who sold and slew Him" — Judas was not saved; make it pardon *held out to* / offered, matching the grace-offered framing the rest of the beat already uses.
3. Optional sharpening: name the penalty explicitly in the substitution line (what *we* owed that He bore), so EW-G9 lands with full weight rather than by implication.
