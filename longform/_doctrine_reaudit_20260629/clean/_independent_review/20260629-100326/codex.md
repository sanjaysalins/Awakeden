# Independent review — codex (OK, 72s)

Findings:

- **EW-INV-4 attribution failures:** Several KJV quotes are verbatim, but not attributed before quoting. Examples: **"Now Israel loved Joseph more than all his children..."**, **"And when his brethren saw..."**, **"And they took him, and cast him into a pit..."**, **"But the LORD was with Joseph..."**, **"He came unto his own..."**, and **"But God commendeth his love toward us..."**. “That same word says” is not attribution. Name the biblical narrator/book/author or speaker before the quote.

- **EW-INV-4 mixed quote attribution:** **"And his brethren said to him, Shalt thou indeed reign over us?... And they hated him yet the more..."** combines speech and narration, but the setup treats it as simply “what the brothers said.” The quote itself is KJV, but the attribution is muddy because the final sentence is narrator, not the brothers.

- **EW-INV-4 weak contextual framing:** **"Fear not: for am I in the place of God?"** is introduced as “the question my life kept asking,” but in Genesis 50:19 it is Joseph’s direct answer to his brothers after Jacob’s death. That framing turns a specific spoken reassurance into a generalized thematic slogan.

- **EW-G9 substitution is under-specified:** The piece says, **"dying in the place of the sinners who hated Him"** and **"He carried your guilt... into death itself"**, but it does not clearly name the penalty Christ bore as judgment/curse/wrath/death due for sin. The substitution is present, but the doctrinal statement is not sharp enough for the stated lens.

- **EW-G7 reveal is partly earned, then becomes asserted sermon:** The Joseph-to-Christ turn is strongest around **"I saved bodies from a famine of bread..."**, but then slides into announcement: **"my whole life had been a shadow thrown ahead of Someone greater"** and **"the very shape of my life was the shadow of His."** The parallels are there, but “shadow/substance” is asserted in abstract terms instead of being consistently shown through Joseph’s lived details.

- **EW-G10 voice break:** **"by a light given long after me, a word written after I was laid in my grave"** sounds like an explanatory device from the author, not Joseph’s own voice. Same with **"So the word written long after me says of Him:"**. The witness becomes a Bible-study narrator wearing Joseph’s costume.

- **First-hearing clarity gap:** Phrases like **"His own received him not"**, **"right hand of heaven"**, **"repentance to Israel"**, and **"shadow... substance"** assume Bible literacy. A zero-knowledge listener can follow the Joseph story, but the Christological reveal uses insider shorthand.

- **Wrestling has one overreach:** **"a faith I had to hold long before I was given any reason for it"** conflicts with the earlier claim that **"God gave me dreams"**. Joseph had a reason, though not an explanation. Better distinction: he had a promise, but not the path.

- **Long-form sag/repetition:** The guilt/bread contrast repeats without much new turn: **"I could keep my brothers breathing, but I could not lift the guilt off a single soul"**, then **"I could feed them, but I could not lift the guilt off one soul. I could not. He can."** Also **"bread, not a sword"** appears in both the palace scene and final CTA. The repetition dulls the close.

- **CTA mostly meets the lens but could be stronger:** **"Trust Jesus..."** names Jesus and the response, and it is grace-anchored. But the invitation leans heavily on image: **"arms thrown wide"**, **"holds out bread, not a sword"**. It should more plainly invite the listener to come to Christ / receive pardon / trust His death and resurrection.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix all KJV attribution issues before quoted Scripture.
2. Sharpen substitution: name the penalty for sin and what Christ bore.
3. Keep Joseph’s voice through the reveal; show the Christ turn through lived details instead of abstract “shadow/substance” exposition.
