## EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim (inside `**"..."**`):** The quoted words themselves are clean substrings of the binding passage — Gen 6:13, Heb 11:7 (clause), Gen 6:14, Gen 6:16, Gen 7:1, Gen 7:16, Gen 7:23, John 10:9, 1 Pet 3:20–21. No doctored words inside the marks.

**Attribution: fails the spec’s “speaker before quoting” bar repeatedly.**

| Quote | Problem |
|-------|---------|
| **`"Make thee an ark of gopher wood"`** (Beat 3) | Divine command from Gen 6:14; no **`[the LORD]`** / **`[God]`** before the span. |
| **`"the door of the ark shalt thou set in the side thereof"`** (Beat 3) | Same — load-bearing hinge line, untagged. |
| **`"and the LORD shut him in."`** (Beat 3) | Setup is only *“a thing happened that I did not do”* — not Moses, not Scripture, not **`[the LORD]`** before the quote. Speaker is inside the quote, not routed. |
| **`"By faith Noah… moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house"`** (Beat 2) | Prose frame *“men who knew the whole story wrote down”* — no book/speaker name (Hebrews), no scripture-voice tag. |
| **`"And every living substance was destroyed…"`** (Beat 5) | *“The record sets it down without flinching”* — anonymous; Genesis/Moses never named. |
| **`"while the ark was a preparing…"`** / **`"The like figure whereunto even baptism…"`** (Beat 6) | *“The men who walked with Him wrote it down”* — Peter / 1 Peter never named before either block. |

Only three lines are properly cast-tagged: Gen 6:13 and 7:1 **`[the LORD]`**, John 10:9 **`[Jesus]`**. Sibling EW pieces were sent to REVISE for this same pattern; it is not cosmetic.

**Label mismatch:** Beat 2 tags Gen 6:13 **`[the LORD]`**, but the verse opens **“And God said unto Noah”** — content is God’s speech, label doesn’t match the verse’s divine name.

**First-hearing hazard on Gen 7:16:** Noah speaks as **“I”** while the quoted line says **“shut *him* in.”** A zero-Bible listener gets third-person narration dropped in Noah’s mouth with no gloss (*Moses wrote… about me*).

## EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**What works:** Cross-time device is signposted — **`"by the light that came long after I was laid in the ground"`** — not the banned *“at last I understood”* stinger. Beats 3–4 genuinely plant **one door** + **I did not shut it** + **the LORD shut me in** before John 10:9. v1.1 keeps refuge and substitution distinct: **`"The flood… did not strike my ark; it bore me safe above the judgment. But the wrath… fell on Him, not the wood"`** — better than collapsing ark and cross.

**What still fails:**

1. **Beat 6 opens by announcing:** **`"I see it now"`** — then 1 Peter does most of the Christ-turn work before the lived scene has carried it.

2. **Metaphor stack before any one image lands:** **`"He is the door I cut… And the ark was Him"`** — Christ is simultaneously door, vessel, and refuge. A first-hearing listener loses the map.

3. **Substitution is imported, not shown from the flood:** Beats 3–5 earn *inside vs outside* and *His seal, not mine*; they do **not** earn **`"the just for the unjust"`** or wrath falling on Christ. That move arrives from later-written word (*1 Peter*, un-attributed) and Noah’s sermon voice, not from what he witnessed in the ark.

4. **Missed free textual payoff:** `passage.txt` includes Gen 9:13 **`"I do set my bow in the cloud"`** — Beat 7 paraphrases **`"God hung His bow over a washed world"`** without quoting it, and fuses rainbow-covenant with door metaphor in one breath. A verbatim mercy beat is sitting in the passage unused while the close feels preached.

## EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**What improved:** Beat 5 names a real objection — **`"how can it be right that a whole world should drown at all?"`** — not only survivor guilt, and refuses a cheap tidy answer: **`"I had no tidy answer then, and will not invent one now."**

**What still fails:**

1. **The steel-man is asked, then softened.** After the theodicy question, the piece pivots to **`"I belonged among the guilty"`** and **`"mercy had cut a single door"`** — grace-for-Noah, not a sustained engagement with *global* drowning (infants, scope of “all flesh,” why judgment had to look like this). **`"judged a real and willing evil, not an innocent one"`** does not carry the weight of the question Noah just asked.

2. **Stronger in-text tension is quoted then dropped.** Beat 3 quotes **`"for thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation"`** while Beat 2 insists **`"I was no better clay than any of them."`** That is Noah’s sharpest internal clash — *Why did God call me righteous and shut me in?* Beat 5 never wrestles that line; it routes through theodicy + inside/outside instead.

3. **Hearability clash:** Beat 2 quotes Hebrews **`"moved with fear, prepared an ark"`** while Beat 5 says **`"not because I feared the ark would fail."`** Different senses of “fear,” but a zero-Bible listener may hear contradiction with no one-line bridge.

4. **Asserted sensory detail not in the binding text:** **`"I could hear the world dying on the other side of those walls"`** — Genesis gives shut-in and total destruction; it does not give Noah hearing the drowned. Presented as lived memory in the wrestling beat, not signposted as interpretation (EW-INV-11 / EW-G10 friction).

## EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Sound core:** Substitution is named on Christ — **`"the wrath that my sin had earned did not pass Him by — it fell on Him… the just for the unjust"`**; Noah as passenger, not savior — **`"Do not come to me. I was only a passenger"`** / **`"I could not close my own door"`**; 1 Pet 3:21 includes the resurrection / good-conscience qualifier and prose afterward steers away from baptismal regeneration. No supersession detected.

**Friction:**

- **`"the just for the unjust"`** appears twice (Beats 6–7) as Noah’s own words with no Scripture frame — a zero-Bible listener hears a formula, not Peter’s witness.
- Beat 7 **`"God hung His bow… holding the door open to you now"`** merges Gen 9 covenant with John 10 door imagery without quoting either at the close — typologically loose and less text-grounded than it could be.

## EW-G10 — Voice / character

Mostly strong Noah — **`"belief in those days looked like a hammer in my hand"`**, **`"the people came to gawk at the madman and his boat"`**, **`"I was no better clay than any of them"`**.

**Slips:**

- Beat 6 sermon blocks: **`"And here is the deepest thing of all"`**, **`"And remember this, for it is the heart of all my comfort"`** — essay-preacher, not an old carpenter at the door.
- **Posthumous frame used before it’s justified:** Beat 2 already quotes Hebrews about Noah *before* Beat 6 explains **`"long after I was laid in the ground."`** Beat 1 **`"I am the only man still standing when the laughing stopped"`** reads as a living old man; the grave vantage arrives late and jars.
- Beat 3 **`"a thing happened that I did not do"`** → third-person **`"shut him in"`** breaks first-person witness contract for hearers.

(v1.1 correctly removed the invented *“hand not mine pull it shut”* threshold image — that fix stands.)

## Hook, spine, first-hearing clarity, long-form runtime

**Hook:** Grips — **`"one door"`** + **`"the LORD shut me in — the one thing I never did with my own hands"`** — now text-grounded.

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (one door → Christ the door). No swap.

**First-hearing weak spots:** Untagged quotes; **`"him"`** vs **`"I"`** on Gen 7:16; stacked ark=Christ + door=Christ; fear-language clash; theodicy and righteousness questions raised but not fully carried.

**Long-form earn:** Beats 1–3 earn setup; Beat 5 adds real wrestling. **Sag in the back half:** Beat 4 restates Beat 3’s *I did not shut the door*; Beat 7 near-duplicates Beat 6’s refuge-vs-substitution block:

- Beat 6: **`"The ark did not bear the wrath — it bore me through the judgment. But Jesus bore the wrath itself… the just for the unjust"`**
- Beat 7: **`"The ark did not bear the wrath — it bore me through the judgment. But Jesus bore the wrath itself… the just for the unjust"`**

Same turn, ~two paragraphs apart — runtime by repetition, not a new late beat (e.g. wrestle **`"righteous before me"`** with Gen 8:21 *“imagination of man's heart is evil from his youth”*, or quote Gen 9:13 bow as witnessed mercy).
