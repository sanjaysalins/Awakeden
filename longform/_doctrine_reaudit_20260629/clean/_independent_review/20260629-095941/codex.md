# Independent review — codex (OK, 67s)

Findings:

1. **EW-INV-4 failure: unattributed Scripture quotations.** Several `**"..."**` KJV quotes have no clear speaker/source attribution immediately before them:
   - **"By faith Noah, being warned of God..."**
   - **"Make thee an ark of gopher wood"**
   - **"the door of the ark shalt thou set in the side thereof"**
   - **"and the LORD shut him in."**
   - **"And every living substance was destroyed..."**
   - **"while the ark was a preparing..."**
   - **"The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us..."**
   
   The artifact sometimes says vague things like “the record sets it down” or “men who walked with Him wrote it down,” but the rule requires attribution before quoting. These need explicit book/speaker framing.

2. **Possible misattribution: Genesis 6:13 is tagged `[the LORD]`, but KJV says “God said unto Noah.”**
   The quote:
   - **[the LORD]** **"The end of all flesh is come before me..."**
   
   In KJV Genesis 6:13, the speaking tag is God, not LORD. Since the piece is strict about KJV attribution, this should be corrected.

3. **Noah’s righteousness is over-flattened.**
   The line **“I was no purer than the lost whose cries I heard”** pushes past biblical humility into tension with Genesis’s own claims that Noah was righteous in his generation. The piece can say Noah was saved by grace, not merit, without implying there was no real moral distinction between Noah and the violent world judged by God.

4. **The reveal is partly earned, but the typology gets muddled.**
   The strongest line is John 10: **“I am the door…”** That earns the door connection. But then the piece says:
   - **“And the ark was Him”**
   - **“The flood that drowned the world did not strike my ark”**
   - **“But the wrath that my sin had earned did not pass Him by — it fell on Him, not the wood”**
   
   If the ark is Christ, “not the wood” becomes confusing. The type shifts between door = Christ, ark = Christ, wood = not Christ. The substitution point is sound, but the imagery needs tightening.

5. **The first-hearing clarity breaks around baptism.**
   A zero-Bible-knowledge listener will stumble over:
   - **“The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us…”**
   - **“It was never the water that saved, then or now…”**
   
   The explanation comes quickly, but the quotation is dense and the contrast between “saved by water,” “baptism doth also now save us,” and “never the water that saved” is not made plain enough.

6. **Voice slips into essay-preacher language.**
   Noah’s first-person voice is strongest in **“I did not shut the door”** and **“I doubted my own right to be dry inside it.”** But it weakens in lines like:
   - **“My ark was the shadow; He is the substance that cast it.”**
   - **“the answer of a heart turned to God and the rising of Christ from the dead”**
   - **“the hand that holds a soul safe inside Christ is His own”**
   
   These sound like a theological narrator, not Noah speaking with lived memory.

7. **Long-form sag: the same beats repeat instead of escalating.**
   The piece circles the same phrases too often:
   - **“One door. Not a row of them — one.”**
   - **“And there was only ever the one door.”**
   - **“There is still one door, thrown wide open.”**
   - **“I was no better clay…”**
   - **“I belonged among the guilty.”**
   - **“I was no purer than the lost…”**
   - **“no purer than the drowning I heard”**
   
   The repetition reinforces the theme, but in long form it begins to feel padded. Beat 4 and Beat 7 especially repeat material already established.

8. **CTA mostly works, but “Will you come in?” is close to the banned tired-template zone.**
   The close does name Jesus and names the response: **“Step in through Jesus.”** That is good. But **“Will you come in?”** is still a fairly bare template question. The stronger CTA is already present in the next sentence; lead with that and make the invitation more specific: come to Christ, receive mercy, enter by faith.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix KJV attribution/source framing for every bold quotation, especially Genesis 6:13.
2. Tighten the typology so door/ark/wood/judgment map clearly to Christ and substitution.
3. Cut repeated “one door / no merit / not my strength” beats and strengthen Noah’s first-person voice.
