## Where it's genuinely strong (so I don't overstate the fixes)

- **Reveal is earned (EW-G7)** — the "one door / I never shut it / His hand on the latch" question is planted in Beats 1, 3, 4 and *paid off* by John 10:9 in Beat 6, then echoed in "the hand that holds a soul safe inside Christ is His own." This is shown, not announced.

## Real critiques (craft / watch-points, not failures)

1. **Repetition risks sag.** The "I was no purer than the drowning" beat fires three times — Beat 2 ("no better clay than any of them"), Beat 5 ("I was no purer than the lost"), Beat 7 ("no purer than the drowning I heard"). Combined with the "who am I, that I should be dry/drawn inside and live" question recurring in Beats 4, 5, 7, the back half leans hard on two phrases. For a ~10-min piece this is *reinforcement*, but it's the closest thing to padding. Cut one of the three "no purer" lines.

2. **Eternal-security line is a denominational lean.** "the hand that holds a soul safe inside Christ is His own, never ours — we come in, and He keeps us." Defensible — it's grounded in the *same chapter* you quote (John 10:28-29) — but it's a perseverance-of-the-saints claim stated as flat fact. For a broad evangelical audience, either anchor it explicitly to John 10:28 or soften the absoluteness.

3. **The 1 Peter 3:21 baptism quote is the one first-hearing snag.** "The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us (not the putting away of the filth of the flesh…)" is the densest line for a zero-Bible listener. You do gloss it well ("A figure — a likeness… It was never the water that saved"), which keeps it doctrinally safe (no baptismal regeneration). Just confirm the audio paces this slowly — read fast it will lose people.

4. **CTA fear-adjacency — threaded, but watch it.** The whole engine is judgment/drowning, so the close has to work to stay grace-anchored. It does: "now there is no fear left in it… nothing to build, nothing to bring." Response is named (come in / step in through Jesus), not the bare "will you trust Him?" template. This passes — I only flag that the survival frame is inherently pressure-adjacent, so don't let the visuals/score re-inject the dread the words removed.

None of these cross the hard bars (KJV, attribution, earned reveal, named substitution, grace CTA, consistent first-person voice). The fixes are polish.
