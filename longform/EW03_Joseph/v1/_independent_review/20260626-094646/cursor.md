## Independent adversarial review — Joseph (eyewitness-long)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim:** Every bold span I checked is an exact KJV substring against the episode `passage.txt`. No altered words inside the quote marks.

**Attribution — still fails systematically:**

1. **Genesis narrator-lines have no source frame.** Unattributed before quote:
   - `**"Now Israel loved Joseph more than all his children..."**`
   - `**"that they stript Joseph out of his coat, his coat of many colours that was on him"**`
   - `**"sold Joseph to the Ishmeelites for twenty pieces of silver"**`
   - `**"But the LORD was with Joseph, and shewed him mercy..."**`  
   Beat 1’s `**"I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt."**` is introduced by *"and I said the words"* — borderline for Joseph’s own speech, but every other OT narrative quote still reads as Joseph reciting statute with no Moses/Scripture frame and no dedicated scripture-voice cue.

2. **Judas quote is mid-verse orphan Scripture.** Prose names him (`He was sold by Judas…`), but the block still opens with narrative glue and no speaker tag:  
   `**"And said unto them, What will ye give me, and I will deliver him unto you? And they covenanted with him for thirty pieces of silver."**`  
   Read aloud, *"And said unto them"* has no subject. Needs `**[Judas]**` (or equivalent) **before** the quote.

3. **NT reveal quotes are vague or absent on speaker.**  
   - `**"Him hath God exalted with his right hand to be a Prince and a Saviour..."**` — only *"So the word written long after me says of Him:"* (Peter/Acts 5:31 unnamed).  
   - `**"But God commendeth his love toward us..."**` — only *"That same word says it plainly:"* (Paul/Rom 5:8 unnamed).  
   - `**"Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do."**` — *"He prayed:"* but no `**[Jesus]**` on a spoken divine line.

4. **Beat 7 closing quote is mis-framed, not mis-quoted.**  
   `**"Fear not: for am I in the place of God?"**` is verbatim Gen 50:19, but prose calls it *"the question my life kept asking."* In KJV Joseph is **answering** fearful brothers (*am I in the place of God?* = *I am not your judge*), not voicing his lifelong doubt. That is misattribution of **function**, and it will confuse a zero-Bible listener at the landing.

### EW-G9 — Doctrine / grounding

**Mostly sound:** Typology is mainstream; substitution is named (`dying in the place of the sinners who hated Him`; Rom 5:8); no supersession (`I was only the shadow; He is the substance`).

**Real timeline error:**

Beat 6: *"I forgave them, and told them what I had come to see"* → then quotes Gen **50:20** (`**"But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good..."**`).

That verse is spoken **after Jacob’s death**, when the brothers fear revenge (Gen 50:15–21) — not at the first palace reunion (Gen 45) that Beat 1 hooks. Beat 7 repeats the conflation: *"When my betrayers knelt before me"* blended with Gen **50:19**. A careful listener loses **when** Joseph said what; the witness is stitching two reconciliation scenes into one.

**Internal contradiction with Joseph’s own canon:**

Beat 4: *"I could see that it had happened, but not yet why it had to."*  
Genesis 45:5–8 (same witness, same life) already has Joseph explaining God’s purpose at the first reunion — *"God did send me before you to preserve life"* / *"it was not you that sent me hither, but God."* The piece wants a long “why?” riddle, then assigns the answer to the wrong scene and the wrong hour.

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned?

**Still asserted / imported:**

- *"There came a Man, Jesus, and the very shape of my life was the shadow of His"* — thesis stated, then proved by parallel checklist (twenty vs thirty silver, right hand, forgiveness prayer), not **drawn out of details Joseph already named** in Beats 3–4.
- Beat 4’s *"not yet why it had to"* is **not paid from Genesis** before the cross-time pivot (*"by a light given long after me, a word written after I was laid in my grave"*). Payoff is NT montage, not Joseph’s lived text.
- **Substitution** (penalty-bearing death) never shadows in the Joseph type — Joseph does not die in his brothers’ place. It arrives only via Rom 5:8. Typology is allowed; under EW-G7 the death-for-enemies link is **announced by apostolic quote**, not shown from the type.

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Palace hour is real steel:** *"Not the pit. The choice in the palace, with my betrayers on their knees and my grief insisting they deserved to die"* — Joseph’s strongest moral tension, in first person. Good.

**Beat 5 improvement is real:** *"The word you heard me quote says the LORD was with me in that prison — and it was true. But hear me: a man can be held by God and still feel let go"* — wrestles **with** Scripture, not against it. Pass on that thread.

**Remaining slippage:**

- *"the bitter question that every betrayed man knows by heart"* — generic essay voice, not Joseph-specific (EW-G10 bleed).
- *"the sword of vengeance lay ready in my fist"* — Genesis gives Joseph power to imprison and accuse, not a sword. Presented as lived memory, not labeled metaphor → borderline invented history (EW-INV-11).
