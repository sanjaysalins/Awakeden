## Independent adversarial review — *Joseph* (eyewitness-short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim:** All four quoted strings match KJV substrings. No altered words inside the bold quotes (including KJV **"Ishmeelites"**).

**Attribution: fail on one quote; weak on another.**

- **FAIL — Genesis 37:28 narrator text, no source named:** After *"and my own blood priced me:"*, **`"sold Joseph to the Ishmeelites for twenty pieces of silver"`** is third-person Moses-narration. Joseph did not speak those words. *"my own blood priced me"* is Joseph’s voice, not attribution of Scripture. Needs an explicit frame (*as Moses wrote*, *the record reads*, `**[scripture]**`, etc.).

- **WEAK — Matthew 11:28:** *"He says:"* before **`"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."`** is barely attribution. *"He"* works only because *"Jesus"* appears two sentences earlier. Format elsewhere expects a named speaker tag (e.g. `**[Jesus]**`).

- **Mis-timed attribution (not just missing):** *"I did not raise the sword; I forgave, and said:"* then **`"But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good..."`** — that is Genesis **50:20**, spoken **after Jacob’s death**, when the brothers feared revenge. It is **not** what Joseph said at the grain-bowl reconciliation in Genesis 45. Words are KJV; the **moment** is wrong. A listener who knows Genesis hears a factual slip; a zero-Bible listener is told forgiveness and that speech happened in one beat when the text separates them by years.

Beat 1’s **`"I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt."`** with *"I wept and said:"* — acceptable self-attribution.

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Partially present, then undercut.**

Beat 6 does voice real objections:

- *"From that pit I cried and heard nothing; where was God?"*
- *"When their lives lay in my fist, why forgive the men who wanted me dead?"*

The second line is Joseph’s genuine steel-man (power to avenge vs. mercy). Good.

**Closing breaks the thread:** *"The question I wrestled **in the pit** He turns to you: **will you come to Him?**"* — the pit question was **God’s silence/absence**, not coming to Christ. The forgiveness question (*why forgive?*) is never carried into the invitation; the script swaps in a CTA template instead. That is bait-and-switch, not resolved wrestling.

Also: *"where was God?"* is generic lament; the **strongest** lived objection at the throne is *why spare betrayers when justice was in your fist* — only half-landed.

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Partly shown, partly announced.**

Earned beats:

- Silver (twenty → thirty), betrayal by brethren, brought low → raised, saving the men who sold you — drawn from Joseph’s arc.
- *"What I did with bread, He did with His blood"* — concrete parallel from Joseph’s lived grain-forgiveness.

**Asserted beats:**

- *"**By a light given long after me**, I see my life foreshadowed a greater Brother"* — typology **announced**, not discovered from the witnessed moment. Same “light long after me” essay pivot flagged on sibling shorts.
- *"a Man named Jesus, sold like me but for thirty, killed and raised to save the world"* — checklist in one breath; **thirty** lands with no Judas/Matthew frame for a first-time listener.
- *"Sold, **condemned**, brought low"* — **condemned** is in the header type-list but **never witnessed** in this short (no Potiphar, no prison). Asserted for the Christ parallel, not shown from what Joseph recounts.

Joseph’s type is **providential savior + forgiver**, not penal substitute. The blood/substitution turn is doctrinally corrected in one line (*"bearing in our place the death our sin had earned"*), but it **leaps** from bread-forgiveness rather than being **shown** stepwise from the type.

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Mostly sound; one structural stretch.**

- Substitution on **Christ** is named: *"bearing in our place the death our sin had earned"* — penalty + what He bore. Good.
- *"We are the brothers who sold Him"* — typological identification, not supersession; acceptable in this format if the silver/betrayal bridge is clear (it’s thin without Judas named).
- **No invented contrarian reading.**
- **Type overreach:** metadata and Beat 6 claim **"falsely condemned"** but the short never stages condemnation; only sale + pit. Blurs the type header for careful listeners.
- **Gen 50:20 at the wrong narrative moment** (see above) is a grounding error, not just craft.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus

**Mixed.**

**Passes:** *"Come not to me, the shadow, but to **Jesus**, the substance"* — names Jesus; response **come** is explicit; grace-anchored; Matthew 11:28 is rest, not fear/gain-loss.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Fix Genesis 50:20 misplacement** — do not attach *"But as for you, ye thought evil against me..."* to *"I forgave, and said:"* at the grain encounter; use Genesis 45 reconciliation language there, or explicitly frame 50:20 as words spoken later after my father died.
2. **Attribute the Genesis 37:28 fragment (EW-INV-4)** — frame **`"sold Joseph to the Ishmeelites for twenty pieces of silver"`** as Scripture/Moses/the record, not Joseph’s spoken price after *"my own blood priced me:"*.
3. **Align wrestling → invitation (EW-G8)** — either carry *"why forgive when their lives lay in my fist?"* (or the pit’s *"where was God?"*) honestly into the close, or rewrite *"The question I wrestled in the pit He turns to you"* so the closing question matches the doubt actually raised; cut *"Be still"* / shorten *"By a light given long after me"* so the reveal is shown from betrayal–forgiveness–salvation, not announced.
