# Independent review — codex (OK, 25s)

Findings:

- **EW-INV-4 attribution failure:** `**"sold Joseph to the Ishmeelites for twenty pieces of silver"**` is KJV wording, but it is not Joseph speaking. It is Genesis narration, and the artifact gives no attribution before the quote. `my own blood priced me:` is not attribution.

- **Possible overreach / invented omniscience:** `By a light given long after me, I see my life foreshadowed...` solves the Joseph-to-Christ reveal by giving Joseph later theological hindsight, but it risks becoming an essay narrator in costume. The witness voice weakens exactly at the turn.

- **EW-G7 reveal partly asserted, not fully shown:** `Sold, condemned, brought low — then raised to save the men who sold me` is a strong pattern, but the move to `a greater Brother... Jesus` is still mostly announced. The artifact should show more from Joseph’s lived moment: the brothers guilty before him, Joseph having power to judge, bread given instead of vengeance, life preserved by mercy.

- **EW-G8 wrestling is underdeveloped:** `where was God? When their lives lay in my fist, why forgive the men who wanted me dead?` is the right objection, but it passes too quickly. Joseph’s strongest internal objection would be sharper: justice, remembered betrayal, years stolen, and the moral scandal of forgiving unrepentant or terrified men.

- **EW-G9 substitution is present but could be more precise:** `bearing in our place the death our sin had earned` names substitution and penalty well. But `killed and raised to save the world — even the ones who slew Him` is broad and slightly loose. Better anchor the guilt universally without implying only the historical killers are in view.

- **First-hearing clarity issue:** `Come not to me, the shadow, but to Jesus, the substance` uses theological typology language that may be unclear to someone with zero Bible knowledge. “Shadow/substance” is meaningful, but not first-hearing plain.

- **CTA is close but formulaic:** `will you come to Him?` names the response, and it names Jesus just before, but the final line is still close to the tired bare-question template. The stronger invitation is already in `Come not to me... but to Jesus`; the ending should carry that grace more freshly.

- **Short-form tightness:** The piece is around the right length and keeps one witness/thread. The metadata and beat labels would not belong in a spoken eyewitness short, but the narration itself is mostly focused.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the unattributed/misattributed Genesis 37 quote; attribute it as Scripture narration or remove the quote.
2. Make the reveal earned from Joseph’s lived moment: guilty brothers, power to judge, bread instead of vengeance, life through mercy.
3. Replace the final `will you come to Him?` with a fresher grace-anchored invitation that plainly names coming to Jesus.
