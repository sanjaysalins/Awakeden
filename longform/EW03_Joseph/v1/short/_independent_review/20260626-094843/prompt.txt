# Joseph — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Joseph, son of Jacob, governor of Egypt · **Core text:** Genesis 37; 45; 50 · **Type→fulfillment:** sold by his own for silver, falsely condemned, brought low, then raised to power and made the savior of the very brothers who betrayed him — a shadow of Christ
---

## Beat 1 — I was there

I am Joseph. I stood at Egypt's throne. Years before, my brothers stripped me, threw me in a pit, and sold me. Now they bowed for grain, not knowing the ruler was the brother they betrayed. I wept and said:

**"I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt."**

## Beat 3 — The act

They tore off my coat and cast me down a dry well, where I cried unheard. A caravan came, and my own blood priced me:

**"sold Joseph to the Ishmeelites for twenty pieces of silver"**

## Beat 6 — The reveal

From that pit I cried and heard nothing; where was God? When their lives lay in my fist, why forgive the men who wanted me dead? I did not raise the sword; I forgave, and said:

**"But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good, to bring to pass, as it is this day, to save much people alive."**

Sold, condemned, brought low — then raised to save the men who sold me. By a light given long after me, I see my life foreshadowed a greater Brother: a Man named Jesus, sold like me but for thirty, killed and raised to save the world — even the ones who slew Him. What I did with bread, He did with His blood — bearing in our place the death our sin had earned.

## Beat 7 — The invitation

Be still. I face the brothers who sold me, my hands holding bread, not a sword: what forgiving costs. Christ held out costlier — His own blood. We are the brothers who sold Him. Come not to me, the shadow, but to Jesus, the substance, who carries the guilt my hands never could. He says:

**"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."**

The question I wrestled in the pit He turns to you: will you come to Him?
