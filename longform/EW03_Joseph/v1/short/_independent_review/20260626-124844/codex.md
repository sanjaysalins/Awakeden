# Independent review — codex (OK, 24s)

**Findings**

- **EW-INV-4:** The quote is exact KJV Matthew 11:28, but the attribution is weak: `He calls:` uses only a pronoun. The rule says the speaker must be attributed before quoting. Make it explicit: Jesus said/called.
- **EW-G7 reveal:** The Joseph-to-Christ turn is mostly asserted in a list: `sold for twenty; Jesus was sold... for thirty. Falsely condemned, killed... then raised`. It connects parallels, but does not sufficiently show Joseph reasoning from the moment he lived. The turn feels like a sermon overlay, not Joseph discovering the pattern through his own wound.
- **EW-G8 wrestling:** There is almost no real internal objection. `Watch what God did with their evil` skips past the hardest question Joseph would have carried: how God could be good while his brothers’ evil and his dungeon years were real. `Every pit became a road that climbed` is too neat for the steel-man.
- **EW-G9 doctrine:** Substitution is present: `dying in the place of the sinners who hated Him.` But the penalty is not named. The lens asks for “the penalty + what Christ bore.” This says blood and death, but not wrath/judgment/curse/sin’s penalty.
- **Voice / character:** Joseph’s first-person voice is strongest in the hook, then collapses into detached typology: `There came a greater Brother... opening His pardon...` That sounds like an essay narrator, not Joseph son of Jacob recounting Egypt, famine, betrayal, and reconciliation.
- **First-hearing clarity:** `Pharaoh's right hand`, `greater Brother`, and `the men who drove the nails` may be followable, but `shadow` and `Type→fulfillment` language are insider-coded. The narration should not rely on typology vocabulary to land.
- **CTA-on-Jesus:** `Come to Jesus. Not when you're clean enough. Now, as you are.` is clear and grace-anchored. It names the response: come. It avoids fear and gain-loss pressure. This is one of the cleaner parts.
- **Short form:** It is within short-form length, but the spine crowds too many threads: silver, false condemnation, dungeon, Pharaoh, famine bread, Judas, crucifixion, pardon to nail-drivers. The witnessed moment is not singular enough.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add Joseph’s real wrestling before the reveal; make the Christ turn earned from his lived moment, not announced by typology.
2. Make the KJV attribution explicit before the quote: name Jesus as the speaker.
3. Strengthen substitution by naming the penalty Christ bore, while keeping the CTA grace-anchored and simple.
