## Independent adversarial review — *Joseph* (eyewitness-short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim:** All four quoted strings match `passage.txt` substrings. No altered words inside the quotes themselves.

**Attribution: mixed — one clear fail, two weak.**

- **FAIL — narrative fragment with no speaker:** After `and my own blood set my price:`, the line **`"sold Joseph to the Ishmeelites for twenty pieces of silver"`** is third-person narrator text from Genesis 37:28, not anything Joseph spoke. Joseph did not say “sold Joseph…”; Moses did. There is no frame like “the record reads,” “Moses wrote,” or a `**[scripture]**` cue. Same class of breach flagged on the Aaron and Abraham shorts.

- **WEAK — Jesus quote:** `He says:` before **`"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."`** is minimal. The format expects `**[Jesus]**` on a dialogue line (see Abraham short). “He” is recoverable only because “Jesus” appears two sentences earlier.

- **Borderline OK — Joseph’s own speech:** `I wept, and said the words they had dreaded for twenty years:` before **`"I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt."`** works as first-person self-attribution. Same for `I forgave them, and told them what I had come to see:` before the Genesis 50:20 quote.

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man) — **absent**

There is no Beat 5 doubt anywhere. Joseph never voices the strongest objection he actually lived:

- When his brothers stood before him, **why forgive men who wanted him dead?**
- While in the pit or in prison, **whether God’s “good” purpose could be trusted** when the evil felt total.
- With **power to destroy them in my fist**, whether justice or mercy was right.

The piece jumps from Beat 3 (`Betrayed for silver. Falsely condemned.`) straight to Beat 6 forgiveness and typology. That is not compressed wrestling — it is **skipped wrestling**. For this format, that is a structural miss, not a acceptable SHORT cut.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned — **partly asserted**

The Genesis 50:20 quote is earned from Joseph’s lived moment (forgiveness under power). What follows is not:

- **`But by a light given long after me, my life was a shadow of a greater Brother.`** — announces the typology before showing it. Same “light given long after me” stinger pattern already flagged on sibling episodes.
- **`There came a Man, Jesus.`** — essay pivot, not something Joseph’s moment resolves into.
- The Christ link then arrives as a **checklist**: twenty vs thirty silver → prison vs grave → saved slayers → bread vs blood. The parallels are asserted in parallel clauses, not drawn out of what Joseph saw when he held back the sword.

Also: **`I told them what I had come to see`** points to providence (Gen 50:20), but the next sentence pivots to typology Joseph did not “come to see” in that scene — the turn is **announced**, not **shown from the text he lived**.

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Mostly sound; substitution under-specified.**

- **`Falsely condemned.`** (Beat 3) — attached to the pit/sale beat. Potiphar’s false accusation (the actual “condemnation”) is never shown. A zero-Bible listener may read “sold into slavery” as the condemnation, which blurs the type header’s “falsely condemned” claim.
- **`who carries the guilt my hands never could`** — guilt-bearing is named; the **penalty Christ bore** (sin, death, judgment) is not. **`What I did with bread, He did with His own blood`** is strong imagery but stops short of naming substitution (our guilt/death on Him).
- Silver typology (20 vs 30), betrayal by brethren, saving betrayers — faithful, in `passage.txt`, not contrarian.
- **`we are the brothers who sold Him`** — typological identification, not supersession; acceptable if the listener already follows the Joseph→Judas bridge.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus

**Passes.** `Come to Jesus` and `Come to the Brother who was sold for you, raised for you, and forgives you still` name Jesus and a concrete response (**come**). Grace-anchored; no bare “will you trust Him?” template, no obvious fear/gain-loss pressure. **`You have wronged Him`** is identification, not manufactured urgency.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Uneven.** Strong openings (`I am Joseph, son of Jacob. I stood at Egypt's throne.`) and vivid act beats (`They tore my father's coat off…`, `power to destroy them lay in my fist`).

Then the voice slips into theological essay mode:

- **`But by a light given long after me`**
- **`There came a Man, Jesus`**
- **`I am only the shadow` / `the substance`**

That is Pauline catechesis, not a governor recounting what his hands did and what his heart wrestled with.

---

### First-hearing clarity

Followable in broad strokes (betrayed → rose to power → forgave → points to Jesus). Weak spots for a listener with zero Bible background:

- Beat 1 **front-loads the reunion** (`Now they bowed for grain…` + Gen 45:4) before Beat 3 rewinds to the pit — workable, but the emotional through-line is split.
- **`He, by Judas, for thirty`** — “Judas” lands only if you already know the betrayal story.
- **`He stands holding bread, not a sword`** — beautiful, but bread/sword only pays off if you tracked Joseph’s grain-forgiveness arc; it is not set up in Beat 6 itself.

---

### Hook, spine, SHORT tightness

- **Hook:** `I stood at Egypt's throne` — grips.
- **One witness / one thread:** Holds; no witness swap.
- **Single moment:** **Crowded.** Beat 1 is identity-reveal-at-the-throne; Beat 6 is forgiveness-under-power + full typology checklist. Two “climax” energies compete; the header’s four-type list (silver / condemned / raised / savior of betrayers) all get packed into one Beat 6 paragraph.
- **Word budget:** ~295–300 spoken words — inside 220–320. Tight enough for ~90s.

### Minor / format

- No `**[Speaker]**` tags on any quote — voice-routing and attribution discipline both suffer (Abraham uses `**[Jesus]**` on Christ quotes).
- Beat numbering skips 2, 4, 5 — fine per SHORT compression, but Beat 5’s **content** is missing, not just its header.
