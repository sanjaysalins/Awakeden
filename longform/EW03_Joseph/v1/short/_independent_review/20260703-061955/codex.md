# Independent review — codex (OK, 25s)

Concrete findings:

- **EW-G8 failure: no real wrestling.** The piece never lets Joseph voice the strongest objection from inside the wound. “**Watch what God did with their evil**” skips past the hard question: betrayal, slavery, false accusation, prison, and forgiving the brothers who caused it. There is no honest “How could this be good?” or “Why should they live?” in Joseph’s own voice.

- **EW-G10 partially weak: Joseph becomes an essay narrator.** “**But I was only a shadow. There came a greater Brother.**” is theological exposition, not strongly Joseph-like testimony. The voice starts vivid, then turns into a typology lecture.

- **EW-G7 mostly works, but the reveal is too announced.** The parallels are clear: “**Sold for twenty… Jesus was sold… for thirty. Falsely condemned… down into death — then raised**.” But “**I was only a shadow**” tells the reveal before the narration has fully made us feel it.

- **EW-INV-4: KJV quote appears exact.** “**Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.**” matches KJV Matthew 11:28. Attribution is borderline: “**He calls:**” is clear from context, but stronger compliance would name the speaker: “Jesus calls:”.

- **EW-G9 needs sharper substitution.** “**dying in the place of the sinners who hated Him**” names substitution, but the penalty is not explicit. The lens asks for “the penalty + what Christ bore.” This should name wrath, judgment, curse, or sin’s penalty more directly.

- **First-hearing clarity issue.** “**Pharaoh’s right hand**,” “**Judas**,” and “**the men who drove the nails**” assume Bible knowledge. A zero-knowledge listener can follow the broad motion, but some references are compressed.

- **Hook is strong.** “**My own brothers sold me for silver. Years later they bowed at my feet**” grips immediately and keeps one witness / one thread.

- **Short form is tight.** It fits the expected length and does not sprawl, but the missing internal wrestle makes it feel too smooth.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add Joseph’s strongest honest objection before resolving it.
2. Make the substitution explicit: name the penalty Christ bore.
3. Replace announced typology with more witnessed, first-person discovery.
