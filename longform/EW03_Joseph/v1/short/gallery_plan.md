## 1 — HOOK: sold for silver
**[beat] Hook · [time] 0:00–0:09 · [NEW]**

Subject_block: A young Hebrew man (Joseph) thrown down into a dry desert cistern, hands grasping the stone rim from below; above him, in warm low sun, the hard silhouettes of older brothers — one stretching out a fist of silver coins toward an unseen merchant. Joseph's upturned face is the one lit point; the brothers are shadow-shapes against a bruised sky. Deep negative space of empty pit-wall. *(common tail)*

Named elements (tour order):
1. Joseph's upturned, betrayed face (the lit point)
2. His hands clutching the stone rim of the pit
3. The brother's fist closing on the silver coins
4. The hard silhouette of the turned-away brothers

Lands on Christ: the betrayal-for-silver image is the seed of the whole type — a brother sold by his own.

## 2 — HOOK turn: the bowing brothers
**[beat] Hook (reveal) · [time] 0:09–0:18 · [NEW]**

Subject_block: The throne hall of Egypt's governor. Gaunt, road-worn brothers kneel low, faces to the floor, empty grain-sacks held out begging for bread. Above them on a dais, a robed Egyptian ruler watches in silence — his face the only one turned toward us, half-recognized. One shaft of high window-light falls between them. Crowd of attendants kept in shadow. *(common tail)*

Named elements (tour order):
1. The kneeling brothers' bowed faces and outstretched empty sacks
2. The ruler's still, watching face on the dais (Joseph, unrecognized)
3. The begging hands lifting toward the throne
4. The single shaft of window-light between ruler and brothers

Lands on Christ: the betrayer kneels before the one he wronged — and finds not a sword but bread; the gospel posture in miniature.

## 3 — STRANGE THING: the four-vignette descent
**[beat] The strange thing · [time] 0:18–0:30 · [NEW — multi-story canvas]**

Subject_block: A single dark canvas holding FOUR soft-edged vignettes bleeding into one another, reading top-to-bottom as a descent then a climb: (a) a hand spilling twenty silver pieces; (b) a falsely-accused man turning away from a pointing accuser; (c) a chained figure in the deep gloom of a dungeon, one barred shaft of light; (d) the same figure risen, standing at the right hand of an enthroned Pharaoh, signet ring on his finger. Each vignette a memory-soft pool of light in surrounding darkness, never framed in panels or arches. *(common tail)*

Named elements (tour order):
1. The hand spilling the twenty silver pieces
2. The pointing accuser's hand (false condemnation)
3. The chained hands in the dungeon's barred light
4. The signet ring placed on the risen Joseph's finger

Lands on Christ: every pit became a road that climbed — the down-then-up shape that Christ will fulfill perfectly.

## 4 — TURN: the two brothers sold (Joseph → Jesus)
**[beat] The turn · [time] 0:30–0:42 · [NEW — pivot painting, two soft vignettes]**

Subject_block: One canvas, two soft-edged vignettes mirrored across a vertical band of shadow. LEFT/upper: young Joseph being handed away as a small clutch of silver changes hands — twenty pieces. RIGHT/lower, larger and lower in the frame: in a torch-lit garden of olive trees, a robed disciple (Judas) presses a kiss while a fist of thirty silver coins glints — Jesus, calm and sorrowful, stands bound at the center light. The lower vignette dominates; Joseph's vignette fades upward into memory. *(common tail)*

Named elements (tour order):
1. Joseph's small clutch of twenty silver pieces (upper memory)
2. The thirty silver coins glinting in Judas' fist
3. The betrayer's kiss against Jesus' cheek
4. Jesus' calm, sorrowful, bound face at the center light

Lands on Christ: names the fulfillment outright — Joseph the shadow, Jesus the greater Brother, betrayed by one of His own for silver.

## 5 — TURN: lower than any pit (the cross & descent)
**[beat] The turn · [time] 0:42–0:54 · [NEW]**

Subject_block: A darkened Golgotha at the ninth hour. The cross stands central against a torn, blackened sky; the crucified Christ's head bowed, a single soldier's hammer and a fallen nail in the foreground gloom. The earth seems to open below into the deepest shadow — the descent into death — but a low edge of dawn-grey light bleeds at the horizon, hinting the climb to come. One dominant hero subject, vast negative sky. *(common tail)*

Named elements (tour order):
1. Christ's bowed head and face on the cross
2. The wounded, nail-pierced hand
3. The fallen iron nail and hammer in the foreground
4. The low grey edge of dawn at the horizon (the climb begins)

Lands on Christ: brought lower than any pit — down into death — bearing the blood that lifts the guilt Joseph's bread never could.

### Doctrine note to check
- The pardon is **opened to** the betrayers (Joseph forgives his brothers; Christ pardons even "the men who drove the nails") — NOT a claim that all are automatically saved. Narration says "opening His pardon even to" — keep the painting's invitation posture (extended hand / open arms), not a universal-salvation tableau.
- **Substitution named correctly:** Christ "dying in the place of the sinners who hated Him" — His blood lifts the guilt Joseph's bread could not. #5 (cross) + #7 (welcome) carry this; do not soften the cross into mere example.
- 20 vs 30 pieces of silver is Bible-grounded (Gen 37:28 — twenty; Matt 26:15 — thirty); keep coin counts visually loose (don't render countable legible numerals).
