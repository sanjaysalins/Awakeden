# Joseph — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Joseph, son of Jacob, governor of Egypt · **Core text:** Genesis 37; 45; 50 · **Type→fulfillment:** sold by his own for silver, falsely condemned, brought low, then raised to power and made the savior of the very brothers who betrayed him — a shadow of Christ
**Status:** v1 — Awakeden eyewitness SHORT, PUNCHY hook-first (Hook→Strange→Turn→Punch). First-person Joseph. ONE verbatim KJV quote (Matt 11:28). Reveal names Jesus; substitution named (His blood lifts the guilt Joseph's bread could not); pardon OPENED to the ones who slew Him (not "all saved"). Grace-anchored, no fear/gain-loss.
---

## Beat 1 — Hook

My own brothers sold me for silver. Years later they bowed at my feet, begging for bread — not knowing the ruler was me.

## Beat 2 — The strange thing

Watch what God did with their evil. Sold for twenty pieces of silver. Falsely condemned. Thrown in a dungeon. Then lifted to Pharaoh's right hand — and made the savior of the very brothers who threw me away. Every pit became a road that climbed.

## Beat 3 — The turn

But I was only a shadow. There came a greater Brother. I was sold for twenty; Jesus was sold by Judas, one of His own twelve, for thirty. Falsely condemned, killed, brought lower than any pit — down into death — then raised to save the world, opening His pardon even to the men who drove the nails. What I did with bread, He did with His own blood, dying in the place of the sinners who hated Him. He calls:

**"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."**

## Beat 4 — The punch

You are the brother who sold Him. Yet Jesus holds out no sword — only His blood, and arms thrown wide. Not when you're clean enough — now, as you are. Trust the Brother who forgives you still.
