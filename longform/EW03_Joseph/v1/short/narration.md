## Beat 1 — Hook

My own brothers threw me into a pit and sold me for silver. Years later they bowed at my feet for bread — not knowing the ruler was me.

## Beat 2 — The strange thing

Sold for twenty pieces. Forgotten in a dungeon. In that dark I asked God: did the dreams You gave me die in that pit? Two years, no answer. Then He raised me to Pharaoh's right hand — and when my brothers bowed, troubled at my presence, the pit opened in me: did they deserve bread? No. But God had meant their evil unto good — I wept, and fed them.

## Beat 3 — The turn

But I was only a shadow of a greater Brother. He too was sold by His own — Judas, for thirty pieces. Condemned, killed, down into death — then raised to save the world, pardoning even the men who nailed Him. I gave my brothers bread; He gave His blood, bearing the death our sin had earned. Jesus calls:

**"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."**

## Beat 4 — The punch

You are the brother who sold Him. Yet He holds out no sword — only arms thrown wide. Not when you're clean enough — now. Trust Jesus — the Brother who forgives you still.
