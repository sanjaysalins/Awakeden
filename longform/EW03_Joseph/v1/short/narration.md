# Joseph — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Joseph, son of Jacob, governor of Egypt · **Core text:** Genesis 37; 45; 50 · **Type→fulfillment:** sold by his own for silver, falsely condemned, brought low, then raised to power and made the savior of the very brothers who betrayed him — a shadow of Christ
**Status:** v1 — Awakeden eyewitness SHORT (B1→B3→B6→B7). First-person as Joseph. Cross-time reveal SIGNPOSTED ("by a light given long after me"). Reveal names Jesus; substitution named (Christ's blood lifts the guilt Joseph's bread could not). CTA grace-anchored on Jesus, no fear/gain-loss.
---

## Beat 1 — I was there

I am Joseph, son of Jacob. I stood at Egypt's throne. Years before, my brothers stripped me, threw me in a pit, and sold me into slavery. Now they bowed for grain, not knowing the ruler was the brother they betrayed. I wept, and said the words they had dreaded for twenty years:

**"I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt."**

## Beat 3 — The act

They tore my father's coat off, cast me into a dry well, while I cried from the bottom. Then a caravan came, and my own blood set my price:

**"sold Joseph to the Ishmeelites for twenty pieces of silver"**

Betrayed for silver. Falsely condemned.

## Beat 6 — The reveal

When the power to destroy them lay in my fist, I did not raise the sword. I forgave them, and told them what I had come to see:

**"But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good, to bring to pass, as it is this day, to save much people alive."**

But by a light given long after me, my life was a shadow of a greater Brother. There came a Man, Jesus. I was sold for twenty pieces of silver; He, by Judas, for thirty. As I rose from prison to save a nation, God raised Him from the grave to save the world — and He too saved the ones who slew Him. What I did with bread, He did with His own blood.

## Beat 7 — The invitation

You have wronged Him — we are the brothers who sold Him. But He stands holding bread, not a sword. Do not come to me; I am only the shadow. Come to Jesus, the substance, who carries the guilt my hands never could. He says:

**"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."**

Come to the Brother who was sold for you, raised for you, and forgives you still.
