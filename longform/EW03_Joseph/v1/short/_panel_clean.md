# Joseph — Awakeden eyewitness (short)

---
## Beat 1 — Hook

My own brothers threw me into a pit and sold me for silver. Years later they bowed at my feet, needing bread — not knowing the ruler was me.

## Beat 2 — The strange thing

Sold for twenty pieces of silver. Falsely accused. Forgotten in a dungeon. In that dark I asked God: the dreams You gave me — did they die in that pit? For two full years, no answer came. Then He lifted me to Pharaoh's right hand and made me the savior of the very brothers who threw me away. And when they bowed, troubled at my presence, the pit opened in me again: did they deserve bread from the brother they sold? No. But God had meant their evil unto good — and I wept, and I fed them. Every pit became a road that climbed.

## Beat 3 — The turn

But I was only a shadow. There came a greater Brother than I. I was sold for twenty; Jesus was sold by Judas, one of His own twelve, for thirty. Falsely condemned, killed, brought lower than any pit — down into death — then raised to save the world, opening His pardon even to the men who drove the nails. What I did with bread, He did with His own blood — bearing the death our sin had earned, dying in the place of the sinners who hated Him. Jesus calls:

**"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."**

## Beat 4 — The punch

You are the brother who sold Him. Yet Jesus holds out no sword — only His blood, and arms thrown wide. Not when you're clean enough — now, as you are. Trust Jesus — the Brother who forgives you still.
