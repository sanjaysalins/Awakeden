God gave me one son after a hundred years of waiting — then told me to carry the knife up a mountain and kill him. I laid the wood on Isaac's back and let my only son carry the wood he would die upon. He asked where the lamb was. I told him God would provide one. I bound him, raised the knife — and a voice stopped my hand. A ram hung caught in a thicket, and it died in my son's place. God gave a ram — but the lamb I spoke of was never found on that mountain. I came down delivered, and still waiting. Generations later, another beloved Son — Jesus — carried His own wood up a hill in that same land. No voice stopped His Father's hand — He gave His own Son. No ram was caught for Him, because He was the Lamb. A man named John pointed: Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world. My boy asked me: where is the lamb? I had no name to give him. You do. The Lamb God provided has a name — Jesus, who climbed the mountain you never could. Lay your knife down, and receive Him.
