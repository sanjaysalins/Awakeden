# Independent review — codex (OK, 27s)

Findings:

- **EW-INV-4 attribution problem:** `**"And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son."**` is exact KJV, but it is introduced only by `I bound my boy on the altar:`. That does not attribute the biblical narrator/text before quoting. It needs something like “Genesis says…” or “Moses records…”.
- **EW-INV-4 passes for Jesus/John quotes:** `**"Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day: and he saw it, and was glad."**` is exact KJV and attributed by `**[Jesus]**`. `**"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."**` is exact KJV and attributed by `John pointed:`.
- **Reveal is partly earned, but overstates one detail:** `and the lamb I promised was never found` is clever but shaky. Genesis 22 does provide a ram, and Abraham names the place around provision. Saying the lamb was “never found” risks sounding like the text failed rather than typology widening toward Christ.
- **Doctrine risk:** `No voice stopped His Father's hand` is vivid, but dangerously compressed. It can sound like the Father personally executes the Son in a crude way. Better to name that Christ bore the penalty of sin under judgment, voluntarily, without making the Father/knife image carry too much.
- **Substitution is named but could be sharper:** `He bore the death we had earned` names death, but not clearly “penalty for sin” until inference. For EW-G9, say the penalty more directly.
- **Wrestling is strong:** `How could the promise stand if the son fell?` is the real objection Abraham would feel. That is not strawman doubt.
- **Voice is mostly consistent:** Abraham’s first-person line holds. But `By the light that came long after me` and `what the hill reached toward` drift into essay-register theology rather than lived witness.
- **CTA mostly passes:** `Come to Him — not because you are driven, but because you are wanted` names a response and avoids fear/gain-loss pressure. `and be carried home` is warm but slightly vague; “receive mercy” or “come in faith” would be clearer.
- **Short-form fit:** Body is in range and focused on one moment. Metadata/header is bulky, but the narration itself is tight.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix quote attribution before the Genesis 22 KJV quotation.
2. Clarify substitution: Christ bore the penalty of our sin, without the risky “Father’s hand” compression.
3. Replace “the lamb I promised was never found” with a cleaner earned reveal from ram-provision to Christ the Lamb.
