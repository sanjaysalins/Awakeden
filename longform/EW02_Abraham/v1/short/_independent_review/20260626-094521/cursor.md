## Independent adversarial review — *Abraham and the Lamb* (eyewitness-short)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Quoted words:** The three `**"..."**` blocks match KJV (Gen 22:10; John 8:56; John 1:29). No altered words inside the quotes.

**Attribution fails on two of three:**

- **`"And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son."`** — Preceded only by *"I bound my boy on the altar:"* That is witness action, not source/speaker attribution. A zero-Bible listener cannot tell this is Scripture being read, not Abraham narrating in third person about himself.

- **`"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."`** — *"John pointed:"* names John but does not attribute before quoting the way `[Jesus]` does for the prior line. *"Pointed"* is not *"said"* (KJV: *"saith"*), and there is no `[John]` tag despite a speaking character in-scene.

**Only clean attribution:** **`[Jesus]`** before *"Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day…"*

**Paraphrase drift (not in quotes, but feeds the lamb thread):** *"I said God would provide Himself a lamb"* — Gen 22:8 is *"My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering."* Capitalizing *"Himself"* smuggles the Christ-reading into Abraham’s mouth before the reveal earns it; Isaac’s actual question is also paraphrased with no **`[son]`** voice despite a named speaker in the scene (EW-INV-6).

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (headline failure)

**Beat 5 is absent.** The short format allows compression (B1→B3→B6→B7), but the steel-man cannot be a single hook line and disappear.

The only doubt is Beat 1’s *"How could the promise stand if the son fell?"* — a real tension, but it is **never returned to, never lived through, never resolved in Abraham’s voice.** Beat 3 moves straight from wood → bind → knife → ram with no interior fight.

**Strongest objections never internalized:**

- Raising a knife over the son *"whom thou lovest"* (Gen 22:2 — the emotional/ethical horror).
- Whether the command contradicts God’s character, not just the promise logically.
- Isaac’s trusting question (*"where is the lamb?"*) hanging unanswered while Abraham binds him — the father/son trust rupture.
- Whether *"God will provide"* is faith or evasion in the moment the blade is raised.

What’s here is a **hook question**, not wrestling. Same structural gap already flagged on sibling shorts (Two Goats, Passover, Boaz, Isaiah).

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine (mostly sound; one real misstatement)

**Substitution passes:** *"a ram caught in a thicket died in my son's stead"* → *"No voice stopped His Father's hand; no ram was caught for Him"* → *"He bore the death we had earned, that we might walk free"* — penalty (earned death) and what Christ bore are named. Abraham-as-shadow-Father (*"my hand stopped… whose Father did not stop"*) is orthodox. No supersession.

**Text misstatement (doctrine-adjacent):**

- Beat 6: *"and **the lamb I promised** was never found"*
- Header: *"the lamb **Abraham promised** but never found"*

Abraham did **not** promise a lamb. **Isaac asked** *"where is the lamb?"* and Abraham **said God would provide** (Gen 22:7–8). This swaps Isaac’s question for Abraham’s pledge and blurs who was waiting for what. It weakens the typology the piece depends on (the lamb-not-yet-seen → Christ) and repeats the long-form error panel reviewers already flagged (*"I had promised the boy a lamb"*).

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal (partly earned, partly announced)

**Earned elements:** Wood on the beloved son; substitute dies in the son’s place; Father’s hand stopped vs not stopped — these parallels **show** the type before naming Christ.

**Announced / outsourced elements:**

- *"**By the light that came long after me, I see what the hill reached toward.**"* — Signposted reflective device (allowed), but it **announces** understanding before the Moriah details close the case for the listener. Same pattern as the long’s *"what that mountain was always reaching toward."*

- *"**Generations later a Man in Jerusalem named me:**"* then John 8:56 — **Misframes the quote.** In context Jesus is speaking **to Jews about Abraham as their father** (*"Your father Abraham…"*), not appearing to Abraham or “naming” him. First-hearing clarity suffers: who is speaking, to whom, and why?

- After the parallels, **John 8:56 + John 1:29 do the heavy lifting.** The turn leans on later Scripture **asserting** Abraham saw Christ’s day, rather than Abraham walking the listener from ram-vs-lamb → beloved-son-with-wood → unprovided lamb in his **own** witnessed details.

The header also telegraphs the fulfillment (*"provided in Christ, the Lamb of God"*) before the witness earns it in the body.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**PASS.** *"The Lamb has a name: Jesus. **Come to Him**"* — names Jesus, names the response (*come*), grace-anchored (*"not because you are driven, but because you are wanted"*). Not the banned bare *"will you trust Him?"* template. No fear/gain-loss pressure.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Opens in character:** *"I am Abraham. Three days I climbed a mountain, fire and knife in my hands…"* — concrete, first-person, embodied.

**Breaks toward essay/preacher:**

- Beat 7: *"**Be still. Feel the weight**"* — direct second-person coaching to the listener, not Abraham recounting.
- Beat 6: *"Generations later a Man in Jerusalem named me"* — abstract, slightly garbled framing for John 8:56.
- Beat 6 summary line reads like typology bullet points (*"The beloved son carried the wood… a substitute ram died… the lamb I promised…"*) rather than a father still haunted by the knife.

---

### First-hearing clarity

- *"a Man in Jerusalem named me"* + Jesus quote = **confusing** without Bible background.
- *"John pointed"* — **Who is John?** Unintroduced for a zero-knowledge listener.
- *"Moriah"* appears only in metadata, not spoken text.
- Promise-vs-death tension (*"How could the promise stand if the son fell?"*) is **raised then abandoned** — listener never sees it wrestled or resolved.

---

### Hook, spine, SHORT form

**Hook:** Strong — fire, knife, only son, covenant stakes in the first breath.

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (Gen 22 → Christ), no swap.

**Tightness:** ~280 spoken words — within 220–320 budget. Single moment mostly holds; Beat 6 packs two NT quotes plus summary, slightly dense but not a second thread.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Add a real Beat 5 wrestling beat** — sustain and resolve the steel-man in Abraham’s voice (promise vs command, knife over the son you love, Isaac’s “where is the lamb?” while you bind him); do not leave *"How could the promise stand if the son fell?"* as a one-line hook only.
2. **Fix the lamb misstatement** — replace *"the lamb I promised was never found"* (and the header’s *"Abraham promised"*) with what Genesis actually gives: Isaac asked where the lamb was; Abraham said God would provide; a **ram** was given in the son’s stead — the lamb awaited is God’s provision, not Abraham’s pledge.
3. **Attribute every KJV quote before it speaks** — e.g. *"as Moses wrote:"* / **`[John]`** *"said:"* for John 1:29; reframe John 8:56 setup (*"Generations later, a Man in Jerusalem said of me:"* not *"named me"*) so attribution, first-hearing clarity, and EW-INV-4 all hold.
