# Abraham and the Lamb — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Abraham, called the friend of God  ·  **Core text:** Genesis 22  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** the binding of Isaac on Moriah — the only beloved son carries the wood up the mountain, a substitute dies in his stead, and the lamb Abraham promised but never found is provided in Christ, the Lamb of God
---

## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Abraham. Three days I climbed a mountain, fire and knife in my hands, my only son beside me. God had promised me Isaac — my whole covenant hung on the boy — and now asked for his death. How could the promise stand if the son fell?

## Beat 3 — The act
I laid the wood on Isaac and let my son carry it. He asked where the lamb was; I said God would provide Himself a lamb. I bound my boy on the altar:

**"And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son."**

Then a voice stayed my hand; a ram caught in a thicket died in my son's stead. Isaac rose alive.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
The beloved son carried the wood for his own death; a substitute ram died in his place; and the lamb I promised was never found. By the light that came long after me, I see what the hill reached toward. Generations later a Man in Jerusalem named me:

**[Jesus]** **"Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day: and he saw it, and was glad."**

That Man was Jesus — the beloved Son carrying the wood of His offering. No voice stopped His Father's hand; no ram was caught for Him. John pointed:

**"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."**

He bore the death we had earned, that we might walk free.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
Be still. Feel the weight — a father's knife raised over his only son. My boy asked me, where is the lamb? I had no name then. You have one now. Do not look to me — the father in the shadow, my hand stopped. Look to the Son whose Father did not stop. The Lamb has a name: Jesus. Come to Him — not because you are driven, but because you are wanted — and be carried home.
