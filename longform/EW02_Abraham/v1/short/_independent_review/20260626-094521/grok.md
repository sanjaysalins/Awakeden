# Independent review — grok (OK, 56s)

Verifying KJV quotes and eyewitness-spec requirements against the artifact.
## Independent adversarial review — *Abraham and the Lamb* (eyewitness-short)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim:** All three quoted strings match KJV (Gen 22:10; John 8:56; John 1:29). No altered words inside the bold quotes.

**Attribution: FAIL on two of three.**

1. **`"And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son."`** — Introduced only by *"I bound my boy on the altar:"*. That is not attribution. This is third-person Genesis narration (Moses), not Abraham’s speech. A first-person witness suddenly quotes Scripture *about himself* with no `**[Scripture]**` / “Moses wrote” / “the record says” frame. Voice rupture plus EW-INV-4 breach.

2. **`"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."`** — *"John pointed:"* is stage direction, not attribution. Which John? A zero-Bible listener hears an unnamed man; the series convention elsewhere uses `**[John]**` (or “John the Baptist said”) **before** the quote.

3. **`[Jesus]` `"Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day: and he saw it, and was glad."`** — Tag present; wording exact. But Abraham is testifying to **you**; Jesus’s line is addressed to *“your”* (the Jews in John 8). Quoted cold in Abraham’s mouth: *who is “your”?* — first-hearing glitch and attribution-by-tag only; content not reframed for the witness voice.

**Outside quotes (not a violation, but note):** *"I said God would provide Himself a lamb"* paraphrases Gen 22:8 and capitalizes **Himself**, pre-loading the Christ-reading before the reveal without the KJV frame (*“God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering”*).

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Passes.** Beat 7 names Jesus (*"The Lamb has a name: Jesus"*), names a response (*"Come to Him"*), grace-anchors (*"not because you are driven, but because you are wanted"*). No bare *“will you trust Him?”* template, no fear/gain-loss pressure.

**Minor:** *"and be carried home"* is warm but vague — *home* to where, by what act (faith, receive, turn)? Borderline under “response must be NAMED, not vague.”

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Shown from Moriah (good):**

- Wood on the son → *"the beloved Son carrying the wood of His offering"*
- Substitute logic → ram *"died in my son's stead"* vs *"no ram was caught for Him"*
- Father contrast → *"my hand stopped"* / *"whose Father did not stop"*

**Announced or shaky:**

- *"By the light that came long after me, I see what the hill reached toward"* — retrospective tell; same family as banned *“at last I understood.”*
- *"and the lamb I promised was never found"* — typologically clever, but **contradicts the lived moment** for a first-time listener: a ram **was** found. Sounds like the text failed, not that the true Lamb was still ahead. Weakens “shown from the text.”
- John 8:56 pivot — Moriah does not show Abraham *rejoicing* or naming *“my day.”* The piece **imports** Jesus’s line and retrofits Moriah onto it. Earned partly via parallels; asserted via the quote.

Reveal is **mixed**, not clean.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Present but thin — borderline REVISE.**

Beat 1: *"How could the promise stand if the son fell?"* is the **right** objection (oath-through-Isaac vs. command-to-kill-Isaac). Not a strawman.

**Problem:** One sentence, then Beat 3 obeys with no cost. Abraham never **sits in** the contradiction — no line on climbing three days with the knife while the promise screamed the opposite. The lamb/ram riddle (*"the lamb I promised was never found"*) appears only in Beat 6 as exposition, never as **doubt** in Beat 3 (*“I promised a lamb — God gave a ram; had I spoken beyond what I saw?”*).

For an adversarial read: minimum viable wrestling — checks the box once, does not inhabit it.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Mostly sound:** Substitution named (ram in Isaac’s stead; Christ as Lamb; *"He bore the death we had earned"*). John 1:29 cites sin-bearing. No supersession. Typology is standard evangelical.

**Risks:**

- *"No voice stopped His Father's hand"* — vivid but compressed. Can sound like the Father personally “slays” the Son the way Abraham nearly slayed Isaac. Penalty/substitution is implied by wood/knife imagery more than stated plainly (*penalty of sin*, *judgment borne in our place*).
- *"He bore the death we had earned, that we might walk free"* — *"walk free"* edges toward outcome/gain framing in Beat 6 (CTA itself stays grace-anchored).
- John 8:56 as proof Abraham “saw” Christ’s day **on Moriah** is interpretive; acceptable only if fully earned — here it leans on import (see G7).

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strong open:** *"I am Abraham. Three days I climbed a mountain, fire and knife in my hands, my only son beside me."*

**Costume / essay slips:**

- Third-person Gen 22:10 quote about himself — detached Scripture voice, not a father remembering.
- Beat 6: *"By the light that came long after me"* / *"Generations later a Man in Jerusalem named me"* — time-jump theologian, not the man on the mountain.
- Beat 7: *"Be still. Feel the weight"* — director speaking to the audience; breaks first-person witness.

---

### First-hearing clarity

**Clear:** Three-day climb, knife, wood on son, ram substitute, Isaac lives, invitation to Jesus.

**Murky:**

- *"Your father Abraham"* — pronoun mismatch (see EW-INV-4).
- *"John pointed"* — which John?
- *"the lamb I promised was never found"* — listener just heard a ram was found; needs a beat to explain *lamb ≠ ram, the true Lamb was still to come*.
- *"my whole covenant hung on the boy"* — covenant stakes are named; **why** God commanded death is not — fine for 90s, but the promise/command clash may zip past zero-Bible ears.

---

### Hook, spine, short-form

**Hook:** Grips — fire, knife, only son, three days.

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (binding → Lamb). No swap.

**Length:** ~280 spoken words — inside 220–320; ~90s plausible.

**Tightness:** No sag, but Beat 6 is **crowded** (ram/lamb riddle + John 8:56 + wood parallel + John 1:29 + substitution in one beat). Compression trades depth (wrestling, Isaac’s KJV question) for NT stack.

---

### Summary

Solid skeleton: strong hook, grace CTA, sound typology. Fails independent bar on: (1) Gen 22:10 and John 1:29 attribution, (2) John 8:56 quoted cold in Abraham’s mouth, (3) *"lamb… never found"* clarity vs. ram found, (4) thin wrestling and partly announced reveal, (5) *"Be still. Feel the weight"* voice break.

Not FAIL — spine and doctrine broadly hold — but not a clean PASS.

---

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Attribute every KJV quote before the bold span (`**[Scripture]**` / Moses frame for Gen 22:10; `**[John]**` for John 1:29) and reframe John 8:56 so *"Your father Abraham"* is not quoted cold — e.g. *"He said I had rejoiced to see His day, and was glad."*
2. Fix the lamb/ram reveal line: replace *"the lamb I promised was never found"* with wording that honors the ram (God provided **a** substitute) while showing the **Lamb** was still ahead — so first-hearing listeners are not told the mountain failed to provide.
3. Deepen EW-G8 by one lived line in Beat 1 or 3 (promise-through-Isaac vs. knife raised) and cut *"By the light that came long after me"* / *"Be still. Feel the weight"* essay-director register so the reveal and CTA stay in Abraham’s voice.
