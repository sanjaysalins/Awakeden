# Independent review — codex (OK, 57s)

**Findings**

- EW-G9 doctrinal accuracy problem: `God gave me one son after a hundred years of waiting` is too blunt. Abraham already had Ishmael. Genesis 22’s “only son” language works covenantally for Isaac, but this line sounds factually false to a first-hearer.

- EW-G8 wrestling is underbuilt. The strongest Abraham objection is not merely `But I had promised a lamb. God gave a ram.` It is: how can God command the death of the son through whom He promised the covenant line? The piece gestures at horror, but not the actual theological crisis Abraham lived.

- EW-G7 risks overplaying the lamb/ram distinction. `But I had promised a lamb. God gave a ram. I came down delivered, and still waiting.` makes the ram sound like an incomplete or lesser answer, when Genesis presents it as God’s real provision `in the stead of his son`. The Christ reveal can build from that without implying Abraham left dissatisfied.

- EW-G9 substitution is not fully named at the close. `the substitute God provided so you never have to climb that mountain` does not name the penalty or what Christ bore. The John quote names sin, but the CTA needs to say Christ bore sin/death/judgment in the sinner’s place.

- EW-INV-2 CTA has a shaky phrase: `so you never have to climb that mountain`. It is vivid, but vague and potentially misleading. The hearer is not being spared Abraham’s test; they are being invited to receive Christ who bore sin in their place.

- EW-G10 voice weakens in the turn. `Generations later, another beloved Son — Jesus — carried His own wood` and `A man named John pointed:` sound more like a teaching narrator than Abraham’s own remembered voice. It remains first-person at the edges, but the middle becomes sermon-summary.

- First-hearing clarity issue: `A man named John pointed:` does not identify John the Baptist or why his testimony matters. The KJV quote itself is exact, but the setup is thin for a listener with no Bible background.

- KJV quote check: `**"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."**` is exact KJV from John 1:29, and the speaker is attributed before the quote. No verbatim error found.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Name Christ’s substitution clearly: He bore our sin, death, and judgment in our place.
2. Replace the weak wrestling with Abraham’s real crisis: God’s promise through Isaac versus God’s command over Isaac.
3. Correct the Isaac/Ishmael problem and avoid implying the ram was not truly God’s provision.
