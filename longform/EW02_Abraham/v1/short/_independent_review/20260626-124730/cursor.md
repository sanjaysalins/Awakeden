## Independent adversarial review — *Abraham and the Lamb* (eyewitness-short)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**The one formal quote is KJV-exact.** `"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."` matches John 1:29.

**Attribution is thin, not clean.** Beat 3: *"A man named John pointed:"* — KJV has John *saith*; *pointed* is not speech attribution, and there is no `[John]` tag. Strict reading: speaker named, but not attributed *before quoting* the way the spec expects for Scripture lines.

**The Genesis hinge is paraphrased away, not quoted.** Beat 2: *"He asked where the lamb was. I told him God would provide one."* Genesis 22:7–8 is Isaac’s full question (*"where is the lamb for a burnt offering?"*) and Abraham’s answer: *"God will provide **himself** a lamb."* The piece drops *for a burnt offering* and, more importantly, *himself* — the very christological hinge the type depends on. Not a quote violation, but it starves the fulfillment the John 1:29 line is meant to pay off.

**No KJV from the core pericope appears in `**"..."**` at all** — unusual for a piece whose spine is Genesis 22.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (headline failure)

**There is no wrestling beat.** Beat 2 is labeled *"The strange thing,"* not doubt. The spec’s steel-man (promise vs. command, knife over the son you love, trust rupture while binding Isaac) never appears in Abraham’s voice.

**What’s here is a puzzle, not internalized objection.** *"But I had promised a lamb. God gave a ram. I came down delivered, and still waiting."* — text-rooted tension, but taxonomy (lamb vs. ram), not the genuine horror Beat 1 raises: *"God gave me one son after a hundred years of waiting — then told me to carry the knife up a mountain and kill him."* Beat 1 names the steel-man; Beat 2 rushes wood → bind → knife → ram with no faith, dread, or *"how can God ask this?"* in Abraham’s mouth. Same structural gap flagged across sibling shorts.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine (one real text error + thin substitution on Christ)

**Text misstatement — do not hand-wave:**

- Beat 2: *"**But I had promised a lamb.**"*
- Metadata: *"the lamb **Abraham promised** but never found"*

Abraham did **not** promise a lamb. **Isaac asked** *"where is the lamb?"* and Abraham said **God would provide** one (Gen 22:8). This swaps Isaac’s question for Abraham’s pledge and blurs who was waiting for what. It weakens the typology the piece depends on and repeats an error already flagged on the long-form sibling.

**Substitution on Isaac is named:** *"A ram hung caught in a thicket, and it died in my son's place."*

**Substitution on Christ is under-named in the witness’s voice.** Beat 4: *"the substitute God provided"* + John’s *"sin of the world"* — but **what Christ bore** (death, judgment, the knife that did not stop) is never spoken by Abraham. The ram-for-Isaac parallel does the work; the Christ-side penalty is implied, not declared. Thin for LOCK / EW-INV-7 standard.

**Typology note:** Header *"Abraham types the Father"* surfaces as *"No voice stopped His Father's hand."* Orthodox in commentaries, but Abraham narrating in first person while implicitly standing in the Father’s typological slot is role-confusing for a zero-Bible listener (Beat 2: Abraham is *the* father with the knife; Beat 3: *"His Father's hand"*).

No supersession. No contrarian reading.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned vs. announced

**Earned from Moriah:** wood on the beloved son; substitute dies *"in my son's place"*; lamb promised (in paraphrase) vs. ram given; *"still waiting."*

**Announced across a time-skip Abraham did not live:** Beat 3 opens *"**Generations later**, another beloved Son — Jesus — carried His own wood up a hill in that same land."* Abraham cannot eyewitness this. The turn leans on **omniscient parallel** (*wood / no voice / no ram / because He was the Lamb*), not on Abraham walking the listener forward from what he saw on the mountain. *"because He was the Lamb"* is **asserted** after the parallel list; the John quote confirms rather than earns.

Per EW-INV-11, cross-time reflection is allowed — but *"Generations later"* is a weak signpost and breaks first-person witness credibility (EW-G10 overlap).

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strong open:** *"God gave me one son after a hundred years of waiting — then told me to carry the knife up a mountain and kill him."* — embodied, urgent, in character.

**Breaks in Beat 3:** *"Generations later… that same land… No voice stopped His Father's hand"* — theological commentary by an essay narrator wearing Abraham’s coat, not memory from Moriah.

**Beat 4 recovers:** *"My boy asked, where is the lamb? I had no name to give him."* — good return to the father’s unresolved ache.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Mostly PASS.** *"He is Jesus"* + *"**Come to Him**, wanted, and be carried home"* — names Christ, names the response (*come*), grace-anchored (*wanted*). Not the banned bare *"will you trust Him?"* template.

**Minor friction:** *"so you **never have to climb that mountain**"* edges toward relief-from-ordeal / self-interest (escape the test) rather than pure grace invitation. The mountain metaphor also lands without setup for a first-time listener.

---

### First-hearing clarity

- *"that same land"* — Moriah/Golgotha link assumed, never named.
- *"A man named John"* — who is John? Unanchored for zero Bible knowledge.
- *"still waiting"* — waiting for what? The lamb? Christ? The payoff in Beat 4 helps, but Beat 2 leaves a new listener hanging.
- *"His Father's hand"* — Father = God is assumed; Abraham-as-Father-typology is not.

---

### Hook, spine, SHORT form

**Hook: strong.** First line grips.

**Spine: holds.** One witness, one thread (lamb → Christ as Lamb), no swap.

**Tightness: under budget.** ~198 spoken words vs. 220–320 spec (~75–85s, not ~90s). Room exists for the missing wrestling — the piece is lean but leaves the steel-man unaddressed rather than trading length for depth.

**Single moment: mostly holds.** Beat 3’s second scene is the sag — not a second thread, but a time-skip that crowds the witnessed moment with unearned omniscience.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Fix the lamb misstatement** — replace *"But I had promised a lamb"* (and header *"Abraham promised"*) with Genesis 22:7–8: Isaac asked where the lamb was; Abraham said **God would provide** a lamb; God gave a **ram** in the son’s stead — the lamb awaited is God’s provision, not Abraham’s pledge.
2. **Add real wrestling in Abraham’s voice** — sustain Beat 1’s *"kill him"* tension (promise vs. command, knife over the son you love, Isaac’s question while you bind him); do not leave lamb-vs-ram taxonomy as the only doubt.
3. **Rebuild Beat 3 from Abraham’s lived horizon** — prophecy/oath/*"still waiting"*/Spirit-light signpost — so the Christ turn is earned without *"Generations later"* omniscience; restore *"God will provide **himself** a lamb"* and name what Christ bore (death/sin/judgment) before the John 1:29 quote lands.
