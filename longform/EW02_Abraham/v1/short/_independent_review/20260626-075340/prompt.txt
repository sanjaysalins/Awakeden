# Abraham and the Lamb — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Abraham, called the friend of God  ·  **Core text:** Genesis 22  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** the binding of Isaac on Moriah — the only beloved son carries the wood up the mountain, a substitute dies in his stead, and the lamb Abraham promised but never found is provided in Christ, the Lamb of God
---

## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Abraham. For three days I climbed a mountain with fire and a knife in my hands, my only son beside me, not knowing if he would come back down. God had asked me for Isaac, in whom my whole promise hung.

## Beat 3 — The act
I laid on Isaac the wood he was to die upon, and let my son carry it. He asked where the lamb was; I could only answer that God would provide Himself a lamb. At the top I bound my boy and laid him on the altar:

**"And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son."**

Then a voice tore open heaven and stayed my hand, and a ram caught in a thicket died in the stead of my son. Isaac rose up alive.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
I had promised the boy a lamb, but God gave a ram; the lamb I named was never found on my mountain. By the light that came long after me, I see what that hill was reaching toward. Generations later a Man taught in Jerusalem and named me:

**[Jesus]** **"Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day: and he saw it, and was glad."**

That Man was Jesus, a beloved, only Son carrying the wood of His offering up that same hill. No voice stopped His Father's hand, no ram was caught for Him; He was the Lamb. John pointed at Him:

**"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."**

He bore the death we had earned, that we might walk free.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
Do not look to me; I was only the father in the shadow, my hand stopped before it fell. Look to the Son whose Father did not stop, who would not come down. The lamb God promised has a name now: Jesus. Come to Him, not because you are driven but because you are wanted, and be carried home.
