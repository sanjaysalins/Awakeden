# Independent review — claude (OK, 106s)

## Findings

**KJV verbatim — clean.** I checked every bolded quote word-for-word: Gen 22:2, 22:5, 22:7, 22:8, 22:10, 22:12, Heb 11:19, John 8:56, John 1:29, Rom 8:32, John 3:16. All eleven are exact KJV, with quote boundaries correctly placed (e.g. Gen 22:8 stops before "so they went both of them together"). No altered words.

**1. Attribution error (EW-INV-4) — `**[the LORD]**` on the Gen 22:2 command.** Genesis 22:1 says *"God did tempt Abraham, and said unto him"* — the command is spoken by **God** (Elohim), not "the LORD." The chapter deliberately reserves "the LORD" for the staying voice (v.11, "the angel of the LORD" — which the artifact tags correctly). Tagging the command "[the LORD]" cuts against the text's own wording. Same slip in Beat 2 prose: *"the LORD had told me plainly that in Isaac shall my seed be called"* — Gen 21:12 is *"And God said unto Abraham."* Same God, so no listener is misled — but this project's bar is textual precision, and it's a two-word fix.

**2. Witness over-claim (the known recurring failure mode).** Beat 2: *"That was the first time in all my years of walking with God that I heard Him speak the word love."* Gen 22:2 is indeed the first occurrence of "love" in Scripture — but the claim as voiced is about Abraham's entire lived experience, which Scripture doesn't record exhaustively. This is exactly the "witness claims beyond what the text assigns" pattern the corpus re-audit flagged. Soften to what the record shows (e.g. "in all the words of His that were ever written down").

**3. Closing metaphor clarity (first-hearing test).** Beat 7: *"Lay your knife down, and receive Him."* The knife was Abraham's — the listener never held one, and the piece never establishes what the listener's knife stands for. A zero-Bible listener will stumble here at the most important moment. Either name what it means (the weight you carry / the payment you're trying to make) or cut it. Also two compressions in the same beat that read as garbled on first hearing: *"Do not look to me — only the father in the shadow, my hand stopped before it fell"* (missing "I was"), and *"not when worthy, but now, freely"* (missing "when you are").

**4. Minor — the climax plays twice.** Beat 3 ends on the knife raised (Gen 22:10 quoted); Beat 4 rewinds and raises it again ("When the knife was lifted, a voice tore open the sky"). The rewind device works and the lamb-riddle payoff justifies it, but the second knife-raise slightly discharges the first. Consider ending Beat 3 a hair earlier so the knife peaks once. Not blocking.

**5. Missed word, not an error.** The echo line flattens *"God will provide **himself** a lamb"* to "God will provide a lamb." "Himself" is the loaded word (God provides Himself the lamb — and, in the fullness, provides Himself *as* the Lamb). Optional but cheap depth.

**What holds:** the hook grips (fire, knife, son, "carry him back or leave him on it"); one witness, one thread (the missing lamb) from Beat 4's riddle straight through John 1:29 — the reveal is genuinely *earned*, not announced. The wrestling (Beat 5) is the real steel-man — God's command contradicting God's own oath — resolved by Heb 11:19, in Abraham's voice. Substitution is explicitly named ("bore the death guilty men had earned — He stood where I deserved to stand"). CTA names Jesus, names the response (come, receive, let Him be your substitute), grace-anchored ("not driven but wanted... freely"), no fear or gain-loss framing, no template close. No supersession. Voice stays in character; no beat sags — the runtime is earned.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the speaker tags to match the text: Gen 22:2 command attributed to **God** (per Gen 22:1), not "[the LORD]"; likewise Beat 2's "the LORD had told me" → "God" for Gen 21:12.
2. Rework the Beat 7 close for first-hearing clarity: define or drop "Lay your knife down," and un-compress "Do not look to me — only the father in the shadow" and "not when worthy."
3. Soften the over-claim "the first time in all my years... I heard Him speak the word love" to what Scripture records (first written mention), and consider restoring "himself" in the lamb echo.
