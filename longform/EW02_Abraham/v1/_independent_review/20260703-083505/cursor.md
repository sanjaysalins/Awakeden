KJV spans checked against `data/kjv_cache.json` (Genesis 22, Hebrews 11:19, John 8:56, John 1:29, Romans 8:32, John 3:16). Default skepticism.

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Quoted text:** Bold strings are verbatim KJV substrings. No altered words inside the quote spans themselves.

**Attribution gaps (inconsistent with the artifact’s own `**[Speaker]**` convention):**

- **`"Abide ye here with the ass; and I and the lad will go yonder and worship, and come again to you."`** — Abraham speaks; no `**[Abraham]**` tag (only *“I said a thing”*).
- **`"My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering."`** — same gap after *“God Himself put it in my mouth.”*
- **`"And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son."`** — third-person Genesis narration; no `**[Scripture]**` frame. Abraham recites Scripture *about* himself in third person — voice/cast rupture.
- **`"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."`** — prose names John, but no `**[John]**` tag before the quote.
- **`"He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things?"`** — only *“Scripture says”*; no Paul / Romans named.
- **`"For God so loved the world… everlasting life."`** — *“the word written of Him”* is thin; Jesus / John 3 is never named as speaker.

**Minor tag precision:** Gen 22:2 is introduced as **`[the LORD]`**, but KJV 22:1–2’s immediate speaker is **God** (*“that God did tempt Abraham, and said… And he said, Take now thy son…”*). Quote is exact; voice ID is slightly off under a strict read.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Passes.** Names Jesus explicitly (*“His name is Jesus”*, *“Come to Him”*). Response is named, not vague: *“receive Him”*, *“let Him be your substitute”*, *“Lay your knife down.”* Grace-anchored: *“not driven but wanted”*, *“not when worthy, but now, freely.”* Not a bare *“will you trust Him?”* template.

**Minor:** *“the fear gone now”* is fine in context; no fear-pressure CTA.

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Earned from Moriah (strong):**

- Wood on Isaac → wood on Christ (*“carried the wood of His own offering up a hill”*).
- Lamb vs ram riddle (*“I had told the boy God would provide a lamb, and God had given a ram”*).
- Substitute logic (*“no ram was caught in the thicket to die in His place… He was the substitute”*).

**Partly announced / imported:**

- *“I tell you now from the far side of my life, by the light that came long after me… what that mountain was **always reaching toward**.”* — lecture frame; the turn is **claimed**, not discovered in-scene.
- **`"Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day: and he saw it, and was glad."`** → *“the day I **glimpsed from afar on Moriah** was His”* — John 8:56 does not mention Moriah, binding, wood, or a mountain. The Moriah↔“my day” link is **retrofitted onto Jesus’ words**, not demonstrated from what Abraham lived.
- Beat 6 is largely an **NT quotation montage** (John 8:56 → John 1:29 → Rom 8:32) doing proof work the mountain should close first.

**Internal contradiction that weakens the earned lamb thread:**

- Beat 4 (fixed): *“I had **told** the boy God would provide a lamb”*
- Beat 6 (regression): *“The lamb I had **promised** Isaac and never found on my mountain”*

That slips a personal pledge back in and fights Beat 4’s precision.

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**The intellectual objection is real:** *“The same God who swore the promise would run through Isaac was now telling me to end Isaac's life. If the boy died, the oath died with him.”*

**Structural failure — wrestling comes after deliverance:**

Beat 4 already resolves: voice stops the hand, ram dies, *“Isaac stood up alive,”* *“I came down that mountain delivered.”* **Then** Beat 5 opens: *“I will not pretend the climb was simple faith with no war underneath it.”* The doubt lands **after** the listener has exhaled.

**Timeline contradiction inside Beat 5:**

- End of Beat 5: *“Even there, **with the knife in my fist**, I was not the point.”*
- Beat 4 already had the voice stop the knife and the ram offered. Abraham cannot be *“with the knife in my fist”* at the summit **and** simultaneously post-deliverance. The wrestling is written for the ascent but **placed after** the summit.

**Under-wrestled for long-form:**

- Beat 2 already states *“Kill him, and the promise died with him.”* Beat 5 **repeats** the same tension with no new turn until Hebrews 11:19 arrives.
- Resolution is **imported**: *“The later Scripture says it of me more kindly than I could”* + Heb 11:19 — commentary, not Abraham’s fought-through reasoning on the path.
- **Missing steel-man the piece itself raises:** Isaac’s *“where is the lamb?”* and Abraham’s answer never wrestle the live doubt: *Did I speak beyond what I could see? Did God give a ram when I said a lamb?*
- **Missing emotional steel-man:** horror at binding his own son; Gen 22:12 *“fearest God”* vs Gen 22:2 *“whom thou **lovest**”* is never internalized in Abraham’s voice.

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Sound core:** Substitution explicit (*“in the stead of my son”*, *“He was the substitute”*, *“bore the death guilty men had earned”*, *“taketh away the sin of the world”*). No supersession. Beat 7 correctly casts Abraham as *“the father in the shadow, my hand stopped before it fell.”*

**Precision problems:**

1. *“I had **no other**”* — Ishmael existed. KJV’s *“thine only son”* is covenant-specific; *“no other”* reads literally false and erodes trust for anyone with partial Bible knowledge.
2. *“The lamb I had **promised** Isaac”* (Beat 6) — misstates Gen 22:8 after Beat 4 corrected it; Abraham did not promise, he said God would provide.
3. *“He stood where **I** deserved to stand, where **you** deserve to stand”* — On Moriah **Isaac** was on the wood; Abraham held the knife (Father-shadow). *“Where I deserved to stand”* blurs Isaac-as-offered-son with Abraham-as-guilty-sinner in one breath. Substitution for **sin** is true of the listener; Abraham-as-typological-Father is not “where I deserved to stand” on the altar.
4. Rom 8:32 — *“freely give us all things”* — gain-adjacent language dropped into the reveal beat (not the CTA, but sloppy placement for grace-anchored discipline).

**Interpretive stretch (not heresy, not in the text):** *“That was the first time in all my years of walking with God that I heard Him speak the word love”* — creative reading of *“whom thou lovest,”* not something Abraham could know from that phrase alone.

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strong:** *“I am Abraham… three days I walked with fire and a knife in my own hands”*; *“I would not let the morning catch me arguing with God”*; *“Come again to you. Both of us.”*; Beat 7’s father-shadow framing.

**Essay-narrator slips:**

- *“and **the Scripture** does not soften what came next”* — meta voice; Abraham would not call his own moment “the Scripture.”
- *“**The later Scripture** says it of me more kindly than I could”* — same rupture; breaks period voice.
- *“Hold on to that word — for here is the riddle that followed me home”* — writer addressing the audience.
- Beat 6: *“I tell you now from the far side of my life… by the word that was written after I was dust”* — theology lecture, not lived memory (signposted cross-time is allowed per EW-INV-11, but the **tone** still sags into essay).

---

### First-hearing clarity

**Clear:** Three-day journey, wood/fire/knife, ram dies / Isaac lives, substitution landing.

**Friction:**

- *“in Isaac shall my seed be called”* — no gloss for zero-Bible listeners.
- *“Moriah”* never explained.
- Ishmael vs *“I had no other”* / *“only son”* — will confuse.
- Beat 3 ends at the raised knife; Beat 4 rewinds (*“come back down the hill with me a moment”*) then *“When the knife was lifted, a voice tore open the sky”* — a new listener may think **two knife moments** unless they track the flashback carefully.
- *“in a figure”* (Heb 11:19) — opaque without a one-line gloss.

---

### Hook, spine, long-form pacing

**Hook:** Strong — fire, knife, son, three days, uncertain outcome.

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (Moriah → lamb → Christ). No swap.

**Runtime earning:**

| Beat | Verdict |
|------|---------|
| 1–3 | Earns — weight, obedience, horror |
| 4 | Earns the lamb/ram riddle, but **packs angel + ram resolution before wrestling** |
| 5 | **Sags** — misplaced after deliverance; repeats Beat 2; thin for ~9–11 min |
| 6 | Dense NT montage; **repeats** wood/lamb/substitution from 3–4 |
| 7 | Strong CTA, but **recaps** Beat 6 instead of adding a new turn |

Spoken body is roughly **~1,250–1,350 words** — at or **below** the LONG floor (1300–1650). Combined with Beat 5 thinness and Beat 6–7 repetition, some length is recap, not new turns. Unlikely to earn a full **9–11 min** without sag unless read very slowly.

TOP FIXES:
1. **Reorder Beats 4–5:** Move the steel-man (promise-death contradiction, binding horror, lamb-vs-ram doubt) **before** angel/ram deliverance; cut the post-resolution wrestling that contradicts *“with the knife in my fist.”*
2. **Fix the lamb-thread contradiction:** Beat 6 *“The lamb I had **promised** Isaac”* must match Beat 4 (*“I **told** the boy God would provide a lamb”*); add `**[Abraham]**` / `**[John]**` / `**[Scripture]**` tags on every bold quote per EW-INV-4.
3. **Re-earn the reveal from Moriah first:** Let wood, stopped hand, ram *“in the stead,”* and unanswered lamb click into Christ **in Abraham’s dawning voice** before the NT montage; drop or sharply shorten *“the day I glimpsed from afar on Moriah was His”* unless earned from the lamb riddle; remove *“where I deserved to stand”* (keep substitution on guilty men / the listener).
