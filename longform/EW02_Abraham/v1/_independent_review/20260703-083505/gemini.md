- **The WRESTLING (EW-G8):** Outstanding. *"It made no sense that the God who cannot lie would command the death of His own word."* This is the exact, biblical steel-man of Abraham's trial (Hebrews 11), internalized perfectly in character.
- **The REVEAL is earned (EW-G7):** Extremely effective. - **CTA-on-Jesus (EW-INV-2):** Cleared on doctrine and grace-anchored tone, but contains a flawed metaphor (see fix #1). 

VERDICT: PASS
TOP FIXES:
1. **Fix the mixed metaphor in the CTA:** *"Lay your knife down, and receive Him"* (Beat 7). For Abraham, holding the knife was the ultimate act of faithful obedience to God. Asking the listener to "lay your knife down" accidentally flips the symbol, implying the listener is holding a weapon of rebellion, self-righteousness, or hostility. Rephrase this to focus on the listener not needing to provide their own sacrifice or climb their own hill, keeping the imagery aligned.
2. **Correct the narrative consistency of the question:** *"So I leave you the question I carried up that hill... where is the lamb?"* (Beat 7). Isaac asked the question on the way *up*; Abraham carried the unfulfilled answer ("God will provide") back *down* the hill. Tweak this line to reflect that Abraham carried the *waiting* or the *search* down the mountain, passing that anticipation to the listener. 
3. **Smooth the 4th-wall break in Beat 3/5:** In Beat 3, Abraham quotes Genesis 22:10 (*"and the Scripture does not soften what came next:"*). In Beat 5, he quotes Hebrews 11 (*"The later Scripture says it of me..."*). While quoting the NT from the "far side of life" works well in Beat 5 to explain the type/shadow, quoting his own actions from the Genesis narrator in Beat 3 momentarily pulls him out of the first-person immersion. Consider rewriting the Beat 3 lead-in to simply own the action: *"I bound Isaac my son, and laid him on the altar upon the wood, and I did what had to be done: **'And Abraham stretched forth his hand...'**"*
