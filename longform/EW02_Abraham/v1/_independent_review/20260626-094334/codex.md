# Independent review — codex (OK, 95s)

**Findings**

- **EW-INV-4 attribution failures:** several bold KJV quotes are exact in wording but lack required source/speaker attribution before the quote: **"Abide ye here with the ass..."**, **"My son, God will provide himself a lamb..."**, **"Accounting that God was able..."**, **"Behold the Lamb of God..."**, **"He that spared not his own Son..."**, and **"For God so loved the world..."**.
- **Misattribution:** **[the LORD]** before **"Take now thy son..."** is not the KJV speaker label in Genesis 22:1-2. The text says **God** spoke.
- **Reveal overclaims:** **"The Son Himself named it"** followed by John 8:56 overstates the verse. Jesus says Abraham saw His day; He does not explicitly identify Genesis 22/Moriah there.
- **Ram/lamb riddle is overpressed:** **"I had promised the boy a lamb, and God had given a ram... The lamb I had named had not yet been brought."** This risks implying God did not really answer Abraham on Moriah. The immediate text presents the ram as the provided substitute.
- **Doctrine risk:** **"On His mountain the Father did not stay His hand"** maps Abraham’s knife too directly onto the Father, risking a crude “Father as executioner” framing. The piece needs the Son’s willing self-offering named clearly.
- **CTA is close but weak:** **"Will you let Him be your substitute"** is not the cleanest named evangelical response. Christ is the substitute; the hearer is called to believe, receive, come, or turn to Him.
- **Voice slips into sermon narrator:** **"by the word that was written after I was dust"**, **"The later Scripture says"**, and **"Scripture says"** make Abraham sound like a detached Bible expositor rather than a first-person witness.
- **Long-form sag:** Beat 7 repeats Beat 6’s reveal: **"ram died in Isaac's stead"**, **"carried the wood"**, **"the Lamb I never found"**. The close needs a new turn into invitation, not another summary of the type.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix all KJV quote attributions, especially Genesis 22:2, and label every quoted source/speaker.
2. Rework the reveal so John 8:56 and the ram/lamb typology are not overstated beyond the text.
3. Rewrite the invitation to name the response plainly: come to Jesus, believe in Him, receive Him by grace.
