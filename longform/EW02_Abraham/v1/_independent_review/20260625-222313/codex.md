# Independent review — codex (OK, 73s)

**Findings**

- EW-INV-4 issue: `**[the LORD]** **"Lay not thine hand upon the lad..."**` is attributed too bluntly. In KJV Genesis 22:11-12 the speaker is “the angel of the LORD,” not simply “the LORD.” Strict attribution fails here.

- EW-INV-4 issue: `**"My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering: so they went both of them together."**` mixes Abraham’s speech with narration. `“so they went both of them together”` is not something Abraham says to Isaac.

- EW-INV-4 issue: `**"He that spared not his own Son..."**` and `**"For God so loved the world..."**` are KJV-exact, but not attributed before quotation. The piece needs named source/speaker attribution before every quoted Scripture.

- Doctrine issue: substitution is present but not sharp enough on penalty. `“Jesus the Lamb died in the stead of guilty men — He stood where I deserved to stand...”` names replacement, but not explicitly the penalty/judgment/curse for sin that Christ bore. The lens asks for penalty + what Christ bore.

- Possible overclaim: `“in that same country”` and `“God provided Him on His”` lean toward a Moriah-to-Calvary geography claim without proving it from the text. Typology is valid, but the wording risks sounding more precise than Scripture warrants.

- Voice issue: the opening `“The world remembers me as...”` sounds like an essay narrator introducing Abraham, not Abraham speaking from lived memory. Later, `“by the word that was written after I was dust”` also makes the witness vantage feel mechanically theological rather than characterful.

- First-hearing clarity issue: `“in the stead,” “in a figure,” “Jehovah-jireh,”` and `“the mount of the LORD it shall be seen”` are not explained enough for a zero-Bible listener. The emotional thread is followable, but several key theological terms are left to church familiarity.

- Long-form sag: Beat 6 repeats the same typological correspondences several times: `“only Son, a beloved Son,” “carried the wood,” “no substitute for Him,” “He was the substitute,” “the lamb...God provided.”` Strong material, but the reveal starts to restate rather than advance.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix all Scripture attribution problems, especially the angel of the LORD quote and mixed Abraham/narrator quote.
2. Explicitly name the penalty for sin that Christ bore in substitution.
3. Tighten Beat 6 so the reveal escalates instead of repeating the same type-to-Christ links.
