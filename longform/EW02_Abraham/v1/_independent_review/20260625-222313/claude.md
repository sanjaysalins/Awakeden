# Independent review — claude (OK, 93s)

I read the full narration and checked every `**"..."**` quote against KJV. Findings below, most concrete first.

## KJV verbatim — solid, with one real attribution defect

I verified all twelve quotes. Every one is **exact KJV** (Gen 22:2, 22:5, 22:10, 22:7, 22:8, 22:12; Heb 11:19; John 8:56, 1:29; Rom 8:32; John 3:16; Gen 22:14). No altered words. Good.

**But one quote attributes narrative prose to Abraham's mouth.** Beat 4:

> What rose up in me I had not planned; I believe God Himself put it in my mouth:
> **"My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering: so they went both of them together."**

The clause **"so they went both of them together"** is the narrator's prose in Gen 22:8, NOT words Abraham *spoke*. The setup ("put it in my mouth") frames the entire quoted span as Abraham's speech. It's verbatim KJV, but it misattributes narrative to the speaker. Fix: end Abraham's spoken quote at "...for a burnt offering." and drop or paraphrase the travel clause outside the quote marks. This is the one defect that actually trips EW-INV-4.

**Minor attribution nits (defensible, but flag-worthy under an adversarial lens):**
- Beat 4 tags Gen 22:12 as **[the LORD]**, but the speaker in the text is *the angel of the LORD* (Gen 22:11). It speaks *as* the LORD ("from me"), so it's tolerable — but a strict reviewer would want "the angel of the LORD."
- Beat 2 tags Gen 22:2 **[the LORD]**; that verse uses *God* (Elohim). Tiny.

- **Substitution is named explicitly** (EW-G9 ✓): "Jesus the Lamb died in the stead of guilty men — He stood where I deserved to stand, where you deserve to stand — and we walk free." Penalty (death for guilt) + what Christ bore are both present. No supersession; it's clean typology.
- **The reveal is earned** (EW-G7 ✓): the lamb/ram riddle is planted in Beat 4 ("I had promised the boy a lamb, and God had given a ram… The lamb I had named had not yet been brought") and paid off in Beat 6 by John 1:29. This is genuinely shown, not announced. The seh-vs-ayil (lamb≠ram) distinction is a real, recognized reading, not contrarian invention. Best thing in the piece.
- **Wrestling is the real steel-man** (EW-G8 ✓): Beat 5 names the genuine strongest objection — "the God who cannot lie would command the death of His own word" — and resolves it via Heb 11:19 resurrection logic, in Abraham's own voice. Not a strawman.
