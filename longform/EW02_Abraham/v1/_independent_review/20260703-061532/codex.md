# Independent review — codex (OK, 92s)

**Findings**

- EW-INV-4: `**[the LORD]** **"Take now thy son..."**` has exact KJV wording, but the immediate Genesis 22 speaker is `God`, not `the LORD`. Under strict attribution, this is misattributed.
- EW-INV-4: `**[the LORD]** **"Lay not thine hand upon the lad..."**` should be attributed to `the angel of the LORD` from Genesis 22:11, not simply `the LORD`.
- EW-INV-4: `**"He that spared not his own Son..."**`, `**"For God so loved the world..."**`, and `**"In the mount of the LORD it shall be seen."**` have no clear speaker/source attribution before the quote. The words are KJV, but the attribution rule is not consistently followed.
- EW-G9: Substitution is present, but the penalty is under-named. `Jesus the Lamb died in the stead of guilty men` and `He stood where I deserved to stand` are close, but the piece never plainly says what He bore: sin’s guilt, judgment, curse, wrath, or penalty.
- EW-G7: The reveal is mostly earned through `only son`, `wood`, `ram...in the stead`, and `lamb`, but `the day I glimpsed from afar on Moriah was His` overstates John 8:56. Jesus says Abraham saw His day; the text does not explicitly identify that sight as Moriah.
- EW-G7 / doctrine precision: `The lamb I had promised Isaac and never found on my mountain` risks implying Genesis 22’s provision was somehow incomplete or failed. The typology can work, but this phrasing presses the lamb/ram distinction too hard.
- EW-G10: The voice sometimes becomes an essay-sermon narrator rather than Abraham. Phrases like `The later Scripture says it of me more kindly than I could` and `by the word that was written after I was dust` are meta-commentary, not strongly embodied eyewitness narration.
- Long-form sag: Beat 2 and Beat 5 repeat the same objection: `Kill him, and the promise died with him` and `If the boy died, the oath died with him.` One of these needs a new emotional or theological turn, otherwise it pads.
- CTA: The close does name Jesus and names response with `Come to Him` and `Trust the Son`. It is grace-anchored. Weakness: `be carried home` is vague after the clearer command; end on the named response, not a soft metaphor.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix KJV attribution, especially `God` / `the angel of the LORD`, and source every quote.
2. Name the penalty Christ bore, not only the substitution.
3. Tighten the reveal so John 8:56 supports the typology without overclaiming that Moriah is explicitly named.
