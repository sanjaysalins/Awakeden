## EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Quoted text:** Spot-checked bold spans — Gen 22:2, 22:5, 22:7, 22:8 (partial), 22:10, 22:12, 22:14; Heb 11:19; John 8:56; John 1:29; Rom 8:32; John 3:16. No altered words inside the quote strings themselves.

**Misattribution (real defect):**

- **`[the LORD]`** before **`"Lay not thine hand upon the lad, neither do thou any thing unto him…"`** — In KJV Genesis 22:11–12 the speaker is **the angel of the LORD**, not the LORD directly. Tagging this as `[the LORD]` misroutes voice and misstates who spoke on the mountain.

**Attribution gaps (inconsistent with the artifact’s own `[Speaker]` convention):**

- **`"Abide ye here with the ass; and I and the lad will go yonder and worship, and come again to you."`** — Abraham speaks; no `**[Abraham]**` tag.
- **`"My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering."`** — same gap.
- **`"And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son."`** — third-person narrative about himself; no `**[Scripture]**` frame. Voice rupture: Abraham recites Scripture narrating *“Abraham… his son.”*
- **`"Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."`** — preceded by *“a man named John pointed straight at Him”* but no `**[John]**` tag.
- **`"He that spared not his own Son… freely give us all things?"`** — no named source (Paul / Romans) before the quote.
- **`"For God so loved the world… everlasting life."`** — no attribution (Jesus in John 3).
- **`"In the mount of the LORD it shall be seen."`** — introduced only as *“the word over it has come true”*; no book/speaker named.

**Non-quote inaccuracy that feeds the central lamb thread:**

- *“I had **promised** the boy a lamb, and God had given a ram”* — Abraham did **not** promise; he said **`"God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering."`** This slips prophecy into a personal pledge and misstates the scene. Beat 7 then says *“The lamb **God** promised has been provided”* — so the piece contradicts itself on who promised the lamb.

## EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Earned from Moriah (strong):**

- Wood on Isaac’s shoulders → wood on Christ (*“carried the wood of His own offering up a hill”*).
- Lamb vs ram (*“God had given a ram… The lamb I had named had not yet been brought”*).
- Substitute logic (*“no ram was caught in the thicket… He was the substitute”*; *“died in the stead of guilty men”*).

**Partly announced, not shown:**

- *“I tell you now from the far side of my life, by the light that came long after me… what that mountain was **always reaching toward**.”* — lecture frame; the turn is **claimed**, not discovered in-scene.
- **`"Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day: and he saw it, and was glad."`** → *“the day I **glimpsed from afar** on Moriah was His”* — John 8:56 does not mention Moriah, binding, wood, or a mountain. The Moriah↔“my day” link is **retrofitted onto Jesus’ words**, not demonstrated from what Abraham lived.
- Beat 6 is largely an **NT quotation montage** (John 8:56 → John 1:29 → Rom 8:32) doing proof work the mountain should close first. The new Beat 7 Jehovah-jireh callback helps, but it arrives **after** the montage, not as the earned click.

## EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**The intellectual objection is real:** *“The same God who swore the promise would run through Isaac was now telling me to end Isaac's life. If the boy died, the oath died with him.”*

**Structural failure — wrestling comes after deliverance:**

Beat 4 already resolves the story: voice stops the hand, ram dies, *“Isaac stood up alive,”* *“I came down that mountain delivered.”* **Then** Beat 5 opens: *“I will not pretend the climb was simple faith with no war underneath it.”* The doubt lands **after** the happy ending. That deflates the steel-man; a listener has already exhaled.

**Timeline contradiction inside Beat 5:**

- End of Beat 5: *“Even there, **with the knife in my fist**, I was not the point.”*
- Beat 4 already had the voice stop the knife and the ram offered. Abraham cannot be *“with the knife in my fist”* and simultaneously post-deliverance. The wrestling is written for the ascent but placed after the summit.

**Under-wrestled for long-form (~9–11 min):**

- Beat 2 already states *“Kill him, and the promise died with him.”* Beat 5 **repeats** the same tension with no new turn until Hebrews 11:19 arrives.
- Resolution is **imported**: *“The later Scripture says it of me more kindly than I could”* + Heb 11:19 — commentary, not Abraham’s fought-through reasoning on the path.
- **Missing steel-man the piece itself raises:** Isaac’s *“where is the lamb?”* and Abraham’s answer never wrestle the live doubt: *Did I speak beyond what I could see? Did I mislead my son when God gave a ram, not a lamb?*
- **Missing emotional steel-man:** horror at binding his own son; the Gen 22:12 *“fearest God”* vs Gen 22:2 *“whom thou **lovest**”* collision is never internalized.

## EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Sound core:** Substitution explicit (*“in the stead of my son”*, *“He was the substitute”*, *“died in the stead of guilty men”*, *“taketh away the sin of the world”*). No supersession. Abraham-as-Father-shadow is clear in Beat 7 (*“Look instead to the Father whose hand was not stopped”*).

**Precision problems:**

1. *“I had **no other**”* — conflicts with Ishmael. KJV’s *“thine only son”* is covenant-specific; *“no other”* reads literally false and erodes trust for anyone with partial Bible knowledge.
2. *“I had **promised** the boy a lamb”* — misstates Gen 22:8 (see above); fights Beat 7’s *“The lamb **God** promised.”*
3. *“He stood where **I** deserved to stand, where **you** deserve to stand”* — On Moriah **Isaac** was on the wood; Abraham held the knife. Beat 7 correctly casts Abraham as *“the father in the shadow.”* *“Where I deserved to stand”* blurs Isaac-as-offered-son with Abraham-as-guilty-sinner in one breath.
4. **Penalty named but thin for zero-Bible listeners:** “sin” and “guilty men” appear; **death/judgment as the specific penalty Christ bore** is implied by knife/wood but never stated plainly in one sentence.
5. Rom 8:32 — *“freely give us all things”* — gain-adjacent language in the reveal (not the CTA, but sloppy placement).

**Interpretive stretch (not heresy, not in the text):** *“That was the first time in all my years of walking with God that I heard Him speak the word love”* — creative reading of *“whom thou lovest.”*

## EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strong:** *“I am Abraham… three days I walked with fire and a knife in my own hands”*; *“I would not let the morning catch me arguing with God”*; *“Come again to you. Both of us.”*; Beat 7’s *“Look instead to the Father… and to the Son who let the wood be laid on Him and chose not to come down.”*

**Essay-narrator slips:**

- *“and **the Scripture** does not soften what came next”* — meta voice; Abraham would not call his own moment “the Scripture.”
- *“**The later Scripture** says it of me more kindly than I could”* — same rupture.
- *“Hold on to that word — for here is the riddle that followed me home”* — writer addressing the audience.
- Beat 6: *“I tell you now from the far side of my life… by the word that was written after I was dust”* — theology lecture, not lived memory.

## First-hearing clarity

**Clear:** Three-day journey, wood/fire/knife, ram dies / Isaac lives, substitution landing.

**Friction:**

- **Flashback / double knife:** Beat 3 ends at the raised knife; Beat 4 rewinds (*“come back down the hill with me a moment”*) then says *“When the knife was lifted, a voice tore open the sky”* — a new listener may think **two different knife moments**.
- *“in Isaac shall my seed be called”* — no gloss for zero-Bible listeners.
- *“Moriah”* never explained.
- Ishmael vs *“only son”* / *“I had no other”* — will confuse.

## Hook, spine, long-form pacing

**Hook:** Strong — fire, knife, son, three days, uncertain outcome.

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (Moriah → lamb → Christ). No swap.

**Runtime earning:**

| Beat | Verdict |
|------|---------|
| 1–3 | Earns — weight, obedience, horror |
| 4 | Earns the lamb/ram riddle, but **packs angel + ram resolution before wrestling** |
| 5 | **Sags** — misplaced after deliverance; repeats Beat 2; thin for long-form |
| 6 | Dense NT montage; **repeats** wood/lamb/substitution from 3–4 |
| 7 | Improved Jehovah-jireh tie-in, but arrives after Beat 6 has already preached the turn |

Spoken body is roughly **~1,650–1,700 words** — at or slightly above the LONG ceiling. Combined with Beat 5 thinness, some of that length is repetition, not new turns.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Reorder Beats 4–5:** Move the steel-man (promise-death contradiction, knife horror, doubt about *“God will provide a lamb”* after the ram) **before** angel/ram deliverance; cut the post-resolution wrestling that contradicts *“with the knife in my fist.”*
2. **Fix lamb-thread precision + misattribution:** Change *“I had promised the boy a lamb”* to *“I told him God would provide a lamb”* (align with Beat 7); tag Gen 22:12 as **`[the angel of the LORD]`** not `[the LORD]`; add missing `**[Abraham]**` / `**[John]**` / Scripture tags on every bold quote.
3. **Re-earn the reveal from Moriah first:** Let wood, stopped hand, ram *“in the stead,”* and unanswered lamb click into Christ **in Abraham’s dawning voice** before the NT montage; drop or sharply shorten *“the day I glimpsed from afar on Moriah was His”* unless earned from the lamb riddle; remove *“where I deserved to stand”* (keep substitution on guilty men / the listener).
