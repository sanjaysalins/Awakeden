# Independent review — gemini (OK, 65s)

This is a strong, gripping narrative that beautifully executes the type-to-Christ reveal (EW-G7) and captures the genuine wrestling of Abraham (EW-G8). The CTA is grace-anchored and names the response perfectly (EW-INV-2). 

However, it fails the strict KJV verbatim and attribution rules (EW-INV-4) in three specific places:

1. **Logical/Verbatim Error in Spoken Quote (Beat 4):**
You wrote: `I believe God Himself put it in my mouth: **"My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering: so they went both of them together."**`
The phrase "so they went both of them together" is the Genesis narrator's description of their action, not words Abraham spoke out loud to Isaac. Including it inside Abraham's spoken dialogue is a major error.

2. **Missing Attribution (Beat 6):**
You wrote: `God spared my Isaac. He did not spare His own: **"He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all..."**`
This quotes Paul (Romans 8:32), but you failed to attribute it to the apostle or Scripture before quoting. "He did not spare His own" is a thematic lead-in, not a speaker attribution.

3. **Missing Attribution (Beat 7):**
You wrote: `this is the love I only ever saw in figure, now given in full: **"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son..."**`
This quotes John 3:16, but you failed to attribute it to Jesus or Scripture before quoting.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the narrator's action ("so they went both of them together.") from Abraham's spoken dialogue in Beat 4.
2. Add explicit attribution to Paul or Scripture before quoting Romans 8:32 in Beat 6.
3. Add explicit attribution to Jesus or Scripture before quoting John 3:16 in Beat 7.
