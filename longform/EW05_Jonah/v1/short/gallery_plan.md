# Gallery Plan — EW05 The Sign of Jonah (Awakeden eyewitness SHORT)

**Witness:** Jonah, son of Amittai · **Core text:** Jonah 1–2 + Matthew 12:40
**Engine:** LOCKED gallery hard-cut — ONE rich Baroque oil painting per beat; whole canvas seen, then hard-cuts to NAMED expressive anchors; later animated as a Kling "gallery tour." Must LAND ON THE RISEN CHRIST.
**Thread:** Three days buried in the deep, then delivered up alive — a shadow of the death, burial, and resurrection of Christ (Matt 12:40). Jonah is the SIGN, never the substitute: he went down GUILTY for his own sin; Christ went down INNOCENT, bearing ours, and rose the third day.
**Arc:** Hook → strange thing → turn → punch. 8 paintings across ~70s.

## Painting 1 — Hurled into the sea
- **[beat]** Hook · **[time]** 0–10s · **[NEW]**
- **subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. A bearded Hebrew prophet flung backward off the gunwale of a wooden ancient ship into a black churning sea at the moment of release; two storm-lashed sailors' arms still extended from the throw, their faces turned away in shadow; a single torch-shaft of light rakes across the prophet's terrified upturned face and falling body; towering black wave-wall rising behind; heavy negative space of dark water below. ≤3 faces, crowd in shadow.
- **named elements (tour order):** 1) the prophet's terrified upturned face · 2) the sailors' releasing hands at the gunwale · 3) the prophet's body mid-fall against the wave-wall · 4) the torch-shaft of light on the black water
- **lands on Christ:** the willing going-down to spare others foreshadows the One who would give Himself for the many.

## Painting 3 — Fleeing God; Nineveh he hated
- **[beat]** Strange thing · **[time]** 18–28s · **[NEW — multi-story canvas]**
- **subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. ## Painting 6 — The turn: Jesus names the sign

### Christ-thread map (every painting points forward, the last IS Him)
1 willing descent → 2 burial → 3 mercy too wide → 4 the guilty one named → 5 three days in tomb-dark → 6 Jesus claims the sign → 7 the innocent's grave → 8 RISEN CHRIST (reuse).

### Doctrine note
- Jonah is the SIGN/type, NEVER the substitute. He went down GUILTY (his own sin/storm); Christ went down INNOCENT, bearing ours. The script and plan keep this distinction explicit (paintings 4 vs 7).
- Matt 12:40 is voiced VERBATIM KJV by Jesus in the narration; painting 6 depicts Him naming it.
- The cross-time leap ("long after I was dust") is signposted — Jonah never claims he understood the resurrection while in the deep. Plan honours this: the resurrection paintings (7–8) belong to the narrator's revealed turn, not to Jonah's in-fish knowledge.
