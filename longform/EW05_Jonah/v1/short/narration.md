# The Sign of Jonah — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Jonah, the son of Amittai, prophet of Israel  ·  **Core text:** Jonah 1–2 (with Matthew 12:40)  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** three days in the deep then delivered up alive — a shadow of the death, burial, and resurrection of Christ
**Status:** v1 — Awakeden eyewitness SHORT (B1→B3→B6→B7). Jesus voices Matt 12:40 (verbatim KJV — He names the sign Himself). Cross-time reveal SIGNPOSTED (Jonah speaks "from the far side," never claims he understood the resurrection in the deep). Substitution named: Jonah went down guilty for his own sin; Christ went down innocent, bearing ours, and rose the third day. Jonah is the SIGN, never the substitute.
---

## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Jonah, the son of Amittai — the prophet the sea swallowed whole and gave back alive. I ran from God because I feared His mercy was too wide: I would sooner drown than watch Him spare the city I hated.

## Beat 3 — The act
When the storm rose to break us, the lot fell on me. I knew one thing would still that sea, so I sentenced myself:

**"Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you."**

The deep closed over my head — yet I did not drown. The LORD had prepared a great fish, and three days and three nights I lay buried alive in its belly.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
I did not understand those days in my own day. I tell you now, by the mouth of the One who claimed them. When scribes demanded a sign, the Lord Jesus gave them only my name:

**[Jesus]** **"For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth."**

I went down guilty, for my own sin; He went down innocent, bearing ours. The penalty I had earned, He took in my place. On the third day the grave could no more hold Him than the deep could hold me. He rose. My fish was the shadow; His empty tomb, the substance.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
I ran from grace, and was hunted down by it in the deep. If you are in your own dark, sure you have fallen too far — that is how far His mercy reaches. Turn from your own way and come to Jesus — who died in your place and rose the third day. Come up out of the deep, and live.
