## Independent adversarial review — *The Sign of Jonah* (eyewitness-short)

Judging only the pasted artifact, not prior drafts.

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution — **PASS (one soft characterization)**

Both quoted spans match `passage.txt` verbatim:

- **"Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you."** — Jonah 1:12. Preceded by *"I sentenced myself:"* — acceptable first-person attribution.
- **"For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth."** — Matthew 12:40. Preceded by **`[Jesus]`** — correct.

**Soft note (not inside-quote):** *"When scribes demanded a sign, Jesus gave only my name"* compresses Matthew 12:39–40. Jesus gives *"the sign of the prophet Jonas"* and cites the three-day parallel — not literally “only my name.” Loose framing around an otherwise clean quote.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus — **PASS (minor edge)**

- Names Jesus: *"Come to Jesus, who died in your place and rose the third day."*
- Response is named: *"Will you stop running?"* / *"Come to Jesus"* — not a bare *"will you trust Him?"* template.
- Grace-anchored close: *"sure you've fallen too far — that is how far His mercy reaches"* — mercy, not fear pressure.

**Edge:** *"Come up out of the deep, and live"* can read slightly self-preservation/gain framing unless the listener already heard mercy as the gift. Not a fail, but it sits right on the line.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned — **weak / partly asserted**

Matthew 12:40 is the right hinge — Jesus Himself claims Jonah’s days. But the turn still leans on **announcement + commentary** more than on Jonah’s lived memory paying off first.

**Announced frame:**
- *"I tell you now, by the mouth of the One who claimed those days"* — cross-time reveal is allowed (EW-INV-11), but this line tells the listener the turn is coming from outside Jonah’s moment before the belly material does the lifting.

**Asserted typology, not walked out:**
- *"He went down innocent, bearing ours; no one cast Him in, He gave Himself"* — orthodox and useful, but it **states** the Christ-side contrast; it does not **show** it from what Jonah survived (except the prior guilty/innocent gloss).
- *"bearing ours"* — **what** ours? Sin? guilt? judgment? The lens wants the penalty named; this stays vague.

**Partial earning (credit where due):**
- *"so for their sake I went down"* — the sailors’ substitute pattern is in Beat 3; good seed, but Beat 6 never explicitly harvests it (*I went down for them / He went down for us*).
- *"The grave could no more hold Him than the deep held me"* — one of the few lines that bridges deliverance from lived parallel.

**Hook–reveal gap:**
- Beat 1 plants: *"I feared His mercy was too wide"* / Nineveh hatred.
- Beat 6 delivers: death–burial–resurrection typology.
- Matthew 12:41’s Nineveh-repentance thread is never touched. Beat 7 partially recovers mercy (*"The mercy I fled as too wide chased…"*) but Beat 6’s reveal and Beat 1’s hook are **two different “signs of Jonah,”** only stitched at the close.

**Core text underused:** Jonah 2’s burial/resurrection language — *"the earth with her bars was about me for ever"*, *"thou brought up my life from corruption"* — is the text that would **show** the turn from inside the moment. Beat 3 compresses it to *"Cast out of His sight, I cried out of the belly of hell, and He heard"* (one allusive line, no quoted KJV, no dramatized “bars/corruption/brought up”). Then Beat 6 jumps to scribes and Jesus. The reveal rides on the NT quote + exposition, not on Jonah’s deep memory landing first.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man) — **FAIL / wrong objection**

Beat 1 names Jonah’s real scandal: *"I feared His mercy was too wide"* / *"sooner drown than see it spared."*

Beat 3 shifts to a **different** doubt: *"I had chosen death — why was I breathing?"*

That is real Jonah 2 tension (chose the sea, yet preserved in a fish), but it is **not** the steel-man the hook planted. The stronger Jonah objection — internalized — would sound like: *If God’s mercy reaches Nineveh, what becomes of justice? Why spare the city I hated — and why spare me, the runaway, before them?*

The piece never lets Jonah wrestle **mercy-to-enemies** from the belly. The hook’s steel is dropped for a survival puzzle, then abandoned before the reveal. That is a strawman swap, not steel-man wrestling.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine — **PASS with reservation**

- Substitution contrast is drawn correctly: Jonah guilty / Christ innocent; *"died in your place"*; Jonah is sign, not savior — sound.
- No supersession, no contrarian reading.
- **Reservation:** *"bearing ours"* and *"died in your place"* name substitution but not the **penalty** (sin, guilt, judgment, wrath). Under this lens, substitution is present but under-specified.

**Minor factual compression:** *"the prophet the sea swallowed and gave back alive"* — the sea cast him in; the **fish** held him three days and vomited him out (Jonah 1:17; 2:10). Poetic, not heresy, but a zero-Bible listener may not know what literally happened.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character — **mixed**

**Strong:** Beat 1 is characterful — mercy-hatred is a real Jonah angle, not generic prophet costume.

**Breaks:**
- Beat 7 third-person slip: *"The mercy I fled as too wide chased this runaway to the sea's floor and carried **him** home"* — Jonah suddenly talks about himself as *"this runaway"* / *"him."* First-person witness voice ruptures at the invitation.
- Beat 6 after the Jesus quote reads like typology exposition (*"None was His… He gave Himself"*) more than a man remembering the belly.

---

### First-hearing clarity — **mixed**

**Tracks:** storm, self-sentence, fish, three days — outer story mostly follows.

**Opaque without Bible background:**
- *"the lot fell on me"* — casting lots unexplained.
- *"When scribes demanded a sign"* — hard cut to a second scene (NT Pharisees) with no grounding.
- *"Son of man"* / *"heart of the earth"* — loaded terms, unexplained.
- Sea-vs-fish confusion (above) weakens the central “three days buried alive” image.

---

### Hook, spine, short-form

**Hook:** Strong — *"mercy was too wide"* grips immediately.

**Spine:** One witness, no swap — but **two Jonah threads** (enemy-mercy hook vs. resurrection reveal) compete; only partially reconciled in Beat 7.

**Length:** ~305 spoken words — inside 220–320, upper band. Acceptable on paper.

**Single moment:** **Thin.** The witnessed climax is three days in the deep; Beat 3 gives it one cluster, then Beat 6 leaves Jonah 1–2 for a NT sign debate. The belly never fully lands before the turn.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Align wrestling with the hook (EW-G8)** — in the fish, let Jonah’s honest doubt be mercy-too-wide / enemy-sparing / justice (*"I fled mercy — why does He still preserve me?"*), not only *"why was I breathing?"*; stop swapping objections mid-spine.
2. **Earn the reveal from Jonah 2 before Matthew confirms it (EW-G7)** — dramatize bars, corruption, and being *"brought up"* from the deep in Beat 3 or early Beat 6; name what Christ bore (sin/guilt/judgment/penalty), not just *"bearing ours"*; harvest *"so for their sake I went down"* into the innocent-for-guilty contrast.
3. **Repair Beat 7 voice + first-hearing clarity (EW-G10 / clarity)** — keep invitation first-person (*"chased **me** home"*); ground *"scribes demanded a sign"* for a zero-Bible listener; fix sea-vs-fish (*swallowed by the fish*, not “the sea… gave back”).
