## Independent adversarial review — *The Sign of Jonah* (eyewitness-short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution — **PASS (with one soft note)**

Both quoted spans match KJV wording in `passage.txt`:

- **"Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you."** — Jonah 1:12, verbatim. Preceded by *"so I sentenced myself:"* — acceptable self-attribution in Jonah’s voice.

- **"For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth."** — Matthew 12:40, verbatim. Preceded by *"I tell you now, by the mouth of the One who claimed them"* and tagged **`[Jesus]`** — correct attribution.

**Soft note (not a quote violation):** *"When scribes demanded a sign, the Lord Jesus gave them only my name"* compresses Matthew 12:39–40. Jesus names *"the sign of the prophet Jonas"* and cites the three-day parallel — not literally “only my name.” Slightly loose characterization around an otherwise clean quote.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus — **PASS**

- Names Jesus explicitly: *"come to Jesus — who died in your place and rose the third day."*
- Response is named: *"Turn from your own way and come"* — not a bare *"will you trust Him?"* template.
- Grace-anchored: *"If you are in your own dark, sure you have fallen too far — that is how far His mercy reaches"* — despair met by mercy, not fear/gain-loss pressure.
- *"Come up out of the deep, and live"* is invitation language, not manufactured urgency.

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned — **weak / partly asserted**

The Matthew 12:40 quote is the right anchor — Jesus Himself claims Jonah’s days. But the turn around Jonah’s **lived** experience is still largely **announced**, not walked out from what he survived.

**Unearned retrospective frame:**
- *"I did not understand those days in my own day"* — declares ignorance from the outside, same retrospective device flagged on sibling shorts (*"For years I could not tell…"*). It tells the listener the reveal was missing; it does not dramatize what those days felt like before the turn.

**Asserted typology labels:**
- *"My fish was the shadow; His empty tomb, the substance"* — theological shorthand, not discovery from Jonah’s memory. Shadow/substance is **named**, not **shown** from the belly.

**Partial earning (credit where due):**
- *"On the third day the grave could no more hold Him than the deep could hold me"* — this **does** bridge Jonah’s deliverance to Christ’s resurrection from lived parallel. Good line; it’s one of the few earned links.
- *"I went down guilty, for my own sin; He went down innocent, bearing ours"* — substitution contrast is drawn correctly.

**Core problem:** `Core text` is Jonah 1–2, but **Jonah 2 is unused**. The prayer from the belly — *"out of the belly of hell cried I"*, *"the earth with her bars was about me for ever"*, *"thou brought up my life from corruption"* — is the text that **shows** burial-and-resurrection language from inside the moment. Beat 3 rushes past it in one line (*"three days and three nights I lay buried alive in its belly"*) and Beat 6 jumps to a NT courtroom. The reveal rides on Jesus’s quote + commentary, not on Jonah’s own deep memory paying off first.

**Hook–reveal thread gap:**
- Beat 1’s distinctive hook: *"I feared His mercy was too wide: I would sooner drown than watch Him spare the city I hated"* — Nineveh / mercy-too-wide.
- Beat 6’s reveal: death, burial, resurrection typology — a **different** Jonah-significance than the hook planted.
- Matthew 12:41’s Nineveh-repentance thread is never touched. Beat 7 partially recovers grace (*"I ran from grace, and was hunted down by it in the deep"*) but the hook’s steel (hatred of enemy-mercy) never connects to the resurrection reveal. Two Jonah “signs,” one hook, one reveal — spine wobble.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man) — **absent / insufficient**

SHORT compresses to B1→B3→B6→B7, but sibling panels treat wrestling as **non-zero** — at least one honest line in the witness’s voice during the **witnessed moment**.

What exists:
- *"I would sooner drown than watch Him spare the city I hated"* — strong **motivation for running**, not doubt **inside** the fish/storm moment. Backstory, not Beat-5 wrestling.
- *"yet I did not drown"* — real tension (chose death, lived anyway), then **immediately dropped**. No line like *Why preserve the man who asked to be cast overboard?* or *Why bury me alive instead of letting the sea take me?*

**Unvoiced steel-man objections Jonah would actually carry:**
- I **chose** the sea as sentence — why am I **alive** in a fish instead of dead as I intended?
- I believed I was *"cast out of thy sight"* (Jonah 2:4) — what kind of “sign” is a disobedient prophet **delivered** from the death he earned?
- If God’s mercy is “too wide,” why extend it to **me** before Nineveh?

The piece never lets Jonah argue with God **from the belly** in his own voice. Jonah 2 — the richest wrestling text in the pericope — is entirely absent.

### EW-G9 — Doctrine sound — **PASS**

- Substitution is named clearly: *"The penalty I had earned, He took in my place"* and *"He went down innocent, bearing ours."*
- Death, burial, resurrection pattern is orthodox; no supersession, no contrarian reading.
- Guilt/innocence distinction (Jonah guilty, Christ innocent) is theologically sound.
- Cross-time reveal is honestly signposted per EW-INV-11: *"I tell you now, by the mouth of the One who claimed them"* — better than third-person Gospel narration in sibling pieces.

**Minor:** *"the prophet the sea swallowed whole and gave back alive"* — the sea cast him forth; the **fish** held him three days. Poetic compression, not doctrinal error, but a careful listener may notice.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character — **mostly strong, one sag**

**Strong:**
- Beat 1 voice is distinctive and in-character — mercy-hatred is a real Jonah angle, not generic prophet costume.
- Beat 7 callback (*"I ran from grace, and was hunted down by it in the deep"*) lands in Jonah’s voice.

**Weak:**
- Beat 6 leans essayist for a stretch: shadow/substance commentary after the Jesus quote reads like typology exposition, not a man remembering the belly.
- Beat 3 → Beat 6 **skips the interior** of the witnessed moment entirely — the witness becomes a reporter of his own shipwreck, then a theologian.

---

### First-hearing clarity — **mixed**

**Clear:**
- Storm, self-sentence, fish, three days — the outer story tracks for a zero-Bible listener.
- Hook is immediately gripping.

**Opaque without Bible background:**
- *"the lot fell on me"* — casting lots unexplained.
- *"When scribes demanded a sign"* — introduces a second scene (NT Pharisees) with no grounding for a first-time hearer.
- *"Son of man"* and *"heart of the earth"* — doctrinally loaded, unexplained.
- Skipping Jonah 2’s *"belly of hell"* language loses the one passage that would make “buried alive” intelligible to someone who’s never heard the story.

---

### Hook, spine, short-form

**Hook:** Strong — *"mercy was too wide"* / *"sooner drown than watch Him spare the city I hated"* is fresh and characterful.

**Spine:** One witness, no swap — but **two Jonah threads** (enemy-mercy hook vs. resurrection reveal) compete; only the second is fulfilled in Beat 6.

**Length:** ~313 spoken words — inside 220–320, at the upper bound. Tight on paper.

**Single moment:** **Sags.** The “moment” is supposed to be three days in the deep; Beat 3 gives it one sentence, Beat 6 leaves Jonah 1–2 for a NT sign debate. The belly — the actual climax of Jonah’s testimony — never lands.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Add internalized steel-man wrestling (EW-G8)** — at minimum one line from inside the fish/storm: why am I alive when I chose death / why does the God whose mercy I fled still preserve me? Pull from Jonah 2’s voice (*"cast out of thy sight"*, *"bars… about me"*) rather than leaving ch. 2 unused.
2. **Earn the reveal from Jonah’s lived days before (or tightly alongside) Matthew 12:40 (EW-G7)** — walk out burial, bars, corruption, and being “brought up” from the deep in Beat 3 or early Beat 6; cut or rework *"I did not understand those days in my own day"* and the asserted *"shadow… substance"* label; let *"the grave could no more hold Him than the deep could hold me"* carry the turn, not announce it.
3. **Reconcile hook and reveal spine** — either connect Beat 1’s mercy-too-wide / Nineveh hatred to the close (even one line on mercy reaching enemies and runaways), or re-aim the hook at the three-days/death-burial thread so Beat 1 and Beat 6 aren’t selling different “signs of Jonah.”
