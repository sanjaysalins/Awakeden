# Independent review — codex (OK, 104s)

Sources checked for KJV wording: [Jonah 1:12 KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jonah+1%3A12&version=KJV), [Matthew 12:40 KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+12%3A40&version=KJV).

**Findings**

- KJV quote accuracy: no wording failure. `**"Take me up..."**` matches Jonah 1:12, minus the unquoted narrative tag. `**"For as Jonas..."**` matches Matthew 12:40. Attribution is acceptable, though `so I sentenced myself:` is looser than a clean speaker/source attribution.

- The reveal is partly asserted, not fully earned. The lived text gives you: `for my sake this great tempest is upon you` and the sea calmed when Jonah was cast in. But the reveal jumps to `The penalty I had earned, He took in my place` without showing the storm/judgment/calm connection. It should make the substitution arise from the witnessed moment, not just from doctrinal summary.

- The strongest wrestling starts well, then gets dropped. `I feared His mercy was too wide... the city I hated` is the real Jonah objection. But the invitation shifts to `If you are in your own dark, sure you have fallen too far`, which is a different objection. Jonah’s scandal is not mainly “am I too far gone?” but “can mercy really reach people I despise?”

- Doctrine is mostly sound, but `He went down innocent, bearing ours` is too compressed. What did He bear? Sin, judgment, death, wrath/penalty. `The penalty I had earned` names penalty generically, but the actual penalty needs sharper naming inside the reveal, not only in the CTA’s `who died in your place`.

- `The grave could no more hold Him than the deep could hold me` risks flattening Christ’s death/resurrection into Jonah’s survival/deliverance. The piece later says `died`, but the reveal should guard the difference: Jonah was preserved alive; Christ truly died and rose.

- First-hearing clarity is mostly good, but `scribes demanded a sign` and `the Son of man` arrive with little help for a zero-Bible listener. Because the KJV phrase must stay, the surrounding narration should clarify that Jesus is speaking of Himself.

- Short form and spine are strong. One witness, one thread, tight enough. The hook works: `the prophet the sea swallowed whole and gave back alive`.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Make the Christ reveal arise from Jonah’s storm/calm/substitution moment, not just the three-days parallel.
2. Keep Jonah’s real wrestling through the close: mercy for hated enemies, not only comfort for the ashamed.
3. Sharpen the doctrine by naming what Christ bore and preserving the difference between Jonah’s preservation and Christ’s true death/resurrection.
