## Independent adversarial review — *The Sign of Jonah* (eyewitness-short)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim:** Both quoted spans match KJV substrings (Jonah 1:12; Matthew 12:40). No altered words inside the quote fences.

**Attribution — one friction, not a hard fail:**
- Jonah 1:12 is preceded by *"so I sentenced myself:"* — functionally attributes Jonah as speaker, but unlike the Jesus line there is no explicit `[Jonah]` (or scripture) tag. In a multi-voice production this can blur cast routing.
- Matthew 12:40: `**[Jesus]**` before the quote — clean.

**Non-quote prose:** *"the LORD had prepared a great fish"* (Beat 3) paraphrases Jonah 1:17’s *"a great fish to swallow up Jonah"* — not inside quotes, so not an EW-INV-4 violation, but it sits next to Jesus’s *"whale's belly"* without explanation (clarity issue below).

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus

**Passes.** Beat 7 names **Jesus**, invites with named responses (**Turn**… **come to Jesus**… **Come up out of the deep, and live**). Grace-anchored via *"that is how far His mercy reaches"* — not fear/gain-loss, no bare *"will you trust Him?"* template.

**Minor:** *"sure you have fallen too far"* walks close to manufactured pressure, but it is immediately reframed as mercy’s reach, not divine threat. Acceptable, not the weakest link.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Partially earned; substitution and tomb are announced, not shown.**

**What is earned:** The three-day type→Christ link rests on Jesus’s own words:

> **"For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth."**

That is legitimate earning via NT fulfillment speech.

**What is not earned from what Jonah lived:**
- *"I did not understand those days in my own day. I tell you now, by the mouth of the One who claimed them."* — classic announce-then-import. The witness declares ignorance, then the meaning arrives from outside his lived moment.
- *"I went down guilty, for my own sin; He went down innocent, bearing ours. The penalty I had earned, He took in my place."* — sound doctrine, but **Matthew 12:40 never says substitution**. Nothing in Beat 3’s storm, casting, or fish belly **walks the listener** to innocent-for-guilty; it is applied exposition after the quote.
- *"My fish was the shadow; His empty tomb, the substance."* — Colossians-style typology vocabulary **not in Jonah’s experience or in Jesus’s cited line**. Listener is told the conclusion; it is not demonstrated step-by-step from the witnessed moment.

**Missed earning opportunity:** Jonah’s own line *"for my sake this great tempest is upon you"* and his voluntary casting to calm the sea for the sailors is a lived “one goes down so others live” pattern — but the script never draws that line before jumping to Christ’s penal substitution. The reveal leans on assertion instead.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Fails. This is the load-bearing gap.**

Beats are labeled **1, 3, 6, 7** — **2, 4, and 5 are gone**. The short Abraham review flagged the same compression as an EW-G8 failure; this piece repeats that pattern.

Beat 1 gives **motive**, not **wrestling**:
> *"I ran from God because I feared His mercy was too wide: I would sooner drown than watch Him spare the city I hated."*

That is Jonah’s scandal (Jonah 4 territory) — but it is never doubted, questioned, or resolved in Jonah’s voice. No line wrestles:
- *What could three days in a fish possibly have to do with the Messiah?*
- *How can my disobedience picture His innocence?*
- *I thought I was simply drowning for my flight — when did it become a sign?*

Instead Beat 7 offers generic *"sure you have fallen too far"* — every repentance short gets that doubt. It is **not Jonah’s distinctive steel-man** (mercy for enemies he hated, or the absurdity of his story as heaven’s sign).

The format’s signature beat is **absent**, not merely compressed.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Core claims pass:** evangelical typology via Matthew 12:40; substitution explicitly named (*"The penalty I had earned, He took in my place"*); no supersession; no contrarian reading.

**Stretch, not heresy:** Jonah’s descent is voluntary self-handover to still a storm **he caused by fleeing**; Christ’s is innocent penal substitution. The parallel is typological (three days + “went down”), not strict one-to-one — fine if **earned**, but here it is **asserted in one sentence** after the Matthew quote. A skeptical listener could ask why Jonah’s guilt-picture proves Christ’s innocence without an intermediate step.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strong in Beats 1 and 3:** *"I would sooner drown than watch Him spare the city I hated"* and *"so I sentenced myself"* are vivid, in-character Jonah.

**Costume slips in Beat 6:**
- *"When scribes demanded a sign, the Lord Jesus gave them only my name"* — Jonah was not in the room. This is Gospel-harmony narrator, not eyewitness recollection. Compare the good-short blind-man fixture: he only reports what he saw; NT typology is applied **from his healed eyes**, not from a scene he never attended.
- *"by the mouth of the One who claimed them"* — vague, essayistic; then *"the Lord Jesus"* arrives a clause later.
- *"My fish was the shadow; His empty tomb, the substance"* — systematic-theology diction a 8th-c. BC prophet would not speak, even with posthumous hindsight, unless **Jesus’s cited words** supplied that vocabulary (they do not — only duration in the earth).

Beat 7 recovers voice: *"I ran from grace, and was hunted down by it in the deep"* — good Jonah.

---

### First-hearing clarity

**Followable in outline; several friction points for zero-Bible listeners:**
- *"the lot fell on me"* — casting lots unexplained.
- *"by the mouth of the One who claimed them"* — who is “the One” before Jesus is named?
- *"great fish"* (Beat 3) vs *"whale's belly"* (Beat 6) — both KJV-faithful, but sounds like a contradiction without a bridging phrase.
- *"heart of the earth"* — burial/tomb meaning never glossed.
- Beat 6 jumps to *"scribes"* and a demand for a sign with no setup — assumes NT literacy.

---

### Hook, spine, short-form tightness

**Hook:** Strong — *"the prophet the sea swallowed whole and gave back alive."*

**Word count:** ~286 spoken words — inside the ~220–320 band.

**Spine problem — two threads, one abandoned:**
- Thread A (Beat 1): hatred of Nineveh / mercy too wide.
- Thread B (Beats 3, 6, 7): three days, fish, death-burial-resurrection, substitution.

Thread A **never pays off** in reveal or CTA. Beat 7’s *"I ran from grace"* gestures at it but the Christ turn is entirely Thread B. That is **thread crowding at the open** and **orphan setup** — not one clean spine.

**Beat 6 is overloaded** for a short: NT courtroom scene + Jesus quote + guilt/innocence contrast + resurrection + shadow/substance — too many turns for one beat; contributes to “announced reveal” feel.

**No sag** in runtime, but **missing wrestling** means the middle has no interior turn — it jumps from act (Beat 3) to imported reveal (Beat 6).

---

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Add a steel-man wrestling beat (or 2–3 lines inside Beat 3)** — Jonah’s real objection: mercy for enemies he hated, and/or *“I was only drowning for my sin — how is that a sign of the Christ?”* Generic *“fallen too far”* in Beat 7 does not satisfy EW-G8.
2. **Earn substitution from Jonah’s lived details before asserting it** — draw from *“for my sake this great tempest”*, voluntary casting, sailors spared, three days buried alive; only then land *“He went down innocent, bearing ours”* — or cut substitution to what Matthew 12:40 actually claims (three days) and let penal substitution live in the CTA only.
3. **Resolve the orphaned Nineveh/mercy hook or cut it** — either connect *“mercy too wide”* to the reveal/invitation, or open on the single moment (storm → fish → three days) without a second thread that never returns.
