# The Sign of Jonah — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Jonah, the son of Amittai, prophet of Israel  ·  **Core text:** Jonah 1–2 (with Matthew 12:40)  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** three days in the deep then delivered up alive — a shadow of the death, burial, and resurrection of Christ
---

## Beat 1 — Hook
I told the sailors to throw me into the sea — and a great fish swallowed me whole, and buried me alive in the dark.

## Beat 2 — The strange thing
I ran from God because His mercy was too wide. He sent me to Nineveh, the city I hated — I'd sooner drown than see her spared. The storm came for my sake; the lot fell on me. To spare the sailors, I had them throw me overboard. Three days buried alive in the fish.

## Beat 3 — The turn
Then, long after I was dust, scribes demanded a sign — Jesus gave them only my name:

**[Jesus]** **"For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth."**

I went down guilty — the storm was mine. He went down innocent, bearing what was ours; He gave Himself. The grave no more held Him than the deep held me. He rose.

## Beat 4 — The punch
You who are sure you've fallen too far — that is how far His mercy reaches. It cost Him the grave. Stop running. Come to Jesus, who died in your place and rose. Come up, and live.
