# Independent adversarial review — *The Sign of Jonah* (eyewitness-short)

## KJV verbatim + attribution (EW-INV-4)

**PASS on the quoted line.** The Matthew 12:40 block matches KJV wording, and Jesus is attributed before the quote:

> **[Jesus]** **"For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth."**

**Minor friction (not an INV-4 fail):** Beat 1 says *"a great fish swallowed me"* while the KJV quote says *"whale's belly."* Legal because it’s outside quotes, but a careful listener may hear a mismatch.

**No other KJV quotes** — but several claims are presented as fact without scriptural support inside the witness frame (see G10/G7).

---

## CTA-on-Jesus (EW-INV-2)

**Mostly PASS.** The close names Jesus and names the response:

> *"Come to Jesus, who died in your place and rose. Come up, and live."*

Substitution is stated (*"died in your place"*). Grace framing is present:

> *"You who are sure you've fallen too far — that is how far His mercy reaches."*

**Flag:** *"Stop running."* — Tied to Jonah’s arc, not bare “trust Him,” but it’s a behavior command without naming *faith*, *receive*, or *turn* in that phrase. The named response lives in *"Come to Jesus"* / *"Come up, and live"* — acceptable, but the imperative stack is slightly template-adjacent (run → stop → come).

---

## Reveal earned (EW-G7) — **FAIL**

The Christ turn is **announced, not shown from what Jonah lived.**

The pivot line:

> *"Then, long after I was dust, scribes demanded a sign — Jesus gave them only my name:"*

That is **NT retrojection**, not something earned from Jonah 1–2 in the witness’s own moment. Jonah did not live through scribes, did not hear Jesus, and did not witness the fulfillment. The reveal depends on **jumping centuries forward** and importing Matthew 12:40, then asserting the parallel:

> *"I went down guilty — the storm was mine. He went down innocent, bearing what was ours; He gave Himself."*

The guilty/innocent contrast is **doctrinally fine** but **theologically asserted** after the quote — not built step-by-step from Jonah’s lived experience (e.g., third-day deliverance from the deep, prayer from “the belly of hell,” mercy to the undeserving). The line *"and at last I understood"* isn’t used, but the same sin: **the typology is told, not demonstrated from the witness’s memory.**

---

## Wrestling — real steel-man (EW-G8) — **FAIL**

There is **no honest doubt or question in Jonah’s voice** about the meaning of what happened — only rebellion backstory:

> *"I ran from God because His mercy was too wide."*  
> *"I'd sooner drown than see her spared."*

That’s **motive**, not **wrestling**. The strongest objection a listener would expect here — *“I was guilty and deserved the deep; how does my shame become a sign of an innocent Man in the earth?”* or *“I wanted judgment, not mercy — what has my story to do with salvation?”* — is **never internalized as a question Jonah actually asks.**

Beat 3 moves straight from fish → scribes/Jesus → atonement. **No steel-man doubt beat.**

---

## Doctrine sound (EW-G9)

**Mostly PASS** for evangelical typology when grounded in Matthew 12:40.

Substitution is named:

> *"bearing what was ours; He gave Himself"* / *"died in your place"*

Penalty is implied (guilt, grave, death) — not as crisp as it could be (*what* was borne: sin, wrath, death), but not empty.

**Flags:**
- *"Jesus gave them only my name"* — compresses Matthew 12:39–40 (“no sign but the sign of the prophet Jonas”). Not heresy, but **oversimplified**; skeptics will read it as slogan, not Scripture.
- No supersession, no contrarian reading detected.

---

## Voice / character (EW-G10) — **FAIL**

Beat 1–2 are credible first-person Jonah. Beat 3 **breaks character**:

> *"Then, long after I was dust, scribes demanded a sign — Jesus gave them only my name"*

An eyewitness who was swallowed by a fish **did not see scribes, did not hear Jesus, and did not know Matthew 12.** This is a **detached essay narrator in a Jonah mask** for the reveal beat.

Same beat:

> *"He went down innocent, bearing what was ours; He gave Himself. The grave no more held Him than the deep held me. He rose."*

Full Pauline atonement/resurrection theology in Jonah’s mouth **without** any lived bridge — costume, not character.

---

## First-hearing clarity

**Mixed.**

- Hook lands: *"throw me into the sea — and a great fish swallowed me whole"*
- **Opaque without Bible literacy:** *"the lot fell on me"* — lots, why, what for?
- **Abrupt jump:** fish → *"long after I was dust, scribes"* — zero-Bible listener loses the thread.
- *"bearing what was ours"* — “ours” works only if guilt/sin was already clear; storm guilt ≠ listener’s sin without one more plain line.

---

## Hook, spine, short-form

**Hook:** Strong.

**Spine:** One witness, but **two threads crowd the single moment:**
1. Storm / sailors / fish / three days (the witnessed moment)
2. Nineveh hatred / mercy-too-wide (backstory in Beat 2)

Beat 2 adds a **second narrative load** (*Nineveh, the city I hated*) that the short never pays off in the Christ turn — the fulfillment thread is fish/death/resurrection, not Nineveh repentance. **Sag + thread swap risk.**

**Length:** ~250 spoken words + quote ≈ **in range** for ~90s. Structurally tight on word count; **not tight on focus** — Beat 2 could be cut without losing the reveal.

VERDICT: REVISE  
TOP FIXES:  
1. **Rebuild the reveal from Jonah 1–2 only** — earn the Christ turn from third-day deliverance and Jonah’s own guilt/death language (e.g., prayer from the deep), then let Jesus’ Matthew 12:40 line *confirm* what the witness already glimpsed — not replace what he never saw (*"long after I was dust, scribes…"* must go).  
2. **Add a real steel-man doubt in Jonah’s voice** — e.g., wrestling how a guilty runaway’s rescue could signify an innocent Savior’s death — before the turn; rebellion alone is not wrestling.  
3. **Restore eyewitness discipline in Beat 3** — cut omniscient NT narration; either stay inside the fish/prayer/deliverance moment or use a single explicit “I did not know then…” frame that still **shows** the parallel from lived memory, not scribes/Jesus scenes Jonah never witnessed.
