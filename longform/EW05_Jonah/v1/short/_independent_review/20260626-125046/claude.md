# Independent review — claude (OK, 63s)

I read the narration closely against the eyewitness lens. It's strong — and the central arc actually lands. Here are the specific findings.

## What holds up

- **KJV verbatim + attribution (EW-INV-4):** The lone quote is exact KJV Matthew 12:40 — *"For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth."* "Jonas," "whale's belly," punctuation all correct. It's attributed before the quote ("Jesus gave them only my name:") and tagged **[Jesus]**. Clean.
- **The wrestling pays off (EW-G8 → CTA):** This is the best feature. Jonah's genuine objection — *"I ran from God because His mercy was too wide… I'd sooner drown than see her spared"* — is his real Jonah-4 grievance, in his own voice. And the punch resolves it: *"that is how far His mercy reaches."* The steel-man and the landing are the same thread. That's earned, not asserted.
- **Substitution named (EW-G9):** *"I went down guilty — the storm was mine. He went down innocent, bearing what was ours… who died in your place."* Penalty + what Christ bore are both named. Sound, no supersession, no contrarian reading.
- **CTA-on-Jesus (EW-INV-2):** Names Jesus, names the response ("Come… Stop running… Come up, and live"), grace-anchored. No fear/gain-loss template, no bare "will you trust Him?".

## Real flaws (tightening, not doctrine)

1. **The overboard moment is told twice.** Hook: *"I told the sailors to throw me into the sea."* Beat 2 repeats it: *"To spare the sailors, I had them throw me overboard."* In a ~210-word short that's a visible sag — the listener already has it from line one. Cut or recast the Beat 2 instance.
2. **"buried alive" is worn out — three uses.** Beat 1 *"buried me alive in the dark"*; Beat 2 *"Three days buried alive in the fish"* (also a fragment, no verb). The phrase loses force by the third hit. Vary it.
3. **"Jesus gave them only my name" overstates the text.** In Matthew 12 the sign of Jonah continues into Nineveh's repentance and the queen of the South (vv. 41–42). "Only my name" is poetic compression but slightly inaccurate — soften to "gave them only my sign" or similar.
4. **Minor first-hearing friction:** *"long after I was dust, scribes demanded a sign"* — a zero-Bible listener has to absorb a dead prophet narrating a scene centuries later, plus the unexplained word "scribes." Context just barely carries it; consider "the learned men" or a beat of breathing room.

None of these rise to a doctrinal, KJV, or CTA violation. The piece is faithful and the arc is genuinely well-built; it needs a polish pass, not a rebuild.

VERDICT: PASS
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the duplicate overboard line in Beat 2 — the hook already delivered it; the repeat is the one real sag in a tight short.
2. De-repeat "buried alive" (3× including a verbless fragment "Three days buried alive in the fish") — vary the imagery and give that fragment a verb.
3. Soften "Jesus gave them only my name" — the sign of Jonah in Matthew 12 includes Nineveh's repentance (vv. 41–42), so "only my name" overstates the text.
