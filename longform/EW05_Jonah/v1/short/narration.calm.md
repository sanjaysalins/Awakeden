# The Sign of Jonah — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Jonah, the son of Amittai, prophet of Israel  ·  **Core text:** Jonah 1–2 (with Matthew 12:40)  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** three days in the deep then delivered up alive — a shadow of the death, burial, and resurrection of Christ
**Status:** v1 — Awakeden eyewitness SHORT (B1→B3→B6→B7). Jesus voices Matt 12:40 (verbatim KJV — He names the sign Himself). Cross-time reveal SIGNPOSTED (Jonah speaks "from the far side," never claims he understood the resurrection in the deep). Substitution named: Jonah went down guilty for his own sin; Christ went down innocent, bearing ours, and rose the third day. Jonah is the SIGN, never the substitute.
---

## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Jonah, son of Amittai — the prophet the great fish swallowed and gave back alive. I ran from God because I feared His mercy was too wide: He sent me to Nineveh, the city I hated, and I fled — sooner drown than see it spared.

## Beat 3 — The act
When the storm rose, the lot fell on me. The tempest was for my sake — so for their sake I went down. I sentenced myself:

**"Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you."**

The deep closed over me. Three days I lay buried alive in the fish's belly. There I wrestled: I had chosen death — why was I breathing? Cast out of His sight, I cried out of the belly of hell, and He heard.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
I tell you now, by the mouth of the One who claimed those days. When scribes demanded a sign, Jesus gave only my name:

**[Jesus]** **"For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth."**

The tempest was mine — I went down guilty. None was His — He went down innocent, bearing the judgment that was ours; no one cast Him in, He gave Himself. The grave could no more hold Him than the deep held me.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
Hear me. Be still. The mercy I fled as too wide chased this runaway to the sea's floor and carried him home. If you are in your own dark, sure you've fallen too far — that is how far His mercy reaches. It cost Him the grave. Will you stop running? Come to Jesus, who died in your place and rose the third day. Come up out of the deep, and live.
