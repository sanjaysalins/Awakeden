## Flat-span fixes (visual only, narration untouched)

**A. Beat 3 (1:48-3:22, storm narrative).** The story itself carries tension; the fix is
pacing, not text: hard-cut acceleration through the storm panels (sailors / lots / rail),
then a full stop — sacred stillness on "the sea ceased from her raging" (the calm-water
still holds 2.5s+, score drops). The contrast IS the re-hook.

**B. Beat 5 (4:22-6:06, the KJV stack).** Four red-letter quotes in ~40s. Do not
illustrate them identically: alternate depth (belly-of-hell wide / weeds-close /
temple-look-up / dry-land slam) so each quote is a new image register. Put the
scandal-of-mercy line ("the grace I had begrudged my enemies was the one grace that
could save me") on its own takeover card — it is the thesis of the whole film and
currently plays as connective tissue.

**C. Beat 6 (6:06-8:46, the reveal).** 160s unbroken is the single biggest retention
risk. Split it visually into two chapters (cards 6 and 7 above):
- 6:06 "HE GAVE THEM MY NAME" — the Matthew 12 scene: scribes demand, Jesus answers.
  The two red-letter Jesus quotes get the staged-KJV centerpiece treatment (score dip,
  slow camera, red-letter takeover) — same recipe as the shorts.
- ~7:30 "HE WENT DOWN INNOCENT" — the substitution paragraph + the Nineveh anger twist.
  "When I saw that city spared, I was not glad" is the most arresting confession in the
  film — give it a direct-to-camera Jonah close-up, the first and only one in the piece.
