# Independent review — cursor (OK, 81s)

## Independent adversarial review — *The Sign of Jonah* (eyewitness-long)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Verbatim:** Every bold quote I checked against Jonah 1–2 and Matthew 12:38–41 is an exact KJV substring. No altered words found.

**Attribution — multiple gaps.** The spec requires the speaker/source to be named *before* the quote, not inferred from surrounding prose.

| Quote | Problem |
|-------|---------|
| **`"I am an Hebrew; and I fear the LORD, the God of heaven, which hath made the sea and the dry land."`** | Preceded only by *"I told them the truth I had been fleeing:"* — no `[Jonah]` or equivalent. |
| **`"Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you."`** | Preceded by *"With my own mouth I pronounced my own sentence and bid them throw me into the deep:"* — still no speaker tag. |
| **`"So they took up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea: and the sea ceased from her raging."`** | Third-person Scripture about Jonah; *"the record they kept of that day tells it plainly:"* is thin attribution. Same pattern as flagged on Moses/Joseph reviews. |
| **`"Now the LORD had prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights."`** | *"So it stands written of me:"* — no book, no `[scripture]`, no named narrator. |
| All Jonah 2 prayer fragments (`**"out of the belly of hell cried I..."**`, **`"I am cast out of thy sight..."**`, etc.) | No attribution that these are his recorded prayer, not fresh speech. |

**[the LORD]** and **[Jesus]** lines are handled correctly.

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Mostly passes.** Beat 7 names Jesus and names the response:

- *"Will you stop running, and let it carry you up?"*
- *"Come up out of the deep. Come to Jesus — and live."*

No banned *"will you trust Him?"* template. Grace-anchored, not fear/gain-loss.

**Minor flag:** *"Come to Jesus — and live"* edges toward outcome-promise framing. Acceptable here, but borderline.

**Strong line:** *"Do not come to me; I am the sign, never the Saviour"* — clear, doctrinally precise.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned

**Weakest structural gate.** The Christ-turn is largely **announced and imported**, not shown from what Jonah lived.

1. **Beat 1 telegraphs the ending:** *"and Whom those three days were always about"* tells a zero-Bible listener the punchline before the story earns it.

2. **Beat 4 asks the right question but never answers it from experience:** *"Why three? Why was the place of my dying made the place of my keeping?"* — then Beat 5 pivots to interior theology and Beat 6 hands off to Matthew. The number never resolves from Jonah's own pattern before Jesus names it.

3. **Beat 6 explicitly outsources the reveal:** *"I did not understand it in my own day. I tell you now from the far side of my life, by the word written long after me — and by the mouth of the One who spoke it."* Signposted reflective device (EW-INV-11) — allowed — but under EW-G7 the **lived material does too little lifting**.

4. **The strongest lived shadow is planted then abandoned.** Beat 3: *"One man given up to the deep, and the storm fell silent over all the rest"* — a clear one-for-many pattern Jonah actually witnessed. Beat 6 never develops it. Instead the turn jumps straight to burial/resurrection via Matt 12:40 and asserted substitution: *"The penalty I had earned — to be cast out, cut off, swallowed by death — He took upon Himself."* Sound doctrine, but **asserted**, not walked out from the storm scene.

5. **Nineveh half of Jesus's sign is retrospective summary, not lived beat:** *"Nineveh heard, and turned, and was spared"* arrives only in Beat 6 after the fish — the preaching mission that makes Jonah *"the sign"* to that generation never gets a full beat in a LONG piece.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Partially strong, partially mis-timed — the main character failure.**

**What works:** The mercy-too-wide motive is the real Jonah objection, well stated in Beat 2: *"I would sooner drown than watch God show mercy to men I hated"* and *"I knew Him to be gracious and slow to anger, and I did not want His grace spent on my enemies."* Beat 6's post-Nineveh anger (*"when I saw that city spared, I was not glad... hot enough to wish myself dead"*) is excellent, honest Jonah 4 steel-man.

**What fails:** Beat 5 **mislabels and conflates** two different doubts from two different moments:

- Quoted belly-prayer doubt: **`"I am cast out of thy sight; yet I will look again toward thy holy temple."`** — abandonment/despair (Jonah 2).
- Then: *"That was my doubt — and hear what strange kind of doubt it was."* — but the next lines are **not** that doubt. They are Jonah 4's mercy scandal: *"I fled because I knew it was too wide... on the very people who had butchered mine."*

In the fish, Jonah has **not yet preached to Nineveh**; the city has **not yet repented**; *"butchered mine"* reads like past atrocity already suffered, not prospective dread. The piece intellectually merges Jonah 2 prayer and Jonah 4 sulking into one belly moment. That is **reconstructed essay theology**, not the witness's honest doubt *at that hour*.

The belly's genuine steel-man — *"I am cast out of thy sight"* while the God whose mercy I hate is my only rescuer — is stated, then **overwritten** by the stronger but chronologically wrong objection.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Passes on the big items:** substitution named (*"He bore it in our place"*), no supersession, Christ explicit, Jonah correctly framed as sign not saviour.

**Nuance flags (not heresy, but sloppy):**

- *"I was a dead man who was carried home"* / *"Buried alive, and not destroyed"* — typologically fine; literally Jonah did not die. A careful listener may hear overstatement.
- Beat 7: *"He went down innocent for sin that was mine"* — Beat 6 correctly says *"for the sin of others"* and *"in our place."* Beat 7 narrows to personal *"mine"* in a way that could sound individualistic unless the listener already heard the plural frame.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Strong in Beats 2–3 and the Nineveh anger section.** Lines like *"the dead sleep of a man running from God"* and *"I never did fully make my peace with it in the days I was given"* feel like Jonah.

**Costume slips:**

- Beat 7: *"Be still, the way the sea went still over my head"* — director addressing an audience, not a drowned prophet on the shore.
- Beat 7: *"Sit with that."* — same register.
- Beat 6: *"the very gospel I had been made to picture"* — meta-commentary; Jonah wouldn't say *"gospel."*

---

### First-hearing clarity

- Beat 1's *"Whom those three days were always about"* assumes Christ before introducing Him.
- **`"Jonas"`** in Jesus quotes is correct KJV but may confuse listeners who only know *Jonah*.
- *"scribes and Pharisees"* get minimal setup — workable, thin.
- The flee → storm → fish → ??? → Jesus arc is followable; the **Nineveh mission** gap makes the second half of the sign harder to track.

---

### Hook / spine

**Hook grips:** *"I am the man the sea swallowed whole and then gave back alive."*

**Spine holds:** one witness, one thread (mercy too wide + three days + substitution). No thread swap.

---

### LONG-form runtime — earn vs. sag

**Word budget:** ~1648 spoken words — inside the 1300–1650 ceiling, but **heavy on repetition, light on new turns.**

Repeated without new development:
- Mercy-too-wide / fled grace: Beat 2 → Beat 5 → Beat 6 → Beat 7 (*"I ran from grace because I knew how wide it was"* / *"The mercy was never too small — always too wide"*).
- Three days in the deep: Beats 1, 4, 5, 7.
- *"Dead man"* / carried up: Beats 1, 4, 5, 7.

**Beat 7 sags:** almost entirely recap before the CTA. No new turn, no lived Nineveh beat, no fresh question — just compression of Beats 2, 5, and 6.

For ~9–11 min LONG, the piece **does not earn its length** with proportional new material; it **re-earns the same insight four times**.

---

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Earn the reveal from Jonah's lived pattern before Matthew** — develop Beat 3's *"One man given up to the deep, and the storm fell silent over all the rest"* and Beat 4's *"Why three?"* into the death-buried-delivered shape; cut Beat 1's *"Whom those three days were always about"* telegraph; let Jesus's Matt 12 quotes confirm what the listener already sees, not introduce it.
2. **Fix Beat 5 wrestling chronology (EW-G8)** — keep belly-prayer doubt (*"I am cast out of thy sight"*) as the fish-hour steel-man; move mercy-too-wide / Nineveh anger wrestling to a lived post-mission beat (or the existing Beat 6 anger section), and stop claiming *"That was my doubt"* about abandonment when the real objection is begrudged Gentile mercy.
3. **Attribute every KJV quote before it speaks (EW-INV-4)** and **replace Beat 7 recap with one new turn** — e.g., a lived Nineveh preaching beat — while trimming the fourfold *"mercy too wide"* restatement to tighten sag without losing runtime.
