## Doctrine — sound, and the hard part is well-guarded
The serpent-as-type danger (Christ-as-sinner) is correctly defused in M5: **"Christ did not become a sinner. He was lifted up bearing our curse, in the likeness of the judgment we deserved"** — anchored to 2 Cor 5:21 + Gal 3:13. Orthodox. The "it is the meaning Jesus put there Himself" framing (resting the whole type on John 3:14, Jesus' own words) is the right load-bearing move and keeps it off contrarian ground.

## Real findings (polish, not blockers)

**1. M6 and M7 hammer the same beat three times.** "The cure was outside the dying man / cost him nothing but a turn of the eyes" lands in M6 close ("It cost the bitten Israelite nothing but a turn of the eyes. It cost the Son everything."), then again in M7 open ("the cure was a *look*… too weak to stand and still lift your eyes"), then again in M7's faith definition ("the empty-handed look of someone who has stopped trying to cure themselves"). For a 6–8 min piece this is one idea restated three ways back-to-back. This is exactly the "~50–80 residual words" the status note already flags — the trim should come out of the M6→M7 seam, not elsewhere.

**2. "will draw all men unto me" (John 12:32) is unglossed.** A listener with zero Bible knowledge can hear universalism in "draw all men." The narration uses the verse correctly (paired with 12:33 to prove "lifted up = the cross"), so the quote earns its place — but "draw" (= draws toward, not saves all) gets no clarifying touch, and it sits right before "God so loved the world." Watch-item: one word of framing, or lean the weight onto the 12:33 proof, so the naive ear doesn't mishear scope.

**3. M5's two-objection apologetics block is the tonal outlier.** "be fair to the doubts, because there are two" shifts from devotional meditation into debate-structure for one movement. It's doctrinally the most important movement so it should stay, but it's the densest stretch and the place a first-time hearer is most likely to drift. Worth a delivery/pacing note (the bracketed-delivery system can carry it), not a rewrite.

## Non-findings I checked and cleared
- "near the end of the wilderness years" — accurate (Num 21 follows Aaron's death, 40th year).
- "this light bread" = manna they despise — accurate.
- "roughly fourteen hundred years" / "Fourteen centuries" — internally consistent, defensible dating.
- John 3:16 speaker softened to "the passage flows straight on" — correctly handles the contested v15/v16 speaker boundary; v14–15 is securely attributed to Jesus, which is standard.
- Landing reuses "look, and live" from the hook — this is the intended thread bookend, not a tired generic CTA. The close does new work (faith as the *empty-handed look*, "whosoever," "stop staring at the bite"). Grace-anchored, no fear/gain-loss/works framing.
- Hook ("one of the strangest cures in the Bible… Look, and live.") grips inside 5 seconds. ✓

Bottom line: no doctrinal or KJV defect blocks the lock. The piece is genuinely strong. The remaining items are the pre-lock tightening the draft already admits is pending — so it's one polish pass short of ready, not done.
