# Independent review — codex (OK, 139s)

Findings:

- KJV failure: **"We have sinned… pray unto the LORD, that he take away the serpents from us."** This is not verbatim KJV; it skips the central clause of confession inside the quotation. If the standard is exact KJV, don’t use ellipses inside quotation marks. Source checked: [Numbers 21:7](https://www.kingjamesbibleonline.org/Numbers-21-7/).

- KJV failure: **"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son…"** Again, ellipsis inside quoted Scripture is not verbatim. Also be careful nearby: John 3:16 KJV ends with **“everlasting life,”** while the narration later quotes John 3:15’s **“eternal life.”** Source checked: [John 3:16](https://biblehub.com/kjv/john/3-16.htm), [John 3:15](https://biblehub.com/kjv/john/3-15.htm).

- Accuracy problem: **"past the temple, past the law — to a snake on a pole in the desert."** The bronze serpent event is not “past the law” chronologically; it occurs during Moses’ wilderness period after Sinai. This is clever rhetoric that creates a false timeline for a Bible-new listener.

- Overread of the text: **"Even their honest confession reached only for the danger outside."** Numbers 21:7 explicitly has them confessing sin against the LORD and Moses. The request is external removal, yes; the confession itself is not merely external. The word **“only”** makes the claim stronger than the passage supports.

- Hook has a self-contradictory phrase: **"God's remedy is not to kill the snakes, or heal the wounds..."** But the remedy does heal/save the bitten: **"he lived."** The intended point is probably “not by ordinary treatment,” but as written it says God’s cure is not to heal, then says it heals.

- Minor verbatim/punctuation issue: **"being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree."** The KJV has a colon at the end, not a period. Same kind of issue appears with **"his own self bare our sins in his own body on the tree."** if punctuation is part of the house “verbatim” standard. Sources checked: [Galatians 3:13](https://biblehub.com/kjv/galatians/3-13.htm), [1 Peter 2:24](https://biblehub.com/kjv/1_peter/2-24.htm).

- Doctrinally, the main typology is orthodox: bronze serpent to Christ lifted up, curse-bearing, faith as empty-handed looking. The risky line is **"God teaching the world one motion that saves"** because “motion” can sound mechanical or decisionistic. The surrounding paragraph mostly rescues it, but the phrase itself is loose.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove ellipses from quoted Scripture or quote full exact KJV text.
2. Fix the historically misleading “past the law” line.
3. Replace overclaims like “confession reached only for the danger outside” and “not to heal the wounds” with text-faithful wording.
