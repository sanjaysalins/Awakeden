# Independent review — grok (OK, 47s)

You asked for steel, not praise. Here is what breaks or weakens the piece under the eyewitness-long lens.

---

## EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Dialogue quotes checked clean** (Numbers 21:5, 21:7, 21:8; John 3:14–15, 12:32): wording matches KJV; `[the people]`, `[the LORD]`, `[Jesus]` tags are present.

**Systematic attribution gap on narrative quotes.** Five KJV blocks have no speaker tag before the quote:

- `**"the soul of the people was much discouraged because of the way."**`
- `**"And the LORD sent fiery serpents among the people, and they bit the people; and much people of Israel died."**`
- `**"And Moses made a serpent of brass, and put it upon a pole, and it came to pass, that if a serpent had bitten any man, when he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived."**`
- `**"being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree:"**` — Galatians 3:13; needs `[Paul]` (or equivalent), not unattributed doctrine in Moses’s mouth.
- `**"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life."**` — John 3:16; spoken by Jesus in context; needs `[Jesus]` like 3:14–15 already have.

If INV-4 means *every* quoted block, this is a pattern, not a one-off.

## EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Moses reads the Bible about his own life:**

> “and the Scripture does not flatter us — it tells the plain truth:”

A man *inside* the event does not call it “the Scripture.” That is modern narrator voice wearing a Moses mask.

**Beat 6 is post-Moses omniscience, not eyewitness recollection:**

> “I tell you this now from the far side of my life — by the light that came long after I laid down my staff, and by the word that was written after me”

> “until a good king of Judah broke it to pieces and called it Nehushtan”

Moses did not live to see Hezekiah, John 3, Galatians, or the crucifixion. The frame licenses it, but under G10 the witness becomes a sermonizer with full NT canon, not someone recounting what *these hands* did and what troubled him *then*.

---

## EW-G7 — Reveal earned vs announced

Beats 4–5 earn the *categories* (curse-image lifted, look-not-works, sign points past itself). Beat 6 then **announces** the turn:

> “what that pole in the sand was always reaching toward”

> “I did not see the answer in my own day.”

The Christ link is **delivered by NT citation** (“For there came a Teacher… He spoke of me”) rather than **shown** from the wilderness riddle Moses actually carried. He admits he could not have told you what it meant — then the answer arrives as imported theology. Typology is *asserted* (“My serpent was never the point… It was a shadow”) more than *derived* step-by-step from what he forged and obeyed. Under strict G7, the reveal is partly earned, partly pasted on.

---

## EW-G8 — Wrestling / steel-man

**Invented eyewitness certainty** (not in Numbers 21):

> “Not everyone looked. Some, I am sure of it, lay in their tents too proud to turn their heads”

> “And they died with the cure in plain sight of their own door.”

The text says look and live; it does **not** record refusers. “I am sure of it” presents application as lived memory. That weakens the steel-man — it’s a sermon illustration, not Moses’s honest doubt.

**Stronger objection underplayed:** Moses ground the golden calf to powder; Israel later burned incense to Nehushtan. The real steel-man is *“Did God command me to set up an image that would become idolatry?”* Beat 5 touches “metal saves no one” but never wrestles **image-of-the-curse vs. second-commandment terror** in Moses’s own voice. Nehushtan appears only *after* the reveal, as proof — not as the doubt that should keep him awake.

---

## EW-G9 — Doctrine

Core is sound: lifted-up typology, curse borne (Galatians 3:13), look/faith not works, anti-relic (Nehushtan). Substitution is named:

> “made to be the curse that was killing us”

> “trust the One who was made your curse and carried it clean away”

**Risk:** unattributed Galatians 3:13 lets Paul’s juridical “curse on the tree” land as Moses’s unexplained line — fine for insiders, muddy for first-hearing listeners who cannot tell law-letter from wilderness event.

No supersessionism flagged. No contrarian reading.

---

## EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Passes.** Jesus is named; response is specific, not a bare trust template:

> “believe on Him”

> “Come to Him”

> “Look, and live.”

Grace-anchored; no fear ledger, no “don’t miss out,” no worthiness gate beyond “you were never asked to” climb. “feel the death let go of you” is experiential but not coercive.

## First-hearing clarity

Mostly clear. Two friction points:

- **Fiery vs. brass:** serpents are “fiery”; the cure is “serpent of **brass**.” Never explained for a zero-Bible listener.
- **Nehushtan** drops without setup — “a good king of Judah… Nehushtan — a nothing, a scrap of brass” will lose cold listeners unless Beat 5 already plants idolatry fear.

---

## Hook, spine, long-form runtime

**Hook holds:** “I am Moses… A pole driven into the sand. And upon it, a serpent of bronze.”

**One witness, one thread:** Moses → look and live → Christ lifted up. No swap.

**Sag / padding:**

- Beats **4 and 5** both circle “why a serpent?” / “why only a look?” — same turn twice before Beat 6 advances.
- Beat **7** restates Beat 3 (“No payment. No climbing…”) and Beat 5 (“Do not look to the bronze”) — intentional callback, but at ~9–11 min target it reads as **reprise without a new beat**, not added interior turn.

Runtime is *mostly* earned by wrestling + reveal; invitation and riddle beats are where it loosens.

---

## Minor KJV / narration notes

Paraphrase outside quotes is fine. Beat 2’s “come up from mount Hor, going the long way round to compass the land of Edom” tracks Numbers 21:4 without overclaiming verbatim status.

VERDICT: REVISE  
TOP FIXES:  
1. Tag **every** KJV quote — at minimum add `[Jesus]` before John 3:16 and `[Paul]` before Galatians 3:13; set a consistent rule for narrative scripture blocks (e.g. `[as Moses wrote]` / `[the LORD said through Moses]`).  
2. **Earn the reveal inside Beat 5–6** from Moses’s lived trouble (sign vs. idol, curse-image, look-not-works) *before* the Nicodemus dump; cut or reframe “I did not see the answer in my own day” so the turn is shown, not announced from the far side of the canon.  
3. **Replace invented proud non-lookers** with the biblically grounded steel-man (golden calf / Nehushtan idolatry fear in Moses’s own conscience), and trim Beat 4–5 overlap + Beat 7 repetition so long-form adds turns, not echoes.
