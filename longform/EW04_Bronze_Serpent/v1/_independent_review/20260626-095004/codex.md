# Independent review — codex (OK, 64s)

**Findings**

- EW-INV-4: Several KJV quotations lack clear attribution before the quote. `**"the soul of the people was much discouraged because of the way."**` is introduced only by “the Scripture… tells,” not book/verse or narrator/source. Same problem with `**"And the LORD sent fiery serpents..."**`, `**"And Moses made a serpent of brass..."**`, and `**"For God so loved the world..."**`.
- EW-G8: The wrestling is partly built on a weak objection: `“I, who had carried down the commandment to make no graven thing, was now told to make one”`. That oversimplifies the commandment. Moses also knew God-commanded images existed in Israel’s worship setting; the sin was idolatrous worship/service, not every crafted image.
- EW-G8: The stronger objection is underdeveloped: `“I expected the LORD to take the serpents away. He did not.”` This is the real steel-man: God sent judgment, left danger present, and offered a strange means of mercy while people were dying. The piece mentions it, then retreats into the image/idolatry puzzle.
- EW-INV-2: The close names Jesus, but the response remains metaphorical: `“will you lift your eyes and look?”` / `“Look to Him, and live.”` Since first-hearing clarity matters, “look” needs to be explicitly named as faith/believing/coming to Christ in the invitation itself.
- EW-G9: `“the spotless Son of God was made to be the curse that was killing us”` is close but risky phrasing. “Made a curse for us” is biblical; “made to be the curse that was killing us” can sound like Christ became evil/the killer rather than bearing the law’s curse and penalty in our place.
- First-hearing clarity: The piece assumes too much Bible literacy in phrases like `“mount Hor,” “compass the land of Edom,” “the Teacher in Israel,” “the apostle,” “Nehushtan”`. Some are useful, but for zero Bible knowledge they need less insider shorthand.
- Voice: Moses is strongest in `“With these same hands…”` and `“I forged it. I could not have told you what it meant.”` But the voice drifts into detached sermon language at `“For so the apostle wrote of Him”` and `“For so the gospel was written”`.
- Long-form sag: The “look/live/no cure of your own” motif repeats without enough new turn in `“No payment. No climbing. No cure…”`, `“only a look”`, `“need only look”`, `“costs you nothing but a look”`, `“Look to Him, and live.”` The repetition is thematically coherent but needs compression or sharper escalation.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Replace the weak graven-image wrestling with the stronger objection: God’s judgment remains present, yet mercy comes by a helpless look.
2. Make the final invitation explicitly name the response as faith/believing/coming to Jesus, not only “look.”
3. Tighten attribution around every KJV quote and reduce insider shorthand for first-hearing clarity.
