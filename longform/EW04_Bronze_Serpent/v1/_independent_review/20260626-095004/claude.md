# Independent review — claude (OK, 114s)

I read the whole piece and checked every bolded quote against KJV. This is a strong, doctrinally careful narration — but I'm here to find the cracks, and there are a few real ones plus one structural concern.

## KJV verbatim + attribution (EW-INV-4)

I verified all eleven quotes. Words are exact in ten of them. One defect:

- **Numbers 21:7 — altered punctuation.** Artifact: **"We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD..."** KJV reads **"We have sinned; for we have spoken against the LORD..."** — a semicolon, not a comma, after "sinned." Words are intact, but a KJV-strict pipeline gates on this. Fix the punctuation.
- Nice authenticity catch worth noting (not a flaw): John 3:15 is quoted **"...have eternal life"** and John 3:16 **"...have everlasting life"** — both are correct KJV. Whoever transcribed this didn't smooth them to match. Good.
- Attribution is clean throughout: the people / the LORD / Jesus / "the apostle wrote of Him" / "the gospel was written" all frame before quoting.

## Structural concern — Beat 6 is overloaded (the real risk)

The reveal beat stacks **five** Scripture quotes back-to-back: John 3:14, John 3:15, Galatians 3:13, John 12:32, John 3:16 — plus the Hezekiah/Nehushtan narrative. That is a proof-text avalanche inside one beat. The turn *itself* is earned (serpent=curse → cross, look=faith), but the density risks reading as verse-stacking rather than one clean revelation. John 12:32 ("draw all men") largely re-states the "lifted up" point John 3:14 already made. Consider trimming one "lifted up" citation so the climax (John 3:16) lands without competing with four prior quotes.

## The eyewitness conceit is strained (flag, not fail)

Moses — who died centuries before John 3, Galatians, and Hezekiah — narrates all of them. The piece licenses this honestly: *"I tell you this now from the far side of my life... by the word that was written after me."* It's handled, and the format permits a post-mortem reveal. But verify this is acceptable house-style: a listener could balk at the dead lawgiver quoting Paul. The device is the load it carries; it doesn't break, but it's the thinnest beam in the structure.

## Where it genuinely succeeds (so my skepticism is calibrated)

- **Wrestling (EW-G8) is the real steel-man, and it's excellent.** *"had God bidden the very sin I had just broken?"* — the golden-calf / second-commandment tension is the single strongest honest objection available to Moses, in his own voice, and the resolution (*"I knew the bronze had no power in it... It was God's word that healed"*) is theologically precise about sign-vs-idol. This is the best part of the piece.
- **Substitution named (EW-G9):** *"the spotless Son of God was made to be the curse that was killing us... He bore, upon that pole, the judgment our sin had earned, taken in our place."* Penalty + what Christ bore + in-our-place, with "spotless" preserved so sinlessness isn't compromised. Sound. No supersession — typology ("a shadow waits for the body that casts it"), not replacement.
- **CTA (EW-INV-2):** names Jesus, names the response (*"will you lift your eyes and look... Look to Him, and live"*), explicitly strips fear (*"now there is no fear left in it"*), and is text-rooted, not the banned bare "will you trust Him?" template.
- **Voice (EW-G10) and hook:** consistent, characterful first-person ("faster than I could dig the graves," "the riddle of it walked home with me every evening"). Grips from line one.

First-hearing clarity is mostly fine; *"came up from mount Hor, going the long way round to compass the land of Edom"* is the one archaic patch a zero-Bible listener may skim past, but it's color, not load-bearing.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Restore the KJV semicolon in Numbers 21:7: **"We have sinned; for we have spoken against the LORD..."** (currently a comma).
2. Thin Beat 6's five-quote stack — drop or fold John 12:32 so John 3:16 lands as the single climax instead of the fifth proof-text in a row.
3. Confirm the post-mortem-Moses device (quoting John/Galatians/Hezekiah) is acceptable house-style; if so leave the "word written after me" license in place, but make sure it's a deliberate format call, not an unexamined one.
