# Look and Live — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Moses, who led Israel through the wilderness  ·  **Core text:** Numbers 21:8-9 → John 3:14  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** the bronze serpent lifted on a pole — the image of the very thing killing them, looked at in faith — fulfilled in the Son of man lifted up on the cross (John 3:14); look and live
---

## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Moses. I was there when my people died faster than I could dig the graves. We had spoken against God, and our rebellion returned as fiery serpents — a judgment we had earned. God told me there was healing only by a look.

## Beat 3 — The act
They confessed their sin, and I prayed for the serpents to be taken away — but the LORD did not. He gave a command stranger than the disease:

**[the LORD]** **"Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live."**

I wrestled with it. Forge the image of the very thing killing us — a graven thing? And heal by looking at bronze, not by clearing serpents away? Yet I obeyed. The bitten lifted their eyes — and the death let go. It was never the bronze that healed them, but the God who said look and live.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
For years its meaning escaped me. I speak now from the far side of my life, by the light that came after — a night I never saw: one they called Teacher answered a seeker:

**[Jesus]** **"And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:"**

My serpent was only a shadow; Christ was the body that cast it. They lifted Jesus on a Roman pole, made the curse our sin had earned.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
Hear me, you who are bitten — every one of us. Be still a moment. The cost was real: a body lifted up, the curse borne, so your healing asks nothing of you. They did not clean their wounds first; they only looked. And the question my life became is now yours: will you lift your eyes and look? Come to Jesus, lifted up for you, and live.
