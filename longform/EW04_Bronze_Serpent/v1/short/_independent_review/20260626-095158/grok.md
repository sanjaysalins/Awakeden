## Independent adversarial review — *Look and Live* (eyewitness-short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution
**Both formal quotes pass.** Attribution comes before each span; wording matches KJV:

- `**[the LORD]** **"Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live."**` — Numbers 21:8, verbatim.
- `**[Jesus]** **"And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:"**` — John 3:14, verbatim.

**Narrative slip (not inside quotes, but accuracy matters):** Beat 1 — *"God told me there was healing only by a look"* — compresses the story. In Numbers 21, God gave Moses the bronze-serpent **command**; the bitten looked afterward. A zero-Bible listener may think Moses already knew the mechanism before forging the sign.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus
**Passes.** Jesus is named; the response is named — *look*, *Come to Jesus*, *live* — not a bare *"will you trust Him?"* template. *"your healing asks nothing of you"* and *"They did not clean their wounds first; they only looked"* are grace-anchored.

**Minor:** *"Be still a moment"* is a soft manufactured pause, not doctrinal harm.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned — **still weak**

The John 3:14 quote is the right anchor. But the turn is **announced and summarized**, not walked out from what Moses lived.

- *"For years its meaning escaped me"* — understanding is declared missing, not shown from the pole moment.
- *"by the light that came after — a night I never saw"* — honest signposting, but still a retrospective essay frame.
- *"one they called Teacher answered a seeker"* — Moses reports a Nicodemus scene he never witnessed; the reveal rides on secondhand Gospel report, not the wilderness day.
- *"My serpent was only a shadow; Christ was the body that cast it"* — typology **asserted** in sermon language, not demonstrated.

Beat 3 plants the real puzzle — *"Forge the image of the very thing killing us"* / *"heal by looking at bronze"* / *"look and live"* — but Beat 6 **does not pay that off** (lifted curse-image → look → live → Christ lifted bearing what kills us) before jumping to shadow/body commentary and John 3:14.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling — **improved, but thin**

The revision adds real steel-man:

- *"Forge the image of the very thing killing us — a graven thing?"*
- *"heal by looking at bronze, not by clearing serpents away?"*

That is Moses-specific and strong. But it resolves in one beat: *"Yet I obeyed."* The **graven-image** dread — the sharpest objection for the calf-smashing lawgiver — is never answered in his voice. *"It was never the bronze that healed them, but the God who said look and live"* answers the bronze/healing paradox, not the idolatry dread. Wrestling is present; it is not fully steel-manned through.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine — **substitution under-named**

Typology is orthodox. No supersession, no contrarian reading.

**EW-INV-7 gap:** The spec requires naming **substitution** — the penalty **and** what Christ bore. You have penalty direction (*"a judgment we had earned"*, *"bitten"*, *"the curse our sin had earned"*) and a hint Christ bore it (*"the curse borne"*, *"a body lifted up"*). You never nail **in our place** — Christ taking the death/judgment the bite earned, the way stronger shorts do (e.g. *"The penalty I had earned, He took in my place"*). *"made the curse our sin had earned"* points at substitution but leaves a cold listener unsure **who** bore **what**.

**Voice bleed:** *"They lifted Jesus on a Roman pole"* — editorial precision Moses would not use; nudges Beat 6 toward commentary, not testimony.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character — **mixed**

**Beat 1–3:** Strong Moses — *"died faster than I could dig the graves"* is vivid, first-person, in-character. Wrestling lines sound like a man who forged bronze in the dust.

**Beat 6 breaks character:**
- *"one they called Teacher answered a seeker"* — detached third-person Gospel narration.
- *"Roman pole"* — anachronistic vocabulary.
- *"My serpent was only a shadow; Christ was the body that cast it"* — polished typology essay, not a wilderness leader's memory.

Moses becomes a **theology narrator in a Moses mask** for the reveal beat.

---

### First-hearing clarity
- Opening crisis is immediate and clear.
- *"a night I never saw"* / *"a seeker"* — assumes John 3/Nicodemus knowledge a zero-Bible listener lacks.
- *"Son of man"* in the Jesus quote is unexplained.
- *"graven thing"* may confuse without Torah context.
- *"made the curse our sin had earned"* is doctrinally right but under-explained for a first-time hearer.

---

### Hook, spine, short-form
**Hook:** Strong — graves, serpents, earned judgment in line one.

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (pole → lifted Christ → look/live). No swap.

**Length:** ~300 spoken words — inside the 220–320 band; structurally tight.

**Land:** The single moment mostly lands. Beat 6's essay pivot is where the earned turn should close but does not.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Earn the reveal from the wilderness moment itself — walk bite → lifted image of the killer → look → live into Christ lifted bearing the curse, **before or alongside** John 3:14; cut or rework *"For years its meaning escaped me"* and the secondhand *"one they called Teacher answered a seeker"* frame.
2. Name substitution explicitly — state the penalty (serpent-bite death / judgment earned) and that Christ bore it **in our place** on the cross; sharpen *"made the curse our sin had earned"* into clear substitution language.
3. Keep Beat 6 in Moses's voice — drop *"Roman pole"*, replace shadow/body sermon with witness-memory parallel (what I lifted / what they lifted / what the bitten did), and answer the *"graven thing?"* dread in one honest line (e.g. God commanded the look, not the worship of bronze).
