# Independent review — codex (OK, 55s)

**Findings**

- **KJV check passes.** `**[the LORD]** **"Make thee..."**` matches KJV Numbers 21:8 after omitting the narrative tag, and the speaker is attributed before the quote. `**[Jesus]** **"And as Moses..."**` matches KJV John 3:14 and is correctly attributed.

- **Substitution is under-named.** The line `They lifted Jesus on a Roman pole, made the curse our sin had earned` gets near substitution, but it does not clearly say Christ bore the penalty **in our place**. `the curse borne` is also too compressed. EW-G9 asks for “the penalty + what Christ bore”; this needs plainer doctrinal weight.

- **First-hearing clarity weakens at the reveal.** `one they called Teacher answered a seeker` is vague for a listener with zero Bible knowledge. So is `Roman pole`; say cross plainly. `Christ was the body that cast it` is theological shorthand, not clear speech.

- **Voice slips into essay-narrator.** `My serpent was only a shadow; Christ was the body that cast it` sounds like a typology lecture, not Moses recounting with lived urgency. The earlier voice is better: `I wrestled with it. Forge the image...`

- **The reveal is partly earned, but the final theological turn is asserted too quickly.** The narration earns “lifted thing / look / live,” but `made the curse our sin had earned` arrives without showing how the serpent-image-as-judgment connects to Jesus bearing sin. The line needs one more concrete bridge.

- **The wrestling is one of the stronger parts.** `Forge the image of the very thing killing us — a graven thing?` is a real internal objection, and `not by clearing serpents away?` sharpens it. This passes EW-G8.

- **Short form is slightly loose.** The piece has a strong single thread, but `For years its meaning escaped me... a night I never saw... one they called Teacher...` creates a second-scene detour. It risks sagging in a ~90s format.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Name substitution plainly: Christ bore the penalty/curse of our sin in our place.
2. Make the reveal clearer for first-hearing listeners: say cross, Jesus, sin, and life plainly.
3. Cut or simplify the “Teacher/seeker/body/shadow” theological shorthand so Moses’ voice stays immediate.
