3. **Beat headers are non-contiguous (1, 3, 6, 7).** Cosmetic remnant of the 7-beat template — confirm beats 2/4/5 being dropped is intended and that headers are stripped from the spoken script.
4. **Length is at the ceiling (~315 words).** Tight but with no margin; any audio-pacing pad will push it past ~90s.

Single moment holds, one witness (Moses), one thread (look and live), no second-thread crowding. Nothing rises to a KJV, CTA, or doctrinal error.
