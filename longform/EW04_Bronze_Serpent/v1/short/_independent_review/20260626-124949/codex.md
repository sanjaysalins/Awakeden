# Independent review — codex (OK, 24s)

Findings:

- **EW-G8 wrestling is too thin.** The strongest objection is only stated as event summary: “I begged Him to take the snakes away. He would not.” Moses’s internal crisis is not really voiced. The real difficulty is sharper: why would God command the image of the curse, and why would looking at it heal instead of removing the serpents? That objection is present, but not steel-manned in Moses’s own voice.

- **EW-G7 reveal is partly earned, but too compressed.** “He told me to forge the image of the very thing killing us” sets up the type well, and the John 3:14 quote legitimately turns it to Christ. But “My bronze was only a shadow” announces the meaning more than it demonstrates it. The line “They lifted Jesus on a Roman pole, made a curse for us” needs one more shown connection between “the thing killing us” and Christ bearing sin/curse.

- **EW-G9 substitution is named, but the penalty could be clearer.** “bearing our judgment in our place” is doctrinally sound and substitutionary. “made a curse for us” is also sound. But the specific penalty is still generalized as “judgment”; if this is meant to be explicit, name death/curse under God’s judgment more directly.

- **First-hearing clarity has one weak phrase.** “a night I never saw, when one they called Teacher answered a seeker” assumes knowledge of Nicodemus and John 3. A zero-Bible-knowledge listener may not know who “Teacher” or “seeker” refers to. The quote helps, but the setup is hazy.

- **Short-form tightness is under target.** The spoken narration appears below the requested ~220-320 words. It is tight, but the shortage contributes to the underdeveloped wrestling and slightly announced reveal.

- **EW-INV-4 passes.** The attributed quote, **“And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:”**, matches KJV John 3:14 verbatim and is correctly attributed to Jesus before quoting.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Strengthen Moses’s internal objection around why God gave a bronze image of the curse instead of removing the serpents.
2. Earn the Christ reveal with one clearer shown connection from serpent/curse/death to Jesus bearing the curse in sinners’ place.
3. Clarify the John 3 setup for first-time listeners without relying on “Teacher” / “seeker” shorthand.
