## Independent adversarial review — *Look and Live* (EW04 short, Moses)

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Clean on the one quoted line.** John 3:14 is KJV-exact, and `**[Jesus]**` tags the speaker before the quote.

**Gap:** No KJV from the core text (Numbers 21:8–9) is quoted at all. God’s command and the “look and live” mechanism are paraphrased only (*“God told me to forge a snake of bronze and lift it on a pole”* / *“The bitten had only to look — and live”*). Not a verbatim failure, but the piece leans on prose where the binding pericope should anchor the ear.

**Minor attribution fuzz:** *“when one they called Teacher answered a seeker”* identifies neither the seeker (Nicodemus) nor the setting. The quote is correctly Jesus’s; the frame is vague for a zero-Bible listener.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man): **missing**

There is **no wrestling beat**. Short form may compress beats, but this draft skips the honest doubt entirely.

Beat 2 has complaint, not steel-man doubt:

> *“I begged Him to take the snakes away. He would not.”*

That is Moses asking for relief — not the **strongest objection to the remedy itself**, which Moses would have felt on the ground:

- Why forge the **image of the thing killing us**? (idolatry/scandal risk — the serpent later became an idol; 2 Kings 18:4)
- Why **look**, not poultice, bronze wash, or prayer?
- Why lift the curse-token **high for all to see** instead of removing the snakes?

The beat header promises it — **“The strange thing”** — but the piece never voices Moses’s bafflement. It states the odd command and moves on. That is outline, not witness interiority.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned: **announced, not shown**

The turn is signposted, then asserted:

> *“I speak now from the far side of my life, by the light that came after — a night I never saw…”*

That is exactly the pattern the lens flags: understanding **announced** (*“by the light that came after”*), not **earned** from what Moses lived.

John 3:14 does the typological work — but **imported from outside Moses’s moment**. Moses did not hear it; the listener gets Jesus’s commentary before Moses walks the parallel himself.

These lines are theology dropped in, not drawn out of the bronze serpent:

> *“My bronze was only a shadow.”*  
> *“They lifted Jesus on a Roman pole, made a curse for us, bearing our judgment in our place.”*

A listener who only knows Beat 1–2 has: lifted image → look → live. They have **not** been shown step-by-step why that maps to Christ (lifted publicly, visible to the dying, faith-as-looking, the death-agent become the cure). *“My bronze was only a shadow”* is one sentence of assertion; the earn is skipped.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Mostly sound.** Substitution is named:

> *“bearing our judgment in our place”*

and judgment is set up earlier (*“the venom was the judgment our sin had earned”*).

**Weak link:** *“made a curse for us”* reads like Galatians 3:13 vocabulary with **no attribution, no KJV quote, no bridge** for a zero-Bible listener. A skeptic could call it imported jargon, not Moses’s earned conclusion.

**No supersession, no contrarian reading.** Typology via John 3:14 is standard and faithful.

**Nit:** *“Roman pole”* blurs pole (נֵס, standard) with cross-beam execution. Typologically defensible; literally sloppy.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

Beat 1–2 sound like Moses. Beat 3–4 slip into **essay narrator in Moses costume**:

> *“by the light that came after”*  
> *“made a curse for us”*  
> *“bearing our judgment in our place”*

That is post-Gospel commentary voice, not a wilderness leader recounting what his own hands made and what he watched bitten men do. The signpost (*“a night I never saw”*) is honest per EW-INV-11, but the lines after the quote are **not** in Moses’s idiom — they are second-hand systematic theology.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Passes.** Names Jesus; names the response (*“Come to Jesus — look, and live”*). Grace-anchored (*“costs you nothing but a look”*). Not the bare *“will you trust Him?”* template.

**Mild pressure:** *“you who are bitten — that is every one of us”* universalizes guilt quickly; still typological, not fear-manufactured.

---

### First-hearing clarity

A zero-Bible listener can follow snakebite → odd bronze cure → look → live → Jesus lifted → come look.

**Unclear without Bible background:**

- *“one they called Teacher answered a seeker”* — who, where, why it matters
- *“made a curse for us”* — undeveloped
- *“My bronze was only a shadow”* — metaphor with no on-ramp

---

### Hook, spine, short-form tightness

**Hook:** Strong. *“I am Moses. My people were dying of snakebite…”* grips immediately.

**Spine:** One witness, one thread (bronze serpent → lifted Christ). No swap.

**Length:** ~**200 spoken words** — **under** the SHORT budget in the spec (220–320; ~75–110s). Too thin to earn wrestling + reveal + invitation; reads closer to ~60–75s. No second-thread crowding, but **under-developed**, not tight-in-the-good sense.

**Sag:** No fat; the problem is **missing steel**, not bloat.

VERDICT: REVISE  
TOP FIXES:  
1. **Add a real wrestling beat in Moses’s voice** — voice the scandal of forging a serpent image and commanding a *look* instead of snake removal (the objection Beat 2 sets up but never asks).  
2. **Earn the reveal from the lived ritual before John 3:14** — walk lifted-high / public / dying-look / death-image-as-cure from what Moses forged and watched; let Jesus’s quote *confirm* what Moses has already seen, not replace the earn.  
3. **Expand to 220+ words and ground substitution in-line** — one attributed Numbers KJV line, and tie *“bearing our judgment in our place”* back to the venom/judgment already named, without unattributed Galatians shorthand (*“made a curse for us”*).
