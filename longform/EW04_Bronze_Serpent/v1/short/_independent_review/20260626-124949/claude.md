# Independent review — claude (OK, 56s)

Reading adversarially. Findings below, each tied to a line.

**KJV + attribution (EW-INV-4) — PASS.** The John 3:14 quote is exact KJV including the trailing colon: *"And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:"* Speaker is tagged `**[Jesus]**` and set up ("one they called Teacher answered a seeker"). No altered word. Clean.

**The WRESTLING is missing — EW-G8 FAIL.** This is the real problem. The format requires the witness's honest doubt to be the genuine strongest objection, internalized in his own voice. Moses never voices it. The obvious steel-man here is glaring and *Moses of all people* would feel it:
- The **scandal of the image** — God had just thundered "thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image" (Ex 20:4), and now commands Moses to forge one and tell people to look at it. That tension is the strongest objection to the whole episode, and it is completely absent.
- The **"this is absurd/magic" objection** — staring at a bronze pole cures a venom bite? A zero-Bible listener feels this immediately; the narration never lets Moses feel it.
The closest thing to wrestling is *"I begged Him to take the snakes away. He would not."* — but that's a single beat of resignation, not a doubt that gets internalized and answered. Right now the strongest objection is strawmanned by silence.

**The turn's voice device is a strain — EW-G10 / first-hearing risk.** *"I speak now from the far side of my life, by the light that came after — a night I never saw, when one they called Teacher answered a seeker."* Moses is quoting a conversation (Nicodemus, John 3) he was never present for, and the script asks the listener to accept a post-mortem narrator in one heady clause. It's *handled* ("a night I never saw"), but it's the most essayistic, least characterful line in the piece, and a zero-Bible listener may not track that Moses has jumped centuries. The hook's plain voice ("I am Moses. My people were dying of snakebite") is far stronger — Beat 3 drops out of character.

**REVEAL (EW-G7) — earned, PASS.** The type→Christ turn isn't asserted; it's shown from the very text Moses lived and from Jesus' own words drawing the parallel. *"My bronze was only a shadow"* lands honestly.

**Doctrine / substitution (EW-G9) — PASS.** Penalty named: *"the venom was the judgment our sin had earned."* Substitution named: *"made a curse for us, bearing our judgment in our place"* (Gal 3:13 echo). No supersession — type/shadow language only. Sound.

**CTA (EW-INV-2) — PASS.** *"Come to Jesus — look, and live."* Names Jesus, names the response (come/look), grace-anchored, *"costs you nothing but a look."* No fear, no gain-loss, not the tired "will you trust Him?" template. Good.

**Tightness — PASS, ~235 words.** One thread ("look and live") held hook→punch, no second thread crowding. Could trim *"by the light that came after"* clutter.

Net: doctrinally sound and well-built, but the single named gate it fails — EW-G8 wrestling — is the spine of this format, and the absence of the bronze-image scandal is a real miss, not a nitpick.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add the genuine wrestling (EW-G8): give Moses the honest objection in his own voice — the scandal that God who forbade graven images now commands one, and/or "how can a look at bronze heal a bite?" — then let the text answer it. This is the format's steel-man and it's currently absent.
2. Rescue Beat 3's voice (EW-G10): the post-mortem "a night I never saw" device and "by the light that came after" are essayistic and break first-person clarity — simplify so the witness stays in character when he hands off to Jesus' words.
3. Sharpen the seeker/teacher handoff for a zero-Bible listener: "one they called Teacher answered a seeker" is vague — make the leap to the cross unmistakable so the John 3:14 reveal isn't lost.
