## Independent adversarial review — *Look and Live* (Moses, eyewitness-short)

Judging the artifact as pasted. Default skepticism.

---

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution: **PASS (narrow)**

The only formal quote is exact KJV John 3:14:

**"And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:"**

Speaker is framed before the quote: *"when one they called Teacher answered a seeker:"* plus `**[Jesus]**`. That clears the bar.

**Residual weakness:** *"one they called Teacher answered a seeker"* is indirect attribution — not *"Jesus said"* or *"as the gospel records."* Not a verbatim breach, but thin for a listener who does not know John 3 / Nicodemus.

No Numbers 21 KJV is quoted. Paraphrase like *"God told me to forge a snake of bronze and lift it on a pole"* is not inside `**"..."**` — fine for EW-INV-4, but it means the witness’s own moment never gets Scripture’s exact wording.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus: **PASS**

Close names Jesus and a specific response:

- *"Come to Jesus — look, and live."*

*"costs you nothing but a look"* and *"The cure was never in you"* are grace-anchored, not gain-loss or fear pressure. No bare *"will you trust Him?"* template.

**Minor:** *"So hear me, you who are bitten — that is every one of us"* universalizes sin cleanly; not a fail, but it is sermon-directive rather than Moses-in-the-camp testimony.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling: **FAIL (absent / strawman-adjacent)**

There is **no internalized steel-man** in Moses’s voice.

*"I begged Him to take the snakes away. He would not."* is **intercession summary**, not wrestling. It states that God refused removal — it does not voice Moses’s **genuine hardest objection** to the command itself.

For **this** witness, the real steel-man is sharper and is missing entirely:

- Why command the **likeness of the curse** (*"forge the image of the very thing killing us"*) instead of removing the serpents?
- Why heal by **looking at an image** at all — especially for Moses, who smashed the golden calf and carried *"Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image"*?
- Why does a cure so **plain** (*"only to look"*) feel like an offense to pride?

Beat 2 labels the paradox (*"The strange thing"*) but Moses never **sits in** the objection. The piece jumps from strange command → obeyed outcome (*"The bitten had only to look — and live"*) with no doubt beat. That is the pattern other EW shorts flag as announced faith, not wrestled faith.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned: **FAIL / weak**

The type→Christ turn is **mostly asserted after an external NT receipt**, not shown from what Moses lived.

Problem lines:

- *"I speak now from the far side of my life, by the light that came after — a night I never saw"* — cross-time signpost is allowed (EW-INV-11), but the **reveal rides on a scene Moses never witnessed**, not on his pole moment.
- John 3:14 is the right hinge — Jesus names Moses’s serpent — but the bridge **after** the quote is announcement:
  - *"My bronze was only a shadow."*
  - *"They lifted Jesus on a Roman pole, made a curse for us, bearing our judgment in our place."*

Those are **typology labels**, not Moses reasoning from bite → lifted likeness of the killer → look → live → therefore the Son of man must be lifted likewise.

Beat 2 plants the puzzle (*"forge the image of the very thing killing us"*) but Beat 3 **does not pay it off** before jumping to shadow/substitution language. The listener is told the meaning; not walked to it through the wilderness detail.

Contrast what an earned short turn needs: one shown step — *the curse-image was lifted so the dying could look and live; so the curse-bearer would be lifted for us* — before or alongside the John 3 quote. Here the quote does most of the work; Moses adds commentary.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine: **PASS with caveats**

Orthodox typology; no supersession; no contrarian reading.

Substitution is **partially** named:

- *"bearing our judgment in our place"* — in-our-place logic present.
- *"made a curse for us"* — Gal 3:13 echo.

**Caveats:**

- **Penalty** stays generalized as *"judgment"* / *"venom was the judgment our sin had earned"* — serpent-bite **death** is vivid in Beat 1 but not explicitly tied to what Christ bore on the pole.
- *"They lifted Jesus on a Roman pole, made a curse for us"* — grammatically muddy: *"They lifted… made a curse"* can read as Roman/crowd action rather than **Christ being made a curse for us** (Gal 3:13 wording is clearer).
- *"Roman pole"* is editorial commentary, not Moses’s lived vocabulary — minor sermon bleed.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character: **mixed / weak in reveal**

**Beats 1–2:** workable Moses — *"I am Moses"*, *"I begged Him"*, first-person act.

**Beat 3 breaks character into essayist:**

- *"by the light that came after — a night I never saw"* — literary retrospective frame dominates.
- *"one they called Teacher answered a seeker"* — third-person Gospel report, not testimony.
- *"My bronze was only a shadow"* — polished typology, not a man who forged bronze in the dust.

Moses becomes a **typology narrator in a Moses mask** at the turn, not a witness threading his own day to Christ.

---

### First-hearing clarity: **weak spots**

- Opening crisis is clear: snakebite, bronze serpent, look and live.
- *"Teacher… seeker"* — opaque without John 3 context.
- *"Son of man be lifted up"* — unexplained for zero-Bible knowledge (crucifixion not named in the quote).
- *"made a curse for us"* / *"My bronze was only a shadow"* — assumes typology literacy.

---

### Hook, spine, short-form

**Hook:** strong — immediate stakes in line one.

**Spine:** one witness, one moment (bronze serpent), one thread to look/live — no swap.

**Length:** ~**186 spoken words** (excluding headers/metadata) — **below** the 220–320 / ~90s spec (EW-INV-8). The shortage is not “tightness”; it correlates with missing wrestling and a compressed, asserted reveal. Room exists to deepen without crowding a second thread.

**Single moment:** mostly lands in Beats 1–2; **sags at the turn** where the arc should close.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Add real wrestling (EW-G8)** — voice Moses’s steel-man in his own words: why God left the serpents, why the cure is the **likeness of the curse**, and (for this witness) the graven-image dread — before the turn, not just *"I begged Him to take the snakes away."*
2. **Earn the reveal from the pole moment (EW-G7)** — walk bite → lifted image of what was killing them → look → live → then John 3:14; cut or rework *"My bronze was only a shadow"* and the secondhand *"Teacher… seeker"* frame so Christ is reached from Moses’s lived detail, not post-hoc commentary.
3. **Hit word budget and name substitution cleanly (EW-INV-8 / EW-G9)** — expand to ~220–320w into the wrestling/reveal gap; rewrite *"made a curse for us"* so both halves are explicit (death/judgment our sin earned + Christ bore it **in our place**), not passive *"judgment"* alone.
