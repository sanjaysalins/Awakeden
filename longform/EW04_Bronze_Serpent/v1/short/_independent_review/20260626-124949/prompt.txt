# Look and Live — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** Moses, who led Israel through the wilderness  ·  **Core text:** Numbers 21:8-9 → John 3:14  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** the bronze serpent lifted on a pole — the image of the very thing killing them, looked at in faith — fulfilled in the Son of man lifted up on the cross (John 3:14); look and live
---

## Beat 1 — Hook
I am Moses. My people were dying of snakebite — and God told me to forge a snake of bronze and lift it on a pole.

## Beat 2 — The strange thing
The serpents were no accident — we had spoken against God, and the venom was the judgment our sin had earned. I begged Him to take the snakes away. He would not. Instead He told me to forge the image of the very thing killing us, and lift it high. The bitten had only to look — and live.

## Beat 3 — The turn
I speak now from the far side of my life, by the light that came after — a night I never saw, when one they called Teacher answered a seeker:

**[Jesus]** **"And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:"**

My bronze was only a shadow. They lifted Jesus on a Roman pole, made a curse for us, bearing our judgment in our place.

## Beat 4 — The punch
So hear me, you who are bitten — that is every one of us. The cure was never in you. It is lifted up in plain sight, and costs you nothing but a look. Come to Jesus — look, and live.
