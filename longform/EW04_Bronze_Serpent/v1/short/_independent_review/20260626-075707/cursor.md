## Independent adversarial review — *Look and Live* (eyewitness-short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**The two formal quotes pass.** Both are attributed before the bold span and match KJV wording:

- `**[the LORD]** **"Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live."**` — Numbers 21:8, verbatim.
- `**[Jesus]** **"And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:"**` — John 3:14, verbatim.

**Accuracy slip (not a quote violation, but real):** Beat 1 says *"And God told a dying man he could be healed by nothing in his own strength — only by a look."* In Numbers 21, God told **Moses** the command; the bitten looked afterward. A zero-Bible listener may think God spoke directly to one victim, not that Moses forged the sign for the camp.

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA-on-Jesus

**Mostly passes.** Jesus is named; the response is named — *believe on Him*, *Turn yours*, *Look, and live*, *Come to Him* — not a bare *"will you trust Him?"* template. *"You need not clean the wound first"* is grace-anchored, not fear/gain-loss framing.

**Minor note:** *"you who are bitten — and that is every one of us"* universalizes sin-death before the cross logic is fully walked out for a first-time listener, but it stays within biblical metaphor, not manufactured pressure.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned — **weak / partly asserted**

The John 3:14 quote is the right anchor — Jesus Himself names Moses's serpent. But the turn around it is **announced and explained**, not shown from what Moses lived.

- *"For years I could not tell what it meant."* — understanding is declared missing, not dramatized from the pole moment.
- *"I speak now from the far side of my life, by the light that came after."* — retrospective essay frame (same device flagged in sibling shorts), not witness discovery.
- *"A Teacher rose in Israel; by night a man sought Him, and He spoke of my serpent:"* — Moses reports a Nicodemus scene he **never saw**; the reveal rides on a secondhand Gospel report, not on the wilderness day itself.
- *"My serpent was only a shadow; Christ was the body that cast it."* — typology **asserted** in theological language, not walked out from bite → lifted curse-image → look → live.

Beat 3 plants the puzzle — *"a command so strange I have pondered it ever since"* — but Beat 6 **does not pay off that pondering** from Numbers 21 before jumping to John 3. The best earned bridge (*"the image of the very thing that was killing us"*) is stated in Beat 3 and then abandoned in favor of shadow/body commentary.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man) — **absent**

There is **no internalized steel-man** in Moses's voice. SHORT compresses to B1→B3→B6→B7, but sibling panels treat wrestling as **non-zero** — one honest line minimum. This piece has almost none.

What exists:
- *"I prayed for the serpents to be taken away — but the LORD did not."* — real tension, then dropped.
- *"A command so strange I have pondered it ever since"* — mild wonder, not doubt.

**Unvoiced objections Moses would actually carry** (and the LONG version voices explicitly):
- Why forge the **image of the very thing killing us**?
- Why heal by **looking at a bronze likeness**, not by removing the serpents when Israel confessed?
- For Moses specifically: why command a **graven image** from the man who smashed the golden calf and carried *"Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image"*?

The piece never lets Moses argue with God in his own voice. That is a gate failure, not a polish note.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine sound

**Core typology is evangelical and orthodox:** lifted image, faith-response, substitution language in *"made to be the curse, bearing the judgment our sin had earned."* No supersession problem.

**Invented detail — serious:** *"The dying child knew enough to turn his head."* Numbers 21 names no child, no age-specific victim, no head-turning vignette. This is **fabricated scene dressing** presented as biblical memory. A listener with zero Bible knowledge will assume it is in Scripture. Same class of failure flagged on Noah's invented eyewitness claims.

**Interpretive color:** *"Roman pole"* is editorial commentary, not Moses's lived vocabulary — minor, but it nudges Beat 6 toward sermon rather than testimony.

**Substitution:** *"bearing the judgment our sin had earned"* names penalty directionally but could be sharper — what penalty (death from the bite / curse of sin) and what Christ bore *in our place*, the way Jonah's short nails it: *"The penalty I had earned, He took in my place."*

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Beat 1–3:** Strong Moses — *"dying faster than I could dig the graves"* is vivid, first-person, in-character.

**Beat 6 breaks the eyewitness contract:**
- *"A Teacher rose in Israel; by night a man sought Him"* — detached third-person Gospel narration.
- *"They lifted Jesus up on a Roman pole"* — posthumous omniscience Moses did not live.

Moses becomes a **typology essayist wearing a Moses mask**, not a man who forged bronze in the dust and is now wrestling what it meant. Compare Jonah's short, which frames the cross-time quote honestly: *"I tell you now, by the mouth of the One who claimed them."*

---

### First-hearing clarity

- Opening crisis is clear and immediate.
- *"A Teacher rose in Israel; by night a man sought Him"* assumes Nicodemus/John 3 knowledge a zero-Bible listener lacks — a second mini-thread that does not help the short.
- *"Son of man"* and *"made to be the curse"* are doctrinally right but **unexplained** for a first-time hearer.
- The invented *"dying child"* actively misleads.

---

### Hook, spine, short-form

**Hook:** Strong — graves, serpents, dust, stakes in line one.

**Spine:** One thread (pole → lifted Christ → look/live), no witness swap.

**Length:** ~260 spoken words — inside the ~220–320 band; structurally tight.

**Land:** The single moment mostly lands, but **sag sits between Beat 3 and Beat 6** where wrestling should live; Beat 6's essay pivot slows the earned turn the format requires.
