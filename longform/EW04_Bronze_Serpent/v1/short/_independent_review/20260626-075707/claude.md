# Independent review — claude (OK, 75s)

Read adversarially. KJV and doctrine hold up; the steel-man and the setup do not. Specifics:

## KJV verbatim + attribution (EW-INV-4) — PASS
- **[the LORD]** quote checks exact against **Num 21:8** KJV ("Make thee a fiery serpent… when he looketh upon it, shall live."). Attributed before quoting. ✓
- **[Jesus]** quote checks exact against **John 3:14** KJV, trailing colon and all ("And as Moses lifted up the serpent… must the Son of man be lifted up:"). Attributed. ✓

## CTA-on-Jesus (EW-INV-2) — PASS
"Lift your eyes to Jesus, lifted up for you, and **believe on Him**… **Come to Him.**" Response is named (believe / come), grace-anchored ("**You need not clean the wound first**"), no fear/gain-loss, not the tired bare template. Clean.

## Doctrine / substitution (EW-G9) — PASS
"made to be the curse, bearing the judgment our sin had earned" — penalty + what Christ bore, both named (Gal 3:13 / 2 Cor 5:21). "My serpent was only a shadow; Christ was the body that cast it" is shadow/substance (Col 2:17), **not** supersession. "It was never the bronze, but the God who said look and live" is a sound anti-idolatry beat. ✓

## EW-G8 Wrestling — **WEAK (main flag)**
This is the failure. The witness's "doubt" is only **"a command so strange I have pondered it ever since"** and **"For years I could not tell what it meant."** That is vague unease, not the genuine strongest objection internalized in Moses's voice. The real steel-man is right there in the text Moses lived and is dodged:
- God **forbids graven images** — yet commands Moses to **forge** one and lift it for the nation to look at. That tension is the live objection, and Israel later proved it real by **worshipping this very serpent as an idol (Nehushtan, 2 Kgs 18:4)**.
- Or the plain absurdity: how does **looking at a metal snake** heal a venom bite?

The narration gestures at "strange" but never voices either. Steel-man it in Moses's own mouth, then let "It was never the bronze, but the God who said look and live" answer it — right now that great line answers a question the script never honestly asked.

## Setup gap — bite=sin is asserted, never established
The serpents' **origin is never given**. We're never told the snakes came as judgment on Israel's sin/murmuring (Num 21:5-6). So "The people **confessed**" (Beat 3) is unmotivated — confessed *what*? A zero-Bible listener has no idea. Then Beat 7 lands the whole application on it: "you who are **bitten** — and that is every one of us," equating bite with sin. The payoff fires on a fuse that was never lit. One clause in Beat 1 ("they had turned on God, and the dust turned on them" or similar) fixes it.

## VOICE / vantage (EW-G10) — mostly holds, one soft spot
First-person Moses is consistent and characterful ("dying faster than I could dig the graves"). The post-death vantage ("I speak now from the far side of my life, by the light that came after") is the format's DNA and is fine. But **"A Teacher rose in Israel; by night a man sought Him"** is detached third-person reportage of an event Moses didn't live — it briefly slips from witness to narrator. Acceptable, but it's the weakest seam.

## Presentation
Beat labels jump **1 → 3 → 6 → 7**. If that's a build artifact, it'll confuse; if intentional, drop the numbers for the spoken cut. Word count ~315 — at the very top of the ~220-320 band, so the wrestling fix must trade words, not just add them.

## Hook / spine — PASS
Hook grips ("dying faster than I could dig the graves"). One witness, one thread (look and live), no swap. Reveal (EW-G7) is genuinely *earned* — Jesus's own words quote Moses's serpent, so the type→Christ turn is shown from the text, not announced.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Replace the vague "so strange" with the real steel-man in Moses's voice — God forbidding images yet commanding one (the Nehushtan/idolatry tension, or the absurdity of healing-by-looking) — so "It was never the bronze, but the God who said look and live" actually answers a question the script asked.
2. Establish in Beat 1 that the serpents came as judgment for Israel's sin, so "the people confessed" is motivated and the closing "you who are bitten… every one of us" (bite=sin) has a laid fuse.
3. Tighten the Jesus/Nicodemus line so it stays in first-person witness voice, and fix/remove the jumped beat numbers (1→3→6→7); keep the total ≤320 words by trading, not adding.
