# Gallery Plan — EW04 Bronze Serpent ("Look and Live")

**Witness:** Moses · **Core text:** Numbers 21:8-9 → John 3:14
**Engine:** guided Baroque-gallery walk — ONE rich painting per beat; eye sees the whole canvas, then HARD-CUTS to the named elements (tour order). Lands on the risen Christ.
**Runtime target:** ~70s, 9:16 vertical. 7 paintings.

Shared style spine (in every subject_block): *Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text.* Animation-clean: ≤3 faces, crowds in shadow, negative space, no tiny repeated detail or legible text.

---

## 1. [Beat 1 — Hook] [0:00–0:11] [NEW]
**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. A single aged Hebrew prophet — Moses — an old weathered shepherd-leader with a deep-lined sun-darkened face and grey beard, wrapped in a rough desert mantle, half-lit by a low amber firelight against deep shadow. He faces the viewer with weary, knowing eyes, one open hand half-raised as if beginning to speak. Vast dark negative space around him. One dominant hero subject, no crowd.
**Named elements (tour order):** 1) Moses' eyes (weary, direct gaze) → 2) his lined brow and grey beard → 3) his raised open hand.
**Lands on Christ:** establishes the eyewitness who will point past himself to the One the bronze only foreshadowed.

## 2. [Beat 2 — The strange thing: the judgment] [0:11–0:24] [NEW]
**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern desert camp at dusk, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. A scene of plague and dread: one or two fallen Hebrew figures collapsed on the sand in the foreground, faces twisted in pain, a coiling bronze-and-amber desert serpent striking near a bare arm. The wider camp and crowd dissolve into deep shadow behind. Firelit anguish, heavy darkness, the venom of judgment fallen on a rebellious people. Crowds in shadow, ≤3 lit faces.
**Named elements (tour order):** 1) the striking serpent's head → 2) the bitten man's anguished face → 3) his outflung hand on the sand.
**Lands on Christ:** names the disease — sin's judgment — that every viewer shares; the wound the cross will answer.

## 3. [Beat 2 — The strange thing: the bronze lifted up] [0:24–0:34] [NEW]
**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern desert camp, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. Moses, lit from below by firelight, lifts high a tall rough wooden pole/standard planted in the sand; bound at its top is a clearly sculpted serpent of polished bronze, glinting warm in the light, raised above the camp. The serpent is unmistakably a cast bronze figure on a plain wooden pole in the open desert — a standard, not a charm, not a symbol. Camp dissolves into shadow below. Dominant single subject, vast sky negative space above.
**Named elements (tour order):** 1) the bronze serpent gleaming at the pole's top → 2) Moses' upraised hands gripping the pole → 3) Moses' upturned face.
**Lands on Christ:** the lifted bronze on a wooden pole is the deliberate visual prefigure of the One lifted on a wooden cross.

## 4. [Beat 2 — Look and live] [0:34–0:42] [NEW]
**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern desert, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. A bitten Hebrew man, fallen on the sand in the foreground, weak and pale, turns his face and lifts his eyes UPWARD toward a distant warm gleam of bronze on a pole — and life returns to his face. A single shaft of warm light falls on him from above. One dominant figure, the source of his rescue small and bright in the upper distance, the rest in shadow.
**Named elements (tour order):** 1) the bitten man's upturned, hope-filled eyes → 2) his outstretched reaching hand → 3) the distant bronze gleam he looks toward.
**Lands on Christ:** the upward look of faith that heals — the exact gaze the punch will ask the viewer to turn toward Jesus.

## 5. [Beat 3 — The turn: the night, the Teacher and the seeker] [0:42–0:51] [NEW]
**subject_block:** Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Jerusalem interior at night, vertical 9:16, no modern elements, no text. Two robed men face each other across a low oil lamp on a stone ledge: on the right, Jesus — calm, luminous, dark-haired and bearded, lit warm by the lamp; on the left, an older seeker (Nicodemus) leaning in from the shadow, listening intently. Only the lamp lights their faces; the room falls away into night. Two faces, deep darkness, intimate hush.
**Named elements (tour order):** 1) the oil lamp's flame → 2) Jesus' face turned to the seeker → 3) the seeker's listening eyes.
**Lands on Christ:** Jesus Himself names the bronze serpent as the picture of His own lifting-up (John 3:14) — His own words make the turn.

## 6. [Beat 3 — Lifted up on a Roman pole] [0:51–1:01] [REUSE: EW01_Two_Goats/v1/short/christ_turn.png — crucifixion]
**subject_block:** *(REUSE existing painting — risen/crucified Christ bank.)* Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting: Christ lifted up on the wooden Roman cross, head bowed, against a darkened sky — the fulfillment of the serpent lifted on the pole, made a curse in our place.
**Named elements (tour order):** 1) Christ's bowed face → 2) His pierced, outstretched hands on the crossbeam → 3) the foot of the upright wooden beam against dark sky.
**Lands on Christ:** the cross IS the answer to painting 3 — the same lifting-up, the serpent's shadow now fulfilled in the Son of man bearing our judgment.

## 7. [Beat 4 — The punch: look and live] [1:01–1:10] [REUSE: EW01_Two_Goats/v1/short/visual_9x16_test/christ.png — risen Christ]
**subject_block:** *(REUSE existing painting — the risen-Christ LANDING.)* Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro, Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting: the risen Christ standing in a torn-veil doorway, robed in light, one hand extended toward the viewer in welcome.
**Named elements (tour order):** 1) Christ's extended open hand → 2) His risen face meeting the viewer's eyes → 3) the light breaking through the torn-veil doorway.
**Lands on Christ:** the CTA — "Lift your eyes to Jesus, lifted up for you. Look, and live." The viewer's upward look from painting 4 lands here on the living Christ; costs nothing but a look.

### Doctrinal notes
- **Type→fulfillment is Jesus' own** (John 3:14) — not invented; the bronze serpent prefigures the Son of man lifted up. Sound.
- **Serpent imagery guarded:** painting 3 specifies a *cast bronze serpent on a plain wooden pole/standard in the open desert camp* — explicitly NOT an occult charm or medical caduceus. Verify the render doesn't drift to a coiled-snake medical symbol.
- **The cure is Christ, not the look itself:** the look is the empty hand of faith ("costs you nothing but a look"), not a meritorious act — grace-anchored, no works framing.
- **Lands on the RISEN Christ** (painting 7), as channel law requires; the cross (painting 6) is the lifting-up, the resurrection doorway is the invitation.
