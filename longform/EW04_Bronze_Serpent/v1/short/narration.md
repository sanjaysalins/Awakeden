## Beat 1 — I was there
I am Moses. I was there the day my people were dying faster than I could dig the graves — fiery serpents in the dust. And God told a dying man he could be healed by nothing in his own strength — only by a look.

## Beat 3 — The act
The people confessed, and I prayed for the serpents to be taken away — but the LORD did not. He gave a command so strange I have pondered it ever since:

**[the LORD]** **"Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live."**

He told me to forge the image of the very thing that was killing us, and lift it high. A bitten man would lift his eyes to that bronze — and live; he looked, and the death let go.

## Beat 6 — The reveal
For years I could not tell what it meant. I speak now from the far side of my life, by the light that came after. A Teacher rose in Israel; by night a man sought Him, and He spoke of my serpent:

**[Jesus]** **"And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:"**

My serpent was only a shadow; Christ was the body that cast it. They lifted Jesus up on a Roman pole — made to be the curse, bearing the judgment our sin had earned. It was never the bronze, but the God who said look and live.

## Beat 7 — The invitation
So hear me, you who are bitten — and that is every one of us. Do not look to me, nor to the bronze. Lift your eyes to Jesus, lifted up for you, and believe on Him. You need not clean the wound first. The dying child knew enough to turn his head. Turn yours. Look, and live. Come to Him.
