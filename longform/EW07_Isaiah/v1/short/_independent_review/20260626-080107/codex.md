# Independent review — codex (OK, 54s)

**Findings**

EW-INV-4 mostly passes on verbatim KJV, but attribution is weak. The Isaiah 53:5 quote is exact KJV, but the line before it, “The blows fell — and every wound was ours, not his own,” does not clearly attribute the quoted words to Isaiah’s prophecy or the LORD before quoting. The Acts 8:35 phrase **"preached unto him Jesus."** is exact KJV, but “where Philip read my words and” blurs narration into quotation; Philip did not say those words as dialogue.

EW-G8 is the main weakness. Isaiah’s wrestling is too thin: “I could not yet tell his name” and “not knowing the One it was about” are mild uncertainty, not the strongest internal objection. A real Isaiah objection would be sharper: How can the innocent Servant bear the guilty? How can the LORD lay iniquity on one who had none?

EW-G7 is partially earned. “He laid our guilt on a Servant who had none” draws from the text well, but “the death I watched turn to life ended in an empty tomb” jumps beyond Isaiah 53:5–6 without showing the textual bridge, especially resurrection from Isaiah 53:10–12.

Doctrine is sound overall: substitution is clear in “He laid our guilt on a Servant who had none” and “Trust the One wounded for your transgressions.” But “he bore us away” is muddy; the KJV says the LORD laid iniquity on him, not that he bore the people away. That could confuse the penalty-bearing substitution.

Voice is competent but somewhat essay-like. “Type→fulfillment,” “by the light that came seven hundred years after me,” and “fulfilled in Christ crucified and risen” feel like external theological framing more than Isaiah’s first-person witness.

First-hearing clarity is mostly strong. The sheep/guilt/wounded Servant thread is followable. The weakest phrase for a new listener is “believe the report I wrote,” which depends on knowing Isaiah 53:1.

Short form is tight enough. One moment mostly lands, though the Acts 8 desert road reference risks becoming a second thread.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Strengthen Isaiah’s real objection around innocent substitution: how can the guiltless Servant bear the guilty?
2. Make every bold KJV quote explicitly attributed before the quote, especially Isaiah 53:5 and Acts 8:35.
3. Earn the resurrection/empty tomb reveal from Isaiah’s own text, or keep the reveal focused on crucified substitution.
