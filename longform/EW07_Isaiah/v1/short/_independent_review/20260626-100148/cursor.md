## Independent adversarial review — *The Suffering Servant* (eyewitness-short)

### EW-INV-4 — KJV verbatim + attribution

**Quotes look verbatim.** Checked against the project KJV cache: Isa 53:5, Isa 53:6, and the Acts 8:35 fragment `"preached unto him Jesus."` match.

**Attribution is weak on the Isaiah blocks — likely FAIL under strict EW-INV-4.**

1. **`The blows fell — and every wound was ours, not his own:`** then the 53:5 quote — no speaker/source tag. *"The blows fell"* is witness paraphrase, not attribution. The listener cannot tell whether this is the LORD, the vision, or Isaiah’s scroll speaking.

2. **`Yet the vision pressed plainer:`** then the 53:6 quote — same problem. *"The vision pressed plainer"* describes Isaiah’s experience; it does not attribute the quote (e.g. *“I wrote,” “Scripture says,” “the Spirit gave me”*) before the bold KJV.

3. Acts 8:35 is better: **`Philip ran to him and`** before **`"preached unto him Jesus."`** — Philip is named. Still no `**[Philip]**` tag per spec convention, but context is adequate.

**Non-quote paraphrase drift (minor):** **`wanted by no one`** is not KJV (53:3 is *“despised and rejected of men”*). Fine outside quotes, but it shows the witness voice sliding toward modern gloss rather than the text he claims he wrote *“word for word.”*

---

### EW-INV-2 — CTA on Jesus

**Names Jesus and names a response:** **`Come to Jesus.`** — clear, grace-anchored, not fear/gain-loss.

**Banned-template spirit violated:**

- **`Will you trust the report I was given to write?`** — this is a dressed-up *“will you trust…”* close. The Isaiah 53:1 echo is clever, but the lens explicitly flags tired bare trust templates; this is the same move with extra words. Deterministic gate may not catch it (not an exact substring of `"will you trust him"`), but panel intent is FAIL here.

**Soft benefit framing at the close:**

- **`Be healed by his stripes.`** — biblically grounded, but it ends on a **healing payoff** rather than on Christ Himself after **`Come to Jesus.`** For a zero-Bible listener, that can sound like “trust the report → get healed,” i.e. response tied to benefit more than to the Person.

---

### EW-G7 — Reveal earned vs. asserted

**Partially earned in Beat 3, mostly asserted in Beat 6.**

**Earned:** Beat 3 actually *shows* substitution from the lived vision — **`every wound was ours, not his own`**, the 53:5–6 quotes, and **`He laid our guilt on a Servant who had none`**. That is the steel of the type.

**Asserted / under-shown:**

1. **`I tell you from the far side of my days, by a light seven hundred years after me. A report reached me:`** — EW-INV-11 allows a signposted cross-time device, but this is still **announcement**, not demonstration. The turn to Christ is delivered as posthumous intel, not traced step-by-step from what Isaiah already wrote.

2. **`That was his name. Jesus — the Servant, the arm of the LORD made bare.`** — **`That was his name`** is a label drop after one Acts fragment. Nothing in Beat 6 *shows* Philip opening Isaiah’s scroll and connecting wounded-for-transgressions → crucified Servant. The listener is told the identification; it is not walked.

3. **`the Servant I had seen wounded did not stay among the dead`** — resurrection is **reported**, not drawn from the pinned core text (53:5–6). For a short pinned to those verses, this adds a second fulfillment claim without textual runway in the artifact.

4. Beat 6 stacks **four** events in one beat: Man led to die → silent → not staying dead → desert road → Ethiopian → Philip. That is not one witnessed moment unfolding; it is a montage.

---

### EW-G8 — Wrestling (steel-man)

**Mostly strong on penal substitution:**

- **`How can the innocent justly bear the guilty? Why would the LORD bruise His own righteous Servant?`** — real objection, in Isaiah’s voice, not a strawman.

**Weak spots:**

1. **Resolved too fast:** **`Yet the vision pressed plainer:`** + quote — the doubt does not bite; the next line overwrites it. No honest “I still did not see how this could be just.”

2. **Misses Isaiah’s strongest interpretive steel-man:** Jewish/corporate-Israel readings of the Servant song are the historic counter-move. This piece never wrestles *“Was the Servant my people — not one Man?”* Penal substitution is valid, but for **Isaiah specifically**, the omitted objection is the sharper one.

---

### EW-G9 — Doctrine

**Sound on substitution:** penalty named (transgressions, iniquities, guilt laid on innocent Servant; stripes/healing; **`he has borne it`**).

**No supersession detected.**

**Doctrinal fuzziness:**

- **`the arm of the LORD made bare`** — Isa 52:10 / 53:1 imagery dropped without setup; zero-Bible listeners won’t know what “arm of the LORD” means or why it proves Jesus.

- Posthumous **`A report reached me`** is allowed as device, but **`they led a Man away to die, and he opened not his mouth`** blends **Isaiah 53:7** language into a **historical crucifixion report** without showing that the silent-Servant line Isaiah wrote is the same line Philip preached. Risk: typology by collage, not by argument.

---

### EW-G10 — Voice / character

**Beat 1 and Beat 3:** credible first-person seer — **`I wrote it word for word`**, **`not knowing the One it was about`**.

**Beat 6 breaks the eyewitness costume:**

- Isaiah did not live to see Philip, the Ethiopian, or the resurrection. The signpost helps, but **`A report reached me`** still reads like an omniscient narrator in prophet drag, not a character recounting *one* lived moment.

**Preacher cadence in Beat 7:** **`Be still, and hear me.`** — pulpit register, not the grief-heavy seer of Beat 1.

---

### First-hearing clarity

A listener with no Bible background will lose the thread in Beat 6:

- Who is **Philip**?
- Who is the **Ethiopian eunuch**?
- What is **`the arm of the LORD made bare`**?
- Why is a dead prophet **`seven hundred years after me`** getting **`reports`**?

Beat 3 is followable; Beat 6 is not.

---

### Hook, spine, SHORT tightness

**Hook:** **`I never understood`** / **`could not tell his name`** — grips; pays off in **`That was his name. Jesus`**. Good single thread on naming.

**SHORT form:** ~290 spoken words — inside budget.

**Single moment:** **FAIL.** Beat 3’s vision is the moment; Beat 6 imports a **second scene** (Acts 8 road) that crowds the one act. Sag is low, but **dual-thread reveal** (vision substitution + road preaching) splits the landing.

**Missing beat compression note:** Wrestling lives inside Beat 3 (`But I wrestled.`) — acceptable for SHORT — but Beat 6’s event pile is where tightness breaks.

---

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. **Earn the reveal from Isaiah’s own 53:5–6 vision before Acts 8 names Jesus** — cut or shrink the posthumous montage (`report reached me`, Ethiopian, Philip) and show the type→Christ turn from substitution/silent-Servant lines Isaiah already wrote; let Acts 8:35 confirm, not carry, the reveal (EW-G7 / first-hearing / single moment).
2. **Fix EW-INV-4 attribution on both Isaiah quotes** — explicit pre-quote tags (`**[Isaiah]**` / *“I wrote:”* / *“the Spirit gave me”*) before **`"But he was wounded…"`** and **`"All we like sheep…"`**; keep Philip tagged before the Acts fragment.
3. **Replace the trust-template close** — drop **`Will you trust the report I was given to write?`** (and probably **`Be healed by his stripes`** as the final beat); end on a named grace response already present (`Come to Jesus`) or a fresh invitation verb (`receive`, `turn`) tied to **`he has borne it`**, not report-trust or healing payoff (EW-INV-2).
