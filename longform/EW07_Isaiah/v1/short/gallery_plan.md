# EW07 Isaiah — Gallery Painting Plan (short, ~70s, 9:16)

**Witness:** Isaiah the prophet, son of Amoz · **Core:** Isaiah 53:5–6 → Acts 8:35
**Engine:** guided gallery walk — one rich Baroque oil painting per beat; the eye sees the WHOLE
canvas, then HARD-CUTS to named elements (the eye-tour targets). LANDS ON THE RISEN CHRIST.
**Beats:** Hook → strange thing (3 panels) → turn (2 panels) → punch (cross → risen).
**Shared style tail (applies to every painting):** *Baroque oil painting, deep chiaroscuro,
Caravaggio/Rembrandt lighting, ancient biblical-period Near-Eastern setting, vertical 9:16,
no modern elements, no text.* Animation-clean: ≤3 faces, crowds in shadow, generous negative
space, no tiny repeated detail, no legible writing.

---

## 1 — [Hook] [0–9s] [NEW]
**subject_block:** An aged Hebrew prophet alone at night, hunched over a half-unrolled scroll on a
low stone table, writing by a single guttering oil lamp; deep chiaroscuro, the warm lamp the only
light; his face lined and haunted; on the far shadowed wall a faint cross-shaped beam-shadow he does
not see; everything else swallowed in darkness; vertical 9:16. *(+ shared style tail)*
**Named elements (tour order):** (1) the prophet's haunted eyes; (2) the writing hand pressing the
quill into the scroll; (3) the lamp flame; (4) the faint cross-shaped shadow on the back wall.
**Lands on Christ:** the unseen cross-shadow seeds the whole short — he writes an execution he cannot name.

## 2 — [Strange thing · the wounded Servant] [9–18s] [NEW]
**subject_block:** A lone despised Servant figure standing bowed in a narrow shaft of pale light,
his back and shoulders marred and striped with wounds, head lowered, hands loosely bound; a ring of
faceless onlookers turned away from him, dissolved into shadow; the wounds catch the light, the crowd
does not; vertical 9:16, vast negative space above. *(+ shared style tail)*
**Named elements (tour order):** (1) the Servant's downcast face; (2) the striped wounds across his
shoulder; (3) the bound hands; (4) one averted face turning away in shadow.
**Lands on Christ:** the wounded, despised, silent Servant is the portrait of the crucified Christ (Isa 53:3–5).

## 3 — [Strange thing · the LORD lays the iniquity / sheep astray] [18–28s] [NEW · multi-vignette]
**subject_block:** A multi-story canvas, 3–4 soft-edged vignettes bleeding into one twilight hillside:
upper — a great shaft of light, a single descending hand of the LORD pressing a dark weight downward;
center — the Servant kneeling beneath the load, shoulders bowed under it; lower and edges — a small
flock of sheep scattering, each wandering a different way down the slope into shadow, one pale lamb
turning its face up toward the viewer; soft edges throughout, no panels or frames, one continuous
scene; vertical 9:16. *(+ shared style tail)*
**Named elements (tour order):** (1) the descending hand of the LORD; (2) the Servant's bowed
shoulders bearing the weight; (3) the one upturned lamb's face; (4) the scattering flock dissolving
into shadow.
**Lands on Christ:** "the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all" — the great substitution the cross fulfills (Isa 53:6).

## 4 — [Strange thing · the scroll cannot lie quiet] [28–37s] [NEW]
**subject_block:** The same aged prophet sitting back from the now-finished scroll, the lamp burned
low, one hand resting on the curling parchment, his troubled gaze fixed past the viewer into the
dark, as if the words still move in him; the scroll's edge lifts faintly as though breathing; heavy
shadow, single warm light; vertical 9:16, negative space around him. *(+ shared style tail)*
**Named elements (tour order):** (1) the prophet's troubled, fixed eyes; (2) his hand resting on the
curling scroll; (3) the low-burning lamp; (4) the lifting edge of the parchment.
**Lands on Christ:** he wrote it word-for-word and it would not rest — a prophecy waiting centuries for its Name.

## 5 — [Turn · the scroll on the desert road] [37–47s] [NEW]
**subject_block:** A bright desert road under open sky — sharp daylight, not night — a richly robed
Ethiopian official seated in a halted chariot, the open scroll raised in both hands, lips parted
reading aloud; the road runs empty into pale distance; dust and heat-haze; the man alone with the
words; vertical 9:16, wide negative sky. *(+ shared style tail)*
**Named elements (tour order):** (1) the Ethiopian's questioning face; (2) the open scroll held in his
hands; (3) his lips parted mid-reading; (4) the empty road vanishing into the desert haze.
**Lands on Christ:** Isaiah's own words travel down the centuries to a stranger asking "of whom speaketh the prophet this?"

## 6 — [Turn · Philip preaches Jesus] [47–57s] [NEW]
**subject_block:** Two men seated close together at the roadside beside the halted chariot, the
traveling evangelist Philip leaning in earnestly toward the seated Ethiopian, one hand open in
explanation toward the scroll spread between them; warm afternoon light; the desert soft behind them;
intimate two-figure composition; vertical 9:16. *(+ shared style tail)*
**Named elements (tour order):** (1) Philip's earnest face; (2) Philip's open explaining hand;
(3) the scroll spread between them; (4) the Ethiopian's listening eyes.
**Lands on Christ:** "began at the same scripture, and preached unto him Jesus" — the prophecy gets its Name (Acts 8:35).

## 7 — [Punch · the wounds were His] [57–64s] [REUSE: EW01 gallery_clips/christ_turn.png — see note]
**subject_block (reused):** Baroque crucifixion — the crucified Christ on the cross, body striped and
wounded, a shaft of stormlit gold breaking the dark sky behind him; deep chiaroscuro; vertical 9:16.
**Named elements (tour order):** (1) Christ's bowed face; (2) the wounded, striped side/chest;
(3) the pierced hand on the crossbeam; (4) the broken light behind the cross.
**Lands on Christ:** the Servant's stripes are Christ's stripes — "by His stripes you are healed" made visible.

> torn temple veil** (EW01 Day-of-Atonement / scapegoat imagery). That is the GOAT motif, off-thread

## 8 — [Punch · His name is Jesus / risen] [64–70s] [REUSE: EW01 visual_9x16_test/christ.png]
**subject_block (reused):** The risen Christ standing in a torn-veil doorway, warm light blazing
behind him, one hand extended toward the viewer in invitation; deep Baroque shadow framing the
opening; vertical 9:16.
**Named elements (tour order):** (1) Christ's living face; (2) the extended, wounded hand;
(3) the torn veil parted around him; (4) the blaze of light behind him.
**Lands on Christ:** the final landing — "His name is Jesus." The risen Christ, wounds still in His
hand, extends it to the viewer: the report is true and the Servant has a Name.
