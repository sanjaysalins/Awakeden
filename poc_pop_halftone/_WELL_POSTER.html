How we got here

4 writers pitched posters, each alone. 3 of 4 named the movie "The Jar Stayed". We painted 5 ideas on 2 models (10 pictures).

4 blind judges (Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Haiku) all ranked the jar poster #1. All 4 said: "make her bigger". Fable said: "give her a teal robe". Done.
