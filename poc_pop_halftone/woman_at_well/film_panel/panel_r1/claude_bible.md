## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** Grace, 40. She goes to church, and she carries a past she doesn't talk about. She's scrolling late at night.

- **Who stops:** Line 1 ("She left without her jar. Why?") with a jar alone on screen is a clean hook.
- **Who finishes:** Believers and people who feel judged. The answer to the gap lands at 15 ("knew her whole story and offered her life").
- **Who shares:** A small-group leader would share it.
- **Who is NOT served:** A skeptic or newcomer who wants to know why a Jew talking to a Samaritan woman was shocking. Line 5 gives only five words for that.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus knew everything she had done and still offered her life that never runs dry, so she left her jar to tell the town."

Line 15 carries it. It is clear, but it is weakened because line 9 cuts "he will tell us all things" (4:25). That is the setup that makes line 13 ("told me all things") hit.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
**Who / what / why:** A Samaritan woman meets a tired Jewish man at a well. He knows her life and says he is the promised Christ. She runs to tell her town, and they believe.

**Where a viewer gets lost:**
- **"Samaritan"** is never explained. Line 5 only says the two groups "kept apart".
- **Picture 11** (she looks at the mountain) has no spoken line. It points at the worship argument in 4:20, which the film never mentions. A newcomer gets nothing from it.
- **Line 16** jumps to the cross and the tomb with no bridge.

**Words that fail in translation:** "thou / askest" (line 4), "Give me to drink" (line 3, odd English to a learner), "the Christ" (helped a little by line 9's gloss).

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **L1 RIGHT.** "came for water… left without her jar." 4:7, 4:28 ("left her waterpot").
- **L2 RIGHT.** "About noon": the usual reading of "sixth hour" (4:6). "Samaritan woman" (4:7). "Jesus, a Jew" (4:9). "sits there, tired": "wearied… sat thus on the well" (4:6).
- **L3 RIGHT.** Word for word, 4:7.
- **L4 RIGHT.** Word for word as far as it goes. It cuts "which am a woman of Samaria?" (4:9), which is acceptable.
- **L5 RIGHT / small sequence slip.** "kept apart" matches "no dealings" (4:9). But "He asks anyway" comes after her objection, which suggests He asks a second time. He doesn't.
- **L6 RIGHT.** Word for word, 4:13.
- **L7 OVERCLAIMED on order, otherwise fine.**
  - "Then He offers" is backwards. The offer of "living water" comes first (4:10), before 4:13.
  - "gift from God" fits 4:10 closely enough.
  - "shall never thirst" is word for word (4:14).
  - "a spring that never runs dry" is a fair gloss of "a well of water springing up". But it drops "everlasting life" and the key phrase "living water".
- **L8 RIGHT.** 4:18.
- **L9 UNSUPPORTED in part.** "the one God promised" is not in 4:25. The text says "Messias cometh… he will tell us all things". It is a defensible gloss, but it is not quoted, and the text that matters is dropped.
- **L10 RIGHT.** Word for word, 4:26.
- **L11 RIGHT** as narration. Matches 4:26 and 4:42.
- **L12 RIGHT.** 4:28 ("waterpot", "city").
- **L13 RIGHT.** Word for word, 4:29.
- **L14 RIGHT but squashed.** It merges 4:39–42: some believed because of her, Jesus stayed two days, more believed, and they spoke "unto the woman". "the Saviour of the world" is word for word.
- **L15 OVERCLAIMED.** "found better water: Jesus". In John 4, Jesus is the one who *gives* the water (4:10, 4:14), not the water itself. "knew her whole story" is RIGHT (4:29, 4:39).
- **L16 RIGHT.** 1 Cor 15:3–4. It leaves out "buried" and "according to the scriptures", which is acceptable.

No wrong facts, and every KJV quote checks out word for word. **I would send it to my church group after fixing lines 7 and 15.**

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would plant "he will tell us all things" (4:25) so that "told me all things" (4:29) pays it off. It would keep the phrase "living water". It would also give one honest second to her line "I have no husband", because that is the moment Jesus knows her and still doesn't shame her. That is the heart of the scene.

**The ONE highest-value change:** restore "he will tell us all things" in line 9.

**Score: 7/10.**

## CURIOSITY GAP
- **Opens:** line 1.
- **Pays off:** line 12 (she leaves the jar), fully at line 15.
- **Score: 7/10.** It is worth the wait. Line 16 is a tacked-on landing that dilutes the payoff a little.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **Line 9:** She says the Christ will come, and "he will tell us all things."
2. **Line 7:** But the water He gives is different, "living water", a gift from God. Drink it, and you "shall never thirst": a spring inside you, "springing up into everlasting life."
3. **Line 15:** The jar stayed. She came for water, and found the One who gives living water: Jesus, who knew her whole story and still offered her life.
