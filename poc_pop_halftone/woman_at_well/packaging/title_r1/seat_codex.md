1. **TITLE:** Why Did She Leave Her Jar?  
   **THUMB WORDS:** SHE MET | JESUS  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Huge clay jar beside the well; behind it, she runs toward town while Jesus sits.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Why would meeting Jesus make her leave her jar? She discovers who He is and goes to tell the town.

2. **TITLE:** Jesus Knew Her Whole Story  
   **THUMB WORDS:** SHE LEFT | THE JAR  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Her face laughing through tears above a large foreground jar, with bright yellow rays.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** What happened after He knew everything? He stayed, offered her life, and she went to share the news.

3. **TITLE:** She Came for Water  
   **THUMB WORDS:** JESUS OFFERED | MORE  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** She holds her big jar between herself and Jesus, looking surprised; teal and orange rays behind them.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** What could He offer beyond water? Jesus offers life from God—a spring that never runs dry.

4. **TITLE:** She Ran Back to Town  
   **THUMB WORDS:** WHAT DID | JESUS SAY?  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** She runs toward the village with open arms; her abandoned jar fills the lower foreground.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** What made her rush to tell everyone? Jesus knew her life and revealed He was the one God promised.

5. **TITLE:** Jesus Offered Better Water  
   **THUMB WORDS:** WHY LEAVE | THE JAR?  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** The jar stands alone beside the well; Jesus and the departing woman appear behind it against red sunburst rays.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Why leave a water jar after that offer? His gift is life from God, and she leaves to bring others to Him.

6. **TITLE:** Jesus Told Her Who He Was  
   **THUMB WORDS:** THE JAR | STAYED BEHIND  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus faces the astonished woman holding her jar, framed by bold yellow rays.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Who did He say He was, and why did that change her plans? He identifies Himself as the one God promised; she goes to tell the town.

**BEST:** “Why Did She Leave Her Jar?” / “SHE MET | JESUS”  
Short, clear, and tied to the opening: the picture shows her choice, while the film explains it.

**BIGGEST RISK:** A jar may feel too ordinary to stop a stranger; her joyful urgency must carry the picture.
