# Seat: Fable — title + thumbnail pairs (blind)

## 1. Six pairs

### Pair 1
- TITLE: `Why Did She Leave Her Jar? | Jesus at the Well` (46)
- THUMB WORDS: `HE KNEW | EVERYTHING`
- THUMB PICTURE: The clay jar big and alone in front by the well; the woman small behind it, running with arms open to the village; sunburst rays from the jar.
- Opens: what did He know, and why would that make her run and leave the jar? Paid off at "five husbands... He knew it all, and stayed" and "She leaves her jar."

### Pair 2
- TITLE: `Jesus Asked Her for Water. She Left Without Any.` (48)
- THUMB WORDS: `WHY IS | THE JAR STILL HERE?`
- THUMB PICTURE: The jar alone on the stone well, nobody near it, hot yellow and orange rays behind.
- Opens: who left it and what was more urgent than water? Paid off at "Give me to drink" and "The jar stayed. She came for water, and found the One who gives better water."

### Pair 3
- TITLE: `He Knew Her Whole Story. He Stayed. | Jesus at the Well` (55)
- THUMB WORDS: `SHE LEFT | HER JAR`
- THUMB PICTURE: Close-up of the woman laughing with tears, the jar small and alone at the well in the corner.
- Opens: what story, why is she laughing and crying, and why leave the jar? Paid off at "He knew it all, and stayed" and her run back to town.

### Pair 4
- TITLE: `The Woman at the Well Left Her Water Behind` (43)
- THUMB WORDS: `WHAT DID | HE TELL HER?`
- THUMB PICTURE: Jesus and the woman at the well, her face surprised, both hands on the big clay jar.
- Opens: what words could make her drop the thing she came for? Paid off at "I that speak unto thee am he" and "Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did."

### Pair 5
- TITLE: `One Talk With Jesus. One Empty Jar.` (35)
- THUMB WORDS: `SHE RAN | TO TELL EVERYONE`
- THUMB PICTURE: The woman running with arms open toward the village, the jar left behind her at the well.
- Opens: tell them what, and why no water? Paid off at "Come, see a man..." and "Many come, hear Him, and call Him the Saviour of the world."

### Pair 6
- TITLE: `She Came for Water. She Found Something Better.` (47)
- THUMB WORDS: `NEVER THIRSTY | AGAIN?`
- THUMB PICTURE: Jesus tired, sitting on the well, the empty jar beside Him, teal and yellow rays.
- Opens: what is better than water, and can anyone never thirst again? Paid off at "shall never thirst... a spring that never runs dry" and the closing line.

## 2. Best pair

**Pair 1** — `Why Did She Leave Her Jar? | Jesus at the Well` + `HE KNEW | EVERYTHING` + the lonely jar with her running away.

- It is the film's own first question, so the first 3 seconds match the title and the gap is paid off honestly.
- Title asks, thumb hints at the answer without giving it: two different halves, simple words, "Jesus" and "well" for search.

## 3. Biggest risk

"HE KNEW EVERYTHING" can read as a dark secret about her, so the picture must show joy (open arms, bright rays), not shame.
