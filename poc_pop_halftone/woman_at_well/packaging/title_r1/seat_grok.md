1. **TITLE:** Why She Left Her Water Jar  
   **THUMB WORDS:** JESUS | WAS THERE  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Big clay jar alone on the stone well; she runs small, arms open, toward the village; teal and orange sunburst.  
   Why leave the jar? The film answers: she found Jesus, who gives better water, and ran to tell the town. The jar stayed.

2. **TITLE:** Jesus Knew Her Whole Story  
   **THUMB WORDS:** AND HE | STAYED  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Close-up of her laughing with tears; Jesus sits calm on the well, still with her; warm sunburst.  
   What did he know, and what did he do? The film answers: he told the truth of her life, stayed, offered her life, and said he is the one God promised.

3. **TITLE:** Jesus Had a Gift at the Well  
   **THUMB WORDS:** NOT FROM | THE WELL  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** She holds the big jar, surprised; Jesus sits tired on the well; bright sunburst behind them.  
   What gift is not well water? The film answers: life from God inside you, a spring that never runs dry.

4. **TITLE:** Jesus Asked Her for a Drink  
   **THUMB WORDS:** SHE STAYED | TO LISTEN  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus tired on the stone well; she stands with the jar, surprised he spoke; hard sunburst.  
   It was only a drink — why stay? The film answers: he speaks of water that ends thirst, knows her life, and says he is the one God promised.

5. **TITLE:** Jesus Said, Never Thirst Again  
   **THUMB WORDS:** HOW CAN | THAT BE?  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus on the well, rays like a spring of light; she looks at him, jar in her arms.  
   How can thirst end? The film answers: Jesus gives life from God that never runs dry. To give that life, he died and rose.

6. **TITLE:** Come See Jesus at the Well  
   **THUMB WORDS:** HE KNEW | HER LIFE  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** She runs, arms open, toward the village; the jar sits alone back at the well; sunburst.  
   Why come see him? The film answers: he told her all she ever did, and he is the one God promised. Many hear him and call him the Saviour of the world.

**BEST:** Pair 1.  
The empty jar is a picture anyone can read, with no Bible knowledge. The film pays it off in full: she found Jesus, left the jar, and told the town.

**RISK:** The title can sound like she just forgot a jar, so the thumb and the running picture must show this is about Jesus.
