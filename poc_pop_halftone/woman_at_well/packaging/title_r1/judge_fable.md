## YOUR PICK

**P3**: TITLE "Why Did She Leave Her Jar? | Jesus at the Well" + THUMB "HE KNEW IT ALL / AND STAYED".

Why:
- Two gaps, not one. Title: why leave the jar? Thumbnail: who knew what, and why is staying a surprise?
- The film pays both off in plain words ("He knew it all, and stayed"; "The jar stayed").
- "And stayed" turns her past from a shameful secret into the good news. P1 does not have that guard.
- A Bible reader gets a fresh angle: not "the woman with five husbands" but "the man who did not walk away".
- Every word is short and common.

## Word fix (max 1)

None. Keep the words as they are. Keep the break exactly as given: "HE KNEW IT ALL" on line 1, "AND STAYED" on line 2, with line 2 the bigger line.

## Biggest risk

Six words is a lot for a phone-size thumbnail. If the text is not readable at small size over the sunburst, the hook is lost. Check it at real phone size; if it fails, fall back to P1 (2 words), accepting a slightly harsher read.

Note: these are simulated viewers, not real ones. My rough odds that P3 beats P1 with real viewers: 60%, give or take 15. A small real seed test would settle it.
