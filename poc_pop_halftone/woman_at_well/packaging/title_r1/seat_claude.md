# Blind writer: title + thumbnail pairs (woman at the well, pop-art short)

## 1. Six pairs

**Pair A**
- TITLE: Why She Left Her Jar | Woman at the Well
- THUMB WORDS: HE KNEW | EVERYTHING
- THUMB PICTURE: The clay jar alone by the well in the front, big and sharp. Behind it she runs, arms open, toward the village, with sunburst rays.
- Question / payoff: Why would she drop the thing she came for, and who knew what? The film pays it off with "told me all things that ever I did… is not this the Christ?" and the jar that stayed.

**Pair B**
- TITLE: He Knew Her Whole Story and Stayed
- THUMB WORDS: SHE RAN | WITHOUT HER JAR
- THUMB PICTURE: The woman's close-up, laughing through tears, with the jar's edge in the corner of the frame.
- Question / payoff: What did he know, and why did she run? The film pays it off with the five husbands, "He knew it all, and stayed," and her run to town.

**Pair C**
- TITLE: A Stranger Asked Her for Water | Jesus
- THUMB WORDS: THEN HE | KNEW HER LIFE
- THUMB PICTURE: Jesus talking to the woman. She holds the big jar and looks surprised.
- Question / payoff: Why would a stranger ask her, and how could he know her life? The film pays it off with the Jew/Samaritan gap, then "I that speak unto thee am he."

**Pair D**
- TITLE: Water That Never Runs Dry? | Jesus
- THUMB WORDS: BETTER | THAN WATER
- THUMB PICTURE: Jesus tired on the stone well, one hand open to the viewer, with teal rays behind.
- Question / payoff: What water never runs out? The film pays it off with "shall never thirst" and life from God, a spring inside.

**Pair E**
- TITLE: The Jar She Left Behind | Woman at the Well
- THUMB WORDS: WHAT DID | SHE FIND?
- THUMB PICTURE: A low angle on the empty jar by the well. Her small figure runs into the sunburst.
- Question / payoff: What was worth more than her water? The film pays it off with "found the One who gives better water: Jesus."

**Pair F**
- TITLE: Woman at the Well: Who Was This Man?
- THUMB WORDS: SHE CAME | FOR WATER
- THUMB PICTURE: A split frame. On one side she holds the full jar. On the other the jar sits alone and she is gone.
- Question / payoff: She came for water, so what did she leave with? The film pays it off with "Come, see a man… is not this the Christ?" and "Saviour of the world."

## 2. Best pair

**Pair A:** "Why She Left Her Jar | Woman at the Well" + **HE KNEW | EVERYTHING** over the lone jar and her running.
- The title is the film's own hook. The thumbnail adds a second, different gap ("who knew what?"). Both are paid off in the film.
- The picture shows the mystery (the empty jar) and joy (her open arms). That is honest and has no shame in it.

## 3. Biggest risk

"HE KNEW EVERYTHING" could read as a threat or exposure. The joyful running picture has to carry the warmth, or the thumbnail tips toward shaming her.
