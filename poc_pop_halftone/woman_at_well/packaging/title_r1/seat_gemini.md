### 2. Best pair

**TITLE:** The woman at the well
**THUMB WORDS:** SHE LEFT | HER JAR
**THUMB PICTURE:** Close-up of the abandoned clay jar by the well, the woman running away in the background.
**WHY:** 
It is highly searchable for newcomers and directly visualizes the film's opening hook. 
The image of the forgotten tool creates an immediate, relatable mystery that demands an answer.

### 3. Biggest risk

The thumbnail text reveals the "left without her jar" mystery before the narrator speaks it, potentially softening the punch of the opening line.
