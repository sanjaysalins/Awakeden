## #1 and video title
Poster: Q2 (THE JAR STAYED).
Title: **T1 "Why Did She Leave Her Jar? | Jesus at the Well"**
Why: it asks the question the poster's "STAYED" opens, and the film answers it (she ran to tell the town about Jesus). It adds the answer's subject, so it does not just repeat the poster words.

## One small fix (max 1)
Make the running woman in Q2 about 1.5x larger, moved up so she sits clear of the title and caption. At 2 cm she is the proof the jar was left for a reason.

## Biggest risk
A stranger reads "THE JAR STAYED" as a riddle about a pot and never connects it to Jesus, so the curiosity opens and nothing on the poster points to who the film is about.
