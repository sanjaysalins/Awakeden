# Blind judge: movie-poster thumbnail for a 91-second Bible short

You judge ALONE. Look at the images yourself (Read tool): Q1.jpg ... Q5.jpg and PHONE_SIZE.jpg (how they look at real phone size), all in this folder. Do not open any other file.

## The film
Bold pop-art comic look (black ink, halftone dots, bright sunburst rays). Opens: "She came for water. She left without her jar. Why?" At noon a woman comes to a well; Jesus, a Jew, sits there tired and asks her for a drink; Jews and her people kept apart. He offers a different water, a gift from God: "shall never thirst", a spring that never runs dry. He tells her the truth of her life (five husbands): "He knew it all, and stayed." He says He is the Christ. She leaves her jar, runs to town: "Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?" Many call Him "the Saviour of the world." End: "The jar stayed. She came for water, and found the One who gives better water: Jesus ... To give that life, He died for our sins and rose the third day."

## Who sees it
A global stranger scrolling fast, often English as a second language, no Bible knowledge, at ~2 cm wide. Also Bible readers.

## Score each poster 0-10
1. STOP: would a stranger stop scrolling? 2. GAP: does it open a question the film really answers? 3. PHONE: clear at phone size? 4. HONEST + KIND: true to the film, no bait, no shame on the woman? 5. CRAFT: does it look like a real film poster? Also name any picture defect you see (extra objects, broken anatomy, odd marks).
