## My #1 + video title
**Q2** with **T1 "Why Did She Leave Her Jar? | Jesus at the Well"**

Why: the poster states the odd fact ("The jar stayed. She came for water."). The title asks the question. Together they open one gap, and the film answers it. T2 just repeats the poster strap. T3 is a good line but "it" is unclear to a stranger. (Runner-up: T3.)

## One small fix
On Q2, make the running woman about 1.5x bigger and give her robe one flat colour (teal), so she is seen at phone size.

## Biggest risk
A jar is not a face. A fast-scrolling stranger may not stop for a pot, and "The jar stayed" means little with no context. Q1 would stop more people but opens a weaker question. Worth a real A/B if the platform allows; simulated judging cannot settle this (my confidence in Q2 over Q3/Q1: about 50%, plus or minus 15).
