## TITLES

1. ★ **The Jar Stayed** | Whose jar? Why did they leave it? | Object plus mystery, like a quiet festival drama.
2. ★ **She Left the Jar** | What did she find that mattered more than water? | A whole story in four words.
3. **Never Thirst Again** | Is that even possible? | A big-promise adventure title.
4. **Noon at the Well** | Who meets at a well in the worst heat? | Place and time, like a western.
5. **He Knew and Stayed** | Knew what? Stayed with whom? | Warm drama, not a scandal.
6. **Come, See** | See what? | Her own line from the film.

### THE JAR STAYED  (title: The Jar Stayed)
- THE IDEA: One huge clay jar alone in front, and far behind it a tiny woman running away with arms wide open; the hero object is the thing left behind.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Top: red-orange-yellow sunburst rays bursting from behind the jar. Middle: tiny mud-brick houses and one tiny ink figure running, arms open. Bottom two thirds: the giant two-handled jar, black ink on white, by the well corner. One teal splash at its foot, the only cool colour.
- LETTERING: Title in thick black comic capitals across the top quarter, full width, over the rays. TAGLINE: "She came for water."
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: Why run off happy and leave your jar? The film answers it at "I that speak unto thee am he," then she leaves the jar.
- PHONE TEST: One big jar shape, one tiny running figure, one burst of rays.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 pop-art comic poster. A giant two-handled clay water jar with a rope, bold black ink with halftone dots and cross-hatching, black-and-white, alone beside the corner of a stone well, filling the lower two thirds of the frame. Far behind it, very small, a woman in a light head-scarf and plain dress with rope belt runs away from us with arms wide open toward a small mud-brick village. Behind everything, huge sunburst rays in red, orange and yellow with halftone dots, bursting from one point behind the jar. One small splash of teal water at the foot of the jar. Clear space at the top. Use film pictures 1 and 6 as references for the jar, well, woman and village. No lettering, no text.

### THE SPRING INSIDE  (title: Never Thirst Again)
- THE IDEA: The woman as a bold black side-view shape, and inside her shape a teal spring of living water rises and bursts into the light (silhouette plus the water itself).
- WHAT YOU SEE: One large side-view head and shoulders, head-scarf clear, chin lifted, solid black. Inside it a teal-and-white spring climbs and breaks out the top as drops. Rays go yellow-orange at the bottom to teal at the top: the water takes the page. No second figure.
- LETTERING: Title in white capitals with black outline in the bottom quarter, full width. TAGLINE: none.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: What is this water inside her? The film answers when Jesus offers water where you "shall never thirst."
- PHONE TEST: A black head shape with a bright teal fountain inside it.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 pop-art comic poster. One large side-view silhouette of a woman's head and shoulders in solid black ink, wearing a light head-scarf, chin lifted, joyful. Inside the black silhouette, a spring of teal and white water rises from her chest and bursts out above her head as bright drops, with comic waves and halftone dots. Background of huge sunburst rays with halftone dots, yellow and orange at the bottom turning teal at the top. Nobody else. Clear space in the bottom quarter. Use film picture 5 as reference for the woman's head-scarf and face shape. No lettering, no text.

### NOON AT THE WELL  (title: She Left the Jar)
- THE IDEA: Seen from straight above, the well is a black circle in the middle of the poster, like a target, with two small people on opposite sides and the jar set down between them (top-down split).
- WHAT YOU SEE: Centre: the well mouth as a solid black disc with one teal glint. Yellow, orange and red rays shoot out from it to every edge. Top rim: Jesus seated, small. Far side: the woman, small, walking away. By the well on her side: the jar, set down. Wide gap between them.
- LETTERING: Title in black comic capitals across the bottom fifth. TAGLINE: "Why?"
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: Two strangers, one well; she walks off without her jar. What was said? The film is that talk.
- PHONE TEST: A black circle with rays, and a small jar.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 pop-art comic poster, bird's-eye view straight down. In the centre a round stone well drawn in black ink with cross-hatching, its mouth a solid black circle with one small teal glint of water inside. Huge sunburst rays in yellow, orange and red with halftone dots shoot outward from the well to every edge. At the top rim of the well, small, a man with long dark hair and beard in a plain off-white robe and tan sash sits resting. At the opposite side, far from him, small, a woman in a light head-scarf and plain dress with rope belt walks away. Next to the well on her side stands a two-handled clay jar with a coiled rope, set down. Figures in black-and-white ink. Clear space at the bottom. Use film pictures 1, 2 and 3 as references for the well, the jar and both characters. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1
**THE JAR STAYED.** One object, one tiny runner, one question a child can ask in any language: why did she leave it?
It is the film's own first and last line, it reads at 2 cm, and it keeps the woman joyful, not judged.
