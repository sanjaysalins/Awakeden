## TITLES

- ★ **The Jar Stayed** | Why did its owner leave it? | An odd, concrete detail becomes a story you need explained.
- ★ **Better Water** | What could be better than water? | Two plain words hold the film’s central mystery.
- **Come and See** | What has she found? | A spoken invitation with the pull of an adventure.
- **At Noon** | What happened that day? | A spare, cinematic title rooted in one life-changing meeting.
- **Never Thirst** | How could that be possible? | A bold promise that asks the film to explain it.
- **The Gift** | What does he give her? | Simple, human, and large enough to gain meaning as you watch.

### LEFT BEHIND  (title: The Jar Stayed)

- **THE IDEA:** Give the abandoned jar the scale and billing of a movie star; its owner is already leaving the picture.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** Across the top, hot yellow and orange rays converge toward a distant mud-brick village. A small but clear woman runs toward it, arms open. Below her, the stone well forms one low arc. The jar dominates the lower two-thirds: enormous, intact, black-and-white, with two handles and slack rope. Heavy ink, coarse halftone, deep cross-hatched shadows. One teal ray cuts through the warm field toward town.
- **LETTERING:** Add real type across the upper quarter: **THE JAR / STAYED**, heavy black condensed capitals. Keep the village and runner just below it. TAGLINE: **Why leave it?**
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Why would someone fetch water and abandon the thing she needs to carry it? The film answers when she learns who Jesus is and runs to tell the town.
- **PHONE TEST:** One huge jar, one strong title. The question survives even when the runner becomes a speck.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 film key art, bold pop-art comic, black ink line drawing, coarse halftone dots, cross-hatching, huge yellow orange red and teal sunburst rays. Use film picture 6 for composition, jar and woman; picture 1 for jar and well details. Monumental intact two-handled clay jar with slack rope fills lower two-thirds, mostly black-and-white ink. Low stone-well arc behind it. Small woman with matching dark hair, light head-scarf, plain dress and rope belt runs arms open toward a distant mud-brick village above. Clear upper quarter reserved for title placement. Strong simple shapes, no lettering, no text

### THE SPRING INSIDE  (title: Better Water)

- **THE IDEA:** Water fills the shape of a person instead of a jar.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The top quarter is bright yellow with widely spaced red sunburst rays. Beneath it stands one large, upright woman. Her joyful face and light scarf remain black-and-white ink; the shape of her dress becomes a flat graphic window filled with teal and blue currents. One white spring curls upward through that blue field. Her rope belt and dress outline remain visible as black marks. A small clay jar sits beside her feet. The water looks like ink taking over the printed page.
- **LETTERING:** Add **BETTER / WATER** in two huge lines across the upper quarter, heavy black capitals. TAGLINE: none.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** What kind of water belongs inside a person? Jesus answers with his offer of life from God—a spring that never runs dry.
- **PHONE TEST:** A woman with a vivid blue interior; two large words. The symbolic change reads before the details do.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 film key art, bold pop-art comic, black ink, halftone dots, cross-hatching, huge yellow orange and red sunburst rays. Use film picture 5 for the woman’s face and picture 6 for her matching scarf, dress, rope belt and jar. One large upright woman beneath a clear upper quarter. Joyful black-and-white ink face, dark hair, light head-scarf. Treat her dress silhouette as a flat printed collage window filled with bold teal and blue ink currents and one white upward-curling spring. Preserve black dress contours and rope belt. Small black-and-white clay jar beside her feet, no lettering, no text

### THE FACE AFTER  (title: Come and See)

- **THE IDEA:** Sell the whole film through one face showing laughter and tears at once.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** An enormous close-up fills the frame beneath a clear strip of yellow sky. She looks directly at us, caught in an open, unmistakably joyful laugh. Her scarf forms a simple white border around her face. Everything is black-and-white ink except one oversized teal tear on her cheek. Orange and red rays burst outward behind her head; dense halftone gives the skin warmth and weight. No other figure, setting or inset.
- **LETTERING:** Add **COME / AND SEE** across the upper quarter in heavy black capitals. Keep every letter clear of her eyes. TAGLINE: none.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** What happened to make her this happy? The film earns that face through Jesus knowing her life, staying, and revealing who he is; her invitation then carries that joy into town.
- **PHONE TEST:** The smile, the blue tear and the invitation remain. Her expression does the selling.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 film key art, bold pop-art comic, thick black ink contours, expressive cross-hatching, coarse halftone dots, huge yellow orange and red sunburst rays. Use film picture 5 as the primary reference, preserving the woman’s likeness and laughing-with-tears expression; use picture 3 for matching dark hair and light head-scarf. Monumental joyful close-up, eyes meeting the viewer, open natural laugh, mostly black-and-white ink. One large vivid teal tear on her cheek. White scarf frames the face. Clear yellow upper quarter for later typography. Single face, strong readable shapes, no lettering, no text

### MY #1 and why

**LEFT BEHIND — The Jar Stayed.** The jar is specific to this film, and its abandonment creates an immediate, honest question.  
At scrolling size, one object and three words give a stranger a reason to watch.
