### THE LEFT JAR  (title: *The Jar Stayed*)
- **THE IDEA:** One huge empty jar is the whole poster. The sunburst rays shoot out from behind it, so the jar looks like the sun has just set behind it.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The top 30% is hot yellow-orange-red rays with big halftone dots. In the middle sits the two-handled clay jar, giant, black ink with hatching, its rope lying loose on the stone. The jar is tipped a little and its dark mouth faces us. At the bottom is the stone well lip in black and white ink. Far back, at the bottom edge, tiny footprints head out of frame. No people.
- **LETTERING:** Title in heavy condensed comic caps across the bottom 20%, ivory on a black band. Tagline: *"She came for water."*
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Where is she, and why did she leave it? The film answers this in the last beats, when she leaves her jar and runs to town.
- **PHONE TEST:** A dark jar shape against bright rays. You can read it as "a jar, left behind" at a glance.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** "Vertical 9:16 pop-art comic poster, Roy Lichtenstein meets Bible storybook. One giant two-handled clay jar with loose rope, slightly tipped, sitting alone on the lip of an old stone well, drawn in bold black ink line with cross-hatching and halftone dots, left black-and-white. Behind it, huge radiating sunburst rays in yellow, orange and red with big halftone dots, as if the sun sits behind the jar. Faint footprints leading out of frame at the bottom. No people. Empty, still, noon light, strong graphic shapes. Match jar and well from refs 1 and 6. No lettering, no text."

### THE WELL EYE  (title: *Never Thirst*)
- **THE IDEA:** We look straight down into the well. Its round black mouth is the poster's centre, and teal living water spirals up out of it and floods over the dry yellow stone like a rising sun.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** A top-down view. The well's stone ring fills the frame, inked and hatched in black and white. Inside is a black circle. From the black, a teal-blue spiral of halftone water climbs up and spills over the rim, turning into teal rays that push the hot orange rays back to the corners. At the top edge, tiny and seen from above, the empty jar sits by the rim. The palette goes from hot orange (thirst) to cool teal (life).
- **LETTERING:** Title in a big curved band that follows the top of the well ring, white with a teal shadow. No tagline, so the shape speaks for itself.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** What water comes up out of a dark well and never runs dry? The film answers it in the middle, when Jesus offers "a spring that never runs dry."
- **PHONE TEST:** A dark circle with a teal swirl, set against orange. It reads like an eye or a target. Very bold.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** "Vertical 9:16 pop-art comic poster, bold black ink line, cross-hatching, halftone dots. Top-down view straight into an old round stone well, the stone ring filling the frame in black-and-white ink. Deep black circle at the centre. A spiral of teal and blue halftone water rising out of the black, spilling over the stone rim and becoming teal sunburst rays that push hot orange and yellow halftone rays out to the corners. Tiny two-handled clay jar on the rim at the top edge. No people. Graphic, symmetrical, striking. Match well and jar from refs 1 and 4. No lettering, no text."

### TWO SHADOWS AT NOON  (title: *He Knew It All*)
- **THE IDEA:** A split by shadow. Jesus sits tired on the well, and his noon shadow on the ground is a pool of teal water instead of darkness.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The top half is yellow-red rays with a white-hot sun at the very top. Below it, Jesus sits on the stone well, side view, at a calm distance: long dark hair, beard, off-white robe, tan sash, head a little bowed, tired, alone. Under him, his short shadow spreads across the stone as teal halftone water, rippling. At the far left edge, half off the frame, the tip of her jar and the rope come in, so she is coming but not seen.
- **LETTERING:** Title in tall black caps down the right edge, stacked. Tagline: *"And He stayed."*
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Knew what? Who is coming? Why is his shadow water? The film pays it off when "He knew it all, and stayed."
- **PHONE TEST:** A lone seated figure, with a teal pool under him against hot colours. Clear and calm.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** "Vertical 9:16 pop-art comic poster, black ink line, halftone dots, cross-hatching. Jesus with long dark hair, beard, plain off-white robe and tan sash, sitting alone and tired on an old stone well at noon, side view, mid-distance, head slightly bowed. White-hot sun at the top, huge yellow, orange and red halftone sunburst rays behind him. His short noon shadow on the stone ground is a rippling pool of teal-blue halftone water. At the far left edge, a clay jar and rope just entering the frame, no person shown. Figure and well in black-and-white ink. Match Jesus from refs 2 and 3. No lettering, no text."

### MY #1 and why
**The Left Jar / *The Jar Stayed*.** It is one object and one question, and it reads in a split second at 2 cm. The film opens and closes on that jar, so the poster tells no lies and makes the stranger ask "why?"
