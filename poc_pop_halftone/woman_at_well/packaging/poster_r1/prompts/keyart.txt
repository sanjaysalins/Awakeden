EVIDENCE PACK:
# EVIDENCE PACK: one vertical short film (YouTube Shorts / Reels / TikTok), 91 seconds

LOOK: bold pop-art comic. Black ink line drawings with halftone dots and cross-hatching, figures and setting left mostly black-and-white ink, behind them huge sunburst rays in teal, yellow, orange and red with halftone dots (Roy Lichtenstein meets a Bible storybook). Motion graphics: living water (teal/blue ink) "takes the page" as the film turns.

SOUND: narrator; Jesus's voice; the woman's voice. Music: oud, hand claps, handpan - warm, rhythmic.

THE FILM, in order (KJV quotes in quote marks):
- "She came for water. She left without her jar. Why?"
- About noon, a Samaritan woman comes to a well. Jesus, a Jew, sits there, tired.
- Jesus: "Give me to drink." She: "How is it that thou, being a Jew, askest drink of me?" Jews and Samaritans kept apart. He asks anyway.
- Jesus: "Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again." He offers a different water, a gift from God: drink it and you "shall never thirst" - life from God inside you, a spring that never runs dry.
- He tells her the truth about her life: five husbands, and the man she has now is not her husband. "He knew it all, and stayed."
- She says the Christ, the one God promised, will come. Jesus: "I that speak unto thee am he."
- (pause) She leaves her jar and goes back to town: "Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?"
- Many come, hear Him, call Him "the Saviour of the world."
- End: "The jar stayed. She came for water, and found the One who gives better water: Jesus, who knew her whole story and offered her life. To give that life, He died for our sins and rose the third day."

FILM PICTURES THAT EXIST (can be used as references for new art; characters must match):

2. Jesus alone, tired, sitting on the stone well at noon.
3. Jesus talking to the woman; she holds a big two-handled clay jar with rope, surprised.

Characters: Jesus - long dark hair, beard, plain off-white robe, tan sash. The woman - dark hair, light head-scarf, plain dress with rope belt.

AUDIENCE: global; many viewers have English as a second language and know no Bible. Most see the poster at ~2 cm wide while scrolling.
HARD RULES: honest to the film (no fake shock, no claim the film does not make); never shame the woman or make her past the joke or a scandal (the film's turn: He knew it all and stayed); no romance reading between Jesus and her (no faces close together, no gazing pose); short common words; no "Samaritan" as a load-bearing word.
