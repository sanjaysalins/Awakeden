# SCORE PLAN - "Jesus and the woman at the well" (88 s)

## 1. SCORE IDEA
A dry plucked question that cannot finish itself until living water gives it its last note.

- **Instruments:** oud (dry, plucked = thirst), handpan (round, wet = water), frame drum + handclaps + round bass (the pop-comic pulse), soft warm strings and felt piano (the calm beats). No voices, no choir, no brass, no trailer hits.
- **Motif, "the Drip":** two short notes on the same pitch, then one step up, left hanging (drip, drip, rise...). It sounds like a question. From 30.7 s it gains a fourth note that lands home (drip, drip, rise, HOME). Dry = 3 notes on oud. Living water = 4 notes on handpan.
- **Key and tempo:** D, 100 BPM throughout. Minor-modal while dry, D major from the flood onward.

## 2. WHY IT FITS
- Claps, frame drum and bass give panel slams and dot wipes a beat to hit, like a pop record, not a film trailer.
- Oud and frame drum place us in 1st-century Samaria without "desert movie" cliche; handpan is modern and watery, so teal has its own sound.
- One motif carries the whole gospel turn: unfinished (thirst) to finished (never thirst). The music says what the words say.

## 4. SPLIT
**54.20 s** (part 1 = 54.2 s, part 2 = 33.7 s). It sits in the 1 s pause after "He is the Christ", where music is almost silent, so the join cannot be heard. Part 2 then starts fresh with the run to town. Same key and tempo both sides.

## 5. MUSIC PROMPTS
**Part 1 (generate 55 s):**
Instrumental only, no vocals. 100 BPM, key of D minor turning to D major. Modern pop groove with Middle Eastern colour: plucked oud, frame drum, handclaps, round bass, handpan, soft warm strings. Playful, bold, warm, not epic, not a trailer. Starts with solo oud playing a short three-note question motif (two same notes then one step up, unresolved), twice. Around 4 seconds in, a light clap and frame drum groove with bass enters. Around 11 seconds in, it thins to bass and drum. Around 14 seconds in, the groove returns, tighter. Around 23 seconds in, all drums stop; a lone dry oud plays the unresolved motif. Around 27 seconds in, a soft rising swell with handpan. Around 30 seconds in, a joyful full lift in D major: handpan plays the motif with a fourth resolving note, strings, claps, bass. Around 37 seconds in, it pulls back to soft bass pulse and handpan. Around 48 seconds in, everything drops to one quiet held string note until the end. Ends very soft.

**Part 2 (generate 36 s):**
Instrumental only, no vocals. 100 BPM, D major. Plucked oud, handpan, frame drum, handclaps, round bass, soft warm strings, felt piano. Joyful, running, bright, warm, not epic. Around 17 seconds in, drums stop; handpan alone. Around 19 seconds in, warm gentle strings and handpan, no drums. Around 27 seconds in, only felt piano and soft strings, very quiet and reverent. Around 30 seconds in, felt piano plays the four-note motif once, slowly; the last note rings out and fades to silence.

## 6. MIX NOTES
- Under speech: music about 18 dB below the voice. In word gaps: let it rise about 6 dB (gaps at 2.8, 7.1, 12.9, 29.8 s).
- Lifts: 30.6-37.4 s (loudest, about 12 dB under voice) and 66.8-71.0 s.
- Nearly out: 48.5-54.2 s, about 30 dB under voice. Also low from 81.5 s.
- Hold (84.74-87.87 s): the final felt-piano note rings alone, fades to silence by 87.6 s. Audio runs the full video length.

## 7. RISKS
1. **Dead air or ignored cues.** Sweep loudness in 1 s windows on each part; check the drop at 23 s, the lift at 30 s, the hush at 48 s land within 1 s. Regenerate, or slide the part, if not.
2. **Duck crushes the groove, or music masks the voice.** Measure the final mix, not the stems: voice-minus-music per window; listen once on phone speakers.
3. **Join or tone goes wrong** (key/tempo jump at 54.2 s, vocals appear, sounds like a trailer). Listen to 50-58 s on loop; check both parts are D and 100 BPM; reject any take with singing or big drums.
