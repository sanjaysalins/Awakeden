[narrator] She came to the well for water. She left without her jar. Why?
[narrator] Noon. A Samaritan woman comes to draw water. Jesus, a Jew, tired from the road, asks her for a drink.
[woman] How is it that thou, being a Jew, askest drink of me?
[narrator] Back then, Jews and Samaritans did not mix.
[jesus] Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again.
[narrator] He offers a different water: life from God, alive inside you. Drink it, and you "shall never thirst."
[narrator] Then He tells her the truth about her life. All of it. He knew everything, and still offered the gift.
[woman] I know that Messias cometh, which is called Christ.
[jesus] I that speak unto thee am he.
(pause)
[narrator] She leaves her jar and runs to town.
[woman] Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?
[narrator] The town comes. They call Him "the Saviour of the world."
[narrator] He died for our sins and rose the third day.
[narrator] She came for water. She found Him. The jar could stay.
