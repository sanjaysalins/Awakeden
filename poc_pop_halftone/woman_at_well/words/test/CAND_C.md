[narrator] At noon, a Samaritan woman comes to a well for water. Jesus, a Jew, sits there, tired.
[jesus] Give me to drink.
[narrator] Their two peoples did not mix. He asks anyway. Then He offers her water so she "shall never thirst": life with God, forever.
[narrator] He speaks plainly: she has had five husbands, and the man she has now is not her husband. He knows everything, and still offers the gift.
[narrator] She speaks of the Christ, God's promised Saviour.
[jesus] I that speak unto thee am he.
(pause)
[narrator] She leaves her jar and runs to town.
[woman] Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?
[narrator] The town comes, hears Him, and calls Him "the Saviour of the world."
[narrator] She came for water. She left without her jar. She had found Jesus, who died for our sins and rose the third day.
