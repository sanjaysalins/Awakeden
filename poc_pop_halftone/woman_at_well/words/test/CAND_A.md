[narrator] She came for water. She went home without her jar. Why?
[narrator] Noon. A Samaritan woman comes to a well. Jesus, a Jew, sits there, tired from walking.
[jesus] Give me to drink.
[woman] How is it that thou, being a Jew, askest drink of me?
[narrator] Jews and Samaritans did not mix. Still, He asks.
[jesus] Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again.
[narrator] But His water? You "shall never thirst." He means life from God, inside you, like a spring that never runs dry.
[narrator] Then He tells her the truth about her life. Five husbands. He knew everything, and still offered the gift.
[narrator] She speaks of the promised Christ.
[jesus] I that speak unto thee am he.
(pause)
[narrator] She leaves her jar and runs to town.
[woman] Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?
[narrator] The town comes and calls Him "the Saviour of the world."
[narrator] He died for our sins and rose the third day.
[narrator] The jar stayed at the well. She had found the One who knew everything, and gave anyway.
