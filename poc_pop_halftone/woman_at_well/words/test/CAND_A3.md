[narrator] She came for water. She left without her jar. Why?
[narrator] About noon, a Samaritan woman comes to a well. Jesus, a Jew, sits there, tired.
[jesus] Give me to drink.
[woman] How is it that thou, being a Jew, askest drink of me?
[narrator] Jews and Samaritans kept apart. He asks anyway.
[jesus] Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again.
[narrator] Then He offers her a different water, a gift from God. Drink it, and you "shall never thirst": life from God inside you, a spring that never runs dry.
[narrator] Then He tells her the truth about her life: five husbands, and the man she has now is not her husband.
[narrator] She says the Christ, the one God promised, will come.
[jesus] I that speak unto thee am he.
[narrator] He is the Christ.
(pause)
[narrator] She leaves her jar and goes back to town.
[woman] Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?
[narrator] Many come, hear Him, and call Him "the Saviour of the world."
[narrator] The jar stayed. She came for water, and found better water: Jesus, who knew her whole story and offered her life.
[narrator] Later, He died for our sins and rose the third day.
