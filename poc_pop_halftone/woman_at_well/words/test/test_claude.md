## 2. My pick: **A**

It has the best gap. It has the best newcomer gloss of the living water ("like a spring that never runs dry"). And its ending line explains the jar and Jesus' grace at once.

## 3. Line fixes for A

1. "She speaks of the promised Christ." → **"She says God's promised Saviour, the Christ, will come."** This explains "Christ" for the newcomer.
2. "Then He tells her the truth about her life. Five husbands. He knew everything, and still offered the gift." → **"Then He tells her the truth about her life: five husbands, and the man she has now is not her husband. He knew everything, and still He did not turn away."** This gives the full v18 and fixes the order problem.
3. "He died for our sins and rose the third day." → **"Later, this same Jesus died for our sins and rose the third day."** This links the creed to the story.

## 4. Biggest risk

The death-and-rising line is the one thing all three put on top of John 4. Without a bridge, a newcomer hears it as a sudden sermon cut into the story. That's where the global viewer drops off, just before the payoff line.
