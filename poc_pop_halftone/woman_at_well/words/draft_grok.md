(d)
Who it is for: Anyone, anywhere, who has tried to fill a thirst and is still thirsty, and anyone who feels known at their worst.
Main take-away: Jesus knew her life and still gave the gift. She left the jar because he is the Saviour of the world, who died for our sins and rose the third day.
The curiosity gap: She came to fill a jar. Why leave it by the well?
Why a newcomer gets it: Water runs out. He offers life that does not. He knows her and does not take the gift back. She drops the jar and runs to tell the town.
