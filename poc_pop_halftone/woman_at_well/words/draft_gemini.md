**(d) 4 Lines**
- **Who it is for:** People who feel defined by their past mistakes and keep chasing things that leave them empty.
- **Main take-away:** Jesus knows everything we have ever done, yet offers us the grace of salvation without shame.
- **The curiosity gap:** Why would a woman walk all the way to a well for water, only to leave her heavy jar behind?
- **Why a global newcomer gets it:** The cultural tension is stated plainly ("Jews and Samaritans did not mix"), and "living water" is immediately defined as a life that satisfies completely, making the metaphor universally clear.
