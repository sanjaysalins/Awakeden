NARRATORShe came for water. She left without her jar. Why?
NARRATORAbout noon, a Samaritan woman comes to a well. Jesus, a Jew, sits there, tired.
JESUSGive me to drink.
WOMANHow is it that thou, being a Jew, askest drink of me?
NARRATORJews and Samaritans kept apart. He asks anyway.
JESUSWhosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again.
NARRATORThen He offers her a different water, a gift from God. Drink it, and you "shall never thirst": life from God inside you, a spring that never runs dry.
NARRATORThen He tells her the truth about her life: five husbands, and the man she has now is not her husband.
NARRATORShe says the Christ, the one God promised, will come.
JESUSI that speak unto thee am he.
NARRATORHe is the Christ.
NARRATORShe leaves her jar and goes back to town.
WOMANCome, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?
NARRATORMany come, hear Him, and call Him "the Saviour of the world."
NARRATORThe jar stayed. She came for water, and found better water: Jesus, who knew her whole story and offered her life.
NARRATORLater, He died for our sins and rose the third day.
