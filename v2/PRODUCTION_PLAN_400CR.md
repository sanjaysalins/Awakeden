## 5. DIRECTOR'S ADDITIONS (the creative-freedom part — new, not in any DNA doc)

1. **The camera never crosses the threshold until you do.**
   The whole piece is shot from OUTSIDE the door, beside the Seeker — pages 1–4 live
   on the doorstep where the fear lives ("that fear keeps you at the door").
   At "So come," the page turn IS the door opening — page 5 is the first and only
   interior, and the one low-angle glory shot. The viewer's point of view physically
   makes the journey the CTA asks for. The grammar (Bowed Camera) becomes the
   sermon's own architecture — nobody scrolling can name it, but everyone feels
   which side of the door they're on.
2. **One True Sound = the latch.**
   The single foreground sound in the whole piece: a heavy iron latch opening,
   placed exactly on "never locked." The fear says locked; the sound says open.
   $0, from `sound_library`, named in the plan not improvised at mix.
3. **The record travels, then stays.**
   The scroll (the Seeker's shame, Col 2:14) is in frame on pages 1–4 and
   visibly LEFT BEHIND in p5b — carried, read, nailed, released. A silent prop
   arc a muted viewer can follow start to finish — the piece works with sound off,
   which the competitive scan says is where half of persona A lives.
