# Scene plan — Independent red-team audit

**Verdict:** `LOCKED`  ·  **Failed gates:** 0

## Visual reading
The arc opens on the crowd's scorn (the shaking heads), lifts to the psalmist who foresaw it, descends through the curled lip / wagging passers-by / sneering rulers into the enduring Christ, turns on 'He could have come down' (held power) and the scorn borne in love, and lands on the mocked King who stayed — the cross at dawn, an open invitation. Hero #7 (the cross) bookends so it lands on Christ.

## Writer's own red-team notes
Jaded Viewer risks: many jeering-face scenes (1,3,5,9) could blur into one angry crowd — varied framings (close/mid/low-angle/wide) and the turn to mercy (13) offset it. The 'script/psalm' beat is the writing trap — solved by a VISION (2), no scroll/text. Back half is crucifixion-heavy (6,7,10,11,12,14) but that is the passage; framings vary and the mocker scenes anchor the front half.
