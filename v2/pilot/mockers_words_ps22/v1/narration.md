# The Mockers' Words — Psalm 22:7-8 short (v2 consistency build, A/B vs v1 #02)

- **Built via:** the v2 skill discipline (`/study` → `/narrate` → `/review`), agent-mode ($0).
- **Spine (one thread):** the crowd mocking Jesus at the cross was reciting Psalm 22 — Matthew 27:43 is
  almost verbatim Psalm 22:8, and the head-wagging (Matt 27:39) is Psalm 22:7. They thought they were
  inventing the cruelty; they were quoting prophecy about the very man they mocked, becoming unwilling
  witnesses that this was the One. Their taunt "if thou be the Son of God, come down" was answered not
  by coming down but by staying — His Sonship proven by bearing the scorn, for the scorners.
- **Cross-ref (airtight):** Psalm 22:7-8 → Matthew 27:39-43 (the mockers' gesture AND words foretold).
- **Format:** 9:16, ~60s, Gospel Five-Beat, grace-anchored CTA-to-Jesus.
- **Distinct from v1 #02:** v1 led on "He wasn't delivered — not weakness but love." v2 leads on the
  mockers as unwitting prophets (the near-verbatim quote) + Sonship proven by staying, not by a sign.

**[mocker — KJV, Matthew 27:43]**
"He trusted in God; let him deliver him now... for he said, I am the Son of God."

**[narrator]**
They thought they were inventing the cruelty. They were reciting prophecy — unwilling witnesses that this was the One.

**[narrator]**
Save yourself and come down, they jeered — if you really are the Son of God. But He could have come down — He proved He was the Son not by escaping the cross, but by staying on it, bearing the scorn He could have silenced, for the very people throwing it.

**[narrator]**
The man they mocked is the One the whole song was about. They told the King to come down — and He never did. He stayed under their scorn, and yours, to win you. Come to the One who would not come down.

## DEPTH & SOURCING  *(not spoken)*
- **KJV verbatim** (self-verified via `pipeline/scripture.fetch_kjv`, 2026-06-16): Psalm 22:7-8 and
  Matthew 27:40,43. Quoted spans are ordered, non-overlapping ellipsis fragments.
  - Ps 22:7-8 span omits the opening "All they that see me laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip," and the closing "seeing he delighted in him."
  - Matt 27:43 omits ", if he will have him:" (the "..." between "now" and "for he said").
  - "If thou be the Son of God, come down" — verbatim Matt 27:40 (spoken as the mockers' line, attributed).
- **The fresh, faithful lever:** Matthew 27:43 is near-verbatim Psalm 22:8, and "wagging their heads"
  (27:39) is Psalm 22:7's "shake the head" — the mockery itself was scripted. The enemies became
  unwitting witnesses. Surprising about the *text*, never about the *truth*.
- **Doctrine:** Sonship proven by staying, not by a sign (no "He couldn't" — He chose; cf. Matt 27:42
  "himself he cannot save" is the mockers' taunt, NOT the narrator's claim). Grace-anchored landing: He
  bore the scorn for the scorners; "come to Him." No fear / gain-loss / manufactured pressure.
- **No invented narrative detail:** the crowd/rulers/passers-by are exactly Matthew's record; no claim
  beyond the text (e.g. no asserting any individual mocker's later conversion).
