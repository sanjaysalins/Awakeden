### Doctrinal soundness

**Mostly sound; one fuzzy Christological line.**

The Psalm→Matthew fulfillment angle (mockers as unwitting witnesses) is standard evangelical typology, not contrarian freshness-chasing. No invented narrative (no convert-the-mockers fanfiction). Grace framing on *"bearing the scorn... for the very people throwing it"* is orthodox.

**Problem line:**  
*"But His Sonship wasn't proven by coming down — it was proven by staying"*  

Sonship is eternal, not earned at Golgotha. In context you mean *the mockers' sign-test was answered by staying, not descending* — but on first hearing a zero-Bible listener can hear: *Jesus became God's Son by endurance*. That is sloppy christology even if unintended. Constitution also asks the landing to go to **who Christ is**; this line answers the **taunt**, not the Person.

**Removed safeguard vs v1:** v1 explicitly blocked impotence misread (*"not because Jesus lacked the power to come down; He had every power"*). v2 replaces that with *"bearing the scorn He could have silenced"* — weaker. *"Silenced"* is ambiguous (miracle? descent? judgment?) and does not clearly assert **He chose not to come down because He could have**.

### Clarity on first hearing

**Several friction points for a Bible-naïve listener:**

1. **"Matthew watched it happen"** — Matthew is the **recorder**, not a named eyewitness at the cross. *"Matthew records"* or *"the Gospel records"* is accurate; *"watched"* injects a false picture and undercuts textual credibility with informed listeners.

2. **"how the Messiah would die"** (beat before the Psalm quote) — Psalm 22:7–8, as quoted, covers **mockery**, not death. Death is true of the wider psalm, but the listener has not heard it yet. This front-loads knowledge the audio has not earned.

3. **"this was the One"** — Messianic shorthand. Fine in-series; opaque cold. Who is *"the One"*?

4. **"almost word for word"** — Ps 22:8 has *"trusted on the LORD"*; Matt 27:43 has *"trusted in God"* plus *"now"* and the *"I am the Son of God"* clause. The **kernel** (*let him deliver him*) parallels; the lines are not near-verbatim as spoken. A careful listener may feel oversold.

5. **Opening crowd flattening** — Opens with *"The crowd mocking Jesus"* but Matthew splits **passers-by** (head-wagging, 27:39), **chief priests/scribes/elders** (27:41–43), and a separate **temple-taunt** (27:40). You partially repair this later (*"passers-by wagging their heads, the rulers sneering"*), but the hook still conflates groups Matthew distinguishes.

**Gesture parallel is handled better than v1:** Ps quote includes *"shake the head"*; narrator prose names *"wagging their heads"* before Matt 27:43. That half of the argument is audible.
