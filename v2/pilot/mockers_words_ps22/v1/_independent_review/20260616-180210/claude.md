# Independent review — claude (OK, 65s)

I read it adversarially, verified each quoted span against KJV from memory, and checked the spine, doctrine, and landing.

## What holds up
- **KJV verbatim — clean.** Ps 22:7-8 fragment ("He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him: let him deliver him") is exact. Matt 27:43 ("He trusted in God; let him deliver him now... for he said, I am the Son of God") is exact for the kept words, with the omitted "if he will have him:" honestly marked by the ellipsis. "If thou be the Son of God, come down" is verbatim Matt 27:40. No altered words.
- **Doctrine sound.** The "Sonship proven by staying, not by a sign" reading is orthodox and avoids the mockers' "himself he cannot save" trap (the depth note correctly keeps "He could have silenced" = He chose). Messianic-Psalm-22 reading is NT-confirmed, not contrarian.
- **Spine is single and tight.** Mockers-as-unwitting-prophets runs hook → middle → CTA without swapping.
- **Grace-anchored landing.** "He stayed — for them, and for you" — no fear/gain-loss.
- **Hook grips.** "reading from a script — they just didn't know it" is a real scroll-stopper.

## Problems

**1. "At the cross, Matthew watched it happen" — overstates Matthew as eyewitness (accuracy).**
The disciples "forsook him, and fled" (Matt 26:56). The Gospel records the mockery; it does not place Matthew at the cross watching. The named eyewitnesses were the women and John (John 19:25-26). This is a claim beyond the text — and it directly contradicts the artifact's own sourcing note: *"the crowd/rulers/passers-by are exactly Matthew's record; no claim beyond the text."* "Matthew watched it happen" IS a claim beyond the text. Fix: "Matthew recorded it" or "Matthew wrote it down."

**2. Length risk (~165 spoken words).** That's high for a 59s lock with two quoted spans needing pre-quote pauses. Likely forces a rushed VO or auto-trim of a line. The 4th-from-last line is 35 words by itself. Worth a trim pass before locking.

**3. Landing softens to a generic tag.** "Come to Him" is the one tired beat in an otherwise fresh script. The line before it ("He didn't come down. He stayed — for them, and for you.") does the new work; the CTA then defaults to a stock close. Consider closing on the inversion itself — e.g. tying the invitation to *staying* ("He stayed for the ones mocking Him — that includes you"), so the CTA carries the thread instead of resetting to generic.

Minor (not blocking): "almost word for word" honestly hedges the Ps "on the LORD" vs Matt "in God" difference — fine. "the gestures, and the words" — only one gesture (head-shake) is kept from the Psalm; "shoot out the lip" is dropped. Acceptable.

The script is strong and nearly lockable; the eyewitness overreach is a real, citable factual flag (and a self-inconsistency), which tips this to a light REVISE rather than PASS.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Change "Matthew watched it happen" → "Matthew recorded it" — the disciples fled (Matt 26:56); the line is a claim beyond the text and contradicts the artifact's own no-invented-detail note.

3. Replace the generic "Come to Him" close with a CTA that carries the staying-thread, so the landing does new work instead of resetting to a stock tag.
