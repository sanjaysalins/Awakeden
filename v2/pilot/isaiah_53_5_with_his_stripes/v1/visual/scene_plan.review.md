## Visual reading
The arc opens on the felt ache (a guilt that will not close), lifts to Isaiah foreseeing healing through another's wounds, descends into the proof (the wounded, bruised, scourged Christ and the cross where it was borne 'in his own body'), then rises to the turn: the promise aimed at YOU, the believer healed, the cross finished at dawn, the open scarred hands, and the opening ache now closed. The cross (scene 6) is the hero bookending open and close so the Short lands on Christ.

## Writer's own red-team notes
Jaded Viewer risks: the stripes/scourge scenes (3,5) could blur or tip toward horror - held in Old-Master restraint, distinct framings. The 'Isaiah wrote' and 'apostle' beats are the trap (a scroll of text = Kling garbles letters) - solved by a VISION (2) and a GESTURE toward the cross (7), no writing anywhere. Scenes 1/14 are an intentional bookend; 6/9/11/12 are crucified-Christ frames in the back half - acceptable since the passage IS the cross, with varied framings (low-angle/mid/close/wide).

## Scenes
- **1. The Wound That Won't Close** ★ `[single/close/opening-hook]`
  · purpose: Make the hook's felt ache visible.
  · rationale: Not a literal gory wound (horror) but the inner ache made reverent.
  · visible: a bowed first-century man, hand over his heart, a faint dark mark beneath worn cloth, deep shadow.
- **2. Isaiah Sees It Coming** `[single/wide/ot-echo]`
  · purpose: The point beat: 700 years early Isaiah foresaw healing through another's wounds.
  · rationale: Show the prophet SEEING, not writing (no scroll, Kling garbles text); a vision in shadow.
  · visible: an aged prophet, eyes lifted, gazing toward a dim distant suffering figure; no text anywhere.
- **3. Wounded For Our Transgressions** ★ `[single/mid/theological-centre/jesus=passion]`
  · purpose: The proof's heart: He was wounded and bruised for us.
  · rationale: The scourged Christ bound to the post, marked but reverent (Old-Master restraint, no gore-horror).
  · visible: Christ bound to a low post, back marked with welts, head bowed, modest cloth, no lurid blood.
- **4. As A Lamb, Silent** `[single/close/ot-echo]`
  · purpose: OT echo (Isa 53:7, cited Acts 8:32): the silent lamb led to slaughter.
  · rationale: A single bound lamb, silent — the cited type, not a stretch.
  · visible: a single young lamb lying bound and still on dark stone, eyes calm, a faint cross-shaped shadow.
- **5. By Whose Stripes** ★ `[single/close/symbolic-support/jesus=passion]`
  · purpose: Macro of the stripes themselves — the lever word made visible.
  · rationale: Extreme close on the welt-lines, reverent and abstract, not a horror crop.
  · visible: an extreme close of a marked shoulder and back, welt-lines raised in raking light, reverent.
- **6. In His Own Body, On The Tree** ★ `[single/low-angle/nt-gospel-link/jesus=passion]`
  · purpose: THE HERO — the gospel-pivot: Christ on the cross, the wounds that heal, the CTA landing.
  · rationale: Iconic, near-still, reverent crucifixion — bookends open and close so the Short lands on Christ.
  · visible: the crucified Christ on a rough cross, body marked with stripes, head bowed, faint light behind.
- **7. The Apostle Lays It On Christ** `[unified/mid/nt-gospel-link]`
  · purpose: Proof: the apostle takes Isaiah's word and applies it to the crucified Christ.
  · rationale: Show the apostle GESTURING toward the cross (no scroll/text); the connection carried in vignettes.
  · visible: an aged apostle, hand opened toward a dim crucified Christ beyond, soft background vignettes of the echo.
- **8. All We Like Sheep** `[unified/wide/ot-echo]`
  · purpose: OT echo (Isa 53:6): we all strayed; the LORD laid the iniquity on Him.
  · rationale: Scattered sheep on a darkening hill + one laden burdened figure — the pericope's own line.
  · visible: scattered sheep on a darkening hillside, one shadowed figure bowed under a great weight, a far cross.
- **9. He Bare Our Sins** `[unified/mid/theological-centre/jesus=passion]`
  · purpose: Proof / theological centre: He bore OUR sins in His body.
  · rationale: Crucified Christ central, the things He bore as vignettes (no written record — abstract).
  · visible: the crucified Christ central and dominant, soft vignettes of broken chains, a lifted yoke, bowed sinners.
- **10. The Healed Believer** ★ `[single/mid/human-response]`
  · purpose: Conviction: the one who receives — the old wound now closed.
  · rationale: Bookend echo of scene 1: the same kind of man, eased, the scar closed, light on the face.
  · visible: a weathered man kneeling, face lifted in eased relief, an open hand at his chest where a scar has closed.
- **11. Aimed At You** ★ `[single/close/revelation/jesus=passion]`
  · purpose: Conviction: 'ye' — the promise turned straight at the viewer.
  · rationale: Christ's eyes turned gently toward the viewer — the pronoun made a gaze.
  · visible: a close reverent view of the crucified Christ's face, eyes turned gently toward the viewer.
- **12. Finished At The Cross** `[unified/wide/nt-gospel-link/jesus=passion]`
  · purpose: Landing: it was finished — the cross against a widening dawn.
  · rationale: The cross at dawn with the finished-work echoes as vignettes; gospel-frame close.
  · visible: the cross against a widening gold dawn, the marked body still, soft vignettes of the finished work.
- **13. Come And Receive** ★ `[single/mid/closing-devotional/jesus=passion]`
  · purpose: Landing CTA: the wounded hands open in invitation — come and receive.
  · rationale: Open scarred hands extended toward the viewer — the invitation made gesture.
  · visible: the open scarred hands of Christ extended toward the viewer, palms up, nail-marks healed, warm light.
- **14. The Wound He Closed** ★ `[single/close/closing-devotional]`
  · purpose: Closing devotional: the ache of scene 1 now at rest — He closed it.
  · rationale: Direct bookend to the hook — the same man, hand fallen from a closed scar, at peace.
  · visible: the same weathered man from the opening, now at rest, the hand fallen from a closed pale scar, soft light.
