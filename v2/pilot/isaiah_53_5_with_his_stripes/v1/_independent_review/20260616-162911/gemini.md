# Independent review — gemini (OK, 39s)

The script is exceptionally strong—the theological guardrails around "healing" as spiritual (guilt/sin) rather than a prosperity-gospel health claim are perfectly executed, and the cross-reference is airtight. However, there are a few distinct friction points in audio delivery and metaphor construction that need tightening.

**1. Audio/Phonetic Friction ("we" vs "ye")**
*Cite: "Isaiah wrote 'we are healed.' Peter writes 'ye were healed' — and points the promise..."*
In a purely audio format, the words "we" and "ye" sound nearly identical. A listener with zero Bible knowledge might just hear a stutter or be confused about what exactly changed. While the script explains it immediately after ("points the promise... at you"), the moment of revelation risks being lost in the phonetics. It needs slight padding to ensure the contrast lands audibly (e.g., "He changes 'we' to 'ye'—shifting it to *you*.").

**2. Missed Textual Depth (Tense Shift)**
*Cite: "Isaiah wrote 'we are healed.' Peter writes 'ye were healed'..."*
The script rightly highlights the pronoun shift, but entirely ignores the tense shift, which is equally profound. Isaiah looks forward (present/prophetic: "are"); Peter looks backward to an accomplished historical fact (past: "were"). You actually use this theology in the landing ("it was finished at the cross"), but you missed the opportunity to anchor that explicitly in Peter's grammar. Highlighting the "were" makes the grace-anchored landing even stronger.

**3. Metaphor Collision in the Landing**
*Cite: "The guilt that keeps reopening, He has already closed."*
The hook establishes a parallel: "a wound... a guilt that keeps reopening." But in the landing, applying the verb "closed" to the noun "guilt" is syntactically and visually awkward (you close a wound, you don't "close guilt"). To keep the spine airtight and the imagery clean, the verb needs to match the exact noun (e.g., "The wound that keeps reopening, He has already closed").

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the metaphor collision in the landing: change "The guilt that keeps reopening, He has already closed" to "The wound that keeps reopening..." so the imagery stays grammatically intact.
2. Unpack the phonetic similarity between "we" and "ye" so the listener doesn't miss the lever in audio (ensure the shift to "you" is unmistakable).
3. Briefly acknowledge the tense shift ("are" to "were") alongside the pronoun shift to perfectly set up your "finished at the cross" landing.
