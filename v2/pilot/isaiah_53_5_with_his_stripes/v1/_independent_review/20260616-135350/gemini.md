**CRITIQUE**

*   **Doctrinal Soundness / Biblical Fidelity (FAIL):** 
    *   *“And Peter, who stood near enough to watch that scourging…”* — This is a severe biblical fabrication. The Gospel accounts are explicit that Peter fled, followed afar off to the courtyard, denied Jesus three times, and went out weeping *before* Jesus was handed over to Pilate to be scourged (Mark 14-15). John was at the cross; there is no scriptural record of Peter witnessing the Roman scourging. Inserting this creates a false narrative for the sake of dramatic effect.
    *   *“Stripes — the marks of a Roman scourge, written down centuries before Rome ever used one.”* — This implies that scourging or whipping (stripes) was a Roman invention predicted by Isaiah. Beating with stripes was a common ancient Near Eastern punishment (Deuteronomy 25:3 explicitly mentions limiting punishment to 40 stripes). The prophetic marvel is that the *sinless Servant* would be scourged for our peace, not that Isaiah predicted a tool Rome hadn't used yet.
*   **Freshness / Faithful Depth (PASS):**
    *   *“Watch the one word Peter changes. Isaiah wrote ‘we.’ Peter writes ‘ye’…”* — This is a fantastic, grounded, text-based observation. It is deeply faithful to the text and provides genuine freshness without needing to invent historical scenarios. 
*   **Hook (PASS):**
    *   *“You carry a wound nothing has been able to close — a guilt that keeps reopening…”* — Excellent. It instantly clarifies that the "healing" in this verse is spiritual (healing from guilt/sin), building an immediate guardrail against physical prosperity-gospel interpretations of the verse.
*   **Landing Does New Work (PASS):**
    *   *“You were never meant to heal yourself. The stripes that mend you have already been borne… Come to Him.”* — The landing beautifully pays off the "ye/you" transition. It relies entirely on grace ("already been borne"), avoiding works/fear. 
*   **KJV Verbatim (PASS):**
    *   Both Isaiah 53:5 and 1 Peter 2:24 are quoted exactly, with ellipses correctly placed where text was cleanly omitted.

**VERDICT: REVISE**

TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the unbiblical claim that Peter "stood near enough to watch that scourging." Rewrite the transition to simply focus on Peter looking back at the cross and applying Isaiah's exact words to the Church.
2. Remove the historically inaccurate claim that "stripes" were written down before Rome invented them. Focus instead on the sheer prophetic weight of the Servant bearing those wounds. 
3. Maintain the strong "we -> ye" textual observation as the core pivot of the script, allowing the text to do the heavy lifting rather than manufactured historical drama.
