# Independent adversarial review — *With His Stripes* (Isaiah 53:5 short)

## Doctrinal soundness

**Mostly orthodox, but the spoken arc under-specifies what “healed” means.**

The hook pins guilt (`"a guilt that keeps reopening"`), and 1 Peter’s spoken quote includes `"bare our sins"`. Good. But Isaiah’s spoken line ends on `"we are healed"` immediately after *transgressions/iniquities* — with no spoken bridge tying *healed* to sin/righteousness. The very clause that locks Peter’s meaning — *"that we, being dead to sins, should live unto righteousness"* — is **omitted from speech** and only defended in unspoken DEPTH notes.

For a cold listener (or anyone carrying prosperity-gospel baggage on Isaiah 53:5), *healed* can still land as emotional or physical relief, not substitutionary forgiveness. The artifact **knows** this risk and **does not close it in what is heard**.

**Timeline slippage:** `"it was finished in His wounds, the moment they fell"` sits right after Peter’s `"on the tree"` quote, but the preceding image is scourging — `"welts of a flogging"`, `"Jesus' back"`. Atonement in 1 Peter 2:24 is anchored to the **tree**, not the lash alone. Collapsing “finished” to the moment stripes *fell* subtly mislocates the finished work.

**Metadata overclaim (shows weak self-audit):** Header says Peter `"quotes Isaiah verbatim"`; DEPTH still calls Peter `"the eyewitness"`. Both were explicitly corrected elsewhere in the same document. Sloppy for a LOCK candidate.

## KJV verbatim

**1 Peter 2:24 fragment:** Pass. `"Who his own self bare our sins in his own body on the tree... by whose stripes ye were healed."` matches the cached KJV with an acceptable ellipsis.

**Isaiah 53:5 fragment:** **FAIL on punctuation.**

- KJV: `"...he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed."`
- Artifact: `"...he was bruised for our iniquities... and with his stripes we are healed."`

The colon after **iniquities** is replaced by comma + ellipsis. Under your own rule — *every word inside quotation marks must be exact KJV* — that is an altered word/punctuation boundary, not a clean fragment.

**Spoken logic error (not a typo, still a fidelity failure):**

- `"the apostle Peter took those exact words and laid them on Christ at the cross"`

Peter does **not** use Isaiah’s exact words. He adapts: *with his stripes we are healed* → *by whose stripes ye were healed* (preposition, pronoun, **and** tense). Line 31 partially admits this, but line 25 contradicts it in the same breath. A listener cannot trust both sentences.

Inline mini-quotes in `"Isaiah wrote 'we are healed.' Peter makes it personal — 'ye were healed'"` are fine; the problem is the **"exact words"** claim, not those snippets.

## Clarity on first hearing

**Hook:** Strong. `"You carry a wound nothing has been able to close — a guilt that keeps reopening"` is concrete and immediate (~5s grip: **pass**).

**Middle:** Assumes a lot without grounding:

- `"Seven hundred years before the cross, Isaiah wrote..."` — **cross** before **Jesus** is named; cold listener asks *whose cross?*
- `"Isaiah"`, `"the apostle Peter"` — no one-line IDs for zero-Bible listeners.
- `"Watch what Peter does with it."` — Peter has not been situated; this is insider shorthand.

**Logic squeeze:** `"present turned to past, because the wounding is finished"` oversimplifies Isaiah’s prophetic present (*we **are** healed*) and Peter’s accomplished aorist (*ye **were** healed*). Not heresy, but it sounds like clever exegesis on first hearing and will confuse anyone who knows Isaiah uses present tense on purpose.

**Self-contradiction:** *Exact words* (line 25) vs *Peter adapts wording* (line 31 + actual quotes). That is a hearable logic break, not a nit.

## Grace-anchored conviction

**Mostly pass.** `"You were never meant to heal yourself"` and `"The healing isn't something you produce"` are anti-works, invitation-shaped.

**Mild tone risk:** `"Stop straining to be whole"` can read as rebuke to someone already exhausted by guilt — borderline, not disqualifying.

**Hook:** `"no matter how hard you try to be good enough"` describes failed self-effort as the problem, not the gospel path — acceptable setup.

No fear/gain-loss/manufactured pressure detected. **Pass with minor tone watch.**

Undercut by:

2. **Landing** doesn’t harvest the freshness (see below).

No thread-swapping.

## Landing does NEW work

**Fail relative to the artifact’s own claim.**

DEPTH asserts the close *"does NEW work (the pronoun aimed at YOU)"* — but the pronoun work is spent in line 31. The actual landing is:

`"Stop straining to be whole; come to Him, and receive it."`

That is competent grace language, but **generic CTA-to-Jesus** — close cousin to *“will you trust Him?”* The fresh lever (**ye were healed** / *aimed straight at you*) is **not echoed or cashed** in the final beat. The landing repeats anti-self-effort instead of closing on Peter’s personalized promise.
