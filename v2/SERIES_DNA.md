## 1. The one grammar: RESTRAINT EARNS RELEASE

The visual DNA's engine: the hero shot is rationed to glory beats, so glory LANDS.
The same shape — the gospel's own shape, power laid down before power revealed —
runs through every channel. Each channel has an everyday register (always on),
and ONE rationed move it spends only where it's true:

| Channel | Everyday register (always) | The rationed move | Spent only at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Line | Black ink outlines | **Gold Seam** — light eats the line | Where the key light strikes; radiant at glory, faint at passion |
| Camera | Level / dynamic mid-angles | **The hero shot** (low-angle) | Glory beats only |
| Composition | Clean staging | **Witness Edge** silhouette | Key beats — we stand in the crowd |
| Text | Parchment-band caption | **The Gold Splash** — full-screen verse takeover | The gospel pivot, ONCE per piece |
| Verse color | Ink-black lettering | **Red letters** | Christ's own words, nothing else ever |
| Score | Full orchestra, always | **The Gold Chord** — full harmonic resolution | The pivot / glory beat |
| SFX | Quiet ambient bed | **One True Sound** — a single foreground hit | The piece's one physical beat |
| Ending | — | **The held gaze** (INV-26 ≥3.0s hold) | Every piece — we don't look away |

One grammar, eight channels. When the pivot comes, they release TOGETHER —
seam widens, camera rises, gold fills the screen, the chord resolves — and that
stacked simultaneous release is what makes the series compelling, not any one trick.

## 3. Text & verse — the Text Ladder (three rungs, strictly rationed)

- **Rung 1 — the parchment band** (default, always): narrator + Scripture, wobbled
  warm-black ink border. The everyday voice.
- **Rung 2 — red letters** (whenever, but ONLY, Christ speaks): red is His voice,
  never an emphasis tool. Nothing else in the whole series ever renders red text.
- **Rung 3 — the GOLD SPLASH** (once per piece, gospel pivot only): the full-screen
  verse takeover already used once for "I will in no wise cast out" (`_compose_pages_v2.py`
  — panels dim to 0.25, the pivot panel goes full-bleed, the verse holds the frame).
  Elevate that one-off into the RULE: it is the text channel's hero shot — never
  twice, never for a non-pivot verse.
- **The unifier (new, tiny, $0):** the splash card's wobbled ink border catches the
  **Gold Seam** — border renders gold on the splash card ONLY; every ordinary band
  stays warm black. The visual signature and the text signature become one move.
- **Rule in one line:** *Red belongs to His voice. Gold belongs to His glory. The
  full screen belongs to the pivot — once.*

## 4. Score — the Gold Chord (the tension, named and resolved)

- **The tension (same shape as the body-gate):** "restraint" sounds like "go sparse
  at passion beats" — and *full orchestral, never sparse* is LOCKED. We cannot and
  do not thin the orchestra.
- **The resolution:** restraint lives in **harmony and register, not instrument
  count** — exactly as visual energy lives in composition, not the body.
  - The orchestra is always full-voiced. What is rationed is **resolution**: through
    law / passion / conviction the writing stays full but WITHHELD — minor colors,
    suspended and unresolved cadences, pedal points, the melody circling its home
    note without landing.
  - **The Gold Chord:** one recurring resolve motif per piece (the score's Gold
    Seam). It appears in fragments, withheld, throughout — and resolves COMPLETELY
    exactly once, at the gospel pivot / glory beat, high register opening as it lands.
  - **Register rule:** passion = low, dark, full (cellos/basses carry it); glory =
    the top of the orchestra opens. Loud-and-dark is not sparse.
  - The locked *no-choir-pad-unless-earned* rule is ALREADY this grammar: voices are
    the score's rationed element. The cold-to-warm arc is the pattern this formalizes.

## 5. SFX — One True Sound

- The ambient bed stays the quiet default (locked). The rationed move on top:
- **At most ONE foreground diegetic hit per piece** — the veil K-KRAAK class: a
  stone rolling, a seal breaking, a nail struck, a door shut. Placed at the piece's
  single most physical beat, **named in the scene plan** (like `hero_candidate`),
  never improvised at mix time. Everywhere else: ambience only.
- Because sound only steps forward once, that once carries the weight of an event —
  the audio hero shot.
- **The stillness carve-out stays senior:** crucifixion / veil-tear / resurrection
  get NO hit; the bed itself thins to wind and room. Silence is the sound of those
  beats.
- **Worth testing (not yet proven):** a one-breath bed dip (~1s, bed only — the
  score is untouched, never-sparse respected) right before the Gold Splash, so the
  pivot arrives on held breath.

## 7. The throughline

> **Every layer holds its glory back — and spends it, once, on Jesus.**

The long form, for the pitch page:

> *The light is His. The camera kneels. The red is His voice. The gold is His
> glory. The chord resolves for Him alone. Sound steps forward once. And when the
> last word is spoken — we do not look away.*

Why this is compelling to WATCH, plainly: a viewer can't name the rules, but they
feel the withholding — and when every channel releases on the same beat, the pivot
hits with everything the piece saved up. The genre spends everything every scene;
we spend it once, together, where it's true.
