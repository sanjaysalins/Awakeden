Comic text-box layer POC — $0, code-drawn, post-animation

Speech bubbles + parchment caption boxes drawn by PIL (never baked into the AI art — baked text garbles under animation), overlaid with timed pop-ins on the already-animated crop-and-recomposite page. Text is KJV verbatim (Luke 23:40–43, checked against data/kjv_cache.json). Jesus’ words in red-letter per printed-KJV convention. The rebuke bubble’s tail points OFF-PANEL LEFT because the figure drawn in that panel reads as Christ — the tail must never attribute the thief’s words to him.

Known POC limitation

Overlays are static in page space while grid_choreography’s virtual camera pushes panels — text placement was chosen to stay safe across all camera states, but the production tool should draw text cell-relative INSIDE the choreography pass so bubbles ride their panels exactly.
