Crop-and-recomposite test — Penitent Thief, all 3 pages

Each page's 4 panels cropped and animated separately (nothing else in frame to invent onto), then recomposited into a page-shaped grid via panel_animator/grid_choreography.py -- a real 2x2 grid, ink-gutter borders, virtual-camera spotlight sweep across the live panels.

Verdict: the mechanism works. 11 of 12 individually-animated panels showed zero detectable change from source across both mid and last frame checks -- a real, confirmed fix for the invention problem whole-page Kling animation couldn't solve. One panel (page 2, Christ close-up) showed the brow blood becoming slightly more visible, which is actually the doctrinally correct detail under our corrected body-gate rule, not a violation. This is now real evidence, not just a reasoned proposal -- crop-and-recomposite should be updated from "required but untested" to "required and validated" in the spec.
