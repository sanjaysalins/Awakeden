Baroque oil painting, vertical 9:16, the fourth watch before dawn on the storm-torn Sea of Galilee — Jesus stands upright and serene on the black heaving water, robes and hair streaming in the boisterous wind, one arm stretched down with his hand firmly clasping the wrist of Peter, who is half-sunk to his chest in the foaming waves, face upturned in terror and pleading, one hand gripping Jesus' forearm as the swells drag at him; whitecaps and wind-driven spray around them, a small storm-tossed fishing boat with faint disciples silhouetted in the distant murk, a bruised indigo-and-charcoal sky splitting to a thin bar of grey dawn light on the horizon; the pale dawn glow rims Christ's calm figure and falls on Peter's frightened face, deep chiaroscuro, wet reflective sea, dramatic Rembrandt lighting, tenebrism, rich impasto texture, ancient first-century Galilean dress, reverent and cinematic, painterly detail, no text.
