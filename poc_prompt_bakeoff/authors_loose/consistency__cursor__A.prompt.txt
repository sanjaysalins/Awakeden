Three Judean women paused at grey-gold dawn before a low rock-hewn garden tomb — young dark-haired Mary Magdalene, middle-aged Joanna, and an older grey-haired woman in wool tunics and head mantles with plain clay spice jars — the great round stone rolled aside beside the dark low opening, an empty waist-low stone burial bench inside the bare unlit chamber, still faces turned toward the gaping tomb.
