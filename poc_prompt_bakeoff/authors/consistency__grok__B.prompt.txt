On a garden path in first-dawn half-light, the risen Jesus stands foremost in plain tunic and mantle, Mary Magdalene a resolute woman in her thirties in muted-ochre wool tunic and head-mantle bowed at his feet with simple hands upon them, Joanna dignified middle-aged in soft-brown tunic and mantle and an older grey-haired woman in faded-olive tunic and mantle soft in shadow beside her bowed in worship, hush of astonished joy.
