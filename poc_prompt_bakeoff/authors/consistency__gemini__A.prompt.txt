In the hushed dawn half-light, Mary Magdalene in her thirties wearing a russet-brown wool tunic and mantle, dignified middle-aged Joanna in olive-green, and an older grey-haired woman in a tan mantle stand startled before a dark, empty rock-cut garden tomb. A massive, heavy stone disc rests rolled aside from the entrance, as the women hold small clay jars of spices in a frozen moment of astonished fear.
