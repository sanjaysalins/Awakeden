At first-dawn half-light before a rock-cut garden tomb, heavy stone disc rests aside from a dark empty doorway, Mary Magdalene stands foremost startled, resolute woman in her thirties in muted-ochre wool tunic and head-mantle, clay spice jar in simple hands, Joanna dignified middle-aged in soft-brown tunic and mantle and an older grey-haired woman in faded-olive tunic and mantle with cloth spice bundles soft in shadow beside her, sandals on dust.
