Jesus, about thirty, crowned with thorns and clothed in a modest loincloth, is nailed to a plain rough-hewn Roman cross on Golgotha's low barren skull-hill outside Jerusalem, head lifted in reverent prayer, pierced wrists resting in shadow against the dark wood, two distant malefactors on flanking crosses lost in dim silhouette, Roman soldiers and a shadowed crowd receding below, oppressive supernatural darkness covering the land at noon.
