On a dusty Judean path in soft morning light, the risen Jesus in a plain tunic and mantle stands as the same three women — thirtyish Mary Magdalene in terracotta wool and rust mantle, middle-aged Joanna in olive-brown wool with charcoal mantle, grey-haired elder in dusty beige — kneel bowed low at his sandaled feet in worship, mantles forward, hands clasped in shadow, spice bundles set beside them on the path.
