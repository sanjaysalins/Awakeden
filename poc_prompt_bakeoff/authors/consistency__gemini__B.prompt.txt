On a dusty path in the early morning light, the risen Jesus stands clad in a plain tunic and mantle. At his feet, the same three women, Mary in her russet-brown mantle, middle-aged Joanna in olive-green, and the older grey-haired woman in tan, kneel bowed to the ground in a frozen posture of worship. Their faces radiate astonished joy, their shadowed hands resting near his sandaled feet.
