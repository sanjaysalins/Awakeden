At pale first-dawn light in a walled garden, three ancient Judean women stand frozen in hushed alarm before a rock-cut tomb whose great round disc roll-stone rests fully rolled aside, the dark hollow interior open and empty; Mary Magdalene in her thirties in a rust-brown tunic with dark hair, dignified middle-aged Joanna in deep-ochre mantle with brown hair, and an older grey-haired woman in muted grey-blue, each clasping small clay spice jars.
