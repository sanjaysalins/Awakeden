Mary Magdalene stands foremost in a muted rust wool tunic and dark mantle, resolute thirties with dark loose hair partly veiled, Joanna behind in an ochre mantle, dignified middle-aged, and an older grey-haired woman in a faded blue mantle, all startled before a rock-cut garden tomb, clay spice jars held close, the heavy disc stone resting aside, dawn half-light revealing a dark empty opening.
