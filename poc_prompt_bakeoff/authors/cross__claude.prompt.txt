Jesus of Nazareth, weathered Judean man of about thirty crowned with a twisted crown of thorns and stripped to a modest cloth loincloth, nailed to a plain rough-hewn wooden Roman cross atop the low skull-hill of Calvary, head lifted in sorrowful prayer, two distant malefactors on flanking crosses dissolving into deep shadow, a mocking crowd and Roman soldiers sunk in darkness below, an oppressive supernatural noon darkness lying heavy over all the land, pierced hands simple and shadowed.
