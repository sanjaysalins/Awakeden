Frozen: inked graphic-novel style + negatives + NBP renderer + character reference. Only the prompt author varies. Labels are BLIND (mapping in blind_key.json).

Scoring rubric (1-5 each):
  • Biblical accuracy: Honours the fact card; no banned anachronism; period-true.
  • Faithful theming: Reads as the right beat; reverent, not sensational.
  • Animation-clean: One dominant subject; simple hands; no legible text; frozen tableau.
  • Composition: Strong focal hierarchy, depth, 9:16 framing.
  • Consistency: (pair only) same people across both frames.

Father, forgive them (cross)

The hero gospel-pivot frame: Jesus crucified at Calvary, and from the cross he prays for the very men who nailed him — 'Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do.' This is the still the whole short bookends on; it must carry the landing.

Lord, save me (water)

The hard 'impossible physics' frame: on the storm-tossed sea in the last dark watch, Jesus walks on the water and reaches out his hand to catch Peter, who stepped out of the boat, saw the wind, was afraid, and began to sink crying 'Lord, save me.'

A
In the fourth watch of night on the black storm-tossed Sea of Galilee, Jesus stands upright and calm on the heaving water in a first-century tunic and mantle, one hand stretched forth and holding fast to Peter, a weathered Galilean fisherman of fifty half-sunk in the dark waves reaching up in fear, a small wooden fishing boat with huddled disciples receding into shadow behind them under a heavy wind-torn sky.
B
In the fourth-watch pre-dawn darkness over the open Sea of Galilee, Jesus stands upright on the black heavy water in a first-century tunic and mantle, calm and dominant, one hand stretched forth to catch; Peter, a weathered Galilean fisherman about fifty, sinks half into the waves reaching upward in fear, a small wooden fishing boat with disciples held in deep shadow behind them.

In pre-dawn fourth-watch darkness on the storm-tossed Sea of Galilee, Jesus stands upright atop the black wave surface, calm and central in first-century tunic and mantle, one hand extended downward, while Peter, a weathered Galilean fisherman about fifty, sits half-sunk among heavy waves below him, fearful face upturned, with a small wooden fishing boat and shadowed disciples far behind.

Jesus stands calmly upright on the surface of the storm-tossed Sea of Galilee in pre-dawn darkness, wearing a first-century tunic and mantle with one arm extended downward in rescue, his hand shadowed, while a fearful fifty-year-old Galilean fisherman remains waist-deep in the heavy waves with an upraised arm, and a small wooden first-century fishing boat holding silhouetted figures rests in deep background shadows.

The women, first witnesses (2 beats) (consistency)

BEAT A — Very early on the first day of the week, at first dawn light, the women come to the rock-cut tomb carrying their burial spices, and find the great stone already rolled away and the tomb open and empty.
BEAT B — Moments later, as the SAME women hurry away to tell the disciples, the risen Jesus himself meets them on the path; they fall and hold him by the feet and worship.
