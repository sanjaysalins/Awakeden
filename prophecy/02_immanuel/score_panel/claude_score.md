**1. SCORE IDEA**
One four-note tune, "Im-ma-nu-el", is asked like a question in fear and answered in grace. It only finishes on the final hold.
- **Instruments:** low strings (cellos, basses), muted then open French horn, soft high violins, harp, felt timpani (2 hits only), warm full strings for the swell.
- **Motif:** four notes that rise step, step, leap, then hang without coming home (in D minor at first). In the hold it lands on D major.

**2. WHY IT FITS**
- Bold black ink and slamming comic words need clear, simple shapes, not texture-mush. One clear tune and hard dynamic steps (quiet, then a swell) match the panel slams.
- The story is a question ("will you take a sign?") and an answer. The motif left hanging under Ahaz and resolved at "God's answer" *is* the story.
- Same family as Bethlehem (strings, horns, sparing timpani, one swell, the tune left open until the hold), so the series sounds like one series, with its own tune.

**4. SPLIT**
**43.5 s**, with a 0.3 s crossfade. That is the film's real turn (fear to grace, minor to major), right before "Joseph obeyed." Part 1 = 43.8 s (fear and promise). Part 2 = 20.3 s (grace, cross, swell, resolve). Each part keeps one mood, so dead air is less likely.
