# IMMANUEL - SCORE PLAN (Fable seat, blind)

## 1. SCORE IDEA
One four-note rising tune asks a question in the dark under Ahaz and is only answered, in the light, in the final hold.

- **Instruments:** low strings (cellos, basses), warm violas and violins, two French horns, one harp, soft timpani (3 hits in the whole film). No choir, no drums.
- **Motif ("the answer tune"):** four slow notes climbing step by step, stopping one step short of home. Minor and thin under Ahaz (solo cello). Major and warm from Joseph on (horn, then full strings). It lands home only at 60.8 s.
- **Tempo:** 60 BPM, so one beat = one second. Key: D minor, then D major.

## 2. WHY IT FITS
- **Ink = clean lines.** Bold black ink wants a bold single line, not a wash. One tune, few instruments, strong silences. The comic words slam; the music must leave room for them.
- **Two sons, one tune.** Ahaz and Joseph both hear "fear not". Same four notes, dark then light. The viewer feels the link without being told.
- **God's answer is the last note.** The tune stays unfinished for 60 s. It finishes right after "That was God's answer." The music says the line again.

## 4. SPLIT
**43.5 s.** This is the film's hinge: dark to light, minor to major. A music model will not change key on cue inside one take. Two takes make the turn certain. The join hides under "Joseph obeyed." Part 1 = 46 s render (trim to 43.8). Part 2 = 23 s render (starts 43.4). Crossfade 0.4 s.

## 6. MIX NOTES
- Under narrator: music about 18 dB below voice.
- Under KJV lines (16.9-23.4, 31.1-43.7, 57.5-59.1): 3-4 dB lower still. Slow 0.4 s ramps, no pumping.
- Lifts: 23.4-24.1 (+5 dB), 55.0-57.3 swell (+4 dB, voice still on top), 60.8-63.8 full level, no duck.
- Never fully out. Lowest point is 51.3-55.0 (the cross) and the one-beat hole at "refused".
- Hold: audio and video both run to 63.8 s. Final chord fades in the last 0.8 s. Pad to whole length, not by an amount.

## 7. RISKS
1. **Dead air or music lost under the voice.** Check: RMS sweep in 0.5 s windows on the music stem and the final mix. No window below -45 dB before 63.0 s.
2. **Cues land late or early (swell misses "He rose", timpani misses "save me").** Check: find the peaks by measurement against the word timings. Slide or trim the part up to 1 s; reroll part 2 if the swell is over 1 s off.
3. **The join is heard, or the tune does not land in the hold.** Check by ear on 42-46 s and 59-63.8 s with headphones. The last chord must sound finished. If not, reroll part 2 only.
