**MY PICK: B.**
It sets up the whole locked ending. "Where is God?" leads to "God with us," then "with us as a baby / at the cross," then "I am with you alway," then "Still true." Every word is common English. It also handles the clash between the prophet's line ("Immanuel") and the angel's line ("JESUS") before the viewer can feel confused.

**Second pick: C.** Same payoff, more grounded for Bible readers, but a weaker gap.

**Biggest risk of B:** "Jesus has another name" can sound like secret-name clickbait. Picky Bible readers may also note that Immanuel is a title ("they shall call"), not a name anyone used for him day to day. On top of that, the answer to "where God is" doesn't come for about 40 seconds. If the middle drags, a newcomer will scroll away before the payoff.

Fix if needed: keep the bridge exactly as written ("His other name, Immanuel, means: God with us"), so the claim matches Matthew 1:23 word for word.
