## YOUR PICK: B

**Second pick: G**

Why B: the hook, the bridge and the last two words ("Still true") are one thread: where is God? With us. Still. Nothing in the hook is left unpaid, and no word needs explaining. G stops harder, but it opens a king story that the locked script does not tell, and it adds words to a 59-second film.

**Biggest risk of B:** it is the calmest hook here. "Jesus has another name" can read as a trivia teaser, with no stake, so a fast scroller may not stop. It also drops the "promise" idea from the first line, and "His other name" slightly overstates (nobody called Him Immanuel day to day; it says who He is). If the stop rate tests weak, G is the fallback, but only if its extra words still fit 59 seconds.
