# Immanuel v2 draft - seat: fable

## 1. Script (* = changed or new line)

*[narrator] A frightened king told God no.
*[narrator] King Ahaz, of David's family, feared two enemy kings. God said, "Fear not," and offered any sign. Ahaz refused, and begged a foreign king instead: "save me." No help came. So God chose the sign:
[prophet] Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.
*[narrator] (pause) Seven hundred years later, Mary, a virgin, was pregnant by God's Holy Spirit.
[narrator] An angel told her husband Joseph:
*[angel] Joseph, thou son of David, fear not.
[angel] thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.
*[narrator] Joseph obeyed. Ahaz asked a king to save him. This child saves. Immanuel means: God with us.
[narrator] With us as a baby. With us at the cross, where He died for our sins. On the third day, He rose from the dead. He promised:
[jesus] I am with you alway.
[narrator] Still true.

## 2. Counts

- Spoken words: 145
- Changed or new lines: 5 (4 narrator, 1 short new angel line). Unchanged and reused: 6 (prophet, angel name line, "An angel told...", the cross/rose line, Jesus, "Still true").

Picture fit:
- Page 1 (scroll / Ahaz afraid on the wall / Isaiah with Ahaz) carries lines 1-2. The opening is now longer than 12 s, so I would add the ONE new picture, Ahaz sending gold away, on "begged a foreign king instead: save me. No help came."
- Picture 2 (Isaiah, hand raised) = the prophet line, as now.
- Page 3 (Mary / Joseph troubled / Joseph asleep) = the Mary line + "An angel told...".
- Picture 4 (angel over Joseph) = both angel lines.
- Page 5 (Matthew writing / Joseph holding the newborn) = "Joseph obeyed... God with us."
- Pages 6-7 unchanged.

KJV check:
- "Fear not" = Isaiah 7:4. "save me" = 2 Kings 16:7. "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not" = Matthew 1:20 (cut short, words exact).
- "offered any sign" = Isaiah 7:11. "refused" = 7:12. "No help came" = 2 Chronicles 28:20-21. "God chose the sign" = 7:14 ("the Lord himself shall give you a sign"). "Joseph obeyed" = Matthew 1:24.
- "instead" makes no time claim (same crisis, not "next day"). Ahaz and Joseph are told as a contrast only. Joseph is called her husband, never the father.

Open question paid off: the hook asks "what happens when a king tells God no?" The end answers: God sent the sign anyway, and the child is the one who really saves, and is still with us.

## 3. What the viewer now learns

The famous "virgin shall conceive" promise was first given to a frightened king of David's family who refused God's sign and begged a foreign king "save me" - and seven hundred years later another son of David heard the same "fear not", obeyed, and held the child who truly saves.
