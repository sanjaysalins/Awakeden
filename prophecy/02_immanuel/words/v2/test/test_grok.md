1. NEWCOMER
A scared king, Ahaz, from King David's family, was afraid. God said "fear not" and offered a sign. Ahaz refused and asked a foreign king to save him. God gave the sign anyway: a virgin would have a son named Immanuel. About 700 years later, Mary, a virgin, was pregnant by God's Holy Spirit. An angel told her husband Joseph to name the baby Jesus, because he would save his people from their sins. Joseph obeyed. That child is God with us: as a baby, at the cross where he died for our sins, and after he rose on the third day. He said he is with you always. That is still true.

Hard lines: "Behold," "virgin shall conceive," "thou," "shalt," "son of David" (sounds like David is his father), "Holy Spirit," "sins," "sign" (a sign of what?), "alway." "Immanuel" is clear only after the next line.

2. BIBLE READER
Yes. Ahaz refused the sign, then said "save me" to a foreign king. That sits next to Jesus, who saves from sins. The same "fear not" goes to Ahaz and then to Joseph. Many people know the virgin and the name. Fewer have heard Ahaz's no in the same minute.

3. CURIOSITY
First line: 8. A king tells God no. You wait for the answer.
Ending: 7. The sign and "this child saves" answer it. "Still true" fits "God with us" better than the king's no. The film never says the foreign king failed him.

4. GUARDRAILS
For: a first-time viewer anywhere.
Take-away: God sent Jesus even after the king refused, and Jesus is still God with us.
Global: mostly yes. The first line is plain. The old English in the middle will lose people.
Accurate checks:
- "Told God no" is too strong. Ahaz said, "I will not ask, neither will I tempt the LORD" (Isaiah 7:12). He refused a sign.
- "Any sign" is a fair short form of "depth, or in the height above" (Isaiah 7:11).
- "Fear not" matches Isaiah 7:4. "Save me" is in 2 Kings 16:7.
- The prophet's line matches Isaiah 7:14 word for word.
- "Seven hundred years" is a fair round number.
- The film skips Isaiah 7:16. Before that child knew good from evil, the two kings Ahaz feared would be gone. Matthew 1:22-23 does apply the virgin sign to Jesus, so the Jesus link is right. The "nothing until 700 years later" feel is a gap.
- The foreign-king plea is the same war (2 Kings 16:7), not one recorded scene inside Isaiah 7.
- Mary "with child of the Holy Ghost" matches Matthew 1:18 and Luke 1:35. "Her husband" matches Matthew 1:19.
- The angel's words match Matthew 1:20-21, shortened, not changed. Joseph obeying matches Matthew 1:24-25.
- "God with us" matches Matthew 1:23. That verse spells the name Emmanuel. Isaiah spells Immanuel.
- "Died for our sins" and the third day match 1 Corinthians 15:3-4.
- "With us at the cross" is not a verse in the pack. The death part is. The "with us" part is a sermon step.
- "I am with you alway" matches Matthew 28:20. "Still true" speaks that line to every viewer. Jesus said it to the disciples he was sending. The verse does say "even unto the end of the world."
One change: right after the prophet, add one plain line: "A virgin will have a son. His name means God with us."

5. OVERALL: 8

Who: Jesus, and also Ahaz, Mary, and Joseph. What: the king refused God's sign, and the later child is Jesus. Why: his other name means God is with us, and that is still true.

Hard lines: the same list as X. Also "where God is." Easy words, but they sound like a place.

2. BIBLE READER
The Ahaz part is the same less-heard piece. The door is a line church people already know: Jesus, Immanuel, God with us.

3. CURIOSITY
First line: 7. A name riddle. It needs you to care who Jesus is already.
Ending: 9. The name, "God with us," the baby, the cross, the rising, and "Still true" answer it directly.

4. GUARDRAILS
For: someone who has already heard of Jesus.
Take-away: Jesus' other name means God is with us, and he still is.
Global: the body is as clear as X. The first line is harder if you do not know Jesus.
Accurate: same quote check as X. No "told God no" problem. "Another name" and "God with us" match Matthew 1:23. "Where" is soft, and the film explains it. Same gaps: Isaiah 7:16 left out, "with us at the cross" is a sermon step, and Matthew 28:20 is widened to every viewer.
One change: same plain line, right after the prophet: "A virgin will have a son. His name means God with us." That pays the riddle before the long jump.
