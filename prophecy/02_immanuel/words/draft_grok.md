[narrator] About seven hundred years before Jesus, God sent Isaiah, a prophet — a man who spoke God's message.
[narrator] A sign is a plain promise from God.
[prophet] Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.
[narrator] Virgin means she had never been with a man. Conceive means become pregnant and give birth. The name means God with us.
(pause)
[narrator] It came true. Mary had not been with a man. An angel told Joseph the child was from the Holy Ghost, God's Spirit.
[angel] thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.
[narrator] Joseph named Him Jesus. Matthew says this child is that sign.
[narrator] Emmanuel, the same name, means God with us.
