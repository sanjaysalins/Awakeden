[narrator] A frightened king was told he could ask God for any sign. He said no.
[narrator] This was Ahaz, about seven hundred years before Jesus. Isaiah, a prophet, a man who spoke God's message, told him:
[prophet] Ask thee a sign of the LORD thy God.
[ahaz] I will not ask.
[narrator] So God gave one anyway, a promise:
[prophet] Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.
[narrator] A virgin, a woman who has never been with a man, would have a son, named God with us. (pause)
[narrator] Mary, a virgin, was expecting a child by God's Holy Spirit. Matthew says the promise came true. Her son was Jesus.
[narrator] A king would not ask. God gave anyway. Jesus died for our sins. On the third day, He rose from the dead. He said,
[jesus] I am with you alway.
