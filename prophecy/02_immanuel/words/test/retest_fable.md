# Retest — independent reviewer (Fable seat)

**1. NEWCOMER.**
Who: a baby, Jesus, born to Mary, a virgin. What: long before, God promised through a man called Isaiah that a virgin would have a son called Immanuel, which means "God with us". Why: he came to save people from their sins, died, rose, and says he is still with us.
One real snag: the promise says the name is **Immanuel**, then the angel says the name is **JESUS**. Nobody tells me these are the same baby with two names. "Immanuel is the sentence" does not close that gap.
Also unclear: "was expecting a child by God's Holy Spirit" (I can guess, not sure).

**2. HOOK.** Yes, I would keep watching. It is short and odd. It is answered ("God with us"). But the answer comes right after a different name, so the payoff is blurred. **7/10**

**3. PROMISE.** God, through Isaiah, about 700 years before: a virgin will bear a son called Immanuel. Came true: Mary, a virgin, had Jesus. Heard clearly; "virgin" repeats in both halves, which helps the ear. "Matthew's book about Jesus says" is honest. **8/10**

**4. HARD WORDS.**
- "alway" (captions will look like a typo)
- "thou shalt", "Behold", "conceive"
- "been with a man" (euphemism, may be missed)
- "was expecting a child" (idiom)
- "Immanuel" (auto-captions may write "Emmanuel" or "a manual")

**5. BIBLE.** Nothing wrong. Quotes are verbatim fragments. "About seven hundred years" is fair. "Matthew's book about Jesus says the promise came true" is attributed to Matthew, so it does not deny the sign's first meaning in Ahaz's day (Isa 7:15-16). Not overclaimed.
Small: Matthew 1:23 says "**they** shall call his name Emmanuel"; the film never says who calls Jesus Immanuel.

**6. LANDING.** Lands on Jesus, in his own words. Cross and rising are stated plainly. No pressure. "Still true." is strong and earned by the "with us" chain. **9/10**

**7. Overall: 8/10**

**8. ONE change.** Bridge the two names. Replace
"Immanuel is the sentence: God with us."
with
"His name is Jesus. Immanuel is the sentence about Him: God with us."

Timing warning: the script is about 139 words plus a 1s pause, about 54.5s before voice-change gaps. This adds 6 words. Pay for it by cutting "about Jesus" from "Matthew's book about Jesus says" and "On the third day," is NOT to be cut; instead trim "a man who spoke God's message" to "God's messenger".
