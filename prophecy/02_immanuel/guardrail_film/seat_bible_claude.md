## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** Grace, 34, a churchgoer in Lagos. She has heard "Immanuel" every Christmas but has never heard of Ahaz.
- **Who stops:** Christmas-adjacent believers. Line 1 ("A frightened king told God no") is a strong hook.
- **Who finishes:** Bible readers who like it when the Old Testament gets a payoff.
- **Who shares:** small-group leaders in Advent season.
- **Not served:** the thoughtful sceptic. They know Isaiah 7:16 ties the sign to Ahaz's own lifetime, and the film never touches that.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"A scared king refused God's sign, and God gave it anyway: Jesus, God with us."
- Line 8 carries it: "This child saves. Immanuel means: God with us."
- Line 11 seals it.
- Clear? Yes.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
- **In my own words:** a king from David's family is afraid of war. He refuses God's offer of a sign and pays a foreign king instead. God gives a sign anyway, and 700 years later it is Mary's son, Jesus.
- **Where newcomers get lost:**
  - Line 2: "begged a foreign king." No name, no country. The SAVE ME panel shows temple gold, but nothing says it was *God's* treasure (2 Kings 16:8). That is the irony, and it gets lost.
  - "Immanuel" stays unexplained from 16.9 s to 51.3 s. That gap is fine on purpose, but it is long.
- **Words that fail in translation:**
  - "alway" (line 10, and on screen) looks like a typo.
  - "thou / unto thee" (line 6).
  - "pregnant by" (line 4) can be heard as crude.
- **Picture mismatch:** line 10's lakeside walk suggests John 21, but Matthew 28:20 was spoken on a mountain in Galilee (Matthew 28:16, not in the pack).

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **Line 1,** "told God no": RIGHT. Isaiah 7:12, "I will not ask."
- **Line 1,** "God answered anyway": RIGHT. Isaiah 7:14.
- **Line 2,** "from King David's family": RIGHT. Isaiah 7:13 ("O house of David"); Matthew 1:9.
- **Line 2,** "afraid of war": RIGHT. Isaiah 7:2, "his heart was moved."
- **Line 2,** "God said, 'fear not'": RIGHT. Isaiah 7:4, spoken by Isaiah for God.
- **Line 2,** "offered any sign": RIGHT. Isaiah 7:11, "in the depth, or in the height above."
- **Line 2,** "as proof": RIGHT, a fair gloss.
- **Line 2,** "Ahaz refused, and begged a foreign king: 'save me'": RIGHT. 2 Kings 16:7, "save me out of the hand…"
- **Line 2,** order of events: OVERCLAIMED, mildly. The film places the plea right after the refusal. Neither text dates the plea against the refusal.
- **Line 2,** "God chose the sign": RIGHT. Isaiah 7:14, "the Lord himself shall give you a sign."
- **Line 3:** RIGHT. Isaiah 7:14, quoted word for word.
- **Line 4,** "Seven hundred years later": UNSUPPORTED by the KJV text given. Historically fair as a round number (about 730 years).
- **Line 4,** "Mary, a virgin, was pregnant by God's Holy Spirit": RIGHT in substance. Matthew 1:18; Luke 1:27, 1:34-35.
- **Lines 6-7:** RIGHT, word for word from Matthew 1:20-21. But "And she shall bring forth a son, and" is cut out without any mark, and that is the very clause that echoes Isaiah 7:14.
- **Line 8,** "Joseph obeyed": RIGHT. Matthew 1:24.
- **Line 8,** "This child saves": RIGHT. Matthew 1:21.
- **Line 8,** "Immanuel means God with us": RIGHT. Matthew 1:23.
- **Line 9,** "died for our sins… third day… rose": RIGHT. 1 Corinthians 15:3-4.
- **Line 9,** "With us at the cross": OVERCLAIMED, mildly. It is a sound theological gloss, not a verse.
- **Line 10:** RIGHT. Matthew 28:20.
- **Missing, not wrong:** Isaiah 7:16 is skipped. The fix is already in the text: Isaiah 7:13 addresses the sign to "house of David" (plural "you"), and the angel hails Joseph as "son of David." That hinge would answer the sceptic.

**Would I send it to my church group?** Yes. No line is WRONG.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would make "house of David" the bridge. The sign was given to David's family, and 700 years later the angel says it to David's son, Joseph. Right now line 2 and line 6 both say "David" but never connect.

**One highest-value change:** add "given to all of David's family" to line 2, so line 6 lands as a payoff.

**Score:** 7/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
- **Opens:** line 1, "God answered anyway."
- **Pays off:** line 8, sealed at line 11.
- **Score:** 7/10. Worth the wait. Stating "Immanuel" in line 3 without its meaning is a good tease.

## PROMISE KEPT
- **The promise:** through Isaiah, God promised David's house a virgin-born son called "God with us."
- **How long before:** about 700 years.
- **What came true:** Mary, a virgin, bore Jesus, and Matthew names it as fulfilment (Matthew 1:22-23).
- **Did it feel like a promise kept?** Mostly. The "700 YEARS LATER" tag and the end card help. The David link would make it click.
- **Score:** 7/10.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **Line 2:** "…Ahaz refused, and paid a foreign king with God's own temple gold: 'save me.' So God gave a sign to all of David's family:"
2. **Line 4:** "Seven hundred years later, Mary, a virgin, was with child by God's Holy Spirit."
3. **Line 7:** "And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins." Keep the spoken "alway" (KJV) but change the on-screen read-along to "I am with you always."
