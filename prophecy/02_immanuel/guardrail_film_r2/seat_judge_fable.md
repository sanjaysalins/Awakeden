## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR (one named viewer; who stops, who finishes, who shares; who is NOT served)
Named viewer: Grace, 34, Manila, churchgoer, English second language. She sings "Immanuel" every Christmas and has never heard of Ahaz.
- Stops: scrollers, on the big NO and line 1.
- Finishes: Bible readers. The SAVE ME / HE SAVES turn (line 8) is new to them.
- Shares: Grace, for line 8 and the end card.
- NOT served: the total newcomer. Nobody says who Joseph is to Mary or why he needs "fear not" (lines 5-6). Also the sceptic who knows Isaiah 7 had a meaning in Ahaz's own day.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)
"God promised to be with us, and Jesus is God with us." Line 8 carries it; line 10 seals it. Clear, helped by GOD WITH US on screen three times. But line 8 packs four ideas into under 8 seconds, so the takeaway is rushed.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (include the ON-SCREEN words and pictures; who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)
Who: a scared king, a prophet, then Mary, Joseph and a baby named Jesus. What: the king refuses God's sign; God gives one anyway; 700 years later the child is born. Why: so God would be with us and save us.

Lost or misled:
- Line 2 is five sentences in about 10 seconds. "In that same war, he begged a foreign king" has no link word, so the contrast with God's offer is easy to miss.
- 12.0-17.7 s: the pack lists no spoken line. A chest of gold goes to messengers and no voice says he paid. A newcomer sees treasure and guesses.
- Line 5, "An angel told Joseph:" — Joseph who? Then line 6, "fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife", is the hardest sentence in the film and it lands on the least explained man.
- "thou son of David" (line 6): a newcomer may think Joseph is David's own son.
- Line 11, "That was God's answer." Works only if line 1 is still remembered 60 seconds later.

Words that fail in translation: "sign" (line 2; "as proof" rescues it), "conceive" (3), "take unto thee", "thy" (6), "alway" (10; the on-screen "always" fixes it — keep that).

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
- "A frightened king told God no" (1): RIGHT in substance (Isa 7:2, 7:12). His refusal was pious-sounding: "I will not ask, neither will I tempt the LORD".
- "God answered anyway" (1): RIGHT (Isa 7:14, "the Lord himself shall give you a sign").
- "from King David's family", "fear not", "offered any sign" (2): RIGHT (Isa 7:2, 7:4, 7:11).
- "begged a foreign king: save me" (2) and the gold: RIGHT (2 Kings 16:7-8). "Temple bowls" are the artist's guess; the text says silver and gold.
- Line 3: RIGHT, KJV verbatim.
- "Seven hundred years later" (4): RIGHT as a round number; history, not Bible text.
- Lines 4, 6, 7: RIGHT (Matt 1:18, 1:20-21, verbatim).
- "Joseph obeyed", "Immanuel means: God with us" (8): RIGHT (Matt 1:24, 1:23).
- "Ahaz begged a king to save him. This child saves." (8): fair inference. The film's own pairing, not one Scripture states.
- Line 9: RIGHT (1 Cor 15:3-4).
- Line 10: RIGHT, shortened (Matt 28:20). The lake path is artistic; Matthew has a mountain.
- "That was God's answer." (11): slightly OVERCLAIMED. Isaiah 7:15-16 also gave Ahaz a sign for his own time. Matthew 1:22-23 supports the fulfilment. No fix needed in a Short.

Nothing WRONG.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE (what would the best explainer in the world do differently; the ONE highest-value change; score 0-10 against that bar)
The best explainer would say out loud that the old promise came true, and would give Joseph one clause of context. This is a prophecy film that never says "came true".

ONE change: in line 8, replace "Joseph obeyed." with "The promise came true." The picture already shows Joseph holding the baby.

Score: 7/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP (opens at line __, pays off at line __; score 0-10; worth the wait?)
Opens at line 1, part-pays at line 3, pays off at lines 8 and 10-11. Score 7. Worth the wait. Weak spot: IMMANUEL / GOD WITH US is shown at 17.7 s, so the answer is out early; "700 YEARS LATER" has to carry the rest.

## PROMISE KEPT (in your own words: who made what promise, how long before, and what came true? did the film make it feel like a promise kept? 0-10)
God, through Isaiah, promised a virgin's son called "God with us". About 700 years later Mary's son Jesus was born, died, rose and said "I am with you alway". The pieces are all there, but the film never joins them in words. I had to do it myself. 6.5/10.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES (line number + exact replacement wording)
1. Line 8: "The promise came true. Ahaz begged a king to save him. This child saves. Immanuel means: God with us."
2. Line 5: "Joseph, her promised husband, planned to leave her. An angel told him:" (Matt 1:18-19). No re-voice: add the tag JOSEPH — ENGAGED TO MARY at 24.7 s.
3. Line 2: "King Ahaz, from King David's family, was afraid of war. God said, "fear not," and offered any sign, as proof. Ahaz refused. Instead, he begged a foreign king: "save me." So God chose the sign:"

Fixes 1-3 need a re-record. That is a paid step and needs its own ask.
