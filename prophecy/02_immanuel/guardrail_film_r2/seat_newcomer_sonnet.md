## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
A commuter on a phone, say a 25-year-old in Manila or Lagos who is curious and has no Bible background. Who stops: the opening (line 1, "A frightened king told God no") and the comic pictures. Who finishes: people who like the music and art, and anyone who wants to know what "God answered" means. Who shares: probably a believer, not me. Not served: a newcomer who needs the names explained. Ahaz, David, Isaiah, Matthew and "his people" arrive with no explanation.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"A king refused God's help, so God promised a child called 'God with us', and that child is Jesus, who is with us always." Line 11, "That was God's answer", plus line 8 ("This child saves. Immanuel means: God with us") carry it. It is mostly clear, but I had to guess how a baby answers a war.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: a king (Ahaz), a prophet (Isaiah, I guess a man who speaks for God), a girl Mary, a man Joseph, an angel, a man called Jesus.
What: the king was afraid, paid another king to help him, and God said a baby would come. Years later a baby did come.
Why: to show that "God is with us".
Where I got lost or misled:
- Line 2: "from King David's family". I do not know David. It is dropped in without a reason, though it matters later.
- Line 2: "offered any sign, as proof". I did not know what a "sign" is. "God chose the sign:" then cuts to a different picture, so I waited for it.
- Line 3: "Behold, a virgin shall conceive". The words "behold", "conceive" and "virgin" are hard in a second language, and the line goes by fast.
- Line 4: "Seven hundred years later". I did not know the first scene was that old. Good, but there was no hint the prophet's baby would be Jesus until line 7.
- Line 6: "thou son of David, fear not, take unto thee Mary thy wife". "Thou", "thy" and "take unto thee" are not how anyone talks. I guessed "marry her".
- Line 7: "save his people from their sins". I do not know what "sins" or "his people" mean here. Saved from the war, or from something else?
- Line 9: "He died for our sins". Who is "He"? I guessed Jesus. Why did he die? Why is a cross there? A big jump.
- Line 10: "alway". Looks like a typo to me. The on-screen text says "always", so the voice and text differ.
- "Save me" (SAVE ME card) and "He saves" (line 8) look like the same idea, but I could not tell if they are linked on purpose.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. What I was TOLD that I could not follow: the "sign" in line 2; why David matters (lines 2 and 6); how a baby seven centuries later answers a king's fear (line 8); why sins and death at the cross (line 9) belong in the same story; what "the third day" means for a newcomer (line 9).

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would give the newcomer a simple promise to hold: "God promised: a baby will come who is God living with us." Then it would show the baby arriving and keep one tight link between "refused God" and "God came anyway". The ONE highest-value change: spend about 5 seconds between lines 2 and 3 saying plainly what the sign was ("God said: I will send a child") and what "sign" means. Score: 6/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1 ("God answered anyway") and again at line 2 ("God chose the sign:"). Pays off at line 3 and fully at line 11. Score 7/10. Worth the wait, but the payoff is a name, "Immanuel", and I only understood its meaning at line 8.

## PROMISE KEPT
The prophet (line 3) said a baby would come named "God with us". About 700 years later (line 4) a baby named Jesus came, and in the end Jesus says he is with us always (line 10). I felt it was a kept promise, but only by line 8. Before that I thought the prophet was talking about a baby in the king's time. Score 6/10.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 2: replace "offered any sign, as proof" with "offered a sign, a clear proof from God".
2. Line 6/7: replace "thou son of David ... take unto thee Mary thy wife" with "Joseph, do not be afraid to marry Mary" in the voice, keeping the KJV on screen.
3. Line 9: replace "where He died for our sins" with "where He died, taking the blame for what we did wrong".
