## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** Grace, 34. She goes to church, knows "Immanuel" from Christmas cards, and has never heard of Ahaz.
- **Who stops:** people who scroll past Christmas content but stop on "A frightened king told God no" (line 1).
- **Who finishes:** Bible readers who want to see how the prophecy connects.
- **Who shares:** small-group leaders in Advent season.
- **Not served:** total newcomers. Line 2 packs six facts into about 10 seconds: Ahaz, David's line, the war, "fear not", the sign offered, and the foreign king. Skeptics who know the debate over Isaiah 7:16 are not served either.

**Church group?** Yes, after the fixes below.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"A scared king trusted a foreign king. God gave a child who really saves: Jesus, God with us." Line 8 carries it. It is clear, but the contrast is half-built. The film never says the foreign king failed Ahaz.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
**Who / what / why, in my words:** a scared king refused God's sign and paid a foreign king for help. God promised a son called "God with us." Centuries later an angel names Mary's son Jesus, "he saves." He dies, rises, and promises to stay.

**Where I got lost or misled:**
- Line 2: "God chose the sign:" — the colon carries the whole turn, which is hard to hear and hard to dub.
- Line 11: "That was God's answer." Answer to what? You have to remember line 1 from about 60 seconds earlier.
- The chest "lifted by its poles" (12.0 s) looks like the Ark of the Covenant. A Bible reader will be confused. 2 Kings 16:8 says only "silver and gold."

**Words that fail in translation:** "thou," "alway" (the screen says "always," which looks like a typo to some viewers), "Immanuel," and "Holy Ghost / Holy Spirit."

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **L1** "told God no. God answered anyway": **RIGHT** (Isa 7:10-12, 14 "the Lord himself shall give you a sign").
- **L2** "from King David's family": **RIGHT** (Isa 7:2, 13 "house of David"; Matt 1:9).
- **L2** "afraid of war": **RIGHT** (7:2 "his heart was moved").
- **L2** "God said, 'fear not'": **RIGHT** (7:4).
- **L2** "offered any sign, as proof": **RIGHT**, with "as proof" a fair gloss (7:11 "in the depth, or in the height").
- **L2** "Ahaz refused": **RIGHT** (7:12).
- **L2** "In that same war, he begged a foreign king: 'save me'": **RIGHT** (2 Kgs 16:5-7). "In that same war" is carefully worded, since Scripture does not give the order of these events.
- **L3** Isaiah 7:14: **RIGHT**, word for word. The picture of Isaiah "alone on a hill" is not in the text. In 7:3 he speaks to Ahaz at the conduit, and the bottom panel at 0.0 s gets that right, boy included.
- **L4** "Seven hundred years later": **RIGHT** as an approximation (outside the KJV, historically fair).
- **L4** "Mary, a virgin, was pregnant by God's Holy Spirit": **RIGHT** (Matt 1:18; Luke 1:27, 35).
- **L5-L7**: **RIGHT** (Matt 1:20-21). Line 7 quietly drops "And she shall bring forth a son." That is acceptable because the on-screen tag reads 1:20-21.
- **L8** "Joseph obeyed": **RIGHT** (1:24). "Ahaz begged a king": **RIGHT** (2 Kgs 16:7). "This child saves": **RIGHT** (1:21). "Immanuel means: God with us": **RIGHT** (1:23).
- **Not stated anywhere spoken:** that this is a fulfilled prophecy. The film never voices Matthew 1:22, "that it might be fulfilled." The Matthew-writing panel only hints at it.
- **L9** "died for our sins… third day… rose": **RIGHT** (1 Cor 15:3-4). "With us at the cross" is a theological framing, not a quote, and it is sound.
- **L10** "I am with you alway": **RIGHT** (Matt 28:20). The lakeside picture is **UNSUPPORTED**: 28:16 puts this on "a mountain" in Galilee.
- **L11**: **RIGHT** but vague.

Nothing in the spoken lines is wrong.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would close the loop on Ahaz. Scripture says the Assyrian king "distressed him, but strengthened him not… helped him not" (2 Chr 28:20-21). That one fact turns line 8 from a comparison into a verdict.

**Highest-value change:** add that fact to line 8.

**Score: 7/10.**

## CURIOSITY GAP
It opens at line 1 ("God answered anyway": how?). It pays off partly at line 3 and fully at line 8. **Score 7/10.** Worth the wait, but line 11 echoes line 1 too faintly.

## PROMISE KEPT
God, through Isaiah, told the house of David that a virgin would bear a son named "God with us." About 700 years later Mary bore Jesus, and Matthew says this was that sign. The film feels like a promise kept emotionally, but it never says the word "fulfilled" aloud. **Score 7/10.**

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **Line 8** → "Joseph obeyed. Ahaz paid a king to save him. That king did not help him. This child saves. Immanuel means: God with us." (2 Chr 28:21)
2. **Line 4** → "Seven hundred years later, it came true. Mary, a virgin, was pregnant by God's Holy Spirit." (Matt 1:22)
3. **Line 11** → "The sign Ahaz refused. God gave it anyway." Also remove the carrying poles from the 12.0 s chest so it doesn't read as the Ark.
