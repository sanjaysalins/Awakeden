### 1. THE KING WHO LOOKED AWAY

- **THE IDEA:** A huge, frightened king turns his face away from the one warm light in the picture, and that light is a small gold man walking straight toward him.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:**
  - Top third: flat night-blue sky, empty, clean. This is the word space.
  - Middle: King Ahaz from the chest up, very big, on the left two-thirds. Dark beard, simple gold crown, deep red robe. His head is turned to the left, eyes wide and wet, looking out into the dark. One hand grips the stone wall. He looks like a scared man, not a villain.
  - Behind his right shoulder, lower right: the night breaks open into a small patch of gold dawn, a grassy path. On it the risen Jesus walks toward us, smiling, cream robe, the flame-like gold glow all around his whole body (like a candle flame, taller than he is, no rays, nothing behind his head). He is about one fifth of the poster's height, but he is the brightest thing in it.
  - His gold light falls on the back of the king's crown and shoulder as a thin warm rim. The king's face stays cold blue. The light is touching him and he has not turned.
  - Bottom: the wall top in ink black, thick comic ink lines, a black gutter border.
  - Palette: 80% night blue and ink black, one deep red robe, one gold flame. Painted comic texture, heavy brush, hard ink outlines.
- **LETTERING:** "A KING TOLD GOD" in one line across the top sky, cream, about 9% of the height. "NO" directly under it, huge, deep red with a black ink outline, about 20% of the height, sitting just above the crown.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** "Who is the glowing man behind him, and why won't the king turn round?" The film answers it at "God chose the sign" and pays it off at "That was God's answer."
- **PHONE TEST:** A big blue face turned left, a red "NO", one gold flame-shape lower right. Three shapes. All read at 2 cm.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 film key art, bold painted comic-book style, thick black ink outlines, heavy brush texture, black ink gutter border. Night. A Middle Eastern king, dark beard, simple gold crown, deep red robe, shown large from the chest up on the left side, gripping the top of a stone city wall, head turned away to the left, eyes wide with fear, human and sympathetic, face lit cold night blue. Behind his right shoulder, lower right, the darkness opens into a small patch of warm gold dawn with a grassy path: the risen Jesus walks toward the viewer, smiling, long brown hair, cream robe, a tall flame-like gold glow wrapped around his whole body, no rays, no halo disc behind his head. His gold light makes a thin warm rim on the back of the king's crown and shoulder. Top third of the image is plain empty night-blue sky. Palette: night blue, ink black, deep red, gold, cream. Use film picture 1 for the king and wall, film picture 8 for Jesus and his glow. No lettering, no text.

### 2. THE ANSWER IN THE DARK

- **THE IDEA:** The night sky over the king's city is torn open in one tall flame shape, and inside the flame is Jesus walking toward us with his arm around a friend.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:**
  - The whole poster is night blue and ink black, except one giant upright gold flame shape in the centre, running from near the top down to the city wall. It is the same flame-like glow from the film, made poster-size. Its edges are ragged like torn paper or a brush stroke, with a black ink line.
  - Inside the flame: warm daylight, lake and grass, and the risen Jesus, full figure, walking toward us, smiling, cream robe, his arm around a young man's shoulders. Jesus is about one third of the poster's height. The glow is the flame shape itself, so there are no rays and no halo.
  - Bottom quarter: the Jerusalem wall as a flat black ink silhouette. On it, small and alone, King Ahaz in his red robe and crown, seen from the side, head bowed, one hand over his eyes, the other on the stone. He stands just to the left of the flame's base. The gold light reaches his feet. He is not looking.
  - Far left on the dark hills: two tiny orange campfires. That is all the war we show.
  - Palette: flat night blue field, ink black wall, one red dot of a king, one large gold flame. Painted comic texture, halftone dots fading at the flame's edge.
- **LETTERING:** "A KING TOLD GOD" across the top in cream, above the flame's tip, about 8% of the height. "NO" at the bottom across the black wall, right of the king, deep red letters with a cream outline, about 18% of the height. The eye reads top line, falls down the flame, lands on "NO" next to the small king.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** "If the king said no, why is the sky opening anyway?" The film answers in its first ten seconds ("God answered anyway") and pays it off at the walk by the lake: "I am with you alway."
- **PHONE TEST:** One gold flame on dark blue with a figure in it, a black bar at the bottom, a red "NO". It reads as a single shape even at 1 cm.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 film key art, bold painted comic-book style, thick black ink outlines, flat graphic shapes, halftone texture. A flat night-blue sky fills the frame. In the centre one giant upright flame-shaped opening of warm gold light with ragged brush-stroke edges, reaching from near the top down to a city wall. Inside the flame shape: a sunlit grassy path by a lake and the risen Jesus, full figure, walking toward the viewer, smiling, long brown hair, cream robe, his arm around a young man's shoulders; no rays, no halo disc behind his head, the flame shape is his glow. Bottom quarter: an ancient stone city wall as a flat black silhouette. Standing on it, small, left of the flame's base, a king in a deep red robe and simple gold crown, side view, head bowed, one hand covering his eyes, afraid, human, gold light touching his feet. Two tiny orange campfires on dark hills at far left. Empty dark sky at the very top, empty black wall at lower right. Palette: night blue, ink black, deep red, gold, cream. Use film picture 8 for Jesus and the glow, film picture 1 for the king, wall and campfires. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1: THE ANSWER IN THE DARK

It is one shape, a gold flame on blue, so it wins the 2 cm test and makes Jesus the hero of the poster, not a detail.
The small bowed king beside a huge red "NO" shows fear and grace in one look, with no scene the film does not have.
