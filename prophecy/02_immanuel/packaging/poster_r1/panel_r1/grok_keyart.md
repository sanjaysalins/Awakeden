### THE DULL GOLD
- THE IDEA in one sentence: The king pushes a chest of temple gold at two messengers, and that gold looks dull beside the risen Jesus walking toward us.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Vertical 9:16, one painted comic panel with a heavy black ink border. Top fifth is empty night blue. Below it, Jesus is the largest figure, upper right, walking toward us on a short strip of grass: cream robe, long brown hair, smile, flame-like gold glow on his body and hair, light on the grass at his feet, one arm around a young man, one friend beside him. Lower left, smaller, a lamplit stone room: Ahaz in a deep red robe and a simple gold crown, a real frightened man, both hands pushing an open chest of gold and silver bowls toward two kneeling messengers in dusty cloaks. Thick ink gutter between the room and the night. Palette: night blue, ink black, deep red, dull metal gold, living flame gold, cream. No rays and no disc behind his head.
- LETTERING: The empty night band at the top holds "A KING TOLD GOD / NO" in two stacked lines, each about two-thirds of the width. Largest type on the poster. Nothing behind it.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: Why is this smiling man worth more than the chest a king is giving away? The film answers: Ahaz refused God's sign and, in that same war, paid a foreign king to save him. God chose the sign anyway. This child saves. Immanuel means God with us.
- PHONE TEST: A red king shoving a gold box, and a bigger cream figure burning gold. The box looks cheap. The face of Jesus still reads as a person.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink contours, one panel. Empty night-blue sky in the top fifth. Largest figure, upper right: the risen Jesus walking toward us, long brown hair, cream robe, gentle smile, flame-like gold glow clinging to his body and hair, warm light on the grass at his feet, his arm around a young man, one friend beside them. Lower left, smaller: King Ahaz, dark beard, simple gold crown, deep red robe, afraid but dignified, pushing an open wooden chest of gold and silver temple bowls toward two kneeling messengers in dusty cloaks, lamplit stone room. Heavy black ink gutter between the room and the night. Night blue, ink black, deep red, cream. His gold is alive. The bowls are dull metal. Reference film pictures 2 and 8. No sunburst, no halo disc, no rays from the head. No lettering, no text.

### THE THIRD LIGHT
- THE IDEA in one sentence: Two enemy campfires sit on the hills, and the third light is Jesus himself, closer than the war.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Full-bleed night. Top fifth is clean dark sky. Mid-left, Ahaz stands on Jerusalem's stone wall, deep red robe, simple gold crown, both hands locked on the stone, eyes on the hills, tired and afraid, never a villain. Two small campfires glow on the dark hills. In front of the wall, lower center, the grassy path and a sliver of lake. Jesus walks toward us, large, cream robe, smile, flame-like gold glow around his body, arm around a young man, friends at his side. He is in front of the war, not above it. His glow lights the path and the near edge of the wall. The campfires stay small and far. Painted comic, heavy ink, flat bold shapes. Same palette. No rays and no disc behind his head.
- LETTERING: Same top band, two lines, "A KING TOLD GOD / NO", wide and high, clear of his hair and clear of the fires.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: The king is staring at the fires. Who is the man walking in front of them, and why is he calm? The film answers: God said "fear not" and offered any sign. Ahaz refused, and begged another king to save him. The sign was a child. He died for our sins, rose, and said, "I am with you alway."
- PHONE TEST: Red figure on a wall, two orange dots, one big gold man in front. Three lights. His is the closest.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink, flat shapes, night blue and ink black. Empty dark sky in the top fifth. Mid-left: King Ahaz, dark beard, simple gold crown, deep red robe, gripping Jerusalem's stone wall, afraid, human, looking at two small enemy campfires on the dark hills. Foreground, lower center, closer to camera: the risen Jesus walking toward us on a grassy path by a lake, long brown hair, cream robe, smile, flame-like gold glow around his body and hair, light falling on the path, his arm around a young man, friends beside them. He stands in front of the wall. The campfires stay small and distant. His glow is the warmest light. Reference film pictures 1 and 8. No sunburst, no halo disc, no rays from the head. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1
THE DULL GOLD. At phone size the chest and the man are one comparison, and the words "A KING TOLD GOD / NO" make a stranger ask what God sent instead. The film pays that off: he begged a king, and this child saves.
