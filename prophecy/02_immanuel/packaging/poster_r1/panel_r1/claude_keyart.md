### THE BACK TURNED

- **THE IDEA:** The frightened king turns his face away from God's offer, and the answer he refused is already walking toward us over his shoulder.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:**
  - **Top third:** clean night-blue sky with faint ink-hatched clouds. This is where the words go.
  - **Middle:** King Ahaz, big, from the chest up, in the left half. He has a dark beard, a simple gold crown and a deep red robe. His head is turned away and his eyes are down. One hand is raised palm-out, a frightened "no", not an angry one. His brow is wet, his mouth tight, and his shoulders hunch. He reads as a scared man, not a villain. Two small enemy campfires glow orange on the dark hills behind him.
  - **Lower right, deep in the frame, past his shoulder:** a warm gold path. The risen Jesus walks down it toward us, smiling, in a cream robe, with his arm around a young man. He is about one fifth of the frame's height. The flame-like gold glow wraps his whole body, with no rays behind his head. The gold spills up the path and lights the edge of Ahaz's raised hand, but Ahaz does not see it.
  - **Palette:** night blue, ink black and deep red on the king. The warm gold lives only around Jesus.
  - **Texture:** bold black ink outlines, painted comic shading, halftone grain in the shadows.
- **LETTERING:** "A KING TOLD GOD" goes across the top in cream comic letters, about 12% of the height. "NO" goes huge in yellow just below it, about 22% of the height, sitting over the night sky and above his raised palm. The palm "pushes" the word.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** What did he say no to, and who is that glowing man behind him? The film answers it: God offers a sign, Ahaz refuses, God gives Immanuel anyway, and the closing walk shows that sign is Jesus, "God with us".
- **PHONE TEST:** At 2 cm wide you still see a red figure with a palm up, a gold glow low on the right and a big yellow NO. Three shapes, and the contrast is clear.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink outlines, halftone shadows. In the foreground left, King Ahaz from the chest up: dark beard, simple gold crown, deep red robe. He turns his face away with his eyes down, frightened and sweating, one hand raised palm-out in a fearful refusal. Human and vulnerable, not evil. The gold light faintly catches the edge of the king's raised hand. The top third is empty night-blue sky. Palette: night blue, ink black, deep red, warm gold, cream. Refs: film pictures 1, 8, 4. No lettering, no text.

### THE TWO GOLDS

- **THE IDEA:** One page, two comic panels: the king's gold going out to buy a saviour, and God's gold walking in.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:**
  - **Top of the page:** a clean cream-and-ink band for the words.
  - **Top panel (about 40%):** a lamplit stone room in deep red and brown. Ahaz's hands and his anxious face push an open chest of gold and silver temple bowls toward two kneeling messengers in dusty cloaks. The gold here is cold, metal and lamplit.
  - **A thick black ink gutter.**
  - **Bottom panel (about 45%, larger):** the risen Jesus walks toward us down a grassy path by a lake, smiling, with his arm around a young man and friends beside them. He is about half the panel's height, with the flame-like gold glow around his body and no head-rays. This gold is warm and alive.
  - The chest's gold glints and the glow's gold echo each other across the gutter.
- **LETTERING:** "A KING TOLD GOD" goes in a yellow comic caption box at the top, about 10% of the height. "NO" goes in giant yellow letters, about 18% of the height, breaking out over the gutter like a sound effect.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Why is he paying away a fortune, and what does that have to do with the man below? The film answers it: Ahaz begs a foreign king "save me", but this child "shall save his people", and he is Immanuel.
- **PHONE TEST:** At 2 cm wide you see a dark red panel above a gold glowing figure, split by a black bar with a yellow NO on it. The two-panel shape reads at once.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book page, two stacked panels with a thick black ink gutter, and a plain cream band across the top. Top panel: a lamplit stone room. King Ahaz (dark beard, simple gold crown, deep red robe) looks anxious and afraid as he pushes an open wooden chest of gold and silver temple bowls toward two kneeling messengers in dusty cloaks, in deep red and brown tones. Bottom panel, larger: the risen Jesus walks toward the viewer down a grassy lakeside path, smiling: long brown hair, cream robe, his arm around a young man, friends beside them, a soft flame-like warm gold glow around his whole body, no rays or sunburst behind his head. Painted comic shading, halftone grain. Refs: film pictures 2, 8. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1 and why
My pick is **THE BACK TURNED**. It has one gesture, one glow and one huge NO. The "no" and the answer it refused sit in the same frame.
It reads at thumbnail size, keeps Ahaz human, and shows only what the film shows.
