- THE IDEA in one sentence: A sharp diagonal comic-panel gutter slices the frame in half, contrasting the cold, fearful night of the human king with the warm, golden daylight of the risen King who answered anyway.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Top-left triangle: Night blue and ink black. King Ahaz (dark beard, deep red robe, simple gold crown) grips a stone wall in profile, looking down in fear at the tiny glowing campfires of his enemies. A heavy black ink line slices diagonally across the middle. Bottom-right triangle: A grassy path in daylight. The risen Jesus (cream robe) walks toward the viewer with his arm around a young man. A flame-like gold glow envelops Jesus's body, spilling warmth into the scene, completely asymmetrical to avoid halo cliches.

### LOOKING THE WRONG WAY
- THE IDEA in one sentence: The king shoves his worldly wealth into the dark foreground to buy a rescue, completely missing the glowing figure of Jesus walking into the frame right behind his back.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Extreme depth of field. Foreground (bottom half): A lamplit stone room with heavy ink shadows. Ahaz's face is seen over his shoulder, anxious and human, pushing a massive wooden chest of gold bowls toward the bottom edge of the frame. Background (top half): The stone wall behind Ahaz dissolves into a bright, painted grassy path. The risen Jesus walks down the path toward Ahaz's back, radiating a flame-like gold glow that catches the edge of Ahaz's red robe. Jesus is smiling, bringing the real rescue, but Ahaz is looking the other way.
- LETTERING: Stacked cleanly in the dark upper-left corner of the stone room, balancing the glowing figure of Jesus on the right: "A KING TOLD GOD / NO".
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: The king is paying off someone with a chest of gold, but a glowing savior is walking right behind him—who is he paying, and does he ever turn around? The film answers it by showing Ahaz's tragic plea to a foreign king compared to God's ultimate, free rescue.
