Checks
- One Jesus: Q1-Q5 yes. Q6 fail risk (left man looks like Jesus).
- Faces and hands: no broken hands seen. Q5 king's face is half hidden by the word NO. Q6 Jesus' pose is twisted.
- Garbled drawn-in text: none in any.
- Words at phone size: readable on all six (same lettering). Cleanest behind the words: Q2, Q4.

## MY PICK: Q5

Why: it is the only one where the risen Jesus with his flame glow is big enough to see on a phone AND the word NO clearly belongs to the king. Dark king above, bright Jesus below = the question "what happened after the no?" The title does not answer that.

Second: Q4 (best looking, but king too small and NO floats over Jesus).

## One fix (Q5)
Move the king down and left a little so his whole face sits clear of the word NO, and change his look from a hard glare to fear. He was afraid, not evil. Keep NO close to him.

## Biggest risk
NO near Jesus. In every poster the word sits in the top middle, above or close to Jesus. A fast viewer may read it as "no" to Jesus, or Jesus saying no. Q4 and Q6 are worst for this (NO is right on top of his flame). Q2 has a second form of it: the king seems to fear Jesus. Q5 is safest only if the king's face stays visible next to the word.

Honest odds: this is one judge looking at stills, not real viewers. I would put Q5 ahead of Q2 for taps at about 55/45, plus or minus 15. Q2 could win on raw thumb-stop; it loses on honesty and on the owner's ask for a clear risen Jesus.
