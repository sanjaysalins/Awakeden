Checks: every poster has exactly one Jesus. No garbled drawn-in text. The title words are readable at phone size on all six. Hands look right on all six, including Q5's two king hands on the wall. Q6 has the most extra figures.

## MY PICK: Q5

**One fix:** nudge "NO" up and right, or make it slightly smaller, so the king's eyes show below it. Also soften his glare toward fear or worry rather than menace. The film's Ahaz is afraid, not defiant, and right now he can read as a villain.

**Biggest risk:** "NO" read as a refusal of Jesus. Q5 is the safest of the six because the diagonal keeps the king and Jesus in separate worlds. The runner-up, Q2, has the best face but fails this test, because the king looks back at an approaching Jesus.
