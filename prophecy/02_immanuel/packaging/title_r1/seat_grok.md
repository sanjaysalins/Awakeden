1. **TITLE:** Why Wasn't Jesus Named Immanuel?
**THUMB WORDS:** A KING SAID | NO
**THUMB PICTURE:** King Ahaz in a gold crown and red robe grips Jerusalem's wall at night, enemy campfires on the hills, and a giant comic NO slams in.
**PAYOFF:** It asks why the promised name and the baby's name differ. The film answers: Immanuel means God with us, the angel names him Jesus because he shall save, and Jesus says, "I am with you alway."

2. **TITLE:** What Does Immanuel Mean?
**THUMB WORDS:** WITH US | WHERE?
**THUMB PICTURE:** The risen Jesus, soft gold glow, walks toward us smiling with his friends, storm crosses faint behind him and the empty tomb glowing to one side.
**PAYOFF:** It asks what the name means and where it shows up. The film says God with us, then the cross, the third-day rising, and "I am with you alway."

3. **TITLE:** Why Is Jesus Called Emmanuel?
**THUMB WORDS:** A SIGN | HE REFUSED
**THUMB PICTURE:** Ahaz turns away on the night wall while Isaiah stands on a dawn hill, hand raised, and the comic word IMMANUEL begins to form.
**PAYOFF:** It asks how a refused sign became a name for Jesus. God gives the sign anyway, Mary is with child by the Holy Spirit, and the end card reads GOD WITH US.

4. **TITLE:** Jesus' Birth Promise Came True Anyway
**THUMB WORDS:** HE REFUSED | THE SIGN
**THUMB PICTURE:** Ahaz pushes an open chest of gold and silver temple bowls to two kneeling messengers as a comic NO hits.
**PAYOFF:** It asks how a promise survives a no. Ahaz refuses God's sign and begs a foreign king, "save me," and 700 years later the sign comes true in Mary and Jesus.

5. **TITLE:** King Ahaz Begged, "Save Me"
**THUMB WORDS:** GOD'S SIGN | STILL CAME
**THUMB PICTURE:** Close on the open chest of temple treasure and the two messengers, Ahaz frightened behind it in red and gold, with no extra lettering.
**PAYOFF:** It asks what God did after that plea. God still chooses the sign, a son named Immanuel, and the film calls that God's answer.

6. **TITLE:** Immanuel: Two Men Heard "Fear Not"
**THUMB WORDS:** ONE SAID | NO
**THUMB PICTURE:** A split panel: Ahaz afraid on the night wall, and a tall wingless angel of soft white light over sleeping Joseph.
**PAYOFF:** It asks what the two men did with the same words. Ahaz refuses and begs a king to save him. Joseph obeys, and the child saves.

**BEST:** Pair 1. It is the real search, in the same short "Why..." shape as episode 1, and it does not give away the answer. The film pays it off in order: the sign-name means God with us, the baby's name means he shall save, and he is still with us.

**RISK:** Viewers who have never heard the name Immanuel may not feel the puzzle, and the king on the thumb can look like a second story.
