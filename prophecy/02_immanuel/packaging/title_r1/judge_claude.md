## My pick: **P2**

It joins the real search ("why is Jesus called Emmanuel") with a human-stakes picture. The film answers that question with no bait.

**One small fix:** drop the full stop on the thumbnail. "A KING TOLD GOD / NO" reads cleaner at phone size.

**Biggest risk:** the thumbnail shows a frightened king, but the title is about Jesus. If the first 2 seconds don't link the king's refusal to the promised child, viewers will feel the switch and swipe away. Also, most people search "Emmanuel", not "Immanuel". Put "Emmanuel" in the description and tags.
