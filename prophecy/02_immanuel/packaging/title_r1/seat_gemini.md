### 1. Six Pairs

**TITLE:** Why wasn't Jesus named Immanuel?
**THUMB WORDS:** THE 700-YEAR MYSTERY
**THUMB PICTURE:** Angel of soft white light over sleeping Joseph.
**QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Asks why the famous prophecy name doesn't match the baby's actual name; paid off when the film explains Immanuel is His identity ("God with us") and Jesus is His mission ("He saves").

**TITLE:** The king who said no to God
**THUMB WORDS:** IMMANUEL: GOD WITH US
**THUMB PICTURE:** King Ahaz in a red crown and robe gripping Jerusalem's wall at night, terrified of the campfires.
**QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Asks who would refuse God and what happened next; paid off by Ahaz rejecting the sign out of fear, but God giving the Immanuel prophecy anyway.

**TITLE:** What does Immanuel mean?
**THUMB WORDS:** THE SIGN HE REFUSED
**THUMB PICTURE:** Ahaz pushing an open chest of gold and silver temple bowls to kneeling messengers.
**QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Asks the definition of the famous Christmas word; paid off at the end when "God with us" connects all the way to the cross and the resurrection.

**TITLE:** The Jesus birth prophecy
**THUMB WORDS:** A COWARD | A SAVIOR
**THUMB PICTURE:** Joseph holding the newborn baby in the dark.
**QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Asks how the ancient prophecy connects to the birth; paid off by the contrast of Ahaz begging a king to save him vs. this child who actually saves us.

**TITLE:** Why is Jesus called Emmanuel?
**THUMB WORDS:** FEAR NOT
**THUMB PICTURE:** Joseph looking troubled at night, before falling asleep.
**QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Asks the reason behind the title; paid off by showing how God being "with us" answers human fear, from Ahaz's terror to Joseph's obedience.

**TITLE:** Prophecies fulfilled by Jesus
**THUMB WORDS:** THE PROMISE KEPT
**THUMB PICTURE:** The prophet Isaiah on a hill at dawn, hand raised.
**QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Asks which specific promise this is; paid off by tracing the 700-year line from Isaiah's spoken words to Joseph naming the child Jesus.

### 2. Best Pair
**Why wasn't Jesus named Immanuel?** / **THE 700-YEAR MYSTERY**
It directly targets a highly searched, genuine point of confusion for everyday people, creating an immediate curiosity gap. It also perfectly matches the simple, question-based format of Episode 1 ("Why Was Jesus Born in Bethlehem?").

### 3. Biggest Risk
Viewers clicking for a quick linguistic dictionary definition might drop off during the historical setup about King Ahaz before the payoff arrives.
