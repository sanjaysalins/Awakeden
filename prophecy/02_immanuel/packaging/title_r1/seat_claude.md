# Title + thumbnail: 6 pairs, written blind

## 1. Six pairs

**Pair 1**
- **TITLE:** What Does Immanuel Mean? (24 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** HE TOLD GOD | NO.
- **THUMB PICTURE:** King Ahaz in gold crown and red robe grips the wall at night. He turns away from a small warm glow of a baby in the dark sky.
- **Opens / pays off:** Who said no to God, and what does that have to do with a name? It pays off when Ahaz refuses the sign, God gives it anyway, and the film says "Immanuel means: God with us."

**Pair 2**
- **TITLE:** Why Wasn't Jesus Named Immanuel? (32 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** TWO NAMES | ONE CHILD
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Joseph holds the newborn. Two comic word-slams sit above them: IMMANUEL on the left, JESUS on the right.
- **Opens / pays off:** Why two names? It pays off when Isaiah says "Immanuel," the angel says "call his name JESUS: for he shall save," and the film ends on "God with us."

**Pair 3**
- **TITLE:** The King Who Told God No (24 chars, no search phrase)
- **THUMB WORDS:** "SAVE ME" | WRONG KING
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Ahaz pushes an open chest of gold temple bowls to two kneeling messengers. The comic slam SAVE ME is behind him.
- **Opens / pays off:** Who did he beg, and who really saves? It pays off on "Ahaz begged a king to save him. This child saves."

**Pair 4**
- **TITLE:** Isaiah's Prophecy About Jesus' Birth (36 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** 700 YEARS | EARLY
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Isaiah on a hill at dawn with his hand raised. Far off in the same frame, a small glowing manger.
- **Opens / pays off:** What was said 700 years ahead, and did it happen? It pays off on "Seven hundred years later, it came true."

**Pair 5**
- **TITLE:** Why Is Jesus Called Immanuel? (29 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** GOD OFFERED | PROOF
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Ahaz with his hand up, refusing. Isaiah faces him. A gap of light sits between them.
- **Opens / pays off:** What proof, and did he take it? It pays off when Ahaz refuses, and God chooses the sign himself: a son called Immanuel.

**Pair 6**
- **TITLE:** The Sign a Scared King Refused (30 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** GOD SENT IT | ANYWAY
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Ahaz is small and afraid on the wall at the bottom. A tall white angel of light (no wings) stands over sleeping Joseph at the top.
- **Opens / pays off:** What did God send after being refused? It pays off with the virgin's son, the angel's "fear not," and "That was God's answer."

## 2. Best pair

**Pair 1: "What Does Immanuel Mean?" + "HE TOLD GOD | NO."**
- The title catches the top real search. The thumb adds a human story the title can't, so neither one repeats the other.
- It's honest end to end. The film opens on the king's "no" and closes on "That was God's answer: God with us."

## 3. Biggest risk

On its own, the title reads like a dry dictionary video. If the thumb's "NO." and Ahaz's fear aren't big and clear on a small phone screen, the curiosity gap is lost.
