## YOUR PICK: P2

- "Why Is Jesus Called Immanuel?" is a question the film really answers.
- Every word is simple. "Immanuel" is the one strange word, and it is the mystery itself.
- The thumbnail adds a second hook the title does not give away: a king told God no. Picture A (fear, crown, fires) reads fast.
- It matches episode 1's shape ("Why Was Jesus Born in Bethlehem?"), so the series looks like a series.

Runner-up: P5, if the channel wants story over search.

## The one biggest risk

Title and thumbnail tell two different stories (a name question vs a king saying no), and Jesus is not in the picture. A fast scroller may not link them. The first 3 seconds of the film must tie them together at once, and the opening line ("A frightened king told God no. God answered anyway.") does only half of that: it pays the thumbnail, not the title. If retention dips early, this is the likely cause.

Honesty flag for the room: P1 and P4 ask "why WASN'T Jesus named Immanuel?" The film shows both names but never answers that "why". That is bait unless one spoken line is added.
