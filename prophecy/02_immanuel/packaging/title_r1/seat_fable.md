# Seat: Fable — title + thumbnail, round 1

## 1. Six pairs

### Pair 1
- TITLE: `Why Is Jesus Called Immanuel?` (29)
- THUMB WORDS: `A KING TOLD GOD | NO.`
- THUMB PICTURE: King Ahaz (gold crown, red robe) gripping the wall at night, afraid, enemy fires on the hills; "NO." huge in comic slam type.
- Question: what does a king saying no to God have to do with a name of Jesus? Paid off: Ahaz refuses the sign, God gives it anyway ("call his name Immanuel"), then "Immanuel means: God with us".

### Pair 2
- TITLE: `What Does Immanuel Mean?` (24)
- THUMB WORDS: `HE BEGGED: | "SAVE ME"`
- THUMB PICTURE: Ahaz pushing the open chest of gold temple bowls toward two kneeling messengers; small glowing manger in the far corner.
- Question: who begged, and who did he beg? Paid off: Ahaz begs a foreign king "save me"; later "This child saves. Immanuel means: God with us."

### Pair 3
- TITLE: `Why Wasn't Jesus Named Immanuel?` (32)
- THUMB WORDS: `TWO NAMES. | ONE CHILD.`
- THUMB PICTURE: Joseph holding the newborn in warm light, the tall white angel behind; the words IMMANUEL and JESUS as two comic name tags.
- Question: the promise said Immanuel, the angel said Jesus, so which is it? Paid off: angel says "call his name JESUS: for he shall save"; narrator says "Immanuel means: God with us" (one is his name, one is who he is).

### Pair 4
- TITLE: `The King Who Told God No` (24)
- THUMB WORDS: `GOD ANSWERED | 700 YEARS LATER`
- THUMB PICTURE: Split poster: left, afraid Ahaz on the dark wall; right, the angel of white light over sleeping Joseph. A torn comic gutter between them.
- Question: how did God answer, and why so late? Paid off: "Seven hundred years later, it came true" through to "That was God's answer."

### Pair 5
- TITLE: `Jesus Birth Prophecy: The Sign a King Refused` (45)
- THUMB WORDS: `"FEAR NOT" | ONE OBEYED`
- THUMB PICTURE: Two faces, same pose, looking up: Ahaz in red and crown (cold blue night), Joseph in plain clothes (warm gold light).
- Question: two men heard the same words, what did each one do? Paid off: Ahaz hears "fear not" and refuses; Joseph hears "fear not" and "Joseph obeyed."

### Pair 6
- TITLE: `Immanuel: God's Answer to a Scared King` (39)
- THUMB WORDS: `HE WANTED | NO SIGN`
- THUMB PICTURE: Isaiah on the hill at dawn, hand raised, with small Ahaz turning his back; a soft glow of a mother and child in the dawn sky.
- Question: why would a king refuse a sign from God, and what sign came anyway? Paid off: "Ahaz refused... God chose the sign: Behold, a virgin shall conceive..."

## 2. Best pair

**Pair 1: `Why Is Jesus Called Immanuel?` + `A KING TOLD GOD | NO.`**

- The title is a real search and matches Episode 1's "Why Was Jesus...?" shape, so the series reads as a set.
- The thumbnail is the film's own first line and first slam word; a gentle name question next to a king defying God makes a gap, and the film closes it fully.

## 3. Biggest risk

Many people search "Emmanuel", not "Immanuel", and the title and thumbnail may look like two unrelated topics to a fast scroller (put "Emmanuel / God with us" in the description and tags; test Pair 2 as the backup).
