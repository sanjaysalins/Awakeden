1. ONE-LINE INSIGHT: The Immanuel sign was given at the very spot where, years later, Assyria's envoy mocked God.
2. VERSES: Isaiah 7:3 "at the end of the conduit of the upper pool in the highway of the fuller's field"; Isaiah 36:2 "And he stood by the conduit of the upper pool in the highway of the fuller's field." (also 2 Kings 18:17). Isaiah 7:9 "If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established." Isaiah 37:6 "Be not afraid of the words that thou hast heard."
3. WHY IT MATTERS: Ahaz, offered a sign, refused to trust God there. On the same spot his son Hezekiah's city is mocked, and Hezekiah trusts God and is saved. Same place, two kings, two answers to "will you trust Me?"
4. LANDS ON JESUS: The sign Ahaz refused is the child born of a virgin, Matthew 1:23 "they shall call his name Emmanuel, which being interpreted is, God with us."
5. HOW KNOWN: 2. Known to Isaiah commentators; almost never preached at Christmas.
6. RISK: The "same spot" link is plain from the text; any claim that Isaiah meant it as a deliberate lesson is inference.
7. HOOK LINE: "Same spot. Two kings. Only one trusted God."

1. ONE-LINE INSIGHT: Isaiah's own children were living signs, and the New Testament puts Isaiah's words in Jesus' mouth.
2. VERSES: Isaiah 8:18 "Behold, I and the children whom the LORD hath given me are for signs and for wonders in Israel"; Hebrews 2:13 "Behold I and the children which God hath given me."
3. WHY IT MATTERS: In Isaiah 7-8 children's names are messages (Shear-jashub, Maher-shalal-hash-baz, Immanuel). Hebrews takes Isaiah's line about his sign-children and gives it to Jesus, speaking of us as His children.
4. LANDS ON JESUS: Hebrews 2:13-14, Jesus shares flesh and blood with the children God gave Him.
5. HOW KNOWN: 2-3. Known to Hebrews scholars; rarely linked to the Christmas Immanuel story.
6. RISK: Abstract; harder to picture than a place.
7. HOOK LINE: "Isaiah's kids were walking messages. One line became Jesus' own."
