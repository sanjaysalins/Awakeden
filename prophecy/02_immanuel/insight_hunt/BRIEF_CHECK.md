Then:
- FATAL FLAGS: list any insight with a real factual or doctrinal error, and what it is.
- TOP 3, best first, with one plain sentence each on why.
- COMBINE?: can two insights join into one 60-second spine without overload? Name the best pair, or say "no".
- HOOK: best first line (under 12 words) for your number 1.

Output a table (insight x 6 scores + total) then the lists. Plain words. Text only.
