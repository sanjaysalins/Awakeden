## Fatal flags

None is fatal. Four need care:

- **I10:** "Checking the water for a siege" is commentary, not text. It also treats Shiloah and Siloam as the same water as a fact. That is likely, but it is not in the verse. The Jesus link (John 7:37) is only a theme. Don't use it as the spine.
- **I2:** "Jesus' last words are 'I am with you alway'" goes too far. Acts 1 has later words. Say "the last words in Matthew."
- **I4 and I8:** The Jesus link is weak or only a theme. I4's "Hezekiah trusted" is a fair reading, but it is still a reading.
- **I5:** "Wrong father" is a reading of Ahaz's own words "thy son." It is fair, but say it as Ahaz's words, not as a fact about him.

## Top 3

1. **I5:** A real king, a real choice and real temple gold: he begged "save me" and got no help, then God's Son is named JESUS, "he shall save," with a direct link to Matthew 1:21.
2. **I1:** One son of David refused God's sign and another obeyed, Ahaz is in Jesus' own family list, and the angel's "fear not" echoes Isaiah's.
3. **I7:** It is the simplest for a newcomer: ask for any sign, from the deep or the sky, and God gives a baby, twice.

## Combine?

**Yes: I5 + I7.**

One spine: God says "ask any sign" → Ahaz refuses → he sends God's gold to Assyria, "save me" → it does not help → God gives the sign anyway: a baby, named JESUS, "he shall save."

It is one king and one choice, with two contrasts. Keep I1's Matthew 1:9 family-list line as an optional one-line add only if time allows.

## Hook (for I5)

"A scared king sent God's gold to buy a saviour."
