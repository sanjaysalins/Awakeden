Immanuel: a story nobody tells

5 blind seats hunted for new finds, then 4 blind judges scored them. All verses were checked word for word against the KJV. $0.

The winner (3 of 4 judges): two sons of David

1. King Ahaz (a son of David) is terrified. God says "fear not" and offers any sign.
He says "I will not ask."

2. Instead he sends God's temple gold to the king of Assyria:
"I am thy servant and thy son: come up, and save me." (2 Kings 16:7)
It does not help him.

3. God gives the sign anyway: a son.

4. 700 years later, an angel speaks to another son of David:
"Joseph, thou son of David, fear not." Joseph obeys.

5. The son's name: "JESUS: for he shall save his people."

Hidden bonus: Ahaz is named in Jesus' own family list (Matthew 1:9).

Cautions the judges gave
