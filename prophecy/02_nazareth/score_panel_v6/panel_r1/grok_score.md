1. SCORE IDEA

A four-note Welcome phrase on solo cello stops one note short of home through Nazareth, then resolves to a warm open chord only when His hand reaches you.

Solo cello, a small string section, concert harp (single notes), and a few soft felt-piano notes. D major, about 62 bpm, slower at the cross. The phrase is D, F-sharp, A, G-sharp. G-sharp is the hanging note. The home note A is withheld until the last line.

2. WHY IT FITS

- An ink page is one black line plus a gold wash. The cello is the line, the harp the ink dot, the strings the gold.
- The story is a sentence stopped in the middle. The tune is the same shape: four notes, then a missing fifth. That fifth returns as welcome.
- The bed is slow, simple, and thin under speech, so any dubbed voice can lead. Page slams stay in sound design.
