## 1. SCORE IDEA

**A musical sentence leaves its last note unspoken; Jesus’ final invitation lets it come home.**

Warm muted strings, solo viola, soft felt piano, and one mellow horn reserved for the ending. No percussion. The motif is three quiet notes—D, F-sharp, E—with the expected final D withheld. Its unanswered ending feels spacious, not troubled. A sustained open fifth keeps the welcome alive beneath it.
