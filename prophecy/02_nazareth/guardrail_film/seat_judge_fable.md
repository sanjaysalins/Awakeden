## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Named viewer: Ana, 27, Manila, grew up near church, English is her second language, scrolling at night.
- Stops: almost anyone. Line 1 ("stopped reading in the middle of a sentence") is a real hook with a picture that matches.
- Finishes: Bible readers and the curious. The cliff (line 9) and the cross (lines 10-12) keep things moving.
- Shares: Bible readers, for the line 8 / line 11 echo.
- NOT served: the total newcomer. Nobody tells him who Jesus claims to be in line 6, what line 8 means, or why a man who died in line 12 is standing alive in the last picture.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus stopped before the judgment line, so God's welcome is still open to me today." Line 13 carries it, set up by line 2 ("a time of God's welcome") and line 5. Clear for anyone who followed lines 3-5. Less clear is how the cross (line 12) connects to it; the film shows the cross but never says why His staying keeps the welcome open.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Jesus, in His home town. What: He reads an old promise, stops before the judgment part, says it is about today, the town turns on Him, later He dies and does not save Himself. Why: so the welcome stays open.

Where I got lost:
- Line 6, "fulfilled in your ears": the central claim ("I am that someone") is in the hardest English of the film, and nothing glosses it.
- Line 8, "Physician, heal thyself": two archaic words, no meaning given. The whole payoff at line 11 depends on hearing this echo. An ESL viewer will miss it.
- Line 9: three events in one line (foreigners, cliff, walks through). "Not to them" is unclear; who is "them"? The reason for the fury is thin. The line also runs ahead of its pictures (cliff shown at 35.4 s).
- Line 4-5, "vengeance": load-bearing and hard. It is never said in a plain word.
- Line 12 to line 13 picture: He dies, then reaches out a hand at sunset. No resurrection is spoken. A newcomer reads this as a flashback or a mistake.

The on-screen "HE STOPPED READING" and the ISAIAH 61:2 tag help. The still frame on line 6 is right.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- Line 1 "stopped reading in the middle of a sentence": RIGHT (Isa 61:2 runs on after a comma; Luke 4:19-20).
- Line 2 "a time of God's welcome": RIGHT as paraphrase of "acceptable year of the Lord".
- Line 4: RIGHT, verbatim.
- Line 5 "left the day of vengeance unread, and sat down": RIGHT on fact. The motive is inferred; the film wisely does not state one.
- Line 6: RIGHT, verbatim Luke 4:21.
- Lines 7-8: RIGHT (Luke 4:23, "Ye will surely say"). Skips verse 22, where they first spoke well of Him; fair compression.
- Line 9 "sent help to foreigners, not to them": RIGHT but loose; the text says not to Israel's many widows and lepers. Cliff and "walked right through them": RIGHT (4:28-30). Picture: jar "full of flour" is OVERCLAIMED; 1 Kings 17:16 says it did not run out. "Stunned" crowd is UNSUPPORTED, minor.
- Lines 10-11: RIGHT, Luke 23:35.
- Line 12 "He stayed, and gave His life": RIGHT (23:46).
- Line 13 "still open, for you, today": application, SUPPORTED by 2 Cor 6:2, not by Luke 4 itself. Good that "still" implies it will close; the vengeance is postponed, not cancelled.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would run one thread, not two. Here the unread line (1-5, 13) and the "save yourself" echo (8, 11, 12) sit side by side and are never joined. ONE change: make line 12 join them and say He rose, paying for it by trimming line 9. Score: 7/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1, half pays at line 5, pays off at line 13. Score 8/10. Worth the wait; the first-line / last-line mirror is the best thing in the film.

## NEW INSIGHT
The stop mid-sentence is known to many church people. The fresher find is that "Physician, heal thyself" (line 8) comes back as "let him save himself" (line 11), and that He walked away from the cliff but stayed on the cross. True to the KJV. 7/10, but only for viewers who understand line 8.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
- Line 5: "He left God's day of judgment unread, and sat down."
- Line 7: "He knew His town would say, prove it:"
- Line 12: "This time, He stayed. He gave His life, and rose again."
