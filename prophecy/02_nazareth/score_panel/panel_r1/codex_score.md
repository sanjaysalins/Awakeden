## 1. SCORE IDEA

The music begins a sentence, stops before its final note when the scroll closes, and finds its completion in Jesus’ open-handed welcome.

**Instruments:** intimate grand piano, broad viola and cello strings, soft bass clarinet; high strings enter only near the end. No percussion or vocals.

**Motif:** three spacious piano notes—A, F-sharp, E—leave an expected D unplayed. That final note arrives after the last spoken “today.” Warm D-major harmony; slow, breathing movement.

## 2. WHY IT FITS a bold inked graphic-comic look AND this story

- Clear piano notes and substantial string chords have the simplicity and weight of inked shapes. Sustained sound connects the panels; sound design supplies their impacts.
- The interrupted musical sentence makes the scroll’s closure audible without making vengeance frightening.
- Complete musical silence gives Jesus’ declaration its own space. The final resolution belongs to the welcome offered to the viewer.
