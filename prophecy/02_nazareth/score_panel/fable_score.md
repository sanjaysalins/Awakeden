# Fable seat - score plan: "He stopped mid-sentence"

## 1. SCORE IDEA
A solo cello "reads" a warm four-note rising phrase that always stops on its held top note; the dark second half is heard once and never taken up, and at the end the very note where it stopped becomes home.

- **Instruments:** solo cello (the reader's voice), harp (the scroll), warm string orchestra, one soft clarinet, two French horns only in the last 10 s. No timpani, no choir, no percussion.
- **Key / tempo:** D major, slow 60 BPM (one beat = one second, easy to cut).
- **Motif:** four notes climbing step by step, last note held long. Early on the chord under that last note is open and waiting. In the final hold the same note gets a full, warm D major chord under it. The melody never changes. Only the ground under it does.

## 2. WHY IT FITS
- **Bold ink = bold lines.** One clear cello line over plain, wide chords is the musical version of clean black ink on gold. No busy texture to fight the slams.
- **The stop is the story.** Film 1 finished an unfinished tune. This film says the stopped phrase was already complete. Different idea, same family.
- **Grace, not fear.** The vengeance half is a low, grave shadow in the cellos, heard once and let go. It is named, not acted out.

## 4. SPLIT
**29.3 s.** It sits in the 1 s pause before Jesus speaks. Both sides are one thin held note, so the join hides inside the stillness. Part 1 = 0-29.3 s. Part 2 = 29.3-59.2 s. Generate each about 3 s long, cross-fade 0.4 s.

## 5. MUSIC PROMPTS

**Part 1 (ask for 32 s)**
> Cinematic orchestral film score, instrumental only, D major, slow 60 BPM, reverent and warm, continuous sound with no gaps or silence. Opens at once with a solo cello playing a simple four-note rising phrase that stops on a long held note, one soft harp pluck. Around 3 seconds in, low strings enter softly with gentle steady harp notes. Around 9 seconds in, the string orchestra warms and widens, a soft clarinet joins the cello melody, hopeful and tender. Around 17 seconds in, the cello phrase begins again and stops on its held note, harp stops. Around 19 seconds in, only low cellos and double basses, a slow grave minor shadow, very quiet, solemn not scary. Around 24 seconds in, the shadow lifts into one warm major chord. Around 27 seconds in, thins to a single high held violin note that keeps sounding to the end. No drums, no percussion, no choir, no vocals, no brass, no ethnic scales.

**Part 2 (ask for 33 s)**
> Cinematic orchestral film score, instrumental only, D major, slow 60 BPM, reverent and warm, continuous sound with no gaps or silence. Opens with one soft held string note, completely still, for five seconds. Around 5 seconds in, strings bloom gently, harp returns, light pizzicato, quiet wonder. Around 10 seconds in, thins to harp and low held strings with a small questioning clarinet. Around 13 seconds in, solo cello plays a simple four-note rising phrase over rising strings. Around 17 seconds in, a steady noble build, two French horns enter warm and low. Around 23 seconds in, full string orchestra, the phrase climbs to a long held high note. Around 27 seconds in, that note rests on a full warm D major chord, held, ringing, then a slow soft fade. Grace and welcome, not triumph. No drums, no timpani, no choir, no vocals, no trailer hits, no ethnic scales.

## 6. MIX NOTES
- Music about 16 dB under the narrator. Under the three KJV lines, 3 dB lower again.
- Jesus' line (29.8-34.2): music down to a bare thread, about 30 dB under. Not zero, so no dead air.
- Lifts: small at 14.5, real lift from 46.8, full at 52.3 but still under the voice.
- At 56.1 the voice ends: raise music 6 dB over 0.5 s. Ring to 58.4, fade to 59.2. Audio runs the full 59.2 s.

## 7. RISKS
1. **Dead air or a bad join.** Sweep loudness in 0.5 s windows over the whole mix; no window may drop to silence. Listen to 28-31 s on loop for a key or tempo jump.
2. **Vengeance turns into horror, or the tool adds drums or choir.** Listen to 18-24 s alone, cold. Any rumble, stab or voice = regenerate part 1.
3. **Music covers the words, or the ending fails to feel like home.** Measure voice-minus-music level on each KJV line (want 15 dB or more). Play 52-59 s to a fresh ear: does the last chord feel finished?
