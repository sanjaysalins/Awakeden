## My pick: **P1**

**One word fix:** change the thumb text to **"THE LINE / HE DID NOT READ"**. "Unread" is a harder word for second-language viewers. "Did not read" is easy.

**Biggest risk:** P1's title has none of the search words ("Isaiah", "Nazareth", "Luke 4"). It will rely on the feed, not on search. Put "Jesus reads Isaiah 61 in Nazareth (Luke 4:16-30)" in the first line of the description. Also, some viewers will expect to be told *why* He stopped. That is fine as long as no text promises a "why", so keep the first spoken line exactly as the title.
