## MY PICK: P1

"Jesus Stopped Reading Mid-Sentence" + picture A, "THE LINE / HE LEFT UNREAD".

Why:
- One clear question a newcomer can hold: what line, and why would He stop?
- The Bible reader also has a reason to tap: most people who know Luke 4 have not noticed where the reading ends.
- The film pays it off exactly: it shows the unread line and makes no claim about why.
- Title and thumb are one unit: the title says He stopped, the thumb shows the shadowed line.

Runner-up: P5 (simpler words and has "Isaiah" for search, but "THE WARNING" is a softer, less honest thumb).

## One small word fix (max 1)

None needed on the thumb. If one change is allowed on the title: "Mid-Sentence" -> "Midway" is worse, so keep it. Leave P1 as written.

## The one biggest risk

The title has no search words (no "Isaiah", "Nazareth" or "Luke 4"), so it depends on the feed, not on search. Put "Jesus reads Isaiah 61 in Nazareth (Luke 4)" in the first line of the description to cover that. Second, smaller risk: the title invites "why did He stop?", and the film does not answer it, so the description must not promise a reason.
