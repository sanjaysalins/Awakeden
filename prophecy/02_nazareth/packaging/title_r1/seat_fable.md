# Seat: Fable (blind) — title + thumbnail, Nazareth short

## 1. Six pairs

### Pair A
- TITLE: `Jesus Stopped Reading in the Middle of a Sentence` (49)
- THUMB WORDS: `THE LINE | HE LEFT UNREAD`
- THUMB PICTURE: Close-up of hands holding the open scroll, one line of writing in shadow, Jesus' lowered eyes at the top edge (use the clean art, not the panel with HE STOPPED READING lettered on it).
- Opens: which line, and what was in it? Paid off at the prophet's voice ("and the day of vengeance of our God") and again at the last line ("He stopped reading at the welcome").

### Pair B
- TITLE: `Jesus Reads Isaiah in Nazareth, Then Stops` (42)
- THUMB WORDS: `HALF A SENTENCE | THEN HE SAT DOWN`
- THUMB PICTURE: Jesus seated, scroll on his knee, one open hand, the row of staring men behind him.
- Opens: what was the other half, and why is the room staring? Paid off at the unread line, then "This day is this scripture fulfilled."

### Pair C
- TITLE: `Why Jesus' Home Town Tried to Kill Him` (38)
- THUMB WORDS: `HE READ | ONE PROMISE`
- THUMB PICTURE: Furious crowd gripping Jesus, his face calm in the centre, small scroll in his hand.
- Opens: how can reading a promise make a town this angry? Paid off at "It was about Him", the wish for miracles, the reminder about foreigners, and the cliff.

### Pair D
- TITLE: `The Dare Jesus Heard Twice` (26)
- THUMB WORDS: `ONCE HE LEFT | ONCE HE STAYED`
- THUMB PICTURE: Split poster: left, Jesus walking calmly through the crowd at sunset; right, the cross under the storm sky.
- Opens: what dare, and why two different answers? Paid off at "Physician, heal thyself", then "let him save himself", then "This time, He stayed."

### Pair E
- TITLE: `Jesus Rejected in His Hometown: The Unread Line` (47)
- THUMB WORDS: `HE STOPPED | RIGHT HERE`
- THUMB PICTURE: Hands on the scroll with one finger resting on a line, warm light on the words above it, dark below.
- Opens: where is "here", and what comes after it? Paid off at the prophet's line and the closing "He stopped reading at the welcome."

### Pair F
- TITLE: `What Jesus Did Not Read in Nazareth` (35)
- THUMB WORDS: `STILL OPEN | TODAY`
- THUMB PICTURE: Jesus at golden sunrise above the town, hand held out open, palm up, walking toward us.
- Opens: what did He skip, and what is still open? Paid off at the unread line, then the final line: "It is still open, for you, today."

## 2. Best pair: A

- The film's own first line is the strongest hook, so the title is that line in plain words. A stranger needs no Bible knowledge to feel the gap.
- The thumbnail adds a new fact ("a line was left unread") instead of repeating the title, and the film answers it in the first 15 seconds and again at the end. It claims no reason for why He stopped.

## 3. Biggest risk with the best pair

The title holds no searched phrase (no "Nazareth", "Isaiah", "hometown"), so search reach is weaker; if search matters more than the scroll-stop, use Pair B's title with Pair A's thumbnail.
