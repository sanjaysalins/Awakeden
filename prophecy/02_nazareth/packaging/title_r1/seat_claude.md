# Blind writer: titles + thumbnails, Nazareth (Luke 4)

## 1. Six pairs

**Pair 1**
- **TITLE:** Jesus Stopped Reading Mid-Sentence (Luke 4)
- **THUMB WORDS:** THE LINE | HE LEFT UNREAD
- **THUMB PICTURE:** A tight shot of the hands on the scroll. The last line sinks into shadow, with Jesus' lowered eyes at the top edge.
- **Opens / pays off:** *Which line, and what happened next?* It is paid off at 0:05–0:12 (the prophet reads "day of vengeance") and again at the close ("He stopped reading at the welcome").

**Pair 2**
- **TITLE:** Jesus Reads Isaiah 61 and Stops
- **THUMB WORDS:** WHAT DID HE | NOT READ?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus close-up, eyes lowered, reading. A big empty speech bubble.
- **Opens / pays off:** *What is in the part He didn't read?* The prophet voice reads it straight away. The welcome line closes it.

**Pair 3**
- **TITLE:** Jesus Rejected in His Hometown
- **THUMB WORDS:** THEY DARED HIM | TWICE
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Split frame. On the left, the furious crowd gripping Jesus. On the right, the cross under a stormy dusk sky.
- **Opens / pays off:** *What was the dare, and what did He do the second time?* "Physician, heal thyself" is the first dare. "Save himself" at the cross is the second, and "This time, He stayed" answers it.

**Pair 4**
- **TITLE:** His Town Tried to Kill Him. He Walked Away.
- **THUMB WORDS:** BUT AT THE CROSS | HE STAYED
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus walking calmly through the crowd, the hill town at sunset behind Him.
- **Opens / pays off:** *Why walk away once and stay the next time?* The cliff shows the first. The cross shows the second ("This time, He stayed").

**Pair 5**
- **TITLE:** The Half-Read Promise (Luke 4:18-19)
- **THUMB WORDS:** STILL OPEN | FOR YOU?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus at golden sunrise above the town, open palm held out toward us.
- **Opens / pays off:** *What promise, and why is it still open?* The last line answers it: "It is still open, for you, today."

**Pair 6**
- **TITLE:** Jesus in Nazareth: The Unfinished Sentence
- **THUMB WORDS:** HE SAT DOWN | TOO SOON?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus seated, one open hand, scroll on his knee, a row of staring men behind.
- **Opens / pays off:** *Why sit down before the end?* The film shows the unread line. It does not claim a reason. It lands on "stopped at the welcome".

## 2. Best pair: Pair 1
"Jesus Stopped Reading Mid-Sentence (Luke 4)" + "THE LINE | HEADING LEFT UNREAD" (THE LINE | HE LEFT UNREAD)
- The title is the film's own first line, so the hook pays off within 5 seconds. "Luke 4" catches the real searches.
- The thumb names a missing line without saying what it is. It uses "left unread", which is the honest word, not "denied" or "cancelled".

## 3. Biggest risk
Viewers may expect a stated *reason* He stopped. The film only shows *where* He stopped (at the welcome), so some may feel the "why" is not fully answered.
