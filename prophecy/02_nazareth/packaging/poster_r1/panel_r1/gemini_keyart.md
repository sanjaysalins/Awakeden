### THE SHADOWED SCROLL
- THE IDEA in one sentence: The physical scroll abruptly drops into pitch-black shadow right where the next sentence would be, creating a literal void that forces the viewer's eye up to His face.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Extreme close-up of Jesus in olive-green and cream linen. His long dark-brown hair frames a kind, steady gaze looking directly up at the viewer, not down at the parchment. His hands hold a wooden-roller scroll that fills the lower foreground. The top of the parchment glows in warm honey-gold light, but halfway down, the light cuts off sharply, plunging the unread portion of the scroll into deep, unlit brown-black shadow.
- LETTERING SPACE: The expansive, clean black shadow over the bottom half of the scroll. It provides perfect high-contrast negative space for 2-6 words of bold, bright comic-book lettering.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: Why did the light stop? What is He refusing to read? The film answers this in the opening hook the moment He rolls it up and sits down.
- PHONE TEST: A warm, beautifully lit face hovering over a stark, golden geometric shape (the scroll) that cuts aggressively into pure darkness. 
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16, bold painted comic-book key-art style with heavy black ink shading. Extreme close-up of Jesus (long dark-brown hair, beard, kind steady eyes, olive-green and cream linen robes) looking warmly and directly at the viewer. His hands hold an open wooden-roller scroll in the lower foreground. The top half of the scroll is bathed in warm gold light, but the bottom half of the parchment plunges completely into deep, unlit brown-black shadow. Warm honey stone wall in the background. Use film pictures 1, 3, and 5 as references. No lettering, no text.

### THE UNTOUCHED PATH
- THE IDEA in one sentence: A claustrophobic, heavily inked sea of chaotic townsfolk is sliced perfectly down the middle by a clean beam of golden light, which Jesus walks out of to offer us His hand.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Framing the left and right edges are dense, heavy-inked silhouettes of first-century townspeople in rough wool and linen—not evil villains, just a chaotic, pressing mob in deep shadow. Dead center is a glowing vertical column of warm sunrise light. Stepping out of that light into the extreme lower-foreground is Jesus, totally calm, His cream and olive robes pristine. One hand is extended forward, palm-up, reaching right out of the frame toward the viewer.
- LETTERING SPACE: The clean, glowing golden vertical column of light in the top third of the poster, framing the space just above His head.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: They have Him totally surrounded and furious—how is He walking right through them, and why is He reaching out to me? The film answers this at the cliff miracle and the final sunrise welcome.
- PHONE TEST: A bright, glowing vertical wedge slicing through a dark textured mass, anchoring on a single open hand reaching out of the screen.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16, bold painted comic-book key-art style with heavy black ink shading. Symmetrical composition: the left and right edges are filled with dense, heavily shadowed figures of a first-century crowd in wool and clay tones pressing inward. Down the center is a clear, empty, glowing path of warm honey-gold sunrise light. Jesus (long dark-brown hair, beard, olive-green and cream robes) walks calmly down the center path into the extreme foreground, completely untouched by the crowd, with one hand extended forward, open palm-up toward the viewer. Use film pictures 8, 9, and 11 as references. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1
THE SHADOWED SCROLL. It perfectly executes the film's core premise ("He stopped reading") as a pure, unforgettable graphic trick, and Jesus making direct eye contact turns a historical scene into an immediate, personal invitation.
