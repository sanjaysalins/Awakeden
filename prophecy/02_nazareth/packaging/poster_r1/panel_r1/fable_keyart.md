### THE UNLIT LINE

- **THE IDEA:** One hard edge of light cuts across an open scroll: everything above it glows gold, the last strip stays in shadow, and Jesus' thumb rests exactly on the edge while His eyes have already left the page and look at us.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:**
  - Top third: Jesus, head and shoulders, big. Long dark-brown hair, beard, olive-green and cream robes. Kind steady eyes lifted straight to the viewer. Honey stone wall behind, plain, slightly dark. Light comes from a high window at the upper left as a side shaft, never from behind His head.
  - Middle: His two hands hold a wooden-roller scroll, tilted toward us, filling the width. The parchment is the brightest thing in the picture, warm cream-gold. The writing is only soft rows of loose brush dashes, not letters.
  - The edge: a crisp diagonal shadow line crosses the scroll low down. Below it the parchment drops to deep brown; the dash rows fade to nothing. His thumb sits on the line.
  - Bottom quarter: the dark lower scroll and deep brown shadow, calm and empty.
  - Texture: bold painted comic panel, thick black ink outlines, flat confident colour shapes, light paper grain. One thin black ink frame.
- **LETTERING SPACE:** The dark bottom quarter. Big cream or yellow comic letters, 2 lines, about 80% of the width. (The type is the title, never Isaiah's unread words.)
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** "Why did He stop there, and what is in the dark part?" The film names the unread half-sentence at about one third in, then pays it off at the end: He stopped at the welcome, and it is still open.
- **PHONE TEST:** Three shapes survive: a face looking at you, a bright scroll, a dark slice cut off the bottom of it. The thumb on the edge reads as "stopped here."
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink outlines, flat painted colour, light paper grain, thin black ink frame. Top third: head and shoulders of a first-century Galilean man, the same man as the references, long dark-brown hair, short beard, olive-green robe over cream linen tunic, kind steady eyes looking directly at the viewer, calm face, plain honey-gold stone wall behind him, light entering as a side shaft from a high window at the upper left, no glow or rays behind his head. Middle: his two hands hold an open parchment scroll on dark wooden rollers, tilted toward the viewer, filling the frame width, the parchment glowing warm cream-gold, its surface covered only with soft abstract rows of loose brush dashes that are not characters. A single crisp diagonal shadow edge crosses the lower part of the scroll; below that edge the parchment is deep brown shadow and the dash rows fade away to nothing; his thumb rests exactly on the shadow edge. Bottom quarter of the image: empty, quiet, deep brown shadow. Palette warm gold, honey stone, olive green, cream, deep brown. Stone, linen, wool only, no buttons. Use film pictures 1, 3 and 5 as references. no lettering, no text

### THE GAP

- **THE IDEA:** A wall of dark figures fills the frame, split by one tall bright slit of sunset gold, and Jesus walks calmly out of that slit toward us.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:**
  - Top quarter: open sunset sky, flat warm gold fading to honey, a small hill town on the far ridge. Quiet.
  - Middle and bottom: the crowd seen from behind and in three-quarter back view, as big deep-brown shapes of wool cloaks and headscarves. No sneers; a few raised hands hang open and empty, grabbing nothing. They lean inward from both sides like two cliffs.
  - Centre: a narrow vertical gap of gold light between them. Jesus is in it, full figure, about one third of the poster's height, walking toward us, face fully lit and calm, olive-green and cream robes untouched, one hand loose at His side. The light is behind the whole gap, wide and flat, not a ring at His head.
  - Bottom edge: rocky ground and a hint of the drop, small stones at the lip.
  - Texture: bold comic ink, heavy black shapes against one bright shape.
- **LETTERING SPACE:** The gold sky band at the top. Dark brown or black comic letters, 1 to 2 lines, about 85% of the width.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** "His own town tried to throw Him off a cliff. How did He just walk through?" The film answers at the cliff scene, then turns it: at the cross the same dare came, and that time He stayed.
- **PHONE TEST:** A dark mass, one bright vertical stripe, one small calm figure in it. Reads like a door opening.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink outlines, heavy flat shadow shapes, light paper grain. A rocky hilltop at sunset in first-century Galilee. Both sides of the frame are filled by a dense crowd of townsmen seen from behind and in back three-quarter view, large deep-brown shapes in wool cloaks and linen headscarves, faces mostly hidden, ordinary people, some hands raised open and empty. Between them, one narrow vertical gap of warm sunset gold runs down the centre. In the gap a first-century Galilean man, the same man as the references, long dark-brown hair, short beard, olive-green robe over cream linen tunic, kind steady eyes, walks calmly toward the viewer, full figure, about one third of the image height, face clearly lit, nobody touching him. Wide flat gold sky, no rays or glow behind his head. Top quarter: empty calm gold sky with a small stone hill town on a far ridge. Bottom: rough rock and loose stones at a cliff lip. Palette warm gold, honey stone, olive green, cream, deep brown shadow. No buttons, no European clothes, no blood. Use film pictures 9, 8 and 11 as references. no lettering, no text

### MY #1 and why

**THE UNLIT LINE.** It is the film's first sentence as one picture, so title and poster make a single question, and His eyes on the viewer already hint the welcome is "for you."
