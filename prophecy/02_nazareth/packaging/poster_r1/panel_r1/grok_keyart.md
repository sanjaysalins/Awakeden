### THE UNLIT LINE
- THE IDEA: One tall synagogue scroll is sliced by a hard horizontal shadow, so the promise is lit and the rest of the sentence is simply dark.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Vertical 9:16, bold painted comic key art, thick black ink edges, honey stone, cream, olive, deep brown. A wooden-roller scroll fills the frame. Oil-lamp gold lights the upper parchment. A hard shadow cuts straight across. Below that cut the parchment is bare and unlit, with no marks. Jesus is large in the lit half: long dark-brown hair, beard, olive-green and cream linen, kind steady eyes just above the shadow, one hand stopping the roller. Warm gold stone wall behind. No rays behind his head.
- LETTERING SPACE: A quiet cream band under the lower roller, full width, about one sixth of the height. Room for 2–6 heavy words.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: Why does the light stop in the middle of the page? The film answers when he sits, leaves the rest unread, and says this day is fulfilled. The sunrise ending says the welcome is still open.
- PHONE TEST: At 2 cm you still see a gold block, a black cut, a face, and a hand on a roller.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink contours, flat honey-gold, cream, olive-green, and deep brown blocks, parchment and rough stone texture. A tall wooden-roller scroll fills the frame in a first-century Galilean stone room. Warm oil-lamp light hits only the upper parchment. A hard horizontal shadow cuts the scroll. The lower parchment is bare, unlit, and unmarked. Jesus is large in the lit half, long dark-brown hair, full beard, olive-green and cream linen robes, kind steady eyes just above the shadow line, one hand holding the wooden roller. No rays behind his head. Empty cream band across the bottom sixth. Use film pictures 1, 3, and 5 as references. No lettering, no text.

### THE GOLD GAP
- THE IDEA: A solid wall of ordinary neighbours is split by one person-shaped gap of sunset gold, and Jesus walks toward us inside it, palm open.
- WHAT YOU SEE: The frame is a packed crowd in wool, linen, and headscarves, honey stone and brown, painted as tight comic panels with black gutters. Faces are intent, like people who came for a show. They stay human. A vertical gold opening runs top to bottom, the shape of a standing man. Inside it Jesus is clear and calm: same long dark-brown hair, beard, olive-green and cream robes, kind eyes, one hand open palm-up. At the foot of the gap, a small rocky hill and the home town at sunset. Dusk stays in the crowd. His robes stay warm.
- LETTERING SPACE: A quiet cream band across the bottom sixth, over the lower town, clear of faces and of his hand. Room for 2–6 heavy words.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: How does one calm man pass through a crowd that brought him to the cliff? The film shows the walk on the hill, then answers it at the cross: the same dare, and this time he stays. The last shot is that open hand at sunrise.
- PHONE TEST: At 2 cm you still see a dark crowd, a gold slit, a pale open hand, and a small robed figure.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink contours and panel gutters, honey stone, cream, olive, deep brown, sunset gold. A dense wall of ordinary first-century Galilean townspeople in linen, wool, and headscarves, neighbours leaning in, wanting a sign. A vertical person-shaped gap of sunset gold splits the crowd. Jesus walks toward us inside the gap, long dark-brown hair, beard, olive-green and cream robes, kind steady eyes, one open palm-up hand. Small rocky hill and stone home town at the bottom of the gap. No rays behind his head. Empty cream band across the bottom sixth. Use film pictures 6, 9, and 11 as references. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1
The Unlit Line. The whole film is one stopped sentence, and at phone size it is still a face, a scroll, and a hard cut of light. That cut is the question a stranger has to close.
