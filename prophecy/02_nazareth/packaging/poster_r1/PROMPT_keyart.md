EVIDENCE PACK
A 59.9-second vertical Bible short, bold comic-book look (painted panels with black ink gutters, yellow-cream comic caption pills, big comic letters), orchestral score, Episode 2 of "Promised, then kept" (Old Testament promises that came true in Jesus).
Story: "Jesus stopped reading in the middle of a sentence." In Nazareth, His home town, He read Isaiah's promise of someone sent with good news for the poor, and a time of God's welcome. Isaiah's sentence goes on: "and the day of vengeance of our God." He left that unread, and sat down. "This day is this scripture fulfilled." The town would want miracles: "Physician, heal thyself." He reminded them God once sent help to foreigners, not to their own. Furious, they took Him to a cliff to throw Him down. He walked right through them. Later, at the cross, the same dare: "He saved others; let him save himself." This time, He stayed, died, and rose again. "He stopped reading at the welcome. It is still open, for you, today."
