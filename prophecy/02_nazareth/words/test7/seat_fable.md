# Seat: Fable (blind)

**1. NEWCOMER.** WHO: yes (Jesus, in His home town). WHAT: mostly yes (He reads, stops, they try to kill Him, later He dies and rises). WHY: only half. Lines they would trip on:
- "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears." Old English word order. "It was about Him" rescues it, but only just.
- "They would want miracles, and say: [jesus] Physician, heal thyself." Confusing. The narrator says "they say", but the Jesus voice speaks it. A newcomer cannot tell who said it, or what "heal thyself" means here.
- "day of vengeance". "Vengeance" is a hard word for second-language viewers and is never explained.
- "not to their own". Unclear whose own.

**2. CLAIM / TURN / LINK.** Claim: yes, weakly ("someone sent" then "It was about Him"). Why the town turns: partly. "God helped foreigners, not you" is there, but the sting is compressed into one line. The heal thyself / save himself link: a Bible reader catches it; a newcomer probably does not, because the first line is muddled and nobody says "the same taunt". "This time, He stayed" is the best line in the film and does most of the work.

**3. BIBLE READER.** Yes, two things. He stopped mid-sentence in Isaiah 61:2, before "the day of vengeance". And the Nazareth taunt returns at the cross. Many churchgoers know the first; few have noticed the second.

**4. CURIOSITY GAP: 6/10.** Strong open. But the film never says WHY He stopped or what happened to the vengeance. The middle switches to a second idea (heal/save), then the end jumps back. The payoff is felt, not spoken, and this film will be dubbed.

**5. ACCURATE.** All quotes are KJV-true. "Middle of a sentence" is fair from Isaiah 61:2 against Luke 4:19. "Time of God's welcome" is a fair paraphrase of "acceptable year". "Rose again" is not in the pack text but is sound (Luke 24). Risks:
- The crowd never said "Physician, heal thyself"; Jesus predicted it. Keep that clear.
- Leaving vengeance unexplained may suggest God's judgment was cancelled, not delayed.
- Script uses a [leader] voice that is not in the voice list; [crowd] is listed but unused.

**6. WEAKEST LINE.** Line 12, "It was about Him. They would want miracles, and say:". Fix (shorter): "He meant Himself. He knew what they were thinking:"
Also swap the last line, same length: "He stopped before the judgment. The welcome is still open, for you, today."

**7. OVERALL: 6.5/10.** Real insight, clean text, strong ending image. The two threads are not yet tied together in spoken words.
