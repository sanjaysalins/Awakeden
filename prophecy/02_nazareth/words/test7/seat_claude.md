## Independent review: Nazareth script (test7)

**1. Newcomer:** Mostly yes. They get WHO (Jesus), WHAT (He reads, says "this is me", the town tries to kill Him) and WHY (the welcome is still open). Lines a non-native speaker would not understand:
- "Physician, heal thyself": old words, and the line comes too fast to follow.
- "fulfilled in your ears": hard to parse.
- "day of vengeance": "vengeance" is a hard word, and it carries the hook.
- "sent help to foreigners, not to their own": they won't see why this makes people *furious*. Insulted pride is never said.

**2. Claim and the link:** "It was about Him" lands the claim clearly. The anger is only half-explained. The "heal thyself" / "save himself" link is the best idea in the script, but it is **never spoken**. A newcomer will miss it. There is also a voice problem: the [jesus] voice speaks "Physician, heal thyself", but those are the crowd's words. It sounds like Jesus is saying it to Himself. (Also, the cast list says [crowd], but the script uses [leader].)

**3. Bible reader:** The mid-verse stop is fairly well known from sermons. The fresh part is the pairing of the cliff ("walked right through them") with the cross ("this time, He stayed"), plus heal-thyself matched with save-himself. That pairing is new to most churchgoers. Right now it is underplayed.

**4. Curiosity gap: 8/10.** The hook is strong, and the payoff in the last line rhymes with the first line well.

**5. Accuracy:**
- Mostly sound. "Rose again" is not in the KJV text supplied, but it is orthodox.
- Small overstatement: "not to their own". Luke says *none* of the Israelite widows or lepers, so it's close enough, but fine.
- Doctrine risk: "It is still open" could suggest vengeance was *cancelled*, not *delayed*. Acceptable, but watch it.

**6. Weakest line:** "They would want miracles, and say: [jesus] Physician, heal thyself."
**Fix (same length or shorter):**
- [narrator] "He knew their dare: 'Doctor, heal yourself. Do miracles here.'"
- Then at the cross: "At the cross, the same dare:" (this replaces "Later, at the cross, leaders mocked:").

Spoken out loud, the link now survives dubbing.

**7. Overall: 7/10.** It's tight and it lands on Jesus. Saying the "same dare" link out loud would take it to 8+.
