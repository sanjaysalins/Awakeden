## 4. Two sentences

Newcomer: He read out God's welcome, his own town refused to welcome Him and tried to kill Him, He walked away - and later, at the cross, He chose to stay and die, so the welcome He stopped at is still open to anyone today.

Bible reader: the "acceptable" year He read (Luke 4:19) and the prophet "not accepted" at home (4:24) are the same word a few minutes apart, and the Nazareth cliff is a first try at killing Him that He walked out of - so "let him save himself" at the cross is answered by a man who had already shown He could leave.
