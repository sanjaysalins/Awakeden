# Rewrite the SECOND HALF of a finished 60 s narration - "He stopped mid-sentence" (Isaiah 61 -> Luke 4). You are ONE blind writer; you have seen no other draft.

## The series
"Promised, then kept": an Old Testament promise and how it came true in Jesus. Bible-explainer shorts that must give viewers a REAL insight from across the Bible, not a story they have heard a million times. Every episode lands on Jesus. Bold graphic-comic look, vertical short. Worldwide audience, many with English as a second language, many with no Bible knowledge. The film will be DUBBED into other languages, so the meaning must be SPOKEN in plain words (not carried by on-screen text or a pun only English has).

## The new insight to carry (verified in the KJV, checked by hostile reviewers)
1. "ACCEPTED": He read "the acceptable year of the Lord" (Luke 4:19) = God's year of welcome. Minutes later He told the same room: "No prophet is accepted in his own country" (Luke 4:24). Same word in Luke's Greek. Say it in plain words so it survives dubbing (e.g. welcome / welcome), don't rely on an English pun. Don't claim the Greek is a pun Jesus made aloud; don't redefine the phrase as "the year God accepts people".
2. "HE WALKED AWAY... THEN HE STAYED": the same synagogue, filled with wrath, thrust Him out of the city to the brow of the hill to throw Him down (Luke 4:28-29). "He passing through the midst of them went his way" (4:30). Years later at the cross: "He saved others; let him save himself" (Luke 23:35). This time He stayed. Do NOT say He "could not" die at Nazareth; do not cite Hebrews; do not claim the crowd said "Physician, heal thyself" (Jesus predicted they would say it, 4:23).
You may use one, or both if it stays clear. Both together is the hoped-for version.

## Rules
- TOTAL film 60 s max incl. a 3 s silent hold at the end. The second half has ~22 s of speech (about 50-60 spoken words) if the first half is kept whole. You MAY trim or cut first-half lines to make room (say exactly which), but every kept first-half line is free and every new line costs a re-record.
- The hook ("stopped in the middle of a sentence") must still be paid off.
- Voices: [narrator], [jesus], [townsman] (or [crowd]), [isaiah]. Jesus may speak once more (e.g. Luke 4:24) if useful; KJV verbatim for any quote.
- Landing: lands on Jesus and His grace; one plain invite earned by the spine; no pressure, no fear, no gain/loss; no stock "come to Jesus" closer; not the "Not X. Y." shape.
- Plain words. Clever in STRUCTURE, never in vocabulary.
- Never invent His thoughts or the crowd's feelings beyond the text. Never claim judgement is cancelled.

## Output
1. The FULL narration (first half as kept + new second half), one line per voice turn, with [voice] tags and (pause) marks.
2. Word count and estimated seconds (narrator ~2.5 words/s).
3. Which lines are NEW (need recording) and which NEW pictures are needed (e.g. cliff edge, the cross).
4. Two sentences: why a newcomer gets it, and what a Bible reader learns that they never noticed.
