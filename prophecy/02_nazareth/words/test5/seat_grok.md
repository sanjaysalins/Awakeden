VERSION 1

1. NEWCOMER. They can get the spine: Jesus, in Nazareth, stopped a reading, people tried to throw him off a cliff, he walked out through them, and at the cross he did not leave. They will miss the point of his speech. "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears" uses "scripture," "fulfilled," and "in your ears." "Day of vengeance" is a hard pair. "This time, He stayed" never says he died, and nothing says the cross was later, so it can sound like the same day. "Prophet" is never explained.

2. BIBLE READER. Yes. Luke 4:19 stops before Isaiah 61:2, "and the day of vengeance of our God." People know the cliff. Far fewer have seen the cut in the verse. The film states that cut, then walks away from it.

3. CURIOSITY GAP. 6/10. "Jesus stopped reading in the middle of a sentence" is a strong hook. It is not a question. The answer comes quickly, then the ending does not return to the cut. It ends on an invitation. "He stayed" is too vague to pay off the cross.

4. ACCURATE. Nazareth, the reading, sitting down, all eyes on him, the fulfillment line, the hill, and passing through the crowd match Luke 4. Risks: "God's welcome" softens "the acceptable year of the Lord." "No prophet is welcomed at home" is not the text. Luke 4:24 says, "No prophet is accepted in his own country." The cliff does not follow that proverb. They first wondered at his gracious words (Luke 4:22). Wrath follows the outsider stories, the widow of Sarepta and Naaman (Luke 4:25–28). "His welcome is still open, for you, today" is a sermon add-on. Spoken this way, he sounds as if he deleted the day of vengeance. Isaiah 61:2 still says it.

5. WEAKEST LINE. "This time, He stayed." Fix: "Years later, at the cross, he did not walk away. He gave his life."

6. OVERALL. 6/10. Clearest place and setup. Death, time gap, the real proverb, and why the crowd turned are blurred.

VERSION 2

1. NEWCOMER. They can say Jesus left out a hard line, said the promise was happening, his home town tried to kill him, he walked through them, and years later he died rather than save himself. The same Jesus line still fails: "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears." Nazareth arrives after that line, so the first half has no place. "Vengeance" and "prophet" stay hard. A dub cannot lean on the pictures to supply synagogue, scroll, or Isaiah.

2. BIBLE READER. The same half-verse insight, and the last line repeats it: "He stopped reading at the welcome." That is the part a regular hearer of the cliff story has often missed. Still missing: vengeance is later, not erased, and the mob turns after grace to outsiders.

3. CURIOSITY GAP. 7/10. Same open. The last line ties the stop to the close. "Years later" and "gave His life" make the cross a real second beat. The hook asks why he stopped. The film answers, "so the welcome stays open," and never says what happened to the vengeance line.

4. ACCURATE. Stronger. Jesus speaks Luke 4:24 in KJV words. "Gave His life" matches Luke 23:46. "Years later" matches Luke's order. Same risks: "welcome" for "acceptable year"; the cliff is blamed on the proverb instead of Luke 4:25–28; [crowd] speaks a line the rulers say (Luke 23:35; the people are there and beholding, so this is mild). "It is still open, for you, today" can teach that he silenced judgment. He says the part he read is fulfilled that day. He does not say the next clause is void.

5. WEAKEST LINE. "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears." Fix: "Today this promise comes true, while you are hearing it."

6. OVERALL. 7/10. Better loop, clearer death, true proverb. The archaic line will lose many listeners. The insight is one step short of honest until vengeance is said to be later, not gone.

YOUR PICK. Version 2. One change: after "He left the day of vengeance unread, and sat down," add, "He did not cross it out. That day was God's welcome. The day of vengeance was still to come." That tells a newcomer why he stopped, and it keeps the ending from sounding like judgment was removed.

VERDICT
- Pick: Version 2
- Scores: V1 6/10, V2 7/10
- Curiosity: V1 6/10, V2 7/10
- Must-fix: say the day of vengeance is later, not cancelled
- Also fix before lock: replace "fulfilled in your ears" with plain speech
