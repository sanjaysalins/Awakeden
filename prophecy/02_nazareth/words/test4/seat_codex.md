1. **ENDING: 7/10.** It closes the film by bringing Jesus’s “This day” into the viewer’s present. The connection is earned by the earlier references to God’s welcome. But the final fragment, “This day,” repeats the ambiguity instead of clearly resolving it.

2. **NEWCOMER:** Most would understand that God’s welcome remains available. The weak phrase is **“This day.”** After hearing it quoted twice, a newcomer could still understand it as the day in Nazareth. “Today” would make the shift clear.

3. **HONESTY:** “Still open” is a fair application when read alongside 2 Corinthians 6:2: “now is the accepted time.” It is the narrator’s application, rather than something Jesus explicitly says in this scene. The passage supports an invitation today; Jesus’s stopping point alone does not establish how long that invitation lasts.

4. **LANDING:** It carries grace without pressure. It is mostly earned, though “welcome is still open” has a familiar devotional sound. The final words focus on the welcome; the held image brings the focus back to Jesus. Naming Him in the last line would let the spoken ending carry that connection too.

5. **OVERALL: 8/10.** The central action is clear and memorable. The main difficulty is the old wording, especially “fulfilled in your ears.” The narrator’s explanation helps, but newcomers must still connect several ideas quickly.

“Through Jesus, God’s welcome is still offered today.”
