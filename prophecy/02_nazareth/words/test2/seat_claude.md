**1. NEWCOMER**
Who: Jesus, in the town where he grew up. What: he reads from an old prophet's book, stops partway, sits down and says "this has come true today." The people like his words but say "he's only Joseph's son." Why: he is claiming to be the one the prophet wrote about, and he stopped before the part about vengeance.

Lines I would struggle with:
- **"This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears."** The grammar is old, and "in your ears" sounds odd. It is the key line, and a second-language viewer may only half get it.
- **"and the day of vengeance of our God."** "Vengeance" is a hard word, and the old voice makes it harder.
- **"closed the book."** The picture shows a scroll. Saying "book" while the screen shows a scroll is a small mismatch. Pick one word.
- **"He read as far as God's welcome, and said: 'This day.'"** On first watch this is a little cryptic. I had to work out that it means the welcome is happening now.

**2. HOOK → MIDDLE → LANDING**
The hook is strong and specific. The middle pays it off cleanly: we hear the unread words, then see him sit down. The townsman line moves the thread from "why did he stop?" to "who is he?" The "local boy" pivot rescues it, but the stopping question gets less weight in the landing than the hook promised. No line truly wanders off.

**3. HONESTY**
- **"He left the day of judgement unread."** That is a fact. But Isaiah says "vengeance", not "judgement", so this is a word swap. It is acceptable, but it softens the text a little.
- **"But they knew Him as a local boy."** The "But" reads v22 as dismissal. Verse 22 alone is ambiguous, though v23-29 support that reading. It is a mild over-read.
- **"That local boy was the one Isaiah promised."** The narrator states this as fact. It is a fair inference from v21, but it goes further than the quoted text.
- Good restraint: the script gives no reason for the stop and no timetable.

**4. LANDING: 7/10**
It lands on Jesus, with grace and no pressure. It is earned by the hook. It loses points for being a bit cryptic.

**5. OVERALL: 7/10**

**ONE CHANGE**
Replace the last sentence with:
"He stopped at God's welcome, and said it is here today."
