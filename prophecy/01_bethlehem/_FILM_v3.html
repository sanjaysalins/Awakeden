Born in Bethlehem: film v3 (motion + transitions)

59.3 s. Voice evened out (all words within about 2 dB). Cost: $0.

The idea: hollow steps, gold steps, one gold line

  • A gold star marks Bethlehem each time it is pointed at.
  • The experts get 7 hollow dots to the town. They never fill. The first one blinks in the silence.
  • The wise men get gold dots from the east, then they fill the 7 dots, and the last one opens like a circle onto Mary and the child.
  • Jesus gets the only solid gold line: down from the stars for "He came down from heaven". It comes back as the line under "He came to us".

11 transitions, all different

Page flip back through time (counter 100 to 700 years) · zoom into Micah's panel · ink wipe in the way Micah points · lamp flare · page flip forward · comic scroll down · page turn back to the experts · whip pan · circle from the star onto Mary and the child · nightfall from the top · dawn light out of the tomb.

Words

Words light up as they are spoken. Gold box on "knew", "Bethlehem" and the wise men's "went". Empty box on the experts' "went". End: small "PROMISED, THEN KEPT".

Calm only on the birth, Mary and the baby, the cross, the tomb and the risen Jesus.
