Born in Bethlehem: pick the words

Micah 5:2 named the town about 700 years early. Matthew 2 and Luke 2 show it came true. Under 60 seconds.

A. The experts (MY PICK)

narrator The religious experts knew the exact town where God's promised King would be born. The Bible never says they went to look.

narrator About seven hundred years earlier, Micah, a prophet, a man who spoke God's message, named one small town.

prophet But thou, Bethlehem... out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel.

narrator When Jesus was born there, visitors from the east came looking for a newborn King. So King Herod asked the experts where He would be born.

scribe In Bethlehem of Judaea.

narrator They knew the town. (pause) And Jesus was already there.

narrator Micah also said this ruler is "from everlasting." He did not begin in Bethlehem. He came as a baby, died for our sins, and rose from the dead on the third day.

~130 words, about 55 s

B. The tax order

narrator A tax order sent a pregnant woman to a small town far from home. Why that town?

narrator Mary and Joseph lived in Nazareth. The Roman emperor ordered everyone taxed in their own city. Joseph was from King David's family, so he went to David's town, Bethlehem.

narrator About seven hundred years before, Micah, a prophet, a man who spoke God's message, had named it.

prophet But thou, Bethlehem... out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel.

narrator God's promised ruler would come from Bethlehem. There, Mary gave birth to Jesus.

angel Unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour.

(pause)

narrator An emperor gave the order. God kept the promise. Jesus died for our sins, and rose from the dead on the third day.

~131 words, about 55 s
