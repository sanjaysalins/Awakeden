## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR (one named viewer; who stops, who finishes, who shares; who is NOT served)

Daniel, a churchgoing teenager, stops for the experts’ apparent failure (1–3), finishes for the prophecy’s fulfilment (7–11), and shares the gospel ending (18–20). Someone seeking evidence that the experts refused to go is underserved (3).

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)

“Micah named Bethlehem, and Jesus was born there.” Lines 7–11 communicate this clearly.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)

Eastern visitors seek a king; Herod consults religious authorities to locate him (9–11). “The Bible never says” (3) suggests culpability without establishing it. “Scribe,” “Judaea,” and “from everlasting” need explanation (11, 16).

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)

- **1–2 RIGHT:** Experts knew “the exact town” for “God’s promised King” (Matthew 2:2–6; Micah 5:2).
- **3 OVERCLAIMED:** “The Bible never says they went to look.” Matthew 2:1–11 records no visit; these extracts cannot establish a whole-Bible negative or their motives.
- **4, 8 UNSUPPORTED:** “About seven hundred years earlier” / “ABOUT 700 YEARS LATER.” The supplied verses provide no numerical interval.
- **5 RIGHT:** “Micah, a prophet, a man who spoke God’s message.” Matthew treats the matching passage as prophetic Scripture (Matthew 2:5–6; Micah 5:2).
- **6 RIGHT:** “named one small town” (Micah 5:2).
- **7 RIGHT:** “But thou, Bethlehem… out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel.” Accurate excerpt; mark omissions on screen too (Micah 5:2).
- **8 RIGHT:** “When Jesus was born there” (Matthew 2:1; Luke 2:4–7).
- **9 RIGHT:** “visitors from the east came looking”; **OVERCLAIMED:** “a newborn King.” Their purpose is explicit; his age when they arrived is unspecified (Matthew 2:1–2, 11).
- **10 RIGHT:** “King Herod asked the experts where He would be born” (Matthew 2:4).
- **11 RIGHT:** “In Bethlehem of Judaea” (Matthew 2:5). **UNSUPPORTED:** “A SCRIBE” identifies an individual spokesperson where the text says “they.”
- **12 RIGHT:** “They knew the town” (Matthew 2:5–6).
- **14 RIGHT:** “Jesus was already there”—a reasonable narrative inference (Matthew 2:1, 8–11).
- **15–16 RIGHT:** “Micah also said this ruler is … from everlasting.” More precisely, his “goings forth” are from everlasting (Micah 5:2).
- **17 RIGHT:** “He did not begin in Bethlehem”—an inference from Micah 5:2, rather than a quotation.
- **18 RIGHT:** “He came as a baby” (Luke 2:7, 12).
- **19–20 RIGHT:** “died for our sins” and “rose from the dead on the third day” (1 Corinthians 15:3–4, named in the brief but omitted from its printed KJV text).

**Pictures:** Manger (8) and house (14) fit Luke 2:7 and Matthew 2:11. Scholar counts, costumes, Micah’s appearance, camels, ox, donkey, three crosses, tomb furnishings, garden, wounds and coloured glow are unverified illustrative details (1–21). Crosses beneath “He came as a baby” confuse the chronology (18).
