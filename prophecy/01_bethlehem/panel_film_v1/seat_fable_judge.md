## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR (one named viewer; who stops, who finishes, who shares; who is NOT served)
Named viewer: Ana, 24, a curious scroller in Manila, church background, English as a second language. She stops at line 3 ("never says they went to look") because it is a small scandal. Bible readers finish and share, since lines 7 + 11 give them a clean "700 years" proof. NOT served: the total newcomer after line 14. Lines 16-20 pack pre-existence, birth, cross and resurrection into 9 seconds, and "died for our sins" (line 19) is never explained.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)
"A prophet named Jesus' birth town 700 years early, and the experts who knew it never went." Lines 7 and 11 carry the first half, lines 3 and 12-14 the second. Clear up to line 14. Then the film swaps to a second takeaway ("He did not begin in Bethlehem", line 17) and the first is dropped. The hook's point, knowing is not seeking, is never said aloud or turned to the viewer.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)
Who: Jewish scholars, a prophet called Micah, King Herod, baby Jesus. What: the town was named long before, and the scholars could quote it. Why it matters: they knew and stayed home, and this baby is older than the town.
- Lost at line 10: "where He would be born". After "King Herod", "He" can read as Herod. Three kings in 8 seconds (promised, newborn, Herod).
- Misled at line 12: "They knew the town" plays over Herod's face. "They" are the scholars.
- Misled at line 18: "He came as a baby" plays over three crosses. Picture is one beat ahead of the words.
- Hard words: line 7 "thou / thee / come forth unto me" (7 seconds, but line 6 pre-explains it, good); "Judaea" (line 11); "from everlasting" (line 16, rescued by line 17).

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
- Line 1-2 "experts knew the exact town": RIGHT (Matt 2:4-6).
- Line 3 "The Bible never says they went to look": RIGHT, and carefully worded. Keep "never says"; do not harden to "never went".
- Line 4 "About seven hundred years earlier": RIGHT with "about".
- Line 7 Micah 5:2: KJV words RIGHT, but the cut ("Ephratah, though thou be little...") is spoken as a pause and NOT marked on the scroll. On screen it reads as one unbroken verse. Minor OVERCLAIM of verbatim.
- Lines 8-9: RIGHT (Matt 2:1-2). Camels, ox, donkey are tradition, not text. Harmless.
- Lines 10-11: RIGHT (Matt 2:4-5). "A SCRIBE" is fair; the text says "they".
- Line 14: RIGHT. The house matches Matt 2:11.
- Lines 15-16 "this ruler is from everlasting": slightly OVERCLAIMED. Micah says his "goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting".
- Line 17 "He did not begin in Bethlehem": RIGHT as doctrine (John 1:1-2, 8:58), an inference from Micah.
- Lines 19-20: RIGHT (1 Cor 15:3-4).

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE (what would the best explainer in the world do differently; the ONE highest-value change; score 0-10 against that bar)
The best version ends where it began. ONE change: use the 3.1 s of silence (line 21) to close the hook, e.g. "They knew where to find Him. Knowing is not going." That joins the two halves and gives every viewer a line to repeat. Also show the distance. Score: 6.5/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP (opens at line 3, pays off at line 12-14; score 0-10; worth the wait?)
