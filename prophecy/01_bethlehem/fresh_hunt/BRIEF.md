# BLIND BRIEF - fresh angle hunt: BETHLEHEM (Micah 5:2 -> the birth of Jesus)

You are ONE independent Bible researcher on a blind panel. Nobody else's work is shown to you.
Do not use tools, do not read files, do not ask questions. Output your answer text only.

## The series
60-second YouTube Shorts. Aim: read the WHOLE Bible (Old + New Testament) with Jesus as the lens.
Every short must LAND ON JESUS. Watched worldwide, many viewers have English as a second language
and no Bible background. KJV only.

## The problem
Our Bethlehem episode tells what everyone has heard. We want an angle that is
OLD IN THE TEXT but NEW TO THE VIEWER - a connection the Bible itself makes (or plainly supports),
that a normal churchgoer has probably never been shown.

## Already told a million times - DO NOT give these as your angle
- Micah 5:2 predicted Bethlehem and it came true.
- The chief priests and scribes knew the verse (Matthew 2:4-6) but did not go.
- The census sent Joseph and Mary to Bethlehem; no room in the inn; the manger; shepherds; wise men and the star.
- "Bethlehem means house of bread" + "I am the bread of life".
- Bethlehem is the city of David, so Jesus is the Son of David.

## Your task
Search your knowledge of the WHOLE canon. Give your 6 best fresh angles connected to Bethlehem
(the place, its people, its history, the verses about it, or the birth there). For EACH angle:

1. TITLE (max 8 words)
2. HOOK QUESTION a newcomer would want answered (max 14 words)
3. THE VERSES - exact references + a short EXACT KJV quote from each (we will check every word)
4. WHAT KIND OF LINK - pick one and be honest: DIRECT PROPHECY (the NT says it was fulfilled) /
   NT-CONFIRMED (the NT itself draws the link) / TYPE-ECHO (a pattern the text supports; the NT does not state it)
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE - the overclaim we must avoid (1-2 lines)
6. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT (1 line), and your guess 0-10 how fresh it is (10 = almost nobody has heard it)
7. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS in one plain sentence
8. YOUR CONFIDENCE it is sound and really in the text, 0-10

Rules: no invented doctrine, no contrarian or clickbait readings, no speculation presented as fact.
No Hebrew/Greek claims unless you are certain and they are standard lexicon facts.
Surprising about the TEXT, never about the TRUTH. Fewer honest angles beat six weak ones.
End with: MY TOP PICK = <title> and one line why.
