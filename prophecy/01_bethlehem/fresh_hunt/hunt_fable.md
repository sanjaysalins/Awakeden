## Angle 1

1. **TITLE:** The First Bethlehem Birth Ended in Tears
2. **HOOK QUESTION:** Why does the Bible's first Bethlehem story end at a grave?
3. **THE VERSES:**
   - Genesis 35:18 - "And it came to pass, as her soul was in departing, (for she died) that she called his name Benoni: but his father called him Benjamin."
   - Genesis 35:19 - "And Rachel died, and was buried in the way to Ephrath, which is Bethlehem."
   - Jeremiah 31:15 - "A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation, and bitter weeping; Rahel weeping for her children refused to be comforted for her children, because they were not."
   - Jeremiah 31:16-17 - "Refrain thy voice from weeping, and thine eyes from tears" ... "And there is hope in thine end, saith the LORD"
   - Matthew 2:17-18 - "Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremy the prophet, saying, In Rama was there a voice heard, lamentation, and weeping, and great mourning, Rachel weeping for her children, and would not be comforted, because they are not."
   - Landing support: Isaiah 53:3 - "a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief"; Hebrews 1:3 - "sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high"
4. **KIND OF LINK:** DIRECT PROPHECY for Rachel weeping (Matthew 2:17 says "fulfilled"). The two names of the child (Benoni / Benjamin) pointing to Jesus is TYPE-ECHO only.
5. **DOES NOT PROVE:** That Benjamin is a prophecy of Jesus, or that Genesis 35 "predicts" Herod. The name meanings ("son of my sorrow" / "son of the right hand") are the standard KJV margin readings, but the NT never applies them to Christ. Also Ramah is not Bethlehem; Jeremiah first spoke of the exile.
6. **WHY NOT HEARD:** People know "Rachel weeping" from Matthew but almost nobody is shown that Rachel died in childbirth on the road to Bethlehem itself. **Fresh: 7**
7. **LANDS ON JESUS:** The town where a mother died naming her son "sorrow" is where the Man of Sorrows was born, and God's answer to the weeping, "there is hope in thine end", is Him.
8. **CONFIDENCE:** 8 (9 for the Rachel-Bethlehem-Matthew chain; 6 for the two-names echo)

## Angle 2

1. **TITLE:** The Bethlehem Water David Would Not Drink
2. **HOOK QUESTION:** Why did a thirsty king pour Bethlehem's water on the ground?
3. **THE VERSES:**
   - 2 Samuel 23:15 - "And David longed, and said, Oh that one would give me drink of the water of the well of Bethlehem, which is by the gate!"
   - 2 Samuel 23:16 - "nevertheless he would not drink thereof, but poured it out unto the LORD."
   - 2 Samuel 23:17 - "is not this the blood of the men that went in jeopardy of their lives? therefore he would not drink it."
   - John 4:14 - "But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst"
   - Matthew 26:27-28 - "Drink ye all of it; For this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins."
4. **KIND OF LINK:** TYPE-ECHO. The NT never mentions David's well.
5. **DOES NOT PROVE:** That the well story is a prophecy, or that Jesus had it in mind at the supper or at Jacob's well. It is a contrast the text allows, not one the Bible states. Say "listen to the echo", never "this was fulfilled".
6. **WHY NOT HEARD:** It sits in a list of David's soldiers at the back of 2 Samuel that sermons skip. **Fresh: 8**
7. **LANDS ON JESUS:** David called the Bethlehem water "blood" and felt unworthy to drink it; the Son of David, born in that town, gives His own blood and says "Drink ye all of it."
8. **CONFIDENCE:** 6 (the verses are solid; the link is our reading, honestly labelled)

## Angle 3

1. **TITLE:** They Used Bethlehem to Reject Him
2. **HOOK QUESTION:** How did knowing the Bethlehem prophecy make people miss Jesus?
3. **THE VERSES:**
   - John 7:41-42 - "But some said, Shall Christ come out of Galilee? Hath not the scripture said, That Christ cometh of the seed of David, and out of the town of Bethlehem, where David was?"
   - John 7:43 - "So there was a division among the people because of him."
   - John 7:52 - "Search, and look: for out of Galilee ariseth no prophet."
   - Micah 5:2 - "whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting."
   - John 7:28 - "I am not come of myself, but he that sent me is true"
   - John 8:58 - "Before Abraham was, I am."
4. **KIND OF LINK:** NT-CONFIRMED. John records the crowd quoting the Bethlehem promise. Matthew 2:5-6 states the fulfilment. The "from everlasting" tie to John 8:58 is a fair reading, not an NT citation.
5. **DOES NOT PROVE:** That John 7 itself says Jesus was born in Bethlehem (John leaves the irony unsaid; Matthew and Luke supply the fact). Do not mock the crowd as stupid; they had the right verse and the wrong assumption. Different group from the priests in Matthew 2 (banned angle) - this is the ordinary crowd, 30 years later.
6. **WHY NOT HEARD:** Bethlehem is taught only at Christmas, and John 7 is never a Christmas text. **Fresh: 7**
7. **LANDS ON JESUS:** They asked "where is he from?" and stopped at Galilee; Micah's own verse gives two answers - Bethlehem, and "from everlasting" - and Jesus is both.
8. **CONFIDENCE:** 9

## Angle 4

1. **TITLE:** Read the Next Verse After Bethlehem
2. **HOOK QUESTION:** What does Micah say the Bethlehem child will actually do?
3. **THE VERSES:**
   - Micah 5:4 - "And he shall stand and feed in the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God; and they shall abide: for now shall he be great unto the ends of the earth."
   - Micah 5:5 - "And this man shall be the peace"
   - Matthew 2:6 - "for out of thee shall come a Governor, that shall rule my people Israel."
   - 2 Samuel 5:2 - "Thou shalt feed my people Israel"
   - John 10:11 - "I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep."
   - Ephesians 2:14 - "For he is our peace"
4. **KIND OF LINK:** DIRECT PROPHECY for verse 2 (Matthew 2:5-6). Verses 4-5 are the same sentence about the same ruler, so NT-CONFIRMED in substance (the NT calls Jesus shepherd and "our peace") though it does not quote Micah 5:4-5 by number.
5. **DOES NOT PROVE:** That Ephesians 2:14 is quoting Micah 5:5 (similar words, no citation). And "great unto the ends of the earth" is not yet fully seen; do not claim it is finished.
6. **WHY NOT HEARD:** Everyone stops reading at verse 2. **Fresh: 6**
7. **LANDS ON JESUS:** Micah did not only say where He would be born but what He would be: a ruler who feeds His flock like a shepherd, and who Himself is the peace.
8. **CONFIDENCE:** 8

## Angle 5

1. **TITLE:** The Empty Woman Who Came Home to Bethlehem
2. **HOOK QUESTION:** Who was the foreign widow in Jesus' family tree?
3. **THE VERSES:**
   - Ruth 1:1 - "there was a famine in the land. And a certain man of Bethlehemjudah went to sojourn in the country of Moab"
   - Ruth 1:21 - "I went out full, and the LORD hath brought me home again empty"
   - Ruth 4:11 - "do thou worthily in Ephratah, and be famous in Bethlehem"
   - Ruth 4:14 - "Blessed be the LORD, which hath not left thee this day without a kinsman, that his name may be famous in Israel."
   - Ruth 4:17 - "There is a son born to Naomi; and they called his name Obed: he is the father of Jesse, the father of David."
   - Matthew 1:5 - "and Booz begat Obed of Ruth; and Obed begat Jesse"
   - Titus 2:14 - "Who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity"
4. **KIND OF LINK:** NT-CONFIRMED for Ruth being in the line of Jesus (Matthew 1:5). Boaz the kinsman-redeemer as a picture of Christ is TYPE-ECHO.
5. **DOES NOT PROVE:** That the NT calls Boaz a type of Christ (it does not), or that the Bethlehem women's blessing was a conscious prophecy of Jesus.
6. **WHY NOT HEARD:** Ruth is taught as a love story; few notice the whole rescue happens in Bethlehem. **Fresh: 5**
7. **LANDS ON JESUS:** In Bethlehem a redeemer paid the price to bring an empty outsider into the family, and from that very marriage came Jesus, who "gave himself for us."
8. **CONFIDENCE:** 8

## Angle 6

1. **TITLE:** Micah Named the Shepherds' Field Too
2. **HOOK QUESTION:** Did Micah point at the shepherds' field before Bethlehem?
3. **THE VERSES:**
   - Micah 4:8 - "And thou, O tower of the flock, the strong hold of the daughter of Zion, unto thee shall it come, even the first dominion; the kingdom shall come to the daughter of Jerusalem."
   - Genesis 35:21 - "And Israel journeyed, and spread his tent beyond the tower of Edar." (the verses just before, 35:19, place this next to Bethlehem)
   - Luke 2:8 - "And there were in the same country shepherds abiding in the field, keeping watch over their flock by night."
4. **KIND OF LINK:** TYPE-ECHO, and the weakest of the six.
5. **DOES NOT PROVE:** That Jesus was born at this tower, that the Luke 2 shepherds stood there, or that their sheep were temple lambs (that comes from later Jewish writings, NOT the Bible). Micah 4:8 most naturally speaks of Jerusalem/Zion. "Tower of the flock" and "tower of Edar" being the same Hebrew phrase is standard, but the place link is a possibility only.
6. **WHY NOT HEARD:** It needs three scattered verses and is mostly passed around online with overclaims. **Fresh: 8**
7. **LANDS ON JESUS:** One chapter before naming Bethlehem, Micah promised the kingdom would come to a "tower of the flock", and the first people told the King had come were men watching a flock.
8. **CONFIDENCE:** 4. I list it honestly but would not build an episode on it.
