For **5**, change only these two spoken lines:

- **Old:** God promised a King from there. Micah called Him [prophet voice:] “ruler in Israel.”  
  **→ New:** Micah called Him [prophet voice:] “ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.”

- **Old:** But Jesus came much farther. He came down from heaven. He died for our sins. On the third day, He rose from the dead.  
  **→ New:** Jesus came from heaven; Bethlehem was not His beginning. He died for our sins and rose on the third day.
