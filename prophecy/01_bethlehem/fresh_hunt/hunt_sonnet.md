# Fresh angle hunt: BETHLEHEM (seat: Sonnet)

Six angles, ranked roughly best to weakest in the order given. All KJV quotes are from memory; please check every word.

---

## 1. The Son of Sorrow Who Became the Son of the Right Hand

1. TITLE: The Mother Who Died at Bethlehem
2. HOOK: Who is the first person the Bible ever buries at Bethlehem, and why?
3. VERSES:
   - Genesis 35:18: "And it came to pass, as her soul was in departing, (for she died) that she called his name Benoni: but his father called him Benjamin."
   - Genesis 35:19: "And Rachel died, and was buried in the way to Ephrath, which is Bethlehem."
   - Genesis 48:7: "the same is Bethlehem."
   - Jeremiah 31:15: "A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation, and bitter weeping; Rahel weeping for her children refused to be comforted for her children, because they were not."
   - Jeremiah 31:17: "And there is hope in thine end, saith the LORD"
   - Matthew 2:17-18: "Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremy the prophet, saying, In Rama was there a voice heard, lamentation, and weeping, and great mourning, Rachel weeping for her children, and would not be comforted, because they are not."
   - Isaiah 53:3: "a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief"
   - Hebrews 1:3: "sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high"
4. KIND OF LINK: NT-CONFIRMED for Rachel weeping at the Bethlehem killings (Matthew 2:17-18 draws it). TYPE-ECHO for the Benoni to Benjamin renaming (the NT does not state it).
5. DOES NOT PROVE: That Benjamin "is" Jesus, or that the renaming is a prophecy. Not a claim about the exact site of the tomb (1 Samuel 10:2 places it near Benjamin's border; the location is debated). Name meanings (Benoni = "son of my sorrow", Benjamin = "son of the right hand") are standard lexicon facts and are not spelled out in the KJV text.
6. WHY UNHEARD: Churches skip Genesis 35 and jump to Matthew 2; the tomb at Bethlehem is a side note. Freshness 6/10.
7. LANDS ON JESUS: At Bethlehem a mother's last word gave her son a name of sorrow and his father turned it to "right hand"; centuries later Rachel's sorrow is heard again at Bethlehem, and the Baby born there is the Man of sorrows who now sits at God's right hand.
8. CONFIDENCE: 8 (text solid; the sorrow-to-right-hand arc is a pattern, so label it honestly).

---

## 2. The Town That Lived Through the Days With No King

1. TITLE: Bethlehem Before It Had a King
2. HOOK: What was Bethlehem like in the dark days when Israel had no king?
3. VERSES:
   - Judges 17:6: "In those days there was no king in Israel, but every man did that which was right in his own eyes."
   - Judges 17:7: "And there was a young man out of Bethlehemjudah of the family of Judah, who was a Levite, and he sojourned there."
   - Judges 19:1: "And it came to pass in those days, when there was no king in Israel, that there was a certain Levite sojourning on the side of mount Ephraim, who took to him a concubine out of Bethlehemjudah."
   - Ruth 1:1: "Now it came to pass in the days when the judges ruled, that there was a famine in the land. And a certain man of Bethlehemjudah went to sojourn in the country of Moab"
   - 1 Samuel 16:1: "for I have provided me a king among his sons."
   - Matthew 2:2: "Where is he that is born King of the Jews?"
   - Luke 2:11: "For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord."
4. KIND OF LINK: TYPE-ECHO (a narrative pattern: Bethlehem appears in the "no king" era, then the king comes from it). The NT does not state the pattern.
5. DOES NOT PROVE: That Bethlehem was a wicked town or that Judges is predicting Jesus. Bethlehem is only where these people came from. It does not prove every Bethlehem person in Judges is a "type" of anything.
6. WHY UNHEARD: People read Judges 17-21 as grim stories and Ruth as a love story, and rarely notice both sit in the same town. Freshness 7/10.
7. LANDS ON JESUS: The Bible's cry of "no king" is answered from the same little town, and the wise men ask for the One "born King."
8. CONFIDENCE: 7 (all verses are real; the thematic stitching is the writer's, say so on screen).

## 3. The Foreign Woman Who Was Told to Be Famous in Bethlehem

1. TITLE: The Outsider in Jesus's Family Tree
2. HOOK: Which foreign woman, a stranger in Bethlehem, ended up in Jesus's family line?
3. VERSES:
   - Ruth 2:10: "Why have I found grace in thine eyes, that thou shouldest take knowledge of me, seeing I am a stranger?"
   - Ruth 4:11: "do thou worthily in Ephratah, and be famous in Bethlehem"
   - Ruth 4:17: "they called his name Obed: he is the father of Jesse, the father of David."
   - Matthew 1:5: "And Salmon begat Booz of Rachab; and Booz begat Obed of Ruth; and Obed begat Jesse;"
   - Matthew 1:16 (for the end of the line): "Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ."
4. KIND OF LINK: NT-CONFIRMED (Matthew 1 names Ruth, and Rachab, in the line to Jesus). The "be famous in Bethlehem" blessing coming true through a Messiah is a reasonable reading, not stated by the NT.
5. DOES NOT PROVE: That Ruth herself foresaw the Messiah. Ruth 4:11 is a wedding blessing. Do not claim the blessing is a prophecy of Jesus.
6. WHY UNHEARD: Ruth is told as a romance and as "kinsman redeemer", but few say the Moabite stranger was in the family tree of the Baby born in the same town. Freshness 5/10 (partly known to churchgoers; the "famous in Bethlehem" tie is less known).
7. LANDS ON JESUS: The Saviour born in Bethlehem came through a family that already had a stranger from another nation welcomed into it, so the door was open to everyone.
8. CONFIDENCE: 8.

## 4. David's Water from Bethlehem That Cost Blood

1. TITLE: The Water David Would Not Drink
2. HOOK: Why did a thirsty king pour out water from Bethlehem's well on the ground?
3. VERSES:
   - 2 Samuel 23:14: "the garrison of the Philistines was then in Bethlehem."
   - 2 Samuel 23:15: "And David longed, and said, Oh that one would give me drink of the water of the well of Bethlehem, which is by the gate!"
   - 2 Samuel 23:16: "nevertheless he would not drink thereof, but poured it out unto the LORD."
   - 2 Samuel 23:17: "is not this the blood of the men that went in jeopardy of their lives? therefore he would not drink it."
   - John 4:14: "but the water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life."
   - Matthew 26:28: "For this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins."
4. KIND OF LINK: TYPE-ECHO. The NT does not connect 2 Samuel 23 to Jesus.
5. DOES NOT PROVE: That David foresaw Jesus, or that the well is a prophecy. It is a contrast of pattern only: do not say "the well is a type of the cross" as fact.
6. WHY UNHEARD: It is a short episode inside a list of David's mighty men, rarely preached. Freshness 8/10.
7. LANDS ON JESUS: David refused a drink bought with other men's blood, but the King born in David's town gives living water and pays for it with his own blood.
8. CONFIDENCE: 6 (the story is solid; the Jesus link is a contrast pattern and must be presented as such).

## 5. The Baby Who Is Older Than His Town

1. TITLE: Born in Bethlehem, Older Than Bethlehem
2. HOOK: What does the second half of Micah 5:2 say about the Baby's age?
3. VERSES:
   - Micah 5:2: "yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting."
   - John 1:1: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."
   - John 8:58: "Verily, verily, I say unto you, Before Abraham was, I am."
   - Colossians 1:17: "And he is before all things"
4. KIND OF LINK: NT-CONFIRMED in substance (John 1 and John 8 teach his pre-existence), but the NT does not quote Micah 5:2b. So: TYPE-ECHO / supported reading, not a stated fulfilment.
5. DOES NOT PROVE: That Micah alone teaches the Trinity or the incarnation. "From of old, from everlasting" can be read as very ancient origin; the stronger reading is supported by John and Colossians, not by Micah on its own. Make no Hebrew word claims.
6. WHY UNHEARD: Everyone quotes the first half (Bethlehem, small, ruler) and stops before the second half. Freshness 5/10.
7. LANDS ON JESUS: The ruler is born in a small town, yet his "goings forth" are from everlasting; the baby in the manger is the One who was there before the beginning.
8. CONFIDENCE: 8 (text is certain; keep the claim at "Micah hints, John says it").

---

## 6. The Bethlehem Prophecy Reaches the Ends of the Earth

1. TITLE: The Little Town's King Is Great to the World
2. HOOK: What does the Bethlehem prophecy say about people far from Israel?
3. VERSES:
   - Micah 5:4: "And he shall stand and feed in the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God; and they shall abide: for now shall he be great unto the ends of the earth."
   - Micah 5:5: "And this man shall be the peace, when the Assyrian shall come into our land"
   - Ephesians 2:14: "For he is our peace, who hath made both one"
   - Ephesians 2:17: "And came and preached peace to you which were afar off, and to them that were nigh."
4. KIND OF LINK: TYPE-ECHO. Ephesians does not cite Micah; the match is in the words ("peace", the reach to the far-off), and in the same prophecy that gives the Bethlehem verse.
5. DOES NOT PROVE: That Micah 5:5 is quoted by Paul or that "the Assyrian" means a specific modern enemy. Micah 5:4-5 may also have more than one fulfilment; do not claim it is "all about Jesus" without the caution.
6. WHY UNHEARD: Preaching stops at verse 2 because that is the quote in Matthew 2. Freshness 7/10.
7. LANDS ON JESUS: The same prophecy that names the small town says its King will be great to the ends of the earth, and Paul says he came and preached peace to those far off, which includes the viewer watching from anywhere.
8. CONFIDENCE: 6.

---

MY TOP PICK = The Mother Who Died at Bethlehem (Rachel, Benoni to Benjamin). It is in the text from the first Bethlehem mention, Matthew himself links Rachel to Bethlehem, it is emotional and clear for a newcomer, and it lands on the Man of sorrows now at God's right hand without overclaiming.
