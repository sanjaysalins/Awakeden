## TOP PICK: Angle 4

Why: a link the NT itself draws (John records it). It mirrors the film's spine: the experts knew the verse and did not go; the crowd knew the verse and used it to turn away. It makes the film's "knowing is not going" point sharper. Fresh: few people notice that the Bethlehem verse is used AGAINST Jesus. John leaves the irony unsaid, so say it softly.

Changes (old -> new), net about the same word count:

- OLD: "About seven hundred years earlier, a prophet called Micah, a man who spoke God's message, named a small town: Bethlehem."
  NEW: "Seven hundred years earlier, the prophet Micah named a small town: Bethlehem."
- OLD: "Mary and Joseph lived far away. A tax order sent them to Bethlehem. There, God's promise came true: Jesus, the King, was born."
  NEW: "A tax order sent Mary and Joseph there. God's promise came true: Jesus, the King, was born."
- OLD: "Herod, the king there, asked the experts."
  NEW: "Herod asked the experts."
- OLD (after "The wise men went, and found the child."): nothing
  NEW: "Thirty years later, a crowd quoted that same verse to argue against Jesus: [crowd voice:] 'Shall Christ come out of Galilee?' They had the right verse. The answer was in Bethlehem: He was born there."
- Keep: "But Jesus came much farther..." through "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us."

## TOP 3
1. Angle 4 - NT-confirmed, true, ironic, fits spine perfectly.
2. Angle 5 - "from everlasting" sets up the ending ("He came down from heaven") and is a direct-text link; swap-in is two lines. Safe backup. Do not claim the experts cut it on purpose.
3. Angle 3 - most fresh and vivid, sound if called a contrast; but a new story, weaker fit and type-echo only.

## REJECT: Angle 8 (tower of the flock)
Micah 4:8 speaks of Zion/Jerusalem; the shepherd/temple-lamb link is later tradition. Freshness through a stretch.

## Missing angle (optional)
Bethlehem means "house of bread" (standard lexicon) - Jesus laid in a manger (a feeding trough, Luke 2:7) and then says "I am the bread of life" (John 6:35). Fresh to many, sound if labelled a word-meaning echo, not prophecy.
