## X

**1. Newcomer.** Who: Jesus, the promised King. What: an old book named His birth town, and He was born there. Why: the experts knew the place but did not go. Strangers went instead, and in the end God came to us.
- "Years later, some questioned Jesus: the King comes from Bethlehem, not Galilee." I can't tell who is talking here. It sounds like the narrator is saying it. It also sounds as if the doubters were right.

**2. New to you.** 6/10. I have heard Bethlehem and the wise men many times. Two things feel fresh: "the experts never went", and "birthplace, not His beginning".

**3. Curiosity.** 6/10. "Did they go?" opens a real question. The honest answer ("The Bible never says the experts went") is a bit soft. The last line ties it up well, but only loosely.

**4. Hard words.**
- "ruler in Israel." This is a piece of a sentence with no subject. Without the picture it means nothing.
- "from everlasting." This will be a hard word for second-language listeners. The next line rescues it.
- "Galilee". Spoken only, with no picture, it is just a name.
- The Galilee line has no voice change, so with captions it reads as one muddled sentence.

**5. Bible.**
- "some questioned Jesus" goes too far. In John 7:41 the people argued among themselves. They did not question Him.
- "Bethlehem was close by" is a map fact, not a verse. That is fine, but know it is not from the KJV text.
- "Micah added" is a little loose, since it is the same verse. Acceptable.
- "The Bible never says the experts went" is accurate and well hedged.

**6. Overall:** 6.5/10

## Y

**1. Newcomer.** Same story as X. The crowd voice makes it clear that other people raised the doubt. "The verse said Bethlehem" links back to Micah.
- "Shall Christ come out of Galilee?" "Shall" is old English, and "Christ" was never explained. The film has only said "King" until now.

**2. New to you.** 6/10. Same as X.

**3. Curiosity.** 7/10. The real quote adds a second puzzle that gets answered ("He was born there"). That makes the "they knew, but..." idea stronger before the ending.

**4. Hard words.**
- "Shall Christ come out of Galilee?" A newcomer may not know that Christ means the promised King. Auto-captions may also write "Christ" oddly, as "crist" or "Chris".
- "from everlasting" and "ruler in Israel." Same problem as in X.

**5. Bible.**
- "some questioned Jesus" has the same overclaim as X, and here it sits right next to a quote that shows it wrong. In the verse the crowd is arguing with each other, not asking Him.
- "The verse said Bethlehem" is fair to John 7:42 ("Hath not the scripture said…").

**6. Overall:** 7/10

## Pick: Y

The real crowd voice makes it clear who doubted. "The verse said Bethlehem" also closes the loop that X leaves muddy.

**One change.** The current line is: "Years later, some questioned Jesus:". Change it to: **"Years later, people argued about Jesus:"**

That matches John 7:41–43 ("there was a division among the people"). It also stops a newcomer from thinking the crowd was talking to Him.
