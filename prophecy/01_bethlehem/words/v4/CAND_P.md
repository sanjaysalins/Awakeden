[narrator] Did they go?
[narrator] God's prophet Micah named a small town: Bethlehem. God promised a King from there:
[prophet] ruler in Israel.
[narrator] Micah added:
[prophet] from everlasting.
[narrator] Bethlehem would be His birthplace, not His beginning.
[narrator] There, God's promise came true: Jesus, the King, was born.
[narrator] Wise men reached Jerusalem, asking where this King was. King Herod asked the experts.
[scribe] In Bethlehem of Judaea.
[narrator] Bethlehem was close by. (pause) The Bible never says the experts went. The wise men went, and found the child.
[narrator] Years later, some questioned Jesus:
[crowd] Shall Christ come out of Galilee?
[narrator] The verse said Bethlehem. He was born there.
[narrator] But Jesus came much farther. He came down from heaven. He died for our sins. On the third day, He rose from the dead.
[narrator] Before anyone went to Him, He came to us.
