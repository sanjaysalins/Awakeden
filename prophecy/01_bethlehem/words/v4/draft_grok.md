(b) 144 spoken words. The last word ends at about 56.1 s. Both insights fit.

(c) A lands at 12.6–17.3, on Micah’s own next words, “from everlasting,” then in plain words: He did not begin in Bethlehem; He arrived. B lands at 39.4–46.1: years later some ask the Galilee question, name Bethlehem, and Jesus was born there. Pictures: add “from everlasting” on the parchment beside “ruler in Israel”; shorten the Micah hill, the walkers, the birth hold, and the Herod–scholars beat; keep a short silence after “close by” (about 0.7 s); keep the throne man as King Herod; add one new shot of about 7 s after the gold footprints — grown Jesus, a few people, the same map with Galilee to the north and Bethlehem marked. Starry heaven, three crosses, and the empty tomb stay, and now answer “from everlasting.” The end card stays.
