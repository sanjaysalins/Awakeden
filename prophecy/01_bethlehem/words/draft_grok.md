Who it is for: Anyone, anywhere — new to the Bible, or sure they already know the Christmas story.
Main take-away: A tax sent pregnant Mary to little Bethlehem, the town Micah named about seven hundred years before, and Jesus the ruler was born there. He died for our sins and rose the third day.
The curiosity gap: Why did a tax send a pregnant woman to a town she did not choose?
Why a newcomer gets it: The people, the tax, the town, and Jesus are named in order, and the old Bible line is said again in plain English.
