**X**

1. **NEWCOMER:** The experts knew the town where a promised King would be born. A prophet named it 700 years before. Jesus was born there. The experts stayed home, but strangers went. The ending says God came to us first. It is clear. The weak spot is the prophet's line "ruler in Israel." It is only three words in a new voice, right after a colon. On a phone you could miss it, or hear it as a broken bit.
2. **PROMISE:** God promised, through Micah, about 700 years earlier. The promise was a King from Bethlehem, and Jesus was born there. "Lived far away" plus the map makes the "came true" moment land. But the voice we hear only says "ruler in Israel." Bethlehem is never in the prophet's voice. **6/10.**
3. **TWO KINGS:** Yes. "Jesus, the King" comes first, then "Herod, the king there." Those are two separate labels.
4. **HARD WORDS:** "Judaea" (captions may write "Judea" or "Judah"). "Tax order" is fine. "Wise men" is OK.
5. **BIBLE:**
   - "Three days later" is a slight overclaim. The KJV says "the third day," and Friday to Sunday is not three full days.
   - "Bethlehem was close by" is true, but it is not in the text given.
   - "Never says the experts went" is accurate.
   - Everything else checks out.
6. **Overall: 7.5/10**

**Y**

1. **NEWCOMER:** Same story. "a King would come, that is to be ruler in Israel" flows as one sentence, which is better. But "that is to be" is old-style English and a bit stiff for a second-language listener. Dropping "lived far away" loses why the trip matters. A newcomer no longer sees that the couple *had* to be moved for the promise to come true. The Nazareth pin on the map goes unexplained.
2. **PROMISE:** More of the prophet's words are heard, which is good. But the "came true" payoff is weaker: "There Jesus was born: the King God promised." **6.5/10.**
3. **TWO KINGS:** Yes. "the King God promised" and then "Herod, the king there."
4. **HARD WORDS:** "that is to be" (old-style, and auto-captions may mishear it), "Judaea," "tax order."
5. **BIBLE:** Same "Three days later" point. Nothing else is wrong.
6. **Overall: 7/10**

**PICK: X.** "Lived far away … God's promise came true" gives the map and the CAME TRUE stamp a real payoff. Y loses that.

**ONE change to X.** Hand the narrator's line to the prophet with a bridge word, so the prophet speaks a full phrase:

> [narrator] God promised a King from there, one
> [prophet] that is to be ruler in Israel.

(Also worth doing in either version: change "Three days later" to "On the third day," to match 1 Corinthians 15:4.)
