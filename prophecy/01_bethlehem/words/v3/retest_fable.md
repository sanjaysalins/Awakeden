# Retest — Fable seat (blind, words only)

**1. NEWCOMER**
- Who: Jesus, a King promised by God. Also Bible experts, wise men, and King Herod.
- What: a prophet named the town, Bethlehem, about 700 years early. Jesus was born there. The experts knew the town but are not said to have gone. Strangers went and found Him.
- Why: Jesus made the longer trip. He came from heaven, died for our sins, and rose. He came to us first.
- Lines I would trip on:
  - "Herod, the king there, asked the experts where." It stops short. Where what?
  - "Micah called Him / ruler in Israel." The voice change splits one thought. A newcomer may not link "ruler" to "King".
  - "He died for our sins." No reason is given. A newcomer hears it as a sudden jump.

**2. PROMISE: 8/10**
God promised, through Micah, a King from Bethlehem, about 700 years before. Jesus was born there. It is heard, not just read: "God promised" and "God's promise came true" are both spoken. Lost points: the promise sits in two short lines and the KJV quote is only three words.

**3. TWO KINGS: yes**
"this newborn King" and "Herod, the king there" keep them apart. A small risk stays for a fast listener, as "King" is said five times.

**4. HARD WORDS**
- "Micah" (auto-captions may write "Mica" or "Michael")
- "prophet" (explained well, keep the gloss)
- "Judaea" (spelling and sound)
- "Herod" (captions may write "Harold")
- "tax order"

- "asked the experts where" (clipped grammar)

**5. BIBLE**
Nothing wrong. Two notes:
- "The Bible never says the experts went" is true and carefully worded. It is an argument from silence, so do not push it harder than this.
- "Bethlehem was close by" and "About seven hundred years" are not in the KJV text above. Both are fair outside facts, and "about" keeps the date honest.
- "found the child" is right. Matthew has a house, not the manger, so keep the wise men out of the manger picture.

**6. OVERALL: 8/10**
Clear, short, lands on Jesus. The last line pays off the opening question.

ONE change:
- From: "Herod, the king there, asked the experts where."
- To: "Herod, the king there, asked the experts where the King would be born."

This fixes the clipped line and echoes line one.
