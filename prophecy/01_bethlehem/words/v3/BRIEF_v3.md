## What a blind test panel found (6 independent reviewers; fix these)
1. The PROMISE is never said out loud. Only on-screen stamps show it. Most viewers remember "who went", not "a promise came true". (5 of 6)
2. "a ruler would come, / that is to be ruler in Israel" says "ruler" twice and the old grammar ("that is to be") breaks for non-native ears and machine captions. "Out of it" has no clear noun. (5 of 6)
3. "He rose again the third day" - third day after what? "rose again" can sound like "rose a second time". "died for our sins" is church language with no help. (4 of 6)
4. Two kings: "the King" (line 1) and "King Herod" (line 7) confuse newcomers. Nobody ever says the baby IS the promised King. (4 of 6)
5. Small: "east" of where? The experts must not read as "the Jews" being blamed - keep it about these experts, and say only what the text says.

## How to revise (hard rules)
1. CHANGE AS FEW LINES AS POSSIBLE. Each unchanged line keeps its recorded voice and animation. Fix the 4 problems with the smallest edits. Keep the hook (lines 1-2), "The Bible never says the experts went.", "The wise men went, and found the child." and the last line unless you have a strong reason.
2. A changed line should take about the same time as the line it replaces (narrator speaks about 2.6 words per second). Total voice under 57 s.
3. The promise must be spoken in plain words at least once at the fulfilment (Bethlehem birth), so "promise -> came true" is heard, not only read.
4. KJV quotes: verbatim, short, and never the only carrier of the meaning; say it plainly too. You may change which KJV words are quoted (e.g. Micah 5:2 "out of thee shall he come forth", or Matthew 2:6 "out of thee shall come a Governor, that shall rule my people Israel") but keep at most one short quote per voice.
5. Honest: no invented feelings, motives, numbers or distances. Do not say the experts refused or never went; "the Bible never says" is the limit. The wise men found the child in a house (Matthew 2:11), not at the manger.
6. Grace, no pressure, no stock closer ("come to Jesus", "will you...?"). Land on Jesus: died for our sins, rose again (1 Cor 15:3-4).
7. Voices: [narrator], [prophet], [scribe]. No AI wording.
