**(c) Fixes**

1. **The promise is never said.** Fixed in the birth line: "There Jesus was born, the promised King." Viewers now hear "promise → came true" at the birth, not just see it on screen.
2. **"Ruler" twice and old grammar.** Fixed in the Micah line and the scroll quote. "Out of it" becomes "From Bethlehem", so the place is named. "Ruler" is said once. The broken "that is to be ruler in Israel" is replaced by a short quote that repeats what the narrator just said plainly.
3. **"Third day" and "rose again" are unclear.** Fixed in the ending line, which keeps the same word count (22). "Rose again the third day" becomes "rise from the dead", so it can't sound like "a second time". "On a cross" gives "died for our sins" a picture people can see.
4. **Two kings.** Fixed across the birth line and the wise-men line. "The promised King" ties line 1's "the King" to Jesus by name. Herod is now "who ruled there", not "King", so only one person is called King.
5. **Small fixes.** "From the east to Jerusalem" answers "east of where?" using the verse's own words. The experts stay "the experts", with nothing about their motives and nothing beyond "the Bible never says".

**(d) KJV fragments**

- Prophet: "out of thee shall he come forth" (Micah 5:2)
- Scribe: "In Bethlehem of Judaea" (Matthew 2:5)
- The ending line follows 1 Corinthians 15:3-4 ("Christ died for our sins … he rose again the third day") in plain words, not as a quote.
