**1. NEWCOMER**
Who: experts in the old holy books, a prophet, Mary and Joseph, baby Jesus, wise men and King Herod. What: a promise about a birthplace came true. The experts knew the place but did not go. Strangers went. Why: Jesus came to us first. I understood every line. The weakest line is "But Jesus came much farther." Farther than what? A listener has to work out that it means "farther than that short road." It's OK, but slow.

**2. PROMISE: 8/10**
You hear who (Micah, God's messenger), what (a ruler from Bethlehem), how long before (about 700 years) and what came true (Jesus was born there). The voice switch "Micah called Him / [prophet] ruler in Israel" is a broken half-sentence. Heard aloud, the prophet voice gives just three words with no verb, so it may sound like a glitch. It's also the only line that comes from the prophet himself, and it's very thin.

**3. TWO KINGS: yes.**
"Herod, the king there" works, and so do "this newborn King" and "Jesus, the King." It's clear enough.

**4. HARD WORDS**
- "Judaea": auto-captions may spell it "Judea" or worse.
- "Micah": fine.
- "a tax order": odd for second-language listeners. "A government order" or "a count of the people" is easier.
- "Bible experts": a slight anachronism, but plain. Keep it.

**5. BIBLE CHECK**
- Nothing is wrong.
- "God promised a King": Micah 5:2 says "ruler," not "King." It's a small stretch. Matthew 2:2 "King of the Jews" covers it, but strictly it's an inference.
- "Bethlehem was close by": true, but not in the verses given. It's geography (about 9 km).
- "The Bible never says the experts went": honest wording that argues from silence. Good, keep it.
- The wise men found Him "in a house" (Matthew 2:11), so the pictures should not show the wise men at the manger.

**6. OVERALL: 8/10**
The hook question opens well, but its answer lands softly because "close by" is vague. Make the distance concrete so the irony hits, and the "came much farther" line then has something to stand on.

**One change:**
"Bethlehem was close by." → **"Bethlehem was only a two-hour walk away."**
