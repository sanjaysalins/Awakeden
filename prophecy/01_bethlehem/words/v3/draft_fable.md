## (c) How each problem is fixed

1. **Promise never spoken.** Line 4 now says "God promised" when the promise is made. Line 6 says "the King God promised" at the birth in Bethlehem. So the ear hears promise, then promise kept, in the same words.
2. **"ruler" twice, old grammar, "Out of it".** Line 4 swaps "Out of it" for "From that town" (clear noun) and "a ruler" for "a King". "Ruler" is now said once, only in the KJV quote. The quote now finishes a plain sentence ("a King would come, that is to be ruler in Israel"), so it is no longer the only carrier of the meaning. The prophet line keeps its recorded voice and scroll.
3. **"third day", "rose again", "died for our sins".** Line 12: "our sins, our wrongs" gives a two-word plain gloss. "The third day after" ties the day to His death. "rose from death" replaces "rose again", so it cannot sound like a second rising. Content stays 1 Corinthians 15:3-4. To fit the time, "He came down from heaven" is shortened to "from heaven".
4. **Two kings.** Line 6 says the baby IS "the King God promised". Line 7 says "this newborn King" (points back to Jesus) and "The king there, Herod" (a local king, tied to a place). Capital-K King is now only ever Jesus.
5. **Small ones.** "reached Jerusalem" gives "east" a place to be east of, and makes "Bethlehem was close by" clear (close to Jerusalem). The experts lines are unchanged: still only "the experts", still only "the Bible never says".

One cost to flag: "Mary and Joseph lived far away" is dropped from line 6 to make room for the promise. "Sent ... to Bethlehem" still fits the journey picture.
