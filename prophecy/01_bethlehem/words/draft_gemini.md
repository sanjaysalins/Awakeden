(d) 4 lines
- Who it is for: Both churched viewers who think they know the Christmas story, and unchurched viewers drawn in by a historical mystery.
- Main take-away: It is possible to know the facts about God's promises but miss the living Savior who died and rose for us.
- The curiosity gap: A monumental event happens, the leading experts know exactly where to find it, but shockingly never bother to go look.
- Why a global newcomer gets it: It avoids theological jargon, clearly defines what a prophet is, and uses the universal human irony of academic experts missing the very thing they study.
