**(a) Script**

[narrator] The experts knew where the King would be born. Did they go?
[narrator] About 700 years before, Micah, a prophet who spoke God's message, named one small town.
[prophet] "Bethlehem… out of thee shall he come forth."
[narrator] A ruler would come from there.
[narrator] Mary and Joseph lived far away. A tax order sent them there, and her baby was born.
[narrator] Later, wise men from the east came, asking for the newborn King. King Herod asked the experts.
[scribe] "In Bethlehem of Judaea."
[narrator] They knew. (pause) The Bible never says they went.
[narrator] The strangers went, and found the child with Mary.
[narrator] That child is Jesus. He died for our sins and rose the third day, as the Scriptures said.
[narrator] The experts had the answer. The strangers found the King.

**(b) Spoken word count:** 124 (about 52–56 seconds)

**(c) KJV fragments**
- "Bethlehem… out of thee shall he come forth" (Micah 5:2)
- "In Bethlehem of Judaea" (Matthew 2:5)
- Said in plain words, not quoted: "Where is he that is born King of the Jews?" (Matthew 2:2); "the young child with Mary his mother" (Matthew 2:11); "Christ died for our sins… rose again the third day according to the scriptures" (1 Corinthians 15:3-4)

**(d) Four lines**
1. **The gap, in the viewer's words:** "Those experts knew exactly where the King would be. Did they actually go and see Him?"
2. **Where and how it is paid:** Out loud near the end: "The Bible never says they went. The strangers went, and found the child." The last line says it again: "The experts had the answer. The strangers found the King."
3. **Main take-away:** God named the town 700 years early and kept His word. Knowing where Jesus is is not the same as going to Him. That child is the one who died for our sins and rose.
4. **Why a global newcomer gets it:** Short, plain sentences, with only one old word ("thee"), and the next line says its meaning plainly. The roles are simple: experts, strangers, a king. Each line fits a picture that's already made, and "He" never comes right after Herod.
