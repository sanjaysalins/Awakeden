## SCRIPT X

1. **NEWCOMER** — WHO: partly (a baby, Mary, Joseph; "Jesus" is named only in line 2 and the last line). WHAT: no. WHY: no.
   - "What kind of king?" — no king has been named yet, so the question has nothing to hold on to.
   - "The Bible never says they went." — who is "they"? Went where? The priests were named one line before, but the point is not set up.
   - "Herod" — never introduced (who is he?).
   - "Wise men worshipped the young child with Mary, in a house." — the wise men appear from nowhere; no link to Herod or the priests.
   - "A rescuer, called the Lord." — a gloss of a quote; two hard lines in a row.
   - "This kind of king." — which kind? The script never says it.
   - "chief priests and scribes" — hard words for ESL.
2. **BIBLE READER** — Mostly the Christmas story as a list. The one fresh point (the scribes knew and did not go) is there but buried in the middle, not framed.
3. **HOOK** — "What kind of king?" I do not need an answer, because I do not know which king. Would not stay. **3/10**
4. **LANDING** — "This kind of king." does not answer the hook out loud. The death/burial/resurrection line is accurate but bolted on; nothing in the story leads to it. Last line is repeatable but only as a creed, not as this story's point. **3/10**
5. **ACCURATE + HONEST**
   - "Out of thee shall he come forth." — verbatim fragment of Micah 5:2. OK.
   - "A Saviour, which is Christ the Lord." — verbatim Luke 2:11. OK.
   - "In Bethlehem of Judaea." — verbatim Matt 2:5. OK.
   - "A rescuer, called the Lord." — drops "Christ". Small loss, not an error.
   - "where the king should be born" — KJV: "where Christ should be born". Small paraphrase.
   - "The Bible never says they went." — true and honestly worded (silence, not a claim that they stayed).
   - "died for our sins, was buried, and rose on the third day" — matches 1 Cor 15:3-4.
   - No blame on a people group. No invented detail.
6. **GRACE** — Yes. No pressure, no stock line. But it is a statement dropped at the end, not an earned landing.
7. **SCORE: 4/10** — accurate, but a newcomer cannot follow it.

## SCRIPT Y

1. **NEWCOMER** — WHO: yes (Micah, Mary, Joseph, Jesus, wise men, Herod, experts). WHAT: yes — experts knew the town and did not go; strangers went and found the child. WHY: mostly.
   - "But Jesus made the greater journey." — what journey? From where? Never said. This is the one line I would have to have explained.
   - "the experts" — experts in what? (Never says "in the Scriptures".)
   - "From it would come one / that is to be ruler in Israel." — the hand-off from narrator to old English mid-sentence is hard to parse by ear.
2. **BIBLE READER** — Yes, something new: the men who could quote the verse never made the short trip, and outsiders did. That is a real, text-true observation most people skip.
3. **HOOK** — "Did they go?" I need to know who the experts are and whether they went. Clear, two short sentences. Would stay. **8/10**
4. **LANDING** — The hook IS answered out loud ("The Bible never says they went"). Then the script turns to a second idea: "the greater journey" / "He came to us". It links by the word "went", so it is not fully bolted on, but it is a new thought in the last 8 seconds and leans on something unstated (He came from heaven). Last line is lovely and repeatable: "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us." **7/10**
5. **ACCURATE + HONEST**
   - "that is to be ruler in Israel." — verbatim Micah 5:2. OK.
   - "In Bethlehem of Judaea." — verbatim. OK.
   - "Mary and Joseph lived far away, in Nazareth." — Luke 2:4 gives Nazareth for Joseph; fair.
   - "A tax order from Caesar" — Luke 2:1. OK.
   - "Wise men from the east reached Jerusalem, asking for the newborn King." — Matt 2:1-2. OK.
   - "Jesus made the greater journey." — interpretation, not in the passages quoted. Defensible (Micah 5:2 "from everlasting"; John 6:51 "came down from heaven") but the script does not show its ground. Mild overreach by vagueness, not an error.
   - "He died for our sins. He rose again the third day." — matches 1 Cor 15; "buried" and "according to the scriptures" dropped. Fine.
   - "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us." — sound.
   - "The Bible never says they went." — honest. No blame on a people group ("the experts", not a nation).
6. **GRACE** — Yes, the best of the three here. Pure gift, no pressure, no stock line.
7. **SCORE: 7.5/10**

## SCRIPT Z

   - "A tax order sent them there" — "there" is two lines away from "one small town". Heard once, "there" sounds like "far away". Unclear.
   - "Mary and Joseph lived far away." — far from where? Nazareth not named.
   - "the experts" — experts in what?
   - "out of thee shall he come forth" — old English, but the next line explains it. OK.
2. **BIBLE READER** — Yes. Same fresh point as Y, and "as the Scriptures said" quietly ties the cross to the same theme (promise kept), which is a real gain.
3. **HOOK** — Same strong hook as Y. Would stay. **8/10**
4. **LANDING** — Answers the hook out loud, twice ("They knew. The Bible never says they went." and the last line). "That child is Jesus" is a clean reveal, and "as the Scriptures said" makes the death and rising part of the same promise-and-fulfilment story, not a second idea. Last line is easy to repeat: "The experts had the answer. The strangers found the King." Small cost: the very last words are about the strangers, and the gospel line sits one line before the end. **8/10**
5. **ACCURATE + HONEST**
   - "Bethlehem… out of thee shall he come forth." — both parts are KJV words, but the quote skips "Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet". On audio the gap is only a pause, so it sounds like one unbroken quote. Flag: an elision the listener cannot hear.
   - "In Bethlehem of Judaea." — verbatim. OK.
   - "Later, wise men from the east came" — fair ("young child", "house").
   - "found the child with Mary" — Matt 2:11. OK.
   - "He died for our sins and rose the third day, as the Scriptures said." — 1 Cor 15:3-4. OK.
   - "The Bible never says they went." — honest. "They knew." — true (Matt 2:5).
   - No blame on a people group. No invented detail.
6. **GRACE** — Yes. No pressure, no stock line. The contrast (answer vs. King) invites without telling me what to do.
7. **SCORE: 8/10**

**My #1: Z.**

**The ONE fix Z most needs (exact words):**
Change
> Mary and Joseph lived far away. A tax order sent them there, and her baby was born.

to
> Mary and Joseph lived far away, in Nazareth. A tax order sent them to Bethlehem, and there her baby was born.

(This is the line where the prophecy comes true. Right now the town is not named at the moment it happens.)

**Still confusing for a newcomer in Z:**
- "the experts" — never told they are experts in the Scriptures (the men who knew Micah's words). One word would fix it: "the Bible experts" / "the experts in the Scriptures".
- "died for our sins" — said, not explained. Normal for 60 seconds, but a total newcomer will not know what it means.
- "wise men from the east" — who they are and why they came (the star) is not said.
- The prophet line: old English ("out of thee") plus a silent cut in the quote. Either say the full clause or keep it as is and accept the flag.
