# Blind judge seat: Scripts X, Y, Z

## SCRIPT X

1. **Newcomer:** They get the "who" (a baby king born in Bethlehem). They do not get the "what" or the "why".
   - The film jumps from Micah to the manger, the angel, Herod and then a house, with nothing joining them.
   - Lines a newcomer would not understand: "What kind of king?", "scribes", "Christ the Lord" and "This kind of king."
   - "The Bible never says they went" is said, but the viewer was never told they should have gone.
2. **Bible reader:** Mostly the Christmas story again. The detail that the scribes never went is buried.
3. **Hook: 4/10.** "What kind of king?" is vague and sets no stakes. I would scroll on.
4. **Landing: 3/10.** "This kind of king" never says what kind. The death and rising line is bolted on with no link to the story. You can't repeat it to a friend.
5. **Accuracy:**
   - All the quotes are correct fragments.
   - "where the king should be born": the KJV says "where Christ should be born".
   - "A rescuer, called the Lord" drops "Christ".
   - Nothing is blamed on a people group.
6. **Grace:** No pressure, but also no warmth. It reads like a list of creed points.
7. **Overall: 4/10**

## SCRIPT Y

1. **Newcomer:** They can say who (experts, a prophet, strangers, Jesus) and what (the experts knew the place, but strangers went). The "why" is mostly clear: "He came to us."
   - Unclear words: "the greater journey" (a journey from where?) and "the strangers" (a newcomer may not link this to the wise men).
   - "He died for our sins" arrives with no "why" around it.
2. **Bible reader:** Yes, something new: knowing the answer but not going. Turning that around into "He came to us" is fresh.
3. **Hook: 8/10.** "Did they go?" is a real, clear question. I would stay.
4. **Landing: 8/10.**
   - "The Bible never says they went" answers the hook out loud.
   - "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us" closes the going/coming idea on Jesus, so the death and rising sit inside the same story.
   - The last line is easy to repeat.
5. **Accuracy:**
   - "that is to be ruler in Israel" is a correct fragment.
   - "newborn King" is a fair way to say Matthew 2:2.
   - "rose again the third day" is correct.
   - "Never says they went" is honest about what the text is silent on. It does not overclaim.
   - Only the "experts" are named, never "the Jews". No blame on a people group.
   - "Greater journey" is not grounded in any verse as it stands.
6. **Grace:** Yes. It lands on God moving first, with no stock call to action.
7. **Overall: 8/10**

## SCRIPT Z

1. **Newcomer:** Mostly clear on who and what. The "why" is weaker, because the last line is about people, not Jesus.
   - Unclear: "the strangers", and "Mary and Joseph lived far away" (far from what?).
   - "That child is Jesus" comes late. That works as a reveal, but the whole middle has a nameless baby.
2. **Bible reader:** Yes, the same fresh detail as Y. "As the Scriptures said" ties the cross back to the experts' own Scriptures, which is smart.
3. **Hook: 8/10.** Same hook as Y.
4. **Landing: 7/10.**
   - It answers the hook ("They knew… never says they went").
   - But the final words are "The experts had the answer. The strangers found the King." Jesus is sandwiched in the middle, so the film ends on a moral contrast, not on Christ.
   - Very repeatable, though.
5. **Accuracy:**
   - "Bethlehem… out of thee shall he come forth" cuts about 12 words out of Micah 5:2. On the ear it sounds like one unbroken verse, so the cut is hidden.
   - "as the Scriptures said" is fair for "according to the scriptures".
   - Ending on "the experts had the answer" risks being heard as a dig at religious leaders. It is mild, but it tilts toward blame.
6. **Grace:** No stock line, but there is an implied "which one are you?" It is a light nudge, not grace.
7. **Overall: 7/10**

## Ranking

**Y > Z > X.** My #1 is **Y**.

**The one fix for Y:** replace "But Jesus made the greater journey." with:

> "But Jesus had already come much farther. He came down from heaven."

The idea of coming down from heaven is grounded in John 6:51. It shows what the journey is, and it sets up "He came to us."

**What could still confuse a newcomer in Y:**
- "The strangers went": say "The wise men, strangers from far away, went" the first time.
- "the experts": add "the Bible experts" once.
- "He died for our sins" has no "why" around it. A short "to bring us back to God" would close the gap, but it adds time.
