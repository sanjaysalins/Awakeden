# Blind re-test: Bethlehem narration

**Overall: 7/10.** The words are true and the arc is clean. It needs one fact to make the ending hit.

## 1. Newcomer (English as a second language)
- **Who:** Jesus, a king, some Bible experts. ✅
- **What:** a prophet named the town, and Jesus was born there. The experts knew, but only the wise men went. ✅
- **Why it matters:** only half clear. "He came to us" lands. "Died for our sins" comes fast, with no reason given.

Lines that may confuse:
- **"that is to be ruler in Israel"**: a broken half-sentence in a new voice. Hard to follow by ear.
- **"the one born King"**: odd grammar for a second-language listener.
- **"Jesus had come much farther"**: farther than what, or than who? The script never says how close the experts were, so there is no "near" to compare with.
- **"Judaea"** and **"wise men"**: unclear, but not load-bearing.

## 2. Bible reader
There is something new here. Most readers miss that the scribes knew the town and the text never shows them going. That angle is fresh. But it feels thin, because the irony (how close they lived) is never spoken.

## 3. Hook: 7/10
"Did they go?" is a real question, and I'd stay. One weak spot: "the King" is undefined for a newcomer.

## 4. Landing: 6/10
- It does answer the hook: "The Bible never says they went." That is honest, and a little soft.
- "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us" can be repeated to a friend. ✅
- "Died for our sins / rose the third day" feels listed, not earned. The "coming" idea carries the landing. The cross rides along with it.

## 5. Accurate and honest: pass
- "In Bethlehem of Judaea" is word-for-word Matthew 2:5. ✅
- "that is to be ruler in Israel" is word-for-word Micah 5:2. ✅
- "came down from heaven" matches John 6:51. "died for our sins… rose again the third day" matches 1 Corinthians 15:3-4. ✅
- "about seven hundred years" is fair.
- "The Bible never says they went" is careful. It is an argument from silence, and it does not overclaim. ✅
- It says "experts", not a people group. No blame. ✅
- Small point: Herod called chief priests *and* scribes. "Experts" covers both. Fine.

## 6. Grace: pass
There is no pressure and no stock "come to Jesus" line. The last line is gift, not demand.

## The ONE fix
Say how close they were, so "much farther" pays off. After the scribe's line, add:

> **"Bethlehem was a short walk away. The Bible never says they went."**

Bethlehem is about 9 km from Jerusalem. That is true geography, but it is not in the KJV text, so keep the wording loose: "a short walk", not a number. With it, the contrast between a short walk and heaven to earth works, and the cross feels part of the same journey.

## Small extras
- Change "the one born King" to **"the newborn King"**.
- Have the narrator say "would come **a ruler**". Then the prophet's line is a short echo, not a broken half-sentence.

**My pick: add the "short walk" line. It should lift the landing to about 8. Say yes?**
