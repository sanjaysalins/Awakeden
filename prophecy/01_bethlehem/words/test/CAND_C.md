[narrator] People said Jesus could not be the promised King, because of where He came from.
[crowd] Shall Christ come out of Galilee?
[narrator] Jesus grew up in Galilee. But about seven hundred years before, Micah, a prophet, a man who spoke God's message, named a different town.
[prophet] But thou, Bethlehem... out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel.
[narrator] And that is where Jesus was born. A Roman tax order had sent Joseph and pregnant Mary there.
[narrator] The crowd knew the promise. They did not know where He was born.
(pause)
[narrator] Micah also said this ruler came "from everlasting": He lived before He was born. He came to die for our sins, and He rose the third day.
