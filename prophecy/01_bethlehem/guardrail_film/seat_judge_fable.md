## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR (one named viewer; who stops, who finishes, who shares; who is NOT served)
Named viewer: a churchgoer in Lagos or Manila who knows the Christmas story but has never noticed the experts stayed home. They stop at line 2 ("Did they go?"), finish, and share, because line 10 gives them something new to tell others. The scroller stops for the same question. Not well served: the total newcomer. They get the story of who went, but line 12 puts heaven, cross and tomb into 8 seconds with no help. The viewer who wants the prophecy itself is also underfed (see PROMISE KEPT).

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)
"The experts knew and did not go, but Jesus came all the way to us first." Line 13 carries it and it is clear. But the film has two take-aways fighting. The end card says PROMISED, THEN KEPT, yet no spoken line says the promise was kept. Most viewers will say back the "knowing is not going" one.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (include the ON-SCREEN words and pictures; who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)
Who: Bible experts, a prophet, wise men, Jesus. What: a prophet named the town 700 years early; the experts quoted it and stayed; strangers went. Why: Jesus made the longer trip, to us. The maps, hollow footprints and the music stop at line 9 do this work without words. That is the best thing in the film.
Lost or misled:
- Line 4 into 5: "a ruler would come, / that is to be ruler in Israel." Doubled "ruler", broken sentence.
- Line 7: "King Herod asked the experts where." Two kings in one breath. A newcomer cannot tell Herod from "the one born King".
- Line 12: "died for our sins" is unexplained church language. "Rose again" can read as "rose a second time" in a second language.
- "The east" and "tax order" are vague but harmless.
- Picture risk: the only people shown badly are visibly Jewish scholars, one "stern". Keep them serious, not sinister.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
- Line 1 "knew where the King would be born": RIGHT (Matthew 2:4-6).
- Line 3 "About seven hundred years earlier... a small town": RIGHT.
- Lines 4-5: RIGHT, quote is verbatim KJV.
- Line 6 "A tax order sent them": RIGHT in KJV wording (Luke 2:1-5).
- Line 7: RIGHT (Matthew 2:1-4).
- Line 8 "In Bethlehem of Judaea": RIGHT (Matthew 2:5).
- Line 9 "close by": RIGHT as geography (about 6 miles), not stated in the text.
- Line 10 "The Bible never says the experts went": RIGHT, carefully worded. The picture ("Nobody walks it") says more than the words. Slightly OVERCLAIMED in picture only.
- Line 11: RIGHT (Matthew 2:9-11).
- Line 12: RIGHT (John 6:38; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4).
- Line 13: RIGHT in substance (Romans 5:8).
- Pictures: camels, ox and donkey are tradition, UNSUPPORTED but harmless. Matthew says the wise men found the child in a house, so the stable and camels in one frame (line 7) blurs the timing.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE (what would the best explainer in the world do differently; the ONE highest-value change; score 0-10 against that bar)
The best version would make the fulfilment a spoken moment. Now the KEPT stamp flashes while line 7 already talks about wise men, so it is read by few and heard by none. ONE change: say it aloud at the stamp (fix 2 below). Bonus missed link: Micah 5:2 itself says His goings forth are "from everlasting", which is line 12's point. Score: 7/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP (opens at line 2, pays off at line 10; score 0-10; worth the wait?)
Opens at line 2, pays off at lines 10-11, deepens at 13. 7/10. Worth the wait. The answer is honest but soft ("never says"); line 11's gold footprints rescue it.

## PROMISE KEPT (in your own words: who made what promise, how long before, and what came true? did the film make it feel like a promise kept, or only a story about who went? 0-10)
God, through Micah, said a ruler would come from small Bethlehem, about 700 years before. Jesus was born there, sent by a tax order. The film shows this but does not make it felt. It is mostly a story about who went. 5/10.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES (line number + exact replacement wording)
1. Line 4: "Out of it, he said, would come one" (then line 5 unchanged).
2. Line 6: "Mary and Joseph lived far away. A tax order sent them to Bethlehem. There, Jesus was born. The very town Micah named."
3. Line 7: "Wise men from the east came asking for the one born King. Herod, the king in Jerusalem, asked the experts where."
