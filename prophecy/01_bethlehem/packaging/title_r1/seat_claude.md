# Title + thumbnail: six pairs

## 1. Six pairs

**Pair 1**
- **TITLE:** Why Was Jesus Born in Bethlehem? A 700-Year-Old Promise *(55 chars)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** THEY KNEW THE TOWN | DID THEY GO?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** The stern scholar raises one finger over the open scroll. Behind him the map's gold star glows on Bethlehem.
- **Question / payoff:** Did the people who knew actually go? The film answers at "The Bible never says the experts went."

**Pair 2**
- **TITLE:** They Knew Where Jesus Would Be Born *(35 chars)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** SO CLOSE. | DID THEY GO?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** The map fills the frame: a short road from Jerusalem to the gold star at Bethlehem, with hollow white footprints on it. The scholar's face sits small in one corner.
- **Question / payoff:** If they knew and it was near, did they go? The film answers at "Bethlehem was close by… the Bible never says the experts went."

**Pair 3**
- **TITLE:** Born in Bethlehem: A Promise Made 700 Years Early *(49 chars)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** WHO WENT?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** A split frame. On the left, scholars bent over the scroll by torchlight. On the right, wise men on camels under the stars.
- **Question / payoff:** Which group went to the King? The film answers at "The wise men went, and found the child."

**Pair 4**
- **TITLE:** King Herod and the Wise Men: Who Went to Jesus? *(47 chars)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** STRANGERS CAME. | THE EXPERTS?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Herod on his throne, pointing. The wise men's camels are small in the night behind him.
- **Question / payoff:** Strangers came from far away, so what did the experts do? The film answers at the scribe's line and "the Bible never says the experts went."

**Pair 5**
- **TITLE:** Old Testament Prophecies Jesus Fulfilled: #1 Bethlehem *(54 chars)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** ONE SMALL TOWN | NAMED EARLY
- **THUMB PICTURE:** The old prophet on the hilltop at sunset points at a far hill town. A gold star hangs over it.
- **Question / payoff:** How could anyone name a birth town ahead of time? The film answers at Micah's promise ("about seven hundred years earlier") and "God's promise came true."

**Pair 6**
- **TITLE:** The Christmas Story: Who Went to Find Jesus? *(44 chars)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** HE CAME FIRST.
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus at dawn with arms open and a warm glow. The manger panel sits as a small inset in its gold frame.
- **Question / payoff:** Who went, and who came first? The film answers at "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us."

## 2. Best pair: Pair 2

**TITLE:** They Knew Where Jesus Would Be Born
**THUMB:** SO CLOSE. | DID THEY GO?

- **Why:** The title gives the fact and the thumbnail adds the tension: they knew the town, it was near, so did they go? That is the film's own hook, in plain words.
- **Search fit:** The title is close to "where was jesus born", and the map picture is easy to read in any language.

## 3. Biggest risk

"DID THEY GO?" can make people expect a clear "no." The film's honest answer is that the Bible never says, so some viewers may feel the payoff is soft. The narrator needs to land that line clearly.
