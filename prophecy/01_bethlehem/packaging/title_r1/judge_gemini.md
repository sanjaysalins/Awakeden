It offers the best of both worlds: the title captures broad, simple search intent for newcomers, while the thumbnail introduces a compelling, character-driven mystery for those who already know the basic Christmas story. 

**WORD FIX:** Remove the period in the thumbnail text. Make it just "THEY KNEW / DID THEY GO?" (Punctuation slows down visual processing at small thumbnail sizes).

**BIGGEST RISK:** The "argument from silence" payoff. Setting up a heavy mystery around "Did they go?" and answering it with "The Bible never says they went" risks feeling slightly anti-climactic to a viewer expecting a definitive historical "No" or a hidden conspiracy, though the script's pivot to the gospel ("He came to us") lands the plane well.
