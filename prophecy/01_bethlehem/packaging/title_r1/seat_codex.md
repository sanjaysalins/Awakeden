   **THUMB WORDS:** THE BIBLE | EXPERTS  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** One large scholar’s face beside the blue-and-gold map, with Jerusalem and Bethlehem visibly close together.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Did the nearby experts make the trip? After “Bethlehem was close by,” the film states that the Bible never says they went.

3. **TITLE:** Born in Bethlehem  
   **THUMB WORDS:** PROMISED | 700 YEARS EARLIER  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** The old prophet points across a bold panel split toward the baby in the gold-framed manger.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** How could this birthplace be promised so long before? Micah names Bethlehem; the birth scene shows the promise coming true.

4. **TITLE:** Who Went to See Jesus?  
   **THUMB WORDS:** FAR AWAY. | CLOSE BY.  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Wise men on a long night road opposite scholars beside the map’s short road to Bethlehem.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** Who made the journey, and what about those nearby? The wise men find the child; the narrator explains the Bible’s silence about the experts.

5. **TITLE:** King Herod and the Wise Men  
   **THUMB WORDS:** THE ANSWER | WAS IN A SCROLL  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Herod points from his dark throne toward a brightly lit scholar holding an open scroll.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** What answer did the experts have? Herod asks where the King would be born, and the scribe names Bethlehem.

6. **TITLE:** Why Did Jesus Come to Us?  
   **THUMB WORDS:** MORE THAN | A BIRTH  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus with open arms at dawn, the glowing empty tomb behind Him.  
   **QUESTION + PAYOFF:** What was His coming for? The closing passage explains that He came from heaven, died for our sins, and rose again.

**Best: Pair 1.** The title matches a real search; the thumbnail adds the strongest mystery.  
Both questions receive clear answers in the film.

**Biggest risk:** Some viewers may read “Did they go?” as implying the experts never went, although the film carefully preserves the Bible’s silence.
