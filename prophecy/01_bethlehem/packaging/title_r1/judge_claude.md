**MY PICK: P2.** Its title and thumb are one clean unit. The title carries the search words and sets up the tension. The thumb asks the question in 3 words over a face that shows "he knew".

**One small word fix:** change the title to "Where Was Jesus Born? **The Bible Experts Knew**". A newcomer may not know which "experts" you mean, and adding "Bible" makes it clear.

**Biggest risk:** the answer is "the Bible never says". Some viewers will want a firm "NO, they didn't" and may feel dodged. The film's stop-the-music line has to land hard and early enough that "the Bible is silent" feels like the twist, not a cop-out. Watch comments and early drop-off at that moment in the first 48 hours.
