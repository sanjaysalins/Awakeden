### 1. Six Title & Thumbnail Pairs

**Pair 1**
`They knew exactly where Jesus was born`
`BUT DID | THEY GO?`
Jewish scholars in prayer shawls over an open scroll by torchlight; one stern scholar raising a finger.
*Opens the question of what the experts did with the truth; paid off when the film reveals the Bible never says they went.*

**Pair 2**
`Why was Jesus born in Bethlehem?`
`NAMED 700 | YEARS EARLY`
An old prophet with a staff on a hilltop at sunset, pointing; his face by lamplight.
*Opens the mystery of how the town was chosen so long ago; paid off by the Micah prophecy showing God promised it centuries before.*

**Pair 3**
`The map to the Christmas story`
`SO CLOSE | YET SILENT`
Hand-drawn map: Jerusalem and Bethlehem joined by a short road with a gold star.
*Opens the question of why proximity didn't matter; paid off by the contrast of the close experts staying behind while far-away strangers traveled.*

**Pair 4**
`The experts knew where to look`
`STRANGERS | FOUND HIM`
Wise men on camels at night.
*Opens the question of who actually acted on the prophecy; paid off by showing the experts had the scroll but the wise men made the journey.*

**Pair 5**
`Prophecies fulfilled by Jesus`
`THE EXACT | TOWN`
Mary and Joseph by the baby in a manger, a gold frame snapping shut around the panel.
*Opens the question of which specific promise is being discussed; paid off by the 700-year-old Micah prophecy of Bethlehem coming true.*

**Pair 6**
`They had the town in writing`
`WHO WENT | TO SEE?`
Hand-drawn map: Jerusalem and Bethlehem joined by a short road, hollow white footprints on it that turn gold.
*Asks who actually followed the written directions; paid off when the wise men go but the experts are never mentioned going.*

### 2. Best Pair
**Pair 1** (`They knew exactly where Jesus was born` / `BUT DID | THEY GO?`)
It perfectly captures the film's core irony (knowing vs. going) using universally understood words, and it directly sets up the narrator's opening hook without giving away the ending. 

### 3. Biggest Risk
Viewers might expect a historical deep-dive into the scholars' motives, but the film quickly pivots to a spiritual landing about Jesus coming to us.
