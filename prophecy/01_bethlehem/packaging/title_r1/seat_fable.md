# Seat: Fable — title + thumbnail, round 1

## 1. Six pairs

### Pair 1
- **TITLE:** Where Was Jesus Born? The Experts Knew (38 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** DID THEY GO?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Stern scholar with raised finger over the open scroll by torchlight, left; far hill town under a gold star, right; dark road between them.
- **Opens / pays off:** "They knew the town, so did they go see?" Paid at "The Bible never says the experts went. The wise men went, and found the child."

### Pair 2
- **TITLE:** They Knew Where Jesus Was Born. Who Went? (41 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** SO CLOSE | ONE SHORT ROAD
- **THUMB PICTURE:** The hand-drawn blue-and-gold map: walled Jerusalem, short road, Bethlehem with gold star, hollow white footprints on the road.
- **Opens / pays off:** "It was that near, so who walked it?" Paid at "Bethlehem was close by... The wise men went", and the footprints turning gold.

### Pair 3
- **TITLE:** Why Was Jesus Born in Bethlehem? (32 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** NAMED | 700 YEARS BEFORE
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Old prophet with staff on the sunset hilltop, pointing at the small far town; gold star over it.
- **Opens / pays off:** "Who named the town that early, and how?" Paid at the Micah beat and "There, God's promise came true: Jesus, the King, was born."

### Pair 4
- **TITLE:** King Herod and the Wise Men: Who Went to Bethlehem? (51 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** STRANGERS WENT | AND THE EXPERTS?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Split panel: wise men on camels at night riding toward the star, top; scholars bent over the scroll, not moving, bottom.
- **Opens / pays off:** "What did the men with the answer do?" Paid at the music stop: "The Bible never says the experts went."

### Pair 5
- **TITLE:** A 700-Year-Old Promise About One Small Town (43 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** IT CAME TRUE | WHO WENT?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Mary and Joseph by the manger inside the gold frame; small map road with hollow footprints leading up to the frame.
- **Opens / pays off:** "What promise, and who came to see it?" Paid at the birth beat, then "The wise men went, and found the child."

### Pair 6
- **TITLE:** Born in Bethlehem: Who Came to See the King? (44 chars)
- **THUMB WORDS:** THE EXPERTS KNEW | THE TOWN
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Scholar's finger on the scroll in torchlight, large in front; tiny wise men on camels on the night road behind, star above.
- **Opens / pays off:** "If they knew, were they the ones who came?" Paid at "The wise men went", then lifted by "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us."

## 2. Best pair: Pair 1
- The title holds a top search ("where was jesus born") and adds a small twist; the thumbnail asks the film's own hook, in the film's own big letters.
- Short, plain words; it asks, it does not accuse, so it stays honest about the Bible's silence.

## 3. Biggest risk
"DID THEY GO?" can read as "they did not go", and the film's true answer is "the Bible does not say", so some viewers may feel the pay-off is softer than the question; also "experts" is vague for a newcomer until the film shows who they are.
