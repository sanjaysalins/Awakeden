## YOUR PICK: P2

"Where Was Jesus Born? The Experts Knew" + picture A + "DID THEY GO?"

- Newcomer: every word is simple. The search question is in front.
- Bible reader: knows the town, but has likely never asked if the experts went. That is the new thing.
- Honest: the film asks this exact question in its first line and answers it straight ("the Bible never says").

Rough odds it out-taps the others: about 45% (plus or minus 15). P1 is a close second at about 30%. These are simulated viewers, not real ones.

## One small word fix

"The Experts Knew" -> "The Bible Experts Knew". A newcomer will ask "experts in what?" The film itself says "Bible experts".

## The one biggest risk

Picture A puts a stern Jewish scholar as the face of "they knew and did not go". At a glance it can read as blaming or mocking Jewish people. Keep his face thoughtful and dignified, not sneering, so the question lands on "people who know but do not go" (us too), not on a group.
