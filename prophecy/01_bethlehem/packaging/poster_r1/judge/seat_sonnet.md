YOUR PICK: Q5. The flame-Jesus is clear even in the phone-size strip, the title is readable, the scholars look respectful and not mocked, and the look is the most film-like.

One fix: in Q5, move the scholars' eyes and poses so they are not both looking up in awe. Let one look down at the scroll and one look toward the distant town. That keeps the question "did they go?" open.

Biggest risk: with Jesus glowing in the scene, a stranger may read "the experts saw Jesus" and think the answer is yes. The poster can then imply the experts went. Q1 and Q6 have the same risk, because the scholar and Jesus share the frame.
