Checks (all six): exactly one Jesus in each. Faces and hands look right (Q1 pointing hand is good; Q6 hands OK). Garbled drawn-in text: only Q2 (scribble lines on the scroll). Poster words readable at phone size on all six.

## MY PICK: Q1

Why: it is the only one where the words and the picture make ONE question. The expert's finger is on the map. Jesus stands a short step away. A stranger asks "so did he go?" The video title does not answer that. The scholar looks grave and thoughtful, not mocked, and nothing shows him refusing. It also looks most like a real film poster (big face, dark room, torch light).

Runner-up: Q3 (most honest, cleverest shape). Third: Q5 (if the owner wants Jesus BIG above all else).

## One fix (Q1)

Make Jesus about 30-40% bigger and a little brighter, so the flame glow reads at phone size. Keep him on the star. Nothing else.

## Biggest risk

Jesus reads as a small toy figure standing on a map, so at phone size the thumb sees "old man with a map" and misses the risen Jesus the owner asked for. Second risk: "EXPERTS KNEW" next to a downcast scholar can feel like blame, so the video must keep its "the Bible does not say" line clear.

Odds note: this is one simulated viewer, not real taps. My confidence Q1 beats Q3 on real taps is about 55% (plus or minus 15). A real two-poster test would settle it.
