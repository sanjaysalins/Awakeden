Checks: every poster has exactly one Jesus. Faces and hands look right on all. Only Q2 has garbled drawn-in text (scroll squiggles, minor). Words "EXPERTS KNEW / DID THEY GO?" read fine at phone size on all six.

## MY PICK: Q5

**One fix:** Make the right-hand scholar look at the scroll or the lamp, not up at Jesus. Right now it can read as "they saw Him" — which the story does not say. (Runner-up: Q1, or Q3 if you want the cleverest idea.)

**Biggest risk:** At phone size Q5 can read as "one more glowing-Jesus poster" — the scholars are small, so a stranger may miss who "they" are. Q1 keeps the expert big. Q5 relies on the glow.
