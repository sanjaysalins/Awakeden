### TWO SETS OF FOOTPRINTS

- **THE IDEA:** One short road, two sets of footprints: hollow white ones leaving the scroll that may or may not have been walked, and solid gold ones coming the other way from the flaming Jesus toward us.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** A top-down hand-drawn map tilted into a steep vertical road, like a comic splash page.
  - Top third: flat deep night blue, almost empty, a few ink stars. Clean space for type.
  - Upper middle: the risen Jesus, small-to-medium (about one fifth of the height), standing at the far end of the road where Bethlehem's gold star sits. Arms open, cream robe, the flame-like gold-and-blue glow wrapping his whole body like a tall candle flame. He is the brightest thing on the poster. No rays, no disc behind the head; the glow follows his outline.
  - Middle: the road, a pale cream ribbon with thick black ink edges, winding down in one S-curve. From Jesus downward: solid GOLD footprints, toes pointing at the viewer, getting bigger as they come.
  - Lower middle: from the bottom upward: HOLLOW WHITE outline footprints, toes pointing up the road. The two trails meet in the middle with a gap of bare road between them.
  - Bottom: an open scroll lit by one small lamp, and a scholar's hand resting on it, one finger on a line. A thoughtful face in three-quarter profile at the bottom edge, eyes lifted toward the road. Calm, dignified, thinking.
  - Palette: night blue, ink black, cream, gold. Gold appears only on Jesus, his footprints and the star. Texture: painted gouache with heavy black ink line and light paper grain.
- **LETTERING:** "EXPERTS KNEW" in the empty top third, full width, cream comic capitals with black outline. "DID THEY GO?" in gold across the bare gap of road between the two trails, slightly larger, the biggest words on the poster.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** "Why are the white footprints empty, and whose are the gold ones coming toward me?" The film answers the first at the music stop ("The Bible never says the experts went") and the second in the last lines ("Before anyone went to Him, He came to us").
- **PHONE TEST:** A bright flame-shaped figure at top, a pale road, gold dots coming down, white dots going up, three big words. The scholar can vanish and the poster still works.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink outlines, gouache colour, paper grain. A hand-drawn blue-and-gold map seen from above tilts into one pale cream road that winds in a single S-curve from the bottom of the frame to the upper middle. At the far end of the road stands the risen Jesus, small but clear, long brown hair, cream robe, arms open, a tall flame-like gold-and-blue glow wrapping his whole body like a candle flame, a small gold star and a tiny hill town at his feet. No sun rays, no halo disc behind his head. Solid gold footprints lead from him down the road toward the viewer, growing larger. From the bottom, hollow white outline footprints lead up the road. A clear empty stretch of road separates the two trails. At the very bottom, an open scroll under a small oil lamp, an old scholar in a prayer shawl in three-quarter profile, one finger on the scroll, eyes raised to the road, thoughtful and dignified. The top third is empty flat night-blue sky with a few ink stars. Palette night blue, ink black, cream, gold. Use film pictures 3, 9 and 1 as references. no lettering, no text

### THE LAMP

- **THE IDEA:** The flame of the scholar's reading lamp rises and becomes the flaming risen Jesus: the light he reads by is the one he is reading about.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** One tall central shape on near-black night blue.
  - Top quarter: empty dark blue. Clean space for type.
  - Centre, top to middle: the risen Jesus, large (half the poster height), arms open, cream robe, long brown hair. His flame-like gold-and-blue glow narrows downward into a single thin stem of fire.
  - Lower middle: that stem lands on the wick of a small clay oil lamp. The lamp sits on an open scroll.
  - Bottom third: two scholars in prayer shawls lean in from left and right, faces lit warm gold from below. One has a finger on the scroll; the other has just looked up, lips parted, noticing the light. Thoughtful, dignified, curious. Their bodies fade into ink black at the edges.
  - Far background, tiny, low on the right: a hill town with one gold star. Close by, and small.
  - Palette: ink black and night blue everywhere; gold and cream only in the flame, Jesus and the lit faces. Texture: bold ink shadow shapes, painted glow with visible brush edges, like a comic cover.
- **LETTERING:** "EXPERTS KNEW" across the top quarter, cream. "DID THEY GO?" in gold in a clean black band at the very bottom, under the scroll, full width.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** "They are holding the answer in their hands. So what did they do next?" The film answers at the music stop, then turns it on the viewer with "He came to us."
- **PHONE TEST:** One candle-flame shape with a figure inside it, two lit faces below, the words top and bottom. Reads as a single glowing icon on black.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, heavy black ink shadows, thick outlines, painted glow with visible brush strokes. Near-black night-blue background. In the lower third, an open scroll on a table with a small clay oil lamp on it. Two old Jewish scholars in prayer shawls lean in from left and right, faces lit warm gold from below, one with a finger on the scroll, the other looking up in quiet wonder, both thoughtful and dignified, bodies fading into black at the frame edges. The lamp's flame rises as a thin stem of gold-and-blue fire and widens into the risen Jesus filling the centre of the frame: long brown hair, cream robe, arms open, his whole body wrapped in a flame-like gold-and-blue glow shaped like a tall candle flame. No sun rays, no halo disc behind his head. Far in the lower right background, a tiny hill town under one small gold star. Top quarter and a bottom band left as empty dark space. Palette ink black, night blue, gold, cream. Use film pictures 9 and 1 as references. no lettering, no text

### MY #1
THE LAMP. It is one shape a thumb-sized screen can read, it puts Jesus big, and it shows knowing without going with no blame and no refusal shown.
TWO SETS OF FOOTPRINTS is the stronger story poster, but its small details may soften at 2 cm.
