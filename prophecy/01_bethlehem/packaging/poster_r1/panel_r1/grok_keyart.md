### THE FINGERTIP TOWN
- THE IDEA in one sentence (the graphic trick): One thoughtful scholar’s finger rests on a painted map where Jerusalem and Bethlehem are a fingernail apart, and the risen Jesus already stands on the gold star.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Vertical 9:16, one painted comic panel with thick black ink edges. Top quarter is an empty cream band. Below it, night blue. One older scholar in a prayer shawl leans over a huge scroll by warm torchlight. His face is calm, eyes on the page, mouth closed. The scroll becomes the blue-and-gold map: walled Jerusalem under his fingertip, a short unmarked ink road, Bethlehem as a gold star. No footprints, no turned back, no door. On the star, small but clear, the risen Jesus at dawn, arms open, long brown hair, cream robe. A flame-like gold-and-blue glow wraps his whole body, hem to hair. It hugs him. It is not a disc and not rays behind the head. The empty road is silence, not a verdict. Brush texture, ink line, gold only on the star and the flame.
- LETTERING: The cream band carries both lines, stacked, heavy comic caps, almost full width: EXPERTS KNEW / DID THEY GO? Nothing else sits there.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: A stranger sees a man who has the place under his finger, and a glowing King already on that spot. Did knowing become going? The film answers: the experts named Bethlehem, it was close, and the Bible never says they went. Then the larger answer: before anyone went to him, he came from heaven.
- PHONE TEST: Three shapes survive at 2 cm: a face looking down, a finger, a gold-blue flame on a gold dot. The cream band still reads as the caption block.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink borders, visible brush, night blue, ink black, cream, warm gold. One dignified older scholar in a prayer shawl, thoughtful closed-mouth face, torchlight, leaning over a large scroll that becomes a hand-drawn blue-and-gold map. His finger touches walled Jerusalem. A short unmarked road leads to a gold star over Bethlehem. No footprints, no one walking away, no door. Small risen Jesus stands on the star at dawn, arms open, long brown hair, cream robe, flame-like gold-and-blue glow wrapped around his whole body, no halo, no sunburst, no rays behind the head. Empty cream band across the top quarter. Use film pictures 1, 3, and 9 as references. no lettering, no text.

### THE LONG ARRIVAL
- THE IDEA in one sentence (the graphic trick): A postage-stamp map shows the town was next door, while the risen Jesus is the whole poster, so the trip looks too small for the person who arrived.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Vertical 9:16 comic key art. The lower two-thirds is the risen Jesus large, coming toward us through a simple dawn garden, arms open, cream robe, brown hair. The same flame-like gold-and-blue glow wraps his whole body, low like firelight on cloth, not a crown of rays. Dark leaves, one pale stone, cream dawn sky. Upper left, a small tilted inset with a heavy black border: only the blue-and-gold map, Jerusalem, a short road, Bethlehem’s gold star. The road is empty. No people, no footprints. The rest of the sky is clean cream, and his head sits low so the empty area never rings his head.
- LETTERING: Two stacked lines in the open cream sky, upper right, large, clear of the inset and clear of his hair: EXPERTS KNEW / DID THEY GO?
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: Why is the King huge and the road tiny? Who knew, and did they go? The film answers with Micah’s promise, the experts’ reply, the Bible’s silence, the wise men who went, and Jesus, who came farther than all of them.
- PHONE TEST: The cream robe and the body flame read first. The gold dot inside the tiny map reads second. The empty sky reads as a place for two words.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, thick black ink line, night blue, ink black, cream, warm gold. Large risen Jesus at dawn in a simple garden, walking toward us, arms open, long brown hair, cream robe, flame-like gold-and-blue glow wrapped around his whole body like firelight, no halo, no sunburst, no rays centred behind the head. His head sits in the lower two-thirds. Upper left, a small tilted comic inset of a hand-drawn blue-and-gold map: walled Jerusalem, a short empty road, Bethlehem marked by a gold star, no people and no footprints. Broad empty cream sky above him for later type. Use film pictures 3 and 9 as references. no lettering, no text.

### MY #1
THE FINGERTIP TOWN. The gap is a distance you can see: the answer is under a finger, the road is unmarked, and Jesus is already on the star. It still reads at phone size, and it does not decide a question the Bible leaves open.
