### THE LIVING SCROLL
- THE IDEA: The prophecy literally comes alive off the parchment, stepping toward the viewer while the expert merely studies it.
- WHAT YOU SEE: An extreme close-up of a massive, unrolled parchment scroll taking up the lower two-thirds of the frame. In the deep ink-black and night-blue background at the top, a dignified, bearded scholar looks down at the scroll, his face lit by a warm, magical light. On the scroll itself, stepping out of the flat 2D parchment into 3D space, is the brilliantly glowing risen Jesus (cream robe, blue-and-gold flame aura enveloping his body). 
- LETTERING: Placed in the top third, hovering in the dark blue negative space next to the scholar's head. Huge, chunky yellow comic-book letters.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: Why is the scholar just watching this miracle happen on the page without moving? The film answers this by revealing the experts knew the exact prophecy but stayed in Jerusalem, while Jesus crossed heaven and earth to reach us.
- PHONE TEST: A giant glowing man stepping out of a bright, textured scroll, watched by a giant, thoughtful face in the dark.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Bold painted comic-book key-art style. A giant unrolled parchment scroll fills the bottom foreground. A dignified, thoughtful Jewish scholar in a prayer shawl looks down at the scroll from the dark night-blue background (Reference 1). Stepping out of the flat parchment into 3D space is the risen Jesus in a cream robe, surrounded by a flame-like gold-and-blue glow around his whole body, no sunburst rays (Reference 9). The scholar's face is illuminated by Jesus's golden glow. Deep ink black shadows. Vertical 9:16. No lettering, no text.

### THE DISTANCE
- THE IDEA: A stark comic-panel split contrasting the scholar's stillness in the dark with the explosive, world-changing arrival of the risen Christ.
- WHAT YOU SEE: A dramatic diagonal composition divided by a thick, jagged ink-black comic gutter. Top left: A tight close-up of a dignified scholar's face in deep night-blue, looking thoughtfully toward the right. Bottom right: The empty tomb glowing from inside, with the risen Jesus stepping out in a massive, radiant burst of gold-and-blue flame-like light, arms open to the viewer.
- LETTERING: Anchored directly inside the thick black diagonal gutter that separates the two scenes, acting as the bridge between the two panels.
- THE CURIOSITY GAP: Who is the man in the dark, and what does his knowledge have to do with the glowing figure emerging from the tomb? The film connects the dots: the experts had the information, but Jesus took the action.
- PHONE TEST: A dark blue top half with a quiet face, a blindingly bright gold bottom half with a glowing figure, violently split by a thick black line.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Bold painted comic-book key-art style. A diagonal split-screen divided by a thick, jagged ink-black comic gutter. Top left panel: Close-up of a dignified, thoughtful Jewish scholar's face in deep night-blue shadows, looking quietly to the side (Reference 1). Bottom right panel: An empty stone tomb glowing from inside (Reference 8), with the risen Jesus stepping out toward the viewer, wearing a cream robe and surrounded by a flame-like gold-and-blue glow around his whole body, no sunburst rays (Reference 9). Vertical 9:16. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1
THE LIVING SCROLL is the winner because it visualizes the exact theological hook of the script in one seamless image. 
It perfectly captures the irony of studying the Word while missing the Word made flesh, reading instantly even at thumbnail size.
