### THE ROAD IS THE QUESTION
- **THE IDEA:** A gold road forms the sweeping hook of a giant question mark; the risen Jesus replaces its dot.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The upper quarter is clean night blue. Below, a broad, hand-painted gold road curls through ink-black terrain into a single unmistakable question-mark shape. This is a symbolic map, not a scene claiming who travelled. At the bottom centre stands Jesus, nearly one-third of the poster’s height: long brown hair, cream robe, open arms, gold-and-blue flames tracing his whole body. His glow supplies the visual weight of the “dot” without becoming a circular light. Thick ink edges and restrained paper grain keep everything bold.
- **LETTERING:** Two centred lines in the clear upper quarter: “EXPERTS KNEW” in cream; “DID THEY GO?” in larger gold Bangers-style lettering. Both span almost the full width.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** They knew the destination—what does the record say they did? The film addresses this when the music stops: “The Bible never says the experts went.” The question remains honest; the wise men’s journey and Jesus’s coming give it significance.
- **PHONE TEST:** Two lines of type, one enormous gold question-road, one unmistakable cream-robed Jesus inside blue-and-gold flame. No small buildings or facial details carry the idea.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 film-release poster, bold painted comic-book key-art style. Use film pictures 3, 6 and 9 as references. Reserve the upper 26 percent as clean flat night-blue negative space for later typography. Below it, create one broad gold road whose curved hook and descending stroke form the upper portion of an enormous question mark, seen as a simplified overhead map against ink-black and deep-blue terrain. Make the road shape immediately legible, with thick black ink contours and minimal texture. At bottom centre, replacing the question mark’s dot, place the clearly recognizable risen Jesus from picture 9, occupying approximately 30 percent of the total image height, long brown hair, cream robe, open arms. Surround his entire body with irregular flame-like gold-and-blue light, strongest along robe, arms and feet; no circular halo, no sunburst, no rays centred behind his head. Symbolic graphic composition, dramatic scale, generous separation between shapes, cream and warm-gold highlights, subtle painted-paper grain, no lettering, no text

### THE JOURNEY IN HIS HAND
- **THE IDEA:** The entire journey between two towns fits inside the enormous open palm of the risen Jesus.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** Clean ink blue across the top, then Jesus emerging at monumental scale, his thoughtful, welcoming face clearly visible. One hand reaches forward in strong foreshortening, filling the lower half. Resting across his palm is a tiny blue-and-gold map: two simple town symbols connected by one very short road. His other arm opens outward. Gold-and-blue flame follows his whole figure and curls around the supporting wrist. The contrast is the image: a small earthly distance held by the One who came from heaven.
- **LETTERING:** The upper quarter holds “EXPERTS KNEW” over “DID THEY GO?” in heavy cream comic lettering. Make the second line about 20 percent larger. Keep the map free of labels.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** If the destination was so close, why does the question matter—and why is Jesus holding the journey? The film establishes Bethlehem’s proximity, acknowledges Scripture’s silence, then turns: “But Jesus came much farther.”
- **PHONE TEST:** A recognizable Jesus, an enormous open hand, and a tiny gold connection between two blue town shapes. The distance comparison survives even when the map’s detail disappears.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 film-release poster, bold painted comic-book key-art style. Use film pictures 3 and 9 as references. Reserve the upper 25 percent as uncluttered deep night-blue space for typography added later. Beneath it, portray the risen Jesus from picture 9 at monumental scale, clearly recognizable long brown hair, dignified welcoming expression, cream robe, visible face and torso. His right hand extends toward the viewer in dramatic anatomically convincing foreshortening, palm upward, dominating the lower half. Across that palm rests a miniature flat blue-and-gold illustrated map, with exactly two simplified town symbols connected by a very short broad gold road. Treat this as a deliberate symbolic scale illusion. His other arm opens outward. Irregular gold-and-blue flames follow his entire visible silhouette and supporting wrist; no circular halo, no sunburst rays behind his head. Strong black ink contours, broad cream shapes, warm-gold highlights, restrained brush texture, minimal background, immediately readable at thumbnail size, no lettering, no text

### MY #1
**THE ROAD IS THE QUESTION:** the central image asks the thumbnail’s question before anyone reads it.  
Jesus completes the graphic, giving the mystery a clear emotional and spiritual centre.
