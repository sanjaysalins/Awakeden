### THE SHORTEST ROAD

- **THE IDEA:** Two roads on one map. The experts' road is a short stub that nobody has walked yet. Jesus's road is huge: a gold trail falling from the top of the frame all the way down to Bethlehem.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The whole poster is the hand-drawn blue-and-gold map (picture 3), turned vertical.
  - **Top third:** deep night-blue sky with ink-hatched clouds. The risen Jesus stands small but sharp (about 18% of frame height), arms open, cream robe. The flame-like gold-and-blue glow wraps his whole body; it does not burst behind his head.
  - **From his feet:** a thick gold trail of footprints sweeps down the full height of the poster in one long S-curve.
  - **Middle:** walled Jerusalem in ink and blue wash.
  - **Bottom:** Bethlehem, small, with a gold star. Between the two towns runs a very short road, about a thumb's width. On it sit hollow white footprints, outlined but never filled in. They don't point toward or away from anything; they just wait.
  - **Texture:** aged parchment, black ink gutters as a frame, gold leaf only on the long trail and the star.
- **LETTERING:**
  - "EXPERTS KNEW" goes top-left over the dark sky, beside Jesus, never covering him. About 12% of the height, cream Bangers-style letters with a black outline.
  - "DID THEY GO?" goes in the bottom band below Bethlehem, on clean parchment. Bigger, about 14%, in gold.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Why are the footprints on the tiny road empty, while the giant road is gold? Who walked the long way? The film answers at the line "The Bible never says the experts went," then pays it off with "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us."
- **PHONE TEST:** At 2 cm wide you still see a glowing figure at the top, one gold line running down, and a short pale stub at the bottom. Long gold against short white reads as an instant contrast.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art. A hand-drawn blue-and-gold parchment map fills the whole frame, with black ink gutter border. Top third: night-blue sky with ink-hatched clouds; a small, clear figure of the risen Jesus, long brown hair, cream robe, arms open, a flame-like gold-and-blue glow around his whole body (no rays behind his head, no halo). From his feet a thick trail of gold-leaf footprints sweeps down the full height of the poster in one long S-curve. Middle: walled Jerusalem drawn in ink and blue wash. Bottom: small hill town of Bethlehem with one gold star. Between Jerusalem and Bethlehem, a very short road with hollow white outlined footprints, unfilled. Clean empty sky at top-left and clean parchment band at the bottom for type. Palette: night blue, ink black, gold, cream. References: film pictures 3, 9, 6. No lettering, no text.

### THE LIGHT ON THE PAGE

- **THE IDEA:** A scholar's finger rests on the open scroll, and the scroll itself glows. That light comes from a tiny risen Jesus standing at the far end of the unrolled scroll.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:**
  - **Top half:** a close, low-angle portrait of one elder scholar in a prayer shawl, lit from below. His face is thoughtful and dignified: eyes down, brow gently raised, lips closed. It is a man who has just understood something. One finger rests on the scroll; it does not point and is not raised.
  - **Bottom half:** the scroll unrolls down toward the viewer. Its parchment turns into a road (a nod to picture 6) that runs to a tiny hill town. At the very end stands the risen Jesus, small and bright, arms open, with the flame glow around his body. The gold light rolls back up the scroll and is what lights the scholar's face.
  - **Texture:** torchlit painted oils with heavy ink lines, deep night-blue shadows, and gold only where the light falls.
- **LETTERING:**
  - "EXPERTS KNEW" runs across the very top, above his head, on the dark background. About 11% of the height, in cream.
  - "DID THEY GO?" sits on a flat dark band across the scroll's mid-section, between his hand and Jesus. About 13%, in gold.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** He clearly knows, and the light is already on his face. So did he get up? And who is waiting at the end of the road? The film answers with the silence beat, then the reveal that Jesus came to us. It's a question about all of us, not about him.
- **PHONE TEST:** At 2 cm wide you see a warm-lit face up top, a glowing dot of a figure at the bottom, and a gold band joining them. Face plus light source makes a strong two-point read.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 bold painted comic-book key art, black ink outlines, chiaroscuro. Top half: close low-angle portrait of an elder Jewish scholar in a prayer shawl, face thoughtful and dignified, eyes lowered, brow gently raised, calm closed lips, one finger resting softly on an open scroll. His face is lit warm gold from below by light rising off the scroll. Bottom half: the scroll unrolls toward the viewer and becomes a winding night road to a small hill town. At the far end of the road stands a small, clear risen Jesus, long brown hair, cream robe, arms open, a flame-like gold-and-blue glow around his whole body (no rays behind his head, no halo). His light travels up the scroll to the scholar. Deep night-blue darkness around the figures. Clean dark space at the very top and a plain dark band across the middle for type. Palette: night blue, ink black, gold, cream. References: film pictures 1, 9, 6. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1 and why

**THE SHORTEST ROAD.** It shows the whole film's twist in one shape: the short road waits while the long road is walked. It reads at 2 cm and it blames no one.
