## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR (one named viewer; who stops, who finishes, who shares; who is NOT served)
Named viewer: Ana, 24, São Paulo, English second language, knows Christmas but not Micah.
- **Stops:** scrollers and churchgoers, on line 2 ("Did they go?"). It is a real question with a yes/no answer.
- **Finishes:** anyone who reaches the map at line 6. The pace is good.
- **Shares:** Bible readers. Lines 11-12 (knew but did not go) is a sermon point they can pass on.
- **NOT served:** the total newcomer at line 13. Heaven, "died for our sins" and rising again go by in 8 seconds with no reason given.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)
"The experts knew where Jesus was and did not go, but Jesus came all the way to us." Line 14 carries it. It is clear, but it competes with a second takeaway, "the promise came true" (line 7 and the end card). The film has two endings. The spoken one is about going. The printed one is about promise.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (include the ON-SCREEN words and pictures; who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)
- **Who:** scholars, a prophet, Mary and Joseph, foreign visitors, Jesus.
- **What:** a town was named 700 years early; Jesus was born there; the scholars stayed home.
- **Why:** God comes to people first.

Where I got lost or misled:
- Line 7, "Jesus, the King, was born": the picture cuts to "a king on a throne, close". A newcomer reads that man as Jesus or as the promised King. It is Herod, who is not named until line 8.
- Line 8, "Wise men reached Jerusalem": the film never says who they are or that they came from far away. So line 13, "But Jesus came much farther", has nothing to be farther than. The only distance on screen is line 10, "close by".
- Line 13, "died for our sins": no bridge for a newcomer.

Words that may fail in translation: "tax order" (line 6) is fine, "ruler in Israel" is fine, and "Bible experts" is good.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
- Line 1, "experts knew where": RIGHT (Matthew 2:4-6).
- Line 3, "About seven hundred years earlier... small town": RIGHT (Micah 5:2, "little among the thousands of Judah").
- Line 5, "ruler in Israel": RIGHT, KJV wording.
- Line 6, "A tax order sent them": RIGHT (Luke 2:1-5, KJV "taxed").
- Line 9, "In Bethlehem of Judaea": RIGHT (Matthew 2:5). The text says "they said", so the tag A SCRIBE is acceptable.
- Line 10, "close by": RIGHT as geography (about 6 miles), not as a Bible statement.
- Line 11, "The Bible never says the experts went": RIGHT and carefully worded. The hollow footprints and "DID THEY GO?" push it toward "they did not go", so the picture is slightly OVERCLAIMED.
- Line 12, "found the child": RIGHT (Matthew 2:11). The picture at line 7 puts the wise men beside the manger night; Matthew has a "house" and a "young child". Mildly UNSUPPORTED, as are the camels, ox and donkey (tradition, not text).
- Line 13: RIGHT (John 6:38; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4).
- Line 14: RIGHT (compare 1 John 4:19).

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE (what would the best explainer in the world do differently; the ONE highest-value change; score 0-10 against that bar)
The best explainer would set up one contrast early: far travellers went, near experts stayed, and Jesus came farthest. The film skips the first leg.

**ONE change:** say at line 8 that the wise men came from far away. That makes lines 10, 12 and 13 work.

Score: 7/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP (opens at line 2, pays off at line 11; score 0-10; worth the wait?)
Opens at line 2, pays off at line 11, lands at line 12. Score 7/10. Worth the wait: the one-second silence helps. But "never says" is an honest half-answer to a yes/no question, and 38 seconds is a long hold.

## PROMISE KEPT (in your own words: who made what promise, how long before, and what came true? did the film make it feel like a promise kept, or only a story about who went? 0-10)
God, through Micah, promised about 700 years ahead that a ruler would come from little Bethlehem. Jesus was born there. The dashed frame turning gold (line 7) is the best device in the film. But the feeling peaks at line 7 and the story then becomes "who went". The end card "PROMISED. CAME TRUE." arrives after a line about something else. Score 6/10.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES (line number + exact replacement wording)
1. **Line 8:** "Wise men from a far country came to Jerusalem, asking where this King was. King Herod asked the experts."
2. **Line 7 (picture only):** remove the throned king from under "Jesus, the King, was born". Start Herod at 27.3 s with a name tag: HEROD.
3. **Line 10:** "Bethlehem was close by. About two hours on foot."
