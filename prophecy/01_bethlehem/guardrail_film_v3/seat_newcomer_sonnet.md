## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
A curious phone viewer, say a 25-year-old in Manila or Lagos who likes story shorts. Stops at line 1-2 ("Did they go?"): a clear question, strong pictures. Finishes if the "never says they went" twist (line 11) feels worth it. Shares? Weakly. There is no simple line to pass on. Not served: anyone who needs to know who "Jesus", "God" or "the King" is. The film never says why a King matters.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
My guess: "A very old message said a King would be born in a small town, and it came true; the experts knew but did not go, and the King came to us anyway." Line 14 carries the end, line 7 carries "came true". It is mostly clear, but I am not sure the film wants me to feel "the experts failed" or "God kept a promise". Two ideas compete.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: experts (scholars), a prophet called Micah, Mary, Joseph, Herod (a king), wise men, Jesus. What: someone said long ago a King would come from Bethlehem. Why: unclear, because I do not know why a King matters or why anyone should go.
Lost or misled:
- Line 1: "the King". Which king? Not explained until line 7. I held two kings (the promised one, and Herod, line 8) and mixed them up.
- Line 3: "a man who spoke God's message". Good help. But I do not know "God" as a person here.
- Line 7: "Jesus, the King, was born." The first time I hear the name. Why is a baby a King?
- Line 8: "wise men" appeared in pictures at 22s before the words explain them (line 8). I did not know who they were or why they came.
- Line 9 "Bethlehem of Judaea": hard words, no explanation of Judaea.
- Line 11: "The Bible never says". I do not know what the Bible is. Is it a book? A strong line, but it points to a book I have not been told about.
- Line 13: "died for our sins", "rose from the dead". Sudden. Sins? Why did He die? I was TOLD it but could not follow it.
- Line 14: "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us." Poetic, nice. But "went" is a long way from line 11 for a second-language ear.
Hard in translation: "ruler", "promise came true", "sins", "experts" (OK), "third day".
Pictures help a lot: the map, gold footprints, the dashed frame turning gold. Without the words, the dashed-then-gold frame is the best explainer.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. What I was told that I could not follow: line 5 "ruler in Israel" (what is Israel?), line 7 "Jesus, the King" (why King?), line 13 "died for our sins" and "rose from the dead" (no cause given), "third day", and the on-screen tags MATTHEW 2:5, MICAH 5:2 (I do not know what these numbers mean).

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would give me one simple reason, early, why this King matters to me, and would say what the Bible is in four words ("an ancient book of God's words"). It would keep the "never says they went" twist, because that is the hook. The ONE highest-value change: line 13 needs one plain sentence on why He came ("to fix what we broke with God"), because the ending is the whole point and now it lands as a list of facts. Score: 6/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 2 ("Did they go?"). Pays off at line 11 (the Bible never says), then partly at line 12 (wise men went) and line 14. Score 7/10. Worth the wait, but the answer is "we do not know", which feels small after the big question. The real payoff (line 14) answers a different question.

## PROMISE KEPT
A prophet called Micah, speaking God's message, said about 700 years before that a King would come from small Bethlehem. Then Mary and Joseph were sent there by a tax order and Jesus was born. That came true. The film does show it: the dashed frame turning solid gold, the "CAME TRUE" tab and the end card "PROMISED. CAME TRUE." But the film spends more time on who went (experts vs wise men), so the promise feels like the setup and "who went" like the real story, until the last line. Score 6/10.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 1: "The Bible experts knew where the King would be born." becomes "The Bible, God's old book, said where a King would be born. The experts knew."
2. Line 13: add after "He died for our sins": "He took the blame for what we did wrong." before "On the third day, He rose".
3. Line 7: "Jesus, the King, was born." becomes "Jesus was born, the King God promised."
