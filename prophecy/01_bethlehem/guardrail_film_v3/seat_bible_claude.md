## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** a churchgoer aged 25 to 45 who knows the Christmas story. Line 2 ("Did they go?") is a question they have never asked, so they stop.
- **Who finishes:** the same person. The answer comes in line 11, then the twist comes in lines 12 to 14.
- **Who shares:** small-group leaders in Advent. Line 14 is a ready-made sermon hook.
- **Who is NOT served:** a total newcomer. Line 1 says "the King", but nobody has said who the King is. The wise men in line 8 arrive with no reason given, because the star is never spoken.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"The experts knew where Jesus would be born and didn't go. But Jesus came all the way to us."
Line 14 carries it, and it lands. The "promise" half (lines 3 to 7) is weaker and reads as setup.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
**In my own words:** God told Micah a ruler would come from tiny Bethlehem. Centuries later Jesus was born there. Foreign visitors travelled far to find Him. The local experts knew the address and stayed put. Jesus came farther still, from heaven.

**Where it fails a newcomer:**
- **Line 8, "Wise men reached Jerusalem":** why? No star, no "from the east" (Matthew 2:2). The gold star on the map is too small to explain it.
- **Line 8, "Herod... asked the experts":** why does a king care? "He was troubled" (Matthew 2:3) is the missing tension.
- **Line 13, "came much farther":** a distance pun. Second-language viewers may hear only "farther".
- **Translation:** "Judaea" is fine because it is a quote. "Tax order" is clear.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would let Micah's own verse pay off the ending. Micah 5:2 says this ruler's "goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting." That is the prophecy itself saying He comes from beyond Bethlehem. Right now line 13 imports John 6:38 when Micah already set it up.

**ONE change:** put "from everlasting" into line 13.

**Score: 7/10.**

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 2 and pays off at lines 11 and 12, then turns again at line 14. Line 11's honest "never says" could feel flat, but line 12's gold footprints rescue it. **7/10.** Worth the wait.

## PROMISE KEPT
God, speaking through Micah about 700 years before, promised a ruler from little Bethlehem. Jesus was born there. The film states this in line 7, but from line 8 on it becomes a story about who went. The gold frame snapping shut in line 7 helps, and "CAME TRUE" on the end card helps. **6/10.**

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **Line 13:** "But Jesus came much farther. Micah said He was 'from everlasting.' He came down from heaven. He died for our sins. On the third day, He rose from the dead."
2. **Line 8:** "Wise men followed a star to Jerusalem, asking where the new King was. King Herod was troubled. He asked the experts."
3. **Line 6:** "Mary and Joseph lived far away, in Nazareth. A tax order sent them to Bethlehem, King David's town." (Luke 2:4)
