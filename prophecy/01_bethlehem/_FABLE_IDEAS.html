Fable's ideas for Bethlehem

Design only. $0 so far.

1. Music: "the tune that waits"

Felt piano + cello. A short 4-note tune that stops before the end.

11 s "Bethlehem" — tune plays, does not finish.

23 s the birth — same tune, still not finished.

36–40 s the experts do not go — music stops. Silence.

40 s "The wise men went" — music walks again.

55 s "He came to us." — music out.

57 s the tune finally finishes. Promise kept.

Cost: about $1.20 a try. Maybe 2 tries.

2. Sounds

9 sounds. Most are free: fire, coins, footsteps, sheep, birds.

No paper or pen sounds in our library yet. One small pack for about $0.40 (scroll, map, pen, camels). It can be reused for every prophecy episode.

3. Three weak spots ($0 to fix)

a. The word box covers Herod's eyes. "GO?" is on screen too briefly to read.

b. "PROMISED, THEN KEPT" is too small. Make it big — it's the series idea.

c. The orange fire + blue glow round Jesus clashes with the ink look. Soften for $0, or redraw for about $0.35.

4. Recipe for every prophecy episode

Same every time: the waiting tune, the time scroll, the prophet's words on a scroll, "hollow then gold", silence at the turn, a big "PROMISED, THEN KEPT" end.

New each time: one drawn idea that fits the prophecy (a place = map, a price = coins, a family = family tree). Plus a new hook and landing.

My pick: first fix a + b + soften c ($0). Then make the music (about $1.20, max $2.50). Say yes?
