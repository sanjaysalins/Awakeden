Bethlehem: a fresh angle

6 blind seats found angles. 6 new blind judges scored them. Every quote checked word for word in the KJV. $0.

Two winners (in every judge's top 3)

BEST FIT

A. The half of Micah 5:2 we never hear

Micah says Bethlehem. Then the same verse says the King's goings forth are "from of old, from everlasting."

So He did not begin in Bethlehem. He arrived there.

It makes our ending, "He came down from heaven," true from the very verse the film is about.

#1 for Fable, Codex, Gemini. New pictures: none. Lines changed: 2 to 4.

MOST NEW

B. They used Bethlehem against Him

30 years later, a crowd argues about Jesus: "Shall Christ come out of Galilee? ... out of the town of Bethlehem" (John 7:41-42).

They had the right verse. They thought He was from Galilee. He was born in Bethlehem.

A second "they knew the verse and missed Him", so it sharpens our hook.

#1 for Claude, Grok, Sonnet. New: 1 crowd shot + 1 short crowd voice line.
