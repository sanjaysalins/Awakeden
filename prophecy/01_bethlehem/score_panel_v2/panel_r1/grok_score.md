1. SCORE IDEA

A wide, slow orchestral promise in G major: the same four rising notes hang open twice, and only the last three seconds let them come home.

Instruments: full string section (violins, violas, cellos, basses), four French horns in the middle register, and timpani used at three turns only. No piano, no solo feature, no voices.

The promise tune: G, B, D, E, and hold the E. A child can hum it. It asks, and it does not answer yet. The completing answer, heard once, is C, B, G.

2. WHY IT FITS

- The hanging E is the dashed promise frame. The final C–B–G is that frame redrawn solid and finished. Same tune, two states.
- Full strings and horns match big ink and full-page panels. The comic booms stay in sound design. The score stays large and quiet.
- G major, slow, and wordless reads at once for a new viewer: a promise left open, then a homecoming at dawn.

4. SPLIT

Split at 39.00 seconds. Part 1 is 0.00–39.00 and ends in the true one-second silence. Part 2 is 39.00–59.50. The join is silence, so no crossfade and no smeared chord. Part 2 is short, so the swell and the finished tune are less likely to fall into dead air.

5. MUSIC PROMPTS

Part 1 (paste as one prompt):

39-second cinematic orchestral cue, Adagio around 68 bpm, G major, graceful and serious. Full string section and four soft French horns, timpani only once. No vocals, no choir, no lyrics, no piano, no solo cello, no cymbals, no snare, no rock drums, no trailer horn stab, no Middle-Eastern scale, no gaps except the marked silences. Keep a soft string bed moving at all other times. From the start: low full strings and a quiet horn pedal, wide and warm, no accents. Around 5 seconds in: one slow distant timpani roll as the strings broaden, then the drum leaves. Around 13 seconds in: unison violins play a simple hummable phrase, the notes G B D E, and hold the E unresolved, the section supporting quietly. Around 16 seconds in: drop to a barely audible string pedal for about 2 seconds, then return. Around 18 seconds in: a dignified walking pulse in low strings, smooth bows, no loud attacks. Around 23 seconds in: the same unfinished G B D E phrase again, warmer, soft horns underneath, still hanging on E, no big swell. Around 28 seconds in: thin and cool, low strings only, calm, no brass, no drums. Around 34 seconds in: nearly out for about 3 seconds. Around 38 seconds in: fade to absolute silence and stay silent to the end.

Part 2 (paste as one prompt):

20.5-second cinematic orchestral cue, G major, the continuation of a promise theme. Full strings, French horns, one timpani roll only at the swell. No vocals, no choir, no lyrics, no piano, no cymbals, no snare, no rock drums, no trailer horn stab, no Middle-Eastern scale. Begin at the edge of silence with one thin held string, hollow and still, for about 3 seconds. Around 3 seconds in: a purposeful legato string pulse near 80 bpm and soft middle-register horns, noble and warm, no impact and no accent. Around 5 seconds in: violins play G B D E again and hold the E, still unresolved, in a warmer glow. Around 8 seconds in: all pulse, drums, and horns leave. High slow strings only, hushed and tender, spacious, through around 13 seconds in. Around 16 seconds in: fall to near silence for just over one second. Around 18 seconds in: complete the tune at last, G B D E then the answering notes C B G, full strings with one horn on the answer, land on a warm G major chord, let it ring and slowly soften through the end.
