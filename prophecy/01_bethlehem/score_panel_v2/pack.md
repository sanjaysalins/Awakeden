# FILM: "Born in Bethlehem" (Micah 5:2 -> Matthew 2 / Luke 2) - 59.5 s vertical gospel short, worldwide audience
First of a new series: Old Testament prophecies fulfilled in Jesus. Many viewers know nothing about the Bible; many have English as a second language.
LOOK: bold inked graphic-comic pages (clean black ink, rich colour), 3-panel comic pages with slow camera pans, comic grammar on top:
panel SLAMS with speed lines and a page shake on key words, WHIPS between pages, an inked night-blue map PANEL with hollow footprints that later turn gold,
a dashed 'promise panel' frame that is redrawn in solid gold when the promise comes true, big comic lettering for 'DID THEY GO?' and 'WENT'.
KEY PICTURES: 0-4 s experts round a scroll by torchlight; 4-6 s time scroll rolls back 700 years; 9-14 s the prophet Micah;
15.8-17.8 s Micah's words on a parchment scroll; 17.8-27.8 s map: Mary and Joseph walk south to Bethlehem, the birth in a manger,
camels from the east; 27.8-33.4 s King Herod and the experts; 33.4-37.4 s map: Bethlehem close to Jerusalem, footprints appear
HOLLOW (nobody walked); 37.4-40.5 s the empty road; 40.5-43.5 s footprints turn GOLD, Mary and the child; 43.5-51.7 s heaven,
cross, open tomb (sacred, calm); 51.7-59.5 s HERO: risen Jesus at dawn, arms open (hold to the end).
Voices: one warm narrator, plus two short KJV lines (an old prophet voice, a scribe voice). Voice is final; music sits UNDER it.
Planned pauses: ~38.0-39.0 s (1 s silence after "Bethlehem was close by.") and a short beat ~54.6-55.0 s before "He came to us."
Comic SLAMS (each gets a low boom in the sound design, not in the music): ~0.94 'knew', ~3.58 'GO?', ~19.86 'tax', ~31.02 'Herod', ~42.80 'WENT'.
Score tool: ElevenLabs Music free-text prompt (music_v1). Long single generations can render dead air, so a score is made in TWO
parts joined at a split second; each prompt can use "around N seconds in" cues. Never use composition_plan. Budget ~2 generations.
