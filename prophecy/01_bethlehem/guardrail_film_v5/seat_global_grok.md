## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR

For Grace, 34, a churchgoer who already knows Herod, the wise men, and the cross. She finishes. She shares line 12.

Farah, 27, in Cairo, has everyday English and knows Mary’s baby. She stalls on line 5, “from everlasting.” If she stays, line 11 — three crosses and “died for our sins” — clashes with the Jesus she knows. She does not share.

Left out: a muted or auto-caption viewer. A Jewish viewer who sees prayer shawls and a raised finger and hears that those teachers never went. Anyone with no church meaning for “our sins.”

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY

“The teachers knew Bethlehem, the book never says they went, the wise men did, and Jesus came to us first.”

Line 9 carries the story. Line 12 is the lesson, and it fights line 9. The trip is clear in the pictures. The lesson is hard to repeat.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE

In my words: Micah said a ruler would come from little Bethlehem. Jesus was born there. Herod’s teachers named the town. The book never says they walked there. Visitors on camels found the child. Then the film says Jesus came from heaven, died for our sins, and lived again.

Line 1 fails: “Did they go?” Where? The sign says BETHLEHEM. The voice has not said it yet. Line 4 fails: “It goes on” can mean “more words” or “walks forward.” Plain: “Micah said more.” Line 5 fails: “from everlasting” has no person and no verb. If you explain it as “he lived before,” many viewers in India will hear a past life. Line 6, “not His beginning,” keeps the riddle, and speech cannot show the capital H. Line 8, “close by,” never says close to what. Plain: “near Jerusalem.” Line 11, “He rose,” becomes a flower or “stood up” in captions. Plain: “He came back to life.” “For our sins” is insider English.

Line 12 misleads. “Before anyone went to Him” means zero people. Line 9 said the wise men went and found the child.

On screen: change gold “GO?” to “GO THERE?” A SCRIBE should read TEACHER. “Judaea” is an old spelling captions often miss. The CAME TRUE stamp is clear. PROMISED. / CAME TRUE. over the tomb makes the cross look like Micah’s promise. Wordless footprints are the one universal picture. The voice never says “cross,” so three crosses can look like three deaths.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE

- Line 2: RIGHT (Micah 5:2). “Named a small town” can mean he invented the name. He pointed to it.
- Line 3: RIGHT, “ruler in Israel.” “King” (lines 1 and 6) is the wise men’s word. A fair join.
- Line 5: RIGHT as a KJV fragment. Line 6, “not His beginning”: OVERCLAIMED as the only sense. “From ancient days” can mean an old royal line.
- Lines 6–7: RIGHT (Matthew 2:1-5; Luke 2:4-7). Herod’s question is never spoken.
- Line 8: RIGHT that the towns are near. No distance is spoken.
- Line 9: RIGHT about silence. The hollow footprints OVERCLAIM: they show a trip that never happened. “Found the child”: RIGHT. Matthew has a house; the manger is Luke.
- Line 10: RIGHT (John 7:41-42).
- Line 11: RIGHT (John 6:38; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4). Four claims in about eight seconds.
- Line 12: OVERCLAIMED. The wise men went.
- End card on the tomb: OVERCLAIMED if Micah promised the cross. He promised a ruler from Bethlehem.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE

When the camera passes NAZARETH, GALILEE, JERUSALEM, and BETHLEHEM, the voice should say those names. Now the map carries them. Keep the footprints and the one-second silence.

ONE change: line 12, so the share line can live with line 9.

6/10.

## NEW TO YOU

I knew sermons that say the scholars stayed home. New to me was the careful line “The Bible never says the experts went” (line 9), set with “birthplace, not His beginning” (line 6) and “can the King come from Galilee?” (line 10). The death and rising were familiar. 5/10.

## PROMISE KEPT

God, through Micah, about 700 years before Jesus by the usual dates, promised a ruler from little Bethlehem. Jesus was born there. The film never says the 700 years, so the long wait has no size. The gold CAME TRUE stamp, and the horn that finishes at the hold, make the birth feel kept. I leave with who walked. 6/10.
