## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** a churchgoing parent who scrolls Shorts in December.
- **Who stops:** they stop at line 1, because "Did they go?" is a fair challenge.
- **Who finishes:** Bible readers finish it, because the Galilee twist in line 10 rewards them.
- **Who shares:** a youth leader would share it.
- **Who is NOT served:** a newcomer. They never hear who "the experts" were, or why Herod cared.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"God promised the King would come from Bethlehem, and He came to us before anyone went to Him."
- Lines 2 and 12 carry it.
- It is mostly clear. But lines 8 and 9 ("who went") pull hard against line 12 ("He came").

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
- **Who:** Jesus, the experts, the wise men, Herod.
- **What:** the promised birthplace came true.
- **Why:** God kept His word, and Jesus came from heaven.
- **Where I got lost (line 6):** it crams four ideas into one breath: "birthplace, not beginning… came true… wise men… Herod."
- **Hard words:** "From everlasting" (line 5) and "Bible experts" (line 1) are hard for second-language viewers.
- **Pictures:** the stable panel with a star under it suggests the wise men came to the manger. Matthew says "house" (Matt 2:11).

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **L1** "The Bible experts knew where the King would be born." **RIGHT.** Matt 2:4-5. The chief priests and scribes answered "In Bethlehem of Judaea."
- **L2** "Micah named a small town: Bethlehem." **RIGHT.** Mic 5:2, "though thou be little among the thousands of Judah."
- **L2/3** "God promised a King… ruler in Israel." **RIGHT.** Mic 5:2 says "ruler," spoken "unto me," so it is God's promise. "King" is a fair gloss, from Matt 2:2.
- **L5** "from everlasting." **RIGHT.** It is accurate but clipped. It drops "from of old."
- **L6** "birthplace, not His beginning." **RIGHT as interpretation.** It is a sound reading of Mic 5:2, backed by John 6:38. It is framed as a fact, which is fine for a believing audience.
- **L6** "God's promise came true: Jesus… was born." **RIGHT.** Matt 2:1; Luke 2:4-7.
- **L6** "Wise men came asking… Herod asked the experts." **RIGHT.** Matt 2:2, 2:4.
- **L7** "In Bethlehem of Judaea." **RIGHT.** Matt 2:5, quoted exactly.
- **L8** "Bethlehem was close by." **UNSUPPORTED by the supplied text.** It is true in geography (about 6 miles), and Matt 2:8 hints at it. Not a fault, but no verse says it.
- **L9** "The Bible never says the experts went." **RIGHT.** This is careful wording. But the hook in line 1, "Did they go?", **OVERCLAIMS** a little. It invites the viewer to convict them from silence.
- **L9** "The wise men went, and found the child." **RIGHT.** Matt 2:9-11.
- **L10** "Jesus grew up in Galilee." **RIGHT.** Luke 2:4 says the family is from Nazareth. John 7:41 also fits.
- **L10** "can the King come from Galilee?" **RIGHT in substance.** The verse says "Christ," not "King" (John 7:41).
- **L10** "The promise said Bethlehem." **RIGHT.** John 7:42.
- **L11** "came down from heaven… died for our sins… third day… rose." **RIGHT.** John 6:38; 1 Cor 15:3-4.
- **L12** "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us." **RIGHT** as a theological summary.
- **Omission:** the shepherds DID go "with haste" (Luke 2:15-16). That is supplied text, and the film ignores it. A sharp viewer will say "but the shepherds went."

**Would I send it to my church group?** Yes, after the fixes below.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
- **What the best explainer would do:** state the time gap. Micah wrote about 700 years before.
- **The ONE change:** put "700 years" into line 2. Without it, "promise kept" has no weight.
- **Score:** 6.5/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
- **Opens:** line 1.
- **Pays off:** lines 9 and 12.
- **Score:** 7/10. Worth the wait. The turn in line 12 ("He came to us") is earned.

## NEW TO YOU
"Shall Christ come out of Galilee?" (line 10). The irony that the critics quoted the very promise He fulfilled is fresh to most pew-sitters. "The Bible never says the experts went" is familiar sermon material.

**Score:** 5/10.

## PROMISE KEPT
- **Who promised what:** God, through Micah, promised a ruler from little Bethlehem, one whose origins are everlasting.
- **What came true:** Jesus was born there, and He came from heaven.
- **What is missing:** the film never says how long before.
- **How it feels:** it feels more like a "who went" story than a promise kept, until the end card.

**Score:** 6/10.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **Line 2:** "Seven hundred years earlier, God's prophet Micah named a small town: Bethlehem. God promised a ruler from there:"
2. **Line 1:** "The Bible experts knew where the King would be born. Did they go and see?"
3. **Line 9:** "The Bible never says the experts went. Shepherds ran there. Wise men came from far away, and found the child."
