## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
A curious viewer on a phone who likes puzzles ("Did they go?"). I stop at line 1 because of the question. I finish if the maps keep me guessing. I would share it with a church friend, not a stranger. NOT served: someone who does not know who Jesus is. The film calls him "the King" (line 6) but never says King of what, or why he matters to me.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"A king was promised to be born in Bethlehem; he was; the experts did not go, but he came to us." Line 12 carries it. Partly clear. Lines 1-9 are about who went. Line 10 turns to Galilee, which I did not follow. Line 11 jumps to heaven, a cross and a tomb, with no bridge.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Micah (an old man, "God's prophet"), wise men, Herod, experts, Jesus. What: a town was named, a baby was born there, experts stayed home, wise men travelled. Why: unclear. Why does the King matter? Why did Herod ask?
Lost or misled:
- Line 1 "Bible experts": what Bible? Who is "the King"?
- Lines 3-5 "ruler in Israel... It goes on: from everlasting": "everlasting" is a hard word, and the scroll split is odd.
- Line 6 is crammed: "Wise men came asking for this King. King Herod asked the experts." Why were they asking? Who is Herod to the King?
- Line 7 "Bethlehem of Judaea": place words I cannot place.
- Line 10 "people argued: can the King come from Galilee?" Which people? When? The big "?" confused me.
- Line 11 "died for our sins", "rose from the dead": I was TOLD these but not shown why. "Sins" does not translate well.
- "Prophet", "scribe", "promise" are used with no definition.
- The scholar's speech balloon (line 7) is tiny on a phone.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. I was told, and could not follow: that Micah's words are "God's promise"; that "from everlasting" means Jesus existed before birth (line 6 explains it, but too fast); that wise men "found the child" (where, how, why?); that he "came down from heaven" (line 11); that his death was "for our sins" (no reason given).

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would give one clear reason I should care, and use fewer facts. Highest-value change: add one plain sentence early, e.g. "Jesus is the King God promised." Score: 5/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1 ("Did they go?"). Pays off at line 9 ("The Bible never says the experts went"). Score 7/10. Worth the wait, but lines 10-11 then open new questions (Galilee? heaven?) that stay unpaid.

## NEW TO YOU
As a churchgoer: the point that the experts never went (line 9) and the Galilee-versus-Bethlehem map (line 10). The "birthplace, not beginning" idea (line 6) is also fresh. 7/10.

## PROMISE KEPT
Micah promised a King from Bethlehem. The film never says how long before; I guess "a long time", because he is "old". It came true: the CAME TRUE stamp and the gold footprints help. The ending, "PROMISED. CAME TRUE.", does make it feel like a kept promise, but the middle feels like a story about who went. 6/10.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 1: "The Bible experts knew where God's promised King would be born. Did they go?"
2. Line 10: "Jesus grew up far north, in Galilee. Some people said: the King must come from Bethlehem. He did."
3. Line 11: "But Jesus came much farther. He came down from heaven. He died to take away our wrong. On the third day, He came alive again."
