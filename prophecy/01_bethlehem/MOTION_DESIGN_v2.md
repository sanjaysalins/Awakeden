# Born in Bethlehem - MOTION DESIGN v2 (film v4)

Film: 1080x1920, 30 fps, 1779 frames (59.3 s). Voice and its timing are untouched. Clips play at 1.0x, trim only.
Build: `_remotion/src/BethlehemV4.tsx` (composition `BethlehemV4`). Pills come from `film_v3_data.json`, patched in memory.

## 1. What was weak in v3 (from the check frames and the film)

- The doodles were **tiny**: 13 px dots and a 52 px star. On a phone they read as specks, not as a journey.
- The time jump (4.0-6.2 s) was **plain cream paper with a number**. Nothing to look at.
- The "short road nobody walked" - the heart of the film - was 7 dots in a dark sky. It never became a picture.
- Nazareth "far away", "the east", "close by" were only said. A newcomer could not see where any of it is.
- Transitions were mostly slides, a flash and a wipe. Two of them were plain paper leaves.
- Lettering worked. Keep it.

## 2. The big idea: "The map in the margin"

One hand-inked map of Judaea, on parchment, comes back **three times** and grows each time. It carries the WENT spine.

| Visit | Time | Voice | What the map shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 17.5-22.7 s | "Mary and Joseph lived far away. A tax order sent them to Bethlehem." | Starts close on Nazareth, glides a long way south to Bethlehem. A tax order (a sealed scroll) lands. Two small walkers go down a dashed road. Gold star lands on Bethlehem. |
| B | 34.3-37.5 s | "Bethlehem was close by." + the 1 s silence | Close view: Jerusalem (big walled city) and Bethlehem. One short road. Seven **hollow** shoe prints. The first print blinks in the silence. Nobody walks. |
| C | 39.5-42.0 s | (silence) "The wise men went," | Wide view. A long **gold** road comes in from "the east" with three camels. They reach Jerusalem. Then the hollow prints turn gold, one by one, down to Bethlehem. The star flares and opens onto Mary and the child. |

Same grammar as v3, but big: **hollow print = a step not taken, gold print = a step taken, one unbroken gold line = Jesus' journey from heaven.**

Map labels (the only words that are not spoken): `Nazareth`, `Jerusalem`, `Bethlehem`, `the east`. No distances, no numbers. The map is a doodle, not to scale: the short road is drawn longer than true so the prints can be seen.

## 6. Rules kept

- All spoken words on screen match the voice. Extra words are only: 4 map labels, the tags "A SCRIBE", "MATTHEW 2:5", "MICAH 5:2", "Micah" (spoken), and the v3 end mark "PROMISED, THEN KEPT".
- Nothing is drawn over writing inside the art. No numbers for distance.
- Pure function of the frame (hash, no random). Interpolations clamped. Fonts loaded with FontFace + delayRender.
- Text inside x 60-1020, y 200-1540.
