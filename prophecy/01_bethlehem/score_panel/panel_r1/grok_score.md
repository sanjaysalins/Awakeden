1. SCORE IDEA

A five-note promise — E, D, C, then E, G — left unfinished for 700 years and finished only when Jesus comes farther than anyone walked. Felt piano and one solo cello. Nothing else. The first three notes step down (they knew, and stayed). The last two rise past the start (He came). A viewer can hum E–D–C–E–G after one listen.

2. WHY IT FITS

- Ink and gold, not a trailer: two acoustic voices, close and dry, like a pen line and one stroke of gold. No drums, choir, synth, or "eastern" color.
- The broken tune is the story. Experts, Herod, and the hollow footprints get only E–D–C. The map's gold footprints are the moment G appears.
- Voice stays king. The melody is a walking whisper under the narrator, a near-silence under both KJV lines, and fully itself only after the last word.
