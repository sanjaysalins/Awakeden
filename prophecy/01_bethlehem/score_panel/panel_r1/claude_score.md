# Score plan: "Born in Bethlehem" (59.3 s)

## 1. SCORE IDEA
**A promise tune that is left open 700 years early and only finishes when He comes to us.**

- **Instruments:** felt piano and one solo cello. A very soft low string drone in the background only. No drums, no choir, no synth pads.
- **The motif ("the Promise"):** five notes in D major that climb gently, 3-4-5-6, and stop on the 2nd note of the scale (E). It sounds like a question that hangs. All through the film you hear it without its last note. In the final hold the cello plays it whole and lands home on D. It is easy to hum after one listen.

## 2. WHY IT FITS
- **Bold ink, few colours, few instruments.** Clean black line works like one clear melody. Two instruments match the comic's clarity, and big orchestral "epic" sound would fight the drawn look.
- **The story is a question and then an answer.** "Did they go?" and the hollow footprints get the unfinished tune. "He came to us" gets the finished tune. The music's shape is the promise's shape.
- **Silence carries the twist.** The experts who knew did not go, so the music goes too: true near-silence over the empty road. The viewer feels the gap before the words name it.

- **Voice is king.** The music bed sits about 18 dB under the narration and about 24 dB under both KJV lines (15.8-17.6 s and 32.2-34.7 s).
- **The lift (43.5-52.4 s)** rises to about 12 dB under the voice, no higher.

- **The hold needs fixing.** The last word ends at 57.2 s and the film ends at 59.3 s. That is a 2.1 s hold, but the house rule (INV-26) needs at least 3.0 s. Extend the hero shot to about 60.2 s so the cello's final D gets the full 3 s to ring out. The audio length must match the video length.

## 7. RISKS
1. **The tool ignores the "around N seconds" cues.** For example, the swell comes early or the drop-out never happens. **Check:** sweep the loudness every 0.5 s across both parts. The music must dip at 15.8, 32.2, 36.3-38 and 52.5 s, and peak at 49-52 s. Reroll the part that misses.
2. **Dead air, or a click at the join.** **Check:** a timeline sweep for any stretch over 1 s below -50 dB outside the planned 36.3-38 s gap, plus an ear check on headphones at 37.0 s.
3. **The tune masks the words** (the cello's middle range clashes with the voice), or the melody gets ducked into nothing. **Check:** an ear test by someone hearing it cold: can they repeat the two KJV lines word for word? Then hum test: after one play, can they hum the five notes? If not, raise the music in the gaps between lines, not under them.
