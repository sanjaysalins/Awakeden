Your 4 questions

Who for? Church people who know Christmas. Not total newcomers yet (newcomer seat: 4/10).

Message? Seats said back: "Micah named the town, Jesus was born there, the experts knew and the Bible never says they went." But it feels like two films: the ending (everlasting, cross, tomb) is a second idea.

Gap closed? Half. "Which town" is answered. "They knew... did they go?" is only hinted. Nobody says it out loud.

Global? Mostly. All 5 seats: the old-English Micah line is hard. 4 of 5: "from everlasting" is misread (sounds like "forever from now").

Free fixes (edit only, $0)

  • "He came as a baby" plays over the crosses. Show the baby/manger there instead. (3 seats)
  • "They knew the town" plays over Herod's face. Show the experts or the empty road. (Fable)
  • Plain-English pill under the Micah scroll: "Bethlehem, a ruler will come from you." (4 seats)
  • Plain pill with "from everlasting": "before time began". (4 seats)

Word fixes (need new voice lines, small cost, blind test first)

  • Say the payoff out loud, honestly. E.g. "The visitors went. The Bible never says the experts did." (4 seats want the hook closed)
  • Tie the ending to the hook so it is not a second film. (4 seats)
  • Micah wording: "his goings forth are from everlasting", not "this ruler is". (3 seats: small overclaim)
  • "where He would be born": "He" can sound like Herod. Say "the promised King". (Fable)

Rejected

Grok's "The experts never did" and Claude's "They stayed home": the Bible does not say that. We keep "the Bible never says". Codex wanted no "700 years": kept, Micah 1:1 kings date it (with "about").
