# SCORE_DESIGN_EARLY — score the narration, not the video

Proposed 2026-09-04. **Status: DESIGN, not yet run for real.** Nothing here has
spent money or rendered audio. Before it becomes the standing process it needs
(a) the open questions in section 9 answered, (b) one cheap smoke test
(section 7), and (c) `independent_review.py --type plan` — this is a new
pipeline stage, so the repo's own rule applies.

## 1. What changes, in one breath

Today the score is the LAST thing built. `generate_score_piano.py` (one per
episode) sends ONE prose prompt to ElevenLabs Music with cues like "around
eighteen seconds in the piano goes plainer..." — timings guessed from word
counts, after the stills and clips already exist.

New: the score is designed **right after the narration locks and the audio is
approved**, from the narration's REAL word timestamps, as an ElevenLabs
**composition plan** — an ordered list of sections, each with an exact
`duration_ms`. The musical identity and the per-episode arc reasoning do not
change. Only two things change: the MECHANISM (timed chunks instead of one
approximate prompt) and WHEN (before visual design, not after assembly).

## 2. Where it sits in the pipeline

```
/narrate  → narration.md LOCKED (5-CLI panel to convergence)
/voice    → narration.mp3 + narration.meta.json (per_turn_synth, natural speed)
             + narration.alignment.json (build_srt.py, ElevenLabs forced alignment)
          → HUMAN GATE 1: audio approved by ear
SCORE DESIGN (this doc)  ← NEW POSITION
          → score_composition_plan.json + score_piano_raw.mp3 + score_piano.mp3
Fable design brief (_DESIGN_BRIEF.md)   ← should now RECEIVE the score arc (section 8)
stills → animation → assemble → sfx → srt / tracker
```

Alignment timing note: `narration.alignment.json` is written by
`pipeline/assembly_align.align()` (ElevenLabs forced alignment, cached,
idempotent). Today the only caller is `build_srt.py`, which runs at the END
of the chain. This stage needs that same call run **right after /voice** —
same function, same cache file, no new code; the later srt step then reuses
the cache for free.

Hard precondition: narration text AND audio are locked. A re-synth changes
every timestamp. The plan file carries sha256 of `narration.md`,
`narration.mp3` and `narration.alignment.json`; if any hash differs at render
time, the plan is stale — rebuild it, do not render it.

## 5. The locked identity (unchanged — restated so it lands on every chunk)

- Intimate modern-classical **felt piano**, slow, rubato, ~60 bpm. ONE small
  motif carries the whole piece. Sparse. Long pedal decays. Real silence
  between phrases.
- **Never:** drums, percussion, bassline, anything rhythmic, a big swell, a
  crescendo, a peak.
- **Strings:** enter only AFTER the piece's true resolution, one section at a
  time (violas → muted violins → low cellos), and only ever widen and warm —
  far behind the piano, never a peak.
- Clean, airy mix, generous headroom, everything soft and underneath.
- Mixing: the piano/duck profile is episode-tuned and measured, never
  inherited (`project_swirls_score_duck_is_track_specific`).

Not this: `SCORE_STYLE_BANK.md`'s dream-trance/groove bank — that was the
Jacob's Ladder pilot's direction, superseded for mainline episodes.

## 6. The mechanism — narration beats → timed chunks

### 6.1 Unit table
One row per page in reading order (front, F01..F10, back for a 10-page
episode). Each row: tag, the exact contiguous narration text, word count.
Word counts must sum to the narration's total. This is the ONE beat map for
the episode; the design brief reuses it.

### 6.2 Boundaries from the alignment
Load `narration.alignment.json` → `words[]`. Keep only tokens with at least
one letter/digit (drops spaces and the em-dash tokens). Walk the unit word
counts through that list. For each unit record its first spoken word's
`start` and last spoken word's `end`.

**Boundary rule:** chunk k starts at the START of unit k's first spoken word.
Chunk 1 starts at 0.000 (not at the first word — the file starts at zero).
The last chunk runs to `narration_end + 3.0s hold + 2.5s margin`. The margin
is trimmed by the fitted fade, exactly as today — the rendered output contract
does not change.

Why first-word start and not the silence before it: it is the same event a
page turn should key to (a page begins when its text begins), and it is
unambiguous. A per-chunk lead (music moves a few hundred ms before the word)
is a possible later refinement — not in v1.

### 6.3 Chunk count
One chunk per unit. A 10-page episode = 12 chunks; the series runs ~8–14.
The API cap is 30 — never a constraint here.

- A unit shorter than **3,000 ms** cannot be its own chunk (API minimum).
  Merge it into its neighbour and carry its direction as a second clause in
  that chunk's `{...}`. Episode 11's front cover is 3,139 ms — 139 ms clear.
  Check this on every episode.
- Do not split a unit into sub-chunks unless a real musical event needs its
  own boundary AND both halves are ≥ 3,000 ms. (Section 9 has the one case
  where this matters.)

### 6.4 The `text` field — direction only, never bare prose
Format, every chunk:

```
[F04 - cleansed as they went]
{on the first beat one gentle warmer interval is struck and left to ring ... }
```

- Line 1: a bracketed section label (the unit tag + a 3–7 word gloss).
- Line 2: ONE `{...}` block holding the whole musical direction.
- **No bare text.** The docs say the model writes lyrics when none are given
  and treats braces as directions, brackets as labels. Anything outside those
  is a lyric candidate. This series has no lyrics, ever.
- The direction wording is the validated prose prompt's own wording, cut at
  the unit boundaries — near-verbatim, per the memory. Each unit gets its own
  clause even where the old prompt merged two (ep11's old prompt merged
  F01+F02 into one "around three seconds" cue; the plan gives them each their
  own — same meaning, more precise).

### 6.5 Styles — the identity on EVERY chunk so it cannot drift
Chunks are independent sections. A rule stated once (in chunk 1) is not a
rule in chunk 7. So:

- `positive_styles` on every chunk = the identity base (`felt piano`, `solo
  piano`, `intimate`, `modern classical`, `sparse`, `rubato`, `slow 60 bpm`,
  `long pedal decay`, `instrumental`) + 3–5 mood tags for that unit.
- `negative_styles` on every chunk = the vocals family (`vocals`, `lyrics`,
  `singing`, `choir`, `humming`) + the rhythm family (`drums`, `percussion`,
  `bassline`, `beat`) + the wrong-palette family (`synth`, `pads`, `guitar`,
  `electronic`) + the swell family (`big swell`, `crescendo`, `epic`).
- **The strings gate is encoded per chunk:** every chunk BEFORE the strings
  entry carries `strings`, `orchestra`, `cello`, `violin` in
  `negative_styles`. From the entry chunk on, those flip to
  `positive_styles` (`soft strings underneath`, `violas`, `muted violins`,
  `low cellos`, `sustained warm string tones`, `widening harmony`, `strings
  far behind the piano`) and the negatives become the cap (`soaring
  strings`, `string swell`, `string peak`, `tremolo strings`).
- Keep the lists short-ish (the cookbook suggests ~6–7 styles set the tone in
  early chunks; the cap is 50). Mood tags, not sentences.
- `context_adherence`: `high` on every chunk — one motif runs through the
  whole piece. Lowering it on the resolution chunk is a knob if a render's
  turn is too subtle, not a default.

### 6.6 Turning points → specific chunks (the project's own rules, mapped)
- **Hero = gospel pivot = the resolution chunk.** The unit that carries
  Christ's own word is the ONLY chunk whose direction says "resolves". Its
  first beat is the chord. Every chunk before it says `unresolved` /
  `withheld` in both text and tags.
- **A gift that is not yet the turn** (ep11's F04 cleansing, ep10's two
  tender pages) gets a brief opening INSIDE its chunk that draws back before
  the chunk ends — never a resolution, never strings.
- **Strings enter in the chunk AFTER the resolution chunk**, never in it
  (the resolution chunk's own text says `piano alone, no strings yet`).
- **The back cover / landing** = the motif slowest and simplest, over the
  string glow, fading to silence — still no peak.

### 6.7 The request
`POST https://api.elevenlabs.io/v1/music` with `Accept: audio/mpeg` and the
plan's `request` object. Differences from the current script, all required
by the API:

| Field | Old (prompt) | New (composition plan) |
|---|---|---|
| `prompt` | the prose | **absent** (mutually exclusive with `composition_plan`) |
| `music_length_ms` | narration + 5.5s | **absent** — total = sum of chunk `duration_ms` |
| `model_id` | `music_v1` | `music_v2` (the chunks variant requires it) |
| `force_instrumental` | `true` | **not allowed** with a plan — instrumental is enforced only by `negative_styles` + no lyrics (see section 9) |
| `seed` | — | allowed; pin one when re-rendering a near-identical plan |

Then the fit step, unchanged: `ffmpeg -af afade=in 1.0s, afade=out 2.0s
-t <narration+3.0>` → `score_piano.mp3`.

   the narration's; chunk durations sum to `generated_seconds`; every chunk
   3,000–120,000 ms; ≤ 30 chunks; ≤ 50 styles per list; every `text` body is

## 8. Downstream consequences (not solved here — flagged)

**8.1 The assembler cuts pages at word-count proportions, not real time.**
`swirls_assemble.py` (`slot = narration_len * u.words / total_words`, lines
257 and 277) places each page by word weight. The score now moves at REAL
word boundaries. For episode 11 the two disagree by up to 1.7s:

| Page turn | proportional (assembler) | real (alignment) | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| F03 | 18.36 | 19.18 | +0.82 |
| F06 | 35.12 | 35.72 | +0.60 |
| F09 hero | 56.27 | 55.32 | −0.95 |
| F10 strings | 63.85 | 62.20 | **−1.65** |
| back | 73.03 | 74.58 | +1.55 |

So the hero chord would sound ~1s before the hero page turns, and the strings
~1.7s before F10 appears. A precisely-timed score buys nothing unless the
pages cut at the same times. **Recommendation:** the assembler should read
the same unit boundaries (from the plan's `derivation.chunks`, or a shared
`unit_timing.json` written by this stage) instead of recomputing proportions.
That is a separate `swirls_assemble.py` change — decide it with the user, do
not silently accept the mismatch and do not fudge the plan toward the
proportional numbers.

**8.2 The visual design brief should now receive the score.** Today
`_DESIGN_BRIEF.md` never mentions music (ep11's brief: zero hits for
score/music/piano). With the score designed first, the Fable stills/animation
pass should be handed, per unit: the chunk's direction, whether it is
`unresolved` / `brief opening` / `resolution` / `strings`, and the exact
boundary times. Whether that changes page pacing, motif dosage, or the
freeze/boomerang choices is an **open design question for
`/swirls-of-life` Step 0** — not redesigned here.

**8.3 Unchanged:** `build_sfx.py`, the duck profile workflow, `episode.py`'s
`ScoreVariant` path and the `score_piano.mp3` length contract.

## 9. Open questions and risks — settle before spending

1. **Instrumental cannot be forced.** `force_instrumental` is prompt-only.
   The docs say to put `vocals` in `negative_styles` and give no lyrics; they
   also say the model writes its own lyrics when none are given. This repo
   has already met "a vocals-injecting music_v2" once (STATE.md, 2026-06-24,
   #31). Section 7 step 2's smoke test is mandatory, not optional.
2. **Duration enforcement on `music_v2` is asserted by the docs, not yet
   measured by us.** `respect_sections_durations` applies to `music_v1`'s
   sections variant only; the docs say v2 chunk durations are always
   enforced. Verify with section 7 step 3 on the first real render.
3. **Where exactly does the resolution land?** The plan puts the chord at the
   F09 page turn (55.32s, "To the one still on his face, he said,") — the same
   event the old prompt's "around fifty-six seconds" pointed at. Christ's own
   word "Arise" is spoken at 57.90s, 2.6s later. Landing the chord ON "Arise"
   is arguably more precise doctrinally, but the narrator lead-in is only
   2.58s — under the 3,000 ms chunk minimum — so it would mean merging that
   lead-in into F08's stillness chunk. This is a real choice the new precision
   exposes for the first time. User's call; the shipped plan keeps the page
   turn.
4. **Text-field semantics.** Bracket labels and brace directions are
   documented; whether a long `{...}` direction is honoured as fully as a
   short one is not. If a render ignores the inside-chunk shape (F04's
   "brighten then draw back"), shorten the direction to its first clause and
   move the rest into style tags.
5. **Billing.** Assumed to be per second of output like the prompt route;
   not confirmed for the plan route. Check the ledger after the smoke test.
6. **The two-second early-fade quirk** (`sfx_pilots/add_music.py`'s
   `reshape_music()` exists because of it) may or may not appear on v2 plans.
   The 2.5s margin + our own fade covers a mild case; a severe one needs the
   reshape.
7. **Panel review.** A new pipeline stage → `independent_review.py --type
   plan` on this document before adoption.
