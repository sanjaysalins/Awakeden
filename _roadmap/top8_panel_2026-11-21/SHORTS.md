## T1
TITLE NOW: A Widow Was Burying Her Only Son. Jesus Stopped the Funeral.
SPOKEN WORDS (as recorded):
A widow was walking behind her only son's body, carried out on a bier to be buried. He was all she had left.
touch a dead man, and you were unclean. Everyone kept their distance. That was survival, not cruelty.
Jesus walked toward her instead of away. And when the Lord saw her, he had compassion on her, and said unto her, "Weep not."
Then he touched what no one else would touch: and he came and touched the bier, and they that bare him stood still.
Under that same law, the touch should have made him unclean. It didn't work that way with him. And he said, "Young man, I say unto thee, Arise."
And he that was dead sat up, and began to speak. And he delivered him to his mother.
Death didn't spread from the boy to Jesus. Life spread from Jesus to the boy.
That is still the only direction he touches anything dead in you. Not to catch what's wrong with you — to give you what's his instead. He touched a funeral, and it became a homecoming.

## T2
TITLE NOW: Martha Asked Jesus If He Cared. He Said Her Name Twice.
SPOKEN WORDS (as recorded):
You do everything for everyone. Does anyone even care?
Martha asked Jesus that. She had opened her home to Him. While she served, her sister Mary sat at His feet and listened.
"Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore that she help me."
His answer? Her name. Twice.
"Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her."
"Careful" means full of worry. Mary had chosen the one thing needed: to listen to Him. And He said it would not be taken away from her.
So, did He care?
Later, the Bible says this about Martha, Mary and their brother: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus."
He did not call Martha's work wrong. Under all her work, He saw her worry. And He loved her.

## T3
TITLE NOW: God Spoke a Promise Before Adam and Eve's Sentence
SPOKEN WORDS (as recorded):
Dust on their skin, fig leaves tied with shaking hands — Adam and Eve stand in the trees, waiting to hear what God will do to them.
But before God ever tells them their sentence, He turns to the snake first and buries a promise for them inside that curse.
To the serpent God says, "And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel." Only then does He turn to Eve, then Adam, to speak their own sentence. Call it a curse formula — the order still stands: rescue spoken before ruin.
It's easy to admire a God who promises rescue. It's harder to believe He meant it for you — before you'd done one thing to earn it, or even asked.
That promise has a name now: Jesus, the Seed who crushed the serpent's head. Grace was spoken before your sentence — come meet the Voice that spoke first.

## T4
TITLE NOW: Asked for a Miracle, Jesus Gave Them a Drowned Prophet
SPOKEN WORDS (as recorded):
I told the sailors to throw me into the sea — and a great fish swallowed me whole, and buried me alive in the dark. I ran from God because His mercy was too wide. He sent me to Nineveh, the city I hated — I'd sooner drown than see her spared. The storm came for my sake; the lot fell on me. To spare the sailors, I had them throw me overboard. Three days buried alive in the fish. Then, long after I was dust, scribes demanded a sign — Jesus gave them only my name:
For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth.
I went down guilty — the storm was mine. He went down innocent, bearing what was ours; He gave Himself. The grave no more held Him than the deep held me. He rose. You who are sure you've fallen too far — that is how far His mercy reaches. It cost Him the grave. Stop running, and turn: the One who went down into your dark and rose again is Jesus. Come up out of the deep, and live.

## T5
TITLE NOW: Why Is Jesus Called Immanuel?
SPOKEN WORDS (as recorded):
A frightened king told God no. God answered anyway.
King Ahaz, from King David's family, was afraid of war. God said, "fear not," and offered any sign, as proof. Ahaz refused. In that same war, he begged a foreign king: "save me." God chose the sign:
Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.
Seven hundred years later, it came true. Mary, a virgin, was pregnant by God's Holy Spirit.
An angel told Joseph:
Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife:
thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.
Joseph obeyed. Ahaz begged a king to save him. This child saves. Immanuel means: God with us.
With us at the cross, where He died for our sins. On the third day, He rose from the dead. He promised:
I am with you alway.
That was God's answer.

## T6
TITLE NOW: She Was Dead. Jesus Went Anyway
SPOKEN WORDS (as recorded):
A father's little girl is dying. Then the worst news comes: she is dead. Later, Jesus says two words to her. Mark kept them in Jesus's own language: Talitha cumi. What do they say?
The father is Jairus. He falls at Jesus's feet. Jesus goes with him. A woman touches His clothes in the crowd, and Jesus stops. Jairus has to wait.
Then men come from the house: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" In plain words: your daughter is dead. Why bother the Teacher now?
Too late.
Jesus hears it. He turns to the father.
"Be not afraid, only believe."
At the house, people are crying loudly. Jesus says she is only sleeping, and they laugh at Him. He sends them out. Only her parents and three followers go in. He takes her by the hand.
"Talitha cumi."
They mean: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up.
She is twelve. She gets up and walks. And Jesus says: give her something to eat.
A hand. "Arise." Then a meal.
Jesus had said to the father: "Be not afraid, only believe." He said it to a man with the worst news. He says it to you. Let Him take your hand.

## T7
TITLE NOW: Two Thieves. Same Cross. Opposite Eternities.
SPOKEN WORDS (as recorded):
Two criminals died beside Jesus that afternoon. Same nails. Same hill. Same distance. Opposite eternities.
Both guilty. Both just as close. Both spoke.
If thou be Christ, save thyself and us.
A taunt, not a prayer.
The other thief rebuked him.
And we indeed justly; for we receive the due reward of our deeds: but this man hath done nothing amiss.
A confession. A verdict: innocent. Then one request.
Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom.
Verily I say unto thee, To day shalt thou be with me in paradise.
He asked for someday. Jesus gave him that same afternoon.
No merit. Just repentance and trust.
We are all one of those two men.
Ask what the thief asked. Remember me. Then hear his answer. With me. Today. Paradise.

## T8
TITLE NOW: He Said No Three Times. Jesus Made Him Breakfast. (title already being changed)
SPOKEN WORDS (as recorded):
Is there one night you can't stop thinking about? The night Jesus was arrested, His friend Peter stood by a fire. People asked if he was one of His followers.
"I am not."
Three times, he said no. Jesus died. He rose from the dead.
Peter went back to fishing. All night, nothing.
At sunrise, Jesus stands on the shore. He tells them where to throw the net. It fills with fish. A friend cries, "It is the Lord." Peter jumps into the sea to reach Him.
On the beach: "a fire of coals." In the King James Bible, these words appear only twice. Here, and the night Peter said no.
"Come and dine."
Come and eat. Jesus hands them bread and fish. After the meal, He asks Peter three times:
"Lovest thou me?"
Do you love me? The third time, Peter is sad.
"Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee."
You know everything. You know I love you.
"Feed my sheep."
Take care of my people. Three times, no, that night. Three times, yes, at the second fire.
And before Jesus asked Peter to feed anyone, Jesus fed Peter.
