The one idea

Find Jesus in the whole Bible, one story at a time.
Lead with a human weight (shame, fear, failure). Turn on a true verse. Land on Jesus.

1. What we learned, in order

1

The idea is strong. The hooks are too "classroom".

  • Viewers scroll past "this prophet wrote this verse".
  • The kids' videos, songs and shorts all on one channel split the audience.
  • Every ending feels the same, so regular viewers leave early.
2

People come for relief, not facts.

  • Comments open up about grief, storms, shame and feeling unworthy.
  • A thread only works when the contrast is sharp (Barabbas, the two goats).
  • The first 3 seconds need: a flip, then the old link, then a landing for you.

Post in mini-series, not one-offs.

2. Do more / do less

✅ DO MORE

  • The Great Exchange: the guilty go free, Jesus steps in
  • Raw failure met by grace: sleeping, denial, betrayal
  • Jesus stops for one when He had every reason to hurry
  • Open on the human weight
  • Answer real viewer questions ("Why did Judas do it?")
  • Series + playlists

❌ DO LESS

  • Titles that start with a verse ("Zechariah 12:10…")
  • "Prophet X wrote Y" trivia hooks
  • The same ending every time
  • Songs and kids' videos in the same feed
  • Long gaps, then many uploads at once

3. The rules: follow every time

Check all 10 before a new short's words lock, and again before upload.

1
Pick a winning shape. Exchange · Failure met by grace · Jesus stops for one. If none fits, think again.
2
Weight first. The first line names a human feeling or problem. No book or verse number in the first 3 seconds.
3
Title = the human dilemma. The verse goes in description line 1, not at the start of the title.
4
A sharp contrast. The twist must be a clear reversal, not a footnote.
5
Vary the ending. Rotate: a question · a quiet picture · the story's own last line. Never the same type twice in a row.
6
True to the text. Fresh-insight hunt, blind panel, KJV check. Studio ideas are leads, not facts.
7
Every short belongs to an arc. Playlist + series label in the description.
8
Bible shorts only in the main feed (songs parked for your call).
9
Steady rhythm: 3 a week, Tue · Thu · Sat.
10
Read the numbers at 48 hours. Log the % watched and % stayed in the scoreboard (section 8). Learn, then adjust.

Still in force: words before money · guardrail questions · global audience · packaging gate · doctrine checked both ways.
