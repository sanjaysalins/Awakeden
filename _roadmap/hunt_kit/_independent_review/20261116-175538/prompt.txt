# Hunt kit - find the fresh angle first ($0)

Rule: **hunt the link first. No strong link, no film.** Contract: `_roadmap\TWO_DRIVERS_ROADMAP.md` (sections 2-3).
One folder per hunt = the **topic dir** (e.g. `encounters\e09_go_home\hunt\`). Run from the repo root.
`PY` = `.venv\Scripts\python.exe`, `KIT` = `_roadmap\hunt_kit`.

> **CHALLENGE GATES (owner, 2026-11-16) - plain words**
> 1. No loom, no hunt. `loom.py` must run on the passage first. The loom is ours only.
> 2. No Sunday-school list, no hunt. `run_seats.py` refuses without `loom.md` and `SUNDAY_SCHOOL.md`.
> 3. The kill list is the Sunday-school list, made by the machine - not written by us.
> 4. TWO-BOOK RULE: every angle links the passage to another book, exact KJV from both.
> 5. WHY-VERSE RULE: name the verse that gives the MEANING of the link. The film claims no more.
> 6. `gate.py` decides: medians vs the bars (incl. DEPTH 7), SOUND all PASS, 2+ books cited. No PASS = no film.
> 7. The owner sees the idea FIRST (`_OWNER_CARD.html`). Words are not written until the owner says NO to "Had you seen this before?"

## Fable + Sonnet seats
The scripts cannot call them. The live Claude session runs them as **blind subagents**: a fresh agent each,
given ONLY `BRIEF.md` + `KJV_PACK.md` (judges: `JUDGE_PROMPT.md`), no repo context, no other seat's work.
Save as `hunt_fable.md` / `hunt_sonnet.md` (judges: `judge_fable.md` / `judge_sonnet.md`).

## Bars (median of 6 judges)
FRESH 7.0+ (6.0-6.9 = "classic", max 1 in 4 films) - TRUE 8.0+ - SOUND all PASS - CLEAR 7.0+ - JESUS 8.0+ - GROWTH 7.0+ (Driver 2) - DEPTH 7.0+ - 2+ distinct books cited. `gate.py` enforces all of these.
Seed viewers decide: 60%+ "had not seen this".
