# Independent review — codex (OK, 203s)

- **E9’s request count is false.** [Plan L191](/F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/_roadmap/TWO_DRIVERS_ROADMAP.md:191) and owner page L60 say “three requests” in Mark 5:1–20. They omit “torment me not” (5:7) and the request not to be sent outside the country (5:10). Three requesting parties is defensible; three requests is not.

- **The pilot misquotes Jesus.** [Owner page L61](/F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/_roadmap/_TWO_DRIVERS.html:61) quotes “what the Lord hath done”; plan E9 similarly quotes “tell what the Lord hath done”. Mark 5:19 says “tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee”. These paraphrases cannot appear as exact quotations under Q3’s “Every quote” rule. The reference-adjacent quote check misses them.

- **Several applications manufacture promises to the viewer.** Plan E3: “He calls you family”; E5: “He sends you”; [E6 L183](/F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/_roadmap/TWO_DRIVERS_ROADMAP.md:183): “He finds you at each one.” These passages address a particular believing woman, Peter’s commission, and one recorded finding after expulsion (John 9:35). They do not establish those unconditional promises to every newcomer. John 1:12 explicitly connects becoming God’s children with receiving and believing Him. Qualify the audience and remove the invented repeated finding.

- **T1 violates the ban on echo-led titles.** [Plan L144](/F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/_roadmap/TWO_DRIVERS_ROADMAP.md:144) titles the film “What was the angel quoting?” while explicitly classifying the Luke connection as “ECHO”. Hebrews 1:5 establishes its own connection to David’s promise; it does not establish that Gabriel was quoting it. The title asserts more than the assigned evidence permits.

- **T6 adds unsupported action and chronology.** [Plan L154](/F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/_roadmap/TWO_DRIVERS_ROADMAP.md:154): “The crowd sang verse 26” and “Days later Jesus quotes it.” Matthew 21:9 says “cried, saying”, without specifying singing. Matthew’s sequence places the later temple exchange after the overnight stay and following morning (21:17–18, 23, 42), not an established interval of multiple days.

- **The owner’s approval prompt misstates the cost.** [Owner page L91](/F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/_roadmap/_TWO_DRIVERS.html:91) proposes “pilot E9 (all $0)”. Plan §4 defines an end-to-end film pilot and budgets film production at approximately $8–14; the page’s own L78 says “film quoted”. Distinguish free preparation from the paid film in the actual approval request.
