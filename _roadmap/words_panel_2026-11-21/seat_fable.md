NOTE: W1 - "buried me alive" / "buried alive" and the throw-overboard beat are each said twice (the repeat could be cut for time); "I ran from God because His mercy was too wide" is an inference from Jonah 4:2, fair but not a quote; quote says "whale's belly" while the narrator says "great fish" - may confuse a newcomer.
NOTE: W2 - "Men screaming for their lives" and "He opens His eyes" are also not in the text (text has only the one cry and "he arose"); the quote runs Jesus' spoken words and the narrator's "Then he arose..." together in one voice - split the voices at "Then"; "He is not asleep because He doesn't care" answers Mark 4:38, not Matthew 8.
NOTE: W3 - "on Simon's back" is not in the text ("on him they laid the cross"); "walking the other way" / "Same road" / "Simon turns" are an inference from "coming out of the country" and "after Jesus", not stated - worth softening the title claim if a checker presses; "sanctify" is a hard word for second-language viewers and is never explained.
