WITH: "The storm came upon them. When trouble comes to you, you can cry to Him too."

TITLE: When Fear Rises, the Disciples Cry to Jesus

W3
REPLACE: "Now he is through the gate. In the King James Bible, outside is “without.” The sin offering, an animal killed for the people's sin, had its blood carried into the sanctuary, God's holy tent. Its body went out, burned “without the camp.” Then it says of Jesus: “Wherefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people with his own blood, suffered without the gate.” Out there, where Simon is walking."
WITH: "Simon carried the cross outside the city. Hebrews says Jesus suffered “without the gate,” to make the people holy through His own blood. Then it says, “Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach.”"
REPLACE: "“Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach.” Reproach is shame. Bearing it is carrying it, as Simon carried the wood. Simon was forced out. You are asked. Turn on your own road, and carry his shame after him, freely this time. Only the shame. He went out through that gate first, and the atoning, the dying, the finishing, that was his, with his own blood."
WITH: "His reproach was shame. He bore it for us, and we can go to Him with empty hands. He went out through that gate first, and gave His own blood to make us holy."
TITLE: A Cross Was Laid on Simon Without Asking
