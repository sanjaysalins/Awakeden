2. The two drivers

🧵

THE THREAD

The headline is a link: an Old Testament verse locks onto a New Testament verse.

"Two verses, far apart, lock together on Jesus."
🤝

THE ENCOUNTER

The headline is a scene with Jesus, and one true thing it changes for you.

"One moment with Jesus. One true thing for you."
Every film says the link type out loud:
PROPHECY OT looks ahead, NT says fulfilled
NT-STATED the New Testament makes the link
ECHO same words, we just show them (never the title)
DETAIL it's right there in the verse

Banned: calling an echo a prophecy · number codes · adding feelings the text doesn't say · fear framing · stock endings.
