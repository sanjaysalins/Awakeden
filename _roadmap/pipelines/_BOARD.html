3. How we work

  1. A lane takes the next idea from its "Up next" list. Free.
  2. The AI panel tests the idea, blind. Fable + Claude + Codex + Gemini (+ Grok when it has credit). Is it fresh? Is it true? Does it land on Jesus?
    About 1 in 3 ideas fail here. That is normal. It costs nothing.
  3. A passing idea comes to you. You pick it, or not.
  4. Words are written and checked by the panel. Everything on screen is also spoken, so dubbing works.
  5. Making the film costs money. I show you the price first. Nothing is spent until you say yes.
  6. Final checks, then it is ready. Title, poster and captions are checked. You post it.

4. Who does what

You
Pick ideas. Say yes to spend. Post films.
The lanes (P1-P4)
Each does its own work, in its own folder. At most 1 film being made and 1 idea being tested per lane.
The hub (me)
Keeps the shared files. Runs the AI panel queue and the paid queue, one at a time. Keeps this board up to date.
