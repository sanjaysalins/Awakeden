### THE CUT LETTERS (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- THE IDEA in one sentence: The title is a hole cut through a sheet of orange paper, and the only fire in the picture burns inside the letters.
- WHAT YOU SEE: A full-bleed vertical sheet of flat burnt-orange folk paper, heavy grain, a few faint lighter sunburst rays near the top, and a wide empty field. Centered, the title is stacked in three huge lines. Every letter is a clean window. Through the windows only: deep teal night, a low bed of coals, coral and mustard flame, a few cream sparks. No people, no faces, no halo, no extra scene.
- LETTERING: Drawn into the art. Thick hand-cut woodblock capitals, slightly uneven, folk, not a computer font. Three centered lines. The only text is "THE SECOND FIRE".
- TAGLINE: none
- WHY IT IS GREAT, not just good: You read the name by looking through it into the story, so the poster and the title are one object.
- PHONE TEST: An orange block with three lines of teal, flame-filled letters.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art woodcut poster, heavy paper grain, limited palette of burnt orange, mustard, coral, deep teal, and cream. A full-bleed sheet of burnt-orange paper with faint sunburst rays near the top and lots of empty orange space. Centered, enormous hand-cut woodblock capitals stacked in exactly three lines reading "THE" then "SECOND" then "FIRE", exactly this text, no other text. Each letter is a sharp hole cut through the paper. Inside the letter holes only, show a flat deep-teal night and a bed of glowing coals with coral and mustard flames and a few cream sparks. The fire exists only inside the letters. No people, no faces, no halo, no border, no logo, no watermark, no extra words.

### THE MISPRINT (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- THE IDEA in one sentence: The same face is printed twice, teal and orange, shifted apart like a bad registration, so the night and the morning will not line up.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Cream paper and a wide empty margin. One head, large, centered. The identical flat folk face is inked twice and offset: the deep-teal print shifts left, cheek turned away, worried. The burnt-orange print shifts right, eye wet, the start of a smile. Where the inks cross, they mix to dark coral. Short curly dark hair, short beard with grey flecks, visible woodgrain and print specks. A cold black coal sits in the teal offset. A lit coal sits in the orange offset. No scene behind him.
- LETTERING: Added later in the bottom margin, burnt-orange woodcut capitals: THE SECOND FIRE. The face stays clear of the words.
- TAGLINE: none
- WHY IT IS GREAT, not just good: One man, printed wrong on purpose, is the whole story of the night he cannot match.
- PHONE TEST: A two-color face, teal ghost to the left, orange face to the right.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art woodcut on cream paper with wide empty margins and heavy grain. One large head of a broad fisherman about 40, short curly dark hair, short beard with grey flecks, printed twice and misregistered. Deep-teal print offset left, face turned away, worried, with one small cold black coal. Burnt-orange print offset right, same face looking forward, one wet eye, a small smile beginning, with one small lit coral coal. Overlap mixes to dark coral. Flat shapes, print specks, no background scene, no halo, no second person. No lettering, no text, no border, no logo.

### THREE COALS (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- THE IDEA in one sentence: Three equal coals in a row, two cold and one just lit, with a small loaf against the living one.
- WHAT YOU SEE: A deep-teal field, grain, almost empty. Near the bottom, a thin cream scalloped line like a shore. Above it, three large flat coals, same size, evenly spaced. Left and middle: cold, dark brown-black, cream crack lines, no flame. Right: the same circle cracked open, coral and mustard flame, a few cream sparks and four-point stars rising only there. A small cream round loaf leans on the lit coal. Three short dotted rays above that coal. Nothing else.
- LETTERING: Added later in the empty teal at the top, tall cream hand-cut woodcut capitals: THE SECOND FIRE. Surrounded by space.
- TAGLINE: none
- WHY IT IS GREAT, not just good: The film's real count becomes three dots a child can read, and only one of them is awake.
- PHONE TEST: Three circles on dark teal. The right one glows.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art print, deep-teal paper, heavy grain, vast empty space. A thin cream scalloped shore line near the bottom. Three large equal flat coals in a row in the lower third. The left coal and the middle coal are cold, dark brown-black, with cream crack lines and no flame. The right coal is the same shape, cracked open with coral, orange, and mustard flame, a few cream sparks, and small four-point stars. A small cream round loaf leans against the lit coal. Three short dotted cream rays above the lit coal only. No people, no faces, no halo, no boat, no sheep. No lettering, no text, no border, no logo.

### MY #1 and why
THE CUT LETTERS. The title is the picture, it still reads at the size of a thumb, and it can fill a cinema wall with no extra scene. Keep the title THE SECOND FIRE: three simple words, and the film answers them.
