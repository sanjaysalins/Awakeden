### FACE TO FACE  (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- **THE IDEA:** Two faces in profile, Jesus and Peter, looking at each other. The empty space between their faces is the shape of one tall flame.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** A cream grain field. On the left is Jesus's profile in deep teal: long hair, beard, and the edge of a teal-and-orange patterned mantle at the bottom. On the right is Peter's profile, also in teal: short curly hair, short beard, rust tunic edge. Their noses and beards nearly touch at the middle of the frame. The gap between them is filled with burnt orange and mustard grain, so it reads as a flame that is sitting between them. Three small coal shapes sit where their chins and chests meet. A few printed sparks rise up the cream top third. Four colours only. Heavy grain.
- **LETTERING:** Added later as type. Use a chunky hand-cut folk serif in cream with uneven ink edges. Set it across the top third in two lines: "THE SECOND" small, "FIRE" huge.
- **TAGLINE:** "He asked three times."
- **WHY IT IS GREAT:** You get two things in one look. It's a flame, and then it's two faces. The whole film is that face-to-face talk by the fire.
- **PHONE TEST:** At 2 cm you still see the orange flame shape between two dark heads, and the word FIRE.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** "Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art print poster, grainy risograph texture, limited palette of deep teal, burnt orange, mustard, cream. Pure silhouette, figure-ground illusion: two head-and-shoulder profiles facing each other, nearly touching. Left: Jesus, long dark hair, beard, teal and orange patterned mantle at the shoulders. Right: Peter, broad fisherman about 40, short curly hair, short beard, rust-brown tunic. Both are solid deep teal silhouettes with no interior detail. The negative space between their faces forms one tall clean flame, filled with burnt orange and mustard grain. Three small glowing coals at the base of the flame. Small folk-art four-point sparks rising into a plain cream upper third. Bold negative space, no halo, no glow ring behind any head. No lettering, no text."

### FIRE OF COALS  (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- **THE IDEA:** The title is the fire. The letters are built from glowing coals and stones on the sand, and flames lick up off the word FIRE.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** At the top is a sunburst dawn sky with dotted rays, mustard and coral, and a tiny boat on scalloped teal waves. In the middle, two small figures sit by the shore: Jesus in cream and teal, Peter in rust. The bottom half is the lettering. "THE SECOND" is carved in cream like driftwood. "FIRE" is laid out in glowing coals inside a ring of stones, with real folk flames and steam curls rising off it toward the figures. Bread and one fish rest on the "I".
- **LETTERING:** Drawn into the art by the image model, as blocky woodcut capitals.
- **TAGLINE:** None. The letters are the image.
- **WHY IT IS GREAT:** Nobody reads the title and then looks at the picture. They are the same thing, so it stays in your memory.
- **PHONE TEST:** The word FIRE glowing orange on dark sand, under a bright sunburst.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** "Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art linocut print poster, grain texture, palette burnt orange, mustard, coral, deep teal, cream. Top: sunburst dawn sky with dotted rays, scalloped teal sea, one small fishing boat, two gulls. Middle: on the sand, two small figures sitting by the shore: Jesus (long dark hair, beard, cream tunic, teal and orange patterned mantle) and Peter (broad, short curly dark hair, short beard, rust-brown tunic). Bottom half: large hand-carved woodcut capital letters reading \"THE SECOND FIRE\". \"THE SECOND\" in cream wood-grain letters. \"FIRE\" built from glowing orange coals inside a ring of grey stones, folk flames and steam curls rising from the letters, a round bread and one fish resting on the letter I. Exactly this text, no other text. No halo behind any head."

### THE FOLD  (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- **THE IDEA:** One fire seen twice, like a page folded in half. Night on top and dawn below are mirror images, with Peter turned the opposite way in each.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The top half is deep teal night with a stone wall, a fire bowl and small stars. Peter is a small dark shape walking away, with his back to the fire. Three coals are dark. The bottom half is a burnt-orange dawn beach with the same fire shape upside down like a reflection. Peter faces the fire and Jesus sits across from him. Three coals are lit. A woven folk band runs across the middle, and sparks cross it from the dawn fire up into the night.
- **LETTERING:** Added later. Set the title in cream on the woven band, with tracked folk capitals.
- **TAGLINE:** "Same fire. New morning."
- **WHY IT IS GREAT:** The question "what was the first fire?" gets its answer on the poster itself, and a no-Bible viewer can follow it.
- **PHONE TEST:** Teal over orange split by a band, a small fire in each half, and the title on the seam.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** "Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art print poster, grain texture, mirrored composition split by a horizontal woven folk-pattern band across the centre. Top half deep teal night: stone courtyard wall, small fire bowl, four-point stars, a small rust-tunic fisherman (Peter, short curly dark hair, short beard) walking away with his back to the fire, three small dark cold coals. Bottom half burnt orange and mustard sunrise beach: the same fire shape mirrored, Peter seated facing the fire, Jesus seated opposite (long dark hair, beard, cream tunic, teal and orange patterned mantle), three small glowing coals, sunburst rays. Printed sparks rising from the beach fire across the band into the night. Empty band for later type. No halo. No lettering, no text."

### GIVEN  (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- **THE IDEA:** Huge and close: one hand gives bread down into two open hands. Under them, three coals are the only light in a dark field.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** Almost all of the frame is near-black teal. Lower centre, Jesus's hand (cream sleeve, a hint of patterned mantle) places a round flatbread into Peter's cupped, rough hands (rust sleeve). Three lit coals glow below and send warm orange up onto the palms. One thread of steam rises. The top 60% is empty.
- **LETTERING:** Added later. Small cream folk capitals in the empty dark.
- **TAGLINE:** "First, He fed him."
- **WHY IT IS GREAT:** It's quiet. On a loud feed, a picture of one tender act stops the thumb.
- **PHONE TEST:** A warm spot of hands and coals glowing in dark space.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** "Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art print poster, heavy grain, near-black deep teal background, vast empty negative space in the top 60%. Lower centre close-up: one hand with cream sleeve and teal-orange patterned cuff gently placing a round flatbread into two open, rough, cupped fisherman's hands with rust-brown sleeves. Three small glowing coals beneath, warm orange and mustard light on the palms, one curl of steam rising. No faces. No lettering, no text."

### MY #1 and why
My #1 is **FACE TO FACE**. It's one graphic trick you only need to see once: a flame that turns into two faces. It shows the true heart of the film, and it is still the clearest of the four at thumb size.

I'd keep the title "THE SECOND FIRE". It opens the question "what was the first?", and the film answers it.
