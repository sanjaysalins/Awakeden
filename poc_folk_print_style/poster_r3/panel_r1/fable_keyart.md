### 1. FOOTPRINTS TO THE FIRE  (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- THE IDEA: Seen from straight above, the "O" of SECOND is the ring of fire stones, and one line of wet footprints runs out of the sea to it.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Top 2/3 is empty cream sand with grain. The title sits in the upper third; its O is the stone ring with glowing coals, one fish, one round bread. From a scalloped deep-teal wave along the bottom edge, dark wet footprints climb in a slightly curved line to the ring. Three small coals lie beside the ring: all lit. Palette: cream, teal, burnt orange, a touch of mustard.
- LETTERING: Drawn into the art. Chunky hand-cut folk capitals, burnt orange with a teal offset shadow, like a misregistered block print. Stacked: THE / SECOND / FIRE.
- TAGLINE: "He ran back." (small, teal, by the footprints)
- WHY IT IS GREAT: No faces, one question (who ran, and to what?), and the title IS the picture.
- PHONE TEST: Cream field, orange ring, a dotted trail from blue. Reads as "someone came out of the sea to a fire."
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art block-print poster, grain texture, limited palette of cream, deep teal, burnt orange, mustard. Top-down view of an empty cream sand beach filling most of the frame. Hand-cut chunky folk capital lettering in burnt orange with a teal offset print shadow, stacked in three lines reading "THE" "SECOND" "FIRE", exactly this text, no other text. The letter O in SECOND is a ring of grey stones seen from above with glowing orange coals, one fish and one round scored bread inside it. Three small glowing coals beside the ring. A single trail of dark wet bare footprints climbs from a scalloped deep-teal wave along the bottom edge up to the stone ring. No people, no halo shapes, bold negative space.

### 2. THE FLAME BETWEEN THEM  (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- THE IDEA: One tall flame whose two edges are the profiles of Jesus and Peter, face to face (a shape hidden in a shape).
- WHAT YOU SEE: Deep teal field, grain, a few four-point stars fading to nothing near the top. Centre: one huge flame in burnt orange, coral and mustard layers. Its left edge is cut as Jesus's profile (long hair, beard), its right edge as Peter's (curly hair, short beard), so the two teal faces look at each other across the fire. Below the flame: three small coals, the first two lit, the third just catching.
- LETTERING: Added later as type. Wide-spaced cream folk capitals at the base, lightly rough-edged.
- TAGLINE: "Do you love me?"
- WHY IT IS GREAT: Pure silhouette; you see a flame first, then the two faces, and you look twice.
- PHONE TEST: An orange flame on teal. At thumb size the two faces still read as notches.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art block-print poster, grain texture, deep teal background with a few tiny cream four-point stars. One tall central flame shape in flat layers of burnt orange, coral and mustard. The flame's left edge is the negative-space silhouette profile of a man with long hair and a beard; its right edge is the negative-space silhouette profile of a man with short curly hair and a short beard; the two teal profiles face each other across the flame. Three small coals below, two glowing, one just catching. Pure silhouette, no facial detail, no halo shapes, large empty teal space at top and bottom. No lettering, no text.

### 3. NIGHT / MORNING  (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- THE IDEA: A playing-card mirror: the same man by the same fire twice, upright in the morning, upside down in the night.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Built from the film's picture 11 idea, made bolder. Top half: sunrise fan of orange and mustard rays, Peter kneeling at the beach fire, open hands held to it. Bottom half, rotated 180 degrees: night teal, Peter at the courtyard fire with his face turned away, three dead coals. A woven folk band crosses the middle and carries the title. Sparks cross the band from the morning fire into the night.
- LETTERING: Drawn into the art. Cream folk capitals stitched into the woven band, like embroidery.
- TAGLINE: "Same fire. New morning."
- WHY IT IS GREAT: It holds the whole film in one object you can turn in your mind.
- PHONE TEST: Orange half, teal half, a fire in each. "Two fires" reads at once.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art block-print poster, grain texture, designed like a playing card with two mirrored halves. Top half: sunrise sky of burnt orange and mustard fan rays, a broad fisherman about 40 with short curly dark hair, short grey-flecked beard, rust-brown tunic, kneeling by a small beach fire in a stone ring, open hands toward it. Bottom half, rotated upside down: deep teal night stone courtyard, the same man beside a fire bowl, face turned away, three dark dead coals. A horizontal woven folk-pattern band across the centre with cream embroidered capital lettering reading "THE SECOND FIRE", exactly this text, no other text. Small sparks crossing the band. No halo shapes.

### 4. THE NIGHT HE CARRIES  (title: THE SECOND FIRE)
- THE IDEA: Double exposure: Peter's dark profile is filled with the night fire, while a tiny morning fire waits ahead of him.
- WHAT YOU SEE: Coral-to-mustard sunburst sky fills the frame. Large teal profile of Peter, looking right. Inside his head: the courtyard, a crescent moon, the fire bowl, three dead coals. Far right, low, very small: a shore, a thin line of smoke, one small fire with a standing figure beside it.
- LETTERING: Added later. Hand-drawn cream capitals; the I of FIRE is a small flame.
- TAGLINE: "One night. One morning."
- WHY IT IS GREAT: It draws the film's first line: the night you can't stop thinking about.
- PHONE TEST: A dark head on orange with a glow inside it.
- IMAGE PROMPT: Vertical 9:16 flat folk-art block-print poster, grain texture. Background: coral, burnt orange and mustard sunburst rays. Large deep-teal side-profile silhouette of a man with short curly hair and short beard, facing right, filling the left two thirds. Inside the silhouette, a double-exposure night scene: stone courtyard, crescent moon, a fire bowl, three dark coals. Far lower right, tiny: a cream beach, one small fire with rising smoke and one small standing figure in a cream tunic with teal and orange mantle. No halo shapes, no lettering, no text.

### MY #1
**Footprints to the Fire.** It is the only one where the title is the art, it needs no face to work at 2 cm, and the trail from the sea asks a question the film truly answers.
