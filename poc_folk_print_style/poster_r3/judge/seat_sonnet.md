# Seat: sonnet (blind)

## 1. First glance
I would tap **Q10**. The sunburst plus the dark profile with a night scene inside him is a real cinema one-sheet, and it makes me ask "what is he carrying?".

## 3. Top 3
1. **Q10**: one clear face, a story told in the shoulder (night, wall, fire), a sunrise burst behind him, and the second fire far off. Best craft and best curiosity.
2. **Q09**: same idea, brighter and more colourful, but the lower corner is busier and the tagline fights the art.
3. **Q02**: the torn-paper hand-and-bread image is the most direct picture of the film's ending, and the scared eye gives emotion.

## 4. For Q10: the one change
Make the second fire and the figure on the beach larger and lighter (bring them up out of the dark corner, clear of the title). That way the "two fires" idea is understood at a glance and the tagline has clean space.
