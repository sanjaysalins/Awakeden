# Seat: opus (blind)

## 1. First glance (phone size)
I would tap **Q09**: a big sad face against hot sunrise rays, with a night fire glowing inside his chest. It is the strongest colour hit on the shelf, and it makes me ask what he carried.

## 3. Top 3
1. **Q09.** Real poster craft. It shows both fires in one image (night inside him, sunrise ahead of him). It has the most emotion and the strongest colour on the shelf.
2. **Q05.** The most intimate "he fed him first" moment, with rich print texture and a strong eye hook. It gives the payoff without spoiling the story.
3. **Q12.** The cleanest cinema-lobby design. It looks great small. It just needs the two figures told apart.

## 4. One change for #1 (Q09)
Lift the sunrise fire and the small Jesus figure **up and out of the title band and make them about 2x bigger**, so the second fire (and who is waiting there) reads at thumb size. Keep the title clear of all art.
