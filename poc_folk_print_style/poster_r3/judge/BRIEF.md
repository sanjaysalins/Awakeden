# Blind judge: 12 movie-poster thumbnails for one short film

The film (88 s vertical short, flat folk-art print style): The night Jesus was arrested, His friend Peter stood by a fire and
said three times he did not know Him. After Jesus rose, Peter went back to fishing. At sunrise Jesus waited on the beach by
another "fire of coals" (in the King James Bible these words appear only twice), cooked breakfast, gave Peter bread, and asked
him three times "Do you love me?" Three no's at the first fire, three yes's at the second. Ending: "Before Jesus asked Peter to
feed anyone, Jesus fed Peter." In the film, three small coals go out on the three no's and light again on the three yes's.

Title on all twelve: THE SECOND FIRE (some have the title drawn into the art, some as type with a small tagline).
Audience: worldwide, many with English as a second language, many with no Bible knowledge, scrolling on a phone.

Look at: PHONE_SIZE.jpg (all twelve at real phone-shelf size) FIRST, then Q01.jpg .. Q12.jpg (bigger). The bar is HIGH: would this hang in a cinema lobby and also stop a thumb?
