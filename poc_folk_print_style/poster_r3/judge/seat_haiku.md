## 3. Top 3

1. **Q02**: strongest stop, the hand and bread tell the "fed him first" line at once, and the torn edge gives depth.
2. **Q12**: cleanest silhouette, reads at a glance, strong lobby feel. Weak on the meal.
3. **Q11**: bold title and the fire-in-the-O idea is clever. Risk: the O swap may read as a typo.

## 4. One change for my #1 (Q02)

Make the coals bigger and brighter in the torn panel, so the second fire is the thing the eye lands on after the face.
