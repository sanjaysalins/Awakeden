## 3. Top 3

1. **Q02** — The only one with a face that hits you plus a clear act (bread given over coals). The tagline "He fed him first" lands the whole film.
2. **Q09** — Best true cinema-lobby craft. Night inside the man, sunrise behind him, both fires in one picture.
3. **Q03** — Biggest, clearest title at phone size, and the three coals and footprints are a quiet, honest hook.

## 4. The ONE change for Q02

Change the look in the eye from fear to being undone by kindness: soften the brow and add one tear on the lower lid. Then the face says "forgiven", not "horror film".
