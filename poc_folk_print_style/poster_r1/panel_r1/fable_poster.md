# Fable seat: movie poster + movie title

## 1. TITLES

1. **★ The Second Fire** | What was the first fire? | Number + noun, like a real drama; the film answers it exactly (picture 11).
2. **★ Three Times** | Three times what? | Short, plain, ESL-safe; pays off twice (three no, three yes).
3. **Two Fires** | Why two? What links them? | Sounds like a quiet festival film; the poster can show both.
4. **A Fire of Coals** | Why does this small fire matter? | The film's own quoted words; literary feel.
5. **The Fire on the Shore** | Who lit it, and for whom? | Place + object, classic film naming.
6. **Before the Rooster** | What happened before it crowed? | Thriller tone; honest (picture 4).
7. **Peter's Fire** | What did Peter do at a fire? | Name + object, simple biopic shape.
8. **Come and Eat** | Who is asking, after what? | Warm, three easy words, the film's turning line.

## 2. POSTERS

### A. "TWO FIRES, ONE MAN" - pairs with THE SECOND FIRE (my lead)
- **IMAGE:** NEW art. Frame split top/bottom. Top 45%: deep teal night, Peter small, back turned, walking away from a fire bowl, crescent moon. Bottom 55%: orange sunrise beach, Peter large, kneeling at a small stone-ring fire, hands open, Jesus's hands (only hands and patterned sleeve) holding out round bread from the right edge. A thin line of sparks rises from the beach fire across the split into the night fire. Same man twice = the story.
- **TITLE TREATMENT:** On the split line, a cream band across the full width. "THE SECOND" small, "FIRE" huge (about 70% width). Chunky hand-cut block capitals, slightly uneven like a woodcut. Dark teal letters, the "I" of FIRE is a small coral flame.
- **TAGLINE:** "He said no at the first."
- **EXTRAS:** Thin mustard border. One tiny credit line at the bottom: "A TRUE STORY FROM THE BIBLE · JOHN 18 & 21". Nothing else.
- **WHY IT STOPS A THUMB:** Cold teal against hot orange with one face in both halves asks "what changed?" at a glance.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Flat folk-art print illustration, vertical 9:16, grain texture, limited palette burnt orange, mustard, coral, deep teal, cream. Composition split into two halves by a plain empty cream horizontal band at the middle. TOP HALF: deep teal night stone courtyard, crescent moon, small four-point stars, a round stone fire bowl with low flames, one broad fisherman about 40 with short curly dark hair, short beard with grey flecks, rust-brown tunic, seen small from behind, walking away from the fire, head down. BOTTOM HALF: sunrise beach, bold sunburst sky in orange and mustard stripes, calm teal sea, the same fisherman large in the foreground, kneeling on sand beside a small ring of stones with glowing coals, his two hands open and raised, a tear on his cheek and a small smile; from the right edge two hands in a cream sleeve with a teal and orange patterned mantle hold out one round loaf of bread toward him; the giver's face is out of frame. A thin trail of small sparks rises from the beach coals up through the cream band into the night fire. Simple shapes, thick outlines, no halo shapes, no lettering, no text, no symbols, no border.

### B. "THE HANDS" - pairs with THREE TIMES
- **IMAGE:** Built from film picture 17, cropped tight: Jesus's hands placing round bread into Peter's open hands, Peter's tear and smile at top left, coals glowing below. Add the film's three small lit coals in a clear row under the bread, each larger than in the film.
- **TITLE TREATMENT:** Bottom third, over a dark teal panel. "THREE TIMES" in two stacked lines, tall narrow cream capitals, wide spacing. Three small coal dots sit centered above the title, like the star row on a film poster.
- **TAGLINE:** "No. No. No. Then this."
- **EXTRAS:** Top edge, small mustard line: "THE NIGHT HE COULD NOT FORGET". No credit block.
- **WHY IT STOPS A THUMB:** A crying man smiling at bread is a strange, human picture; the three dots make you count.

### C. "THE FACE" - pairs with THE SECOND FIRE (16:9 friendly)
- **IMAGE:** Built from film pictures 1 and 14. Peter's face, split down the middle: left half night (teal, worried, picture 1), right half sunrise (orange, wet eyes, hand on chest, picture 14). A small fire sits at the bottom of each half. For 16:9: face in the left 45%, title in the right 55% on cream.
- **TITLE TREATMENT:** Vertical 9:16: title across the lower chest area in a coral band, cream block capitals, "FIRE" twice the size of "THE SECOND". 
- **TAGLINE:** "One man. Two fires."
- **EXTRAS:** Thin woven folk band (from picture 11) as the divider line. Small date-line feel at the bottom: "88 SECONDS".
- **WHY IT STOPS A THUMB:** A big face reads at any size, and a two-colour face signals a before and after.

## 3. RISKS

1. **Too much poster furniture, unreadable on a phone.** Credit blocks turn to grey mush at thumbnail size. Fix: one title, one tagline, one tiny line at most. Test at 120 pixels wide; if "FIRE" is not readable, cut something.
2. **Looks like a happy picnic, or promises a disaster film.** The beach art is sunny; a fire title alone can suggest burning or danger. Fix: always show the cold teal night beside the warm fire (posters A and C), and keep the tagline about a person ("He said no"), never about flames or doom.
