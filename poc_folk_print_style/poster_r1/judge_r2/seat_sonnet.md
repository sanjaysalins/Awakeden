## 4. For #1 (P5)
Add a small sign of rescue to the right (sunrise) half: a hand holding bread, or Jesus' hand, reaching into the frame, so the sunrise side says "he was fed" and not just "he stopped crying". The two fires also read as bigger and brighter than they are, so make the left fire weak and the right fire strong.
