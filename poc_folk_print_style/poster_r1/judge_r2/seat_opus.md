# Seat: opus (blind)

## 1. First glance (phone size)
I'd tap **P5**. One big sad face split into night and day reads in half a second, and "ONE MAN. TWO FIRES." is the clearest tagline here.
Hard to read at phone size: the taglines on **P1** ("He said no at the first") and **P2** ("One night. One sunrise.") because they sit over busy art with low contrast. **P4**'s tagline sits on the fire pit and almost disappears. **P6**'s bottom credit line is faded and broken up.

## 4. One change for P5
Make the two fires **different**: on the night side a cold, dying fire (three dark, burned-out coals); on the sunrise side a bright coal fire with a loaf of bread on it. Then the poster itself shows the turn from three "no"s to being fed, not just "two fires".
