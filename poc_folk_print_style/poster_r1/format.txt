OUTPUT FORMAT (use these headings, under 800 words):
1. TITLES - 8 movie titles (1-4 words each). For each: the title | the question it opens | why it sounds like a real film.
   Star your top 2.
2. POSTERS - 3 poster concepts. For each:
   - NAME + which title it pairs with
   - IMAGE: exactly what is in the picture (who, where, pose, light, colours); say if it is built from film picture N or NEW art
   - TITLE TREATMENT: where the title sits, size, letter style (in words), colour
   - TAGLINE: 2-7 plain words (or none)
   - EXTRAS: any poster furniture (credit block, date line, border, etc.) - keep it readable at phone size
   - WHY IT STOPS A THUMB: one line
   - IMAGE PROMPT (only if NEW art): the exact image-generation prompt, folk-art style, 9:16, NO lettering in the art
     (the title is added later as real type).
3. RISKS - 2 ways a poster thumbnail could fail here, and how to avoid them.
