## TITLE
The Second Fire

## WHO / TAKEAWAY / CURIOSITY GAP / WHY IT EARNS ITS PLACE
**WHO:** Someone who failed badly and now thinks, "I can't go back."
**TAKEAWAY:** Jesus met Peter again at the same kind of fire where he failed. He fed him, and gave him work to do.
**CURIOSITY GAP:** Peter failed by a fire. What happens when he stands by a fire again?
**WHY IT EARNS ITS PLACE:** The Bible uses "a fire of coals" only twice. Few viewers know this, and the story turns on it.

## NARRATION
NARRATOR: The last time Peter stood by a fire, he said he did not know Jesus. Three times.
PETER: "I am not."
NARRATOR: After Jesus rose, Peter went back to fishing. All night, they caught nothing. At sunrise, a man on the shore calls out:
JESUS: "Children, have ye any meat?"
NARRATOR: Any food? No. Throw the net on the right side. It fills, too heavy to pull. "It is the Lord." Peter jumps into the sea. On the beach: a phrase the New Testament uses only one other time. "A fire of coals." The same kind of fire where Peter failed. Fish on it. Bread.
JESUS: "Come and dine."
NARRATOR: Come and eat. After breakfast, Jesus asks him three times:
JESUS: "Lovest thou me?"
NARRATOR: Do you love me?
PETER: "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee."
JESUS: "Feed my sheep."
NARRATOR: Three times, Peter said "I am not" by one fire. Three times, he says "I love thee" by another. Jesus did not send him away. He fed him, then gave him others to feed. Same kind of fire. A new ending.

**Word count: 172** (about 70 seconds)

## PICTURES
1. **"The last time… Three times."** It is night in a courtyard. Peter stands among servants, warming his hands at a coal fire. **Moves:** the coals glow and pulse, and sparks rise.
2. **"I am not."** A close-up of Peter's face, half in firelight, half in teal shadow, as he turns away. **Moves:** the firelight flickers on his face. A small rooster shape on a far wall.
3. **"After Jesus rose… caught nothing."** A small boat on a dark teal sea under stars, with an empty net hanging over the side. **Moves:** the boat rocks slowly and drips fall from the net.
4. **"At sunrise… have ye any meat?"** A big orange sun comes up behind the palms. A figure stands small on the shore. **Moves:** the sun climbs and light spreads over the water.
5. **"Any food? No… too heavy to pull."** The net is on the right side, bulging with fish. The men lean back and pull. **Moves:** the net strains and the fish flash in the light.
6. **"It is the Lord." Peter jumps…** Peter, his coat tied on, leaps from the boat toward the shore. **Moves:** a splash, and the waves spread out in rings.
7. **"On the beach… Fish on it. Bread."** A coal fire on the sand, with fish laid on it and loaves beside it. The framing matches picture 1. **Moves:** the same pulse of the coals, but the light is now warm sunrise, not night.
8. **"Come and dine… Do you love me?"** Jesus hands out bread, then sits facing Peter across the fire. The others eat behind them. **Moves:** the smoke curls up between them, and Jesus's hand holds out bread.
9. **"Lord, thou knowest… Feed my sheep."** Peter looks up at Jesus, both lit by the fire. A flock of sheep fades in softly on a hillside behind. **Moves:** steam rises from the bread and the sheep drift into view.
10. **"Three times… A new ending."** A split frame: the night fire on the left, the sunrise fire on the right. Peter's posture is turned away on one side and facing Jesus on the other. **Moves:** the left side fades to dark, and the right side's sunrise fills the whole frame.

## WHY THE OPENING HOLDS SOMEONE WHO FEELS DISQUALIFIED
The first line starts with a real failure, with no lesson attached: a man lied about Jesus three times. The viewer who replays their own worst moment sees themselves in Peter before any comfort is offered.

## WHY THE ENDING IS EARNED
It pays off "a fire of coals" (John 18:18 / 21:9) and Peter's three answers. The "I am not" set up in picture 2 is answered by "I love thee" at the second fire. "Feed my sheep" pays off the breakfast: the man who was fed is sent to feed others.

## RISKS
**Newcomer:** "Have ye any meat" and "dine" could confuse, so each one is glossed right away ("Any food?", "Come and eat"). "Sheep" could be read literally, so picture 9 and "others to feed" point to people.
**Bible reader:** John never says the three questions answer the three denials, so the script only shows the pattern ("three times… three times") without claiming John says it. It never says who lit the fire. "Feed my lambs" and the second question are left out for length; the line "asks him three times" keeps the count true.
