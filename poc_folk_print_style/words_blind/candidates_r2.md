## Film C — "The Second Fire" (ends on the meal)
NARRATOR: Is there one night you keep replaying? The night Jesus was arrested, His friend Peter stood by a fire. People asked: are you one of His followers?
PETER: I am not.
NARRATOR: Three times, he said no. Jesus died. He rose from the dead. Peter went back to fishing. All night, nothing. At sunrise, Jesus stands on the shore. He tells them where to throw the net. It fills. "It is the Lord." Peter jumps into the sea. On the beach: "a fire of coals." In the whole Bible, those exact words appear only twice. Here, and the night Peter said no. This one has fish and bread.
JESUS: Come and dine.
NARRATOR: Come and eat. Jesus hands them bread and fish. After the meal, He asks Peter three times:
JESUS: Lovest thou me?
NARRATOR: Do you love me? The third time, Peter is hurt.
PETER: Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee.
NARRATOR: You know everything. You know I love you.
JESUS: Feed my sheep.
NARRATOR: Take care of my people. Three times, no, at the first fire. Three times, yes, at the second. And before Jesus asked Peter to feed anyone, Jesus fed Peter.
