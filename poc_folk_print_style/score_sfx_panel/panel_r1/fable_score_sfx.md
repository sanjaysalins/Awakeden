# The Second Fire - score + SFX plan

## 1. SCORE IDEA
One small folk tune, heard broken and falling at the night fire, then whole and rising at the sunrise fire.

- **Instruments:** fingerpicked nylon-string guitar (lead), low wooden flute, one cello on long low notes, soft hand frame drum (morning only).
- **Motif:** three notes. At night they step DOWN and stop short (three no's). In the morning the same three step UP and resolve (three yes's).

## 2. WHY IT BEATS a sparse solo felt piano
- Plucked strings and wood flute sound like a shore and a hand-made print. Piano sounds like a room.
- The motif lets you HEAR "three no's undone by three yes's".
- Four colours give a real arc, yet each plays few notes, so the voice stays on top.

## 4. SPLIT
Two parts, split at **23.7 s**, the night-to-sunrise hard cut, 0.4 s crossfade. Part 1 D minor, Part 2 D major: same root, same instruments, so the join sounds like the sun coming up.

## 5. MUSIC PROMPTS
**Part 1:** "Instrumental, 24 seconds, D minor, slow 60 bpm. Intimate folk score: fingerpicked nylon-string guitar over one sustained low cello note. No drums, no piano, no choir. Opens with three slow guitar notes stepping downward that stop unresolved. Sparse, lonely. Around 13 seconds in the three falling notes return. Around 16 seconds in almost only cello, then one warm guitar chord. Around 19 seconds in a few thin guitar notes. Ends held on a low D cello note, no fade-out."

**Part 2:** "Instrumental, 65 seconds, D major, gentle 72 bpm. Warm folk score: fingerpicked nylon-string guitar, low wooden flute, one cello, very soft hand frame drum. No piano, no choir, never epic. Starts immediately on a low D cello note; the flute plays a simple hopeful three-note rising melody like a sunrise. Around 5 seconds in a gentle rolling guitar pattern with soft drum. Around 13 seconds in it thins to cello and single guitar notes. Around 24 seconds in warm full guitar with flute. Around 30 seconds in the drum stops and the three rising notes repeat three times, each quieter. Around 44 seconds in flute and guitar lift gently. Around 61 seconds in solo guitar plays the three rising notes once, slowly, and the final D major chord rings to the very end. No early fade-out, no silent gaps."

## 7. MIX NOTES
- Music ~16-18 dB under voice; SFX beds 20+ dB under. Sidechain duck both.
- Music nearly out at 15.7-17.0 and under "I am not" (11.7-12.2): Peter's line stands alone with the fire.
- 56-71: only soft fire and waves under the questions.
- Hold: music lifts ~6 dB, rising motif once, bread tear at 85.0, fire and waves to the last frame (audio runs the full 87.9 s).

## 8. RISKS
1. **Join at 23.7 clashes or leaves dead air.** Listen to 22-26 s alone; sweep loudness in 0.5 s windows, none may fall silent.
2. **Score too busy, or fades early.** Measure music-only level at 84.7-87.9 (must still sound at 87.5). By ear: every word clear on a phone speaker.
3. **Rooster or splash sounds cartoon.** Hear each new sound solo; make 2 takes each (~$0.40), pick by ear. Still comic? Drop the rooster.
