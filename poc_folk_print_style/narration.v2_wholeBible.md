NARRATOR: Is there one night you can't stop thinking about? The night Jesus was arrested, His friend Peter stood by a fire. People asked if he was one of His followers.
PETER: I am not.
NARRATOR: Three times, he said no. Jesus died. He rose from the dead. Peter went back to fishing. All night, nothing. At sunrise, Jesus stands on the shore. He tells them where to throw the net. It fills with fish. A friend cries, "It is the Lord." Peter jumps into the sea to reach Him. On the beach: "a fire of coals." In the whole Bible, those exact words appear only twice. Here, and the night Peter said no.
JESUS: Come and dine.
NARRATOR: Come and eat. Jesus hands them bread and fish. After the meal, He asks Peter three times:
JESUS: Lovest thou me?
NARRATOR: Do you love me? The third time, Peter is sad.
PETER: Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee.
NARRATOR: You know everything. You know I love you.
JESUS: Feed my sheep.
NARRATOR: Take care of my people. Three times, no, that night. Three times, yes, at the second fire. And before Jesus asked Peter to feed anyone, Jesus fed Peter.
