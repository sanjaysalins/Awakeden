# Folk Print style (POC) — owner ref 2026-11-12

Ref: `refs/ref_01_teaching_by_sea.jpg` (Jesus seated on a rock, teaching a crowd by the sea).

## What the style is
- Flat mid-century folk-art illustration, 16:9 in the ref.
- Warm palette: burnt orange, coral, mustard sky; deep teal / bottle green; cream.
- Risograph grain + speckle texture over everything. No gradients except sun rays.
- Bold simple shapes, thin dark outlines, big friendly eyes.
- Clothes carry woven geometric patterns (dots, zigzags, stripes).
- Stylised palms, olive trees, spiky foreground plants, sunburst sky, flat-roof stone houses, Sea of Galilee with small boats.

## Era / consistency watch-list (fix in prompts, per past POCs)
- Ref has a sun disc behind Jesus's head that reads as a HALO -> keep the sun, but not centred on His head.
- Several men wear keffiyeh-style head cloths with cords -> earlier era rule: plain draped head cloths, no agal cords.
- Arched doorways -> prefer square lintel doorways, flat mudbrick/limestone roofs.
- Faint fake glyphs top-left -> no lettering, no symbols in the art.
- Jesus: long dark hair, beard, cream tunic, teal + orange patterned mantle, sandals. Keep this exact look across every still (chain the ref).
