How this maps to the redo work. Each still that gets re-rendered forces two downstream steps in that piece: (1) re-animate the one Kling clip built from it (frozen-tableau crop-cut, per /animate), and (2) re-assemble that piece's cut so the fixed clip lands in place (/assemble or /livingpage), then re-score, re-caption, and re-extract the website read-page frames.

Two systemic root causes drive most of the list — fix once at the prompt/ref level before re-rolling piece by piece:
  1. Bent / floating / lying-across nails. Nearly every crucifixion close-up renders the nail flat on the palm, gripped in the fingers, or floating — instead of a single spike driven straight through. Pin the pose in the prompt ("iron spike driven flat through the centre of the open palm, head flush, no sideways shaft") and use it_is_finished_john1930/visual/nail_through_hand.png as the reference — the second pass confirmed it's rendered correctly.
  2. Candle / lantern instead of clay oil lamp. The renderer defaults to wax candles + glass/metal lanterns in every night/scribe scene. Positively specify "low open clay oil lamp, pinched spout, open flame, no wax, no glass, no candlestick." (Second pass cleared 4 false candle-calls where the light was actually an oil-lamp-on-a-stand — check the pixels before re-rolling a "candle.")
Other repeat traps in the list: Dome-of-the-Rock / minaret / Gothic / church-steeple skylines in period Jerusalem; Greek-Parthenon temples; legible pseudo-Hebrew on hero scrolls + garbled Latin titulus (also a Kling morph risk — hold as still); cloned faces + Christ-lookalikes standing in the sinner crowd; cartoon "dog-bone" lots and bones; a Batman bat-wing logo embossed on the thirty-pieces coins; ornate finery / corpse-grey figures on crucified bodies; and recurring art-style drift — individual stills render as Hokusai's "Great Wave", glossy anime/cel-shade, or painterly Baroque instead of the inked house style.
