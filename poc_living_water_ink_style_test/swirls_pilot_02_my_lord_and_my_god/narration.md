Ten men said the same thing: "We have seen the Lord."

But Thomas answered them: "Except I shall see in his hands the print of the nails, and put my finger into the print of the nails, and thrust my hand into his side, I will not believe."

Eight days he stayed with them anyway — the men he wouldn't believe.

Then came Jesus, the doors being shut, and stood in the midst, and said, "Peace be unto you."

He turned straight to Thomas: "Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands; and reach hither thy hand, and thrust it into my side: and be not faithless, but believing."

Thomas didn't touch a thing. He only said, "My Lord and my God."

And Jesus answered him — answered you too: "Because thou hast seen me, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed."
