Elevate Swirls

My read of what holds the series back, and the fixes in order. Based on files, ledger and notes. I have not re-watched the films for this page.

The short answer
Quality is not the problem. Rework and blind spots are. We re-draw the same defects again and again, and nobody outside has seen one episode.

Ep 13 repeats one defect: the cross draws as an X (saltire) on at least 6 of 9 pages. Also stray digits, garbled captions, figures that vanish in the clip, and panels that wrap. Each is found after paying for the render.

Money (ledger, rough, names are split): ep 13 about $19, ep 8 about $24, ep 11 about $14, ep 5 about $10, ep 4 about $5.

2. Gates were put off. The wait is over.

The pipeline doc says: "build real gates once 4-5 episodes have shipped clean." We have 12 finished. The defect list is long enough to turn into checks.

Today swirls_verify.py has cover-lighting, text-lock, refs-exist, word-count and freeze-budget checks, plus a Vision audit. The Vision audit is "no auto-retry, a human looks". That is why the cross went wrong 6 times.

3. Nobody outside has seen it

0 Swirls episodes posted. 12 finished. All held until 30.

So we guess at the hook, the cover hold (Jacob's Ladder opens on a ~10 s still), the CTA, and the thumbnail. No retention data at all. This is the largest unknown.

4. Smaller drags

  • Same ending shape in the first 4 episodes ("Ready to / Will you / Come and see"). Fixed in later words, not checked by a gate.
  • Bridge needs a live session. OpenArt has no API, so a build is never unattended and one stall blocks everything.
  • Motion is judged by eye or by 3 frames. Pixel-diff and first/last-frame checks are known, but not automatic.
  • Score and SFX were fixed by hand each time (dead air, duck). The two-part score and the felt-piano direction are already proven, so they can become defaults.
  • Open bugs: ep 12 "cuts off", pilot 02 has no episode.py, Jacob's Ladder thumbnail shows the title twice.

5. The plan

#FixCost
1Defect-to-check. Turn the repeat defects (X-cross, stray digits, garbled caption, vanishing figure, panel wrap, doubled title) into automatic pre-flight and post-render checks, so a bad frame never reaches the clip stage.$0
2Shape refs for hard props. The cross, a bier, a ladder: draw one approved side-on ref first, chain it into every page. Use a camera-angle change after 2 failed word fixes.$0 to set up
3Cheap draft pass. Render a page on the cheap model first, check composition, then pay for the final.saves money
4Motion check built in. Pixel-diff, first and last frame, on every clip. Fail = $0 push-in, no re-prompt.$0
5Words gate on every episode. Blind panel with a Fable seat, plus an ending-shape check against the shipped list.CLI use only
6Real-world test. Post 2 or 3 finished episodes, watch the first 48 hours, then judge hook and cover. This is your call because it ends the 30-episode hold early.your decision
7Make the bridge batchable. Queue a whole episode's requests, service them in one pass.$0

  • Whether the story and look are better than other Bible channels. Only viewers can say.
  • Whether "0 rejects" on old episodes is real.
  • Ledger totals are rough. Rows use many names.
