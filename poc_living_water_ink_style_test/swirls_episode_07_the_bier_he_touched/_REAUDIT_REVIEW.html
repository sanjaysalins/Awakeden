Second look at Episode 7's stills 5 real problems found

"The Bier He Touched" — a proper vision check ran on all 8 pictures before building the finished video. It's stricter than the check that ran when GATE 2 was approved. It found 5 real mistakes, all missed before.

What happened: before building the final video, every picture gets a computer check. That check never actually ran on this episode's pictures until now — so these 5 mistakes slipped through GATE 2 and even into last night's "GATE 3 LOCKED" clips.

The pattern: 4 of the 5 mistakes are the same thing going wrong — the little blue-ink swirl (this show's signature touch) drifting away from where it's supposed to be: off Jesus's hand, into a puddle, or turning into a literal ring/jewelry shape. The 5th is the empty bier on F06 looking like a ladder instead of a stretcher.

What this means for cost: fixing these needs new picture renders (rerolls) for F02, F04, F05, F06, and the back cover — real spend, same as any reroll. F04's clip was built as a free camera-push over its picture, so fixing the picture fixes that clip for free. F02/F05/F06/back already have paid animated clips from last night — fixing the picture means those clips likely need re-animating too (more spend) unless the picture-only fix turns out to be enough on a look.

Nothing has been changed yet. I'm asking before spending anything.

F04 — "Arise" ink dripping into a puddle

The turning point. This is the still that became the "clean" $0 camera-push clip in last night's GATE 3 lock — so this mistake is already in the locked video.

the whole page

zoomed — blue ink drips down from his fingers and pools on the ground

What's wrong: the swirl is supposed to only rise up into the air above his hand. Instead some of it drips DOWN and forms a puddle on the ground — the exact "ground puddle" problem that caused 7 failed animation tries on this same page last night, except this time it's baked into the still itself.

F05 — "he sat up" 2 problems: panel layout + floating ink

The boy sits up. Jesus's hand reaches out to give him back to his mother.

the whole page

zoomed — the swirl floats in mid-air between the two men, not touching either

What's wrong: (1) the 3 small panels at the top are supposed to be equal size, like every other page — here one is drawn full-width and the other two are squeezed underneath it. (2) The blue swirl floats free in the empty air between Jesus and the boy, not connected to his hand.

F06 — "the other direction" the empty bier looks like a ladder

The crowd goes home. The empty bier is left leaning by the gate.

the whole page

zoomed — rungs across it like a ladder, not the flat plank-and-poles bier

What's wrong: the empty bier should be the same flat plank-and-two-poles stretcher used on every other page. Here it's drawn with rungs across it, like a ladder or a drying rack, with the folded cloth sitting on one rung like a shelf. Same kind of mistake this episode already had to fix twice before (F03/F04's bier looking like a different object).

Back cover — "It became a homecoming" 2 problems: split object + ink is a literal ring

Dawn. The empty bier by the gate, the folded graveclothes on it.

the whole page

zoomed — a plain board leans on the wall (no poles, no cloth on it)

zoomed — the real bier (with poles) lies separately on the ground, holding the cloth and a ring-shaped blue object

What's wrong: (1) the empty bier is supposed to be ONE object leaning against the wall with the folded cloth resting on it. Instead there are two different wooden things — a plain board leaning on the wall, and the real bier lying flat on the ground with the cloth on it. (2) The little touch of blue ink that should sit on the folded cloth like a small ink curl instead renders as a solid, shiny ring or bracelet — a real object, not a swirl of ink.
