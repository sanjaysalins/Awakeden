Swirls audit: 12 finished films

$0. I measured the final video files and looked at 3 contact sheets myself (ep 12, ep 13, Jacob's Ladder).

What I found
  1. Most films are not 60 s. Only 4 of 12 are near 60. Ep 10 and ep 13 are about 106 s. Ep 11 is 84 s. Ep 12 is 78 s.
  2. Cover holds are real. Ep 13 shows the title card for 5.7 s, Jacob's Ladder for about 6 s, ep 12 for about 4 s, ep 11 for 3.1 s. The narrator is speaking from 0.1 s, so it is not dead air. But the picture is a title card.
  3. Low resolution. 11 of 12 are 720x1280. Only ep 12 is 1080x1920.
  4. 11 of 12 are not "fast-start". The file index is at the end. Uploads are fine. Browser or preview play can stall. A $0 re-wrap fixes it. I did not touch any file.
  5. No file is broken. All 12 decode with zero errors. Audio and video lengths match within 0.02 s.
  6. Ep 12 "cuts off": not reproduced. The file I found is complete (78 s, clean, audio = video). The bug may be in the player or an older file. Ep 12 has no pinned final file yet.
Not done: stray-digit and doubled-title scan. No OCR tool is installed here. From my eye, the Jacob's Ladder doubled title is a thumbnail problem, not in the film. Muted view: I saw only the title and small page art, so a muted viewer gets little. That is an eye read, not a test.
