Where I was wrong or too big

  • Do not build 6 generic gates. Build only exact ones: stray digits and doubled title by OCR, plus the ending-shape check. The soft ones (X-cross, vanishing figure) need Vision, which gives false alarms.
  • $0 push-in is not free. It costs the look that makes Swirls different. Never on the hook or the landing page. Re-render first.
  • "Ready to ship: 12" is closer to 9 once the open bugs count.
  • Money numbers are too rough to set targets from.
