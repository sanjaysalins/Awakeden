## Doctrine, both ways — PASS

- **Line 8 "That Lord is Jesus: God himself, come to take away sin."** Faithful, not over-reach. Isaiah 40:3 names the one whose way is made ready as "the LORD" and, in the same verse, "our God". Matthew 3:3 says John is that voice. John then points at Jesus. So the one who comes is the LORD, our God. "Come to take away sin" is John 1:29 in plain words.
- One soft spot: the proof for "God himself" is the half of Isaiah 40:3 the film does not quote ("a highway for our God"). The claim is sound, but a newcomer hears "Lord" and then gets "God himself" with the bridge off screen.
- "God himself" does not deny the Father or the Son. A newcomer may pause at "Lamb of God" (line 7) then "God himself" (line 8). That is the real mystery of the text, not an error.
- Dropping "of the world" narrows nothing false.

## Line 4 — questions in mouths? PASS, with a clarity flag

"Elijah, then? The Prophet?" is recorded: John 1:21 "What then? Art thou Elias? ... Art thou that prophet?" "No. No." matches "I am not" and "No." "They must have an answer" matches verse 22. Nothing invented, and it is not inside quote marks.

Flag: by ear, nobody is named as asking or answering. One narrator voice reads questions and answers with no "they" or "he". An ESL newcomer may not know who says "No. No." "The Prophet" is also unexplained.

## Other checks

- **Lands on Jesus:** yes, last line, by name.
- **One thread:** yes. Who is this man, not the Christ, only a voice, the voice makes ready the LORD's way, the LORD arrives. Holds end to end.
- **Hook:** "Who is this desert man?" is clear and short. Mild, not strong. It is paid off at line 5 and then turned onto Jesus, which works.
- **Plain words:** good. Risks: "crying" can read as weeping to ESL; "That Lord" in line 8 points back past line 7 to line 6, a long reach by ear.
- **Landing:** fresh, a statement not a stock closer. No gain/loss, no pressure. Note it has no invitation at all; fine for grace-anchored, but the owner should know it is a declaration, not a CTA.

## Fixes (all optional, none blocking)
