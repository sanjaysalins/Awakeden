### Conclusion

**MY PICK:** **Version C**.  
It has the strongest theological spine. Tying IsaiahΓÇÖs "Prepare ye the way of the LORD" directly to "That Lord is Jesus" in the closing beats is a massive, satisfying payoff.

**FAIL LINES IN ANY VERSION:**
*   **"Not Elijah. Not the Prophet." (A, B, C):** This is a FAIL for a global/newcomer audience. You cannot spend 2 seconds introducing and dismissing two massive Old Testament concepts. It creates confusion instead of clarity.
