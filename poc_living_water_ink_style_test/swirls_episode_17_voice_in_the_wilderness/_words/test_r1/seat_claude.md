# Seat: claude (blind, cold read)

## Version A

**Role 1 - newcomer: 7/10**
- Who: a desert man named John, then Jesus. What: leaders ask who he is; he says "not the Christ", only "a voice"; next day he points to "the Lamb of God". Why: the Lamb is Jesus, and God came near.
- Trouble spots: "the Christ", "Elijah", "the Prophet" are three names with no help (OK as a list of "nots"). Line 7 does not say WHO speaks or WHO is seen. "Lamb of God" is never explained. "taketh away" is old English.
- Line 1 "desert man" is concrete and easy. Good.

**Role 2 - Bible reader: 7/10**
- Accurate. Line 2 "at the river" fits John 1:28 (beyond Jordan). Line 4 "cannot go home with nothing" is a fair paraphrase of 1:22.
- Line 8 "God himself had come near" is orthodox but the film never links Isaiah's "way ... for God" to Jesus in words; the jump is left to the viewer. Lands on Jesus, but softly.
- Fresh: little beyond the plain story.

**Role 3 - curiosity gap: 7/10**
- Line 1 opens a real question. The answer ("just a voice") is a good twist. But the payoff moves to Jesus without saying why that answers "who is this man".

## Version B

**Role 1 - newcomer: 6/10**
- Line 1 "Who is John really?" assumes I already know a John. A newcomer has no "really" to compare to. Weak open.
- "Levites" is an unknown word. "Behold" (line 7 and the closing words) is old English and the ending leans on it.
- Who/what/why still comes through by line 8: "This Jesus takes away the world's sin" is the clearest "why" of all three.

**Role 2 - Bible reader: 8/10**
- Accurate. "Before any guess" (line 3) is a real, fresh catch: in John 1:19-20 nobody has named the Christ yet, and he denies it first. "No name" (line 5) is also true and fresh: he answers with a job, not a name.
- Line 7 cuts the quote at "Lamb of God" and line 8 finishes it in plain words. Good.
- Line 8 "Behold him" is an earned landing: John's own verb. Sound.
- Lighter on "Jesus is the LORD of Isaiah" than C; line 6 "God himself" then line 7 Jesus coming implies it.

**Role 3 - curiosity gap: 7/10**
- Best payoff (the man without a name points away from himself). Weakest opening question for a stranger.

## Version C

**Role 1 - newcomer: 7/10**
- Who: a man baptizing, John, then Jesus. What: same story. Why: line 8 says Jesus is the Lord Isaiah wrote about, God himself.
- Trouble spots: "baptizing" in line 1 may be unknown. "Levites", "ye" small. Line 8 "taking the world's sin" is unclear English: taking it where? "away" is missing.
- Line 7 names both John and Jesus. Clearer than A.

**Role 2 - Bible reader: 8/10**
- Accurate. Line 6 quotes Isaiah 40:3 verbatim, so line 8 "That Lord is Jesus" closes a real loop (Matthew 3:3 makes the same link). Best Christ landing of the three.
- Small over-reach: line 7 "cries" - John 1:29 says "saith". Echoes "crying" nicely, but it is not the text. Minor.
- Line 8 "taking the world's sin" drifts from "taketh away"; it can read as "bearing" (also true) but it is not the verse's word.
- Fresh: the Isaiah "LORD" = Jesus link. Missing B's "before any guess" / "no name".

**Role 3 - curiosity gap: 7/10**
- Line 1 opens a question; payoff is strongest (the voice was getting the road ready for the LORD, and the LORD walks in). "baptizing" makes the hook slightly harder than A's "desert man".

## 5 guardrail questions

**A**
1. For: curious people with no Bible background.
2. Take-away: John was only a voice; the Lamb he pointed to is Jesus.
3. Global: mostly yes; line 7 speaker unclear, "Lamb" unexplained.
4. Accurate: yes; line 8 leap is not argued on screen.
5. One change: line 8 - tie it to Isaiah: "The Lamb was Jesus: the Lord that voice was making ready for."

**B**
1. For: people who already know John the Baptist a little.
2. Take-away: John refused every big name; he pointed to Jesus, who takes away sin.
3. Global: weaker - line 1 assumes prior knowledge; "Behold" carries the CTA.
4. Accurate: yes, and freshest.
5. One change: line 1 - "Who is this wild man by the river?"

**C**
1. For: newcomers and Bible readers both.
2. Take-away: the voice got the road ready for God; the one who walked in was Jesus.
3. Global: yes, apart from "baptizing" and the missing "away".
4. Accurate: yes; minor "cries" vs "saith".
5. One change: line 8 - put "away" back.

## PICK: C

Strongest landing on Jesus (Isaiah's LORD = Jesus, said out loud), clear speaker in line 7, simple hook. Graft B's two fresh lines.

## TOP 3 FIXES for C

1. **Line 8:** "That Lord is Jesus. God himself, come to take away the world's sin."
2. **Line 3 (graft from B):** "Before anyone guesses: "I am not the Christ." Not Elijah. Not the Prophet."
3. **Line 5 (graft from B):** "No name. Only this: "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness.""
   - Also (small): line 7 "cries" -> "says" to match John 1:29.

## FAIL lines

- **B line 1** "Who is John really?" - FAIL for a global newcomer: the hook needs prior knowledge of John.
- No doctrinal FAIL in any version.
- Near-fail: **A line 7** - no speaker, no Jesus named; a newcomer does not know who says it or who the Lamb is until line 8.
