# Blind viewer test (45-second Bible short, 3 versions)

Below are 3 versions (A, B, C) of the narration for a 45-second vertical video. Each line is spoken over one hand-drawn ink-and-watercolor storyboard page (about 5-6 seconds each). Lines in quote marks are spoken by a character voice. The film ends with 3 seconds of silence.

Answer for EACH version, in three roles:

**Role 1 - total newcomer** (no Bible knowledge, English is a second language). After one listen: who is this about, what happened, why does it matter? Any word or line you would not understand? Score clarity 0-10.

**Role 2 - Bible reader.** Is it accurate to the KJV text below? Any over-reach, wrong fact, or a question put in someone's mouth the text does not record? Does it land soundly on Jesus? Did you learn or notice anything fresh? Score 0-10.

**Role 3 - the curiosity gap.** Does line 1 open a real question? Does the film pay it off? Score 0-10.

Then the 5 guardrail questions, for each version: (1) who is this for, (2) what is the main take-away, (3) does it work for a global audience, (4) is it biblically accurate, (5) is this the best version possible - what one change would make it better?

End with: your PICK (A, B or C), the TOP 3 FIXES for your pick (each concrete: line number + new wording), and any line that is a FAIL in any version.

Be blunt. You have not seen any other review.

## The 3 versions

# A

1. Who is this desert man?
2. Priests from Jerusalem find John at the river: "Who art thou?"
3. He says, "I am not the Christ." Not Elijah. Not the Prophet.
4. They cannot go home with nothing. What does he call himself?
5. John answers, "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness."
6. Long ago Isaiah wrote of a voice making the way ready for God.
7. Next day: "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."
8. The Lamb was Jesus. God himself had come near.

# B

1. Who is John really?
2. Priests and Levites came from Jerusalem to ask, "Who art thou?"
3. Before any guess: "I am not the Christ." Not Elijah. Not the Prophet.
4. They pressed harder: then who are you? Tell us about yourself.
5. No name. "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness."
6. Isaiah wrote of that voice long before: a road made ready for God himself.
7. Next day John sees Jesus coming: "Behold the Lamb of God"
8. This Jesus takes away the world's sin. Behold him.

# C

1. Who is this man baptizing?
2. Priests and Levites left Jerusalem to ask him, "Who art thou?"
3. He says, "I am not the Christ." Not Elijah. Not the Prophet.
4. They cannot go home with nothing. What does he call himself?
5. John answers, "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness."
6. Long ago Isaiah wrote of a voice: "Prepare ye the way of the LORD."
7. Next day John sees Jesus coming and cries, "Behold the Lamb of God"
8. That Lord is Jesus, God himself, taking the world's sin.
