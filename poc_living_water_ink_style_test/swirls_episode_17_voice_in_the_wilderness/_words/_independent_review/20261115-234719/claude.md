# Review: Swirls Short #17 "Only a Voice", candidate G

**Verdict: REVISE.** The hook scores 5 and the landing scores 6. Both are under 8.

## What holds up
- **KJV:** all 5 quoted runs are exact. I checked John 1:19, 1:20, 1:23, 1:29 and Isaiah 40:3.
- **Doctrine:** sound. Reading Isaiah 40's "God comes" / "the LORD" as fulfilled in Jesus is standard. Mark 1:2-3 and John 1:23 support it, and Isaiah 40:9-10 ("Behold your God… the Lord GOD will come") backs "God comes."
- **Thread:** one spine, John points away from himself to Jesus. It never swaps threads.
- **Conviction:** no fear, no gain/loss, no works framing.

## Problems

**1. Hook: 5/10.** *"Is John the promised one?"*
- A stranger doesn't know who John is, or who promised what. It's a quiz question about two unknowns.
- Nothing visual, no stake, no irony in the first 2 seconds.
- The real irony (crowds think he's the Messiah, he calls himself "only a voice") isn't in the hook. It doesn't appear anywhere.

**2. The title word "only" is never spoken.**
- The curiosity-gap note says "only a voice (5)", but line 5 is just the KJV quote.
- The idea the whole piece hangs on is in the title and the notes, not in the audio. A dub would lose it entirely (your "dubbing means spoken" rule).

**3. Landing: 6/10.** *"The voice is John. The Lord is Jesus. Behold him."*
- **It breaks the locked global-audience rule.** "Behold" is an archaic word carrying the whole CTA. Your own "still weak" list admits it.
- **The fallback "Look to him" is a stock closer** (cap 5). So the plan B is worse.
- **It's two labels and an order.** It drops the gospel payload, the Lamb who takes away sin, at the exact moment it matters. The viewer is told who Jesus is, not what he does for them.
- **Mostly reusable elsewhere.** "The Lord is Jesus. Behold him." could end many shorts. Only "The voice is John" ties it to this piece.

**4. Line 4 is unclear by ear.** *"They guess two great names. No, twice."*
- "No, twice" is a fragment. It could be heard as "no, they guessed twice."
- "Two great names" hides the names, so a newcomer gets nothing and a Bible reader gets less. A clever gap that never pays off is a clarity fail.

**5. Line 6 "its cry" is ambiguous.**
- Whose cry? "Its" points back to "the voice" across a speaker change.
- An ESL listener will likely miss it.

**6. Line 3 word order is hard on the ear.** *"Not the promised one, he says first:"*
- Inverted syntax, and "first" is confusing.
- Plainer: "He says no: 'I am not the Christ.'"

**7. The time budget is fiction.**
- 41.6 s assumes a flat 2.2 words per second.
- But the cues ask for *slow, ringing* on Isaiah and *slow on "sin of the world"* in the longest line. Those slow reads alone will push it past 42 s.
- 88 words at the cap, with slow-read cues, will overrun.

**8. Small points.**
- "taketh" is fine inside the full speech.
- "Priests from Jerusalem" is acceptable shorthand for "priests and Levites."
- Luke 3:15 for the hook is accurate, but it's outside the pack's verse table, so the Bible seat should confirm it.

## Fix directions (not a rewrite)
- **Hook:** open on the irony, spoken. For example: *"Crowds thought he was the Messiah. He said he was only a voice."* That puts "only a voice" in the first 3 seconds and in the audio.
- **Landing:** build it from "voice" and the Lamb's own verb ("taketh away"), in plain words. Something in the shape of: *"John was only the voice. Jesus is the one it was calling for: the Lamb who takes your sin away."* Before you lock any wording, check that the gloss on "taketh away" stays honest to the text.
- **Free up words:** cut line 4 to something plain like *"They try other guesses. He says no."* Drop "first" from line 3. Spend the savings on slower reads or on "only".

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Rebuild the hook around the spoken irony ("they thought he was the Messiah; he said he was only a voice"), and put the word "only" in the audio, not just the title.
2. Replace the "Behold him" landing: archaic load-bearing CTA word, with a stock-closer fallback and no Lamb/sin payload. Land on "voice" plus "takes away sin" in plain English.
3. Fix the ear-clarity lines (line 4 "No, twice", line 6 "its cry", line 3 inverted gloss), and re-time with the slow-read cues counted. 88 words will overrun 42 s.
