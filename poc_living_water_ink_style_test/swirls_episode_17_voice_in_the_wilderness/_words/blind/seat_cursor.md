## Curiosity gap

**Gap:** Jerusalem sends one question — who is John? — and he keeps saying who he is *not* instead of naming himself.  
**Payoff:** **Line 5** — he finally answers: not a title, but *a voice*; the story then opens toward the Lamb and the way on **7–8**.
