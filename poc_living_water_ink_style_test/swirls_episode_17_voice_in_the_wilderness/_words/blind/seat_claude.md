# Seat: claude (blind)

## The 8 lines

1. **Who are you, wild man?** (5)
2. Priests came from Jerusalem to the river with one question: *[questioner, firm, suspicious]* "Who art thou?" (12)
3. Before any guess: *[john, quick, plain]* "I am not the Christ." Not Elijah. Not the Prophet. (13)
4. They pressed harder: then who are you? Tell us about yourself. (11)
5. **The turn.** No name. *[john, quiet, sure]* "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness." (12)
6. Taken from Isaiah: a voice clearing the road for God himself. (11)
7. Next day Jesus comes. John points: *[john, glad, hushed]* "Behold the Lamb of God." (11)
8. **Landing.** He takes away the sin of the world. Behold Jesus. (10)

*(then 3 seconds of silence)*

## Counts

- Total words: **85**

Line 8 says "takes away the sin of the world" in the narrator's plain words (allowed option; the KJV sentence is not split across lines). "Not Elijah. Not the Prophet." on line 3 is the narrator reporting, not a quote. No question is put in the priests' mouths that the KJV does not record. Isaiah is said to be the *source* of the voice ("taken from Isaiah"), never "the same words".

## The three truths

1. John points away from himself to Jesus: **lines 3, 5, 7** (not the Christ; only a voice; "John points").
2. Jesus is the Lord whose way is made ready, God himself coming: **line 6** ("clearing the road for God himself"), then line 7 shows who walks down that road.
3. Jesus is the Lamb who takes away the sin of the world: **lines 7-8**.

## Curiosity gap

Line 1 asks who this wild man is; it pays off on line 8, where the answer turns out to be "nobody but a voice", and the one worth looking at is Jesus.
