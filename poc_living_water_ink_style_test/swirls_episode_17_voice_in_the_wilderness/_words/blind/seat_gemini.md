### The Three Truths

1. **John points away from himself to Jesus:** Line 7 (*"The next day he saw Jesus..."*)
2. **Jesus is the Lord whose way is being made ready — God himself coming:** Line 6 (*"...preparing the way for God himself to come."*)
3. **Jesus is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world:** Lines 7 and 8 (*"Behold the Lamb of God" / "He takes away the sin of the world."*)

### Curiosity Gap

The curiosity gap asks who this mysterious baptizer is and why he denies titles no one asked about, paying off on line 5 when he reveals he is merely a voice preparing the way.
