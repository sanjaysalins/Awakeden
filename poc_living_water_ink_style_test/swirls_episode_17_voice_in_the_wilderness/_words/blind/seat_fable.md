# Seat: fable — blind draft

## The 8 lines

| # | Line | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who is this desert man? | 5 |
| 2 | Priests from Jerusalem find John at the river: "Who art thou?" | 11 |
| 3 | He says, "I am not the Christ." Not Elijah. Not the Prophet. | 12 |
| 4 | They cannot go home with nothing. What does he call himself? | 11 |
| 5 | John answers, "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness." | 12 |
| 6 | Long ago Isaiah wrote of that voice, making the way ready for God. | 13 |
| 7 | Next day, Jesus walks toward him. John points: "Behold the Lamb of God" | 13 |
| 8 | God came to take away the world's sin. Behold him. | 10 |

**Total: 87 words.** (5 + 11 + 12 + 11 + 12 + 13 + 13 + 10)

**Voiced character quotes: 4** (at most 1 on any line).

**Time check:** 87 / 2.2 = 39.5 s, plus 0.4 x 4 = 1.6 s. **Sum = 41.1 s** (inside 38-42). Then 3 seconds of silence.

## KJV quotes (each one unbroken run)

| Line | Quote | Ref | Speaker | Emotion cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Who art thou? | John 1:19 | questioner | sharp, official |
| 3 | I am not the Christ. | John 1:20 | john | firm, quick, plain |
| 5 | I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness | John 1:23 | john | quiet, steady, sure |
| 7 | Behold the Lamb of God | John 1:29 | john | glad, full of wonder |

- No Isaiah reading is voiced. Line 6 says in plain words that Isaiah wrote *of* that voice. It never says "the same words".
- The questioners ask only "Who art thou?". Line 4 is the narrator's own plain question (John 1:22 in plain words). No "Are you the Christ?".
- "Not Elijah. Not the Prophet." is the narrator reporting (John 1:21).
- "takes away the world's sin" on line 8 is the narrator's plain words for John 1:29. The KJV sentence is not cut across lines 7 and 8.

## Where the three truths are said

1. **John points away from himself to Jesus** — line 3 (who he is not), line 5 (only a voice), line 7 ("John points").
2. **Jesus is the Lord whose way is made ready, God himself coming** — line 6 ("the way ready for God") and line 8 ("God came"). "God" said plainly.
3. **Jesus is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world** — line 7 (Lamb of God) and line 8 (takes away the world's sin).

## Curiosity gap

Who is this man who will only say who he is *not*? It pays off on line 5 (he is just a voice) and closes on lines 7-8 (the voice was for Jesus).
