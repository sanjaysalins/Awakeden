# Seat: Fable (blind, pack only)

## Version D

**Role 1 - newcomer. Clarity 6.5/10**
- Who: a desert man called John. Priests ask who he is.
- What: he says he is not the promised one, only "the voice". Then Jesus comes.
- Why: Jesus is God himself.
- Trouble spots:
  - L7 has no speaker. "Jesus comes. 'Behold the Lamb...'" A newcomer can think Jesus says it.
  - "Lamb of God" and "taketh away the sin" are never put in plain words. The reason it matters sits only in old English.
  - "art thou", "crying" (sounds like weeping), "wilderness" are hard but short.
  - L4 "What does he call himself?" is clear and helps.

**Role 2 - Bible reader. 7/10**
- L2-L6 are accurate. L5 and L6 are verbatim (John 1:23, Isaiah 40:3).
- L4 is fair: two names, two noes (John 1:21). The question is the narrator's paraphrase of "What sayest thou of thyself?", so it is not put in a mouth.
- L7 drops "the next day" (John 1:29). It reads as if Jesus walks up while the priests are still there. That is compression past the text.
- L8 lands soundly: Isaiah's LORD = Jesus. Good and fresh.
- The landing is identity only. The sin-bearing is left inside the quote.

**Role 3 - curiosity gap. 6/10**
- L1 opens a real but mild question. "Desert man" is flat.
- L4 re-opens it well.
- L8 "The voice was John" pays it off, then pivots to Jesus. Clean, but the twist (he points away from himself) is not said.

**Guardrails**
1. For: newcomers and casual scrollers; also Bible readers who like the Isaiah link.
2. Take-away: John was only the voice; the Lord he announced is Jesus, God himself.
3. Global: partly. L7 is the weak point (no speaker, all old English).
4. Accurate: yes, except the L7 time compression.
5. Best version: no. One change: L7 -> "Next day John sees Jesus and says, 'Behold the Lamb of God.'" and move the sin line into plain words in L8.

## Version E

**Role 1 - newcomer. Clarity 7.5/10**
- Who: a wild man by a river; his name comes at L5 (John).
- What: priests ask who he is; he says not the promised one, only a voice; next day he sees Jesus and points to him.
- Why: Jesus is God, and he takes away the world's sin. This is said in plain words. Big win.
- Trouble spots:
  - "wild man" can mean crazy or savage to an ESL ear.
  - John is not named until L5. "John answers" arrives with no set-up.
  - L8 "That Lord" points back to L6, but L7 just said "Lamb". By ear, Lord/Lamb can blur.
  - "God, taking away" is a colon on paper. Spoken, it runs together.
  - "Lamb of God" still not explained.

**Role 2 - Bible reader. 8.5/10**
- L4 "They must have an answer" matches John 1:22. Nice touch, accurate.
- L6 "Long ago" is true and helps.
- L7 keeps "Next day" and names the speaker. Accurate. The cut quote is fine.
- L8 is sound: Isaiah's LORD is Jesus, and he takes away the sin of the world (John 1:29 in plain words).
- No over-reach. No invented question.
- Small loss: no line says John was only the voice, so John's humility is felt, not stated.

**Role 3 - curiosity gap. 7/10**
- L1 is a stronger hook than D. "Wild" has more pull, with the risk above.
- L4 "They must have an answer" adds pressure. Good.
- Payoff is good on Jesus, weaker on the L1 question. It never closes "so who is the wild man?" in one plain line.

**Guardrails**
1. For: newcomers worldwide; Bible readers get the Isaiah-to-Jesus link.
2. Take-away: the man in the desert was pointing at Jesus, who is God and takes away sin.
3. Global: mostly yes. Best plain-word landing of the two.
4. Accurate: yes.
5. Best version: close. One change: make L8 close the loop on John AND stop the Lord/Lamb blur.

## PICK: E

Why: accurate L7, plain-word landing, more pressure in L4. D's only edge is "The voice was John" - graft it in.

## TOP 3 FIXES (for E)

1. **L8** -> "John was only the voice. The Lord is Jesus: God himself, come to take away the world's sin."
   (Closes L1. Ends the Lord/Lamb blur. Keeps the plain landing.)
2. **L2** -> "Priests from Jerusalem find John at the river and ask, 'Who art thou?'"
   (Names John early, so L5 is not a surprise name.)
3. **L1** -> "Who is this man in the wild?"
   (Keeps the pull of "wild", drops the "crazy man" reading. Also sets up "wilderness" in L5.)

## FAIL lines

- **D L7 - FAIL.** No speaker (a newcomer hears Jesus saying it) and it drops "the next day", so it reads as the same scene with the priests. Text says next day (John 1:29).
- No FAIL in E. Warnings only: E L1 "wild man", E L8 "That Lord" by ear.
