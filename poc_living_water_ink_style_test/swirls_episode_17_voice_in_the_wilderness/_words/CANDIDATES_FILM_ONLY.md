# A

1. Who is this desert man?
2. Priests from Jerusalem find John at the river: "Who art thou?"
3. He says, "I am not the Christ." Not Elijah. Not the Prophet.
4. They cannot go home with nothing. What does he call himself?
5. John answers, "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness."
6. Long ago Isaiah wrote of a voice making the way ready for God.
7. Next day: "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."
8. The Lamb was Jesus. God himself had come near.

# B

1. Who is John really?
2. Priests and Levites came from Jerusalem to ask, "Who art thou?"
3. Before any guess: "I am not the Christ." Not Elijah. Not the Prophet.
4. They pressed harder: then who are you? Tell us about yourself.
5. No name. "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness."
6. Isaiah wrote of that voice long before: a road made ready for God himself.
7. Next day John sees Jesus coming: "Behold the Lamb of God"
8. This Jesus takes away the world's sin. Behold him.

# C

1. Who is this man baptizing?
2. Priests and Levites left Jerusalem to ask him, "Who art thou?"
3. He says, "I am not the Christ." Not Elijah. Not the Prophet.
4. They cannot go home with nothing. What does he call himself?
5. John answers, "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness."
6. Long ago Isaiah wrote of a voice: "Prepare ye the way of the LORD."
7. Next day John sees Jesus coming and cries, "Behold the Lamb of God"
8. That Lord is Jesus, God himself, taking the world's sin.
