# D

1. Who is this desert man?
2. Priests from Jerusalem find John at the river: "Who art thou?"
3. He says, "I am not the Christ", the one God promised.
4. They guess two names. No. No. What does he call himself?
5. John answers, "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness."
6. Isaiah wrote of that voice: "Prepare ye the way of the LORD."
7. Jesus comes. "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world."
8. The voice was John. The Lord is Jesus, God himself.

# E

1. Who is this wild man?
2. Priests from Jerusalem reach the river and ask, "Who art thou?"
3. He says, "I am not the Christ", the one God promised.
4. They try two names. No. No. They must have an answer.
5. John answers, "I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness."
6. Long ago Isaiah wrote of that voice: "Prepare ye the way of the LORD."
7. Next day John sees Jesus coming and says, "Behold the Lamb of God."
8. That Lord is Jesus: God, taking away the world's sin.
