# Red team seat: Opus (blind)

## 1. STILLS: what is weak
- **20-34.5 s (F03): the prompt shows up as text on the page.** Under the panels: "single washed foot, dry, resting no floor, close-up, no face" / "soft row of ordinary faces, unindividuated, no one singled out." / "gesturing as if reciting close-up, no other feature". That is prompt wording drawn onto the art, plus a typo ("no floor"). It sits on screen for 15 s. This is the worst defect in the film.
- **20-34.5 s (F03), panel 2 "not all":** the "no one singled out" row is led by a Jesus-like face in front. It reads as "Jesus + 3", not "ordinary disciples".
- **20-34.5 s (F03), main panel:** Jesus stands in a full outer vest while a disciple kneels in front of him. That flips the story (Jesus kneels, he took his garments off). The towel lies on the floor and is not girded on.
- **4.0 s (F01):** the page is on screen for about 1.5 s. Nobody can read it. Panel 2 "Judas" is just a cup, with no meaning. The label "Jesus knew" shows at 4 s, but the voice says it at 11.9 s.
- **6-12 s:** a full-frame, cleanly rendered Jesus portrait. It is smoother and more photo-like than the ink storyboard style, so it reads as a different series. The eyes close at 8 s, which reads as sleepy or calm.
- **36-46.6 s (F04):** a bed and pillows sit behind them, so the room reads as a bedroom, not a supper table. Everyone stands. The panel 1 bread has a bite out of it.
- **Table changes between frames:** a low table and cushions at 4 s and 20 s, a high Western bench table at 14 s, a white tablecloth table at 52 s. The world is not consistent.
- **52-63.5 s (F06):** Jesus kneels alone at an empty basin with no feet and no Judas. A blue smudge or wisp floats by his head (52-58 s), and it looks like an artifact. The label "knowing he wouldn't" is a half-sentence: wouldn't what?
- **63.5-78 s (end card):** the blue and gold rings float like an empty hoop or halo with no meaning. The card cites "1 JOHN 1:9", which the voice never mentions.

## 2. ANIMATION: what is weak
- **20-62 s: 42 s of near-still pictures (0.03-0.19, freeze flags at 31.3, 33.3, 44.5-46.6 and 60.5-63.2 s).** More than half the film is a slideshow, on a channel where 1.0-3.0 is normal.
- **Holds are too long for Shorts:** F03 lasts 14.9 s, F04 12.1 s, F06 13 s, and the end card 14.5 s. Only 8 pictures in 78 s.
- **0-4 s** has the most motion (1.8-3.9), then motion falls off a cliff. The film starts strong and then dies.
- **71.4-78 s:** 6.6 s of dead tail after the voice ends, on a static card (0.19-0.27). That is far past a 3 s landing hold.
- **47-49 s:** the only mid-film motion (1.0-1.4) is spent on a figure walking into the dark. That is right, but it is late (see 3).

## 3. PICTURE vs WORDS
- **5.3-10.8 s:** "the devil had already put into the heart of Judas" plays over a calm Jesus face whose eyes close. There is no Judas and no threat. Picture and words disagree.
- **27.4-31.9 s:** "lifted up his heel against me" has no heel and no bread on screen. We still see F03 "not all".
- **44.1-47.1 s:** "went immediately out: and it was night" plays over the F04 handshake. The door picture comes at 46.6 s, after the words are almost done.
- **48.8-50.5 s:** "Jesus did not wash those feet" plays over Judas in the dark doorway.
- **61.6-65.9 s:** the cut to the end card at 63.5 s lands in the middle of "He knows your heart".
- **63.5 s:** "BRING HIM YOUR WORST" is on screen about 6 s before it is spoken (about 70 s), so the text spoils the landing line.
- **67.1 s:** "He has not put the towel down" plays over a towel lying on the floor beside the basin. The picture says the opposite of the words.

## 4. ACCURACY
- **20-34 s:** Jesus standing in full outer clothes while a disciple kneels to him goes against v4-5 (he laid aside his garments, girded himself with the towel, and washed).
- **36-46 s:** the sop is handed over standing, in a bedroom. v26 is at the supper table, dipped. The bed is not supported.
- **14 s / 52 s:** a high bench table and a white tablecloth are Western and do not match the low-table frames.
- **52 s:** "knowing he wouldn't" has no basis in the KJV text, and it is unclear.
- **64-78 s:** 1 John 1:9 is outside John 13 and is never cited in the voice. It is fine as doctrine but not explained on screen.
- **4 s:** F01 already isolates a standing figure apart from the table before the washing. That is a minor over-reach.

## 5. VERDICT
**FIX. Score 4.5 / 10.** The opening hook (0-4 s) and the door shot (48-50 s) work. But the prompt text on F03 alone blocks shipping, and 42 s of near-frozen pictures will lose viewers.
