- **33.0 s:** "He handed Judas the sop" starts 1.5 s before the sop picture (34.5 s).

- **61.6 s:** "He knows your heart" starts on F06, card cuts in mid-sentence (63.5 s).

## 4. ACCURACY
- **14-18 s:** Jesus washes in a full robe with a sash; towel on the floor. KJV v4-5: laid aside garments, girded with the towel, wiped with it.
- **36-46 s:** sop passed standing mid-room like a handshake, no dish, no table. KJV v26: He dipped it, at supper.
- **36 s:** label "buy food". KJV v29: "those things that we have need of against the feast".
- **36 s:** quote label drops the comma: "That thou doest, do quickly."
- **64-78 s:** "1 JOHN 1:9" is never spoken or explained.
- **20 s:** a man kneels before a standing Jesus. No such moment in the text given.

## 5. VERDICT
**FIX. Score 4.5 / 10.** Strong open (0-4 s) and a good door shot. But leaked prompt text, a towel that contradicts the last line, and about 45 s of near-still picture.
