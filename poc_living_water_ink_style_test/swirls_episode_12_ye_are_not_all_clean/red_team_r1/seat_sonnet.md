## 1. STILLS: what is weak
- **20-34.5 s (F03): prompt text leaked into the art.** The three panel captions under the sketches read "single washed foot, dry, resting no floor, close-up, no face", "soft row of ordinary faces, unindividuated, no one singled out", "gesturing as if reciting, close-up, no other feature". That is generation-brief language, not handwritten notes. It sits on screen for 15 s. It looks like a bug, a global viewer will read it as nonsense, and it breaks the "found storyboard page" illusion.
- **20-34.5 s: who is Judas?** One man kneels in a brown robe in front of a standing Jesus. A viewer cannot tell whether this is Judas, Peter or anyone else. The line "not all" has no visible "not all", only a gesture and a row of anonymous faces.
- **0-3.8 s vs 36-46 s: Judas looks different and costumes drift.** Judas has a purse on the cover and at 36 s, but at 20 s the kneeling man has no readable purse. At 36 s Jesus wears a yellow sash and Judas a grey tunic with an ochre mantle, a different palette from F03. The disciples in red and green are saturated and flat compared with the earlier pages. Jesus changes between a plain white robe (14 s) and a tan vest (20 s, 52 s).
- **36-46.6 s (F04): a bed in the supper room.** A made bed and a bedroll stand behind the group. It reads as a bedroom, not a supper table, and it does not match the low tables in F01-F03.
- **52-63.5 s (F06): the large picture does not show what the words say.** Jesus kneels alone over a basin, with no feet and no Judas. At 56.9 s the voice says "what His love does", and the picture has only a sad man and steam.
- **3.8-5 s (F01 page): too brief to read.** The labels ("the heart", "Judas", "Jesus knew") are on screen for about 1.5 s before the picture zooms into a face. They are never legible. The F01 scene itself does not show Judas leaving or the devil.
- **64-78 s (end card): a floating blue and gold ring hovers over the wall.** It reads as a halo or a loose hoop and has no meaning on its own. The towel lies on the floor next to the basin.
- Two pictures, F05 and the end card, are a different format from the 3-panel pages. That is fine as variety, but the 6-7 sketch panels per page are mostly illegible at phone size.

## 3. PICTURE vs WORDS
- **5.3-10.8 s:** the voice says the devil put it into Judas's heart. The picture is a close-up of Jesus's face (eyes shut at 8 s). Nothing shows Judas, a heart or the devil. The F01 labels flash and vanish before the sentence.
- **27.4-31.9 s, "lifted up his heel":** the picture is still the "not all" standing scene. The "heel" idea appears only as a "single washed foot" panel labelled "clean", which says the opposite. There is no heel, no bread, no table meal, even though the quote is "He that eateth bread with me".
- **33.0-34.5 s, "handed Judas the sop":** the voice is already there, but the picture is still F03 until 34.5 s. The bread handoff is late by 1.5 s.
- **44.1-47.1 s, "went immediately out: and it was night":** the cut to the door comes at 46.6 s, so the picture is late by about 2.5 s for "went immediately out". The door image arrives as the sentence ends.
- **48.8-50.5 s:** the voice says "Jesus did not wash those feet", while the picture is Judas walking away into the dark (F05, motion 1.0-1.4). Reading the picture as "Jesus did not wash" is wrong. F06 does not arrive until 50.5 s.
- **67.1-71.4 s, "He has not put the towel down":** the end card shows the towel lying on the floor beside the basin. The picture contradicts the words. This is the most damaging single mismatch.
- **0-3.8 s:** the picture (Judas leaving) is well matched. It is the film's strongest hook.

## 4. ACCURACY
- **F03 caption "not all" and "single washed foot ... clean":** the verse is "ye are clean, but not all" and the picture's panel text only repeats that. The standing/kneeling blocking is not in John 13. Jesus does not stand and speak while someone kneels in front of him. The washing, the "ye are clean" line (13:10) and the sop (13:26) come at different points in the meal.
- **Rooms and people:** the bed in the F04 room is not in John 13. Jesus has a vest or sash and the disciples are in bright red and green. These are costume inventions, not text errors, but the bed risks a different reading (a bedroom).
- **F05 note "alone":** the KJV says he "went immediately out: and it was night". It does not say he was alone. Mild.
- **F06 caption "knowing he wouldn't":** it is vague. "Wouldn't" does what? Repent? It is an interpretation. The KJV text is 13:11 "For he knew who should betray him", which supports the knowing but not the label.
- **End card "1 JOHN 1:9":** the verse is "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness". The card says "Bring Him your worst" and attributes it to that verse. It is a paraphrase of the idea of confession, not a quotation, and it is not mentioned in the voice. The reference is outside the John 13 text. It is doctrinally fine but misattributed as a title.
- **Words:** "Some at the table thought he had gone to buy food" is supported by 13:29 (buy those things for the feast, or give to the poor). "Judas had the bag" is correct on the cover and at 36 s.
- **0 s cover "JOHN 13":** the picture of Judas walking out lit by a doorway and the sentence "Judas walked out ... on feet Jesus had washed" are not in the KJV in those words, but they are fair summary.

## 5. VERDICT
**FIX.** Score **5.5 / 10**. The story is clear, the hook (0-3.8 s) is strong, and the end card has a clean line. But a prompt-text leak, about 45 s of near-frozen pictures, a towel that is plainly "put down" while the voice says it is not, and several cuts 1.5-2.5 s off the voice stop it from shipping as is.

3. **Re-time the cuts to the voice (free re-edit).** Move the cut to the sop picture to about 33.0 s, the door picture to about 44.1 s, and F06 to about 48.8 s. This costs nothing and fixes the late or early pictures in section 3. It also lets the "heel/bread" quote land on a bread or table shot at 27.4 s.
4. **Give F03 and F06 real motion (new clips).** About 28 s of the film moves at 0.03-0.18. Re-animate with a clear completing gesture (Jesus's hand opening, the sop passing, Judas turning away, water moving). Free partial help: a gentle push-in or pan on the existing stills. Cut F03 into two pictures (the "clean" foot and then the "heel/bread" shot) so the picture changes every 6-8 s.
