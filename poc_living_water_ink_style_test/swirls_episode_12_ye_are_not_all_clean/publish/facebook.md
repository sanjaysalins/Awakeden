## TITLE
Jesus Knew Who Would Betray Him. He Picked Up a Towel.

## DESCRIPTION
John 13: on the night before the cross, Jesus sat at supper with His closest friends. He knew Judas would betray Him. He knew Peter would say three times that he did not know Him.

He got up, tied a towel around Himself, poured water into a bowl, and washed their feet. He knew about Judas the whole time: "For he knew who should betray him; therefore said he, Ye are not all clean."

John says: "having loved his own which were in the world, he loved them unto the end." The next day He died on the cross, for them.

Judas went out: "and it was night." The one who knew the worst about them was not the one who left. He was the one holding the towel. He knows you too, and you do not have to hide from Him.

Read it: John 13:1-30, John 13:38.
