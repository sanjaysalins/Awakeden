# Review: John 13 Short, upload kit (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram)

## What holds up

- **KJV quotes are exact.** I checked every one against the text:
  - 13:11 "For he knew who should betray him; therefore said he, Ye are not all clean."
  - 13:1 "having loved his own which were in the world, he loved them unto the end."
  - 13:30 "and it was night."
  - Starting "He loved them unto the end" with a capital H is fine as a pull quote.
- **The doctrine is sound.** The focus is on grace. There is no fear or works framing. "He died on the cross, for them" stays inside the text.
- **"Did Jesus wash Judas's feet" is a fair tag.** John 13:5, 13:10-11 ("ye are clean, but not all") and 13:30 (Judas only leaves after the washing) all support it. The copy hints at it but never claims more than the text says.
- **Hashtag counts are sensible.** YouTube 5, TikTok 5, Facebook 3, Instagram 5.
- **The YouTube tags are real search phrases.** Nothing is stuffed in.

## Problems

1. **No AI disclosure anywhere (house packaging rule).** The rule needs "honest AI disclosure once" in each pack. None of the four files has it. The film is stylised, so YouTube's own label may not be required, but the house rule still is. Add one plain line to the YouTube and Facebook descriptions, and the TikTok and Instagram captions.

2. **"Read it: John 13:1-38" claims more than the film covers.** The rule says the range must match what is really covered. The film uses 13:1-30 plus 13:38 (Peter's denial). It never touches 13:31-37, which includes the "new commandment" in 34-35. Someone who searches "john 13 explained" will expect that part. Use "John 13:1-30, 38", or "John 13 (see verse 38)".

3. **"Peter would say three times... that he did not know Him."** This is a fair paraphrase of 13:38 ("denied me thrice"). But the film speaks the words "I do not know Him" like a quote, and that wording is not in John 13 (it is close to Luke 22:57, "I know him not"). The copy is fine because it uses no quote marks. Keep it that way, and never put quote marks or "KJV" on that line.

4. **The TikTok first line is the weakest of the four.** "Jesus knew Judas would betray Him." is a known fact, not a hook. The film's real hook is the personal question, which Instagram uses well. Open TikTok with something like: "If they really knew you, would they stay?"

5. **YouTube title: the search words come too late.** "He Knew Who Would Leave. He Washed Their Feet | Jesus Bible Story" is honest, since all the disciples fled (Mark 14:50). But "| Jesus Bible Story" is a stock ending, and the searched phrase ("Jesus washing feet") is not in the title. Option: "He Knew Who Would Betray Him. He Washed His Feet Anyway | John 13". That is true to the text, based on 13:5 + 13:11 + 13:30.

6. **The footer says "one panel at a time".** Check that this Short really is a panel or comic style. If it isn't, the brand line promises a look the video doesn't have.

7. **The invitation to Jesus is implied, never stated.** "Jesus knows. All of it." plus the towel line is earned and full of grace. But no platform says what to do with that (for example, "He has not left. Come to Him."). This matches the film, so it is a call for the owner to make, not a lie in the copy. Flag it against the series' CTA-to-Jesus rule.

8. **Small point:** the tags "he loved them unto the end" and "and it was night" will get almost no searches. They're harmless but use up tag space. Swap one for "jesus knew judas would betray him".

None of these is a lie or a doctrine error. The fixes are about the house rules, scripture-range honesty, and the hooks.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add one honest AI-disclosure line to each platform's copy (house packaging rule).
2. Change "Read it: John 13:1-38" to "John 13:1-30, 38" so the range matches what the film covers.
3. Open TikTok with the personal hook ("If they really knew you, would they stay?"), and put "washed his feet" / "betray" in the YouTube title in place of the stock "| Jesus Bible Story" ending.
