NARRATOR: Do you ever think: if people really knew me, they would leave? One night, Jesus sat at supper with His closest friends. And He knew them. He knew one of them, Judas, would betray Him. He knew another, Peter, would say three times before morning: I do not know Him. So what does He do with friends like that? He gets up from the table. He ties a towel around Himself. He pours water into a bowl, and He washes their feet. A servant's work. And Judas is still in the room.

JESUS: "Know ye what I have done to you?"

NARRATOR: The story says it in one line: "he loved them unto the end." And the next day, He would die on a cross, for them. Later that night, Judas walks out. And the story adds four small words: "and it was night." Maybe it is night where you are. Maybe you are sure nobody would stay, if they knew. Jesus knows. All of it. And look at that room again. The one who knew the worst about them was not the one who left. He was the one holding the towel.
