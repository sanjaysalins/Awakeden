## TITLE
Jesus Knew Who Would Betray Him. He Washed Their Feet | Bible Story

## DESCRIPTION
Jesus washing the disciples' feet in the Bible (John 13): He knew Judas would betray Him, and still tied a towel around Himself and washed their feet.

"For he knew who should betray him; therefore said he, Ye are not all clean." In plain words: He knew about Judas, and He washed their feet anyway. Later that night He told Peter that before morning Peter would say three times that he did not know Him (John 13:38).

John says: "having loved his own which were in the world, he loved them unto the end." The next day He died on the cross, for them.

Judas went out into the dark: "and it was night."

Maybe you are sure nobody would stay if they really knew you. Jesus knows. All of it. The one who knew the worst about them was the one holding the towel. He knows you too, and you do not have to hide from Him.

Read it: John 13:1-30, John 13:38.
