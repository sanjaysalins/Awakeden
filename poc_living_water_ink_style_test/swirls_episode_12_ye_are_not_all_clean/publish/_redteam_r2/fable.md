# Red team (Fable seat) — ep12 upload copy, John 13

Checked clean: every quoted line is exact KJV (13:1, 13:11, 13:12, 13:30). "Four small words" is right. No AI wording. No fear or pressure. Range 13:1-30 + 13:38 is honest. Captions match the spoken words word for word.

## Problems

1. **YouTube + Facebook description — wrong gloss of a verse.**
   Phrase: `"...Ye are not all clean." In plain words: He knew exactly who was in the room.`
   Why: "In plain words" presents this as the meaning of 13:11. It is not. The verse means one of them (Judas) was not clean. Also "Ye are not all clean" is archaic, never spoken in the film, and left unexplained on Facebook, where it sits right after "washed their feet" and can read as an accusation at the reader.
   Replace (YouTube): `"For he knew who should betray him; therefore said he, Ye are not all clean." In plain words: He knew about Judas, and He washed their feet anyway.`
   Replace (Facebook): `He got up, tied a towel around Himself, poured water into a bowl, and washed their feet. He knew about Judas the whole time: "For he knew who should betray him; therefore said he, Ye are not all clean."`

2. **Facebook title — no name, no search words.**
   Phrase: `He Knew Who Would Betray Him. He Picked Up a Towel.`
   Why: "He" is never named. Nobody searches this. A newcomer cannot tell it is about Jesus.
   Replace: `Jesus Knew Who Would Betray Him. He Picked Up a Towel.`

3. **YouTube title — misses the main search phrase; "Who ... Their" is muddled.**
   Phrase: `Jesus Knew Who Would Betray Him. He Washed Their Feet | Bible Story`
   Why: people search "Jesus washes the disciples' feet". "Their" has no clear owner and can read as "the betrayers' feet" (Peter denied, he did not betray).
   Replace: `Jesus Knew Judas Would Betray Him. He Washed the Disciples' Feet | John 13`

4. **TikTok — tense shift makes a present-day claim the text does not make.**
   Phrase: `Jesus knows all of it, and He is the one holding the towel.`
   Why: film and John 13 say "was". "Is" turns a story detail into a promise about now.
   Replace: `Jesus knows. All of it. The one who knew the worst about them was the one holding the towel.`

5. **YouTube description — double "if", hard for non-native readers.**
   Phrase: `If you are sure nobody would stay if they really knew you: Jesus knows.`
   Replace: `Maybe you are sure nobody would stay if they really knew you. Jesus knows. All of it.`

6. **Instagram — share line is vague and presumes someone's state.**
   Phrase: `Send this to someone who is sure nobody would stay if they knew.`
   Why: "if they knew" — knew what? Also mild share-bait.
   Replace: `Share this with someone who feels they must hide who they really are.`

7. **YouTube description — idiom.**
   Phrase: `still tied on a towel`
   Replace: `still tied a towel around Himself`

8. **All platforms — reference format + thumbnail mismatch.**
   Phrase: `John 13:1-30, John 13:38.` (thumbnail shows only `John 13:1-30`)
   Why: Peter's denial is named in the film but its verse is not on the thumbnail. Small.
   Replace: `John 13:1-30 and 13:38.` Thumbnail may stay; no rebuild needed.

9. **All platforms — brand line jargon.**
   Phrase: `one panel at a time`
   Why: "panel" means nothing to a newcomer or a non-native reader.
   Replace: `Find Jesus in the whole Bible, one story at a time.`

10. **TikTok + Instagram — quote capital.**
    Phrase: `"He loved them unto the end."`
    Why: KJV has lowercase mid-verse; the film and captions use lowercase. Be consistent.
    Replace: `"he loved them unto the end."`

11. **Captions — two small things.**
    - Cue 17 has no speaker. The voice changes; a deaf viewer cannot tell. Replace: `Jesus: "Know ye what I have done to you?"`
    - Cues 11, 21, 31 start on the exact end of the cue before (24.18, 49.48, 73.84) and cue 11 runs 2.55 s for six words. Likely the start time swallowed a pause, so the line shows early. Check cue 11 against the audio.

12. **YouTube tags — tag asks a question the film does not answer out loud.**
    Phrase: `did jesus wash judas feet`
    Why: the film only says "Judas is still in the room". The text implies yes (13:5, 13:12, 13:30), so it is fair, but the description should say so once. Fix 1's wording covers it. Add tags: `jesus washes the disciples feet kjv, maundy thursday`.
