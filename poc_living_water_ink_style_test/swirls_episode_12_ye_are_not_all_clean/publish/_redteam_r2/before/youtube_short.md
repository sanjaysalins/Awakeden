## TITLE
Jesus Knew Who Would Betray Him. He Washed Their Feet | Bible Story

## DESCRIPTION
Jesus washing the disciples' feet in the Bible (John 13): He knew Judas would betray Him, and still tied on a towel and washed their feet.

"For he knew who should betray him; therefore said he, Ye are not all clean." In plain words: He knew exactly who was in the room. He knew Peter too: before morning, Peter would say three times that he did not know Him.

"Having loved his own which were in the world, he loved them unto the end." The next day He died on the cross, for them.

Judas went out into the dark: "and it was night."

If you are sure nobody would stay if they really knew you: Jesus knows. All of it. The one who knew the worst about them was the one holding the towel.

Read it: John 13:1-30, John 13:38.
