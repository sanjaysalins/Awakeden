# Independent review — claude (OK, 54s)

Adversarial review of the John 13 "Holding the Towel" upload kit. I checked every quoted verse against the KJV text supplied.

## What holds up
- **KJV quotes are word-for-word.** John 13:1, 13:11, 13:12 and 13:30 all match the KJV text given. The capital "H" on "Having" and "He" just starts a sentence, so it is fine.
- **The titles are honest.** YouTube's "Jesus Knew Who Would Betray Him. He Washed Their Feet" and Facebook's "…He Picked Up a Towel." are both true to John 13:11 and 13:4–5. Neither is bait.
- **The tone is grace.** There is no fear, no "gain or loss" framing and no works framing.
- **The paraphrase suits a global audience.** "Ties a towel around Himself" in place of "girded himself" is clear for non-native speakers.

## Findings

1. **The scripture range does not match the film (thumbnail plus all 4 platforms).**
   - The thumbnail says **"John 13:1-30"**.
   - But the film's Peter line ("three times before morning… I do not know Him") comes from **13:38**.
   - The copy patches this with "John 13:1-30, John 13:38". That reads awkwardly and still leaves the thumbnail wrong.
   - The packaging rule says the range must equal what the film really covers. Use **"John 13"** or **"John 13:1-38"** on the thumbnail and in every "Read it:" line.

2. **The timing of Peter's denial is blurred (YouTube description).**
   - The text quotes v11 ("For he knew who should betray him…") and then goes straight on: *"In plain words: He knew exactly who was in the room. He knew Peter too…"*
   - Laid out like that, v11 seems to prove Jesus knew about Peter's denial. It does not: v11 is about Judas only.
   - In John, the Peter prediction comes **after** the foot-washing and after Judas leaves (v36–38).
   - The claim itself can be defended, since John 2:24–25 says Jesus "knew all men". But the copy should not hang it on v11.
   - Fix: put the Peter line next to its own reference, e.g. "Later that night He told Peter… (John 13:38)".

3. **There is no call to Jesus. Only the channel CTA is there.**
   - The only call to action in every file is "Find Jesus in the whole Bible… Subscribe/Follow Awakeden". That points to the channel.
   - The Christ-pointing landing ("Jesus knows. All of it… holding the towel") is assurance only. Nothing invites the viewer to respond.
   - "He died on the cross, **for them**" stops at the disciples. It never says why He died, and never reaches the viewer.
   - One plain, no-pressure line would close this, e.g. *"He knows the worst about you too, and He still came to serve and to save. You can come to Him as you are."* Check it against your earned-landing gate first, because that gate fails stock "come to Jesus" closers.

4. **The "did jesus wash judas feet" tag (YouTube).** The film hints at the answer ("Judas is still in the room") but never says it. The text supports it: v11 says "not all clean", and v2 and v26 place Judas there. Either add half a line to the description ("He washed Judas's feet too") or drop the tag. A tag should not promise an answer the content does not give.

5. **The brand line may not fit the film.** "One panel at a time" only fits if this film is in the panel/comic style. If it is not, it is a templated line on the wrong film. Please check.

6. **Smaller points.**
   - **"The next day He would die."** In Jewish reckoning the supper and the cross fall on the same day. "The next day" is fine in plain speech and a pedant might push back, but "Within hours, He would die…" is just as plain and has no gap.
   - **Instagram share line.** "Send this to someone who is sure nobody would stay" could land as an insult to the person who receives it. A softer option: "Send this to someone who needs to hear it."
   - **Facebook title** never names Jesus ("He Knew…"). In a cold feed, a newcomer may not know who "He" is. Consider "Jesus Knew Who Would Betray Him. He Picked Up a Towel."
   - **Landing hold.** The last caption ends at 1:18.29. On an 81 s file that is about 2.7 s of hold, and the house rule (INV-26) wants at least 3.0 s. Run `check_landing_hold.py` to confirm.
   - **AI disclosure.** The owner says "no AI wording" in public text, but the packaging rule wants one honest disclosure. Make sure YouTube Studio's "altered or synthetic content" box is ticked, so disclosure is handled without wording in the copy.
   - **YouTube title** "| Bible Story" is a generic tail. "(John 13)" would be more specific and helps search.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Change the scripture range to "John 13" (or 13:1-38) on the thumbnail and in every "Read it:" line, so it covers the 13:38 Peter content the film actually uses.
2. Add one plain, grace-toned line that points the viewer to Jesus (why He died, and that it reaches them), not just "Subscribe/Follow Awakeden"; run it through the earned-landing gate.
3. In the YouTube description, move the Peter claim off the v11 quote and cite John 13:38 for it; either answer "did Jesus wash Judas's feet" in the copy or drop that tag.
