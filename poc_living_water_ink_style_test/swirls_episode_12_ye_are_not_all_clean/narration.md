Judas walked out into the night on feet Jesus had washed.

John says the devil had already "put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, Simon's son, to betray him." Jesus knew. He knew as He poured the water. He knew as He dried their feet with the towel.

Then He told them, "ye are clean, but not all." And He quoted the old word for betrayal, from Scripture: "He that eateth bread with me hath lifted up his heel against me."

He handed Judas the sop — a piece of dipped bread. "That thou doest, do quickly." Some at the table thought he had gone to buy food. Judas "went immediately out: and it was night."

Jesus did not wash those feet because Judas earned it. He washed them knowing Judas would betray Him anyway. That is what His love does when it already knows the worst.

He knows your heart, every corner of it, like that. He has not put the towel down — bring Him your worst.
