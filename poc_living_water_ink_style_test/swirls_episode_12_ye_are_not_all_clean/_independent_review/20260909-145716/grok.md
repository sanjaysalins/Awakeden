### Landing — 6
Exact last sentence: **“He has not put the towel down.”**

Not a banned **“Come to Jesus.”** The towel is from this room, so this is not capped at 5.

It does not land in the bones.

- John 13:12: after the washing He **takes His garments and sits down again**. In the scene, the towel **is** put down. The closer fights the next beat of the text it just used.
- The hook already spent the finished wash: Judas left **on washed feet**. Then the CTA pretends the wash is still in His hands. That is a logic trick, not a turn.

Judas had the towel and still walked into night.

### Doctrine — orthodox instinct, wrong split
Jesus knowing the betrayal is in the text: **“For he knew who should betray him”** (John 13:11). Enemy-love is orthodox. This is not clickbait Judas-revisionism.

**“Jesus did not wash those feet hoping Judas would change.”**  
Still invented motive. Milder than the last pass’s **“because / knowing he wouldn't.”** Still a claim John never makes. John gives Jesus’ own reasons: the bath/feet split and **“ye are clean, but not all”** (13:10–11), **“If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with me”** (13:8), **“I have given you an example”** (13:15). The script replaces those with psychology: He washed without hope of a turn.

**“He washed them knowing Judas would betray Him anyway.”**  
The *knowing* is fair. The *anyway* turns foreknowledge into the reason for the wash. That maxim — **“That is what His love does when it already knows the worst”** — is worn Maundy Thursday homiletics, not what this pericope is doing.

Then the close fuses what John keeps apart. Love offered, wet feet, and being **clean** are three different things here. Judas gets the first two. He is the **“not all.”** A listener can hear: He knows the worst and the towel is still in His hand — so I am already in. The verse the script quoted will not let that stand.

**“Then He told the room, ‘ye are clean, but not all.’”**  
False. John 13:10 is **“Jesus saith to him”** — to **Peter**, not a room announcement.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Rebuild the last two sentences off John’s real split (love offered / wet feet / **“not all”** clean). Do not map the listener onto Judas-who-left as if **“He has not put the towel down”** were already salvation — in the scene the towel-work finished and he still went into the night (and 13:12 He sat down again).
