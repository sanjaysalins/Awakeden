### Hook — 7
**“Judas walked out into the night on feet Jesus had washed.”**  
Not a template open. Concrete. One image.

A stranger with zero Bible knowledge does not get a stopper from it. The line never says who Judas is, that this is the supper, or that he is leaving to hand Jesus over. The irony only fires if you already know the irony. That is a church-ear line. It does not clear 8.

---

### Landing — 6
Exact last sentence: **“Whatever is only washed in you, Jesus can still make clean — every whit.”**

Not a banned stock closer. **“every whit”** is this chapter’s phrase. That is why this is not capped at 5.

It still does not land in the bones.

- **“only washed in you”** is muddy on the ear. Washed *in* you? A first hearer cannot parse it.
- Strip **“every whit”** and the sentence could close Naaman, a baptism piece, or any “outer religion” short: **“Jesus can still make clean.”** The KJV sticker is glued to a portable line, not built from the scene.
- There is no invitation. It asserts. The basin image in the sentence before it — **“The basin was still in the room”** — is the real close, and then the script lectures over it.

---

### Doctrine — orthodox destination, false mechanism
The claim *Judas was washed and still not clean* is in the text (John 13:10–11). Not clickbait. Not a hot take.

The landing’s mechanism is not.

John 13:10: **“He that is washed needeth not save to wash his feet, but is clean every whit: and ye are clean, but not all.”**  
The eleven are already **clean every whit** *and* they still get the basin. Judas is the **“not all.”** His problem is not a half-finished rinse Jesus might upgrade.

This script takes Judas’s wet feet, labels that **“only washed,”** and offers the listener an inner completion to **“every whit.”** That is the old outer-rite / inner-upgrade sermon. It is not this verse.

It also fights John 13:8: **“If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with me.”** In the scene, the washing Jesus does is not a failed skin-wash. Treating it as **“only washed”** cheapens the act the chapter is about.

**“Some, He said, were already ‘clean every whit.’ Judas was not.”** — outcome is fair (13:11). Then the CTA applies the eleven’s category (complete the wash) to Judas’s category (not clean). That is a quiet swap, not a landing.

### Clarity on first hearing
**“the bread that marked him”** — marked how? A novice does not know a sop.

**“That thou doest, do quickly”** is framed only as **“let him go.”** On first hearing, Jesus sounds like He is ordering the betrayal. 13:27’s **“And after the sop Satan entered into him”** is the beat that blocks that reading. It is missing.

**“Whatever is only washed in you”** is the worst line in the piece. It asks a tired stranger to hold a private glossary (washed ≠ clean) that the script never showed, only declared.

Cold-open, then flashback, then he walks out again. The hook already spent the night. The sop paragraph does not return. Two objects (basin, bread). Only one is the thread.

---

### Grace / freshness / spine
No fear, no gain/loss, no “you’ll be Judas if you don’t.” The close is grace-shaped. That part holds.

Freshness: **washed feet + night** is the real textual edge. **“Jesus washed Judas anyway”** is a worn Maundy Thursday observation. The unused edge is still **“If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with me”** / the bath-then-feet split in 13:10.

Spine starts as: near Jesus, wet feet, still not clean, out into the night.  
Spine ends as: leftover basin, Jesus can finish your partial wash.  
That is a thread-swap. Judas’s story is the one who walks *out*. The CTA is a leftover prop talking to the empty chair.

---

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Rewrite the last sentence from John 13:10’s real split (already clean every whit vs “not all”) — kill “only washed in you”; the final line must be un-portable and must pierce.
2. Stop teaching Judas as an incomplete outer rinse Jesus can upgrade. The eleven are clean every whit *and* get the basin; Judas is “not all.” Do not swap those categories at the CTA.
3. Make the first hearing work: who Judas is, that wet feet were not being His; recast or drop “the bread that marked him” / “do quickly” so Jesus is not heard as ordering the betrayal, and so the sop does not fork the spine.
