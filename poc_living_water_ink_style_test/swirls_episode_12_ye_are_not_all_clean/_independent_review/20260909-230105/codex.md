3. **Four duration targets are unavailable as single Flow generations.** The plan asks for 10.6–13.6-second clips ([lines 146–150](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/poc_living_water_ink_style_test/swirls_episode_12_ye_are_not_all_clean/FLOW_RUN_SHEET.md:146)). Current Flow documentation lists Veo frame-to-video lengths up to 8 seconds and Gemini Omni up to 10 seconds; Veo extension is a separate, model-specific generation. There is no generic “longest available” above 10 seconds. The plan forbids extra steps and budgets no extensions. It also says “three marked LONG” while the table marks four. [Google’s current model limits](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en)

4. **The cost ceiling is arithmetically false.** “20–45 generation attempts” ([line 107](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/poc_living_water_ink_style_test/swirls_episode_12_ye_are_not_all_clean/FLOW_RUN_SHEET.md:107)) cannot follow from 20 steps with up to three attempts each. Every step has a CRITICAL condition, so the stated policy permits 60 attempts, before required video extensions. Leaving the model as “whatever ... is currently set to” also leaves costs uncontrolled: current video generations range from single-digit credits to 100 credits each. [Google’s credit table](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234?hl=en)

5. **The fail-forward policy can multiply a bad reference into paid downstream failures.** Rule 5 says to retain a failed generation and continue ([lines 76–80](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/poc_living_water_ink_style_test/swirls_episode_12_ye_are_not_all_clean/FLOW_RUN_SHEET.md:76)). A defective REF_JUDAS can contaminate five stills and their animations. The repository deliberately has human gates so a rejected image is not paid to animate ([CLAUDE.md lines 57–61](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/CLAUDE.md:57)). “Unattended” is unjustified for these load-bearing checks.

6. **Animating baked typography ignores a known repository failure mode.** Every still contains numerous exact labels and captions, then every still is passed through generative I2V. The repository records recurring garbled-letter failures and directs legible writing to a still hold or deterministic ffmpeg motion ([validators.py lines 146–181](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/pipeline/validators.py:146)). The pilot itself lost and invented text. Checking only first and last frames does not catch mid-clip corruption. Typography should be composited after animation.

7. **“Prompts are verbatim” makes retries wasteful and conflicts with existing practice.** The plan requires unchanged retries ([line 57](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/poc_living_water_ink_style_test/swirls_episode_12_ye_are_not_all_clean/FLOW_RUN_SHEET.md:57)). Repeating a contradictory prompt three times is spend, not mitigation.

8. **Cover-style continuity has no conditioning mechanism.** S1 must match S8 before S8 exists; S8 later must match S1, but S1 is not attached as an Ingredient ([lines 380 and 705](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/poc_living_water_ink_style_test/swirls_episode_12_ye_are_not_all_clean/FLOW_RUN_SHEET.md:380)).
