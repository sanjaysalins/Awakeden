# Independent review — cursor (OK, 45s)

## Independent adversarial review

### Doctrinal soundness
Mostly sound and orthodox: Jesus knows the betrayal beforehand, washes Judas anyway, love that persists after knowing the worst. No invented doctrine, no contrarian reading.

**Nit (precision, not heresy):** `"Then He told the room, 'ye are clean, but not all.'"` — In John 13:10 Jesus is answering **Peter** (`"Jesus saith to him"`), not making a general room announcement. Small narrative flattening; a careful listener may notice.

**Application risk:** `"He knows your heart, every corner of it, like that."` parallels the listener with Judas’s known treachery. Grace-framed, but on first hearing it can feel accusatory unless the **offer** (cleansing, welcome, mercy) is named — and it mostly isn’t.

### KJV verbatim — **FAIL on quoted line**

| Quoted phrase | Verdict |
|---|---|
| `"put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, Simon's son, to betray him."` | ✓ Exact substring of John 13:2 KJV |
| `"ye are clean, but not all."` | **✗ NOT exact KJV** — John 13:10 reads: `"but ye are clean, but not all **of you**."` The words `"of you"` are dropped inside quotation marks. |
| `"He that eateth bread with me hath lifted up his heel against me."` | ✓ Exact (John 13:18) |
| `"That thou doest, do quickly."` | ✓ Exact (John 13:27) |
| `"went immediately out: and it was night."` | ✓ Exact substring (John 13:30) |

**Additional scripture-handling problem:** If the spine is “not all **clean**,” the closer KJV line is John 13:11 — `"Ye are not all clean."` The script instead quotes a **truncated** John 13:10 fragment and presents it as the key line. That’s two errors: wrong verse emphasis and non-verbatim quotation.

For a narration pipeline where KJV inside quotes is binding, this alone warrants **REVISE**.

### Clarity on first hearing
**Strong:** Opening image, `"a piece of dipped bread"` for sop, `"and it was night"` as a beat.

**Weak:**
- `"Simon's son"` — a zero-Bible listener doesn’t know which Simon; could read as Peter’s father-son confusion.
- `"ye are clean, but not all."` — without `"of you"` or naming Judas at that moment, **who** is unclean is unclear until later.
- `"Jesus did not wash those feet because Judas was going to change."` — correct theology, but the negated purpose (`not because…`) asks the ear to hold one extra logical step on a fast listen.

No self-contradiction. Thread is followable.

### Grace-anchored conviction
**Pass.** No fear, no gain/loss, no works ladder. `"That is what His love does when it already knows the worst"` keeps conviction in **His** posture, not the listener’s performance.

### Freshness = faithful depth
**Pass.** The hook’s paradox — betrayer leaving on **washed** feet — is faithful to John 13 and genuinely fresh without being clickbait.

### One thread spine (hook → middle → CTA)
**Pass.** Single thread: washed feet → Jesus knew → love that doesn’t quit → He still hasn’t quit on **you**. No thread-swapping.

### Hook score: **8/10**
`"Judas walked out into the night on feet Jesus had washed."` — concrete, ironic, non-template. Not a 9–10 only because “Judas” assumes minimal cultural literacy; the image still pulls, but the name does some unpaid work.

---

### Landing score: **7/10**
`"He has not put the towel down."` is **this piece’s** verb/image — good, not a stock closer.

But:
- `"He knows your heart, every corner of it, like that."` could cap many “God sees you” pieces.
- The close **observes** Jesus’s persistence; it doesn’t **invite** a response (trust Him, let Him wash you, come to Him) in plain language. For a 60s gospel short, the CTA is implied, not landed in the bones.
- Under the rubric: anything under **8** → **REVISE**.

---

### Summary of concrete problems
1. **Non-verbatim KJV quote:** `"ye are clean, but not all."` (missing `"of you"`).
2. **Wrong/emphasis-mangled anchor verse** if “not all clean” is the thesis — should engage John 13:11, not a truncated 13:10.
3. **Landing invitation too implicit** — beautiful image, thin call to respond.
4. **Minor clarity leaks:** `"Simon's son"`, room-vs-Peter address, Judas-as-unclean not signposted at the quote.

---

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the quoted line to exact KJV — either full John 13:10 (`"but ye are clean, but not all of you."`) or, if “not all clean” is the spine, quote John 13:11 verbatim: `"Ye are not all clean."`
2. Sharpen the landing with one plain invitation drawn from the foot-washing image (e.g. let Him wash you / don’t walk out on feet He’s already served) — keep `"He has not put the towel down"` but give the listener something to *do*.
3. At the `"ye are clean"` beat, name Judas in the next breath or use the exact `"not all clean"` wording so a first-time listener knows **who** is excluded without Bible background.
