# Independent review — gemini (OK, 36s)

**FINDINGS**

**Doctrinal Soundness & Biblical Faithfulness**
- **FAIL**: "He gave Judas the bread that told the others who it was" is biblically inaccurate. John 13:28 explicitly states, "Now no man at the table knew for what intent he spake this unto him." The sop identified Judas to John (who asked privately), but the rest of the disciples thought Judas was leaving to buy things for the feast or give to the poor. 

**Clarity on First Hearing**
- **CAUTION**: "A washing is something Jesus does to you. Clean is something only He can make true in you." This is slightly abstract and clunky. It risks confusing the physical act of washing with the spiritual state of being clean in a way that might trip up a listener with zero Bible knowledge. 

**KJV Verbatim**
- **PASS**: The quoted phrases ("put into the heart of Judas Iscariot...", "ye are clean, but not all", "That thou doest, do quickly", "went immediately out: and it was night") are accurate to the KJV text.

**Grace-Anchored Conviction & One Thread Spine**
- **PASS**: The thread remains tightly focused on the basin, the washing, and the contrast between outward washing and inward cleanliness. No fear or works-based framing is used.

**Scores**
- **SCORE THE HOOK: 9**. "Judas walked out into the night on feet Jesus had washed." This is an exceptional, arresting opening. It uses a striking visual paradox that immediately hooks the listener without relying on cheap templates.
- **SCORE THE LANDING: 6**. "The basin was still in the room. Whatever is only washed on the outside, Jesus can still make clean, all the way through. Ask Him." The setup is fantastic and deeply tied to the piece's imagery. However, the final imperative—"Ask Him"—is a generic, stock closer that fails the test: it could be pasted onto the end of literally any other video. It abandons the earned imagery right at the final syllable.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the biblical error claiming the bread "told the others who it was" (John 13:28 contradicts this).
2. Replace the generic stock closer "Ask Him" with a final imperative that actually uses the basin/washing/clean imagery earned by the script.
3. Smooth out the clunky logic of "A washing is something Jesus does to you. Clean is something only He can make true in you" so it is instantly clear to a non-believer.
