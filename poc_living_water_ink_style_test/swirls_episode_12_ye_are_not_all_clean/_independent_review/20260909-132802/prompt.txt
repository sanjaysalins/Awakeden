Judas walked out into the night on feet Jesus had washed.

John says the devil had already "put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, Simon's son, to betray him." Jesus knew — and washed his feet anyway. Every one of them. Then He said what the water alone could not fix: "ye are clean, but not all." A washing is something Jesus does to you. Clean is something only He can make true in you. Judas had one. Not the other.

He gave Judas the bread that told the others who it was, and let him go: "That thou doest, do quickly." Judas "went immediately out: and it was night."

The basin was still in the room. Whatever is only washed on the outside, Jesus can still make clean, all the way through. Ask Him.
