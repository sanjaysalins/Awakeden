# Independent review — codex (OK, 109s)

**Hook: 8/10.** “Judas walked out into the night on feet Jesus had washed” is arresting, concrete, and tied directly to the passage. However, John strongly implies rather than explicitly narrates Judas’s individual washing, so the hook states an inference as observed fact.

**Landing: 5/10.** It retains the basin image, but the application is both invented and theologically muddled:

- “The basin was still in the room. He hadn’t put it away” is unsupported stage business. John says Jesus resumed His garments and sat down; he says nothing about where the basin remained.
- “Whatever is in yours” has no clear antecedent on first hearing: your heart, hand, feet, or room?
- “The basin is still out for you too” conflates outward foot-washing with inward cleansing. The passage explicitly distinguishes them: Judas could receive the washing while remaining “not clean.” The metaphor therefore promises more than the basin itself signifies.
- The conclusion never explains how the listener receives Christ’s true cleansing. Grace becomes a vaguely available object rather than cleansing received through faith, with repentance left unarticulated.

**Textual accuracy:** Every word placed inside quotation marks is found verbatim in the KJV text of John 13; I found no altered quoted word. Several quotations are shortened excerpts, with surrounding grammar supplied by the narration, but the enclosed wording itself is accurate. [John 13 KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+13&version=KJV)

**Misreading of verse 28:** “No one at that table could see what Jesus already saw” overstates “no man at the table knew.” Verse 28 says they did not understand the intent of “That thou doest, do quickly.” Immediately beforehand, Jesus had answered the beloved disciple’s question by identifying the betrayer through the sop. Not understanding the command’s purpose is not the same as being unable to identify Judas.

**Invented characterization:** “Then, quieter” assigns Jesus a vocal delivery John never records. It adds atmosphere but presents imagination as narration of the text.

**Doctrinal center:** The piece notices Christ’s knowing service toward Judas, but omits John’s stated interpretive anchors: Jesus “loved them unto the end,” knew His divine authority and origin, and gave the washing as an example for His disciples. The result shifts from John’s themes of Christ’s sovereign love, cleansing, and servant discipleship to the less precise idea that Jesus will overlook whatever the listener is concealing.

**Thread spine:** The betrayal/foot-washing thread remains substantially intact from hook to ending. The problem is not thread-swapping; it is that the final metaphor changes the meaning of the basin.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Replace “the basin is still out for you” with an application that distinguishes Jesus’ outward washing of Judas from the inward cleansing Judas lacked.
2. Remove the invented claims “The basin was still in the room. He hadn’t put it away” and “Then, quieter.”
3. Correct “No one at that table could see” so it accurately states that they misunderstood Jesus’ command, not that Judas had not been identified.
