## A "He Knew, and He Stayed"  (spine: Fable; grafts: Claude CLI "Watch.", "one after another"; Opus "ties a towel")
NARRATOR: Do you ever think: if people really knew me, they would leave?
NARRATOR: One night, Jesus sat at supper with His closest friends. And He knew them.
NARRATOR: He knew one of them would betray Him that same night. He knew another would deny Him three times before morning.
NARRATOR: So what does He do with friends like that? Watch.
NARRATOR: He gets up from the table. He ties a towel around Himself. He pours water into a bowl, and He kneels and washes their feet, one after another. The work of a servant. And Judas is still in the room.
JESUS: "Know ye what I have done to you?"
NARRATOR: John says it in one line: "he loved them unto the end."
NARRATOR: Soon after, Judas walks out. And John adds four small words: "and it was night."
NARRATOR: Maybe it is night where you are. Maybe you are sure nobody would stay, if they knew.
NARRATOR: Jesus knows.
NARRATOR: And look at that room again. The one who knew everything was not the one who left. He was the one holding the towel.

## B "The Towel Got There First"  (spine: Opus; grafts: Claude CLI "all the way round", "He knew exactly who would leave"; Codex "what did He do with those hands")
NARRATOR: A man walked out into the night, alone. Earlier that evening, someone had knelt on the floor and washed his feet.
NARRATOR: It was Jesus. And Jesus knew this man would betray Him.
NARRATOR: John says Jesus knew the Father "had given all things into his hands." What did He do with those hands? He "took a towel."
NARRATOR: He poured water into a bowl and knelt at His friends' feet, one pair after another. Peter pulled back.
JESUS: "What I do thou knowest not now; but thou shalt know hereafter."
NARRATOR: He knew exactly who would leave. He kept going, all the way round.
NARRATOR: Then Judas took bread from Jesus' own hand, and "went immediately out: and it was night."
NARRATOR: Maybe tonight you feel like the one outside the door. Look at what happened first. Before that door closed, Jesus had been down at his feet.
NARRATOR: John says He loved His own "unto the end."
NARRATOR: Your night is real. But His love got there before it. The towel got there first.
