## TITLE
He Picked Up the Towel

## WHO / TAKEAWAY / CURIOSITY GAP / WHY IT EARNS ITS PLACE
WHO: Someone afraid that being truly known would mean being unwanted.
TAKEAWAY: Jesus knows Peter’s coming failure and still washes his feet.
CURIOSITY GAP: What will Jesus do with his hands when he knows what these people will do?
WHY IT EARNS ITS PLACE: The hands holding all things take up a towel.

## NARRATION

NARRATOR:
Being known can feel more frightening than being alone.

What happens when Jesus knows the worst?

At supper, he knows someone will betray him. He knows Peter will deny him three times.

And he knows that all things have been given into his hands.

Watch what those hands do.

He takes a towel. Pours water. Begins washing his disciples’ feet.

Peter tries to stop him.

Jesus answers:

JESUS:
“If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with me.”

NARRATOR:
Peter needs to receive what Jesus is doing for him.

This is not a room where everyone is innocent. Jesus says they are not all clean. He does not pretend betrayal is love.

But he does not wait for perfect friends before picking up the towel.

Later, Peter promises his life. Jesus tells him about the denial still ahead.

He knew.

And he had washed Peter’s feet.

If you are alone tonight, look at those hands.

Being known by Jesus is not the end of being loved.

He knew Peter’s failure was coming.

He picked up the towel.

## WHY THE OPENING HOLDS A LONELY VIEWER
It names the fear beneath loneliness: someone might leave if they really knew you.
The next line turns that fear into a question the washing will answer.

## WHY THE ENDING IS EARNED
The concrete thing is the towel, introduced as the surprising choice of hands entrusted with all things.
Returning to that same action after Peter’s coming denial gives the lonely viewer evidence of Jesus’s love.

## RISKS
A newcomer could hear acceptance as approval of harm; the narration explicitly distinguishes betrayal from love.
A Bible reader could hear universal cleansing; the film retains Jesus’s distinction that not all are clean.
