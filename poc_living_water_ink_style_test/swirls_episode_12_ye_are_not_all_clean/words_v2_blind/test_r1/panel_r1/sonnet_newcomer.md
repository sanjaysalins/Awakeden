## 1. WHO / WHAT / WHY
**X:** A man leaves alone at night. Earlier, Jesus washed his feet, even though Jesus knew this man would betray Him. Jesus kept going with everyone. Message: Jesus loved him before he left. Maybe that means He loves me before my bad night too. I only half get it.
**Y:** Jesus eats with friends, knows one will betray Him and one will deny Him, and still washes all their feet, like a servant. Judas leaves. Message: Jesus knows the worst about people and does not leave. Clear.

## 2. FIRST 2 SECONDS
**X: 5/10.** "A man walked out into the night, alone." Moody, and "alone" speaks to me. But it is a stranger's story, not about me.
**Y: 8/10.** "Do you ever think: if people really knew me, they would leave?" This is exactly my fear. I would stop.

## 3. ENDING
**X: 6/10.** "Your night is real. But His love got there before it. The towel got there first." Nice image, but "got there before it" is abstract. Close to a slogan.
**Y: 8/10.** "The one who knew everything was not the one who left. He was the one holding the towel." It answers the opening fear and feels earned by the story. The short "Jesus knows." before it is strong.

## 4. WHERE I GOT LOST OR MISLED
- X: "someone had knelt... washed his feet" then "It was Jesus." I did not know who "he" was at first. Confusing.
- X: "What did He do with those hands? He 'took a towel.'" and "had given all things into his hands": unclear what this means.
- X: "Peter pulled back." Who is Peter, and why? Not explained.
- X: "the one outside the door" and "Before that door closed": I do not know which door.
- X: "unto the end" is archaic. Also "Look at what happened first" is vague.
- Y: "Judas" is never introduced, but I guess he is the betrayer. "deny Him three times" is not explained. "unto the end" is odd English.
- Y: "Know ye what I have done to you?" is old English with no answer given. Why washing feet is humble is not clear to a newcomer.
- Y: "And Judas is still in the room" is slightly puzzling but works.

## 5. ACCURACY vs KJV
**X:** Mostly RIGHT. "took a towel" (13:4), "went immediately out: and it was night" (13:30), "unto the end" (13:1). "had given all things into his hands" is RIGHT (13:3). "Judas took bread from Jesus' own hand" is RIGHT (13:26). OVERCLAIMED: "Before that door closed, Jesus had been down at his feet" implies Judas was washed, which is implied but not stated. "Peter pulled back" is RIGHT (13:8).
**Y:** Mostly RIGHT. "he loved them unto the end" is RIGHT; "them" is a small change from "his own... loved them." "Deny three times before morning" is RIGHT (13:38). "Judas is still in the room" while washing is OVERCLAIMED: the KJV never says when Judas leaves, only after the sop (13:26-30); the washing comes first, so it is an inference, not stated. "Soon after, Judas walks out" is fine. "Jesus knows" ending is RIGHT in spirit.

## 6. PICK
**Y.** Overall: **X 5/10, Y 8/10.**
ONE change: in Y, replace "John says it in one line: 'he loved them unto the end.'" with "John says it in one line: 'he loved them to the very end.'" so the old phrase is clear. Ideally also name Judas once as "Judas, the one who would betray Him."
