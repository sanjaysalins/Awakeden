**What it is:** a narrated short for a channel that finds Jesus across the whole Bible, for a worldwide audience. Retro mid-century cartoon pictures (grainy gouache texture, warm lamplight against blue night), each gently animated. One narrator voice, a separate voice for Jesus's own words. A soft arpeggiated music score from start to end, quiet room sounds. English only.
