## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
- 5.8 "sat at supper... He knew them": RIGHT (John 13:1-3, 11).
- 11.3 Judas would betray: RIGHT (13:11, 21). Peter "three times before morning": RIGHT (13:38, before the cock crows).
- 22.7 towel, water, washes feet: RIGHT (13:4-5). "A servant's work": RIGHT as inference (13:13-16).
- 33.9 "Judas is still in the room": RIGHT (13:10-11; he leaves at 13:30). The film wisely does not claim more.
- 36.6 "Know ye what I have done to you?": words RIGHT (KJV verbatim), placement slightly OFF. In 13:12 He says it after He has finished, dressed and sat down; here it plays over the washing.
- 39.1 "he loved them unto the end": RIGHT (13:1). "The end was the cross": mildly OVERCLAIMED. "Unto the end" also means "to the uttermost"; the cross is a fair reading, not the only one.
- 48.5 "and it was night": RIGHT (13:30). "Four small words": English only.
- 63.4 "Jesus knows. All of it.": RIGHT (John 2:24-25; 13:11).
- 66.5: RIGHT. Peter drawn old and grey (frame 16.0) is UNSUPPORTED but harmless.
