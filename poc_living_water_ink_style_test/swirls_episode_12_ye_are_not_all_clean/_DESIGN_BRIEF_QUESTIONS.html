Episode 12 — Design Questions

From the visual design brief. 10 items, none silently decided.
Default plan: if you don't say otherwise, I'll go with my recommendation on all 10 and move to rendering. Just flag any number you want changed.
1
Where the blue-ink motif "lives" on the page
Recommend: anchor it to the water in the basin, not to a person's hand (every past episode used a hand or threshold).
Why: this episode needs the motif to never touch Judas — anchoring it to the water is the only way to guarantee that. First time the series has done it this way, so flagging the change.
2
Does Jesus's figure appear on the hero page (F06)?
Recommend: yes, show him — a page about his love with no trace of him risks feeling generic.
Alternative: just the basin + towel + water, no figure — echoes the back cover a page early.
3
F03's baked caption — full line or short?
Recommend: just "not all" (2 words) — the full "ye are clean but not all" (6 words) may be too long to render legibly.
The full line stays as the front-cover title instead.
4
F06's baked caption
Recommend: "knowing he wouldn't" — a short authored paraphrase, since no verbatim fragment of that line is short and strong enough.
Breaks this episode's own all-verbatim-caption pattern — flagging that trade explicitly. Alternative: "he wouldn't" (verbatim but unclear alone), or no caption on this page at all.
5
How to draw "the old word" (the Scripture quote) on F03
Recommend: a hand-gesture-as-speech panel, not a drawn scroll.
Matches how the rest of the episode avoids literalizing quotations. Minor call either way.
6
Doorway reference image — reuse or fresh?
Recommend: just a heads-up, not really a decision — if F04 doesn't naturally show a doorway, F05 needs its own fresh approval pass rather than reusing a crop.
Flagging so the render step doesn't assume a crop exists that isn't there yet.
7
Back cover Scripture subtitle
Recommend: 1 John 1:9 ("if we confess our sins... cleanse us") — matches the landing's "bring Him your worst" line best.
Trade-off: it's outside John's Gospel, breaking the pattern of every other episode staying within its own passage. Alternative: John 13:35 (in-passage, but about love shown, not being made clean).
8
Running header title (seq_title)
Recommend: "THE BASIN AND THE DOOR" — names the episode's own visual geometry.
Alternative: "NOT ALL CLEAN" — closer to verbatim, matches the front-cover title's own words.
9
F02's animation style (which AI model)
Recommend: not a real decision yet — this gets judged against the actual rendered image, not on paper.
Called it "kling" for now; may switch to "veo" once we see the real still.
10
F03's composition — wide room shot or tight on Jesus+Judas?
Recommend: wide (the text says Jesus spoke "to the room").
Fallback: a tighter frame is easier to keep visually clean of the blue-ink motif near background figures, if the wide version proves hard to render cleanly.
