**The synthesis (not either/or)**

Luke’s contrast is **destination**, not two packages:

- nine: mercy received, road continued, Giver not returned to
- one: same mercy, then **at Jesus’s feet**, glory to God
- Jesus’s word to the one who came: **thy faith hath made thee whole**

The “more” is not a withheld sacrament. The “more” is **Him**. Faith’s object is the Giver, not the benefit. Returning does not earn `sozo`. Returning is where that faith went.
