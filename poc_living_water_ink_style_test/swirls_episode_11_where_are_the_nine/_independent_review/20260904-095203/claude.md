# Independent review — claude (OK, 131s)

## Independent Adversarial Review — "Where Are the Nine" (Luke 17:11-19)

**KJV verbatim check — PASS.** I checked every quoted clause against Luke 17:12-19 word for word: "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us" (v13), "Go shew yourselves unto the priests" (v14), "And it came to pass, that, as they went, they were cleansed" (v14), "and with a loud voice glorified God, and fell down on his face at his feet, giving him thanks: and he was a Samaritan" (v15-16, two verses merged with a lowercased "and" — punctuation/case only, no word altered), "Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine? There are not found that returned to give glory to God, save this stranger" (v17-18), "Arise, go thy way: thy faith hath made thee whole" (v19). All exact. No fabricated or substituted words.

**Doctrine — sound.** "Luke calls it thanks. Jesus calls it faith" correctly tracks the text's own distinction between the physical *cleansing* given to all ten and the deeper *wholeness* (sozo) Jesus attributes only to the one who returned — that's the passage's actual point, not an invented reading. No contrarian or clickbait doctrine.

**Grace-anchored — clean.** No fear, no threat of losing the healing, no works-merit framing. "Whatever he's already done for you, he's still asking where you are" invites; it doesn't pressure.

**Thread spine — mostly one thread, with a loose end.** The hook sets up two possible threads at once: (1) they were healed *after* obeying, not before ("They obeyed before they could see any difference"), and (2) only one came back. The piece resolves thread (2) fully at the landing ("Turn back" ties directly to "turned back"), but thread (1) — faith-before-sight — is raised in paragraph 2 and never paid off anywhere in the conviction or landing. It reads as scene-setting that got promoted to its own beat without a resolution, slightly diluting the single-thread discipline the spec requires.

**Clarity gap:** "They'd stood at a distance, as their disease required" assumes the listener already knows Levitical purity law (lepers had to keep separate, Lev 13:46). A zero-Bible-knowledge listener gets no reason *why* — it's stated as if self-evident. Small, but it's exactly the kind of unexplained assumption the clarity bar exists to catch.

**HOOK SCORE: 6/10.** Not a template opener, so it avoids the automatic cap — but the actual curiosity payload, "Only one of them came back," is the *third* sentence, roughly 8-10 seconds of natural narration in. The first ~16 words ("Jesus told ten men with leprosy to go — before a single one of them was healed") require the listener to already expect an instant-miracle trope in order to register the "before healed" detail as surprising; on its own it reads as flat exposition, not a scroll-stopper. The real hook is buried past the 5-second window that matters for Shorts retention.

**LANDING SCORE: 8/10.** "Turn back" passes the stock-closer test — it's not "come to Jesus / turn to Him / look to Jesus," it's pulled directly from the passage's own verb (Luke 17:15, "turned back," echoed twice in the narration's own prose before the CTA lands). Could this exact final sentence end a different piece? Only weakly — it's specifically earned by the leper's own physical turn described earlier in this piece, not a generic spiritual phrase grafted on.

Hook under 8 forces REVISE per the scoring rule, even though the landing and doctrine are solid.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Restructure the hook so the "only one came back" tension lands in the first breath (first ~5 seconds), not the third sentence — lead with the twist, not the setup.
2. Cut or clarify "as their disease required" — a listener with no Bible background won't know why lepers kept their distance; either drop the clause or make the reason explicit in a half-line.
3. Resolve or trim the "obeyed before they could see any difference" beat — it opens a faith-before-sight thread that's never picked back up in the conviction/landing; either connect it to the ending or cut it to keep one clean thread.
