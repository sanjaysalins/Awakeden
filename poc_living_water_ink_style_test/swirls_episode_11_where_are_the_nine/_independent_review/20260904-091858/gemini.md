# Independent review — gemini (OK, 35s)

**Doctrinal soundness**: Sound. Faithfully recounts the narrative of the ten lepers (Luke 17) without adding invented theology or twisting the text for clickbait.
**KJV verbatim**: Clean. All quoted phrases ("Jesus, Master, have mercy on us", "Go shew yourselves...", "Were there not ten cleansed?", etc.) match the Luke 17 KJV text perfectly. 
**Clarity on first hearing**: Mostly clear, but the phrase "Only one closed it for good" in the final paragraph is slightly ambiguous for an audio listener. They have to mentally link the pronoun "it" back to the "distance" mentioned in the previous sentence.
**Grace-anchored conviction**: The tone is rooted in grace, but the conviction is too passive. It states a fact rather than inviting a response.
**Freshness = faithful depth**: Strong. Highlighting the spatial elements—the "distance" they were required to keep, and the fact that the healing happened "as they went" on the road—is a grounded, textually accurate angle.
**One thread spine**: Excellent. The motif of "distance" is established early, maintained through the middle, and paid off directly in the landing.
**SCORE THE HOOK**: 8. "Jesus sent ten men with leprosy away — before a single one of them was healed." This is a strong, narrative-driven hook that presents an immediate paradox without relying on a cheap template.
**SCORE THE LANDING**: 7. The imagery is perfectly tailored to the piece (using "distance" and "standing"), which successfully avoids a stock "come to Jesus" closer. However, the final phrase—"you already know the way back to where he's standing"—is merely an observation. It lacks an active, invitational verb to actually call the listener to action. 

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Make the CTA an active invitation rather than a passive observation. Instead of just stating "you already know the way back," actively invite the listener to stop walking and turn back to Him.
2. Fix the pronoun ambiguity in the final paragraph by changing "Only one closed it for good" to explicitly say "closed the distance," ensuring the listener doesn't lose the thread.
