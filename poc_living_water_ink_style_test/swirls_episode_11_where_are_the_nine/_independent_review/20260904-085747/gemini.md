# Independent review — gemini (OK, 50s)

Here is the independent review of the narration artifact:

**Doctrinal Soundness & Clarity**
- **Flawed Binary:** *"Ten were cleansed the moment they obeyed. One was made whole the moment he believed..."* This is a false theological binary. The nine *did* believe—they walked to the priests while still covered in leprosy, acting on Jesus' word before seeing the healing. The distinction in the text isn't obedience vs. belief; it is seeking the *gift* vs. worshiping the *Giver*. Flattens the actual spiritual depth of the Samaritan's return (worship/gratitude vs. mere belief).
- **Theological Economy:** *"Cleansing is free for the asking."* This implies a transactional split where physical healing is "free" but spiritual wholeness requires a different kind of effort ("turning back"). It muddies the grace-anchored spine.

**KJV Verbatim**
- **Capitalization Alteration:** The script quotes: `"...and with a loud voice glorified God, and fell down on his face..."` In the KJV, verse 16 begins with a capital letter: "...glorified God, And fell down on his face...". The script alters the casing to artificially splice the quote. 

**One Thread Spine & Grace-Anchored Conviction**
- The script successfully tracks the contrast of the 9 vs 1, and the distinction between physical healing and spiritual salvation (pointing out the Greek connection to Luke 7:50). However, the climax stumbles on the flawed "moment he believed" premise. 

**Scores**
- **SCORE THE HOOK: 8** 
  *"Jesus healed ten men at once — then asked how many hadn't come back. Nine."* Very strong, narrative-driven, creates immediate tension, and avoids templates.
- **SCORE THE LANDING: 6** 
  *"Whole is still there for anyone who turns back believing, the way he did."* It is not a stock closer, which is good, but it is grammatically jarring to force the adjective "Whole" into a noun state ("Whole is still there"). Combined with the flawed doctrinal setup above ("the moment he believed"), the landing loses its impact and theological precision.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the false "obeyed vs. believed" binary; the nine had faith too, but the one returned to worship the Giver rather than just walking away with the gift.
2. Reword the grammatically clunky CTA ("Whole is still there") to deliver a smoother, stronger landing.
3. Fix the KJV verbatim capitalization splice at "...glorified God, And fell down..." to exactly match Luke 17:15-16.
