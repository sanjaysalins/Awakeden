Bottom line

No model was clean on both pieces — every one of the 8 had at least one real defect somewhere. Two patterns showed up across MULTIPLE models, not just one, which matters more than any single model's score:

  • Bare-chest exposure (Seedream 4.5, Nano Banana 2, Seedream 5 Pro, Grok — half the field) shows up whenever a model is asked for "torn open at the breast." That phrase itself may need rewording project-wide, not just picking a different model.
  • Garbled baked text (Seedream 5 Lite badly, Nano Banana 2 and Kling to a lesser degree) is a real, repeat risk on the cheaper tier — captions need a real proofread, every time, on any model outside the two currently used (Nano Banana Pro / NBP).

If I had to pick a cheaper alternative to trial next: Seedream 4.5 (15 credits, about a third of Nano Banana Pro's 40). It was the only model that got the harder interior panel fully right — correct count, correct Samaritan mark, clean text — and its one flaw (chest exposure) is fixable with the same wardrobe-wording fix every model needs anyway. Seedream 5 Pro (30cr) is the safer but pricier runner-up. Wan 2.7 is remarkably good stylistically at just 6 credits but keeps undercounting figures, which is a harder defect to prompt away.

Nothing here is a decision to switch — this is a first look, not a validated replacement. A real swap would still need the wardrobe/headcount issues fixed and re-tested clean before touching a real episode.
