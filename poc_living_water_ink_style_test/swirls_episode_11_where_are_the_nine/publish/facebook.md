## TITLE
Ten Were Healed. Only One Turned Back.

## DESCRIPTION
Luke 17: ten men with leprosy cried out to Jesus for mercy. He told them to go show themselves to the priests, and they were cleansed on the way there, before they could see any change.

Nine kept walking. One turned back to fall at Jesus's feet and give thanks, and he was a Samaritan.

"Arise, go thy way: thy faith hath made thee whole." (Luke 17:19)

Nine had exactly what they asked for. Only one had Jesus.
