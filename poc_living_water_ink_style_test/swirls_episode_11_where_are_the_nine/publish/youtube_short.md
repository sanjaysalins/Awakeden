## TITLE
Ten Were Healed. Only One Came Back.

## DESCRIPTION
Luke 17: ten men with leprosy cried out to Jesus. All ten were cleansed on the road, before they even reached the priests. Only one turned back to thank him, and he was a Samaritan.

Jesus asked the question out loud: "Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine?"

"Arise, go thy way: thy faith hath made thee whole." (Luke 17:19)

Getting exactly what you asked for is not the same as turning back to the One who gave it.
