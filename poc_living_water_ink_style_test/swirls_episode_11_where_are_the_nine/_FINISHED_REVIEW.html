What this build covered

  • Narration: drafted, Fable pass, then 7 rounds of the 5-CLI panel to real convergence on a genuinely hard doctrinal question (the ending had to land on "gift vs. Giver," not a "cleansed vs. whole" two-tier reading) — locked, voiced at natural speed (80.61s, no time-stretch).
  • 10 pages + 2 covers designed by Fable, solving two hard problems: showing the healing itself with Jesus off-page (a single ink thread stands in for his word), and keeping the nine-vs-one ending from reading as "the nine got less."
  • Real consistency defect caught after first review: the Samaritan wore a different outfit almost every page, and "the ten" didn't read as the same group. Fixed with one clean reference image and a full re-render pass across all affected pages.
  • Back cover rejected outright and completely reimagined (a pressed-face-in-dust device read as death, not welcome) — replaced with Jesus standing, the Samaritan already reverent at his feet, the road empty behind.
  • All 12 clips animated (PixVerse V6 primary, Kling-3-Omni fallback per the user's own call after a real bake-off) — 7 passed first try, 4 needed the fallback, 1 (F03) never animated cleanly after 4 attempts and ships as a static hold.
  • A real defect caught by the user's own eye after GATE 3 approval: F02's panel 2 developed a dark hole on the man's chest mid-clip. Took 2 more attempts (a different failure each time) before a clean fix landed.
  • A second real defect caught on my own final check of the assembled cut (after this episode had already been assembled and called finished once): the back cover's animation showed the Samaritan visibly rising from prostrate to standing over its own 6 seconds — directly contradicting the design and the caption's own meaning ("at his feet"). PixVerse's retry improved it but still drifted; Kling's retry held the pose pixel-for-pixel from first frame to last. Re-assembled and re-SFX'd on the corrected clip.
  • Felt-piano score written fresh for this episode's own arc — a small honest opening at the free universal cleansing (f04), held back again through the fork and the question, resolving only at the personal encounter (f09).
  • User asked to move score design early (right after narration locks) and use ElevenLabs' precise timed-composition feature. Fable designed it; the 5-CLI panel found the specific mechanism (a structured "composition plan") is a confirmed dead end in this exact project — tried twice before, never worked, once injected vocals — verified directly against this repo's own history. Corrected to the proven mechanism (a free-text prompt, same as always) but with every timing cue now a REAL per-word timestamp instead of a word-count guess. Also fixed swirls_assemble.py (backward-compatible, opt-in) so the video itself now cuts at those same real timestamps — the hero's resolution chord and the video's own page-turn now land within a fraction of a second of each other, instead of ~1s apart as before. Regenerated the score and rebuilt the assembly + SFX on the corrected timing.
  • SFX: a continuous desert wind carries the whole outdoor wilderness setting, footsteps mark the one stretch where the text has the company actually walking, dawn ambience under the back cover.
