Jesus told ten men with leprosy to go — before a single one of them was healed. They were cleansed on the road. Only one of them came back.

They'd stood at a distance, as their disease required, and cried out, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us." He told them, "Go shew yourselves unto the priests." They obeyed before they could see any difference. "And it came to pass, that, as they went, they were cleansed."

Nine kept going. One turned back, "and with a loud voice glorified God, and fell down on his face at his feet, giving him thanks: and he was a Samaritan."

Jesus asked it out loud, naming exactly what nine men had skipped: "Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine? There are not found that returned to give glory to God, save this stranger."

To the one still on his face, he said, "Arise, go thy way: thy faith hath made thee whole."

Nine had exactly what they'd asked for, and kept walking. One turned back to him. Luke calls it thanks. Jesus calls it faith. That question didn't end with the nine. Go back and find him — at his feet, not on the road.
