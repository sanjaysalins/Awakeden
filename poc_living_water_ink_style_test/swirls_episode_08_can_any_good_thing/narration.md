A man insulted Jesus behind His back. Jesus complimented him to his face.

Philip told Nathanael the news: the Messiah — Jesus, of Nazareth. Nathanael's answer:

"Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth?"

Philip did not argue — he invited. Jesus spoke first:

"Behold an Israelite indeed, in whom is no guile!"

Nathanael asked:

"Whence knowest thou me?"

Jesus answered:

"Before that Philip called thee, when thou wast under the fig tree, I saw thee."

A moment only heaven had seen — named. The doubt did not fade. It fell. Nathanael said:

"Rabbi, thou art the Son of God; thou art the King of Israel."

And Jesus went further:

"Thou shalt see greater things than these... Hereafter ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man."

That is Jacob's ladder — the one Jesus said He is, standing open, permanently.

He is not offended by honest questions. He already sees you, under your fig tree.

Come and see.
