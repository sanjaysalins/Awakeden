This hook overclaims the history and borders on clickbait. Nathanael asked, "Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth?" He insulted a *town* based on a regional stereotype, not Jesus personally. They need at least a fragment of the KJV text (e.g., Nathanael's actual question about Nazareth) to honestly ground the video.
