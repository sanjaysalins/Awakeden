# Independent review — codex (OK, 51s)

The kit needs revision. Its “ALL PASS” status contradicts both its embedded review and the copy provided.

1. **YouTube Short — explicit doctrinal overstatement:** “That is the ladder Jacob saw, and Jesus says it is Himself.” A Christ-centred reading of the Jacob connection is defensible; claiming Jesus explicitly identifies Himself as the ladder misrepresents His words. John 1:51 describes angels ascending and descending upon the Son of man. Mark the connection as interpretation. [John 1:45–51, KJV](https://www.biblestudytools.com/kjv/john/passage/?q=john+1%3A45-51)

2. **YouTube Short title, TikTok, Facebook title, Instagram — sensationalised motive:** “A man insulted Jesus behind His back.” Nathanael disparages Nazareth before meeting Jesus. “Behind His back” adds the suggestion of a deliberate personal slight or covert gossip that the passage does not establish. The embedded auditor’s acceptance does not resolve that problem. “Jesus complimented him” also reduces Jesus’ recognition of Nathanael’s character to a social reversal.

3. **Title options 2 and 3 — unsupported promises:** “Jesus Heard That” presents an inference as a reported event; the passage does not say Jesus heard Nathanael’s remark. “What Jesus Saw Under the Fig Tree” promises a revelation of what happened there, which the passage never supplies. The latter risks a clear bait-and-switch. [John 1:45–51, KJV](https://www.biblestudytools.com/kjv/john/passage/?q=john+1%3A45-51)

4. **YouTube Short and Long — invented exclusivity:** “A moment only heaven had seen” asserts that nobody else witnessed Nathanael beneath the tree. Scripture supplies neither that detail nor what he was doing. Poetic language still makes a factual claim here.

5. **YouTube Short, Facebook, Instagram — generalisation exceeds the episode:** “He is not offended by honest questions” / “Jesus is not offended by honest questions.” This turns one encounter into an unrestricted claim about Jesus’ response to questions. “He already sees you under your fig tree” also blurs the historical event with personal application. Distinguish application from what the passage establishes.

6. **YouTube Short and Facebook — the conversion is flattened:** “The doubt did not fade. It fell.” The copy omits Nathanael’s confession identifying Jesus as the Son of God and King of Israel. That confession would substantiate the claimed change and give the captions a stronger Christ-pointing centre than the “compliment” motif. [John 1:49, KJV](https://www.biblestudytools.com/kjv/john/passage/?q=john+1%3A45-51)

7. **Kit verification — corrections were never applied:** The embedded reviewer requests changes to the ladder claim, “He did not argue. He invited,” and Facebook’s generalisation. All remain. “Pre-checked, red-teamed and panel-reviewed” therefore conveys unjustified assurance. “Plain ASCII” also conflicts with the supplied copy’s ✝ characters.

8. **Platform packaging — posting instructions conflict:** “Copy each block straight into the platform’s upload form” includes a “Long-form 16:9” block for an artifact explicitly labelled “short.” The auditor calls that block a stub, but the posting section does not. TikTok and Instagram have immediate hooks, but both depend on the questionable insult framing.

**KJV check:** No altered words found in the explicitly quoted verse excerpts. Facebook’s combined quotation paragraph includes verse 46 but ends with “(John 1:47, KJV),” so its reference needs widening. The tags are relevant; the visible footer URLs are intact. “Come and see” supplies an invitation, though its connection to Jesus could be clearer. Video fidelity and sibling-title collisions remain unverified.

VERDICT: REVISE

1. Remove unsupported claims about the ladder, the private fig-tree moment, and Jesus hearing the remark.
2. Replace the “insulted Jesus behind His back” hook and centre Nathanael’s confession of Christ.
3. Apply the outstanding review corrections, fix Facebook’s reference, and remove the misleading verification and long-form posting instructions.
