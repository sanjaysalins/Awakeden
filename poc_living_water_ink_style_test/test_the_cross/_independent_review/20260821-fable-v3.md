# Independent creative review — Fable (v3 pass)

Reviewed: SWIRLS_OF_LIFE_SERIES_PLAN_V3.md
Method: fresh agent (Fable model). Independently re-ran the full collision audit from scratch (shipped manifest, all 11 series.json series, the full narration tree incl. unpublished folders, longform slate) before judging anything creative — result: v3's 15-pick slate is genuinely clear, confirmed independently.

## 1. Do the fixes land?

**Nazareth (#14) — lands.** The push-to-the-edge design is the first time in the whole grammar human action (not Christ's presence) moves the ink; correctly guarded ("never dies, never dims"); John 1:11 citation is apt. Two gaps: (a) designs the swirl, says nothing about the dead ink — the crowd's "is not this Joseph's son" is textbook doubt/Fray territory, left undiagnosed without saying so is a choice that should be named; (b) missing from the motif-balance count (5+5+3+1=14, not 15).

**Judas (#12) — the best idea in v3.** "The washing happens; the stain stays" (13:10-11 verbatim) is real design with real doctrine under it. Three execution gaps: (a) the pericope (13:1-17) ends too early — the text's own exit beat is 13:30 ("he then... went immediately out: and it was night"), outside the stated range; (b) the page this episode exists for (Jesus at full presence washing a stained Judas) trips the codebase's own existing `stainDose + swirlStage <= 4` rule (`NORTH_STAR_PROMPT.md:167`) — needs a designed answer, not a render-time surprise; (c) the QUAD lock says stain and swirl never touch — foot-washing is literal contact between grace and the stained man, the tightest page in the season.

**Emmaus (#15) — repeats v2's exact failure one row down.** v2 was flagged because Nazareth had no motif design; v3 fixed Nazareth and shipped a new finale with no motif design of its own. Worse: it quietly breaks the season's own tagline — "clears the instant Jesus is present" — but on the Emmaus road Jesus is present for miles while the Fray does NOT clear ("their eyes were holden"). A second designed exception nobody named. Suggested design: dramatic irony — the viewer sees the swirl at Stage 2-3 the whole road while the disciples' own line stays frayed; dies at the breaking of bread; he vanishes and the swirl stays (24:32). Note: Emmaus is this project's own house thesis text (already the proof-text of the "Second Look" format elsewhere in the repo) — most on-brand possible finale, also the least surprising; only the motif design earns "never seen that before."

## 2. Martha out, Sheba/Nain in — better or worse shape?

Martha cut is right on the device (no on-page resolution) but costs the season its only non-miracle domestic episode — closest register to the stated target viewer. Now wall-to-wall miracles/spectacle plus one execution scene.

**Queen of Sheba — right pick, wrong tag.** Marked swirl-only; wrong. 1 Kings 10:7 hands the Fray motif verbatim: "I believed not the words, until I came, and mine eyes had seen it." The OT's own Doubting Thomas. Also: Jesus cites her by title ("the queen of the south"), not name — she's unnamed in Scripture; fix the wording.

**Widow of Nain — right pick, tag is a rule violation.** Fray requires fear/doubt; Luke 7:11-17 has zero such vocabulary from the widow, it's grief met with compassion. The exact class of error v3 exists to fix, committed on a new pick. Fresh alternative already inside the slate: Jesus touches the bier (7:14) — the precise contact-with-death Numbers 19 (#2) exists to purify. Retag Stain (ceremonial uncleanness, never guilt); creates a second OT/NT rhyme (#2→#7) alongside #3→#8.

## 3. Still safe, push further

- Ten Lepers (#11): the stains clear "as they went" (17:14) on a road page with no Jesus in frame — the one place the surface grammar visibly doesn't hold unless designed (a swirl-thread carrying his spoken word onto the road page).
- Simon of Cyrene (#13): "motif-free" reads as swirl-absent too, but Jesus is bodily on the page — a swirl-less Via Dolorosa says the gospel is absent from the road to the cross. If deliberate (he doesn't know what he's carrying), it wants the hindsight payoff: Mark 15:21 names his sons Alexander and Rufus; Romans 16:13 greets a believing Rufus — the swirl could arrive years later, in a closing caption.
- Numbers 19 (#2): "whosoever" used defensively (excusing namelessness) — could be used offensively instead (the anonymity is the promise: the viewer's own name fits).

## 4. New problems nobody's caught

1. Emmaus has no motif design (the biggest one — v2-Nazareth's failure, repeated).
2. The Nain Fray tag breaks the system's own "fear/doubt only" rule.
3. Build-order self-contradiction: pilot line offers Talitha Cumi as having "no multi-ref complications" while the plan's own risk table lists Talitha as the multi-ref example three paragraphs down. The Ladder is the only clean pilot candidate on the page.
4. John 13:30 missing from the Judas pericope; the high-tide exclusion caps the exact page this episode needs.
5. A rule silently vanished: v2 had "OT episodes cap the swirl at Stage 1-2." v3's condensed system section dropped it without saying so.
6. Motif-balance line sums to 14 of 15 (Nazareth unclassified).

## Verdict

v3 is a real improvement, not cosmetics — the collision audit now survives independent re-checking, and the Judas rebuild is the strongest single idea in either version. But the scrutiny went to the patches, not the three new elements (Sheba, Nain, Emmaus) — each carries exactly the class of defect the fixed elements were flagged for. One more pass over the new rows recommended before lock.
