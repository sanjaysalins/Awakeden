# Swirls of Life — the whole Bible in living ink (v2, full restart)

JesusInTheBible · series design · 2026-08-21 · supersedes `SWIRLS_OF_LIFE_SERIES_PLAN.md`

> **Why this exists.** The v1 plan (2026-08-20/21) built its 15-episode slate around stories this project had already told elsewhere — checked and found 8 of 15 already produced, some 3-4 times over, including two full existing long-form films. The user's explicit call: drop every story already made anywhere in this catalog, in any style, and rebuild the slate from zero — nothing carried over, not even the pieces this format had already validated (The Well, The Hem, Doubting Thomas, the Storm test). This document is that rebuild. Every one of the 17 candidate passages below was checked against the full catalog (`_website/manifest.yaml`, `data/series.json`, the real narration folder, `longform/`, every `poc_*` directory) and confirmed clear — full verification detail available on request, not repeated here.

## Who this is for, and what it's trying to do (unchanged from v1 — this part wasn't the problem)

Swirls of Life is for the viewer who has quietly decided one specific thing about themselves disqualifies them — a failure, a status, a fear that won't quiet down. Every episode's dead-ink motif (see below) hands you that episode's own "one real person" for free: the motif IS the diagnosis. A repeat viewer gets a bonus second layer (learning to recognize the ink grammar across episodes) — but no single episode ever depends on prior viewing to land.

**Takeaway:** "the cut is the miracle — everything else is dosage." Whatever you're carrying has a name, and it leaves all at once, not gradually, the moment Jesus is present.

**What makes this series distinct, per the user's own words:** *"Jesus from every lens — old testament, new testament, every perspective of people, location, event... the most beautiful, most original, most unique way... never the feeling of heard that, seen that before... truly biblically grounded, never assuming anything not brought from thin air."* This is the actual design brief. Every pick below was chosen against that bar, not just against collision-clearance.

## The dead-ink system (unchanged mechanics from v1)

A blue-and-gold "living ink" swirl marks the gospel's presence, dosed Stage 0 (absent) → Stage 3 (diffused), page to page. Its counterpart, DEAD INK, falls as the swirl rises:
- **Stain** — sin/guilt/uncleanness soaked into the paper. Two sub-cases, never conflated: moral sin/guilt (Isaiah 1:18, Psalm 51:7), and ceremonial/Levitical uncleanness-as-barrier (Leviticus 15) — the second is never framed as personal guilt.
- **Fray** — the afflicted figure's own linework destabilized (fear/doubt, James 1:6, Matthew 14:31).

Both are dispelled only at hard cuts between pages, never within a clip. Never assign either motif to suffering the text frames as neither sin nor uncleanness (John 9:3 discipline). Never mark Christ with either motif. OT-type episodes cap the swirl at Stage 1-2; Stage 3 is reserved for pages where fulfilment in Christ is on-page.

## Cost model (same ledger-backed methodology as v1, unproven for this exact slate — no episode here has been rendered)

- **Short:** budget $20-46/episode (art/audio) + ~$5-6 narration tournament/panel/red-team, until 3-4 episodes ship clean and give a tighter number.
- **Long:** budget $50-95/episode, same caveat — zero ledger evidence for this format's own long-form yet.
- **Total season-one estimate:** 14 shorts (~$280-644 + ~$70-84 narration) + 2 longs (~$100-190) ≈ **$450-920** for the full slate, before any regen overruns. Get explicit `/cost` sign-off before each build, not just once at plan-commit.

## Build order (proposed, first build spends nothing beyond one still)

Sequenced to prove the newest, least-tested things first — every episode here is unbuilt, so there's no "most-tested ground" to start on this time, only textual/doctrinal strength.

1. **Melchizedek** or **Talitha Cumi** — pick whichever the user wants to see first; both are simple, single-scene, low-risk starting points to re-prove the still+animation template on completely new content.
2. **Nathanael + Jacob's Ladder pairing** — proves the new crossing-arc device (Jesus's own words linking old to new) works visually before committing to the full Nazareth-sermon long-form.
3. Remaining shorts, in whatever order best spreads Stain/Fray/OT/NT variety across the season.
4. **This Day Is This Scripture Fulfilled** (Luke 4) — the crossing-arc long-form, once several shorts have proven the grammar.
5. **The Name She Knew** (Joseph's tomb + Mary Magdalene) — the finale, built last, same reasoning as v1 (needs the season's own weight behind it).
