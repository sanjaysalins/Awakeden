# Swirls of Life — the whole Bible in living ink (v3)

JesusInTheBible · series design · 2026-08-21 · supersedes v2 pending final review

> **Why v3 exists.** v2 claimed a full catalog check and was wrong twice over: the finale ("The Name She Knew," Mary Magdalene at the tomb) directly overlapped an already-shipped, studio-complete piece (`the-empty-tomb`, `John 20:8`) that a verse-literal search missed; two OT/NT picks (Melchizedek, Walking on Water) turned out to be sitting in another series' planned backlog. A Fable creative pass and a 4/5 independent-review panel (2 REVISE, 2 FAIL) also caught: a real doctrine error (tagging the Syrophoenician woman's story "uncleanness" for being Gentile — backwards theology, Acts 10 exists to reverse exactly that framing), an episode (Martha) whose signature "hard cut" would have to be invented because the text records no resolution, a motif tag ("shame at being served") that fit neither defined Stain sub-case, and simple arithmetic errors (claimed 15 episodes, built 16). Full detail: `_independent_review/20260821-141811/` and the Fable critique referenced there. Every fix below responds to a specific finding, not a vibe.

## Who this is for, what it's trying to do (unchanged, this part passed review clean)

For the viewer who's quietly decided one specific thing disqualifies them. Every dead-ink motif hands you that episode's own "one real person" — the motif IS the diagnosis. Takeaway: "the cut is the miracle — everything else is dosage." Design brief, in the user's own words: *"Jesus from every lens... the most beautiful, most original, most unique way... never the feeling of heard that, seen that before... truly biblically grounded, never assuming anything not brought from thin air."*

## The dead-ink system (unchanged)

Swirl: blue-gold living ink, Stage 0→3. **Stain** — two sub-cases only, never a third: moral sin/guilt (Isaiah 1:18, Psalm 51:7), or ceremonial/Levitical uncleanness-as-barrier (Leviticus 15), never framed as guilt. **Fray** — fear/doubt only (James 1:6, Matthew 14:31). Never assign either to suffering the text frames as neither. Never mark Christ with either. A story can also be **swirl-only** (no dead ink at all) when forcing a diagnosis onto it would be the invention the brief forbids — that's a legitimate choice, not a gap.

## Build order — one full pilot short first, not "one still" **[FIX]**

v2's build order treated a single rendered still as a proof gate. Two reviewers correctly pointed out that's not a valid gate — it proves nothing about narration, motif readability, ref-chaining, animation, captions, or assembly. Revised gate:

1. **One complete pilot short, start to finish**, on genuinely new content — full page set (not one page), still + animation for every page, narration written and voiced, assembled through the `northstar_shortform/`-style script (per `PRODUCTION_PIPELINE.md`; swirls pages don't go through `cli_visual.py`), landing-hold checked. Candidate: **Talitha Cumi** or **The Ladder He Saw** — both single-location, small cast, no multi-ref complications.
2. Only after the pilot ships clean: sequence the remaining shorts, spreading Stain/Fray/OT-NT variety.
3. **This Day Is This Scripture Fulfilled** (#14) — after the grammar is proven on several shorts, and only after Naaman (#4) and the widow of Zarephath (#5) have themselves shipped, since the sermon names them — building the payoff before its own proof-texts exist would be backwards.
4. **The Road to Emmaus** (#15) — the finale, built last.
