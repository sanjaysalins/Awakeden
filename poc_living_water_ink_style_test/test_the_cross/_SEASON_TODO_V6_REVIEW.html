#12 — Ye Are Not All Clean

John 13:1–30 — Jesus washes Judas's feet, knowing what's coming.

Who's it for: the person who's been "in church" for years and is quietly already gone.
What they take away: Jesus washed the feet of the man He knew would betray Him. Grace wasn't withheld — it was refused, with the towel still in the room.
Why this one, now: every other "unclean" episode this season clears by the end. This is the first one that doesn't — a real gap in what we've shown so far.

Why it's not a straight "go" yet: this style has a hard rule — the "stain" ink and the "living" ink must never visually touch. But foot-washing is Jesus literally touching Judas's feet. There's already a fix on paper (camera stays on the basin, Judas's own feet get a clean gap around them) — it just needs a quick page-by-page check before we spend money on art.

Where Art Thou?

Genesis 3:8–21 — Adam and Eve hide; God comes looking, and speaks a promise before a punishment.

Who's it for: the person hiding from God, assuming He's coming to condemn them.

Why this one: fills a real gap — plain "wrongdoing" hasn't been the motif yet, it's mostly been sickness/uncleanness. Written fresh for this style (a locked script on this same verse already exists elsewhere in the project — leaving it alone, not reusing it).
