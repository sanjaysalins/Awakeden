## The dead-ink system

Swirl: blue-gold living ink, Stage 0→3. **[FIX — a real rule dropped in v3's rewrite, restored]** OT-type episodes cap the swirl at Stage 1-2; Stage 3 diffusion is reserved for pages where fulfilment in Christ is on-page (Hebrews 10:1).

**Stain** — two sub-cases only, never a third: moral sin/guilt (Isaiah 1:18, Psalm 51:7), or ceremonial/Levitical uncleanness-as-barrier (Leviticus 15), never framed as guilt. **Fray** — fear/doubt only (James 1:6, Matthew 14:31) — a story only earns this tag if the text itself uses fear/doubt vocabulary; grief, anxiety, and shame are NOT Fray by default (this is the exact mistake that cut Martha in v3, and — caught this round — nearly recurred on the widow of Nain, see below).

**High-tide exclusion** (existing rule, `NORTH_STAR_PROMPT.md`): both motifs wet-and-big never share a page — `stainDose + swirlStage <= 4`. Zone/chromatic/form/substrate separation locks apply whenever Stain and swirl do share a page.

A story can be **swirl-only** (no dead ink) when forcing a diagnosis would be invention. Swirl-only does NOT mean swirl-absent — the living ink can still appear, marking Christ's presence, without a Stain or Fray diagnosis attached to anyone.

### Long-form (2)

| # | Episode | Text | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | This Day Is This Scripture Fulfilled | Luke 4:16-30 | LONG, 7 movements, 16:9 | Jesus's first sermon: reads Isaiah 61, declares it fulfilled, names Naaman (#4) and the widow who fed him in famine (#5) as proof grace has always crossed to outsiders — the hometown crowd tries to throw Him off a cliff. Swirl reaches Stage 3 the instant He says "fulfilled," then — the season's one page where a human crowd, not Christ's own presence, acts on the ink — is pushed toward the frame's edge by their rising hostility; never dies, never dims; holds steady as He passes through the midst of them untouched (John 1:11). **[FIX — named, not left undiagnosed]** the crowd's own doubt ("is not this Joseph's son?") deliberately gets no Fray tag: they're a corporate hostile force on this page, not an individual "one real person" this episode is naming — a stated choice, not an oversight. |
| 15 | The Road to Emmaus | Luke 24:13-35 | LONG, 7 movements, 16:9, season finale | **[FIX — real motif design, v3 had none]** Two disciples walk for miles with the risen Jesus and don't recognize Him — "their eyes were holden" (24:16) — the one long stretch in the whole season where Jesus is fully present and the Fray does NOT clear on contact, a second designed exception to the tagline alongside Judas's unresolved Stain. Design: the *viewer* sees the swirl at Stage 2-3 walking beside them the entire road, visible to us, invisible to the two disciples, whose own linework stays frayed throughout — dramatic irony, not a continuity gap. The fray dies, all at once, at the breaking of bread (24:31, "their eyes were opened, and they knew him"); he vanishes from their sight the same instant — but the swirl, for the first time all season, **stays** after he's gone ("did not our heart burn within us," 24:32). Closes the season on Luke 24:27 in His own mouth: "beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them... the things concerning himself" — the series' mission statement, spoken by Him. |
