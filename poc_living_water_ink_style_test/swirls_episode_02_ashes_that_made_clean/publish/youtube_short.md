## TITLE
Why Touching the Dead Made You Unclean for a Week

## DESCRIPTION
Numbers 19. Touch a dead body and the law called you unclean for seven days.

"He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days." (Numbers 19:11, KJV)

To be made clean again, you needed the water of separation: a red heifer burned to ash, mixed with water, and sprinkled on you by someone clean.

You do not have to have sinned to feel unclean. Living close to death can leave a stain water alone cannot reach. Hebrews 9:13 says the ashes sprinkled on the unclean did something real but limited: they purified the flesh. Then it asks how much more the blood of Christ can "purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God" (Hebrews 9:14, KJV)

Jesus touched what makes us unclean, like the bier at Nain (Luke 7:14), and did not become unclean. Ready to let Him make you clean?
