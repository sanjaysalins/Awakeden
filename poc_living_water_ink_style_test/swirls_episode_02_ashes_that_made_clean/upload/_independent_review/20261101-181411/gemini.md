# Independent review — gemini (OK, 46s)

The upload kit correctly quotes the KJV verbatim, avoids clickbait, and sets up a doctrinally sound, grace-anchored hook without manufacturing fear. However, it fails on platform-specific formatting and drops the required spiritual CTA on secondary platforms.

Here are the specific findings:

- **Missing CTA-to-Jesus on TikTok/Facebook/Instagram:** The YouTube description rightly includes the spiritual CTA ("Ready to let Him make you clean?"). However, TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram abruptly end at the doctrinal point ("He touched what makes us unclean and did not become unclean") and jump straight to the brand subscribe line, completely dropping the CTA-to-Jesus required by the brand rules.
- **Format Copy-Paste Error (Platform Fit / SEO Honesty):** The artifact header explicitly marks this as `Format: short`, yet the kit generates a full `YouTube (Long-form 16:9)` section. Worse, that long-form section is a 100% copy-paste of the Short section, meaning it incorrectly includes `bible shorts` in its tags—which is factually wrong and bad for SEO if this were ever actually uploaded as a long-form video.
- **Punctuation (Facebook):** The Facebook description is missing a period after the citation: `"purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God" (Hebrews 9:14, KJV) He touched...`

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Restore the CTA-to-Jesus ("Ready to let Him make you clean?") to the TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram descriptions so they end on an invitation, not just a fact.
2. Remove the "YouTube (Long-form 16:9)" section entirely if this is only a Short, or at minimum remove the `bible shorts` tag from the 16:9 block to prevent tag-stuffing.
3. Add the missing period after `(Hebrews 9:14, KJV)` in the Facebook description.
