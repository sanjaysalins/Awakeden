# Independent review — claude (OK, 44s)

**Findings**

**Honesty and accuracy (YouTube Short, YouTube Long-form, TikTok, Instagram)**
- "By nightfall the law calls him unclean" is invented. Numbers 19:11 says seven days. It does not say "by nightfall". This is an embellishment in the opening hook.
- "He could not cleanse himself" is overstated. Numbers 19:19 has the unclean person wash himself and his clothes on day 7. What he could not do was supply the ashes or sprinkle himself. Rephrase it as "He could not cleanse himself *without* the ashes".
- "Hebrews says those ashes could only point forward" goes further than Hebrews 9:13. That verse says the ashes "sanctifieth to the purifying of the flesh", and it contrasts them with Christ's blood. Add the verse, or soften the claim.
- "You do not have to have sinned to feel unclean" blurs two things: ceremonial uncleanness under the law, and a felt moral stain. The video may intend the link, but "feel unclean" sits awkwardly beside "the law calls him unclean".
- The red-team called the "touched what makes us unclean" claim "not cited." Add Luke 7:14 or Mark 1:41 to the long and Facebook captions. It is a doctrinal claim and the Short is the only place it can be sourced.

**KJV**
- Both quotes are exact: Numbers 19:11 and the Hebrews 9:14 fragment. The closing "?" is dropped from the Hebrews quote, which is fine. Add a period or comma between the closing quote and "(Hebrews 9:14, KJV)" in the YouTube and Facebook text.

**Platform fit**
- The kit has a "YouTube (Long-form 16:9)" block, but the Format line says "short". It has the same title and description as the Short, with fewer hashtags (3 versus 5). Either this is a mislabeled duplicate, or it promises a long-form video that does not exist. Delete it, or give it real long-form copy.
- The YouTube Short has no `#Shorts` hashtag and no "Short" signal.
- TikTok and Instagram open with "Numbers 19." That is a citation, not a hook. Open with the stake instead, for example "Touch a dead body and you were unclean for seven days."
- The TikTok and Instagram captions drop the "Ready to let Him make you clean?" CTA and end only on "Follow". The CTA-to-Jesus is thinner on exactly those two platforms. "Christ" appears, but no invitation does.
- The "Follow: YouTube: @awakeden (https://youtube.com/@awakeden)" line is redundant inside a YouTube description. It is also repeated on Facebook.

**SEO and hashtags**
- `#RedHeifer` and the `red heifer` tag are a mixed signal. Today that search term mostly draws Temple Institute and end-times prophecy audiences, who are not the people this typology video is for. The tag is honest to the content, but it is a risk for audience fit. Keep the tag for the topic, but think about dropping the hashtag on the Short.
- The tag list is thin on the intent behind "unclean", with nothing like "purification", "ashes" or "Jesus cleanses". "Swirls of life" as a tag is a brand term nobody searches for.

**Titles**
- The chosen title ("Why Touching the Dead Made You Unclean for a Week") is honest but flat. It points at nothing and never mentions Jesus, so the curiosity gap closes in the title itself. Option 3, "You Don't Have to Sin to Feel Unclean", is the stronger hook, and the video does deliver on it.
- Options 2 and 4 are templated "The X That Y" shapes.

**Gate and lint contradiction**
- UK-G7 says "plain ASCII", but every block contains the non-ASCII "✝" glyph. Either the gate is wrong or the copy is. A gate that passes that is not checking what it claims.

**Brand**
- The footer is intact and the KJV attribution is present. The CTA is the same "Ready to…" shape the red-team already flagged as repetitive. The kit shipped it anyway.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the invented and overstated claims: "by nightfall", "could not cleanse himself" (see Numbers 19:19), and "could only point forward". Cite Hebrews 9:13 and Luke 7:14 or Mark 1:41 for the load-bearing Christ claim.
2. Remove or properly differentiate the "YouTube (Long-form 16:9)" block, which duplicates a Short. Add `#Shorts` to the Short. Reopen the TikTok and Instagram captions with a stake instead of "Numbers 19.", and add a short Jesus CTA to both.
3. Use the stronger hook as the title ("You Don't Have to Sin to Feel Unclean"). Fix the "plain ASCII" lint conflict with "✝". Reconsider `#RedHeifer` for audience fit.
