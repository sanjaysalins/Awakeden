Numbers 19. A man touches a dead body. Not sin, just closeness to a grave. By nightfall the law calls him unclean.

"He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days." (Numbers 19:11, KJV)

He could not cleanse himself. Someone else had to burn a red heifer to ash and mix it with water, and that person became unclean too, just for making it.

You do not have to have sinned to feel unclean. Living close to death can leave a stain water alone cannot reach. Hebrews says those ashes could only point forward, to the blood of Christ who can "purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God" (Hebrews 9:14, KJV)

He touched what makes us unclean and did not become unclean, so that we could finally be clean. Ready to let Him make you clean?
