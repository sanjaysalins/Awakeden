A man touches a dead body — not sin, just proximity to a grave — and by nightfall, the law calls him unclean.

He cannot cleanse himself. Someone else must burn a heifer to ash, mix it with water — and becomes unclean too, just for making it.

Scripture says: "He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days... he shall purify himself with it on the third day, and on the seventh day he shall be clean."

You don't have to have sinned to feel unclean. Living close to death — a grave you can't stop visiting — can leave a stain water alone can't reach.

Hebrews says the ashes could only point forward: "How much more shall the blood of Christ... purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God?"

He touched what makes us unclean, and did not become unclean — so we could finally be.

Ready to let Him make you clean?
