## TITLE
She Fed a Stranger Before She Fed Her Own Dying Child

## DESCRIPTION
1 Kings 17: a widow was gathering sticks for her own funeral fire. She had one handful of meal and a little oil left, enough for one last meal before she and her son starved.

A stranger passing through, Elijah, asked her for water. Then bread. She told him plainly: "I have not a cake, but an handful of meal in a barrel, and a little oil in a cruse."

He answered: "Fear not; go and do as thou hast said: but make me thereof a little cake first, and bring it unto me, and after make for thee and for thy son."

She fed the stranger first. "The barrel of meal shall not waste, neither shall the cruse of oil fail, until the day that the LORD sendeth rain upon the earth."

God did not need her barrel full. He needed it open.
