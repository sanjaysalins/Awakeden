Bake-off v2 (after the panel)

5 blind seats read my first design. All 5 said it could mislead you. This is the fixed version. Still design only: nothing built, nothing spent.

What was wrong
  • Each arm changed 3–4 things. A win could not be traced.
  • "2 of 3 layers" let cheap numbers and AI seats outvote real viewers.
  • "If control wins, stop" could lock 18 episodes on no real data.
  • Ep 8 is the cleanest film and may not even have a long title card.
  • "Hook spoken at once" changes nothing. The narrator already speaks at 0.1 s. The real variable is the title card over the picture.
