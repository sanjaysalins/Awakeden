2. A great bake-off: the rules

  1. One story, same words. Hold the narration, voice, score and art constant. Only the format changes.
  2. Write the scoring before looking. Metrics and the win rule are fixed first.
  3. Three layers, not one. Numbers + blind panel + real viewers. A win needs 2 of 3.
  4. Paired, not pooled. Compare arms of the same story. Never compare different stories.
  5. Error bars. AI viewers are simulated. Real viewers are few. Say "likely", never "proved".
  6. Cost and your time count. A winner that doubles your hours is not a winner.

5. Win rule (fixed now)

  • An arm wins if it beats Control in 2 of 3 layers, and no one loses who/what/why.
  • Ties go to the cheaper and faster arm to build.
  • If Control wins, we stop changing the format and say so.
  • If seats disagree, we report the split. No averaging it away.
