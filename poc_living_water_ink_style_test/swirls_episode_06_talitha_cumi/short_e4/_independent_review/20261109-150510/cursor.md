## Adversarial review

### Doctrinal soundness
Mostly faithful to Mark 5:35–43 (Jairus’s daughter): death report, “Be not afraid, only believe,” scorn at the house, hand taken, Aramaic + KJV English, resurrection, command to feed. **“Only the living eat”** is inference from Jesus’s post-resurrection meal command—not invented theology. No contrarian readings.

**Skeptical flag:** The piece never names **who** spoke the first line (synagogue messengers). A zero-Bible listener may think the **father** got two messages both framed as oracle-like, without knowing the first is **bad news from household servants**, not Jesus.

**Routing / integrity nit (production, not spelling):** Jesus speaks only **“Talitha cumi.”** The KJV English of the same utterance is in **NARRATOR** voice as a “quote.” That splits one Jesus sentence across voices; a purist KJV+multi-voice check may want the full dominical line on **JESUS** or narrator-only framing.

**Non-quote:** *“give her something to eat”* paraphrases Mark 5:43 (*“he commanded that something should be given her to eat”*)—acceptable as narration, not presented as KJV.

### Clarity on first hearing
Strong beats: death → don’t fear → laugh → hand → arise → eat. Paraphrases (**“Why bother Him now?”**, **“Little girl, get up.”**) help global listeners.

**Logic snag:** Hook: **“Two messages about his daughter.”** You then give **Jesus: “Be not afraid, only believe.”** before the house—that’s another **word to the father about the same crisis**, not folded into the “two messages” payoff. Payoff pairs only **dead** vs **feed her**. Attentive listeners can hear **three** father-facing words, not two.

**“At the house, people laughed at Him.”** Who “people” are (mourners/wailers) is unstated—minor blur, not fatal.

### Grace-anchored conviction
**“Be not afraid, only believe”** is Christ’s invitation, not scare tactics. No gain/loss or works salvation pitch. **“Only the living eat”** is observational closure, not “do X or else.”

**Gap:** No beat that **extends belief/arise/life to the listener**—conviction stays on the damsel, not graciously pointed outward.

### Freshness = faithful depth
The **feed-her-after-arise** detail is text-true and underused in shorts; **“Only the living eat”** is a legitimate pay-off from that command. Orthodox, not clickbait.

### One thread (hook → middle → CTA)
Spine is mostly **death sentence vs life sentence (eat)**. **“Two messages”** is the hook thread; **“only believe”** sits on the spine but **isn’t counted** in the closing binary—mild thread wobble, not a full swap.

It could loosely fit other **life-vs-death** pieces, though the **eat** tie is distinctive. For your lens (bones + Christward close), it’s strong prose, weak **gospel-short landing**.
