NARRATOR: One father got two messages about his daughter that day. The first: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother Him now?

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: At the house, people laughed at Him. Jesus took the parents in and held her hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She stood and walked. Then the second message, from Jesus: give her something to eat. The first said it was over. The second said: feed her. Only the living eat.
