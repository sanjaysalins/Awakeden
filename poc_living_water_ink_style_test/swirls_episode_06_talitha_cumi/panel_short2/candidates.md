# Talitha Cumi SHORT v2 - gap = "What do you say to a father told 'too late'?"
Two blind seats (Fable, Opus) CONVERGED on the last line "He went further" (turns the messenger's "any further", Mark 5:35, around). Note: the KJV gloss "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise" stays in NARRATOR voice (it is Mark's translation, not Jesus's words).

## D1 - ~30-33s (70 words)
NARRATOR: A father begged Jesus to come. His daughter was dying. Then the news: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Too late. Why bother Him? What do you say to a father who hears that? Jesus answered the father first.
JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."
NARRATOR: He took her hand.
JESUS: "Talitha cumi."
NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She stood. Why bother Him any further? He went further.

## D2 - ~40s (90 words)
NARRATOR: A father fell at Jesus's feet: help my dying girl. Jesus came. But the news came faster: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Too late. Why bother Him? What do you say to a father who hears that? Jesus heard. He answered the father first.
JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."
NARRATOR: At the house, they laughed at Him. He took her hand.
JESUS: "Talitha cumi."
NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She stood and walked. Why bother Him any further? He went further.
