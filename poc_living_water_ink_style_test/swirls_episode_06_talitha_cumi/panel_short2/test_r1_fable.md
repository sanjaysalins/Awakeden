# Blind review r1 — Fable seat (D1 vs D2 only)

## 1. Newcomer / ESL read
**D1** — Who: a father, his dying girl, Jesus. What: men say she is dead, Jesus says "only believe", takes her hand, she stands. Why: Jesus did not stop at "too late".
Unclear lines: "answered the father first" (first before what?). "He went further" — an ESL ear may take it only as "walked further" (still true, but the turn is half-heard).
**D2** — Who/what/why: same, clearer. "Fell at Jesus's feet" and "the news came faster" are vivid and plain.
Unclear: "they laughed at Him" — who is "they"? Same "went further" risk.
Both gloss every KJV line at once ("Too late. Why bother Him?", "Little girl, get up") — right pattern.

## 2. Curiosity gap
"What do you say to a father who hears that?" opens it plainly; "Be not afraid, only believe" pays it; "She stood" pays the second question (does believing matter?).
D1: 7/10. D2: 8/10 — the laughter re-opens the gap once more, so "further" has two obstacles to pass.

## 3. Ending
Specific and earned: the closing word is the piece's own KJV word, set up in line 3. Not stock — a real change from "come to Him" endings.
D1: 7/10 (thin middle, one obstacle). D2: 8/10.
"He went further" lands, but it is 3 words at the very end. Spoken fast it gets swallowed. Needs a clear half-beat before it.

## 4. Hook / pace
Hook: D1 6/10 (story-start, calm). D2 7/10 (father on the ground, "help my dying girl" is urgent).
Pace: both ~130 wpm, 8/10 comfortable.

## 5. Doctrine
Sound, narrative only, no promise, no fear or gain. "Only believe" is spoken to the father, not generalised to the viewer. Agency stays with Jesus ("He went further"). Lands on Jesus. No explicit invitation — the viewer-turn is only implied by "What do you say to a father who hears that?" Acceptable for a 35s panel; do not bolt on a stock CTA.
"Any further → He went further": fair. Jesus literally went further (to the house, into the room, 5:38-40) and did more than asked (raised, not healed). It is a play on the KJV English, not the Greek, but the script makes no Greek claim.
KJV: 5:35, 5:36, 5:41 all verbatim. "Damsel... arise" correctly in NARRATOR voice. "Laughed at Him" is a fair gloss of 5:40.

## 6. Rank + verdict
**1st: D2 — PASS** (fixes optional)
1. "the mourners laughed at Him" — name "they".
2. Mark a pause before "He went further" (direction, not words).
3. Cut "first" or keep "Jesus heard. He answered the father." — "first" puzzles a newcomer.

**2nd: D1 — REVISE (minor)**
1. Add the laughter beat; without it the callback has only one obstacle.
2. Add "Jesus heard." before "He answered the father" (or drop "first").
3. Same pause before the last line.
