# Seat: Fable (blind) — Talitha Cumi, SHORT narration

Passage: Mark 5:21-24, 35-43 (KJV). Jairus POV only. Read: _CARD.md only.

---

## VERSION A — 70 words (~33 s at 2.1 w/s)

NARRATOR: A father begged Jesus to come. His daughter was dying.
"Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?"
Too late. Stop bothering Him.
What do you say to that father?
Jesus heard, and answered the father first.

JESUS: Be not afraid, only believe.

NARRATOR: He took her hand.

JESUS: Talitha cumi. Damsel, I say unto thee, arise.

NARRATOR: Little girl, get up. She did.
Don't trouble Him further. He went further.

Word count: 70.

---

## VERSION B — 90 words (~43 s at 2.1 w/s)

NARRATOR: A father begged Jesus to come. His daughter was dying. Jesus came. The news came faster:
"Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?"
Too late. Don't bother the Teacher.
What do you say to that father?
Jesus heard, and answered the father first.

JESUS: Be not afraid, only believe.

NARRATOR: At the house, they laughed. He took her hand.

JESUS: Talitha cumi. Damsel, I say unto thee, arise.

NARRATOR: Little girl, get up. She walked. He said: give her food.
They said not to trouble Him any further. He went further.

Word count: 90.

---

CURIOSITY GAP: "What do you say to a father who has just been told 'too late'?" — opened by the cut from "dying" to "Thy daughter is dead," then asked out loud.
PAYOFF: Mark 5:36 — Jesus heard the news and answered the FATHER first ("Be not afraid, only believe"); the hand and "Talitha cumi… arise" is the climax that proves He meant it, and the last line turns the messengers' own "any further" into His answer: He went further.
