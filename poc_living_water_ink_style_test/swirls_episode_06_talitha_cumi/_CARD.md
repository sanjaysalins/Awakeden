# Talitha Cumi - Episode card (plan only, $0)

Passage: Mark 5:21-24, 35-43 (KJV). Jairus-POV ONLY. Motif: Fray (fear / "too late").
Status: WORDS NOT WRITTEN. Nothing paid. Nothing recorded.

## 3 questions (house rule)
- WHO: the person who asked for help, and it got worse while they waited.
- TAKEAWAY (a change, not a fact): "Too late" is not the end of the story to Jesus. He heard it, said "Be not afraid," and went on in.
- INTENTION: the "too late" person is not yet in the season. Hook = Mark keeps the Aramaic: Talitha cumi.

## Hard rules for the words
- Jairus only. The bleeding woman = ONE half-line at most ("someone touched Him"), never her story, never named as unclean.
- Keep "Be not afraid, only believe" (5:36) as Jesus's own words, KJV verbatim. Not a threat, not a deal ("believe or else").
- "Talitha cumi" is the hook AND must pay off (KJV gives its meaning: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise").
- Grace, no pressure. No gain/loss framing. Do not promise every dead person will be raised today.
- Simple global English. No obscure words as the load-bearing word ("damsel", "straitly", "ado" = quote once at most).
- Lands on Jesus. Earned landing from the piece's own words (e.g. "arise", "hand", "give her to eat") - not a stock closer ("come to Jesus").
- 59 s target, about 150-175 words natural speed. Narrator + Jesus voice for the quoted lines (multi-voice gate G9).
- Facts to keep true: she was 12 (5:42); Jesus took only Peter, James, John and the parents (5:37, 40); the mourners laughed (5:40); He told them to give her food (5:43).
