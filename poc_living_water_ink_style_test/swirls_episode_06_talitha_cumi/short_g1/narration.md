NARRATOR: A father asks Jesus for help. Then the news comes: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Too late. Why bother Him now? Jesus heard it.

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: He did not turn back. He went into the room with her father and mother, and took her by the hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She stood and walked. "Too late," they had said. Jesus heard it too. He kept going.
