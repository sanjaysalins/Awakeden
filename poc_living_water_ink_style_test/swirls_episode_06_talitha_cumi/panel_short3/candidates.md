# Talitha Cumi SHORT v3 - gap = "two messages to one father"; ending resolves the FATHER
Two blind seats (Fable, Opus) converged: drop "went further"; father is taken INTO the room (5:40), sees her stand and walk (5:42), then the ordinary job (5:43 "give her something to eat"). Frame from Opus, ending from Fable.

## E1 (~80 words)
NARRATOR: One father got two messages that day. The first: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother Him now?

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: At the house, people laughed. Jesus took the parents in and held her hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: Mark tells us: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She stood and walked. Her father could only stare. The second message: give her something to eat. That, he could do.

## E2 (~80 words) - same, Opus ending
(identical to E1 up to "The second message: give her something to eat.") then: "First: it's over. Last: she's hungry."
