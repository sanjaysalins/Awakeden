NARRATOR: One father got two messages that day. The first: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother Him now?

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: At the house, people laughed. Jesus took the parents in and held her hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She stood and walked. Her father could only stare. Then the second message: give her something to eat. The first one said it was over. Jesus had the last word.
