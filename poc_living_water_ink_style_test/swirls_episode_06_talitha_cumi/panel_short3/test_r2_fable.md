# E3 blind review (Fable, r2)

**1. Resolved?** Mostly. The two-message loop closes; "give her something to eat" is an earned, ordinary payoff. Soft spots: "Her father could only stare" is passive filler; "Jesus had the last word" is a stock tagline — half-rescued by the literal message/word frame, but the one line every viewer has heard before, sitting 13 words after the concrete payoff, diluting it. Safe only in its last sentence.

**2. Gap.** "Two messages" opens at word 5; the second is withheld to the end, lands ordinary and surprising. 8/10. Wobble: a newcomer counts "Be not afraid, only believe" as message two.

**3. Doctrine.** Sound. Grace-anchored; no outcome promise. No fear or gain/loss. Lands on Jesus. KJV 5:35, 5:36, 5:41 verbatim. 5:43: command given to those in the room, father included (5:40) — compression, not false. Omits "not dead but sleepeth" (5:39), so the laugh has no cause.

**4. Newcomer.** Who: a father whose little girl died; Jesus. What: told "dead, stop bothering Him"; Jesus goes anyway, raises her, says feed her. Why: Jesus' word outranks the death notice. Unclear: "people laughed" — at whom? "Talitha cumi", "troublest" glossed well.

**Scores:** hook 8 · gap 8 · resolved ending 7 · pace 8.

**5. VERDICT: REVISE (light, words only; near PASS).**

1. Hook: "One father got two messages **about his daughter** that day."
2. "At the house, people laughed **at Him**." (5:40)
3. Replace "Her father could only stare. Then the second message: give her something to eat. The first one said it was over. Jesus had the last word." with:
   "Then the second message: give her something to eat. The first said: it's over. The second said: feed her. The dead don't eat. Jesus had the last word."
   (Too blunt? "Only the living eat.")
