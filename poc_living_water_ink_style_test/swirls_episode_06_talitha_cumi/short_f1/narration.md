NARRATOR: One father heard two voices that day. The first: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother Him now?

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: Jesus brought her father and mother into the room, and took her by the hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She stood and walked. Then He said: give her something to eat. One voice said it was over. Jesus said, arise. Listen to Him.
