# Seat: Sonnet (newcomer read)

## P
1. WHO: A father, Jairus, and Jesus. WHAT: Jairus's daughter dies while Jesus is delayed; Jesus raises her with two words. WHY: Even when it seems "too late," Jesus still has the last word.
2. Re-read: "Damsel" (explained after). "Why do these two matter?" is vague at the start. "Spoken into a room full of 'too late'" is poetic and hard. "Jairus heard 'too late.' Jesus had the last word" is a bit jumbled.
3. Opening question answered: 7. Mostly earned, but the answer is abstract.
4. Score: 7.
5. False claims: none. "If it got worse while you waited, Jesus heard 'too late' and still went in" is a mild stretch from the text but fair.

## Q
1. WHO: Jairus, his daughter, and Jesus. WHAT: Told his daughter is dead, Jairus sees Jesus take her hand and say "Arise," and she lives. WHY: Jesus still has the last word after the worst news.
2. Re-read: "Damsel" (explained). "Jesus answers with two words" is a little odd, because the two words come later, but the hook is clearer. "A hand. 'Arise.' Then a meal." is clipped but works.
3. Opening question ("What do they say?"): 8. The hook opens with a concrete stake and is answered in the middle and the ending. Feels earned.
4. Score: 8.
5. False claims: "Jesus answers with two words" to the news is slightly off. In the text he first says "Be not afraid, only believe," and Talitha cumi comes later. This is a minor unfairness.

## Verdict
Q is best. Its hook is concrete (a father, a dead child) and its ending is short and does not overpromise. P's ending ("If it got worse while you waited...") promises a bit too much by addressing the viewer's own situation.
One fix: in Q, change the opening to "Jesus is told she is dead. Later he says two words," so the opening matches the order of events.
