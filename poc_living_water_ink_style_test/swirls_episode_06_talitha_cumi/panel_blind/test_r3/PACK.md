# Two narrations of one Bible story. Text only. Read each one cold.

## TEXT P
 NARRATOR: A man named Mark wrote a book about Jesus, in Greek. But here, he kept the sound of Jesus's own language. Two words: Talitha cumi. Why do these two matter?

 NARRATOR: A father, Jairus, falls at Jesus's feet. His little girl is dying. Jesus goes with him. A crowd is pushing close. Someone touches Jesus, and Jesus stops. Jairus has to wait.

 NARRATOR: Then men come from the house: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" In plain words: your daughter is dead. Why bother the Teacher now?

 NARRATOR: Too late.

 NARRATOR: Jesus hears it. He turns to the father.

 JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

 NARRATOR: At the house, people are crying loudly. Jesus says she is only sleeping. They laugh at Him. He sends them all out. Only the mother, the father, and Peter, James and John go in. He takes her by the hand.

 JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

 NARRATOR: Mark tells us what it means: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up.

 NARRATOR: She is twelve. She gets up and walks. And Jesus says: give her something to eat.

 NARRATOR: Here is why those two words matter. They were spoken into a room full of "too late." Jairus heard "too late." Jesus had the last word. A hand. "Arise." Then a meal.

 NARRATOR: If it got worse while you waited, Jesus heard "too late" and still went in.

## TEXT Q
NARRATOR: A father is told his little girl is dead. Jesus answers with two words. Mark, who wrote this book in Greek, kept them in Jesus's own language: Talitha cumi. What do they say?

NARRATOR: The father is Jairus. He falls at Jesus's feet. His little girl is dying. Jesus goes with him. A crowd is pushing close. A woman touches His clothes, and Jesus stops. Jairus has to wait.

NARRATOR: Then men come from the house: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" In plain words: your daughter is dead. Why bother the Teacher now?

NARRATOR: Too late.

NARRATOR: Jesus hears it. He turns to the father.

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: At the house, people are crying loudly. The girl is dead. Jesus says she is only sleeping, and they laugh at Him. He sends them all out. Only the mother, the father, and Peter, James and John go in. He takes her by the hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: Mark tells us what the words mean: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up.

NARRATOR: She is twelve. She gets up and walks. And Jesus says: give her something to eat.

NARRATOR: A hand. "Arise." Then a meal. Jairus had heard the worst news. Jesus still had the last word.
