# Seat: opus (blind, newcomer view)

## TEXT P
1. WHO: Jesus and a father, Jairus, whose daughter dies. WHAT: news comes that she is dead; Jesus goes in, takes her hand, says "Talitha cumi", she gets up and eats. WHY: Jesus speaks after "too late" and wins.
2. "Someone touches Jesus" - who? Why does that matter? Felt like a missing piece. "Thy... troublest thou" (but the plain gloss saved it). Last line "If it got worse while you waited..." - had to re-read; who is "you"?
3. Answered: 7/10. "Room full of too late" pays off "why do they matter" well. Earned, mostly.
4. Score: 7/10.
5. Fair to Mark 5. No false claim.

## TEXT Q
1. WHO: Jesus and Jairus and his dying girl. WHAT: she dies while Jesus is delayed; He raises her with two words and asks for food. WHY: Jesus has the last word over the worst news.
2. "Damsel" (glossed, fine). Nothing else hard. "A woman touches His clothes" is clearer than P.
3. "What do they say?" answered plainly: 8/10. Simple question, so easy to earn - but smaller payoff.
4. Score: 7.5/10.
5. Small one: the opening says Jesus "answers" the dead-news with these two words. In Mark 5:36 His answer to the news is "Be not afraid, only believe"; Talitha cumi is said later, to the girl.

## Verdict
Best: Q (clearer, no over-promise). Ending: P's last line promises too much - it suggests every "too late" in your life gets reversed. Q's ending is honest.
One fix: give Q P's stronger payoff line ("spoken into a room full of 'too late'"), and change Q's opening "Jesus answers" to "Jesus later says".
