Two strange words, kept in the Bible exactly as Jesus said them. Talitha cumi. Wait for what they mean.
Jairus fell at Jesus's feet. His little girl was dying. Jesus went with him, but the crowd was thick, and every step was slow.
Then men came from his house.
"Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?"
He had asked for help, and it got worse while he waited. Too late. Jesus heard it, and turned to him.
"Be not afraid, only believe."
Jesus took three friends and the parents. Inside, people were wailing. He said she was only sleeping. They laughed at him. He sent them out, went in, and took her hand.
"Talitha cumi."
Mark tells us what it means.
"Damsel, I say unto thee, arise."
Girl, get up. She stood, and she walked. She was twelve. Then Jesus said, give her something to eat.
Jairus heard "too late." Jesus had the last word. A hand. "Arise." Then bread.
