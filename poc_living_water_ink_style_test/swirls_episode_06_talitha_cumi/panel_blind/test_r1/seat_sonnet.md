# Seat: sonnet (newcomer read)

## X
1. WHO: Jairus, a father, and Jesus. WHAT: Jesus goes to heal Jairus's dying girl, is told "too late", and raises her anyway. WHY: Jesus can act even when it seems too late.
2. Unclear: "Mark kept two words in the exact sound" (who is Mark? which two words?), "Damsel", "troublest", "lay thy hands", "Master". The ending "Jesus had the last word. A hand. Arise. Then bread." is cryptic.
3. Opening question: 4/10. The two words are never explained as an answer to "why these two?" Not earned.
4. Overall: 5/10.
5. Fine to the text. "He did not turn back" is an inference not in the text.

## Y
1. Same story as X.
2. Same old words. "Those are the two words. Spoken into a room full of too late" helps me find the two words.
3. Opening question: 5/10. Names the two words, but "why" is still thin. Partly earned.
4. Overall: 6/10.
5. Same "did not turn back" inference. Direct claim "Jesus heard that too" is application, not text.

## Z
1. Same story, told in simple present tense.
2. "Damsel", "troublest", "Master". Opening is clearest: Greek vs Jesus's own language. "Last word. A hand." is a little cryptic.
3. Opening question: 6/10. Context is clear, answer is implied (a personal, tender moment), not stated. Better earned than X and Y.
4. Overall: 7/10.
5. Adds "she is only sleeping" and "people crying loudly", which are in Mark 5. Accurate.

## Best
Z. Clearest opening, plain verbs, details that help a newcomer.
One fix: after "Little girl, get up", add one plain line answering the opening: why Mark kept those two words (the exact voice of Jesus, as an eyewitness remembered it).
