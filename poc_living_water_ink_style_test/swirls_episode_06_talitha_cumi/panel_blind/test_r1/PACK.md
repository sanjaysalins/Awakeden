# Three narrations of one Bible story. Text only. Read each one cold.

## TEXT X
NARRATOR: In this story, Mark kept two words in the exact sound Jesus spoke them. Why these two?
NARRATOR: A father named Jairus fell at Jesus' feet. His little girl was dying. "Come and lay thy hands on her," he begged. And Jesus went with him.
NARRATOR: The crowd pressed in. Someone touched Him. He stopped.
NARRATOR: While Jairus waited, men came from his house. "Thy daughter is dead. Why troublest thou the Master any further?"
NARRATOR: Too late.
NARRATOR: Jesus heard it, and turned to Jairus.
JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."
NARRATOR: He took only Peter, James, John, and the parents. The mourners laughed at Him. He put them out. Then He took the girl's hand. She was twelve.
JESUS: "Talitha cumi."
NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. And she got up, and walked. Then He said: give her something to eat.
NARRATOR: If you asked for help, and it got worse while you waited, Jesus heard that too. He did not turn back. He went on in.
NARRATOR: Jairus heard "too late." Jesus had the last word. A hand. "Arise." Then bread.

## TEXT Z
NARRATOR: Mark wrote his book in Greek. But here, he kept the sound of Jesus's own language. Two words: Talitha cumi. Why keep those two?
NARRATOR: A father, Jairus, falls at Jesus's feet. His little girl is dying. Jesus goes with him. But the crowd is slow. Someone touches Him. They stop.
NARRATOR: Then men come from the house: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?"
NARRATOR: Too late.
NARRATOR: Jesus hears it. He turns to the father.
JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."
NARRATOR: At the house, people are crying loudly. Jesus says she is only sleeping. They laugh at Him. He sends them all out. Only the mother, the father, and Peter, James and John go in. He takes her by the hand.
JESUS: "Talitha cumi."
NARRATOR: Mark tells us what it means: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up.
NARRATOR: She is twelve. She gets up and walks. And Jesus says: give her something to eat.
NARRATOR: Jairus heard "too late." Jesus had the last word. A hand. "Arise." Then bread.
