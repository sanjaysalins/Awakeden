# Doctrine re-read — Fable seat (blind, narration.md only)

**1. Doctrine.** Sound. Last paragraph is grace-anchored: it repeats Jesus's own imperative to Jairus, promises no outcome (no "your dead will rise"), applies no fear or gain/loss pressure. One precision point: in v41 *Jesus* takes the dead girl's hand — she does nothing. "Take His hand" quietly flips the agency onto the viewer. Not an error (John 1:12 "received him"), but the story's own grace note is stronger the other way. Also fine: "she is only sleeping" is kept as Jesus's words while the narration states she is dead (Luke 8:53 agrees).

**2. KJV.** All three quotes verbatim (Mark 5:35, 5:36, 5:41). "Give her something to eat" and "she is twelve" are paraphrase, not framed as quotes. Clean.

**3. Timeline.** Dying → dead → two words: matches Mark's order. No confusion. Aramaic correctly called "Jesus's own language" vs Mark's Greek. Minor: "Mark, who wrote this book" — a newcomer does not know who Mark is or what "this book" is.

**4. Newcomer.** Who (Jesus, a father, his girl), what (raised with two words), why (fear → believe) all land. ESL-safe: "damsel" and "troublest" are both glossed. Invitation is clear. The unexplained woman is tolerable (her function = delay).
