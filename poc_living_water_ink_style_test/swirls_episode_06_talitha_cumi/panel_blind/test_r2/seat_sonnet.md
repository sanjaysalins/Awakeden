# Seat: Sonnet (blind, newcomer view)

## TEXT P
1. WHO: Jairus, his sick daughter, and Jesus. WHAT: Jesus is told the girl is dead, goes in anyway, takes her hand, and she gets up. WHY: Jesus can act even when people say it is too late.
2. Re-read: "Mark wrote his book in Greek... kept the sound of Jesus's own language" (who is Mark? why does language matter?). "Damsel" (explained after). "Jesus had the last word" is a bit abstract. "Then bread" is odd (earlier it was "something to eat").
3. Opening question ("Why do these two matter?"): only half answered, by the closing line. 5/10. Not very earned; the answer is implied.
4. Overall: 6/10.
5. No false claim. "Crowd is slow" is a soft reading of the text.

## TEXT Q
1. WHO: same. WHAT: same. WHY: the two words were spoken into "too late," and Jesus still went in, even for your own late situations.
2. Re-read: same opening about Greek. "A room full of 'too late'" is a little poetic. "Had the last word" idiom.
3. Question answered clearly with "Here is why those two words matter." 8/10. Felt earned, since the gap is closed directly.
4. Overall: 7.5/10.
5. "Jairus has to wait" and "Jesus heard 'too late' and still went in" are fair readings. The final "If it got worse while you waited" line is application, not text, but is gentle and not false.

## Best
Q is best.

## One fix
Replace the opening with a plain hook, for example "Who is Mark, and why keep two words in another language?" Or add one short line saying who Mark is. Newcomers stumble on the first sentence in both texts.
