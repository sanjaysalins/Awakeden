## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** a church-going mum of about 40 who scrolls Reels at night. She **stops** at Line 1, because a parent begging for a child is a strong opening. She **finishes** because Line 3 ("Too late") makes her ask "then what?" She **shares** it to a prayer group for someone waiting on a diagnosis.
**Not served:** a parent whose child actually died. Line 9 can sound like "believe and yours comes back too," and nothing in the film catches that. A sceptic is also not served, because the film never says why this man can beat death.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"When people say it's too late, Jesus doesn't stop. Listen to Him instead." Lines 3 and 8 carry it, and Line 9 states it. It comes through clearly. It is about Jesus not giving up. It never quite says *who He is*.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
**Who:** a desperate dad. **What:** his girl dies on the way, and Jesus raises her. **Why:** to show Jesus ignores "too late."
- **Where it slips:** Line 2's "why troublest thou the Master any further?" is hard for second-language viewers. The "Too late." box saves it.
- "Damsel" (Line 7) is glossed straight away, which is good.
- "Talitha cumi" is translated, which is good.
- **Visual problem:** Line 3 shows "followers behind" on the road. That clashes with 5:37 (see below).

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **Line 1:** "my little girl is dying." **RIGHT.** 5:23 says "My little daughter lieth at the point of death." Kneeling matches "fell at his feet" (5:22).
- **Line 2:** "On the road, the news comes." **RIGHT.** 5:24 says "Jesus went with him," and 5:35 says "While he yet spake." The quote is word for word. But the label "The messenger" is **WRONG on number**. KJV says "certain which said," which means more than one person.
- **Line 3:** "Too late." This is a fair reading of "why troublest thou the Master any further." "Jesus heard it" is **RIGHT** (5:36). "Did not turn back" is not stated, but 5:38 says "he cometh to the house," so it is **fairly inferred**.
- **Line 3 picture:** "followers behind" is **WRONG** if it shows a crowd. 5:37 says "he suffered no man to follow him, save Peter, and James, and John."
- **Line 4:** **RIGHT.** Word for word from 5:36, and spoken to the ruler.
- **Line 5:** "into the room with her father and mother" is **RIGHT but partial**. 5:40 also has "them that were with him," meaning the three disciples. The picture shows only the parents, so it **leaves them out**.
- **Lines 6-7:** **RIGHT.** 5:41 and 5:42 are word for word. "About 12" matches "the age of twelve years."
- **Line 8:** **RIGHT.** The bowl of food is a nice hidden nod to 5:43, "something should be given her to eat." The crying hug is artistic licence. It is fine next to "astonished with a great astonishment."
- **Line 9:** Is the application faithful? **Mostly yes.** It says "hear Him," not "you will get your miracle," and that is careful. But in 5:36 the command went to one man, with Jesus present and about to act. Applied to every "too late," it can **OVERCLAIM** for the bereaved. Fix: anchor it in who Jesus is (Lord over death), not in a promised result.
- **Biggest gap:** the film leaves out 5:39-40, "the damsel is not dead, but sleepeth" and "they laughed him to scorn." That is the text's *second* "too late" voice, and the film's own theme.

**Would I send it to my church group?** Yes, after fixing the label and the crowd on the road.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would use the mockers. "Too late," then "They laughed," and Jesus says "she is only sleeping." That doubles the theme and points to Christian hope over death.
**ONE highest-value change:** add one beat from 5:39-40 between Lines 4 and 5, and cut words from Line 8 to make room.
**Score: 7/10.**

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at **Line 2** and gets sharper at **Line 3**. Pays off at **Line 7**. **8/10.** Worth the wait: the crossed-out "Too late" is a strong visual promise, and the film keeps it.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **Line 2 label:** "The messenger" → **"Men from his house"**
2. **Line 3 picture:** show only **three disciples** behind Jesus and the father, not a crowd. Or change Line 5 to: **"He took her father and mother, and three disciples, and took her by the hand."**
3. **Line 9:** → **"If you hear that it is too late, hear Him instead. Even death obeys Him: 'Be not afraid, only believe.'"**
