## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Named viewer: Amara, 34, a nurse in Lagos, scrolling on a phone between shifts. She has a worry about a sick relative. She stops at Line 1 ("my little girl is dying") because a child in danger is understood in any language. She finishes because the girl gets up (Line 7) and the pictures are easy to read. She shares if someone she knows is afraid. Not served: a viewer who needs to know WHO Jesus is. The film never says. A skeptic who wants proof also gets nothing.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"When people say it is too late, listen to Jesus: do not be afraid, just believe." It is carried by Line 9 and the closing text box. It is clear as a slogan. It is not clear what "believe" means or believe in what.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
In my own words: a father asks a man called Jesus to save his dying daughter. Someone says she is dead. Jesus keeps walking, says "be not afraid", goes into the room, speaks, and the girl gets up. Then the film tells me to hear Jesus when I hear "too late".
Where I got lost or misled:
- Line 1: Who is Jesus? Why does the father beg HIM? I guessed: a healer, or a holy man. The film never says.
- Line 2: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" "Thy", "troublest" and "Master" are old words. I do not know who the Master is. I guessed it means Jesus.
- Line 3: "did not turn back" is clear. "Too late" is clear and strong.
- Line 4: "Be not afraid, only believe." Believe what? In whom? In that he can heal?
- Line 6: "Talitha cumi." I do not know this language. Line 7 translates it, which helps, but "Damsel" fails in translation.
- Line 7: "got up" is fine. Was she dead or asleep? The messenger said dead, so it seems she came back to life. It is stated only as a guess from the picture of her lying still.
- Line 8: "gone on" is a bit idiomatic but understandable.
- Line 9: "hear Him instead" is a nice turn. "Him" is Jesus, but I only learn that from the picture.
Words that fail: Damsel, thy, troublest, Master, Talitha cumi (until translated), "gone on".

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. What I was TOLD but could not follow: who Jesus is, why he can do this, what "believe" means, what "Mark 5:35" and "Mark 5:36" are (a book? a place? I guessed a source), and what "the Master" means.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer in the world would give me the one fact I am missing in the first seconds: who Jesus is and why a father would trust him, in one plain line. It would also say what "believe" means in this story (trust him even when it looks hopeless). The pictures, the strike-through of "Too late" and the silent hold at the end are strong craft.
ONE highest-value change: add a plain line in Line 1 or 3 stating who Jesus is for this viewer (for example "Jesus, a teacher people said could heal").
Score: 6/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at Line 1 (will the girl live?) and sharply at Line 3 ("Too late" and Jesus does not turn back). Pays off at Lines 6 and 7 (she gets up). The second gap, "believe what?", is only partly paid off at Line 9. Score: 7/10. Worth the wait: yes, at 54 seconds.
