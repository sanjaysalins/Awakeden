## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR (one named viewer; who stops, who finishes, who shares; who is NOT served)
Grace, 34, a churchgoer in Manila, scrolling at night while she waits on bad news. She stops at line 1 (a dying child in five seconds). She finishes, because line 2 makes it worse and line 3 promises a turn. She shares line 9 with a friend in trouble.
- Stops: parents, believers, anyone caught by the cross-out at line 3.
- Finishes: most who pass line 3. The story is simple and moves.
- NOT served: the total newcomer (the film never says who Jesus is or why He can do this) and the parent whose child did not get up (line 9 can sound like a promise it will always turn out this way).

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)
"When people say it is too late, Jesus says do not be afraid, only believe." Line 9 carries it, set up by line 3. Clear, yes. But thin: lines 3, 8 and 9 say the same thing three times, and none of them says WHY His word beats the news. "Only believe" has no object. Believe what? In whom?

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)
Who: a father, his girl, Jesus. What: she dies while they walk; Jesus goes on anyway and raises her. Why it matters: left for me to guess.
- Line 2, "why troublest thou the Master any further?" Heard, never shown, never explained. A second-language viewer loses three seconds. Line 3 "Too late." rescues it.
- Line 6, "Talitha cumi." No one says this is her own home language. A newcomer hears a magic word.
- Line 7, "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Third version of one sentence in a row. "Damsel" and "unto thee" fail in translation, and "Little girl, get up" lands straight after anyway.
- Line 6 picture: her eyes are already open at 25.3 s, before the words at 25.4 s finish. The miracle is shown before it is spoken.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
- Line 1 "my little girl is dying": RIGHT (Mark 5:23, fair paraphrase).
- Line 2 quote: RIGHT, word for word (Mark 5:35). Label "The messenger": slightly off for Mark, which has several people ("certain which said"); Luke 8:49 has one. Minor.
- Line 3 "Jesus heard it, and did not turn back": RIGHT (5:36-38). "Too late" is the film's summary, fair.
- Line 4: RIGHT, word for word (5:36).
- Line 5 "with her father and mother": RIGHT but incomplete. Peter, James and John also went in (5:37, 40). The picture shows parents only.
- Lines 6-7: RIGHT (5:41-42). Age 12 and the bowl of food (5:43) are right and a lovely touch.
- Line 8: RIGHT.
- Line 9 "If you hear that it is too late, hear Him instead": OVERCLAIMED as worded. The words were said to one father about one girl. Said to everyone with no anchor, it implies every "too late" gets reversed. The last picture (Jesus and father outdoors) is not in the text; harmless.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE (what would the best explainer in the world do differently; the ONE highest-value change; score 0-10 against that bar)
The best version would spend one line on who this is: the one for whom death itself is not too late. That gives "believe" an object, serves the newcomer, feeds the Bible reader, and moves the promise off my outcome and onto Him.
ONE change: line 8 is a recap of line 3. Swap it for the meaning line (fix 1 below). Same length.
Score: 7/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP (opens at line 2-3; pays off at line 6-7; score 0-10; worth the wait?)
Opens at line 3 (she is dead, He keeps walking: why?). Pays off at lines 6-7. Score 7. Worth the wait. The title gives away part of it, and the open eyes at 25.3 s spend the payoff a beat early.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES (line number + exact replacement wording)
1. Line 8: "They said it was over. For Jesus, even death was not too late."
2. Line 7: "In her own language: Little girl, get up. And she got up and walked." (Keep "Mark 5:41" on screen. Hold her eyes closed until "Talitha cumi" ends.)
3. Line 2 text box, add a second plain line under "Thy daughter is dead": "Why bother the Teacher now?"
