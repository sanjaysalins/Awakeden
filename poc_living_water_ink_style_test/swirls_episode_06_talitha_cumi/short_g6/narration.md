NARRATOR: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" A father heard that on the road. He had begged Jesus to come: my little girl is dying. Too late. Jesus heard it too, and did not turn back.

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: He went into the room with her father and mother, and took her by the hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She got up and walked. The news had said it was over. Jesus had heard it, and gone on. If you hear that it is too late, hear Him instead: "Be not afraid, only believe." Trust Him when hope has stopped.
