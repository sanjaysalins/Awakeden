# Independent review — gemini (OK, 62s)

**REVIEW FINDINGS**

**Doctrinal soundness**: PASS. Biblically faithful to Mark 5. It grounds Christ's authority over death and highlights His grace in ignoring the world's dismissive verdict. No invented doctrine.

**KJV verbatim**: PASS. Every quote ("Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?", "Be not afraid, only believe.", "Talitha cumi.", "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise.") is a 100% flawless match to Mark 5:35-41.

**Clarity on first hearing**: FAIL. There is a major auditory collision at the climax. The JESUS voice says "Talitha cumi," and then immediately the NARRATOR speaks in the first person: *"Damsel, I say unto thee, arise."* Without visual text, a listener will hear the narrator suddenly adopting the persona of Christ and issuing a command. You must include an auditory bridge (e.g., "Which means...") or assign the English translation to the JESUS voice actor to prevent confusion. 

**Grace-anchored conviction**: PASS. The transition from the narrative into the listener's life ("If someone has told you 'too late,' He heard that too") is deeply inviting. It relies entirely on the Spirit and the text for comfort, with zero works/fear framing.

**Freshness (faithful depth)**: PASS. Building the entire narrative around the messengers' interruption ("why troublest thou... too late") rather than just the miracle itself is a highly effective, fresh lens.

**One thread spine**: PASS. The thread of "too late" / "Jesus heard it" is established in line 2 and carried flawlessly into the final CTA. Excellent structural integrity.

**SCORE THE HOOK**: 8/10. It starts *in media res* and immediately establishes life-or-death stakes without relying on lazy templates. However, the pacing is slightly jarring ("A father begs Jesus... Then the news comes"). A tiny grounding phrase (e.g., "On the way, the news comes") would smooth the time jump and push this to a 9.

**SCORE THE LANDING**: 9/10. Superb. The closing application ("If someone has told you 'too late,' He heard that too") is entirely bespoke to this piece's unique thread. It is completely un-interchangeable with any other video, and seamlessly hands the final word back to Christ.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the Aramaic translation clarity: Either give the KJV English translation ("Damsel, I say unto thee...") to the JESUS voice, or have the narrator introduce it with "Which means, '...'" so the narrator doesn't sound like they are commanding the girl.
2. Cut the triple-repetition at the climax: You currently have Aramaic ("Talitha cumi") -> KJV English ("Damsel...") -> Modern English ("Little girl, get up"). It slows down the miracle's impact. Drop the modern paraphrase.
3. Smooth the hook's time jump: Add a brief spatial/temporal anchor (e.g., "On the way, the news comes") to help the listener track the scene change.
