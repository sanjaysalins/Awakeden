NARRATOR: A father's little girl is dying. Then the worst news comes: she is dead. Later, Jesus says two words to her. Mark kept them in Jesus's own language: Talitha cumi. What do they say?

NARRATOR: The father is Jairus. He falls at Jesus's feet. Jesus goes with him. A woman touches His clothes in the crowd, and Jesus stops. Jairus has to wait.

NARRATOR: Then men come from the house: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" In plain words: your daughter is dead. Why bother the Teacher now?

NARRATOR: Too late.

NARRATOR: Jesus hears it. He turns to the father.

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: At the house, people are crying loudly. Jesus says she is only sleeping, and they laugh at Him. He sends them out. Only her parents and three followers go in. He takes her by the hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: They mean: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up.

NARRATOR: She is twelve. She gets up and walks. And Jesus says: give her something to eat.

NARRATOR: A hand. "Arise." Then a meal.

NARRATOR: Jesus had said to the father: "Be not afraid, only believe." He said it to a man with the worst news. He says it to you. Let Him take your hand.
