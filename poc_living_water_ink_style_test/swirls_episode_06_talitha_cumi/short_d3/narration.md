NARRATOR: A father begged Jesus to come. His daughter was dying. Then the news: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Too late. Why bother Him? What do you say to a father who hears that? Jesus answered the father first.

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: The mourners laughed at Him. He took her hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She stood. Why bother Him any further? He went further.
