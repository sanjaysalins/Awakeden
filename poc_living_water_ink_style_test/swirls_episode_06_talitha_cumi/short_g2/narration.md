NARRATOR: A father begs Jesus: my little girl is dying. Then the news comes: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Too late. Why bother Him now? Jesus heard it.

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: He did not turn back. He went into the room with her father and mother, and took her by the hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She got up and walked. "Too late," they said. Jesus heard it, and took her hand anyway. If someone has told you "too late," He heard that too.
