# Blind review — Talitha Cumi short_g2 (Fable seat, round 1)

**Verdict: REVISE** — close; two wording fixes.

**1. Takeaway.** "Jesus heard it" lands for the girl AND for "you." The "He kept going" half reaches the girl ("did not turn back") but never reaches "you" — the last line stops at hearing. Half-delivered.

**2. Curiosity gap.** Opens at "Too late… Jesus heard it" (what will He do with that word?). Pays off at the hand and "arise." Works; mild rather than sharp, since the payoff is the known story.

**3. Ending.** "He heard that too" is honest, not preachy, no promise — but under-powered: it ends on a fact when the story's point is what He did AFTER hearing. Ends on the weak word "too." The recap "took her hand anyway" eight seconds after "took her by the hand" reads as a stumble: near-repeat, not exact echo.

**4. Doctrine.** Sound, grace-anchored, no outcome promise, no pressure, lands on Jesus. 5:35, 5:36, 5:41 verbatim. "Jesus heard it" is itself 5:36 ("As soon as Jesus heard the word") — well earned. No false claim. Minor: `"Too late," they said` puts a gloss in quote marks; harmless on audio.

**5. ESL.** Clean. Every archaic word is glossed at once (Damsel → little girl; troublest → why bother Him). Nothing fails.

**Scores:** hook 8 · gap 7 · ending 6 · pace 7

**Fixes (exact wording):**

1. `Jesus heard it, and took her hand anyway.` → `Jesus heard it, and kept walking.` (delivers "kept going", kills the repeat; Mark 5:24, 38)
