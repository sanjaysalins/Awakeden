## Version A (~30s) - 72 words

NARRATOR: Talitha cumi. Two strange words. Jairus begged Jesus to heal his dying daughter. Then a messenger came. "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother Him now? She was gone.

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: Inside, Jesus took her hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. Maybe you feel too late. He says to you:

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

## Version B (~40s) - 95 words

NARRATOR: Talitha cumi. Two strange words. Jairus ran to Jesus, begging for his dying daughter. A messenger met him: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother the Teacher now? She is gone. Jesus heard. He turned to the father.

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: In her room, He took her cold hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She was twelve. She stood up and walked. If it feels too late for you, hear what He told her father:

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

## Notes
Cut: bleeding woman, the crowd, the laughter, Peter/James/John, "give her food" (A also drops her age).
Newcomer may miss: who "the Master" is beyond context (Jesus), that the room scene is a short walk later, and that Talitha cumi is Aramaic, which I did not claim as unique to Mark.
