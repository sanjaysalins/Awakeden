NARRATOR: "Talitha cumi." Two words, in Jesus's own language, said to a dead girl. What do they mean?
Her father, Jairus, begged Jesus to come. His little girl was dying. Then the news: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother Him now?

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR: He took her hand.

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up. She stood up. He took a dead girl's hand. Let Him take yours.
