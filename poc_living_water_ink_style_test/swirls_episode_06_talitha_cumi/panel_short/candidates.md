NARRATOR: "Talitha cumi." Two words Jesus said to a dead girl. What do they mean?
Her father, Jairus, begged Jesus: come, she is dying. Then the news: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother Him now?
JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

## C2 - 40s (~100 words): Fable B spine + Opus hook
NARRATOR: "Talitha cumi." Two words Jesus said to a dead girl. What do they mean?
Jairus begged Jesus: come, my little girl is dying. On the way, someone touched Him. He stopped. Then a messenger: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother Him now? Jesus heard.
JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."
NARRATOR: At the house, He said she was sleeping. They laughed. He sent them out, went in, took her hand.
JESUS: "Talitha cumi."

JESUS: "Be not afraid, only believe."

## C3 - 40s (Opus B, laughter-gap structure, landing swapped to full line)
NARRATOR: Two words: "Talitha cumi." Before Jesus said them, people laughed. Why?
A father, Jairus, fell at His feet: my little girl is dying, come. The crowd pushed. Someone touched Him. Word came: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?" Why bother Him?

JESUS: "Talitha cumi."
