The words (Q)

NARRATOR Mark wrote his book in Greek. But here, he kept the sound of Jesus's own language. Two words: Talitha cumi. Why do these two matter?

NARRATOR A father, Jairus, falls at Jesus's feet. His little girl is dying. Jesus goes with him. A crowd is pushing close. Someone touches Jesus, and Jesus stops. Jairus has to wait.

NARRATOR Then men come from the house: "Thy daughter is dead: why troublest thou the Master any further?"

NARRATOR Too late.

NARRATOR Jesus hears it. He turns to the father.

JESUS "Be not afraid, only believe."

NARRATOR At the house, people are crying loudly. Jesus says she is only sleeping. They laugh at Him. He sends them all out. Only the mother, the father, and Peter, James and John go in. He takes her by the hand.

JESUS "Talitha cumi."

NARRATOR Mark tells us what it means: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." Little girl, get up.

NARRATOR She is twelve. She gets up and walks. And Jesus says: give her something to eat.

NARRATOR Here is why those two words matter. They were spoken into a room full of "too late." Jairus heard "too late." Jesus had the last word. A hand. "Arise." Then a meal.

NARRATOR If it got worse while you waited, Jesus heard "too late" and still went in.
