# Upload kit review: Jairus' daughter, 54 s Short (Mark 5)

The kit is mostly clean. Five quotes are exact KJV: Mark 5:35, 5:36, "Talitha cumi" (5:41), 5:42, and the film's "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." The titles are honest. The problems are small but real.

## Problems

**1. The Instagram share line can hurt grieving people.**
- "Send this to someone who has been told it is too late." A person whose child has a terminal diagnosis may read this as a promised healing.
- YouTube and Facebook guard against this with "Jesus did not promise that every story ends like this one." Instagram, the platform most built for sharing, leaves the guard out.
- Fix: add the guard, or re-aim the line at Jesus, not the outcome. For example: "Send this to someone who needs to hear: be not afraid, only believe."

**2. The AI disclosure is missing on all 4 platforms.**
- The house packaging rule asks for an honest AI disclosure once. None of the 4 files has one.
- YouTube's altered-content toggle doesn't replace a line in the copy.

**3. Two phrases claim more than the film delivers.**
- YouTube tag "mark 5 explained": the film retells the story, it doesn't explain it. Drop the tag or use "mark 5 bible story".
- "(Mark 5:35-43)" is given as what's covered. The film leaves out verses 37–40 (Peter, James and John, and the mourners laughing) and verse 43 (give her food). "Read it: Mark 5:21-24, 35-43" is fine as a reading link. Just don't present it as what the film covers.

**4. The words in quote marks don't match one standard.**
- YouTube: "in her own language 'Little girl, get up.'" The quote marks make a paraphrase look like Scripture, right beside real KJV.
- Better: *"Talitha cumi" — KJV: "Damsel, I say unto thee, arise." (Little girl, get up.)*
- Instagram: "Too late, they said." The KJV messengers never say "too late." Change it to "The news came: too late."

**5. The CTA points to the channel, not to Jesus.**
- "Find Jesus in the whole Bible, one panel at a time. Subscribe to Awakeden." is the brand line. Only the YouTube and Facebook copy has a Christ-pointing line: "the one even death obeys."
- TikTok and Instagram have no line that points to Jesus apart from the quote itself.
- "One panel at a time" is template copy. Check that this film really is in panel/comic style. If it's the vector style, the line is wrong.

**6. Platform fit (minor).**
- The TikTok first line "A father begs Jesus to save his dying girl." is flat. Instagram's opener ("Thy daughter is dead." Too late…) is stronger. Use a stake-first opener on TikTok too.
- Hashtag counts are fine (5, 5, 3, 7). The tags are real search terms, and "talitha koum" is a good spelling variant to include.
- The film never says the name "Jairus." Using it in the metadata is fine for search, but newcomers only see the name in the text.

## Works well
- "She Was Dead. Jesus Kept Walking" is a true hook. Mark 5:36 has Jesus going on as soon as He hears the news.
- No fear, gain/loss or works framing.
- "In plain words: it is too late, do not bother Him now." reads well for a global audience.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Instagram: replace or guard "Send this to someone who has been told it is too late" so it doesn't promise a healing outcome. Point it at Jesus ("be not afraid, only believe"), not at the result.
2. Add the one-line honest AI disclosure to all four platforms (house packaging rule).
3. Make quotes and claims honest: label "Little girl, get up" as a gloss beside the KJV wording, change "Too late, they said" to "The news came: too late", drop the "mark 5 explained" tag, and check that "one panel at a time" fits this film's style. Add a short Christ-pointing line to TikTok and Instagram.
