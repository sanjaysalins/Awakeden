# Independent review — codex (OK, 56s)

- **Hook: 7/10.** “A father begs Jesus: my little girl is dying” establishes immediate stakes, but it is familiar crisis narration. The sharper tension—“why troublest thou the Master any further?”—arrives after the opening five seconds.

- **Landing: 5/10.** “If you have been told it is too late, He has heard that too” echoes the hearing motif, but gives no invitation or concrete response. This exact sentence could close a piece about Lazarus, a failed marriage, or an abandoned ambition. It therefore meets your stock-closer cap.

- **Doctrinal risk: the application leaves the promised outcome undefined.** Following “She got up and walked,” the phrase “If you have been told it is too late” can sound like assurance that Jesus will reverse the listener’s particular loss. The narration does not explicitly promise that, but its emotional logic invites that inference. Distinguish Christ’s authority and care from a guarantee of present circumstances being reversed.

- **First-hearing clarity: the translation relationship is missing.** “Talitha cumi” followed by the narrator’s “Damsel, I say unto thee, arise” can sound like two separate utterances. Mark explicitly identifies the latter as the interpretation of the former. A listener without Bible knowledge needs that connection. [Mark 5:41, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark%205%3A35-43&version=KJV)

- **KJV wording: no altered words found inside quotation marks.** All four quotations match the KJV wording. “Little girl, get up” is outside quotation marks, so it does not violate the stated rule. [Mark 5:35–41, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark%205%3A35-43&version=KJV)

- **Thread and conviction:** “Jesus heard it” → “kept walking” → “He has heard that too” maintains one thread. There is no explicit works bargain or fear-based pressure. The weakness is that the ending offers reassurance without making its grace-grounded meaning clear.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Bound the closing application so it cannot imply guaranteed reversal of the listener’s loss.
2. Build a concrete invitation from this scene’s hearing, hand, or command; replace the transferable reassurance.
3. Identify the English quotation as the interpretation of “Talitha cumi.”
