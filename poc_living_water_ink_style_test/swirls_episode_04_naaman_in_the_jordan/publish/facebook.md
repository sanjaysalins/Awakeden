## TITLE
He Told One Story and They Tried to Kill Him For It

## DESCRIPTION
A great general got leprosy. A servant girl pointed him to a prophet. The prophet would not even come outside, he just sent one line: wash in the Jordan, seven times.

Naaman was furious: "Are not Abana and Pharpar, rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? may I not wash in them, and be clean?"

His own servants talked him down: "My father, if the prophet had bid thee do some great thing, wouldest thou not have done it? how much rather then, when he saith to thee, Wash, and be clean?"

He went down. He washed, seven times. New skin, like a child's.

The best thing Naaman ever did was lose that argument.
