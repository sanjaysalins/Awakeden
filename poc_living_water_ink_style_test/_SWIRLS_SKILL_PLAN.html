Swirls: what we learned, and a new skill

I read all 12 episodes and the 2 pilots. Nothing was spent.

1. Two ways we made them

Old way (eps 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13)

Storyboard page with the words drawn in. 6-9 pages. Freeze and loop fill. 720p. 62-106 s. $5-30 each.

New way (eps 6, 12, 16)

Each one has its own look. No words in the pictures. A real clip on every shot. Words added on top (Remotion). 1080p. 54-81 s. Blind panels at every step. $6-22 each.

2. Problems that kept coming back

ProblemFix that worked
Faces and clothes driftUse only 1-2 favourite stills as refs. One ref per costume.
Clones, extra people, people vanishNear/big plus far/small layout. Count people in every still.
Kling adds speech bubblesRemove the motion. Rewording does not fix it.
Cross drawn as an X, or a logChange the camera angle after 2 failed tries.
Stray text drawn in (labels, dashes)No words in stills. Words go on top.
Freeze and backwards loopsNow banned. Each clip is longer than its slot. Trim only.
Music silent or too loudMake the score in 2 parts. Measure the sound, don't trust your eyes.

3. The current skill is out of date

The /swirls-of-life skill still teaches the old way. It uses freeze fill, Higgsfield, and words drawn in.

The best recipe now sits in ep 16's notes and the Bartimaeus playbook. No skill uses it.

4. My plan: a new /swirls-episode skill

  1. Words: blind writers, guardrail panel, you lock. $0
  2. Voice: natural speed, emotion cues, 2 takes. ask $
  3. Shot plan: built from the real word times, one shot per 3-6 s. $0
  4. Refs first: one per character and each costume. ask $
  5. Pictures, with a check that counts people. ask $
  6. Clips, Kling pro, each longer than its slot. Checks the first and last frame. ask $
  7. Draft film: 1.0x, hard cuts, 3 s hold at the end. Fails if a clip is short. $0
  8. Music in 2 parts, then SFX. ask $
  9. Words on top: Remotion. Keeps the blue-gold ink as the Swirls mark. $0
  10. Film red team, blind, with a Fable seat. $0
  11. Packaging: title, poster, publish pack, copy red team. $0, poster ask $

Plus one helper, swirls_status.py. It reads the episode folder and says: "you are on step 5, next do X". It also keeps HANDOVER_RESUME.md up to date.

It reuses the tools we already have and adds no new engine. The old skill stays for the storyboard look.

Cost to build: $0. The plan gets the panel review before I build.
