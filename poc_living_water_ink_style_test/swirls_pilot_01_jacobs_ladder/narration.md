A man fled his own brother's threat to kill him — and slept on open ground with a rock for his pillow.

God does not wait for you to reach a holy place before He speaks. He meets the runaway exactly where guilt and exhaustion finally put him down.

Scripture says he dreamed of "a ladder set up on the earth... and the angels of God ascending and descending on it."

And the LORD Himself stood above it and said:

"I am with thee... I will not leave thee."

Some would call it just a guilty man's dream. But God does not audition people before He comforts them.

Jacob woke and said:

"Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not... How dreadful is this place!"

You may be lying in your own version of that field right now, certain you are alone. You are not.

Jacob saw a ladder to heaven in a dream. Jesus said He is that ladder, standing open, permanently.

Ready to stop running, and climb toward Him?
