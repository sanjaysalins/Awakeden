## Independent adversarial review — Swirls ep10 (Luke 7:36–50)

### Doctrinal soundness
**Mostly sound; one live tension, not heresy.**

The core claim — forgiveness precedes love; tears/ointment are response, not currency — is standard evangelical reading and matches the parable’s “frankly forgave them both” before “which… will love him most.” **“Thy faith hath saved thee”** anchors salvation correctly.

**Problem:** You quote Jesus verbatim, then immediately reframe:

> **"Her sins, which are many, are forgiven; for she loved much..."**  
> **"Forgiven first — love was the receipt, not the price."**

On first hearing, KJV English reads **“for she loved much”** as the *reason* she was forgiven — i.e. works/merit. Your gloss fixes that for a rehearing, but a zero-context listener gets the works reading from Jesus’s own line *before* your correction lands. That is a clarity/doctrine-communication risk, not invented doctrine — but it is real, not nitpick.

**“Not thy tears. Not thy ointment. Thy faith.”** — faithful to 7:50 and good anti-works framing. Minor compression: 7:47 foregrounds **love**; 7:50 foregrounds **faith**. Both are in-text; the script doesn’t explain how they relate. Dense but orthodox; a hostile ear could call it thread-jump (love → faith) in the last 10 seconds.

No contrarian readings, no clickbait, no fear gospel. **Simon's stain (labeling)** vs **her peace (carrying out)** fits the brief without forcing the word “stain.”
