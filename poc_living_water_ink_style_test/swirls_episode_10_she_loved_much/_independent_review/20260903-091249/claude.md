6. **Direct listener address at the landing** — this is the one that doesn't actually land. The added line is: *"Thy faith — still his word over whatever you walked in carrying."* Two problems:
   - **Grammar is broken.** There's no verb linking "still" to "his word" ("still his word over X" isn't a sentence — it's missing "is": *his word is still [true/in force] over...*). On first hearing, with zero setup, this reads as a fragment, not a claim.
   - **Pronoun collision.** The sentence opens in KJV "thy" (addressing the woman: "Thy faith...") and switches mid-clause to "you" (addressing the listener: "...whatever you walked in carrying"). A first-time listener has no signal that the addressee just changed — it can easily be heard as still describing *her*, not turning to the viewer. This is exactly the "clever-but-confusing" trap the brief warns against, and it's happening at the single moment (the landing) where confusion is most costly.

7. Small, non-blocking nuance loss: KJV has Simon answer hedgingly — *"I suppose that he, to whom he forgave most"* — but the narration frames it as *"Simon knew:"* which flips a tentative answer into a confident one. Not a doctrinal error, just a flattened characterization.
