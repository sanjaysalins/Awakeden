### Clarity issue (new finding, not raised in rounds 1-2)
`"Simon said nothing out loud: "This man, if he were a prophet, would have known... for she is a sinner.""` — the colon immediately followed by a quotation is a self-contradicting construction on first hearing: "said nothing out loud" primes the listener for silence, then a colon+quote is the exact grammatical signal for *what was said*. A listener has to backtrack to realize this is an unspoken inner thought (correctly matching Luke 7:39's "spake within himself"). Multi-voice audio casting might rescue this in production, but as written text it's a stumble. Fix: reword the frame, e.g. "Simon thought it, not out loud:" — removes the contradiction without touching the quote.
