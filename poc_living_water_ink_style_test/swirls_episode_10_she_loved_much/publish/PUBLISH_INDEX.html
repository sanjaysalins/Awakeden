TITLE
No Water. No Kiss. No Oil. What the Host Never Gave Jesus, a Stranger Did.
DESCRIPTION
Luke 7: no water for his feet. No kiss. No oil. That is what Simon the Pharisee never gave Jesus at his own table. A woman he had not invited walked in anyway, and gave him all three.

"And stood at his feet behind him weeping, and began to wash his feet with tears, and did wipe them with the hairs of her head, and kissed his feet, and anointed them with the ointment."

Simon thought it, not out loud: "This man, if he were a prophet, would have known who and what manner of woman this is that toucheth him: for she is a sinner."

Jesus answered with a story about two forgiven debts, then turned to Simon: "Her sins, which are many, are forgiven; for she loved much: but to whom little is forgiven, the same loveth little."

To her: "Thy faith hath saved thee; go in peace."

She came in carrying an alabaster box. She left carrying his peace.
