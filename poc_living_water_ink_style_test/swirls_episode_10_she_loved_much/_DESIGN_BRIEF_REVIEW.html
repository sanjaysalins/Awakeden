Episode 10 — the picture plan

Fable's full visual design for "She Loved Much." Nothing rendered yet — this is the paper stage, $0 so far.

The hard problem this episode poses: the "stain" (her sin/guilt) has to sit on a real person, not an object — and her love must NOT look like what washes it away, or the picture would contradict the narration ("Not thy tears. Not thy ointment. Thy faith."). Fable's fix: the stain sits in the paper under her, unmoved through both weeping pages, and only starts clearing the instant Jesus SPEAKS forgiveness. The cut is the miracle — no picture ever shows it happening.

The shape: 9 pages + 2 covers

Longer than usual because this episode's narration is 1.5× the series' normal length. Every page happens in one room — Simon's dinner table — so the whole episode reads as one continuous scene.

New characters & objects for this episode

The woman — unnamed in the text, so Fable designed her fresh: warm olive skin, a deep red mantle (the one bright color in the whole room — quietly nodding at Isaiah's "sins as scarlet").
Simon the Pharisee — a different Simon from any disciple elsewhere in this project. Composed, well-dressed, not a villain — just a man who "saw the sin, never the woman."
The alabaster box — the little stone perfume flask she carries in and leaves behind. Bookends the whole episode: sealed on the cover, open and left behind on the last page.
Simon's dining room — one consistent room used on every page, including a dry, unused wash-basin by the door (the water Simon never gave her).

Three things worth your call

1. Should the first 3 pages carry zero blue ink? Fable's plan: the opening (her arrival, her weeping, her kiss) shows no living-ink motif at all — deliberate, because those pages show real tears and ointment, and the narration says "Not thy tears. Not thy ointment." was NOT what saved her. But you flagged on episode 2 that middle pages can feel "swirl-less." A ready fallback exists (one quiet thread from Jesus's resting hand) if you'd rather see blue earlier.

2. Back-cover Bible verse: "We love him, because he first loved us" (1 John 4:19, matches the piece's own thesis) or "Though your sins be as scarlet" (Isaiah 1:18, the stain's root verse)?

3. Which page is the hero (the one that opens AND closes the finished video)? Fable recommends F08 — the moment Jesus SAYS "her sins are forgiven." The alternative is F07 — the moment just before, where he first truly SEES her. Both are strong; F08 is the doctrinal declaration, F07 is the emotional turn.
