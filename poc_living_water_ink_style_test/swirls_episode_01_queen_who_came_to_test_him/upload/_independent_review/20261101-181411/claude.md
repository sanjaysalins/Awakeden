# Independent review — claude (OK, 43s)

**Findings**

1. **The "plain ASCII" gate claim is false.** UK-G7 says "plain ASCII", but the YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram copy all end with "✝". Either the gate doesn't check this or the claim is wrong. The ✝ may also render as an emoji box on some platforms.

2. **"Test a rumor" and "Jesus noticed" are slightly off the text.**
   - 1 Kings 10:1 says she heard of Solomon's fame "concerning the name of the LORD" and came "to prove him with hard questions". The copy drops the LORD part and frames it as a rumor check.
   - "Jesus noticed" is vague. In Matthew 12:42 Jesus cites her as a witness against his own generation: she "shall rise up in the judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it". The red-team admits this is left out. It is selective but not false, and not clickbait. The title can still mislead a viewer about what Jesus actually did with her.

3. **The distance wording is inconsistent.** The title says "crossed a desert". The description says "crossed a continent". Sheba's location is debated, so "continent" is an overclaim. "Centuries later" for roughly 1,000 years is also loose.

4. **The long-form listing is a copy of the Short.**
   - The YouTube Long-form title, description and tags are identical to the Short's.
   - The tags include "bible shorts", which is wrong for 16:9 long-form.
   - It has only 3 hashtags against 5 on the Short, for no stated reason.
   - If this is really the same Short, a separate long-form block is misleading.

5. **The first lines are weak on TikTok, Instagram and the YouTube description.**
   - Each opens with a bare reference ("1 Kings 10." / "1 Kings 10:1-13."). The hook is buried in sentence two.
   - The Short description is long (925 characters) for a 60-second Short.
   - The Facebook caption also starts with the reference.

6. **There is some template and brand clutter.**
   - "Follow: YouTube: @awakeden" on a YouTube listing is redundant, and it repeats the awakeden.com link.
   - "swirls of life" is a brand tag, not a search keyword.
   - Titles 2 to 4 are fine alternates. The chosen title shares the "[Person] did X. Jesus [verb]." shape with sibling episodes, which risks the templated feel noted in the project's landing-fatigue memory.

7. **The CTA is soft.** "Bring Him your hardest question" points to Jesus and is grace-friendly, with no fear or gain/loss framing. It never names what He did (the cross or the resurrection), so the gospel landing is thin.

**What passes**
- The quoted 1 Kings 10:6-7 and Matthew 12:42 text matches KJV as I recall it. I did not re-check it against the KJV cache.
- Doctrine is sound and the claims are honest overall.
- Hashtag counts are sane.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove "✝" (or fix the UK-G7 check), and make "desert" and "continent" agree. Say "crossed the desert" and drop "continent".
2. Replace "Jesus noticed" and "test a rumor" with wording closer to the text. For example: "A Queen Came to Test Solomon. Jesus Said Someone Greater Was Here." Add that she came because of what she heard about the LORD.
3. Give the YouTube Long-form block its own title, description and tags (or delete it). Remove "bible shorts" from it. Open the TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube copy with the hook line, not the verse reference.
