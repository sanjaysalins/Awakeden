## TITLE
A Queen Came to Test Solomon. Jesus Said Someone Greater Was Here.

## DESCRIPTION
1 Kings 10:1-13. The queen of Sheba heard of Solomon's fame and came to prove him with hard questions. She did not believe the report until she saw it herself.

She said so plainly: "It was a true report that I heard in mine own land of thy acts and of thy wisdom. Howbeit I believed not the words, until I came, and mine eyes had seen it: and, behold, the half was not told me" (1 Kings 10:6-7, KJV)

Centuries later, Jesus pointed back to her to say something about Himself: "behold, a greater than Solomon is here" (Matthew 12:42, KJV)

She came a long way to check a king's wisdom. Someone greater is already in front of you. Bring Him your hardest question.
