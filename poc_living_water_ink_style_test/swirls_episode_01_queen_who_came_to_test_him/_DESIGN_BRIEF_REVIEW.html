Episode 1 — Visual Design Brief

The Queen Who Came to Test Him · designed by Fable · nothing rendered yet, nothing spent yet
Shape
9 units: front cover → 7 story pages (F01–F07) → back cover.
One more page than episode 8 used — because the queen's and Jesus's two long KJV quotes each needed their own page to breathe, instead of one crowded page each.

New characters

Queen of Sheba NEW
Tall, poised South Arabian queen, bronze skin, fine braids under a sheer gold-edged veil, small matte gold jewelry (never a glow — gold stays "real metal," blue ink stays for the sacred thread). Empty hands — she needs them free for her hard-question gesture.
Solomon NEW
Settled, calm king in his 50s, grey-streaked beard, plain gold diadem (no tall crown), cream linen + clay-red mantle. Deliberately NOT out-dressed by Jesus later — his greatness is gold and a throne; Jesus's greatness is only ever the ink.
Jesus REUSED
Same build and the same approved reference image from episode 8. No redesign, no new approval cycle needed.

The two arcs, in one line each

The blue thread (truth) rises the whole way, and crosses into full bloom exactly on F06 — "a greater than Solomon is here." That's the gospel pivot, on purpose.

Her doubt (the frayed, shaky drawing) dies early — gone by F03, the moment she says "mine eyes had seen it." Three pages and centuries pass between her doubt dying and the truth's own thread finishing its rise. That gap is the whole sermon: she got everything she came for, and there was still more.

Worth knowing before this goes further

  • Solomon's throne is drawn as "broad steps, small stone lions" — not Scripture's exact six-steps/twelve-lions count. Rendering exact counts tends to come out wrong; the KJV caption text stays word-for-word regardless.
  • F07 has Jesus look straight at the camera — the only page in the episode that breaks the fourth wall, earned by the line "already in front of you."
  • The scribes who doubt Jesus in F05/F06 are drawn plain, not "frayed" like the queen was — the shaky-doubt drawing is reserved for a doubter whose story we actually watch resolve, which isn't them.
