A queen crossed a desert to test if a rumor was true.

She had heard about Solomon's wisdom and did not believe it. So she came herself, with hard questions, to see for herself.

She said it plainly:

"It was a true report that I heard in mine own land of thy acts and of thy wisdom. Howbeit I believed not the words, until I came, and mine eyes had seen it: and, behold, the half was not told me."

She had doubted a report. Seeing it changed everything.

Centuries later, Jesus pointed straight back to her, to make a claim about Himself:

"The queen of the south shall rise up in the judgment with this generation... for she came from the uttermost parts of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and, behold, a greater than Solomon is here."

She crossed a continent to verify a king's wisdom.

A greater than Solomon is already in front of you. You do not have to travel anywhere to find out if it is true.

Just ask Him your hardest question.
