## 1. Format and timing math (45 s)

A **unit** = one picture + one clip. 8 units: `front, f01..f06, back`.

**One chain of numbers** (v2 had 40 / 42 / 45 fighting each other):

```
film  = voice + 3.0 s hold           target 45.0   PASS 42.0-48.0   FAIL outside 40.0-50.0
voice = film - 3.0                   target 42.0   PASS 39.0-45.0
words = about 86-94                  pace 2.2 words per second (measured ep13, ep16)
estimate (before money) = words / 2.2 + 0.4 x (character turns)   must be 38.0-42.0 s
```

| Unit | Spoken line | Slot PASS | WARN | FAIL | Clip to render |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| front | hook, 4-6 words | 2.0-3.0 s | 3.0-3.5 | under 1.8 or over 3.5 | 3 or 4 s |
| f01-f06 | 11-15 words each | 5.0-7.0 s | 4.5-5.0, 7.0-7.5 | under 4.5 or over 7.5 | 6-8 s |
| back | last line, 10 words or fewer, then the hold | 6.0-8.5 s | 8.5-9.0 | over 9.0 | 7-10 s |

Check: 2.7 + 6 x 5.9 + 7.0 = 45.1 s.

- **Slot of unit k** = from its first word's start to the next unit's first word's start. Front starts at 0.0. Back ends at `duration(narration.mp3) + 3.0`.
- **First word starts by 0.40 s** (voice from frame 0).
- **Clip length** `clip_duration = min(15, max(3, ceil(slot + 0.5)))`. An integer 3-15. Always longer than its slot. The tail is trimmed. Nothing else.
- **No fill, ever:** no freeze, boomerang, ping-pong, speed change, zoom, crop into a panel, or panel insert. 1.0x. Hard cuts.
- **Why no 4 s page** (v2 allowed one): the pace test only showed that 6 s is tight. Nothing shorter than 5.0 s passes without a warning.

**Frame-exact cuts (new in v3).** 30 fps. Boundaries are counted from the start of the FILM, not per unit:

```
B(0) = 0      B(k) = round(end_s(k) x 30)      frames in unit k = B(k) - B(k-1)
B(last) = round(total x 30)
```

Each piece is encoded with `-frames:v N`, not `-t`. Every cut lands within half a frame of its word. No drift builds up, and the back unit no longer soaks up other units' rounding.

**The moving hold.** In a trim episode the picture track is exactly `total` long because the back clip itself runs through the last line and the 3.0 s hold. The voice mux does not use `-shortest`. `score_mix.mix_tail` is called unchanged; its cloned frames land after `total` and are cut by `-t total`.

**Models.** Stills: nano-banana-pro, 2K, 9:16 (covers always; pages by default). Clips: Kling 3 Omni on OpenArt, `pro`, sound off. Real clip size 1072x1928, scaled to 1080x1920.

## 2. Page text rules (sized for a 6 s read on a phone)

**What the pace test found** (`POC\_pace_test\`, 12 old pages, 3 blind readers at 390 px):
big caption = fast; 3 labels = read, a bit small; `SEQ` / `F` header = slow; `NOTE` = slowest;
only 2-3 of 12 pages read fully in 6 s; old pages had text drawn twice ("without the camp"),
the same words in a label and the caption ("led him out"), two-line captions, quote marks, old words.

**Baked text on a page — exactly these, nothing else:**

| String | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `SEQ: <TITLE>` | 2-3 words. **The same on all 6 pages. It is the front cover's title.** | Read once on the cover; after that it is furniture, not reading |
| `F01` .. `F06` | frame number | No reading needed |
| 3 panel labels | 1-2 words each (one label may be 3). **6 words or fewer across the three.** Each is a word the narrator says on this page, in spoken order. All different | Ear and eye agree; labels are the "small" text |
| ONE caption | 2-5 words, 28 characters or fewer, one line. **A word-for-word piece of this page's spoken line.** The biggest writing on the page, right under the hero scene | The caption is the one thing every viewer reads |
| corner NOTE | **gone.** `corner_note` must be empty | Slowest string in the test |

**Reading load:** labels + caption = **11 words or fewer per page.**

**More rules (all linted in SWS-G4):**
- No string appears twice on a page. A label may not equal the caption, or sit inside it, or the caption inside a label.
- Letters, spaces, apostrophe, comma, full stop, `?`, `!` only. The colon only in `SEQ:`. No dash, no ellipsis, no quote marks, no digits (digits live only in `F0n`, panel numbers 1 2 3, and Scripture references on covers).
- Plain modern words in labels. A KJV word (thee, saith, Esaias) only inside a caption that is a short direct quote.
- Caption prompt wording (authored field, not the locked template): "in large bold brush lettering, the largest writing on the page".
- Cover: title 2-3 words; subtitle = one Scripture reference. The cover title should carry the hook's key word, so the eye and the ear say the same thing in those 2-3 s.

**Proof, before any clip money:** the phone-size frame (`_qc\phone_<id>.jpg`, 390 px wide) and the stills animatic in step 5. Model readers are a legibility floor only. The owner on a real phone is the speed test.

## 4. Page design rules (authoring; checked at plan time and by the blind plan check)

1. Three panels set up, the hero scene pays off.
2. Panels show things, not faces (object, place, hands, silhouette).
3. Panel motion is light only (light warms, haze drifts). At most one panel has real motion.
4. The hero scene has one human action by one figure, done in the first half, then small living motion to the last frame. Never fully still.
5. A figure whose line is being spoken holds still, mouth closed in the still. The motion goes to a second figure or a prop, named in `page_plan.json` (`motion_carrier`).
6. 3 or fewer full figures. Crowds are small, far, unfinished hatched shapes. Near/large + far/small, never two equal groups.
7. Phone safe zone: caption right under the hero scene, above 85% of frame height. Hero subject left of 85% width.
8. The back cover carries the last line and the hold as ONE moving clip. Its motion must run to the end (water, cloth, light) — design it so.

**Motif (blue and gold ink):** stage by unit as in SWS-G6. Never goes down. The first stage-2 page is the gospel turn.
- In stills: inside the scene sentence as "a brushstroke of blue ink" + one direction + flat properties (thin, matte, dry-edged). Never: thread, cord, ribbon, drip, drop, bloom, flower, petal, ring, loop, pool, puddle, glow, sparkle.
- `material_closer`: stage 0 = "the page carries no unusual ink anywhere." Stage 1-3 = "the one brushstroke of blue ink is the only unusual ink on the page, and every figure and the paper beneath them are free of any ink."
- **In clips the motif is never named.**

## 5. Spend guard SWS-G0 (fail closed, at the request boundary)

**The boundary is `openart_bridge.submit()`** — the one function every OpenArt request passes through. v2 wrapped the runner; the panel showed that cannot see receipts. v3 puts the guard inside the door.

**In `POC\test_the_cross\openart_bridge.py` (about 15 lines):**
- Module flags `GUARD = None`, `REQUIRE_GUARD = False`.
- `submit(payload)`: if `REQUIRE_GUARD` and no `GUARD` -> raise (no request file is written). If `GUARD`: `rid = GUARD.before(payload)` **before** the request file is written; `GUARD.after(rid, resp)` on a reply; `GUARD.failed(rid, err)` on a timeout or error.
- Old episodes never set the flag, so nothing changes for them.

**In `POC\swirls_short.py`:**
- `with guard(episode_dir, step, unit):` sets `openart_bridge.GUARD`. The runner sets `REQUIRE_GUARD = True` as soon as it loads a trim episode. So a trim episode **cannot** send an unguarded request, whichever function builds it.
- **Price comes from the payload that is really sent**, not from what the runner believes:

| Payload | Price | Else |
|---|---|---|
| `kind=anim`, `kling-3-omni`, `resolution=pro`, `generate_sound=False`, integer `duration` 3-15 | 35 x duration | refuse: unknown price |
| `kind=still`, `nano-banana-pro`, `2K` | 40 | refuse |
| any other model | the `--price model=cr` on that step's approval | refuse if absent |

- `before()` refuses unless: `approvals.json` is readable; the step is approved; the approval is under 12 hours old; the step's prerequisite owner gate is recorded (table below); the unit is allowed (when the approval names units); no earlier row is `requested` or `UNRESOLVED`; the step's spend + this price is within the cap. Then it appends a `requested` row to `<episode>\spend_log.jsonl`: `id, ts, step, unit, kind, price, payload_sha256, prompt_sha256, input_sha256` (start image or refs).
- `after()`: `credits_spent` must be a number and at most 110% of the price -> `confirmed`, with `output_sha256` (the file written) and the reply's job / history id. Anything else -> `UNRESOLVED`. (Sound left ON bills 40 cr/s = 114% -> caught.)
- `failed()` (timeout after a possible charge) -> `UNRESOLVED`.
- **An open row blocks every later paid command** until a person runs `RUN confirm <rid> --credits N --note "..."` (real number from OpenArt history) or `RUN confirm <rid> --void --note "..."` (checked: not charged).
- `_overrides.json` can excuse a gate FAIL. It can never excuse the guard.

**Approvals and owner gates** (`<episode>\approvals.json`):
- `RUN approve <step> --cap N --unit cr|usd [--balance N] [--unit-id TAG] [--price model=cr] --note "<owner's words, date>"`. For `cr` steps `--balance` is the live `openart_account_get` number; refused if balance < cap.
- `RUN ok <gate> --note "<owner's words, date>"` records a $0 owner gate.

| Paid step | Needs gate | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| `voice` | `words` | usd |
| `northstar` | `voice_ear` | cr |
| `stills` | `northstar_look` | cr |
| `clip_test` | `stills_animatic` | cr |
| `clips` | `clip_test_look` + a confirmed `clip_test` row | cr |
| `score` | `draft_cut` | usd |
| `poster` | (film final) | cr or usd |

**ElevenLabs steps** (voice, alignment, score) do not pass through the bridge. They run as `RUN paid <step> -- <command>`: same approval and gate checks, a `requested` row at the quoted price, the command runs, and the row stays open until `confirm` records the real cost from the usage page or ledger. Honest limit: there is no machine receipt for these; the block-until-confirmed rule is the protection.

Each ask also gets its row in `REPO\data\spend_ledger.jsonl` (servicer, as today).

## 7. Panel rules (quorum, timeouts)

- **Every panel has a blind Fable seat** (a fresh subagent that sees only the pack). No Fable seat = no panel.
- **Text panels** (words, page plan, film pack, copy): Fable + Claude subagent + codex + cursor + grok + gemini. **Quorum = Fable + 3 others with a verdict.**
- **Image panels** (reading stills, poster key art): Fable + Claude + codex (+ cursor when it takes images). **Gemini is out** (image input is dead as of 2026-11-15). **Grok is never counted on.** Quorum = Fable + 2.
- **Timeout:** 600 s per seat. A failed seat gets ONE retry, alone, at 900 s.
- **Still under quorum:** add ONE fresh blind Claude subagent, labelled "substitute" in the index. Still under: stop and tell the owner. Never proceed on a thin panel and never count a seat that gave no verdict.
- `independent_review.py` keeps its own 4-of-5 quorum; the Fable seat is run beside it by the session.
- Fix by convergence: 2+ seats, or any factual / Bible point from one seat.
