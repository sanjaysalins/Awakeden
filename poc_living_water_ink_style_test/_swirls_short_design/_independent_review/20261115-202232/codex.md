The plan still has material gaps in spend control, approval validity, and acceptance testing.

1. **HIGH — The OpenArt “receipt” can still be an estimate.** Section 5 says `after()` confirms when “`credits_spent` must be a number and at most 110% of the price.” But the existing [servicer instructions](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/AGENT_BRIDGE.md:125) explicitly permit populating that field from `openart_model_cost`, an estimate. The plan does not change those instructions or require an authoritative charge record. A numeric quote can therefore masquerade as actual spend. Require a job-bound charge record; otherwise keep the request unresolved.

2. **HIGH — Timeout recovery can cause duplicate charges.** Section 5 allows `confirm <rid> --void` after checking “not charged.” The current [bridge timeout path](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/poc_living_water_ink_style_test/test_the_cross/openart_bridge.py:64) leaves the request file queued. The owner could void an uncharged request, retry, and then have the attended servicer execute both files. Neither T6 nor the recovery procedure covers late servicing. Require cancellation acknowledgement or durable claim/job reconciliation before allowing a void and retry.

3. **HIGH — Equal word counts do not establish that the approved words were spoken.** Step 3 calls its “Count check” fail closed, but it checks counts across `units.json`, `narration.md`, and alignment. The actual [alignment transcript comes from `narration-tagged.md`](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/pipeline/assembly_align.py:49), whose narrator wording is not included in that equality check. Equal-length substitutions or reordered lines can pass. Furthermore, `ok words`, `voice_ear`, and `stills_animatic` have no specified artifact hashes or invalidation rules. Require ordered text equality and bind approvals to the exact reviewed revisions.

4. **HIGH — Alignment has an unresolved paid-command contract.** Step 2 runs `RUN paid voice`, then `RUN align`; Section 5 says the voice row remains open until manual confirmation and blocks every later paid command. If alignment follows those rules, it is blocked; if it bypasses them, its charge is unguarded. Define whether synthesis and alignment share one transaction or have separate confirmed rows. Step 9 also incorrectly equates `force=False` with “no second paid alignment”: [the existing implementation](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/pipeline/assembly_align.py:249) calls ElevenLabs when the cache is missing or unreadable. SRT generation needs a cache-only path that refuses such cases.

5. **HIGH — The 110% tolerance contradicts the hard cap.** Section 5 checks “spend + this price” against the cap, then automatically confirms receipts up to 110% of that price. With 245 credits remaining, a quoted 245-credit request returning a 260-credit charge passes confirmation while exceeding the owner’s cap. Reserve the allowed maximum before submission, and explicitly handle actual cumulative spend exceeding the cap. T6 tests a 114% receipt but misses this accepted-over-cap case.

6. **HIGH — T12 cannot pass its stated sequence and does not test the claimed workflow.** Its sequence reaches `approve northstar` without recording `ok voice_ear`, which Section 5 makes mandatory. It substitutes “synthetic voice” and “align meta” for the paid voice/alignment lifecycle, hiding finding 4. It also omits score generation and Step 9 while expecting `status` to reach Step 10. The retained [status implementation](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/poc_living_water_ink_style_test/swirls_short.py:805) requires an SRT and `FINAL_VIDEO.txt` for Step 9. Specify a complete executable sequence, mock provider calls beneath the real command paths, and test failure/recovery transitions.

7. **MEDIUM — The proposed KJV gate duplicates an existing, better-grounded facility.** SWS-G1k introduces a new verifier using fetched, cached text and fails when fetching is unavailable. The repo already has [a pinned local KJV corpus and strict verifier](F:/slk/PycharmProjects/JesusInTheBible/pipeline/kjv_strict.py:1), including `verse_texts()` and `verify_span()`, plus tests. That module documents defects previously found in the bible-api cache. Reuse its local lookup and adapt the continuous-word requirement. Creating another source of authority adds an external dependency and inconsistent verification behavior.

8. **MEDIUM — The visual checks do not establish the claimed defect prevention.** Step 6’s five sampled frames can miss a brief speech bubble, merged figure, or invented motion between samples. Including the first and last frames does not close that gap. Separately, Section 10 says structural G15 prevents a “Frozen landing hold,” but removing cloned padding cannot prove that the generated source itself keeps moving; G15f may remain WARN indefinitely. Require full playback of each used span before the next clip purchase, with explicit acceptance of the landing motion when automated detection remains advisory.

9. **MEDIUM — The pilot’s central assumption is tested after substantial prerequisite work.** Section 9.2 requires freeze calibration, border calibration, and the broader dry-run implementation before the owner tests the six-second pages. Yet Section 11 admits that readability and the short cover have no supporting viewer data. Calling panels and Vision reads “$0” also omits attended effort and subscription usage; Section 7 permits 600 seconds plus a 900-second retry per failed seat. Put an explicit phone-animatic go/no-go milestone after the minimum guarded production path, before the remaining calibration work, and budget operator time.
