How one film is made

  1. Words. Blind writers + panel. You pick.
  2. Voice. Money stop.
  3. Stills. Money stop.
  4. You watch the stills with the voice, on your phone. $0 test.
  5. One test clip, then the back cover. Money stop.
  6. The other clips, one at a time. Stop on the first fault.
  7. Score, sound, final panel. Then publish.

Safety built in

  • No paid step without your own yes and a price.
  • A real receipt from OpenArt for every clip.
  • The 3 s ending must move. Checked by code.
  • Speech bubbles, new text, cloned people = stop.
  • No small NOTE line. 11 words or less on each page.
