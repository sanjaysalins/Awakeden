## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)

- **RIGHT:** “that he should touch them,” the disciples’ rebuke, Jesus being “much displeased,” His command, and the embrace and blessing follow Mark 10:13–16 (lines 2–5, 8).
- **RIGHT, interpretive paraphrase:** “Angry” reasonably conveys “much displeased”; identifying the disciples as its target fits the sequence (line 4).
- **OVERCLAIMED:** “They wanted one thing” asserts an exclusive motive; “They sent them away” makes completed departure more definite than the quoted rebuke establishes (lines 2–3).
- **RIGHT, compressed:** Receiving the kingdom like a child reflects Mark 10:15, though “comes the way a little child does” leaves that meaning vague (line 6).
- **UNSUPPORTED as a viewer-specific claim:** “Someone told you ‘not now’” assumes the viewer’s experience (line 10). The pictured girls, shoulders and arch are imaginative details, not details established by line 8.
