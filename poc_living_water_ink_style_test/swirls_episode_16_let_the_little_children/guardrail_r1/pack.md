# EVIDENCE PACK: one vertical short film (YouTube Shorts / Reels), about 65 seconds

What the viewer gets: a narrator speaks; Jesus's own words are in a second, separate voice. The pictures are warm hand-drawn pencil-and-watercolour storybook scenes. The last picture: Jesus, smiling, holds two laughing little girls, one hugging Him from the side, one up on His shoulders, under an old stone arch with an olive tree in warm sunlight.

## THE SPOKEN LINES (numbered)
1. NARRATOR: Has someone told you "not now"? Then stay. This story is yours.
2. NARRATOR: People were bringing young children to Jesus. They wanted one thing: "that he should touch them."
3. NARRATOR: But His own disciples "rebuked those that brought them." They sent them away.
4. NARRATOR: Then Jesus saw it. And the Bible says He was "much displeased." Angry. Not with the children. With the ones who sent them away.
5. JESUS: "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God."
6. NARRATOR: In plain words: let them come. Do not stop them. God's kingdom belongs to people like these children. Anyone who comes in, comes the way a little child does.
7. NARRATOR: And here is what He did.
8. NARRATOR: "He took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them."
9. NARRATOR: They asked for a touch. He gave the children His arms.
10. NARRATOR: Someone told you "not now." Look at who Jesus picked up.
