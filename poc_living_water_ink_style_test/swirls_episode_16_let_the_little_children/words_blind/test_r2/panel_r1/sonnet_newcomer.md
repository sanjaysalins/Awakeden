## 1. WHO / WHAT / WHY in your own words
Some people bring small children to Jesus. They only want Him to touch them. His helpers say no and send them away. Jesus sees this and is upset with the helpers, not the children. He says: let the children come, God's kingdom is for people like them. Then He holds them and blesses them. Why: people who get pushed away are the ones He picks up. I get it, even with no Bible knowledge.

## 2. FIRST 2 SECONDS: would a pushed-aside viewer stop? 8/10
"Has someone told you 'not now'?" It is a direct question and it is my day today. Short, easy English. "Then stay. This story is yours" is a gentle invitation. Slightly weak: no picture or tension yet, but the question does the work.

## 3. ENDING: does it land and feel earned, or like a slogan? 8/10
"The ones others turned away are the ones Jesus picked up." It is earned. The "touch / arms" pair sets it up, and the last picture (Jesus holding two laughing girls) shows it. It is a little summary-like, but it answers the opening "not now" directly. Small repeat: "They hoped for one touch" appears twice, lines 7 and 9, so the pay-off arrives twice.

## 4. WHERE I GOT LOST OR MISLED (quote the words)
- "Suffer the little children to come unto me": "suffer" sounds like pain to me. The plain-words line fixes it, but only after.
- "forbid them not" and "for of such is the kingdom of God": old, hard words. "Kingdom of God" is unclear. What is it?
- "much displeased": I thought of a mild word. "Upset" helps.
- "rebuked": the next line says "told them no", so it is fine.
- "His own disciples": I do not know who they are. "Helpers/followers" would be easier.
- "People like these": like who? Small children, or pushed-aside people? A little blurry.
- Nothing says why the helpers sent them away.

## 5. ACCURACY vs the KJV (RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / WRONG, quote)
- "that he should touch them", "rebuked those that brought them": RIGHT.
- "much displeased": RIGHT. "Not with the children. With the ones who turned them away": RIGHT in sense. The KJV says "displeased" at the disciples' act; the gloss is fair.
- "Suffer the little children..." quote: RIGHT.
- "In plain words... God's kingdom belongs to people like these": RIGHT enough.
- "He took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them": RIGHT.
- "They hoped for one small thing... Just one touch": mild OVERCLAIMED. The KJV says "that he should touch them", not "just" or "small". It is a light framing.
- "The ones others turned away are the ones Jesus picked up": a fair application, slightly broader than the text (it only shows these children).
- Omission: verse 15 (receive as a little child) is left out. Fine for 65 seconds.
