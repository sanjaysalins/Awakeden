## 1. WHO / WHAT / WHY in your own words
WHO: Parents (or others) carrying small children to Jesus, His disciples, and Jesus.
WHAT: The disciples block them. Jesus is angry at the blocking, orders the children let through, then does more than was asked: He lifts them, lays hands on them, blesses them.
WHY: To tell a viewer who has been brushed off that Jesus sides with the ones sent away. All three are clear from the words alone, no Bible knowledge needed.

## 2. FIRST 2 SECONDS: would a pushed-aside viewer stop? score 0-10 and why
**7/10.** "Has someone told you 'not now'?" is short, direct, and names the wound in two plain words. "Then stay. This story is yours." is a good promise. Weakness: "not now" is a mild hurt; many will answer "yes, so what?" It does not yet say who is about to be shown, so the stop depends on the picture.

## 3. ENDING: does it land and feel earned, or like a slogan? score 0-10; quote the line
**7/10.** "The ones others turned away are the ones Jesus picked up." It is earned: it only restates what the story just showed, and it fits the fixed last picture. But it stays in the third person. The hook promised "This story is yours" and the ending never hands it back to the viewer, so it reads slightly like a caption, not a landing.

## 4. WHERE I GOT LOST OR MISLED (quote the words)
- "Suffer the little children" — a newcomer hears "suffer" as pain. The next line rescues it, but there is a 5-second wobble.
- "Upset." — too soft, and can mean "sad". The text means angry.
- "They hoped for one touch. Here is what He gave." then "They hoped for a touch. He gave the children His arms." — the same idea three times (also "Just one touch"). The second repeat drains the payoff.
- "God's kingdom belongs to people like these." — who "these" are for an adult viewer is left unsaid.

## 5. ACCURACY vs the KJV (RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / WRONG, quote)
- RIGHT: all four quoted phrases and the Jesus line are verbatim Mark 10:13-16.
- RIGHT: "Not with the children. With the ones who turned them away." Fair reading of v13-14.
- RIGHT: "God's kingdom belongs to people like these" for "of such is".
- OVERCLAIMED (mild): "one small thing" / "Just one touch" — KJV says only "that he should touch them"; "small" and "one" are added.
- OVERCLAIMED (mild): "The ones others turned away" — the disciples rebuked "those that brought them", the adults; Jesus picked up the children.
- WRONG: nothing.

## 6. OVERALL score 0-10, and the ONE change that would most improve it (exact wording)
**7/10.** Clear, accurate, warm; slightly repetitive and the ending does not close the hook.

ONE change — replace the last line with:
**"Someone told you 'not now.' The ones others turned away are the ones Jesus picked up."**
