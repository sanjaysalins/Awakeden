## 1. WHO / WHAT / WHY in your own words
**X:** People brought children to Jesus. His helpers told them "go away." Jesus got angry at the helpers, said let the children come, and then picked the children up and blessed them. Why: He welcomes people others push away. I am being told this is for me.
**Y:** Same story. Children sent away, Jesus angry at the men blocking, He calls them to the front, then holds them. Why: Jesus answers people who are sent away with arms, not rejection.

## 2. FIRST 2 SECONDS (pushed-aside viewer)
**X: 8/10.** "Has someone told you 'not now'? Then stay." It speaks to me directly. It names my exact day and gives a reason to stay.
**Y: 6/10.** "They were sent away before they even got close." Sad and clear, but "they" is unknown. I do not know it is about me yet. Still intriguing.

## 3. ENDING
**X: 8/10.** "They hoped for a touch. He gave them His arms." Earned by the "one touch" setup. The last line, "The ones others turned away are the ones Jesus picked up," is a little slogan-like, but it matches the picture of Him holding the girls.
**Y: 7/10.** Same strong "arms" line, then "That is how Jesus answers the ones others send away." A bit more general and flatter. It is a summary, not an image.

## 4. WHERE I GOT LOST OR MISLED
- Both: "Suffer the little children" is confusing. X explains it ("In plain words: let them come to Me"); Y explains it too ("'suffer' means 'let'"). Y's is clearer for a learner of English.
- Both: "for of such is the kingdom of God" and "kingdom" are abstract. X's "God's kingdom belongs to ones like these" helps a bit.
- Both: "rebuked" is a hard word. X adds "Not now. Go away," which makes it clear. Y leaves it bare, so I had to guess.
- Y: "the only time Mark's book says that about Jesus." I do not know what Mark's book is. This is a Bible-reader detail and it confuses me.
- Y: "the men in the way." Who are the men? I assume the helpers, but "disciples" is never explained in either.
- X: "Mark says" is unclear for a newcomer. Same in Y.
- Both: "much displeased" is stiff but I guess angry.

## 5. ACCURACY vs the KJV
- X "much displeased... Not with the children. With the ones who sent them away": RIGHT (implied by v14).
- X "Not now. Go away.": RIGHT as interpretation of "rebuked."
- X "God's kingdom belongs to ones like these": RIGHT.
- Y "It is the only time Mark's book says that about Jesus": OVERCLAIMED. The pack says the phrase is used once more, of the ten disciples (10:41), so it is "the only time about Jesus" is supportable but cannot be checked from the KJV text here. Risky.
- Y "'suffer' means 'let'": RIGHT.
- Y "Mark tells us" before the quote: RIGHT.
- Both omit v15, which is fine.

## 6. PICK
**X. Overall: X 8/10, Y 6.5/10.**
X talks to me from the first word, explains hard words in plain English, and the "Now remember what they hoped for. One touch." line sets up the payoff. Y has a good hook idea and a clearer "suffer" gloss, but its "only time Mark" fact is distracting.

**ONE change to X:** Replace the opening-adjacent slogan end "The ones others turned away are the ones Jesus picked up." with: "You were told 'not now.' He says, 'Come here.'"
