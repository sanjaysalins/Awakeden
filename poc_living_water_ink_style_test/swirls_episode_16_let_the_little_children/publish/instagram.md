## CAPTION
Pushed away? Told 'not now'? In Mark 10, Jesus' disciples tried to stop people bringing children to Him. The Bible says Jesus was "much displeased" with the disciples. Not with the children. "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not." Here, 'suffer' means 'let': let them come. Then "he took them up in his arms". You can come to Him too. Mark 10:13-16. (The words 'not now' are ours, not the Bible's.)
