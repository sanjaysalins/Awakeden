## TITLE
Why Jesus Was Angry

## DESCRIPTION
Jesus and the little children in the Bible (Mark 10:13-16): His disciples tried to stop people bringing children to Him, and Jesus was angry.

"Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God." Here, 'suffer' means 'let'. So: let the children come, do not stop them. The kingdom of God belongs to people like them.

The Bible says Jesus was "much displeased". That means: angry. Not with the children. With the disciples who tried to stop them.

He also said: "Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a little child, he shall not enter therein." In plain words: everyone must receive God's kingdom like a little child.

The words 'not now' in the film are ours, not the Bible's. The Bible says the disciples "rebuked those that brought them."

And "he took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them."

Has someone told you 'not now'? Jesus did not send those children away. You can come to Him too.

Read it: Mark 10:13-16 (also Matthew 19:13-15, Luke 18:15-17).
