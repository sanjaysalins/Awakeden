## TITLE
His Followers Tried to Stop the Children. Jesus Was Angry.

## DESCRIPTION
Mark 10:13-16: people brought young children to Jesus, "that he should touch them." His own followers "rebuked those that brought them."

Jesus was "much displeased", angry, and said: "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God." ('Suffer' is old English for 'let'.)

Then He said grown-ups must come the same way, like a little child.

'Not now' in the film is our short way to say it, not a Bible quote.

Has someone told you 'not now'? Jesus did not send those children away. You can come to Him too.

Read it: Mark 10:13-16.
