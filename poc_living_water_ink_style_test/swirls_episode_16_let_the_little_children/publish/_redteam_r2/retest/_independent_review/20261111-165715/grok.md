# Independent review — grok (OK, 273s)

The KJV lines inside the YouTube and Facebook descriptions match Mark 10:13-16, and the “not now” disclaimer is clear. The public titles, the thumbnail, and one on-screen card still overreach the verse. Fix those before upload.

**Thumbnail and the last line can be read the wrong way.** The thumbnail is two laughing little girls, one on His shoulders with her hand in His hair, one hugging His side, under the words “JESUS TOOK THEM IN HIS ARMS”. Mark 10:16 says he “took them up in his arms”, put his hands on them, and blessed them. It does not say girls, laughing, shoulders, or a hand in His hair. The same image is used for 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9, so the title or the children will crop. The film’s last line, on screen and in the SRT, is “Look at who Jesus picked up.” Next to that picture, “picked up” can be heard the wrong way. Use: “Look who Jesus took up in his arms.” Thumbnail words: “HE TOOK THEM UP IN HIS ARMS”. Picture: children held in his arms.

**“Angry” is stronger than the verse, and one title aims it badly.** YouTube title: “Let the Children Come to Me: Why Jesus Was Angry | Bible Story”. Facebook title: “His Disciples Tried to Stop the Children. Jesus Was Angry.” YouTube tags include “why was jesus angry” and “jesus was angry”. The KJV word is “much displeased”. The Facebook title can be read as anger at the children. The body copy is more careful (“Not with the children”), but the title is what people see. YouTube title: “Let the Children Come to Me | Mark 10:13-16”. Facebook title: “His Disciples Tried to Stop the Children. Jesus Was Much Displeased.” First line of the YouTube description, “and Jesus was angry”, should be “and the Bible says he was much displeased”. TikTok’s standalone “Angry.” should be “In plain words, he was very upset. Not with the children. With the disciples.”

**One on-screen line is labeled as Mark 10:14 and is not the KJV.** The card says “Let the children come to Me” with the label “Jesus · Mark 10:14”. The KJV is “Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not.” Put the KJV on the verse card. Put “Plain words: Let the children come to me” on a second card with no verse number. SRT cues 24–25 say “He took them up in his arms…”. The KJV is “And he took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them.” The YouTube description already quotes that correctly.

**Smaller flags.** Instagram opens with “Pushed away?” The SRT opens with “Has someone pushed you away?” Plain replacement: “Has someone told you to go away?” SRT cue 7, “His own close followers”, is unclear. Use “His own disciples”. SRT cue 21, “grown-ups”, should be “adults”. The next teaser, “a mother who kept asking”, is not this passage. “Kept asking” has to match the next film’s text before it is posted. Hashtags are already inside each caption, and each file also has a HASHTAGS block. Paste only one. Pasting both makes TikTok 10 hashtags. The stated TikTok range is 3–6. Counts as written are fine: YouTube 5, TikTok 5, Instagram 5, Facebook 3. YouTube title length is fine. No unrelated tag-stuffing. No AI-slop phrasing. There is no public AI line. The brief puts disclosure on the platform switch. This kit does not name that switch, so an uploader can miss it. Descriptions do point to Jesus (“You can come to Him too”) and the links are intact.
