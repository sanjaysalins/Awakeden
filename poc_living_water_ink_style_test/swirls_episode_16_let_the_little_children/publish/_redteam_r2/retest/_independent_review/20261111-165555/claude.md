# Red team: upload kit for "Let the Little Children" (Mark 10:13-16)

**Short answer:** the kit is close. Every KJV quote checks out word for word. Five problems need fixing before upload, and one of them is a KJV rule break inside the film itself.

## 1. Bible accuracy

**All quotes are exact KJV.** I checked "that he should touch them", "rebuked those that brought them", "much displeased", the verse 14 line, the verse 15 "Whosoever…" line and the verse 16 line. Dropping "And" at the start of verse 16 is fine.

**Problem: a non-KJV line is labelled as Jesus' words.** In the film, the on-screen card reads **"Let the children come to Me."** with the small label **"Jesus · Mark 10:14"**. That presents it as a quote, but it is a modern paraphrase, not the KJV. It breaks the channel's "quoted Scripture must be exact KJV" rule. Fix one of two ways:
- Change the label to **"In plain words · Mark 10:14"**, or
- Put the real KJV words on the card: **"Suffer the little children to come unto me"**.

**Film and description disagree on who must receive the kingdom.**
- The film says **"grown-ups must receive it the same way."**
- The YouTube description says **"Everyone must receive God's kingdom…"**
- KJV says "Whosoever".

The description is the more accurate one, and the two should agree. If the voice is locked, keep "Everyone" in the text. That is fine.

**"Angry" for "much displeased" is defensible** (the Greek word means indignant), and the text says it openly. That is OK.

## 2. Clickbait or misleading

**YouTube title: "…Why Jesus Was Angry"** is true, and the film answers it. That is OK.

The tags "why was jesus angry" and "jesus was angry" are true. They will also pull in people looking for the temple story. Keep them, but this is the only edge of bait.

**Thumbnail text vs picture:** the text says **"JESUS TOOK THEM IN HIS ARMS"**, but the drawing shows one girl **on His shoulders** and one **hugging His side**. Neither is "in His arms". Either:
- Redraw so at least one child is held in His arms, or
- Change the text to **"JESUS PICKED THEM UP"**, which matches the film's last line.

## 3. Global / ESL clarity

- **Facebook:** "His disciples 'rebuked those that brought them.'" never explains "rebuked". Add: **"(they told them to stop)"**.
- **All four platforms:** "'Not now' in the film is our short way to say it, not a Bible quote." The phrase "Our short way to say it" is vague and will translate badly. Replace with: **"The words 'not now' are ours, not the Bible's."**
- **YouTube:** "We say it in one word: angry." This is a bit odd. Replace with: **"That means: angry."**
- **YouTube and Facebook:** the "Whosoever… therein" verse is hard for a non-native reader. YouTube follows it with a plain gloss. Facebook does not. Add the same line to Facebook: **"Everyone must receive God's kingdom like a little child."**

## 4. Children and safety

**Missing from the kit: the YouTube "Made for kids" setting.** An animated storybook film about children will likely be judged "made for kids" under COPPA, whatever you pick. If it is marked that way, comments are turned off, so the pinned comment and the "What did Jesus do…" prompt will not work.
- Decide this on purpose and write it in the kit.
- If you choose "not made for kids", note why: the film speaks to adults ("Has someone pushed you away?").

The thumbnail itself is wholesome: laughing girls, modest clothes. It is still an adult man in physical contact with small girls, which some auto-moderation flags. Holding a child in His arms (see point 2) reads better than a shoulder-ride with "her hand in His hair".

## 5. Platform rules

- YouTube title: about 62 characters. OK.
- Hashtags: YouTube 5, TikTok 5, Instagram 5, Facebook 3. All OK.
- YouTube tags: 13, well under the limit, all relevant. OK.
- The hashtags appear both at the end of the description and in a separate HASHTAGS field. Make sure they are only pasted once.

## 6. AI wording and disclosure

- No slop words found. The text is plain and human.
- Handling disclosure through the platforms' own AI switches is honest, as long as they really get turned on. Add it as a checkbox on the upload checklist.
- Small point: "KJV, public domain" is not true in the UK, where the KJV is under Crown copyright. Use **"Scripture: King James Version (KJV)."**

## 7. Consistency

- The captions file matches the spoken words exactly.
- Only the "grown-ups/everyone" gap (point 1) and the "arms vs shoulders" gap (point 2) don't agree.
- **TikTok:** the brackets around "('Not now' is our short way…)" break the flow right after the hook. Move that line to the end of the caption.
