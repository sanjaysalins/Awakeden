# Red team (Fable seat) — ep16 upload copy, Mark 10:13-16

Checked clean: all long KJV quotes match Mark 10:13-16 word for word; YouTube title 62 chars; hashtag counts (5 / 5 / 3 / 6) and tags (~330 chars) inside limits; no AI wording in any public text; thumbnail text agrees with the film's last line; the picture is warm, fully clothed, nothing unsafe.

## Problems

1. **Pinned comment** — "What did Jesus do when His own followers said that to the children?" Two errors. Nobody says "not now" in Mark 10:13 (the YouTube description itself admits this). And the followers "rebuked those that brought them", not the children. Replace: "Has anyone ever told you 'not now'? What did Jesus do when His own followers tried to stop people bringing children to Him? Read it: Mark 10:13-16."

2. **TikTok + Instagram** — "Suffer the little children to come unto me" with no gloss. Machine translation turns "suffer" into pain ("make the children suffer"). YouTube and Facebook explain it; these two do not. Add right after the quote: "('Suffer' is old English for 'let'.)"

3. **TikTok + Instagram** — "Jesus was angry" given as bare fact. KJV says "much displeased" (Mark 10:14); "angry" is our word. TikTok's "Jesus was angry, with them, not with the children." is also broken grammar for translation. Replace (both): "The Bible says Jesus was "much displeased". Angry. Not with the children. With the followers who tried to stop them."

4. **Facebook** — "Then He said grown-ups must come the same way, like a little child." Verse 15 says "Whosoever" and "receive the kingdom of God", not "grown-ups" and "come". Replace: "Then He said everyone must receive God's kingdom the same way: like a little child." Same fix on **YouTube**: "Grown-ups come to God the same way" -> "Everyone receives God's kingdom the same way: like a little child."

5. **TikTok + Instagram** — 'not now' sits in quote marks beside real Scripture, with no note. It reads like a Bible quote. Add one line: "'Not now' is our short way to say it, not a Bible quote."

6. **Next teaser** — "a mother who would not take no for an answer". Idiom; fails for ESL and translation. It also promises a film; post it only if that film is real. Replace: "Next: a mother who kept asking Jesus for help and did not give up."

7. **Instagram** — "tried to stop children coming to Him". KJV: they stopped the people who brought them. Replace: "tried to stop people bringing children to Him" (matches TikTok).

8. **captions.srt** — bad splits. Cue 8 is the single word "them." for 0.37 s. Cues 15-16 and 25-26 break mid-phrase ("and / forbid", "put his / hands"). Re-split: "But His own close followers / "rebuked those that brought them."" ; ""Suffer the little children to come unto me, / and forbid them not:"" ; ""He took them up in his arms, / put his hands upon them, and blessed them.""

9. **captions.srt cue 25 + film** — quote opens with capital "He". KJV: "And he took them up in his arms". Use lower case "he" inside the quote marks.

10. **On-screen label** — "Let the children come to Me." tagged "Jesus · Mark 10:14". That is a paraphrase carrying a verse reference. Change the label to "Jesus, in plain words · Mark 10:14".

11. **Thumbnail** — "PICKED UP" can read as "chose" or "collected" to ESL readers, and Jesus carries a girl on His shoulders, which Mark 10:16 does not say ("in his arms"). Low risk; keep it only because it matches the film's last line. Safer text: "JESUS TOOK THEM IN HIS ARMS".

12. **YouTube description** — never says "disciples", the word people search. Change the first line to: "His own followers (the disciples) tried to stop people bringing children to Him".

13. **YouTube title** — "Why Jesus Got Angry": "Got" is casual and weak in translation. Use "Why Jesus Was Angry".
