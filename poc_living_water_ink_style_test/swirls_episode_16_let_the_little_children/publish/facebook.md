## TITLE
His Disciples Tried to Keep the Children Away. Jesus Was Angry.

## DESCRIPTION
Mark 10:13-16: people brought young children to Jesus, "that he should touch them." His disciples "rebuked those that brought them" (they told them to stop).

Jesus was "much displeased", angry, and said: "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God." (Here, 'suffer' means 'let'.)

Then He said: "Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a little child, he shall not enter therein." In plain words: everyone must receive God's kingdom like a little child.

And "he took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them."

The words 'not now' in the film are ours, not the Bible's.

Has someone told you 'not now'? Jesus did not send those children away. You can come to Him too.

Read it: Mark 10:13-16.
