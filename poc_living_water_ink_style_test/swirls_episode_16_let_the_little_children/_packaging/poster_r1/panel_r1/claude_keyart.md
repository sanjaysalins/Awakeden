## TITLES
1. **NOT NOW** ★ | Who was told "not now", and who said "now"? | Two hard words, like a thriller title. It hits the hook on its own.
2. **THE HAND** ★ | Whose hand: the one that blocks or the one that blesses? | One noun, mythic, like *The Mission*. It pays off twice in the film.
3. **Let Them Come** | Who was stopping them? | A command, like a war-film rallying cry.
4. **Forbid Them Not** | Forbid whom, and why did someone try? | Old-fashioned grandeur, prestige-drama feel.
5. **The Smallest Ones** | Why would the biggest man pick the smallest ones? | Warm and soft, an awards-season family film.
6. **Much Displeased** | What made gentle Jesus angry? | Odd, quiet and curious, a festival-darling title.

### THE WALL  (title: NOT NOW)
- **THE IDEA:** A wall of grown-up backs and robes fills the frame like a cliff. One raised flat palm stands huge against the sky. A tiny boy at the bottom looks up through a gap where gold light comes in.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The top 15% is warm sky. In the upper middle a giant raised palm in silhouette, rim-lit gold (a stop sign, not a fist). The middle is a dark wall of robes: deep umber, ochre, dusty rose. In the lower third, one narrow gap glows gold. Through it, far away and small, Jesus in a blue patched robe looks this way. At the very bottom a small boy from behind (pic 1) looks up, lit warm and calm, not scared. Wet watercolour edges, gouache highlights, paper grain.
- **LETTERING:** "NOT NOW" in big hand-cut serif across the palm, top 20%, about 30% of the width. Tagline at the bottom: *"He said: let them come."*
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Who is blocking him, and who is the man in the gap? The film answers it at 0:10 (the followers block) and 0:20 (Jesus is "much displeased").
- **PHONE TEST:** A dark mass, one gold slit, one blue dot, one raised hand. It reads as "kept out".
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 cinematic film poster, rich painted storybook in warm watercolor and gouache with ink line, dramatic late-afternoon backlight. Low angle from a small child's height: a towering wall of grown-ups' backs and robes in umber, ochre and dusty rose fills the frame. Above them one adult's raised flat open palm in a gentle stop gesture, silhouetted and rim-lit gold against a warm sky. Low in the wall one narrow gap glows golden. Through it, small and distant, Jesus with long dark hair and beard, in a patched blue robe with a rope belt, looks toward the viewer, kind. At the bottom a small boy seen from behind looks up, calm and hopeful, warmly lit. No menace, no evil faces, no halo, no rays behind the head. Paper texture, soft wet edges. Use pics 1 and 3 for the crowd, hand and boy, and pic 4 for Jesus. No lettering, no text.

### TWO HANDS  (title: THE HAND)
- **THE IDEA:** A vertical split. The top half is a hand held up to stop. The bottom half is a hand laid down to bless. The same gesture, flipped.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The top half is cool dusk blue-grey: a follower's raised palm, close and big, with stone lane edges. A torn-paper seam runs across the middle. The bottom half is molten gold: Jesus's hand (blue patched sleeve) resting on a little girl's dark hair, her face tipped up, smiling, eyes closed (pic 8). Gouache impasto in the gold, thin cool washes above.
- **LETTERING:** "THE HAND" sits small and bold on the torn seam, about 40% of the width. Tagline: *"One said stop."*
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Why do the two hands answer each other? The film pays it off at 0:35: "He put his hands upon them."
- **PHONE TEST:** Cold top, warm bottom, two big hand shapes. It reads in half a second.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 cinematic film poster, painted storybook in watercolor and gouache with ink line, split horizontally by a torn paper seam. Top half in cool blue-grey dusk: a close-up of an adult man's raised flat open palm in a "stop" gesture, plain linen sleeve, stone lane behind, calm and not angry. Bottom half in warm golden late-afternoon light: a close-up of Jesus's hand, his sleeve a patched blue robe, resting gently on a small smiling girl's dark hair, her eyes closed in peace. Strong contrast, rich impasto highlights, paper grain. No halo, no rays. Use pic 3 for the top hand and pic 8 for the blessing hand. No lettering, no text.

### THE OPEN ARMS  (title: LET THEM COME)
- **THE IDEA:** A giant single figure in negative space. Jesus, arms wide, forms a huge "Y" against a gold sky. The tiny children running to him are the only motion.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The top 60% is mostly empty warm sky, a gold-apricot wash. Jesus stands from behind and slightly low, arms open, blue robe glowing, filling the middle. At his feet, very small, children run up the lane toward him (pic 5). In the soft-focus foreground at the edges, two followers turn, one hand half-lowered, caught out but not cruel. There is an olive tree and an arch.
- **LETTERING:** "LET THEM COME" stacked in the empty sky, top 25%, very big. No tagline.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Who tried to stop them, and why is the man welcoming them? The followers in the corner pose the question. The film answers it at 0:10 to 0:25.
- **PHONE TEST:** A blue "Y" on gold, tiny dots running toward it. Pure shape.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 cinematic film poster, painted storybook in warm watercolor and gouache with ink line, golden-hour backlight. Vast empty apricot-gold sky across the top 60%. Jesus, long dark hair, in a patched blue robe with a rope belt, stands seen from behind, arms opened wide, large in the middle frame. Small joyful children run up a stone village lane toward him past an olive tree and an arch. At the soft-focus foreground edges, two of his followers in plain robes turn in surprise, one hand lowering, faces kind but caught out. Glowing rim light on the figures, no halo, no rays behind the head. Use pic 5 for the layout, pic 9 for Jesus and the arch, and pic 3 for the followers. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1 and why
**THE WALL (NOT NOW).** The block is the hero, the hook is the title, and at 2 cm the tiny boy facing that palm pulls the viewer in. It's honest: the film opens and closes on "not now."
