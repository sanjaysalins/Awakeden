## TITLES

1. **NOT NOW** ★ | Who said it, and to whom? | Two hard little words everyone has heard; reads like a drama title and is the film's own first and last line.
2. **LET THEM COME** ★ | Let who come, and who was stopping them? | A command, like a film's big line; plain words for the KJV verse.
3. **THE RAISED HAND** | Whose hand, and what did it stop? | Object titles sound like real films; it names the poster's image.
4. **WHO HE PICKED UP** | Who? And who did he pass over? | Sounds like a mystery; it is the film's last line.
5. **THE DAY JESUS WAS ANGRY** | Angry at whom? | Event title; pairs with the YouTube title.
6. **DO NOT STOP THEM** | Stop who, from what? | Urgent, simple, a warning.

### 1. THE HAND  (title: NOT NOW)
- **THE IDEA:** One huge raised palm fills the frame, and through the gap between its fingers we see a small blue figure turning to look.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** Child's-eye view, low camera. Top two thirds: a follower's open flat hand, palm toward us, large, in warm shadow (ochre, umber), soft edge, not a fist, not a claw. His face is out of frame or a soft blur, calm, not evil. Between thumb and first finger, far down the sunlit stone lane: Jesus in the patched blue robe, small but sharp, head turned toward us, serious. Bottom edge: the tops of three children's heads from behind, looking up, curious, one on tiptoe. Palette: gold light, brown hand, one hit of blue. Thick gouache, visible paper grain.
- **LETTERING:** Title across the palm itself, very big, two stacked words, about 40% of width height. Tagline at the bottom: "Then Jesus saw it."
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** What does the man in blue do about that hand? Answered at "He was much displeased" and the lift into his arms.
- **PHONE TEST:** A big hand, a small blue man. "Stop" reads in every language.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 painted film poster, rich cinematic storybook, warm watercolor and gouache with ink line, golden late-afternoon light. Low child's-eye camera. A large open flat hand of a bearded man in a brown tunic fills the upper two thirds of the frame, palm facing the viewer in a calm "stop" gesture, in soft warm shadow, five fingers, natural anatomy, the man's face out of frame. Through the gap between thumb and first finger, far down a sunlit stone village lane with an olive tree, stands Jesus, small and in sharp focus: long dark hair, beard, patched blue robe with rope belt, head turned toward the viewer, serious and displeased but not cruel. At the bottom edge, the backs of three small children's heads looking up, curious and calm. Strong depth, dark warm foreground, bright gold background, a single accent of blue. No halo, no light rays behind the head. Use film pictures 3 (hand, followers, lane) and 4 (Jesus's face and robe) as references. No lettering, no text.

### 2. THE WALL  (title: LET THEM COME)
- **THE IDEA:** Scale. A wall of tall grown-up backs, one narrow gap, a strip of blue in the gap, and one small boy looking up at it.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** Top 70%: giant backs in ochre, tan and rust robes, shoulder to shoulder, cropped above the heads so they read as a wall, not a mob. Dead centre: a thin bright slit of gold light between two robes, and in it a sliver of blue robe and one open hand held out. Bottom: a small barefoot boy in a white tunic, seen from behind, on tiptoe, his mother's hand holding his at frame edge so he is not alone. Mood is hopeful, not dark. Long warm shadows on stone.
- **LETTERING:** Title low, under the boy's feet, wide, medium-large. No tagline, or "They tried to stop them."
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Does he get through, and who is holding out that hand? Answered when the children run to Jesus.
- **PHONE TEST:** Brown wall, bright slit, tiny white figure. Big versus small reads instantly.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 painted film poster, rich cinematic storybook, warm watercolor and gouache with ink line, golden late-afternoon light. A wall of tall adults seen from behind in ochre, tan and rust first-century robes fills the top two thirds, shoulder to shoulder, cropped above the shoulders. In the exact centre, a narrow bright gap between two robes glows with gold light; inside the gap, a sliver of a patched blue robe with rope belt and one open welcoming hand. In the lower third, a small barefoot boy with brown hair and a short white tunic, seen from behind, stands on tiptoe looking up at the gap, calm and hopeful, his hand held by his mother's hand entering from the right edge. Stone village lane, long soft shadows. Hopeful mood, no menace. Use film pictures 1 (boy and wall of backs) and 5 (Jesus's robe and open arms) as references. No lettering, no text.

### 3. THE LOOK  (title: WHO HE PICKED UP)
- **THE IDEA:** One face, two feelings: a laughing child safe in his arms while his eyes go hard at someone off-frame.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** Tight portrait. Jesus holds a laughing small boy against his chest, the boy's arms round his neck, eyes shut in joy. Jesus's arms are gentle, but his head is turned, brow set, eyes fixed past the frame edge at someone we cannot see. Across the lower left corner falls the long shadow of a lowered hand on the stone. Light: warm gold on the boy, cooler side light on Jesus's face. Deep blue robe as the big colour block. Stone arch, olive leaves, dark top.
- **LETTERING:** Title at the top in the dark area, large, three stacked lines. Tagline: "Not angry at the child."
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Who is he looking at, and what did they do? Answered at "They tried to stop them."
- **PHONE TEST:** A happy child and a stern face in one hug. The clash is the hook.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 painted film poster, rich cinematic storybook, warm watercolor and gouache with ink line, dramatic late-afternoon light. Tight portrait of Jesus, long dark hair, beard, patched blue robe with rope belt, holding a laughing small brown-haired boy safe against his chest, the boy's arms around his neck, the boy's eyes closed in joy. Jesus's hold is gentle, but his head is turned and his eyes look past the edge of the frame, serious and displeased, not cruel, not looking at the child. Warm gold light on the boy, cooler side light on Jesus's face. On the stone ground in the lower left, the long soft shadow of a lowered hand. Stone arch and olive branches behind, dark upper area. No halo, no light rays behind the head. Use film pictures 7 (the hug, the boy) and 4 (the serious face) as references. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1: THE HAND (NOT NOW)
A raised palm means "stop" to every person on earth, with no English needed, and it still reads at 2 cm.
It shows the block and the one who will break it in a single shape, and it sits perfectly beside "Why Jesus Was Angry."
