# EVIDENCE PACK: one vertical short film (YouTube Shorts / Reels / TikTok), about 50 seconds

LOOK: warm hand-drawn storybook. Ink line with soft watercolor and gouache, golden late-afternoon light, stone village lanes, olive trees, arches. Jesus wears a patched blue robe with a rope belt. Felt-piano music, gentle.
For the POSTER you may go richer and more cinematic than the film (painted, dramatic light, film-poster craft) - but the characters must match.

THE FILM, in order (KJV quotes in quote marks):
- "Has someone pushed you away? Told you 'not now'? This story is for you."
- People were bringing young children to Jesus. They wanted one thing: "that he should touch them."
- But His own close followers "rebuked those that brought them." They tried to stop them.
- Then Jesus saw it. The Bible says He was "much displeased." Angry. Not with the children. With the ones who tried to stop them.
- Jesus: "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God."
- In plain words: let them come. Do not stop them. God's kingdom belongs to people like these children, and grown-ups must receive it like a little child.
- "He took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them." The people came for a touch. He gave the children His arms.
- End: "Someone told you 'not now.' Look at who Jesus picked up."

YOUTUBE TITLE ALREADY CHOSEN: "Why Jesus Was Angry". Current thumbnail words: "They Tried to Stop Them". (You may propose a different movie title for the poster.)

FILM PICTURES THAT EXIST (references for new art; characters must match):
1. A small boy alone at the edge of a crowd of grown-ups' backs in a village lane, looking up.
2. Families walking a sunny olive-tree road carrying small children (a father with a boy on his shoulders, a grandmother holding a girl's hand).
3. Two of Jesus's followers in a stone lane, one with a raised flat palm "stop", blocking a mother with a toddler and a father with two small boys.
4. Close-up of Jesus, serious, displeased (not cruel), long dark hair, beard, blue patched robe.
5. From behind: children running toward Jesus down a lane; He stands with arms open, smiling.
6. An old woman kneeling, holding a small girl's hands under an arch.
7. Jesus lifting and hugging a laughing small boy, both eyes closed in joy.
8. Jesus kneeling, his hand on a little girl's head, blessing her; other children watch.
9. The owner's favourite still: Jesus smiling, carrying two laughing girls (one on his shoulders, one on his hip), stone arch and olive tree behind.

AUDIENCE: global; many viewers have English as a second language and know no Bible. Most see the poster at ~2 cm wide while scrolling.
HARD RULES: honest to the film (no fake shock, no claim the film does not make); Jesus's anger is at the blockers, never at a child, and never cruel or violent; children are never frightened, hurt, crying in terror or in danger; no child shown alone in a menacing or exploitative way; the followers are wrong but not villains (no evil faces); no halo / sunburst rays centred behind Jesus's head; short common words; no "suffer" as a load-bearing word (it means "let").
