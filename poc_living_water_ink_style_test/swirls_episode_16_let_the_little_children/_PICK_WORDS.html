The words (157 words, about 65 s)

Has someone told you "not now"? Then stay. This story is yours.

People were bringing young children to Jesus. They wanted one thing: "that he should touch them."

But His own disciples "rebuked those that brought them." They sent them away.

Then Jesus saw it. And the Bible says He was "much displeased." Angry. Not with the children. With the ones who sent them away.

JESUS: "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God."

In plain words: let them come. Do not stop them. God's kingdom belongs to people like these children. Anyone who comes in, comes the way a little child does.

And here is what He did.

"He took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them."

They asked for a touch. He gave the children His arms.

Someone told you "not now." Look at who Jesus picked up.

4 questions

Who for: someone told "not now", who feels too small to matter.
Take-away: Jesus was angry at the people who pushed the children away, and gave more than was asked: His arms.
Curiosity gap: they asked for one touch and were sent away. What did Jesus do? (Pays off: His arms.)
Global: "suffer" = "let" is explained right away. No hard word carries the ending.
