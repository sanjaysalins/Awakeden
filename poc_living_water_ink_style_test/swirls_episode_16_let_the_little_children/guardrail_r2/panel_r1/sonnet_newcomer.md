## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
A 30-year-old woman on her phone, tired, who was just told "not now" at work or at home. She stops because of line 1 ("Has someone told you 'not now'?"). She finishes because the pictures are warm and the story is short. She shares it with a friend who feels pushed away (line 10). Not served: people who want facts or history. Also not served: people who do not know who "Jesus" is, because the film never says (lines 2-5).

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus does not push you away; He picks you up." Lines 9-10 carry it. It is fairly clear, but I had to guess how the "not now" in line 1 connects to the children in line 3. The film does not say that the followers' "stop" is the same as my "not now."

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Jesus, a man people respect. What: people bring children, some other people block them, Jesus is angry at the blockers, then He hugs the children. Why: so I know I am not rejected.
Where I got lost:
- Line 2: "that he should touch them." Why touch? I guess it is a blessing, but I am not told.
- Line 3: "His own close followers 'rebuked'." I do not know "rebuked" or "followers." I guessed "workers who shouted."
- Line 4: "much displeased." Confusing, but "Angry" right after helps.
- Line 5: "Suffer the little children." In my language "suffer" means pain. I thought Jesus wanted the children to hurt. This is the worst problem. Line 6 repairs it, but only after I was confused. "forbid" and "kingdom of God" are also unclear.
- Line 6: "the kingdom of God belongs to people like these children." What is that? Is God a king? I do not know. "receive it like a gift" is clear.
- Line 8: "blessed" is not explained.
- Line 1: "Keep watching. This story is for you." Good hook.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. What I was TOLD but could not follow: who the "followers" are (line 3); what "blessed" means (line 8); what "kingdom of God" means (lines 5-6); why Jesus is "much displeased" at being stopped (line 4); why "suffer" is used (line 5); and who is speaking line 5 until the voice changed.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would say plainly, in one short phrase, who Jesus is and who the "followers" are, and would put the plain meaning of "Suffer the little children" BEFORE the old words, or use a modern version of the quote. It would also tie "not now" to the followers' "stop" out loud. The ONE highest-value change: fix line 5 so a second-language viewer does not hear "suffer" as pain. Score: 6/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1 ("Has someone told you 'not now'?"). Pays off at line 10 ("Look at who Jesus picked up"), with a lift at line 9. Score: 7/10. Worth the wait, because the last picture (two laughing girls) delivers the feeling. The payoff is more emotional than logical.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 5: add before it, as the narrator: "Jesus said: let the children come." Then keep the quote. Or replace "Suffer the little children to come unto me" with "Let the little children come to me."
2. Line 3: replace "His own close followers 'rebuked those that brought them.'" with "His own close helpers 'rebuked' them, which means they told them off. 'Go away.'"
3. Line 8 (add after it): "Blessed means He asked God to be good to them."
