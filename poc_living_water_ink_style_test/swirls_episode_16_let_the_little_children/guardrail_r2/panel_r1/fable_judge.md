## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR (one named viewer; who stops, who finishes, who shares; who is NOT served)
Named viewer: a tired mother, 34, loosely Christian, who feels pushed to the edge of church and of life. She stops on line 1 ("not now" is her week), finishes because the film is short and warm, and shares the last picture with line 9.
- Stops: anyone who has felt brushed aside; parents; Sunday-school teachers.
- Finishes: most who pass line 4. "Angry" is the real hook, more than line 1.
- Shares: parents and children's workers.
- NOT served: the total newcomer who does not know who Jesus is or why His welcome matters (never said). Also the sceptic: line 10 gives a picture, not a reason.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)
"People tried to keep the children away, and Jesus got angry and picked them up, so He will not push me away either."
Line 9 carries the story half and is the best line in the film. The "me" half rests on line 10, which only hints. Clear for a churchgoer. Half-clear for a newcomer, who must make the jump from "children" to "me" alone. Line 6 tries to build that bridge but hides it at the end of a crowded line.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)
Who: Jesus, His followers, parents with small children. What: the followers block the children; Jesus is angry, calls them in, holds them. Why: God's kingdom is for people like them.
Lost or misled:
- Line 1, "not now": not now for what? It is never tied to anything. And nobody in the story says "not now"; the followers just stop people.
- Line 5, "Suffer": in modern English this means pain. A second-language viewer hears "make the children suffer." Line 6 repairs it, but only after the damage.
- Line 3, "rebuked": rare word; "They tried to stop them" saves it.
- Line 6: four ideas in one line. "Receives a gift" arrives too fast to land.
- Line 10, "picked up": in translation can mean "chose" or "collected."
- Line 1, "Keep watching": a command, not a reason.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
Source is Mark 10:13-16 (KJV).
- Line 2, "that he should touch them": RIGHT, verbatim.
- Line 3, "rebuked those that brought them": RIGHT, verbatim. "His own close followers" = "his disciples": RIGHT.
- Line 4, "much displeased": RIGHT, verbatim. "Angry": RIGHT but at the strong end; "indignant" is the closer sense. "Not with the children. With the ones who tried to stop them": RIGHT (He "said unto them").
- Line 5: RIGHT, verbatim.
- Line 6, "belongs to people like these children": RIGHT ("of such"). "We receive it like a little child receives a gift": OVERCLAIMED, mildly. Verse 15 says receive the kingdom "as a little child"; "gift" is added, and the verse's warning ("shall not enter therein") is dropped.
- Line 8: RIGHT, verbatim (opening "And" dropped).
- Line 9: RIGHT. A fair observation from verses 13 and 16.
- Lines 1 and 10, "not now": UNSUPPORTED as a quote. The text gives no words and no reason for the rebuke. It is application, not Scripture; acceptable if never presented as what they said.
- Last picture: two girls, one on His shoulders: UNSUPPORTED detail (text says "young children," "in his arms"). Harmless.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE (what would the best explainer in the world do differently; the ONE highest-value change; score 0-10 against that bar)
The best version would say the viewer's bridge out loud, once, in Jesus's own plain words, and would open on the anger, which is the surprise.
ONE change: rewrite line 10 so the viewer hears that the welcome is for them, not just sees a picture.
Score: 7/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP (opens at line 1, pays off at line 9-10; score 0-10; worth the wait?)
Opens at line 1, re-opens well at line 7, pays off at lines 8-9, and only half-closes at line 10. Score 6/10. Worth the wait for the story; the personal promise of line 1 ("This story is for you") is under-paid.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES (line number + exact replacement wording)
1. Line 10: "Someone told you 'not now.' Jesus said: let them come. Look at who He took in His arms."
2. Line 6: "In plain words: let them come. Do not stop them. God's kingdom belongs to people like these children. And grown-ups must receive it the same way: like a little child."
3. Line 1: "Has someone pushed you away? Told you 'not now'? This story is for you."
