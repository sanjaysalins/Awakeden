Jesus and the little children in the Bible (Mark 10:13-16): His own followers tried to stop people bringing children to Him, and Jesus was angry.

"Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God." In old English, "suffer" means "let". In plain words: let the children come, do not stop them. The kingdom of God belongs to people like them.

The Bible's words are "much displeased". In one plain word: angry. Not with the children. With the ones who tried to stop them.

He also said: "Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a little child, he shall not enter therein." Grown-ups come to God the same way: like a little child.

"Not now" in the film is our short way to say it. It is not a Bible quote. The Bible says the followers "rebuked those that brought them."
