Little Children: listen

Has someone pushed you away? Told you "not now"? This story is for you.
People were bringing young children to Jesus. They wanted one thing: "that he should touch them."
But His own close followers "rebuked those that brought them." They tried to stop them.
Then Jesus saw it. And the Bible says He was "much displeased." Angry. Not with the children. With the ones who tried to stop them.
JESUS: "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God."
In plain words: let them come. Do not stop them. God's kingdom belongs to people like these children. And grown-ups must receive it the same way: like a little child.
And here is what He did.
"He took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them."
The people came for a touch. He gave the children His arms.
Someone told you "not now." Look at who Jesus picked up.
