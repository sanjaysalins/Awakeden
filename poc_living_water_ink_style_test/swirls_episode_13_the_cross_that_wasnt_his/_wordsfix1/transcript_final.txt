The man who would carry the cross of Jesus was walking the other way. Mark says the soldiers led him out to crucify him. And they compel one Simon, a Cyrenian, who passed by coming out of the country. Jesus out of the city. Simon in from the country. Same road. They put the cross on Simon's back. Nobody asked him. Luke says on him they laid the cross that he might bear it after Jesus. Simon turns and walks out too. Now he is through the gate. Long before, in God's law, the animal offered for sin was burned outside the camp. Then it says of Jesus, Wherefore Jesus also that he might sanctify the people with his own blood suffered without the gate. Out there where Simon is walking. Hebrews turns to you. Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach. Reproach means shame. Simon was forced out there. You are invited. If shame has put you outside, Jesus is already there. The dying, the finishing was his alone. You only come to him.
