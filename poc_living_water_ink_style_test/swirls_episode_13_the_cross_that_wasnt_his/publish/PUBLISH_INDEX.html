TITLE
The Man Who Carried Jesus' Cross Was Walking the Other Way
DESCRIPTION
Hebrews 13 says Jesus "suffered without the gate." But the first man to walk outside that gate carrying a cross wasn't Jesus.

Mark says the soldiers "compel one Simon a Cyrenian, who passed by, coming out of the country." The cross meant for Jesus went onto Simon's own shoulders. Nobody asked him.

Then Hebrews turns the same invitation toward us: "Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach."

Simon carried it forced. We're only asked to carry the shame, freely this time. The atoning was His alone, with His own blood.

TITLE
The Cross Simon Never Chose to Carry
DESCRIPTION
Hebrews 13 ties an ordinary man's forced errand straight to the gospel: "Wherefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people with his own blood, suffered without the gate."

That man was Simon of Cyrene. He wasn't walking toward Jesus. Mark says the soldiers "compel" him, coming in from the country, and put the cross on his shoulders. Nobody asked him.

Hebrews then turns the same invitation toward us: "Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach."

Simon carried it forced. We're only asked to carry the shame, freely this time. The atoning was His alone.
