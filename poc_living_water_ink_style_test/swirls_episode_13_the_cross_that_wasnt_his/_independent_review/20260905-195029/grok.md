**Doctrinal soundness — orthodox spine, one real overclaim.**

Hebrews 13:11–13 (bodies burned *without the camp* → Jesus suffered *without the gate* → *go forth… unto him*) is the letter’s own argument. Not a hot take. Simon-as-illustration of 13:13 is *your* glue. Hebrews never names him. Preach it as analogy. Do not sell it as the text’s own join.

**The new slogan is the miss:** *“Outside is where the atonement was made.”*

You just said the blood *“was carried into the sanctuary for sin.”* That is the Levitical atonement act (blood *in*). Hebrews 13:11 then says the *bodies* are burned *without the camp*. The letter locates Jesus’ *suffering* outside the gate, *“that he might sanctify the people with his own blood.”* It does not say the atonement rite happened outside. Your slogan flips the map you just drew. A careful ear hears a contradiction. A pulpit cliché is not a license.

Paraphrase drift in the same beat:
- KJV: *“the bodies of those beasts… are burned without the camp.”*
- You: *“The offerings… were burned outside the camp, never brought back to where the people lived.”*  
  Offerings ≠ bodies. *Never brought back to where the people lived* is not in the verse.

Landing vs the verse you quoted:
- Quote: *“bearing his reproach”* — *we* go out bearing it.
- You: *“the one who already carried the shame for you.”*  
  Substitution is true. It is not what 13:13 is doing in that sentence. You quote the cost, then overwrite it with *he already did it*. First hearing cannot hold both.

*“You are not forced”* recasts a corporate exhortation to believers (*Let us go forth*) as a conversion invitation with no cost. Grace-anchored, yes. Hebrews-accurate, no.
