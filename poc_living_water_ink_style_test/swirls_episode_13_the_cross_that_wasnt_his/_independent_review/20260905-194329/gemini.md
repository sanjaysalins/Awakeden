TOP FIXES:
1. The transition "Why outside the city? Hebrews gives the reason." is slightly abrupt. You could explicitly state that Simon was forced to turn around and walk *back outside the city gates* to bridge the physical narrative to the Hebrews theology more seamlessly.
2. The phrase "Wrong road, wrong minute" feels a touch modern/colloquial compared to the gravitas of the surrounding text. Consider a slight elevation in tone (e.g., "An unexpected collision of paths").
3. For a listener with zero Bible knowledge, "the sin offering" might benefit from a tiny clarifier (e.g., "the ancient sin offering") so they immediately grasp it as an Old Testament law reference.
