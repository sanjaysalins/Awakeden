# Two candidate scripts (voices in brackets; (pause) = a beat of silence)

## Script X
[narrator] A man climbed a tree to look for Jesus. He did not know Jesus was looking for him.
[narrator] Zacchaeus was a rich chief tax collector in the town of Jericho. The crowd was in his way, and he was "little of stature."
[narrator] Jesus came to that place, "looked up, and saw him," and said his name first.
[jesus] Zacchaeus, make haste, and come down; for to day I must abide at thy house.
[narrator] They all murmured:
[crowd] That he was gone to be guest with a man that is a sinner.
[narrator] Jesus went to his house anyway. (pause) Only then did Zacchaeus stand and say:
[zacchaeus] Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor.
[jesus] This day is salvation come to this house. For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.
[narrator] He came to look. Jesus came to find. He died for our sins, and rose again the third day.

## Script Y
[narrator] A rich man wanted one look at Jesus. But who was really looking for whom?
[narrator] Zacchaeus, chief tax collector in the town of Jericho. The crowd blocked him, and he was "little of stature." So he ran ahead and climbed a tree.
[narrator] Jesus came to that place. He looked up, saw him, and called his name.
[jesus] Zacchaeus, make haste, and come down; for to day I must abide at thy house.
[crowd] That he was gone to be guest with a man that is a sinner.
[narrator] They all murmured. Jesus stayed with him anyway. (pause)
[zacchaeus] Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor.
[narrator] The giving came after. Jesus came first.
[jesus] This day is salvation come to this house. For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.
[narrator] He thought he was the seeker. Jesus was. He died for our sins, and rose again the third day.
