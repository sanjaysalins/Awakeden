# Seat: fable (blind, read only Q.md, candidates.md, KJV.txt)

## 1. Newcomer (no Bible, English second language)

- **Who:** Yes. A short, rich tax man named Zacchaeus, and Jesus.
- **What:** Yes. He climbs a tree to see Jesus. Jesus sees him, says his name, goes to his house. The man gives half his goods to the poor.
- **Why:** Mostly. "Jesus came to find him" is clear. Why Jesus died is said in one fast line at the very end.

Lines I would not fully understand:

- "make haste ... I must abide at thy house" - old words. I only work it out from the next narrator line ("Jesus went to his house anyway").
- "They all murmured" - "murmured" is not a common word. I would not know it means "complained".
- "That he was gone to be guest with a man that is a sinner" - odd grammar, starts mid-sentence.
- "a man people called a sinner" - I am not told WHY a tax collector is hated. So the crowd's anger has no cause.
- "the Son of man" - nothing tells me this is Jesus speaking about himself.
- "salvation" and "the lost" - not explained, but the last narrator line half-covers it.

## 2. Bible reader

- **Quotes:** All exact KJV, checked word by word against Luke 19:3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10.
  - "little of stature" - exact.
  - "looked up, and saw him" - exact.
  - "Zacchaeus, make haste, and come down; for to day I must abide at thy house." - exact.
  - "That he was gone to be guest with a man that is a sinner." - exact.
  - "Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor" - exact words, but the verse is cut at a semicolon and shown with a full stop. The "restore him fourfold" half is dropped.
  - "This day is salvation come to this house" - exact words, cut before "forsomuch as he also is a son of Abraham".
  - "For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost." - exact.
- **Added / inferred (narrator voice, not quoted):**
  - "said his name first" - fair. Jesus speaks before Zacchaeus says anything.
  - "the crowd blocked his view" - fair for "could not for the press".
  - "a man people called a sinner" - fair, from verse 7.
  - "Jesus went to his house anyway" - fair, implied by verses 6-7. But it drops "received him joyfully", which is the man's glad response.
  - "He did not know Jesus was looking for him" - a small inference, supported by verse 10. Fine.
  - Closing "Jesus died for our sins, and rose again the third day" - matches 1 Corinthians 15:3-4, not in quote marks. Sound.
- **Works-based?** No. "Jesus came first. The giving came after." is a clear guard. Small risk remains: the ear hears "I give to the poor" and then straight away "This day is salvation". The guard line is what stops that. Keep it.
- **Blame on a people group?** None. "They all murmured" names only the crowd, same as the text.
- **Wrong?** Nothing wrong found.

## 3. Hook

- First line makes me stop: a grown man up a tree is a strong picture, and it suits the ink-comic style.
- The gap ("He did not know Jesus was looking for him") pays off twice: Jesus says his name, and the last line mirrors it ("Zacchaeus came to look. Jesus came to find him.").
- Weak spot: the name "Jesus" is used twice in the first 3 seconds, but the man is unnamed and has no stake yet (rich? hated?). The stake comes in line 2.

**First 3 seconds: 8 / 10**

## 4. Overall

**7.5 / 10**

Good: true to the text, clean one-thread arc (look / find), lands on Jesus, grace before giving.
Holds it back: three KJV lines in a row with old words and no plain gloss; no reason given for the crowd's anger; "Son of man" not tied to Jesus; the death-and-rising line feels added on at the end, and there is no line turned to the viewer.
Length: about 170 words plus two pauses and four voices. That is tight for 60 seconds.

## 5. Three most important line fixes

1. Say why he was hated.
   - Old: `Zacchaeus was a rich chief tax collector in Jericho, a man people called a sinner.`
   - New: `Zacchaeus was the chief tax collector in Jericho. He was rich. People called him a sinner.`
   - (Shorter sentences. "Rich" beside "tax collector" lets a newcomer guess the reason, without claiming he stole. The text only says "if".)

2. Give the old words a plain gloss before the crowd speaks.
   - Old: `They all murmured:`
   - New: `Jesus asked to stay at his house. The crowd complained:`
   - (Explains "make haste / abide" and replaces "murmured" in narrator voice. The KJV quote stays exact.)

3. Tie "Son of man" to Jesus, and make the pause do work.
   - Old: `(pause)` between the two Jesus lines
   - New: `[narrator] Then Jesus said why he came:`
   - (A newcomer now knows "the Son of man" is Jesus, and "come to seek" answers the hook.)

Optional, if words allow: cut "He was short," - "little of stature" plus the tree picture already says it. This buys back time for fix 2 and 3.
