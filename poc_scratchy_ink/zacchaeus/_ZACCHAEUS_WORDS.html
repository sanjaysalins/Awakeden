Zacchaeus — the words

Luke 19:1-10. Angle A: "Who was looking for whom?" ~65 s. $0.

NARRATOR A man climbed a tree to look for Jesus. He did not know Jesus was looking for him.

NARRATOR Zacchaeus was the chief tax collector in Jericho. He was rich. People called him a sinner. He was too short to see past the crowd.

NARRATOR Jesus came to that place, "looked up, and saw him," and said his name first.

JESUS "Zacchaeus, make haste, and come down; for to day I must abide at thy house."

NARRATOR Jesus said He would stay at his house. The crowd complained:

CROWD "That he was gone to be guest with a man that is a sinner."

NARRATOR Jesus went in anyway. (pause) Jesus came first. The giving came after. Zacchaeus stood and said:

ZACCHAEUS "Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor."

JESUS "This day is salvation come to this house."

NARRATOR Then Jesus said why He came:

JESUS "For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost."

NARRATOR Zacchaeus came to look. Jesus came to find him. To save the lost, Jesus died for our sins, and rose again the third day.

Who for: anyone judged or written off. Take-away: Jesus looks for us first; the change comes after. Gap: who was really looking? Global: plain words; old KJV words are explained.

Still weak: "salvation" not explained · "received him joyfully" and "fourfold" left out for time.

My pick: lock these words. Locking does not spend money.
