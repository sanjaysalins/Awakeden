Zacchaeus — pick an angle

Luke 19:1-10. 4 blind seats: Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Codex. $0.

A. Who was looking for whom? (all 4 seats; picked by Opus + Codex)

Opens: He climbed a tree to look at Jesus. He did not know Jesus had come to look for him.

Turn: he "sought to see Jesus" (19:3). Jesus stops, looks up, says his name first. Last verse: "the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost" (19:10).

Ends: He died for our sins and rose again, and He is still the one doing the looking.

B. The blame changed sides (all 4 seats; Fable's + Sonnet's pick)

Opens: A whole crowd got angry at Jesus. All He did was choose whose house to stay in.

Turn: "they all murmured... a man that is a sinner" (19:7). The complaint lands on Jesus. He took one man's bad name, then took far more on a cross.

C. Two rich men, one page apart (3 seats)

Opens: Jesus said it was nearly impossible. A few verses later, it happened. (camel and needle, Luke 18:25-27)

My pick: A, with B as the middle line. Jesus searching + the crowd's "sinner" landing on Him. Grace first, giving after (no "earn it").
