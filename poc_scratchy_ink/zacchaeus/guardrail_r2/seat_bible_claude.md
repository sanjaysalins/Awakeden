## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** Ama, 34, in Accra. She went to Sunday school as a child and has since drifted. She believes she has to clean up her life before God will want her.
- **Who stops:** line 1 grabs her. A man climbs a tree to see Jesus, and Jesus turns out to be the one looking.
- **Who finishes:** someone who has been told they are "a sinner" (line 2, line 6).
- **Who shares:** church youth leaders and small-group hosts. I would send it to my church group after the line 10 fix.
- **Not served:** a viewer who asks "lost from *what*?" Also anyone who notices that Jesus' actual answer to the grumbling, "he also is a son of Abraham" (v9), has been cut.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus came for Zacchaeus first, and the change came after." It is carried by line 8 and sealed by line 14. Yes, it is clear. Line 8 is the best line in the film.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
**In my own words:** a rich tax boss nobody liked (who) climbs a tree to see Jesus. Jesus invites himself to his house (what). Jesus says he came to find lost people (why).
- Line 5 handles the KJV in line 4 well: "make haste" becomes "hurry" and "abide" becomes "stay".
- Line 13: newcomers will not know what "Son of man" means. Being told it *is* Jesus helps, but it still reads like a title with no meaning.
- Line 9: "the half of my goods" is clunky for second-language listeners. Line 10 rescues it.
- Line 12 defines "salvation" in plain words. Good.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **L1** "climbed a tree to see Jesus": **RIGHT** (v4). "Jesus was looking for him": **RIGHT, as theology** from "to seek" (v10). The scene itself only says "he looked up" (v5).
- **L2** "chief tax collector": **RIGHT** ("chief among the publicans", v2). "at Jericho": **RIGHT** (v1). "Tax men often took extra money": **UNSUPPORTED** by Luke 19 itself. It is fair background and hinted at by v8, but it is not in this text. "rich": **RIGHT** (v2). "called a sinner": **RIGHT** (v7). "too short to see over the crowd": **RIGHT** ("could not for the press… little of stature", v3).
- **L3** "looked up, and called him by name": **RIGHT** (v5).
- **L4**: word-for-word **RIGHT** (v5).
- **L5** "Today I will stay": **WEAKENED**. The KJV says "I **must** abide." That "must" is the text's own proof of line 1, and it is lost here. "came down fast, full of joy": **RIGHT** ("made haste… received him joyfully", v6).
- **L6** "The crowd grumbled… a sinner's house": **RIGHT**. The text says "they **all** murmured" (v7). "Jesus went anyway": **RIGHT** ("gone to be guest", v7).
- **L8** "Jesus came first. The change came after": **RIGHT on the order of events** (v5, then v8). It is a mainstream reading. Note that "I give… I restore" is present tense, and some readers take it as what he already does. "stood": **RIGHT** (v8).
- **L9**: word-for-word **RIGHT** (v8).
- **L10** "Half of all he had": **RIGHT**. "four times back to anyone he cheated": **OVERCLAIMED**. The KJV is conditional: "**if** I have taken any thing… by false accusation." The narrator turns the "if" into an admission. Fourfold is also the sheep-theft penalty in Exodus 22:1, which explains why he chose four.
- **L11**: word-for-word **RIGHT** (v9), but cut short. The "son of Abraham" clause that answers line 6 is missing.
- **L12**: a sound gloss that fits "bring again that which was lost" (Ezek 34:16). **RIGHT.**
- **L13–14**: line 14 is word-for-word **RIGHT** (v10).
- **L15** "Zacchaeus came to look": **RIGHT** ("sought to see", v3). "died for our sins, and rose again the third day": **RIGHT** (1 Cor 15:3-4). "Later" is accurate: Jerusalem follows in Luke 19:28.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would make the word **"must"** do the work. The hook says "Jesus was looking for him," and "I must abide" (v5) is the evidence in the text itself. Right now line 5 throws that word away.
- **Highest-value change:** in line 5, say "Today I *must* stay at your house." Then add a beat: "Must. He had planned it."
- **Score: 7/10.**

## CURIOSITY GAP
- Opens at line 1 ("But Jesus was looking for him").
- Pays off at line 14, then echoes at line 15.
- **Score: 7/10.** Worth the wait. It would be a 9 if line 5 paid it off early with "must".
