## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** Grace, 34. She grew up near church, now drifts, and feels "too far gone." The hook (line 1) stops her. The turn ("Jesus came first," line 9) keeps her watching. The last line (15) is the one she would share.
**Not served:** a total newcomer who doesn't know why a tax collector counts as a sinner. Line 2 states it but never explains it.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus came looking for me before I changed." Line 9 and lines 14-15 carry it. It is clear. Line 9 is the strongest line in the film.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
**Who / what / why:** A rich, disliked tax man climbs a tree to see Jesus. Jesus calls him by name and stays at his house. The man changes, and Jesus says He came to save the lost.
**Where viewers get lost or misled:**
- **Line 2, "People called him a sinner."** We never learn why. In many countries tax officials are just civil servants. Without the fourfold line, his "sin" is only gossip from the crowd.
- **Line 4, "make haste... abide... thy."** These words fail in translation. Line 5 does rescue them.
- **Line 6 opens with "That he was..."** Spoken alone, it sounds broken.
- **Line 14, "Son of man"** is never explained. Newcomers will read it as "a human."

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **L1 "climbed a tree to look for Jesus":** RIGHT (v3-4: "sought to see Jesus... climbed up into a sycomore tree to see him").
- **L1 "He did not know Jesus was looking for him":** OVERCLAIMED. The text never says what Zacchaeus knew. It's a fair inference from v10, but it is an inference.
- **L2 "chief tax collector in Jericho":** slightly OVERCLAIMED. v2 says "chief among the publicans." It doesn't say "of Jericho." Better: "at Jericho."
- **L2 "He was rich":** RIGHT (v2).
- **L2 "People called him a sinner":** RIGHT (v7).
- **L2 "too short to see past the crowd":** RIGHT (v3, "could not for the press... little of stature").
- **L3 "looked up, and saw him":** word-for-word, RIGHT (v5).
- **L3 "said his name first":** RIGHT (v5). The first word is "Zacchaeus."
- **L4 quote:** word-for-word, RIGHT (v5).
- **L5 "came down fast, full of joy":** RIGHT (v6, "made haste... received him joyfully").
- **L5 "The crowd complained":** RIGHT (v7, "they all murmured").
- **L6 quote:** word-for-word, RIGHT (v7).
- **L7 "Jesus went in anyway":** RIGHT in substance (v7, "gone to be guest").
- **L9 "Jesus came first. The giving came after":** RIGHT as the narrative order (v5-6, then v8). Note: "I give" is present tense in v8. Some read it as habit, not a new vow. The order is still true.
- **L10 quote:** word-for-word but TRUNCATED. It drops "and if I have taken any thing from any man by false accusation, I restore him fourfold." That clause is his confession, and it echoes Exodus 22:1. Cutting it weakens the repentance the passage shows.
- **L11 quote:** word-for-word, RIGHT (v9). It drops "son of Abraham," which is acceptable.
- **L12 "God rescues you and makes you His":** a sound gloss. "son of Abraham" (v9) supports "makes you His."
- **L14 quote:** word-for-word, RIGHT (v10).
- **L15 "Jesus came to find him":** fair for "seek and to save."
- **L15 "died for our sins, and rose again the third day":** RIGHT (1 Cor 15:3-4).

**Errors found:** none. The doctrine is sound, and I would send it to my church group, after the fourfold fix.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
A top explainer would show the cost. This man cheated people, and he pays it back four times. Grace that changes a thief beats grace that changes a vague "sinner." It would also briefly link v10 to Ezekiel 34:16, where God Himself says "I will seek that which was lost." That shows Jesus doing God's own job.
**ONE change:** restore the fourfold clause (line 10).
**Score: 7/10.**

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1 and pays off at lines 14-15. **Score: 8/10.** Worth the wait. The tree/seek mirror is tight, and line 15 closes the loop cleanly.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **Line 10:** "Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor; and if I have taken any thing from any man by false accusation, I restore him fourfold."
2. **Line 2:** "Zacchaeus was a chief tax collector at Jericho. Tax men often took extra money. He was rich. People called him a sinner. He was too short to see past the crowd."
3. **Line 5:** "'Abide' means stay. Zacchaeus came down fast, full of joy. The crowd complained:" This replaces the repeated "Jesus said He would stay" and explains the hard word.
