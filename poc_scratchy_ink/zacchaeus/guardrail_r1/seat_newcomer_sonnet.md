## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
A curious phone scroller, like me, who likes a short story. I stop at line 1: a man climbs a tree, and someone looks for him. That is a clear picture. I finish if the story stays simple. I share only if I feel something. Not served: me, if I want to know who "Jesus" is. The film never says (lines 1-4). People who need slow English are also not served: the narrator talks fast and the old-style quotes are hard (lines 4, 6, 10).

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus looked for a rich man everyone hated, and he saved him." Line 14 and line 15 carry it. It is only half clear. Line 15 adds "died for our sins, and rose again the third day". I did not follow this. Who died? What sins? Why does dying save a man? It arrives at the very end with no setup.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Zacchaeus, a short rich man. What: Jesus goes to his house, and the man gives money away. Why: Jesus came to find lost people.
Where I got lost:
- Line 2: "tax collector", "sinner". Why is that bad? Not explained.
- Line 3: "said his name first". Why does that matter?
- Line 4: "make haste", "abide at thy house". Old words. I guess "hurry" and "stay".
- Line 6: "That he was gone to be guest with a man that is a sinner". The sentence is broken and hard to follow.
- Line 9: "Jesus came first. The giving came after." I do not know what giving. The man had not given anything yet.
- Line 10: "the half of my goods". Hard to parse. "Behold" is strange.
- Line 11: "salvation". Line 12 explains it, but "makes you His" is unclear. Whose?
- Line 14: "Son of man" and "lost". Lost where? He is in his own town.
- Line 15: "died for our sins", "third day". Nothing before this prepared me.
I do not know who Jesus is or why people care. The story is moving, but the end feels like a new topic.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. What I was TOLD that I could not follow: "sinner" (line 2, 6), "salvation" (line 11, 12), "Son of man" (line 14), "died for our sins" (line 15), "rose again the third day" (line 15), "Jesus" himself (all). Also unclear is why a crowd would complain when a guest is coming (line 6).

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would say in the first 10 seconds who Jesus is, in plain words ("a teacher many believed was God's Son"). It would show why a tax collector was hated, and it would end on the one rescue story without a new idea at the end. The ONE highest-value change is to cut or set up line 15's second sentence. Score: 5/10. The story is good, but the first and last lines ask for background I do not have.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1 ("He did not know Jesus was looking for him"). Pays off at lines 3-4 when Jesus calls his name, and again at line 14. Score 7/10. Worth the wait. The tree is a strong image. But I am left asking: Why was Jesus looking for him? What did Zacchaeus do wrong? Why did giving money matter? Why did Jesus die?

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 1: "A man climbed a tree to see Jesus, a teacher many people followed."
2. Line 2: replace "People called him a sinner" with "He took money from his own people for a foreign ruler. They hated him."
3. Line 15: replace the last sentence with "Jesus came to rescue lost people. He later died to take their wrong away." (Short and plain. Drop "third day".)
