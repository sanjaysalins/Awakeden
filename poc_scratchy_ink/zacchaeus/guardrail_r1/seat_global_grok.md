## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY

"He went up to see Jesus. Jesus had come to find him."

Line 1 sets it up. Line 15 says it: "Zacchaeus came to look. Jesus came to find him." Those lines are clear.

The film then stacks "salvation" (line 12), "Jesus came first. The giving came after." (line 9), and "died for our sins" (line 15). The last sound is the cross, so the tree is no longer the sentence she repeats.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE

A short rich tax man climbs a tree. Jesus says his name and stays at his house. The crowd objects. He promises money. Jesus says this house is saved, because Jesus came to find him. Lines 1, 2, 5, and 15 can carry that. The middle falls apart.

Line 4, exact: "make haste, and come down; for to day I must abide at thy house." "Make haste" is rare. "For" means "because," and drops out in translation. "To day" looks like "two days" or a typo. "Abide" also means "obey a rule." "Thy" is dead English. Plain: "hurry and come down. Today I must stay at your house."

Line 6, exact: "That he was gone to be guest with a man that is a sinner." A fragment on a caption. No "a" before "guest." "He" can be Jesus or Zacchaeus. "Sinner" comes out "criminal" in one language and "immoral" in another. "Chief tax collector" (line 2) is an ordinary official in many countries. Foreign rule and extra money are never said.

Line 9: "Jesus came first" means he arrived earlier, or it means the gift is not the way in. "The giving" never says giving what.

Line 10, exact: "Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor." "Behold" is dead. "Goods" means shop products. "I give" sounds like an old habit, against line 9.

Line 12: "makes you His" can mean romance, property, or slavery. "You" jumps from his house to the viewer.

Line 14, exact: "the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost." "Son of man" sounds like "a man's son." "That which" sounds like a lost object. "Is come" looks like a mistake, so the climax caption looks fake. Plain: "I came to find lost people and save them."

Line 7 "anyway" loses its "even so." Line 15 "for our sins": "for" splits into "because of" or "in place of." "Our" counts her as already inside the faith.

What works with the sound off: short man, tree, crowd, joy, a teacher who knows a name. Universal. The shame and the rescue stay insider.

In Arab and South Asian hospitality, "I must stay at your house" is public honor, and eating with a shamed man is social dirt. Both beats die in the old sentences. The belonging line, "son of Abraham," is cut after line 11.

Picture order misleads. The gift (line 10) comes, then "salvation" (line 11). That is louder than line 9. Easy misread: give money, get saved.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE

Line 1 — RIGHT as a reading of "seek" (Luke 19:10). The narrator's sentence.

Line 2 — RIGHT: chief tax collector, rich, short, crowd. "Sinner" is that crowd's word.

Line 3 — RIGHT. He looked up, saw him, and said his name.

Line 4 — RIGHT, KJV of Luke 19:5.

Line 5 — RIGHT. Joy, then the complaint.

Line 6 — RIGHT as KJV words, spoken as a fragment.

Line 7 — RIGHT. He stays.

Line 9 — OVERCLAIMED as a lesson. The visit does come before the gift. "Jesus came first" is sermon wording.

Line 10 — the half quoted is RIGHT; the result is OVERCLAIMED. Luke 19:8 goes on: if he took anything by false accusation, he pays back four times. Without that, charity is the whole change.

Line 11 — RIGHT as a partial quote. Missing: "he also is a son of Abraham."

Line 12 — OVERCLAIMED. "Rescues" fits "save." "Makes you His" is wider than "this house."

Line 14 — RIGHT, KJV Luke 19:10.

Line 15, "came to look" / "came to find him" — RIGHT. "Died for our sins, and rose again the third day" — UNSUPPORTED in Luke 19:1-10. A later claim set on this same afternoon.
