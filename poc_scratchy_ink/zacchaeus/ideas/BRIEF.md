# Ideas round — Zacchaeus (Luke 19:1-10). You are ONE blind voice; you see no one else's ideas.

## The piece
A 45-60 s vertical Short (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels) for a channel that finds Jesus in the whole Bible (KJV). Style: bold scratchy-ink comic on a grungy mustard-yellow ground, expressive cartoon faces. Every piece lands on Jesus (who He is, His death and rising), earned by the story.

## Who it is for
Worldwide, many with English as a second language, many with no Bible knowledge. People who feel judged, disliked, "too bad", left out, or who have hurt others. Also Bible readers who know "the wee little man" kids' song and want one thing they missed.

## Your job
Give 3 DIFFERENT angles (spines) for this short, each in 4 lines:
1. Name of the angle (3-6 words).
2. The first 3 seconds: the exact opening line (plain words) — it must make a viewer stop and open a curiosity gap.
3. The verse it is pinned to (KJV, from the file below) and the turn: what the viewer did not notice.
4. The last line: lands on Jesus (His death and rising, or who He is), earned by the story, grace not pressure.
Then say which one of your 3 is best and why, in 2 lines.

## Hard rules
- Faithful to the text: no invented feelings, reasons or details. Outside facts (e.g. what tax collectors did in Rome's system, or that "Zacchaeus" may mean "pure/clean") must be labelled as background or left out; never present them as Scripture.
- Do not mock his height. Do not say he "repented to be saved" in a works way: salvation came to the house when Jesus came (19:9).
- KJV quotes exact and short; never let an old word ("publicans", "press", "abide") carry the meaning alone.
- No stock closer ("come to Jesus", "just ask Him", "He's waiting", "will you...?"). No fear, no pressure, no blanket promise.
- Never blame "the Jews" or any people group: the crowd is "the crowd" / "they all".
- No wording about AI or how the film is made.
Output only your 3 angles + your pick.
