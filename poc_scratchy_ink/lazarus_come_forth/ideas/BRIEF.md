# Ideas round — "Lazarus, come forth" (John 11:38-44). You are ONE blind voice; you see no one else's ideas.

## The piece
A 45-60 s vertical Short (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels) for a channel that finds Jesus in the whole Bible (KJV). Style: bold scratchy-ink comic on a grungy mustard-yellow ground. It is the NEXT episode after "Jesus Wept" (John 11:21-36: two sisters said "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died"; Jesus wept). Do NOT retell the weeping; this one starts at the tomb.

## Who it is for
Worldwide, many with English as a second language, many with no Bible knowledge. People who feel stuck, buried, past hope, or "too far gone". Also Bible readers who know the story and want one thing they missed.

## Your job
Give 3 DIFFERENT angles (spines) for this short, each in 4 lines:
1. Name of the angle (3-6 words).
2. The first 3 seconds: the exact opening line (plain words) — it must make a viewer stop and open a curiosity gap.
3. The verse it is pinned to (KJV, from the file below) and the turn: what the viewer did not notice.
4. The last line: lands on Jesus (His death and rising, or who He is), earned by the story, grace not pressure.
Then say which one of your 3 is best and why, in 2 lines.

## Hard rules
- Faithful to the text: no invented feelings, reasons or details. A detail the text does not state (e.g. "if He had not said the name, every grave would open") must be labelled as an old saying, not as Scripture — or left out.
- KJV quotes exact and short; never let an old word ("stinketh", "napkin") carry the meaning alone.
- No stock closer ("come to Jesus", "just ask Him", "He's waiting", "will you...?"). No fear, no pressure, no blanket promise.
- Never blame "the Jews" or any people group: "the leaders", "the chief priests and Pharisees".
- No wording about AI or how the film is made.
Output only your 3 angles + your pick.
