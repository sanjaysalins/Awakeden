**Alive, Still Wrapped**
“He came out alive. Why was he still wrapped for burial?”
John 11:44 — “bound hand and foot with graveclothes”: Lazarus has received life before anyone removes his burial cloths. Jesus then says, “Loose him, and let him go.”
Jesus does not tell a dead man to fix himself. He gives the life the man cannot give himself.

**Thanks Before The Answer**
“At this tomb, Jesus says thank you. Nobody has come out.”
John 11:41–42 — “Father, I thank thee that thou hast heard me.” Before calling Lazarus out, Jesus explains that He speaks so the crowd may believe the Father sent Him.
The voice that calls Lazarus out belongs to the Son whom the Father sent.

**Life That Leads To Death**
“Jesus opens a grave. Then the leaders plan his death.”
John 11:43, 53 — “Lazarus, come forth” leads into “they took counsel together for to put him to death.” The giver of life becomes the target of a death plot.
Jesus will enter death Himself: “Christ died for our sins”—and “he rose again the third day” (1 Corinthians 15:3–4).

My pick: **Life That Leads To Death**.
It reveals the story’s sharpest reversal and earns a clear ending at Jesus’ own death and rising.
