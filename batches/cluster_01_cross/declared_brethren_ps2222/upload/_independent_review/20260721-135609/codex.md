Findings:

- **Instagram / “Hebrews names the singer”** is too loose. Hebrews attributes the quoted words to Christ, but it does not literally “name the singer.” Use tighter wording like “Hebrews shows the speaker is Jesus.”
- **YouTube Long-form / “from Forsaken Cry to Family Song”** feels like long-form framing for a short devotional, but the artifact says the video format is **short**. If there is no actual long-form version, this block is metadata for a non-existent format.
- **Facebook / “The Psalm of the Cross Ends in a Family Song”** over-compresses the ending. Psalm 22 does not simply end in “family”; it expands to congregation, nations, future generations, and God’s righteousness. Not false, but rhetorically narrower than the text.
- **TikTok / “Most people stop reading Psalm 22 at the cross”** is an unsupported sweeping claim. It works as a hook, but “Many people” or “It is easy to stop” would be more honest.
- **YouTube/Facebook/Instagram CTA / “Subscribe to Awakeden”** is awkward platform fit outside YouTube, especially Instagram and TikTok. “Follow Awakeden” fits those platforms better.
