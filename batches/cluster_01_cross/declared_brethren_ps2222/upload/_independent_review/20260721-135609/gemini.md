# Independent review — gemini (OK, 49s)

Here is my adversarial review of the upload kit. 

### Findings

**1. Platform Fit / CTA Verbiage (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook)**
*   **Phrase:** `"Find Jesus in the whole Bible, one panel at a time. Subscribe to Awakeden. ✝"`
*   **Critique:** "Subscribe" is YouTube-native terminology. Using it on TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook breaks platform immersion and makes the copy feel copy-pasted. The CTA for these three platforms should use their native action: "Follow Awakeden."

**2. Grammar / Missing Word (YouTube Long-form)**
*   **Phrase:** `"The One who cried forsaken now lives, and he is not ashamed to call his own family."`
*   **Critique:** This sentence is missing an object and reads like a typo. You cannot "call your own family" in this context without a direct object. It should be "...he is not ashamed to call *us* his own family" or "...to *claim* his own family." 

**3. Precision / Textual Fidelity (Instagram)**
*   **Phrase:** `"Hebrews names the singer: the risen Jesus..."`
*   **Critique:** I agree with the in-engine red-team's hesitance here. Hebrews 2 does not "name a singer" of a song, it attributes the spoken/declared words of the Psalm to Christ. While it's a minor compression, since this artifact requires a revision for the typo above, this should be tightened to something like `"Hebrews says the speaker is: the risen Jesus..."` for maximum doctrinal accuracy.

**4. KJV Verbatim Check**
*   **Phrase:** `"I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee"` and `"not ashamed to call them brethren"`
*   **Critique:** Clean. These perfectly match KJV Psalm 22:22 and Hebrews 2:11 without alteration. 

**5. Hook Honesty & Doctrine**
*   **Phrase:** `"Psalm 22 Does Not End at the Cross"` / `"The same Jesus who cried out forsaken now lives, and he is calling you into that family."`
*   **Critique:** The hook is strong, curious, but entirely fact-based. The doctrine is sound: it lands on Christ and relies wholly on an invitation of grace without resorting to fear or works-based pressure. 

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Change "Subscribe to Awakeden" to "Follow Awakeden" in the TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook descriptions.
2. Fix the missing object in the YouTube Long-form copy ("...not ashamed to call his own family" -> "call us his own family").
3. Update the Instagram copy from "Hebrews names the singer" to "Hebrews says the speaker is" for better fidelity to the text.
