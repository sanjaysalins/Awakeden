## TITLE
Thirty Coins Hit the Temple Floor - 500 Years Apart

## DESCRIPTION
Zechariah was paid thirty pieces of silver, the price of a slave, and threw them down in the house of the LORD, to the potter (Zechariah 11:13). Five hundred years later Judas sold Jesus for the same sum, threw it down in the same temple, and it bought the potter's field.

Nobody was trying to fulfil prophecy. Every coin found its mark. The cross was no accident, and his blood bought you back.

Stop weighing out your own price. Receive his, and go free.
