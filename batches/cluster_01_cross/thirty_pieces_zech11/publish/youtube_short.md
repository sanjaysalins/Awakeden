## TITLE
Thirty Coins Hit the Temple Floor - 500 Years Apart

## DESCRIPTION
Thirty silver coins hit the temple floor. Each one lands on a mark written five hundred years early.

Zechariah asked Israel for his shepherd's wages, and they weighed out thirty shekels, the law's price for a slave. The LORD took it personally: "a goodly price that I was prised at of them." Then he told the prophet exactly where the coins would land: "And I took the thirty pieces of silver, and cast them to the potter in the house of the LORD" (Zechariah 11:13, KJV).

Five centuries later Judas sold Jesus for the same sum, threw it down in the temple, and the priests bought the potter's field. Nobody in that story was trying to fulfil prophecy. Every coin found its mark.

The cross was no accident. Jesus let himself be weighed like a slave, and his blood bought you back. Stop weighing out your own price. Receive his, and go free.
