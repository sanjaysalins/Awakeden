# Thirty Pieces of Silver — Zechariah 11:12-13

Series: Jesus in the OT · Cluster 1 (The Cross) · style: inked motion-comic (living page)
Note: deliberate clause-boundary partial quotes, tagged on screen — Zech 11:12b, 11:13a, 11:13b, Matt 27:5a. On-screen bars carry exact KJV casing ("a goodly price...") with the fragment tag.

[narrator] Thirty silver coins hit the temple floor. Each one lands on a mark written five hundred years early.
[narrator] Five centuries before, Zechariah asked Israel for his shepherd's wages.
[scripture] So they weighed for my price thirty pieces of silver.
[narrator] Thirty shekels. The law's price for a slave. The LORD took it personally.
[scripture] A goodly price that I was prised at of them.
[narrator] And Zechariah threw them where the LORD said.
[scripture] And I took the thirty pieces of silver, and cast them to the potter in the house of the LORD.
[narrator] Then Judas sold Jesus for the same sum.
[scripture] And he cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed.
[narrator] Cast down. In the temple. The priests took it. Blood money. It bought the potter's field.
[narrator] Nobody was trying to fulfil prophecy. Every coin found its mark.
[narrator] The cross was no accident. Jesus let himself be weighed like a slave.
[narrator] The silver bought a field for the dead. His blood bought you back. Stop weighing out your own price. Receive his price, and go free.
