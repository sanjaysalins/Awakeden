## TITLE
A Song Predicted the Dice Game at the Cross

## DESCRIPTION
David wrote Psalm 22 in the first person, but he died an old man, never executed. The song describes someone else: "They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture" (Psalm 22:18, KJV).

A thousand years later, John watched soldiers divide Jesus' clothes and cast lots for the seamless coat, "that the scripture might be fulfilled."

The cross was no accident. It was the plan, and it was to win you back.
