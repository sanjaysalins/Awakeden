## TITLE
A Song Predicted the Dice Game at the Cross

## DESCRIPTION
Ten centuries before the cross, a song recorded exactly how a dying man's clothes would be divided up.

Psalm 22. David wrote it in the first person, but David was never executed; he died an old man. He was describing someone else: stripped, surrounded, his life poured out. And one line lands with strange precision: "They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture" (Psalm 22:18, KJV).

A thousand years later, John stood at the cross and watched soldiers divide Jesus' clothes, then cast lots for the seamless coat, "that the scripture might be fulfilled."

God had it written down early so that when it happened, you would know: the cross was no accident. It was the plan. They rolled dice for the clothes off his back, never seeing what he was doing: laying down his life to win you back.
