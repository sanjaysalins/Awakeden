## TITLE
Why the Giver of Living Water Hung Dry

## DESCRIPTION
Two words from the cross (John 19:28): I thirst.

Psalm 22 painted the thirst behind the cry a thousand years early: "My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death."

The One who offers living water hung there dry, in your place. Bring him your thirst, and drink. That water is himself.
