## TITLE
A Last Line and a Last Word, a Thousand Years Apart, One Finished Work

## DESCRIPTION
Psalm 22's final line looks ahead to God's saving work, accomplished and declared to a people not yet born (Psalm 22:31; John 19:30).

"They shall come, and shall declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this" (Psalm 22:31, KJV).

As Jesus hung dying, John records his final word: "It is finished" (John 19:30). Different words, in different tongues, but the same note: a finished work. Nothing is left for you to finish. Only Someone to come home to: the One who said it is done.
