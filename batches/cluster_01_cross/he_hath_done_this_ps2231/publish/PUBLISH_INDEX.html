TITLE
Psalm 22's Last Line Meets "It Is Finished"
DESCRIPTION
A psalm's last line and Jesus' dying word in John's gospel close on the very same note (Psalm 22:31; John 19:30).

"They shall come, and shall declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this" (Psalm 22:31, KJV).

The psalm ends looking ahead to God's saving work, accomplished, and told to a people not yet born. Its final line: he hath done this. Done. And as Jesus hung dying, John records his final word: "It is finished" (John 19:30). Different words, in different tongues, a thousand years apart, but the same note: a finished work.

So what is left for you to finish? Nothing. Only Someone to come home to: the One who said it is done.
