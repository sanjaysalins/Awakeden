## TITLE
A King Described a Death He Never Saw

## DESCRIPTION
A king described a dying body so exactly it reads like an eyewitness account of a crucifixion he never saw (Psalm 22:14-17).

"I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint" (Psalm 22:14, KJV). Drained, every joint pulled loose, the way a body hangs when it is suspended by the arms.

"I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me" (Psalm 22:17, KJV). Every bone stretched and exposed, while onlookers stood and stared.

David wrote it in the first person, but he was describing someone else's death. He never saw a crucifixion; Rome would not make it its instrument for centuries. Yet the dying man of his song bears the marks of one, written early.

This is what Jesus bore to bring you home: every wrenched joint, every cold stare, recorded centuries early so you would know none of it was chance. He was crushed in your place. Come home to him.
