## TITLE
The Death David Described Was Not His Own (Psalm 22)

## DESCRIPTION
David described a dying body so exactly it reads like an eyewitness account of a crucifixion he never saw (Psalm 22:14-17).

"I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint" (Psalm 22:14, KJV). "I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me" (Psalm 22:17, KJV).

He wrote in the first person, but he was describing someone else's death. Rome would not make crucifixion its instrument for centuries. This is what Jesus bore to bring you home, recorded early so you would know none of it was chance. He was crushed in your place. Come home to him.
