That is probably fine in the video, but metadata presents it with high certainty. If the video does not actually explain this, it overpromises.

It avoids saying crucifixion did not exist, which is good, but the phrasing is still easy to hear as “crucifixion was not around yet.” Consider tightening to “before Roman crucifixion became the emblematic form of execution” if the video supports that.

- TikTok first line: `"A king described a dying body so exactly it reads like an eyewitness account..."` is clear but long and not especially native to TikTok. The stronger hook is buried in the second sentence: `"Poured out like water, bones out of joint..."`

- Tags: YouTube tags are mostly relevant, but `"the crucifixion"` is broad and generic. Not a major SEO honesty problem, but it is weaker than specific search terms like `psalm 22 crucifixion` or `psalm 22 prophecy`.
