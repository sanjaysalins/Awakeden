### Problems (cite exact platform + phrase)

**1. Facebook title is forgettable and content-blind**  
**Facebook TITLE:** `First Person, Someone Else's Death`  
No Psalm 22, no David, no Jesus, no body/crucifixion cue. On a feed that could be fiction, crime, or memoir. Does not earn *this* click honestly. Weakest line in the kit.

**2. YouTube Long title drops the video’s honesty hedge**  
**YouTube (Long-form) TITLE:** `The Body in Psalm 22: Crucifixion Detail Written Centuries Early`  
Spoken piece says “**reads like** an eyewitness account” and “**bears the marks** of one.” Narration accuracy guard: **descriptive match**, not over-certainty. This title states “Crucifixion Detail … Written … Early” as flat fact. That is a mild overclaim relative to the text and to the short title’s better paradox framing.

**3. TikTok compresses KJV without quote marks (borderline)**  
**TikTok:** `Poured out like water, bones out of joint, counted and stared at`  
Beside a named psalm, this can read as Scripture. “Counted and stared at” is **not** KJV (v17 is “I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me”). In-engine waved this as “non-blocking.” Still a honesty risk on a platform that strips context. Safer: one short attributed quote, or clearly non-quote paraphrase.

`"I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint" (Psalm 22:14, KJV)`  
Words are exact, but full KJV v14 continues: *my heart is like wax; it is melted in the midst of my bowels.* Spoken script used an ellipsis. Kit presents a half-verse as if it were the whole citation. Not a word-change FAIL; still sloppy KJV attribution.

**YouTube Long** description is basically a clone of the short (1093 vs 1100 chars), with tag `bible study` and no long-form structure (chapters, movement list). Harmless if unused; misleading if pasted as if a 16:9 deep-dive exists.
