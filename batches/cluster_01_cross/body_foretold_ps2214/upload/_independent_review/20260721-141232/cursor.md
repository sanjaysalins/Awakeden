## Independent adversarial review — Upload Kit: *The Body Foretold*

### HOOKY BUT HONEST / bait-and-switch

**YouTube (Short) + TikTok + Instagram — repeated hook:** *“reads like an eyewitness account of a crucifixion he never saw”* — The hedge *“reads like”* matches the locked narration, so this is curiosity, not a lie. Acceptable.

**TikTok + Instagram — historical shorthand:** *“centuries before Rome made crucifixion its instrument”* — Softer than the video’s careful line (*“Rome would not make it its instrument for centuries”*). It still nudges readers toward “Rome invented crucifixion,” which is historically sloppy (crucifixion-like execution existed before Rome). Not full clickbait, but the kit is less honest here than the video itself.

**YouTube (Long-form 16:9) block — format mismatch:** Header says **Format: short**, yet a full **YouTube (Long-form 16:9)** section is included with title *“The Body in Psalm 22: Crucifixion Detail Written Centuries Early.”* If someone copy-pastes that for this Short, metadata promises a study-length framing the asset is not. Operator error waiting to happen.

**YouTube (Short) description — added CTA not in audio:** *“Come home to him”* appears in YouTube/Facebook copy but is **not spoken** in the finished short (audio ends at *“He was crushed in your place”*). Invitation, not fear — but it is metadata the viewer will not hear. Mild promise drift, not doctrine failure.

### KJV verbatim

**YouTube + Facebook — formal quotes:**  
- *“I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint”* (Psalm 22:14) — **verbatim KJV.**  
- *“I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me”* (Psalm 22:17) — **verbatim KJV.**

**Instagram — attribution gap:**  
*“…out of joint” (Psalm 22:14)* — verse text is fine, but every other quoting platform adds **“, KJV”**. Inconsistent with the kit’s own KJV-strict claim.

**Instagram — half the anchor range dropped:** Anchor is **Psalm 22:14–17**, but the caption quotes **only v14**. v17 (*“they look and stare upon me”*) is half the video’s Scripture spine and is missing entirely.

**TikTok — pseudo-quotation without marks:**  
*“Poured out like water, bones out of joint, counted and stared at”* — Presented as compressed Scripture beside the psalm reference, but **“counted” is not KJV**. Psalm 22:17 says *“I may tell all my bones”*, not “counted.” The red-team waved this as “acceptable summary”; for a KJV-strict channel, that is too lenient — it reads like a mangled verse to anyone who knows the text.

**Red-team self-check is overstated:** It claims quotes are *“attributed at the point of quotation on every platform that quotes them.”* TikTok has no quote marks or refs on that compressed line; Instagram omits v17 and the KJV label.

**Weak invitation on short-native platforms:** TikTok and Instagram stop at *“He was crushed in your place”* with no *“Come home to him”* — weaker explicit invitation where the caption *is* the whole pitch.

### Bottom line

The kit is **not a FAIL** — core hook is honest, formal KJV quotes check out, doctrine is sound, and the chosen YouTube title is strong. It **should not ship as-is** because TikTok compresses KJV into non-KJV wording, Instagram drops half the anchor Scripture, Facebook’s title is a discovery dead end, and the long-form YouTube block misframes a short-only asset. The in-engine **“ALL PASS ✅”** is too generous.
