# Independent review — gemini (OK, 98s)

Here are the specific findings from reviewing the upload-metadata kit:

- **Platform fit (Terminology):** On TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram, the channel CTA reads exactly: `Find Jesus in the whole Bible, one panel at a time. Subscribe to Awakeden. ✝` The word "Subscribe" is exclusively YouTube terminology. This should be changed to "Follow Awakeden" on these platforms.
- **Brand (CTA-to-Jesus truncated):** The explicit invitation to the viewer is missing on two platforms. While YouTube and Facebook correctly conclude with the direct CTA `Come home to him.`, the TikTok caption stops abruptly at `Jesus bore it to bring you home.` and the Instagram caption stops at `He was crushed in your place.` The actual call-to-action is lost on both.
- **Platform fit (Footer logic):** In the Facebook section, the external link block says `Follow: YouTube: @awakeden`. This is backward: it uses "Follow" for the YouTube link (where users Subscribe), while using "Subscribe" for the native Facebook post (where users Follow).
- **KJV attribution (Minor):** On Instagram, the scripture is quoted verbatim but drops the translation tag, citing it only as `(Psalm 22:14)`. The other platforms correctly cite it as `(Psalm 22:14, KJV)` or include the public domain footer. 

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Change "Subscribe to Awakeden" to "Follow Awakeden" in the captions for TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram.
2. Restore the explicit "Come home to him." invitation to the end of the TikTok and Instagram descriptions.
3. Fix the Facebook footer to say "Subscribe: YouTube" instead of "Follow: YouTube".
