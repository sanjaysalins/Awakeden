# Independent review — claude (OK, 106s)

Reviewed the kit line by line: checked both KJV quotes against the actual KJV text, the historical claims, doctrine, and every platform block.

**What holds up**

- **KJV verbatim — clean.** "I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint" (Ps 22:14) and "I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me" (Ps 22:17) are both exact KJV.
- **Historical claim is honestly scoped.** "Rome would not make it its instrument for centuries" never claims crucifixion didn't exist before Rome (Persians/Carthaginians did use it) — the wording survives a hostile read on every platform.
- **Restraint on v16** ("pierced my hands and my feet" — the disputed Masoretic/LXX verse) is the right call: it's in the anchor range but never quoted or claimed.
- **Doctrine and grace framing pass.** "He was crushed in your place. Come home to him" is invitation, not fear; CTA-to-Jesus and footers present.

**Findings**

1. **Wrong-format block shipped in the kit.** The header says **Format: short**, yet a full **"YouTube (Long-form 16:9)"** section is included, with a near-identical description (1093 vs 1100 chars). There is no long-form video at this path — this is a paste-ready block for a video that doesn't exist, and its copy isn't shaped for a long anyway (no chapters/timestamps). One wrong paste at the upload desk publishes short copy under a long-form title.

2. **TikTok caption never names the psalm.** The caption body has zero scripture reference — "Psalm 22" appears only in the hashtag `#Psalm22`. This contradicts the UK-G7 claim "verse front-loaded," and it breaks the in-engine red-team's own defense of the unmarked KJV compression: it excused "Poured out like water, bones out of joint, counted and stared at" as "summary allusion **beside the named psalm**" — but the psalm is *not* named in that caption. The defense rests on a false premise. Fix is one edit: "…an eyewitness account of a crucifixion he never saw (Psalm 22:14-17)."

3. **Instagram quotes KJV with no KJV attribution.** IG has `"I am poured out like water…" (Psalm 22:14)` — no "KJV" tag and no public-domain footer line, while every other platform carries both. Inconsistent with the kit's own UK-G2 standard.

4. **Gate-claim integrity (minor).** UK-G7 says "plain ASCII," yet every caption contains the non-ASCII `✝` (U+271D). Either the gate has an undocumented exception or the checkmark is overstating what was checked. Worth knowing which.

5. **Very minor.** Long-form title is 64 chars; YouTube clips titles around ~60 on some surfaces, so "…Early" may truncate. Moot if fix #1 removes the block.

The copy itself is honest, hooky without bait, and doctrinally sound — the problems are kit hygiene, not content dishonesty.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the "YouTube (Long-form 16:9)" block (or mark it N/A) — the kit declares Format: short and no long-form video exists at this path; a near-duplicate paste-ready block for a nonexistent video is a live mispublication risk.
2. Add the scripture anchor to the TikTok caption body — e.g. "…a crucifixion he never saw (Psalm 22:14-17)" — so the unmarked KJV compression actually sits beside a named psalm and UK-G7's "verse front-loaded" claim is true on all platforms.
3. Add KJV attribution to the Instagram quote — "(Psalm 22:14, KJV)" — matching the kit's own UK-G2 convention used everywhere else.
