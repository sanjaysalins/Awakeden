# Today in Paradise — Luke 23:39-43

Series: Words from the Cross · Cluster 1 (The Cross) · style: inked motion-comic (living page)
Note: deliberate clause-boundary partials, tagged on screen — Luke 23:39b (mocker), 23:40b-41 (thief, contiguous across the verse break), 23:42b (thief), 23:43b (Jesus). KJV "To day" spelling preserved.

[narrator] Two criminals died beside Jesus that afternoon. Same nails. Same hill. Same distance. Opposite eternities.
[narrator] Both guilty. Both just as close. Both spoke.
[mocker] If thou be Christ, save thyself and us.
[narrator] A taunt, not a prayer.
[narrator] The other thief rebuked him.
[thief] And we indeed justly; for we receive the due reward of our deeds: but this man hath done nothing amiss.
[narrator] A confession. A verdict: innocent. Then one request.
[thief] Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom.
[jesus] Verily I say unto thee, To day shalt thou be with me in paradise.
[narrator] He asked for someday. Jesus gave him that same afternoon.
[narrator] No merit. Just repentance and trust.
[narrator] We are all one of those two men.
[narrator] Ask what the thief asked. Remember me. Then hear his answer. With me. Today. Paradise.
