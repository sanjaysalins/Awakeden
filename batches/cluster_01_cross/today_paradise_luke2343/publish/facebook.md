## TITLE
He Asked for Someday. Jesus Gave Him That Afternoon.

## DESCRIPTION
Two criminals died beside Jesus (Luke 23:43). Both guilty, both close, both spoke. One taunted. One asked: Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom.

"And Jesus said unto him, Verily I say unto thee, To day shalt thou be with me in paradise" (Luke 23:43, KJV).

No merit. No time left to earn anything. Just repentance and trust, answered the same afternoon.

We are all one of those two men. Ask what the thief asked, and hear his answer.
