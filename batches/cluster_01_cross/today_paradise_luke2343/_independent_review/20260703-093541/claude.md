**Thread spine — strong.** "Same afternoon to die" (hook) → "He got that afternoon" (middle) → "Today" (landing). One thread, no swapping.

Same hill." triplet is good rhythm, but the hook never teases the SPLIT — the whole pull of this piece is "same everything, opposite endings," and the hook only gives the "same" half. A stranger has no promised payoff to stay for.

Rescue, without repentance." — fragments are fine for the format, but "Rescue, without repentance" is the one line a zero-Bible listener may need a beat to parse.
