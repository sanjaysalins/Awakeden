## TITLE
Jesus Asked Three Fishermen for One Thing. They Failed in an Hour.

## DESCRIPTION
Matthew 26:40 - the night before the cross, Jesus asked Peter, James and John to watch with him one hour in Gethsemane. They slept.

"And he cometh unto the disciples, and findeth them asleep, and saith unto Peter, What, could ye not watch with me one hour?" (Matthew 26:40, KJV)

His question was not a curse. It was a diagnosis full of mercy - the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak. Then he drank the cup he had asked to pass. On the cross. For you.

He watched that hour alone. Now no hour of yours is watched alone. Bring Jesus the weak flesh he already knows.
