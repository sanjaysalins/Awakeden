# Could Ye Not Watch One Hour — Matthew 26:36-46 (Gethsemane)

Series: Questions Jesus Asked · Cluster 1 (The Cross) · style: inked motion-comic (living page)
Note: clause-boundary partials tagged on screen — Matt 26:38b, 26:39b, 26:40 (split at the speech), 26:41.

[narrator] Jesus asked three fishermen for one thing. They failed inside an hour.
[narrator] Gethsemane. The night before the cross.
[jesus] tarry ye here, and watch with me.
[narrator] Then he stepped away to pray.
[jesus] O my Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me: nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt.
[scripture] And he cometh unto the disciples, and findeth them asleep, and saith unto Peter,
[jesus] What, could ye not watch with me one hour?
[narrator] Not a curse. A diagnosis full of mercy.
[jesus] Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.
[narrator] He returned to the same prayer.
[narrator] The cup he asked to pass, he drank. On the cross. For you.
[narrator] So bring him the weak flesh he already knows. When you cannot watch, pray anyway. He is watching.
[narrator] He watched that hour alone. Now no hour of yours is watched alone.
