## Critical: citation truth / wrong bucket

### 1. “Bare rise” is not Scripture (false **[SPECIFIED]**)
> **[SPECIFIED]** Golgotha… — **a bare rise** outside the city (Hebrews 13:12…)

- **Supported:** “Golgotha… place of a skull” (Matt 27:33); “suffered without the gate” (Heb 13:12).
- **Not supported:** “bare rise.” No KJV verse states barrenness, elevation shape, or lack of vegetation.
- **Worse:** John 19:41 — “in the place where he was crucified **there was a garden**.” Absolute “bare” can fight that text.
- **Bucket:** only “place of a skull” + outside the gate = specified. Landscape = free/constrained.

### 2. “Lots = small unmarked objects” under **[SPECIFIED]**
> Four soldiers; **lots = small unmarked objects**.

- John 19:23–24 supports parting garments, seamless coat, casting lots, four parts → four soldiers (fair).
- Shape/size/marking of lots: **never stated**. Licence dressed as specified.

### 3. Hyssop apparatus over-harmonised as **[SPECIFIED]**
> Hyssop = **a SMALL herb bunch bound to a reed** — never a leafy branch…

- John 19:29: sponge on **hyssop**. Matt 27:48: sponge on a **reed**. Neither verse says “herb bunch bound to a reed.”
- Harmonisation may be reasonable as **[CONSTRAINED]**; as **[SPECIFIED]** it invents a prop the text does not describe.
- “Never a leafy branch” is botanical assertion, not a biblical visual fact.

### 4. Scarlet robe only — incomplete against full witness
Mocking bullet cites only Matt 27:28 **scarlet**. Mark 15:17 / John 19:2,5: **purple**.

- Forcing scarlet-only as THE specified robe can wrongly fail a purple mocking still that is also textual.
- At minimum: note the wording difference; do not treat scarlet as exclusive canon for the sheet.

### 5. Crown-at-cross nested inside a **[SPECIFIED]** bullet
> The crown of thorns remains… traditional… Scripture never records its removal **([CONSTRAINED]** either with or without…)

- Correct substance, wrong packaging: one **[SPECIFIED]** card mixes hard musts with tradition.
- Split: robe removed before the road = specified (Matt 27:31); crown at Calvary = constrained only.

## Coverage gaps (painting can go wrong; sheet silent)

For a sheet that claims to drive **all 11 Cross shorts** and opens with **“the mocking and the road”**:

1. **Simon of Cyrene** (Matt 27:32; Mark 15:21; Luke 23:26) — road stills with only Jesus under the beam can contradict the synoptics; John 19:17 (“bearing his cross”) needs a constrained note, not silence.
2. **First drink refused** — wine mingled with gall/myrrh (Matt 27:34; Mark 15:23) vs later vinegar sponge. Easy to merge into one cup scene.
3. **Matt 27:44 / Mark 15:32** — both robbers revile (before Luke’s penitent turn). `today_paradise` alone can paint a one-sided “always penitent” pair.
4. **Women afar off** (Matt 27:55–56; Mark 15:40; Luke 23:49) vs John “by the cross.” Not a contradiction to resolve away, but a constrained note if distant crowds appear.
5. **Earthquake / rocks / graves** (Matt 27:51–53) at the death beat — missing if any still is “finished / into thy hands / ninth hour” landscape.
6. **Centurion** (Matt 27:54 etc.) — optional, but common Calvary error if invented dialogue/props.
7. **Nails:** Ps 22:16 + John 20:25/Luke 24:39 support piercings; “through hands and feet” as crucifixion method is fair **constrained**, but the sheet’s wording “are textual” slightly oversells feet-nails as a Gospel narrative of the nailing act (no Gospel narrates the hammer blows). Keep as constrained, not quasi-specified.
