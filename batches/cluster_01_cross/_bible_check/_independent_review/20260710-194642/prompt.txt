# Biblical fact sheet — cluster_01_cross (shared core + per-piece anchors) — v1

Buckets: **specified** = the Bible states it (image MUST match) · **constrained** = must not

## The mocking and the road (Matt 27:27-33)
- **[SPECIFIED]** Crown of thorns, reed, and the soldiers' robe belong to the MOCKING scene: "they stripped him, and put on him a scarlet robe. And when they had platted a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand" (Matthew 27:28-29). Before the road they "took the robe off from him, and put his own raiment on him" (Matthew 27:31). The crown of thorns remains on him at the cross in traditional depiction — Scripture never records its removal ([CONSTRAINED] either with or without at the cross is defensible; never the scarlet robe at the cross).
- **[SPECIFIED]** Golgotha, "that is to say, a place of a skull" (Matthew 27:33) — a bare rise outside the city (Hebrews 13:12 "suffered without the gate").

## The crucifixion scene (shared by every cross still)
- **[SPECIFIED]** TWO thieves, one on either side: "two thieves crucified with him, one on the right hand, and another on the left" (Matthew 27:38); "they crucified him, and the malefactors, one on the right hand, and the other on the left" (Luke 23:33). A wide Calvary frame shows three crosses; a deliberate tight frame on Christ alone is fine.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The garments were PARTED and LOTS CAST, by soldiers, for a seamless coat: "They parted my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture" (Psalm 22:18); "Then the soldiers... took his garments, and made four parts, to every soldier a part; and also his coat: now the coat was without seam... Let us not rend it, but cast lots for it" (John 19:23-24). Four soldiers; lots = small unmarked objects.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The title over him was WRITTEN by Pilate in three languages: "JESUS OF NAZARETH THE KING OF THE JEWS... written in Hebrew, and Greek, and Latin" (John 19:19-20). RENDER GUARD (production): the titulus must never carry LEGIBLE text (models garble script) — distant/illegible marks only, or omit the board in tight frames.
- **[SPECIFIED]** DARKNESS from the sixth to the ninth hour: "Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land unto the ninth hour" (Matthew 27:45). It is DARKNESS — not a lightning storm, not rain (weather is never mentioned).
- **[SPECIFIED]** The vinegar came on a SPONGE, on hyssop/a reed: "they filled a spunge with vinegar, and put it upon hyssop, and put it to his mouth" (John 19:29); "took a spunge, and filled it with vinegar, and put it on a reed" (Matthew 27:48). Hyssop = a SMALL herb bunch bound to a reed — never a leafy branch, never a cup lifted by hand to his lips.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The women and John stood BY the cross: "Now there stood by the cross of Jesus his mother, and his mother's sister, Mary the wife of Cleophas, and Mary Magdalene" and "the disciple standing by, whom he loved" (John 19:25-26).
- **[SPECIFIED]** His legs were NOT broken; his SIDE was pierced AFTER death, with blood and water: "when they came to Jesus, and saw that he was dead already, they brake not his legs: But one of the soldiers with a spear pierced his side, and forthwith came there out blood and water" (John 19:33-34). A spear-thrust still shows Christ already dead (head bowed, eyes closed).
- **[CONSTRAINED]** Crucifixion form: nails through hands and feet are textual ("the print of the nails", John 20:25; "they pierced my hands and my feet", Psalm 22:16; "Behold my hands and my feet", Luke 24:39). Render note: tight nail/wound close-ups blob in this engine — prefer mid framings; ropes may steady the arms alongside nails, never replace the nails in a visible-wound frame. Christ stripped of garments (John 19:23) — the loincloth is the standing reverence convention. Wounds restrained, never gore (not-grotesque axis).
- **[CONSTRAINED]** Period: Roman soldiers in 1st-century kit (no medieval plate armour), Judean dress for onlookers, no halos, no modern objects.

## Per-piece anchors
- **father_forgive_them (Luke 23:34):** "Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots." Spoken FROM the cross while soldiers gambled below.

- **woman_behold (John 19:26-27):** "Woman, behold thy son! Then saith he to the disciple, Behold thy mother!" Mary and John TOGETHER beneath the cross; "from that hour that disciple took her unto his own home."

- **it_is_finished (John 19:30):** "he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost." The bowed head follows the word — a "finished" still shows the work complete, not agony.
- **into_thy_hands (Luke 23:44-46):** darkness over all the earth, "the veil of the temple was rent in the midst", "Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit." A veil still tears TOP to bottom (Matthew 27:51 "from the top to the bottom").

Coins carry no legible lettering.
