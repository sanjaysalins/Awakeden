Cache confirms my main citation catch. Findings below, most serious first.

## Findings

**1. KJV misquote — Psalm 22:18 (garments card + crucifixion_foretold anchor).**
The sheet quotes Psalm 22:18 as *"They parted my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture."* KJV Psalm 22:18 is present tense: **"They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture."** "They parted…" is the NT quotation form (Matthew 27:35 / John 19:24 — and those read "raiment"/"did they cast lots", so the sheet's wording matches *no* verse exactly). In a KJV-verbatim project, a fact sheet that itself mis-splices KJV fails its own standard. Verified against `data/kjv_cache.json` (Psalm 22:18 = "They part").

**2. Invented fact in SPECIFIED — Golgotha "a bare rise."**
"[SPECIFIED] Golgotha… **a bare rise** outside the city." Neither Matthew 27:33 ("a place of a skull") nor Hebrews 13:12 ("without the gate") says it was a rise, hill, or bare. The hill is pure tradition. "Outside the city" is supported (Heb 13:12; John 19:20 "nigh to the city"); "bare rise" is licence dressed as specified — exactly what the SPECIFIED bucket must never contain. Move the topography to free/constrained.

**3. Harmonization presented as definition — "Hyssop = a SMALL herb bunch bound to a reed."**
John 19:29 says hyssop; Matthew 27:48 says a reed. Neither verse says the hyssop was *bound to* a reed — that's a plausible reconciliation (and an existing memory note), not Scripture. Inside a [SPECIFIED] card it reads as biblical. Keep the guard, but mark the bound-to-reed mechanics as constrained inference.

**4. Non-biblical production detail inside SPECIFIED — "lots = small unmarked objects."**
No verse describes the lots' appearance. Fine as a render guard, wrong inside the specified bucket (contrast the titulus card, which correctly labels its RENDER GUARD).

**5. Bucket inconsistency — nails.**
The nails card sits in [CONSTRAINED] while its own text says the prints are "textual" (John 20:25, Luke 24:39, Psalm 22:16). If it's textual, the fact "pierced hands and feet" is SPECIFIED; only the render accommodations (mid framings, steadying ropes — ropes being pure licence with zero textual basis) are constrained. As written, a checker could treat nail-less hands as merely "don't contradict."

**6. COVERAGE GAP — darkness timing per word (a painting could easily get this wrong and nothing guards it).**
The sheet locks sixth-to-ninth-hour darkness but never maps it to the per-piece anchors. Mark 15:25 ("it was the third hour, and they crucified him") isn't cited at all. Consequence: *father_forgive_them*, *today_paradise*, *woman_behold* (early words, lots still being cast per Luke 23:34) happen **before** the darkness — a still of "Father, forgive them" under a blackened sky contradicts the sequence; conversely *i_thirst* / *it_is_finished* (John 19:28 "after this") and *into_thy_hands* sit **in/at the end of** the darkness. Only *forsaken_cry* gets a timing note. Add a light-state line to each cross-word anchor.

**7. Minor over-reaches / smaller gaps.**
- "eyes closed" for the dead Christ (spear card) — inference, not stated; harmless but unmarked.
- "an olive garden" (watch_one_hour) — Scripture says "a place called Gethsemane" / "a garden" (John 18:1); *olive* is inference from the Mount of Olives. NIGHT is fine (John 18:3 lanterns/torches would strengthen the citation — currently uncited).
- "David writing/seeing **a thousand years early**" — a historical estimate stated as fact in a fact card; say "centuries before."
- Missing specified prop: John 19:29 opens "there was set **a vessel full of vinegar**" — the vessel is biblical and the sponge/i_thirst stills could legitimately include it; no card mentions it.
- Four soldiers (John 19:23 "four parts, to every soldier a part") — sound inference, no complaint.

**What's solid:** every other quotation I checked is verbatim KJV (Matt 27:28-31, 27:38, 27:45-46, 27:51; Luke 23:33-46; John 19:19-37, 20:25; Zech 11:12-13, 12:10; Psalm 22:1, 22:15; Matt 26:36-46). The Luke-only angel/bloody-sweat flag, the titulus no-legible-text guard, the penitent-thief-on-a-cross guard, and the dead-before-piercing card are all honest and correctly bucketed. No doctrinal or Christ-lens distortion found.
