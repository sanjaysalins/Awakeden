# Independent review — codex (OK, 53s)

Findings:

1. **Wrong bucket / invented visual fact:** “**[SPECIFIED] Golgotha… — a bare rise outside the city**” overstates the citations. Matthew 27:33 supports “Golgotha” and “place of a skull”; Hebrews 13:12 supports “without the gate.” Neither states **bare** nor **rise/hill**. Move “bare rise” to free/constrained or delete it.

2. **Wrong bucket / citation does not state interpretation:** “**Hyssop = a SMALL herb bunch bound to a reed**” is asserted under **[SPECIFIED]**, but John 19:29 only says “put it upon hyssop,” and Matthew 27:48 says “on a reed.” The “herb bunch bound to a reed” harmonization may be plausible, but the text does not specify that object construction. Also “never a leafy branch” is too strong unless supported elsewhere.

3. **Wrong bucket:** “**The garments were PARTED and LOTS CAST, by soldiers, for a seamless coat… Four soldiers; lots = small unmarked objects.**” John supports soldiers, four parts, seamless coat, and casting lots. It does **not** specify the lots were “small unmarked objects.” That belongs in free/constrained, not specified.

4. **Citation overreach:** “**Crucifixion form: nails through hands and feet are textual**” is partly overstated. John 20:25 supports nails in Jesus’ hands. Psalm 22:16 says “pierced my hands and my feet,” but does not say nails. Luke 24:39 shows hands and feet after resurrection, but not nails. “Nails through feet” is a defensible synthesis, not cleanly “textual” from the cited verses.

5. **Potentially misleading bucket:** “**Christ stripped of garments (John 19:23)**” is not exactly what John states. John says soldiers took his garments and coat; it supports removal of clothing, but not total visual nakedness. The note correctly treats loincloth as reverence convention, but “stripped” should be worded carefully.

6. **Citation wording error / possible theological distortion:** “**into_thy_hands… darkness over all the earth**” quotes Luke 23:44-46, but KJV Luke 23:44 says “there was a darkness over all the **earth**,” while Matthew 27:45 says “over all the **land**.” This sheet elsewhere uses “land.” It should not flatten the scope without noting the textual wording per Gospel.

7. **Misleading specified detail:** “**A veil still tears TOP to bottom**” cites Matthew 27:51, which says “from the top to the bottom,” but in the **into_thy_hands** line the direct Luke citation only says “rent in the midst.” The Matthew cross-reference fixes it, but the fact should be explicitly marked as drawn from Matthew, not Luke’s wording.

8. **Wrong bucket / invented scene detail:** “**crucifixion_foretold… David writing/seeing a thousand years early**” is not stated by Psalm 22:18. “David writing” depends on the psalm superscription/tradition; “seeing” is interpretive; “a thousand years early” is chronology, not in the cited verse. This should not be treated as a biblical visual anchor unless separately justified.

9. **Citation truth problem:** “**pierced (Zechariah 12:10 + John 19:34-37): ‘they shall look upon me whom they have pierced’ — the spear-thrust**” compresses prophecy and fulfilment correctly in John 19:37, but Zechariah itself does not specify a spear-thrust or crucifixion scene. The spear-thrust comes from John 19:34. Keep those distinct.

10. **Missing guard:** The sheet omits that Jesus is crucified at **Golgotha/Calvary** in the crucifixion stills, not merely in the road section. Since every cross still depends on location, the shared crucifixion section should guard the place.

11. **Missing checkable facts:** The soldiers/mockers at the cross say “He saved others; himself he cannot save” and the chief priests/scribes/elders mock him (Matthew 27:39-43). If any still shows hostile crowd behavior, this is a specified guard worth including.

12. **Missing checkable fact for Gethsemane:** Matthew 26:37 specifies Jesus took **Peter and the two sons of Zebedee** apart. The sheet says Peter, James, and John sleep, which is correct by identification, but the cited Matthew line in the artifact does not itself name James and John there. Add Matthew 26:37 or mark the identification as harmonized.

VERDICT: REVISE

1. Remove or re-bucket invented “specified” visuals: “bare rise,” “small unmarked lots,” and “hyssop herb bunch bound to a reed.”
2. Separate what each citation actually says from harmonized/traditional inference, especially nails through feet, Zechariah’s pierced prophecy, and David “seeing” the crucifixion.
3. Add missing guards for crucifixion location and other easily misrendered specified details.
