## Independent adversarial review — `cluster_01_cross` biblical fact sheet

Overall: the shared crucifixion core is mostly well-sourced and several honest guards (crown/robe distinction, sponge-not-cup, darkness-not-storm, penitent thief on a cross) are good. The problems are citation over-reach, synoptic blind spots, and a few facts parked in **specified** that the cited KJV does not actually state.

---

### CITATION TRUTH — concrete flags

**1. Golgotha bullet smuggles in two claims the cited verse does not make**

> `**[SPECIFIED]** Golgotha, "that is to say, a place of a skull" (Matthew 27:33) — a bare rise outside the city (Hebrews 13:12 "suffered without the gate").`

- Matthew 27:33 names the place and glosses it as “a place of a skull.” It does **not** say “bare,” “rise,” “hill,” or “outside the city.”
- “Bare rise” is inference/tradition, not a stated visual fact.
- Hebrews 13:12 (“suffered without the gate”) is theological commentary on the passion, not eyewitness narrative for Golgotha’s appearance. It does not describe terrain.
- **Problem:** secondary/historical material is folded into a **specified** line as if Matthew states it.

**2. Scarlet robe is Matthew-only; Mark says purple — synoptic conflict unguarded**

> `"...put on him a scarlet robe." (Matthew 27:28-29)`

- Matthew 27:28 KJV: “scarlet robe” — supported **for Matthew**.
- Mark 15:17 KJV: “clothed him with **purple**.”
- Footer claims derivation from “Matthew 26…; **Mark 15**; Luke…; John…” but the mocking robe is locked to **scarlet** with no Mark guard.
- A Mark-faithful still would be purple; this sheet would wrongly FAIL it while calling itself synoptic.

**3. “Four soldiers” is inference, not diction**

> `Four soldiers; lots = small unmarked objects.` (garments card, citing John 19:23-24)

- John 19:23 says “made **four parts**, to every soldier a part” — four **parts**, not “four soldiers.”
- Four soldiers is a reasonable inference, but the text does not count soldiers.
- “lots = small unmarked objects” is also unstated; John/Matthew only say “cast lots.”

**4. Hyssop botanical detail exceeds John 19:29**

> `Hyssop = a SMALL herb bunch bound to a reed — never a leafy branch`

- John 19:29: “put it upon **hyssop**” — no size, no “bunch,” no binding method.
- Matthew 27:48 (paired citation) says **reed** only — no hyssop at all for the sponge event.
- “Never a leafy branch” is botanical opinion, not KJV content.

**5. `thirty_pieces` conflates Zechariah’s potter action with Judas’s temple action**

> `"cast them to the potter in the house of the LORD."` + `Judas casts them down IN THE TEMPLE (Matthew 27:5)`

- Zechariah 11:13: the prophet casts silver **to the potter in the house of the LORD**.
- Matthew 27:5: Judas casts pieces **in the temple** and departs — no potter, no “house of the LORD” wording in that verse.
- Matthew 27:6-7: chief priests take the money and buy the potter’s field — a different actor and beat.
- **Risk:** a still of Judas throwing coins at a potter is supported by Zechariah, **not** by Matthew 27:5. The bullet blends them without separating which visual beat each citation authorizes.

**6. `pierced` — “the LOOKING is the beat” over-reads the cited crucifixion texts**

> `"they shall look upon me whom they have pierced"` … `the LOOKING is the beat`

- Zechariah 12:10 prophesies mourners looking on the pierced one — valid typology.
- John 19:34 narrates the spear thrust and blood/water; it does **not** narrate a crowd “looking” in that moment.
- John 19:37 cites the prophecy after the fact; still no described onlooker tableau at the thrust.
- **Risk:** mandating a “looking” composition as if John 19:34 states it.

**7. `into_thy_hands` — veil direction is Matthew, not Luke**

> `A veil still tears TOP to bottom (Matthew 27:51 "from the top to the bottom").`

- Anchor is Luke 23:44-46. Luke 23:45: “the veil of the temple was rent **in the midst**” — no top-to-bottom language.
- Top-to-bottom is Matthew 27:51 only. Using it under a Luke anchor without a cross-gospel harmonization note is a citation stretch.

**8. `crucifixion_foretold` — “a thousand years early” is not Scripture**

> `David writing/seeing a thousand years early`

- Psalm 22:18 says garments parted and lots cast — no time gap, no “seeing” the future crucifixion as a vision.
- “Thousand years” is arithmetic/tradition, not a biblical visual fact.

### COVERAGE GAPS (checkable facts missing — paintings could go wrong unguarded)

**Road / mocking section (Matt 27:27-33) is thin**

Missing specified/constrained guards for:
- **Simon of Cyrene** bearing the cross (Matt 27:32; Mark 15:21; Luke 23:26) — common road image, unprotected.
- **Jesus going forth / bearing cross** (John 19:17) — unmentioned.
- **First vinegar offer at crucifixion** — Matt 27:34: “vinegar to drink **mingled with gall**” before the later sponge (27:48). No guard against showing gall-wine as the `i_thirst` sponge, or conflating the two vinegar events.
- **Mocking reed vs vinegar reed** — Matt 27:29 sceptre-reed vs 27:48 sponge-reed; same word, different objects/beats — easy render confusion.

**Chronology guards missing for John-ordered pieces**

- John 19:28-30: **I thirst → vinegar → “It is finished” → bowed head.** The `it_is_finished` anchor cites only 19:30 but does not guard against pairing “finished” with an active sponge-offer (John places vinegar **before** the last word). Downstream `it_is_finished` prompts already mix sponge + finished beat.

**Titulus wording varies across Gospels**

- John 19:19 full form vs Matthew 27:37 / Mark 15:26 / Luke 23:38 shorter variants — sheet cites John only; no note that “JESUS OF NAZARETH THE KING OF THE JEWS” is not the only biblical form.

**Women at the cross**

- John 19:25 list is guarded, but Matthew 27:55-56 / Mark 15:40-41 give overlapping but not identical presence lists — shared core is John-centric only (acceptable if cluster is John-default, but footer claims wider derivation).

**Darkness + forsaken + sponge order differs by Gospel**

- Matthew: darkness (27:45) → ninth-hour cry (27:46) → sponge (27:48).
- John: thirst/sponge/finished without Matthew’s forsaken cry in the same sequence.
- Per-piece anchors don’t warn painters which timeline governs which short.
