## The mocking and the road (Matt 27:27-33)
- **[SPECIFIED]** Crown of thorns, reed, and the soldiers' robe belong to the MOCKING scene: "they stripped him, and put on him a scarlet robe. And when they had platted a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand" (Matthew 27:28-29; Mark 15:17 has "purple" — the robe may render scarlet OR purple, never bind one colour). Before the road they "took the robe off from him, and put his own raiment on him" (Matthew 27:31). ([CONSTRAINED] the crown of thorns at the cross: Scripture never records its removal — with or without is defensible; the mock robe at the cross is a FAIL.)
- **[SPECIFIED]** Simon of Cyrene carried the cross on the road: "him they compelled to bear his cross" (Matthew 27:32). John 19:17 "he bearing his cross went forth" covers the setting-out — either beat is textual; the synoptic road scene shows Simon.
- **[SPECIFIED]** At arrival they offered "vinegar to drink mingled with gall: and when he had tasted thereof, he would not drink" (Matthew 27:34) — this REFUSED drink is DISTINCT from the later sponge (John 19:29-30, which he received).
- **[SPECIFIED]** Golgotha, "that is to say, a place of a skull" (Matthew 27:33). ([CONSTRAINED] outside the city — "suffered without the gate" (Hebrews 13:12) — with a garden nearby (John 19:41); Scripture never calls it a hill, so a rise is licence and barren terrain is never forced.)

## The crucifixion scene (shared by every cross still)
- **[SPECIFIED]** TWO thieves, one on either side: "two thieves crucified with him, one on the right hand, and another on the left" (Matthew 27:38); "they crucified him, and the malefactors, one on the right hand, and the other on the left" (Luke 23:33). A wide Calvary frame shows three crosses; a deliberate tight frame on Christ alone is fine.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The garments were PARTED and LOTS CAST, by soldiers, for a seamless coat: "They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture" (Psalm 22:18); "Then the soldiers... took his garments, and made four parts, to every soldier a part; and also his coat: now the coat was without seam... Let us not rend it, but cast lots for it" (John 19:23-24). Four soldiers. ([CONSTRAINED] lots as small plain pieces is period licence; render guard: no legible marks.)
- **[SPECIFIED]** The title over him was WRITTEN by Pilate in three languages: "JESUS OF NAZARETH THE KING OF THE JEWS... written in Hebrew, and Greek, and Latin" (John 19:19-20). RENDER GUARD (production): the titulus must never carry LEGIBLE text (models garble script) — distant/illegible marks only, or omit the board in tight frames.
- **[SPECIFIED]** DARKNESS from the sixth to the ninth hour: "Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land unto the ninth hour" (Matthew 27:45). It is DARKNESS — not a lightning storm, not rain (weather is never mentioned).
- **[SPECIFIED]** TIMING of the words vs the darkness: crucified at "the third hour" (Mark 15:25); darkness runs sixth to ninth. EARLY sayings — father_forgive_them (Luke 23:34), today_paradise (Luke 23:43), woman_behold (John 19:26-27) — fall in DAYLIGHT before the darkness; LATE beats — forsaken_cry (ninth hour, Matthew 27:46), i_thirst, it_is_finished, into_thy_hands — fall during or at the end of it. A black sky on an early word (or bright noon on a late one) contradicts the record.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The vinegar came on a SPONGE, raised on hyssop/a reed: "they filled a spunge with vinegar, and put it upon hyssop, and put it to his mouth" (John 19:29); "took a spunge, and filled it with vinegar, and put it on a reed" (Matthew 27:48). Never a cup lifted by hand to his lips. ([CONSTRAINED] hyssop rendered as a small herb bunch bound to a reed harmonizes John and Matthew; never a leafy tree branch.)
- **[SPECIFIED]** The women and John stood BY the cross: "Now there stood by the cross of Jesus his mother, and his mother's sister, Mary the wife of Cleophas, and Mary Magdalene" and "the disciple standing by, whom he loved" (John 19:25-26).
- **[SPECIFIED]** His legs were NOT broken; his SIDE was pierced AFTER death, with blood and water: "when they came to Jesus, and saw that he was dead already, they brake not his legs: But one of the soldiers with a spear pierced his side, and forthwith came there out blood and water" (John 19:33-34). A spear-thrust still shows Christ already dead — "he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost" (John 19:30). ([CONSTRAINED] closed eyes are the natural rendering of death, not a stated fact.)
- **[CONSTRAINED]** Crucifixion form: nails through hands and feet are textual ("the print of the nails", John 20:25; "they pierced my hands and my feet", Psalm 22:16; "Behold my hands and my feet", Luke 24:39). Render note: tight nail/wound close-ups blob in this engine — prefer mid framings; ropes may steady the arms alongside nails, never replace the nails in a visible-wound frame. Christ stripped of garments (John 19:23) — the loincloth is the standing reverence convention. Wounds restrained, never gore (not-grotesque axis).
- **[CONSTRAINED]** Period: Roman soldiers in 1st-century kit (no medieval plate armour), Judean dress for onlookers, no halos, no modern objects.

## Per-piece anchors
- **father_forgive_them (Luke 23:34):** "Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots." Spoken FROM the cross while soldiers gambled below.
- **today_paradise (Luke 23:39-43):** two malefactors speak; one rebukes the other: "we receive the due reward of our deeds: but this man hath done nothing amiss"; "Lord, remember me"; "To day shalt thou be with me in paradise." The penitent thief is ON A CROSS beside him — never kneeling at Jesus' feet. (Matthew 27:44: at first BOTH "cast the same in his teeth" — the penitence is a TURN; an early still may show both reviling.)
- **woman_behold (John 19:26-27):** "Woman, behold thy son! Then saith he to the disciple, Behold thy mother!" Mary and John TOGETHER beneath the cross; "from that hour that disciple took her unto his own home."
- **i_thirst (John 19:28-29 + Psalm 22:15):** "I thirst" precedes the vinegar sponge (see sponge card). Psalm 22:15 imagery: "my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death" — dried clay/dust licence for the psalm beat.
- **it_is_finished (John 19:30):** "he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost." The bowed head follows the word — a "finished" still shows the work complete, not agony.
- **into_thy_hands (Luke 23:44-46):** darkness over all the earth, "the veil of the temple was rent in the midst", "Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit." A veil still tears TOP to bottom (Matthew 27:51 "from the top to the bottom").
- **forsaken_cry (Matthew 27:46 + Psalm 22:1):** "about the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani" — quoting David's "My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?" (Psalm 22:1). Ninth-hour darkness applies.
- **crucifixion_foretold (Psalm 22:18):** David writing/seeing a thousand years early; the fulfilment = the soldiers' gambling (see garments card). David scenes: Iron-Age Israel, lyre/scroll licence — RENDER GUARD: no legible script on any scroll.
- **pierced (Zechariah 12:10 + John 19:34-37):** "they shall look upon me whom they have pierced" — the spear-thrust (dead-already card applies); the LOOKING is the beat.
- **thirty_pieces (Zechariah 11:12-13 + Matthew 26:15; 27:3-10):** "they weighed for my price thirty pieces of silver... And I took the thirty pieces of silver, and cast them to the potter in the house of the LORD" (Zechariah 11:12-13, the PROPHETIC voice). Fulfilment beats are distinct: JUDAS "cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed, and went and hanged himself" (Matthew 27:5); the PRIESTS "bought with them the potter's field" (Matthew 27:6-7). Thirty SILVER coins; a temple-casting still shows Judas, a field still the priests' purchase. Coins carry no legible lettering.
- **watch_one_hour (Matthew 26:36-46):** "a place called Gethsemane" at NIGHT; he "took with him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee" (Matthew 26:37 — Peter, James, John); Jesus withdraws "a little farther" (Matthew 26:39; Luke 22:41 "about a stone's cast") and prays; the three SLEEP: "What, could ye not watch with me one hour?" (Matthew 26:40). Pre-arrest: no soldiers, no wounds. ([CONSTRAINED] olive trees — Gethsemane is on the mount of Olives (Matthew 26:30); "garden" is John 18:1.) The angel and sweat "as it were great drops of blood" are Luke 22:43-44 — permitted only if drawn from Luke, sweat never as gore.
