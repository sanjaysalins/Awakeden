## TITLE
He Saw Her Wound From the Cross

## DESCRIPTION
At the cross (John 19:26), a thirty-year-old warning landed: Simeon had told Mary (Luke 2:35) that a sword shall pierce through thy own soul also. Thirty years later she stood at the cross, and Jesus saw her wound and bound it: "Woman, behold thy son!" (John 19:26, KJV). From that hour John took her into his own home.

While judgment fell on him, he cared for someone else. Your wound is not invisible to him either. Stand where Mary stood.
