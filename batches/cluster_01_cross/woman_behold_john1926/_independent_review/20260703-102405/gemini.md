## Nitpick / Minor Polish
"Jesus looks down, sees her wound, and **stops** to bind it." 
A crucified man cannot physically "stop" what is happening to him. While it works metaphorically (pausing his own internal agony to focus outward), the physical verb "stops" slightly jars against the reality of being pinned to a cross. A simpler "sees her wound, and binds it" or "turns to bind it" avoids the physical paradox while keeping the narrative momentum, but this is a stylistic micro-polish, not a blocking failure.
