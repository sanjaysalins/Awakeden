# Whom They Pierced — Zechariah 12:10

<!-- HOOK -->
[narrator] A Roman soldier raised his spear on a Friday afternoon, and finished a sentence God had started over five hundred years earlier.

<!-- POINT -->
[narrator] In Zechariah twelve, the LORD himself is speaking. And he claims the wound as his own.

<!-- PROOF -->
[scripture] and they shall look upon me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for him, as one mourneth for his only son

[narrator] Look upon ME. Mourn for HIM. The same person. The LORD who speaks is the Son who bleeds.

[narrator] John stood close enough to watch the spear go in. He wrote:

[scripture] And again another scripture saith, They shall look on him whom they pierced.

<!-- CONVICTION -->
[narrator] The soldier saw a dying man. John saw God keeping his own word. God did not send a stranger to bleed for you. He came himself. And the promise begins with what he pours out. Not wrath. The spirit of grace and of supplications. He gives the tears he asks for.

<!-- LANDING -->
[narrator] So do what the verse says. Look at him. Look at the pierced One, and let that look lead you home.
