## TITLE
The Wound God Called His Own - 500 Years Early

## DESCRIPTION
Five hundred years before the cross, the LORD said something astonishing through Zechariah: "and they shall look upon me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for him, as one mourneth for his only son" (Zechariah 12:10, KJV).

Look upon ME - God is speaking. John watched the spear go in and knew: this was God keeping his own word (John 19:37). And the promise begins with grace, not wrath - he gives the very tears he asks for.

Look at him - and live.
