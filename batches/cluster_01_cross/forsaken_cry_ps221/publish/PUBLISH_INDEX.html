TITLE
Jesus' Darkest Cry Was a 1,000-Year-Old Quote
DESCRIPTION
From the cross (Psalm 22:1), in his darkest hour, Jesus reached for words already written: a psalm David had penned a thousand years before.

David's very first line: "My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?" At the ninth hour, Jesus cried the very same words from the cross.

And listen closely: this was no cry of lost faith. Even forsaken, he still calls God MY God. It was the sinless Son taking our place, bearing the forsaking that our sin deserved.

He was forsaken so that you never will be. However far you have run, the way home is open, and he opened it from the dark.
