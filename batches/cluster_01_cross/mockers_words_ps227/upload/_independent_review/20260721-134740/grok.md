## Blocking

### 1. Wrong Psalm citation for “wagging heads” (YouTube Short + Long + Facebook)

Exact phrases:

- **YouTube Short:**  
  `(Psalm 22:8; Matthew 27:43)` + later *“the wagging heads, the sneer, the very words”*
- **YouTube Long:**  
  *“the gestures, the sneer, nearly line for line (Psalm 22:8)”*
- **Facebook:**  
  *“the wagging heads, the sneer, the very words (Psalm 22:8)”*

**KJV fact:** head-shaking is **Psalm 22:7** (*“they shake the head”*). **Psalm 22:8** is the taunt-words (*“He trusted on the LORD…”*). Pinning heads + words to **22:8 only** is a citation error a careful reader will catch.  
**Fix everywhere they appear together:** `(Psalm 22:7-8)` (or cite 7 for heads / 8 for words).

Tags reinforce the error: **`psalm 22 8`** with no **`psalm 22 7`**.

### 2. TikTok overclaim — “word for word”

Exact phrase:

> *“Matthew heard them repeated **word for word**.”*

False on two counts:

1. **Video/description elsewhere** already uses the honest bar: *“nearly line for line”* (YT Short/Long/FB).

Also loose: *“Matthew **heard** them”* — Matthew **records** them. Prefer *“Matthew records them repeated nearly line for line.”*
