# Independent review — codex (OK, 67s)

Findings:

- **Kit verification**: It claims **“Gates: ALL PASS”** and “pre-checked, red-teamed and panel-reviewed,” but the embedded red-team says **“VERDICT: REVISE”** and identifies an unfixed TikTok issue. That is internally dishonest metadata packaging.

- **TikTok — “nation after nation has turned to the LORD”**: This overstates the claim. It implies whole nations converted, while the stronger and more accurate wording elsewhere is **“people in nation after nation have turned.”**

- **YouTube Short / YouTube Long / Facebook — “ends with every nation on earth”**: This is a bit too neat. Psalm 22:27 says “all the kindreds of the nations,” and the psalm does not literally end at verse 27. It is defensible as a hook, but still imprecise.

- **Instagram — “Then: all the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the LORD.”** This is a KJV fragment without quotation marks or immediate citation. The wording is KJV-exact as a fragment, but attribution is too loose for a scripture-led caption.

- **Title option 4 — “The Song That Swept the Whole Earth”**: This overclaims. The video appears to argue the gospel went to the nations, not that the psalm/song itself swept the whole earth.

- **TikTok / Instagram / Facebook — “Subscribe to Awakeden”**: Platform CTA is off. “Subscribe” fits YouTube; TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook should say “Follow Awakeden.”

- **YouTube hashtags**: The kit presents **“HASHTAGS (copy)”** as a separate block, but YouTube does not have a normal standalone hashtag upload field like tags. If used, these need to go in the description/title workflow, not as a separate field.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix TikTok’s “nation after nation has turned” to “people in nation after nation have turned.”
2. Remove the false “ALL PASS” packaging while the kit itself contains a REVISE verdict.
3. Tighten “every nation on earth” / “ends with every nation” so it does not overstate Psalm 22:27 or imply verse 27 is the final line.
