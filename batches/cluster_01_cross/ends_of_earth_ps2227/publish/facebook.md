## TITLE
It Was Never a Local Tragedy. It Was for the Nations.

## DESCRIPTION
Psalm 22 opens with one forsaken man dying alone, and ends with every nation on earth (Psalm 22:27).

"All the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the LORD: and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee" (Psalm 22:27, KJV).

From the cross and the empty tomb the gospel went out, and people in nation after nation have turned to the LORD, worshipping the One who died and rose. And the ends of the world includes wherever you are, right now. The Lord this song promised still has room for you to turn to him.
