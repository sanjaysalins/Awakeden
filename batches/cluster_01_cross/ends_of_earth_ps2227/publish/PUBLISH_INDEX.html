TITLE
The Psalm That Starts With One Dying Man and Ends With Every Nation
DESCRIPTION
Psalm 22 opens with one forsaken man dying alone, and ends with every nation on earth (Psalm 22:27).

"All the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the LORD: and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee" (Psalm 22:27, KJV).

A man dying in one corner of the Roman Empire, and his song says the ends of the earth will turn to God. It sounded impossible. But from that cross and the empty tomb, the gospel went out, and people in nation after nation have turned to the LORD, worshipping the One who died and rose. It was never a local tragedy. It was for the nations.

And the ends of the world includes wherever you are, right now. The Lord this song promised still has room for you to turn to him.
