# It Is Finished — John 19:30 (with Genesis 2:1, John 1:3, John 17:4)

Series: Words from the Cross · Cluster 1 (The Cross) · style: inked motion-comic (living page)
Note: quotes are whole verses or clause-boundary fragments - Gen 2:1, John 1:3a, John 17:4, John 19:30 (full).

[narrator] The first time the Bible says finished, nothing was broken yet.
[scripture] Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them.
[narrator] Creation complete. God rested. He does not leave work half done.
[narrator] When sin wrecked that world, the Son came down to mend it. The Bible says of Jesus:
[scripture] All things were made by him.
[narrator] The hands that finished creation came to finish redemption. Before the cross, he prayed.
[jesus] I have glorified thee on the earth: I have finished the work which thou gavest me to do.
[narrator] He carried it to the end.
[scripture] When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost.
[narrator] Two finished works. One Workman. He finished creation, and rested. He finished redemption, and rested in the tomb through the sabbath. And on the first day, morning came.
[narrator] You were never asked to finish it. You are asked to rest in it.
[narrator] He bowed his head so you could lift yours.
[narrator] The work is finished. Enter his rest.
