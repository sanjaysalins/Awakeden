## Adversarial review — `vinegar_sponge_reed` fact sheet v2

### CITATION TRUTH

**C3 — “He wore His OWN garments (no purple robe at the cross)” — over-reach / mis-citation for a John-primary scene.**

Matthew 27:31 only says own raiment was put on **before** he was led away to crucify. It does **not** say he still wore those garments **at** the cross during the vinegar moment.

John 19:23–24 (same episode, verses before 19:28–29) explicitly says that **when they had crucified Jesus, they took his garments** and divided them. At the sponge moment, John’s narrative has already stripped and parted his clothing. The quoted Matthew verse does not support “He wore His OWN garments” **at the cross**; it supports “no purple mock-robe on the way to Golgotha,” which is a weaker, earlier claim.

This is the sheet’s worst citation error: a **CONSTRAINED** negative (no purple robe) is bundled with a **positive garment state the cited text does not state for this moment**.

---

**C1 footnote — “only ‘a bunch’ and ‘small,’ which the verses state” — false.**

- Exodus 12:22 says **“a bunch of hyssop”** — supports *bunch*, not *small*.
- 1 Kings 4:33 contrasts cedar with hyssop **“that springeth out of the wall”** — implies scale by contrast; the KJV never uses the word **“small.”**

The inference may be reasonable in **CONSTRAINED**, but the parenthetical claims explicit biblical wording that is not there.

---

**C2 — Matthew 27:47–48 cited as warrant for the John 19:29 actor — harmonization, not direct support.**

John 19:29 says **“they filled a spunge”** — that alone supports “unidentified plural actor.” Fine.

Matthew 27:47–48 describes vinegar offered after **“This man calleth for Elias”** (mocking/Elias context), not after John’s **“I thirst.”** Using Matthew to characterize the offerer as a neutral “bystander” for **this** John beat imports a Synoptic scene that may or may not be the same event. It does not contradict the plain-man depiction, but it is **misapplied as citation backing** for John’s moment.

---

**S4 — Luke 23:44–45 quoted accurately, but not anchored to John’s vinegar beat.**

Luke does say **“darkness over all the earth”** and **“the sun was darkened.”** Citation text is correct.

However, **John 19:28–30 never mentions darkness or hour.** S4 is a **cross-gospel composite** for a still keyed to John. That is defensible for cluster shorts, but the sheet presents S4 as **SPECIFIED** with no note that John’s pericope is silent on darkness/timing, and no note of the known **sixth-hour** tension (John 19:14 vs Luke 23:44 / Mark 15:25). The claim is biblically true in Luke; it is **not** textually tied to the John vinegar action.

---

**S1 — accurate but incomplete relative to its own quote.**

John 19:29 KJV: **“Now there was set a vessel full of vinegar…”** then sponge/hyssop/mouth. S1 guards sponge + vinegar + hyssop + mouth but drops **“a vessel full of vinegar.”** That object is explicitly stated in the same verse; if the lower scene is visible, it is a checkable **SPECIFIED** omission.

---

**S2, S3 — citations support the claims; scope is narrative, not purely visual.**

- **S2:** John 19:28 does say **“I thirst.”** ✓  
- **S3:** John 19:30 does say **“had received the vinegar, he said, It is finished.”** ✓  

Both are valid for the dual shorts, but they guard **speech/sequence**, not what a single still of “sponge to mouth” must visibly show. Minor bucket blur, not a false citation.
