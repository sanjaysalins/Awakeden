## Adversarial review — `vinegar_sponge_reed` fact sheet

### CITATION TRUTH

**S1 — PASS.** John 19:29 KJV directly states sponge + vinegar + hyssop + to His mouth. No over-reach.

**S2 — PASS with nuance.** John 19:30 supports sequence: received vinegar → “It is finished.” The citation is accurate. But “It is finished” is **auditory/narrative**, not a visual fact for the sponge still; bundling it with “received the vinegar” blurs visual vs narrative guardrails.

**S3 — PASS but weakly anchored to *this* beat.** John 19:2 does say a crown of thorns was put on His head. The text does **not** say it was still on at the vinegar moment. Reasonable cross-scene inference (nothing says it was removed), but the cited verse is from the mocking, not the cross beat. Same verse also specifies a **purple robe** — if S3 is “specified,” the sheet is oddly silent about robe *removal* (Matt 27:31 / Mark 15:20), which a painter could get wrong.

**C1 — PARTIAL MISAPPLICATION.** Matthew 27:48 / Mark 15:36 do **not** describe the John 19:28–29 “I thirst” event. They tie vinegar to the **Eli/Elias** cry (Matt 27:47; Mark 15:35–36), a different verbal trigger. Using Synoptic “one of them” to license the **John-centered still** assumes harmonization the texts do not state. Luke 23:36 (“soldiers… offering him vinegar”) is explicitly **earlier mockery** — the sheet notes that, but then still treats “Roman soldier” as a live option for *this* scene; Luke **cannot** support that for John 19:29.

**C2 — PASS.** Reed (Synoptics) vs hyssop (John) is correctly flagged. John 19:29 supports hyssop for these shorts.

**C3 — OVER-REACH in the depiction, not in the citation.** Luke 23:44 supports **darkness**, not “storm sky” (see depiction line: “Dark storm sky”). Luke says “darkness over all the earth” / “the sun was darkened” (Luke 23:45) — supernatural darkening, not necessarily storm clouds. “Storm” is imported detail the citation does not support.

### NO INVENTED FACTS / RULE VIOLATIONS

**Depiction header smuggles unbucketed “specified” details** (violates the sheet’s own rule: *“ONLY faces / character look are free”*):

- **“near hand nailed through the palm”** — No Gospel specifies palm vs wrist; nail placement is tradition, not KJV text. This is either **FREE** or should be absent. As written, it is asserted as scene truth with no bucket and no citation.
- **“Roman soldier in helmet and armour”** — Not in John 19:29 (“**they** filled a spunge…”). Identity/costume is interpretation, not Scripture.
- **“leafy green branch… laurel-like”** (panel Q3) — John says “hyssop,” not “large leafy branch.” Ex 12:22 KJV: “a **bunch** of hyssop” (small sprinkling implement). A laurel-sized branch is botanical invention unless bucketed as FREE.
- **“barren 1st-century Judea”** — Period/terrain not stated in the cited verses.

**“pale porous sea-sponge”** — John says “spunge,” not “sea-sponge.” Minor, but “sea-” is an unstated material claim.
