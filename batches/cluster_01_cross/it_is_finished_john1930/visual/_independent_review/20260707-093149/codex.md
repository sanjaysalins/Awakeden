# Independent review — codex (OK, 148s)

**Findings**

- **S3 overreaches.** “Jesus wore a crown of thorns” cites John 19:2, but that verse only says the soldiers put it on His head during the mockery. It does **not** state He still wore it on the cross. “Thorn-crowned Christ hangs on his cross” is traditional/compatible, not specified.

- **C1 is not Scripture-driven enough.** “Our image shows a Roman soldier” is not stated in John 19:29, Matthew 27:48, or Mark 15:36. Luke 23:36 says soldiers offered vinegar, but not the sponge-on-hyssop/reed action. A named Roman soldier with helmet/armour is licence, not a biblical fact.

- **C2 does not resolve the reed/hyssop problem.** John 19:29 says hyssop; Matthew 27:48 and Mark 15:36 say reed. Saying “our image shows hyssop…not a bare reed” lets the image contradict the Synoptics unless the visual is harmonized. A “large laurel-like leafy branch” is not supported by any cited verse.

- **C3 is bucketed wrongly.** Darkness is explicitly stated in Luke 23:44, so it belongs in specified if cross-Gospel facts are allowed. But “dark storm sky” is not stated; darkness does not equal storm clouds.

- **S1 omits a specified object from its own quote.** John 19:29 says “there was set a vessel full of vinegar.” The fact only guards the sponge/vinegar/hyssop/mouth. If the lower scene is visible, the vessel is a checkable biblical detail.

- **“Pale porous sea-sponge” is over-specific.** John 19:29 says “spunge.” It does not specify colour, texture, or sea-sponge material.

- **“Pressing the sponge gently” is invented.** The cited texts say “put it to his mouth” and “gave him to drink.” They do not specify gentleness; Mark 15:36 even places the action beside “Let alone; let us see whether Elias will come…”

- **“Near hand nailed through the palm” is unguarded and over-specific.** John 20:25-27 supports nail marks in His hands, not palms specifically. Palm placement should not be treated as specified.

- **Setting details are under-cited.** “Barren 1st-century Judea” is not in the cited texts. More concrete biblical setting facts are missing: Golgotha/place of a skull, near the city, and Jesus crucified with two others if the scene is wide enough.

- **Christ-lens coverage is thin.** For `i_thirst`, John 19:28 is essential: Jesus says “I thirst” knowing Scripture is being fulfilled. The sheet treats the scene mainly as prop accuracy and misses that stated fulfillment frame.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Move crown-at-cross, Roman soldier, large leafy hyssop branch, storm clouds, sea-sponge details, and palm nail placement out of “specified” treatment unless directly cited.
2. Harmonize or honestly constrain John’s “hyssop” with Matthew/Mark’s “reed”; do not depict a large laurel-like branch as biblically specified.
3. Add missing specified facts from John 19:28-30 and 19:29: fulfillment/“I thirst,” vessel full of vinegar, received vinegar, then “It is finished.”
