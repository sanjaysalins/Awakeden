# Vinegar-Sponge Still — Biblical Fact Sheet (for the 5-CLI accuracy panel)

**Scene slug:** `vinegar_sponge_reed` — used in the shorts `i_thirst` (John 19:28) and `it_is_finished` (John 19:30).

**Depiction being judged (the rendered still):** The crucified, thorn-crowned Christ hangs on his cross (near hand nailed through the palm). A Roman soldier in helmet and armour stands below and reaches up a **leafy green branch of hyssop** bearing a **pale porous sea-sponge soaked in vinegar**, pressing the sponge gently to Christ's lips. Dark storm sky; barren 1st-century Judea.

**Rule for this project:** every still must be biblically accurate; ONLY faces / character look are free artistic licence — everything else (objects, actions, plant/material, who does the action, setting, period) must be Scripture-driven.

## SPECIFIED facts (Bible explicitly states this visual fact → the image MUST match)

**S1 — A sponge filled with vinegar was put upon hyssop and put to His mouth.**
> John 19:29 (KJV): "Now there was set a vessel full of vinegar: and they filled a spunge with vinegar, and put it upon hyssop, and put it to his mouth."

**S2 — Jesus received the vinegar, then said, It is finished.**
> John 19:30 (KJV): "When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost."

**S3 — Jesus wore a crown of thorns.**
> John 19:2 (KJV): "And the soldiers platted a crown of thorns, and put it on his head, and they put on him a purple robe,"

## CONSTRAINED facts (not stated exactly, but the depiction could contradict the text)

**C1 — WHO lifted the sponge.** Our image shows a **Roman soldier**. The nearest accounts say "one of them" — i.e. one of the bystanders standing there who were mocking; Luke separately has soldiers offering vinegar during the earlier mocking.
> Matthew 27:47-48 (KJV): "Some of them that stood there, when they heard that, said, This man calleth for Elias. And straightway one of them ran, and took a spunge, and filled it with vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink."
> Mark 15:36 (KJV): "And one ran and filled a spunge full of vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink, saying, Let alone; let us see whether Elias will come to take him down."
> Luke 23:36 (KJV): "And the soldiers also mocked him, coming to him, and offering him vinegar,"

**C2 — Reed vs hyssop.** The Synoptics say the sponge was on a **reed**; John says **hyssop**. Both these shorts quote John, and our image shows **hyssop** (a green plant), not a bare reed.
> Matthew 27:48 (KJV): "...and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink."
> Mark 15:36 (KJV): "...and put it on a reed..."
> John 19:29 (KJV): "...and put it upon hyssop..."

**C3 — Setting: crucifixion under darkness.**
> Luke 23:44 (KJV): "And it was about the sixth hour, and there was a darkness over all the earth until the ninth hour."
