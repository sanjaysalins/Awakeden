[narrator] In Aramaic, "Barabbas" means "son of the father."
[narrator] He was a robber in prison.
[narrator] Pilate, the governor, offered to free one man. Pilate knew the leaders were jealous of Jesus.
[pilate] Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?
[crowd] Barabbas.
[pilate] Why, what evil hath he done?
[crowd] Let him be crucified.
[narrator] Listen: Bar-ABBA. Jesus called God "Abba." It means Father.
[narrator] So the robber went free. The true Son of the Father did not.
[narrator] Barabbas only had the name. Jesus died so we could call God Father too.
