## TITLE
In Aramaic, Barabbas means "son of the father": look who went free

## DESCRIPTION
In Aramaic, Barabbas means "son of the father." The crowd chose him to go free, and Jesus, the true Son of the Father, was sent to the cross. Matthew 27:17: "Whom will ye that I release unto you? Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?" Say the name slowly: Bar-Abba. Then watch who goes free.
