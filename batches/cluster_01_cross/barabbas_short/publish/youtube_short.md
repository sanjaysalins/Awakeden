## TITLE
In Aramaic, Barabbas means "son of the father": look who went free

## DESCRIPTION
In Aramaic, Barabbas means "son of the father." The crowd chose him to go free, and Jesus, the true Son of the Father, was sent to the cross. Matthew 27:17: "Whom will ye that I release unto you? Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?" Quoted word for word from the KJV: Matthew 27:17, 21-23. "Robber" is from John 18:40. "Abba" is from Mark 14:36. The closing line follows Galatians 4:5-6. Say the name slowly: Bar-Abba. Then watch who goes free.
