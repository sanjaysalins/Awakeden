## Weak spots, honestly
1. **"He was a robber"** comes from John 18:40, not Matthew 27. Matthew only calls him "a notable prisoner" (27:16). The scripture range in the description does not cover that line. Fix: add "(see John 18:40)" in the description, or accept it.
2. **Meaning of the name:** "son of the father" is the standard reading, but a few scholars read it differently (for example "son of the teacher"). We state it as plain fact. That is fair for a Short, but it is not 100% settled.
3. **"Bar-ABBA" sound link:** the Abba wordplay works best for viewers who see the on-screen card. Listeners with sound only, or ESL viewers, may miss it.
4. **Last line jump:** "Jesus died to give it to us" moves fast from the name to being adopted as God's children. A newcomer may not link "it" to "the name / being a son of the Father". Odds a newcomer gets it with no help: about 55% (+/- 15%).
5. **"The crowd":** the public text must keep saying "the crowd" only. No people group named. Card is clean on this.
6. **Title A** gives the meaning away up front. That is good for search, but it weakens the 0-2 s surprise for people who read titles first. Title B keeps the gap but is more generic.
7. **Search guesses are unverified.** Odds the "who was barabbas" class drives real traffic: unknown. Seed viewers and the 48-hour retention read matter more than these words.
