## The 4 questions
- Who is it for: anyone curious about the Easter story, plus Bible readers who half-know the name Barabbas.
- Take-away: the robber's name means "son of the father." Jesus, the true Son of the Father, took his place.
- Curiosity gap: "What does Barabbas' name mean, and why does it matter?" Opens in the first 2 seconds, pays off at "Bar-ABBA" (about 0:20) and the last line.
- Global audience: yes. Short plain words, one idea, no church jargon. Weak spot: "governor", "crucified" and "Abba" need the on-screen cards.
