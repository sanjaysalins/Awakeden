## Weak spots (honest)
1. **"Robber" is John's word, not Matthew's.** Matthew 27:16 says "notable prisoner"; John 18:40 says "robber"; Mark/Luke say insurrection and murder. The film says "robber in prison." Fine, but keep public copy to "the man set free" or "the prisoner" where possible, and expect one sharp comment.
2. **Verse order on screen.** Pilate's 27:23 line is heard before the crowd line tagged 27:22. "Let him be crucified" is in both 22 and 23 in the KJV, so the tag is defensible — but a viewer following along may notice 23 before 22.
3. **"Son of the father" is the standard reading, not the only one.** Some scholars read Bar-Rabban ("son of the teacher"). Bar-Abba is the mainstream view and safe to state; just never write "proves" in copy.
4. **The landing is sound but uncited.** "Jesus died to give it to us" rests on Romans 8:15 / Galatians 4:6 ("Abba, Father"). The film does not cite them. If a commenter asks "where does it say that?", reply with those two verses — do not add them to the title or description.
