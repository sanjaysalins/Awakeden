## TITLE
The One Word Jesus Added to the Psalm

## DESCRIPTION
His last words (Luke 23:46) were an evening prayer children learned for the dark, Psalm 31:5. But Jesus began with a word the Psalm does not have: "Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit."

He died like a child falling asleep in his father's arms, and on the third day, morning came.

You can close your eyes in those same hands. Not because death is small. Because the hands are strong. He keeps the morning.
