## TITLE
Jesus Died Praying a Child's Bedtime Prayer

## DESCRIPTION
A dying man's last words (Luke 23:46). And they were a child's prayer.

Jewish tradition made Psalm 31:5 an evening prayer, words a child could learn for the dark: into thine hand I commit my spirit: thou hast redeemed me, O LORD God of truth.

On the cross, after the three hours of darkness, Jesus prays those evening words with his last breath: "And when Jesus had cried with a loud voice, he said, Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit: and having said thus, he gave up the ghost" (Luke 23:46, KJV).

And he begins with a word the Psalm does not have. Father. Not judge. Not stranger. Father.

The Psalm he prayed says thou hast redeemed me. On that cross, he was buying that redemption. He dies like a child falling asleep in his father's arms. And on the third day, morning came.

You can close your eyes in those same hands. Not because death is small. Because the hands are strong. He keeps the morning.
