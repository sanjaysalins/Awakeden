## TITLE
They Were Still Gambling When He Forgave Them

## DESCRIPTION
From the cross comes a word no one expected (Luke 23:34): "Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots" (Luke 23:34, KJV).

As they divided his clothing, Jesus prayed for the people putting him to death. The prayer does not excuse the sin; it intercedes for the sinner. And the sin that put him there was ours too.

While we were yet sinners, Christ died for us. That mercy is held out to you now. Come, and receive it by faith.
