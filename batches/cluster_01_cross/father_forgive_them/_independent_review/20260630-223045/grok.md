### Findings (real issues only)

**1. Chronology slip — *"As they nail him up and divide his clothes"***  
Hook already has *"Nails through his hands"* and soldiers *"gambling for his clothes"* — post-crucifixion, matching Luke 23:33–34 (crucified, then prayer while raiment is parted).  

POINT line rewinds to *"as they nail him up"* — sounds like the prayer happens **during** nailing. Luke does not say that. Internal contradiction on a plain hearing. Not heresy, but a **grounding/clarity** error the v2 panel already flagged in different wording.

**2. Minor compression — *"the sin that held him there"***  
Sin necessitated the cross; nails held him physically; he **chose** to stay (John 10:18). Next line *"he gave himself willingly for it"* mostly corrects this. Slight imprecision only — not a doctrinal fail.

**3. Gospel label stacks two truths — *"This is the gospel: while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us — pleading mercy before we ever knew we needed it"***  
Rom 5:8 is **atoning death**; *"pleading mercy"* comes from Luke’s intercession. The em dash fuses them. Synthesis is evangelical-standard and the prior **died for us → interceded** error is fixed. At most imprecise framing for a careful listener — **not** a grounding/citation error under your narrow brief.
