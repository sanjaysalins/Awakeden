## Review — Luke 23:34 "Father, forgive them"

**KJV-verbatim — all quoted Scripture is exact. ✅**
- "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do." — exact KJV (Luke 23:34a).
- The full quote "Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots." — verbatim KJV, including the attribution and 34b. Good that you carried "And they parted his raiment, and cast lots" exactly.
- The partial "They know not what they do" — exact words (drops a mid-clause "for," which is legitimate for an extraction).

**Doctrine — sound and Christ-landing. ✅**
- "does not excuse the sin; it intercedes for the sinner" — this is the careful, correct distinction. It pre-empts the universalist/"ignorance acquits" misreading. Strong.
- "Come to the cross, and receive the forgiveness he was already asking for" — grace-anchored, response-conditioned ("come… receive"), no fear/gain-loss/works framing. ✅
- Hook grips in the first 5 seconds; one thread (intercession) runs hook→middle→CTA without swapping; landing does fresh work ("the hands being nailed down are the hands still reaching for you"). ✅

**Concerns (advisory, not disqualifying):**

1. **"while we were yet sinners, Christ interceded"** — this borrows the unmistakable cadence of Romans 5:8 ("while we were yet sinners, Christ **died for us**") but swaps the verb to "interceded." It is NOT in quotation marks, so it is not a KJV-verbatim violation, and the claim is doctrinally true (Isa 53:12 / Heb 7:25). But a listener who knows the verse will hear a near-miss misquote. Either quote Rom 5:8 properly, or reword so it doesn't echo the verse while changing its verb.

2. **Extending "them" to "us"** ("our sin held him there… he was already praying for us"). The prayer's immediate referent is the executioners. The extension to all sinners is a legitimate evangelical application (his intercession is for sinners broadly), and the script grounds it honestly ("our sin held him there"). Defensible — just be aware it's homiletical application, not the verse's direct sense.

3. **Note (no action needed for a KJV channel):** Luke 23:34a is textually disputed in the manuscript tradition, but it is in the KJV/Received Text — so it is fully appropriate here. Flagging only for awareness.

Nothing rises to invented doctrine, contrarian reading, or an altered quote.

VERDICT: PASS
TOP FIXES:
1. Reword "while we were yet sinners, Christ interceded" so it either quotes Romans 5:8 accurately or stops echoing its cadence with a changed verb (avoid the near-miss misquote).
2. Optionally sharpen the "them → us" move so it's clearly application (Christ's intercession reaches every sinner) rather than implying the soldiers and the listener are the identical referent.
3. No KJV fix needed — all in-quote Scripture is verbatim; keep it.
