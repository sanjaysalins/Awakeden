# Independent review — codex (OK, 23s)

Findings:

1. **Overextension from the text:** “**he was already praying for us**” moves from Jesus praying for His executioners in Luke 23:34 to Jesus specifically praying for the listener. That may be devotionally intended, but Luke 23:34 does not directly say this. Ground it instead in Romans 5:8 / Hebrews 7:25 if you want to broaden to sinners generally.

2. **Doctrinal wording needs tightening:** “**receive the forgiveness he was already asking for**” could imply forgiveness is automatically granted to all because Jesus asked, rather than received through repentance and faith in Christ. It should land on Christ’s finished work and offered forgiveness without blurring response.

3. **Possible biblical precision issue:** “**the very men driving the nails**” is plausible, but Luke 23:34 immediately connects the saying with “**they parted his raiment, and cast lots**.” The text clearly includes the soldiers gambling for His clothes; it does not explicitly identify only the nail-drivers. Safer: “the men crucifying Him” or “the soldiers beneath Him.”

4. **KJV accuracy:** The full verse line, “**Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots.**” is exact KJV from Luke 23:34. The quoted phrase “**They know not what they do**” uses KJV words, though the KJV phrase appears lower-case after “for.” No altered word.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Tighten “he was already praying for us” so it is biblically grounded beyond Luke 23:34.
2. Revise “receive the forgiveness he was already asking for” to avoid implying automatic forgiveness apart from faith.
3. Change “the very men driving the nails” to a text-grounded phrase like “the men crucifying Him” or “the soldiers beneath Him.”
