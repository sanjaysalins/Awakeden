<!-- HOOK -->
[narrator] Nails through his hands. Soldiers at his feet, gambling for his clothes. And from the cross comes a word no one expected — not a curse.

<!-- POINT -->
[narrator] It is a prayer. As they divide his clothing, Jesus prays for the very people putting him to death.

[jesus] Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do.

<!-- PROOF -->
[narrator] Luke records it as they gambled:

[scripture] And they parted his raiment, and cast lots.

[narrator] "They know not what they do" does not excuse the sin; it intercedes for the sinner.

<!-- CONVICTION -->
[narrator] And the sin that put him there was ours too. He gave himself willingly — and the Lord who prayed for his executioners still lives to make intercession for sinners.

<!-- LANDING -->
[narrator] This is the gospel: while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us — pleading mercy before we knew we needed it. That mercy is held out to you now: come, and receive it by faith.
