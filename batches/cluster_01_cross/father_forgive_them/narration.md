# Father, forgive them — Luke 23:34
Series: The 7 Words from the Cross · Cluster 1 (The Cross) pilot · style: inked motion-comic
Rev: v3 — LOCKED 2026-06-30 (red-team + 3 panel passes; final 4/4 working reviewers PASS,
0 doctrinal/KJV/citation errors; cross-checked Luke 23:34 + Rom 5:8 + Heb 7:25 + John 10:18)

THREAD: In the same breath the soldiers gamble for his clothes, Jesus is praying for them —
mercy pleaded before anyone understands their guilt; and the Lord who prayed for his
executioners still lives to make intercession for sinners.

<!-- HOOK -->
[narrator] Nails through his hands. Soldiers at his feet, gambling for his clothes. And from the cross comes a word no one expected — not a curse.

<!-- POINT -->
[narrator] It is a prayer. As they divide his clothing at the foot of the cross, Jesus prays for the very people putting him to death.

[jesus] Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do.

<!-- PROOF -->
[narrator] Luke records the moment exactly.

[scripture] Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots.

[narrator] He asks the Father to forgive them in the same breath they divide his clothing. "They know not what they do" does not excuse the sin; it intercedes for the sinner.

<!-- CONVICTION -->
[narrator] And the sin that put him there was not only theirs. It was ours — and he gave himself willingly for it. The same Lord who prayed for his executioners still lives to make intercession for sinners.

<!-- LANDING -->
[narrator] This is the gospel: while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us — pleading mercy before we ever knew we needed it. That same mercy is held out to you now. Come to the cross, and receive it by faith in him.
