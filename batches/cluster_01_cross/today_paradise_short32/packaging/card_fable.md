## 4. First-3-seconds hook label

**Keep: `PARADISE FOR A CRIMINAL?`** (4 words)

Why keep it:
- It states the stake in two plain words every viewer knows: *paradise* and *criminal*.
- It is the narrator's first sentence, so eye and ear say the same thing in the first 2 seconds. That helps a viewer whose first language is not English.
- It holds the payoff. The payoff is *today* and *with Him* and *he asked for less than he got* — none of that is on the card.

I tried improving it and every version was worse: adding "WHY?" repeats what the narrator says one second later; naming "remember me" spends the turn. Keep.
