One goat died. One goat was sent away alive. Why two? I am Aaron, the first high priest, and once a year I handled both. The law gave me two goats, and together they were one sin offering for the people. The first I killed, and I carried its blood inside the veil, the curtain that shut people out of God's presence. The second I did not kill. I laid both hands on its head, and confessed over it all the sins of the people. Then it was led into the wilderness, to a land where no one lived. Two goats pictured one rescue. One showed sin paid for by death. One showed sin carried far away. Long after me, Jesus did what my goats only pictured. Isaiah wrote of Him: "the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all". He died for our sins, and He bore them away. When He died, "the veil of the temple was rent in twain from the top to the bottom". The curtain is open. For all who trust Him, sin has gone where that goat went, to a land where no one lives. Walk in.
