TITLE
One Goat Died. One Was Sent Away. Why Two?
DESCRIPTION
Leviticus 16:21-22: on the Day of Atonement, Aaron the high priest handled two goats that together made one sin offering. One was killed and its blood carried behind the curtain. The other had the people's sins confessed over it and was sent away alive. Why two? Christians see both halves of one rescue, and Jesus did both.

"And Aaron shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat, and confess over him all the iniquities of the children of Israel, and all their transgressions in all their sins, putting them upon the head of the goat, and shall send him away by the hand of a fit man into the wilderness: And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited: and he shall let go the goat in the wilderness." (Leviticus 16:21-22, KJV)

When Jesus died, the temple curtain tore from the top to the bottom. Through Jesus, the curtain is open. Walk through, to God.
