## TITLE
Genesis 3:15 Names Two Wounds: a Heel and a Head

## DESCRIPTION
Genesis 3:15. One wound is to the heel. The other is to the head.

"And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel." (Genesis 3:15, KJV)

The promise names two wounds in one breath. The head is the vital place. The heel is where the woman's Seed is hurt. Christians read this as the cross: Jesus was wounded, and the serpent's power was broken.

Come to Jesus, who carried the wound for you.
