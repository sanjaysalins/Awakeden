# Independent review — claude (OK, 117s)

**Findings**

1. **The red-team's REVISE was ignored, and the header hides it.** The kit says "Gates: ALL PASS ✅", but the in-engine auditor returned `VERDICT: REVISE` on the chosen title. "God Hung Up a Snake. It Was the Cure." is still chosen on every platform: YouTube Short, YouTube Long, and the Facebook title "God hung up a snake. It was the cure." UK-G3 only checks tokens, so it can't catch this. Numbers 21:8-9 says the cure was *looking*, not the snake. The title implies the object itself healed, which is the idolatry that 2 Kings 18:4 condemns. The body does say "look and live", but the title is the click promise and it overclaims. The auditor's own fix, "Looking at It Saved Them", was not applied. Title option 2, "The Cure Looked Like the Thing That Was Killing Them", is more honest and a better hook.

2. **"God hung up a snake" is inaccurate.** Per v.8-9, God commanded it and *Moses* made it and set it on the pole. The YouTube description says "In Numbers 21, God hung up a snake", and the title and TikTok caption repeat the same wording. This is a small history slip, but it sits in the first line.

3. **The Christ link has no Scripture behind it.** "Christ took your curse" and "He took the curse itself" are asserted with no verse. The typology rests on John 3:14 (and Gal 3:13), and neither is quoted or cited anywhere. The only verse is Numbers 21:9. Add "As Moses lifted up the serpent… even so must the Son of man be lifted up" (John 3:14, exact KJV) so the claim is grounded and not just stated.

4. **Two unsupported or unclear lines in the YouTube description.**
   - "God never dressed it up as something else" has no basis in the text.
   - "He did not become a general sacrifice" is confusing, especially for non-native English speakers, and doesn't explain itself. This breaks the global-audience, simple-words rule.

5. **The Long-form entry is a copy of the Short.** The video is a short (`Format: short`), yet the Long-form section has an identical title, description and tags, with the tag "bible shorts" on a 16:9 listing. That tag is wrong there and the section has no distinct long-form framing. Drop the section, or write it properly.

6. **Weak SEO in the title and first line.** The title has no searchable anchor (no "Numbers 21", "bronze serpent" or "Moses"), and the YouTube description opens with "You would expect a cure to look hopeful", which is soft. The TikTok and Instagram captions open much better, with "God hung up a snake." The YouTube Short hashtags also lack `#Shorts`.

7. **Brand and platform details.**
   - The Facebook caption carries a "Follow: YouTube: @awakeden" footer, which is odd on Facebook.
   - The CTA is "Christ took your curse. Look and live." It points at Christ but never invites a response to Him. This is acceptable but thin compared with the series standard.
   - The KJV quote is verbatim and correct.

**What passes:** The KJV quote matches the text exactly. Hashtag counts are sane (3 to 8). There is no fear or works framing.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Replace the chosen title with an honest one ("Looking at It Saved Them", or title option 2), and update the matching Facebook title. Also fix the header so it no longer says "ALL PASS" while the red-team says REVISE.
2. Correct "God hung up a snake" (Moses made it at God's command), and add John 3:14 in exact KJV to ground "Christ took your curse".
3. Delete or properly rewrite the Long-form section. Remove the unclear lines "God never dressed it up as something else" and "general sacrifice", add `#Shorts`, and put a searchable anchor (Numbers 21 or bronze serpent) in the title or first line.
