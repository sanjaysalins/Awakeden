## TITLE
God Hung Up a Snake. Looking at It Saved Them.

## DESCRIPTION
Numbers 21:9. You would expect a cure to look hopeful. God told Moses to put a snake on a pole, and everyone who looked at it lived.

"And Moses made a serpent of brass, and put it upon a pole, and it came to pass, that if a serpent had bitten any man, when he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived." (Numbers 21:9, KJV)

It was not a comforting symbol. It was the shape of what was killing them, lifted up where the whole camp could see it. Centuries later Jesus pointed back to it: "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:" (John 3:14, KJV)

Christ was lifted up for you. Look to Him, and live.
