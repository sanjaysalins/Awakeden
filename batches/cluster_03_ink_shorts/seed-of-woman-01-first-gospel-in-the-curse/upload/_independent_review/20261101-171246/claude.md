# Independent review — claude (OK, 115s)

**Findings (adversarial review of the upload kit)**

**1. Factual overclaim in the copy (honesty).**
- Genesis 3:9-13 has God questioning Adam and Eve *before* He speaks to the serpent in 3:14-15.
- The red-team flagged this for title #3 only. The same false claim is still in the live copy:
  - YouTube description (short and long): "But God turns to the serpent first".
  - TikTok first line: "He spoke to the snake first".
  - Facebook and Instagram: "Come meet the Voice that spoke first" and "Grace was spoken first". This is only true if it means first in the *sentencing*.
- The chosen title is defensible because it says "before He gave the sentence". The body copy drops that qualifier.
- Title option #3 is false and still sits in the kit as a selectable option.
- "Waiting to hear what God will do to them" skips the interrogation of 3:9-13.
- "Rescue was spoken before ruin" is loose. The fall had already happened, and the ruin (the ground cursed, death) comes in Adam's sentence.

**2. Gate UK-G7 is failing, and the kit is marked RED-TEAMED.**
- "Genesis" is not in the first 157 characters of the YouTube description. The description opens with the Adam and Eve framing.
- The red-team verdict is REVISE and it was not applied.
- The header says "Gates: FAILURES", yet the kit tells the user the fields are "pre-checked, red-teamed and panel-reviewed". No panel evidence is shown.
- UK-G3 "no clickbait/overclaim" shows a pass while the false title #3 is in the kit. The gate is not catching real overclaims.

**3. Wrong format.**
- "YouTube (Long-form 16:9)" is a verbatim copy of the Short entry: same 47-second vertical file, same title, same description and tags.
- This is a wrong or duplicate entry and should be removed.
- It fails G7 for the same reason as the short.
- The hashtags differ between the two (5 vs 3) for no stated reason.

**4. Templated and weak copy.**
- "Come meet the Voice that spoke first" is vague. "The Voice" is unexplained for a newcomer or an ESL viewer. TikTok repeats "first" three times in two sentences.
- "Gave the sentence" is ambiguous for ESL readers (prison sentence vs grammar).
- "Grace was spoken before your sentence" is muddy. It implies the viewer is under sentence, which edges toward a gain/loss frame, and "your" is not explained.
- The brand line says "one panel at a time", but this is a living-sketchbook piece made of spreads.
- The "Follow: YouTube: @awakeden" footer is redundant on YouTube itself.

**5. Doctrine and KJV.**
- Gen 3:15 is verbatim KJV. The punctuation and words match.
- "The Seed who crushed the serpent's head" paraphrases KJV "bruise". That is acceptable, but no NT anchor is cited (for example Gal 3:16, Rom 16:20). Naming Jesus as the Seed is asserted, not shown.
- There is no fear or works framing, and the Jesus CTA is present.

**6. SEO and tags.**
- The title has no "Genesis 3:15", so it misses the most searchable term.
- "protoevangelium" is jargon with little search volume.
- The other tags are fine, and the tag count is sane.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove the false claim "God turns to the serpent first" / "He spoke to the snake first" from the YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram copy. Say "before He speaks their sentence" everywhere. Delete title option #3.
2. Front-load "Genesis 3:15" in the first 157 characters of the YouTube description to clear UK-G7. Delete the duplicate "Long-form 16:9" block, which is identical to the Short. Re-run the gates before marking it RED-TEAMED.
3. Rewrite the vague, repeated CTA ("the Voice that spoke first", "Grace was spoken before your sentence"). Use plain words, add one NT anchor naming Jesus as the Seed, and put "Genesis 3:15" in the title or the TikTok first line.
