# Independent review — grok (OK, 137s)

The kit is not safe to paste as-is. The chosen title is narrowly fair, and the Genesis 3:15 quote matches the KJV, but the live captions overclaim the order of Genesis 3, and a title the red-team already called false is still in the kit.

**Order claims that are not true**

Genesis 3:9–13 is God speaking with Adam and Eve before the serpent’s curse. The promise in Genesis 3:15 comes after that talk, and before the sentences in Genesis 3:16–19. The fall itself is already done by Genesis 3:6–8.

- Title option 3, **“Before God Spoke to Adam, He Spoke to the Snake”**, is false. The red-team said to drop it. It is still listed under titles to copy.
- YouTube Short and YouTube Long both open: **“But God turns to the serpent first”**. “First” reads as first words in the garden. That is the same false order.
- Those same two descriptions say **“Rescue was spoken before ruin.”** Ruin (they ate, hid, were afraid) comes before Genesis 3:15. The careful claim is “before their sentence.” This line breaks that.
- TikTok: **“He spoke to the snake first and hid a promise”**. Same false “first,” plus “hid.”
- Every platform ends with **“Come meet the Voice that spoke first.”** God speaks to Adam in Genesis 3:9 before He speaks the promise. “Spoke first” is the false hook again, this time in the CTA.

**“Hidden” is not what the text does**

God says the promise out loud. He does not hide it.

- YouTube: **“buries a promise for them inside that curse.”**
- TikTok and the alt title **“The First Gospel Was Hidden Inside a Curse”**: **“hid a promise.”**
- Instagram: **“He hid a promise inside the curse.”**

“In the curse” is fair. “Hid” and “buries” are not.

**Verse and doctrine**

- The quoted line **“And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.”** is exact KJV Genesis 3:15. No altered word in the quote.
- **“Jesus, the Seed who crushed the serpent’s head”** is commentary, and it upgrades KJV **“bruise”** to **“crushed”** as a finished fact. Keep “bruise,” or say the promise points to Jesus.
- **“Grace was spoken before your sentence”** is a soft push toward the viewer’s guilt. The invite **“Come meet…”** is Christ-pointing and is not a works pitch. It can stay if “spoke first” is fixed.

**Platform and SEO**

- The kit’s own gate is already red: **UK-G7**. Both YouTube descriptions put the story first. **“Genesis”** is not in the first 157 characters, so the reference sits under the “more” fold. Facebook does this right by opening **“Genesis 3:15.”** TikTok and Instagram also name Genesis 3:15 inside the first screen.
- YouTube Long uses the Short description unchanged, and its tags still include **“bible shorts”**. That tag does not match a 16:9 long upload.
- Other tags (**genesis 3 15, protoevangelium, seed of the woman, curse of the serpent**) match the film. Hashtag counts are sane (YouTube Long 3, Short/TikTok 5, Instagram 8). Instagram’s **#Faith** and **#Grace** are generic, not a stuffing dump.
- The chosen title **“God Promised Rescue Before He Gave the Sentence”** is specific enough to earn a click if the caption stays inside that claim. Alt 4, **“Genesis 3: Grace Came First,”** is vague and repeats the shaky “first.”
- Brand footer on YouTube and Facebook is intact: Awakeden line, `https://youtube.com/@awakeden`, `https://awakeden.com`, and the KJV public-domain line. TikTok and Instagram have the Jesus CTA and no broken links. They also quote no verse, so the missing KJV footer there is fine.
- The header says the blocks are pre-checked and ready to copy, while **Gates: FAILURES** and the red-team says **REVISE**. Those cannot both be true.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Delete title 3, and rewrite every live line that says the serpent was spoken to “first,” the promise was “hid/buried,” or rescue came “before ruin” — say only that the promise in Genesis 3:15 comes before Adam and Eve’s sentence.
2. Put “Genesis 3:15” in the first line of both YouTube descriptions so the reference sits above the fold.
3. Remove “bible shorts” from the YouTube Long tags, and change “crushed” back to the KJV word “bruise” (or “the Seed the promise points to: Jesus”).
