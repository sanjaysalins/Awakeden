### Hooky but honest / bait-and-switch

- **Alt title 2:** *“The First Gospel Was **Hidden** Inside a Curse”* — The KJV promise in 3:15 is **stated outright**, not hidden. “Hidden” is sensational / thumbnail language (`publish_meta.json` thumb: **“HIDDEN IN A CURSE”**), not text-faithful. Risk if someone swaps off the chosen title.
- **Alt title 4:** *“Genesis 3: Grace Came First”* — **“Grace”** is theological gloss, not Genesis vocabulary; fine as devotional framing, weak as **history/honest hook** (sounds like a claim the chapter “says grace” in those words).

Narration uses the same “crushed” line — consistent with video, not with KJV diction.

Chosen title **“God Promised Rescue Before He Gave the Sentence”** is **interpretive** (protoevangelium → rescue) but aligned with the piece; not the worst offender.
