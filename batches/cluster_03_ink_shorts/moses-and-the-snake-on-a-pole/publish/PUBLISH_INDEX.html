TITLE
God Answered Snakebite With a Snake on a Pole
DESCRIPTION
Numbers 21:8-9: snakes were killing Israel, and God did not take the snakes away. He told Moses to make a serpent of brass and set it on a pole, and whoever looked at it lived. Jesus pointed back to this scene in John 3:14, and said the Son of man must be lifted up the same way.

"And the LORD said unto Moses, Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live. And Moses made a serpent of brass, and put it upon a pole, and it came to pass, that if a serpent had bitten any man, when he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived." (Numbers 21:8-9, KJV)

The bitten looked at a snake, and lived. Believe in Jesus, lifted up on the cross, and live.
