Snakes were killing my people. God's cure? A snake. I am Moses. My people spoke against God. He sent snakes. Many died. They cried, "We have sinned". I prayed. God did not take the snakes away. He said: make a snake, set it on a pole. I made one of brass, and lifted it up. Anyone bitten who looked at it, lived. The cure looked like the thing that was killing them. I lived long before Jesus, God's Son. But He said: "as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up". Lifted up: He meant Himself, on a cross. A later writer, Paul, said that on the cross God "made him to be sin for us". The bitten looked at a snake, and lived. Believe in Jesus, lifted up on the cross, and live.
