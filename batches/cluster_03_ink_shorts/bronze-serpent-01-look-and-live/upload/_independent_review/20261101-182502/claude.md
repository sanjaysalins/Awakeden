# Independent review — claude (OK, 57s)

**Findings**

**KJV and doctrine**
- Numbers 21:8 is verbatim KJV, including the omitted "And the LORD said unto Moses," lead-in. No altered words.
- The paraphrase "put a bronze one on a pole" is fine, since v9 says "brass". But the kit says "bronze" everywhere, including the "bronze serpent" tag, while claiming "KJV-strict". That is acceptable for tags and a weakness only if the narration quotes v9.
- "Just a look at the very thing that was killing them" (YouTube, both blocks) is slightly inaccurate. A bronze image was lifted up, not the biting snakes. The title "a Look at a Snake" has the same issue. It is a hook, not a lie, but "a bronze snake" would be truer.
- "Not medicine… Just a look" skips the repentance and confession in Num 21:7 that triggers the cure. It is not a works framing. But there is no faith or belief language either. John 3:14-15 ("whosoever believeth") is never quoted. "Look and live" never says that looking means trusting Jesus.
- The CTA "Look at what was lifted up for you, and live" is the only gospel landing, and it works only if the viewer knows John 3:14. Nothing in the text says "lifted up" means the cross. A newcomer or non-native speaker gets no cross, no Christ named as Savior, and no "believe". It is weak as a CTA-to-Jesus, because Jesus appears only as a pointer ("pointed back to it").

**Platform fit**
- **YouTube Long-form 16:9** is a byte-identical copy of the Short. The tags still include "bible shorts", and the description has no long-form framing or chapters. The kit says Format: short, so this block is either wrong or filler. It should be dropped, or truly written for long-form.
- **TikTok and Instagram** both open with "Numbers 21:8-9." That is a citation, not a hook. The real hook comes in sentence two. TikTok and IG show the first line before truncation, so the hook should come first.
- **TikTok** has no handle or link. **Instagram** has no "link in bio" and no handle. IG has 8 hashtags, which is fine but includes generic #Faith and #BibleStudy.
- **Facebook** repeats the citation: "Numbers 21:8. "…" (Numbers 21:8, KJV)".

**SEO**
- The title has no "Bible", "bronze serpent", or "Moses". It depends entirely on curiosity, with weak search discoverability. The alt title "God Chose a Snake on a Pole as the Cure" is more searchable but leaves out the hook.
- The tags are relevant and not stuffed. They lack "nehushtan" and "John 3:14 explained".
- "Snakebite" and "snake" are modern wording. The tags say "serpent", which matches the KJV, so the two are consistent.

**Brand and gates**
- UK-G7 claims "plain ASCII", but the "✝" glyph appears in the YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram copy. Either the gate is not checking, or the claim is false.
- The "panel-reviewed" line in the header is unsupported. Status is only RED-TEAMED, and the red-team text is three short bullets.
- The footer is clean, and the link and handle are not mangled.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Make the gospel landing explicit. Name the cross and belief, for example "Jesus said He must be lifted up (John 3:14-15): whoever believes in Him will live", and quote or paraphrase it in plain words. Do this in every platform's CTA.
2. Remove or properly rewrite the duplicate YouTube Long-form block, and drop "bible shorts" from it. Lead the TikTok and Instagram captions with the hook ("The cure for snakebite was a look at a snake") rather than "Numbers 21:8-9."
3. Fix accuracy and consistency. Say a "bronze snake" was lifted up, not "the very thing that was killing them". Remove "✝" or fix the ASCII gate. Add "Bible" or "bronze serpent" to the title or its first line for search.
