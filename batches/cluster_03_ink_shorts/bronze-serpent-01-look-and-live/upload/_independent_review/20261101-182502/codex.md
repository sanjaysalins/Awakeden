# Independent review — codex (OK, 95s)

The kit needs revision. Its “ALL PASS” claim overlooks a platform limit, ambiguous gospel language, and unsupported verification claims.

- **Instagram — eight hashtags:** “#Bible #Jesus #BronzeSerpent #Numbers21 #KJV #LookAndLive #BibleStudy #Faith” exceeds Instagram’s announced five-hashtag limit. “UK-G5 … hashtag counts … OK” is therefore incorrect. [Platform announcement coverage](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)

- **All platforms — unclear salvation CTA:** “Look at what was lifted up for you, and live” leaves Jesus as an unnamed “what” and never explains that looking means believing in Him. Alongside TikTok’s “Not medicine, just a look,” this risks making the application sound like a visual act that produces life. John 3:14–15 explicitly connects Christ’s lifting up with believing in Him and eternal life. This is a clarity defect, not evidence of explicit works doctrine. [John 3, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+3&version=KJV)

- **YouTube Short/Long descriptions; title option 3 — misleading identity:** “the very thing that was killing them” and “Look at the Thing That Was Killing Them” blur the distinction between the living serpents and the metal representation. The object on the pole was not killing anyone. “The cure was shaped like the curse” conveys resemblance; “the very thing” overstates it. [Numbers 21:6–9, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Numbers+21&version=KJV)

- **YouTube Long-form — mismatched packaging:** The kit declares “Format: short,” supplies one video, then provides an identical “Long-form 16:9” description with the tag “bible shorts.” No separate horizontal version is identified. The long-form block needs a specified matching asset and appropriate tags. YouTube classification depends on the uploaded video’s dimensions and duration, not this heading. [YouTube guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en-GB)

- **TikTok/Instagram — weak opening placement:** Both captions begin “Numbers 21:8-9.” That puts a reference before the curiosity hook. The snakebite sentence supplies the actual reason to keep reading; the reference delays it.

- **Kit-wide — overstated verification:** “plain ASCII” contradicts the copied “✝” footer. “No title collisions with siblings” has no supporting comparison in the supplied artifact. The video’s contents are also not supplied here, so metadata-to-video fidelity cannot be certified from this kit alone.

**KJV finding:** The quoted Numbers 21:8 passage is an exact excerpt; no altered word appears. It omits the opening attribution, so “anchor verse quoted verbatim” should distinguish an excerpt from the complete verse. “Bronze” occurs in paraphrase, not inside the quotation. The supplied links are visibly intact; the chosen title is specific rather than templated.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Make the CTA explicitly name Jesus and connect looking with believing in Him.
2. Reduce Instagram to five hashtags and correct the false “ALL PASS” verification claims.
3. Remove “the very thing” overstatement and align the long-form block with an identified matching video.
