## TITLE
The Cure for Snakebite Was a Look at a Brass Serpent

## DESCRIPTION
Numbers 21:8-9. You would think the cure for a snakebite would be anything but a snake. God told Moses to make a serpent of brass and put it on a pole.

"Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live." (Numbers 21:8, KJV)

Not medicine. Not an antidote for the wound. Just a look at a brass serpent shaped like the ones that were killing them, lifted up where everyone could see it.

Jesus said the Son of man must be lifted up the same way, so that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have eternal life (John 3:14-15). Look at what was lifted up for you, and live.
