TITLE
Jesus Slept Through a Storm. Then He Asked One Question.
DESCRIPTION
Matthew 8:23-27. A great tempest arose on the sea, "insomuch that the ship was covered with the waves: but he was asleep." (Matthew 8:24, KJV)

His disciples woke Him: "Lord, save us: we perish." He asked them one question: "Why are ye fearful, O ye of little faith?" (Matthew 8:26, KJV) Then He arose, and rebuked the winds and the sea, and there was a great calm. The men marvelled: "What manner of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him!" (Matthew 8:27, KJV)

The One who could sleep through the storm is the One who silenced it with a word. When the water reaches you, come to Him.
