# Independent review — codex (OK, 77s)

The “ALL PASS” claim is not supported by the copy. The main problems are overstated narration, an unanswered title promise, and conflicting format instructions.

1. **YouTube Short, YouTube Long-form and Facebook titles — “Why Was Jesus Asleep in the Storm?”** This promises an explanation for His sleeping. The descriptions establish His authority over the storm but never answer why He slept. Matthew does not explicitly supply that explanation either. This is a promise/payoff mismatch in the supplied metadata; whether the video resolves it remains unverified.

2. **YouTube descriptions — “certain He does not care”; TikTok/Instagram — “sure He did not care.”** These assert the disciples’ inner certainty. Matthew records their plea for rescue; Mark records a question about His care. Neither establishes the certainty claimed here. YouTube’s “in the stern” also imports Mark’s detail without identifying the parallel account. Harmonising accounts is defensible; presenting inference as Matthew’s explicit narration is not. [Matthew 8:23–27; Mark 4:37–40](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+8%3A23-27%3BMark+4%3A37-40&version=KJV)

3. **Title option 2 and TikTok/Instagram opening — “The storm was killing them.”** This turns mortal danger into an asserted process of dying. The account supports a terrifying storm and their fear of perishing, not documented deaths or injuries. **YouTube — “The men are screaming for their lives”** adds an unrecorded delivery detail. These intensify the account beyond what the cited text establishes. [Matthew 8:23–27](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+8%3A23-27&version=KJV)

4. **YouTube descriptions — “Then He rose, and rebuked…”** This resembles a Scripture quotation but changes KJV **“arose”** to **“rose.”** It sits outside quotation marks, so it is technically narration rather than an altered explicit quotation. Nevertheless, the “KJV-strict” presentation blurs that distinction. The explicitly quoted portions of Matthew 8:26–27 contain no altered words. [Matthew 8:26–27](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+8%3A26-27&version=KJV)

5. **YouTube Long-form — “Long-form 16:9” and tag “bible shorts.”** The kit identifies the asset as **“Format: short”**, supplies one video path, and then duplicates its metadata into a long-form section. It establishes no separate long-form deliverable. The Shorts keyword is also mismatched to that section’s claimed format.

6. **TikTok/Instagram first line — “Matthew 8.”** The opening spends its first words on a reference before reaching the hook. The subsequent hook is concrete but overstated. **Title option 3 — “What the Sleeping Christ Shows Us”** is the opposite problem: vague, interchangeable devotional wording with little specific curiosity.

7. **Verification — “plain ASCII” and “ALL PASS ✅.”** The upload copy itself contains **“✝”**, contradicting the ASCII claim. The embedded auditor also acknowledges an attribution problem while issuing PASS. These badges overstate the reliability of the checks.

CTA-to-Jesus is present across platforms, and the supplied footer URLs are not visibly mangled. No clear works, gain/loss, or fear-based conversion demand appears. Those points do not resolve the accuracy problems above.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove unsupported certainty, screaming, and “killing them” claims; identify any use of Mark’s account.
2. Make the chosen title’s question match the explanation actually delivered.
3. Resolve the short/long-form mismatch and correct the overstated verification claims.
