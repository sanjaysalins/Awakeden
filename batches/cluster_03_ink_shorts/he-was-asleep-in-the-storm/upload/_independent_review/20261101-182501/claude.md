# Independent review — claude (OK, 58s)

**Review of the "He Was Asleep in the Storm" upload kit**

**1. Added details that Matthew 8:23-27 doesn't contain (honesty)**
- YouTube Short and Long-form, TikTok and Instagram all say the disciples are "certain He does not care" or "sure He did not care". That comes from Mark 4:38 ("carest thou not?"). Matthew 8:25 only records "Lord, save us: we perish."
- The kit anchors every post to "Matthew 8:23-27" and the red-team flagged this as "unsourced for Matthew". It was accepted anyway.
- YouTube: "The men are screaming for their lives" is not in the text. Matthew says the boat was "covered with the waves".
- YouTube: "in the stern" is also Mark 4:38, not Matthew.
- YouTube: "He opens His eyes" is invented. It sits right next to the quoted verse, so a viewer will read it as scripture.
- TikTok and Instagram: "The storm was killing them" is dramatization. Matthew says "we perish", not that the storm was killing them. It is the lead hook on two platforms, and it was also title option 2, so the kit's own "honest hook" label doesn't apply to it.
- All platforms: "the Christ who slept unafraid". Scripture records sleep, not the absence of fear. This is an inference presented as fact, and it is the CTA line on every platform.

**2. KJV check**
- The two quoted verses are exact: 8:26 "Why are ye fearful, O ye of little faith?" and 8:27 "What manner of man is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him!"
- Facebook: "They woke Him, saying, Lord, save us: we perish." is KJV 8:25 but sits unquoted. Quote it or cite it, since it is a verse excerpt.
- YouTube: "Then He rose, and rebuked the winds and the sea, and there was a great calm" paraphrases 8:26 without quote marks, which is fine. It changes "arose" to "rose" and drops the semicolon.

**3. Gate contradictions**
- UK-G7 claims "plain ASCII", but "✝" appears in every description. Either the gate is not checking or the glyph is not ASCII. That is a false ✅.
- UK-G2 and UK-G3 are marked clean even though the Mark 4:38 import (point 1) passed through them.

**4. Platform fit**
- The video is `Format: short`, but a full "YouTube (Long-form 16:9)" block is emitted with the same title and description. The long-form block's tags include "bible shorts", which is wrong. Either drop this block or write a real long-form one.
- First lines are wasted. YouTube, TikTok and Instagram all open with "Matthew 8:23-27." or "Matthew 8." The hook is sentence two. On TikTok and Instagram the first line is the whole feed preview. Lead with the stake ("Jesus was asleep while the storm was drowning the boat... why?").
- The footer on YouTube says "Follow: YouTube: @awakeden" with a YouTube link on YouTube itself. It is redundant, and the same footer shows on Facebook. YouTube says "Subscribe" while the footer says "Follow".
- "one panel at a time" may not match what this video actually is, a living-sketchbook spread film. Check it against the film.
- Instagram uses #Faith #Peace #BibleStudy, which are generic and don't bring in search traffic.
- Hashtag counts differ without reason: YouTube Short has 5, Long-form 3.

**5. Title and SEO**
- The chosen title, "Why Was Jesus Asleep in the Storm? (Matthew 8:23-27)", is generic and heavily competed. It is also the only title whose cost is paid up front: the scripture range takes about 20 characters that could carry the hook.
- Title option 4, "Why Are Ye Fearful? The Storm Question", is the strongest, because it is the verse and ties to the series name "Questions Jesus Asked". Option 2 is punchy but fails the honesty test (point 1).
- The description's turn is garbled: "It is easy to say, easy for Him, He is God. Exactly." It reads as a typo, not a rhetorical turn.
- Tags are real and relevant. There is no tag-stuffing, apart from "bible shorts" on the long-form block.

**6. Doctrine and brand**
- There is no fear, gain/loss or works framing, and the conviction is grace-anchored. Good.
- The landing is the stock "Come to Him / come to the Christ" shape that `narration_gate.py` flags as a stale closer. It does not earn its landing from the piece's own KJV, which ends on "obey him". A concrete word from verse 27 or "Lord, save us" would be fresher and earns more than "come to".
- The CTA to Jesus is present, but there is no gospel or cross connection.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove Mark-only and invented details from every platform ("certain He does not care", "screaming for their lives", "in the stern", "He opens His eyes", "The storm was killing them", "slept unafraid"). Rewrite them using only what Matthew 8:23-27 says, or add the Mark 4:38 citation if you want the line.
2. Rework the title and first lines. Replace the templated title with something like "Why Are Ye Fearful? The Storm Question". Open YouTube, TikTok and Instagram with the stake, not "Matthew 8:23-27.". Fix the garbled "easy for Him, He is God" line.
3. Fix the kit mechanics. Delete or properly rewrite the long-form block, since the video is a short. Drop "bible shorts" from long-form. Remove the redundant YouTube footer. Replace "✝" so UK-G7's ASCII claim is true. Replace the stock "come to the Christ" landing with one built from the piece's own KJV.
