# Independent review — claude (OK, 117s)

**Review of the "Even So Must the Son of Man Be Lifted Up" upload kit: REVISE**

**1. The kit says "ALL PASS" but the red-team said REVISE, and its fix was never applied.**
- The in-engine red-team flagged: "'John uses the same word of lifting again at the cross' is an unsourced claim. Fix: add John 12:32-33."
- None of the YouTube, Facebook, TikTok or Instagram copy cites John 12:32-33. The header still reads "Gates: ALL PASS ✅".
- The kit's own audit trail is therefore inconsistent. The sourcing fix is also still outstanding.

**2. The "one word" claim is imprecise and may overclaim.**
- Title: "One Word Joins Jesus' Shame and His Glory".
- YouTube: "the lifting that shames Him is what John calls His glory".
- Facebook: "The lifting that shamed Him is the word John uses for His glory".
- Is "lifted up" the word John uses for glory? John's Greek has *hypsoo* (lift up or exalt) in 3:14, 8:28 and 12:32-34. It has *doxazo* (glorify) in 12:23 and 13:31. The two are different words.
- The honest claim is that *hypsoo* carries both the physical lifting on the cross and the exaltation. "The word John uses for His glory" (Facebook) misstates that. Rewrite it or source it.
- The title makes the same promise, but neither the title nor any description names the word. The curiosity gap never pays off in text. That is mild bait on a platform where the description is the only place to resolve it.

**3. The title is weak for search.**
- "One Word Joins Jesus' Shame and His Glory" has no search anchor: no serpent, no John 3:14, no Nicodemus.
- Alt #3, "Why Did Jesus Compare Himself to a Snake on a Pole?", matches real search behaviour and is honest to the text. It is the stronger Short title.
- Alt #4, "John Says Both", attributes to John what Jesus says in 3:14. Do not use it.

**4. The kit duplicates one YouTube block as both "Short" and "Long-form 16:9".**
- The format is "short", yet a long-form block with the same title, description and tags is emitted.
- The Short has no `#Shorts` hashtag. It could just be a copy-paste leftover.

**5. The UK-G7 claim of "plain ASCII" is false.** The `✝` in every description and caption is non-ASCII. Either the gate is wrong or the character should go. Facebook, TikTok and Instagram also say "Follow Awakeden" with no handle. The Facebook footer lists only a YouTube link.

**6. TikTok and Instagram are identical, and the first line is weak.**
- Both open with "By night, Jesus tells a Pharisee:", which is a setup, not a hook.
- "the lifting that shames Him is His glory" is abstract and hard for non-native English speakers, which breaks the global-audience rule.
- Instagram omits the verse, so it is thinner than the others.
- `#Nicodemus` appears in the tags and hashtags, but the copy never names him.

**7. Small points.**
- "Centuries earlier" understates roughly 1,400 years. "Long ago" or "over a thousand years earlier" is safer.
- "You cannot want Christ's glory without His cross" sits close to law framing. It is acceptable in context, and the grace close ("Believe on Christ… and live") offsets it.

**What passes:**
- The John 3:14-15 quote is exact KJV.
- The CTA points to Jesus and uses grace framing, with no fear or gain-loss language.
- Hashtag counts are sane.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Add the missing John 12:32-33 source to the copy. Fix the "word John uses for His glory" wording to a precise claim: one word, *hypsoo* / "lifted up", names both the cross and the exaltation. Name the word in the description so the title's gap pays off. Then re-run the red-team so the status header is not "ALL PASS" over a REVISE.
2. Retitle the Short with a real search anchor, such as alt #3 or "Lifted Up: Why Jesus Compared the Cross to a Snake on a Pole". Drop the duplicate "Long-form 16:9" block, or make it a genuinely different long-form kit. Add `#Shorts`.
3. Rewrite the TikTok and Instagram first lines as a concrete hook in plain words, for example "Why did Jesus say He'd be lifted up like a snake?". Give each platform its own copy. Remove the non-ASCII `✝` or fix the G7 gate. Add the real handle to the footers.
