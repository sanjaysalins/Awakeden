## TITLE
Why Paul Wrote 'Made of a Woman' About Jesus

## DESCRIPTION
Galatians 4:4. Paul could have called Jesus a son of David or a son of Abraham. He wrote "made of a woman."

"But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law," (Galatians 4:4, KJV)

Many read that as an echo of the promise about the woman's seed in Genesis 3:15. If so, Jesus was not a late fix. He was the plan from the start.

The plan was never yours to write. He has already come for you.
