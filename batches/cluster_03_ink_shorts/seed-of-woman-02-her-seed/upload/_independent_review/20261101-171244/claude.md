# Independent review — claude (OK, 117s)

**Overall:** Doctrine and the Galatians 4:4 quote are sound, but the kit has a failed lint gate, an unexplained interpretive claim, and a long-form block that shouldn't exist. Verdict is REVISE.

**Gate failure (blocking)**
- UK-G7 fails. "Galatians" is not in the first 157 characters of the YouTube description. The kit header says "Gates: FAILURES ❌" while the red-team says PASS, so it can't ship as is. The opening line is about "Plan B", and the verse comes four lines later.

**Hooky but honest**
- Title "Genesis Says No": Genesis never addresses "Plan B". The title attributes a rebuttal to Genesis that is really the video's inference from Gen 3:15. The red-team called this "slightly strong", and I agree. It is mild bait.
- The description says "Genesis already named this seed", but Genesis 3:15 is never cited or quoted in any description or caption. It appears only in the YouTube tags. The central claim has no verse behind it for the viewer.
- "Paul could have written son of David or son of Abraham. He wrote made of a woman, the woman promised in the garden." This states Paul's intent and a Gen 3:15 link as settled fact. Paul does not cite Gen 3:15 there. It is a defensible reading, but it should be framed as one ("echoes", "points back to").
- "kept word for word" is vague and unsupported. It sounds like a claim about verbatim fulfilment.

**KJV**
- The Galatians 4:4 quote matches the KJV. It stops on a trailing comma and leaves out v.5 ("To redeem them that were under the law…"). The clip ends on "made under the law," without the redemption that gives the verse its gospel point.

**Doctrine and CTA**
- "The plan was never yours to write, and He has already arrived for you." The first half is odd and could be read as "your efforts don't count". The second half is a statement, not an invitation to Jesus. The only real call to action is "Subscribe to Awakeden", which is a channel CTA, not a CTA to Jesus. UK-G4 passes, but weakly.
- Facebook, TikTok and Instagram repeat "He has already arrived for you" with no setup. It is vague for a viewer who has never heard the story.

**Platform fit**
- The kit is labelled Format: short, yet it includes a full "YouTube (Long-form 16:9)" block. That block has the same title and body as the short, with a different hashtag set and the "bible shorts" tag. A 60-second short has no long-form upload, so this block should be removed or marked N/A.
- TikTok and Instagram open with "Plan B? Genesis already named this seed." For a global audience, "this seed" is unexplained jargon. The first line should say who or what in plain words, for example "Was Jesus God's backup plan?".
- Hashtag sets differ without a reason. Instagram has 8 (#PlanB, #BibleStudy and #Faith added). YouTube Short has 5, YouTube long has 3, and Facebook has 3. There is no #Shorts tag on the YouTube Short.

**SEO**
- "types and shadows" is an internal series name, not a search term. "bible shorts" is on the long-form block. "genesis 3 15" is a tag, but the video never cites that verse in the metadata, which is a mismatch.

**Brand**
- The footer and links are intact. The "Follow: YouTube: @awakeden" block plus a bare URL is repetitive, but it isn't mangled.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix UK-G7: put "Galatians 4:4" and the hook in the first ~150 characters of the YouTube description. Also cite Gen 3:15 explicitly in the body, since the "Genesis" claim in the title has no verse behind it. Soften "Genesis Says No" or frame the Paul/garden link as an echo, not Paul's stated intent.
2. Remove or mark N/A the duplicate YouTube Long-form block (this is a short). Align the hashtag sets, and use a plain-English first line for TikTok and Instagram instead of "this seed".
3. Replace "the plan was never yours to write… He has already arrived for you" with a clear, grace-anchored invitation to Jesus. Optionally include v.5, "To redeem them that were under the law", so the gospel lands.
