## TITLE
Why Does the God of Peace Bruise Satan? Romans 16:20

## DESCRIPTION
Romans 16:20. Paul ends his letter to the believers in Rome with a promise that echoes Eden:

"And the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen." (Romans 16:20, KJV)

In Genesis 3:15 God promised that the woman's Seed would bruise the serpent. Christians read the cross as where the decisive blow fell, and Paul says that victory will reach you under your own feet. Stand where His grace has set your feet.
