# Independent review — codex (OK, 117s)

**REVISE.** The main problems are doctrinal ambiguity, weak mobile hooks, and unreliable “pre-checked” claims. Video delivery cannot be verified from the metadata alone.

1. **YouTube Short + Long — “It is not finished yet.”** This opens with an unqualified statement that can suggest Christ’s saving work remains unfinished. “Because the blow already fell” does not clearly resolve that ambiguity or even name the cross. Distinguish Christ’s completed victory from the promised defeat of Satan still awaiting fulfilment.

2. **YouTube Short + Long — “The crushing is not handed to a warrior.”** This creates an unsupported opposition between peace and warfare. Romans 16:20 identifies God as the agent; it does not establish that contrast. The line risks implying that Christ’s identity as victor or warrior conflicts with his peace.

3. **YouTube, TikTok + Facebook — “That head was never yours to crush.”** The intended rejection of self-earned victory is understandable, but the absolute wording obscures the verse’s explicit promise concerning believers’ feet. Explain God’s agency and believers’ participation through grace without making them sound mutually exclusive.

4. **Instagram — eight hashtags, ending “#BibleStudy #Faith.”** This exceeds the current five-hashtag limit for posts and Reels. The claim “UK-G5 platform — hashtag counts + link rules OK” is therefore unreliable. [Current Instagram hashtag guidance](https://later.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-using-instagram-hashtags/)

5. **TikTok + Instagram — “A crushing verb given to a peace-title.”** This is commentary about wording before viewers know the subject. “Peace-title” is awkward, abstract language. The actual tension—God’s peace and Satan’s defeat—is stronger than this first line.

6. **YouTube Long-form — “bible shorts”; kit header — “Format: short.”** The long-form block duplicates the Short metadata and retains a format-specific keyword. No separate 16:9 deliverable is identified. Only offer this block for an actual corresponding upload, with accurate format tags.

7. **Alternate titles — “The Garden’s Promise Is Not Old History” / “The Serpent-Crusher Is Called the God of Peace.”** The former is vague and gives little reason to click; the latter sounds like an explicit scriptural naming formula, although “Serpent-Crusher” is an interpretive label. The chosen question is more defensible, but the video must actually answer *why*, rather than merely repeat the apparent paradox.

8. **Kit-wide — “Copy each block straight…” / “pre-checked, red-teamed and panel-reviewed.”** Those assurances conflict with “Gates: FAILURES” and the unresolved lint failure. The 157-character rule is an internal gate, not an established universal YouTube fold boundary. Present validation honestly.

**KJV and brand:** The complete Romans 16:20 quotations in both YouTube descriptions and Facebook match the KJV. TikTok and Instagram use shortened, unquoted excerpts; these should be clearly treated as excerpts rather than complete verse quotations. “Crush” outside quotation marks is not an altered KJV quote. The Jesus-directed CTA and footer strings are present; link functionality and video correspondence remain unverified. [KJV text](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+16%3A20&version=KJV)

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Clarify Christ’s completed victory and God’s promised action under believers’ feet; remove the misleading peace-versus-warrior contrast.
2. Reduce Instagram to five hashtags and correct the long-form deliverable/tag mismatch.
3. Replace the abstract TikTok/Instagram opening and correct the unsupported “pre-checked” assurances.
