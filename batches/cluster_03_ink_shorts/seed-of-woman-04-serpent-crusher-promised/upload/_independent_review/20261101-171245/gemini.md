# Independent review — gemini (OK, 117s)

Here is the independent review of the upload kit.

### Findings

**1. Doctrine: The "Not Finished" Landmine**
- *Cite:* `It is not finished yet.` (YouTube descriptions)
- *Problem:* While the red-team auditor gave this a pass, this phrasing is a massive doctrinal landmine for an evangelical audience. It directly contradicts Christ's final words on the cross ("It is finished" - John 19:30). Even if the intended meaning is eschatological (that the *final* crushing under the believers' feet is future), using this exact phrasing undermines the complete, finished work of the atonement. It needs to be rewritten (e.g., "The final blow is imminent" or "The final victory approaches").

**2. Platform Fit / Ignored Lint Failure**
- *Cite:* `❌ **UK-G7 lint** — youtube_short: anchor ref 'romans' not in the first 157 chars...`
- *Problem:* The kit explicitly flagged that the UK-G7 lint failed, but no adjustment was made. In both YouTube descriptions, "Romans 16:20" appears around character 276, well past the "Show more" fold. 
- *Fix:* The verse reference must be moved up into the first 157 characters so mobile users see it before clicking to expand.

**3. SEO Honesty / Tag Stuffing**
- *Cite:* `bible shorts` (YouTube Long-form 16:9 TAGS)
- *Problem:* The tags for the Long-form video are a blind copy-paste of the Shorts tags. Tagging a 16:9 long-form video with "bible shorts" is inaccurate and violates SEO honesty. 

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Rewrite "It is not finished yet" to avoid contradicting the finished work of the cross.
2. Move "Romans 16:20" into the first 157 characters of the YouTube descriptions to resolve the UK-G7 lint failure.
3. Remove the "bible shorts" tag from the YouTube Long-form 16:9 metadata.
