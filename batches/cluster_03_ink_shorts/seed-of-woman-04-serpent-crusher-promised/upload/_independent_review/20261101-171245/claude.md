# Independent review — claude (OK, 117s)

**Findings**

**Honesty / doctrine**

1. **Description opener, YouTube + Long-form: "It is not finished yet."** This is the biggest risk. The line comes straight after "A curse in a garden. A serpent's head crushed…", and "It is finished" (John 19:30) is the cross cry. A viewer who knows that will hear "not finished" as a contradiction of the cross. The red-team flagged it as "Acceptable", but it is a hook that can mislead. Later the text says "the blow already fell," so the metadata contradicts itself: the head is "crushed" in line 1, and "not finished" in line 1. Cut it or reword it, for example "The final blow is still ahead for Satan, and the cross already won."

2. **"The crushing is not handed to a warrior. Scripture gives a crushing verb to a peace-title."** This is clever but obscure, and it breaks the global-audience rule. "Peace-title" and "crushing verb" are jargon. TikTok and Instagram use it as the *first line*, so a newcomer won't know what it means.

3. **"Because the blow already fell, the fight is not in doubt."** The doctrine is sound, but it needs a Christ anchor. The kit never says that Jesus is the one who struck the blow, or what the blow was (the cross). Nothing in the copy says Genesis 3:15 → the cross → Romans 16:20. The Jesus mention is only in the verse's closing benediction. For a CTA-to-Jesus brand, the Christ link is implied, not stated. The only mentions are "Find Jesus in the whole Bible" (a generic footer) and the quote.

4. **"That head was never yours to crush. Stand where His grace has set your feet."** "His" has no clear antecedent in TikTok and Instagram, where "the God of peace" is the nearest noun. It also reads close to a quiet "you are the crusher" mismatch with "under *your* feet". Make it clear that Christ crushes and believers share in the victory.

**KJV**

5. The Romans 16:20 quote is exact KJV: "And the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen." No problem. But the title and TikTok/Instagram text say "crush Satan" while the KJV says "bruise". A title that sets "Crush" beside a verse reference invites a "that's not what the verse says" comment. The kit never explains that it is bridging Genesis 3:15's "bruise" and "crush." Consider "bruise" in the title, or add a Gen 3:15 link.

**Platform fit**

6. **UK-G7 lint FAILS and the kit still says "RED-TEAMED".** "Romans" is not in the first 157 chars of the YouTube description. The header says **Gates: FAILURES ❌**, so this is not copy-ready. The fix is easy: lead with "Romans 16:20:" before the fold.
7. **Long-form block is a byte-for-byte copy of Short.** Same title, same description, "bible shorts" in the tags, and a different hashtag set (3 vs 5). This is a short (the Format says short), so the long-form block should be removed or marked N/A. A pasted "Bible shorts" tag on a 16:9 upload is dishonest SEO.
8. **Title is a templated question + reference**, "Why Does the God of Peace Crush Satan? Romans 16:20". It is honest, but it is flat and does not open a real curiosity gap. The video's actual twist (the crusher has a *peace* title) is better, and Option 2 ("Paul Echoed Eden's Promise in One Verse") is more concrete.
9. **Facebook** drops the hook sentence entirely and leads with the reference, which is weak for a feed. **Instagram** has 8 hashtags, which is fine, but #Faith and #BibleStudy are generic filler. TikTok's first line is the jargon in finding 2.
10. **Tags:** "serpent crusher" and "bible shorts" are fine. "bruise satan" is a real search term. "apostle paul" is a loose fit. No stuffing, so this is OK.
11. **Footer:** the footer repeats the handle and a URL. This is fine. Dropping the "YouTube: @awakeden" line on a YouTube upload is cosmetic.

**Brand:** the CTA is present, but it is a generic footer. A Jesus-specific landing is missing from the body.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix UK-G7: put "Romans 16:20" in the first ~150 chars of the YouTube description. Remove or rewrite "It is not finished yet." (it echoes John 19:30 and contradicts "the blow already fell").
2. Add an explicit Christ link in the body and in every platform caption (Genesis 3:15 promise → fulfilled in Jesus at the cross → the believer shares that victory). Replace the jargon "crushing verb given to a peace-title" in the TikTok, Instagram and YouTube text with plain words, and give "His" a clear antecedent.
3. Align the "crush" in the title and TikTok/Instagram copy with the KJV's "bruise", or tie it to Genesis 3:15. Remove the duplicate Long-form block (and its "bible shorts" tag) for this short, and switch to a stronger title such as Option 2.
