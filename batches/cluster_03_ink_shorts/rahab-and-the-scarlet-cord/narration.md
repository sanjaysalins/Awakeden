When Jericho fell, one family walked out alive. Her name was Rahab. She lived in Jericho's wall. The Bible calls her a prostitute. Two spies came from God's people, Israel. She hid them, because she believed in their God. They told her: hang a red cord in your window, and your family will be safe. Israel marched. The people shouted. And "the wall fell down flat". Rahab and her family were spared. The cord was the sign. Trusting the promise saved her. A book of the Bible, Hebrews, says it in two words: "By faith". She is named in the family line of Jesus, God's Son, who "taketh away the sin of the world". Rahab could not stop a wall. She tied one cord to a promise. Tie your trust to Jesus, and bring everyone you love inside.
