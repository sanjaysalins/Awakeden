## TITLE
Many Said Be Quiet. Jesus Stood Still.

## DESCRIPTION
Mark 10:46-52: blind Bartimaeus sat begging by the road near Jericho. He cried out to Jesus, Son of David, and many told him to be quiet. Jesus was on His way to Jerusalem, where He would die. What would He do for one blind beggar?

"And Jesus stood still, and commanded him to be called. And they call the blind man, saying unto him, Be of good comfort, rise; he calleth thee." (Mark 10:49, KJV)
