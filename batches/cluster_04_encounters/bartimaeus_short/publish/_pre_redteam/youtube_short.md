## TITLE
On His Way to Die, Jesus Stopped | Blind Bartimaeus

## DESCRIPTION
Blind Bartimaeus in the Bible (Mark 10:46-52): many told him to be quiet, and Jesus was on His way to die. Would He stop?

He sat begging by the road near Jericho and cried out to Jesus as the Son of David, God's promised King. Many told him to hold his peace. He cried out even more.

"And Jesus stood still, and commanded him to be called. And they call the blind man, saying unto him, Be of good comfort, rise; he calleth thee." (Mark 10:49, KJV)

Read the whole story: Mark 10:46-52 (and Mark 10:32-34).

"And immediately he received his sight, and followed Jesus in the way." (Mark 10:52, KJV)

On His way to die and rise again, Jesus stood still for one man.
