On His Way to Die, Jesus Stopped | Blind Bartimaeus

## DESCRIPTION
Blind Bartimaeus in the Bible (Mark 10:46-52): many told him to be quiet, and Jesus was on His way to die. What would He do for one blind beggar?

He sat begging by the road near Jericho and cried out to Jesus, "Son of David", a name for God's promised King. Many told him to be quiet. He cried out even more.

"And Jesus stood still, and commanded him to be called. And they call the blind man, saying unto him, Be of good comfort, rise; he calleth thee." (Mark 10:49, KJV)

Read the whole story: Mark 10:46-52, with Mark 10:32-34 and 1 Corinthians 15:3-4.

"And immediately he received his sight, and followed Jesus in the way." (Mark 10:52, KJV)

On His way to die and rise again, Jesus stood still for one man.
