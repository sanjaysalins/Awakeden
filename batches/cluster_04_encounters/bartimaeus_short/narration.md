BARTIMAEUS: Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me.
NARRATOR: Many told the blind beggar to be quiet.
NARRATOR: His name was Bartimaeus. He sat begging by the road near Jericho. "Son of David" meant God's promised King.
NARRATOR: Jesus was on the road to Jerusalem. He had told His followers He would be killed there. Would He stop for a beggar?
NARRATOR: But Bartimaeus shouted even louder.
BARTIMAEUS: Thou Son of David, have mercy on me.
NARRATOR: "And Jesus stood still." A whole crowd was moving. He stopped for one man, and called for him.
NARRATOR: Bartimaeus threw off his cloak and came. Jesus asked him what he wanted:
JESUS: What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?
BARTIMAEUS: Lord, that I might receive my sight.
JESUS: Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole.
NARRATOR: He trusted Jesus. Jesus made him well. At once he could see.
NARRATOR: He was free to go anywhere. He chose to follow Jesus.
NARRATOR: Up the road to Jerusalem.
NARRATOR: There Jesus died on a cross for our sins, for the wrong things we have done.
NARRATOR: On the third day, He rose to life.
NARRATOR: On His way to die and rise again, Jesus stood still for one man.
