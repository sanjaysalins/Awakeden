# Upload kit review: "Why Did She Leave Her Jar?" (John 4)

I checked the kit text only. I did not watch the film.

## What is wrong

**1. The kit is not ready to ship. A gate failed.**
- The header says "Gates: FAILURES ❌", and UK-G7 failed: the Facebook copy has no "John" in the first 157 characters, so the verse falls below the "more" fold.
- The kit still says "pre-checked, red-teamed and panel-reviewed". That is misleading while a gate is red.

**2. The red-team asked for REVISE, and its fixes were not made.**
- **Instagram:** "Then she ran to tell the town" is still there. KJV John 4:28 says she "went her way into the city". "Ran" comes from the film's picture, not the text, and it sits right before a KJV quote, so it reads as Scripture.
- **Facebook:** "offered her a water from God" is still there. "A water" is awkward English and hard for non-native readers.

**3. The YouTube Short title gives up its search half.**
- "| Jesus at the Well" is not what people search. The searched phrase is "woman at the well", and the red-team says "jesus at the well" shows no autocomplete.
- Option 4 ("The Woman at the Well: Why Did She Leave Her Jar?") and the long-form title already show the fix.
- The title is owner-locked, so this is the owner's call. Flag it again; don't change it quietly.
- The tag "jesus at the well" is weak for the same reason.

**4. There is a long-form block, but the video is a Short.**
- The header says "Format: short" and points to one 9:16 file, yet a "YouTube (Long-form 16:9)" block is included.
- If no long-form video exists, that block describes a video nobody can upload. If it does exist, it needs its own file path.
- There is a second problem. The clearest gospel line ("He died for our sins and rose the third day") is only in the long-form block. The Short, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram copy never mention the cross. Their only call is "share this" plus "Subscribe/Follow Awakeden". The brand rule (point every piece to Jesus) is only weakly met where it matters most.

**5. Small wording points (not blocking)**
- "at noon": KJV says "about the sixth hour" (4:6). Noon is the usual reading, but "about noon" is the honest version.
- "a spring of life from God that never runs dry" (long-form): KJV 4:14 says "a well of water springing up into everlasting life". This is a paraphrase placed right next to a KJV quote. "Never runs dry" is not in the text.
- "He knew it all, and stayed" is used on every platform. It holds up (He kept talking with her, and 4:40 says He "abode there two days"). On its own it is a little interpretive. Fine as the film's line; don't build more on it.

## What is fine

- **KJV is exact.** I checked each quote word for word: John 4:28-29, 4:29, 4:26 "I that speak unto thee am he", 4:14 "shall never thirst", 4:42 "the Saviour of the world". No errors.
- **Tone:** no shame on the woman, no fear or gain/loss pressure, no clickbait. The title's question is answered by the story.
- **Hashtag counts are fine:** 5 on YouTube, 5 on TikTok, 4 on Facebook, 7 on Instagram. Tags are real search terms, with no stuffing.
- **TikTok's first line is a strong hook.**

VERDICT: REVISE

2. Make the red-team's text fixes. Instagram: "ran" → "Then she went back to tell the town:". Facebook: "a water from God" → "water from God, a spring that never runs dry".
