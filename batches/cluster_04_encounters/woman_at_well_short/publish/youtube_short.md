## TITLE
Why Did She Leave Her Jar? | Woman at the Well

## DESCRIPTION
Jesus and the woman at the well in the Bible (John 4): she came for water and went back to town without her jar. Why?

At noon a Samaritan woman meets Jesus, a Jew, who asks her for a drink. He offers her a different water, a gift from God, and tells her the truth about her whole life. He knew it all, and stayed. Then He tells her who He is.

"The woman then left her waterpot, and went her way into the city, and saith to the men, Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?" (John 4:28-29, KJV)

To give that life, He died for our sins and rose the third day.

Read the whole story: John 4:4-42. Who in your life needs to hear about a man who knows everything and still stays?
