## TITLE
Why Did She Leave Her Jar?

## DESCRIPTION
The woman at the well (John 4): she came for water and went back to town without her jar. Why?

Jesus asked a Samaritan woman for a drink, offered her water from God, a spring that never runs dry, and told her the truth about her whole life. He knew it all, and stayed.

"The woman then left her waterpot, and went her way into the city, and saith to the men, Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?" (John 4:28-29, KJV)

To give that life, He died for our sins and rose the third day.

Read it: John 4:4-42.
