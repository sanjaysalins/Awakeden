# Zacchaeus: Who Was Looking for Whom? — Luke 19

Style: scratchy-ink comic (owner's Google Flow look) · 85s short · Words LOCKED v3.1 2026-11-11.
KJV quoted: Luke 19:5, 19:8 (first half), 19:9 (first half), 19:10. Told: 19:2-4, 19:6, 19:7, 19:8 (fourfold). Background: Luke 3:12-13. Gospel: 1 Corinthians 15:3-4.

[narrator] A man climbed a tree to see Jesus. But Jesus was looking for him.
[narrator] Zacchaeus was a chief tax collector at Jericho. Tax men often took extra money from their own people. He was rich, and called a sinner. He was too short to see over the crowd.
[narrator] Jesus came by, looked up, and called him by name.
[jesus] "Zacchaeus, make haste, and come down; for to day I must abide at thy house."
[narrator] Jesus must stay at his house today! Zacchaeus hurried down, full of joy.
[narrator] The crowd grumbled: Jesus is going to a sinner's house! Jesus went anyway.
(pause)
[narrator] Jesus came to him first. Then Zacchaeus stood up and said:
[zacchaeus] "Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor."
[narrator] Half of all he had. And if he had cheated anyone, he would pay back four times.
[jesus] "This day is salvation come to this house."
[narrator] Salvation means God rescues you and brings you back to Him.
[narrator] Jesus, the Son of man, said why He came:
[jesus] "For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost."
[narrator] Zacchaeus came to look. Jesus came to find him. He still looks for the lost. To save them, He later died for our sins, and rose again on the third day.
