## Fixes (most important first)

1. **thumb_9x16.jpg: the text band covers Jesus' eyes and upper face.** Move the band down to the chest area (about 55-75% of the height), or put the text on the yellow background beside the head. The eyes must show. Today the face reads as hidden or masked at phone size.

2. **All 3 thumbnails: "WHO FOUND WHOM?" gives a newcomer no story.** There is no tree, no name, and no small man, and "whom" is a formal word for someone with English as a second language. Better: "HE CLIMBED A TREE. JESUS FOUND HIM." or "WHO WAS LOOKING FOR WHO?" If you keep the question, add one small line under it: "ZACCHAEUS - LUKE 19".

3. **thumb_9x16.jpg: shows only Jesus.** The question "who found whom" needs two people. If you can, use the 1x1 art (Jesus with his hand on Zacchaeus) cropped tall. It is the strongest of the three images.

4. **tiktok / facebook / instagram hashtags: #Shorts is a YouTube-only tag.** It is wasted on the other platforms.
   - facebook: "#Shorts" -> "#Reels"
   - tiktok: "#Shorts" -> "#BibleTok"
   - instagram: "#Shorts" -> "#Reels" (keep #ChristianReels)

5. **youtube_short TAGS: add the common misspelling.** Add "Zaccheus, Zacheus, wee little man". Real people type these.

6. **youtube_short DESCRIPTION: the source line reads as if the description quotes 19:8 and 19:9.**

10. **tiktok CAPTION: unclear pronoun.**
