## What passed

## Fixes (most important first)

1. **thumb_9x16.jpg — the dark band covers Jesus's eyes and forehead.** Face is covered. This is the cover for Shorts, TikTok and Reels, so it is the most seen one.
   Old: band + text at the top, over the eyes.
   New: move the band and text to the bottom third (chest area), like the 1x1. Keep the whole face clear.

2. **thumb_9x16.jpg — wrong picture for this story.** It shows Jesus alone, front on, with an open-mouth grin. No Zacchaeus, no tree, no meeting. A newcomer cannot tell what the film is about.
   New: use the 1x1 art (Jesus greeting Zacchaeus at the door), or a still of the man in the tree with Jesus looking up. That is the hook of the film.

3. **All 3 thumbnails — "WHO FOUND WHOM?" is hard for a newcomer.** "Whom" is a rare word for second-language readers, and the line says nothing about a tree, a man, or Jesus.
   Old: `WHO FOUND WHOM?`
   New: `HE CLIMBED A TREE. JESUS FOUND HIM.` (or shorter: `JESUS FOUND HIM FIRST`). It matches the YouTube title too.

4. **thumb_16x9.jpg — the band covers about 65% of the picture.** Both men's hands, the bowl and the table are hidden under it. Zacchaeus also looks as tall as Jesus here, but the film says he is short.
   New: shrink the band to the bottom 35-40%, or use the 1x1 art, where he is clearly shorter.

5. **tiktok / facebook / instagram — `#Shorts` is a YouTube tag.** It does nothing on the other three.
   Old (TikTok): `#Zacchaeus #Jesus #Luke19 #BibleStory #Shorts`
   New (TikTok): `#Zacchaeus #Jesus #Luke19 #BibleStory #BibleTok`
   New (Facebook): `#Zacchaeus #Jesus #Luke19 #BibleStory #Reels`
   New (Instagram): `#Zacchaeus #Jesus #Luke19 #BibleStory #ChristianReels #Reels`
   Fix both the body line and the HASHTAGS block in each file.

6. **tiktok CAPTION — the verse ref is loose.** The quote is verse 10 only, but the ref right after it says 1-10.
   Old: `..."to seek and to save that which was lost." Luke 19:1-10.`
   New: `..."to seek and to save that which was lost." (Luke 19:10) Full story: Luke 19:1-10.`

7. **facebook TITLE — "Whom" again, and the name comes late.**
   Old: `Who Was Looking for Whom? Zacchaeus and Jesus (Luke 19)`
   New: `Zacchaeus Went Looking for Jesus. Jesus Was Looking for Him (Luke 19)`

8. **facebook DESCRIPTION — "too far gone" is an idiom.** Hard for second-language readers.
   Old: `Send this to someone who thinks they are too far gone to be wanted.`
   New: `Send this to someone who feels too far from God to be wanted.`

9. **instagram CAPTION — the question is hard to follow.**
   Old: `Have you ever been found when you were only looking?`
   New: `Have you ever gone looking for God, and found He was already looking for you?`

10. **youtube_short DESCRIPTION — two small honesty points.**
    a. Jericho (verse 1) is told in the film but not listed.
       Old: `Told in plain words: Luke 19:2-4, 19:6-8`
       New: `Told in plain words: Luke 19:1-4, 19:6-8`
    b. "rich, short tax collector" reads a bit like a joke. He was the chief one.
       Old: `a rich, short tax collector climbed a tree`
       New: `a rich chief tax collector, too short to see over the crowd, climbed a tree`
       (Same line in facebook DESCRIPTION.)
