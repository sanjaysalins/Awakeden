**MY PICK: P1.**

**One word fix:** "Why **Would** Jesus Pick Him?" The word "would" brings in the crowd's shock ("him, of all people?"), and the film pays that off.

**Biggest risk:** The question only works if the viewer knows he is a bad man. The picture shows a man in a tree, not a cheat. So the first 3 seconds must say "rich, a cheat, hated". Without that, "why him?" has no bite for a newcomer.
