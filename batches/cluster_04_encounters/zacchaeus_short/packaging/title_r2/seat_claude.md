# Blind writer: title + thumbnail words, Zacchaeus short

## 1. Six pairs

**Pair A**
- **TITLE:** Why Did Jesus Pick Him? | Zacchaeus
- **THUMB:** WHY IS HE | UP THERE?
- **Question:** Why is a grown man in a tree, and why would Jesus choose *him*? The film pays it off three times: "too short to see" (~0:12), Jesus calls him by name (~0:20), and "seek and save the lost" (~1:05).

**Pair B**
- **TITLE:** What Made a Rich Man Give Half Away?
- **THUMB:** ONE LOOK | ONE NAME
- **Question:** What changed a greedy tax man so fast? It pays off at "Jesus came to him first. *Then* Zacchaeus stood up" (~0:40–0:50). Grace comes before the giving.

**Pair C**
- **TITLE:** Who Was Looking for Whom? (Jesus & Zacchaeus)
- **THUMB:** HE CAME TO SEE | WHO CAME?
- **Question:** Who was really searching? The hook line answers it (0:00): "Jesus was looking for him." The close nails it down (~1:15): "Jesus came to find him."

**Pair D**
- **TITLE:** The Man the Crowd Didn't Want Near Jesus
- **THUMB:** THEY GRUMBLED | HE SMILED
- **Question:** Why is Jesus smiling at someone people are angry about? It pays off at the crowd grumbling and "Jesus went anyway" (~0:28–0:35), then "salvation came to this house" (~0:55).

**Pair E**
- **TITLE:** Jesus Knew His Name | Zacchaeus
- **THUMB:** A STRANGER? | NOT TO JESUS
- **Question:** How does Jesus know a man He's never met, sitting in a tree? It pays off at "called him by name" (~0:20) and "seek and save the lost" (~1:05).

**Pair F**
- **TITLE:** Why Jesus Stopped Under This Tree
- **THUMB:** THE CROWD WAITED | HE LOOKED UP
- **Question:** What was so important in that tree? It pays off at "today I must abide at thy house" (~0:22) and "seek and save that which was lost" (~1:05).

## 2. Best pair: A

- **TITLE:** Why Did Jesus Pick Him? | Zacchaeus
- **THUMB:** WHY IS HE | UP THERE?

**Why:** The picture already asks "why is a man in a tree?", and the words make that question loud. The title adds a second, deeper question. Both use simple global words, and the film answers both: the tree early, the "pick" at the end with "seek and save the lost."

## 3. Biggest risk

"Why did Jesus pick him?" could read as "Zacchaeus earned it." The film's "He came to him *first*" line has to land clearly to close that off.
