# Seat: Fable — title + thumbnail words (blind)

Timestamps are estimates from the narration order (~85 s film).

## 1. Six pairs

**Pair 1**
- TITLE: `Why Jesus Stopped for Zacchaeus` (31)
- THUMB WORDS: `ME?` | `YES. YOU.`
- Opens: why would Jesus stop a whole crowd for this one startled man? Paid off at ~20-30 s (He calls him by name) and ~65-75 s ("to seek and to save that which was lost").

**Pair 2**
- TITLE: `The Man Nobody Wanted Jesus to Visit` (36)
- THUMB WORDS: `COME DOWN.` | `I'M STAYING WITH YOU.`
- Opens: who is he, and why did nobody want this? Paid off at ~6-20 s (tax man, called a sinner) and ~35-45 s (the crowd grumbles, Jesus goes anyway).

**Pair 3**
- TITLE: `He Climbed a Tree to See Jesus` (30)
- THUMB WORDS: `WHO FOUND` | `WHO?`
- Opens: who was really looking for whom? Paid off in the first line (~0-6 s) and closed at ~75 s ("Zacchaeus came to look. Jesus came to find him.").

**Pair 4**
- TITLE: `Why Did Jesus Pick This Man's House?` (36)
- THUMB WORDS: `THE CROWD` | `DID NOT LIKE IT`
- Opens: what was wrong with this man, and why choose him anyway? Paid off at ~35-45 s (the grumbling) and ~58-75 s ("This day is salvation come to this house").

**Pair 5**
- TITLE: `Too Short to See Jesus` (22)
- THUMB WORDS: `BUT JESUS` | `SAW HIM`
- Opens: what happens when the man who cannot see gets seen? Paid off at ~20-30 s (Jesus looks up and calls his name).

**Pair 6**
- TITLE: `Jesus Looked Up and Said One Name` (33)
- THUMB WORDS: `OF ALL PEOPLE,` | `HIM?`
- Opens: whose name, and why is that a surprise? Paid off at ~20-30 s (the name) and ~35-45 s (the crowd's complaint), answered at ~65-75 s (He came for the lost).

## 2. Best pair

**Pair 1 — `Why Jesus Stopped for Zacchaeus` + `ME?` | `YES. YOU.`**

- The thumb words are the picture talking: the startled man in the tree says "Me?", the smiling Jesus says "Yes. You." Three tiny words any English learner can read.
- The title asks "why", the thumb shows the shock, and neither tells the ending. The film's last lines are the answer (He came to find the lost).

## 3. Biggest risk

"Zacchaeus" is a hard, unknown word for a newcomer, and viewers who already know the story may feel no gap; if that tests badly, swap the title to Pair 2's and keep the same thumb words.
