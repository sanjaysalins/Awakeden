1. **TITLE:** He Just Wanted to See Jesus  
   **THUMB WORDS:** `WHY | MY HOUSE?`  
   Opens: Why does Jesus want to visit him? Invitation at ~20–35s; reason at ~65–80s.

2. **TITLE:** Jesus, Why Him?  
   **THUMB WORDS:** `THE CROWD | COMPLAINED`  
   Opens: Why choose someone the crowd dislikes? His reputation at ~10–20s; Jesus’ purpose at ~65–80s.

3. **TITLE:** Why Give Half Away?  
   **THUMB WORDS:** `ONE VISIT | FROM JESUS`  
   Opens: What moved a rich man to give so much? Jesus comes first; the promise follows at ~40–60s.

4. **TITLE:** Too Bad for Jesus?  
   **THUMB WORDS:** `COME | DOWN`  
   Opens: Will his bad reputation keep Jesus away? The personal invitation and visit answer at ~20–40s.

5. **TITLE:** Why Did the Crowd Complain?  
   **THUMB WORDS:** `JESUS | WENT ANYWAY`  
   Opens: What was wrong with this visit? The crowd objects to his host at ~30–40s.

6. **TITLE:** Who Found Who?  
   **THUMB WORDS:** `HE CAME | TO SEE JESUS`  
   Opens: Which man was actually seeking the other? Introduced at ~0–6s; fully explained at ~65–85s.

**BEST:** He Just Wanted to See Jesus / `WHY | MY HOUSE?`  
A simple wish becomes an unexpected personal visit. It leaves the reason open.

**Biggest risk:** “My house?” could be read as Jesus speaking unless its placement clearly connects it to Zacchaeus.
