1. **Six different PAIRS**

`TITLE`: Zacchaeus just wanted to look. Jesus wanted more.
`THUMB WORDS`: COME | DOWN
*Opens/Payoff*: What did Jesus want from him? Paid off at ~0:25 when Jesus unexpectedly invites Himself over.

`TITLE`: Why Zacchaeus gave away half his money
`THUMB WORDS`: JESUS | LOOKED UP
*Opens/Payoff*: What made a greedy tax collector change instantly? Paid off at ~0:45 when Zacchaeus repents after being chosen.

`TITLE`: The crowd hated Zacchaeus. Jesus called his name.
`THUMB WORDS`: I'M COMING | OVER
*Opens/Payoff*: Why would Jesus pick the worst guy in town? Paid off at ~0:55 when Jesus says He came to save the lost.

`TITLE`: Zacchaeus climbed a tree to watch Jesus.
`THUMB WORDS`: BUT JESUS | FOUND HIM
*Opens/Payoff*: Who is actually looking for who? Paid off at ~1:05 with the final lesson that Jesus seeks the lost.

`TITLE`: Jesus invited Himself to a sinner's house.
`THUMB WORDS`: THEY ALL | GRUMBLED
*Opens/Payoff*: Why cause a scandal for this corrupt man? Paid off at ~1:00 when Jesus explains His rescue mission.

`TITLE`: Zacchaeus thought he was just watching Jesus.
`THUMB WORDS`: HE GOT | CAUGHT
*Opens/Payoff*: What happens when Jesus spots you hiding? Paid off at ~0:20 when Jesus stops, looks up, and speaks directly to him.

***

2. **BEST pair**

`TITLE`: Zacchaeus climbed a tree to watch Jesus.
`THUMB WORDS`: BUT JESUS | FOUND HIM
*Why*: It perfectly captures the core twist of the story (we think we are seeking God, but God is seeking us) using incredibly simple, global English. The thumbnail text plays directly off the visual of Jesus pointing up at the startled man in the tree.

***

3. **Biggest risk**

*Risk*: It relies on warmth and irony rather than high-stakes drama, which might make it slightly too gentle to compete with aggressive, fast-paced secular shorts in the feed.
