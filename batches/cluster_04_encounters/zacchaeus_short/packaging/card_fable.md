- **Told in plain words, not quoted:** Luke 19:1-4 (Jericho, chief tax collector, rich, short, climbed a tree). 19:6 (came down with joy). 19:7 (the crowd grumbled, "a sinner"). 19:8 second half (pay back four times, as an "if").
- **Not covered:** Luke 19:9 second half ("forsomuch as he also is a son of Abraham"). Do not claim it.
- **Background, not from this passage:** "Tax men often took extra" comes from Luke 3:12-13. It is about tax men in general. The film does not say Zacchaeus did cheat; the text only says "if".
- **The end:** 1 Corinthians 15:3-4, said plainly (died for our sins, rose the third day). Not a word-for-word quote. It is not in Luke 19.
- **Order kept:** Jesus came first. The giving came after. Nothing says Zacchaeus earned or bought salvation.

## Weak spots, honestly
- **Search is a guess.** No real autocomplete was checked. Check before upload.
- **Title A is long (64).** It may cut off on small screens. The name is first so the search word survives.
- **Hook card and title say almost the same thing.** Safe, but the title adds no new pull.
- **Three old words:** "make haste", "abide", "Son of man". The film explains "abide" (stay) and names Jesus as the Son of man, but never says what "Son of man" means. A newcomer may miss it.
- **"Called a sinner"** is not explained. A newcomer may not know why a tax man was hated, beyond one line.
- **The tree:** KJV says "sycomore". The hook picture is a fig tree. Tree experts may comment. Keep the public text to "a tree".
- **Luke 19:8 is read as a new promise.** Some readers take "I give" as his habit already. Our words follow the common reading. Expect a few comments.
- **The end moves fast.** The cross and empty tomb come in one sentence. True, but short. It leans on the picture.
- **85 s is long for a Short.** The 1 s silence after "Jesus went anyway" could lose some viewers. Watch the retention graph there.
