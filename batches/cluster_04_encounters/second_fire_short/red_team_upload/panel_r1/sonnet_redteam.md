## 1. CURIOSITY GAP (score 6/10)
Stranger's question: "What is the second fire, and why did he feed him first?" The 9:16 cover is strong: tear plus burned hole plus bread over coals sells "hurt, then mercy." But "HE FED HIM FIRST." is a payoff line, not a question. It answers before the viewer asks, and it uses "him" with no named person. The film's last line then repeats it ("Jesus fed Peter"), so the cover and the ending say the same thing and the ending lands softer. The 16:9 "HE CARRIED THAT NIGHT." is the better gap (which night? what did he carry?), and the film does pay it off (the three denials). The gap leaks in the YouTube title: "Why a Fire Was Waiting for Peter" gives away that a fire was waiting, and the description's last line gives the film's closing line away. Every platform text repeats the "fed Peter" line.
The wide thumbnail has a split-image problem. Peter's silhouette holding the courtyard is clever but tiny at phone size, and the right two thirds is an empty teal slab. In PHONE_SIZE the art is a small strip and the text carries it.

## 4. GLOBAL WORDS
- "Lovest thou me?", "thou knowest", "dine": archaic. Quoted KJV is fine once, but the description repeats "Lovest thou me" and "Come and dine" without gloss. Swap or add: "Come and dine" (come and eat).
- "a fire of coals": non-native readers may read "coals" as unusual. Gloss: "a fire of burning coals."
- "Feed my sheep": an unexplained metaphor for a no-Bible-knowledge viewer. Add "(care for my people)".
- "Followers" is fine. "Disciple" absent, good.
