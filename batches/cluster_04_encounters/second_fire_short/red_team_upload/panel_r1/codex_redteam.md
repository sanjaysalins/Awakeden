## 1. CURIOSITY GAP
**4/10.** A stranger’s question is: **“What happened at the first fire?”** That is stronger than “Why was a fire waiting for Peter?”

The film pays off the connection through Peter’s three denials, Jesus’s three questions, and the meal. It does **not** explicitly establish why Jesus chose another fire.

The vertical cover gives away the strongest reversal with “HE FED HIM FIRST.” The bread image already supplies that clue; the tagline repeats it. Meanwhile, the eye, tear, torn opening, hands, loaf, coals, and three text levels compete for one second of attention.

The wide cover spends two thirds of its space repeating the title while squeezing its narrative clues into one third. “HE CARRIED THAT NIGHT” adds an unexplained metaphor and an unidentified “he.”

## 3. FACTS
- **“Peter said no to Jesus three times.”** This suggests Jesus asked him something directly. Peter denied being Jesus’s follower when other people questioned him. **Fix:** “Peter denied being a follower of Jesus three times.”
- **“‘I am not’ (three times)” in the film summary.** John 18 records those exact words twice, in verses 17 and 25. Verse 27 reports the third denial without quoting its words. Repeating the quotation a third time is dramatization. **Fix:** quote it twice and narrate the third denial.
- **“Why a Fire Was Waiting for Peter.”** The film shows breakfast for the disciples and a conversation with Peter. It does not establish that the fire was prepared specifically for him or explicitly explain Jesus’s intention in choosing coals. **Fix:** “The Second Fire.”

The other supplied biblical quotations and the two occurrences of “a fire of coals” present no wording problem.

## 4. GLOBAL WORDS
Use these swaps in explanatory prose; preserve quotations labelled KJV.

- **“Lovest thou me?”** → “Jesus asked Peter if he loved him.”
- **“Come and dine.”** → “Jesus invited them to eat.”
- **“a fire of coals”** → “a charcoal fire.”
- **“HE CARRIED THAT NIGHT.”** → “TWO FIRES. THE SAME MAN.”

“HE FED HIM FIRST” requires readers to resolve two unnamed people and an unstated “before what?”
