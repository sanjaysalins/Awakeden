## 1. CURIOSITY GAP — 6/10
Stranger's question: "Second fire? What was the first one?" "THE SECOND FIRE" opens that gap well. The film pays it off (John 18:18 / 21:9, the same rare words). Where it fails:
- **The 9:16 cover only shows ONE fire.** The burned tear shows the beach coals. Nothing shows the first fire. The WIDE gets this right (courtyard fire bowl inside his silhouette, beach fire outside). The cover, which matters more on Shorts, doesn't.
- **"HE FED HIM FIRST." gives away the last line.** It gives the payoff before the film starts. "FIRST" sits right above "SECOND", so a phone reader gets confused ("first fire? fed first?").
- **"HE CARRIED THAT NIGHT."** is vague. A stranger can't tell who "he" is or what night. It doesn't open a question. It only hints at feeling.
- At phone size the WIDE wastes two thirds of the frame on flat teal. Jesus is a few pixels tall. The art that carries the gap is the smallest thing in it.
- The YT title repeats the noun ("Fire ... Fire").

## 2. MOVIE TITLE?
- Plain "THE SECOND FIRE": **8/10.** It would work on a cinema sign.
- YT "The Second Fire: Why a Fire Was Waiting for Peter": **3/10.** "Why..." is explainer/sermon language. It also says "fire" twice. And it promises a WHY that the film shows as an echo but never states.

## 3. FACTS
- "Peter said no to Jesus three times by a fire" (YT and FB). This is wrong. Peter said no to the servants who asked him, not to Jesus. Fix: "Three times by a fire, Peter said he was not Jesus' follower."
- "He asked Peter three times... Peter answered, 'Lord, thou knowest all things...'" This sounds like the same answer three times. That exact wording is the third answer only (21:17). The first two were "Yea, Lord; thou knowest that I love thee." Fix: "The third time, Peter answered, ..."
- "Jesus fed Peter": 21:13 says "giveth THEM". Fine (Peter is included).
- "a fire of coals appears only twice": true for the exact phrase. "coals of fire" appears elsewhere (e.g. Rom 12:20, Ps 18:12), so expect comments. Fix: say "the exact words".
- The 9:16 bread has a cross-scored, modern-bakery boule look. Minor.
- **Not a fact error, but blocking:** the TikTok and Instagram sections are EMPTY. The YT "## TAGS" and FB "## HASHTAGS" are empty too. Two of the four platforms have no upload text.
