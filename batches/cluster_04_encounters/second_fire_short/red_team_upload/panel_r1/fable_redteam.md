## 1. CURIOSITY GAP (score 5/10)

The question a stranger asks: "Second fire? What was the first?" That is a good gap, and the film pays it off (courtyard fire, then beach fire, "a fire of coals" twice).

Where it leaks:
- **Shorts cover gives the ending away.** "HE FED HIM FIRST." is the film's last line, and the picture (Jesus's hand, bread, Peter's hands, coals) is the film's last shot. Answer shown before the question.
- **"HE FED HIM FIRST." has two unknown "he"s.** A stranger cannot tell who fed whom, or "first" before what. It reads as a riddle, with nothing at stake.
- **Wide: "HE CARRIED THAT NIGHT."** has a real gap (what night?), but "he" could be Jesus. Two thirds of the frame is a blank teal panel. At phone size the art is a small strip, Jesus is tiny, and the tagline is close to unreadable.
- **The two taglines tell two different stories.** One title, two promises.
- **Neither thumbnail shows the FIRST fire clearly.** The title says "second", the picture never sets up "first" (the wide has it, but hidden small inside the silhouette).
- **YouTube description line 1 tells the whole story**, ending included. Facebook's body does the same.
- **Repeat:** "fire" twice in both titles; "Peter" + "fire" + "John 21" stacked across title, cover and description.

## 3. FACTS

- **"Peter said no to Jesus three times"** (YouTube line 1, Facebook line 1). Peter never said no *to Jesus*. He told other people he was not a follower. Fix: "Three times, by a fire, Peter said he was not with Jesus."
- **"I am not" x3.** In John the words come twice (18:17, 18:25); the third is "Peter then denied again" (18:27). The description cites it right. Check the film does not put a third "I am not" inside quote marks.
- **"Peter had one."** (Facebook). John never says Peter kept thinking about that night. Soften: "Peter had a night like that."
- **Tear on the cover.** Peter weeps in Luke 22:62 and Matthew 26:75, not in John. The cover says "A STORY FROM JOHN 21" (21:17 says "grieved"). Small; accept or drop the John-only label.
- **"Jesus fed Peter."** John 21:13 says "giveth them". Fair reading, but 21:13 is missing from the cited verse list while being the punchline. Add it.
- "a fire of coals" only twice in KJV: correct as worded. All quotes match KJV.
- "Art and voices made with AI tools": if the music is AI too, say "Art, voices and music".
