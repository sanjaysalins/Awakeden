## TITLE
The Second Fire: Why a Fire Was Waiting for Peter

## DESCRIPTION
Bible story: Peter said no to Jesus three times by a fire. After Jesus rose, another fire was waiting on the beach. (John 18, 21)

On the night Jesus was arrested, Peter stood by a fire. When people asked if he was one of Jesus' followers, he said, "I am not." After Jesus rose from the dead, Peter went back to fishing. At sunrise Jesus stood on the shore, and on the beach there was "a fire of coals." Jesus said, "Come and dine." Then He asked Peter three times, "Lovest thou me?" Peter answered, "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee." Jesus said, "Feed my sheep." Before Jesus asked Peter to feed anyone, Jesus fed Peter.

In the film, quoted word for word from the KJV: John 18:17, 18:25, 21:7, 21:9, 21:12, 21:15-17. Told in plain words: John 18:15-27 (in part) and John 21:1-17. In the King James Bible, the words "a fire of coals" appear only twice: John 18:18 and John 21:9.
