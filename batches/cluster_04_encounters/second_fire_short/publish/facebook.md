## TITLE
The Second Fire

## DESCRIPTION
Is there one night you can't stop thinking about? At the first fire, Peter said he was not one of Jesus' followers. Three times. (John 18, John 21)

On the night Jesus was arrested, Peter stood by a fire. When people asked if he was one of Jesus' followers, he said, "I am not." After Jesus rose from the dead, Peter went back to fishing. At sunrise Jesus stood on the shore, and on the beach there was "a fire of coals" (a small charcoal fire). Jesus said, "Come and dine." (Come and eat.) Then He asked Peter three times, "Lovest thou me?" (Do you love me?) The third time, Peter answered, "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee." (Lord, You know everything. You know I love You.) Jesus said, "Feed my sheep." (Take care of my people.)
