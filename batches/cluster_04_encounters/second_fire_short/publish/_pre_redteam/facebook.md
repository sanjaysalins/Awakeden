## TITLE
The Second Fire: Peter and a Fire of Coals (John 21)

## DESCRIPTION
Is there one night you can't stop thinking about? Peter had one. He said no to Jesus three times by a fire. (John 18, John 21)

On the night Jesus was arrested, Peter stood by a fire. When people asked if he was one of Jesus' followers, he said, "I am not." After Jesus rose from the dead, Peter went back to fishing. At sunrise Jesus stood on the shore, and on the beach there was "a fire of coals." Jesus said, "Come and dine." Then He asked Peter three times, "Lovest thou me?" Peter answered, "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee." Jesus said, "Feed my sheep."

Before Jesus asked Peter to feed anyone, Jesus fed Peter. Send this to someone who can't stop thinking about one bad night.
