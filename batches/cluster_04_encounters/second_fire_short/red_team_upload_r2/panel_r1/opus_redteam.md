## 3. FACTS
- YouTube "Peter said three times that he did not know Jesus" and Facebook "Three times, he said he did not know Jesus. (John 18, John 21)": John never has Peter say this. In John he says "I am not" (one of his disciples), 18:17, 18:25; 18:27 just "denied again". "I know not the man" is Matthew 26:72. Wrong source. Fix: "said three times that he was not one of Jesus' followers."
- TikTok quotes "I am not" then cites "(John 21:9-12, KJV)". That line is John 18. Fix: "(John 18:17; 21:9-12, KJV)". Instagram "(John 21, KJV)": same. Fix: "(John 18 and 21, KJV)".
- TikTok/IG/FB "Peter had one" (a night he can't stop thinking about): John never says this. Soft overclaim. Fix: "Peter had a night like that."
- Minor: the tear is Matthew/Luke, not John.
- "a fire of coals" only twice in KJV (John 18:18, 21:9): holds. "Lovest thou me?", "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee.", "Feed my sheep.", "Come and dine." match KJV.
