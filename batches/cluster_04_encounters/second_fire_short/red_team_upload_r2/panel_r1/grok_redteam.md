In one second the stranger asks, "He said no to what?" The picture answers: Jesus is putting bread into Peter's hands. The pair says Peter refused the meal. That is the wrong story. He said "I am not" to people at the courtyard fire. At this fire he takes the bread. Tear + loaf + "NO" sells a refusal. The film pays off a meal and a third yes.

"The Second Fire" never becomes the question. The wide poster already shows both fires and the sunrise. The tall poster already shows the last shot: bread, coals, hands.

The film's real turn — Jesus fed Peter before he asked Peter to feed anyone — is never opened, so it cannot pay off.
