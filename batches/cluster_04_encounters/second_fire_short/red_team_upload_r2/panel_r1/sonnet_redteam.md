## 1. CURIOSITY GAP (score 7/10)
Stranger's question: "He said no to WHAT? What is the first fire, and why is there a second?" The tagline "HE SAID NO AT THE FIRST ONE." plus the weeping eye plus bread-into-hands over coals reads in about a second on the phone (PHONE_SIZE.jpg). The tear says guilt, the bread says forgiveness. Good.
Pay-off: the film does pay it off (three "I am not" at fire 1; three "Lovest thou me" at fire 2; "Jesus fed Peter").
Leaks:
- "He" is unnamed on both thumbnails. A non-Bible viewer does not know who "he" is; "a story from John 21" does not help them. The gap is felt, but vague.
- The tagline gives away the redemption: the bread image already shows he is fed. The gap is "why", not "what happens".
- Wide: Peter and Jesus are both shown, but Jesus is tiny and Peter's silhouette is dark. The wide image is weaker than the vertical one. At phone size the wide thumb's art is about 40% and the title block dominates.
- Repeat: the description paragraph repeats the film beat by beat, so the surprise is spent before play. The "fire of coals appears only twice" fact is good bait but is only in the description, not on the thumbnail.
- "No" is oddly soft. Peter said "I am not" / "I know not the man". "Said no" is plain, but it can misread as refusing something (a request), not denying Jesus.

## 3. FACTS
- Tagline "HE SAID NO AT THE FIRST ONE." vs film: Peter said "I am not" (John 18:17, 25) and, per the Gospel, denied knowing Him. "Said no" is an acceptable paraphrase of "I am not", but the description frames it as "he did not know Jesus" (Facebook) while the quote is "I am not [one of his disciples]". Slight mismatch: John 18:17,25 is "I am not" (to "art thou also one of this man's disciples"); the "I know not the man" line is in the other Gospels (Matt 26:72, Luke 22:57). Facebook line "he said he did not know Jesus" is then not John's wording. Fix: "Three times, he said he was not one of Jesus' followers."
- YouTube first line: "Peter said three times that he did not know Jesus." Same issue. Fix: "Peter said three times that he was not one of Jesus' followers."
- "fire of coals" appears only twice in KJV (John 18:18, 21:9): correct (the Greek word anthrakia is used twice; KJV renders "fire of coals" in both). Claim is true as stated; keep "in the King James Bible".
- "Peter answered" the third time with "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee" correct (21:17). But the description says "The third time, Peter answered..." and the film has Peter replying that line to the third question only; fine.
- "Jesus said, 'Come and dine'" is John 21:12, correct. TikTok/IG cite "(John 21:9-12)": fine.
- Wide thumbnail shows Jesus standing by a small beach fire with Peter's courtyard fire inside the silhouette: matches the film. Cover shows a loaf with a cross-like scoring and Peter's hands: film says bread and fish; the cover omits fish. Minor, not an overclaim.
- "Jesus' followers" vs "A story from John 21": the film includes John 18 events too; the thumb says only John 21. Minor; the description covers both.
- Description claims "Told in plain words: John 18:15-27 (in part)": fine.

## 5. TOP 3 FIXES
1. YouTube and Facebook descriptions, first line: replace "Peter said three times that he did not know Jesus" / "Three times, he said he did not know Jesus" with "Three times, Peter said he was not one of Jesus' followers." This keeps it exact to John 18:17,25.
2. Wide thumbnail: enlarge Jesus and brighten the second fire so the "second fire" is findable at phone size, or move the tagline so it reads above the art. Also consider "HE SAID NO AT THE FIRST ONE." becoming "PETER SAID NO AT THE FIRST ONE." on the wide only if the wide runs alone; otherwise the unnamed "he" stays a leak. Cover is fine as is.
3. TikTok and Instagram: open with the gap, not the self-help question. "Peter said no at the first fire. Then Jesus lit a second one." Then keep the "I am not" quote and the "only twice" fact.

## VERDICT: REVISE
Packaging is strong (the vertical cover is a clear 8/10 on a phone). The revise is for one factual wording point (Peter said "I am not", not "I do not know Jesus" in John) and a weaker wide thumbnail and unnamed "he".
