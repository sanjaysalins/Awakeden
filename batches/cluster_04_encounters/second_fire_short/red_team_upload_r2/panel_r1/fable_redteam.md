## 1. CURIOSITY GAP: 6.5/10

Stranger's question: "What was the first fire, and what did he say no to?" The film pays it off in full (two fires, three no, three yes, bread). The gap is real, but it leaks and stalls:

- **The cover shows the ending.** The bread passing hand to hand over the coals is the film's last image. The poster gives away the payoff picture before the film earns it. The tear and the burned tear-hole are the hook; the loaf is the answer.
- **"HE SAID NO AT THE FIRST ONE."** "ONE" points forward to a word the reader has not read yet. A second-language reader hits "first one... first what?" and has to loop back. At phone size the tagline is thin and small, so the line that opens the gap is the hardest to read.
- **Wide (16:9):** 60% is a flat teal text panel. Jesus and both fires are tiny at phone size. The two-fires idea (the whole gap) is invisible; it reads as "man's head + sunrise".
- **"A STORY FROM JOHN 21"** is unreadable at phone size on both, and means nothing to a non-Bible stranger. Harmless, but it is dead ink.
- **Repeat:** TikTok and Instagram captions open with the film's own first line word for word. The caption adds no new gap; it says the same thing twice. Both captions then tell the whole fire reveal ("only twice"), which is the film's best surprise.

## 3. FACTS

1. **"Peter said three times that he did not know Jesus. (John 18, John 21)"** (YouTube) and **"Three times, he said he did not know Jesus. (John 18, John 21)"** (Facebook). In John 18, Peter never says he does not know Jesus. He is asked if he is a disciple and says "I am not." "I know him not" is Luke 22:57. The line cites John and quotes another Gospel's idea. Fix: "Peter said three times that he was not one of Jesus' followers."
2. **"I am not" three times.** In John KJV the words "I am not" are spoken twice (18:17, 18:25); the third time is only "Peter then denied again" (18:27). The upload text is careful here (cites 18:17 and 18:25 only). Keep it that way; never write "said 'I am not' three times".
3. **"quoted word for word from the KJV: ... 21:7"**. The film summary in my pack shows no 21:7 quote ("It is the Lord"). If the film does not speak it, remove 21:7.
4. **TikTok "(John 21:9-12, KJV)"** sits after "I am not", which is John 18. Fix: "(John 18 and John 21, KJV)".
5. **The tear on the cover.** John has no weeping Peter (that is Luke 22:62 / Matthew 26:75); John 21:17 says "grieved". Acceptable as poster art only if the film shows a tear. Check.
6. "a fire of coals" only twice (John 18:18, 21:9): correct as worded. "Come and dine", "Lovest thou me?", "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee", "Feed my sheep": KJV exact.
