# RED TEAM: upload details for one 88 s vertical Bible short (YouTube Short, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram)

## The film (what the viewer actually gets)
Flat folk-art print animation (burnt orange, teal, cream, grain). Narrator + Peter's voice + Jesus's voice, oud/flute/cello music.
The night Jesus was arrested, Peter stood by a fire and said "I am not" (three times). Jesus died and rose. Peter went back to
fishing, caught nothing. At sunrise Jesus on the shore; the net fills; Peter jumps into the sea. On the beach: "a fire of coals"
- in the King James Bible these words appear only twice (John 18:18, John 21:9). "Come and dine." Jesus hands them bread and fish,
then asks Peter three times "Lovest thou me?"; Peter: "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee." "Feed my
sheep." Last line: "Three times, no, that night. Three times, yes, at the second fire. And before Jesus asked Peter to feed anyone,
Jesus fed Peter." Ends on Jesus's hands giving bread into Peter's hands over the coals. Opening line: "Is there one night you
can't stop thinking about?"

## The two thumbnails (movie-poster style)
- SHORTS COVER (9:16): a big painted close-up of Peter's eye with one tear; a burned, torn hole in the picture shows Jesus's
  patterned sleeve and hand putting a round loaf into Peter's open hands, glowing coals below. Type at the bottom: tagline
  "HE SAID NO AT THE FIRST ONE." above the big title "THE SECOND FIRE", small line "A STORY FROM JOHN 21".
- WIDE (16:9): left ~40% = painted poster: Peter's dark profile silhouette with the night courtyard and a fire bowl inside it,
  a sunrise burst behind, Jesus standing by a small beach fire. Right = dark teal panel with tagline "HE SAID NO AT THE FIRST
  ONE." and the big title "THE SECOND FIRE" on two lines, small "A STORY FROM JOHN 21".

# youtube_short — YouTube (Short)

## TITLE
The Second Fire

## DESCRIPTION
Bible story: Peter said three times that he did not know Jesus. After Jesus rose, a second fire was waiting on the beach. (John 18, John 21)

On the night Jesus was arrested, Peter stood by a fire. When people asked if he was one of Jesus' followers, he said, "I am not." After Jesus rose from the dead, Peter went back to fishing. At sunrise Jesus stood on the shore, and on the beach there was "a fire of coals." Jesus said, "Come and dine." (Come and eat.) Then He asked Peter three times, "Lovest thou me?" (Do you love me?) The third time, Peter answered, "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee." Jesus said, "Feed my sheep." (Take care of my people.)

In the film, quoted word for word from the KJV: John 18:17, 18:25, 21:7, 21:9, 21:12, 21:15-17. Told in plain words: John 18:15-27 (in part) and John 21:1-17. In the King James Bible, the words "a fire of coals" appear only twice: John 18:18 and John 21:9.
