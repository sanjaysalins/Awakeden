## Weak spots, honestly

1. **Search terms are guesses.** No real autocomplete check was done by this seat.
2. **The "only twice" fact is an English KJV fact.** Commenters may point to "coals of fire" (other wording, other books). The line is safe only with "In the King James Bible" and the exact words "a fire of coals".
3. **The hook is about the viewer's night; the film is about Peter's.** The film never says "and so with you". That is good (grace, no pressure), but the send-line must not promise more than the film gives.
4. **88 s is long for a Short.** The fact lands near the half-way mark. Some viewers will leave before it. The first 2 seconds and the fishing stretch (about 15-35 s) are the risk.
5. **Title A says the fire was "waiting for Peter".** That is a reading, not a quote. It is gentle and fits the text, but B is the safer pick if a stricter seat objects.
6. **"Three times, yes"** is the film's own summary. In v17 Peter does not say the word "yes". Keep public text to "Jesus asked him three times".
7. **Pay-off times are estimates** from word count, not measured on the cut.
8. **The teaser is a placeholder.**
9. **Odds:** no honest number can be given from words alone. Title and tags are a small lever. The first 2 seconds, real seed viewers and the 48-hour retention read matter more.
