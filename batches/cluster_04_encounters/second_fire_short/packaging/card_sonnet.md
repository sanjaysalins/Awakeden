## The 4 questions
- **Who is it for:** Anyone who carries one bad night they replay - and Bible readers who know Peter's story.
- **Take-away:** Jesus did not wait for Peter to fix it. He fed him first, at a fire like the one where Peter failed.
- **Curiosity gap:** Why does a fire on a beach matter - what links it to the night Peter said no?
- **Global audience:** Yes. Plain words, short lines, a simple story (fire, fish, bread). The one hard KJV phrase ("a fire of coals") is explained by the film.

## Weak spots (honest)

5. The ending is quiet. It rewards people who stay; it will not shock anyone into a share. The send-line must do that work.
