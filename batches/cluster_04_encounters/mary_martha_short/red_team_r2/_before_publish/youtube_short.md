## TITLE
Martha Asked Jesus. He Said Her Name Twice.

## DESCRIPTION
Mary and Martha (Luke 10, John 11:5): Martha asked Jesus, "Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone?" His answer began with her name, twice.

Martha opened her home to Jesus and was busy with much serving, while her sister Mary sat at His feet and listened. One thing is needful, He said, and Mary had chosen it: to listen to Him. He did not call Martha's work wrong. He saw the worry under it. And later the Bible says this about Martha, Mary and their brother: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus." (John 11:5)
