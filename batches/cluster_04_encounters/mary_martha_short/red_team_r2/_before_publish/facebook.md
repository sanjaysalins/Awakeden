## TITLE
Mary and Martha: Did Jesus Care? (Luke 10)

## DESCRIPTION
In Luke 10 and John 11:5, Martha opened her home to Jesus and was busy with much serving, while her sister Mary sat at His feet and listened. Martha asked Him, "Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone?" He answered with her name, twice. One thing is needful, He said, and Mary had chosen it: to listen to Him. He did not call Martha's work wrong. He saw her worry, and He loved her. Later the Bible says this about Martha, Mary and their brother: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus."
