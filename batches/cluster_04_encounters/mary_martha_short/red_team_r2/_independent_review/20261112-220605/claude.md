# Red-team review: Martha and Mary upload kit

All three KJV quotes are word for word: Luke 10:40b, Luke 10:41-42 and John 11:5. Martha is never put down. The tone is grace, with no fear. The AI line appears once on each platform. The problems are small honesty and clarity issues, not doctrine.

## 1. Bible accuracy

- **"Martha asked Jesus that." (TikTok, Instagram, and the film's line 3)** stretches the text. Martha did not ask "Does anyone even care?" She asked Jesus whether *He* cared ("dost **thou** not care"). The hook turns a question aimed at Jesus into a general complaint. The film is locked, but the captions can say it plainly. Fix: "Martha asked Jesus almost that: 'Lord, dost thou not care…?'"
- **"He answered with her name, twice." (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram)** makes it sound as if His whole answer was her name. The YouTube wording is the honest one: "His answer **began** with her name, twice." Use that on all four platforms.
- **"Mary had chosen it: to listen to Him." (YouTube, Facebook)** reads one interpretation as fact. Jesus does not define the "one thing", but verse 39 ("sat at Jesus' feet, and heard his word") supports it. Fix: "Mary had chosen it: to sit at His feet and hear His word." That stays close to the KJV.
- **"their brother" (YouTube, Facebook):** that Lazarus is their brother comes from John 11:2, not 11:5. Fix: change the YouTube ref list to "John 11:2, 5", or drop "their brother".
- **Film only (no change needed here):** "He would not send her away" is a loose reading of "shall not be taken away from her". In the verse it is the good part that is kept, not Mary. Note it for future scripts.

## 2. Clickbait and the title + thumbnail pair

- **The pair works.** The thumbnail "DOES ANYONE EVEN CARE?" asks, the title "He Said Her Name Twice" adds, and John 11:5 answers. That is not bait.
- **Small mismatch:** the thumbnail says "DOES ANYONE **EVEN** CARE?" but the on-screen text says "Does anyone care?". The voice does say "even", so this is acceptable.
- **YouTube title "Martha Asked Jesus." reads unfinished:** asked what? It is a weak fragment for second-language readers, and it misses the search phrase "Mary and Martha". Fix: "Mary and Martha: Jesus Said Her Name Twice" (42 characters).

## 3. Global / ESL clarity

- **The YouTube description's first 150 characters** are mostly "dost thou… hath left me". That is the part shown before "more", and auto-translate garbles it. Lead with plain words, then the quote. Fix: "Martha asked Jesus, 'Don't You care?' (KJV: 'Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone?')"
- **"busy with much serving" (YouTube, Facebook)** is clunky. Fix: "busy serving".
- **"Later, the Bible says"** is unclear: later in what? Fix: "Later, in John 11, the Bible says".

## 4. Respect

- No put-down of Martha. "He did not call Martha's work wrong" is true and kind.
- Mary is not set up as the winner beyond Jesus' own words. This passes.

## 5. Platforms

- **YouTube:** 5 hashtags, and the first 3 are good. The hashtags appear both in the description and in a separate HASHTAGS section, so paste them once only.
- **TikTok:** 5 hashtags and a strong first line. Also turn on TikTok's own AI-content label so it matches the AI line.
- **Facebook:** the description is one big wall of text with a weak opener ("In Luke 10 and John 11:5,…"). Start with the hook line ("You do everything for everyone. Does anyone even care?") and split it into 3 short paragraphs. #Reels is a low-value tag.
- **Instagram:** 6 hashtags, fine.
- **Tags:** real search terms. "Bible short" is filler but harmless.

## 6. Slop and the AI line

The AI line is honest and appears once per platform. No slop. The "Find Jesus in the whole Bible" footer is a brand line, not an invitation to Jesus. The Christ-pointing landing ("And He loved her") is carried by John 11:5, and that is enough. Do not add a stock "come to Jesus" closer.

## 7. Consistency

- The captions match the spoken words.
- The next teaser ("another person who brought Jesus a hard question") is a promise. Make sure the next episode really fits it, or cut the teaser.
