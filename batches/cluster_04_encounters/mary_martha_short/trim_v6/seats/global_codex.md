## Version X

**NEWCOMER**
- About: Martha, Mary, and Jesus.
- What happens: Martha is busy serving while Mary listens to Jesus; Jesus says Mary chose what matters.
- Why it matters: Jesus notices Martha’s worry and loves her.
- Hardest line: “Thou art careful and troubled about many things” — “careful” may sound like “cautious.”

**BIBLE READER**
- It may highlight that Jesus’ words to Martha are personal and that John says he loved her.
- Nothing plainly contradicts Luke 10 or John 11:5. “Under all her work, He saw her worry” is an interpretation, not a direct statement in the passages.

**CURIOSITY:** Yes, the opening question is paid off by the later line about Jesus loving Martha. **8/10**

**ENDING:** **Yes.** “And He loved her” lands on Jesus’ care for Martha and closes the thought.

## Version Y

- About: Martha, Mary, and Jesus.
- What happens: Martha is busy serving while Mary listens to Jesus; Jesus says Mary chose what matters.
- Why it matters: Jesus notices Martha’s worry and loves her.
- Hardest line: “Mary hath chosen that good part” may be unclear, even after “careful” is explained.

**BIBLE READER**
- The explanation of “careful” helps modern listeners hear the older sense of “full of worry.”
- Nothing plainly contradicts the cited passages. As in X, “Under all her work, He saw her worry” is an interpretation.

**CURIOSITY:** Yes, the opening question is paid off by the later line about Jesus loving Martha. **8/10**

**ENDING:** **Yes.** It ends on Jesus’ love for Martha.

**PICK: Y.** The brief definition helps a newcomer understand the KJV line. **One cut-only fix:** delete “Mary had chosen the one thing needed: to listen to Him.” It repeats the preceding KJV line’s point.
