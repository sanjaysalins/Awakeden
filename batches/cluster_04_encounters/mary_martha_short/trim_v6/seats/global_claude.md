# Blind seat: Version X vs Version Y

## VERSION X

**NEWCOMER**
- **Who:** Martha, her sister Mary, and Jesus.
- **What:** Martha works hard, gets upset that Mary just sits, and asks Jesus if He cares. He says her name twice and answers.
- **Why it matters:** Someone who does everything still matters, and Jesus loved her.
- **Line they would not follow:** "thou art **careful** and troubled". Today "careful" means "cautious", so they hear "you are cautious". The point is lost until the last line ("He saw her worry"), which comes late. "That good part" and "needful" are hard too.

**BIBLE READER**
- **What they get:** the hook is a good one. The "dost thou not care" in Luke 10:40 is answered by "Jesus loved Martha" in John 11:5, and Martha is named first there. Most people don't link these two verses.
- **Is it true to the text?** Yes. Luke 10:40-42 and John 11:5 are quoted correctly.
- **"Mary had chosen... to listen to Him":** fair. She "heard his word" (10:39). This is a reading of the text, not a direct quote.
- **"Later":** a soft spot. John 11 is a different Gospel, so the order of the two events is not certain.

**CURIOSITY: 7/10.** "Does anyone even care?" opens in second 2 and gets its answer at the end. But the newcomer may get lost in the middle on "careful".

**ENDING: yes.** "And He loved her" is about Jesus and answers the opening question. It feels finished.

## VERSION Y

**NEWCOMER**
- Who, what and why are the same as X.
- **Line they would not follow:** "that good part" and "needful" are still a little hard. But "Careful means full of worry" fixes the biggest snag, and it sets up "He saw her worry".

**BIBLE READER**
- They get the same link between the two verses. "Careful = full of worry" is right: the KJV word means "full of care", and the Greek word means anxious.
- **Loss:** it cuts "bid her therefore that she help me", so Martha's demand is gone. The quote is cut short but still true. It just feels a little less sharp.
- "Later" is the same soft spot as in X.

**CURIOSITY: 8/10.** Same hook and payoff, and the middle stays clear now.

**ENDING: yes.** Same strong last line, and the "worry" thread now ties together from middle to end.

## MY PICK: Y

**One fix (cut only):** delete **"Later,"** so the line reads "The Bible says this about Martha, Mary and their brother:". That removes the one claim about timing that can't be checked, and loses nothing.
