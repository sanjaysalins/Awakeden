## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** Grace, 42. She runs the church kitchen, works full time and feels unseen.
- **Who stops:** tired carers and servers. Lines 1–2 ("You do everything for everyone. / Does anyone even care?") speak straight to them.
- **Who finishes:** church women's groups and people who know the story and like a gentle twist.
- **Who shares:** people forwarding it to a worn-out friend, with a message like "this is you ❤️".
- **Who is NOT served:** the Mary-type viewer, and anyone who wants to know what the "one thing" is. The film quotes line 10 and never comes back to it.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus saw the worry under Martha's work, and He loved her." Lines 16–17 carry it.
It comes across clearly. But it lands as comfort only. The redirect in lines 10–11 (sit, hear, choose the good part) gets lost, so a viewer could walk away thinking "keep doing it all, He loves you."

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
**Who / what / why, in my words:** A busy woman complains that Jesus doesn't care. He says her name twice, names her worry, and points to her sister's choice. Another passage says He loved all three of them.

**Where I got lost or misled:**
- **Line 10, "one thing is needful":** never explained. A newcomer won't know what the one thing is.
- **Line 11, "that good part":** this is old English. In translation it becomes "a good piece" or "a good role".
- **Lines 12–13** play on *care* (line 5) against *careful*. That link works in English only. In Greek the words differ (*melei* in 10:40, *merimnas* in 10:41; worth a human check). Dubbed versions will lose it. This fails the dub rule.
- **Line 14, "Later":** later in what? Viewers will assume it's later in the same story, the same day.

**Would I send it to my church group?** Yes, with a note about what "the one thing" is.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would pay off line 10. Mary's posture in line 4 *is* the answer ("sat… heard his word"). Show that, then show the love.

**The ONE highest-value change:** add one line after line 12 saying the one thing is sitting with Him and hearing Him.

**Score: 6/10.**

## CURIOSITY GAP
- **Opens:** line 2, and again at line 13.
- **Pays off:** lines 15–17.
- **Score:** 7/10.

The wait is worth it emotionally. But the second question (what is the "one thing"?) opens at line 10 and is never paid off.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **Line 3:** "Martha asked Jesus: Lord, don't You care?"
2. **Line 14:** "In John's Gospel, the Bible says this about Martha, her sister Mary, and their brother Lazarus:"
3. **Add after line 12:** "The one thing? What Mary did: sit with Him, and hear Him."

Also, cut or soften the "Martha kneeling, calm" picture, or keep it clearly as imagined.
