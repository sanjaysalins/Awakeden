You do everything for everyone. Does anyone even care? Martha asked Jesus that. While she served, her sister Mary sat at His feet and listened. Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore that she help me. His answer? Her name. Twice. Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her. "Careful" means full of worry. Mary had chosen the one thing needed: to listen to Him. So, did He care? Later, the Bible says this about Martha, Mary and their brother: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus." He did not call Martha's work wrong. Under all her work, He saw her worry. And He loved her.
