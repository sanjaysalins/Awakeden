## TITLE
Martha Asked Jesus If He Cared. He Said Her Name Twice.

## DESCRIPTION
Does anyone even care? Martha asked Jesus if He cared. His answer began with her name, twice. (Mary and Martha, Luke 10, John 11:5)

Martha opened her home to Jesus and was busy serving, while her sister Mary sat at His feet and heard His word. Martha asked, "Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone?" Jesus said one thing is needful, and Mary had chosen that good part. He did not call Martha's work wrong. He saw the worry under it. Later, in John 11, the Bible says this about Martha, Mary and their brother: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus." (John 11:5)

In the film, quoted word for word from the KJV: Luke 10:40 (second half), Luke 10:41-42, John 11:5. Told in plain words: Luke 10:38-40; Lazarus as their brother: John 11:1-2. In Luke 10:41, "careful" means full of worry.
