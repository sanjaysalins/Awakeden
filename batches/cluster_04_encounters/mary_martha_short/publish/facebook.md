## TITLE
Mary and Martha: He Said Her Name Twice (Luke 10)

## DESCRIPTION
Does anyone even care? Martha asked Jesus if He cared. His answer began with her name, twice. (Luke 10, John 11:5)

Martha opened her home to Jesus and was busy serving, while her sister Mary sat at His feet and heard His word. Martha asked Him, "Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone?" Jesus said one thing is needful, and Mary had chosen that good part. He did not call Martha's work wrong. He saw her worry.

Later, in John 11, the Bible says this about Martha, Mary and their brother: "Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus." (John 11:5) Send this to the one who does everything for everyone.
