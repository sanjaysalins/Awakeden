# youtube_short — YouTube (Short)

## TITLE
Jesus Walks on Water. Job Said Only God Does.

## DESCRIPTION
Long ago, a man named Job said God alone walks on the sea. Then Jesus did it. In the Bible: Job 9:8 and Mark 6:48.

Job 9:8 says God alone "treadeth upon the waves of the sea" (walks on the waves). Job also said of God: "Lo, he goeth by me, and I see him not." (Job 9:11, KJV) And: "he is not a man, as I am." (Job 9:32, KJV) Job did not name Jesus. He described what only God does.

In Mark 6, at night, Jesus' followers row against the wind. Jesus comes to them "walking upon the sea, and would have passed by them." (Mark 6:48, KJV) The Bible does not say why. But unlike Job, they saw Him, and He spoke: "It is I; be not afraid." (Mark 6:50, KJV)

The Bible later calls Jesus "the man Christ Jesus" (1 Timothy 2:5, KJV), and says He can be "touched with the feeling of our infirmities" (Hebrews 4:15, KJV): He feels our weakness.

Read it yourself: Job 9:8; Job 9:11; Job 9:32-33; Mark 6:45-51; 1 Timothy 2:5; Hebrews 4:15.
