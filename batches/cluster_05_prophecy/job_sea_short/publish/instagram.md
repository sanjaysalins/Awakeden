## CAPTION
Long ago, a man named Job said God alone walks on the sea. Then Jesus did it.

Job 9:8 says God alone "treadeth upon the waves of the sea" (walks on the waves). In Mark 6:48 Jesus comes to His followers "walking upon the sea." The Bible does not say why He "would have passed by them." Job did not name Jesus. He described what only God does.

Read it: Job 9:8; Job 9:11; Job 9:32-33; Mark 6:45-51; 1 Timothy 2:5; Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15 (KJV).
