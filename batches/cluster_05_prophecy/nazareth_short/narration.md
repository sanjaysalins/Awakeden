NARRATOR: Jesus stopped reading in the middle of a sentence.
NARRATOR: In Nazareth, His home town, He read God's promise of someone sent with good news for the poor, and a time of God's welcome.
NARRATOR: The prophet Isaiah's sentence goes on:
ISAIAH: and the day of vengeance of our God.
NARRATOR: He left the day of vengeance unread, and sat down. (pause)
JESUS: This day is this scripture fulfilled.
NARRATOR: It was about Him. They would want miracles, and say:
TOWNSMAN: Physician, heal thyself.
NARRATOR: He reminded them God once sent help to foreigners, not to their own. Furious, they took Him to a cliff to throw Him down. He walked right through them. (pause)
NARRATOR: Later, at the cross, the same dare:
LEADER: He saved others; let him save himself.
NARRATOR: This time, He stayed. He died, and rose again. (pause)
NARRATOR: He stopped reading at the welcome. It is still open, for you, today.
