NARRATOR: One warning in the Bible ends with "healed." In Mark, when Jesus gives it, it ends "forgiven." Can one man give both?
NARRATOR: Through Isaiah, God warns: people will hear, but not understand, or else they would turn back, and
GOD: be healed.
NARRATOR: Jesus repeats it. What they would miss:
JESUS: their sins should be forgiven them.
NARRATOR: In Matthew, Jesus says they closed their own eyes. (pause)
NARRATOR: A man who cannot walk is brought. Jesus first says:
JESUS: Son, thy sins be forgiven thee.
NARRATOR: Some sit thinking: only God can forgive sins. So Jesus heals him,
JESUS: that ye may know that the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins.
NARRATOR: He means Himself. The man stands up and walks out. Forgiven, and healed. Jesus has power to give both.
NARRATOR: Turn back to God, and trust Jesus. He forgives your sins, and heals your heart.
