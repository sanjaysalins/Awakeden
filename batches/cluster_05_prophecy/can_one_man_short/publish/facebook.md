## DESCRIPTION
Bible story: Jesus heals the paralyzed man (Mark 2:1-12). But what did He give him first?

In Isaiah 6, God warns that people will hear and not understand, "lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and convert, and be healed." (Isaiah 6:10, KJV)

When Jesus gives that warning in Mark 4:12, it ends differently: "and their sins should be forgiven them." (Mark 4:12, KJV) In Mark, forgiveness is what outsiders would miss. Matthew 13:15 ends "and I should heal them," and says "their eyes they have closed." The Bible does not say why the wording differs.

In Mark 2, a man who cannot walk is lowered through a roof. Jesus first says: "Son, thy sins be forgiven thee." (Mark 2:5, KJV) Some who sit there think only God can forgive sins. So Jesus heals the man, "that ye may know that the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins" (Mark 2:10, KJV), and the man stands up and walks out.

Forgiven, and healed. In the synagogue Jesus read that He was sent "to heal the brokenhearted" (Luke 4:18, KJV). The film ends with an open invitation: turn back to God, and trust Jesus.

Read it yourself: Isaiah 6:9-10; Mark 4:11-12; Matthew 13:13-15; Mark 2:1-12; Luke 4:18.
