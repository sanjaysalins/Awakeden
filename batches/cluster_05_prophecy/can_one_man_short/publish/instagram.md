Bible story: Jesus heals the paralyzed man (Mark 2:1-12). But what did He give him first?

God's warning in Isaiah 6:10 ends "and be healed." When Jesus gives that warning in Mark 4:12, it ends "and their sins should be forgiven them." The Bible does not say why the words differ.

Read it: Isaiah 6:9-10; Mark 4:11-12; Matthew 13:13-15; Mark 2:1-12; Luke 4:18.
