**Upload kit review: Why Was Jesus Born in Bethlehem? (Micah 5:2)**

**What's fine**
- **The quotes are exact KJV.** Micah 5:2 and John 6:38 match word for word.
- **The doctrine is sound.** "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us" points to grace, with no fear or works framing.
- **The title is honest.** It's a real search phrase and the film answers it (the prophecy plus the tax order in Luke 2).

**Problems**

1. **The kit claims more than it has done.** The header says "RED-TEAMED · Gates ALL PASS ✅", but the red-team's own verdict is **REVISE**, and its first fix was never applied. Every platform still ends with "Scripture quoted from the King James Version (KJV), public domain." The KJV is under Crown letters patent in the UK. Change the footer to "Scripture quotations: King James Version (KJV)." in every block, then re-run the gates.

2. **The first line of the YouTube and Facebook descriptions is wrong as written.** "Bethlehem was named 700 years before Jesus" reads as if the town got its name then. It already had that name in Genesis 35:19, about a thousand years earlier. It also drops the "about" that the body text uses. Fix: "Micah named Bethlehem about 700 years before Jesus."

3. **"The experts knew. Who went to see the King?" hints at something the Bible doesn't say.** Read together, the two lines suggest the chief priests and scribes didn't go. Matthew 2 is silent on that. The red-team let it pass because the film "pays it off honestly", so check the film's last beat really does. If it's only a hint, soften it, e.g. "The experts knew where. Strangers came." "Bible experts" is fine as plain English for "chief priests and scribes".

4. **There's a long-form section for a short video.** The kit says "Format: short" and points to `bethlehem_final.mp4` in the short's `visual` folder. There's no 16:9 cut, so the "YouTube (Long-form 16:9)" block shouldn't be there. Delete it or mark it N/A.

5. **The video path may point to the wrong file.** Memory says the locked film is `film/BETHLEHEM_FINAL_v4.mp4`. The kit points to `F:\slk\PycharmProjects\JesusInTheBible\batches\cluster_05_prophecy\bethlehem_short\visual\bethlehem_final.mp4`, the only mp4 in that folder. Confirm that file is the locked v4 before posting.

6. **TikTok and Instagram have no line about Jesus.** Both end on "Follow Awakeden. ✝", which is the brand line, not a call to Jesus. Add "Before anyone went to Him, He came to us." to both. It's the best line in the kit and the reels are missing it.

7. **The tags are padded.**
   - "king herod and the wise men" and "the wise men and herod" say the same thing. Drop one.
   - "1 corinthians 15 3-4" and "who was micah in the bible" don't fit what the film is about. 1 Corinthians 15:3-4 is only touched on, and the film doesn't explain who Micah was. Cut both.

8. **Small things**

   - The Instagram hashtags #WiseMen and #ChristmasStory are broad. If you keep them, put the post up near Advent.
