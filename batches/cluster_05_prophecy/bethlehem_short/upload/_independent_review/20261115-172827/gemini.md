You cannot claim a PASS on gates when a mandatory legal/copyright fix was ignored.

**2. SEO Honesty (Tag Stuffing)**
In the YouTube Short tags, you included `"who was micah in the bible"` and `"1 corinthians 15 3-4"`. A video answering "Why Was Jesus Born in Bethlehem?" is not a biographical video about the prophet Micah, nor is it an exposition on Paul's letter to the Corinthians. Including these simply because the verses are briefly cited in the description is tag-stuffing. It dilutes the core SEO focus (nativity and prophecy) and promises content the video doesn't actually deliver.

On TikTok and Instagram, the first line of the caption is exactly the title: `"Why was Jesus born in Bethlehem?"`. While this matches the search phrase, repeating the exact title/on-screen text in the first line of the caption wastes the crucial first 3 seconds of reading time. The caption should hook the viewer with the *next* thought or the stakes, such as starting immediately with: "A prophet named this small town 700 years early..."
