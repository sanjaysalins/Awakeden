Why Was Jesus Born in Bethlehem?
DESCRIPTION
copy
Why was Jesus born in Bethlehem? About 700 years earlier, Micah pointed to this small town (Micah 5:2). The Bible experts knew. Did they go?
Mary and Joseph reached Bethlehem because of a tax order. Wise men came to Jerusalem asking where the King was, and Herod asked the Bible experts. The Bible does not say whether the experts went. The wise men did, and found the child.
"But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting." (Micah 5:2, KJV)
Jesus said: "For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me." (John 6:38, KJV)
"Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures" (1 Corinthians 15:3-4, KJV)
Read it yourself: Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1-11; Luke 2:1-7; John 6:38; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4.
Before anyone went to Him, He came to us.
