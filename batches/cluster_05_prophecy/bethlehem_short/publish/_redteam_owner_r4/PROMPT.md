Why Was Jesus Born in Bethlehem?

## DESCRIPTION
Why was Jesus born in Bethlehem? The Bible experts knew where He would be born. Did they go? Micah pointed to it 700 years earlier (Micah 5:2).

Wise men came to Jerusalem asking where the King was, and Herod asked the Bible experts. The Bible does not say whether the experts went. The wise men went, and so did the shepherds (Luke 2:15-16).

"But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting." (Micah 5:2, KJV)

Micah adds "from of old, from everlasting." With Jesus' own words, "I came down from heaven" (John 6:38), Christians read it this way: Bethlehem was His birthplace, not His beginning.

Jesus said: "For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me." (John 6:38, KJV)
