4. **TikTok "Who can view" | Posting as Private now and switching to Public later is a trap.** TikTok gives a video its first push when it is posted, so a private-then-public video can lose that push. → Use "Save draft", or on desktop schedule it within TikTok's scheduling window (about 10 days). 5. **Facebook captions | Uploading the SRT may show captions automatically**, for example for viewers watching on mute. Those captions would sit on top of the burned-in words. → Skip the SRT on Facebook, or confirm captions are not on by default.

6. **`captions.srt` | Some cues flash too fast and some sentences are split badly.**

   - Cues 1 and 6 (43 and 48 characters) go past the usual 42-character line limit.
   - Fix: merge cue 1+2 into one cue (0.000–2.480), split over 2 lines: "The Bible experts knew / where the King would be born." Merge cue 8+9 the same way (14.980–17.720).
   - Mark the quoted lines so they do not read as the narrator: `God promised a King from there: "ruler in Israel."` / `It goes on: "from everlasting."` / `Scribe: "In Bethlehem of Judaea."`
