## TITLE
Why Was Jesus Born in Bethlehem?

## DESCRIPTION
Bible prophecy (Micah 5:2): a prophet pointed to Bethlehem 700 years before Jesus. The experts knew. Who went to see the King?

About 700 years before Jesus, the prophet Micah pointed to a small town. Mary and Joseph reached it because of a tax order. Wise men came to Jerusalem asking where the King was, and Herod asked the Bible experts.

"But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting." (Micah 5:2, KJV)

"For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me." (John 6:38, KJV)
