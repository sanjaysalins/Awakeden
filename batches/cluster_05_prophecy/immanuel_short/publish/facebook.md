## TITLE
Why Is Jesus Called Immanuel?

## DESCRIPTION
Why is Jesus called Immanuel? In the Bible, a frightened king told God no. God answered anyway, with a sign (Isaiah 7:14).

King Ahaz, from King David's family, was afraid of war. God offered him any sign. He refused, and begged a foreign king to save him (2 Kings 16:7).

God gave the sign anyway:
"Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel." (Isaiah 7:14)

About 700 years later it came true in Jesus. Matthew says Emmanuel means "God with us" (Matthew 1:23).
