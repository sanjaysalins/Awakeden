## TITLE
Why Is Jesus Called Immanuel?

## DESCRIPTION
Why is Jesus called Immanuel? In the Bible, a frightened king told God no. God answered anyway, with a sign (Isaiah 7:14).

About 700 years before Jesus, King Ahaz of Judah, from King David's family, was afraid of war (Isaiah 7:1-2). God said "fear not" and offered him any sign. Ahaz refused. In that same war he begged a foreign king: "I am thy servant and thy son: come up, and save me" (2 Kings 16:7).

"Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel." (Isaiah 7:14, KJV)

About 700 years later it came true. An angel told Joseph, another son of David, to fear not, and the child was named JESUS, "for he shall save his people from their sins" (Matthew 1:21). Matthew says the name Emmanuel means "God with us" (Matthew 1:23).

Read it yourself: Isaiah 7:1-14; 2 Kings 16:5-8; Matthew 1:18-25; Matthew 28:20; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4.
