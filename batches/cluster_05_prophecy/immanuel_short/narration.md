NARRATOR: A frightened king told God no. God answered anyway.
NARRATOR: King Ahaz, from King David's family, was afraid of war. God said, "fear not," and offered any sign, as proof. Ahaz refused. In that same war, he begged a foreign king: "save me." God chose the sign:
PROPHET: Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.
NARRATOR: Seven hundred years later, it came true. Mary, a virgin, was pregnant by God's Holy Spirit.
NARRATOR: An angel told Joseph:
ANGEL: Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife:
ANGEL: thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.
NARRATOR: Joseph obeyed. Ahaz begged a king to save him. This child saves. Immanuel means: God with us.
NARRATOR: With us at the cross, where He died for our sins. On the third day, He rose from the dead. He promised:
JESUS: I am with you alway.
NARRATOR: That was God's answer.
