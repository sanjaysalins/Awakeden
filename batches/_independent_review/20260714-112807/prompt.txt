## Known risks & mitigations

- **Kling QC lottery** (~1 in 3 first rolls fails: face-morph, wound-bleed, disobedient
  motion) → filmstrip QC EVERY clip by eye before use; re-roll margin in budget; rejects
  parked in `_fx_pilot/_rejected/`-style folders, never deleted.
- **Kling disobeys "frozen"** (walked the tiny dawn figures) → acceptable ONLY at extreme
  wide; a moving figure on a CU/medium = reject. QC rubric includes this.
- **Double-lighting** — beats carried by a living-light clip must NOT also carry `fx.rays`
  (gold master promotion dropped them); temp grade stays.
- **Shipped-piece regression** — every current final backed up pre-rebuild; publish packs
  keep the same filename → publish stays GREEN; the classic/smooth motion switch is
  per-piece spec, defaulting classic for anything untouched.
- **Grid crops chopping heads** — panel_fit fit-gate + anchors; hand-tune anchors on faces
  (the Women piece needed several rounds); fit-gate warnings reviewed per piece.
- **Doctrine** — no still/clip content changes except light/atmosphere; any NEW de-dup
  still (Wave D) goes through the full bible-check fact-card + vision-audit + stills-gate
  path like any production still.
