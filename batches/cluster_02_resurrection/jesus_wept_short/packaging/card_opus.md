# Packaging Card — "Jesus Wept" (seat: opus, blind)

## The 4 questions
- **Who is it for:** anyone who has lost someone and quietly blamed God for not stopping it, believer or not.
- **Take-away:** Jesus did not argue with grief. He wept with it. And He is alive to hear you say it.
- **Curiosity gap:** You said it too. Two sisters said the exact same sentence to Jesus. What did He do?
- **Global audience:** Yes. Short plain words, one family, one grave, one feeling everyone knows. Only KJV quotes are old English, and each is short.

## Scripture honest
- **Quoted (KJV, exact):** John 11:21, 11:28, 11:32, 11:34, 11:35, 11:36.
- **Told, not quoted:** 11:17 (four days in the grave), 11:20 (Mary stayed in the house), 11:43-44 (Lazarus came out alive), 11:53 (the leaders planned to kill Him).
- **Beyond John 11, told not quoted:** the cross, the grave, and the rising (the Gospel end, no verse cited).
- **Public text check:** 11:36 in KJV says "Then said the Jews". Our film says "mourners". Keep "mourners" / "the leaders" in all public text. Never "the Jews".

## Weak spots (honest)
- **Opening assumes loss.** "You said it to God, alone" fits a grieving viewer. A viewer who has not lost anyone may feel it is not for them. The hook card helps.
- **Same line twice.** Mary repeats Martha word for word. That is the point (11:21 = 11:32), but a fast scroller may think it is an edit mistake. A small on-screen cue ("the same words") would help.
- **"Jesus wept" needs space.** The pause before it carries the whole film. If the cut rushes it, the payoff is lost.
- **Ending is fast.** Raising, plot, cross, grave, rising: all in about 15 seconds. A newcomer may not catch why the plot matters.
- **"Say it to Him."** Must read as an invitation to pray, not a demand. Keep the voice soft.
- **Search guesses are unverified.** Check real autocomplete before locking the title.
- **69 s is long for a Short.** The middle (Martha whispering, Mary coming) is where people may swipe away.
- **Odds (guess, wide error bars):** strong share potential with grieving viewers; average reach outside that. Real seed viewers will tell more than any panel.
