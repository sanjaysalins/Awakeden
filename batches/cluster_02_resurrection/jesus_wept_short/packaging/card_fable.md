## Scripture honest
- **Quoted, KJV exact:** John 11:21 (Martha), 11:28 (Martha's whisper), 11:32 (Mary — same words as 11:21), 11:34 ("Where have ye laid him?" / "Lord, come and see."), 11:35 ("Jesus wept."), 11:36 ("Behold how he loved him!").
- **Told, not quoted:** 11:17 (four days in the grave), 11:20 (Mary sat in the house), 11:43-44 (Lazarus came out alive), 11:53 (the plot to kill Him).
- **Not from John 11 at all:** the cross, the burial and the rising of Jesus. That is the wider gospel, added by the narrator. Do not list it as John 11.
- **Partial quotes:** 11:28, 11:32, 11:34 and 11:36 are the spoken parts only, not the whole verse. Say "quoted from", not "the full verse".
- **Speaker label:** in the KJV the people who speak 11:36 are named as a group. The film calls them "mourners". Keep that word in all public text. Use "the leaders" for 11:53.

## Weak spots, honestly
1. **Title B over-promises.** The film never says in the narrator's voice *why* He wept. The only answer is the mourners' line, "Behold how he loved him!" A viewer who searched "why did Jesus weep" may feel half-answered.
2. **"Shortest verse" needs the words "King James".** It is true in English KJV. In the Greek, another verse is shorter. Never write "shortest verse in the Bible" as a flat fact in the description; a tag is fine as a search term.
3. **The opening assumes the viewer.** "You said it to God, alone" will miss anyone who has never prayed or never lost someone. They may swipe in the first 2 seconds.
4. **Title A has no search words.** No "Lazarus", no "why". It lives on the feed only.
5. **"So the leaders planned to kill Him" is very fast.** It skips the council in between. A newcomer may not see why giving life leads to a plot.
6. **"Say it to Him" — "it" is far from its sentence.** The sentence was said about 60 seconds earlier. Some viewers will not link "it" back to "If You had been here."
7. **Bible readers know the answer.** "Jesus wept" is famous. For them the gap is not *what* He did but the two matching sentences. The title has to sell that, not the weeping.
8. **The teaser is soft.** This film already shows Lazarus coming out, so a Lazarus follow-up may feel like a repeat.
9. **Grief content can be heavy.** No fear or pressure in it, but the send-line must stay gentle. Do not sharpen it.
