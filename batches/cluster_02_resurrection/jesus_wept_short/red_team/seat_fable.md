1. **tiktok.md + instagram.md — verse range is too wide.**
   The caption quotes only v21/32 and points at v35. "21-36" also claims v22-27 ("I am the resurrection"), which the film does not cover.
   Old: `John 11:21-36.`
   New: `John 11:21, 32, 35.`

2. **thumb_9x16.jpg — text sits in the app's cover zone.**
   "WEPT." and "JOHN 11:35" are in the bottom ~20% (ref is at the very bottom edge). Shorts / TikTok / Reels put the caption and buttons there, and the profile grid crops the bottom off.
   Old: text block from about y=1340 to y=1890.
   New: move the whole block up about 250 px, so "JOHN 11:35" ends near y=1620. Keep it below the beard.

3. **thumb_1x1.jpg (and thumb_16x9.jpg) — "JOHN 11:35" too small for a phone.**
   On 1x1 it is tiny and touches the bottom edge (rounded corners may clip it).
   Old: ref about 30 px tall at y≈1050 (1x1); small ref under "WEPT." (16x9).
   New: make the ref about 1.6x bigger on both; on 1x1 lift it at least 40 px off the bottom edge.

4. **facebook.md — "Say it to Him" has lost its meaning here.**
   The line before it is "Jesus wept", so "it" is unclear. Two commands back to back ("Say it to Him. Send this...") also read as push, not grace.
   Old: `The Jesus who wept is alive. Say it to Him. Send this to someone who is missing somebody today.`
   New: `Have you ever said, "If You had been here"? The Jesus who wept is alive. You can say it to Him. Send this to someone who is missing somebody today.`

5. **youtube_short.md — the first 150 characters waste the hook.**
   It opens "John 11:" (already in the title) and the ref list sits in the middle of the paragraph. The viewer's own question is buried.
   Old (opening): `John 11: two sisters said the same sentence to Jesus: "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died." He did not argue. He asked where they had laid him, and then John 11:35: "Jesus wept."`
   New (opening): `Have you ever said to God, "If You had been here"? Two sisters said it to Jesus: "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died." He did not argue. "Jesus wept." (John 11:35)`
   Then a blank line, then: `The Jesus who wept is alive. Say it to Him.`
   Then move the "Quoted word for word... / Told in plain words... / shortest verse..." lines to their own block lower down.
