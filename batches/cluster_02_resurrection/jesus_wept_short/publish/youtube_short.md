## TITLE
Jesus Wept: The Sentence Two Sisters Said to Him (John 11)

## DESCRIPTION
Have you ever said to God, "If You had been here"? In John 11, two sisters said that same sentence to Jesus: "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died." When Mary said it, He did not argue. He asked where they had laid him, and then John 11:35: "Jesus wept." The Jesus who wept is alive. You can say it to Him.

Quoted word for word from the KJV: John 11:21, 28, 32, 34, 35, 36. Told in plain words: John 11:11, 11:17, 11:20, 11:43-44, 11:53; the cross, grave and rising: 1 Corinthians 15:3-4. "Jesus wept" is the shortest verse in the King James Bible.
