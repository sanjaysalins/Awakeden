# Independent review — codex (OK, 30s)

Findings:

- **Kit metadata / Verification:** `Anchor:` is blank, and `UK-G2 KJV-strict — could not resolve KJV text for` is an actual blocker. This kit claims it is “pre-checked” while admitting the KJV gate failed.

- **YouTube Short + Facebook description:** `"And their words seemed... believed them not... Why seek ye..." (Luke 24:11, 5-6)` is risky Scripture presentation. The words are KJV, but the quote reverses chapter order and stitches non-contiguous verses into one quoted sentence. That is not clean “KJV-strict” metadata.

- **YouTube Long title:** `The First Witnesses of the Risen Christ Were Women No Court Would Believe` overstates the historical claim. “No court would believe” is stronger than the description’s softened `almost no weight`, and it implies a universal legal rule.

- **Title option 3 / angle:** `a woman's word carried no legal weight` is too absolute. The copy elsewhere says `almost no weight`; this line drops the qualifier and becomes historically overclaimed.

- **TikTok caption:** `women no court would believe` has the same overclaim problem, and it appears in the first line, making the hook more sensational than the evidence supports.

- **YouTube Short / Facebook:** `the risen Christ is seeking you` is a strong CTA-to-Jesus, but paired with `Stop seeking the living among the dead` it risks sounding like the viewer is personally mis-seeking Christ in a way the text does not directly say. Not fatal, but tighten it.

- **Tags:** YouTube Short includes `Mary Magdalene, Joanna` though those names are not in the short caption. Relevant to Luke 24, but slightly SEO-forward if the video itself does not name them.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the failed/blank KJV verification and stop stitching Luke 24:11 with Luke 24:5-6 as one quoted line.
2. Replace “No Court Would Believe” / “no court would believe” / “no legal weight” with the softer, defensible “carried little weight” framing.
3. Remove or justify tags/names not actually present in the short video metadata/content.
