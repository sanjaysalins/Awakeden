## TITLE
Even the Apostles Called the Resurrection an Idle Tale

## DESCRIPTION
Luke 24:5-6: "Why seek ye the living among the dead? He is not here, but is risen." The first report that Jesus had risen was dismissed as nonsense by His own apostles. It came from women, whose word carried almost no weight in that world's courts. No one inventing a resurrection would have opened it there.

The idle tale was true, and it went first to the witnesses the world would not hear. Stop seeking the living among the dead. The risen Christ is seeking you.
