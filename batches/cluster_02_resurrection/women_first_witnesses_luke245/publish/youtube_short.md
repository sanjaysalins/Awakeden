## TITLE
Even the Apostles Called the Resurrection an Idle Tale

## DESCRIPTION
Luke 24:5-6: "Why seek ye the living among the dead? He is not here, but is risen." The first report of the risen Christ was dismissed as an idle tale, and His own apostles dismissed it. It came from women, whose word counted for little in many courts of that day. Someone inventing a resurrection story would hardly start there.

The idle tale was true, and it went first to the witnesses the world would not hear. He still sends His word to the overlooked. Stop seeking the living among the dead. The risen Christ is seeking you.
