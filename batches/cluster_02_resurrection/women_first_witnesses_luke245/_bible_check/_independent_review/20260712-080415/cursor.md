### CITATION TRUTH

**1. Luke 8:2 is still mis-paired with the “own means” claim**

> **[SPECIFIED]** They ministered to Him of their OWN MEANS: "Mary called Magdalene, out of whom went seven devils" **(Luke 8:2)**; "…which ministered unto him of their substance" (Luke 8:3).

Luke 8:2 states deliverance from seven devils. It does **not** state ministry, substance, or “own means.” Only Luke 8:3 carries that. Keeping 8:2 as co-citation under this claim is citation misapplication — the same defect grok/codex flagged; v2 split Galilee out but did not finish the 8:2/8:3 split.

**2. Luke 24:1 quote is truncated; a stated detail is dropped**

Artifact quote ends at “which they had prepared.” KJV continues: **“and certain others with them.”** That is explicit Luke text, not harmonization. The sheet never guards it — a still could wrongly show only the three named women from 24:10 with no “others.”

**3. Luke 23:55 image directive slightly over-reads the verb**

> Image: women **looking on as the body is laid**

Luke 23:55: they “**beheld** the sepulchre, and **how** his body was laid.” That is observation of the result; it does not require simultaneous depiction of the laying-in act. Minor, but it is still claim > citation.

**4. Apostles’ reaction: “turning away”**

> Image: …men … who react with disbelief **/ turning away**

Luke 24:11: “idle tales” and “believed them not.” No turning away, no facial direction. Visual licence sitting under a **[SPECIFIED]** image line.

### COVERAGE GAPS (checkable Luke facts a still could get wrong)

**9. Tomb sequence (24:2–5) — not guarded**

Luke order: stone found away → **entered in** → no body → **much perplexed** (24:4) → **then** two men stood by them → afraid, bowed (24:5). Sheet has the pieces scattered but no sequence fact. Painter can still do Matthew/Mark bleed (angels on/near stone before entry, women only outside).

**10. Luke 24:4 “much perplexed thereabout” — missing [SPECIFIED]**

Explicit pre-angel emotional state; easy to paint wrong (immediate awe instead of confusion at empty tomb).

**11. Luke 24:8 “they remembered his words” — under-specified**

Mentioned only as subtext under angels’ words. If any still depicts post-speech beat, this is the specified response — recognition, not scrolls (correctly ruled out).

**12. Luke 23:56 — missing entirely**

Women “prepared spices and ointments” and “**rested the sabbath day**” before 24:1. Guards against Sabbath-at-tomb or continuous overnight timeline errors. 24:1’s “which they had prepared” alludes to it but never cites 23:56.

**13. Luke 23:53 — missing but stronger than inferred “hewn”**

“Sepulchre **hewn in stone**, wherein **never man before was laid**” is explicit Luke tomb specification, better sourced than the undocumented “hewn resting place” in the 24:3 line.

**14. Luke 23:49 “all his acquaintance” also stood afar off**

If a crucifixion still is painted women-only at distance, fine; if men are shown at the foot of the cross, that contradicts 23:49’s wider “afar off” group. No guard either way.
