### CITATION TRUTH

**1. Luke 8:2 does not support “ministered … of their OWN MEANS”**

Claim: *“They ministered to Him of their OWN MEANS”*  
Quoted under it: *“Mary called Magdalene, out of whom went seven devils”* (Luke 8:2).

Luke 8:2 states healing / seven devils. It says nothing about substance, means, or support. Only Luke 8:3 carries *“which ministered unto him of their substance.”*  
v2 claimed citations were split so each verse carries only what it states — this bullet still fails that rule.

**2. “long-standing supporters” is not in the cited text**

Image line: *“women as long-standing supporters of Jesus.”*  
8:2–3 = healed women who ministered of substance; 23:49/55 = followed from Galilee. Neither says “long-standing.” That is narrative colour dressed as specified image law.

**3. Luke 24:1 quote is truncated; a stated group detail is dropped**

Sheet quote ends at *“bringing the spices which they had prepared.”*  
Full KJV: *“…bringing the spices which they had prepared, **and certain others with them**.”*  
That is checkable group-size fact at the dawn beat. Omitting it undercuts “never a lone figure” and misrepresents the verse as fully quoted.

**4. “rock-cut” / “hewn” under dawn facts without Luke 23:53**

- Dawn SPECIFIED (citing only 24:1): *“toward a rock-cut tomb.”* 24:1 says *sepulchre*, not rock-cut.
- Empty-tomb SPECIFIED (24:3): *“empty **hewn** resting place.”* 24:3 does not say hewn.

Luke does say hewn elsewhere: Joseph *“laid it in a sepulchre that was hewn in stone”* (Luke 23:53) — not cited. So either cite 23:53 as SPECIFIED for this same sepulchre, or demote rock-cut/hewn out of the 24:1/24:3 image MUST lines. As written, SPECIFIED image rules outrun their citations.

**5. Angel block: 24:23 is secondhand naming, not the tomb narrator’s label**

24:4 = *“two men … in shining garments.”*  
24:23 = Emmaus report: *“a vision of angels.”*  
Using 24:23 to label the figures is fair as *later naming*, but the sheet should not treat 24:23 as a same-scene visual specification equal to 24:4. Men + shining garments is the tomb-scene specified; “angels” is the disciples’ later word.

### BUCKET / INVENTED-FACT ISSUES

**6. SPECIFIED ministry bullet mixes two different claims**

One bucket item joins (a) seven devils and (b) substance ministry. Only (b) matches the heading. Seven devils is true of Mary Magdalene, but it is not a visual “own means” fact and does not belong as support for that claim.

**7. Constrained risen-Christ negatives over-reach the text**

*“no gore, no crown of thorns, no death pallor”* is not stated by 24:39–40.  
Hands/feet-if-shown is rightly Luke-scoped. The rest is pious art direction. Fine as free/taste; weak as constrained “must not contradict” law — Scripture does not use those bans.

**8. Tomb typology still smuggles archaeology as near-mandatory form**

Constrained list: crawl-height opening, disc/boulder in a groove, waist-low arcosolium, tool-marked walls. Correctly labeled secondary — but the sheet already forbids many forms while prescribing a full archaeological kit. Risk: stills get graded as if groove-disc were almost specified. Keep as “may / period-plausible,” not a checklist the paint must hit.

**9. Susanna / “many others” vs tomb named list**

8:3 names Susanna; 24:10 names Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary of James, and other women — not Susanna. Overlap of some names ≠ identity of the whole support group with the tomb party. Do not force Susanna (or a fixed cast from 8:3) into dawn stills as specified.

### COVERAGE GAPS (paintings can get these wrong; sheet does not guard them)

**12. Sequence at the tomb (Luke 24:3–4) is missing as specified narrative order**

KJV order: *entered in* → *found not the body* → *much perplexed thereabout* → *two men stood by them.*  
Sheet has empty body + two standing men as separate cards, not the order. Easy stills error: angels outside before entry, or men present while body is still imagined present.

**13. Burial facts that guard a “body laid” still (if that beat is painted)**

Sheet requires women *looking on as the body is laid* (from 23:55) but does not pin:
- Joseph lays the body (23:50–53), not the women as buriers  
- wrapped in linen (23:53)  
- sepulchre hewn in stone, wherein never man before was laid (23:53)  
Without these, a burial still can invent women handling the corpse, a used family coffin-tomb, etc., while still “passing” 23:55 alone.

**14. Spices prepared after burial / sabbath rest (23:56)**

24:1 says spices *they had prepared*; 23:56 is the prepare + rest sabbath step. Minor, but blocks “spices already in hand at the deposition” mistakes.

**15. “Entered in” as visual constraint**

24:3 specifies entry. Empty chamber is noted; “women go in / are inside when perplexed and when the men stand by them” is not. Stills can freeze everyone permanently outside with stone away and still look “Luke-ish.”
