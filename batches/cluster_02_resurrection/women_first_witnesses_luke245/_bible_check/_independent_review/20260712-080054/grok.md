## Critical — citation does not support the claim

### 1. Luke 8:2 used for the wrong claim
**Claim:** “They followed Jesus from GALILEE and ministered to Him of their OWN MEANS”  
**Cited:** Luke 8:2 *“Mary called Magdalene, out of whom went seven devils”*

That verse states **only** Magdala’s deliverance. It does **not** state Galilee following, ministry, or substance.

- Galilee following is Luke 23:49 / 23:55 (*“followed him from Galilee”*).
- Own means is Luke 8:3 (*“ministered unto him of their substance”*).

**Finding:** Luke 8:2 is a misapplied citation under a multi-part specified fact. Split the claims and cite each verse only for what it says.

### 2. Dress rules smuggled into [SPECIFIED]
Under the same bullet:  
*“Image: women as long-standing supporters/disciples, 1st-century Judean dress with mantles drawn over the head.”*

Luke 8:2–3 and Luke 24 name and ministry facts. They do **not** specify:
- mantles over the head  
- “Judean dress” cut  
- “disciples” as a title  

**Finding:** Visual costume is licence/constrained, not specified. This is licence dressed as “image MUST match.”

### 3. “Tomb being closed” is not in Luke
**Claim/image:** *“women observing the burial / the tomb being closed”* under Luke 23:55.

Luke 23:55: they *“beheld the sepulchre, and how his body was laid.”*  
That is location + manner of laying. It does **not** say they watched a stone rolled, a door shut, or the tomb sealed. Stone closure is explicit in Matthew/Mark, not Luke’s women’s notice.

**Finding:** “How laid” ≠ “tomb closed.” Over-reach under [SPECIFIED].

### 4. Stone bullet packs archaeology into [SPECIFIED]
Luke 24:2: stone *rolled away* — solid.

Image extras under the same [SPECIFIED] line:
- *“great stone”* — Luke does not say “great” (Matthew does)
- *“LOW opening”* — not in the verse
- *“never small”* — not biblical

**Finding:** Rolled-away stone = specified. Size/height of opening = constrained or free. Wrong bucket.

### 5. Apostles scene invents “indoor”
**Claim/image:** *“indoor 1st-century setting”* under Luke 24:9, 24:11.

Luke: they told the eleven (and all the rest); words seemed idle tales. **No place.** Indoor is not specified.

Also incomplete: Luke 24:9 is *“unto the eleven, **and to all the rest**.”* Sheet collapses that to “the eleven” / “apostles only.”

## Christ-lens / passage focus

### 6. Landing leans on Luke 19:10, not Luke 24’s own Christ claim
Sheet: landing = living Christ seeking the overlooked, citing Luke 19:10 (Zacchaeus).

Luke 24’s own spine is already on the sheet as text:
- *“He is not here, but is risen”*
- *“remember… Son of man… crucified… third day rise again”* (24:5b–7)
- later: hands and feet (24:39–40)

**Finding:** Luke 19:10 is true Lukan theology but **not** this pericope’s stated climax. Using it as the binding landing image can pull stills off empty-tomb / “He is risen” / remembered passion prophecy. Hands-and-feet constraint is good; the seek-and-save frame is thematic graft, not Luke 24 fact.

### 7. “Alive and WHOLE” vs wounds
*“WHOLE — no gore… IF wounds are shown, hands and feet”* is mostly careful, but “WHOLE” can be read as unscarred and fight Luke 24:39–40. Tighten wording so “whole” ≠ “no wounds.”

## What is solid (not praise — just accuracy)

These are actually supported as written:

- Group of named women + others (24:10)  
- Afar at the cross (23:49) — Luke-scoped vs John’s foot-of-cross  
- Beheld sepulchre and how body was laid (23:55) — *without* “closed”

- Stone rolled away (24:2)

- Two men, shining garments, stood by them (24:4); not sitting-on-stone  
- Afraid; faces to the earth (24:5)  
- Report dismissed as idle tales (24:9–11)  
- Wounds = hands and feet if shown (24:39–40), not side  

Core Luke-scope policy (dawn / two men / hands-feet) is coherent.

## Coverage gaps (painters can still get Luke wrong)

Obvious checkable Luke facts **not** guarded:

1. **Sequence at the tomb (24:2–5):** find stone away → **enter** → no body → **perplexed** → then two men **stood by them**. Sheet can still produce “women only outside + angels on the stone” (Mark/Matthew bleed) or “angels before entry.”
2. **Luke 23:56:** prepared spices/ointments; **rested the sabbath**. Guards wrong “night raid” or continuous-action timelines.
3. **Luke 23:53** (tomb context): sepulchre **hewn in stone**, **never man before laid** — stronger specified base for empty bench than vague “hewn resting place.”
4. **24:9 “and to all the rest”** — not apostles-only.
5. **24:8** *“they remembered his words”* — the remember beat after the angel speech; only partially covered as “spoken content.”
6. **Who brings spices:** 24:1 “they” / 24:10 names — sheet is OK on group, but never ties spice-carriers explicitly to the named list (avoid lone Magdalene-only spice shot in report/dawn beats — partially covered under first bullet only).
