# Independent review — claude (OK, 109s)

I checked each quoted KJV string against the text and each bucket assignment. The citations are, on the whole, accurate and verbatim — this is careful work. But an adversarial pass turns up a few real over-reaches where licence/harmonization is dressed as Luke-specified fact, plus one mis-paired reference.

## Findings

**1. "Breaking dawn — NOT pitch-dark" is not what the cited Luke verse says (bucket over-reach).**
Under *Dawn at the tomb*, the SPECIFIED fact cites Luke 24:1 — "very early in the morning" — and then the Image line asserts "first light / breaking dawn — NOT the pitch-dark of John 20:1." The quoted KJV does **not** state daylight or first light. "Very early in the morning" is fully compatible with darkness; the dawn reading rests on Mark 16:2 ("at the rising of the sun") and the Greek, **neither of which is cited here**. So a positive "breaking dawn" *requirement* is licence/harmonization presented under a SPECIFIED banner. Fix: either cite Mark 16:2 to ground the dawn, or downgrade the light-level to CONSTRAINED/free ("Luke permits dawn and does not require darkness") rather than a specified Luke fact.

**2. "Followed Jesus from GALILEE" is pinned to Luke 8:2-3, which does not say it.**
The second SPECIFIED bullet claims they "followed Jesus from GALILEE and ministered to Him of their OWN MEANS," citing Luke 8:2-3. Luke 8:2-3 supports *ministered of their substance* but says nothing about "from Galilee" — that element comes from Luke 23:49 / 23:55 (both cited elsewhere in the sheet). Minor mis-pairing, but the reference under the claim doesn't carry the "from Galilee" half. Fix: add Luke 23:49/55 to this bullet, or drop "from Galilee" from the claim tied to 8:2-3.

**3. Cultural detail embedded inside a SPECIFIED fact.**
Same bullet's Image line: "1st-century Judean dress with mantles drawn over the head." Head-mantles are a CONSTRAINED/cultural assumption, not stated by any cited verse, yet they sit inside a [SPECIFIED] item. The bucket label technically covers only the biblical claim, but a painter reading this will treat "mantles over the head" as required. Fix: move dress/mantle guidance to the constrained tomb-typology line or explicitly tag it (constrained) inline.

**4. Christ-lens: the mandatory landing anchors on Luke 19:10, not on this passage's own climax.**
The risen-Christ bullet makes the landing "the living Christ seeking the overlooked" (Luke 19:10, "seek and save that which was lost"). That's a homiletical graft onto the narration's angle. This passage's *own* witness to Christ is the fulfilled resurrection prophecy — Luke 24:6-7, "He is not here, but is risen... the third day rise again" — which the sheet already quotes verbatim. Tying the mandatory landing to 19:10 rather than the risen-Christ-as-foretold climax is defensible but subtly shifts the Christ-lens off the text's spine. Not a citation error (it's constrained/free), but flag it so the landing doesn't drift from "He is risen, as He said."
