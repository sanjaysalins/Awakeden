## Independent adversarial review — narration

**Artifact:** Women as First Witnesses (Luke 24:5–6 + 24:11) · DRAFT v3

---

### KJV verbatim — mostly clean

**Quote 1** — *“And their words seemed to them as idle tales, and they believed them not.”*  
Exact Luke 24:11 (cache / passage). **PASS.**

**Quote 2** — *“Why seek ye the living among the dead? He is not here, but is risen: remember how he spake unto you when he was yet in Galilee”*  
Words match Luke 24:5b–6 to the stated stop. KJV 24:6 ends with a comma; no word is altered. **PASS** on wording.

No false “KJV” inside quote marks.

---

### Doctrinal soundness — core OK; edges overclaim

**Sound and text-true:**
- Women report first; apostles dismiss as idle tales (24:10–11).
- Mary Magdalene + Joanna named earlier and at the tomb (8:2–3; 24:10).
- Cross / burial / dawn return (23:49, 23:55, 24:1).
- Angel cites Jesus’ Galilee word about rising (24:6–7).
- Risen Christ seeking the lost (echo Luke 19:10) is orthodox.

**Problems (not full heresy — still wrong weight):**

1. **“They never abandoned Him.”**  
   Absolute claim the text will not carry. They prepare spices for a **corpse** (23:56; 24:1) and rest on the sabbath. Faithful nearness ≠ unbroken loyalty as if they never left hope or the body. Over-romanticizes the long-arc thread.

2. **“He had told them He would rise — and they had stayed close enough to hear it.”**  
   Status note says “remember” was re-anchored to **prophecy**, not proximity. The line still sells **proximity credential**: they earned the angel’s “remember” by staying close. Luke’s point is *Jesus said it*; “close enough to hear” is a soft invented hinge.

3. **“No one inventing a resurrection would open it there. Luke gains nothing by that verse — unless it is simply what happened.”**  
   Classic criterion-of-embarrassment apologetics, not exposition of the verse. Not false doctrine, but **contested historical inference** used as proof. “Almost no weight in court” is softened, still a broad cultural claim presented as fact. Fine for the series charter only if it stays support — here it still eats a full beat.

4. **“He still trusts the overlooked.”**  
   Pastoral slogan. Text shows first announcement to women the world dismissed — not a standing principle that God “trusts” a sociological class. Transferable to any “marginalized” short.

### Clarity on first hearing — strained by the forger beat

Zero-Bible listener can follow: dismissed → women → low standing → faithful presence → angel → risen → invited.

**Confusing / clever-but-meta:**
- *“No one inventing a resurrection would open it there.”*  
  Forces a counterfactual about forgers before the story finishes.
- *“Luke gains nothing by that verse — unless it is simply what happened.”*  
  Shifts from **what happened** to **authorial strategy**. Logic-trick apologetics, not gospel story. Costs clarity for one clever point.

**Mild muddle:** legal “court” register + “never left” loyalty + “overlooked” social frame + Luke 19:10 seeker close = several frames in ~60s.

### Grace-anchored conviction — mostly OK

No fear, gain/loss, or works-salvation push.  
*“You need no standing in any court to receive His word”* and *“already seeking you”* invite rather than pressure. **PASS** on this lens.

### Freshness = faithful depth — mixed

**Strong / text-deep:** idle-tale dismissal; named women across Luke; angel’s “remember… Galilee.” Surprising about **Luke**, not about truth.

**Stale / thin:** inventing-forger line is apologetics stock; “overlooked” is soft modern frame; landing grafts Luke 19:10 rather than closing the idle-tales / never-left spine.

---

### One thread spine — wobbles

Claimed spine: **witnesses who never left** + angel’s remember → Jesus’ word.

What the script actually runs:
1. Idle-tale / apostle dismissal  
2. Court-weight / inventing apologetic  
3. Long-arc never-left loyalty  
4. Prophecy-remember  
5. Overlooked / no standing / Christ seeks you  

Related, not one clean hook→middle→CTA line. The inventing beat and the grace-seek landing both pull away from “women who stayed / world would not hear.”

### Hook score: **7 / 10**

*“The first report that Jesus was risen was dismissed as nonsense — by His own apostles.”*

- Not a template open (no Did you know / Imagine / What if).  
- Text-first, good twist.  
- Still more **idea-surprise** than scroll-stop image; “apostles” is thin for zero-Bible ears in ~5s. Strong, not elite. **Under 8 → cannot PASS.**

### Landing score: **6 / 10**

*“Stop seeking the living among the dead — the risen Christ is already seeking you.”*

- First half uses **this** angel verb — good.  
- Second half is a **portable grace closer** (could end Zacchaeus, lost sheep, any “seek” piece). Rule: if the exact final sentence can end a different piece, cap hard.  
- Prefaced by stock-adjacent *“He still trusts the overlooked.”*  
- Luke 19:10 is an echo from another episode, not this piece’s earned image (idle tales / court / women the world would not hear / never left).  
**Under 8 → REVISE.**

| Line / phrase | Problem |
|---|---|
| *“They never abandoned Him.”* | Absolute overclaim vs spices, sabbath, corpse-expectancy |
| *“…stayed close enough to hear it.”* | Proximity credit after “remember = prophecy” fix |
| *“No one inventing… Luke gains nothing…”* | Meta apologetics; clarity cost; thread diluter |
| *“He still trusts the overlooked.”* | Soft, transferable; not the text’s claim |
| Final *“…already seeking you.”* | Semi-stock; not locked to idle-tales / first witnesses |

---

VERDICT: REVISE  
TOP FIXES:  
1. Rebuild the **final sentence** from *this* spine only (idle tales / world would not hear / first report / women who stayed) so it cannot close another short; keep grace, drop portable “already seeking you” as the whole close.  
2. Cut or rewrite **“They never abandoned Him”** and **“stayed close enough to hear it”** so claims match Luke (faithful nearness + Jesus’ word recalled — not absolute loyalty or proximity-as-credential).  
3. Demote or delete the **inventing / “Luke gains nothing”** beat so one thread (never-left witnesses → first news dismissed → word received without standing) runs clean for a first hearer.
