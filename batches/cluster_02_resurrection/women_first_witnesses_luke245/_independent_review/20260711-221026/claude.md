## Doctrine / accuracy
- The spine claim is genuinely sound and genuinely in the text: Luke 8:2-3 names Mary Magdalene and Joanna ministering "of their substance," Luke 23:49/23:55 has the Galilee women at the cross and the burial, Luke 24:10 names the same two at the tomb, and the angel's "remember how he spake unto you… in Galilee" really is addressed to women who were there to hear it. This is the fresh, faithful lever — no contrarian reading.
- **"No forger keeps that verse."** ([narrator], line 4) — the status note says "forger-line overclaim cut," but this IS still a categorical overclaim. A skeptic's stock counter is that embarrassing details can be included for verisimilitude. The defensible form is about cost, not impossibility: "A forger gains nothing by that verse" / "That is not the verse you write if you're selling a story." One word of softening buys you immunity.
- **"counted a woman's word as nearly worthless in court"** — "nearly worthless" is the defensible strength (Josephus, Ant. 4.8.15); acceptable. But note the piece leans on this historical claim twice (hook paragraph + "witnesses the world would not hear" + "the world's court"), so it carries a lot of weight for an extra-biblical claim. It holds, but it's at its ceiling.
- **"They never left"** vs. **"they were back"** — literal tension three sentences apart (Luke 23:56: they went away, prepared spices, rested the sabbath). The loyalty sense is obvious, but a pedantic commenter will quote Luke 23:56 at you. Cheap fix if desired: "They never abandoned Him."
- "first news" (not "first appearance") is correctly Luke-scoped — the earlier red-team fix landed properly.

## Clarity on first hearing
- **"supporting Him from their own substance"** — "substance" is KJV archaism *outside* the quote marks, in narrator voice. A zero-Bible listener hears "substance" and gets nothing. Say "from their own money" / "out of their own pockets" — the KJV word isn't protected here because it isn't quoted.
- "Remember — you can only remember what you stayed close enough to hear" — clever AND clear; this is the best line in the piece and it's earned by the verse.
- "Your standing in the world's court was never the question" — follows from the thread; fine.

## Grace-anchored conviction
Clean. No fear, no gain/loss, no works framing. The invitation is built from the angel's own words. The pivot to the listener ("Your standing in the world's court…") is grace, not pressure.
