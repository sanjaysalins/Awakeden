# Seat verdict: Luke 24:1-11 Short, X vs Y

## VERSION X

**NEWCOMER**
- **Who:** Jesus, some women, and his followers.
- **What:** He died. The women went to his grave at dawn. Angels said "he is risen." The men did not believe them.
- **Why it matters:** God gave the biggest news first to people nobody listened to.
- **Lines they would not follow:**
  - "idle tales" (old English, unclear)
  - "Son of man" (who is that?)
  - "Galilee" (where?)
  - "The promise had been kept" (it says the same thing as the line before, so it feels flat)

**BIBLE READER**
- **What they get:** The women are the same ones who watched him die (23:49) and saw where he was buried (23:55). That is a good link.
- **What is not in Luke 24:**
  - The "no weight in court" line comes from history outside the Bible. It is not in Luke 24. It is fair to say, but it is not text.
  - Luke says "two men in shining garments" (24:4). Calling them "angels" is still fine, because 24:23 calls it "a vision of angels."

**CURIOSITY: 6/10**
- The opening question is strong: why didn't his own men believe it?
- The answer is only "they were women." Nothing new is shown after that.

**ENDING: Yes**
- It lands on "the risen Christ is seeking you."
- It flips the angel's question back onto the viewer, so it feels finished.

## VERSION Y

**NEWCOMER**
- **Who, what, why:** Same as X.
- **Lines they would not follow:** Same old-English problems as X.
- **What is better:** "They had followed Him closely enough to hear that promise." This tells the newcomer why the angels said "remember." The women were his followers, not strangers.

**BIBLE READER**
- **What they get:** More than X. The line matches the text:
  - 24:8 says "they remembered his words."
  - 8:2-3 says these women travelled with him in Galilee.
- So the women were disciples who heard the promise themselves. This is rarely taught.
- **What is not in Luke 24:** Only the court line again, which is outside history, not text.

**CURIOSITY: 7/10**
- The opening question is the same as X.
- The payoff is better: the women were the ones listening when he made the promise.

**ENDING: Yes**
- Same strong last line as X.

## PICK: Y

- Y's middle line is backed by the text (24:8 and 8:2).
- X's "the promise had been kept" just repeats the line before it.

**ONE CUT:** Delete "He still sends His word to the overlooked."
- It is preachy.
- It says the same thing as the line just before it.
- Without it, the ending goes straight from "the witnesses the world would not hear" to "the risen Christ is seeking you," which hits harder.
