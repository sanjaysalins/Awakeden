The first report that Jesus was risen was dismissed as nonsense — by His own apostles. And their words seemed to them as idle tales, and they believed them not. The report came from women — and in that world, a woman's word carried almost no weight in court. No one inventing a resurrection would open it there. They watched Him die. They saw where He was buried. At dawn, they were back. So the angels pointed them to His own promise: Why seek ye the living among the dead? He is not here, but is risen: remember how he spake unto you when he was yet in Galilee, Saying, The Son of man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and the third day rise again. They had followed Him closely enough to hear that promise made. The idle tale was true, and it went first to the witnesses the world would not hear. He still sends His word to the overlooked. Stop seeking the living among the dead — the risen Christ is seeking you.
