LINE1: He sent me to wicked Nineveh; I knew His kindness, so I fled to Tarshish.
LINE2: He sent me to Nineveh, that great, wicked city, but I fled to Tarshish.
LINE3: He told me to cry out against wicked Nineveh, but I fled from His presence.
WHY: LINE1 is best because three plain active clauses match the 4.3-second pace for ESL listeners, connect "His mercy" to wicked Nineveh via Jonah's stated motive ("I knew His kindness," so no one thinks he fled out of fear), and launch his flight to Tarshish straight into the storm.
