## The job
A finished short is narrated by Jonah himself. ONE line in it is not true to the Bible and must be replaced. Everything else is locked.
The bad line (spoken from 10.8 s to 15.1 s, about 4.3 seconds, 14 words):
  "He sent me to Nineveh, the city I hated - I'd sooner drown than see her spared."
Why it is bad: no verse says Jonah hated Nineveh, and no verse says he would rather drown than see her spared. Jonah 1:12 gives his reason for the sea as "for my sake this great tempest is upon you". "Very angry" (Jonah 4:1) comes only AFTER the city was spared.

The KJV text you may draw on:
- Jonah 1:2 "Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and cry against it; for their wickedness is come up before me."
- Jonah 1:3 "But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the LORD"
- Jonah 4:2 "was not this my saying, when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish: for I knew that thou art a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil."
- Jonah 3:10 "God repented of the evil, that he had said that he would do unto them; and he did it not."

## The line BEFORE (locked)
