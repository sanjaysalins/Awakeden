LINE1: He sent me to Nineveh, that great city, but I fled to Tarshish.
LINE2: God said, "Go to Nineveh and speak against it." I fled toward Tarshish.
LINE3: I knew He would be merciful to Nineveh, so I fled to Tarshish.

WHY: Line 1 is best. It uses only facts from Jonah 1:2-3: God sent him to Nineveh, "that great city", and he fled to Tarshish. It adds no feelings. The line before already gives the reason (God's mercy), so Line 1 doesn't repeat it, and the escape leads straight into the storm line. Line 3 is closest to Jonah 4:2, but it says "mercy" twice in a row, and "merciful to Nineveh" goes slightly beyond what 4:2 says.
