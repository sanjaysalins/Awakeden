## TITLE
He Ran Because Mercy Was Too Wide

## DESCRIPTION
The sign of Jonah (Matthew 12:40): a prophet who ran from mercy, three days buried alive in the deep, and the Savior who chose that story as his only promised sign.

"For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth" (Matthew 12:40, KJV).

Jonah went down guilty. Christ went down innocent, bearing what was ours. And he rose.

However far you think you have fallen, that is how far his mercy reaches. Come up out of the deep, and live.
