## Critical — citation does not carry the claim

### 1. “Heart of the earth” → dark sealed tomb (false specified)
**Phrase:**  
`**[SPECIFIED]** The "heart of the earth" darkness (Matt 12:40): the sealed tomb interior is DARK — no lamps or candles… no window light.`

**Problem:** Matt 12:40 says the Son of man will be *“three days and three nights in the heart of the earth.”* That is the **Jonah parallel (duration + burial/death)**, not a lighting plan. The verse does **not** state:
- the chamber is dark  
- no lamps/candles  
- no window light  

Those are **period/architecture inferences** (maybe fair as **[CONSTRAINED]** for a sealed rock-cut tomb with no windows). Putting them under Matt 12:40 as **[SPECIFIED]** is **misapplication of the central Christ-typology verse** — the most important citation on the sheet for this title.

**Fix:** Split typology from décor. Keep Matt 12:40 for the three-days / Jonah sign. Put darkness / no lamps under **[CONSTRAINED]** (secondary), not under that verse as if Scripture mandated it.

---

### 2. Lots = “small plain stones/pebbles” (invented as specified)
**Phrase:**  
`**[SPECIFIED]** The lot fell upon Jonah… Image: small plain stones/pebbles as lots — NO legible marks or writing`

**Supported:** *“they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah”* (Jonah 1:7) — casting lots, result on Jonah.  
**Not supported:** material (stones/pebbles), size, plainness, no writing.

Lots-as-pebbles is **commonplace/traditional**, not biblical. That image rule must not sit in **[SPECIFIED]**.  
**Buckets:** outcome = specified; form of lots = **free** (or soft constrained: no anachronistic dice/cards if you want).

---

### 3. Stooping → “entrance is LOW” architecture (over-reach as specified)
**Phrase:**  
`**[SPECIFIED]** The entrance is LOW — a grown man STOOPS` … cites John 20:5; Luke 24:12 … `never a tall standing-height doorway`

**Supported:** disciples **stooped and looked in**.  
**Not strictly stated:** opening height, crawl doorway, “never tall.”

Stooping strongly *suggests* a low opening, but the text specifies **the action**, not the blueprint. As a hard image MUST this is **inference**. Better: **[SPECIFIED]** viewers stoop to look in; **[CONSTRAINED]** low/crawl-height opening (must not show people walking upright through a cathedral door *if* you also show the stooping texts).

---

### 4. “Rich man” under quotes that never say “rich”
**Phrase:**  
`A rich man's NEW rock-cut tomb…` citing only Matt 27:59–60 + John 19:41

Matt 27:59–60 support **new**, **hewn in the rock**, Joseph’s tomb.  
**“Rich man”** is Matt **27:57** (*“a rich man of Arimathaea, named Joseph”*) — **not** in the quotes given.

Garden / “never man yet laid” from John 19:41 is fine.  
**Citation truth:** claim is true *if* 27:57 is cited; as printed, the “rich man” limb is **unsupported by the shown KJV**.

## Serious — bucket honesty / over-claim

### 5. “Merchant” vessel as specified
Jonah 1:3: ship to Tarshish, **paid the fare**. Commercial traffic is fair inference; the word **“merchant”** and *“working merchant vessel at sea”* are not in the verse (boarding is at **Joppa**; “at sea” is later).

**Bucket:** ship + fare + destination = specified; “merchant / at sea” image line = tighten to what the verse actually places, or mark merchant-as-period as constrained.

---

### 6. Burial face-cover stack is mostly OK — one tight spot
- Wound in linen (John 19:40; Matt 27:59) — good.  
- Napkin *about his head* (John 20:7) — good evidence the head was covered in burial.  
- **“never an exposed … hand inside the wrappings”** — text does not name hands; full winding *implies* enclosure, but “never a hand” as **[SPECIFIED]** is one notch past the wording. Safer as constrained or “no casually exposed limbs as if sleeping undressed.”

---

### 7. “ONLY Jonah” — acceptable, keep the wording tight
Matt 12:39: no sign *but* the sign of Jonas — **correct**.  
Do not expand this card into “Jesus never gave any other signs in the Gospels”; the sheet’s scope is this demand-scene. As written, OK.

## Christ-lens

**Intact in intent:** Jonah three days ↔ Son of man three days (Matt 12:40); fish not species-dogma; sailors cast, not self-leap; tomb/burial imported for the “heart of the earth” sign.

**Risk:** Treating Matt 12:40 as a **dark-interior production rule** flattens the **typological claim** (death/burial duration) into set-dressing. That is the main Christ-lens distortion risk on this sheet — not heresy, but **mis-grounding the sign**.

Risen hands/side (John 20:20, 27) and pre-passion unwounded ministry scene are sound for chronology.

## Severity

Not a doctrinal FAIL of the Sign of Jonah. Core narrative guards (cast not jump; LORD’s storm; fish; dry land; Matt 12:38–40 text; garden rock tomb; stone outside) hold.

It is **not PASS**: multiple **[SPECIFIED]** lines assert **image MUST** rules the **cited KJV does not state**, including a **wrong use of Matt 12:40**. For a sheet that “drives + checks the stills,” that is enough to require rewrite before it gates art.
