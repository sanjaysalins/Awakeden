# Independent review — claude (OK, 90s)

I checked every quoted KJV line against the text and audited each bucket. The citations are verbatim and honest — but there are two bucket errors and some coverage gaps.

## Citation truth — verified
All 20+ KJV quotes check out verbatim (Jonah 1:3, 1:4, 1:7, 1:12, 1:15, 1:17, 2:1, 2:5, 2:6, 2:10; Matt 12:38-40; Matt 27:59-60; Mark 16:4; John 19:40-41; John 20:5, 7, 20, 27; Luke 24:12). The "whale's belly ≠ species claim" note and the FREE-labelled mercy-reach card are honest. Christ-lens (Jonah's three days → the Son of man's) is intact.

## Bucket errors

1. **"Heart of the earth" darkness card — wrongly SPECIFIED.** Matt 12:40 says "in the heart of the earth"; it says nothing about light, lamps, or windows. "The sealed tomb interior is DARK — no lamps or candles… no window light" is a physical inference from a sealed rock tomb, not a stated biblical fact. Scripture nowhere forbids a lamp in the tomb. This is the exact "licence dressed as specified" failure the standard warns against. Move to **CONSTRAINED** (a lit sealed tomb would strain plausibility, not contradict a verse).

2. **Lots card smuggles an invented physical detail.** Jonah 1:7 states only "the lot fell upon Jonah." The image directive "small plain stones/pebbles as lots" asserts a physical form Scripture never gives. The no-legible-writing rule is a good render guard, but the pebble form belongs in constrained/free — the SPECIFIED fact is only *that lots were cast and fell on Jonah*.

## Minor over-reaches

3. **"Merchant ship" (Jonah 1:3).** The quoted verse says "he paid the fare thereof" — commercial is a fair inference, but the stronger proof is uncited: Jonah 1:5, "cast forth the **wares** that were in the ship." Add 1:5 to the citation or soften "merchant" to "a ship carrying paying passengers and cargo."

4. **"FACE COVERED by the napkin."** John 20:7 says the napkin "was about his head." Head-covered is textual; "face covered — never an exposed face" stretches slightly beyond the words. Defensible, but say "head wrapped (napkin about his head)" to stay inside the verse.

## Coverage gaps (checkable facts with no guard)

5. **Nicodemus + the spices (John 19:39)** — the sheet cites John 19:38-42 as a source range but has no card for it: ~100 pounds of myrrh and aloes, and **two** men (Joseph and Nicodemus) handling the burial. A burial still showing Joseph alone, or no spices, has no fact to catch it.
6. **The seal and the watch (Matt 27:66)** — "sealing the stone, and setting a watch." Any sealed-tomb exterior still between burial and resurrection should show (or at least not contradict) the guard. Nothing guards it.
7. **Jonah asleep in the sides of the ship (Jonah 1:5)** — "he lay, and was fast asleep" during the storm. If a ship still shows Jonah on deck mid-tempest before the lot scene, no fact catches it.
8. **The mariners rowed hard first (Jonah 1:13)** — they tried to reach land before casting him in; a scene implying eager sailors tossing him has no guard. Low priority, but it protects the sailors' reluctance, which the text stresses.

## History-secondary check
The 8th-century-BC ship card and the Nineveh card are correctly CONSTRAINED and don't override Scripture. Fine.
