I have reviewed the `sign_of_jonah_matt1240` biblical fact sheet. There are several clear violations of the bucket rules, where historical assumptions and render limitations are being passed off as explicit Scripture, alongside a missing highly-visual textual fact.

Here are the specific findings:

**1. BUCKET DISHONESTY / RENDER LEAK (Invented Specification)**
* **Line:** `[SPECIFIED] The lot fell upon Jonah: "So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah" (Jonah 1:7). Image: small plain stones/pebbles as lots — NO legible marks or writing (script garbles in render).`
* **Critique:** You have placed a render-engine limitation ("script garbles in render") and an archaeological assumption ("small plain stones/pebbles") squarely into the `[SPECIFIED]` bucket. The Bible says "they cast lots"—it does *not* specify what the lots were made of, nor does it specify the absence of writing on them. This is artistic/technical licence masquerading as biblical specification. Move the exact nature of the lots to `[CONSTRAINED]` or `[FREE]`. 

**2. OVER-REACH ON THE TEXT (Invented Specification)**
* **Line:** `[SPECIFIED] Jonah fled on a merchant ship... Image: a working merchant vessel at sea.`
* **Critique:** The quoted text (Jonah 1:3) states he "found a ship going to Tarshish: so he paid the fare". It does *not* state it was a "merchant ship". While this is historically probable, it is not explicitly stated in the biblical text. Dictating "merchant vessel" as a biblical mandate is an over-reach; the fact that it is a "merchant" ship belongs in `[CONSTRAINED]`. 

**3. MISSING COVERAGE (The Sailors' Struggle)**
* **Line:** The jump from Jonah 1:12 (Jonah saying cast me in) to Jonah 1:15 (the sailors casting him in).
* **Critique:** You missed a highly specific, checkable visual fact right in the middle of this sequence. Jonah 1:13 explicitly states: *"Nevertheless the men rowed hard to bring it to the land; but they could not: for the sea wrought, and was tempestuous..."* The text specifies that the men furiously attempt to row the ship to shore before giving up and throwing him in. This is a massive omission for a visual scene plan.

**4. CHRIST-LENS / TYPOLOGICAL GAP**
* **Line:** `The sign demanded (Matthew 12:38-40)` section.
* **Critique:** The entire point of the Matthew 12 passage is the typological parallel: *"For as Jonas was... so shall the Son of man be..."* (Matt 12:40). You have isolated the Jonah facts and isolated the tomb facts, but you lack any fact/constraint connecting them. The sheet fails to guard the visual *parallelism* of the "whale's belly" and the "heart of the earth", which is the literal visual sign Jesus is pointing to. There should be a constraint ensuring these two dark, enclosed states echo one another visually without contradicting their respective literal texts.

VERDICT: REVISE

1. Remove render instructions ("no legible marks") and historical assumptions ("merchant", "pebbles") from the [SPECIFIED] bucket.
2. Add the missing biblical fact of the sailors rowing hard to reach land (Jonah 1:13).
