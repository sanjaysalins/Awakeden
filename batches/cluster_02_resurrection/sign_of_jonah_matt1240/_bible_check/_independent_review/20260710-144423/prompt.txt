## The storm and the ship (Jonah 1)
- **[SPECIFIED]** Jonah fled on a merchant ship from Joppa toward Tarshish: "But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the LORD, and went down to Joppa; and he found a ship going to Tarshish: so he paid the fare thereof" (Jonah 1:3). Image: a working merchant vessel at sea.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The storm is the LORD's and it threatens to break the ship: "But the LORD sent out a great wind into the sea, and there was a mighty tempest in the sea, so that the ship was like to be broken" (Jonah 1:4). Image: raging sea in every ship scene BEFORE Jonah goes over.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The lot fell upon Jonah: "So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah" (Jonah 1:7). Image: small plain stones/pebbles as lots — NO legible marks or writing (script garbles in render).
- **[SPECIFIED]** The SAILORS cast Jonah in, at his own word — he never jumps: "Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea" (Jonah 1:12); "So they took up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea: and the sea ceased from her raging" (Jonah 1:15). Image: sailors lifting/casting him; never a self-leap.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The sea ceased ONLY after he was cast in (Jonah 1:15 above). A calm sea with Jonah still aboard contradicts the text.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** Period ship (8th-century BC Levantine merchant): wooden hull, single square sail, steering oars — no modern rigging, no metal hull, no anachronisms.

## The great fish and the deep (Jonah 1:17 – 2:10)
- **[SPECIFIED]** A great FISH prepared by the LORD: "Now the LORD had prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights" (Jonah 1:17). Species unspecified — "whale's belly" is Jesus' KJV wording (Matt 12:40), not a species claim. Image: one vast dark fish, no cartoon teeth/eyes.
- **[SPECIFIED]** Jonah is ALIVE and PRAYING inside: "Then Jonah prayed unto the LORD his God out of the fish's belly" (Jonah 2:1). Image: living, praying posture — never a drowned corpse in the belly.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The sinking is total and weedy: "The waters compassed me about, even to the soul: the depth closed me round about, the weeds were wrapped about my head" (Jonah 2:5). Image: weeds about the head are textual in sinking scenes.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The fish vomited Jonah onto DRY LAND: "And the LORD spake unto the fish, and it vomited out Jonah upon the dry land" (Jonah 2:10). Image: shore at the sea's edge, Jonah alive.
- **[FREE/symbolic]** The mercy-reach into the deep (light shaft / hand of light) is a symbolic rendering of "yet hast thou brought up my life from corruption, O LORD my God" (Jonah 2:6) — permitted as clearly non-literal light, never a photoreal human arm from the sky.

## The sign demanded (Matthew 12:38-40)
- **[SPECIFIED]** Scribes and Pharisees demand a sign and Jesus gives ONLY Jonah: "Master, we would see a sign from thee" (Matt 12:38); "An evil and adulterous generation seeketh after a sign; and there shall no sign be given to it, but the sign of the prophet Jonas" (Matt 12:39); "For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth" (Matt 12:40).
- **[CONSTRAINED]** Ministry scene, PRE-passion: Jesus calm and unwounded (no nail wounds, no cross motifs on his body); scribes/Pharisees in 1st-century Judean dress; use the JESUS anchor face.
