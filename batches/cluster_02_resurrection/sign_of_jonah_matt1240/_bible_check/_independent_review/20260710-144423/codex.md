# Independent review — codex (OK, 131s)

**Findings**

- **“Jonah fled on a merchant ship…” (Jonah 1:3)**: the quoted verse says “a ship going to Tarshish” and that Jonah “paid the fare.” It does not state “merchant.” Jonah 1:5 mentions “wares,” which would support cargo, but that citation is not used here. Bucket should not be **specified** as written.

- **“raging sea in every ship scene BEFORE Jonah goes over” (Jonah 1:4, 1:15)**: overbroad. The storm starts in Jonah 1:4. A scene of Jonah finding/boarding the ship in Jonah 1:3 need not show raging sea.

- **“small plain stones/pebbles as lots — NO legible marks or writing” (Jonah 1:7)**: invented visual detail. The verse only says “they cast lots.” The medium, shape, markings, and anti-writing rule are production constraints/free licence, not **specified** biblical fact.

- **“one vast dark fish, no cartoon teeth/eyes” (Jonah 1:17)**: “great fish” supports large and singular. “Dark,” and “no cartoon teeth/eyes” are style constraints, not stated by Scripture. Mixed bucket: part specified, part free.

- **“shore at the sea’s edge” (Jonah 2:10)**: the verse says “upon the dry land.” “Sea’s edge” is a reasonable inference from a fish vomiting Jonah out, but it is not explicitly stated.

- **“The entrance is LOW — a grown man STOOPS” (John 20:5; Luke 24:12)**: the verses say the men stooped while looking in. They do not explicitly state the entrance height, “low, small opening,” or that a grown man must stoop to enter. This belongs in constrained, not specified.

- **“FACE COVERED by the napkin — never an exposed face or hand inside the wrappings” (John 19:40; Matt 27:59; John 20:7)**: overreach. “Napkin… about his head” does not explicitly mean the face was covered, and nothing cited says the hands were covered or must never be visible.

- **“The ‘heart of the earth’ darkness… sealed tomb interior is DARK — no lamps or candles… no window light” (Matt 12:40)**: misapplied citation. Matthew 12:40 states the Son of man will be “in the heart of the earth”; it does not specify darkness, lamps, candles, windows, or interior lighting. At most this is constrained by tomb/stone facts, not specified from Matt 12:40.

- **“sealed tomb”**: the sheet uses “sealed” language, but none of the cited burial/tomb facts quote Matthew 27:66, where the sealing of the stone occurs. If “sealed” is meant as biblical, cite it; otherwise say closed/covered by stone.

- **“wounds visible in the hands when shown” (John 20:20, 20:27)**: broadly true from the resurrection appearance context, but the quoted snippets do not include John 20:25’s “print of the nails,” and the John 20:27 quote is partial. Strengthen the citation if this is a visual rule.

- **“A rich man’s… tomb”**: the quote shown under the fact does not include Matthew 27:57, where Joseph is called “a rich man.” The claim is biblical, but the displayed quotation does not support that part.

- **“use the JESUS anchor face”**: this is a production continuity rule, not a biblical/historical constraint. It should not sit in the same bucket as Scripture-constrained visual facts.

**Missing Guards**

- Jonah 1:5: during the storm, the mariners are afraid, cry to their gods, and cast “wares” into the sea. If storm scenes are painted, this is an obvious visual fact missing from the sheet.

- Jonah 1:5-6: Jonah is below deck and asleep during the storm until the shipmaster wakes him. That guards against depicting him already alert on deck throughout the storm.

- Jonah 1:13-14: the men row hard to bring the ship to land and pray before casting Jonah overboard. If the casting scene is depicted, this guards against portraying them as casual or malicious.

- Jonah 2:6: “the bottoms of the mountains” / “the earth with her bars” are cited nowhere, though the sheet specifies deep-sinking imagery from Jonah’s prayer.

- John 19:39: Nicodemus brings myrrh and aloes, “about an hundred pound weight.” If burial preparation is depicted, this is a major specified detail missing.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Move invented visual rules out of **specified**, especially lots-as-pebbles, low entrance, face/hand coverage, and tomb darkness.
2. Fix citations where the shown KJV text does not support the full claim, especially “rich man,” “merchant ship,” and visible hand wounds.
3. Add missing specified guards from Jonah 1:5-6, Jonah 1:13-14, and John 19:39.
