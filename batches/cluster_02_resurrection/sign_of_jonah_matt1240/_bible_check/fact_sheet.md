## The storm and the ship (Jonah 1)
- **[SPECIFIED]** Jonah fled on a ship from Joppa toward Tarshish, paying the fare: "he found a ship going to Tarshish: so he paid the fare thereof" (Jonah 1:3). It carried cargo: "the mariners... cast forth the wares that were in the ship into the sea" (Jonah 1:5). Image: a working cargo vessel. ([CONSTRAINED] "merchant ship" styling is inference — period 8th-c BC Levantine: wooden hull, square sail, steering oars, no anachronisms.)
- **[SPECIFIED]** The storm is the LORD's and threatens to break the ship: "But the LORD sent out a great wind into the sea, and there was a mighty tempest in the sea, so that the ship was like to be broken" (Jonah 1:4). Every ship scene BEFORE Jonah goes over shows the raging sea.
- **[SPECIFIED]** During the storm Jonah was BELOW DECK, asleep, until the shipmaster woke him: "But Jonah was gone down into the sides of the ship; and he lay, and was fast asleep" (Jonah 1:5); "So the shipmaster came to him..." (Jonah 1:6). Image guard: no still shows Jonah alert on deck during the early storm.
- **[SPECIFIED]** Lots were cast and the lot fell on Jonah: "So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah" (Jonah 1:7). The text gives NO physical form for the lots. ([CONSTRAINED] small plain unmarked stones are a period-plausible rendering; render guard: no legible marks — script garbles.)
- **[SPECIFIED]** The sailors were RELUCTANT — they rowed hard for land first and prayed: "Nevertheless the men rowed hard to bring it to the land; but they could not" (Jonah 1:13); "Wherefore they cried unto the LORD... lay not upon us innocent blood" (Jonah 1:14). Image guard: the casting is sorrowful necessity, never casual or malicious.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The SAILORS cast Jonah in, at his own word — he never jumps: "Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea" (Jonah 1:12); "So they took up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea: and the sea ceased from her raging" (Jonah 1:15). Image: sailors lifting/casting him — never a self-leap, never a fall he chose.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The sea ceased ONLY after he was cast in (Jonah 1:15). A calm sea with Jonah still aboard contradicts.

## The great fish and the deep (Jonah 1:17 – 2:10)
- **[SPECIFIED]** A great FISH prepared by the LORD swallowed him; three days and three nights in its belly: "Now the LORD had prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights" (Jonah 1:17). Species unspecified — "whale's belly" is Jesus' KJV wording (Matt 12:40), not a species claim. ([CONSTRAINED] one vast dark fish; not a cartoon monster — no exaggerated teeth/eye.)
- **[SPECIFIED]** Jonah is ALIVE and PRAYING inside: "Then Jonah prayed unto the LORD his God out of the fish's belly" (Jonah 2:1). Never a corpse in the belly.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The sinking is deep and weedy: "the depth closed me round about, the weeds were wrapped about my head" (Jonah 2:5); "I went down to the bottoms of the mountains; the earth with her bars was about me for ever: yet hast thou brought up my life from corruption, O LORD my God" (Jonah 2:6). Image: weeds about the HEAD in sinking scenes; deep-sea floor imagery is textual.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The fish vomited Jonah onto DRY LAND: "And the LORD spake unto the fish, and it vomited out Jonah upon the dry land" (Jonah 2:10).
- **[FREE — declared]** `mercy_hand_into_deep` and `man_rising_from_water` are symbolic beats (Jonah 2:6 deliverance; rising typology). Symbolic LIGHT is safe licence; a literal photoreal arm of God from the sky risks depicting the Father — prefer light-shaft renderings (user precedent exists for hand-of-God imagery; user's call).

## The sign demanded (Matthew 12:38-40)
- **[SPECIFIED]** Scribes and Pharisees demand a sign; Jesus gives ONLY Jonah: "Master, we would see a sign from thee" (Matt 12:38); "there shall no sign be given to it, but the sign of the prophet Jonas" (Matt 12:39); "For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth" (Matt 12:40). Matt 12:40 asserts the THREE-DAYS PARALLEL — nothing about lighting or set dressing.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** TYPOLOGY GUARD (the piece's spine): the fish's belly and the closed tomb are the SAME sign — the two enclosed-dark states should visually echo each other (palette/composition), without contradicting either text.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** Ministry scene, PRE-passion: Jesus calm, unwounded, no cross motifs; scribes/Pharisees in 1st-century Judean dress.

## The burial and the tomb (Matt 27:57-66; John 19:38-42; Mark 16:4; John 20)
- **[SPECIFIED]** Joseph of Arimathea was a RICH man with a NEW rock-cut tomb: "there came a rich man of Arimathaea, named Joseph" (Matthew 27:57); "and laid it in his own new tomb, which he had hewn out in the rock" (Matthew 27:60); in a GARDEN: "in the garden a new sepulchre, wherein was never man yet laid" (John 19:41).
- **[SPECIFIED]** TWO men buried him, with a great weight of spices: "And there came also Nicodemus... and brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound weight" (John 19:39); "Then took they the body of Jesus, and wound it in linen clothes with the spices, as the manner of the Jews is to bury" (John 19:40). Image: a burial still shows Joseph AND Nicodemus and the spice bundles/jars; the body WOUND in linen clothes (plural), the napkin "about his head" (John 20:7) — the head wrapped. ([CONSTRAINED] an exposed bare face/hand in the finished wrapping strains "wound... as the manner of the Jews"; keep the body fully wound.)
- **[SPECIFIED]** A GREAT STONE rolled TO THE DOOR: "and he rolled a great stone to the door of the sepulchre" (Matthew 27:60), later SEALED and WATCHED: "sealing the stone, and setting a watch" (Matthew 27:66). Image: stone at the entrance; a between-burial-and-resurrection exterior should not contradict the guard/seal. ([CONSTRAINED] stone never inside the chamber — it seals from outside.)
- **[SPECIFIED]** A man STOOPS to look in: "And he stooping down, and looking in" (John 20:5); "stooping down, he beheld" (Luke 24:12). ([CONSTRAINED] therefore a low, small opening — never a tall standing-height doorway.)
- **[SPECIFIED]** At the resurrection the stone is rolled AWAY: "they saw that the stone was rolled away: for it was very great" (Mark 16:4); the EMPTY linen lies where the body was, the napkin apart: "saw the linen clothes lying" (John 20:5-6); "the napkin, that was about his head, not lying with the linen clothes, but wrapped together in a place by itself" (John 20:7). Image (post-resurrection tomb): empty collapsed linen ON the bench, napkin wrapped together APART — never an occupied shroud, never the napkin discarded on the floor.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The risen Christ SHOWED the wounds: "he shewed unto them his hands and his side" (John 20:20); "behold my hands" (John 20:27); "except I shall see in his hands the print of the nails" (John 20:25). Wounds visible in the hands when shown.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** Sealed-tomb interior ("heart of the earth" beat): darkness is the natural state of a sealed rock tomb — no windows, no burning lamps INSIDE the sealed tomb, no light shaft through a sealed door. (A lamp during the OPEN-tomb burial work is period-fine.) Tomb typology: low hewn chamber, waist-low bench, disc stone or great boulder; no columns, church forms, sarcophagus.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** The risen Christ alive and whole — no crown of thorns, no gore; serene.

## Nineveh (Jonah 1:2 backdrop)
- **[CONSTRAINED]** "that great city" (Jonah 1:2): ancient Assyrian walled city — massive walls, gate towers, ziggurat forms, ROUND or square-lintel gate openings. NO Islamic-era architecture (no pointed/ogee arches, no minarets, no onion domes), no Greco-Roman columns.
