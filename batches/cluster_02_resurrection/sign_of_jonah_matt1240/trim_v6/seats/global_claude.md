# Blind seat: Jonah Short, X vs Y

**The only difference:** X has "Three days buried alive in the fish." Y cuts that and adds "— the storm was mine."

---

## VERSION X

**NEWCOMER**
- **Who:** a man ("I") who ran from God, and later Jesus.
- **What:** he is thrown into the sea, a fish swallows him, and years later Jesus uses his story.
- **Why it matters:** Jesus also went down into the dark and came back, so nobody has fallen too far.
- **Lines they would lose:**
  - The speaker never says "I am Jonah." The only name they hear is "Jonas," inside the quote. A newcomer may not link "Jonas" to "me."
  - "Son of man" is not explained, so they may not know it means Jesus.
  - "Whale" in the quote clashes with "great fish" earlier.
  - "The grave no more held Him than the deep held me" is hard grammar for someone with English as a second language.

**BIBLE READER**
- **What they get:** "Three days in the fish" set up first, then mirrored by Jesus' own words. The parallel is clean.
- **What is true:**
  - "For my sake" (Jonah 1:12), "the lot fell" (1:7), "great fish" (1:17), "certain of the scribes" (Mt 12:38): all true.
- **Flags (no errors):**
  - "Mercy too wide" and "sooner drown" come from Jonah 4:2-3, not chapters 1-2. They are fair to the book, but outside the given range.
  - "Bearing what was ours" and "He rose" are not stated in Matthew 12. They are fair as preaching, but they are not in that passage.

**CURIOSITY: 6/10.** The cold open (a man asking to be drowned) grabs you. "Why?" pays off fast ("I ran from God"). The second hook ("only my name") pays off in the quote.

**ENDING: Yes.** Jesus is named in the second-to-last sentence. "Come up out of the deep, and live" closes the sea picture and feels like an end.

---

## VERSION Y

**NEWCOMER**
- Same who, what and why as X.
- **Weaker:** without "three days in the fish," the "three days and three nights" in the quote comes out of nowhere. The newcomer misses the match.
- **New confusion:** "the storm was mine" may read as "I made the storm."

**BIBLE READER**
- They get the guilty / innocent contrast, which is a bit sharper here.
- **Weakness:** "the storm was mine" repeats "the storm came for my sake," which is already said earlier.
- **Accuracy:** same flags as X. No new errors.

**CURIOSITY: 5/10.** Same hook as X, but the three-days payoff is weaker.

**ENDING: Yes.** Same reasons as X.

---

## PICK: X

The three-days setup is what makes Jesus' quote click for a newcomer. Y throws that away for a line that repeats itself.

**ONE fix (cut only):** "Three days ~~buried alive~~ in the fish."
- "Buried alive" is already used in line 1, so it is said twice.
- The shorter line mirrors the quote more cleanly.

**Still weak, and no cut can fix it:** the speaker is never named Jonah. If he says his name only once, the newcomer may never link "Jonas" to the narrator.
