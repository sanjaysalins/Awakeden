## TITLE
John Saw No Angel, Only the Linen. And He Believed.

## DESCRIPTION
When Peter and John ran to the empty tomb, no angel spoke and no risen Lord stood there. Only the linen, lying where the body should have been, and the face-cloth wrapped together in a place by itself. What thief does that? John saw, and believed.

"Jesus saith unto him, Thomas, because thou hast seen me, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed." (John 20:29, KJV)

You were not at the tomb. Jesus was speaking about you. Believe what John believed, the Lord is risen.
