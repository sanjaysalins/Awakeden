**NEWCOMER**
- Who: Jesus is a man whose body is missing from his tomb.
- What: Mary, Peter and John find the tomb empty; later Thomas sees the risen Jesus.
- Why: The short argues that Jesus rose from the dead and asks viewers to believe.
- Least clear line: “He did not yet know the scripture — that Christ must rise from the dead.” *Scripture* may be unfamiliar, and the sentence is dense.

**BIBLE READER**
- Gets: The contrast between John believing before understanding the scripture and Thomas believing after seeing.
- Not true to John 20: John’s belief is left unspecified in the passage; it does not say he believed Jesus had risen. Also, Thomas says “My Lord and my God,” which the script omits. “What thief wraps the face-cloth…” is speculation, not a detail John gives.

**CURIOSITY:** Yes—the missing body and cloth raise a question early, and John’s belief answers it. **7/10.**

**ENDING:** **No.** It ends on a direct appeal to believe, but the final line centers the viewer’s response rather than landing on Jesus.

**OVERALL:** **6/10.** Weakest line: “Believe what John believed — the Lord is risen.”
