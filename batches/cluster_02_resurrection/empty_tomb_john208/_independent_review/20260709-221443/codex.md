# Independent review — codex (OK, 83s)

KJV sources checked: [John 20:2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_20%3A2), [20:7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_20%3A7), [20:8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_20%3A8), [20:9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_20%3A9), [20:29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_20%3A29).

**Findings**

- KJV verbatim: no altered words found in the scripture lines. “They have taken away…,” “And the napkin…,” “and he saw, and believed,” and “blessed are they…” match the KJV excerpts used.

- Doctrinal/textual risk: “John believed before he ever saw the risen Lord” states a debated reading of John 20:8 as settled. John 20:9 immediately complicates that moment: “they knew not the scripture.” The narration can still land orthodoxly, but it should not build freshness on a contested certainty.

- Overclaimed apologetic: “What thief wraps the face-cloth and lays it aside?” pushes the linen detail into a courtroom-style proof. The text says the napkin was “wrapped together in a place by itself”; it does not directly argue the psychology of a thief. That line risks sounding cleverer than the passage.

- Clarity issue: “You have only heard. But the linen still speaks” is poetic, but a zero-Bible listener may not understand how linen “speaks” unless the narration more plainly connects the grave clothes to the missing body.

- Thread strain: the piece starts with “Someone took the body,” shifts to “the linen disproves theft,” then lands on “Believe him without seeing him.” Those are related, but the final emphasis is Thomas/faith while the strongest image is the linen. The CTA should land from the same image, not merely from the verse tag.

- Hook score: 7/10. “Someone took the body” has tension, but “at the empty tomb of Jesus” quickly makes it familiar church-language rather than a sharper first-five-seconds grab.

- Landing score: 5/10. “Already, he calls that faith blessed” could close many John 20:29 devotionals. It is biblical, but not bone-deep from this piece’s own image of linen, napkin, and an undisturbed tomb.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove or qualify the contested certainty that John’s belief in 20:8 was fully resurrection faith.
2. Rework the thief/face-cloth line so it observes the text without overclaiming what the folded cloth proves.
3. Make the final CTA land through the linen/tomb image instead of a reusable “faith blessed” closer.
