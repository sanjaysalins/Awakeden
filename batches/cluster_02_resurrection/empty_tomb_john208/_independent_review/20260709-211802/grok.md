# Independent review — grok (OK, 57s)

## Independent narration review

### Hook score: **8 / 10**
**Landing score: **7 / 10****

Hook is not a template open. “The resurrection was first reported as a grave robbery” is specific and scroll-stopping. Landing is mostly built from this piece (linen / blessed-without-seeing), but the close muddles **hearing** with **believing** and drops a stock “Believe him.” Under 8 on landing → cannot PASS.

---

### Doctrinal soundness
Mostly orthodox: empty tomb, ordered grave-clothes, John believes before seeing Jesus, then 20:29 applied to later hearers. No contrarian twist.

**Problem:** the landing treats mere hearers as already the blessed group.

- Exact lines: *“You have only heard. That is who he blessed.”*
- Jesus blesses *“they that have not seen, and yet have believed”* — not people who only heard.
- Two beats later: *“Believe him.”* So the script first puts the listener inside the blessing, then asks them to believe. That undercuts the beatitude.

**Softer overclaim:** *“Thieves snatch — they do not stop to set a grave in order”* and *“The linen still speaks: the body was not stolen.”* The napkin detail supports that reading; John does not state a forensic proof. Fine as strong inference; too absolute as settled fact.

### Clarity on first hearing
**Spine is clear:** reported as theft → ordered linen → John believes without seeing Christ → Jesus blesses that kind of faith → you.

**Weak spots:**

1. **Hook is clever, slightly loose.** *“The resurrection was first reported as a grave robbery.”* Mary reported a missing body, not “the resurrection.” A cold listener can mishear the first claim.
2. **“nothing had appeared”** — vague. Appeared what? Better as “he had not yet seen Jesus.”
3. **Landing logic glitch** (same as doctrine): heard ≠ blessed. First-hear confusion.

---

### Grace-anchored conviction
No fear, no gain/loss, no works ladder. Spirit invites; script invites. Good.

Minor stock pressure only in *“Believe him.”* — invitation is fine; the stock shape is the issue, not threat.

### Freshness = faithful depth
Surprise is in the text: first report sounds like theft; napkin “wrapped together… by itself”; John believes before sight; 20:29 lands on the hearer. Claim and landing stay orthodox. Good thread choice.

---

### One thread spine
Holds from hook → middle → CTA: **not stolen → believed without seeing → you are in that path.** No thread-swap. Strong.

---

### Landing score (why 7)
Piece-owned material is there: linen, not stolen, blessed without seeing.

But:

- *“Believe him.”* could close many other pieces → stock beat.
- *“You have only heard. That is who he blessed.”* mis-assigns the blessing.
- Final line *“Already, he calls that faith blessed”* is strong **if** faith is invited first and hearing is not equated with blessing.

---

### Summary judgment
KJV is clean. Doctrine core is sound. Thread is one spine. Hook is good enough (8). Landing is not (7): logic error on who is blessed + stock “Believe him.” **REVISE**, not FAIL.

```
VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Fix the landing logic: do not say hearers are already “who he blessed.” Order it as: you weren’t at the tomb / you’ve only heard → the linen still speaks (Jesus is risen) → believe without seeing → *that* faith is what he already calls blessed.
2. Drop or rewrite stock “Believe him.” End on this piece’s own verb/image (e.g. the blessing of unseen faith / the linen that still speaks)—final sentence must not fit a different short.
3. Tighten the hook’s first claim so it matches Mary’s report (body taken / empty tomb misread as theft), not a slightly loose “resurrection was reported as grave robbery”; and replace vague “nothing had appeared” with plain “he had not yet seen Jesus.”
```
