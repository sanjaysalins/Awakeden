## The tomb itself
- **[SPECIFIED]** A NEW rock-cut tomb in a GARDEN, near the crucifixion site: "Now in the place where he was crucified there was a garden; and in the garden a new sepulchre, wherein was never man yet laid" (John 19:41); "his own new tomb, which he had hewn out in the rock" (Matthew 27:60); "the sepulchre was nigh at hand" (John 19:42). Image: tomb hewn into living rock in a garden — never a freestanding masonry building or dome.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The entrance is low enough that a grown man STOOPS to look in: "And he stooping down, and looking in" (John 20:5); "she stooped down, and looked into the sepulchre" (John 20:11); "stooping down, he beheld" (Luke 24:12). Image: a LOW opening — never a tall standing-height doorway.
- **[SPECIFIED]** A GREAT STONE was ROLLED to the door and later found taken away: "he rolled a great stone to the door of the sepulchre" (Matthew 27:60); "for it was very great" (Mark 16:4); "seeth the stone taken away from the sepulchre" (John 20:1). Image: a very large stone moved aside OUTSIDE the entrance — never inside the chamber, never small.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The place where the body lay has a HEAD-end and a FEET-end, and the linen was visible from outside the entrance: "seeth two angels... the one at the head, and the other at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain" (John 20:12); "stooping down, and looking in, saw the linen clothes lying" (John 20:5). Image: a defined resting place with head/feet ends, sightline from the doorway.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** 1st-century Judean rock-cut tomb typology (secondary): small squarish crawl-height opening, disc-shaped rolling stone in a carved groove or a great rolled boulder, low hewn chamber with a waist-low rock bench/arcosolium, tool-marked walls. No columns, no church forms, no sarcophagus/coffin, no candles or lamps burning in the tomb.

## The grave clothes
- **[SPECIFIED]** PLURAL linen cloths, wound about the body with spices: "wound it in linen clothes with the spices, as the manner of the Jews is to bury" (John 19:40); "a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound weight" (John 19:39); "saw the linen clothes lying" (John 20:5). Image: linen CLOTHES (plural) lying, visibly without a body in them — never a form that reads as an occupied shroud.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The face-napkin is SEPARATE from the linen clothes and wrapped together: "the napkin, that was about his head, not lying with the linen clothes, but wrapped together in a place by itself" (John 20:7). Image: a small wrapped/rolled cloth clearly apart from the other linen.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** (secondary): wrappings as layered strips/folds, residual bulk or spice dust, napkin at the head-end of the bench — period-faithful renderings, not asserted by the text.

## People and time
- **[SPECIFIED]** Mary Magdalene comes EARLY, WHILE IT IS STILL DARK, finds the stone taken away, and RUNS to Peter and John (John 20:1-2). Image: pre-dawn darkness; the tomb dark and unattended — NO angel and NO risen Christ visible in the discovery/running/Peter-and-John beats (John 20:1-10 records none seen then).
- **[SPECIFIED]** Peter and John RAN together; John outran Peter, stooped and looked in but DID NOT ENTER FIRST; Peter went in first; then John went in, "and he saw, and believed" (John 20:4-8). Image: running beats show two men; any John-outside beat shows him stooped at the opening; inside beats may show John within (20:8).
- **[SPECIFIED]** The risen Jesus showed "his hands and his side" (John 20:20); to Thomas: "Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands; and reach hither thy hand, and thrust it into my side" (John 20:27) — EIGHT DAYS after the first appearance, indoors, "the doors being shut" (John 20:26). Image: wounds in the hands visible when shown; the side wound must never be contradicted (a robed side is fine); Thomas scene is indoors.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** the risen Christ is alive and whole in appearance — no gore, no crown of thorns. Dress: 1st-century Judean, simple wool tunics, woman's mantle drawn over the head, sandals, muted natural dyes.
- **Out of scope:** angel scenes (John 20:12 two angels in white, at head and feet) — no angel beat exists in this piece; if one is ever added, that verse becomes its specified card.
