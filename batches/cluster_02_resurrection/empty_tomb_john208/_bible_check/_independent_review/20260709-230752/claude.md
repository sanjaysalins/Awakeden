# Independent review — claude (OK, 97s)

**Findings — citation truth & bucket honesty**

1. **"never one flat bedsheet" ([SPECIFIED], grave-clothes) over-reaches and sits against a verse the sheet itself uses.** Matthew 27:59 — which the derivation line cites — says "he wrapped it in a clean linen cloth" (singular *sindon*). John's plural "linen clothes" (John 19:40) coexists with the synoptic single sheet; many faithful harmonizations show a large sheet plus bindings. Banning a sheet as *biblically specified* is a harmonization choice, not a statement of Scripture. Move the ban to **constrained** (e.g. "must read as plural cloths per John; must not read as a body").

2. **Napkin "at the HEAD-end of the bench" is invented placement inside a [SPECIFIED] item.** John 20:7 says the napkin "that was about his head" was "in a place by itself" — the verse tells you what the cloth *was*, not where it now *lies*. Its location on the bench (head-end or anywhere) is licence. Split it: separate + wrapped together = specified; position = free.

3. **"massive disc/round stone… rolled aside in its channel" is archaeology dressed as [SPECIFIED].** Scripture specifies *rolled* and *very great* (Matthew 27:60; Mark 16:4). Disc shape and a cut channel/track are archaeological reconstruction (and disc stones were actually rare in 1st-c. tombs — most used square plug stones). The sheet even repeats the groove/track correctly under [CONSTRAINED], so the specified line double-claims it. Keep "rolled / very great / moved away from the door" as specified; shape + channel go to constrained.

4. **"~1 m crawl-height square opening" — the number and the shape are not in the text.** The verses (John 20:5, 20:11; Luke 24:12) specify *stooping*, nothing more. "Square" and "~1 m" belong in the constrained (archaeology) bucket, not inside a [SPECIFIED] image directive. Same issue with **"waist-low"** on the bench fact — height is never stated — and with **"arcosolium"**, which is a later tomb typology term.

5. **"he is alive, whole, serene"** — "serene" is an aesthetic direction embedded in a [SPECIFIED] fact. Also this fact under-quotes its own verse: John 20:20 is "his hands **and his side**" — the side wound is a checkable specified detail a painting could easily omit, and right now no fact guards it. (Which side is pierced is unstated — that part is genuinely free.)

**Coverage gaps (checkable facts with no guard)**

6. **Angels, if any beat shows them:** two angels, **in white**, **sitting**, one at the head and one at the feet (John 20:12; cf. Mark 16:5 "clothed in a long white garment"). The sheet only uses 20:12 to prove the bench exists. A renderer could paint one angel, standing, winged, glowing — nothing stops it. Either add an angel fact or state explicitly that angel beats are out of scope.
7. **Mary weeping outside (John 20:11) and the gardener mistake (John 20:15)** — if the still set reaches those beats, "Jesus looked ordinary enough to be taken for the gardener" is a strong constrained fact (no halo/glow at that moment). Nothing guards it.
