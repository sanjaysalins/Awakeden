# Independent review — codex (OK, 46s)

Findings:

- **“small crawl-height square opening (~1 m)”** under **[SPECIFIED]** is invented. John 20:5, John 20:11, and Luke 24:12 say people **stooped to look in**; they do not specify crawl-height, square shape, or a one-metre opening. This should be constrained at most.

- **“massive disc/round stone … rolled aside in its channel”** under **[SPECIFIED]** overreaches. Matthew 27:60 and Mark 16:3-4 support a **great stone** rolled to/from the door, but not a disc shape, visible channel, or exact resting position “beside the opening.”

- **“rock SHELF/BENCH … arcosolium … waist-low”** is partly over-specified. John 20:12 supports distinguishable **head** and **feet** positions where Jesus had lain; Mark 16:5 supports an angel “on the right side.” The text does not state a bench, shelf height, arcosolium, or that it was visible from the entrance in the architectural sense claimed.

- **“disc stone runs in a cut groove/track”** in the constrained historical note is presented too strongly. It may be plausible archaeology, but Scripture only says the stone was rolled. This belongs as tentative historical background, not a hard visual constraint.

- **“No candles/lamps burning inside”** is not a biblical fact. Scripture does not mention lamps either way. If this is meant to preserve the darkness of Mary’s arrival, say that; otherwise it is extra-biblical visual control.

- **“packed with spices” / “residual bulk from the spices”** overstates John 19:39-40. The text says Jesus was wound in linen clothes **with** spices, about a hundred pound weight. It does not say the empty wrappings remained visibly bulky from spices, nor that they collapsed in a particular visual form.

- **“layered strips/folds”** is not specified. John says “linen clothes” plural, not strips. Plural cloths are biblical; “strips” is an artistic or historical inference.

- **“face-napkin … at the HEAD-end of the bench”** goes beyond John 20:7. The napkin was “in a place by itself,” but the verse does not say it remained at the head-end.

- **“no angel visible in her discovery/running beats”** is correct for John 20:1-2 as a Johannine sequence, but the sheet should say this is scoped to those beats. Other Gospel accounts include angelic figures at the tomb; the constraint must not imply angels are unbiblical in every empty-tomb depiction.

- **“Peter entered first”** is potentially misleading. John 20:5-6 says the other disciple arrived first and did not enter, then Peter entered. John 20:8 says the other disciple entered afterward. So Peter entered first **into the tomb**, but not first to arrive; the wording should be tightened.

- **“inside-the-tomb beats show a man BENT/CROUCHED under the low chamber”** overreaches. The cited stooping concerns looking in. John 20:6 says Peter “went into the sepulchre”; it does not say he remained bent or crouched inside.

- **“The risen Jesus later SHOWS the wounds in his hands”** is incomplete. John 20:20 says he showed “his hands and his side”; John 20:27 includes hand/finger and hand/side language. The side wound is a specified visual fact and is missing from the claim.

- **“he is alive, whole, serene”** mixes biblical fact with devotional interpretation. “Alive” is supported; “whole” needs care because the wounds remain visible; “serene” is not stated by John 20:20 or 20:27.

- **“no crown of thorns on the risen Christ”** is reasonable as a constrained guardrail, but not because the cited resurrection verses explicitly say it. The sheet should not make it look like a directly stated resurrection-detail fact.

- Coverage gap: the sheet omits that the tomb was **near at hand** because of the Jews’ preparation day: John 19:42. The first bullet says “near the crucifixion site,” but cites John 19:41 rather than the clearer John 19:42.

- Coverage gap: John 20:12 specifies **two angels in white** sitting where Jesus had lain. Since the sheet covers John 20:12 for head/feet placement, it should guard the number and whiteness if angel scenes are in scope.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Move invented architectural specifics out of **[SPECIFIED]**: crawl-height square opening, disc stone/channel, bench/arcosolium/waist height.
2. Tighten grave-cloth claims: plural linen cloths and separate napkin are specified; strips, collapsed form, residual spice bulk, and head-end placement are not.
3. Add missing specified details from John 19:42 and John 20:12, especially the tomb being near at hand and the two angels in white for angel scenes.
