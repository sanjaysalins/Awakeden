# Adversarial review — biblical fact sheet `empty_tomb_john208`

Default: skepticism. Several **core** claims are sound; the sheet repeatedly promotes **archaeology and inference** into **[SPECIFIED]**.

## CITATION TRUTH (primary)

### Real support (keep)
- **Garden + new sepulchre never used** — John 19:41 actually says this.
- **Hewn in rock / Joseph’s new tomb** — Matthew 27:60 supports rock-cut, new tomb.
- **Great stone rolled / taken away** — Matt 27:60; Mark 16:3–4; John 20:1.
- **Stooping to look in** — John 20:5, 20:11; Luke 24:12 (they stooped and looked).
- **Linen clothes (plural) with spices at burial** — John 19:39–40; empty clothes seen — John 20:5–7.
- **Napkin separate, wrapped, not with the clothes** — John 20:7 is on point.
- **Mary early, yet dark, stone already gone, runs** — John 20:1–2.
- **John outran Peter; Peter went in first; then the other disciple** — John 20:4–8.
- **Hands (and, in the same verses, side) shown** — John 20:20, 20:27.

### Over-reach / verse does not state the claim

**1. Entrance = crawl-height ~1 m square opening (SPECIFIED)**  
Phrase: *“small crawl-height square opening (~1 m), NEVER a tall standing-height doorway.”*  
Cited texts only show people **stooping to look in**. That does **not** fix height, shape, or “crawl-height.” A person can stoop at a doorway that is still taller than ~1 m. **~1 m**, **square**, and **NEVER standing-height** are **not** in KJV. **Bucket wrong → at most constrained (and ~1 m should not be fixed).**

**2. Stone = disc/round in a channel (SPECIFIED)**  
Phrase: *“massive disc/round stone RESTING OUTSIDE … (rolled aside in its channel).”*  
Text: stone **rolled** / **very great** / **taken away**. No disc geometry, no track/channel, no “resting beside the opening” layout. Rolling makes a round stone **plausible**; it is **not specified**. Channel/groove is pure archaeology (already partly in the constrained tomb bullet — don’t double it as specified).

**3. Interior = waist-low bench / arcosolium (SPECIFIED)**  
Phrase: *“waist-low hewn bench/arcosolium along the chamber wall — never a sunken pit, trough, coffin or sarcophagus.”*  
What the citations actually give:
- Linen seen from outside (John 20:5) → something visible from the entrance when looking in.
- Angels at **head** and **feet**, “where the body of Jesus had lain” (John 20:12) → a lying-place with identifiable ends.
- Mark 16:5 “sitting on the right side” → **position of a figure**, not tomb architecture (bench height, arcosolium, pit ban).

**Arcosolium / waist-low shelf / ban on pits** = period typology, not KJV. Head/foot ends can stay specified; the furniture form cannot.

**4. Napkin “at the HEAD-end of the bench” (SPECIFIED)**  
John 20:7: napkin *“not lying with the linen clothes, but wrapped together in a place by itself.”*  
It was **about his head** earlier; the empty-tomb location is only **“by itself,”** not “at the head-end of the bench.”

**5. “Residual bulk from the spices” on Easter-morning clothes (SPECIFIED)**  
John 19:39–40 is **burial preparation**. John 20 only has clothes **lying** / napkin apart. **Visible spice bulk** in the empty wrappings is **not** stated.

**6. “The tomb is DARK — no light glowing from inside” (under Mary SPECIFIED)**  
“Yet dark” (John 20:1) is **outdoor time of arrival**. Interior darkness / no glow is **not** in the verse. Fine as free/constrained art rule; dishonest as specified biblical fact.

**7. “Square opening”** — geometry not in Scripture (same entrance bullet).

### CITATION TRUTH (claims the verse does not state)

**1. Tomb entrance “~1 m / crawl-height / square opening” as SPECIFIED**  
Cited: John 20:5, 20:11; Luke 24:12 (“stooping down… looking in”).  
Those verses support: people **bent to look in**.  
They do **not** state height, crawl vs stoop, or square shape.  
“Small crawl-height square opening (~1 m), NEVER a tall standing-height doorway” is archaeology + design rule dressed as Bible.  
**Bucket fix:** SPECIFIED = stoop-to-look entrance; dimensions/shape → CONSTRAINED or FREE.

**2. “Massive disc/round stone… rolled aside in its channel” as SPECIFIED**  
Cited: Matt 27:60; Mark 16:3–4; John 20:1.  
Supported: a **great stone**, **rolled** to/away from the **door**, later **taken away**.  
Not stated: disc form, track/channel, rest pose “beside the opening.” “Rolled” allows a rolling stone; it does not specify disc-in-groove.  
**Bucket fix:** SPECIFIED = great stone, rolled/taken from the door, not small, not inside the chamber. Disc + channel → CONSTRAINED.

**3. “Waist-low hewn bench/arcosolium” as SPECIFIED**  
Cited: John 20:5 (linen seen from outside); John 20:12 (angels at head and feet); Mark 16:5 (“right side”).  
Supported: body had **head-end and foot-end**; linen visible when looking in; something on a **side** where a figure can sit.  
Not stated: waist height, “bench,” or **arcosolium**. That is tomb typology, not text.  
“Never sunken pit / trough / coffin / sarcophagus” is a fair **constraint** (hard to reconcile with head/feet seating and outside sight of linen), not a direct verse claim.  
**Bucket fix:** SPECIFIED = head/foot axis + visible burial place from entrance; form/height → CONSTRAINED.

**4. Face-napkin “at the HEAD-end of the bench” as SPECIFIED**  
Cited: John 20:7 — napkin “not lying with the linen clothes, but wrapped together in a place by itself.”  
Supported: **separate**, **wrapped together**, **not with** the other clothes.  
Not stated: exact placement at head-end. “About his head” is past use, not current map.  
**Bucket fix:** head-end is CONSTRAINED/FREE inference; only separation + wrapping is SPECIFIED.

**5. “Residual bulk from the spices” on Easter-morning clothes as SPECIFIED**  
Cited: John 19:39–40 (spices at burial); John 20:5 (clothes lying).  
Supported: wound in linen clothes **with spices** at burial; empty clothes **lying** on the third day.  
Not stated: spice bulk still visible as volume in the collapsed cloths. That is a reconstruction.  
**Bucket fix:** keep plural wound clothes + empty/lying as SPECIFIED; “residual bulk” → FREE or soft CONSTRAINED.

**6. “Man BENT/CROUCHED under the low chamber” inside as SPECIFIED**  
John 20:4–8: they **ran**, John **outran**, Peter **went in first**, then the other disciple, and believed.  
Stooping is for looking in (and entry implication). Scripture does **not** say the man stays bent/crouched once inside.  
**Bucket fix:** bent posture inside → CONSTRAINED (low chamber), not SPECIFIED.

Minor wording: “**cultivated** garden” (John 19:41 says **garden**, not cultivation type). Mild over-reach.
