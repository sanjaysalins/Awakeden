## The tomb itself
- **[SPECIFIED]** A NEW rock-cut tomb in a GARDEN, near the crucifixion site: "Now in the place where he was crucified there was a garden; and in the garden a new sepulchre, wherein was never man yet laid" (John 19:41). "his own new tomb, which he had hewn out in the rock" (Matthew 27:60). Image: tomb hewn into living rock in a cultivated garden — never a freestanding masonry building, mausoleum or dome.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The entrance is LOW — you must STOOP to look in: "And he stooping down, and looking in, saw the linen clothes lying" (John 20:5); "she stooped down, and looked into the sepulchre" (John 20:11); "stooping down, he beheld the linen clothes laid by themselves" (Luke 24:12). Image: small crawl-height square opening (~1 m), NEVER a tall standing-height doorway.
- **[SPECIFIED]** A GREAT STONE was ROLLED to (and away from) the door: "he rolled a great stone to the door of the sepulchre" (Matthew 27:60); "Who shall roll us away the stone...? for it was very great" (Mark 16:3-4); "seeth the stone taken away from the sepulchre" (John 20:1). Image: a massive disc/round stone RESTING OUTSIDE beside the opening (rolled aside in its channel) — never inside the chamber, never small.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The interior burial place is a rock SHELF/BENCH with a head-end and a foot-end, visible from the entrance when stooping: the linen was seen from OUTSIDE (John 20:5); angels later sat "the one at the head, and the other at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain" (John 20:12); "sitting on the right side" (Mark 16:5). Image: waist-low hewn bench/arcosolium along the chamber wall — never a sunken pit, trough, coffin or sarcophagus.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** 1st-century Judean rock-cut tomb: low hewn chamber (a grown man bends inside), tool-marked walls, disc stone runs in a cut groove/track across the facade. No columns, no steps up, no church forms, no candles/lamps burning inside.

## The grave clothes
- **[SPECIFIED]** PLURAL wound linen cloths, packed with spices: "wound it in linen clothes with the spices, as the manner of the Jews is to bury" (John 19:40); "a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound weight" (John 19:39); "saw the linen clothes lying" (John 20:5). Image: wound linen WRAPPINGS lying collapsed on the bench — layered strips/folds with residual bulk from the spices, visibly EMPTY (no head, no limbs, no body silhouette) — never one flat bedsheet, never a wrapped bundle that reads as a body.
- **[SPECIFIED]** The face-napkin is SEPARATE and wrapped together: "the napkin, that was about his head, not lying with the linen clothes, but wrapped together in a place by itself" (John 20:7). Image: small rolled/folded cloth apart from the wrappings, at the HEAD-end of the bench.

## People and time
- **[SPECIFIED]** Mary Magdalene comes EARLY, WHILE IT IS STILL DARK, finds the stone already moved, and RUNS to Peter and John: "cometh Mary Magdalene early, when it was yet dark, unto the sepulchre, and seeth the stone taken away" (John 20:1-2). Image: pre-dawn darkness; the tomb is DARK — no light glowing from inside, no angel visible in her discovery/running beats.
- **[SPECIFIED]** Peter and John RAN together; John outran Peter; Peter entered first, John entered after and believed (John 20:4-8). Image: two men running hard; inside-the-tomb beats show a man BENT/CROUCHED under the low chamber (he "went in" through a stooping entrance).
- **[SPECIFIED]** The risen Jesus later SHOWS the wounds in his hands (John 20:20, 20:27); he is alive, whole, serene. **[CONSTRAINED]** no gore, no crown of thorns on the risen Christ.
