# Independent review — codex (OK, 81s)

**Findings**

- YouTube Short TITLE: “What Thief Folds the Face-Cloth?” is the main failure. John 20:7 says “wrapped together,” not “folds,” and the title leans into the “folded napkin” legend the video does not teach.
- TikTok DESCRIPTION: “What thief folds the face-cloth?” repeats the same dishonest hook.
- YouTube Short TAGS: “folded napkin” and YouTube Long TAGS: “folded napkin meaning” are SEO bait. They target a legend/search intent that the content should avoid.
- Facebook TITLE: “No Angel. No Risen Lord. Only the Linen.” overclaims when isolated. It needs the Peter/John scope, otherwise it sounds like a denial of angels at the tomb.
- YouTube Long DESCRIPTION: “They have taken away the Lord out of the sepulchre...” is KJV wording but not marked as Scripture or cited. That is sloppy in a KJV-strict kit.
- Kit-level claim: “Copy each block straight into the platform’s upload form” conflicts with “Gates: FAILURES” and “UK-G2 KJV-strict — could not resolve KJV text for”. This is not ready-to-upload.
- TikTok/Instagram CTA: “Subscribe to Awakeden” is not native platform language. Use “Follow” there.
- Instagram DESCRIPTION: “Jesus called your faith blessed” assumes the viewer already has faith. Better: “Jesus called that faith blessed.”

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Replace every “folds/folded napkin” phrase with “wraps/wrapped together” language.
2. Scope the Facebook title so it does not imply there were no angels at the tomb.
3. Fix the failed KJV/anchor verification and cite the John 20:2 wording properly.
