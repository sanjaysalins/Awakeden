### Findings

**1. Hooky but Honest & KJV Verbatim: The "Folded" Myth**
*   **Cite:** YouTube Short TITLE: `"What Thief Folds the Face-Cloth? The Empty Tomb"`
*   **Cite:** TikTok DESCRIPTION: `"What thief folds the face-cloth?"`
*   **Critique:** The KJV explicitly says the napkin was "wrapped together" (John 20:7). By changing "wraps" to "folds", you are deliberately baiting the debunked internet legend about Jewish dinner-table customs (the "folded napkin" meaning "the master returns"). If your brand is strict text-accuracy, using a myth's vocabulary to get clicks is a bait-and-switch. 

**2. SEO Honesty: Tag Stuffing a Myth**
*   **Cite:** YouTube Short TAGS: `"folded napkin"`
*   **Cite:** YouTube Long TAGS: `"folded napkin meaning"`
*   **Critique:** You are SEO-targeting a myth that the video's doctrine actively avoids. This will pull in an audience expecting the myth and leave them disappointed. It is dishonest tagging.

**3. Hooky but Honest: Contradicting Scripture**
*   **Cite:** Facebook TITLE: `"No Angel. No Risen Lord. Only the Linen."`
*   **Critique:** As a standalone headline, this is a flatly false claim that contradicts the rest of the chapter (Mary sees two angels in John 20:12, and Jesus in John 20:14). While accurate to the *exact second* Peter and John were in the tomb, stripping the context makes it read like a liberal/secular debunking of the resurrection. It must be scoped to John's perspective.

**4. KJV Verbatim / Formatting: Unmarked Quotes**
*   **Cite:** YouTube Long DESCRIPTION: `"They have taken away the Lord out of the sepulchre, and we know not where they have laid him."`
*   **Critique:** This is an exact, verbatim quote of John 20:2, but it is completely unmarked. Later in the exact same description, John 20:29 is properly quoted and cited `(John 20:29, KJV)`. Inconsistent sourcing looks sloppy and invites accusations of misquoting. 

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Replace "folds" with "wraps" in all titles/descriptions, and purge "folded napkin" and "folded napkin meaning" from all tags.
2. Scope the Facebook title to John's perspective (e.g., "John Saw No Angel... Only the Linen") so it doesn't read as a blanket denial of the resurrection appearances.
3. Add quotation marks and a proper `(John 20:2, KJV)` citation to the unmarked Mary Magdalene quote in the YouTube Long description.
