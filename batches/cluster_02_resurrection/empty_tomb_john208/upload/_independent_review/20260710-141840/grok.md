### HOOKY BUT HONEST

**YouTube Short TITLE**
```
What Thief Folds the Face-Cloth? The Empty Tomb
```
- KJV John 20:7 uses **“wrapped together”**, not “folds.”
- Kit’s own angle note under option 1 says *“the napkin wrapped apart”* — then the CHOSEN title says **Folds**.
- “Folds” pulls the **folded-napkin / master-returns** internet legend. If the video never teaches that, this is bait.

**TikTok caption**
```
What thief folds the face-cloth?
```
Same offense on a platform where the first lines *are* the pitch.

**Facebook TITLE**
```
No Angel. No Risen Lord. Only the Linen.
```
Standalone headline reads as “there was no angel / no risen Lord at the tomb.” That collides with Matthew 28:2–5, Luke 24:4, and John 20:12. Body text scopes it to Peter/John’s entry; the **title strips that scope**. Misleading for careful readers.

Title option 3 is the same line; option 1 is the CHOSEN bad short title. Better honest options already exist in the kit (2, 4, and the long-form title) and were not used for Short/Facebook.

**YouTube Long body** is safer: “John stepped inside. No angel spoke…” keeps scope. Do not promote the bare FB title as the public claim.
