You've heard this before - a curse in a garden, a serpent's head crushed under a heel. It isn't finished yet. That crushing was never handed to a warrior. Scripture calls Him the God of peace, not force. Paul writes this to ordinary believers in Rome, echoing Eden's promise: 'And the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.' If Christ won at the cross, why still future? Because the blow already fell - the fight isn't still in doubt. You want to be the one who crushes what's crushing you - your willpower, your vigilance. But that head was never yours to crush. The garden's promise isn't old history - it's Christ, finishing what He won. Stand where His grace has set your feet.
