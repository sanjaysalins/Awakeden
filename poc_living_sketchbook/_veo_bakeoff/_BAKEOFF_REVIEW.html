It does NOT reliably execute a designed/cued gesture (Test 3) — it just defaults to full stillness instead, so Kling stays the tool for genuine acting-spread beats. And it needs positive-only prompt phrasing on bright/glowing content — negative "no glitter" language doesn't work on veo the way it does on Kling.
