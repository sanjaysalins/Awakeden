Real defects caught before they shipped

  • SP-G6 s42's first render came back as three equal side-by-side panels with visible torn-paper borders -- a real violation of this project's locked "vignettes never in panels" rule, caused by briefing the prompt as "on the LEFT... in the CENTER... on the RIGHT" (reads like three separate boxes). Rewrote it to match this project's own PROVEN multi-vignette recipe from Day of Atonement: one dominant near-foreground figure (Jesus, vivid, gold) with the other two vignettes staggered at different depths behind Him, smaller and duller, blending into shared paper texture. Re-rendered clean.
  • Camera s41's paid clip was tried twice and both providers invented content on this densely-detailed still (dozens of fanned book pages): a duration=6 wasn't valid for seedance1_5 (only 4/8/12 -- the estimator doesn't validate this, only the live API call does), so it silently fell back to Kling, which then re-folded several page-tips between frames. The retry on the intended Seedance provider (duration fixed to 8s) instead bloomed new ink-blot marks that weren't in the source, visible within 3 seconds. Two different providers hallucinating the same still is the signal to stop paying and go straight to a $0 procedural camera move -- replaced with a gentle real pan across the same pixels, zero risk of invention. Saved to memory: this is the reason to prefer $0 devices FIRST on busy/detailed multi-object stills, not as a fallback after burning money.

Gates -- real, iterative fixes (not noise-chasing)

motion_lint flagged 5 real FROZEN-SPREAD FAILs (s36, s37, s38, s39, s45) plus a STATIC-RUN warning (4 consecutive low-motion spreads in a row). All 5 are genuinely quiet-by-design beats (a thread sprouting, a cooled desk, a margin note, a naming-plate tail) -- the fix wasn't to invent motion that isn't there, it was to make sure each device's own real motion actually registers on the gate's whole-frame luminance metric:

  • s37, s45 (thread devices) -- thin gold line on a huge frame barely moved the metric even with a real fade-in; widened the stroke (10px -> 26px, matching the same fix batch 4 already proved on s21/s25).
  • s36, s39 (naming-plate tail / keeper-hand margin note) -- added a line_boil grain pass on top of the existing content, same $0 post-process already proven on s27/s23. Cleared on the first try for s36; s39 needed the grain amount pushed from 0.5 to 0.8 on a second pass.
  • s38 (raking-light) -- the hardest one. Widened the sweep band and raised its strength twice, still short (0.020 -> 0.059 -> 0.091); raking-light alone had diminishing returns past a point. Added a supplementary line_boil pass rather than pushing the sweep to an unnatural, obvious-video-filter strength -- cleared on the third attempt, eye-checked to confirm it still reads as a natural, quiet, cooled room and not a filter effect.

layer_check: s36 needed registering in EXTERNAL_LETTERING (real hand-lettering, the naming plate's own shared asset) -- done; only the not-yet-built future spreads (s47/s53/s56) show FAIL now, expected.
