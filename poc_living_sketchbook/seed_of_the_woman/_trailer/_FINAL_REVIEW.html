Real problems caught along the way

  • You caught a real one: the first running-couple clip (Kling, real character motion) had genuine face distortion under dense frame sampling — Adam and Eve's features were subtly warping frame to frame. Replaced with a $0 camera push over the same approved still: zero distortion possible since it's the same pixels re-cropped, not regenerated.
  • Applying that lesson project-wide caught a second, quieter case myself: the "hiding behind the roots" shot pushed the camera in far tighter than asked and changed Eve's expression (mouth opening) despite an explicit "hold exact expression" instruction. Fixed the same way.
  • Every other human/creature shot was either distant enough that identity drift doesn't read (the sentencing, the cross) or non-human (the fruit, the shadow) — those were left as real paid motion since the risk genuinely doesn't apply there.
  • Hit a run of transient Higgsfield API timeouts mid-batch — not content rejections, just slow responses. Cost some real retry spend (~$0.90) figuring that out, tracked honestly in the ledger.
  • Follow-up fix (next session): you caught a real AI motion glitch in the tomb doorway around 0:22 — a subtle geometric wobble reading as "gate opening and closing." Confirmed by frame-by-frame and frame-difference analysis: the wobble's real onset is ~0.55-0.7s into that clip; the first 0.5s is clean. Fixed with a $0 trim (cut S10 to its clean first 0.5s) rather than a re-render — no new paid spend. The removed 1.7s was recovered by holding the montage's own closing tree/cross frame a little longer, so the total trailer length and narration sync are unchanged.
  • Second follow-up: you flagged the closing title card as a blank black screen. Rebuilt it over the film's own s45_eden_to_cross.png hero still (the same image the montage already lands on, so the title fades in as a continuation rather than a cut to black) with a dark vertical scrim behind the text band for legibility. $0, no new render.
