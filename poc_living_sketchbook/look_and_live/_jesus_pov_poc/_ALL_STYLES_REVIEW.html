Everything rendered so far for the Jesus-POV Look and Live style pivot, in order. Each round changed one thing on purpose: round 1 tested mediums, round 2 tested a reference (Prince of Egypt / Hercules), round 3 tested how to make that reference feel like it was made today, round 4 tested a stripped-down “viral edit” composition, round 5 tested whether a bright version of that language holds together across five different scenes, round 6 tested color schemes on the Landing (cross) scene, round 7 diagnosed why those didn't match Moses/Nicodemus, and round 8 fixed it: Christ's bare skin now gets the exact same flat-silhouette treatment Moses's robe does.
