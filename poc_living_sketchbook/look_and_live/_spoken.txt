You'd think the cure for a snakebite would be anything but a snake. God put a bronze one on a pole and said look. Not medicine. Not an antidote for the wound. Just a look at the thing that was killing them. "Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live... if a serpent had bitten any man, when he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived." Call it strange if you like. But God chose the exact shape of their death to become the shape of their life. We keep reaching for our own cure - our own effort. God hung the remedy in plain sight and asked only that we look. The cure was always shaped like the curse, lifted up - and so was Christ. Look at what was lifted up for you, and live.
