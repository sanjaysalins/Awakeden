You'd think the cure for a snakebite would be anything but a snake. God put a bronze one on a pole and said look. Not medicine. Not an antidote for the wound. Just a look at the thing that was killing them. "Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live... if a serpent had bitten any man, when he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived."
