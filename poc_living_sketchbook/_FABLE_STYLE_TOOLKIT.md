# Fable — the living-sketchbook STYLE TOOLKIT (round 5, funded bake-off)

**Date:** 2026-07-30 · **Brief:** the user funded up to 200cr to design and PROVE complementary
sketchbook styles beyond Style 1 — "some will have hero animation, some will need more text and
info graphics, some could be fun and some could be serious... part of our tool kit to use as
skills that we can apply as we need."

**What was actually built:** 5 new styles, 12 real nano_banana_pro stills (10 first-pass + 2
QC re-rolls), 3 real animation tests (1 Kling, 2 Seedance), every render eye-verified at full
resolution against SKILL.md §8a before it counted. Every job ledgered under `LS_StyleBakeoff`
in `data/spend_ledger.jsonl`. All outputs in `poc_living_sketchbook/_style_bakeoff/`.
Gallery: `_style_bakeoff/_STYLE_BAKEOFF_REVIEW.html`.

**The design idea in one line:** a real sketchbook is not one drawing over and over — it is a
few finished paintings surrounded by fast studies, working diagrams, warm storybook pages,
grave night pages, and one or two gold-ground plates the artist saved up for. Style 1 stays
the spine; these five are the registers the spine reaches for when an episode's content asks.

Rules carried into EVERY style (none of this is per-style negotiable): doctrine sound + proven
both ways; whole Bible through Jesus; KJV verbatim when verses appear (none baked into pixels
here — references only); period lock (sandals/tunics/scrolls); no named artist/book in
prompts; positive end-state wording; §8a full-res QC on every still; gold = His glory only.

## Style 1 — WATERCOLOR WASH (the spine — unchanged, for reference)

The locked living-sketchbook look: graphite-and-ink + muted watercolor wash on warm cream,
torn collage edges, halftone dots, gold-leaf strip. The default narrative register. Everything
below is a COMPLEMENT, not a competitor.

## Style 2 — CHARCOAL GESTURE ("the field study")

- **Prompt approach:** vine-charcoal gesture study on warm gray toned newsprint; strokes
  smudged/dragged by thumb; sweeping kinetic motion lines; unfinished trailing edges; white
  chalk lights; ONE dry ink-red accent max; courtroom-artist-at-speed energy. No lettering.
- **What it's for:** action and transitional beats that don't earn full hero rendering —
  urgency, motion, crowd chaos. The "fast" register that makes Style 1's finished spreads
  feel MORE finished by contrast.
- **Series fit (real episodes):** Miracles as Signs (temple-adjacent action, storm rowing,
  crowds pressing); The Last Week's kinetic hours (cleansing the temple — rendered here,
  Matt 21:12, correctly WITHOUT the John-2 whip); Encounters/Questions transitional beats
  (Bartimaeus rising, Mark 10:50 — rendered); Resurrection on Trial's re-enacted objections.
- **Proof stills:** `style2_gesture_temple.png` (strong; flag: Jesus's feet read bare vs
  canon sandals — forgivable at gesture looseness, name sandals explicitly in production),
  `style2_gesture_bartimaeus.png` (v2 — v1 rendered separate trouser legs, the exact §8a.2
  drift; re-rolled with ONE-continuous-garment hem language, now clean).
- **Animation-ready?** [PENDING — Seedance stress test in flight; my prior: NO for generative
  motion, YES for $0 line-boil/parallax/raking-light. Verdict below after the filmstrip.]
- **Cost:** 2cr/still. **Confidence: HIGH as a still register.**

## Style 3 — SCHOLAR'S MARGIN ("the working diagram")

- **Prompt approach:** iron-gall sepia ink study sheet on aged parchment, Renaissance-
  workshop-notebook idiom: small precise diagrams, ruled construction lines + compass arcs,
  hand-drawn open-chevron comparison arrows, cross-hatched shading, SHORT hand-lettered
  antique-serif labels (scripture references in rubric-red), exactly ONE gold-leaf element —
  always the Christ element. A scholar's own analysis, never a corporate slide.
- **What it's for:** the typology/evidence/timeline series — content whose POINT is a
  relationship between two things, which a single narrative image cannot say. This is the
  still-layer Measuring Reed / Chrono Sweep / Voyage Camera were always waiting to sit on.
- **Series fit (real episodes):** Types & Shadows (all 8 episodes are two-panel comparisons
  by construction — bronze serpent/cross rendered here, Num 21:9 → John 3:14); Jesus in the
  OT (all 10 — prophecy→fulfilment IS this sheet); Resurrection on Trial (objection→answer
  as an evidence sheet); The Last Week (the hour-by-hour spine — timeline rendered here).
- **Proof stills:** `style3_margin_typology.png` — the native lettering test PASSED:
  "NUMBERS 21" / "JOHN 3" rendered letter-perfect in rubric-red; serpent on a plain pole
  (correctly NOT cross-shaped — the type points, it isn't identical); gold only on the
  cross. Honest flag: faint garbled pseudo-writing scraps in the far margins — illegible
  texture at video scale, but production prompts add "margins bare of any writing."
  `style3_margin_lastweek.png` — the zero-text variant came back perfectly clean (palm →
  temple → cup → gold cross → rolled-stone tomb down one ruled line). Flag: temple vignette
  reads generic Greco-Roman pediment, not Herodian facade — name it in production.
- **Lettering doctrine:** baked text stays SHORT (references, single words — never KJV
  sentences; verses remain deterministic Scribed-Ink overlays). Any spread carrying baked
  text is never generatively animated (never-animate-writing rule) — it takes $0 deterministic
  treatment only (raking-light, measuring-reed, annotators-circle, grid camera).
- **Animation-ready?** By design NO generative motion — this style's motion language is the
  $0 deterministic device family, which is exactly why it can carry baked lettering safely.
- **Cost:** 2cr/still. **Confidence: HIGHEST of the round — it fills the biggest gap.**

## Style 4 — HEARTH STORYBOOK ("the parable page")

- **Prompt approach:** gouache + colored pencil on cream cotton paper; soft rounded
  brushwork; honey-amber/olive/terracotta; golden-hour light; dry-brush grain; softly
  feathered painted edges; "tender folk-tale warmth carried with real human dignity — never
  cartoonish, never cute." No lettering.
- **What it's for:** the storytelling register — parables told AS stories, inviting and warm,
  without dropping the stakes. Also the natural children's-adjacent entry point.
- **Series fit (real episodes):** Parables of Jesus (The Father Who Ran — rendered, Luke
  15:20, robes hitched in his fists because a patriarch running was shameful, the son far
  off; the sower — rendered, Matt 13:3-4 with all four grounds READABLE: wayside+birds,
  stony, thorns, good earth); lighter Encounters (Zacchaeus); I AM pastoral images (Good
  Shepherd, Door of the sheep, True Vine).
- **Proof stills:** `style4_hearth_father_ran.png`, `style4_hearth_sower.png`.
- **Honest flags:** the father's face drifts one notch too animated-film — the exact "cute"
  edge the design brief warned about; iteration direction is folk-storybook texture (older
  print-illustration feel), not rounder faces. The sower's over-mantle drapes slightly like
  a European hooded cape rather than a simlah. Both are one-iteration fixes, not style
  failures — the sower page is already strong.
- **Animation-ready?** Plausibly (soft painted surfaces are Seedance-friendly), untested this
  round — I spent the animation budget where the answer was less predictable.
- **Cost:** 2cr/still. **Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH, needs one face-register iteration.**

## Style 5 — PASSION VIGIL ("the grave page")

- **Prompt approach:** grave chiaroscuro ink on ash-gray toned paper; near-black wash masses
  holding most of the page; ONE pale cold light source; slate/ash/bone palette; at most one
  dried-blood red accent; "the shadow and the emptiness carry the weight." No lettering.
- **What it's for:** Passion-week weight and any beat where Style 1's warmth would be wrong —
  restraint as reverence.
- **Series fit (real episodes):** The 7 Words from the Cross (all 7); The Last Week's final
  hours (Gethsemane — rendered, Matt 26:39; the darkness — rendered, Matt 27:45); the heavy
  Questions episodes (Could You Not Watch With Me One Hour); Encounters: the thief on the
  cross.
- **Proof stills:** `style5_vigil_gethsemane.png` — the best still of the round: one
  moonbeam, Jesus fallen forward kneeling (canon face/hair held with NO anchor chained),
  three sleeping disciples as shadow shapes, the single red accent landing on his sash.
  `style5_vigil_darkness.png` — the model rendered the page itself creased and folded (the
  paper grieving — a found device worth keeping deliberately). Honest flags: the washes
  around the veiled sun drift toward reading as weather (the locked fact: darkness, NOT a
  storm — production wording should say "cloudless, the sun itself failing"); TWO small red
  marks at the cross's foot where the style budget allows one deliberate accent — strip
  before production, and never feed marks like that to Kling (it regenerates blood).
- **Animation-ready?** [PENDING — Seedance 8s moonbeam test in flight; verdict below.]
- **Cost:** 2cr/still. **Confidence: HIGH — hero-tier on first pass.**

## Style 6 — GILDED PROCLAMATION ("the gold-ground plate") — my own 5th addition

- **The gap it fills:** deity-claim and glory beats (I AM, Names & Titles, Revelation,
  Transfiguration) where even Style 1's gold-leaf STRIP is too quiet. Palette theology says
  gold = His glory only — this register lets glory own the whole ground, and its scarcity
  (1-2 plates per episode, ever) is what keeps the theology of gold expensive.
- **Prompt approach:** illuminated-manuscript plate on aged vellum; the scene's LIGHT
  rendered as burnished real gold leaf (visible seams + burnish marks) over iron-gall
  underdrawing; lapis + vermilion mineral accents; psalter-miniature formality with
  accurate ancient-Judean dress. No lettering.
- **Series fit (real episodes):** The 7 I AM Statements (the claim beat each episode); Names
  and Titles (Alpha and Omega, King of Kings, Lion of Judah); Jesus in the OT (The Great
  I AM — burning bush rendered here, Ex 3:14→John 8:58; theophany beats); Miracles as Signs
  glory pivots (Transfiguration rendered, Matt 17:2); every episode's LANDING spread when
  the landing is a glory claim.
- **Proof stills:** `style6_gilded_bush.png` (v2 — v1 grew an ambiguous blue/red drape that
  read as a cropped figure's garment; re-rolled with claim-every-region wording: bare ochre
  rock all sides, three-color discipline, sandals held — now a clean museum-grade leaf).
  `style6_gilded_transfiguration.png` — the round's showpiece: full gold ground, Christ's
  face rendered at icon grade WITHOUT any anchor chained, luminous Moses/Elijah, disciples
  fallen in lapis shadow.
- **Honest flags for the user:** the Transfiguration render went fully Byzantine — closer to
  an Orthodox icon leaf than a sketchbook page. I think that formality is exactly what a
  glory register is FOR, but it is a real voice decision for the show, not mine to bury:
  say the word and I iterate it toward "illuminated but hand-sketched." Also: Jesus reads
  as an emerging bust, not standing on the summit as prompted (icon idiom won — arguably
  stronger); his raiment is cream rather than "white as the light" (the text's own detail —
  production prompt should insist on blazing white).
- **Animation-ready?** [PENDING — Kling 5s gold-sheen-breathing test in flight; verdict
  below.]
- **Cost:** 2cr/still. **Confidence: HIGH as a register; MEDIUM on voice-fit (user call).**

## What I did NOT spend on, deliberately
