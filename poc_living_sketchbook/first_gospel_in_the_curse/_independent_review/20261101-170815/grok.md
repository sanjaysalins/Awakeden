# Independent review — grok (OK, 133s)

The quoted sentence matches Genesis 3:15 KJV word for word. The protoevangelium reading is sound. The problems are the slogan built on top of it, the lag before the real turn, and a closer that leaves this text’s images.

**Hook: 6/10.** The first five seconds are only “Dust on their skin, fig leaves tied with shaking hands.” That is mood. The turn — God speaks to the snake before He sentences them — arrives after the scroll has moved on. “Tied” is also looser than the text’s “sewed” (Genesis 3:7). “Dust on their skin” never comes back, and the dust sentence is spoken later, to Adam.

**“Call it a curse formula — the order still stands: rescue spoken before ruin.”** Genesis shows one speech order. It does not give a standing formula. “The order still stands” makes that order a law. In the story the promise does not replace the sentence. Pain, sweat, death, and exile are still spoken after Genesis 3:15. “Grace was spoken before your sentence” can be heard as if the sentence is skipped.

**“Buries a promise for them inside that curse.”** God says the promise out loud to the serpent. “Buries” makes it sound hidden. A first-time listener then gets “thee / thy seed / it shall bruise” with no plain gloss until much later.

**“Jesus, the Seed who crushed the serpent’s head.”** The quote just said “bruise,” and “shall bruise” is still ahead in the garden. Romans 16:20 is also “shall bruise.” “Crushed,” as a finished fact, is a different word and a different tense from the line just read. The heel is dropped, so half the quoted promise never lands.

**“Before you'd done one thing to earn it, or even asked.”** The grace claim is right: they had not earned it or asked. “Done one thing” can also be heard as “before they had done anything.” They had already eaten, sewn leaves, and hidden. The promise comes before the spoken sentence, not before the sin.

**Landing: 6/10.** “Come meet the Voice that spoke first” introduces “the Voice,” which this piece never built. The working images were the snake, the seed, the head, and the sentence spoken second. “Come meet the Voice” could close a creation piece or a John 1 piece. It does not land in the bones of this one.

The spine (promise spoken before their sentence → Jesus the seed → grace before your sentence) is one thread. The “formula” label and the “Voice” closer are what bend it.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Delete “curse formula” and “the order still stands.” Say only this: in this speech God promised the woman’s seed before He sentenced Eve and Adam, and He still spoke their sentence after.
2. Put that order in the first sentence, then say in plain words what the quote means (a child of the woman will strike the snake’s head), and keep “bruise” unless you explain the cross in one clause.
3. Close on seed, head, and the word spoken before the sentence. Cut “come meet the Voice that spoke first.”
