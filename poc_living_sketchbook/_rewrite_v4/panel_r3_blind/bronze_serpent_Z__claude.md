1. **WHO / WHAT / WHY**
   - Who: Moses, an old leader, tells his own story.
   - What: His people were bitten by snakes and dying. God told him to make a brass snake and lift it on a pole. Anyone who looked at it lived.
   - Why it matters: Jesus said this was a picture of Him on the cross. Look to Him in trust, and live.

2. **Audience:** People curious about the Bible, and Christians who want to see how the Old Testament points to Jesus.

3. **Takeaway:** As the bitten looked at the snake and lived, so you can believe in Jesus on the cross and live.

4. **Global:**
   - "Lifted up the serpent in the wilderness" (old words: "serpent", "wilderness").
   - "made a curse for us" is unclear. Who cursed? Why?
   - "Son of man" is odd. I'd think it means a person's son.
   - "A later writer, Paul" came from nowhere.
   - "Believe in Jesus" and "live" are vague. Live how? Forever?
   - "Cross" is not explained. I'd need to know it was a death by hanging.
   - "Brass", "pole", "sinned" are OK but "sinned" needs a little context.

5. **Bible:**
   - The John 3:14 quote is fine: "as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up."
   - Galatians 3:13 says "being made a curse for us". The narration drops "being", which is a small change. It is also not flagged as a quote.
   - Numbers 21 says people looked at the snake and lived. This is right.
   - "God's cure? A snake" is a bit loose. In Numbers, God told Moses to make the snake, so it is fair.
   - "Anyone bitten who looked at it, lived" is correct.
   - "Lifted up: He meant Himself, on a cross" is the Christian reading, but John 3 itself and 12:32-33 support it. It's stated as fact. That is fine for a Christian short, though it could say "Jesus said".
   - "Believe in Jesus and live" matches John 3:15. It is not exactly the full gospel, because it leaves out "eternal life".
   - Nothing is heretical.

6. **Hook / payoff**
   - Hook: 8/10. "God's cure? A snake." is odd and I wanted to know why.
   - Payoff: 7/10. The answer comes at "The cure looked like the thing that was killing them", then Jesus. The cross link is clear, but I'm still unsure why a snake stands for Jesus. The "curse" line helps, but only if I understand it.

7. **Landing:** 8/10. It uses the bitten, the snake, and "lifted up", so it is tied to this story. The "Believe in Jesus and live" part is a stock ending, but the first half is specific.

8. **Clarity:** 7/10. It is not yet the best version. The ONE change: explain the link in plain words, for example "Jesus took on our sin, like the snake looked like the poison." Replace "made a curse for us" with that simple line.
