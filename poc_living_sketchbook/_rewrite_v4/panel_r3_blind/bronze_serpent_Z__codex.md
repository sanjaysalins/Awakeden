1. **WHO:** Moses tells his story and connects it to Jesus.
   **WHAT:** God sent serpents after Israel sinned; bitten people lived when they looked at the brass serpent.
   **WHY:** Jesus used this event to explain His lifting up and eternal life through faith.

2. **AUDIENCE:** A general audience, especially people unfamiliar with this Bible story.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** As bitten Israelites looked and lived, believers in the crucified Jesus receive eternal life.

4. **GLOBAL:** NONE.

5. **BIBLE:** “God did not take the snakes away” is an inference; Numbers 21 does not explicitly say that. Both quotations match the KJV. Jesus Himself makes the connection in John 3:14–15, and John 12:32–33 supports the cross explanation. “Live” should mean eternal life, not protection from physical death.

6. **HOOK: 9/10.** Yes—the surprising cure makes me want the explanation. **PAYOFF: 8/10.** It connects the serpent to Jesus, though “made a curse” could use explanation.

7. **LANDING: 9/10.** The bitten people looking at a snake make this ending specific to this story.

8. **CLARITY: 9/10.** Not quite the best possible. My one change: end with “Believe in Jesus, lifted up on the cross, and have everlasting life.”
