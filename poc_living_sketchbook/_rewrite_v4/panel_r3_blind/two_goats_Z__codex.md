1. WHO: Aaron narrates his priestly work and points to Jesus.
   WHAT: One goat died; another carried confessed sins into the wilderness.
   WHY: The film connects these pictures with Jesus’ sacrifice and our access to God.

2. WHO FOR: Christians and curious newcomers who need the Old Testament ceremony explained.

3. MAIN TAKEAWAY: Jesus’ sacrifice deals with sin and opens the way to God.

4. GLOBAL: “The living goat could not pay.” Pay what, and in what sense?

5. BIBLE: Both quotations match the KJV. The two goats depicting two aspects of Jesus’ work is a Christian interpretation, not something Leviticus explicitly states. “The living goat could not pay” needs care: neither goat could actually take away sins (Hebrews 10:4), and Leviticus 16:10 assigns the living goat an atoning role.

6. HOOK: Yes, I wanted the answer. Hook: **9/10**. Payoff: **7/10**—the connection is memorable, but “did both” leaves the explanation compressed.

7. LANDING: **6/10**. “Walk through, to God” could close another short about the torn curtain, though Aaron’s yearly entrance gives it context.

8. CLARITY: **8/10**. Not the best possible version. ONE CHANGE: Replace the central comparison with: “One pictured death. One pictured sins carried away. Neither could take away sins. Jesus’ sacrifice does.”
