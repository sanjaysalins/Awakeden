1. **WHO:** A guy named Moses. **WHAT:** God sent killer snakes, then told him to put a metal snake on a pole so bitten people could look at it and survive. **WHY:** It was a preview of Jesus being put on a cross to save people later.
2. **WHO IS THIS FOR:** Christians, or people trying to understand how random old Bible stories connect to Jesus. 
3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** Just like looking at the snake saved those people from dying, believing in Jesus on the cross saves you.
4. **GLOBAL:** "made a curse for us." (Wait, Jesus is a curse? That makes zero sense to me without more context).
5. **BIBLE:** NONE. (Breaking character to check KJV: John 3:14 and Galatians 3:13 are quoted perfectly, and Jesus explicitly made this exact doctrinal claim about Himself).
6. **HOOK / PAYOFF:** Hook: **9/10**. ("God's cure? A snake" is weird and made me stop scrolling). Payoff: **8/10**. The connection of both being "lifted up" to save people made sense.
7. **LANDING:** **9/10**. It specifically references "the bitten" and "a snake." You couldn't paste this ending onto a story about Noah's Ark or David and Goliath.
8. **OVERALL CLARITY:** **7/10**. The ONE thing I would change: cut the line about Paul and being "made a curse." It introduces a heavy, confusing new idea right at the very end when the snake-to-cross comparison was already making perfect sense on its own.
