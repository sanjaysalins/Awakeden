1. **WHO:** It's about a prostitute named Rahab who lived inside a city wall.
**WHAT:** She hid some spies, hung a red cord out her window, and her family survived when the whole wall collapsed.
**WHY:** Because she trusted their God, she was saved and ended up in Jesus's family line.

2. **WHO IS THIS FOR:** People who feel helpless and need to know they just have to trust God to be saved.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** You don't have to be strong enough to stop disaster yourself; you just have to trust Jesus.

4. **GLOBAL:** "taketh away the sin of the world" (the word "taketh" sounds super old and weird, I'd probably tune out for a second).

5. **BIBLE:** NONE. (The KJV quotes "the wall fell down flat" [Joshua 6:20], "By faith" [Hebrews 11:31], and "taketh away the sin of the world" [John 1:29] are exact, and her place in the genealogy is accurate to Matthew 1).

6. **HOOK & PAYOFF:** 
- **Hook: 9/10.** It sounded like an action movie trailer; I definitely wanted to know how one family survived.
- **Payoff: 6/10.** The survival story was cool, but the ending suddenly pivoted into a church message rather than just wrapping up the history.

7. **LANDING: 8/10.** The word "Tie" specifically links back to her tying the red cord, and "invite everyone you love" links back to her saving her family. It wouldn't make much sense slapped onto a completely different story.

8. **OVERALL CLARITY: 9/10.** It's very easy to follow. 
**ONE CHANGE:** Swap the old-school "taketh" for a normal word like "takes" so it doesn't break the modern, conversational flow.
