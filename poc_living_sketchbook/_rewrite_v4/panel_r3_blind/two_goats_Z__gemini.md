1. **WHO**: An ancient high priest named Aaron.
**WHAT**: He explains a weird old ritual with two goats that Jesus eventually replaced by doing both of their jobs at once.
**WHY**: It matters because the barrier to God is gone and anyone can go to Him now.

2. **WHO IS THIS FOR**: People scrolling on their phones who feel like God is blocked off behind rules or religion.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY**: Jesus finished what ancient rituals couldn't, tearing down the barrier so we can access God directly.

4. **GLOBAL**: "to put away sin by the sacrifice of himself" (sounds like thick, old textbook English). Also, "watch my goats" sounds a little funny/random.

5. **BIBLE**: NONE. (The quotes match Hebrews 9:26 and Matthew 27:51 perfectly in the KJV, and the Day of Atonement typology is orthodox Christian doctrine). 

6. **HOOK / PAYOFF**: 
**Hook**: 9 (Starting with "Why two?" is a great quick mystery).
**Payoff**: 10 ("The dead goat could not walk away. The living goat could not pay. Jesus... did both" perfectly and satisfyingly answers the hook).

7. **LANDING**: 9. You couldn't paste "the curtain is open" onto a video about Noah's Ark or David and Goliath; it only works here because the script set up the curtain earlier. 

8. **OVERALL CLARITY**: 9. Is it the best version possible? Almost. The ONE thing I would change is the phrase "so watch my goats." It sounds slightly comical for a serious video—saying something like "look at these two goats" would keep the teenager from laughing at the wrong moment.
