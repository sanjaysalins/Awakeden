## MY NARRATION

1. A man cheated his own brother. Now the brother wants to kill him. So he runs, alone, with nowhere to go.
2. *(silence, 0.6s: wide empty hills, a small figure walking)*
3. The book of Genesis says night fell, and he lay down in the open. He took a stone from the ground and put it under his head.
4. *(silence, 0.8s: Jacob asleep, stone pillow, stars)*
5. He dreamed. "Behold a ladder set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven: and behold the angels of God ascending and descending on it."
6. Then God Himself stood above the ladder. He spoke to the runaway. "Behold, I am with thee."
7. *(silence, 0.6s: gold thread glows above the sleeper)*
8. God also said: "I will not leave thee."
9. Jacob woke up afraid and amazed. He said: "Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not."
10. *(silence, 0.8s: Jacob sitting up, hand on the stone)*
11. Many years later, Jesus told a man named Nathanael: "Hereafter ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man."
12. The runaway saw a ladder in a dream. Jesus said the way between heaven and earth is open to those who follow him.
13. *(silence, 1.2s: the stone, the ladder, the thread, held still)*
14. God came to the runaway first, while he still held the stone. And He did not leave.

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY
- Line 1 now says plainly he cheated his brother and why he runs (P6, P5). Opens a question: where does a runaway go?
- Removed "guilt and exhaustion" and "guilty man's dream"; the text now only states what he did, what happened, where he slept (P5).
- All quotes are single unspliced KJV runs, no "..." (P2). I split "I am with thee" and "I will not leave thee" into two separate quotes, each verbatim from different parts of v15.
- Dropped "God does not audition people" (P7).
- Dropped "Jesus said He is that ladder". Line 11 quotes John 1:51 verbatim instead (P3). Line 12 is a plain gloss; see risk.
- Dropped "How dreadful is this place". Kept only "afraid and amazed" in plain words plus the verse 16 quote (P4).
- New closing line 14 is built from the stone and "did not leave", not a stock invite (P1). No question, no "Ready to".

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT
The ladder quote, "I am with thee", "I knew it not", and the Jesus link to Nathanael. These carry the story and are real text.

## THE FIRST TWO SECONDS AND THE MOMENT THE VIEWER KNOWS WHO TO ROOT FOR
First heard: "A man cheated his own brother." A newcomer knows a man is in trouble by second 3 to 4, and that the film is about the runaway by "he runs, alone" at about second 8.

## THE RISK
Line 12 is a paraphrase of what the quote means and could read as a claim beyond the text ("those who follow him"); a Bible reader may object, and a newcomer may not see how the ladder links to Jesus. Also, "I will not leave thee" is separated from "I am with thee" and presented as one saying, but they are in the same verse, so this is fine.
