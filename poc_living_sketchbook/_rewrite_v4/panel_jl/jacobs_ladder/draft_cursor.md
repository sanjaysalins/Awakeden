## MY NARRATION
1. In Genesis, Jacob cheated his brother Esau out of their father's blessing. Esau said, then will I slay my brother Jacob. Jacob ran.
2. [2s — wide: lone figure on the road at dusk]
3. Because the sun was set, he took of the stones of that place, and put them for his pillows, and lay down in that place to sleep.
4. [1.5s — close: head on the stone]
5. And he dreamed, and behold a ladder set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven: and behold the angels of God ascending and descending on it.
6. [1s — ladder rises in the dream]
7. The LORD stood above the ladder. And, behold, I am with thee, and will keep thee in all places whither thou goest; for I will not leave thee, until I have done that which I have spoken to thee of.
8. [1s — hold on the promise]
9. When he woke, he said, Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not.
10. Holy awe filled him—this is none other but the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven.
11. You may lie in an ordinary place tonight and not know God is there.
12. [1.5s — ink thread toward the New Testament]
13. Jesus said, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Hereafter ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man.
14. [2s — heaven open above the sleeping ground]
15. A stone pillow, an open field, and I will not leave thee—God said it to the runaway before he took one step toward home.

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY
- **P1:** Replaced the stock “Ready to stop running…” invite with a concrete, story-owned close (stone, open field, “I will not leave thee,” runaway).
- **P2:** Removed ellipses; each KJV line is one contiguous verbatim run; LORD speech is 28:15 only, not mixed with 28:13.
- **P3:** Dropped “Jesus is that ladder”; kept John 1:51 quoted as Jesus actually said it.
- **P4:** Framed “dreadful” as holy awe and quoted the gate-of-heaven line without leaning the landing on archaic “dreadful.”
- **P5:** Cut guilt, exhaustion, and “guilty man’s dream”; kept deceit, threat, flight, sleep from the narrative.
- **P6:** Opened with why Jacob runs (blessing stolen, Esau’s murder intent).
- **P7:** Removed “God does not audition people.”
- **P8:** Trimmed to 142 spoken words for ~60s at a calm pace.

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY
- The runaway-on-open-ground hook and the “you are not alone / I knew it not” turn—they carry the grace beat without pressure.
- Full dream-ladder quote and the 28:15 promise before Jacob’s response—God speaks first, grace-anchored.
- Lands on Jesus only through John 1:51, not invented typology.

## THE FIRST TWO SECONDS AND THE MOMENT THE VIEWER KNOWS WHO TO ROOT FOR
In the first two seconds they hear a man who cheated his brother and is running because his brother vowed to kill him. By about second 4–5, when Jacob is named and shown fleeing to sleep on stones, they know the film is about this runaway—and that God meets him there.

## THE RISK
Typology-minded viewers may still *infer* a ladder-to-Son-of-man link though the script only quotes John 1:51; newcomers may not connect angels on the ladder to angels on the Son of man without one plain narrator bridge (omitted for word economy). “Cheated” summarizes Genesis 27; a strict reader may want “deceived” or more detail on Rebekah’s role.
