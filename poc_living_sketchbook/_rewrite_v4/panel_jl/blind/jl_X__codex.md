1. **WHO:** Jacob, with a later connection to Jesus.
   **WHAT:** Jacob fled after deceiving his father, dreamed of a ladder, and heard God promise his presence.
   **WHY:** God’s promise came before any recorded attempt by Jacob to put things right.

2. **FOR:** Christians and Bible-curious viewers exploring how Old Testament stories connect to Jesus.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** God met guilty, fleeing Jacob with a promise, and Jesus later echoed Jacob’s dream in speaking about himself.

4. **GLOBAL:** NONE.

5. **BIBLE:** The quoted excerpts match the KJV. Two qualifications: “Jacob had fixed nothing” goes beyond what the text establishes; no restitution is recorded. “Heaven opened over a runaway” is poetic interpretation—Genesis describes a dream, without explicitly saying heaven opened. The connection to Jesus is well grounded in John 1:51.

6. **HOOK: 8/10.** Yes—the deception creates immediate interest. **PAYOFF: 8/10.** God’s promises answer the question; the Jesus connection extends it.

7. **LANDING: 9/10.** The runaway, stone, and reference to Jesus make it specific to this story.

8. **CLARITY: 9/10.** Not quite the best possible. My one change: replace the final line with, “Jacob dreamed of angels ascending and descending on a ladder. Jesus said they would ascend and descend upon Him.”
