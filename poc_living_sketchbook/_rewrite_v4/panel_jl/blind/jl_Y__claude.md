1. **WHO / WHAT / WHY**
- WHO: A man named Jacob, and God. Later, a man called Jesus.
- WHAT: Jacob lied to his family and ran away. He slept on a stone. He dreamed of a ladder to heaven. God said, "I am with thee."
- WHY: God stayed with Jacob even though he did bad things and had earned nothing.

2. **Audience:** People who want to know if God stays with people who failed. Bible readers will get the most from it.

3. **Takeaway:** God's promise to be with us does not depend on being good.

4. **Hard words or lines:**
- "Son of man". I don't know why Jesus calls himself this.
- "ye shall see heaven open". The meaning is unclear to me.
- "thee" and "ye" are old English.
- "ascending and descending" is hard for a second-language listener.
- "the LORD". I don't know who this is.
- "Nathanael" comes with no setup.
- The link between Jacob's ladder and Jesus is the biggest gap. I don't see why the ladder and Jesus are the same thing.
- "the blue thread joins the gold". I only see this on screen, and I don't know what it means.

5. **Bible check:**
- The quotes look right against the KJV (Gen 28:12, 15–16; John 1:51).
- Gen 28:15 reads "I will not leave thee". Gen 28:15 also says "I am with thee". Both are fine.
- The film says Jesus "called himself the Son of man". That is correct.
- It leaves out that Jesus is the ladder or the way between heaven and earth. The film hints at it but never says it. A Christian reading is presented as if it were plain fact: Jesus takes the place of the ladder. That should be stated openly as the Christian reading.
- "Jacob slept on a stone, not knowing". Jacob did know, because he woke and said "I knew it not". That is a minor slip.
- The phrase "He had done nothing to earn anything" is fair. Grace is the theme.

6. **Hook / Payoff**
- Hook: 6/10. "Cheated... Esau planned to kill him. So Jacob ran" is clear and tense. It asks what will happen to him.
- Payoff: 5/10. The answer is "God stays with him". The Jesus jump in the middle muddles it, and the question I was left with (why Jesus?) is never answered.

7. **Landing:** 6/10. "I will not leave thee" is a strong line, but it could close many other God-and-person stories. The stone and the open sky at the end tie it to this one. The final "Jacob slept on a stone, not knowing" is specific.

8. **Clarity: 6/10.** It is not the best version possible.
- ONE change: add one plain sentence that links the ladder to Jesus. For example: "Jesus said: I am that ladder. In me, heaven and earth meet." Then cut or simplify "Son of man" to "Jesus".
