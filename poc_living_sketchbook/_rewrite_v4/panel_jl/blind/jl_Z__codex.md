1. **WHO:** Jacob, with a later connection to Jesus.
   **WHAT:** Fleeing Esau, Jacob dreamed of a ladder to heaven and heard God promise his presence.
   **WHY:** God met Jacob despite his deceit; Jesus later echoed the dream.

2. **FOR:** Christians and curious viewers, though some Bible vocabulary assumes familiarity.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** God approached guilty Jacob before Jacob put things right.

4. **GLOBAL:** NONE.

5. **BIBLE:** The quotations match the KJV excerpts. “The blessing meant for his brother” needs qualification: Isaac intended it for Esau, but God had already foretold Jacob’s precedence (Genesis 25:23). The connection to Jesus is well grounded in John 1:51, though Jesus does not explicitly call himself Jacob’s ladder.

6. **HOOK: 8. PAYOFF: 9.** Yes—the deception creates tension, and God’s promise answers what he would say to Jacob.

7. **LANDING: 9.** “Jacob never climbed that ladder” anchors the ending firmly to this story.

8. **CLARITY: 9.** Not quite the best possible. Change “the blessing meant for his brother” to “the blessing his father intended for Esau.”
