1. **WHO / WHAT / WHY**
- WHO: A man named Jacob. Also God, and later a man called Jesus.
- WHAT: Jacob lied to his father and cheated his brother. He ran away. He slept on the ground and dreamed of a ladder to heaven. God said, "I am with you."
- WHY: God came to a bad man first, before he fixed anything. Jesus said the same open heaven would be over Him.

2. **FOR WHO:** People who feel they failed and wonder if God still wants them.

3. **TAKEAWAY:** God stays with people who fail, and Jesus is the way heaven is opened to them.

4. **GLOBAL:**
- "ye shall see heaven open… upon the Son of man". It is old English, and "Son of man" is confusing. I don't know why Jesus calls himself that.
- "thee" is old English, but I can guess it.
- "the LORD" vs "God": are they the same? I'm not sure.
- "Nathanael": who is he? Why does Jesus talk to him? This jump is sudden.
- "Centuries later": I understand the time jump, but I can't see how it connects to Jacob.
- "blessing": I don't know why it was so valuable.
- "a man like that": I get it, but I'm a bit lost on who the "He" is until "Jacob" comes in line 2.

5. **BIBLE:**
- The quotes match the KJV closely. Genesis 28:15 reads "I am with thee… and will not leave thee", and John 1:51 reads "Hereafter ye shall see heaven open…". The word "Hereafter" is dropped. The clipped quote is fine, but it's still an elision.
- The claim "Jesus said it would open over Him" is a Christian reading of John 1:51 (Jesus is the ladder or the meeting place). Most Christians hold this, and the narration states it as plain fact. It needs light framing.
- "Jacob had fixed nothing. God spoke first." This is true to the text. Jacob made his vow only after he woke.
- Nothing is doctrinally wrong.

6. **HOOK / PAYOFF**
- Hook: 6/10. "He tricked his blind father" is a strong, clear opening. But it's a bare "He" with no name, and the question comes late.
- Payoff: 6/10. "What does God say to a man like that?" is answered: "I am with thee." The Jesus ending is a second answer, and it is less clear to a newcomer.

7. **LANDING:** 6/10. "Heaven opened over a runaway asleep on a stone" is specific to Jacob. But "Jesus said it would open over Him" is vague and could end other Jesus-themed shorts.

8. **CLARITY:** 6/10. The first half is clear and strong. The last paragraph jumps to Jesus and Nathanael without setup.

**ONE change:** Add one plain line before the Jesus part. For example: "Jesus, who Christians believe is God's Son, later told a man named Nathanael: heaven opens through Him. He is the ladder." That would explain who Jesus is, who Nathanael is, and why this ties back to Jacob.
