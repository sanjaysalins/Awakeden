1. **WHO / WHAT / WHY**
- Who: Jacob, a man who cheated his brother, and God.
- What: Jacob ran away. He slept outside and dreamed of a ladder with angels. God promised to stay with him.
- Why it matters: God came to Jacob first, before Jacob did anything good.

2. **Audience:** People who feel they have failed and wonder if God would still stay with them.

3. **Takeaway:** God comes to us first. We do not have to earn it.

4. **Words I'd have to re-hear:**
- "ye shall see heaven open" (old English)
- "I will not leave thee" (old English, but I can guess it)
- "the Son of man" (confusing. Is it Jesus or another person?)
- "Nathanael" (who is he?)
- "the LORD" vs "God" (are they the same?)
- "ascending and descending" (hard words)
- "Centuries later" jumps to Jesus fast. I don't see how it connects.

5. **Bible check:** Mostly NONE, but:
- The Genesis 28 quotes look right. The KJV has "and behold a ladder set up on the earth," "I will not leave thee," and "Surely the LORD is in this place; and I wot not." **"I knew it not" is not KJV.** KJV says "I wot not." That's a small misquote inside quotation marks.
- "Before Jacob had done anything to make things right" is a fair reading. It is the narrator's interpretation, not stated in the text.
- John 1:51 is quoted close to KJV: "Hereafter ye shall see heaven open." The narration drops "Hereafter."
- "Jacob never climbed that ladder" is fair. But the idea that the ladder points to Jesus is a Christian reading. The narration says Jesus himself said it, which is true, but it doesn't say Jesus is the ladder.

6. **Hook:** 6/10.
- The first line is a plain story summary. The question "What would God say to a man like that?" comes at about 10 seconds, so it is late.
- Payoff: 8/10. "I will not leave thee" and "God came to him first" answer it clearly.

7. **Landing:** 8/10. "Jacob never climbed that ladder. God came to him first." names the ladder and Jacob, so it could not close another short. It is also a little abstract. It says nothing about what the viewer should do or feel.

8. **Clarity:** 7/10.
- It's not the best version yet.
- The one change I'd make is the Jesus and Nathanael section. Say it simply: "Jesus said, *I am that ladder. Heaven and earth meet in me.*" Then a newcomer would see why Jesus is in a story about Jacob.
