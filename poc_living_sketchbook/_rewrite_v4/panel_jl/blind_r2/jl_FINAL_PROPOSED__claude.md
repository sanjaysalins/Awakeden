1. **Who/what/why**
- WHO: Jacob, a man who cheated his brother and father. God speaks to him.
- WHAT: Jacob ran away, slept outside, dreamed of a ladder with angels. God promised to stay with him.
- WHY: God came to him first, before he fixed anything.

2. **For:** Anyone who feels they have done wrong and wonders if God still cares. Also Bible readers.

3. **Takeaway:** God comes to us first, before we do anything to deserve it.

4. **Global:**
- "Blessing" (what was it, and why did it matter?)
- "ascending and descending" (hard words)
- "Son of man" (I don't know who this is)
- "Jesus used that same picture about himself" (who is Jesus? I'm not told)
- "Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not" (old English)
- "Thee" (old English)

5. **Bible:**
- The quotes look close to the KJV. I can't check every word.
- "Before Jacob did anything to make things right, God promised to stay." This is a fair reading of the text. Genesis 28 gives no repentance before the promise.
- "Jesus used that same picture about himself" is a Christian reading. It is fair, because John 1:51 links the two. It is stated as fact, which is fine for this audience.
- The first words of the quote "ye shall see heaven open" are in John 1:51. The wording seems right.
- "Centuries later" is too small. About 1,800 years is nearer.
- NONE doctrinally wrong.

6. **Hook:** 6/10. "Jacob tricked his blind father" is a clear start. The question "What would God say to a man like that?" is the real hook, and it comes after a few seconds. **Payoff:** 8/10. "God promised to stay" and "God came to him first" answer it well.

7. **Landing:** "Jacob never climbed that ladder. God came to him first." Specificity is 8/10. It uses the ladder from this story. The second sentence could close other grace stories, but the ladder part ties it to Jacob.

8. **Clarity:** 7/10.
- Not the best version yet.
