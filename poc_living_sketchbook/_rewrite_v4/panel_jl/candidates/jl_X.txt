He tricked his blind father and took his brother's blessing.
His brother wanted him dead. So Jacob ran.
[silence 1s: the road, the sun going down]
He slept on open ground, a stone for his pillow.
What does God say to a man like that?
[silence 1s: closed eyes, the stone]
Genesis says he dreamed of a ladder to heaven, and "the angels of God ascending and descending on it."
The LORD stood above it, and said: "I am with thee."
Jacob had fixed nothing. God spoke first.
"I will not leave thee."
Jacob woke and said: "Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not."
[silence 1.5s: the stone in morning light]
Centuries later, Jesus spoke about himself to a man named Nathanael: "ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man."
Heaven opened over a runaway asleep on a stone. Jesus said it would open over Him.
