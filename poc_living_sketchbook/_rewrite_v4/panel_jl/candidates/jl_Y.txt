Jacob cheated his brother Esau and tricked their blind father. Esau planned to kill him. So Jacob ran.
Alone at night, he lay down in the open, with a stone for a pillow.
[silence 0.8s: wide empty field, one gold thread starts]
He had done nothing to earn anything. Still, he dreamed of a ladder reaching to heaven, and "the angels of God ascending and descending on it."
And the LORD stood above it and said: "I am with thee."
[silence 0.8s: the ladder glows]
Jacob woke, afraid. "Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not."
Centuries later, Jesus called himself the Son of man, and told Nathanael: "ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man."
[silence 0.8s: open sky, the blue thread joins the gold]
Jacob slept on a stone, not knowing. And the promise was already spoken: "I will not leave thee."
