Jacob had deceived his father and taken the blessing meant for his brother. Now Esau wanted him dead.
He ran. What would God say to a man like that?
Alone outdoors, Jacob lay down with a stone for his pillow.
In a dream he saw "a ladder set up on the earth," with "the angels of God ascending and descending on it."
The LORD stood above it and promised: "I will not leave thee."
Before Jacob had done anything to make things right, God promised to stay.
Jacob woke afraid: "Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not."
[silence 1.5s: Jacob sits beside the stone as dawn comes]
Centuries later, Jesus told Nathanael, calling himself the Son of man: "ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man."
Jacob never climbed that ladder. God came to him first.
[silence 2s: the stone, the open sky]
