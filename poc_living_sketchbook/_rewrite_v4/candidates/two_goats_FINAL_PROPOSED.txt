One goat died. One goat lived. Why two?
I am Aaron, the high priest. I lived long before Jesus, so watch my goats.
The first I killed, to pay for the people's sin. I carried its blood behind the curtain, where God's presence was, and no one else could go.
The second I did not kill. I laid both hands on its head, and confessed the people's sins. Then it was sent away alive, into the wilderness.
