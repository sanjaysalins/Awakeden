One red cord marked one family when a whole city fell.
Her name was Rahab. She lived in Jericho's wall, and her city knew her for her sin.
Two spies came from Israel. She hid them, because she believed in their God.
They promised: "thou shalt bind this line of scarlet thread in the window".

Israel marched. The people shouted. And "the wall fell down flat".
Rahab and her family were spared.
The cord was the sign. Trusting the promise saved her. Hebrews calls that faith: "By faith".
Matthew lists her in the family line of Jesus, the One who "taketh away the sin of the world".
