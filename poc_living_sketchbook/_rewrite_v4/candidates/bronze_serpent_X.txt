Snakes were killing my people. God's cure? A snake.
I am Moses.
My people spoke against God. He sent snakes. Many died.
They cried, "We have sinned". I prayed.
God did not take the snakes away. He said: make a snake, set it on a pole.
I made one of brass, and lifted it up.
Anyone bitten who looked at it, lived.
[silence 1.5s]
The cure was shaped like what bit them.
I lived long before Jesus. But He said:
"as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up".
Lifted up: He meant Himself, on a cross.
There, Paul wrote, He was "made a curse for us".
[silence 1.5s]
The cure took the shape of the curse that bit us.
Do what my bitten people did. Look up at Jesus, lifted like my snake, and live.
