One goat died. One walked away alive. Why two?
I am Aaron, the high priest. I went in to God for the people.
I lived long before Jesus. Watch my goats.
The first I killed, and carried its blood behind the curtain between people and God.
The second lived. I laid both hands on its head, confessing the people's sins.
It was led into the desert, carrying them.
