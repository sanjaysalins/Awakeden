When Jericho fell, one family walked out alive.
A woman named Rahab lived in its wall. Her city knew her for her sin.
Two spies came from Israel. She hid them, because she believed in their God.
They promised: a red cord in her window, and her family would be spared.
[silence 1.5s]
Israel marched. Trumpets. A shout. And "the wall fell down flat".
Rahab and her family were spared.
The cord did not save her. It marked her house.
Hebrews says what saved her, in two words: "By faith".
Long after, Jesus was born, and her name is in His family line.
The woman known for sin, in the family of the One who "taketh away the sin of the world".
[silence 1.5s]
Rahab tied one red cord to a promise, and brought her family in.
Tie your trust to Jesus, and bring everyone you love.
