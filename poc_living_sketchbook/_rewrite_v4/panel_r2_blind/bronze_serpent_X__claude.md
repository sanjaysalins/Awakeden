1. **WHO / WHAT / WHY**
- Who: Moses, a leader long ago, tells his own story.
- What: His people were bitten by snakes and were dying. God told him to make a brass snake on a pole. Those who looked at it lived.
- Why it matters: Jesus on the cross is the same kind of cure. Look to Him and live.

2. **Who it's for:** People who are open to Christianity, including Bible newcomers and Christians. It is not aimed at people who are already hostile to it.

3. **Main takeaway:** The cure took the shape of the curse, so look up to Jesus on the cross and live.

4. **Global:**
- "made a curse for us" is unclear. Who cursed us, and what curse?
- "the Son of man" is odd. A newcomer won't know it means Jesus.
- "lifted up" is used with two meanings, a pole and a cross. This gets clear only after "He meant Himself."
- "brass" and "wilderness" are fine but a bit old.
- "I lived long before Jesus. But He said:" is a jump. Who is Jesus? This is the first time he appears.
- "The cure was shaped like what bit them" is OK but abstract.

5. **Bible:** Mostly fine, with a few points.
- The Moses story matches Numbers 21. "Many died" is fine.
- John 3:14 KJV reads "as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up." This quote is accurate.
- "Made a curse for us" is from Galatians 3:13. The KJV is "being made a curse for us." That is a trimmed quote and acceptable.
- The Paul line is stated as fact, but it is a Christian reading. A viewer could take "the snake is the curse Jesus became" as the Bible's own claim. Jesus does link the serpent to Himself in John 3, but the "shaped like the curse" idea is a Christian interpretation. Note also that the brass snake is not itself called a curse in Numbers.
- Moses speaking after his death about Jesus is a device. It is fine because "I lived long before Jesus" signals it.
- Overall: no real error. Slightly overstated certainty in the "took the shape of the curse" line.

6. **Hook / payoff**
- Hook: 8/10. "Snakes were killing my people. God's cure? A snake." is a clear puzzle.
- Payoff: 8/10. "The cure was shaped like what bit them" answers it directly, and the final line repeats it with Jesus as the answer.
- The payoff is a little late and a bit abstract for newcomers.

7. **Landing:** 8/10 for specificity. "Lifted like my snake" and "look up... and live" tie to this story. "Look up at Jesus" alone could close other shorts, but "my snake" anchors it.

8. **Overall clarity: 7/10.** It is not the best version yet.
- One change: explain the Jesus jump. After "I lived long before Jesus," add a short line saying who He is (for example, "God's Son, who came later"). Then "Son of man" and "made a curse" make sense. A newcomer is lost there.
