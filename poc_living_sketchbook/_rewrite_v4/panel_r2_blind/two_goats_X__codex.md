1. **WHO:** Aaron narrates; the story points to Jesus.
   **WHAT:** One goat died; another carried confessed sins into the wilderness.
   **WHY:** These pictures help explain Jesus’ sacrifice and the removal of sin.

2. **FOR:** People unfamiliar with Old Testament sacrifices, especially those exploring Christianity.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** Jesus died for our sins, takes them away, and opens access to God.

4. **GLOBAL:** “A dead goat cannot carry sin away. A living goat has not died for it.” I would re-hear this to check the distinction.

5. **BIBLE:** The quotations match the KJV. Reading both goats as pictures of Jesus is Christian interpretation; Leviticus does not explicitly make that identification. The dead/living contrast is too absolute: Hebrews 9:26 says Christ puts away sin “by the sacrifice of himself.” His death itself accomplishes removal. Hebrews 10:4 says animal blood cannot actually take away sins.

6. **HOOK:** **9/10.** Yes—the contrast makes me want the answer. **PAYOFF: 8/10.** “Jesus did both” answers it, though the ending shifts toward the curtain.

7. **LANDING:** **7/10.** “The curtain is open” connects directly; “Walk through, to God” could close several other Christian shorts.

8. **CLARITY:** **8/10.** Not the best possible version. Change one thing: replace the dead/living claim with **“One goat pictured death for sin. The other pictured sins carried away. Jesus accomplished both.”**
