1. **WHO:** Moses speaks, pointing forward to Jesus.
   **WHAT:** Bitten Israelites lived when they looked at the brass serpent Moses raised.
   **WHY:** Jesus used that event to explain His own lifting up, so believers might have eternal life.

2. **AUDIENCE:** People exploring Christianity, including Christians unfamiliar with this connection.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** Trust Jesus, lifted up on the cross, and receive eternal life.

4. **GLOBAL:** “The cure took the shape of the curse that bit us.” I would re-hear that to understand exactly what is being claimed.

5. **BIBLE:** Both quotations match the KJV excerpts. Jesus’ reference to His crucifixion is supported by John 12:32–33. However, “The cure took the shape of the curse that bit us” is an interpretive metaphor, not an explicit biblical explanation. Numbers 21 also does not explicitly say God left the snakes there. The final “look” should mean believing in Jesus, as John 3:15–16 explains.

6. **HOOK: 9/10.** Yes—the surprising cure makes me want the explanation. **PAYOFF: 8/10.** The Jesus connection answers it, though the curse metaphor needs interpretation.

7. **LANDING: 10/10.** “My bitten people” and “lifted like my snake” tie it directly to this story.

8. **CLARITY: 8/10.** Not the best possible version. My one change: replace “The cure took the shape of the curse that bit us” with **“Jesus bore our curse, so that everyone who believes in Him might live.”**
