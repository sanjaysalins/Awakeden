1. **WHO:** Aaron narrates his priestly work and points to Jesus.
   **WHAT:** One goat died; another carried confessed sins into the wilderness.
   **WHY:** Jesus’ sacrifice removes sin and gives believers access to God.

2. **AUDIENCE:** Christians and curious viewers learning how Old Testament ceremonies point to Jesus.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** Jesus fulfils what the two goats picture: sacrifice for sin and its removal.

4. **GLOBAL:** “The living goat could not pay.” Pay what, and in what sense?

5. **BIBLE:** All three quotations match the KJV. The two goats jointly picturing Jesus is a Christian typological reading, not something Leviticus explicitly states. “Once a year” describes the annual ceremony; Aaron made multiple entries during it.

6. **HOOK: 9/10.** Yes, I wanted the answer. **PAYOFF: 8/10.** “Jesus did both” answers it, though “pay” needs explaining.

7. **LANDING: 8/10.** It could close another short about the temple veil, but it connects closely to Aaron entering alone.

8. **CLARITY: 8/10.** Not the best possible yet. One change: replace the “could not” lines with “Together, these goats picture Jesus dying for sin and carrying it away.”
