1. **WHO:** Moses is speaking, telling his story and pointing to Jesus.
   **WHAT:** God judged Israel with serpents, then gave bitten people life when they looked at the brass serpent.
   **WHY:** Jesus connected His own lifting up with this event, promising life to believers.

2. **AUDIENCE:** People exploring Christianity, including those unfamiliar with this Bible story.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** As bitten Israelites looked and lived, sinners must believe in Jesus to receive eternal life.

4. **GLOBAL:** NONE.

5. **BIBLE:** The quotations match the KJV. Two cautions: “God did not take the snakes away” is inferred, not explicitly stated in Numbers 21. “Look up at Jesus on the cross, and live” should clearly mean believing: John 3:15 says, “whosoever believeth in him.”

6. **HOOK: 9/10.** Yes—it raises an intriguing question. **PAYOFF: 8/10.** The ending connects the serpent to Jesus, but needs clearer language about faith.

7. **LANDING: 9/10.** The paired snake-and-Jesus comparison makes it specific to this story.

8. **CLARITY: 9/10.** Not the best version yet. Change one thing: end with “The bitten looked at the serpent and lived. Believe in Jesus, lifted up on the cross, and have eternal life.”
