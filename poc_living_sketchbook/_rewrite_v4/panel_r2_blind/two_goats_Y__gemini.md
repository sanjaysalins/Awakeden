1. **WHO:** A guy named Aaron talking about his two goats and Jesus.
**WHAT:** He killed one goat behind a curtain and sent the other away, and Jesus basically did both of those things later.
**WHY:** Because Jesus dying tore the curtain so now anyone can go in.

2. **WHO is this short for:** Christians scrolling on their phones, or people curious about how weird old animal sacrifices connect to Jesus.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** Jesus did the job of both goats to pay for sins and take them away, opening the way for everyone.

4. **GLOBAL:** "the iniquity of us all" (what is iniquity?) and "taketh".

5. **BIBLE:** NONE. The KJV quotes are exact (Isaiah 53:6, John 1:29, Mark 15:38/Matthew 27:51), and the two-goat Day of Atonement typology is standard orthodox doctrine.

6. **HOOK:** Hook 9/10 (it's a catchy riddle). Payoff 10/10 (it perfectly answers exactly why you needed two goats with "The dead goat could not walk away. The living goat could not pay. Jesus did both.").

7. **LANDING:** 9/10. It couldn't really close a different short because it specifically ties back to "I went in once a year" (Aaron) and "the curtain" from the beginning of the video.

8. **Overall clarity:** 8/10. ONE thing to change: Swap "iniquity" for a normal word like "sins" or "guilt" so a teenager doesn't get distracted trying to figure out what it means.
