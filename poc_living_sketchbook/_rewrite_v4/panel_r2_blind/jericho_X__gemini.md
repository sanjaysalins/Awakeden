1. **WHO:** A woman named Rahab who had a bad reputation in her city.
**WHAT:** She hid spies, marked her window with a red cord, and her family survived a massive wall collapsing.
**WHY:** Her faith saved her, and she ended up in the family line of Jesus.

2. **WHO IS THIS FOR:** People who feel like their past mistakes are too big, but want to save themselves and their family.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** If you trust Jesus, your past doesn't matter, and you can bring your whole family to safety.

4. **GLOBAL:** "Hebrews says" — I would be confused and wonder who the guy named Hebrews is. "Taketh" sounds like old Shakespeare English.

5. **BIBLE:** NONE. (The KJV quotes are perfectly accurate: Joshua 6:20 "the wall fell down flat", Hebrews 11:31 "By faith", John 1:29 "taketh away the sin of the world". The lineage claim is grounded in Matthew 1:5, and the doctrine of faith preceding works is solid.)

6. **HOOK:** 9/10. "When Jericho fell, one family walked out alive" sounds like a disaster/survival movie hook. **PAYOFF:** 9/10. It perfectly explains exactly *how* that one family survived.

7. **LANDING:** 9/10. You couldn't use "Rahab tied one red cord to a promise" on any other video because it specifically names her and the exact physical object from the story. 

8. **CLARITY:** 8/10. It's really easy to follow. ONE thing I'd change: swap "Hebrews says" to "The Bible says" so someone who doesn't know the books of the Bible doesn't get tripped up trying to figure out who is talking.
