1. **WHO:** Rahab, a harlot in Jericho who believed in Israel’s God.
   **WHAT:** She hid Israel’s spies; she and her household survived Jericho’s destruction.
   **WHY:** Her faith matters, and she appears in Jesus’ ancestry.

2. **AUDIENCE:** Christians and people exploring the Bible, including those unfamiliar with Rahab.

3. **MAIN TAKEAWAY:** Trust Jesus, as Rahab trusted God, and encourage your loved ones to trust Him too.

4. **GLOBAL:** NONE.

5. **BIBLE:** The quotations match the KJV. Two qualifications:
   - “Her city knew her for her sin” adds a claim about her reputation that Scripture does not explicitly make.
   - Hebrews 11:31 says that by faith she “perished not”; this directly concerns surviving Jericho. The ending should not imply that your faith guarantees your family’s salvation.

6. **HOOK: 9/10. PAYOFF: 8/10.** Yes, I wanted to know which family and why. Their survival answers that, though the opening already reveals the outcome.

7. **LANDING: 7/10.** “Tie” and “bring” connect to the cord and household, but the line could close other rescue stories unchanged.

8. **CLARITY: 9/10.** Not quite the best version. My one change: end with **“Rahab brought her family into the house. Point everyone you love to Jesus—and trust Him yourself.”**
