## MY NARRATION
1. The wall of Jericho fell flat. One house on that wall stayed standing.
2. A woman named Rahab lived in it.
3. Two spies came from Israel, and she hid them.
4. She told them: "he is God in heaven above".
5. She asked them to spare her family.
6. They answered: "thou shalt bind this line of scarlet thread in the window".
7. Hang the red cord. Bring your family home.
8. She hung it. She trusted their word.
9. Israel marched. The people shouted. And "the wall fell down flat".
10. Her house was spared, and her family with her.
11. The cord was the sign. Trusting the promise saved her. Hebrews calls that faith.
12. Matthew lists Rahab among the ancestors of Jesus.
13. Christians see her red cord as a picture of His blood, which forgives.
14. Jesus came from the woman at the window. Trust His promise, and come home.
(silence 3s, final landing picture holds)

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY
- Hook opens on the fallen wall and the one house left (specific, a mystery). Fixes P1, P6.
- Cause chain stated plainly: cord = sign, trust in the promise = what saved her (P1).
- Dropped "harlot" and the "Rachab" quote; Matthew line is plain paraphrase (P3, P5).
- Cord-as-blood marked "Christians see", tied to Ephesians 1:7 meaning (P2).
- Landing built on window and home (from Joshua 2:18), lands on Jesus from her line (P4).

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY
