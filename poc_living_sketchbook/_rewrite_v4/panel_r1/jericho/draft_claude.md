## MY NARRATION

1. One red cord saved a woman when her whole city fell.
2. Her name was Rahab. She lived in the wall of Jericho.
3. Two men from Israel came to spy. She hid them, because she believed in their God.
4. [silence 0.5s. Rahab's feet, the spies climbing down]
5. They said to her: "thou shalt bind this line of scarlet thread in the window".
6. That was the promise. Hang the cord, and your family is safe.
7. Then Israel marched. "The people shouted with a great shout, that the wall fell down flat".
8. The city fell. But her house stood, and her family lived.
9. The Bible says: "By faith the harlot Rahab perished not".
10. She believed the promise, so she hung the cord. Believing is what saved her.
11. Years later, a child was born in her family line: Jesus.
12. [silence 1.0s. A quiet line picture]
13. Rahab could not hold up a wall. She hung one cord, and believed a promise.
14. Jesus gave a promise too. Hang your trust on His.
15. [silence 3.0s. Final landing picture, held]

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY
- Lines 1, 6, 10: The cord is the sign, belief is the cause. "Hang the cord, and your family is safe" then "Believing is what saved her" says it plainly. Fixes P1.
- Line 11: Removed "Salmon begat Booz of Rachab" and the Christians-see-blood claim. Matthew 1:5 and 1:16 do tie her family line to Jesus. "Born in her family line" is a fact of the genealogy, not a symbol. Fixes P2, P3.
- Line 9: Cut "harlot" to the first words only and dropped the hard clause. The quote stops at "perished not". "Harlot" is still in the quote, so I tied it to the Bible as the speaker. Fixes P5, partly P3.
- Line 2: Removed "known for her sin" and added where she lived. This gives a newcomer a place and a person. Fixes P3.
- Lines 13–14: Changed "only hung" to "hung one cord, and believed". Landing uses cord and promise, not "hope". Fixes P4.
- Cut lines 7 (old), 10 (old) and compressed the rest. Fixes P6.

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY
The red cord hook, the Joshua 2:18 spy quote, and the "wall fell down flat" quote. They are short, concrete and word-for-word KJV.

## THE FIRST TWO SECONDS AND THE MOMENT THE VIEWER KNOWS WHO TO ROOT FOR
They hear "One red cord saved a woman when her whole city fell." They know a woman is in danger at about second 3. They know her name and home by about second 8.

## THE RISK
A Bible reader may say the line "Jesus gave a promise too" is vague, since the source text does not name which promise. A newcomer may still wonder why "harlot" appears, since it is not explained.
