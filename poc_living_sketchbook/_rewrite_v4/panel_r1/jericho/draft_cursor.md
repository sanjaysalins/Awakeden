## MY NARRATION

1. One red cord marked the house spared when Jericho fell.
2. Rahab lived in the wall.
3. She hid the spies, believing: "he is God in heaven above, and in earth beneath."
4. They said, "thou shalt bind this line of scarlet thread in the window."
5. That cord was the sign. Everyone inside would be spared.
6. Trumpets blew. The people shouted. "The wall fell down flat."
7. Joshua saved Rahab alive, and her father's household, because she hid the messengers.
8. Faith spared her—not her past. She kept the sign and trusted their word.
9. Matthew lists her on the road to Christ: "of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ."
10. Christians see that scarlet thread like blood that saves—"redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins."
11. She could not stop the wall.
12. She bound the thread, hid the men, and believed the promise.
13. Bind your trust to Jesus as she bound that thread—and wait on His promise.

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY

- **P1:** Lines 4–5, 7–8 chain promise → sign on the house → rescue because she hid the messengers and kept the cord; one clear cause after one hearing.
- **P2:** Line 10 labels the blood link as what Christians see and anchors it only to Ephesians 1:7, not Joshua.
- **P3:** Dropped "harlot" and the Salmon/Booz/Rachab quote; line 9 uses plain words plus the Matthew 1:16 Christ line only.
- **P4:** Lines 8 and 12 state faith and keeping the promise; line 13 closes on bind/trust/wait/promise, not generic "hope."
- **P5:** Dropped the full Hebrews 11:31 wording; line 8 carries the same point in plain English.
- **P6:** Cut from 180 to 133 spoken words.

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY

- Hook-first scarlet cord and the Joshua 2:18 window line—they match the locked pictures and the story’s irony.
- Joshua 6:20 wall quote and the spared-house beat—they are the factual spine newcomers must remember.

## THE FIRST TWO SECONDS AND THE MOMENT THE VIEWER KNOWS WHO TO ROOT FOR

A stranger hears first that a **red cord** decided which house lived when a city fell. By about **4–5 seconds** (line 2, “Rahab lived in the wall”), they know the film is about **Rahab** and can root for her.

## THE RISK

A careful listener may still wish Hebrews 11:31 were quoted verbatim for the faith claim. The Ephesians line is Christ’s blood, not Jericho’s cord—line 10 must be heard as Christian reflection, not as something Joshua’s spies said.
