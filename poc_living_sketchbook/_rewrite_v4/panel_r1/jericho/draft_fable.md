## MY NARRATION
1. When Jericho's wall fell, one house in that wall still stood.
2. It was Rahab's. Her city knew her for her sin.
3. Two spies came from Israel. She hid them, because she believed in their God.
4. They promised: a red cord in her window, and all inside would be spared.
5. [silence ~1.5s — the red thread hangs alone in the window]
6. Israel marched. Trumpets. A shout. And "the wall fell down flat".
7. Her house was spared, with everyone in it.
8. The cord did not save her. It marked her house.
9. Hebrews says what saved her, in two words: "By faith".
10. Long after, Jesus was born, and her name is in His family line.
11. The woman known for sin, in the family of the One who "taketh away the sin of the world".
12. [silence ~1.5s — the quiet line picture holds]
13. Rahab tied one red cord to a promise, and brought her family in.
14. Tie your trust to Jesus, and bring everyone you love.

## WORD COUNT
143

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY
- P1: one clear chain. She believed (3) → the cord only marked the house (4, 8) → "By faith" is what saved her (9). The cord is never named as the cause.
- P2: dropped "the cord is the blood of Jesus" (no verse in the pack says it). The Christ link is now the grounded one: her name in Jesus's family line (Matthew 1:5, 1:16) and John 1:29.
- P3: no "harlot", no "Rachab", no "begat". The list of fathers is said plainly: "her name is in His family line".
- P4: landing built from this story's own verbs and nouns (tie, cord, promise, bring family in). "Tie your trust to Jesus, and bring everyone you love" could not close another film.
- P5: Hebrews 11:31 cut to its two clear words, "By faith".
- P6: 180 → 143 words. Old beats 8-10 became lines 8-9; old beat 12 removed; old beats 13-14 merged into line 13.

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY
- "Her city knew her for her sin" — it sets up the payoff in line 11 without the word "harlot".
- "the wall fell down flat" — short, exact, the one KJV sound a stranger can hear whole.
- The red cord stays the visual spine (hook → window → landing) because the pictures are locked around it.

## THE FIRST TWO SECONDS AND THE MOMENT THE VIEWER KNOWS WHO TO ROOT FOR
First heard: "When Jericho's wall fell, one house in that wall still stood" — a wall falls, one house stands. At about second 5, "It was Rahab's. Her city knew her for her sin" — a woman with a bad name, in the one house that stood. From there she is the person to watch.

## THE RISK
A careful reader may note the text never says in words that her house "stood" (it says she and her household were saved alive and her house was upon the wall); the locked picture of the spared house among the ruins commits to it anyway. A newcomer who misses "she believed in their God" in line 3 may not know what "By faith" is faith in.
