## MY NARRATION

1. Two goats. One died. One lived.
2. Why?
3. I am Aaron, a priest of God. Once a year, I did this work for my people.
4. (silence 0.4s: the two goats side by side held)
5. The first goat I killed. Its blood went behind the curtain, the veil, where God's presence was.
6. The second goat I did not kill.
7. I put both hands on its head. I confessed all the wrongs of my people.
8. Then it walked into empty desert. Alive. Carrying our sin away.
9. One goat paid for sin by dying. The other took sin away.
10. One goat could not do both.
11. Years later, Jesus came. Christians see both goats in Him.
12. Isaiah wrote: "the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all".
13. Jesus died, and sin was put away. Scripture says so.
14. "The veil of the temple was rent in twain from the top to the bottom."
15. The curtain that kept me out is open.
16. Come in, to the God I could only visit once a year.
17. (silence 3.0s: the final landing picture held, quiet music)

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY
The hands-on-head confession, the Isaiah quote and the torn-veil quote are kept word for word. They carry the doctrine and match the locked pictures.

## THE FIRST TWO SECONDS AND THE MOMENT THE VIEWER KNOWS WHO TO ROOT FOR
They hear "Two goats. One died. One lived." That is a puzzle in about 3 seconds. They know the speaker is Aaron, a priest, at about 8 seconds, in line 3.

## THE RISK
"Years later, Jesus came" still puts Aaron's voice past his own time; a newcomer may wonder how he knows. Line 16 ("the God I could only visit once a year") goes slightly past the text, since Leviticus 16 speaks of Aaron entering on the Day of Atonement and Hebrews 10:19 of boldness to enter. A Bible reader might want a verse tied to it.
