## MY NARRATION
1. One goat died. One walked away alive. Why two?
2. I am Aaron, the high priest. I went in to God for the people.
3. I lived long before Jesus. Watch my goats.
4. The first I killed, and carried its blood behind the curtain between people and God.
5. The second lived. I laid both hands on its head, confessing the people's sins.
6. It was led into the desert, carrying them.
7. [silence ~1.5s — the two goats side by side]
8. A dead goat cannot carry sin away. A living goat has not died for it.
9. Jesus did both.
10. Isaiah said: "the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all".
11. He died, and He "taketh away the sin of the world".
12. When He died, that curtain tore, "from the top to the bottom".
13. [silence ~1.5s — the torn curtain hangs open]
14. Lay your sins on Jesus, as I laid them on that goat.
15. Walk through the torn curtain. He is the way in.

## WORD COUNT
144

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY
- P1: the desert image now sits early (line 6). The ending is curtain only (12, 14, 15). "A land where no one lives" is gone, so nothing points to the wilderness at the close.
- P2: one plain answer in line 8: a dead goat cannot carry sin away, a living goat has not died for it. "Jesus did both" (9) is the turn.
- P3: "He bore them away" replaced with John 1:29, "taketh away the sin of the world". The literal-place line is dropped.
- P4: "I lived long before Jesus" says the viewpoint out loud. "High priest" is explained as "I went in to God for the people". "Veil" and "sin offering" are gone; it is "the curtain between people and God".
- P5: landing built from this story's own acts: lay sins on a head, walk through the torn curtain. "He is the way in" is Hebrews 10:20 in plain words.
- P6: 195 → 144 words. Old beats 10-12 became line 8; old 13-15 became 9-11; old 16-17 became 12; old 18-19 became 14-15.

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY
- The two-goat hook and "Why two?" — it is the film's real question and the pictures open on it.
- Isaiah 53:6 "laid on him" — the exact echo of Aaron's hands laid on the goat's head.
- "from the top to the bottom" — short, exact, and the locked tearing pictures need it.

## THE FIRST TWO SECONDS AND THE MOMENT THE VIEWER KNOWS WHO TO ROOT FOR
First heard: "One goat died. One walked away alive." — two animals, two fates. At about second 4, "I am Aaron, the high priest" — the man who held both goats is speaking, and the film is about what his hands did.

## THE RISK
A source-literate viewer may object that "Watch my goats" plus "Jesus did both" asserts the type without saying "Christians see", though both halves rest on Hebrews 9:26 and John 1:29. A newcomer may not know who "Isaiah" is, though the line stands on its own.
