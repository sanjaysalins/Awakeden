## MY NARRATION

1. I am Moses. My people were dying from snakebites. And God's cure was a snake on a pole.
2. My people had spoken against God. So He sent snakes among them, and they bit the people, and many died.
3. They came to me: "We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD, and against thee".
4. I prayed.
5. God did not take the snakes away. He told me to make a serpent of brass, and set it on a pole.
6. I made it. Whoever was bitten looked at it, and lived.
7. Now hear this. Long after me, a teacher named Jesus spoke with a man named Nicodemus, a religious leader.
8. Jesus said: "As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up."
9. Lifted up on a cross. Jesus called Himself the Son of man.
10. Christians see it like this. The cure looked like the thing that bit us. And on the cross, Jesus was "made a curse for us".
11. That is Paul, a follower of Jesus, writing in Galatians.
12. Look at the pole, and live. Look at Jesus lifted up, and live.
- (silence, 3 sec, the lifted-up landing picture held)

## MY NARRATION (FINAL, CUT TO BUDGET)

1. I am Moses. My people were dying of snakebites. God's cure was a snake on a pole.
2. They had spoken against God. So snakes came, and many died.
3. They begged me: "We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD, and against thee".
4. I prayed.
5. God did not take the snakes away. He told me to make a serpent of brass, and set it on a pole.
6. Whoever was bitten looked at it, and lived.
7. Long after me, Jesus told a man named Nicodemus: "As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up."
8. Lifted up, on a cross. Jesus is the Son of man.
9. Christians see it so: the cure looked like the thing that bit us. And Paul wrote that Jesus was "made a curse for us".

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY
- Line 1: says "I am Moses" first, then the cure. Names the people as dying before the cure. Fixes P1.
- Line 2: separates cause (they spoke against God) from the bites. Snakes come first, cure comes later. Fixes P1.
- Line 5: kept God as the one who commands, "He told me to make". Fixes P5.
- Cut "I made it", "My brass serpent was only a picture", and the long Nicodemus quote tail. Fixes P5 and P6.
- Line 7: "a man named Nicodemus" kept, "Long after me" tells the viewpoint. Fixes P4 in part.
- Line 8: "Lifted up, on a cross" explains the phrase. Fixes P3. (This is John 12:33's meaning, "what death he should die", not quoted.)
- Line 9: marks the irony as "Christians see it so", and names Paul as the source. Fixes P2 and P5 (H5).
- Line 10: the landing uses the pole, looking and living, the story's own words. Fixes P2.

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY
The crowd's confession quote and "set it on a pole" stay word for word. They are short, clear, and already work.

## THE RISK
"Son of man" is still unexplained, and Jesus's and Paul's words are still carried by one voice. Line 8 says "Jesus is the Son of man" as a plain claim, which a Bible reader would accept, but a newcomer may not see why that title matters. Also, "the cure looked like the thing that bit us" is a Christian reading, not Numbers' own claim, and I flagged it as such.
