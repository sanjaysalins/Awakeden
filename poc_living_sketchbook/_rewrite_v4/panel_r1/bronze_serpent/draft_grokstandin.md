## MY NARRATION
1. The cure for snakebite was a snake.
2. I am Moses. I made it.
3. My people spoke against God. He sent snakes, and many died.
4. They came to me: "We have sinned".
5. I prayed.
6. God did not take the snakes away. He told me to make a serpent of brass and set it on a pole.
7. Anyone bitten who looked at it lived.
8. The cure looked like the killer. They only had to look.
9. Long after me, Jesus said He would be "lifted up" like that serpent.
10. John tells us that meant the death He would die.
11. He was lifted up on a cross.
12. Paul wrote that Christ was "made a curse for us".
13. Bitten people looked up at a snake, and lived. Look up at Jesus, and live.
(silence 3s, final landing picture holds)

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY
- Hook keeps the snake-cure irony; order is now sin, snakes, prayer, remedy (P1).
- Landing reuses the story's own irony: look up at a snake, live; look up at Jesus (P2).
- "Lifted up" explained as the cross via John 12:33; Nicodemus and "Son of man" dropped (P3).
- "Long after me" marks the viewpoint; Jesus' words are short quotes (P4).
- "Only a picture" removed; God's command (Num 21:8) and Paul named as source (P5). Cut to 126 (P6).

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY
The irony hook and "We have sinned": vivid, exact, and Moses stays in first person.

## THE FIRST TWO SECONDS AND THE MOMENT THE VIEWER KNOWS WHO TO ROOT FOR
First: "The cure for snakebite was a snake." Moses is named at about 3 seconds; the dying people carry the sympathy.

## THE RISK
"Look up at Jesus" after a snake picture can sound odd; the Paul line must carry the bridge.
