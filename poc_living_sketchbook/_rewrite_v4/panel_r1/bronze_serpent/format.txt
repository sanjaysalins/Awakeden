OUTPUT FORMAT (Markdown, max ~450 words in total, exactly these headings):
## MY NARRATION
Numbered lines, one beat per line, in the same beat shape as the current text (you may split a beat into two lines, or merge two short ones, if you say so). Mark every silence as its own line with a rough length in seconds and what the picture is doing. Keep every hard rule from the pack (the closing formula, the name's placement, the single narrator, etc). Nothing else in this section.
## WORD COUNT
The exact number of spoken words in MY NARRATION (not counting silence lines).
## WHAT I CHANGED, AND WHY
A short list. For each change, name the problem it fixes (by its P-number from the pack).
## WHAT I DELIBERATELY KEPT, AND WHY
One to three lines.
## THE FIRST TWO SECONDS AND THE MOMENT THE VIEWER KNOWS WHO TO ROOT FOR
What does a stranger hear first, and at what second do they first understand who the film is about?
## THE RISK
What could a source-literate viewer object to, or a newcomer still not follow? (1-2 sentences)
