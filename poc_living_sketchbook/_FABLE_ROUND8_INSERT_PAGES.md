# Fable — INSERT PAGES (round 4 of the funded style bake-off)

**Date:** 2026-07-31 · **Brief (the user's own words):** *"we keep our current sketchbook style…
but create pages we can insert in between to keep the story moving but with differnt perspectives
and brillinat ways of telling it… like we watching some one do a great sketchbook and has a
consistent team, but each page is told slight diffently… each of them become a skill style."*
The reaction was to Style 3's letter-perfect label sheet and Style 13's crossing chart: not
"replace the look" — **one page, in a different pictorial MODE, dropped into an otherwise-Style-1
episode, when the content asks for it.**

**What was built:** 12 new insert page-modes, one proof still each, spanning the whole corpus
(not just Storm — the user asked for the whole toolkit). 15 renders total (12 first-pass + 3
QC re-rolls), every one eye-verified at full resolution against SKILL.md §8a before it counted.
All ledgered under `LS_StyleBakeoff`, notes `[bakeoff-r4-insertpages]`.
Gallery: `_style_bakeoff/_ROUND4_INSERTPAGES_REVIEW.html`.

**The design idea in one line:** a real field journal is mostly sketches — and then, when a
sketch can't say it, the same hand switches page-grammar: a lineage tree, a cutaway, a chart of
the night sky, a reckoning sheet, a ruled hymn leaf. Twelve grammars, one hand, all in the
Style 3/13 iron-gall-on-parchment family so every insert reads as the SAME keeper reaching for
a different instrument.

Rules carried (non-negotiable, same as every round): doctrine sound + proven both ways; whole
Bible through Jesus; KJV verbatim only via deterministic overlay — baked text stays SHORT
labels in rubric-red (Style 3's doctrine; ceiling re-proven at 3 labels TWICE this round);
period lock; no named artist/book; positive end-state wording; gold = His glory only (three
pages deliberately carry NO gold); §8a full-res QC on every still; ≤3 distinct faces.

## ⚖️ THE LAW THIS ROUND FOUND — the count belongs to the code

Three independent failures, one lesson, now hard evidence:

- **Lineage tree:** asked for three groups of FOURTEEN tally strokes (Matt 1:17's own number)
  → rendered ~16 / ~15 / ~16 (counted stroke-by-stroke on 3x crops).
- **Thirty pieces:** asked for thirty coins in three rows of ten → rendered FIFTY (5 rows).
  Fixed on re-roll ONLY by re-framing the count as a small number ("exactly THREE rows…
  each holding ten") — three rows of ten came back exact.
- **Sealed scroll:** asked for SEVEN seals, then re-rolled with "count them, one two three
  four five six seven" → FIVE, both times. Honest fail after 2 tries.

**The law:** when Scripture states a number (14 generations, 30 pieces, 7 seals, 12 loaves,
153 fish), the generative page may NOT carry the count. Render the page with a blank counting
lane (the psalm leaf proves models CAN leave ruled space empty) and let a $0 deterministic pass
draw exactly N marks — the same doctrine measuring-reed already enforces for measured spans,
now extended to every counted thing. Model draws the PAGE; code draws the COUNT. Small counts
survive only when re-framed as structure ("three rows") — never rely on it above ~7.

## The 12 insert modes (each = one future skill-style)

### Insert 1 — THE LINEAGE TREE ("the generations page") — Matthew 1
- **Gap:** the "whole Bible through Jesus" thread as ONE image — the canon's spine drawn as a
  climbing olive tree, Abraham at the root, David's crown midway, the gold star-burst of Christ
  at the crown, three fourteens tallied along the trunk (Matt 1:17).
- **Proof:** `insert01_lineage_tree.png` — first-pass pass as a MODE. All 3 labels letter-perfect
  (ABRAHAM · DAVID · MATTHEW 1 — the 3-label ceiling re-proven). Gold only on the star-burst.
- **Honest flags:** tally counts render ~15-16 not 14 (see THE LAW — production = blank tally
  lanes + deterministic strokes); two of the three groups sit below the crown where Matt 1:17
  puts one; faint codex-edge at right (minor crop).
- **Episodes:** Jesus in the OT (Born in Bethlehem, The Seed of the Woman), Names & Titles
  (Son of Man, Immanuel), any Christmas piece; the series-thesis page ANY episode can cite.
- **Distinct from:** Chrono Sweep ($0 timeline overlay — linear, annotational); this is a full
  rendered TREE page. Complements elder-leaf (the tree cites the line; the leaf cites a verse).
- **Motion:** $0 only (baked labels): reading-order pan root→star; gold flare on the burst;
  deterministic tallies could draw ON (measuring-reed's tick grammar).

### Insert 2 — THE SEALED SCROLL ("the document page") — Revelation 5
- **Gap:** the Bible-as-object with its own theology: the book no man could open, and the one
  gold Lamb-device seal — "the whole Bible, through Jesus" as a physical document study.
- **Proof:** `insert02_sealed_scroll.png` (v2) — **HONEST FAIL on the seven-count, twice**
  (5 seals both rolls; v1 kept as `insert02_sealed_scroll.v1_fiveseals_defect.png`, which also
  had a stray page-numeral + codex framing — both fixed in v2). The MODE passes: gold Lamb
  seal-impression is superb, label perfect, flat page clean.
- **Adopt only with:** deterministic seals — render the scroll bare-ribboned, stamp all seven
  from a banked seal texture ($0, THE LAW). Or accept the study as "the Lamb's seal" close-up
  where no count is claimed.
- **Episodes:** Names & Titles (The Lion of the tribe of Judah — Rev 5:5 IS this page), Alpha
  and Omega, any Revelation landing.
- **Distinct from:** frottage ($0 rubbing reveal of ONE object) — this is a full document-study
  page; the two could even stack (frottage reveals the Lamb seal ON this page).

### Insert 3 — THE TABERNACLE CUTAWAY ("the builder's page") — Exodus 26 / Hebrews 9
- **Gap:** architecture beats no narrative sketch can explain: court → Holy Place → veil →
  Holy of Holies as one cut-open drawing, "the patterns of things in the heavens."
- **Proof:** `insert03_tabernacle_cut.png` — first-pass pass, one of the round's best. Both
  labels letter-perfect; gold EXACTLY and only the mercy seat between the cherubim; 7-branch
  lampstand correct; laver + horned bronze altar in the court; dimension lines carry plain
  ticks and NO numerals (left open for measuring-reed — by design).
- **Honest flags:** incense altar sits mid-chamber, should stand hard against the veil
  (Ex 30:6); shewbread loaves ~13 not 12 (THE LAW again); cherub faces slightly angel-generic.
- **Episodes:** Types & Shadows (Day of Atonement/scapegoat — the Two Goats episode's missing
  establishing page; Passover Lamb; Melchizedek), The Last Week (the veil rent — this page,
  then the tear), Hebrews long-forms. Noah's ark + Temple variants are the same grammar.
- **Distinct from:** measuring-reed ($0 span overlay — this page is its natural STAGE);
  nothing in styles 1-15 is architectural.
- **Motion:** $0 pan through the chambers (outer→inner = the gospel's own direction), reed
  drawing the cubits, raking-light. Never generative (baked labels).

### Insert 4 — THE STAR CHART ("the heavens page") — Genesis 15 / Galatians 3
- **Gap:** "Tell the stars, if thou be able to number them… so shall thy seed be" answered by
  "to thy seed, which is Christ" (Gal 3:16) — the promise counted, as a charted night sky with
  ONE gold star low over the desert hills.
- **Proof:** `insert04_star_chart.png` — first-pass pass, the round's most beautiful page.
  Both labels letter-perfect; hundreds of parchment-prick stars in a deep ink-blue wash
  (ink-blue = night, per palette theology); hairline joins; the gold Seed-star ringed twice
  with hairlines converging on it from the whole field.
- **Honest flags:** one mid-field ringed star carries a warm cream ring that could read
  gold-adjacent at thumbnail (verified at 3x: ink, not leaf — but production wording should
  reserve warm rings for the one gold star); the gold star is drawn emblem-style (5-point in
  a ring) rather than a prick — right for a chartmaker marking THE star, noted for taste.
- **Episodes:** Jesus in the OT (The Seed of the Woman), the Magi (a second chart, one
  traveling star), "he calleth them all by their names" (Ps 147), Abraham episodes.
- **Distinct from:** Deep Vigil (Style 15 — a nocturne PAINTING; this is a CHART of night);
  candle-only/papermakers-mark ($0 light devices) untouched.
- **Motion:** $0: slow drift-pan across the field, raking-light, ONE gold flare on the Seed
  star timed to "which is Christ." Never generative.

### Insert 5 — THE SPECIMEN PLATE ("the naturalist's page") — Matthew 6
- **Gap:** the parables' living objects (lily, mustard seed, sparrow, wheat/tares, fig)
  rendered as a field scholar's botanical plate — "Consider the lilies" taken literally:
  the page considers one.
- **Proof:** `insert05_lily_plate.png` — first-pass pass. Whole plant root-to-bloom center,
  bud + open-face + leaf studies around it, and the honest withered-stem study in the corner
  ("the grass of the field, which to day is"). Label letter-perfect. **Deliberately NO gold**
  — "Solomon in all his glory" was not His glory; the discipline of absence is the theology.
- **Honest flags:** the crimson bloom reads garden-lily (turk's-cap) rather than the scarlet
  field anemone many take Matt 6 to mean — name "scarlet windflower of the Galilean field" if
  botanical fidelity becomes load-bearing; open-face study reads a slightly different bloom
  than the main stem's.
- **Episodes:** Parables of Jesus (sower's four soils as a seed-study plate; mustard seed
  smallest-to-greatest), Questions (Why Are You Afraid → sparrows, two farthings), I AM the
  True Vine (a vine-and-branches plate).
- **Distinct from:** margin-study ($0 graphite doodles derived from the spread's own art —
  quick, monochrome, reactive); this is a full rendered color plate the episode plans FOR.
- **Motion:** $0 pan between studies, annotators-circle on the detail the narration names.

### Insert 7 — THE RECKONING SHEET ("the ledger page") — Zechariah 11 / Matthew 26
- **Gap:** the price of the Shepherd as a money-changer's own working sheet: a balance with

- **Proof:** `insert07_thirty_pieces.png` (v2). v1 rendered FIFTY coins (kept as
  `insert07_thirty_pieces.v1_fiftycoins_defect.png`); v2 with rows-first wording came back

- **Honest flags:** the lower third is generously blank (an asset: keeper-hand's entry lane);

- **Episodes:** Jesus in the OT (Thirty Pieces of Silver — the episode is literally waiting),

- **Distinct from:** frottage (the denarius rubbing is ONE coin's texture; this is the

- **Motion:** $0: pan balance→rows; the rows could be REDRAWN deterministically coin-by-coin

### Insert 8 — THE TRIAL DOCKET ("the record page") — John 19
- **Gap:** the legal-record register for trial content: the titulus board (the only charge
  ever recorded against Him), a blank Roman wax diptych, stylus, laurel seal — the empty

- **Proof:** `insert08_trial_docket.png` — first-pass pass, and the round's lettering
  ceiling-raise: **"KING OF THE JEWS" baked letter-perfect** on the wood-grain board (a
  4-word object inscription — letters as OBJECT, the frottage-titulus precedent) + "JOHN 19"
  label clean. Wax faces stayed utterly blank (the no-pseudo-text discipline held).
- **Honest flags:** Scripture's verbatim inscription is "THE KING OF THE JEWS" (Mark 15:26)
  / "JESUS OF NAZARETH THE KING OF THE JEWS" (John 19:19) — production should bake Mark's
  exact five words (and may relabel "MARK 15"), not my four-word fragment; stylus reads
  slightly needle-like.
- **Episodes:** Resurrection on Trial (all 6 episodes are evidence-sheet shaped), The Last
  Week (Gethsemane→trial), 7 Words (the board hung over every word), Questions (Why Do You
  Call Me Lord).
- **Distinct from:** Style 3 (compares two things; this RECORDS one proceeding); frottage
  (could take a rubbing of THIS board later — stack, not clash).
- **Motion:** $0 hunt-and-lock to the board; annotators-circle on KING. Never generative.

### Insert 10 — THE PSALM LEAF ("the hymn leaf") — Psalm 22
- **Gap:** the page-mode built FOR the Word: an illuminated leaf whose ruled lanes are
  rendered EMPTY, so the KJV arrives exclusively as deterministic Scribed Ink on real ruled

- **Proof:** `insert10_psalm_leaf.png` — first-pass pass, and the round's mechanism

- **Honest flags:** the leaf is cleaner/less foxed than its siblings (defensible — a hymn
  leaf would be finer stock); the drop-cap must match the overlay verse's first word (M =
  "My God" — production must keep initial and verse in sync or bake no initial).
- **Episodes:** the Psalm 22 short series (~12 shorts locked from the long), 7 Words (My God

- **Distinct from:** Illuminated Rubric ($0 lettering register — it letters ONTO existing

- **Motion:** $0 by definition: Scribed Ink writes the verse onto the lanes (narration-voice
  reveal) or the verse ARRIVES WHOLE for red-letter; annotators-circle on the emphasis word.

### Insert 11 — THE DROUGHT ALMANAC ("the weather page") — 1 Kings 18
- **Gap:** time-under-judgment as a page: three ruled month-rows of moon phases, a rain-tally

- **Proof:** `insert11_drought_almanac.png` — first-pass pass. Label clean; the empty lanes

- **Honest flags:** the man's-hand cloud rendered a touch large and puffy — "a tiny distant
  cloud low over the sea" in production; moon-phase counts per row are uncountable (THE LAW:
  irrelevant here, the emptiness is the payload).
- **Episodes:** Elijah episodes (backlog), Questions (Where Is Your Faith), Revelation's
  famine seals; the grammar reuses for ANY duration-of-affliction beat (40 days of rain

- **Distinct from:** nothing in styles 1-15 or the $0 kit touches charted time-rows; tide-mark/
  wash-creep are wet-paper devices, not calendars.
- **Motion:** $0: reading-order pan L→R along the rows (the silence lengthening), keeper-hand
  could STRIKE the empty months one by one; hold on the cloud.

## What I did NOT build, deliberately

- **Portrait-gallery cast sheet for crowded epics** (Judges/Kings) — the witness roll proves
  the 3-portraits + anonymous-rows grammar; a dedicated cast-sheet variant can reuse it when
  a Judges series is greenlit. Not worth 2cr to prove twice.
- **Fisherman's catch-tally (153, John 21) / shepherd's fold count (99+1, Luke 15)** — same
  counting grammar as inserts 7/12; blocked on THE LAW anyway (deterministic marks); designed,
  shelved, one render away when Peter-restored or Good-Shepherd episodes call.
- **Casting-lots page (Ps 22:18/John 19:24)** — collapses into Style 3's two-panel comparison;
  no new grammar earned.
- **Any animation tests** — out of scope; every motion claim above is $0-device inference,
  labeled as such. Baked-lettering pages NEVER go to generative motion (standing law).
- **Any touch of the shipped Storm episode** — everything lives in `_style_bakeoff/`.
