# jericho - spoken narration (KJV quotes) for the doctrine panel

Jericho had the strongest walls in the world. And God told His army to march around them... in silence. Six days they circled. Thirteen laps in all. Not one arrow. Just feet on sand... and one scarlet cord, hanging from one window. Inside those walls lived Rahab. A woman with a past... who hid God's spies, and believed. Their answer: “Bind this line of scarlet thread in the window.” On the seventh day the trumpets tore the sky. “The people shouted with a great shout... the wall fell down flat.” Every stone came down. One window stood. Scripture says it plainly: “By faith the harlot Rahab perished not.” Not spared for being good. Spared for believing. Now read Jesus' family tree: “Salmon begat Booz of Rachab.” The woman behind the scarlet cord became His family. There is a scarlet line running from her window to a cross. His blood. Bind everything to it.
