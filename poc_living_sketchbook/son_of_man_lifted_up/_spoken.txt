By night, Jesus tells a Pharisee something strange: this defeat is what John calls His glory. Jesus means it literally: what shames Him is what glorifies Him. Centuries earlier, a bronze serpent healed dying Israel with a look. Jesus tells Nicodemus, 'And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up: That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life.' A skeptic hears cruelty. John uses that word again at the cross: shame and glory, one lifting. That word will not let you split them. You cannot want Christ's glory without His cross. He was shamed to become your glory. Believe on Christ, lifted up for you, and live.
