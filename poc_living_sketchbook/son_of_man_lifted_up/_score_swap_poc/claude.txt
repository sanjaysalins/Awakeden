A slow-building dream-trance instrumental for a reverent short film: a single arpeggiated piano figure cycling and slowly layering over warm, sustained atmospheric pads, joined by a soft rising string swell and distant airy choir textures that thicken gradually across the full 58 seconds. No drums, no bassline, no drop, no vocals — the arpeggio and pads simply keep ascending in intensity and density, like Robert Miles' "Children" frozen in its opening build, but sacred and hushed rather than euphoric, evoking hushed awe and quiet reverence rather than club energy. The piece should feel like it is perpetually rising toward a holy revelation it never quite resolves, ending still mid-ascent, unresolved and suspended.
