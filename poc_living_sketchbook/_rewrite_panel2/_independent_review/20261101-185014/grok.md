# Independent review — grok (OK, 208s)

Hook: 8. Landing: 5. Under 8, so this is a revise.

"One goat died. One goat was sent away alive. Why two?" is concrete in the first breath. A stranger can picture it. It is not a "Did you know / Imagine / What if" open.

The close fails its own test. The last sentence is "Walk in." That line could end any open-door piece. "Come, and your sin is carried away" is a stock forgiveness offer. The two-goat picture is dropped right where it should land.

**KJV.** Both quotes are exact. "the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all" matches Isaiah 53:6. "the veil of the temple was rent in twain from the top to the bottom" matches Matthew 27:51 and Mark 15:38. No altered word inside the quotation marks.

**Doctrine.** The typology is the normal evangelical one: two goats, one sin offering (Leviticus 16:5), blood brought inside, sins confessed with both hands, the live goat bearing them to a land not inhabited, Jesus doing what the pair pictured. Isaiah 53 and the torn veil are fair, orthodox links. This is not a trick reading.

The order still over-claims. "carrying those sins away" and "One goat carried the sin away" and "One goat paid with its blood" are spoken as finished facts. "only pictured" comes later. On a first hear, the goat is the one who removes sin. "Paid" is sermon language. Leviticus says the goat is killed and its blood is brought in. Hebrews 10:4 still stands behind this text: the blood of goats cannot take away sins. The script knows that, and then says it too late.

"The first I killed, and I carried its blood inside the veil" also skips Aaron's own bull, whose blood he brought within the veil before the people's goat (Leviticus 16:11–15). The people's goat is the right goat for this short. The line sounds like that goat was the only blood he carried in.

**Clarity.** "I am Aaron, the first high priest" and "one sin offering" assume the listener already knows the job and the rite. "inside the veil" never says what the veil is. "rent in twain" is exact KJV and opaque on a first hear. "Twain" will not land. "The way to God is open" tries to gloss it after the hard words have already passed.

**Spine.** The hook asks why there are two. The answer arrives on time: "He paid, and He carried it away. The sins laid on that goat were laid on Him." Then the piece changes subject. The torn veil, "The way to God is open," and "Walk in" answer a second question, about access. The goat's last move is "led into the wilderness." The listener's last order is "Walk in." Out and in are both in the air, with no curtain shown as a door.

**Grace.** There is no threat and no works list. The breach is the last beat. "He carried it away" and "were laid on Him" are finished. "Come, and your sin is carried away" puts the carrying back on the listener's coming. That is a bargain on the ear. "Walk in" then adds a second imperative, so the hearer sounds like the one who finishes the offering.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Replace "Come, and your sin is carried away. Walk in." The last sentence has to use this piece's own picture (two goats, sins laid on Him, sin borne away) so it could not end a different film, and it has to invite onto a carrying Jesus already finished.
2. Say "pictured" before any line that sounds like the goat paid or took sin away, and retire "paid" for the animal. Keep the people's goat distinct from Aaron's bull.
3. Keep "Why two?" as the single spine through the last line. Before the veil quote, say in plain words that the veil was the curtain that shut people out. Then let that curtain serve the two goats, or cut it from the close.
