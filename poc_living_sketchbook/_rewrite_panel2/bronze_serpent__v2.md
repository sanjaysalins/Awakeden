# bronze_serpent v2 - spoken narration (KJV quotes) for the doctrine panel

I am Moses. My people were dying of snakebite, and God did not take the snakes away. The people came to me: "We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD, and against thee". I prayed. And God answered with something strange. He told me to make a serpent and put it on a pole. So I made a serpent of brass, like the ones that bit them, and set it on a pole. Whoever was bitten looked at it, and lived. Long after me, Jesus told a man named Nicodemus: "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up: That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life." My brass serpent was only a shadow. Jesus was lifted up on a cross, and Paul the apostle says He was "made a curse for us". Sin bites us all, and the cure is not in you. It was lifted up where you can see it. To look at Jesus is to believe in Him. Look, and live.
