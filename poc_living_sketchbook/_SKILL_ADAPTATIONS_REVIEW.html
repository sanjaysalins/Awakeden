1 · Thread Device v2

CHOSEN — Variant A, No Box

Two verified verses (Gen 1:1 → John 1:1) hand-written in Scribed Ink, connected by a real gold thread through open whitespace — no card at all. The existing scarlet/blue ribbon on this still sits right between them, so the gold thread reads as ONE MORE thread on a page that already has threads on it, not a UI element laid over the art.

Real bug found and fixed here, worth carrying back into the CORE lettering system: Kunstler Script's comma/period glyphs are nearly invisible at render size (~16×7px) — verbatim KJV punctuation was silently disappearing. Fixed by drawing punctuation from a 1.7×-larger stroked instance of the same font. This affects every Scribed Ink verse render already shipped tonight (Door episode, Jericho) — flagged for backport below.
REJECTED

Variant B, unified torn-scrap card. A genuine object (pinned parchment, both verses inside one scrap), but it visually competes with the still's own ribbon motif right below it — two separate "thread" ideas stacked in one frame — and front-loads all content into the top third, leaving the page's natural centerpiece unused.

2–6 · Five vox-type treatments

ADOPT — Ghost

Word pressed into wet paper, not floating on top

"BELIEVED" built as a real shallow deboss (offset highlight/shadow rims + darkened interior + blurred ink-bleed halo) — the watercolor wash and paper grain visibly show through the letter strokes. Zero flat-overlay artifact, confirmed legible even shrunk to thumbnail size. Worked on the first attempt.

ADOPT — lowerThird

Chapter/act title card

"ACT TWO — THE FALL OF THE WALL," rubric kicker + italic serif title + hairline rules, no card. The agent ran a genuine A/B rather than guessing: a real cropped gold-leaf texture (this still's own strip, rotated) beat a drawn-and-noised rectangle outright — the drawn version stayed a flat mustard bar no matter how much noise was added; the real texture carries genuine tonal variegation. Minor polish note: a faint rectangular halo is visible just behind the gold strip.

ADOPT — Echo

Print-misregistration refrain word

"COME" as 3 offset letterpress plates (rubric / gold / faded-ink) with a stamped, grain- roughened edge instead of a clean channel-split. Placed at the open doorway threshold — the word and the image now say the same thing at once. Reads as an old three-plate press slightly out of register, not a digital glitch, because the colors are our muted triad, not saturated RGB, and the edges are rough, not symmetric.

ADOPT — Typeset

The mechanical register, alongside Scribed Ink's handwritten one

"IN THE BEGINNING" set in rigid stamped slab caps with a gold fleuron ornament standing in for a cursor — deliberately the OPPOSITE hand from Scribed Ink (compositor vs. scribe). Genuinely distinct side by side, not redundant. Gives the show two legitimate lettering voices instead of one.

CONFIRMED SKIP — Flap

The one device asked to prove itself wrong

A genuinely good-faith attempt — hand-carved wooden tally tiles, rope lashing, wobbled frame — still reads as a departure board/scoreboard on sight, because the GRID-OF-CELLS shape is the anachronism, not the material. This is the exact lesson from the original caption-box diagnosis, proven a second time: you cannot texture your way out of a borrowed structure. I verified the image myself and agree — it reads as a display board before the wood grain registers at all. Recommendation: skip outright, or redesign from zero as a single hand-carved numeral with no frame at all (not attempted here).

10–11 · Two non-visual adaptations

REAL, TESTED, ADOPT

payoff-ledger — a real deterministic gate, run against Jericho's actual narration

Not a mockup: a working Python validator (poc_living_sketchbook/_skill_adaptations/payoff_ledger/payoff_ledger.py) adapted to this project's line/timestamp narration format (ArkAIology's version assumes shot numbers we don't have). I independently re-ran both the self-test and the real check myself rather than trusting the report:

Self-test: a ledger with a real verbatim payoff phrase PASSED; a planted fake ("Apollo 11 lands on the Moon") was REJECTED, exit 1, naming the exact bad claim. Fail-closed behavior confirmed, not assumed.

Real result against Jericho's actual narration: GATE PASS — 3 of 4 loops PAID, 1 genuinely PARTIAL. The hook asks four questions; three get explicit verbatim payoffs later in the script. The fourth — "why silence?" — is never answered by name; it's paid off only by dramatic CONTRAST (six days of silence broken by one shout), which the ledger correctly flags as PARTIAL rather than a false PASS. That's a genuine, honest finding about our own finished episode, produced by a tool that didn't exist three hours ago.
REAL, GROUNDED, ADOPT

produce-episode → RUNBOOK.md — grounded in real files, not invented

Written to .claude/skills/living-sketchbook/RUNBOOK.md. Every stage traces to a script that actually exists (verified by reading the real files, not assumed) across both proven episodes. It found two real gaps rather than describing an idealized pipeline that doesn't exist:

Gap 1: Jericho's narration was hand-authored and synthesized directly (_j2_audio.py), completely bypassing this repo's own locked cli.py draft-tournament + G1-G7 self-review + red-team + narration_gate.py chain — unlike the Door episode, which reused a fully-gated prior narration. Neither is documented as equivalent; the runbook says so plainly.

Gap 2: check_landing_hold.py (the repo's real INV-26 deterministic hold gate) only scans batches/ and longform/ — neither POC folder is in its path. The "3.3s/3.65s hold" claims for both finished episodes were checked by manual ffprobe, never by the actual gate script.
