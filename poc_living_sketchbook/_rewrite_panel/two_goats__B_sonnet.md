# two_goats candidate B_sonnet - spoken narration (KJV quotes) for the doctrine panel

I am Aaron, the first high priest. Once a year, on the Day of Atonement, I went behind the curtain to stand before God for the people. The law gave me two goats for one offering. I killed the first, and carried its blood inside the veil. The second goat I did not kill. I laid both my hands on its head, and confessed the sins of the people over it. Then it was sent away into the wilderness, carrying their sins with it. One goat paid. One goat carried the sin away. Christians see in these two goats a picture of Jesus. Isaiah wrote of Him: "the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all". He gave His own life, and the guilt was carried away. Hebrews says He did it once, and sat down. When He died, the curtain of the temple was torn in two, from the top to the bottom. I was only the sign. He is the real thing. The way in is open for you. Walk in.
