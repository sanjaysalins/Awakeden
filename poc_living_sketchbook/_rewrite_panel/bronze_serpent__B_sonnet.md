# bronze_serpent candidate B_sonnet - spoken narration (KJV quotes) for the doctrine panel

I am Moses. My people were dying of snakebite, one after another. They came to me and said, "We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD, and against thee". I prayed for them. And the LORD told me to make a serpent of brass, and put it on a pole. Anyone who was bitten only had to look at it, and they lived. Nothing else was asked of them. That brass serpent was only a shadow. Many years later, Jesus told a man named Nicodemus: "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up: That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life." Jesus was lifted up on a cross. Galatians says "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us". So hear me, you who are bitten. That is every one of us. The cure is not in you. It hangs in plain sight. Look to Jesus, lifted up for you. Believe in Him, and you will live.
