# bronze_serpent candidate A_fable - spoken narration (KJV quotes) for the doctrine panel

I am Moses. In the wilderness, serpents bit my people, and they were dying. They knew why, and came to me: "We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD." They asked me to pray that God would take the serpents away, and I prayed. God answered, but not by taking the serpents away. He told me to make a serpent and set it on a pole, so that "every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live." So I made a serpent of brass, like the ones that bit them, and put it on a pole. Whoever was bitten looked at it, and lived. Long after me, Jesus said to a man named Nicodemus: "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up: That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life." My serpent of brass was only a shadow. Jesus was lifted up on a cross, and Paul the apostle wrote that He was "made a curse for us." He took our judgment, in our place. Sin bites us all, and the cure is not in you. It was lifted up where you can see it. To look at Jesus is to believe in Him. Look, and live.
