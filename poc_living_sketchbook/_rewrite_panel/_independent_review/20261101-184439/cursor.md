## Independent adversarial review — `two_goats` candidate A (Aaron voice)

### Doctrinal soundness
- **Mostly orthodox typology** (Leviticus 16 → Christ’s blood + bearing sin → torn veil → access). The **paid / carried** pairing matches common evangelical reading and is anchored by Isaiah 53:6 in the script.
- **Soft spot — two goats collapsed into one antitype without a beat of clarification:** You say the second goat **“I did not kill”**, then land **“Jesus did what my two goats only pictured. He paid, and He carried.”** A listener who just learned “one died, one didn’t” may stumble: *Jesus was killed—so is He the first goat, the second, or both?* You answer in one sentence, but on **first hearing** the ritual logic and the Christ logic are easy to mis-merge. Not heresy, but **clarity risk**.
- **Veil continuity (pedantic but real):** **“The veil I stood behind ‘was rent in twain…’ when He died.”** Aaron’s yearly entry is **tabernacle** imagery; Matthew 27:51 is the **temple** veil. Preachers routinely fuse them; a hostile ear hears **voice slip** (Aaron didn’t live to see that rending). Worth one plain bridge word (“the same veil that kept the people out”) if you keep Aaron POV.

### KJV verbatim (quotation marks)
- **PASS:** **“the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.”** — exact Isaiah 53:6 substring.
- **PASS:** **“by his own blood”** — exact Hebrews 9:12 substring.
- **PASS:** **“sat down”** — exact Hebrews 10:12 substring.
- **PASS:** **“was rent in twain from the top to the bottom”** — exact Matthew 27:51 substring (full verse adds *“the veil of the temple”* before *“was rent…”*; elision is fine if the quoted run is contiguous KJV).
- **FAIL — altered / spliced “quote”:** **“once for ever”** inside the Hebrews line. KJV does **not** have that contiguous phrase. Hebrews 9:12 has **“he entered in once into the holy place”**; Hebrews 10:12 has **“one sacrifice for sins for ever, sat down…”**. Presenting **“once for ever”** as quoted Scripture is a **word-level splice**, not verbatim KJV. That is a hard gate in this engine.

### Clarity on first hearing
- **Strong:** Two-goat walkthrough (**“One goat paid… One goat carried the sin away”**) is concrete; **“Why two? Together they made one picture.”** is a clean hinge.
- **Weaker:** The **Hebrews** line is three quoted fragments plus **“once for ever”** — sounds like one verse, is actually **9:12 + 10:12 mashed**. Zero-Bible listeners won’t catch the error, but they **will** feel a vague “Bible quote salad” rhythm.
- **“Walk in.”** — grammatically clear, but **access-only**; no object (*walk in where / to whom?*) except the prior **“Jesus is the way in.”** Depends on the last two sentences being heard perfectly.

### Grace-anchored conviction

Not works-framing, but **thin** compared to pieces that land on Christ by name *and* a personal invite verb from *this* story’s vocabulary (blood, carried away, behind the veil).
