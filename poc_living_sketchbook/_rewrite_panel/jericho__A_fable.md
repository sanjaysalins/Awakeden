# jericho candidate A_fable - spoken narration (KJV quotes) for the doctrine panel

Jericho was a walled city, shut against God's people. God told them to march around it in silence. Six days, one lap a day. From one window in that wall hung a red cord. Rahab lived there. A prostitute, who hid God's spies, and believed. The spies had promised: "bind this line of scarlet thread in the window," and all inside live. On the seventh day, trumpets sounded. "The people shouted with a great shout, that the wall fell down flat." The city fell, but Rahab and her family were saved alive. Hebrews says why: "By faith the harlot Rahab perished not." Not for being good. For believing. Matthew lists Jesus' family line: "Salmon begat Booz of Rachab." Rachab is Rahab. The woman with the red cord became family to Jesus. Christians read that cord as a picture of His blood. Rahab could not hold up a wall. She could only hang a cord, and trust the promise. Trust Jesus like that. His promise holds.
