The Second Look — Plant Test

The 4 double-designed Act-1 shots for the bronze-serpent pilot (Plant 2 got two angle variants). This is a rough proof pass, not a finished style set — the outside review panel (degraded, 3/5 voices) and my own read both found real problems, marked below.

Plant 1

Moses raises the pole

Doesn't land

The two side elements are tent-stake tripods, not upright poles — they don't read as a three-crosses echo. Also flagged by the panel as inventing a detail Numbers 21 doesn't describe.

Plant 2a

Serpent coil — angle A

Too strong

Reads as a bowed head on a crossbar immediately, even unprimed — fails “invisible on first watch.” Good asset to save for the actual Turn reveal instead.

Plant 2b

Serpent coil — angle B

Closer to right

Reads as a serpent first; only suggests a figure once you're looking for it. The better Act-1 candidate of the two.

Plant 3

The pole's shadow

Partial

The cross-shaped shadow is there, but falls next to the figure, not across him. Also built on a lighting claim (“Noon Frieze needs no new lighting for this”) the panel found is false — the style is bright daylight, not low-sun.

Plant 4

Moses's cruciform pose

Works

Ordinary raising gesture on first read, unmistakably cruciform once you look for it. The one plant that landed cleanly as spec'd.

Where this stands

1 of 4 plants clean, 1 close, 2 need real rework. The panel also found a pipeline-timing bug in the Turn mechanic and a script conflict with the existing Jesus-POV narration that need resolving before more spend.
