The story, in one line

An aging priest relives his one day a year behind the veil — one goat killed, its blood carried in; one goat sent alive into the desert, carrying the people's confessed sin away. He spent years not understanding why it took two. Then: a Man named Jesus did what the two goats only pictured — the price paid, and the One the guilt was laid on. The curtain tears. "I was only the sign; He is the substance." The way in is torn wide open. Walk in.

Real defects hit and fixed during this build (honest log)

  • Jesus identity drift, caught by the user's own eye (g08 vs g10): the first g08 render (front-facing pivot) matched the reused Door-episode anchor loosely but drifted on a straight retry — a re-roll naming the drift (hair curl, beard fullness, skin tone) fixed it. Then g10 (a different pose, seen from behind) was generated against the SAME anchor alone and drifted AGAIN — visibly different hair color/length and beard fullness from g08, even though both used the identical reference file. Root cause: one anchor image alone doesn't lock identity tightly enough across dramatically different poses within a new episode. Fixed by chaining the NOW-APPROVED g08 render itself as a second reference alongside the original anchor when regenerating g10 — using our own best output as an additional lock, not just the source anchor. The clip that had already been animated from the bad still was caught and re-animated in the same pass (a still re-roll invalidates its clip — standing rule, held again here).
  • Isaiah 53:6 verse card lingering onto Jesus's face: the Illuminated Rubric card's on-screen window ran 0.6s past its own spread into the next one (g10, Jesus at rest) — a real violation of the letterer law "type never covers a face," caught on the first full assembly QC pass, not before. Fixed by tightening the card's exit time to close before the next spread begins; the whole ~2100-frame assembly was rebuilt to apply the fix, not patched around it.
