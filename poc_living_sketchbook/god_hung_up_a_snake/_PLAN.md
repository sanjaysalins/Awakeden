# God Hung Up a Snake — Bronze Serpent short #2 (Numbers 21:9)

Manifest slug: `bronze-serpent-02-the-thing-that-killed-them`. Source narration
(LOCKED, already voiced, reused verbatim, $0): `PythonProject1/jesus/narration/
42_God_Hung_Up_a_Snake/v1/` — narration.md + narration.mp3 (57.68s). Real

Distinct from Look and Live (short #1): that piece kept Moses OUT (the text
never named him) and centered on the invitation to look. THIS narration
directly quotes Moses's own forging action ("And Moses made a serpent of
brass, and put it upon a pole") and its own theology is starker — honesty/
no-softening ("God never once dressed it up as something else") building to
Christ BECOMING the curse (Gal 3:13 territory), not just being lifted up for
healing. Different key beat (the forge), different emotional register
(heavier, less invitational) — applying the textual-refrain lesson from #1
by design, not after the fact.
