# Independent review — gemini (OK, 58s)

**FINDINGS**

**Hook Score: 9/10**
The hook is excellent. "You'd expect the cure to be hung up looking hopeful - a light, a flag. God hung up a snake." It immediately establishes a stark paradox, avoids all template clichés, and demands attention in the first 5 seconds. 

**Landing Score: 7/10**
The landing imagery is fully earned and perfectly tied to the piece's spine ("carrying your exact curse" maps flawlessly to the bronze snake). However, it suffers from a fatal syntactical stumble on the very last word. 
*Line:* "He hung up Christ, His own Son, carrying your exact curse, so you could too."
*Critique:* Read this aloud. "So you could too" is meant to parallel "so the camp could live" from the previous sentence. But grammatically, it hooks onto the closest active verb phrase ("carrying your exact curse"). A listener with zero Bible knowledge will momentarily process this as "so you could *carry your exact curse* too?" before having to mentally backtrack. You cannot afford a cognitive stumble on the final four words of a CTA. The verb "live" must be explicit.

**Doctrinal Soundness & Grace-Anchored Conviction**
Excellent. Framing the cross not as a "gentler version" or a "general sacrifice" but as Christ becoming the "exact curse" is profoundly orthodox (Galatians 3:13) and avoids all works/fear framing. The conviction rests entirely on what Christ completed.

**KJV Verbatim**
The quote matches Numbers 21:9 perfectly. 

**Clarity & Thread**
The central thread—God not dressing up the cure, but using the "precise shape of their death"—tracks perfectly from the hook to the pivot to the CTA. It never swaps threads. 

VERDICT: REVISE

1. Fix the syntactical stumble in the final sentence. Make the verb explicit so listeners don't subconsciously connect "so you could too" with "carrying your exact curse." (e.g., "...carrying your exact curse, so that you could live.")
2. Maintain the stark, unapologetic tone of the ending when adjusting the grammar; do not soften "exact curse."
