You'd expect the cure to be hung up looking hopeful - a light, a flag. God hung up a snake. Not a comforting symbol. The precise shape of their death, lifted up where the whole camp could see it. "And Moses made a serpent of brass, and put it upon a pole, and it came to pass, that if a serpent had bitten any man, when he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived." Call it too grim for a cure if you like. But God never once dressed it up as something else. It stayed exactly what it was. We keep reaching for a gentler version of what Christ did - something less costly. He didn't become a general sacrifice. He became your curse. God hung up a snake so the camp could live. He hung up Christ, His own Son, carrying your exact curse, so you could too.
