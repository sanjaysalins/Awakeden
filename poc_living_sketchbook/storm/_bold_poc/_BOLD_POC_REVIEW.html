BOLD — four brave devices · taste test

2026-07-30 · The "less subtle" round. All $0, real Storm spreads, nothing locked — your eye decides what lives. Watch loud things quietly and quiet things loudly.

1 · The Erasure — He didn't fight the storm; He removed it

The storm spread is SCRUBBED OUT in ~1.3 seconds — wide back-and-forth eraser strokes, crumbs left on the sheet, the drawing gone to blank cream (the collage border survives: the eraser takes the drawing, not the book). Hold on the blankness. Then one steady Keeper line: "and it was gone."

Where it belongs: the rebuke beat, once per series-moment — this is a sacred-scale gesture, not a transition.
The theology IS the boldness: calm as removal, not struggle. If the blank page holds for a breath before the line arrives, the silence does the preaching.
Fable's eye-check: v1 ended foggy; rebuilt. v2's find — the erased page keeps the storm as a faint PENCIL GHOST: the whole scene still there, but with no ink left in it. The storm remembered, and powerless. The mid-scrub frame (erased above, still raging below) may be the strongest single image in either POC.

2 · The Torn-Out Page — the Keeper gives up on fear

The screaming-disciples page gets GRABBED — a slight lift, a shadow — then ripped out and away, white deckle flashing on the flying page, the "asleep" spread already waiting underneath. Rip SFX cue exported at the release frame.

Where it belongs: a hard act turn, ≤1 per episode — the fastest, most physical cut this book can make.
Governor, absolute: a page carrying the WORD is never torn out. Only the Keeper's own failed pages go.
Fable's eye-check: works as-is — the mid-flight frame (deckle flashing, shadow on the page beneath) reads exactly like a hand's violence, not a slide transition.

3 · The Bleeding Word — rain or tears; the page doesn't say

The panic entry writes itself (strikes, heave, skid) — then at 3.2s a single drop hits "fear." and the word blooms: ink darkening, edges dissolving, three thin trails finding their way down the page.

Where it belongs: the emotional bottom of an episode — one drop, one word, once. The word that bleeds should be the word the episode is about.
Keeper's words only — the Word never bleeds, never smears, never runs. Human ink is the fragile one; that contrast is the doctrine.

4 · The Dive — 9:16 is a fall waiting to happen

The camera opens at the masthead, holds a breath, then PLUNGES — down the mast, past the deck, into the waterline — arriving with a two-frame shudder exactly where the wave hits. One drawing, one drop.

Where it belongs: hooks and descents (the first 3 seconds of a Short is exactly this shape). This is the "rostrum camera" craft skill at its bravest setting — the same engine, calm, gives every episode its push-ins and drifts.
Honest note: this POC crops the 2k still directly, so the zoomed frames are slightly soft — production renders the composite supersampled (~2.2x) first. Judge the MOVE, not the pixels.
