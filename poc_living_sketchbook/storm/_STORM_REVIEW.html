The story, in one line

The storm is real, the fear is real, and Jesus sleeps through it — not because He doesn't care, but because He has nothing to fear from the very thing that's killing them. He wakes, asks one question, and silences the sea with a word. You've admired this scene your whole life — but when the water reaches your own knees, you don't trust the Christ in the boat either. He isn't asking for your composure. He's asking: why are you afraid?

v2 — what the user's feedback caught, and how it was fixed

  • No proper Christ reference: the Jesus anchor reused across every living-sketchbook episode so far was a single quick roll from the Door-episode POC, no iteration, no real /cast-bible rigor. Fixed: a full canon sheet (face geometry, hair/beard shape, skin, build, hands, garment) and a close-crop anchor, built via `poc_living_sketchbook/_r1_worldbible.py`, saved repo-level at `poc_living_sketchbook/cast/` for reuse across future episodes, user-approved before any stills were touched.
  • Boat design drifted scene to scene — masted in some v1 shots, oar-only in others, because nothing locked it. Fixed: one boat reference image, chained into every boat-visible shot.
  • T5 render-guardrail violation (`data/render_guardrails.md`, ≤3 sharp crowd faces or the animator hallucinates on push-in) — s09/s10 rendered 5-6 individually sharp disciple faces. Fixed: a 3-person Disciples reference, capped at exactly 3 in every crowd shot.
  • Stills wasted page real estate — large blank cream/kraft margins around a small inset scene. First fix pass helped some shots (s04/s09/s10) but not others; caught on full-resolution review, not the thumbnail contact sheet. Fixed with a stronger, more explicit framing instruction ("zoom in until the subject fills the entire frame, corner to corner, no blank kraft-paper region") and re-rendered the 8 shots that still had the problem.
Full redo: 3 world-bible references + 13 stills (rendered twice — first pass, then 8 re-rendered for the real-estate fix) + 13 clips + full re-assembly. The Scribed Ink and torn-page-landing fixes from v1 carried over clean (re-verified on the v2 render, no drip, verse still legible).

v3 — the user's own eyes caught 4 more real defects; a self-applied checklist caught a 5th

  • s02_water / s12_knees read as modern trousers, not a period tunic — the hitched-tunic hem was rendering as two separate leg-shapes at the ankle/knee. Fixed by describing the hem explicitly as ONE continuous garment ending above the knee, "never separate trouser legs."
  • s05_hands rendered TWO overlapping hands when the prompt asked for one resting hand alone — a duplicate-limb glitch invisible at contact-sheet scale. Fixed by stating "only ONE hand visible in frame" explicitly; "a hand" alone doesn't reliably read as singular to the model.
  • s07_eyes: "a giant Jesus in a small boat" — the close crop left his head disproportionately huge against a sliver of boat rail. Fixed by pulling the framing back to a mid-shot that keeps the boat's mast/bench/rail in frame as a scale reference.
  • The Fisherman read as Jesus — the two canon sheets both said "dark hair, short beard" and were never compared against EACH OTHER, only each against its own text. Fixed: Fisherman is now short-cropped hair (Jesus is long/wavy), a thick rough beard (Jesus is close-cropped), broader/ stockier build, forties (Jesus is early thirties) — a fully re-rendered anchor and s02/s06/s12.
  • Self-caught applying the new checklist: s09/s10 still showed 4 disciples, not the capped 3 — the "exactly three" wording in the prompt wasn't being enforced strictly enough. Fixed with an explicit numbered list + "no fourth person, no partial figure, even partially hidden."
These 5 defects prompted a standing process fix, not just a one-off patch: a new full-resolution Still QC Checklist is now locked into .claude/skills/living-sketchbook/SKILL.md §8a — anatomy (hand/limb count), period costume at full res (not gestalt), scale/proportion vs. the intended shot type, and cross-character distinctness checked BEFORE any scene stills are rendered. The rule it replaces ("eye-QC vs anchors + period + no baked text" on a thumbnail contact sheet) is exactly what let all 5 of these through undetected in v2.

v4 — two more user notes: s12 scale, and the landing needed to feel more alive

  • s12_knees: "this man looks like a giant on a boat" — same scale problem as v3's s07 fix, just missed on this spread. Fixed the same way: pulled the framing back to a mid-shot that keeps the boat's hull/mast/bench in view as a scale reference.
  • s13_landing: "should be more animated" — SKILL.md's "sacred stillness, camera never moves" rule is deliberate everywhere else, but the user wanted the FINAL spread to feel more alive. Chose a slow push-in toward the golden light. First attempt used ffmpeg's zoompan filter and produced NO visible zoom at all (3 attempts, various fixes, all static) — a real environment quirk, not a scripting typo. Switched to a fully deterministic approach instead: a Python/PIL loop that crops+resizes the still at 420 discrete zoom steps (1.0→1.32 over 14s) and encodes the sequence with ffmpeg — the same frame-sequence technique already proven reliable in this project's own assembly script. $0 cost, no AI re-render needed.

v1 — real defects hit and fixed during the first build (honest log, kept for the record)

  • The Scribed Ink verse card was rendering but was effectively invisible: v1's first assembly placed the Matthew 8:26 card low, over Jesus's robe/lap — the thin hand-inked strokes had almost no contrast against the busy tunic linework. Fixed by moving the card to the open stormy sky above his head, clear of his face.
  • Seedance invented a dripping trail on the landing spread (s13) that isn't in the original still — the same failure class as Jericho's invented blood-pool. Fixed by switching Seedance→Kling and locking the exact boundary with zero liquid-adjacent vocabulary. Re-verified clean on v2's fresh render of the same spread.

What this proves

Scribed Ink has a real, moving, finished proof, now on a properly world-bible-anchored episode: Jesus's identity holds across his widest pose range in this style (asleep, shaken, speaking, commanding, calm) against a real /cast-bible reference, not an ad-hoc one-off; the boat reads as the SAME boat in every shot; crowd scenes hold to the ≤3 sharp-face discipline this repo already had a rule for but I wasn't applying; and the page is used, not wasted. Two rounds of "look at the actual render, not the exit code" caught real defects each time in v1/v2 — and a third round (v3) confirms this needed to become a repeatable checklist, not repeated luck.
