The ship is covered with the waves. And in the stern — He is asleep. The Bible does not say why He sleeps. They wake Him: Lord, save us: we perish. He opens His eyes: Why are ye fearful, O ye of little faith? Then he arose, and rebuked the winds and the sea; and there was a great calm. You'll say — easy for Him, He's God. Exactly. The One who could sleep through it is the One who silenced it with a word. Maybe your own storm is loud right now. You can pray their short prayer: Lord, save us. He doesn't despise little faith. He grows it by showing you Himself. Their faith was little, and He still stood up for them. He is asking you — why are you afraid?
