# Independent review — gemini (OK, 57s)

Here is the independent, adversarial review of the narration artifact:

**Doctrinal Soundness & Grace-Anchored Conviction**
The theology is rock solid. The substitutionary atonement connection is accurate, and the grace framing is excellent: *"The cure was never in you; it hangs in plain sight, and costs you nothing but a look."* This perfectly avoids works-based framing and relies entirely on the Spirit's invitation. 

**KJV Verbatim**
The quote used (*"And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:"*) is an exact verbatim match for John 3:14. However, it is missing quotation marks in the text, which creates a visual bleed into the next sentence (*"My bronze was only a shadow."*). 

**Clarity on First Hearing**
The bridge connecting the Old Testament to the New Testament is convoluted and stalls the pacing: *"I speak now from the far side of my life, by the light that came after — a night I never saw, when one they called Teacher answered a seeker:"* This is 30 words of poetic time-travel mechanics just to introduce a quote. It forces the listener to solve a riddle ("Teacher" and "seeker" = Jesus and Nicodemus) rather than focusing on the cross. It is a clever-but-confusing conceit that distracts from the main point.

**One Thread Spine**
The spine is excellent. The verbs "bitten," "lifted," and "look" carry seamlessly from the hook through the middle and directly into the CTA. 

**SCORE THE HOOK: 7/10**
*"I am Moses. My people were dying of snakebite..."* 
It avoids templates, which is good, but starting with a theatrical "I am Moses" wastes the most critical first two seconds of a short. It buries the actual hook (the paradox of God using a snake to cure a snakebite). It needs to start immediately with the tension/paradox to stop a scroller.

**SCORE THE LANDING: 9/10**
*"So hear me, you who are bitten... Lift your eyes to Jesus, lifted up for you. Look, and live."*
This is a fantastic, earned landing. It relies entirely on the piece's own specific imagery ("bitten," "look, and live") and could not be pasted onto the end of any other video. 

Because the hook scores under an 8 and the bridge is overly convoluted, this requires a revision.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Cut the slow "I am Moses" intro. Start immediately with the crisis and the paradox to grab the scroller faster (e.g., "My people were dying of snakebite, and God's cure was a paradox...").
2. Rewrite the convoluted time-travel bridge ("I speak now from the far side of my life..."). Drop the "Teacher/seeker" riddle and bridge to Jesus' words simply and directly.
3. Add explicit quotation marks around the KJV text so the boundary between the scripture and the narrator's return ("My bronze was only a shadow") is instantly clear for the voice actor.
