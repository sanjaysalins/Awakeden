Character

Character

Aaron

83 at his first Day of Atonement (Exodus 7:7 + 40:17), died at 123 (Numbers 33:39) — already an old man at the start of the story, so one anchor covers his whole ~39-year priesthood. Signature garment: plain white linen (Leviticus 16:4).

Objects + Settings

Setting

The Tabernacle (exterior)

Plain hide roof outside, gold only glimpsed at the base (Exodus 26:14). The door-curtain shown here — plain needlework, no cherubim — is deliberately simpler than the inner veil.

world/tabernacle_ref.png

Object

The Veil

Blue/purple/scarlet linen with cherubim woven in gold thread (Exodus 26:31-33). Must read as the same curtain whole (Beats 1/3) and torn (Beats 6/7).

Fixed after 1st roll: cherubim first came out as Western cherub-babies with halos — anachronistic and inconsistent with the Ark's own cherubim. Re-rolled to ancient composite winged forms (Ezekiel-style lion/man/eagle faces).

world/veil_ref.png

Setting + Object

Holy of Holies + Ark + Mercy Seat

Exodus 25:10-22. A dark, windowless cube lit only by the glory-cloud, containing NOTHING but the ark — the emptiness is the point (Beat 6: "no chair behind that veil").

world/holyofholies_ref.png

Object

The Altar

Bronze, horned at each corner, five cubits square, three cubits high (Exodus 27:1-8) — rough, scorched, humble metal, a deliberate contrast to the gold furnishings inside.

world/altar_ref.png

Object

The Two Goats (one design, both animals)

Scripture gives no physical difference between the two goats (Leviticus 16:5-10) — the lot decides, not the animal's appearance. One design reused for both; distinguish by staging (altar/blood vs. led into the wilderness), never by marking.

Fixed after 1st roll: the page style's ink-red/ink-blue accent wash bled onto the goat's legs as streaks — readable as blood/dye on a sacrificial animal. Re-rolled with the accent confined to the paper border only.
