Built 2026-08-07 for the Seed of the Woman episode (Genesis 3), following
the same repo-level cast-bible pattern as `cast/AARON.md`/`cast/MOSES.md`.
Anchor: `cast/adam_ref.png` -- a regenerated portrait is a DIFFERENT face,
never lose the anchor. Flagged as a real gap by the independent-review

**Age/state verified against the text, not estimated:** Genesis 2:7 -- made
that same day, in the full prime of life, on the FIRST day fear or shame
ever existed anywhere. No numeric age is stated or estimable (Genesis
names no years before the fall); "prime of life, never old, never a boy"
is the only text-grounded age claim available, recorded per the standing
KJV-number discipline rather than inventing a figure.

**Cross-character distinctness checked against EVE** (SKILL.md sec.2's
anchor-approval-gate rule): Adam = short natural dark hair, close natural
beard, broader/more powerful build, bare-chested but for the fig-leaf
apron. Eve = long loose unbound hair, no beard (obviously), softer build,
fig-leaf covering wrapped to the body. Differs on hair length/style AND
build/silhouette at minimum, plus the garment cut itself differs.

## Canon description (paste VERBATIM into every prompt that shows Adam)

> Adam: the first man, freshly made that same day (Genesis 2:7) -- a man in the full prime of life, never old, never a boy. Face geometry: a strong open brow, straight nose, firm jaw, unweathered skin -- a face with no lines of age yet, only the new shock of fear and shame. Hair: dark brown, short, natural, uncut (no barber has ever touched it). Beard: short, close, natural growth, never groomed or shaped by a blade. Skin: warm olive Near-Eastern complexion, entirely unweathered -- this is the FIRST day anyone has ever been afraid. Build: powerfully made, broad-shouldered, an unspoiled human frame in its prime. Eyes: wide, stricken, ashamed -- a man who has never once before felt fear. Garment: a crude covering of stitched fig leaves tied at the waist as an apron (Genesis 3:7) -- NOT a coat of animal skin (that comes later, Genesis 3:21, after this story's own timeframe) -- otherwise bare-chested, no other clothing of any kind.
