## Independent adversarial review (narration)

### KJV verbatim — **FAIL on quoted block**
The attributed quote omits the opening word of the verse:

- **Artifact:** `'I will put enmity between thee and the woman…'`
- **KJV (project cache):** `And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.`

Everything after that matches the KJV text in your cache; the dropped **“And”** is still an altered quotation under a strict verbatim rule.

**Outside the quotes**, the script repeatedly says **“crushed skull”** while the only KJV wording is **“bruise thy head.”** That is acceptable exposition if you do not present “crushed skull” as KJV; here it is clearly paraphrase. No issue unless a listener thinks the Bible says “skull.”

---

### Doctrinal soundness
Mostly standard evangelical protoevangelium → Christ crushes the serpent’s head, the seed’s heel is struck. **No invented doctrine or clickbait reading.**

**Pressure points (skeptical):**

1. **“survivable bruise on the other”** — The cross is not a survivable bruise in any plain sense (death, burial, resurrection). The line **“a bruised heel that carried a cross”** partly repairs that, but **“survivable”** still risks sounding like the atonement was a minor wound. A careful listener could hear minimization of the cost of the cross.

2. **“Christ already delivered the blow that ends it”** — Past tense is fine for believers, but the piece never names **resurrection** or **ongoing** victory over sin/Satan in the believer’s life. That is not heresy; it is **compressed** to the point where “ends it” might mean “ends the cosmic duel” while **“what haunts you”** sounds personal—without saying *how* the crushed head relates to the listener’s haunt (forgiveness, indwelling Spirit, etc.). Slight **claim/application mismatch**, not a new doctrine.

3. **Capital “His”** on the seed’s heel — Implies Christ before the quote lands for a novice; orthodox, but the **“it shall bruise”** KJV line does not say “His” in the same clause (the quote correctly has **“his heel”** lowercase in KJV).

### Clarity on first hearing (zero Bible knowledge)
**Weak opening for strangers.**

- **“You've heard Genesis 3:15 means…”** — Assumes they know the reference and a popular summary. A listener with **zero Bible knowledge** does not know what “Genesis 3:15” is or what they have “heard.”
- **“thy seed and her seed”** inside the quote is KJV-faithful but **opaque on audio** without a one-beat gloss (“the woman’s descendant”).
- The **tie / blow / haunt** metaphor in the middle is followable, but **“what haunts you”** is vague (guilt? anxiety? the devil?) and never tied to the serpent/enmity line from the verse.

No obvious self-contradiction; the **asymmetry (not a tie)** thread is logically consistent.

### Grace-anchored conviction
**Mostly invitation-by-declaration**, not fear/gain-loss stock.

**Friction:**

- **“You keep trying to land your own blow against what haunts you, hoping for a tie you can live with”** — Second-person, continuous **“you keep trying”** can land as **moral diagnosis** (you’re striving wrong) without a parallel **grace release** in the same sentence (e.g. rest in his victory). The next line **“Christ already delivered…”** pivots to gospel, but the beat still feels **accusatory** for ~5 seconds. Not works-salvation, but not fully **Spirit convicts / script invites** in tone.

No “if you don’t…”, no hell scare, no bargain framing — good.
