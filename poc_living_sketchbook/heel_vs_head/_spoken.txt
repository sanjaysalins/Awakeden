You've heard Genesis 3:15 means Jesus beats the devil. It says something sharper - one of them gets a bruise, and the other gets a crushed skull. This isn't a tie. God tells the serpent his head will be crushed, and tells the woman's seed only His heel will be bruised - the outcome was fixed before the fight began. God says to the serpent, 'I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.' You might expect matching wounds for a promise this old - instead you get a crushed skull on one side and a survivable bruise on the other. You keep trying to land your own blow against what haunts you, hoping for a tie you can live with. Christ already delivered the blow that ends it. Not a tie - a bruised heel that carried a cross, and a crushed head that stays crushed forever.
