No-camera hold POC — plain static vs the "spotlight" family

These are spreads that used a slow camera push/arc as their MAIN animation (a substitute for a generative clip that invented bad content). With all camera movement removed, they become plain frozen stills unless something else carries them. Compared here: a true static hold (nothing moving at all) against 3 light-only "spotlight" treatments from panel_animator/focal_tour.py — light settles on the face, everything else dims slightly. No camera, no crop, no zoom in any of these.
