You've heard it as Plan B — Eden fell apart, so God improvised a rescue and sent His Son. That is not what the oldest promise says. Genesis already named this seed — a promise spoken over a woman in the garden. Jesus wasn't a rescue invented in the fulness of time. He was the plan, named first. Paul writes: 'But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law.' Paul could have written son of David, son of Abraham — a line that runs through the man, like every title before it. Here, he doesn't. He writes: made of a woman, the woman promised in the garden. You still reach for your own rescue plans — the fix, the resolution, the version of you that finally gets it right. But the plan was never yours to write. This was never Plan B. It was the plan, kept word for word — and He has already arrived for you.
