Her Seed — s03 / s05 motion concepts

Design pass by Fable, this preview built in CSS/SVG over the actual rendered stills - a rough approximation of what a real keyframed motion graphic (Remotion or hand-built) could look like, not the final render. Loops every ~4.5-5s.
s03

The Lamp Finds It Finished

The only living thing is the lamplight. It sweeps the page once like a reading eye and finds every line already there - motion of discovery, never of writing. The page never gains a stroke; it only ages a little warmer where the light has passed, and a reader's tick lands beside the topmost line at the end.

s05

The Rubber-Stamp Genealogy

Five identical stamp-presses in metronome rhythm, cadence tightening - a bored scribe's habit, "like every title before it." The line runs through each man in turn. Past the fifth, it continues into blank ruled paper... and stops mid-stroke. A small ink blot feeds where the sixth entry should be. The unwritten name is the trapdoor the next spread falls through.

Rough concept only - real timing/easing (hand-wobbled stroke fronts, raking-light specular masking on the actual ink strokes, true squash-press per figure) would need the real device library (panel_animator/raking_light.py, blue_line.py, keeper_hand.py's starve/blot behavior) working directly against each still's own pixel geometry, or a Remotion composition if we want a genuinely programmatic build. This is close enough to judge the direction before committing engineering time.
