**Doctrinal soundness & Biblical Precision:** 
There is a factual biblical error here. You write: *"a promise spoken over a woman in the garden."* Genesis 3:15 (the protoevangelium) was spoken directly to the **serpent**, not the woman ("And the LORD God said unto the serpent... I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed"). While it concerns her, saying it was "spoken over" her misrepresents the text's actual confrontation. 

**KJV verbatim:** 
The quote from Galatians 4:4 (*"But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law."*) is exact KJV. 

**Clarity on first hearing:** 
You have a massive missing antecedent. You write: *"Genesis already named this seed..."* What seed? You haven't mentioned a seed yet. You jumped straight from "God improvised a rescue" to "named this seed." A listener with zero Bible knowledge will be completely lost because you assumed they already know Genesis 3:15 is about the "seed of the woman." 

Additionally, the phrasing *"He writes: made of a woman, the woman promised in the garden"* blurs the line between Scripture and commentary. Because it's spoken aloud, a listener might think Paul actually wrote the phrase "the woman promised in the garden."
