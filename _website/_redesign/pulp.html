About Awakeden

Awakeden makes hand-inked comic Bible shorts that find Jesus in the whole Bible, one panel at a time. We take the craft seriously and the Word more seriously: Scripture is never paraphrased on our pages. It is quoted verbatim from the King James Version and always printed in red, so you can see exactly where our ink ends and His words begin.
