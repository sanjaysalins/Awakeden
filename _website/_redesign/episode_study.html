New Testament

John 19:24

John stands at the cross and names the fulfilment outright, citing the psalm word for word.

The New Testament itself points back at the verse.

Honest scholarship

Why we didn't quote "they pierced my hands and my feet" (Psalm 22:16): the Hebrew text of that word is genuinely disputed. This piece rests its whole case on the undisputed garments verse - which John 19:24 cites outright.

The deeper treatment lives in the film.

Context

David died an old man in his bed (1 Kings 2:10). The psalm is first-person, but it describes a death David never died.

Where it lands

Promised a thousand years before He came, and given for you.
