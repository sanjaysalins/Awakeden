# THE LIVE INK — rationale

## The structural bet

There is no lobby. "The website is the main meal" is taken literally: the front
page IS a full study (real Bronze Serpent narration, real stills), one
continuous sheet of paper that is *made while you read it*. Navigation lives at
the END, as a manuscript colophon ("set table"), because a book earns its index.
No hero/eyebrow/CTA stack, no card grid, no parallel margin column — replaced by
a title leaf that inks the wordmark on, and by ONE structural element: a gold
thread (a single SVG path, ~18,000px long) that runs the whole document. Its
drawn tip rides the reader's reading line, so it is simultaneously the progress
indicator, the OT→NT connective theology (it ties a knot at Numbers 21:8, a
second at John 3:14), and the ending — it dives into the torn page where Christ
stands. Structure IS the doctrine: one thread, whole Bible, lands on Jesus.

## Device → interaction mapping (mechanics, not vibes)

- **blue_line.py** — the opening plate's underdrawing asset was generated by the
  repo's own `make_underdrawing_plate`; `_wobbled_diagonal_mask` (step/jitter/
  feather constants) is ported to JS; ink arrives once, ~1s, on first view.
- **Scribed Ink (SKILL §5)** — KJV verses letter themselves under the reading
  line, seeded per-glyph rotation/baseline wobble, wet-ink darkening. **LAW 1
  kept:** divine speech (Numbers 21:8, John 3:14–15) never letter-reveals — it
  arrives WHOLE between two frames (a visibility flip with deliberately no
  transition).
- **annotators_circle.py** — same math (seeded 3–4 sine radius wobble, two
  passes at PASS1_END 0.6, pads 0.32/1.05, 1.4s) draws around "looketh."
  Governor kept: one circle on the whole page.
- **ink_transition.py** — `make_reveal_field` (3-octave value noise + distance,
  0.32/0.68 mix) ported to JS; thresholded per scroll frame exactly like
  `render_mask_frames`. The paper itself turns to night and back — no fade.
- **hunt-and-lock + grid_choreography.py** — two 320vh sticky camera sections:
  scroll scrubs full→push→hold→rack→release; vignette = rack-focus dimming.
- **marginalia.py** — labels are real chosen words with a 12-step jittered
  leader line that draws at lock (and points at the true detail under
  reduced motion).
- **raking_light.py** — a scroll-scrubbed grazing band over the shadow-cross
  plate; the flare fires on the page's own gold-leaf edge.
- **held_breath.py** — the reader is the narrator: scroll = speech, dwell =
  silence; energy eases to floor 0.25 (never a freeze), with authored gaps in
  scroll-space at the confession and the landing. It multiplies the thread
  glint, label line-boil, and camera breathing.

Deliberately not used: bleeding_word (its governor is one word per episode —
this page's word got the circle).

## The honest tradeoff

A page that draws itself taxes the first visit: a continuous rAF loop, two
canvas fields, and 450+ letter spans. Costs are bounded (fields computed once at
~520×640, canvases redrawn only on real progress change, widgets skipped when
off-screen), but this page will always burn more CPU than a static one, and a
reader who stops mid-verse sees ink parked mid-word — the price of reader-driven
time. `prefers-reduced-motion` is fully honored (everything renders finished,
labels point at their details; tested with an emulated pass) — but that version
loses the "being made" thesis, which is a real loss, not a footnote.
