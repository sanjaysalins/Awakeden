The house rules

What this place is

Awakeden reads the whole Bible — Old Testament and New — as one story that lands on Jesus. The Shorts are the spark. The films go deeper. These pages are the table, where you sit down and actually eat. Four promises hold everywhere on this site:

  • Every verse verbatim. King James Version, word for word, reference given — so you can check us. Please do.
  • Hard texts faced in the open. The verse that seems to break the story gets quoted on the page, not buried. If we hid it, why would you trust us on the rest?
  • No fear, no pressure, no countdown. The invitation here is grace or it is nothing. Nothing on this site will ever frighten you toward God.
  • Every page ends at Jesus. Old Testament or New, every thread walks to Him. The destination is printed on the signpost — you will never be ambushed by it.
