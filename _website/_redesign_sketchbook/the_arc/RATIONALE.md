# THE ARC — rationale

**The structural bet:** the Gospel Five-Beat is no longer just how the films are written —
it is the page. The study page IS five stops on one gold thread (Hook → Point → Proof →
Conviction → Landing), and the thread is drawn as the page's visible spine: a five-knot
rail that fills as you read, seams that pass through a knot at every act-turn, a tie-off
after the landing. **The destination is announced before you walk** ("this road ends at
Jesus — you should know that before you set out"). That one honesty device is the round's
signature: it serves reader #3 (no bait-and-switch, no ambush — grace can't be sprung on
someone), reader #1 (five stops, ~8 min, jump anywhere — including straight to the proof),
and reader #2 (the arc is a teachable outline, visible at all times).

## The arc, stop by stop — and who each choice serves

- **Hook — "Healed by looking at a bronze snake."** The receipt (Numbers 21:8, verbatim,
  reference) sits inside the first screen and a half. Reader #1 came asking "is that
  actually in the Bible?" — they get the answer before a single scroll decision. No cover
  to open, no menu: round 2's book-cover gate was cut precisely because it taxed this reader.
- **Point — the full Numbers 21 walk.** Every quote verbatim KJV; the film's Moses appears
  only as attributed pull-quotes. Reader #2's meat: nothing paraphrased where it matters.
- **Proof — Jesus' own words.** The proof is not our typology; it is John 3:14 — Jesus
  citing this story about Himself. Red letter arrives whole (LAW 1: never letter-revealed,
  never entrance-animated; arc.js enforces it with a filter that strips reveals from
  anything containing scripture). Gal 3:13 answers "why a serpent." The **honest objection
  (Nehushtan, 2 Kings 18:4 in full)** sits at the END of proof, BEFORE conviction: a skeptic
  who has just seen receipts is silently asking "what are they hiding?" — so we raise the
  hardest counter-verse ourselves, on kraft paper, in the open, and say why: "if we hid it,
  you would be right not to trust us on the easy ones." Readers #2 and #3 equally.
- **Conviction — "That is you. That is me."** Moses' plate faces the reader; shorter
  measure, more air. Diagnosis honest, zero fear-lever: the hinge line is "you were never
  asked to." Reader #3, protected by the studio's grace-anchored lock.
- **Landing — gold is finally allowed.** John 3:16 illuminated (gold drop cap), the film's
  torn-page plate, "Look to Him, and live." No CTA button: the landing offers **a written
  prayer** ("if you want words —") plus the anti-CTA promise: *no form, nothing to join,
  nothing to buy; that part is between you and Him.* The reader is given something to do
  with God, not with the website. Navigation (film, shelf) comes only AFTER the tie-off —
  a book earns its index (kept from round 2, now justified: the landing is never upstaged).

**The homepage is the same arc in sixty seconds:** the visitor's own thought in the film's
hand ("wait — is that actually in the Bible?"), the two slips — Numbers 21:8 and red-letter
John 3:14 — physically tied by the gold thread, one walk-line into the study (reader #1's
whole journey), then the four house rules as trust promises (readers #2/#3), then the shelf
as an honest leader-dot index with "pages uncut" statuses — not a card grid — and even the
porch's colophon lands on John 3:16.

**Cut from rounds 1–2:** the book-cover gate, the interactive tear (no tricks at the sacred
beat — the film's own torn-page plate carries it in stillness), per-paragraph ink-write
delays. **Kept:** the gold thread (promoted from scrollbar to argument), the objection
instinct, one annotator's circle ("looketh" — the word the study hangs on), reduced-motion
renders everything finished. Palette theology intact: blue = apparatus, red = His words,
gold = thread + landing only.
