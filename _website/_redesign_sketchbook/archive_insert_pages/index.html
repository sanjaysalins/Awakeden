How this archive is inked

One hand — and its insert pages

The whole archive is drawn in one plain hand: Style One. A few pages are deliberately different — insert pages, each register kept for one kind of moment. Never a costume change; always a reason.

Style One — the spine

Aged paper, ink and wash. Every story page, every reading page. The default is the identity.

Scholar’s Margin

Diagrams, threads, cross-references. The index is filed in this hand — and so is every “shadow → body” breakdown.

Gilded Proclamation

Cracked gold ground. Kept for declared Scripture and Christ’s glory — one gold moment per page, at most.

The Blueprint (cyanotype)

Cold blue for what is announced but not yet inked. Coming episodes are filed as blueprints in the index.
