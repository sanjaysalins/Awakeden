About ◆ Awakeden

Finding Jesus in the whole Bible, one panel at a time.

How people saw Him, and how everything led to Him: Old Testament and New, fully grounded and rooted in the Bible.

Awakeden reads the whole Bible with one question: where is Jesus here? He is the same Lord across all of Scripture, the thread running from the first promise to the empty tomb. We read closely, go a little deeper, and follow that thread until it lands on Him, because every piece lands on Jesus.

It is one hand-inked world, three ways in: sixty-second shorts, ten-minute films, and this site, where you sit down and read the whole study: every panel, every verse, laid out to be read slowly. Edgy, never irreverent. The red is sacred: bright red is reserved for the words of Scripture.

The creed

This is not a retelling. Every word of Scripture is quoted verbatim from the King James Version, and every quoted word is printed in red. The ink is ours. The words are His. Each piece is reviewed for faithfulness to the text before it ships.

What to expect

Series work through one window of Scripture at a time: Jesus in the Old Testament, the last words from the cross, the Questions Jesus Asked, the people who met Him, His titles and "I AM" sayings, the Kingdom of God. The first cluster is The Cross (Psalm 22, Zechariah, and the seven last words), with Isaiah 53 in the studio and more on the way.
