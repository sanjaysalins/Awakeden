- **THE_VISION** [motif]: the prophet beholding a far-off prophetic vision of the suffering and execution of the Servant, the whole scene seen clearly seven hundred years before it came to pass
- **THREE_DAYS_DARKNESS** [motif]: the deep enclosing dark of three days and three nights buried away in the heart of the earth, the lightless interval before the appointed return to life
- **THREE_DAYS_DEEP** [motif]: three days and three nights buried alive in the swallowing dark of the deep, then given back living to the light, the prophet's ordeal standing as the sign of resurrection
- **TWO_LOTS** [motif]: the high priest casting lots over the two goats standing side by side at the tabernacle door, one lot fallen for the LORD and one for the scapegoat, their fates divided
- **UNBROKEN_BONE** [motif]: the roasted Passover lamb held whole and entire, not one of its bones broken, the strict command honored that finds its fulfillment in Christ's own unbroken legs
- **WOUNDS_BORNE** [motif]: the Servant's body marked with stripe-wounds and bruises that glow faintly with the weight of substitution, the sins of many visibly laid upon and absorbed into his flesh
