# Independent review — grok (OK, 95s)

**CITATION TRUTH and NO INVENTED FACTS failures dominate the artifact.** The entire document presents detailed visual specifications as "LOCKED canonical reference descriptions" and "PERMANENT visual description for one Bible element" with zero quoted KJV text, zero verse citations, and zero "specified / constrained / free" buckets anywhere. The opening lens requirement ("for every fact, does the quoted KJV text (shown under each fact) ACTUALLY support the claim?") is not met by the artifact at all.

**SCAPEGOAT [charsheet] asserts a non-biblical detail as canonical visual fact:**
> "a lone shaggy Near-Eastern male goat with rough tawny-and-white hair, curved horns and dark sorrowful eyes, a single crimson scarlet cord of wool tied about one horn, driven solitary across a barren cracked-earth wilderness"

Leviticus 16:8, 21-22 mentions the lot for the scapegoat and sending it away by the hand of a fit man. It says nothing about a crimson cord on the horn. This is later rabbinic practice (Mishnah Yoma), not Scripture. Presented as locked reference.

**MARY [charsheet] locks post-biblical artistic tradition as fact:**
> "a gentle Hebrew maiden of about sixteen with smooth olive skin and dark hair veiled under a soft blue head-mantle... dressed in a simple modest robe of deep blue over undyed linen"

Luke 1 gives no age and no clothing color. "About sixteen" and "deep blue" are medieval/Byzantine iconographic conventions, not biblical. No verse supports either.

**JESUS [charsheet] imposes a specific non-scriptural physical portrait:**
> "long dark brown hair parted in the middle to the shoulders, a full dark brown beard... face + full standing body anchor grid (head about one-seventh of standing height)"

No Gospel or NT text describes Jesus' hair length, parting, color, beard details, or head-to-body proportion. This is a locked artistic choice presented as the permanent canonical reference for all reuse.

**SEATED_PRIEST [motif] doctrinally blurs type and antitype:**
> "the high priest, his atoning work at last complete, sitting down in rest at the right hand of God, the seated posture itself declaring the offering finished forever"

Hebrews 10:11-12 explicitly contrasts the Levitical priests who "standeth daily ministering" with Christ who "sat down on the right hand of God". The OT high priest is never described sitting at God's right hand. This locks a visual that erases the distinction the passage exists to make.

**NINEVITES [group] adds an extra-biblical visual setting:**
> "faces bowed in penitence, scattering dust and ashes upon their heads before the carved winged-bull gates of the great cruel city"

Jonah 3-4 and Nahum describe neither the gates nor "winged-bull" features. This is archaeological reconstruction asserted as the locked scene element.

**ADULTEROUS_WOMAN [charsheet] adds unstated physical details:**
> "her plain earth-toned robe disordered and slipping from one shoulder, standing alone and trembling on the dusty temple ground"

John 8:3-11 places her "in the midst," states the accusers leave, and leaves her alone with Jesus. It describes no robe disarray, no slipping garment, no specific "trembling on the dusty temple ground."

**HEZEKIAH [charsheet] fabricates clothing and pose:**
> "wearing a richly dyed blue-and-crimson royal robe with an embroidered hem and a simple gold circlet, his strong hands raised to break the bronze serpent in pieces"

2 Kings 18:4 says only that "he brake in pieces the brasen serpent." No garment description, no circlet, no raised-hands pose is given.

**SERPENT [charsheet] (Eden) assigns invented appearance:**
> "a great sinuous ancient snake with iridescent dark-green and bronze scales catching a cold gleam"

Genesis 3:1 gives only "more subtil than any beast of the field." No color, iridescence, or gleam is stated.

**ANGEL_OF_THE_LORD [charsheet] over-specifies wings and face:**
> "a luminous youthful face of unearthly beauty framed by flowing light-touched hair... great softly-glowing wings half-spread"

Multiple OT appearances of the Angel of the LORD (Gen 16, 22, Exod 3, Judg 6, 13) describe no wings. Wings belong to cherubim (on the ark) and seraphim (Isa 6). This risks conflating distinct categories.

**Redundant/split entries without clear distinction:**
- ARK_AND_MERCY_SEAT and ARK_OF_THE_COVENANT are near-duplicates (both describe the gold-overlaid acacia chest with cherubim mercy seat, both distinguish from "Noah's barge-ark").
- BLOOD_ON_DOORPOST, HOUSE_DOOR, DOORWAY, and BLOOD_MARKED_DOOR heavily overlap.

**Systemic problems with the "canonical" claim:**
- Repeated assertions of exact ages ("about eighty", "about a hundred", "about sixteen", "about ten", "about thirty", "about fifty", "thirty-eight years" where only the lame man has the number in text).
- Repeated unstated clothing colors, embroidery, headgear, and skin/hair specifics presented at the same locked level.
- No buckets anywhere, so artistic choices (exact skin tones for every Hebrew character as "weathered olive skin", specific poses, "about X years") are not marked as free or constrained.
- Coverage gaps: no Joseph (husband of Mary), no explicit anti-hallucination constraints on the CROSS entry (titulus, corpus, nail count), no separate guard for the temple veil being the later temple vs. tabernacle version in some descriptions.
- THE_LORD entry correctly restricts to "never a human face or body" and uses "overwhelming radiant golden light... never a human face or body." This is one of the few entries that meets the doctrinal test in the additional context.

**CHRIST-LENS issues:** Several motifs (SEATED_PRIEST above, also the direct application of "high priest... at the right hand of God") risk flattening typology by visually merging the shadow with the substance. The library title asserts these are "LOCKED canonical" for a "reusable Bible-art reference library" while most visual specifics are not canonical.

The artifact repeatedly dresses artistic reconstruction, tradition, and inference as permanent locked facts without the required citations or bucket honesty.
