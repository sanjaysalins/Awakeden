# Independent review — codex (OK, 145s)

**Systemic Findings**

1. The artifact has **no KJV quotations, no verse references, and no specified/constrained/free buckets at all**. That fails the main audit target. A line like `MARY ... about sixteen ... soft blue head-mantle` may be artistic licence, but the artifact gives no way to distinguish licence from Bible-stated fact.

2. Many entries assert visual specifics as locked canonical facts when Scripture does not state them: ages, hair lengths, skin tones, garment colors, props, facial expressions, architectural details, and exact object forms. In a reusable “canonical reference” library, that is not harmless style; it hardens invention into visual doctrine.

**Concrete Findings**

3. `AARON ... barefoot and bare-headed` contradicts Leviticus 16:4, which specifies linen garments including the linen mitre. Barefoot is not stated either. This is a direct biblical visual error.

4. `HIGH_PRIEST_LINEN_GARMENT ... plain undyed white linen tunic and sash` omits the linen breeches and linen mitre from Leviticus 16:4. If this is meant to lock Day of Atonement visuals, it is incomplete and misleading.

5. `ANGEL_OF_THE_LORD ... great softly-glowing wings` is unsupported. Appearances of the angel of the LORD are not described as winged in the cited OT narratives, and the identity often carries theophanic weight. This risks turning a divine/theophanic appearance into a generic winged angel.

6. `THE_FATHER` is the prodigal’s father, but the element name itself is doctrinally dangerous in a Bible-art library. It can easily be confused with God the Father, especially because the brief forbids giving the Father a human face/body.

7. `SCAPEGOAT ... a single crimson scarlet cord of wool tied about one horn` is rabbinic/traditional, not Leviticus 16. It cannot be in a biblical “specified” bucket.

8. `BRONZE_SERPENT_STANDARD ... MOUNTED ON TOP ... does NOT spiral, wind or coil down the pole` overreaches Numbers 21:8-9. The text says Moses put the serpent “upon a pole”; it does not specify summit placement or forbid a coiled form.

9. `SLAIN_GOAT ... lying killed upon the altar stones` is wrong for the tabernacle setting. The tabernacle altar was bronze, not a stone altar. Leviticus 16 does not place the goat “upon altar stones.”

10. `ISAIAH_SCROLL ... aged seven-hundred-year-old parchment scroll` is false for Acts 8. The Ethiopian eunuch was reading Isaiah’s prophecy; the scroll itself was not a 700-year-old original.

11. `TEMPLE_LAMPS ... four giant golden menorah-lampstands ... Feast of Tabernacles` is historical/Mishnah background, not Scripture-stated visual fact. It must not be presented as biblical specification.

12. `DROPPED_STONES ... execution stones dropped and forsaken` is not stated in John 8. The accusers leave; the text does not say they had stones in hand or dropped them.

13. `WATERPOT ... dropped on its side at a well's stone rim` overstates John 4:28. The woman “left her waterpot”; it does not say she dropped it, nor that it lay on its side.

14. `WELL ... a water jar and rope at its rim` may contradict John 4:11, where the woman says, “thou hast nothing to draw with.” A visible rope at the rim weakens that textual fact unless carefully qualified.

15. `DREAM_SHEAVES_STARS ... Joseph's twin dreams rendered as a single nocturnal vision` wrongly conflates two distinct dreams from Genesis 37.

16. `FACES_OF_DEAD_PROPHETS ... John the Baptist, Elias and Jeremias` visualizes speculative identifications from Matthew 16:14 as ghostly apparitions. Also Elijah is not a “dead prophet” in the ordinary biblical sense.

17. `DARKENED_SKY ... midday darkness ... at the ninth hour` misstates the timing. The darkness was from the sixth hour to the ninth hour, not merely “at the ninth hour” (Matt. 27:45).

18. `STIRRING_WATER ... false and unreachable hope of healing` contradicts the KJV form of John 5:4, where the troubled water is associated with actual healing of the first entrant.

19. `SEATED_PRIEST ... the high priest ... sitting down in rest at the right hand of God` conflates the OT high priest with Christ’s completed priesthood in Hebrews. If depicted as an Aaronic priest at God’s right hand, it is doctrinally wrong.

20. `CAESAR_TEMPLE` and `PAGAN_SHRINE` lock Caesarea Philippi historical reconstruction as if it were biblical visual fact. Greco-Roman, not Buddhist, is sensible, but Scripture does not specify the temple/shrine visuals in Matthew 16.

21. `SHEPHERD ... lying across the open gap ... as a living gate` and `SHEPHERD_AS_DOOR` rely on a common sermon/custom claim, not a stated biblical scene. John 10 says Christ is the door; it does not specify this sheepfold posture.

22. `GOLGOTHA ... scattered with old bones` is an invented visual from “place of a skull.” The Gospels do not state exposed bones at the crucifixion site.

23. `GOPHER_WOOD ... pale gopher-wood timber` overreaches. The identity and appearance of gopher wood are unknown.

24. `GRAIN_SACKS ... jute and burlap sacks` is an anachronistic/material overclaim. Genesis says sacks; it does not specify jute or burlap.

25. `MARY ... about sixteen ... soft blue head-mantle ... deep blue` is tradition/art convention, not Scripture. It should be explicitly licence.

26. `JESUS ... long dark brown hair parted in the middle to the shoulders` is not biblically specified. A locked Jesus face is necessary for consistency, but the artifact must not imply Scripture states these features.

27. `REDEMPTION_PRICE ... silver pieces ... full purchase price paid at the city gate` invents money in Ruth 4. The legal redemption is stated; a counted silver price is not.

28. `GOLDEN_CALF_DUST ... powder strewn in the dust at its base` misstates Exodus 32:20, where Moses ground the calf to powder, strewed it upon the water, and made Israel drink it.

**Coverage Gaps**

29. The Day of Atonement set misses or under-guards several specified details: linen breeches and mitre, the bullock for Aaron, the two goats presented before the LORD, the lot “for the LORD” and the lot “for the scapegoat,” and the cloud of incense before the mercy seat.

30. Noah’s ark entries mention door, pitch, dimensions, and broad box form, but do not guard the three stories/lower-second-third structure from Genesis 6:16.

31. Crucifixion motifs need tighter guards for specified facts: darkness from sixth to ninth hour, the veil torn from top to bottom, lots cast for garments, vinegar on sponge/hyssop or reed according to account, and no broken bones.

VERDICT: FAIL
TOP FIXES:
1. Add verse references, quoted KJV support, and honest specified/constrained/free buckets for every locked visual claim.
2. Remove or relabel invented specifics: ages, colors, props, facial details, historical reconstructions, rabbinic traditions, and sermon-custom details.
3. Correct direct biblical errors: Aaron bare-headed, Isaiah scroll age, altar stones for the tabernacle goat, combined Joseph dreams, Bethesda “false hope,” and the timing of the crucifixion darkness.
