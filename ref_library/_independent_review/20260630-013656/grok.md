## Independent adversarial review — Awakeden Base-Elements Library v2

Review lens: depiction faithfulness (no KJV buckets here — not penalized). Skeptical pass on doctrine, invented “biblical” detail, type confusion, and internal contradictions.

---

### Doctrinal / depiction errors

**FACES_OF_DEAD_PROPHETS** — Headline says “dead prophets,” but the line itself admits Elijah was “taken up alive.” Scripture never calls Elijah dead (2 Kings 2:11). Putting him under “faces of **dead** prophets” is a factual error and blurs a live prophet with dead ones (Jeremiah, and John only after beheading).

**MAN_CAST_INTO_SEA** — “while above and around him the violent storm falls suddenly still” contradicts Jonah 1:15–17. They cast Jonah in; the sea **raged more** (v. 15), then calmed later (1:15–16 / ch. 2). A locked image of instant stillness at the splash teaches the wrong sequence.

**ARM_OF_THE_LORD** — “a strong outstretched **divine arm** of light” risks the same fault you fixed in **THE_LORD**: an anatomical divine limb. If painters render a literal arm, that breaks the light/glory-only rule for God’s presence.

**SHUT_IN_BY_GOD** — “the LORD's own unseen **hand** drawing the great ark door closed” — same risk. Genesis 7:16 says the LORD shut him in; it does not describe a hand. Easy slide into anthropomorphic God-body art.

**SEATED_PRIEST** — “seated down in rest at the **right hand of God**” does not say how God is shown. Any seated-Christ + Father depiction here will tempt a human divine figure unless the sheet locks Father to glory/light only (as **THE_LORD** does).

---

### Internal contradictions (library will fight itself)

**SERPENT_LIFTED_HIGH** vs **BRONZE_SERPENT_STANDARD** — Object line is explicit: “the serpent does **NOT** spiral, wind or **coil** down the pole.” Motif says “its dark **coiled** form standing out against the open sky.” Same bronze-serpent asset, opposite instructions. One will render as Rod-of-Asclepius by accident.

**SHEPHERD** [charsheet] vs **SHEPHERD_AS_DOOR** [motif] — Charsheet: shepherd “**lying across the open gap of a dry-stone sheepfold as a living gate**.” That is John 10 Christ typology, not a generic Near Eastern herdsman. Reusing **SHEPHERD** for David, Moses, or any non-Christ shepherd **conflates** Christ-the-Door with ordinary shepherds.

**THREE_DAYS_DARKNESS** — Name points to Exodus 10:22–23 (land darkness). Description is burial/resurrection interval: “**buried away in the heart of the earth**.” That is Jonah/Christ typology, not the Egyptian plague. Wrong label + mixed types in one locked motif.

**RAISED_TO_RIGHT_HAND** — Starts as Joseph (“lifted out of the **pit and prison**… **high throne**”) but ends “**at the right hand**” — language of Psalm 110 / Hebrews 1, not Joseph’s Egyptian court. Type and antitype are blurred in one canonical tile.

**GLASSY_STILL_SEA** — “water smooth as **polished stone**” / “glassy” reads like Revelation 4:6’s sea before the throne, while the scene is Mark 4:39 Galilee calm. Two distinct “seas” merged.

### Invented or traditional detail presented as canonical

**TEMPLE_LAMPS** — “**four giant golden menorah-lampstands** blazing in the temple court at the Feast of Tabernacles” — the four colossal lampstands come from later rabbinic tradition (Mishnah Sukkah), not KJV narrative. Locked as if biblical scene fact.

**MARY** — “**soft blue head-mantle**” and “robe of **deep blue**” — Marian blue is church tradition; Scripture does not give her colours. Fine as art licence; wrong as implicit biblical specification in a locked library.

**ADULTEROUS_WOMAN** — “**loose dark dishevelled hair**,” robe “**slipping from one shoulder**” — not in John 8. Sensational tradition, not text.

**FIRSTBORN_SON** — “young boy of **about ten**” — age unstated in Exodus 12.

**ANIMAL_PAIRS** — “going up **two by two**” — incomplete. Genesis 7:2–3: clean animals by **sevens**; unclean by pairs. A procession of only pairs misstates the flood boarding.

---

### Scripture over-reach on character sheets

**ABRAHAM** — “about **a hundred**” on the patriarch sheet conflicts with Moriah binding. Isaac was born when Abraham was 100 (Gen 21:5); the offering is years later — Abraham would be roughly **115+**, not ~100. Same locked face for both scenes.

**ISAAC** — “Abraham's **only** beloved son” — true only inside Genesis 22:2’s rhetorical frame. Ishmael exists (Gen 16). As a global charsheet label it overstates outside Moriah.

**COSTLY_REDEMPTION** — “gaining no profit for himself but **her alone**” — Ruth 4:5–10: Boaz also redeems **land** and perpetuates the family name. Not “her alone.”

**PHILIP** — “Philip the **evangelist**” for the Acts 8 chariot scene — Acts 8 is commonly the apostle Philip; “the evangelist” is a different figure in Acts 21:8. Risk of conflating two Philips.

---

### Wrong culture / anachronism (lighter, still real)

**PILATE** — “**white toga** bordered with a deep **purple senatorial stripe**” — senatorial toga pattern; a prefect’s dress would differ. Roman-period, but rank-wrong details in a locked reference.

**JERUSALEM_CITY** — “ancient Jerusalem of the Old Testament, **about 700 BC**” — one locked plate cannot serve David (~1000 BC), Solomon, post-586 exile, Nehemiah, etc. Easy systematic anachronism when this plate is reused as “OT Jerusalem.”

**PHARAOH** — full New Kingdom nemes/uraeus kit — conventional, but Exodus pharaoh period is debated; locked “Exodus Pharaoh” look may be wrong for the narrative date.

---

### Christ-lens / typology intact? (mixed)

Good: **THE_LORD** glory-only; **FORBIDDEN_FRUIT** not an apple; **BRONZE_SERPENT_STANDARD** anti-Asclepius; **PAGAN_SHRINE** / **CAESAR_TEMPLE** Greco-Roman not Buddhist; **CROSS** bare; **BLOOD_ON_DOORPOST** lintel + posts, not threshold.

Weak: **THREE_DAYS_DARKNESS** / **THREE_DAYS_DEEP** both do resurrection-sign work without clear Exodus-plague guard; **SHEPHERD** charsheet bakes Christ-door into every shepherd use.

### Coverage gaps (checkable facts missing)

- **ISAAC** charsheet: wood on back, not **bound on the altar** — but **STAYED_HAND** / **SUBSTITUTION** assume a “**bound son**.” No bound-Isaac state in the library.
- **HIGH PRIEST** on Yom Kippur: **AARON** has linen, but no locked fact that he alone enters the Most Holy Place that day while the nation waits outside (Lev 16:17) — easy to paint a crowd inside the veil.
- **ADULTEROUS_WOMAN** “**standing alone**” — John 8:3–9: scribes, Pharisees, then Jesus; crowd dynamics missing; pairs badly with generic **CROWD** unless rules say which moment.
- **LAVER** / bronze altar as standalone objects for tabernacle court scenes — only partly implied by **SLAIN_GOAT** / **TABERNACLE_DOOR**.

### Redundancy (not fatal, causes drift)

**ARK_DOOR** / **ONE_DOOR**; **HOUSE_DOOR** / **DOORWAY** / **BLOOD_ON_DOORPOST**; **REDEEMERS_SHOE** / **REDEMPTION_PRICE**; **TABERNACLE_INTERIOR** / **HOLY_OF_HOLIES** — duplicate locks invite inconsistent renders across episodes.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Reconcile **SERPENT_LIFTED_HIGH** with **BRONZE_SERPENT_STANDARD** — remove “coiled” from the motif; serpent mounted at pole top only, bare shaft.
2. Fix **MAN_CAST_INTO_SEA** — storm must still rage when Jonah hits the water; stilling is a separate beat (Jonah 1:15–16).
3. Split or rename **THREE_DAYS_DARKNESS** (Exodus plague) from burial/resurrection darkness (**THREE_DAYS_DEEP**), and detach **SHEPHERD** charsheet from the Christ-as-door pose reserved for **SHEPHERD_AS_DOOR** / **JESUS**.
