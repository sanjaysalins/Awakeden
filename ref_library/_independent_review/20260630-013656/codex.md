# Independent review — codex (OK, 288s)

**Findings**

1. **ARM_OF_THE_LORD**: “a strong outstretched divine arm of light” violates the brief’s rule that God/THE_LORD must not be given body parts. Isaiah 53:1 uses “arm of the LORD” as prophetic metaphor, not a visible divine limb.

2. **SHUT_IN_BY_GOD**: “the LORD’s own unseen hand drawing the great ark door closed” is over-literal. Genesis 7:16 says “the LORD shut him in,” but does not depict a hand. Keep this as light/glory/providence, not anatomy.

3. **FACES_OF_DEAD_PROPHETS**: “dead prophets” wrongly includes “Elias,” while the same line admits “Elijah taken up alive.” 2 Kings 2:11 says Elijah did not die. Matthew 16:14 also does not present these as visible apparitions.

4. **ADULTEROUS_WOMAN**: “robe disordered and slipping from one shoulder” invents a sexualized visual detail. John 8:3 only says she was “taken in adultery” and “set… in the midst.”

5. **DROPPED_STONES**: “where the accusers dropped them and went away” is not stated. John 8:7-9 mentions casting a stone, but never says the accusers had stones in hand or dropped them.

6. **SHEEPFOLD** / **SHEPHERD_AS_DOOR**: “single narrow gap and no gate” conflicts with John 10:1-3, where the sheepfold has a “door” and a porter who “openeth.” The “shepherd lying across the gap” is a sermon-custom image, not the stated biblical scene.

7. **TABERNACLE_INTERIOR**: “the inner Most Holy Place… a close lamplit chamber” is wrong for the Holy of Holies. The lampstand was outside the veil in the Holy Place; Leviticus 16 emphasizes incense cloud and the mercy seat, not lamplight inside.

8. **ANIMAL_PAIRS**: “pairs of beasts and birds… Matched male-and-female pairs” under-guards the Flood detail. Genesis 7:2-3 distinguishes clean beasts and fowls by sevens from unclean beasts by two.

9. **REDEMPTION_PRICE**: “a worn leather sandal… sealing the kinsman-redeemer purchase… NOT counted modern money, NOT gold” conflates token and price. Ruth 4:7-8 says the sandal confirmed the transaction; it was not the redemption price.

10. **COSTLY_REDEMPTION**: “gaining no profit for himself but her alone” overstates Ruth 4. Boaz buys the land and takes Ruth “to raise up the name of the dead”; the text does not say he gained no benefit.

11. **PILATE**: “white toga bordered with a deep purple senatorial stripe” is historically wrong for Pontius Pilate, an equestrian Roman governor/prefect, not a senator. The Gospels identify his office, not senatorial dress.

12. **MARY**: “soft blue head-mantle” and “deep blue” lock in later Western/medieval Marian iconography. Luke 1-2 gives no clothing colour.

13. **BREAD**: “a single round flat loaf of ancient unleavened bread… no yeast-puffed crust” is too broad for generic biblical bread. Scripture distinguishes unleavened bread from leavened bread; not all “bread” in biblical scenes is unleavened.

14. **LAMB** / **PASSOVER_LAMB**: “clean white wool” and “white-fleeced” are not specified. Exodus 12 requires a male lamb “without blemish” of the first year, not a white lamb.

15. **BRONZE_SERPENT_STANDARD**: “MOUNTED ON TOP at the very summit” and “does NOT spiral, wind or coil down the pole” over-specify Numbers 21:8-9. The text requires a bronze serpent set upon a pole and lifted up, not that exact geometry.

16. **ABRAHAM** / **ISAAC**: Abraham is “about a hundred” while Isaac is “about twenty” in the Moriah setup. Genesis 21:5 says Abraham was 100 when Isaac was born, so those two locked ages cannot both fit that scene.

17. **RICH_MANS_TOMB**: “great round disc-shaped sealing stone rolled in its channel” is not specified. Matthew 27:60 says a “great stone” was rolled to the door; the disc-and-channel form is an archaeological reconstruction, not a biblical fact.

18. **EMPTY_TOMB**: “linen grave-clothes lying folded” overstates John 20:5-7. The linen clothes are “lying”; only the napkin is separately “wrapped together in a place by itself.”

19. **ISAIAH_SCROLL**: “right-to-left ancient Hebrew script” is over-locked for the Ethiopian eunuch scene. Acts 8:28-35 says he read Esaias but does not specify Hebrew; a Greek Isaiah scroll is historically plausible.

20. **CLIFF**: “the pagan ‘Gates of Hades’” turns a debated Caesarea Philippi background theory into a locked visual. Matthew 16:18 says “the gates of hell shall not prevail”; it does not identify a physical cave-mouth as that phrase.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:
1. Remove all visual divine anatomy for THE_LORD/God motifs; use light, cloud, glory, providential action only.
2. Strip out locked invented details that Scripture does not state, especially dropped stones, exposed clothing, white lambs, blue Mary, and exact ages.
3. Correct conflations and historical errors: Elijah as “dead,” Ruth’s sandal as “price,” Pilate’s senatorial dress, and the John 10 no-gate sheepfold.
