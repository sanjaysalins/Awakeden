# Independent review — claude (OK, 213s)

I read the whole sheet against Scripture, element by element. It's strong — the prior panel's lessons are clearly baked in (the Buddha guard on **PAGAN_SHRINE/CAESAR_TEMPLE/CLIFF**, the apple guard on **FORBIDDEN_FRUIT**, KJV-accurate numbers: Joseph's **TWENTY_PIECES_SILVER** = Gen 37:28, ark **three hundred cubits**, **NOAHS_HOUSEHOLD** eight souls, **LAME_MAN** thirty-eight years, Ishmaelite **spicery/balm/myrrh** = Gen 37:25, **DRY_PIT** "no water in it" = Gen 37:24, Joseph **clean-shaven** = Gen 41:14). Most typology lands cleanly on Christ. But there are real flags.

**1. ANGEL_OF_THE_LORD — human face collides with the light-only rule for deity.**
The charsheet gives it "a luminous youthful face of unearthly beauty... robed in shining white-gold raiment." But in the key Angel-of-the-LORD passages, the Angel is *identified as the LORD/God Himself*: Gen 16:13 ("Thou God seest me"), Exod 3:2–6 (the angel in the bush → "God called unto him"), Judg 13:22 ("we have seen God"). If any episode uses this charsheet for those scenes, a human-faced figure is depicting God with a face — the exact thing **THE_LORD** [glory] correctly forbids ("never a human face or body"). This is the one item with genuine doctrinal weight and it's currently ambiguous in a *locked* reference. Needs an explicit ruling: restrict this charsheet to created-messenger appearances and route identity-as-God scenes to **THE_LORD** glory, or justify the choice in the note.

**2. MARY — "deep blue" robe + "blue head-mantle" is a medieval Western convention, not a period/biblical fact.**
Scripture says nothing of Mary's clothing colour. Blue-mantled Mary is Renaissance/Marian-blue iconography (costly ultramarine = "heaven/queen"), and a costly blue dye is also implausible for a poor Nazareth maiden. The brief explicitly asks me to flag "European/medieval anachronism" — this is the textbook one. Recommend undyed/earth-toned with at most a muted veil; drop the doubled "deep blue / blue head-mantle."

**3. TEMPLE_LAMPS — "four giant golden menorah-lampstands" is Mishnaic, not biblical.**
The four great lampstands at the Tabernacles night ceremony come from Mishnah Sukkah 5:2–3, not Scripture. It's a reasonable historical reconstruction for the John 8:12 setting, but as a *locked* description the specific number "four" reads as if biblical. This is the same over-reach pattern the facts panel caught before (Mishnah dressed as text). Soften the count or tag it as historical, not specified.

**Lesser notes (not blocking):**
- **STORM_SEA vs STORMY_SEA** are near-identical names for two *distinct* events — Galilee night tempest (Mark 4, Jesus asleep) vs Jonah's Mediterranean storm. Same with **STORM_TOSSED_SHIP**/**BOAT**/**CUSHION**. Nothing is doctrinally wrong, but the labels invite cross-wiring Jonah's vessel into a Galilee slot (or vice-versa). Rename one pair to make the story binding (e.g. GALILEE_STORM vs JONAH_STORM).
- **ANIMAL_PAIRS** "two by two" silently omits that clean beasts went by *sevens* (Gen 7:2). The pairs image is the iconic one and not wrong, but the description asserts only pairs.
- **ARK_DOOR** and **ONE_DOOR** are functional duplicates (both "the single door in Noah's ark"); likewise **BINDING_KNIFE**/**SACRIFICIAL_KNIFE**. Redundancy, not error — consider merging.
- **SHEPHERD_AS_DOOR** "lying across the gap as a living gate" is the traditional Phillip-Keller illustration, extrabiblical — defensible as "free" reading of John 10:9 ("I am the door"), just noting it's tradition, not the text.

Everything else I checked holds: the **BRONZE_SERPENT_STANDARD** set-upon-a-pole (not a caduceus), **GOLDEN_CALF_DUST** strewn on water to drink (Exod 32:20), **RENT_VEIL** top-to-bottom (Matt 27:51), **SCAPEGOAT/TWO_LOTS/LAYING_ON_OF_HANDS** (Lev 16), **SPREAD_SKIRT** (Ruth 3:9), **ten** city elders (Ruth 4:2), **WELL** "nothing to draw with" (John 4:11), **CHARCOAL_FIRE** (John 21:9), Jonah's **weeds wrapped** (Jonah 2:5). No Buddha-in-wrong-culture, no supersession, no contrarian readings, no type/antitype confusion. **THE_LORD** is correctly glory-only.

VERDICT: REVISE
TOP FIXES:

2. MARY — remove the medieval "deep blue robe / blue head-mantle" convention; use period-honest undyed earth tones (blue is artistic licence, not biblical or period-poor-Nazareth).
3. TEMPLE_LAMPS — drop or label the Mishnaic "four golden lampstands" so a historical detail isn't locked as if Scripture stated it; and de-conflate the STORM_SEA/STORMY_SEA (Galilee vs Jonah) labels to stop cross-story reuse.
