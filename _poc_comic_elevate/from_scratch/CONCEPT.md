# FROM SCRATCH — "The Sentence" (Pierced / Zech 12:10)

Not a re-render of production beats. A new comic, built as if the short
had never been made.

## Thesis (one line)
God started a sentence in Zechariah. A spear finished it. Looking is life.

## What I refused to copy from production
- Worm's-eye soldier lifting a spear under lightning
- Prophet on a rooftop with blood on his chest
- Standard spear-in-the-side crucifixion close
- Three stacked mourning faces
- Liquid-gold "waterfall" pour
- Kneeling figure under an empty cross at dawn

## The 7 original stills

| # | slug | The idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ink_drop` | A black ink drop hits parchment. Inside the splash-shape, a tiny cross is forming. The sentence begins. |
| 2 | `century_thread` | One continuous ink thread runs from a night prophet across empty desert years to a distant hill with three crosses. Time as a line of ink. |
| 3 | `spear_tunnel` | FIRST-PERSON down the spear shaft — the spear is a dark tunnel; the cross is a small bright end. We are the soldier's act. |
| 4 | `me_him` | One face, split by a pure white vertical gutter: left = radiant "ME" (divine), right = thorn-crowned "HIM" (flesh). Same man. |
| 5 | `double_sight` | John's young face leaning over a puddle of blood-and-water; in the reflection he is older, prophetic — he sees the old sentence. |
| 6 | `sky_opens` | After the storm: clouds tear into the soft shape of an open hand of light (not liquid gold). Grace as a hand, not a pour. |
| 7 | `eyes_live` | Extreme close of two human eyes just opening into warm light. The response. No cross. Just look → live. |

## Page structure (how the strip is READ)

```
PAGE 1  (strip)     [ink_drop] over [century_thread]     "He started writing…"
PAGE 2  (splash)    [spear_tunnel] full                    "…a spear finished it."
PAGE 3  (splash)    [me_him] full                          "Look upon ME. Mourn for HIM."
PAGE 4  (splash)    [double_sight] full                    "John saw the old sentence."
PAGE 5  (splash)    [sky_opens] full                       "I will pour… grace."
PAGE 6  (splash)    [eyes_live] full                       "Look, and live."
```

## Motion (not six identical push-ins)
1. drop → slow push into the ink splash
2. thread → slow lateral drift along the line of centuries
3. tunnel → push INTO the spear toward the cross
4. me_him → hold, barely breathe the camera center
5. double_sight → push toward the reflection in the water
6. sky → gentle crane up into the open hand of light
7. eyes → very slow push into the eyes
