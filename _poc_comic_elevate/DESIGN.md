## What "elevated" means (vs current living-page)

| Current living-page | This POC elevation |
|---|---|
| Inked stills, but often soft / illustration-like | **Harder comic ink**: thick outlines, limited flat palette, heavy hatch, poster contrast |
| Borders added by the compositor | **Page-first design**: paper gutters always on, thick hand-wobbled frames, caption bands as part of the page |
| Mostly full-bleed singles with occasional grids | **Strip rhythm**: splash → strip → impact splash → landing |
| Slow push-in camera | **Comic camera**: push-in + impact holds; living energy only on grace-light |
| Captions float as kinetic type | **Comic caption boxes** under panels (parchment strip) |

## Story spine (6 panels, ~24s showcase)

1. **SPEAR** — soldier raises the spear (hook splash)
2. **THE WORD** — Zechariah night: the LORD claims the wound
3. **PIERCED** — the spear finds the side (impact)
4. **MOURN** — look upon me / faces lifted in tears
5. **POUR** — "I will pour" grace, not wrath (sky opens)
6. **LOOK + LIVE** — landing: look, and live
