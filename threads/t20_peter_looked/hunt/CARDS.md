# Angles on "the Lord turned, and looked upon Peter" (Luke 22:54-62; read with Luke 22:31-34)
Order is random. Every quote is checked word for word against the KJV. Each card names where the link comes from and what it does NOT prove.
Note for every card: Luke 22:61 says only that the Lord "turned, and looked upon Peter". No card says what the look meant or what Jesus or Peter thought or felt beyond "wept bitterly" (Luke 22:62).

## ANGLE A - The Shepherd Line Before the Warning
HOOK: In Mark, just before Jesus warns Peter about the cock, he quotes an old prophecy. Which one?
ANCHOR-1: Luke 22:61 "how he had said unto him, Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice"
ANCHOR-2: Zechariah 13:7 "smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered"
WHY-VERSE: Mark 14:27 "All ye shall be offended because of me this night: for it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered"
LABEL: PROPHECY (Mark 14:27: Jesus says "it is written" and applies Zechariah 13:7 to "this night". Luke does not quote Zechariah)
CLAIMS:
- Hook: In Mark, Jesus quotes an old prophecy about this night. <- Mark 14:27 "All ye shall be offended because of me this night: for it is written, I will smite the shepherd"
- Hook: Right after, he warns Peter about the cock. <- Mark 14:30 "before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice"
- In the courtyard, Peter remembered the word of the Lord about the cock. <- Luke 22:61 "And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he had said unto him, Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice"
- In Mark, Jesus first says they will all be offended this night, for it is written. <- Mark 14:27 "All ye shall be offended because of me this night: for it is written"
- The written line is from Zechariah: smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered. <- Zechariah 13:7 "smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered"
- In Zechariah, the LORD calls the struck one my shepherd, the man that is my fellow. <- Zechariah 13:7 "Awake, O sword, against my shepherd, and against the man that is my fellow, saith the LORD of hosts"
- In the same breath, Jesus says that after he is risen he will go before them into Galilee. <- Mark 14:28 "But after that I am risen, I will go before you into Galilee"
- Peter answers that even if all are offended, he will not be. <- Mark 14:29 "Although all shall be offended, yet will not I"
- Then Jesus tells Peter he will deny him thrice. <- Mark 14:30 "this day, even in this night, before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice"
INSIGHT: In the courtyard, Peter remembered the word of the Lord about the cock. In Mark, Jesus first says they will all be offended this night, for it is written. The written line is from Zechariah: smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered. In Zechariah, the LORD calls the struck one my shepherd, the man that is my fellow. In the same breath, Jesus says that after he is risen he will go before them into Galilee. Peter answers that even if all are offended, he will not be. Then Jesus tells Peter he will deny him thrice.
DOES NOT PROVE: Zechariah does not name Peter, and Luke does not quote Zechariah. Luke gives the warning at the table (Luke 22:34) and Mark on the way to the mount of Olives (Mark 14:26-30); the card does not say which came first or that the words are identical (Mark says "twice", Luke does not). It does not say Peter's following "afar off" (Luke 22:54) is the scattering.

## ANGLE B - Peter Remembered the Word of the Lord, Twice
HOOK: Peter remembered the word of the Lord, and wept. Later he says the same words again. What did he remember then?
ANCHOR-1: Luke 22:61 "And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he had said unto him"
ANCHOR-2: Acts 11:16 "Then remembered I the word of the Lord, how that he said"
WHY-VERSE: John 14:26 "he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you" (speaks of the Holy Ghost bringing the words of Jesus to remembrance in general; it does not name either scene)
LABEL: ECHO (same words, "remembered ... the word of the Lord, how", in two books; the Bible does not state the link)
CLAIMS:
- Hook: Peter remembered the word of the Lord, and went out and wept. <- Luke 22:61-62 "And Peter remembered the word of the Lord ... And Peter went out, and wept bitterly"
- Hook: Later, Peter says "Then remembered I the word of the Lord". <- Acts 11:16 "Then remembered I the word of the Lord, how that he said"
- Hook: That time, the word was a promise of the Holy Ghost. <- Acts 11:16 "ye shall be baptized with the Holy Ghost"
- The Lord turned and looked upon Peter, and Peter remembered the word of the Lord. <- Luke 22:61 "And the Lord turned, and looked upon Peter. And Peter remembered the word of the Lord"
- That word was: before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice. <- Luke 22:61 "Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice"
- Peter went out, and wept bitterly. <- Luke 22:62 "And Peter went out, and wept bitterly"
- In Acts, Peter tells how the Holy Ghost fell on the Gentiles as he began to speak. <- Acts 11:15 "And as I began to speak, the Holy Ghost fell on them, as on us at the beginning"
- Then he says: then remembered I the word of the Lord. <- Acts 11:16 "Then remembered I the word of the Lord, how that he said"
- That word was: ye shall be baptized with the Holy Ghost. <- Acts 11:16 "John indeed baptized with water; but ye shall be baptized with the Holy Ghost"
- Those who heard said God had granted the Gentiles repentance unto life. <- Acts 11:18 "Then hath God also to the Gentiles granted repentance unto life"
- So what: the Lord turned and looked upon Peter after his denial. <- Luke 22:60-61 "the cock crew. And the Lord turned, and looked upon Peter"
- So what: later Peter remembered a word of promise. <- Acts 11:16 "Then remembered I the word of the Lord, how that he said, John indeed baptized with water; but ye shall be baptized with the Holy Ghost"
- So what (for every believer): Jesus is the advocate for anyone who sins. <- 1 John 2:1 "And if any man sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous"
INSIGHT: The Lord turned and looked upon Peter, and Peter remembered the word of the Lord. That word was: before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice. Peter went out, and wept bitterly. In Acts, Peter tells how the Holy Ghost fell on the Gentiles as he began to speak. Then he says: then remembered I the word of the Lord. That word was: ye shall be baptized with the Holy Ghost. Those who heard said God had granted the Gentiles repentance unto life.
SO WHAT: The Lord who turned and looked upon Peter after his denial is the same Lord whose word Peter later remembered as a promise, and Jesus is the advocate for anyone who sins.
DOES NOT PROVE: No verse says Acts 11 answers Luke 22, that the writer planned the repeat, or that Peter thought of his denial in Acts 11. It says nothing about Peter's feelings in Acts 11. In Acts 11:16 the speaker is Peter; in Luke 22:61 the narrator tells it.

## ANGLE C - Reckoned Among the Transgressors That Night
HOOK: That night, which old line did Jesus say must be fulfilled in him?
ANCHOR-1: Luke 22:54 "Then took they him, and led him"
ANCHOR-2: Isaiah 53:12 "he was numbered with the transgressors"
WHY-VERSE: Luke 22:37 "this that is written must yet be accomplished in me, And he was reckoned among the transgressors"
LABEL: PROPHECY (in Luke 22:37 Jesus says this line of Isaiah 53:12 must yet be accomplished in him. The other half of Isaiah 53:12, "made intercession for the transgressors", set beside Luke 22:32 is an ECHO only)
CLAIMS:
- Hook: That night Jesus said a written line must be fulfilled in him. <- Luke 22:37 "this that is written must yet be accomplished in me"
- That night, Jesus said a written line must be accomplished in him: he was reckoned among the transgressors. <- Luke 22:37 "this that is written must yet be accomplished in me, And he was reckoned among the transgressors"
- The line is from Isaiah: he was numbered with the transgressors. <- Isaiah 53:12 "he was numbered with the transgressors"
- At his arrest he asked if they had come out as against a thief. <- Luke 22:52 "Be ye come out, as against a thief, with swords and staves?"
- Then they took him and led him to the high priest. <- Luke 22:54 "Then took they him, and led him"
- The same verse of Isaiah ends: and made intercession for the transgressors. <- Isaiah 53:12 "and he bare the sin of many, and made intercession for the transgressors"
- Earlier that night Jesus had told Peter: I have prayed for thee. <- Luke 22:32 "But I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not"
- In the courtyard, the Lord turned, and looked upon Peter. <- Luke 22:61 "And the Lord turned, and looked upon Peter"
INSIGHT: That night, Jesus said a written line must be accomplished in him: he was reckoned among the transgressors. The line is from Isaiah: he was numbered with the transgressors. At his arrest he asked if they had come out as against a thief. Then they took him and led him to the high priest. The same verse of Isaiah ends: and made intercession for the transgressors. Earlier that night Jesus had told Peter: I have prayed for thee. In the courtyard, the Lord turned, and looked upon Peter.
DOES NOT PROVE: Jesus quoted only the first half of Isaiah 53:12; no verse says Luke 22:32 fulfils "made intercession for the transgressors". It does not call Peter a transgressor or say what the look meant.
