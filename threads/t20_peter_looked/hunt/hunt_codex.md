1. **TITLE:** A Word Remembered  
**HOOK QUESTION:** What did Peter remember when the Lord looked upon him?  
**ANCHOR-1:** Luke 22:61 “And Peter remembered the word of the Lord”  
**ANCHOR-2:** Psalm 119:11 “Thy word have I hid in mine heart”  
**WHY-VERSE:** No verse joins Peter’s remembered word to Psalm 119:11; this is an echo of remembering God’s word.  
**LABEL:** ECHO  
**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** It does not say Peter had memorized this verse, or that Psalm 119 predicts his denial.  
**FACTS:** Luke 22:61 says Peter remembered the Lord’s word after the cock crew and the Lord looked upon him.  
**SO WHAT:** Jesus had spoken a word Peter remembered after failure; his word can stay with us in shame (Psalm 119:11).  
**WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT:** The familiar focus is the denial and the look, not the Bible’s language of remembering; freshness: 5/10.  
**HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS:** Jesus spoke beforehand, and Luke says Peter remembered his word.  
**CONFIDENCE:** 8/10

2. **TITLE:** Jesus Named Peter’s Next Work  
**HOOK QUESTION:** What did Jesus say Peter should do after he turned again?  
**ANCHOR-1:** Luke 22:32 “strengthen thy brethren”  
**ANCHOR-2:** 2 Corinthians 1:4 “that we may be able to comfort them”  
**WHY-VERSE:** No verse says these passages refer to each other; 2 Corinthians describes comforting others after receiving comfort from God.  
**LABEL:** ECHO  
**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** It does not say Peter wrote 2 Corinthians, or that Paul’s words explain Peter’s specific commission.  
**FACTS:** Jesus told Peter to strengthen his brethren after he was converted (Luke 22:32). Paul says God comforts him so he can comfort others (2 Corinthians 1:4).  
**SO WHAT:** Jesus gave Peter work beyond his failure; God’s comfort can reach people in trouble through others (2 Corinthians 1:4).  
**WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT:** The link is usually discussed through Peter’s later story, not Paul’s words about passing on comfort; freshness: 6/10.  
**HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS:** Jesus spoke of Peter’s return and gave him a task that served other people.  
**CONFIDENCE:** 6/10

3. **TITLE:** Jesus Interceded for Peter  
**HOOK QUESTION:** What does the Bible say Jesus does for people who come to God?  
**ANCHOR-1:** Luke 22:32 “I have prayed for thee”  
**ANCHOR-2:** Hebrews 7:25 “he ever liveth to make intercession for them”  
**WHY-VERSE:** Hebrews 7:25 says Jesus makes intercession for those who come unto God by him; it does not identify Peter’s prayer as the same event.  
**LABEL:** ECHO  
**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** It does not say Hebrews is referring to this prayer, or that Jesus’ prayer meant Peter would avoid failure.  
**FACTS:** Jesus told Peter he had prayed for him (Luke 22:32). Hebrews says Jesus makes intercession for those who come to God by him (Hebrews 7:25).  
**SO WHAT:** Jesus prayed for Peter before his denial, and he lives to speak for those who come to God by him (Hebrews 7:25).  
**WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT:** The prayer is usually kept within Luke’s account rather than compared with Hebrews’ description of Jesus; freshness: 5/10.  
**HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS:** The scene shows Jesus praying for Peter, and Hebrews describes Jesus as living to intercede.  
**CONFIDENCE:** 6/10

4. **TITLE:** The Denial Was Heard  
**HOOK QUESTION:** Who heard Peter say he did not know Jesus?  
**ANCHOR-1:** Luke 22:57 “Woman, I know him not.”  
**ANCHOR-2:** Psalm 139:4 “there is not a word in my tongue, but, lo, O LORD, thou knowest it altogether.”  
**WHY-VERSE:** No verse joins this denial to Psalm 139:4; the link is an echo about words being known to God.  
**LABEL:** ECHO  
**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** It does not say Psalm 139 is about Peter, or describe what God thought about Peter’s denial.  
**FACTS:** A maid said Peter was with Jesus, and Peter denied knowing him (Luke 22:56–57). Psalm 139:4 says the Lord knows a person’s words (Psalm 139:4).  
**SO WHAT:** Jesus was denied in words spoken before others; the Lord knows even the words we wish we could take back (Psalm 139:4).  
**WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT:** Sermons tend to centre on Peter’s response, rather than the Bible’s claim that the Lord knows our words; freshness: 4/10.  
**HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS:** Peter’s words denied knowing Jesus, yet the scene keeps Jesus at the centre as the one Peter denied.  
**CONFIDENCE:** 5/10

5. **TITLE:** A Failure, Then a Message  
**HOOK QUESTION:** What message did Jesus give Peter before his failure?  
**ANCHOR-1:** Luke 22:32 “when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren.”  
**ANCHOR-2:** Romans 15:4 “that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.”  
**WHY-VERSE:** No verse links Romans 15:4 to Peter’s future task; Romans says Scripture gives hope, patience and comfort.  
**LABEL:** ECHO  
**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** It does not say Peter’s words or later teaching are the Scriptures Paul means.  
**FACTS:** Before the denial, Jesus spoke of Peter turning again and strengthening his brethren (Luke 22:32). Romans says Scripture was written for our learning and hope (Romans 15:4).  
**SO WHAT:** Jesus spoke to Peter about life after turning back; Scripture gives hope when failure makes the future hard to see (Romans 15:4).  
**WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT:** The passage is commonly treated as a warning about denial, while this link focuses on the future Jesus named; freshness: 5/10.  
**HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS:** Before Peter’s denial, Jesus spoke of his return and the help he would give others.  
**CONFIDENCE:** 5/10
