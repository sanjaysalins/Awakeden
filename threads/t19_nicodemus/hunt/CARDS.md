# Angles on Nicodemus at the burial of Jesus (John 19:38-42; read with John 3:1-21 and John 7:45-52)
Order is random. Every quote is checked word for word against the KJV. Each card names where the link comes from and what it does NOT prove.
Note for every card: John 19 does not say what Nicodemus believed, felt or meant, and does not give the time of day. "They" in John 19:40 and 19:42 are Joseph of Arimathaea and Nicodemus (John 19:38-39). No Bible writer names Nicodemus outside John.

## ANGLE A - Spices for a Body That Saw No Corruption
HOOK: Nicodemus brought about a hundred pounds of spices. What had God already said about that body?
ANCHOR-1: John 19:39 "brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound weight"
ANCHOR-2: Psalms 16:10 "neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption"
WHY-VERSE: Acts 2:31 "He seeing this before spake of the resurrection of Christ, that his soul was not left in hell, neither his flesh did see corruption"
LABEL: PROPHECY (Psalm 16:10; Acts 2:31 says David spoke it before of the resurrection of Christ. Setting the spices beside the psalm is a DETAIL; no verse joins John 19:39 to Psalm 16)
CLAIMS:
- Hook: Nicodemus brought about a hundred pounds of spices. <- John 19:39 "brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound weight"
- Hook: God had already spoken, through David, about his Holy One. <- Psalms 16:10 "neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption"
- Nicodemus brought myrrh and aloes, about a hundred pounds. <- John 19:39 "And there came also Nicodemus, which at the first came to Jesus by night, and brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound weight"
- They wound the body of Jesus in linen with the spices, the way the Jews buried their dead. <- John 19:40 "Then took they the body of Jesus, and wound it in linen clothes with the spices, as the manner of the Jews is to bury"
- Long before, David wrote that God would not let his Holy One see corruption. <- Psalms 16:10 "neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption"
- Peter says David himself died and was buried. <- Acts 2:29 "the patriarch David, that he is both dead and buried, and his sepulchre is with us unto this day"
- Peter says David spoke this before, of the resurrection of Christ. <- Acts 2:31 "He seeing this before spake of the resurrection of Christ, that his soul was not left in hell, neither his flesh did see corruption"
- Paul says the same: the one God raised saw no corruption. <- Acts 13:37 "But he, whom God raised again, saw no corruption"
INSIGHT: Nicodemus brought myrrh and aloes, about a hundred pounds. They wound the body of Jesus in linen with the spices, the way the Jews buried their dead. Long before, David wrote that God would not let his Holy One see corruption. Peter says David himself died and was buried. Peter says David spoke this before, of the resurrection of Christ. Paul says the same: the one God raised saw no corruption.
DOES NOT PROVE: What the spices were for, or what Nicodemus expected or believed; John only says "as the manner of the Jews is to bury". It does not prove the spices were wasted or wrong, or that John had Psalm 16 in mind. It makes no claim about burial customs beyond John 19:40.

## ANGLE B - They Touched the Dead
HOOK: God's law said whoever touched a dead body was unclean. Whose hands took the body of Jesus?
ANCHOR-1: John 19:40 "Then took they the body of Jesus, and wound it in linen clothes"
ANCHOR-2: Numbers 19:11 "He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days"
WHY-VERSE: Hebrews 9:13-14 "the ashes of an heifer sprinkling the unclean, sanctifieth to the purifying of the flesh: How much more shall the blood of Christ"
LABEL: NT-STATED (Hebrews 9:13-14 names the heifer ashes of Numbers 19 and says the blood of Christ does "much more". That the law of Numbers 19:11 applies to the men in John 19:40 is a DETAIL; John does not say they became unclean)
CLAIMS:
- Hook: God's law said whoever touched a dead body was unclean. <- Numbers 19:11 "He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days"
- Joseph of Arimathaea took the body of Jesus, and Nicodemus came too. <- John 19:38-39 "He came therefore, and took the body of Jesus. And there came also Nicodemus"
- They took the body and wound it in linen clothes. <- John 19:40 "Then took they the body of Jesus, and wound it in linen clothes"
- Nicodemus was a Pharisee and a ruler. <- John 3:1 "There was a man of the Pharisees, named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews"
- God's law said whoever touched a dead body was unclean seven days. <- Numbers 19:11 "He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days"
- The law's cleansing used the ashes of a heifer, a purification for sin. <- Numbers 19:9 "gather up the ashes of the heifer ... it is a purification for sin"
- Earlier that day, the leaders would not go into the judgment hall, lest they should be defiled. <- John 18:28 "they themselves went not into the judgment hall, lest they should be defiled; but that they might eat the passover"
- Hebrews names those heifer ashes, and says the blood of Christ does much more. <- Hebrews 9:13-14 "the ashes of an heifer sprinkling the unclean, sanctifieth to the purifying of the flesh: How much more shall the blood of Christ"
INSIGHT: Joseph of Arimathaea took the body of Jesus, and Nicodemus came too. They took the body and wound it in linen clothes. God's law said whoever touched a dead body was unclean seven days. The law's cleansing used the ashes of a heifer, a purification for sin. Earlier that day, the leaders would not go into the judgment hall, lest they should be defiled. Hebrews names those heifer ashes, and says the blood of Christ does much more.
DOES NOT PROVE: John never says the two men became unclean, missed the passover, counted a cost, or why they did it. It does not settle the timing of the passover meal. Numbers 9:6 shows defilement could stop a man keeping the passover "on that day", but John is silent about these two.

## ANGLE C - "Bury Him That Day"
HOOK: Why did the body of Jesus have to be in a tomb the same day he died?
ANCHOR-1: John 19:42 "There laid they Jesus therefore"
ANCHOR-2: Deuteronomy 21:23 "His body shall not remain all night upon the tree, but thou shalt in any wise bury him that day"
WHY-VERSE: Galatians 3:13 "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree"
LABEL: NT-STATED (Galatians 3:13 quotes the law of Deuteronomy 21:23 about Christ. That "bury him that day" matches the same-day burial in John 19 is an ECHO inside the same verse; John does not quote Deuteronomy 21)
CLAIMS:
- Hook: The body of Jesus was laid in a sepulchre that was near, because of the preparation day. <- John 19:42 "There laid they Jesus therefore"
- The law said a hanged man must not stay all night on the tree, but be buried that day. <- Deuteronomy 21:23 "His body shall not remain all night upon the tree, but thou shalt in any wise bury him that day"
- The same verse says he that is hanged is accursed of God. <- Deuteronomy 21:23 "for he that is hanged is accursed of God"
- Paul quotes that law about Christ, who was made a curse for us. <- Galatians 3:13 "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree"
- In John, the Jews asked that the bodies should not stay on the cross on the sabbath day. <- John 19:31 "that the bodies should not remain upon the cross on the sabbath day"
- Joseph and Nicodemus laid Jesus in a sepulchre that was near. <- John 19:42 "There laid they Jesus therefore ... for the sepulchre was nigh at hand"
- Paul also says they took him down from the tree and laid him in a sepulchre. <- Acts 13:29 "they took him down from the tree, and laid him in a sepulchre"
INSIGHT: The law said a hanged man must not stay all night on the tree, but be buried that day. The same verse says he that is hanged is accursed of God. Paul quotes that law about Christ, who was made a curse for us. In John, the Jews asked that the bodies should not stay on the cross on the sabbath day. Joseph and Nicodemus laid Jesus in a sepulchre that was near. Paul also says they took him down from the tree and laid him in a sepulchre.
DOES NOT PROVE: John does not quote Deuteronomy 21; the reason John gives is the preparation day and the sabbath (John 19:31, 19:42). It does not prove Joseph or Nicodemus acted to keep Deuteronomy 21 or knew of the curse. Deuteronomy 21:22 speaks of a man who "committed a sin worthy of death"; Galatians says Christ was made a curse "for us", not for his own sin. Acts 13:29 "they" is not named as Nicodemus.
