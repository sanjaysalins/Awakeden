1. TITLE: More Than A Teacher
2. HOOK QUESTION: Why did the smartest religious leaders get Jesus so wrong at first?
3. ANCHOR-1: John 19:40 "Then took they the body of Jesus"
   ANCHOR-2: Hebrews 10:5 "Sacrifice and offering thou wouldest not, but a body hast thou prepared me:"
3b. WHY-VERSE: Hebrews 10:10 "By the which will we are sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all."
4. LABEL: NT-STATED
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE: This does not prove Nicodemus understood the theology of the ultimate sacrifice while he was wrapping the body.
6. FACTS:
- Nicodemus first approached Jesus calling him merely a "teacher come from God" (John 3:2).
- In the end, Nicodemus physically takes and wraps the dead body of Jesus (John 19:40).
- The New Testament states God prepared a physical body for Christ's sacrifice, not just a mind for teaching (Hebrews 10:5).
7. SO WHAT: Jesus didn't just come to be a "teacher", but gave his physical "body" to be broken so you can be forgiven for your worst failures. (Hebrews 10:10)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: We focus on the spices Nicodemus brought, missing the profound shift from debating a teacher to holding the prepared sacrifice. (Freshness: 9)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus is the ultimate sacrifice whose broken body accomplishes what no earthly teaching or advice ever could.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 9

1. TITLE: When The Miracles Stopped
2. HOOK QUESTION: What happens when Jesus doesn't give you the miracle you asked for?
3. ANCHOR-1: John 19:41 "Now in the place where he was crucified"
   ANCHOR-2: 1 Corinthians 1:23 "But we preach Christ crucified, unto the Jews a stumblingblock"
3b. WHY-VERSE: 1 Corinthians 1:24 "But unto them which are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God."
4. LABEL: NT-STATED
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE: This does not prove Nicodemus lost his faith or stopped wanting signs at the cross.
6. FACTS:
- Nicodemus initially sought Jesus out specifically because of the visible miracles he performed (John 3:2).
- At the burial, there are no miracles happening, only a crucified man in a garden (John 19:41).
- Paul states that while Jews require a sign, the true power of God is found in Christ crucified (1 Corinthians 1:22-24).
7. SO WHAT: Jesus doesn't always perform the "miracles" we demand, but he was "crucified" in total weakness to rescue you from your deepest shame. (1 Corinthians 1:24)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: We assume Nicodemus was eventually rewarded with a grand sign, but his final biblical act is handling a casualty. (Freshness: 8)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus saves the world not through a dazzling display of power, but through the ultimate weakness and shame of the cross.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 9

1. TITLE: The Man Who Lost His Voice
2. HOOK QUESTION: Why did the greatest speaker in history die completely silent?
3. ANCHOR-1: John 19:40 "Then took they the body of Jesus"
   ANCHOR-2: Acts 8:32 "like a lamb dumb before his shearer, so opened he not his mouth:"
3b. WHY-VERSE: 1 Peter 2:23 "Who, when he was reviled, reviled not again; when he suffered, he threatened not; but committed himself to him that judgeth righteously:"
4. LABEL: NT-STATED
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE: This does not prove Nicodemus realized Jesus was fulfilling Isaiah's prophecy of the silent lamb while wrapping him.
6. FACTS:
- Nicodemus previously argued that the law should not judge a man before hearing him speak (John 7:51).
- Jesus was judged and killed without a fair hearing, and Nicodemus now wraps his silent body (John 19:40).
- The New Testament explicitly identifies Jesus as the lamb who opened not his mouth in his own defense (Acts 8:32).
7. SO WHAT: Jesus gave up his right to speak and "judge", becoming the silent sacrifice to cover the times you have failed. (1 Peter 2:23)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: We rarely connect Nicodemus's legal defense in John 7 to the prophetic, voluntary silence of Jesus at the cross. (Freshness: 8)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus submitted to an unjust, unheard judgment to become the silent lamb who takes away our sin.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 9

1. TITLE: Touching The Word Of Life
2. HOOK QUESTION: What did it actually feel like to bury the Son of God?
3. ANCHOR-1: John 19:40 "Then took they the body of Jesus"
   ANCHOR-2: 1 John 1:1 "which we have looked upon, and our hands have handled, of the Word of life;"
3b. WHY-VERSE: 1 John 1:2 "For the life was manifested, and we have seen it, and bear witness, and shew unto you that eternal life"
4. LABEL: NT-STATED
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE: This does not prove Nicodemus is the author of the epistle of John or fully grasped the incarnation that day.
6. FACTS:
- Nicodemus debated heavenly things with Jesus in the dark but could not understand them (John 3:9-12).
- Later, Nicodemus uses his own hands to take and wind the physical body of Jesus (John 19:40).
- John writes that believers have literally handled the physical Word of life with their hands (1 John 1:1).
7. SO WHAT: Jesus isn't just a distant idea in the "night", but a real person who gave his "body" to heal your real loss. (1 John 1:2)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: We treat the burial as a mere logistical step rather than the profound physical reality of handling the incarnate God. (Freshness: 7)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus took on real flesh and bone so that he could physically die and manifest eternal life to us.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 8

1. TITLE: Bearing The Ultimate Curse
2. HOOK QUESTION: How do you escape the harsh judgment of religious people?
3. ANCHOR-1: John 19:38 "that he might take away the body of Jesus:"
   ANCHOR-2: Galatians 3:13 "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us:"
3b. WHY-VERSE: Galatians 3:13 "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree:"
4. LABEL: NT-STATED
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE: This does not prove Nicodemus consciously connected the Pharisees' insult to the cross as he carried the body.
6. FACTS:
- The Pharisees, including Nicodemus, declared that the common people who do not know the law are cursed (John 7:49-50).
- Nicodemus later comes to take away the dead body of Jesus from the cross (John 19:38-39).
- Paul states that Christ redeemed us by being made a physical curse for us on the tree (Galatians 3:13).
7. SO WHAT: Jesus took the "cursed" label that religious people throw around, letting his "body" be broken to take away your shame. (Galatians 3:13)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: We don't link the Pharisees' arrogant insult in John 7 to the literal curse Jesus bore on the cross in John 19. (Freshness: 9)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus absorbed the ultimate curse of the law in his death so that we could inherit the blessing.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 9
