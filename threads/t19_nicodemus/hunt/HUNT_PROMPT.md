## The 4 labels - pick ONE per link, and be honest
- PROPHECY - the OT looks forward and the NT says it was fulfilled in Jesus.
- NT-STATED - the NT itself makes the link, but the OT text was history, a pattern or a vision, not a forecast.
- ECHO - same words or shape in two places. The Bible does not state the link.
- DETAIL - something in the passage itself that most people read past. (DETAIL alone never passes the gate.)
The strongest label sets the headline. An ECHO can never be the headline claim.

## TWO-BOOK RULE
Every angle must link THIS passage to at least one OTHER book of the Bible, with an exact KJV quote from
BOTH books. A detail inside one chapter may be a surprise inside the angle, never the whole angle.
An angle that stays in one book is thrown out. (John 3 and John 7 are the SAME book as John 19: they can be
part of the story, but they never count as the second book.)
It is a GOOD answer to write "NO HONEST LINK" for this passage instead of forcing one. A forced link is worse than none.

## WHY-VERSE RULE
For each link, name the ONE verse that gives the MEANING of the link (the verse that says why the two
passages belong together). If no verse says it, say so and label the link ECHO. The film must never
claim more than that verse says.

## The lesson from 70 past angles (read this first)
Past angles almost never failed for being stale. They failed on TRUTH: in 59 of 70 a claim went further
than the verse says, and in 50 of 70 a verse was used out of its own context. So:
- Say ONLY what the KJV verses say. Every claim must be checkable word by word against a quoted verse.
- The text does NOT say what time of day Nicodemus came in John 19. Do not say "in daylight" or "openly".
- The text does NOT say Nicodemus believed, was saved, or was a secret disciple (John 19:38 says that of
  Joseph, not of Nicodemus). Do not put faith, fear, courage or motives into Nicodemus unless a verse states them.
- Read the other book's passage WHOLE before you link it. Do not hide what the chapter around it says.
- No claims about Hebrew or Greek words that you cannot show from the KJV text itself.
- No claims about burial customs, prices or the cost of the spices unless a Bible verse states them.

## KILL LIST - the common sermons on John 19:38-42 (Nicodemus brings myrrh and aloes to bury Jesus; read with John 3:1-21 and John 7:45-52). DO NOT give any of these as your angle, and do not give a re-worded copy of one.

- Nicodemus first came to Jesus "by night" out of fear, but at the cross he finally comes out into the open as a disciple (John 3:2; John 19:39)
- Secret faith is not enough; real faith must one day stand up and be seen, as Joseph and Nicodemus did (John 19:38; John 12:42-43)
- Nicodemus shows that faith often grows slowly: from a curious question, to a quiet defence, to open devotion (John 3:4; John 7:50-51; John 19:39)
- When the bold disciples ran away, two fearful, secret followers were the ones who showed courage (John 19:38-39; Mark 14:50)
- The huge amount of spices, about a hundred pounds, shows great love and costly devotion, a burial fit for a king (John 19:39)
- Jesus really died and was truly buried, which sets up the truth of the resurrection (John 19:40-42; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4)
- Jesus was laid in a new tomb in a garden where no one had ever been laid, so there could be no mix-up about whose body rose (John 19:41)
- God uses people of rank and wealth too; even members of the ruling council can come to Christ (John 19:38-39; John 7:48)
- Cross-ref: Joseph of Arimathea was a rich man, so Jesus was buried "with the rich" as foretold (Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:57-60)
- Cross-ref: "As Moses lifted up the serpent," Nicodemus heard it at night and then saw it fulfilled at the cross (John 3:14-15; Numbers 21:8-9)
- Cross-ref: Joseph was a good and just counsellor who had not agreed to the council's plan (Luke 23:50-51; Mark 15:43)
- Cross-ref: Many rulers believed but would not confess him, for fear of being put out of the synagogue (John 12:42-43)
- Cross-ref: Myrrh as a gift at his birth and spices at his burial frame Jesus' life (Matthew 2:11; Mark 16:1)
- Joseph of Arimathaea, once a secret disciple, came forward openly to care for Jesus’ body (John 19:38).
- Nicodemus, who had first come to Jesus by night, now publicly helped bury him (John 19:39; cf. John 3:1–2).
- A costly gift can express love and devotion to Christ (John 19:39).
- Jesus truly died and was buried; his body was wrapped and laid in a tomb (John 19:40–42).
- The burial preparations fulfilled the Jewish custom of honoring the dead (John 19:40).
- God can bring faith and courage to people who once hesitated or feared others (John 19:38–39; cf. John 7:50–52).
- Jesus was buried in a new tomb in a garden near the place of crucifixion (John 19:41–42).
- Cross-ref: Isaiah foretold that the suffering servant would be with the rich in his death (Isaiah 53:9).
- Cross-ref: Joseph of Arimathaea asked Pilate for Jesus’ body and laid it in his own tomb (Matthew 27:57–60).
- Cross-ref: Nicodemus had earlier come to Jesus by night and heard of the need to be born again (John 3:1–21).
- Cross-ref: Nicodemus had defended hearing Jesus before judging him (John 7:50–52).
- Cross-ref: The women saw where Jesus was laid and prepared spices for his burial (Luke 23:50–56).

## John 3:1-21
John 3:1 There was a man of the Pharisees, named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews:
John 3:2 The same came to Jesus by night, and said unto him, Rabbi, we know that thou art a teacher come from God: for no man can do these miracles that thou doest, except God be with him.
John 3:3 Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.
John 3:4 Nicodemus saith unto him, How can a man be born when he is old? can he enter the second time into his mother’s womb, and be born?
John 3:5 Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God.
John 3:6 That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.
John 3:7 Marvel not that I said unto thee, Ye must be born again.
John 3:8 The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit.
John 3:9 Nicodemus answered and said unto him, How can these things be?
John 3:10 Jesus answered and said unto him, Art thou a master of Israel, and knowest not these things?
John 3:11 Verily, verily, I say unto thee, We speak that we do know, and testify that we have seen; and ye receive not our witness.
John 3:12 If I have told you earthly things, and ye believe not, how shall ye believe, if I tell you of heavenly things?
John 3:13 And no man hath ascended up to heaven, but he that came down from heaven, even the Son of man which is in heaven.
John 3:14 And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:
John 3:15 That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life.
John 3:16 For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.
John 3:17 For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved.
John 3:18 He that believeth on him is not condemned: but he that believeth not is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God.
John 3:19 And this is the condemnation, that light is come into the world, and men loved darkness rather than light, because their deeds were evil.
John 3:20 For every one that doeth evil hateth the light, neither cometh to the light, lest his deeds should be reproved.
John 3:21 But he that doeth truth cometh to the light, that his deeds may be made manifest, that they are wrought in God.

## John 19:38-42
John 19:38 And after this Joseph of Arimathaea, being a disciple of Jesus, but secretly for fear of the Jews, besought Pilate that he might take away the body of Jesus: and Pilate gave him leave. He came therefore, and took the body of Jesus.
John 19:39 And there came also Nicodemus, which at the first came to Jesus by night, and brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound weight.
John 19:40 Then took they the body of Jesus, and wound it in linen clothes with the spices, as the manner of the Jews is to bury.
John 19:41 Now in the place where he was crucified there was a garden; and in the garden a new sepulchre, wherein was never man yet laid.
John 19:42 There laid they Jesus therefore because of the Jews’ preparation day; for the sepulchre was nigh at hand.
