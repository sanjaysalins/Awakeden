# Blind seat answer: Nicodemus at the burial (John 19:38-42)

I found 3 honest angles. The other 2 slots are "NO HONEST LINK". Ideas I looked at and threw out are listed at the end.

---

## ANGLE 1

**1. TITLE:** Buried "that day": the law of the tree

**2. HOOK QUESTION:** Why did Jesus' body have to be in a tomb before dark?

**3. ANCHOR-1:** John 19:42 "There laid they Jesus therefore because of the Jews' preparation day; for the sepulchre was nigh at hand."
**ANCHOR-2:** Deuteronomy 21:23 "His body shall not remain all night upon the tree, but thou shalt in any wise bury him that day; (for he that is hanged is accursed of God;)"
(Support: Acts 13:29 "they took him down from the tree, and laid him in a sepulchre.")

**3b. WHY-VERSE:** Galatians 3:13 "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree:"

**4. LABEL:**
- Deut 21:23 → Jesus' death on the tree: **NT-STATED** (Gal 3:13 quotes it about Christ).
- Deut 21:23 "bury him that day" → the same-day burial in John 19: **ECHO**. John does not cite Deut 21. He gives "preparation day" as the reason (19:42), and the sabbath in 19:31.
- Acts 13:29 says the taking down and laying in a tomb was part of "fulfilled all that was written of him": **NT-STATED** in general terms.

**5. WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:**
- It does not prove that Joseph or Nicodemus acted to obey Deut 21, or knew they were doing so.
- It does not prove Paul's "they" in Acts 13:29 names Nicodemus.

**6. FACTS:**
- Joseph asked Pilate for the body, and Pilate let him. (John 19:38)
- Nicodemus came too and brought the spices. (John 19:39)
- "They" wound the body in linen with the spices. (John 19:40)
- They laid him there because of the preparation day, and because the tomb was near. (John 19:42)
- The Jews asked that the bodies "should not remain upon the cross on the sabbath day." (John 19:31)
- Nicodemus was "a ruler of the Jews." (John 3:1)
- Nicodemus was "one of them," meaning the Pharisees. (John 7:50)
- Paul says the Jerusalem dwellers "and their rulers" condemned him, and that "they took him down from the tree, and laid him in a sepulchre." (Acts 13:27-29)
- The law said a hanged man's body must be buried "that day," because "he that is hanged is accursed of God." (Deut 21:23)

**7. SO WHAT:** Jesus was hung on a tree, the death the law called cursed, and he became that curse "for us", so your shame does not get the last word.
- Verse for all believers: Galatians 3:13 "redeemed us... being made a curse for us."

**8. WHY MOST HAVEN'T HEARD IT:** Sermons keep Gal 3:13 at the cross. They rarely carry Deut 21's second half ("bury him that day") into the burial scene, or note that Acts 13:29 puts the tomb inside "all that was written."
- Freshness: **6/10**

**9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS:** The quick burial before dark marks Jesus as the hanged man the law called cursed, and Paul says he took that curse for us.

**10. CONFIDENCE:** **8/10**. The Deut, Gal and Acts quotes are solid. The burial-day link is ECHO only, so the film must not say John "fulfils" Deut 21.

## ANGLE 2

**1. TITLE:** They touched the dead. His blood cleanses.

**2. HOOK QUESTION:** By God's own law, what happened to anyone who touched a dead body?

**3. ANCHOR-1:** John 19:40 "Then took they the body of Jesus, and wound it in linen clothes"
**ANCHOR-2:** Numbers 19:11 "He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days."

**3b. WHY-VERSE:** Hebrews 9:13-14 "For if the blood of bulls and of goats, and the ashes of an heifer sprinkling the unclean, sanctifieth to the purifying of the flesh: How much more shall the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit offered himself without spot to God, purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God?"

**4. LABEL:**
- Num 19 heifer cleansing → Christ's blood: **NT-STATED** (Heb 9:13-14 names the heifer ashes).
- Num 19:11 → Joseph and Nicodemus handling the body: **ECHO/DETAIL**. John never says they became unclean.

**5. WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:**
- It does not prove they missed the passover. The text does not say so. Numbers 9:6 shows that could happen, but John is silent.
- It does not prove they knew or cared about the rule, and we must not give them feelings.

**6. FACTS:**
- "They" (Joseph and Nicodemus) took the body and wound it. (John 19:40)
- Touching a dead body made a person unclean for seven days. (Num 19:11)
- The cure was water with the ashes of a red heifer, "a purification for sin." (Num 19:9)
- Men "defiled by the dead body of a man... could not keep the passover on that day." (Num 9:6)
- The leaders stayed out of the judgment hall "lest they should be defiled; but that they might eat the passover." (John 18:28)
- Hebrews names "the ashes of an heifer sprinkling the unclean" and says Christ's blood does "much more." (Heb 9:13-14)

**7. SO WHAT:** The two men who "took the body" became unclean by the law, yet Jesus' blood is the thing that makes the unclean clean, so no stain puts you out of his reach.
- Verse for all believers: 1 John 1:7 "the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanseth us from all sin."

**8. WHY MOST HAVEN'T HEARD IT:** Few people know Numbers 19. Fewer set it next to John 18:28, where the leaders avoid defilement while these two handle a corpse.
- Freshness: **7/10**

**9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS:** The body that made its handlers "unclean" belonged to the One whose blood Hebrews says cleanses far more than the law's water could.

**10. CONFIDENCE:** **7/10**. Num 19:11 and Heb 9:13 are firm. The contrast with John 18:28 is fair to show but must not be pushed into "they gave up the passover."

## ANGLE 3

**1. TITLE:** Myrrh and aloes: the King's robe smell

**2. HOOK QUESTION:** Where else does the Bible put myrrh and aloes on one king?

**3. ANCHOR-1:** John 19:39 "brought a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound weight."
**ANCHOR-2:** Psalm 45:8 "All thy garments smell of myrrh, and aloes, and cassia, out of the ivory palaces, whereby they have made thee glad."

**3b. WHY-VERSE:** Hebrews 1:8 "But unto the Son he saith, Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: a sceptre of righteousness is the sceptre of thy kingdom." It quotes Psalm 45:6.

**4. LABEL:**
- Psalm 45 → the Son: **NT-STATED** (Heb 1:8-9 quotes Ps 45:6-7).
- Myrrh and aloes in Ps 45:8 → John 19:39: **ECHO**. No verse joins them. This cannot be the headline claim.

**5. WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:**
- Psalm 45:8 is not a prophecy of the burial. It is a wedding psalm, and its spices mean gladness, not death.
- The pair also appears in Proverbs 7:17 and Song 4:14, so it is not unique to Psalm 45.
- Hebrews quotes verses 6-7, not verse 8.

**6. FACTS:**
- Nicodemus brought myrrh and aloes, about 100 pound weight. (John 19:39)
- Psalm 45 is addressed to a king: "Thy throne, O God." (Ps 45:6)
- The king is "anointed... with the oil of gladness." (Ps 45:7)
- His garments smell of myrrh, aloes and cassia. (Ps 45:8)
- The psalm goes on to a bride and a wedding. (Ps 45:9-15)
- Hebrews says these words are spoken "unto the Son." (Heb 1:8)
- Myrrh and aloes also appear together in Prov 7:17 and Song 4:14.

**7. SO WHAT:** The man they wrapped in myrrh and aloes is the King God calls "O God", and this King "liveth, and was dead", so loss is not his last chapter or yours.
- Verse for all believers: Revelation 1:18 "I am he that liveth, and was dead; and, behold, I am alive for evermore."

**8. WHY MOST HAVEN'T HEARD IT:** Commentators sometimes note it, but sermons pair the burial myrrh with Matthew 2:11 (the gifts at his birth), not Psalm 45.
- Freshness: **6/10**

**9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS:** The spices on Jesus' body are the same pair the psalm puts on the robes of the King that Hebrews says is the Son.

**10. CONFIDENCE:** **5/10**. The quotes are sound, but the link is only ECHO, so this is weak as a headline.

## ANGLE 4: NO HONEST LINK

**Garden (John 19:41) and Eden (Genesis 2:8).** This is ECHO only. No verse joins them, so I reject it.

## ANGLE 5: NO HONEST LINK

**Nicodemus' "Doth our law judge any man, before it hear him" (John 7:51) and Deuteronomy 1:16-17.** This is a real link, but it is not anchored inside John 19:38-42, so it fails the rules.

**Also rejected:**
- "Born when he is old" (John 3:4) set against the "new sepulchre" (John 19:41). Both are in John, so there is no second book.
- "By night" read as Isaiah 9:2 "darkness." That turns a time of day into a symbol, which is an overclaim.

---

**MY TOP PICK = Buried "that day": the law of the tree.** It has the strongest label: Galatians 3:13 itself ties Deuteronomy 21 to Christ, and Acts 13:29 puts the taking down and burial inside "all that was written of him."
