Swirls uplift kit - what the pencil marks look like

This is a mock, not real art. Grey boxes stand in for the pictures. Watch the pencil. The blue swirl never moves.

  1. Look-up ring. A pencil ring draws round one small top panel when the voice names it.
  2. Idea doodle. One tiny sketch that shows the point. Here: an eye and a dotted line for "I saw thee".
  3. Underline. A pencil line under the hand-written words already on the page.
  4. Red circle. One red-pencil circle per film, on the key word.
  5. Margin arrow. Points at one detail in the picture.
  6. Verse tag. A Bible reference writes itself in the margin (optional).
  7. Page turn. The page slides away like paper. Used 3 times at most.
  8. Tick burst. Three small ticks on one hit. Once per film.
Never: no pencil on the blue swirl. No glow. No zoom. No captions. No mark on Jesus' face or hands. One mark at a time. At most 8 marks a minute.
