The Bier He Touched: redo plan

Design only. Nothing made. Nothing spent. Film looked at: ep07_uplift_fix1.mp4 (71.9 s, 14 shots). Every shot checked at 2 frames a second, plus first and last frame.

Short summary

You are right. The people change from page to page. That is the main fault.

  • Jesus has 4 outfits. Green cloak (good). Brown cloak. Brown robe. Rope belt.
  • The mother has 5 looks. Grey veil (good). White scarf. Nun-like wrap. Black robe. Blue veil.
  • The bier has 6 shapes. Flat boards. Legs. A tray. A ladder. Metal rings. Once it is empty (no body) just before "Arise".
  • The carriers are young men in short tunics, then older men in turbans.
  • The gate is stone, then wood posts, then a castle with a spiked grille.
  • Moving faults: feet walk out of the small boxes, fog and dust appear, a caption fades, the blue ink changes shape, hair blows out, people walk in.

What we do: keep 5 clean shots. Redraw 8 pages with one fixed look. Remake 9 clips. Lock the top boxes and words so they cannot move ($0).

Cost: $4.47 first take. Up to $6.71 with redos.

No change to the voice, the words, or the music.

My pick: do the full redo, up to $6.71. Say yes?

3. The redo plan

Step A. 5 clean reference sheets first (5 stills). One subject per sheet, plain paper, no one else in the picture.
  1. Widow: full body + face, sad and smiling.
  2. Son: wrapped, sitting up, going home.
  3. 4 carriers in a row.
  4. Bier: carried, on the ground, leaning.
  5. Gate of Nain: outside view, front and side.
Jesus uses the series reference plus the F02 page. I look at each sheet before going on.
Step B. 8 page stills, in story order. Each one gets the sheets it needs. Each is checked at 2x zoom before any clip: count the people, check clothes, bier, gate, era, every word.

Step C. 9 clips. Clip rules (from past lessons):
  • Short brief. Movement only. One small action that ends, then hold.
  • Say what moves. Say nothing about the ink, the boxes, the words, dust, or bubbles. No "do not" lines: they backfire.
  • Camera locked. People stay in place: no walking toward the camera.
  • Each clip is longer than its slot. Trim only. No stretch, no freeze.

Step D. $0 finish.
  • Lock the top band and captions: paste the still's title, small boxes and words over every clip. The camera does not move, so it fits. This ends vanishing feet, fog in boxes and fading captions for good. Test on one clip first and zoom on the join.
  • Lock the blue ink the same way where it stands in open paper.
  • Back cover: paste the still's cloth and hook back.
  • Move the 5 pencil marks to fit the new pictures. Re-cut. Same sound, bit for bit.
  • Gates: sound match, every clip longer than its slot, first/last frame check on every clip, people count per page, then a blind red-team look before you see it.
