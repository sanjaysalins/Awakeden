The kit: 6 hand-drawn marks

Same 6 marks in all 9 films. They move in this mock (it loops). Pictures here are plain colour blocks, not the real art.

1. Gold thread

Ties two things that match. A shadow to the real cross. David's line to Jesus' cry. Max 2 a film.

2. Ink ring

Circles ONE word the voice is saying, or one small thing (the folded cloth). Two passes, like a real pen.

3. Strike   4. Underline

Strike a word the voice rejects ("Not judge"). Underline the word that matters. No new words are added.

5. Tally

Counts what the voice counts. Three days. Eight days. Two finished works. Max 1 a film.

6. Dotted route

A walk, a run, "went the other way". Here: John takes Mary home. Max 1 a film.

Rules, so it never gets busy

How manyMax 6 marks a film. One at a time.
Never on JesusNot His face, body or hands. None while He speaks. None on the last shot.
No new wordsMarks point at words already there. Films get dubbed, so meaning stays spoken.
On the wordEach mark starts on its spoken word. Never early.
SoundNot touched. Voice, music and effects are copied as they are.
