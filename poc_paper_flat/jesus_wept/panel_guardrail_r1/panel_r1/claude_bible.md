## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Named viewer:** Ana, 34. She lost her father last year and still asks God "where were You?" when she is alone.
- **Who stops:** Line 1 speaks straight to the grieving viewer, so grieving people stop.
- **Who finishes:** Believers who know the story finish, because they wait for line 12.
- **Who shares:** A churchgoer who wants something to send a friend in mourning.
- **Who is NOT served:** A grieving person whose loved one stayed dead. Line 14 says Lazarus came back. The film never tells that viewer what Martha was told (11:25), so the miracle can hurt them instead of comforting them.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus cries with me, and He is alive." Lines 12 and 18 carry it, and the film makes that clear. What it does not make clear is the answer to line 1's question, "if You had been here." It never says He is here now.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
**In my own words:** A man's two sisters blame Jesus for being late. He cries, raises the brother, and is then killed and rises again.

Where a newcomer gets lost:
- **"my brother had not died" (4, 8):** This old KJV form means "would not have died." A second-language viewer may hear "he did not die."
- **"planned to take His" (15):** "His" what? Say "His life."
- **"He went into one" (16):** "one" means a grave, which is hard to follow. The screen also shows a cross, not a grave.
- **Pictures:** The woman in blue in line 1 wears Mary's dress. Is the viewer Mary? That mixes two people up.
- **Words:** "calleth," "thee," "ye" and "thou hadst" are fine once each, but they are stacked together here.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **(1)** "You said it to God, alone": not a Bible claim. It talks to the viewer. Fine.
- **(2)** "Two sisters said that same sentence": **RIGHT** (11:21, 11:32).
- **(3)** "Lazarus, Jesus's friend… grave four days": **RIGHT** ("Our friend Lazarus" 11:11; "in the grave four days already" 11:17).
- **(4), (6), (8), (10), (11), (12), (13):** the quotes are **RIGHT** and word for word.
  - (11) "They said unto him" names no speaker, but "mourners" is a fair reading.
- **(5)** "stayed in the house, until Martha whispered": **RIGHT** ("sat still in the house" 11:20; "called Mary… secretly" 11:28).
- **(7)** "asking for the one who stayed behind": **RIGHT** (11:28).
- **(9)** "He did not argue": **RIGHT** for Mary. But it skips "he groaned in the spirit, and was troubled" (11:33). With Martha, He did answer (11:23-26).
- **(14)** "Then He called Lazarus out… alive": **RIGHT** (11:43-44). It squeezes in time the stone, Martha's warning and the prayer.
- **(15)** "He gave a dead man life, so the leaders planned to take His": **RIGHT, squeezed.** The trigger was "this man doeth many miracles" (11:47), and the result was "took counsel… to put him to death" (11:53). But they had wanted to stone Him before this (11:8).
- **(16)-(17)** "went into [a grave]… for us… died… rose": **RIGHT** as doctrine, but **UNSUPPORTED on screen.** No verse is shown. The pack already holds the bridge: Caiaphas, "one man should die for the people… gather together… the children of God" (11:50-52). The film does not use it.

**Pictures:**
- **(7)** Jesus kneeling with His hand on her head: **UNSUPPORTED.** The text says "she fell down at his feet" (11:32).
- **(5)** Mary shown alone: **WRONG.** "The Jews then which were with her in the house" (11:31).
- **(13)** Set "outside a house": **WRONG.** They are out of town, near the grave.
- **(14)** "Loose" linen, stepping out: **WRONG.** The text says "bound hand and foot… face was bound about with a napkin" (11:44).

**Missing, and a Bible reader will notice:**
- Jesus waited on purpose: "abode two days still" (11:6). That is the real sting behind "if You had been here."
- A third person says the same complaint: "Could not this man… have caused that even this man should not have died?" (11:37).

**Send to a grieving friend?** Yes, if 11:25 is added. Without it, only with care.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would use the line Jesus gave the first grieving sister: **"I am the resurrection, and the life"** (11:25). Lazarus was raised once. 11:25 is a promise to Ana. That one line answers the hook. It also stops line 14 from hurting viewers whose loved ones stayed dead.

**ONE change:** After line 4, add Jesus saying "I am the resurrection, and the life." Pay it off at line 18.

**Score: 6.5/10**

## CURIOSITY GAP
- **Opens:** line 1 ("If You had been here").
- **Pays off:** half at line 12, more fully at lines 17-18.
- **Score: 6/10.** "Jesus wept" is worth the wait. But the question in line 1 is "where were You?", and the ending answers "He's alive." Nothing says He is with you now.
