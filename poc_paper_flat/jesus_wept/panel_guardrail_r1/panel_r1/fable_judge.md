## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR (one named viewer; who stops, who finishes, who shares; who is NOT served)
Ana, 34, lost her father last year, prayed, heard nothing, stopped going to church. She stops at line 1: it says her private sentence out loud. She finishes, because line 2 promises that someone said it to Jesus's face. Bible readers share it: lines 5-7 (He asked for the sister who stayed away) is a fresh, true detail.
NOT served: the skeptic who wants to know WHY He was not there (never answered), and the raw griever who hears line 14 and thinks "He raised theirs, not mine."

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)
"When I blamed Jesus for not being there, He did not argue. He cried with them, then died and rose." Lines 9, 12 and 18 carry it. Clear for the sisters. Not clear for the viewer: line 1 says "You", but the film never comes back to "you". Line 18 is a fact, not a landing for the person in line 1.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)
Who: two sisters, their dead brother, Jesus. What: both blame Him, He weeps, raises the brother, then dies and rises Himself. Why: He meets grief with tears, and death did not hold Him.
- Line 1: the woman in blue is Mary, but the voice says "You". A newcomer cannot tell if she is "me" or a character. Also "they would not have died" (singular "they") is hard for second-language readers.
- Lines 4, 8: KJV "my brother had not died" can read as "he did not die". Line 1 pre-translates it, which saves it. Keep that.
- Line 15: "planned to take His" drops the noun. Hard to translate, and "the leaders" is vague.
- Line 16: "He went into one," — "one" is unclear, and the screen shows a cross, not a grave. Words and picture disagree, and the order runs grave, cross, rose.
- Lines 6, 13: "calleth for thee", "Behold" — fine, short, and the picture carries them.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
- Line 2 "Two sisters said that same sentence" — RIGHT (John 11:21, 11:32).
- Line 3 "Jesus's friend... four days" — RIGHT (11:11, 11:17).
- Lines 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13 — KJV wording RIGHT, word for word.
- Line 5 "stayed in the house... whispered" — RIGHT (11:20, 11:28 "secretly").
- Line 7 screen: Jesus kneeling with a hand on Mary's head — UNSUPPORTED (11:32 only says she fell at His feet). Gentle, not harmful.
- Line 9 "He did not argue" — RIGHT for Mary. It skips 11:33 (He groaned and was troubled) and 11:6 (He stayed away on purpose).
- Line 11 tag "MOURNERS" — slightly OVERCLAIMED; the text says "They", which may include the sisters.
- Line 14 — RIGHT (11:43-44). Grave clothes and face cloth match.
- Line 15 "so the leaders planned to take His" — RIGHT (11:53).
- Lines 16-17 "went into one, for us... died... rose" — RIGHT (John 19:41-42, 1 Cor 15:3-4).
- Line 4 screen: Martha drawn "older" — UNSUPPORTED, harmless.
- Missing, not wrong: John 11:25 "I am the resurrection, and the life", the centre of the chapter.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE (what would the best explainer in the world do differently; the ONE highest-value change; score 0-10 against that bar)
The best version closes the circle. Line 1: you said it alone. Lines 2-8: they said it to Him, and He came close. So the end must hand the viewer the same move.
ONE change: end line 18 on the viewer — "The Jesus who wept is alive. Say it to Him." It is earned by the story, it is not a stock call, and it also answers "He raised theirs, not mine."
Score: 7/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP (opens at line 2, pays off at line 12; score 0-10; worth the wait?)
Opens at lines 1-2: what does Jesus say back to that accusation? Pays off at line 12 (He wept) and again at line 18. Score 7/10. Worth the wait, and line 7 feeds it mid-way. It loses points because the second question it opens (why was He not there?) is left open, and the "You" from line 1 gets no payoff.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES (line number + exact replacement wording)
1. Line 18: "The Jesus who wept is alive. Say it to Him."
2. Lines 16-17: "He did not only cry at a grave. He died on a cross for us, was laid in a grave, and rose." (matches the cross-then-tomb pictures)
3. Line 15: "He gave a dead man life, so the leaders planned to kill Him."
