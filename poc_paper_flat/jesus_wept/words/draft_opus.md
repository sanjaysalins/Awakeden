# Jesus Wept: draft (opus seat)

Angle: **A. He already knew.**

## (a) Script

[narrator] Jesus knew His dead friend would walk out alive in minutes. So why cry?
[narrator] His friend Lazarus had died. Four days in the grave.
[narrator] Days earlier, Jesus had said:
[jesus] "I go, that I may awake him."
[narrator] He knew the ending.
[narrator] Then He saw Lazarus's sister, Mary, crying at His feet. He saw the friends crying with her.
[narrator] And then, the shortest verse in the Bible.
[narrator] "Jesus wept."
[crowd] "Behold how he loved him!"
[narrator] Knowing the ending did not stop His tears.
*(pause, about 1 s)*
[narrator] He is "the resurrection, and the life." And He still stopped to cry with them.

## (c) KJV fragments used

- "I go, that I may awake him" (John 11:11)
- "Jesus wept" (John 11:35)
- "Behold how he loved him!" (John 11:36)
- "the resurrection, and the life" (John 11:25)
- Background facts, put in plain words: four days in the grave (11:17), Mary at His feet, weeping (11:32-33), the mourners weeping with her (11:33)

## (d) The 4 questions

- **Who is this for:** People who know "Jesus wept" only as trivia, and newcomers who have never heard the story.
- **Main take-away:** Jesus knew He would raise Lazarus, and He still cried with the people who were hurting.
- **Curiosity gap:** Why cry if you already know the happy ending? Answer: the ending did not stop His tears; He saw them crying and wept with them.
- **Why a global newcomer gets it:** It names Lazarus as Jesus's dead friend and Mary as his sister in plain words. Short sentences. The only KJV quotes are short and easy, and none of them carries the meaning alone.
