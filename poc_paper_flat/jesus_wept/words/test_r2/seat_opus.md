# Seat: opus (blind)

## P
1. NEWCOMER: yes, mostly. Who (Jesus, dead friend Lazarus, sisters), what (He cried, then raised him), why (He saw them weeping) all come through. Hard lines: "Thy brother shall rise again" (Thy), "I am the resurrection, and the life" ("resurrection" is a hard word for ESL and sits mid-story with no help), "Behold how he loved him!" (Behold). Pictures carry "come forth".
2. BIBLE READER: 5. "He knew the ending and still cried" is a known sermon point; well put, not new.
3. CURIOSITY GAP: 8. Line 1 opens the question in under 3 s. "What moved Him? He saw them weeping" answers it directly. Answer is a bit plain.
4. ACCURACY: Quotes match KJV. "Friends cried too" softens "the Jews also weeping which came with her" (they came to comfort; fine, no group blamed). "He saw them weeping" as the cause fits v33 "therefore". "Knew the ending" fits v11. "The people He loved": v5 names Martha, Mary, Lazarus; widening it to the crowd is a slight stretch. No blanket promise.
5. LANDING: 6. Plain, earned, no stock closer. But it lands on Jesus' feeling, not on who He is; the big claim was spent mid-script.
6. FIX: drop "And" and end "He knew the ending. He still wept with them." (keep it to what the text says).

## Q
1. NEWCOMER: yes. Same who/what/why. Same hard words, plus the last line ends on "resurrection", a hard word as the payoff. Also: the quote comes AFTER the raising, so a newcomer may think He said it then.
2. BIBLE READER: 6. Putting "I am the resurrection" after the tomb opens shows the claim proved, not just spoken. That is a small fresh turn.
3. CURIOSITY GAP: 8. Same hook. "He saw them weeping, and He wept with them" pays it off cleanly in one line.
4. ACCURACY: Quotes match KJV. Same soft "friends" gloss. Final quote is out of order (said to Martha in v25, before the raising) with no marker; risk of reading as said at the tomb. Only the first clause is used, so no blanket promise. No group blamed.
5. LANDING: 8. Ends in Jesus' own voice, on who He is, earned by what we just saw. Not preachy.
6. FIX: add a short marker before the last line, e.g. "[narrator] He had told her:" so the order is honest, and the picture should show Him with the sisters, not at the tomb.

## RANK
1. Q - overall 7.5/10
2. P - overall 6.5/10

Q wins on the landing. Both share the "resurrection" word risk for ESL viewers.
