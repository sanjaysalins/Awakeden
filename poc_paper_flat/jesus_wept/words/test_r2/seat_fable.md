# Seat: Fable — blind test, round 2

## P
1. **Newcomer:** WHO yes (Jesus, Lazarus, two sisters). WHAT yes. WHY yes ("He saw them weeping"). Not understood: "I am the resurrection, and the life" (hard word, dropped mid-story with no picture to explain it yet); "Thy", "Behold", "come forth"; "wept/weeping" is never tied to "cried".
2. **Bible reader:** 6. "He knew the ending and still cried" is a good angle, but known.
3. **Curiosity gap:** 7. Line 1 opens it fast. Payoff is there, but asking the question a second time ("What moved Him?") makes it feel like a quiz, and the answer comes before the pause, so the pause is wasted.
4. **Accuracy:** Quotes are verbatim and in story order. "Four days" (11:17), "fell at His feet" (11:32), "saw them weeping" (11:33), "knew the ending" (11:11), "people He loved" (11:5) all hold. Small overclaim: "cried FOR a dead friend" is the crowd's reading (11:36); the text ties it to seeing them weep. Left out: "groaned in the spirit, and was troubled". No blanket promise. No group blamed ("friends" for "the Jews" is fair, 11:19).
5. **Landing:** 7. On Jesus, earned, not preachy. But soft: it ends on His feeling, not on who He is.
6. **Fix:** Cut "What moved Him?" and put the pause before the answer. Too many "Then" (4).

## Q
1. **Newcomer:** WHO yes. WHAT yes. WHY yes, in one line. Not understood: "resurrection" is still a hard word, but now it comes right after the dead man walks out, so the picture explains it. "Thy", "Behold", "come forth", "wept" as in P.
2. **Bible reader:** 7. Moving the "I am" line to the end makes it land as proof, not a claim. Fresh.
3. **Curiosity gap:** 8. Same open. Cleaner payoff: one answer line after the pause.
4. **Accuracy:** Same checks pass. One issue: the last quote is out of order. He said it to Martha BEFORE the tomb (11:25). With no lead-in, a viewer will think He said it after Lazarus came out. Same small "cried for" overclaim. No blanket promise (the "shall never die" half is rightly left off). No group blamed.
5. **Landing:** 8. Ends on Jesus' own words, earned by the miracle. No stock closer. Risk: the claim can cover the tears, which are the point.
6. **Fix:** Add a short honest lead-in: "He had already told her:" before the last quote. Change "He wept with them" to "He cried with them".

## Rank
1. **Q — 8/10**
2. **P — 7/10**

Q is tighter and pays off once, cleanly. P's last line is warmer; Q's is stronger. Simulated viewer only, about ±1 on each score.
