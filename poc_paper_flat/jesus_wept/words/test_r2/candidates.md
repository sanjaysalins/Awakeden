# Two candidate scripts for a ~38 s vertical Short: "Jesus wept" (John 11)
Voices in [brackets]. Quoted lines are King James Version (KJV). (pause) = about 1 second of silence.
Pictures will show each moment simply (Jesus, the sisters, mourners, the tomb, Lazarus coming out).

## P
[narrator] Jesus cried for a dead friend He was about to bring back. Why?
[narrator] The friend was Lazarus. He had been dead four days.
[narrator] Jesus told Lazarus's sister:
[jesus] "Thy brother shall rise again."
[narrator] And then:
[jesus] "I am the resurrection, and the life."
[narrator] Then Mary, the other sister, fell at His feet, crying. Friends cried too.
[narrator] Then, two words:
[narrator] "Jesus wept."
[crowd] "Behold how he loved him!"
[narrator] Then He called:
[jesus] "Lazarus, come forth."
[narrator] And the dead man came out.
[narrator] What moved Him? He saw them weeping.
(pause)
[narrator] He knew the ending. And He still cried with the people He loved.
