# Seat: opus (blind)

## X
1. Newcomer: YES, I get who, what and why. Small snag: "awake him" (it works because death is said first). "Behold" is old English, but you can still follow it.
2. Bible reader: 5. A familiar framing.
3. Curiosity gap: 8. The hook is clear in under 3s. The payoff "stopped to cry with them" answers it, but late and thin. It never says WHY he cried.
4. Accuracy: "in minutes" / "minutes later" is overclaimed (the text gives no times). "Walked out" is soft: he "came forth, bound hand and foot". "Stopped to cry" is invented colour. "Friends" in place of "the Jews" is fine, and no group is blamed. "Awake him" is cut short ("...out of sleep"), which is OK. No blanket promise.
5. Landing: 7. Lands on Jesus by name and claim ("resurrection and the life"), with no stock closer.
6. Fix: drop "minutes", and say "came out". Add "He saw them weeping" as the reason.

## Y
1. Newcomer: PARTLY. Who is Martha? She is never introduced. The raising is never shown, so "live again" is never paid off. A newcomer cannot say how the story ended.
2. Bible reader: 5.
3. Curiosity gap: 5. The hook is good, but the payoff is missing (no "come forth").
4. Accuracy: the Martha line is correct (11:21). But the final "I am the resurrection" is moved from the Martha talk (11:25) to after the weeping, so it reads as said at the tomb. "In minutes" is overclaimed. No group is blamed.
5. Landing: 6. Strong Jesus words, but out of order and not earned by a shown resurrection.
6. Fix: put "Lazarus, come forth" plus "he came out" before the close, and cut the Martha line.

## Z
1. Newcomer: YES. Clearest of the three. "Then, two words" helps. Small snag: "awake him" comes before we are told Lazarus is dead ("dead friend" in the hook saves it).
2. Bible reader: 7. Naming the text's own trigger ("When Jesus therefore saw her weeping", 11:33) is a real answer, not a guess.
3. Curiosity gap: 9. The question is open in 2s and answered directly from the text.
4. Accuracy: the cleanest. "About to" in place of "minutes", and "Before He even arrived" is right. "Walked out" is soft (came forth bound). "Friends" is fine. No blanket promise, no blame.
5. Landing: 7. Earned and warm, but it ends on Jesus' feeling, not His identity. The question is asked twice, which is a little long for 38s.
6. Fix: cut "So why cry, if you know the ending?" (the hook already asked it). Use the time for a short "I am the resurrection, and the life" before the final line.
