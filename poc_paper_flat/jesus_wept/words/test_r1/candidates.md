# Three candidate scripts for a ~38 s vertical Short: "Jesus wept" (John 11)
Voices in [brackets]. Quoted lines are King James Version (KJV). (pause) = about 1 second of silence.
Pictures will show each moment simply (Jesus, Mary at His feet, mourners, the tomb, Lazarus walking out).

## X
[narrator] Jesus knew His dead friend would walk out alive in minutes. So why did He cry?
[narrator] His friend Lazarus had died. Four days in the grave.
[narrator] Days earlier, Jesus had said:
[jesus] "I go, that I may awake him."
[narrator] Then He saw Lazarus's sister Mary crying at His feet, and their friends crying with her.
[narrator] "Jesus wept."
[crowd] "Behold how he loved him!"
[narrator] Minutes later:
[jesus] "Lazarus, come forth."
[narrator] And the dead man walked out.
(pause)
[narrator] He is the resurrection and the life. And He still stopped to cry with them.
