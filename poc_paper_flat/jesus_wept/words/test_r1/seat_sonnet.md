# Seat Sonnet

## X
1. Newcomer: mostly yes. WHO Jesus/Lazarus/Mary, WHAT he died and walked out, WHY Jesus cried (love, with them). Unclear: "I go, that I may awake him" (a newcomer hears "wake a sleeper", not "raise the dead"); "come forth" is archaic but the picture explains it. "He is the resurrection" is a big word for ESL.
2. Bible reader: 7. The "knew the ending yet wept" tension is familiar but crisp.
3. Gap: 9. First line opens it at once; the end answers it ("still stopped to cry with them").
4. Accuracy: Quotes match KJV (the "awake him" quote is trimmed, "out of sleep" dropped, fair). "Four days" correct. "In minutes" and "Minutes later" are not in the text (invented timing, mild). The "resurrection" line is reworded from "I am", and it was said to Martha earlier, not at the tomb; the script leaves the order loose. No blanket promise. No group blamed ("crowd").
5. Landing: 8. Earned by the story: the resurrection claim sits beside the tears. No stock closer.
6. Fix: swap "in minutes" for "that day", and add "Lazarus is asleep" so "awake him" lands.

## Y
1. Newcomer: partly. Martha is never introduced. "Awake him" is unexplained. The "Come and see" speaker is unclear. The ending jumps to a quote with no resurrection scene, so the WHAT (he lives) is never shown.
2. Bible reader: 6. Martha's complaint adds texture, but little is new.
3. Gap: 7. The opening is strong, but the payoff is a bare quote and no one walks out.
4. Accuracy: Quotes accurate. The "resurrection" line is moved to after the weeping (said earlier, to Martha). With no context it could read as a bare claim. "Live again in minutes" is invented timing. Martha's "had not died" is accurate, not blame. No group blamed.
5. Landing: 6. It ends on a Jesus quote, but with no scene behind it the line is a title and not a landing.
6. Fix: add "And the dead man walked out" before the final line, and name Martha as "Lazarus's sister."

## Z
1. Newcomer: yes on who and what, mostly on why. Lines that may confuse: "awake him"; "John says: He saw them weeping." (who is John?). Asking "why cry" twice feels repetitive.
2. Bible reader: 7. The "two words" beat and the "He saw them weeping" anchor (John 11:33) are nice.
3. Gap: 8. It opens fast, but the second "why" restates the gap and slows it. The end pays off.
4. Accuracy: Accurate. "John says: He saw them weeping" is close to the KJV ("saw her weeping"). No resurrection claim and no blanket promise. No group blamed. It is probably too long for 38s.
5. Landing: 6. It lands on Jesus' love, but it never states who He is (the life-giver), so the gospel weight is light. It does avoid a stock closer.
6. Fix: cut the repeated "So why cry" and the "John says" lines to fit 38s; add the "resurrection and life" line.
