# Seat: Fable — blind test, round 1

## X
1. NEWCOMER: Yes. Who = Jesus, friend Lazarus, sister Mary. What = He cried, then raised him. Why = He cried with them. Hard lines: "I go, that I may awake him" (awake a dead man?), "Behold", "come forth", "the resurrection and the life".
2. BIBLE READER: 6. The "knew, still wept" angle is known; nothing from the text they missed.
3. CURIOSITY: 8. Strong first line. Payoff is implied, never said as an answer.
4. ACCURACY: "in minutes" and "Minutes later" are invented timing (said twice). Quote is cut mid-sentence (KJV: "awake him out of sleep"). "Walked out": KJV "came forth, bound hand and foot". "Cry with them" is a fair reading of v33-35, not stated. No blanket promise. No group blamed ("friends" for "the Jews" is safe).
5. LANDING: 7. On Jesus, earned. "He is the resurrection and the life" is a title dropped in unexplained.
6. FIX: Cut "Minutes later"; say "Then". Make the last line answer the "why" out loud.

## Y
1. NEWCOMER: No. Martha is never introduced. Lazarus never comes out in the words, so the hook's promise ("live again") is not shown. Hard lines: "if thou hadst been here", "Where have ye laid him?", "I am the resurrection, and the life".
2. BIBLE READER: 6. More real KJV, good "Come and see" beat, but no new sight.
3. CURIOSITY: 5. Good opener; the miracle is missing, so payoff is half.
4. ACCURACY: Quotes are verbatim. "Come and see" drops "Lord". "I am the resurrection" was said to Martha before the weeping; used last it reads out of order. "In minutes" invented. No blanket promise (wisely stops before "he that believeth"). No blame.
5. LANDING: 6. Ends on Jesus' own words, but as a stock verse card, not earned by the story shown.
6. FIX: Swap the Martha line for "Lazarus, come forth" and the man walking out.

## Z
1. NEWCOMER: Yes, the clearest. Plainest words. Hard lines: "awake him", "Behold", "come forth", and "John says" (who is John?).
2. BIBLE READER: 7. "John says: He saw them weeping" points at v33, a detail most skip.
3. CURIOSITY: 9. Opens fast, asks again, answers plainly.
4. ACCURACY: Best. No invented timing. "Before He even arrived" is right. Small overclaim: John says He saw them weeping and "groaned in the spirit, and was troubled"; John does not state it as the reason for the tears. No blanket promise. No blame.
5. LANDING: 8. Earned, not preachy. Weak point: never says who Jesus is, so He could be only a kind man.
6. FIX: "John says" -> "The Bible says: He saw them weeping." Add four words to the end: "The one who beats death still cried with the people He loved."

## RANK
1. **Z — 8/10**
2. **X — 7/10**
3. **Y — 5/10**

Best build: Z's body + a short who-He-is beat from X's last line.
