# Jesus wept — draft (Sonnet) — Angle A: He already knew

## (a) Script
1. [narrator] Jesus knew His friend would live again in minutes. So why did He cry?
2. [narrator] Lazarus had died. Days before, Jesus said, "I go, that I may awake him."
3. [martha] "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died."
4. [narrator] Mary and the mourners were weeping. Jesus saw them.
5. [jesus] "Where have ye laid him?"
6. [crowd] "Come and see."
7. [narrator] "Jesus wept."
8. [crowd] "Behold how he loved him!"
9. [narrator] He knew the ending, and still He wept with those He loved.
10. [pause ~1 s]
11. [jesus] "I am the resurrection, and the life."

## (d) The four questions
- Who is for: anyone who has lost someone, and anyone who only knows "shortest verse" as trivia.
- Take-away: Jesus cried even though He knew the happy ending, because He loved Lazarus and saw their grief.
- Curiosity gap: why cry if you know the ending? Paid off by "He wept with those He loved," then He names Himself the resurrection and the life.
- Global newcomer: line 1 says who (Jesus, His friend), what (he died, Jesus cried), and why we ask; every sentence is short and plain, and Lazarus's death is stated outright.
