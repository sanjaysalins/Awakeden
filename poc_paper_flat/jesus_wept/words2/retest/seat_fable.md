# Seat: Fable — blind test of F

**1. NEWCOMER: yes, mostly.**
WHO: Jesus, two sisters, their dead brother Lazarus. WHAT: they say "if You had been here"; Jesus cries, then raises him. WHY: He loved him.
Lines I would not get:
- "The Master is come, and calleth for thee." Who is "the Master"? Old words.
- "Where have ye laid him?" / "Behold how he loved him!" Old words, but the pictures can carry them.
- "From that day, the leaders planned to kill Jesus." Why? He just did a good thing. No reason given.
- "He went into one, for us." "One" = a grave. Easy to miss by ear.
The plain-English opening teaches the "hadst been here" line before it comes. That works.

**2. FEEL SEEN: 8.** The first line is the grieving person's own sentence. Jesus "did not argue" and then cries. No lecture. Small risk: "You said it to God" assumes the viewer prays.

**3. BIBLE READER: 6.** Two fresh things: both sisters say the same sentence, and "calleth for thee" read as Him asking for the one who stayed home. The rest is familiar.

**4. CURIOSITY GAP: 7.** Opens fast: what does Jesus answer to that sentence? Pays off: no answer, tears. The cross tail is a second payoff, a bit rushed.

**5. ACCURACY: sound, three notes.**
- All six KJV quotes are word-perfect and in order (11:21, 28, 32, 34, 35, 36).
- "He was asking for the one who stayed behind": the text only has Martha reporting the call (11:28). Fair inference, not a recorded act.
- "He did not argue": true with Mary. With Martha He did answer with teaching (11:23-26), and the script skips that He "groaned in the spirit" (11:33). Slight smoothing, not wrong.
- "The leaders planned to kill Jesus" matches 11:47-53. No group blamed; "mourners" and "leaders" are careful.
- No blanket promise. Gospel is clear: died on a cross, rose, "for us".

**6. LANDING: 8.** "He did not only cry at a grave. He went into one" is earned by the story. "The Jesus who wept is alive" is not a stock closer. No command to the viewer.

**7. SHARE: yes.** It says the friend's own angry sentence out loud and does not correct it. It gives no promise that could turn out false.

**8. ONE FIX.** Give the "leaders" line its reason and tie it to the grave, e.g. "For giving a dead man life, the leaders planned His death." As it stands it is the one line a newcomer cannot follow.

**RANK K and L:** cannot rank. candidates.md holds only F; no K or L is in the pack.

**OVERALL for F: 7.5 / 10. Verdict: PASS with one fix.**
