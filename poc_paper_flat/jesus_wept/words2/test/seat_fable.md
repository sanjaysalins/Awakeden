# Seat: Fable (blind, hostile)

## K
1. NEWCOMER: Mostly yes. WHO: Jesus, two sisters, dead brother. WHAT: He cried, then raised him. WHY He cried: never said. Hard lines: "if thou hadst been here" (saved by the plain hook version), "The Master is come, and calleth for thee", "Where have ye laid him?". "The leaders planned to kill Jesus" has no reason given.
2. FEEL SEEN: 8. The hook is the viewer's own sentence. The end is a little creed-like, not warm.
3. BIBLE READER: 8. "He asked for the one who stayed behind" is a fresh, true detail. So is "the same sentence".
4. CURIOSITY GAP: 8. Opens fast (what did He answer?). Pays off: no argument, tears.
5. ACCURACY: Quotes verbatim and in order. Both sisters' lines are identical in KJV: correct. "He asked for the one who stayed behind" rests on Martha's report (11:28): fair. "He did not argue" skips "groaned in the spirit": omission, not error. "From that day" is the day of the council (11:53), slightly compressed. "The leaders" is fair (chief priests and Pharisees); no group blamed. No blanket promise. Gospel clear and sound: cross, grave, rose.
6. LANDING: 7. Earned by the grave link. The last line is flat; it stops on a fact, not on the viewer.
7. SHARE: Yes. The first line says what grief says, and nobody scolds it.
8. FIX: Add [mourners] "Behold how he loved him!" after "Jesus wept" to give the WHY.

## L
1. NEWCOMER: Partly. "Where were You?" is unclear by ear (who is "You"?). "I am the resurrection, and the life" is abstract and unexplained. Same hard KJV lines as K, with no plain version of the sisters' sentence first.
2. FEEL SEEN: 8. Hook is true to life. "The Jesus who wept is alive" comforts.
3. BIBLE READER: 6. Well told, but nothing new. Loses the "asked for the one who stayed" beat.
4. CURIOSITY GAP: 7. "Where were You?" opens well. The answer (He was coming; He wept) is implied, not landed.
5. ACCURACY: Quotes verbatim and in order. "Wondered it too" is a soft overclaim: the sisters said "if", not "where were you". "I am the resurrection, and the life" is cut mid-sentence but honest. Same "from that day" compression. No group blamed, no blanket promise. GAP: never says He died or names the cross. "Went into one" is too thin for a newcomer.
6. LANDING: 8. Best last line of the two, earned by the tears.
7. SHARE: Yes. The last line is one a grieving friend can hold.
8. FIX: Say "He died on a cross" before "And He rose".

## RANK
1. **K: 7.5/10**. Stronger hook, fresh detail, complete gospel.
2. **L: 7/10**. Better final line and the "loved him" beat, but the gospel is incomplete and the hook is fuzzier.

Best version: K plus L's "Behold how he loved him!" and L's closing line "The Jesus who wept is alive."
