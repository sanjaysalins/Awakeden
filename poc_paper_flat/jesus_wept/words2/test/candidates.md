# Two candidate scripts for a ~60 s vertical Short: "Jesus wept" (John 11)
For people who feel lost, lonely or grieving. Voices in [brackets]. Quoted lines are King James Version (KJV). (pause) = about 1 second.
Pictures show each moment simply in a flat, paper-texture illustration style.

## K
[narrator] Maybe you said it to God, alone: "If You had been here, they would not have died."
[narrator] Two sisters said that same sentence to Jesus.
[narrator] Their brother Lazarus, Jesus's friend, had been in the grave four days.
[martha] "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died."
[narrator] Mary stayed in the house, until her sister whispered:
[martha] "The Master is come, and calleth for thee."
[narrator] He asked for the one who stayed behind.
[mary] "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died."
[narrator] He did not argue. He asked:
[jesus] "Where have ye laid him?"
[mourners] "Lord, come and see."
(pause)
[narrator] "Jesus wept."
[narrator] Then He called Lazarus out of the grave, alive. From that day, the leaders planned to kill Jesus.
[narrator] He did not only cry at a grave. He went into one, for us. He died on a cross, and He rose.

## L
[narrator] You prayed. Help came too late. And you wondered: where were You?
[narrator] Two sisters, Martha and Mary, wondered it too. Their brother Lazarus, Jesus's friend, had been dead four days.
[martha] "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died."
[narrator] Jesus told her:
[jesus] "I am the resurrection, and the life."
[narrator] Mary stayed in the house, until her sister whispered: "The Master is come, and calleth for thee."
[mary] "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died."
[narrator] The same words. He did not argue. He asked:
[jesus] "Where have ye laid him?"
[mourners] "Lord, come and see."
(pause)
[narrator] "Jesus wept."
[mourners] "Behold how he loved him!"
[narrator] He called Lazarus out, alive. From that day, the leaders planned to kill Him.
[narrator] He did not only cry at a grave. He went into one, for us. And He rose. The Jesus who wept is alive.
