# (d) The 4 questions

- Who it is for: someone grieving or alone who prayed and felt God came too late.
- Main take-away: Jesus does not argue with your grief; He wept at a grave, then went into one for us, and rose.
- The curiosity gap: two sisters said your sentence straight to Jesus — what did He do?
- Why a global newcomer gets it: short plain words, three named people, one repeated sentence, and every old word has plain English beside it.
