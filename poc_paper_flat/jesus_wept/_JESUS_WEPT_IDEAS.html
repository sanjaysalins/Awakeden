Jesus Wept — 3 big ideas

For people who feel lost, lonely, or that God came too late.
7 blind seats: Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Codex, Gemini, Grok, Cursor.

A. "The sentence you said too"

7 of 7 seats · 5 picked it as best

Start: "You prayed. And someone you loved still died."

Turn: Both sisters say the same words: "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died." Jesus does not argue. He asks where the grave is. They say "Come and see." Jesus wept.

End: "You can say that sentence to Him. He has heard it before. He did not walk away. He came closer."

B. "She stayed in the house"

6 of 7 seats · 2 picked it as best

Start: "Still sitting in that room, since the day you lost them?"

Turn: Mary could not get up. Her sister whispers: "The Master is come, and calleth for thee." He asked for her. She runs to Him. Jesus wept with her — then called the dead man out.

End: "He is near to the broken-hearted. Even in that room."

C. "The tears that cost Him"

3 of 7 seats

Start: "When did someone last cry for you?"

Turn: He cries at His friend's grave and gives him life. From that day, the leaders plan to kill Jesus.

End: "He did not only cry at a grave. He went into one — for you."

My pick: A, with B's whisper ("calleth for thee") inside it, and C's last line as the ending — so it lands on the cross, not just on comfort. ~55 s.
