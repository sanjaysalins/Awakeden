Simulated viewers, not real ones. About ±1. Real seed viewers still matter most.

4 questions

  • Who for: anyone grieving, and anyone who knows "Jesus wept" only as the shortest verse.
  • Take-away: Jesus knew He would raise Lazarus, and He still cried with them.
  • Curiosity gap: why cry if you know the happy ending? Paid off at the end.
  • Global: short plain lines. Lazarus is named as His dead friend.

Still weak

• "resurrection" is a hard word (the picture of Lazarus coming out helps).
• "Thy", "Behold", "come forth" are old words, but short.
• Bible readers know this angle (6-7 out of 10).

Picture style (your refs)

Flat, paper-texture, earthy. Gold halo on Jesus only.
