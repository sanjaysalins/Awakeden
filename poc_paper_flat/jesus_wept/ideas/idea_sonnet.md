# Ideas — voice: Sonnet

## PART 1 — THREE IDEAS

### 1. LOVED, AND LATE
FIRST 3 SECONDS: "You asked for help. It came too late. Now you wonder if He even cared."
SETUP: Two sisters send word that their brother is sick. The text says Jesus loved the family, and then stayed two more days. Lazarus dies.
THE TURN: Love comes first in the text (11:5), then the delay (11:6). Both sisters say, "Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died" (11:21, 11:32). Jesus scolds neither one. He asks where Lazarus is laid, and He weeps (11:35). The mourners say, "Behold how he loved him!"
LAST LINE: "Late did not mean cold. Your tears are not unseen."
WHY SHARE: It names the secret fear, "God was late, so I was not loved," and answers it from the page.
RISK: A newcomer may hear "God delays on purpose." Fix: never give a reason for the delay beyond what 11:4 says. Do not explain it.

### 2. THE SAME SENTENCE
FIRST 3 SECONDS: "Two sisters said the exact same sentence to Jesus. He answered each one differently."
SETUP: Martha runs out to meet Him. Mary "sat still in the house" until Martha whispers that He is calling for her.
THE TURN: Same words (11:21, 11:32). Martha is standing and talking, and He talks with her (11:23-26). Mary is on the ground, and He sees her weeping, groans, and weeps (11:33-35). He meets each grief where she is.
LAST LINE: "However you grieve, loud or silent, He meets you there."
WHY SHARE: It gives people permission to grieve differently from the person next to them.
RISK: Reading too much into "how He answered." Keep it to what the text shows. "Silent" is a stretch.

### 3. LORD, COME AND SEE
FIRST 3 SECONDS: "There is a place you cannot show anyone. A grave. An empty room. A hospital chair."
SETUP: Jesus asks, "Where have ye laid him?" They answer, "Lord, come and see." These were the very words Jesus once used to invite His first followers (John 1:39). Now grieving people use them to invite Him in.
THE TURN: He does not stay outside. "Jesus therefore again groaning in himself cometh to the grave" (11:38). He goes into the worst place with them.
LAST LINE: "Show Him the place. He has already walked into one like it."
WHY SHARE: It turns shame about private pain into an invitation.
RISK: The John 1:39 link is easy to over-explain, and a newcomer may find it confusing. Keep it as one quiet echo.

**BEST: #1, Loved, and Late.** It meets the viewer in the first three seconds and answers the hardest feeling.

## PART 3 — THE 4 QUESTIONS

WHO FOR: Anyone who prayed, got no answer in time, and now feels forgotten, grieving or alone.
MAIN TAKE-AWAY: Late is not the same as unloved; Jesus loved them, and He wept with them.
CURIOSITY GAP: "If He loved them, why did He wait?" The film does not explain the delay. It shows that He cried, and it leaves that with the viewer.
GLOBAL NEWCOMER: It needs only three facts: a family, a death, and a friend who came late and cried. The quotes are short, and every line around them is plain.
