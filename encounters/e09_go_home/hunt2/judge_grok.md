YOUR TOP 3
1. E — The link is inside one verse: same deed, "God" in Jesus' words and "Jesus" in Luke's report, easy to say back, and it lands on him. The Samuel line is extra and thin.
2. G — Same cry to a man of God and to Jesus, and this Gospel has already had Jesus name that widow (Luke 4:26). It stays an echo. Cut "life given back" and Romans 8:1 if it is ever used.
3. B — "The raging of the" is Psalm 89:9's phrase sitting in Luke 8:24, the tightest KJV match in the set, and Psalm 65:7 really does still sea and people in one line. Third because the man-half is the card's own comparison and the so-what joins the common inner-storm sermon.

REJECT
C — One shared word is turned into a place the devils dread and a place Jesus was brought up from. Paul is waving that descent off. Luke puts the herd in the lake. A viewer can leave thinking Jesus was in the demons' pit. Freshness makes this worse, because the mistake is memorable.

MISSING
DETAIL: Jesus "suffered" the devils' plea to enter the swine (Luke 8:32) and refused the healed man's plea to stay with him, sending him to his own house to tell what God had done (Luke 8:38-39).

VERDICT: ANGLE E is the one that is fresh enough and true. G is second. B is third, with the inner-storm line left off. REJECT C.
