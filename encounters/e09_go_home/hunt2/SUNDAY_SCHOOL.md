## KILL LIST - the common sermons on Luke 8:22-39. DO NOT give any of these as your angle, and do not give a re-worded copy of one.

- Jesus is Lord over the storms of life, and he can calm yours with a word (Luke 8:24)
- Jesus was in the boat with them. Having him with you does not mean no storms, but it does mean you won't sink (Luke 8:22-23)
- "Where is your faith?" Fear and faith fight each other, so trust him in the storm (Luke 8:25)
- Jesus is fully man (he slept) and fully God (wind and water obey him) (Luke 8:23, 25)
- Jesus has power over demons, and no one is too far gone or too bound for him to free (Luke 8:28-33)
- One man's soul is worth more to Jesus than a whole herd of pigs. People are worth more than money (Luke 8:32-33, 37)
- The townspeople asked Jesus to leave. They were more afraid of change and losing money than glad about a miracle (Luke 8:37)
- Go home and tell what God has done for you. Your story is your witness (Luke 8:39)
- Cross-ref: The same storm story in Mark, with "Peace, be still" (Mark 4:35-41)
- Cross-ref: God stills the storm and brings them to their haven (Psalm 107:28-30)
- Cross-ref: The same demoniac story told in Mark, with "Legion" and Decapolis (Mark 5:1-20)
- Cross-ref: The same events in Matthew, with two demoniacs (Matthew 8:23-34)
- Cross-ref: Jonah asleep in a storm at sea, a contrast to Jesus (Jonah 1:4-6)
- Following Jesus does not mean we will never face storms (Luke 8:22-23)
- When trouble comes, trust Jesus instead of giving in to fear (Luke 8:24-25)
- Jesus has divine authority over the wind and the waves (Luke 8:24-25)
- Even demons recognize Jesus as the Son of God and must submit to his authority (Luke 8:28-33)
- Jesus can free and restore people whom no one else can help (Luke 8:27-29, 35-36)
- People sometimes respond to Jesus with fear and rejection instead of faith (Luke 8:35-37)
- Tell the people at home what great things God has done for you (Luke 8:38-39)
- Cross-ref: Matthew also records Jesus calming the storm and correcting his disciples’ fear (Matthew 8:23-27)
- Cross-ref: Mark’s storm account emphasizes Jesus’ power and the disciples’ lack of faith (Mark 4:35-41)
- Cross-ref: Matthew’s account of the deliverance records the demons entering the swine (Matthew 8:28-34)
- Cross-ref: Mark gives a fuller account of the man’s deliverance and his commission to testify (Mark 5:1-20)
- Cross-ref: The Lord stills the storm when distressed sailors cry out to him (Psalm 107:23-30)
