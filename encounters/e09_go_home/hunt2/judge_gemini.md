YOUR TOP 3:
1. ANGLE E - A brilliant, undeniable surface-level word swap in a single verse that powerfully elevates Christ's identity without needing complex cross-references.

REJECT OUTRIGHT:
ANGLE A - I would reject this outright for stretching a prophetic picture of rebellious Israel (Isaiah 65) into a parallel for a possessed Gentile just because of the words "graves" and "swine", and for inventing the feeling of being "unseen" which the text never states.

MISSING ANGLE:
The Transfer of Fear: The disciples fear the storm (8:25), the demons fear the deep (8:31), and the town fears the cure (8:37)—everyone is terrified of Jesus' authority except the man who used to be the terror of the town, who is now at perfect peace at His feet (8:35).
