YOUR TOP 3
1. A - Strongest mix: a real, rarely seen text match (Isaiah 65:4 graves and swine), a step Paul himself states (Isaiah 65:1 to Rom 10:20), and a life-size SO WHAT (unseen). Reword the motive: "Luke does not say why" rather than "he crossed for him".
2. C - Most original and the deepest link (Luke 8:31, Rom 10:7, Rev 20:3, Heb 2:14-15 all hang together on one word), but it needs a plain-words rewrite and must not blend the two meanings of "the deep".
3. F - Clean, honest, easy to say back: no house, then "thine own house"; Psalm 68:6 gives a real second voice. Lower depth, but it is safe and warm.

REJECT OUTRIGHT: G. The tie is a common idiom ("What have I to do with thee"), the widow and the devils do not feel the same fear, the card gives the devils a motive, and a newcomer must hold two stories to follow it.

MISSING (optional): Luke uses "besought" four times (8:31 the devils, 8:32 the devils, 8:37 the town, 8:38 the man). Jesus grants the devils' ask and the town's ask, and refuses only the man's good ask to come with him, then gives him a harder job. This is a DETAIL in the text (Luke 8:31, 32, 37, 38) and is not on the kill list.
