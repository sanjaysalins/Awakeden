# BLIND JUDGE - which Luke 8:22-39 (the storm and the man from the tombs) angle is fresh AND true?

You are ONE independent judge on a blind panel. You did not write any of these angles and you do not know who did.
Do not use tools, do not read files, do not ask questions. Output your answer text only.

## The series
60-second YouTube Shorts that read the whole Bible (Old + New Testament) with Jesus as the lens. Every short
lands on Jesus. Global audience: many viewers have English as a second language and no Bible background. KJV only.
Rule: surprising about the TEXT, never about the TRUTH. No invented doctrine. An ECHO is never called a prophecy.

Labels used on the cards: PROPHECY (OT looks forward, NT says fulfilled) / NT-STATED (the NT makes the link; the
OT text was not a forecast) / ECHO (same words or shape; the Bible does not state the link) / DETAIL (in the
passage itself, usually read past).

## Score EVERY angle below. One line each, in exactly this form:
ANGLE n | FRESH x | TRUE x | SOUND PASS or FLAG (why) | CLEAR x | JESUS x | GROWTH x | DEPTH x | COMMON? yes/no/partly

- FRESH 0-10: how few ordinary churchgoers have ever been shown this (10 = almost nobody).
- TRUE 0-10: is it really in the text, and is the label honest? Use the KJV text at the bottom. Flag any wrong quote, count, order or "who said what".
- SOUND: PASS / FLAG - any doctrinal or overclaim risk; any motive or feeling the text does not state; any line for the viewer that is not backed by a verse that speaks to all believers. Name it.
- CLEAR 0-10: can a newcomer with no Bible background follow it in 60 seconds and say it back in one sentence?
- JESUS 0-10: does it land on Jesus naturally, from the piece's own verses, without forcing?
- GROWTH 0-10 (Driver 2): is the SO WHAT from the passage's own word or act, about Him first, one true sentence, and life-size (fear, shame, failure, being unseen, purpose, loss)? A to-do list or "trust God more" scores low.
- DEPTH 0-10: how STRONG is the link, and does it change how you read BOTH passages? This is NOT a count of books.
  A forced or thin link scores low, even with many books. A link that makes both passages read differently scores high.
- COMMON?: is this angle already in typical sermons or commentaries? yes / no / partly. Be honest; say where you have seen it.
  The KILL LIST below is the common-sermon list: an angle that matches or re-words an item on it is COMMON yes.

Each card shows ANCHOR-1, ANCHOR-2 and WHY-VERSE. Judge the card as written; do not fix it.

Then:
- YOUR TOP 3, in order, with one line why each.
- The ONE angle you would REJECT outright, and why.
- Any fresh, sound angle you think is MISSING (one line, with verses) - optional.

Be hard. A pattern the text only allows is weaker than a link the NT itself draws. Freshness never excuses a stretch.
