**ANGLE 1**
1. TITLE: The First Thing Jesus Fixes
2. HOOK QUESTION: Why did the healed man immediately need to be given clothes?
3. ANCHOR-1: Luke 8:35 "sitting at the feet of Jesus, clothed"
   ANCHOR-2: Genesis 3:21 "did the LORD God make coats of skins, and clothed them"
3b. WHY-VERSE: No verse explicitly states this link (ECHO)
4. LABEL: ECHO
5. WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE: This does not prove the Gadarene man's nakedness was identical in nature to Adam's original innocence, nor that clothing possesses magical spiritual properties.
6. FACTS:
   The man wore no clothes for a long time (Luke 8:27)
   Jesus commanded the unclean spirit to come out (Luke 8:29)
   The townspeople found the man clothed and in his right mind (Luke 8:35)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus steps into your wilderness to leave you "clothed", covering the shame and exposure that the dark seasons of your life stripped away. (Backed by Galatians 3:27)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: Sermons usually focus on the pigs and the dramatic exorcism, completely missing the quiet, Genesis-like restoration of human dignity at the end. 9/10
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus acts as the new Creator who reverses the humiliation of the Fall, returning a shattered human to his right mind and covering his shame.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 8/10

**ANGLE 2**
1. TITLE: The Man Who Swapped The Name
2. HOOK QUESTION: Did you notice how the healed man slightly altered Jesus' final command?
3. ANCHOR-1: Luke 8:39 "shew how great things God hath done... published... how great things Jesus had done"
   ANCHOR-2: Psalm 126:3 "The LORD hath done great things for us"
3b. WHY-VERSE: No verse explicitly states this link (ECHO)
4. LABEL: ECHO
5. WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE: This does not prove the healed man had a fully developed academic theology of the Trinity, only that he knew exactly who was standing in front of him.
6. FACTS:
   Jesus commanded the man to return home (Luke 8:39)
   Jesus told him to show what God had done for him (Luke 8:39)
   The man published throughout the city what Jesus had done for him (Luke 8:39)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus commands you to share what God has done, and you do it perfectly just by telling people the "great things" Jesus did for you. (Backed by John 14:9)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: The two halves of verse 39 are read so fast that the seamless switch from "God" to "Jesus" is totally overlooked. 9/10
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus does not correct the man for swapping "God" with "Jesus", because to experience the saving power of Christ is to experience the direct action of God.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 10/10

**ANGLE 3**
1. TITLE: Clearing The Land Of The Unclean
2. HOOK QUESTION: Why did Jesus send the spirits into the lake instead of just out?
3. ANCHOR-1: Luke 8:29 "commanded the unclean spirit to come out"
   ANCHOR-2: Zechariah 13:2 "I will cause the prophets and the unclean spirit to pass out of the land."
3b. WHY-VERSE: No verse explicitly states this link (ECHO)
4. LABEL: ECHO
5. WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE: This does not prove Zechariah was specifically predicting the Gadarene swine incident, but rather the overall cleansing marker of the Messianic age.
6. FACTS:
   The spirit inside the man was called an unclean spirit (Luke 8:29)
   The demons begged to enter the swine (Luke 8:32)
   The demons entered the swine and drowned in the lake, removing them from the region (Luke 8:33)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus drives the "unclean spirit" not just out of your mind, but permanently out of your territory so you can finally live in peace. (Backed by 1 John 4:4)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: Modern readers focus on the bizarre nature of the pigs drowning, missing the prophetic visual of the physical land being permanently cleansed of dark forces. 9/10
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus is the Lord of the prophets who actively cleanses the earth of the dark forces that ruin human life.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 9/10

**ANGLE 4**
1. TITLE: The Demon Stole A Widow's Prayer
2. HOOK QUESTION: Did you know the screaming demon quoted an Old Testament mother in mourning?
3. ANCHOR-1: Luke 8:28 "What have I to do with thee, Jesus"
   ANCHOR-2: 1 Kings 17:18 "What have I to do with thee, O thou man of God?"
3b. WHY-VERSE: No verse explicitly states this link (ECHO)
4. LABEL: ECHO
5. WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE: This does not prove the demon was intentionally quoting the Old Testament, but that raw fear of divine holiness produces the exact same desperate idiom.
6. FACTS:
   The man fell down before Jesus (Luke 8:28)
   He cried out with a loud voice (Luke 8:28)
   He begged Jesus not to torment him (Luke 8:28)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus does not come to "torment" you when you feel utterly broken and afraid, but to silence the voices that keep you chained in fear. (Backed by Romans 8:1)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: It requires knowing the exact phrasing of Elijah's encounter with the grieving widow of Zarephath in the Old Testament KJV. 10/10
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus arrives not to bring the fiery torment and judgment the dark forces expect, but to bring absolute, gentle liberation to the captive.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 8/10

**ANGLE 5**
1. TITLE: The Terror Of The Deep
2. HOOK QUESTION: What were the demons actually terrified of when they saw Jesus step ashore?
3. ANCHOR-1: Luke 8:31 "that he would not command them to go out into the deep."
   ANCHOR-2: Romans 10:7 "Who shall descend into the deep? (that is, to bring up Christ again from the dead.)"
3b. WHY-VERSE: Romans 10:7 "Who shall descend into the deep? (that is, to bring up Christ again from the dead.)"
4. LABEL: NT-STATED
5. WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE: This does not prove the Sea of Galilee is the literal geographic entrance to hell, but that "the deep" represents the spiritual abyss of the dead.
6. FACTS:
   The demons begged not to be sent into the deep (Luke 8:31)
   They asked to go into the feeding swine instead (Luke 8:32)
   The swine violently ran down a steep place into the lake and choked (Luke 8:33)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus holds absolute command over "the deep", meaning no chaos or hidden terror in your life can override his voice. (Backed by Romans 8:38-39)
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: English readers just picture "the deep" as deep water, entirely missing the biblical definition of the cosmic abyss or the realm of the dead. 8/10
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus is the sovereign Lord over both the physical water of the lake and the terrifying spiritual reality of the abyss.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 10/10

MY TOP PICK = The Man Who Swapped The Name. It is hiding in plain sight in the very last verse, requires zero theological gymnastics to prove, and perfectly forces the viewer to confront the deity of Jesus through the simple, joyful disobedience of a healed man.
