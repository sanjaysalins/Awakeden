**Me first**

**1. Who / what / why**
- **Who:** Jesus, a man who was ruled by devils, the pigs, and the scared town.
- **What:** Jesus frees the man. The man begs to go with Him. Jesus says no and sends him home to tell what God did. The man tells what *Jesus* did.
- **Why it matters:** The man swaps "God" for "Jesus", and the text lets it stand. Quietly, that says Jesus is God.
- **Could N say all three?** Who and what: yes. Why: **no**. N will miss the God = Jesus point.

**2. Lines that need explaining**
- **"Same deeds. Two names. It's right there in the text."** This is the key line, but it only hints. N hears "two names" and doesn't get that the video means Jesus is God. R gets it, but may feel it's said too fast.
- **"The town begged Him to leave."** The video never says why. It never says the pigs ran into the sea and drowned, so the town's fear seems random to N. R fills it in.
- **"shew… thine… hath… unto thee"** Old English is hard for N. It's short and the meaning carries, but "shew" may sound like "shoe".
- **"Devils"** N may picture cartoon devils. It's OK, just a little odd.
- **"Now in his right mind"** Idiom. N may not know it means sane or healed.

**3. First 3 seconds**
- **Question it opens:** Why would Jesus say yes to devils but no to the man He saved?
- **Does the end answer it?** Yes. He said no so the man would go home and tell. It's a strong, odd hook, and it pays off.
- **Score: 8/10.**

**4. Take-away:** Jesus sent the healed man home to tell what God did, and the man told about Jesus, because they are the same.

**5. Who it's for:** Bible readers and curious Christians first. Newcomers second; they get the story but miss the twist.

**6. Scores**
- N follows it: **6/10**
- R learns something new: **8/10**. Most readers never notice the God / Jesus swap (it's in Luke 8:39).

**7. Lands on Jesus, not preachy: 8/10.** It points at the text instead of preaching. The last line is clean. The "It's right there in the text" line is a bit defensive.

**8. The one change**
- **Old line:** "Jesus said: tell what God did. He told what Jesus did. Same deeds. Two names. It's right there in the text."

**9. Overall: 7.5/10**
