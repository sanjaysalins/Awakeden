# Watch-and-tell test. You are ONE viewer. Do not use tools, do not read files, do not ask questions. Output your answer only.

Imagine you just watched a 55-second vertical video on your phone. Below is EXACTLY what the voice says (the [labels] show who speaks; you hear the voices, you do not see the labels). Pictures show the scene simply.

Answer as yourself first, then as two imagined viewers: (N) a total newcomer, no Bible knowledge, English is their second language; (R) a regular Bible reader who thinks they know this story.

1. WHO is in it, WHAT happened, WHY it matters - in your own plain words (3 short lines). Then: could N say all three? yes/no.
2. Any line N or R would need explained? Quote it exactly, say why.
3. The first 3 seconds: what question did they open? Did the end answer it? Score 0-10.
4. The main take-away, in one sentence.
5. Who is this video for?
6. N follows it: 0-10. R learns something new: 0-10.
7. Does it land on Jesus without feeling forced or preachy? 0-10.
8. The ONE change that would most improve it (quote the line, give the new line).
9. OVERALL 0-10.
