**As myself**

**1. Who, what, why**
- **Who:** Jesus, a man He freed from devils, a whole town, and some pigs.
- **What:** Jesus gave the devils, and then the town, what they asked for. When the freed man begged to come with Him, Jesus said no and sent him home to tell his story.
- **Why:** Jesus said "tell what God did." The man told what Jesus did. Luke writes it that way on purpose, and it quietly says Jesus is God.

Could N say all three? **No.** N gets "Jesus said no and sent him home." N misses why there are devils, why the town wanted Jesus gone, and the God = Jesus point.

**2. Lines that need explaining**
- **"the man He had just set free"** (N): Set free from what? Jail? We never hear that the man had devils in him. The whole hook rests on this, and it has no setup.
- **"The town begged Him to leave"** (N and R): Why? The pigs ran into the sea and drowned, but the video never says so. The cause is missing.
- **"he suffered them"** (N): Old word, but the next line ("He let them") fixes it. Fine.
- **"shew"** (N): Old spelling. They can guess it from context.
- **"Jesus said: tell what God did. He told what Jesus did."** (N): This is the payoff. N hears two names and may not see why the swap matters. R gets it, and it's the best line.
- **"It's right there in the text"** (R): Sounds a bit defensive. It works only if R believes it, and they can check Luke 8:39.

**3. First 3 seconds**
- **Question opened:** "Why would Jesus say yes to devils and no to a man He saved?" That's a strong, odd hook.
- **Answered?** Yes. The "no" meant "go home and tell."
- **Score: 8/10.** The hook works, but N doesn't know who "the man" is.

**4. Main take-away**
Jesus' "no" was a mission, not a rejection: go home and tell, and what you tell is that Jesus did what God does.

**5. Who it's for**
Mostly church people and Bible readers, who will love the God/Jesus swap. It's less for newcomers as written.

**6. Scores**
- N follows it: **5/10**
- R learns something new: **8/10.** Most readers have never noticed the God/Jesus swap in Luke 8:39.

**7. Lands on Jesus, not forced: 8/10.** The text does the work. "It's right there in the text" is the only line that pushes.

**8. The one change**
Old line: "The devils begged to go into the pigs. 'And he suffered them.' He let them."

New line: **"A man was ruled by devils. Jesus drove them out. The devils begged to go into the pigs. 'And he suffered them.' He let them. The pigs rushed into the sea."**

This one fix explains "set free" and gives the reason the town begged Him to leave. It adds about 5 seconds.

**9. Overall: 7/10.** It goes up to about 8.5 once N can follow the setup.
