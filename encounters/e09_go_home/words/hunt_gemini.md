[jesus] Go home to thy friends, and tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee.

[narrator] (pause) The text puts his action right beside that command. He tells...

[man] ...how great things Jesus had done for him.

[narrator] Jesus said, tell what the Lord did. The man told what Jesus did. It's right there in the text. Jesus left His compassion with a town that pushed Him away.

(d) 4 lines
Who it is for: A global, unchurched audience, and familiar Bible readers who usually only focus on the pigs and the demons.
Main take-away: Jesus answers human rejection not with anger, but by leaving behind a witness of His compassion.
The curiosity gap: The first three seconds reveal three requests were made but only two were granted, forcing the viewer to watch to find out who received the only "no."
Why a global newcomer gets it: It avoids cultural jargon and complex backstory, relying on a simple, universal contrast of three requests to show exactly who Jesus is.
