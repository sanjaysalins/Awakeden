**(a) Script**

[narrator] Jesus said yes to devils. And no to the man He had just set free.
[narrator] The devils begged to go into the pigs. Jesus let them.
[narrator] The town begged Him to leave. He got in the boat.
[narrator] Then the healed man asked to come, "that he might be with him."
[narrator] "Jesus suffered him not." He would not let him.
[jesus] "Go home to thy friends, and tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee."
[narrator] (pause) So he went, and told "how great things Jesus had done for him."
[narrator] Jesus said, "the Lord." The man said, "Jesus." It's right there in the text.
[narrator] He asked to stay with Jesus. Instead, Jesus sent him to tell how the Lord had compassion on him.

**(d) Four lines**
- **Who it is for:** Total newcomers, and Bible readers who think this story is only about the pigs.
- **Main take-away:** The only "no" in the story sent the man out to tell about Jesus. Jesus sent him with "the Lord", and the man told it as "Jesus".
- **Curiosity gap:** Why would Jesus say yes to devils and no to the man He just saved? The no is explained when He sends him home, and the "Lord"/"Jesus" swap is the extra payoff.
- **Why a global newcomer gets it:** It is three short asks with yes, yes, no, in plain words. Old words are explained ("suffered him not" means he would not let him). The two lines sit side by side so anyone can see the swap without knowing the Bible.
