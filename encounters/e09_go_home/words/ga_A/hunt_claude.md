## Me (one viewer)

**1. Who / What / Why**
- **Who:** Jesus, a man He freed from devils, the devils, and a scared town.
- **What:** Three asked Jesus for things. The devils and the town got a yes. The healed man asked to go with Jesus and was told no. He was sent home to tell his story.
- **Why:** Jesus told him to tell what "the Lord" did. The man told what "Jesus" did. So the man saw that Jesus is the Lord. The "no" gave him a job.

**Could N say all three?** Partly. N gets who and what. N likely misses the why, because the Lord = Jesus point goes by too fast.

**2. Lines that need explaining**
- **"The no went to the man He had just healed."** Healed from what? N is never told the man had devils in him. The pigs line comes next with no link. N may think the devils and the man are not connected.
- **"The devils asked to go into the pigs."** Why pigs? Out of whom? N has no setup. R knows it's Legion.
- **"Jesus suffered him not."** Old English, but the next line, "He did not let him," explains it well.
- **"Jesus said, the Lord. The man said, Jesus. It's right there in the text."** This is the key line, and it's too quiet. N won't get that this is a claim that Jesus is God. R may hear it as a small word note, not a big point.
- **"carrying compassion."** This comes from nowhere. "Compassion" is in the verse ("hath had compassion on thee"), but the video cut that part from the quote. Both N and R will ask: what compassion?

**3. First 3 seconds**
- **Question opened:** "Why would Jesus say no to the man He just healed, but yes to devils?" That's a strong hook.
- **Answered?** Yes: He said no so the man could go home and tell his story. The answer is a little soft, though.
- **Score: 7/10**

**4. Main take-away**
Jesus's "no" to the healed man was a call to go home and tell people, and the man understood that what "the Lord" did was what Jesus did.

**5. Who it's for**
Mostly Bible readers who like noticing small details in the text. It's less for newcomers.

**6. Scores**
- **N follows it:** 5/10
- **R learns something new:** 7/10 (the Lord/Jesus swap is a real find)

**7. Lands on Jesus, not forced or preachy?** 7/10. It's not preachy. But the strongest Jesus point ("He is the Lord") is hinted at and never said, so it lands softly.

**8. The ONE change**
Old: *"Jesus said, the Lord. The man said, Jesus. It's right there in the text."*
New: *"Jesus said, tell what **the Lord** did. The man told what **Jesus** did. He knew they were the same."*

(Second pick: change "carrying compassion" to "carrying the news of what Jesus did," or put "and hath had compassion on thee" back into the quote.)

**9. OVERALL: 6.5/10**
