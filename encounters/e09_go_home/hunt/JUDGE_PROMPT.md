# BLIND JUDGE - which angle on THE MAN AMONG THE TOMBS (Mark 5:1-20) is fresh AND true?

You are ONE independent judge on a blind panel. You did not write any of these angles and you do not know who did.
Do not use tools, do not read files, do not ask questions. Output your answer text only.

## The series
60-second YouTube Shorts that read the whole Bible (Old + New Testament) with Jesus as the lens. Every short
lands on Jesus. Global audience: many viewers have English as a second language and no Bible background. KJV only.
Rule: surprising about the TEXT, never about the TRUTH. No invented doctrine. An ECHO is never called a prophecy.

Labels used on the cards: PROPHECY (OT looks forward, NT says fulfilled) / NT-STATED (the NT makes the link; the
OT text was not a forecast) / ECHO (same words or shape; the Bible does not state the link) / DETAIL (in the
passage itself, usually read past).

Already told a million times (not on the cards; do not reward a card for merely repeating these): "Legion" means many
demons; the pigs run into the sea; "Come out of the man"; the town asks Jesus to leave; "go home and tell" as a general
lesson; no chains could hold him; "clothed and in his right mind" as a before-and-after.

## Score EVERY angle below. One line each, in exactly this form:
ANGLE n | FRESH x | TRUE x | SOUND PASS or FLAG (why) | CLEAR x | JESUS x | GROWTH x

- FRESH 0-10: how few ordinary churchgoers have ever been shown this (10 = almost nobody).
- TRUE 0-10: is it really in the text, and is the label honest? Use the KJV text at the bottom. Flag any wrong quote, count, order or "who said what".
- SOUND: PASS / FLAG - any doctrinal or overclaim risk; any motive or feeling the text does not state; any line for the viewer that is not backed by a verse that speaks to all believers. Name it.
- CLEAR 0-10: can a newcomer with no Bible background follow it in 60 seconds and say it back in one sentence?
- JESUS 0-10: does it land on Jesus naturally, from the piece's own verses, without forcing?
- GROWTH 0-10: is the SO WHAT from the passage's own word or act, about Him first, one true sentence, and life-size (fear, shame, failure, being unseen, purpose, loss)? A to-do list or "trust God more" scores low.

Then:
- YOUR TOP 3, in order, with one line why each.
- The ONE angle you would REJECT outright, and why.
- Any fresh, sound angle you think is MISSING (one line, with verses) - optional.

Be hard. A pattern the text only allows is weaker than a link the NT itself draws. Freshness never excuses a stretch.


# CARDS - The man among the tombs (Mark 5:1-20)

Twelve angles. Labels: PROPHECY / NT-STATED / ECHO / DETAIL. An ECHO is never a headline claim.

---
## ANGLE A - Expecting torment, given compassion
HOOK: What did the spirit ask Jesus not to do? What did Jesus say about the man?
VERSES
Mark 5:7 "I adjure thee by God, that thou torment me not."
Mark 5:19 "tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee."
LABEL: DETAIL (two words in the same scene: "torment" and "compassion").
DOES NOT PROVE: That spirits can receive compassion, or that Jesus never judges evil. It does not say what the spirit expected; it only says what the spirit asked.
SO WHAT: The spirit begged "torment me not", and Jesus told the man to say the Lord "hath had compassion on thee", so what Jesus did first was show mercy to the one who was hurt. (For all believers: Psalm 145:8 "The LORD is gracious, and full of compassion; slow to anger, and of great mercy.")

---
## ANGLE B - Graves and swine, together
HOOK: Where else does the Bible put graves and swine in one line?
VERSES
Mark 5:2 "immediately there met him out of the tombs a man with an unclean spirit"
Mark 5:11 "Now there was there nigh unto the mountains a great herd of swine feeding."
Isaiah 65:4 "Which remain among the graves, and lodge in the monuments, which eat swine's flesh"
Isaiah 65:1 "I am found of them that sought me not"
Romans 10:20 "I was found of them that sought me not"
LABEL: ECHO (graves and swine in both places; the Bible does not link them). Romans 10:20 is NT-STATED only for Isaiah 65:1 and the Gentiles, not for this scene.
DOES NOT PROVE: That Isaiah 65 forecasts this man, or that Mark was quoting Isaiah. Nobody in Mark 5 eats the swine.
SO WHAT: Jesus went to a place of graves and swine and was met by a man nobody could tame, so he is the one who is "found of them that sought me not". (For all believers: Luke 19:10 "For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.")

---
## ANGLE C - "The Lord" said Jesus; the man said "Jesus"
HOOK: Jesus told him to say "the Lord". What name did the man use?
VERSES
Mark 5:19 "tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee."
Mark 5:20 "began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him"
Luke 8:39 "shew how great things God hath done unto thee"
Luke 8:39 "published throughout the whole city how great things Jesus had done unto him"
LABEL: DETAIL (Mark 5:19 to 5:20, and the same pair in Luke 8:39).
DOES NOT PROVE: That the man understood who Jesus is in full. The text reports what he was told and what he then said. It does not say he disobeyed.
SO WHAT: Jesus said the Lord had shown the man mercy, and the man named Jesus as the one who did it, so Jesus' mercy is the Lord's own. (For all believers: Romans 10:13 "For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.")

---
## ANGLE D - Across the sea and straight back
HOOK: Jesus crossed a storm, met one man, and sailed back. Why?
VERSES
Mark 4:35 "Let us pass over unto the other side."
Mark 5:1 "And they came over unto the other side of the sea, into the country of the Gadarenes."
Mark 5:2 "immediately there met him out of the tombs a man with an unclean spirit"
Mark 5:21 "And when Jesus was passed over again by ship unto the other side"
LABEL: DETAIL (the order: cross, one meeting, cross back).
DOES NOT PROVE: That Jesus crossed only for this man. Mark gives no reason for the trip.
SO WHAT: Jesus sailed through a storm to a graveyard, met the man nobody could tame, and then sailed back, so no one is too far gone to be found. (For all believers: Luke 19:10 "For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.")

---
## ANGLE E - Told to tell here; "no man should know" there
HOOK: Jesus told others to keep quiet. Why did he tell this man to speak?
VERSES
Mark 5:19 "Go home to thy friends, and tell them"
Mark 5:43 "And he charged them straitly that no man should know it"
Mark 1:44 "See thou say nothing to any man"
Mark 5:20 "began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him"
LABEL: DETAIL (opposite instructions in the same book, even the same chapter).
DOES NOT PROVE: Why Jesus told one to speak and others to be silent. The text gives no reason; do not state one.
SO WHAT: Jesus sent this man out to "tell" of his compassion, so the man nobody could tame is the one handed the message. (For all believers: 2 Corinthians 1:4 "Who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble")

---
## ANGLE F - He ran and worshipped before the cry
HOOK: Who moved first when the man saw Jesus?
VERSES
Mark 5:6 "But when he saw Jesus afar off, he ran and worshipped him"
Mark 5:7 "What have I to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of the most high God?"
Mark 5:8 "For he said unto him, Come out of the man, thou unclean spirit."
Mark 3:11 "And unclean spirits, when they saw him, fell down before him, and cried, saying, Thou art the Son of God."
LABEL: DETAIL (order of verbs in 5:6-8). Mark 3:11 is an ECHO (same shape; Mark does not say they are the same spirits).
DOES NOT PROVE: Why he ran. Mark gives no motive, and does not say whether the man or the spirit chose it. It does not prove the man had settled faith.
SO WHAT: The man ran to Jesus, and the word "For" in 5:8 shows Jesus had already told the spirit to come out, so Jesus was already acting before the man reached him. (For all believers: Romans 10:13 "For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.")

---
## ANGLE G - Three requests; one "no"
HOOK: Jesus said yes to the devils. Why did he say no to the healed man?
VERSES
Mark 5:12 "And all the devils besought him, saying, Send us into the swine"
Mark 5:13 "And forthwith Jesus gave them leave."
Mark 5:17 "And they began to pray him to depart out of their coasts."
Mark 5:18 "he that had been possessed with the devil prayed him that he might be with him."
Mark 5:19 "Howbeit Jesus suffered him not, but saith unto him, Go home to thy friends"
Mark 3:14 "And he ordained twelve, that they should be with him"
LABEL: DETAIL (three requests in Mark 5). Mark 3:14 is an ECHO (same words "with him"; Mark does not say the man asked to be one of the twelve).
DOES NOT PROVE: That Jesus favours devils, or loved the man less than the twelve. The text gives no reason for the "no" beyond the sending.
SO WHAT: Jesus "suffered him not" and in the same reply spoke of the Lord's compassion, so a "no" from Jesus can come with a purpose and not with a closed door. (For all believers: Hebrews 13:5 "I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.")

---
## ANGLE H - From "his dwelling" to "Go home"
HOOK: He lived among the dead. Who still had a home for him?
VERSES
Mark 5:3 "Who had his dwelling among the tombs"
Mark 5:19 "Go home to thy friends"
Luke 8:39 "Return to thine own house"
Psalm 68:6 "God setteth the solitary in families: he bringeth out those which are bound with chains"
LABEL: DETAIL (Mark 5:3 to 5:19). Psalm 68:6 is an ECHO (same word "chains"; the Bible does not link it to this man).
DOES NOT PROVE: Who his friends were, how long he had been away, or how they received him. It does not promise every believer a restored household.
SO WHAT: Jesus took a man whose "dwelling" was "among the tombs" and said "Go home to thy friends", so he gives back a place to belong and not only an end to the pain. (For all believers: Ephesians 2:19 "Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God")

---
## ANGLE I - The coast that said "leave"
HOOK: They asked Jesus to leave. What did he leave behind, and did he return?
VERSES
Mark 5:17 "And they began to pray him to depart out of their coasts."
Mark 5:20 "began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him: and all men did marvel."
Mark 7:31 "he came unto the sea of Galilee, through the midst of the coasts of Decapolis."
Mark 7:32 "And they bring unto him one that was deaf, and had an impediment in his speech; and they beseech him to put his hand upon him."
LABEL: DETAIL (5:17 to 5:20: Jesus goes, the man stays and speaks). Mark 7:31-32 is an ECHO (Mark does not say the man caused the later welcome).
DOES NOT PROVE: That the man's telling caused the welcome in Mark 7, or that Mark 7's people are the same people. Decapolis was a wide region.
SO WHAT: Jesus left when asked, left one man who told of his compassion, and later came "through the midst of the coasts of Decapolis", where the man had told it, so a place that said "leave" is not shut for good. (For all believers: Matthew 28:20 "lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.")

---
## ANGLE J - The boat's question, answered on the shore
HOOK: The disciples asked who Jesus is. Who answered first?
VERSES
Mark 4:41 "What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"
Mark 5:7 "What have I to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of the most high God?"
Mark 5:15 "sitting, and clothed, and in his right mind: and they were afraid."
LABEL: DETAIL (the order in Mark across a chapter break). The fear after the calm sea (4:41) and the calm man (5:15) is an ECHO.
DOES NOT PROVE: That Mark planned it. The answer is spoken by the man with the unclean spirit, so it is true words, not a believer's witness.
SO WHAT: The question asked in the boat was answered on the shore with the words "Son of the most high God", so Jesus is who those first words say he is. (For all believers: John 20:31 "But these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God")

---
## ANGLE K - The devils had to ask
HOOK: Who had to ask permission in the graveyard?
VERSES
Mark 5:10 "And he besought him much that he would not send them away out of the country."
Mark 5:12 "And all the devils besought him, saying, Send us into the swine, that we may enter into them."
Mark 5:13 "And forthwith Jesus gave them leave."
LABEL: DETAIL
DOES NOT PROVE: That Jesus made a deal with evil, or that spirits have rights. It does not say what became of the spirits after the herd was choked.
SO WHAT: The devils "besought" Jesus and he "gave them leave", so the man they held was never in a fight between equals. (For all believers: Romans 8:39 "Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.")

---
## ANGLE L - From "crying" to "publish"
HOOK: What happened to the voice that cried night and day?
VERSES
Mark 5:5 "And always, night and day, he was in the mountains, and in the tombs, crying, and cutting himself with stones."
Mark 5:19 "tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee."
Mark 5:20 "began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him: and all men did marvel."
LABEL: DETAIL
DOES NOT PROVE: That he never cried again, or that suffering exists to make a story. It does not say hurting people must speak in public.
SO WHAT: Jesus took a man who was "crying" in the tombs and gave him words about compassion to "publish", so a voice that only cried can become one that gives comfort. (For all believers: 2 Corinthians 1:4 "Who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble")
