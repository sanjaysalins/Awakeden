# hunt_sonnet - Mark 5:1-20 - five fresh angles (blind seat: Sonnet)

---
## ANGLE 1
1. TITLE: He Said "Lord." The Man Said "Jesus."
2. HOOK: Why did one man swap a single word when he retold Jesus' sentence?
3. VERSES
Mark 5:19 "tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee"
Mark 5:20 "began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him"
Romans 10:9 "confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus"
4. LABEL: DETAIL (5:19 vs 5:20 word swap). Romans 10:9 = NT-STATED link for believers.
5. DOES NOT PROVE: That the man understood the full doctrine of who Jesus is. It shows what he repeated, not what he thought. Not a proof text alone for Jesus' deity.
6. FACTS
Jesus' words in 5:19 say "the Lord hath done"; Mark 5:19.
The man's report in 5:20 says "Jesus had done"; Mark 5:20.
The same phrase "how great things" is in both verses; Mark 5:19, 5:20.
Jesus' words also say the Lord "hath had compassion on thee"; Mark 5:19.
7. SO WHAT: Jesus pointed to "the Lord" who "had compassion," and the man told it as "Jesus" - so when you see Jesus' mercy, you are seeing the Lord's own. (Believers' verse: Romans 10:9.)
8. WHY NOT HEARD: Readers hurry to the "go tell" lesson and never lay the two verses side by side. Fresh: 6.
9. LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus talks about the Lord's mercy, and the man names Jesus as the one who did it.
10. CONFIDENCE: 8

---
## ANGLE 2
1. TITLE: The Twelve Got "With Him." He Got "Go."
2. HOOK: He begged to stay with Jesus. Why did Jesus say no?
3. VERSES
Mark 5:18 "prayed him that he might be with him"
Mark 5:19 "Howbeit Jesus suffered him not"
Mark 3:14 "And he ordained twelve, that they should be with him"
Hebrews 13:5 "I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee"
4. LABEL: DETAIL for the request and the refusal (5:18-19). ECHO for the match with Mark 3:14 (Mark does not state a link).
5. DOES NOT PROVE: Why Jesus said no. The text gives no reason beyond the sending. It does not mean the man loved Jesus less than the twelve, or that "being with him" was wrong.
6. FACTS
The man asked to be "with him"; Mark 5:18.
Jesus did not allow it and sent him home; Mark 5:19.
The same words "with him" are used when the twelve are ordained; Mark 3:14.
The man was sent to "thy friends"; Mark 5:19.
7. SO WHAT: Jesus said no to "with him" and said "go home" - yet He never leaves; "I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee" (Hebrews 13:5, said to all believers). Jesus' "no" was not Him leaving.
8. WHY NOT HEARD: The refusal is read as a mission lesson; the 3:14 phrase sits two chapters back. Fresh: 6.
9. LANDS ON JESUS: The one who would not let him come along is the one who promises never to leave him.
10. CONFIDENCE: 7 (the echo is soft; keep it as the surprise inside, not the headline)

---
## ANGLE 3
1. TITLE: The Boat Crossed a Storm. For Whom?
2. HOOK: Jesus crossed a deadly storm. What did He do on the far shore?
3. VERSES
Mark 4:35 "Let us pass over unto the other side."
Mark 5:1 "they came over unto the other side of the sea"
Mark 5:21 "when Jesus was passed over again by ship unto the other side"
Luke 19:10 "For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost."
4. LABEL: DETAIL (the sequence of crossings). Luke 19:10 = NT-STATED statement of Jesus' purpose.
5. DOES NOT PROVE: That the man was the only reason for the trip. Mark never states a purpose for the crossing. It only says what happened there.
6. FACTS
Jesus gave the order to cross; Mark 4:35.
They arrived on the other side; Mark 5:1.
The first person who met them there was the man from the tombs; Mark 5:2.
The only event Mark records on that shore is this man's story; Mark 5:1-20.
Jesus crossed back; Mark 5:21.
7. SO WHAT: Jesus crossed the sea to a tomb-dweller everyone had given up on - and He "is come to seek and to save that which was lost" (Luke 19:10, for all), so no one is too far gone to be found.
8. WHY NOT HEARD: The storm (ch. 4) and the tombs (ch. 5) are taught as separate stories. Fresh: 5 (some sermons make this link).
9. LANDS ON JESUS: He walked into the worst place on the map, and that is who He is.
10. CONFIDENCE: 8 (only the sequence is claimed; the purpose is only supported by Luke 19:10)

---
## ANGLE 4
1. TITLE: He Ran to Jesus While the Spirit Begged
2. HOOK: Who moved first: the man, the spirit, or Jesus?
3. VERSES
Mark 5:6 "when he saw Jesus afar off, he ran and worshipped him"
Mark 5:7 "What have I to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of the most high God?"
Mark 5:8 "For he said unto him, Come out of the man, thou unclean spirit."
Romans 10:13 "For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved."
4. LABEL: DETAIL (the order of verbs). Romans 10:13 = NT-STATED for believers.
5. DOES NOT PROVE: Why he ran. Mark gives no motive, and the text does not say whether the man or the spirit chose it. Do not claim the man was "free" at that moment.
6. FACTS
He saw Jesus "afar off"; Mark 5:6.
He ran and worshipped; Mark 5:6.
The cry "torment me not" came after that; Mark 5:7.
The word "For" in 5:8 shows Jesus had already told the spirit to come out; Mark 5:8.
7. SO WHAT: Before the man said a word, he ran to Jesus, and Jesus had already spoken to what held him - the one who runs to the Lord is not turned away: "whosoever shall call... shall be saved" (Romans 10:13). 
8. WHY NOT HEARD: The loud cry in 5:7 steals attention; the running in 5:6 is skimmed. Fresh: 4.
9. LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus' word was already moving before the man reached Him.
10. CONFIDENCE: 7 (the "For" reading of 5:8 is plain but should be stated softly)

---
## ANGLE 5
1. TITLE: He Was Asked to Leave. Then He Came Back.
2. HOOK: A region told Jesus to go away. Did He ever return?
3. VERSES
Mark 5:17 "And they began to pray him to depart out of their coasts."
Mark 5:20 "began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him"
Mark 7:31 "through the midst of the coasts of Decapolis"
Revelation 3:20 "Behold, I stand at the door, and knock"
4. LABEL: DETAIL for 5:17 and 5:20. ECHO for the return in Mark 7:31 (Mark does not say the man caused it).
5. DOES NOT PROVE: That the man's telling caused the welcome in Mark 7. It does not say where in Decapolis the 5:17 coast sits. Mark 7 only says Jesus passed through Decapolis.
6. FACTS
They asked Him to depart; Mark 5:17.
He left a man to speak in Decapolis; Mark 5:20.
Later Jesus came through the "coasts of Decapolis"; Mark 7:31.
7. SO WHAT: Jesus went when asked, left one witness behind, and later came back through the same region - He does not force the door, He knocks (Revelation 3:20, to all).
8. WHY NOT HEARD: Mark 5 and Mark 7 are in different sermons. Fresh: 7.
9. LANDS ON JESUS: He answers "go away" with a witness, and the door stays open.
10. CONFIDENCE: 5 (the Mark 7 link is an echo and the weakest here; keep it only as the surprise inside)

---
MY TOP PICK = He Said "Lord." The Man Said "Jesus." - It is a plain word swap sitting in two adjacent verses, it is easy to say in one breath, and it lands straight on Jesus without any stretch.
