## MY TOP 3

1. **G - Three requests; one "no".** All in the passage, in order, with exact words. It meets a life-size hurt (a prayer Jesus refused) and the answer is in the same verse: the "no" came with a home, a task and the word "compassion". Fix the count wording and swap the backing verse.
2. **C - "The Lord" said Jesus; the man said "Jesus".** The truest card on the table (TRUE 10) and the strongest landing on Jesus, confirmed twice (Mark and Luke). Weaker as a stand-alone life lesson, so it is best used as the closing beat of G or H.
3. **H - From "his dwelling" to "Go home".** Simple, clean, no stretch, and "a place to belong" is a real need worldwide. Less fresh than G, but the safest for a newcomer to say back in one line.

Best build: G as the spine, H's "dwelling among the tombs" as the opening picture, C's Lord/Jesus pair as the landing. L is a near miss for third place if its "comfort" claim is cut back to "tell".

## THE ONE I WOULD REJECT

**B - Graves and swine, together.** It is the freshest card, but the freshness is the stretch. The headline is an echo. Isaiah 65:4 scolds rebels who eat swine; it does not describe a victim being rescued. And the man in Mark "ran" to Jesus, so "found of them that sought me not" does not sit cleanly on him. A newcomer cannot follow three books in 60 seconds either.

(F is the second weakest: its key claim about the order in 5:6-8 goes beyond what "For he said" proves.)

## MISSING ANGLE (optional)

**"They were afraid" - of the healed man.** Mark 5:15: they see him "sitting, and clothed, and in his right mind: and they were afraid." Mark 4:41: after the calm sea the disciples "feared exceedingly". DETAIL for 5:15; the pairing with 4:41 is an ECHO. The fear lands on who Jesus is, not on the wild man. Care: Mark does not say the town felt no fear before, so do not claim that.
