**REJECT OUTRIGHT**
**ANGLE B**. It is a massive stretch that builds a theology on a mere coincidence of setting (graves and swine), forcing Isaiah's "sought me not" onto a man who literally ran to Jesus. Freshness does not excuse importing an unrelated prophecy.

**MISSING ANGLE**
**The chains of men vs. the word of Christ**: Men tried and failed to bind him with physical chains and fetters (Mark 5:3-4), but Jesus completely subdued him with a single spoken command (Mark 5:8), showing human effort cannot tame what only Christ's word can heal.
