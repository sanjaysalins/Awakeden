1. TITLE: The Demons Couldn't Stop Him Running
2. HOOK QUESTION: Can the darkest evil stop a body from bowing to Jesus?
3. THE VERSES:
Mark 5:6 "But when he saw Jesus afar off, he ran and worshipped him,"
Mark 5:7 "And cried with a loud voice, and said, What have I to do with thee, Jesus"
4. LABEL: DETAIL
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE:
It does not prove that demons genuinely worship Jesus in spirit, or that the man was fully in control of his own words.
6. FACTS:
The man saw Jesus from a distance (Mark 5:6)
The man ran to Jesus (Mark 5:6)
The man physically worshipped Jesus (Mark 5:6)
The voice speaking out of him belonged to the demons (Mark 5:7)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus is so sovereign that even a man completely overtaken by evil can run to Him and find "compassion", meaning your deepest shame cannot keep you from His reach (Romans 8:38-39).
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: We focus so much on the demons talking that we miss the physical act of the broken man running to Jesus first. (8/10)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus commands such absolute authority that even a legion of demons cannot physically prevent a broken man from falling at His feet.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 9/10

1. TITLE: The Time Jesus Said No To A Convert
2. HOOK QUESTION: Why did Jesus reject a healed man's prayer to follow Him?
3. THE VERSES:
Mark 5:18 "he that had been possessed with the devil prayed him that he might be with him."
Mark 5:19 "Howbeit Jesus suffered him not, but saith unto him, Go home to thy friends"
4. LABEL: DETAIL
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE:
It does not prove that Jesus pushes believers away, or that every Christian must physically stay in their hometown.
6. FACTS:
The healed man prayed to be with Jesus (Mark 5:18)
Jesus did not allow him to come along (Mark 5:19)
Jesus told him to go to his friends instead (Mark 5:19)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus "suffered him not" because He had a specific purpose for him in the dark places, showing that when God says no to your good plan, He is positioning you for His greater mission (Proverbs 19:21).
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: We usually teach that Jesus always says "come follow me," making His direct refusal here deeply surprising. (9/10)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus doesn't just save people to keep them comfortable in His boat; He commissions them to bring His light into the exact places they used to suffer.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 9/10

1. TITLE: The Demons Begged Jesus For Permission
2. HOOK QUESTION: Who was actually terrified in the graveyard that day?
3. THE VERSES:
Mark 5:10 "And he besought him much that he would not send them away out of the country."
Mark 5:12 "And all the devils besought him, saying, Send us into the swine"
Mark 5:13 "And forthwith Jesus gave them leave."
4. LABEL: DETAIL
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE:
It does not prove that demons have rights, or that Jesus negotiates compromises with evil spirits.
6. FACTS:
The demons asked Jesus not to send them out of the country (Mark 5:10)
All the devils asked to be sent into the swine (Mark 5:12)
Jesus gave them permission to go (Mark 5:13)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus only "gave them leave" to move, proving that evil has absolutely no authority over your life without passing through the hands of the Lord who has "compassion" on you (1 John 4:4).
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: Pop culture teaches us that demons are powerful and hard to fight, but the text shows them begging for permission just to move. (8/10)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus does not struggle against evil in a balanced fight; He rules over it with effortless, absolute authority.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 10/10

1. TITLE: Jesus Crossed A Sea For One Man
2. HOOK QUESTION: How far would God travel just to find you?
3. THE VERSES:
Mark 5:1 "And they came over unto the other side of the sea"
Mark 5:2 "And when he was come out of the ship, immediately there met him"
Mark 5:18 "And when he was come into the ship"
4. LABEL: DETAIL
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE:
It does not prove that Jesus ignored the rest of the Gadarenes, or that He only does one-on-one ministry.
6. FACTS:
Jesus arrived by ship to the other side of the sea (Mark 5:1)
Jesus was met immediately by the man from the tombs (Mark 5:2)
Jesus got back into the ship after this encounter (Mark 5:18)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus crossed a dangerous sea just to have "compassion" on one outcast, showing that He will enter your deepest isolation when the rest of the world has locked you away (Psalm 34:18).
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: We read it as just another stop on a preaching tour, missing that Jesus made the entire boat trip essentially for this single rescue. (7/10)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus is the relentless Savior who will brave storms and cross boundaries just to rescue a single forgotten person.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 9/10

1. TITLE: The Demons Feared What Jesus Freely Gave
2. HOOK QUESTION: What did the darkest spirits expect from Jesus?
3. THE VERSES:
Mark 5:7 "I adjure thee by God, that thou torment me not."
Mark 5:19 "tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee."
4. LABEL: ECHO
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE:
It does not prove that demons can receive compassion, or that Jesus never judges evil.
6. FACTS:
The demons expected Jesus to torment them (Mark 5:7)
Jesus told the man the Lord had compassion on him (Mark 5:19)
7. SO WHAT: Jesus brings "compassion" to the broken while evil only expects "torment," meaning His presence is a terror to your darkness but absolute healing for your soul (Lamentations 3:22).
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT: We miss the stark contrast between the demons' expectation of Jesus and the actual gift Jesus leaves with the man. (8/10)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS: Jesus perfectly divides the evil that holds us from the soul He loves, destroying the darkness while gently restoring the person.
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE: 9/10
