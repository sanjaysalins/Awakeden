# BLIND BRIEF - fresh angle hunt: THE MAN AMONG THE TOMBS (Mark 5:1-20)

You are ONE independent Bible researcher on a blind panel. Nobody else's work is shown to you.
Do not use tools, do not read files, do not ask questions. Output your answer text only.

## The series
60-second YouTube Shorts. Aim: read the WHOLE Bible (Old + New Testament) with Jesus as the lens.
Every short must LAND ON JESUS. Watched worldwide; many viewers have English as a second language
and no Bible background. KJV only.

## The problem
We want an angle that is OLD IN THE TEXT but NEW TO THE VIEWER - something the Bible itself says
(or plainly supports) that a normal churchgoer has probably never been shown. Never new doctrine.

## KILL LIST - told a million times. DO NOT give these as your angle
- "Legion" means the man had many demons.
- The pigs: the herd runs down the steep place and drowns in the sea.
- Jesus commands the unclean spirit, "Come out of the man."
- The people of the town are afraid and ask Jesus to leave.
- "Go home and tell" as a general lesson that every Christian should share their story.
- No chains could hold him / nobody could tame him.
- "Clothed, and in his right mind" as a simple before-and-after picture.

## The 4 labels - pick ONE per link, and be honest
- PROPHECY - the OT looks forward and the NT says it was fulfilled in Jesus.
- NT-STATED - the NT itself makes the link, but the OT text was history, a pattern or a vision, not a forecast.
- ECHO - same words or shape in two places. The Bible does not state the link.
- DETAIL - something in the passage itself that most people read past.
The strongest label sets the headline. An ECHO can never be the headline claim.

## Your task (Driver 2 - THE ENCOUNTER)
The scene is Mark 5:1-20. The full KJV text is at the bottom. Give your 5 best fresh angles on THIS scene.
A link to another part of the Bible may be the surprise inside the angle, never its headline. For EACH angle:

1. TITLE (max 8 words)
2. HOOK QUESTION a newcomer would want answered (max 14 words)
3. THE VERSES - one per line, in exactly this form (straight quotes, no "..."): Book C:V "exact KJV words"
   (at least two anchors; they may be from the same chapter)
4. LABEL - PROPHECY / NT-STATED / ECHO / DETAIL (one per link)
5. WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE - the overclaim we must avoid (1-2 lines)
6. FACTS - every claim that is NOT a quote (counts, order, who said what, who was there), one per line, with the verse that shows it
7. SO WHAT - ONE plain line. It must:
   - use a word or an act from the passage's own KJV (not "so trust God more");
   - say what Jesus is like or did FIRST, then what it means for you (not a to-do list);
   - be one true sentence a newcomer can repeat (no church words);
   - touch one real thing: fear, shame, failure, being unseen, purpose, loss.
   Any line for the viewer must be backed by a verse that speaks to all believers - name that verse.
8. WHY MOST CHURCHGOERS HAVE NOT HEARD IT (1 line) + your guess 0-10 how fresh (10 = almost nobody has heard it)
9. HOW IT LANDS ON JESUS in one plain sentence
10. YOUR CONFIDENCE it is sound and really in the text, 0-10

## Rules
No invented doctrine. No contrarian or clickbait readings. No speculation presented as fact.
No numerology or codes. No "the church got it wrong". No fear or gain/loss framing.
No Hebrew/Greek claims unless they are plain standard lexicon facts.
Do not put motives or feelings into a text that does not state them.
Surprising about the TEXT, never about the TRUTH. Fewer honest angles beat 5 weak ones.
End with: MY TOP PICK = <title> and one line why.
