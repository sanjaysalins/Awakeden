## YOUR TOP 3
1. F - It is the only card where the surprise is in the verb order and it lands on Jesus acting first, before the man or the town does anything. Growth is about Him, not a to-do list. Needs one clear line to explain "For" in 5:8.
2. C - A real, checkable detail (Mark 5:19 "the Lord" to 5:20 "Jesus", backed by Luke 8:39 "God" to "Jesus"). Lands on who Jesus is straight from the verses. Gentle and sound.
3. G - Easiest for a newcomer to follow and say back, and life-size (a "no" to a good wish). Honest only if the film says no reason is given for the "no" beyond the sending.

(Close fourth: H, the best feeling of belonging, but Jesus is a step removed and the friends' welcome is not in the text.)

## REJECT OUTRIGHT
B. Its headline rests on an ECHO that works against the passage: Isaiah 65:4 speaks of rebels who remain among graves and eat swine, and the man in Mark 5:6 ran to Jesus, so "found of them that sought me not" does not fit him. Romans 10:20 is about the Gentiles, not this scene. It is the freshest card and the weakest on truth. Freshness does not excuse the stretch. Also very hard for a newcomer.

## MISSING (optional)
Luke 8:35 "sitting at the feet of Jesus, clothed, and in his right mind" - the man who ran wild now sits at Jesus' feet, and the town's fear is of that sight. Fresh in how it is told, lands on Jesus, and uses the "before and after" without repeating it as the usual lesson. Verify the exact KJV wording before use (it is not in the pack).
