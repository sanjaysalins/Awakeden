# THE MAN AMONG THE TOMBS (Mark 5:1-20): five fresh angles

---

## 1. Three asked Jesus for something. Only one heard "no".

**HOOK:** Jesus said yes to the demons. Why did he say no to the healed man?

**THE VERSES**
Mark 5:12 "And all the devils besought him, saying, Send us into the swine"
Mark 5:13 "And forthwith Jesus gave them leave."
Mark 5:17 "And they began to pray him to depart out of their coasts."
Mark 5:18 "he that had been possessed with the devil prayed him that he might be with him."
Mark 5:19 "Howbeit Jesus suffered him not, but saith unto him, Go home to thy friends"
Mark 3:14 "And he ordained twelve, that they should be with him, and that he might send them forth to preach"

**LABEL**
- DETAIL: three requests, two granted, one refused (Mark 5).
- ECHO: "be with him" also appears in Mark 3:14. This is only the surprise inside, not the headline.

**WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE**
- It does not show that Jesus agreed with the demons or the townspeople.
- It does not make this man a 13th apostle.
- It does not tell us how the man felt about the "no".

**FACTS**
- The devils asked first, and Jesus allowed it (5:12-13).
- The people asked him to leave, and he got into the ship (5:17-18).
- The healed man asked to be with him, and Jesus did not allow it (5:18-19).
- Jesus gave the reason himself: go home and tell (5:19).
- He did go, and he told people in Decapolis (5:20).

**SO WHAT:** Jesus said no to the man who wanted to stay "with him", because he was sending him home with a story. So a "no" from Jesus can be a sending, not a rejection. (Backing verse for all believers: Matthew 28:20 "lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.")

**WHY UNHEARD:** Sermons tell each request on its own. They rarely line up all three side by side. **Freshness: 8/10.**

**LANDS ON JESUS:** Jesus gives a "no" to the one who loves him, and it comes with a job and a home to go back to.

**CONFIDENCE: 9/10**

## 2. He was told "the Lord" and said "Jesus"

**HOOK:** Jesus told him what to say. Why did he change one word?

**THE VERSES**
Mark 5:19 "tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee."
Mark 5:20 "began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him"
Mark 1:44 "See thou say nothing to any man"

**LABEL**
- DETAIL: "the Lord" in 5:19 becomes "Jesus" in 5:20.
- DETAIL: in Mark 1:44 Jesus told a healed man to stay quiet. Here he says "tell".

**WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE**
- The text does not say the man was making a statement about who Jesus is. Mark simply records the swap.
- It does not explain why Jesus said "tell" here and "say nothing" elsewhere. Don't guess at reasons as fact.

**FACTS**
- Jesus' words say "the Lord" (5:19).
- Mark's report of what the man told people says "Jesus" (5:20).
- The man told people across Decapolis, not only his friends (5:19-20).
- In Mark 1:44, Jesus told a healed leper to say nothing.

**SO WHAT:** Jesus said "the Lord" had shown him mercy, and the man who had lived among graves put Jesus' name on it. So when your own life has been mended, the name to give is Jesus. (Backing verse: Romans 10:13 "For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.")

**WHY UNHEARD:** The two verses sit side by side, so the word change slips past. **Freshness: 8/10.**

**LANDS ON JESUS:** Mark lets "the Lord" in verse 19 and "Jesus" in verse 20 stand side by side.

**CONFIDENCE: 8/10**

## 3. A storm crossing, then straight back

**HOOK:** Jesus crossed a sea through a storm. What did he do on the other side?

**THE VERSES**
Mark 4:35 "Let us pass over unto the other side."
Mark 5:1 "And they came over unto the other side of the sea, into the country of the Gadarenes."
Mark 5:2 "immediately there met him out of the tombs a man with an unclean spirit"
Mark 5:21 "And when Jesus was passed over again by ship unto the other side, much people gathered unto him"

**LABEL**
- DETAIL: in Mark's account, the only event on that shore is this one man.

**WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE**
- The text never says Jesus crossed *for* this man. Do not state that as fact. Say what happens; let the viewer notice.
- Do not claim the storm was an attack on the trip.

**FACTS**
- Jesus chose to cross (4:35).
- A great storm came on the way (4:37).
- The man met him "immediately" when he left the ship (5:2).
- The town asked him to leave, and he got back in the ship (5:17-18).
- He crossed back again (5:21).
- Mark records no other event on that shore.

**SO WHAT:** Jesus came through a storm to a graveyard, met one man nobody could reach, and then sailed back. So a person everyone has given up on is not unseen by him. (Backing verse: Luke 19:10 "For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.")

**WHY UNHEARD:** Chapter 4 (the storm) and chapter 5 (the man) are taught as two separate stories. **Freshness: 7/10.**

**LANDS ON JESUS:** In Mark, the whole trip across and back holds one meeting with one man.

**CONFIDENCE: 8/10.** The text is solid. The risk is only in how it gets worded.

## 4. The boat's question, answered on the shore

**HOOK:** The disciples asked who Jesus was. Who answered them first?

**THE VERSES**
Mark 4:41 "And they feared exceedingly, and said one to another, What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"
Mark 5:7 "What have I to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of the most high God?"
Mark 5:15 "sitting, and clothed, and in his right mind: and they were afraid."

**LABEL**
- DETAIL: the order in Mark. The question is asked in 4:41, and the next spoken words about who he is come in 5:7.
- ECHO: fear after a calm sea (4:41), then fear after a calm man (5:15). Only the surprise inside.

**WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE**
- The text does not say Mark arranged this on purpose.
- The answer comes from the unclean spirit's voice. That makes it true *words*, not a believer's witness.

**FACTS**
- The disciples asked their question in the boat, to each other (4:41).
- The next words in Mark that name who Jesus is are shouted on the shore (5:7).
- The speaker in 5:7 is the man with the unclean spirit (5:2, 5:7).
- Both the disciples and the townspeople were afraid after a calm (4:41, 5:15).

**SO WHAT:** The question asked in the boat was answered on the shore, by a voice that knew exactly who Jesus was. So when you are afraid and asking who he really is, the answer is already in the text. (Backing verse: John 20:31 "But these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God")

**WHY UNHEARD:** It crosses a chapter break. **Freshness: 7/10.**

**LANDS ON JESUS:** The storm asks "who is this?" The shore answers: "Son of the most high God".

**CONFIDENCE: 8/10**

## 5. The region that sent him away

**HOOK:** They asked Jesus to leave. What happened when he came back?

**THE VERSES**
Mark 5:20 "began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him: and all men did marvel."
Mark 7:31 "he came unto the sea of Galilee, through the midst of the coasts of Decapolis."
Mark 7:32 "And they bring unto him one that was deaf, and had an impediment in his speech; and they beseech him to put his hand upon him."

**LABEL**
- DETAIL: Jesus is back in Decapolis, and this time people bring someone to him.
- ECHO: "beseech" him to heal, where in Mark 5:17 they prayed him to leave.

**WHAT IT DOES *NOT* PROVE**
- Decapolis was a large region, not one town. Mark 7:32 does not say these are the same people.
- The text never says the man's telling caused the change. Show the two scenes; do not claim cause.

**FACTS**
- The man told people in Decapolis, and all men marvelled (5:20).
- Later, Jesus passed through the coasts of Decapolis (7:31).
- There, people brought a deaf man and begged Jesus to touch him (7:32).

**SO WHAT:** Jesus was sent away, sent one healed man back to tell, and returned to the region. So a place that pushed him out is not shut for good. (Backing verse: 2 Peter 3:9 "The Lord is not slack concerning his promise ... but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish." Note: this needs a shorter quote for the card, because "..." is not allowed.)

**WHY UNHEARD:** Mark 7 is seldom read next to Mark 5. **Freshness: 7/10.**

**LANDS ON JESUS:** Jesus goes back to the region that asked him to leave.

**CONFIDENCE: 6/10.** It is real in the text, but very easy to overclaim.

---

**MY TOP PICK = Three asked Jesus for something. Only one heard "no".**

It is plainly in the verses, it is new to most viewers, and Jesus' own words in 5:19 explain the "no", so we add nothing to the text.
