## Beat 1 — I was there

I have heard this account told many times, and I will set it down here as it was first given to me. There was a man born blind who sat at the gate of the temple every morning of his life. The crowds passed him by, while the years wore on without any change for the beggar.

## Beat 2 — The act

One day a stranger stopped before him, made clay, and spread it upon his eyes, and sent him to the pool to wash. He washed, and the darkness broke apart, and for the first time the beggar beheld light, faces, and his own two hands.

**[the Pharisees]** They summoned the healed beggar and demanded to know who had dared to do such a work upon the sabbath day.

## Beat 3 — The reveal

When the rulers pressed him, the man answered with the one plain thing:

**"one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see."**

The stranger who had healed those eyes was Christ, the true Light of the world, come for every soul still sitting in the dark.

## Beat 4 — The invitation

If you have lived in such a darkness, hear this account. He still opens blind eyes. Come to Jesus, and trust Him, and receive the light that the beggar received.
