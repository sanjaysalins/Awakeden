# The Man Born Blind — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** the man born blind  ·  **Core text:** John 9  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** Christ the Light of the world
**Status:** fixture — BAD, trips ONLY EW-G5 (detached third-person essay, not a witness)

## Beat 1 — I was there

The blind man sat at that gate every morning of his life, a beggar, his hand stretched out, his eyes shut to a sun he had never once seen. People stepped around him. He had learned the sound of pity and the sound of scorn, and he had stopped hoping that either one would ever change for him.

## Beat 2 — The act

Then a man stopped in front of the beggar. He spat on the ground and made clay and spread it across the blind eyes. He told the beggar to go and wash. The beggar felt his way down to the pool, washed, and the darkness broke apart. He saw light. He saw faces. He saw his own two hands for the very first time.

**[the Pharisees]** They dragged the man in and demanded that he explain who had dared to do such a thing upon the sabbath.

## Beat 3 — The reveal

They argued over Him, whether He was a sinner. The healed man could not settle their clever riddles, but he knew one plain thing, and he said it to their faces:

**"one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see."**

And the man understood it at last. The one who had opened those eyes was Christ, the Light of the world Himself, come for every soul still groping in the dark.

## Beat 4 — The invitation

If you have lived in the dark, then hear this witness. He is still the One who opens eyes. You do not have to make yourself clean before you go. Come to Jesus, and trust Him, and receive the very light that he was given.
