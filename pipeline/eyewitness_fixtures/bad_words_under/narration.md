# The Man Born Blind — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** the man born blind  ·  **Core text:** John 9  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** Christ the Light of the world
**Status:** fixture — BAD, trips ONLY EW-G3 (too few spoken words)
---
## Beat 1 — I was there

I sat blind at the gate, my hand out, my eyes shut to a sun I had never seen.

## Beat 2 — The act

A man spread clay on my eyes and sent me to wash. I washed, and the dark broke. I saw light, and I saw my own hands.

## Beat 3 — The reveal

I said it plainly to them:

**"one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see."**

And I understood that He was Christ Himself, the Light of the world.

## Beat 4 — The invitation

Come to Jesus, and trust Him, and receive the light I was given.
