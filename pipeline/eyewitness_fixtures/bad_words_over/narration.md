# The Man Born Blind — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** the man born blind  ·  **Core text:** John 9  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** Christ the Light of the world
**Status:** fixture — BAD, trips ONLY EW-G3 (over the spoken-word ceiling)

## Beat 1 — I was there

I sat at that gate every morning of my life, a blind beggar, my hand stretched out, my eyes shut to a sun I had never once seen. People stepped around me. I had learned the sound of pity and the sound of scorn, and I had stopped hoping that either one would ever change. I knew the gate by its smell and its echoes. I knew the merchants by their footsteps. I knew the hours by the heat that crossed my face. I counted my whole life in coins dropped into my open hand, and I told myself this was all that I would ever be, a fixed and forgotten shape beside the road that everyone else was free to walk.

## Beat 2 — The act

Then a man stopped in front of me. He spat on the ground and made clay and spread it across my eyes. He told me to go and wash. I felt my way down to the pool, I washed, and the darkness broke apart. I saw light. I saw faces. I saw my own two hands for the very first time in my life. I wept, and I laughed, and I turned my face up to the sky and let the colours pour into me, and I could not stop naming everything I saw aloud, as if I were a child learning the world from the very beginning, because in truth that is exactly what I was, born twice, once into darkness and now at last into the light.

**[the Pharisees]** They dragged me in and demanded that I explain who had dared to do such a thing upon the sabbath.

## Beat 3 — The reveal

They argued over Him, whether He was a sinner. I could not settle their clever riddles, but I knew one plain thing, and I said it to their faces:

**"one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see."**

And then the deeper truth broke over me. The man who had washed my eyes was Christ, the Light of the world Himself, come for every soul still groping in the dark. My healed eyes were only a small picture of what Jesus longs to do for the blind heart, and I knew that I would spend the rest of my days telling anyone who would listen exactly what He had done for me.

## Beat 4 — The invitation

If you have lived in the dark, then hear me. He is still the One who opens eyes. You do not have to make yourself clean before you go. Come to Jesus, and trust Him, and receive the very light that I was given.
