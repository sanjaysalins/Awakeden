# The Man Born Blind — Awakeden eyewitness (short)
**Witness:** the man born blind  ·  **Core text:** John 9  ·  **Type→fulfillment:** Christ the Light of the world
**Status:** fixture — BAD, trips ONLY EW-G4 (close names Jesus + a verb but adds fear / gain-loss pressure)
---
## Beat 1 — I was there

I sat at that gate every morning of my life, a blind beggar, my eyes shut to a sun I had never once seen. I had stopped hoping that anything would ever change.

## Beat 2 — The act

Then a man stopped in front of me. He made clay and spread it across my eyes. He told me to go and wash. I washed, and the darkness broke apart. I saw light. I saw faces. I saw my own two hands for the first time.

**[the Pharisees]** They dragged me in and demanded that I explain who had dared to do such a thing upon the sabbath.

## Beat 3 — The reveal

They argued whether He was a sinner. But I knew one plain thing, and I said it to their faces:

**"one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see."**

And then the deeper truth broke over me. The man who had washed my eyes was Christ, the Light of the world, come for every soul still groping in the dark.

## Beat 4 — The invitation

If you have lived in the dark, then hear me. He is still the One who opens eyes. Come to Jesus and trust Him before it's too late, or you will lose everything when the door is shut against you.
