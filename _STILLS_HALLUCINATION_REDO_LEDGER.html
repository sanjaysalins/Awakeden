Recurring root causes across the whole corpus (fix once at the prompt/ref level, clears dozens of instances):
  1. Bent/floating nail hands — the single biggest repeat defect. Nearly every crucifixion close-up (face_on_cross, bowed_head_finished, vinegar_sponge_reed, golgotha_hill_wide across almost all 11 pieces) shows a nail lying flat/bent across the palm or floating in front of fingers instead of piercing through. it_is_finished_john1930/nail_through_hand.png proves the model CAN render it correctly — good redo reference.
  2. Candle-vs-clay-oil-lamp trap — hit 14+ times across night-writing/scribe scenes (psalm_scroll_night in 4 shorts, david_writing_psalm in 3 shorts, coin_on_scroll, child_sleeping_lamp, plus 8 hits in the long-form: quill_ink_drop, david_hands_lyre, david_old_deathbed, david_scroll_sealed, disputed_word_marks, first_century_reader, scholar_hand_on_text, scholars_debate_two, scribe_over_manuscripts, synagogue_listeners_lean). This is the single most repeated defect in the whole corpus — reinforce "clay oil lamp, wick in spout, never a candle/lantern" in every scribe/night-reading scene prompt.
  3. Legible gibberish Hebrew/text on scrolls — psalm_scroll_night (x4), prophet_scroll (x2), coin rims, zechariah scrolls. Any close-up scroll draws real-looking letterforms in nonsense sequences.
  4. Dome of the Rock / Islamic-era skyline — zechariah_night_scroll (2 pieces) + storm_over_jerusalem (longform) — the named anachronism trap keeps recurring in period-Jerusalem night skylines.
  5. Cloned/duplicated faces — look_up_faces in both forsaken_cry and pierced_zech1210 shows the same Jesus-like face repeated 3x for a crowd of onlookers.
  6. Church steeple/cross-finial towers in period skylines — grace_poured_sky (2 pieces), john_leads_home, golgotha_hook.
