What changed

  • Stake early: Jesus is walking to His death. Then we ask: will He stop?

The 4 questions

  • Who for: anyone told to be quiet; newcomers; Bible readers.
  • Take-away: on His way to die, Jesus still stopped for one man.
  • Curiosity gap: "Would He stop for a beggar?" (~15 s), answered "Jesus stood still" (~25 s), then paid off again in the last line.
  • Global: yes. Being hushed is felt everywhere. Every old line is explained.

Still weak

  • Old English ("What wilt thou...") still slows a second-language ear.
  • "Mercy" is never explained (1 seat).
  • Film gets longer: about 78 s + 3 s hold = 81 s.
  • These are AI viewers, not real people. Real seed viewers are the true test.
