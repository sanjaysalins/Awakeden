## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
A phone viewer like me: someone who feels ignored or talked over. I stop at line 1 (a man shouting for help) and line 2 ("Would Jesus even stop?"). I finish if I care what happens to the man. I share if I know someone who feels unseen. Not served: people who want the "why" behind the story. Nobody tells me who Jesus is (never explained as a person or as God). I also get no reason to care about Jerusalem.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus stopped for one man who was ignored, even on his way to die." Line 7 and line 13 carry it ("He stopped for one man"). It is fairly clear. But line 12 ("trust made him well") and line 13 ("die for our sins") add two more ideas, so the one message gets crowded.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
**Who/what/why in my words:** A blind man begs. People tell him to be quiet. He shouts more. A man called Jesus stops, asks what he wants, and the blind man can see. Then he follows Jesus. I guess Jesus is a very important teacher or healer.

**Where I got lost or misled:**
- Line 1: "Jesus, thou Son of David" opens with a name I do not know, then "Son of David" (explained only later in line 3). "Thou" and "have mercy" are unclear words.
- Line 3: "God's promised King" brings in "God" and "King". Is Jesus a king or God? I am unsure. Jericho is just a place name.
- Line 7: "Jesus stood still" is quoted, then explained well ("A whole crowd was moving"). This works.
- Line 9-11: "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?", "Go thy way", "hath made thee whole" are old English. "Whole" may confuse me.
- Line 12: "that trust made him well" feels odd. He asked to see, and he saw. Why is trust the cause?
- Line 13: "Jerusalem", "die for our sins", "rise to life on the third day", "the cross" are all unexplained. "Sins" is explained ("the wrong we do") but not why Jesus dying fixes it. "Third day" counts from what? "His way to the cross" appears with no earlier mention of a cross.

**Words that fail in translation:** thou, wilt, hath, whole, mercy, Son of David, cloak, sins, cross.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. What I was TOLD that I could not follow: why "Son of David" means a promised King (line 3); who God is; why Jerusalem matters; why Jesus "would die" (line 13, never set up earlier); what "the cross" is; why dying and rising "for our sins" is connected to a blind man.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would do one thing at a time: the blind man is ignored, Jesus stops, and that is the lesson. It would drop the death and rising ending, or earn it with one clear setup line earlier ("Jesus was walking toward his death"). The ONE highest-value change: line 13 asks me to accept death, sins, rising and the cross in one breath. Cut it down to one clear idea. Score: 6/10. The core story (lines 2, 7, 9-10) is strong and clear. The ending loses a newcomer.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 2 ("Would Jesus even stop?"). Pays off at line 7 ("Jesus stood still"). Score 8/10. Worth the wait. But line 13 opens a NEW gap (why will Jesus die, and what does that have to do with me?) that the film leaves open.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 1: replace with "Jesus! Please, have mercy on me!" (no "thou", no "Son of David" yet).
2. Line 13: replace with "He followed Jesus down the road. Jesus was on His way to die for people like him. And still, He stopped for one man."
3. Line 12: replace with "He believed Jesus could help him. And at once, he could see."
