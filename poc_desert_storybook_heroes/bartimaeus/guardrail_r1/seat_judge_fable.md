## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR (one named viewer; who stops, who finishes, who shares; who is NOT served)
Named viewer: Ana, 24, Manila, English is her second language, half-remembers church, feels overlooked.
- **Stops:** anyone who has been told to be quiet. Lines 1-2 are a cry and a shushing in under 8 seconds.
- **Finishes:** most who reach line 7. The story is short and concrete.
- **Shares:** believers, to a friend who feels unseen ("He stopped for one man", line 7).
- **NOT served:** the total newcomer at the end. Line 13 jumps from a healed man to "die for our sins" with no bridge. The Bible reader gets a clean retelling but only one new thought (the road, line 13), and it is stated, not shown to matter.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)
"The crowd said be quiet, but Jesus stopped for him." Line 7 carries it and line 13 repeats it. Clear.
The problem: there are two takeaways. Line 13 adds "Jesus died for our sins" as a second, unconnected one. A viewer will say back the first and drop the second.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)
- **Who:** a blind beggar, Bartimaeus, and Jesus.
- **What:** he shouts, is shushed, shouts louder; Jesus stops, asks, heals; the man follows.
- **Why:** he trusted Jesus (line 12). Good plain gloss of line 11.
- **Lost:** line 3 stacks three facts between the question (line 2) and its answer. "God's promised King" - promised to whom? It passes, barely.
- **Lost:** line 13, "die for our sins, all the wrong we do, and rise again". Heard aloud, the inserted gloss breaks the sentence. "Rise again" can sound like "rise a second time".
- **Hard words:** "have mercy on me" (lines 1, 5) is never put in plain words, and it is the hook. "Thou / wilt / thy / hath" are covered by lines 8 and 12. "Cloak" (line 8) is fine.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
- Lines 1, 5, 9, 10, 11 and "And Jesus stood still" (7): RIGHT, KJV verbatim (Mark 10:47-52).
- "The crowd told the blind beggar to be quiet" (2): RIGHT ("many charged him", v48).
- "by the road near Jericho" (3): RIGHT (v46).
- "'Son of David' meant God's promised King" (3): RIGHT as a standard reading; the passage itself does not explain the title.
- "A whole crowd was moving" (7): RIGHT ("a great number of people", v46).
- "and called him" (7): slightly OVERCLAIMED. Jesus "commanded him to be called"; others called him (v49).
- "threw off his cloak, got up, and came" (8): RIGHT (v50).
- "He trusted Jesus, and Jesus healed him. At once he could see" (12): RIGHT (v52).
- "followed Jesus on the road" (13): RIGHT ("in the way", v52).
- "That road led to Jerusalem" (13): RIGHT (Mark 10:32-33).
- "die for our sins... rise again the third day" (13): RIGHT (1 Cor 15:3-4; Mark 10:34).
- "all the wrong we do" (13): acceptable simplification, not a full definition.

No WRONG claims found.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE (what would the best explainer in the world do differently; the ONE highest-value change; score 0-10 against that bar)
The best version would make the cross the reason the stop matters. Jesus is walking to his death, and he still stands still for one beggar. Right now line 13 states the road and the cross as extra facts.
**ONE change:** end line 13 by joining the two: "On his way to the cross, he stood still for one man." That makes one takeaway, not two, and gives the Bible reader something new.
Also missing: any place for the viewer. The film ends on a fact, not an invitation.
**Score: 6.5 / 10.**

## CURIOSITY GAP (opens at line 2, pays off at line 7; score 0-10; worth the wait?)
Opens at line 2 ("Would Jesus even stop?"), pays off at line 7. **Score: 6.5 / 10.** Worth the wait, and the pause (line 6) is well placed. But it pays off about 40% in. Line 9 opens only a small second gap. Lines 12-13 run on goodwill. The join in section 5 would give the ending its own payoff.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES (line number + exact replacement wording)
1. **Line 13:** "They told him to be quiet. Jesus stood still for him. And the man followed Jesus on the road. That road led to Jerusalem. There Jesus would die for our sins, the wrong things we do, and rise to life on the third day. On his way to the cross, he stood still for one man."
2. **Line 3:** "His name was Bartimaeus. He sat begging by the road near Jericho. He was calling Jesus God's promised King, and asking for help."
3. **Line 7:** "'And Jesus stood still.' A whole crowd was moving. He stopped for one man, and said, 'Call him.'"
