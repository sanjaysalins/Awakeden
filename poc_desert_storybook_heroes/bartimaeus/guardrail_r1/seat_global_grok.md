## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY (one sentence a viewer could say back; which line carries it; did the film make it clear?)
**Takeaway:** Jesus stopped for one blind man who kept calling, and that man’s trust brought healing.

Carried best by **line 7** (“He stopped for one man”) and sealed by **line 12**. Clear on mercy and healing. The death-and-rise ending in **line 13** is a second takeaway piled on late.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)
**Who / what / why:** Blind Bartimaeus calls Jesus. The crowd shuts him up. Jesus stops, asks what he wants, heals him because he trusted. Then the road points to Jesus dying and rising.

Where I get lost:
- **Line 9:** “What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?” — captions and translators choke on *wilt / thou / unto / thee*.
- **Line 11:** “Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole.” — *Go thy way* sounds like “leave my road.” *Whole* means “healed,” but many hear “complete / not broken into pieces.”
- **Line 1 / 5:** “thou Son of David” — *thou* is dead English; *Son of David* is insider (line 3 helps, but it comes late after the first shout).
- **Line 13:** “die for our sins, all the wrong we do” — the gloss helps, but the jump from one healing to worldwide sin-death-rise is sudden for outsiders.

Universal: a ignored poor man, a crowd that silences him, one person who stops, sight restored.  
Insider: “Son of David,” KJV speech, “for our sins,” “the third day.”

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE (claim by claim: RIGHT / OVERCLAIMED / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG, quote the line; say only what you can support)
- **Line 1 / 5** cry “Son of David, have mercy” — **RIGHT** (Mark 10:47–48).
- **Line 2** crowd tells him to be quiet — **RIGHT** (10:48).
- **Line 3** name, begging near Jericho; “Son of David” = God’s promised King — **RIGHT** on setting; title gloss is fair teaching, not a quote from the scene.
- **Line 4** shouted louder — **RIGHT** (10:48).
- **Line 7** “Jesus stood still… called him” — **RIGHT** (10:49).
- **Line 8** threw off cloak, came; Jesus asks what he wants — **RIGHT** (10:50–51).
- **Lines 9–11** dialogue — **RIGHT** in sense; wording is KJV-shaped.
- **Line 12** trusted / healed / saw at once — **RIGHT** (10:52); “trusted” fairly restates faith.
- **Line 13** followed on the road to Jerusalem; die for sins; rise third day — road/follow **RIGHT** (10:52; cf. 10:32–34). “Die for our sins… rise again the third day” — **RIGHT** as later teaching (1 Cor 15:3–4) tied to that road; **OVERCLAIMED** if heard as words spoken in this roadside scene.
