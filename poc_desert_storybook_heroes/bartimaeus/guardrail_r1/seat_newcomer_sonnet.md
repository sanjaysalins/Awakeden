## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus stops for the one person everyone tells to be quiet." Line 7 and line 13 carry it. It is clear. But line 13 then adds a second idea (Jesus will die and rise), and that confuses the first.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
My own words: A blind poor man shouts to a man called Jesus. The crowd says stop. He shouts more. Jesus stops, asks what he wants. He says "I want to see." Jesus heals him. He follows Jesus. Then something about a road, dying, and rising.

Where I got lost:
- Line 1: I do not know who Jesus is, or why "Son of David" matters. Line 3 explains it as "God's promised King." That helps, but it comes late and fast. Who is David? What is "God's promised King"?
- Line 1 and 5: "thou", "have mercy on me" are hard English. "Mercy" is unclear.
- Line 9: "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?" is hard to parse.
- Line 11: "thy faith hath made thee whole." What is "faith"? "Whole" is odd. Healed? Forgiven?
- Line 7: "Jesus stood still" next to "A whole crowd was moving" is clear and good.
- Line 13: "Jerusalem, where Jesus would die for our sins, all the wrong we do, and rise again the third day." I cannot follow this. Why does he die? Who killed him? "Rise again" means come alive? "Third day" after what? It feels like a different film started. "Our sins" assumes I am part of something.
- Line 12: "He trusted Jesus, and Jesus healed him." Why does Jesus heal, with what power? Is Jesus a doctor, a king, a god? Not told.
- Line 8: "threw off his cloak" has no meaning for me.

The best explainer would say in the first 5 seconds who Jesus is ("a teacher many believed was God's chosen King") and keep one idea only. The ONE highest-value change: tell me who Jesus is in plain words before the shout, then trim line 13 so it lands on "Jesus stops for you," not on a death I was never prepared for. Score: 5/10. The story beat in 7 is strong. The ending jumps.
