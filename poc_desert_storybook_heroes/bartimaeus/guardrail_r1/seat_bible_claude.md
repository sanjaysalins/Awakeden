## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
**Viewer:** Grace, 40, a church member. She knows the story but has never noticed the words "stood still."
- **Who stops:** someone who feels unheard. Line 2 asks "Would Jesus even stop?" and that question grabs them.
- **Who finishes:** someone who wants the answer that comes at line 7.
- **Who shares:** a small-group leader, because line 7 is a good line to teach from.
- **Not served:** a total newcomer at line 13. Jerusalem, the cross and the third day arrive in one rushed sentence, with nothing set up earlier.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"When everyone told him to be quiet, Jesus stopped for him."
- Line 7 carries it and line 13 repeats it. It is clear.
- The second idea, "that road led to the cross," is attached at the end rather than earned along the way.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
- **Who / what / why:** A blind beggar shouts for Jesus. People hush him. Jesus stops, heals him, and the man follows Jesus toward Jerusalem, where Jesus will die and rise.
- **Line 9** ("What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?") is hard for second-language speakers. Line 8 explains it first, which is good.
- **Line 11** ("made thee whole") could be heard as being made whole in body only. Line 12 helps a little.
- **Line 11** ("Go thy way") then line 13 ("followed Jesus") could confuse a newcomer: was he told to leave? This is also the script's best unused idea (see section 5).
- **Line 13** is too full: recap, road, Jerusalem, death, sins, and the third day all in one breath.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **L1** "Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me." **RIGHT.** Verbatim Mark 10:47.
- **L2** "The crowd told the blind beggar to be quiet." **Slightly OVERCLAIMED.** Mark 10:48 says "many charged him" and Luke 18:39 says "they which went before." That is many people, not the whole crowd.
- **L3** "His name was Bartimaeus… begging by the road near Jericho." **RIGHT.** Mark 10:46: "as he went out of Jericho… sat by the highway side begging."
- **L3** "'Son of David' meant God's promised King." **Sound, but not in the supplied text.** It is orthodox. The pack's Isaiah 35:5 and Luke 4:18 ("recovering of sight to the blind") would ground it, and neither is used.
- **L4** "he shouted even louder." **RIGHT.** "He cried the more a great deal" (10:48).
- **L5** **RIGHT.** Verbatim.
- **L7** "And Jesus stood still." **RIGHT.** Verbatim (10:49).
- **L7** "A whole crowd was moving." **RIGHT.** "A great number of people" were going out of Jericho (10:46).
- **L7** "and called him." **Slightly OVERCLAIMED.** Jesus "commanded him to be called" (10:49): others called him. Those same people then say "Be of good comfort, rise; he calleth thee." Leaving that out loses the story's own twist.
- **L8** "threw off his cloak, got up, and came." **RIGHT.** "Casting away his garment, rose, and came" (10:50).
- **L9–L11** **RIGHT.** Verbatim (10:51–52).
- **L12** "He trusted Jesus, and Jesus healed him. At once he could see." **RIGHT.** "Thy faith… immediately he received his sight" (10:52).
- **L13** "The man followed Jesus on the road." **RIGHT.** "Followed Jesus in the way."
- **L13** "That road led to Jerusalem." **RIGHT.** Mark 10:32: "going up to Jerusalem."
- **L13** "die for our sins… rise again the third day." **RIGHT.** 1 Corinthians 15:3–4 and Mark 10:34.

**Verdict:** No doctrinal errors. Yes, I would send it to my church group after the two small fixes.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would land on the text's own word. Jesus says "Go thy way" (L11). The man is free to go anywhere, and he chooses Jesus' way, and Mark says "in the way." That way leads to the cross. The last line would then grow out of the story's own words, not out of a recap.

**The one change:** rebuild L13 around "Go thy way."

**Score:** 7/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
- Opens at line 2 ("Would Jesus even stop?") and pays off at line 7.
- **Score:** 7/10. Worth the wait.
- A second question (where does this road go?) is never opened, so the Jerusalem ending feels tacked on.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **L2:** "Many in the crowd told the blind beggar to be quiet. Would Jesus even stop?"
2. **L7:** "A whole crowd was moving. He stopped for one man, and had him called."
3. **L13:** "Jesus said, 'Go thy way.' He was free to go anywhere. He followed Jesus, on the road up to Jerusalem, where Jesus would die for our sins and rise again the third day."
