(d) The 4 Questions

- **Who it is for:** People worldwide who feel silenced, pushed aside, or ignored by the crowd, as well as newcomers learning who Christ is.
- **Main take-away:** Jesus stands still for the marginalized, and the exact same grace that healed the beggar drove Him to the cross for our sins.
- **The curiosity gap:** The crowd demanded silence—so how did Jesus react to the disruption? 
- **Why a global newcomer gets it:** The story relies on simple, universally understood actions (begging, shouting, a crowd pushing back, a man stopping) without requiring prior theological knowledge to feel the impact of the cross.
