# Blind test: global audience. You judge ONE narration for a ~60 s vertical video short. You see only the words below.

Pretend, in turn, to be:
1. A total newcomer: no Bible knowledge, English is your second language.
2. A Bible reader who knows this story well.

Answer for the narration, short lines:
- Newcomer: who is in it? what happens? why does it matter? (say plainly if any word or line confused you, quote it)
- Bible reader: did you get anything new? anything inaccurate to Mark 10:46-52 / the KJV?
- Curiosity gap: does the start open a question, and does the end pay it off? Score 0-10.
- Overall score 0-10.
Then: the TOP 3 fixes (quote the exact line).
Rules of the series (do not argue them): KJV quotes stay word-for-word; the ending must land on Jesus's death and rising; no pressure or "come to Jesus" closers.
