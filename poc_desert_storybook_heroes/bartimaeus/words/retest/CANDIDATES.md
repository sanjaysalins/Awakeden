# One narration for a ~60-second vertical short (Mark 10:46-52). Voices in [brackets]. (pause) = a short silence.

[bartimaeus] "Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
[narrator] A blind beggar shouted for Jesus. The crowd said, be quiet. What did Jesus do?
[narrator] His name was Bartimaeus. He sat begging by the road near Jericho. "Son of David" was a name for the promised King.
[narrator] Many told him to be quiet. But he shouted even louder.
[bartimaeus] "Thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
(pause)
[narrator] "And Jesus stood still." A whole crowd was moving. He stopped for one man, and called him.
[narrator] Bartimaeus threw off his cloak, got up, and came. Then Jesus asked the blind man what he wanted:
[jesus] "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?"
[bartimaeus] "Lord, that I might receive my sight."
[jesus] "Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole."
[narrator] At once he could see.
[narrator] They told him to be quiet. Jesus stood still for him. The man followed Him up the road to Jerusalem, where Jesus would die for our sins, and rise again the third day.
