## (a) Full script

```
L1  [bartimaeus] "Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
L2  [narrator] Many told the blind beggar to be quiet. Would Jesus even stop?
L3  [narrator] His name was Bartimaeus. He sat by the road near Jericho. "Son of David" meant God's promised King.
L3b [narrator] Jesus was on His way to Jerusalem. He had said men would kill Him there.
L4  [narrator] But the beggar shouted even louder.
L5  [bartimaeus] "Thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
    (pause)
L6  [narrator] "And Jesus stood still." A whole crowd was moving. He stopped for one man, and called for him.
L7  [narrator] Bartimaeus threw off his cloak and came. Jesus asked him what he wanted:
L8  [jesus] "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?"
L9  [bartimaeus] "Lord, that I might receive my sight."
L10 [jesus] "Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole."
L11 [narrator] At once he could see.
L12a [narrator] He was free to go. He followed Jesus.
L12b [narrator] The road went up to Jerusalem.
L12c [narrator] There Jesus died on a cross, for the wrong we have done.
L12d [narrator] On the third day, He rose to life.
L12e [narrator] He was on His way to die for us. And He stopped for one man.
```

## (b) Word count
About 178 spoken words (opening 61, L5-L10 unchanged 64, ending 53).

## (c) What changed and why
The stake (Jesus walking to His death, Mark 10:32-34) now comes at 15 s, before the stop, so the stop costs something. The crammed L12 is split into five short one-idea lines, one per picture. The last line turns the old callback around ("on His way to die for us... He stopped") so it lands alone on Jesus.

## (d) Four answers
- Who it is for: people who feel unheard or told to be quiet, worldwide, plus Bible readers.
- Main take-away: Jesus stopped for one unwanted voice while walking to die and rise for us all.
- Curiosity gap: opens at 0-5 s ("Would Jesus even stop?"), pays off at about 25 s ("Jesus stood still"). The new pull is the stake at 15 s: why is He going to Jerusalem, and what will happen there? It pays off at the cross and rising in the ending.
- Why a newcomer gets it: plain short sentences; who (a blind beggar, Jesus), what (he calls, Jesus stops, heals), why it matters (Jesus is walking to die for us, and still stops) are all said in the film itself.
