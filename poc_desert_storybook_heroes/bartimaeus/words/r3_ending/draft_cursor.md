**(a) Full script**

L1 [bartimaeus] "Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me."

L2 [narrator] Many in the crowd told the blind beggar to be quiet. Would Jesus even stop?

L3 [narrator] The road led up to Jerusalem. Jesus had told His friends He would die—and rise on the third day.

L4 [narrator] His name was Bartimaeus, begging by that road near Jericho. "Son of David" meant God's promised King. But he shouted even louder.

L5 [bartimaeus] "Thou Son of David, have mercy on me."

(pause)

L6 [narrator] "And Jesus stood still." A whole crowd was moving. He stopped for one man, and called for him.

L7 [narrator] Bartimaeus threw off his cloak and came. Jesus asked him what he wanted:

L8 [jesus] "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?"

L9 [bartimaeus] "Lord, that I might receive my sight."

L10 [jesus] "Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole."

L11 [narrator] He trusted Jesus, and Jesus made him well. At once he could see.

L12 [narrator] He followed Jesus on the road up. Jesus died for our sins. He was buried. He rose again the third day. On the way to the cross, Jesus stood still for one man.

**(b) Total word count:** 170

**(c) What changed and why:** The opening now names the Jerusalem death-and-resurrection stake before the stop, so the pause costs something; the ending splits follow, gospel, and callback into short beats so the last line carries the whole film.

**(d)**

- **Who it is for:** Anyone who has been hushed in a crowd—and anyone who does not yet know this road story.
- **Main take-away:** Jesus on His way to die and rise still stopped for one voice—and that same Jesus is the one who died, was buried, and rose for us.
- **Curiosity gap:** Opens at L2 ("Would Jesus even stop?"); pays off at L6 ("And Jesus stood still.").
- **Why a global newcomer gets it:** Plain road, death, third day, and cross in small steps—no church jargon holding the meaning, and the final line repeats the one surprise they already saw happen.
