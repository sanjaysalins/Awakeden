L3  [narrator] His name was Bartimaeus, begging by the road near Jericho. "Son of David" meant God's promised King.
L3b [narrator] That road climbs to Jerusalem. Jesus had told His followers He would be killed there.
L4  [narrator] But he shouted even louder.
L5  [bartimaeus] "Thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
    (pause)

L8  [jesus] "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?"

L13 [narrator] Up the road to Jerusalem.
L14 [narrator] There Jesus died on a cross for our sins, the wrong we have done.

    (pause)

Picture fit: L1 shout / L2 crowd hushing / L3 Jericho road + beggar / L3b Jesus far up the road in the crowd | L11 eyes open / L12 follows from behind / L13 road climbing / L14 empty cross / L15 empty tomb / L16 walking together.

## (b) Word count

184 spoken words (opening 58 + L5-L10 unchanged 64 + ending 62).

## (c) What changed and why

Moved the cost up front (new L3b: this road goes to Jerusalem, where Jesus had said He would be killed, Mark 10:32-34) so the stop is costly when it happens, and broke the old L12 into five short one-idea lines so the last line stands alone after a pause.

## (d) Guardrail lines

- **Who it is for:** anyone told to "be quiet" or who feels unheard, anywhere in the world; Bible readers get the Mark 10:32-34 link they may have missed.
- **Main take-away:** Jesus was on His way to die and rise for us, and He still stood still for one man who called.
- **Curiosity gap:** gap 1 "Would Jesus even stop?" opens at about 3 s (L2) and pays off at L6 ("And Jesus stood still"); gap 2 "He would be killed there" opens at about 14 s (L3b) and pays off at L14-L16 (the cross, the rising, the last line).
- **Why a global newcomer gets it:** short plain sentences, one idea each; every old word is explained beside it ("Son of David" = God's promised King, "made thee whole" = made him well, "sins" = the wrong we have done); death and rising now come as two separate lines, each with its own picture.
