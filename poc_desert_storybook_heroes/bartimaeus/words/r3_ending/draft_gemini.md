L15 [narrator] On His way to the cross, Jesus stood still for one man.

**(b) Total Word Count**
158 spoken words.

**(c) What changed and why**
I moved the stakes (Jesus knowingly walking to His death) to the opening so His choice to stop carries immediate weight, and broke the breathless ending into short, single-idea sentences so the gospel and the final hook land with clear, uncrowded impact.

**(d) Required Lines**
- **Who it is for:** People who feel unseen, burdensome, or silenced by the crowd around them.
- **Main take-away:** Jesus is never too busy, too important, or too burdened to stop for one person who cries out to Him.
- **The curiosity gap:** Opens at L3 ("Would He stop for one man?") and pays off at L6 ("And Jesus stood still.").
- **Why a global newcomer gets it:** It uses universal, physical concepts (a blind beggar, a crowd, a road to death, an empty cross, rising to life) without relying on complex theological jargon or assumed biblical geography.
