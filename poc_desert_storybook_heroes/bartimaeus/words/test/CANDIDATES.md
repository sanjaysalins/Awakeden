# Two candidate narrations for a ~55-second vertical short (Mark 10:46-52). Voices in [brackets]. (pause) = a short silence.

## Candidate X
[narrator] A crowd told a blind man to be quiet. Watch what Jesus did.
[narrator] His name was Bartimaeus. He sat begging by the road, near the town of Jericho. He heard Jesus was passing, and shouted:
[bartimaeus] "Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
[narrator] Many told him to be quiet. He shouted louder.
(pause)
[narrator] "And Jesus stood still." A great crowd was moving, and He stopped for one man.
[narrator] Bartimaeus threw off his cloak, jumped up, and came. Then Jesus asked the blind man what he wanted:
[jesus] "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?"
[bartimaeus] "Lord, that I might receive my sight."
[jesus] "Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole."
[narrator] At once he could see. He followed Jesus up the road to Jerusalem, where Jesus would die for our sins, and rise again the third day.
[narrator] They said, be quiet. Jesus stood still.

## Candidate Y
[crowd] "Be quiet!"
[narrator] A blind man was shouting for Jesus, and the crowd told him to stop. What did Jesus do?
[narrator] His name was Bartimaeus. He sat begging by the road near Jericho.
[bartimaeus] "Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
[narrator] He shouted even louder.
(pause)
[narrator] And Jesus stood still. A great crowd was moving, and He stopped for the one they told to be quiet.
[narrator] Bartimaeus threw off his cloak, jumped up, and came.
[jesus] "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?"
[narrator] Jesus asked a blind man what he wanted.
[bartimaeus] "Lord, that I might receive my sight."
[jesus] "Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole."
[narrator] At once he could see, and he followed Jesus on the road. That road went up to Jerusalem, where Jesus died for our sins and rose again the third day. He stopped for the one pushed aside.
