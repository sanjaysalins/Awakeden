# Bartimaeus - draft_sonnet

## (a) Script

[narrator] A crowd told a blind man, be quiet. What did Jesus do?
[narrator] He was Bartimaeus, begging by the road near Jericho. Jesus was passing.
[bartimaeus] Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me.
[crowd] Be quiet!
[narrator] Many told him to hush. He cried out more.
(pause)
[narrator] And Jesus stood still. With a great crowd moving, He stopped for the one they hushed.
[narrator] They called him. He threw off his cloak and came.
[jesus] What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?
[narrator] Jesus asked a blind man what he wanted.
[bartimaeus] Lord, that I might receive my sight.
[jesus] Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole.
[narrator] At once he could see, and followed Jesus on the road.
[narrator] That road went up to Jerusalem, where Jesus died for our sins and rose the third day. He stopped for the one pushed aside.

## (d) The 4 questions
- Who it is for: anyone told to be quiet or pushed aside, and Bible readers who know the story.
- Main take-away: they told him to be quiet, and Jesus stopped for him.
- Curiosity gap: the crowd said "be quiet" - what did Jesus do?
- Why a newcomer gets it: plain who/what (blind man, crowd, Jesus), one clear turn (Jesus stops), and a simple ending (He walked on to die and rise for us).
