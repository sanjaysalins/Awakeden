# Blind test: global audience. You judge FOUR candidate narrations (W, X, Y, Z) for a ~70-75 s vertical video short (Mark 10:46-52, blind Bartimaeus). You see only the words below. Pictures will match each line.

Pretend, in turn, to be:
1. A total newcomer: no Bible knowledge, English is your second language.
2. A Bible reader who knows this story well (KJV text is below).

Answer for EACH candidate, short lines:
- Newcomer: who is in it? what happens? why does it matter? (quote any word or line that confused you)
- Bible reader: anything new? anything inaccurate to Mark 10 / the KJV below?
- Curiosity gap: where does a question open, where is it paid off? Score 0-10.
- Ending: does the last line land, alone and clear? Score 0-10.
- Overall score 0-10.
Then: rank all four (best first, 1-2 lines why), and the TOP 3 fixes for the winner (quote the exact line).
Rules of the series (do not argue them): KJV quotes stay word for word; the ending must land on Jesus's death and rising; no pressure or "come to Jesus" closers.
