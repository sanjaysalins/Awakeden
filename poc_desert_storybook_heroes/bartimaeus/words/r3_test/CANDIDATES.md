## Candidate W

[bartimaeus] "Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
[narrator] Many told the blind beggar to be quiet. Would Jesus even stop?
[narrator] His name was Bartimaeus. He sat by the road near Jericho. "Son of David" meant God's promised King.
[narrator] Jesus was on His way to Jerusalem. He had said men would kill Him there.
[narrator] But the beggar shouted even louder.
[bartimaeus] "Thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
(pause)
[narrator] "And Jesus stood still." A whole crowd was moving. He stopped for one man, and called for him.
[narrator] Bartimaeus threw off his cloak and came. Jesus asked him what he wanted:
[jesus] "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?"
[bartimaeus] "Lord, that I might receive my sight."
[jesus] "Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole."
[narrator] He trusted Jesus, and Jesus made him well. At once he could see.
[narrator] He was free to go. He followed Jesus.
[narrator] The road went up to Jerusalem.
[narrator] There Jesus died on a cross, for the wrong we have done.
[narrator] On the third day, He rose to life.
[narrator] He was on His way to die for us. And He stopped for one man.
