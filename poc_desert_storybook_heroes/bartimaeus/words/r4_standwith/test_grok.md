**Bible reader**

"Jesus stood still" is what He did that day for Bartimaeus. "He will stand with you" is a promise spoken to every viewer.

The nearby lines are smaller. In 2 Timothy 4:17 the Lord stood with Paul in Paul's trial. Matthew 28:20, "I am with you alway," is said to His disciples. Hebrews 13:5, "I will never leave thee," is for His people. Those promises belong to His own.

The line also skips faith. Bartimaeus asked for mercy and trusted Jesus: "thy faith hath made thee whole." A hearer can take "He will stand with you" as "Jesus is on your side no matter what." That is wider than this text.
