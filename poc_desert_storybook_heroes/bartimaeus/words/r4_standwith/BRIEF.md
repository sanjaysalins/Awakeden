# Blind test: the LAST LINE. You judge TWO candidate narrations (A and B) for a ~80 s vertical video short (Mark 10:46-52, blind Bartimaeus). They are identical except B adds one final line. You see only the words below. Pictures match each line.

Pretend, in turn, to be:
1. A total newcomer: no Bible knowledge, English is your second language.
2. A Bible reader who knows the Bible well (KJV text below).

Answer, short lines:
- Newcomer: how does each ending feel? Does B's last line make sense to you? What do you think "stand with you" means here?
- Does B's last line make the film feel "full circle"? Does it add, or does it weaken the line before it?
- Bible reader: is B's last line sound and grounded in Scripture as said here, to any viewer? Is it a promise the Bible makes to everyone, or only to some? Any concern?
- Series rules (do not argue them): no pressure, no stock closer ("come to Jesus", "He's waiting", "just ask Him"), no blanket promise. Does B's line break any of these? Yes/no + why.
- Ending score 0-10 for A and for B. Overall 0-10 for A and for B.
- Winner (A or B) in 1-2 lines. If B's idea is good but the wording is not, give ONE better wording of the same idea (keep it short, plain, biblically sound).
