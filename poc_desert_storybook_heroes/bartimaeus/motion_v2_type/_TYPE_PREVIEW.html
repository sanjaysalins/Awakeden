Bartimaeus - a new look for the words

Only the words change. All the dust, light, rings and wipes stay.

The idea: three hands

Colour = who is speaking. The hand = how.

1. THE SHOUT (him)
HAVE MERCY
Rough red paint. Thrown from his mouth. Gets bigger.
2. THE TELLER (the story)
He stopped for one man.
Soft brush. Ink on the wall. No outline.
3. THE WORD (Bible)
And Jesus stood still
Old Bible type. It never moves.
Ink Red earth = him Sage = Jesus speaks Bone paper Gold = only after he sees
