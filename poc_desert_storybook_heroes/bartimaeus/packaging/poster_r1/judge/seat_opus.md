**MY PICK: Q2.**

**One fix:** make the tagline "Everyone said hush." larger/bolder (about 1.5x) so it reads at thumbnail size - it carries half the question.

**Biggest risk:** Jesus is tiny at the top in Q2; at phone size a stranger may see only "a man shouting" and miss that Jesus is in it. Check he still reads as Jesus at the smallest thumb, or lift his glow a little.
