## Checks
- One Jesus, one blind man: yes on all six.
- Faces: all fine. Eyes shut reads as blind on all six.
- Hands: fine on Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5, Q6. Q4's raised hand is fine; the low hand by the knee is a little muddy.
- Text at phone size: "LOUDER" and "STOOD STILL" both read. Red "for one blind man." is hard to read small (Q1, Q3, Q6). White "Everyone said hush." reads on Q2, but is lost over the figure on Q4 and Q5.
- Text over Jesus: Q3 (touches head), Q4 (across head), Q5 (across neck). Q1, Q2, Q6 are clean.

## MY PICK: Q2

It is the only one where picture, word and video title each do a different job. Poster: a man is told to hush and gets louder. Video title: Jesus stopped. Together they make a question, then a promise.

## One fix
Jesus is very small at phone size. Make the top band a little taller, or lift the glow behind Him, so the one still figure reads at a glance. Do not move the text.

## Biggest risk
At thumb size Jesus may not be seen at all. Then the poster reads as "an angry man shouting", not a Bible story. The video title has to carry the Jesus part alone.
