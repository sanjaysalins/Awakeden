### THE FROZEN CROWD  (title: STOOD STILL)
- **THE IDEA:** Scale contrast and motion against stillness. A river of blurred, moving people, with one sharp figure who has stopped, and the blind man huge in front.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** Top third: a desert road climbing to Jerusalem in hazy gold. Middle: the crowd as one long umber blur of streaking cloaks, all leaning uphill. In it, small but crisp, Jesus in sage green stands still and turns back toward us. He is the only still and sharp thing in the middle. Bottom 45%: Bartimaeus, big and close. Head back, eyes shut, hands cupped at his mouth, mid-shout. Red-brown cloak, clay bowl at his knee. A dark brushed edge frames his side. Thin gold light runs down the road from Jesus to him. Soft gouache, paper grain, sand and red earth.
- **LETTERING:** STOOD STILL across the top sky, about 22% of the height, warm off-white with a soft brush edge. TAGLINE (small, above his head): *"Everyone kept walking."*
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Why did the one man everyone follows stop for a blind beggar? The film answers it at "And Jesus stood still" (the music cut). Then the last line pays it off: He was on His way to die.
- **PHONE TEST:** At 2 cm you still see a big shouting face with cupped hands, a pale streak of crowd, and one green dot standing still. The title reads in two short words.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 movie poster, warm gouache storybook painting, cartoon-storybook faces, soft desert light, visible paper grain. Foreground bottom half: blind beggar Bartimaeus, large and close to camera, head tilted back, eyes shut, hands cupped at his mouth shouting, red-brown cloak, clay bowl beside his knee, dark brushed vignette on the left edge. Middle distance: a long crowd on a paved desert road painted as motion blur, cloaks streaking uphill in umber and sand. In the middle of the blur, one sharp, still figure: Jesus, long brown hair, beard, cream tunic, sage-green mantle, standing still and turned back toward the beggar. A thin path of gold light runs along the road from Jesus to the beggar. Top third: open hazy gold sky over distant hills toward Jerusalem, left empty for a title. Palette: sand, umber, red earth, sage green, gold. Use film pictures 3 (beggar's pose) and 4 (Jesus) as references. No lettering, no text.

### THE SPLIT FACE  (title: LOUDER)
- **THE IDEA:** Light against dark on one face. The left half is blind, the right half sees, and it sits next to a dark wall of hushing hands.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** His face fills the poster, with the split running down the nose. Left half: deep umber, the dark brushed edge eating in, eye shut. Black silhouettes of hushing hands, fingers to lips, crowd in from that side. Right half: bathed in gold, eye open and wet with a tear, looking up and off-frame right toward a soft sage-green glow (Jesus, only hinted at). His mouth is open across both halves, so the shout bridges dark and light. Gouache texture, dry-brush where the halves meet.
- **LETTERING:** LOUDER big and bold along the bottom, about 18% of the height. Cream on the gold side, fading into dark on the left. TAGLINE: none.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Why does half his face see? And who is he shouting at, past all those hands? The film answers it in three steps: the hush, the louder shout, and the gold close-up when his eyes open.
- **PHONE TEST:** At 2 cm the face is the whole poster. Half dark, half gold, one open eye. The shape reads instantly and the word reads too.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 movie poster, warm gouache storybook painting, cartoon-storybook face, visible paper grain. Extreme close-up of blind beggar Bartimaeus's face, filling the frame, split vertically down the middle. Left half in deep umber shadow, painted dark brushed edge, eye closed, with black silhouettes of hushing hands, fingers to lips, reaching in from the left edge. Right half lit by warm gold light, eye open, tear on the cheek, looking up and to the right toward a soft sage-green glow just off-frame. Mouth open in a shout across both halves. Dry-brush seam where dark meets gold. Lower fifth kept simple and dark-to-gold for a title. Palette: umber, red earth, sand, gold, a hint of sage green. Use film pictures 2 (hushing hands), 3 (shout) and 6 (open eyes, tears, gold light) as references. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1 and why
**THE FROZEN CROWD / STOOD STILL.** It shows both of them and the whole film in one idea: everyone moving, one man standing still for one man. The title is the KJV's own line, so the film pays it off exactly.
