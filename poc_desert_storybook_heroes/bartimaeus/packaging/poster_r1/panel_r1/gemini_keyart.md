### THE WEDGE OF LIGHT (title: The Man Who Stopped Him)
- **THE IDEA:** A stark light-vs-dark graphic split where the beggar's dark, brushed-edge world is physically pierced by a solid diagonal beam of golden light containing Jesus.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The bottom and left edges are heavy, rough umber and black brushstrokes representing the blindness. From the top right, a sharp, geometric wedge of brilliant gold light cuts diagonally across the canvas. In the dark bottom-left foreground, a massive, highly detailed Bartimaeus sits on the ground, head thrown back, shouting up toward the light. Standing perfectly still inside the distant top of the golden wedge is Jesus. The hushing crowd is just a muddy, blurred texture trapped in the dark edges.
- **LETTERING:** Title in bold, elegant cream serif, stacked tightly inside the golden wedge above Jesus. TAGLINE: He stopped for one.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** How does this desperate man trapped in the dark reach the glowing figure so far away? The film answers this when Jesus bridges the gap and calls him into the light.
- **PHONE TEST:** At 2 cm wide, you see a striking diagonal slash of gold cutting across a dark rectangle, anchoring a giant face at the bottom and a tiny figure at the top.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16, warm gouache storybook style. A stark diagonal composition. The bottom and left edges are heavy, dark umber brushstrokes representing blindness. A sharp wedge of bright gold desert light cuts down from the top right. In the dark bottom-left foreground, a massive close-up of a beggar with eyes shut and hands cupped to his mouth shouting (use Ref 3). Inside the distant top of the golden light wedge stands Jesus in a cream tunic and sage-green mantle, perfectly still (use Ref 4). The hushing crowd is blurred into the dark background edges. No lettering, no text.

### THE CAST OFF CLOAK (title: The Final Pause)
- **THE IDEA:** A frozen-action graphic trick where a massive red cloak suspended in mid-air acts as a sweeping canopy to frame a man's first moment of sight.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:** The entire top half of the poster is dominated by a sweeping, textured red-brown cloak, frozen in mid-air as it is thrown off. Below the flying fabric, filling the bottom half, is a massive, screen-filling close-up of Bartimaeus' face. His eyes are wide open, filled with tears, and reflecting a brilliant gold light. The background is pure, warm desert sand color, completely empty of the crowd.
- **LETTERING:** Title in large, weathered gold sans-serif, curving along the bottom edge of the flying cloak. TAGLINE: Leave the dark behind.
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Why is this man violently throwing away his only possession, and what is he staring at with such absolute awe? The film answers this in the climax of his healing.
- **PHONE TEST:** At 2 cm wide, you see a bold red-brown wave crashing over a giant, brightly lit, smiling face.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16, warm gouache storybook style. A bold graphic composition. The top half is filled with a massive red-brown cloak frozen in mid-air, thrown upward, creating a sweeping canopy of fabric (use Ref 5). Below the cloak, filling the bottom half, is an extreme, massive close-up of the beggar's face, his eyes wide open, tearing up, and smiling, bathed in brilliant gold light (use Ref 6). The background is a flat, warm desert sand color. No lettering, no text.

### MY #1
THE WEDGE OF LIGHT. It instantly communicates the core narrative (darkness vs light, the distance between the beggar and the Savior) through a single, undeniable graphic shape that pops instantly on a phone screen.
