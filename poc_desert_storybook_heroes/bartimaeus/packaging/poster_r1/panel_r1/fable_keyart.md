## TITLES

1. ★ **LOUDER** | Why is he shouting, and what happens when they tell him to stop? | One hard word, like *Whiplash* or *Speed*. It is a verb you can feel, and it names the turning point: he was hushed and shouted more.
2. ★ **ONE MAN** | Which man, and who stopped for him? | Sounds like a prestige drama. It sets one against many, which is the whole film.
3. **STOOD STILL** | Who stood still, and why does it matter? | The film's own KJV beat, where the music stops. Quiet and odd, like *Still Alice*.
4. **THE CLOAK** | Why would a beggar throw away the one thing he owns? | An object title, like *The Robe* or *The Piano*.
5. **HE HEARD ME** | Who heard, over all that noise? | First person and intimate. It reads like a true-story film.
6. **THE ROAD UP** | Up to where, and why follow? | A journey title that hints at Jerusalem without saying it.

### THE ONE WHO TURNED  (title: LOUDER)

- **THE IDEA:** Scale contrast and the frozen crowd. A giant shouting face fills the poster, and far above it a tiny river of walkers all lean one way, except one small figure who has stopped and turned.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:**
  - **Top fifth:** a thin band of pale sand road against gold sky. Twelve to fifteen tiny umber silhouettes all lean right, mid-stride. Dead centre, one small upright figure in a sage-green mantle stands straight and faces down toward us, with a soft gold glow behind him. He is the only vertical line in the picture.
  - **Middle:** empty warm sand, clean negative space, with one faint gold path of light running down from the green figure.
  - **Bottom three-fifths:** the blind man, huge, chest up. His head is thrown back, his eyes are shut tight, his mouth is wide open and his hands are cupped at his mouth. He is painted in red earth and umber.
  - **Edges:** a dark, rough brushed edge crowds in from both sides and the bottom (his blindness). It breaks open only where the gold path reaches his face.
  - **Texture:** dry-brush gouache with visible paper grain.
- **LETTERING:** LOUDER in the sand gap, in heavy condensed capitals about 70% of the width, cream with a red-earth shadow. TAGLINE: "Everyone said hush."
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** Why did only that one stop, and who is he to stop for a beggar? The film answers at "And Jesus stood still", when the music cuts.
- **PHONE TEST:** A big open mouth and shut eyes at the bottom, one green upright dot at the top among leaning dark marks, and one word.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 movie poster, warm gouache storybook painting, soft dry-brush texture, visible paper grain, cartoon-storybook faces. Bottom three-fifths: an extreme close, chest-up view of a blind beggar in a rough red-brown tunic, head thrown back, eyes shut tight, mouth wide open shouting, both hands cupped around his mouth, lit by warm gold light from above. A dark umber rough brushed edge closes in from the left, right and bottom like a vignette of darkness. Middle: a calm empty band of pale sand with a single soft gold beam of light falling diagonally down onto his face. Top fifth: a far-away paved desert road against a gold sky, a line of very small dark umber silhouette people all walking and leaning to the right, and in the exact centre one small man standing perfectly upright and still, long brown hair, beard, cream tunic, sage-green mantle, turned to face down toward the beggar, soft gold glow behind him. Strong scale contrast, simple shapes, lots of clean space in the middle band. Palette: sand, umber, red earth, sage green, gold. ### HALF IN THE DARK  (title: ONE MAN)

- **THE IDEA:** Split face, light against dark. One face is cut down the middle, with the blind half in dark brush and the seeing half in gold, and a tiny green figure reflected in the open eye.
- **WHAT YOU SEE:**
  - **Whole frame:** the beggar's face, front on, from forehead to beard. It is very big and very simple.
  - **Left half:** deep umber and near-black dry brush. The eye is shut and the brow is tight. Paint drags across it as if it has been scrubbed out.
  - **Right half:** gold light, warm red-earth skin, the eye wide open and wet, one tear, and the start of a smile.
  - **In the open eye:** a tiny reflected figure in a sage-green mantle, standing still.
  - **The seam:** not a ruled line but a torn, brushed edge with gold bleeding into the dark.
  - **Lower corner:** a strip of red-brown cloak slipping off his shoulder and out of frame.
- **LETTERING:** ONE MAN low across the chest area, in wide-spaced cream capitals about 60% of the width. TAGLINE: "The crowd kept walking."
- **THE CURIOSITY GAP:** What did he see first, and who is in his eye? The film answers in the eyes-open close-up, then in the last shot of the two on the road.
- **PHONE TEST:** One face that is half black and half gold, with one bright open eye. It reads at any size.
- **IMAGE PROMPT:** Vertical 9:16 movie poster, warm gouache storybook painting, soft dry-brush texture, visible paper grain, cartoon-storybook face. A single huge front-facing close-up of a bearded Middle-Eastern beggar's face filling the whole frame, forehead to beard. The face is split down the middle by a rough torn brushstroke seam. Left half: painted in deep umber and near-black dry brush, the eye shut tight, the brow tense, dark paint dragged across it. Right half: glowing gold light on warm red-earth skin, the eye wide open and shining, one tear on the cheek, the corner of the mouth lifting into a smile. Reflected small in the open eye: a tiny standing man with long brown hair, a beard, a cream tunic and a sage-green mantle. Gold paint bleeds across the seam into the dark side. Bottom right corner: a red-brown cloak slipping off his shoulder. Plain soft sand background, one bold graphic shape, high contrast. Palette: umber, red earth, sand, sage green, gold. ### MY #1: THE ONE WHO TURNED (LOUDER)

It shows both men and the film's whole idea, many walking and one stopped, in a single shape, and it adds to the video title rather than repeating it.
The split face is the stronger phone icon, but it shows the healing and so gives away the answer; LOUDER leaves the question open.
