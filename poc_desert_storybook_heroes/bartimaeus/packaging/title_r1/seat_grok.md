## 1. Six pairs

**Pair 1**
TITLE: Bible: A Blind Man Would Not Quit
THUMB WORDS: Jesus | stood still
THUMB PICTURE: The beggar on the ground, arms out, shouting, dark edge around him, while people hold a finger to their lips and Jesus stands sharp and still in a blurred crowd.
Opens: why stand still for a man who will not quit? Pays off when Jesus stops, calls him, and heals him.

**Pair 2**
TITLE: Would Jesus Stop for a Blind Man?
THUMB WORDS: On his way | to die
THUMB PICTURE: Jesus standing still in a frozen, blurred crowd, with the road behind him running toward the sunset.
Opens: would he stop for one blind man while he was on his way to die? Pays off when he stands still, heals him, and goes on to the cross and rises.

**Pair 3**
TITLE: Jesus Heals the Blind Man. Then?
THUMB WORDS: Free to | go anywhere
THUMB PICTURE: His eyes open, looking up, tears and a smile in gold light, with the open road behind him.
Opens: once he can see, does he leave? Pays off when he is free to go anywhere and chooses to follow Jesus.

**Pair 4**
TITLE: Jesus Heals Blind Man: Why Him?
THUMB WORDS: A whole | crowd moved
THUMB PICTURE: One beggar on the ground, eyes shut, arms out, while Jesus stands still and the crowd blurs past.
Opens: why this one man, when the whole crowd kept moving? Pays off when Jesus stops for him alone and calls him.

**Pair 5**
TITLE: Who Is Blind Bartimaeus? (Bible)
THUMB WORDS: He would | not be quiet
THUMB PICTURE: Close-up of his face, eyes shut, one tear, mouth open in a shout, dark painted edge.
Opens: who is this man, and why will he not be quiet? Pays off when the film names the beggar by the Jericho road and Jesus heals him.

**Pair 6**
TITLE: The Blind Man Can See. Why Follow?
THUMB WORDS: This road | led to a cross
THUMB PICTURE: The two walking side by side up the road toward the sunset.
Opens: why follow, once he can see, on a road that leads to a cross? Pays off when he chooses to follow, and Jesus dies and rises.

## 2. Best pair

Pair 2. The title asks the film’s own question in plain words. The thumbnail adds the stake the title leaves out, and the film’s last line pays it off.

## 3. Biggest risk

“On his way to die” can make people think the blind man will die, or that they will see the cross. The death is spoken at the end. The pictures are the stop, the healing, and the walk.
