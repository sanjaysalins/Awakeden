# Bartimaeus: title + thumbnail pairs (blind seat)

## 1. Six pairs

**Pair 1**
- **TITLE:** Why Jesus Stood Still for Blind Bartimaeus *(42 characters)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** THEY SAID | "BE QUIET"
- **THUMB PICTURE:** The beggar on the ground, eyes shut, arms out, shouting. Hands come in from the edges, fingers to lips.
- **Question:** The crowd said hush, so why did Jesus stop? The film pays it off at "And Jesus stood still… He stopped for one man."

**Pair 2**
- **TITLE:** Jesus Heals the Blind Man Everyone Tried to Silence *(51)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** HE SHOUTED | LOUDER
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Bartimaeus mid-shout, mouth wide. A blurred crowd behind him leans in to hush him.
- **Question:** What happens when he won't stop? The film pays it off at "shouted even louder" and then "Jesus stood still."

**Pair 3**
- **TITLE:** On His Way to Die, Jesus Stopped | Bartimaeus *(45)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** FOR ONE | BLIND BEGGAR
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus standing still and sharp inside a frozen, blurred crowd. A small beggar sits at the lower edge.
- **Question:** Why stop for one beggar on the way to death? The film sets this up with "He would be killed there… would He stop?" and pays it off at "stood still for one man."

**Pair 4**
- **TITLE:** Jesus Asked a Blind Man One Question | Bible *(44)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** WHAT DO | YOU WANT?
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Close-up of Bartimaeus's face, eyes shut, a tear, lit by warm light.
- **Question:** What did he ask for, and did he get it? The film pays it off at "that I might receive my sight… At once he could see."

**Pair 5**
- **TITLE:** Bartimaeus Could Go Anywhere. He Chose This. *(44)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** BLIND MAN | SEES
- **THUMB PICTURE:** The two walking side by side up the road toward the sunset. The red cloak is left on the ground.
- **Question:** Where did a healed man choose to go? The film pays it off at "He chose to follow Jesus. Up the road to Jerusalem."

**Pair 6**
- **TITLE:** Jesus Heals Blind Man | Mark 10:46-52 *(36)*
- **THUMB WORDS:** THE CROWD | SAID NO
- **THUMB PICTURE:** A split frame. On one side, eyes shut and hands hushing him. On the other, eyes open in gold light, smiling up.
- **Question:** The crowd said no, so who said yes? The film pays it off with the crowd hushing him, Jesus calling him, and then his eyes opening.

## 2. Best pair: Pair 1

**Why Jesus Stood Still for Blind Bartimaeus** + **THEY SAID | "BE QUIET"**

- It has the top search name in it. "Stood still" is the film's own key line, so the gap is paid in plain words.
- The thumbnail adds the conflict (everyone said hush), and the title adds the surprise (Jesus stopped). Neither repeats the other.

## 3. Biggest risk

"Stood still" is a quiet verb. It may not show the stake (He was on His way to die), so a newcomer might read it as calm, not surprising. Pair 3 carries that stake better but is weaker on search.
