# Seat: Fable — title + thumbnail pairs (Bartimaeus, 79 s short)

## 1. Six pairs

### Pair 1
- **TITLE:** Why Jesus Stopped for a Blind Beggar (36)
- **THUMB WORDS:** THEY SAID | "BE QUIET"
- **THUMB PICTURE:** The beggar on the ground, eyes shut, arms out, mouth wide open shouting, with two hushing hands (finger to lips) pushing in from the edges.
- **Question + payoff:** Who was told to be quiet, and why did Jesus stop anyway? Paid off at "And Jesus stood still... He stopped for one man" and the last line, "On His way to die and rise again, Jesus stood still for one man."

### Pair 2
- **TITLE:** Jesus Was Walking to His Death. Then He Stopped. (49)
- **THUMB WORDS:** FOR ONE | BLIND MAN
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Jesus standing still and sharp in the middle of the blurred, moving crowd, the small beggar with arms out in the bottom corner.
- **Question + payoff:** Why would a man on his way to die stop for one beggar, and what did he do for him? Paid off at "He had told His followers He would be killed there," the music stop, and the healing.

### Pair 3
- **TITLE:** Blind Bartimaeus: The Man Who Would Not Be Quiet (49)
- **THUMB WORDS:** THEN JESUS | STOOD STILL
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Split frame: top, the beggar shouting with eyes shut; bottom, Jesus standing still in the frozen crowd, looking his way.
- **Question + payoff:** What was he shouting, and what happened when Jesus stopped? Paid off at "he shouted even louder" and "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?"

### Pair 4
- **TITLE:** Jesus Heals the Blind Man Nobody Wanted to Hear (47)
- **THUMB WORDS:** HE SHOUTED | LOUDER
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Close-up of the beggar's face, eyes shut, mouth open in a shout, one hushing finger at the edge of the frame.
- **Question + payoff:** Why did nobody want to hear him, and did shouting work? Paid off at "Many told the blind beggar to be quiet" and "At once he could see."

### Pair 5
- **TITLE:** Blind Bartimaeus Could Go Anywhere. He Chose This. (51)
- **THUMB WORDS:** HIS EYES | JUST OPENED
- **THUMB PICTURE:** Close-up of his eyes open, tears, smiling, looking up, gold light on his face.
- **Question + payoff:** What does a man choose in the first minute he can see? Paid off at "He was free to go anywhere. He chose to follow Jesus. Up the road to Jerusalem."

### Pair 6
- **TITLE:** The Blind Man Who Stopped Jesus (31)
- **THUMB WORDS:** A WHOLE CROWD | SAID "SHH"
- **THUMB PICTURE:** The beggar small on the ground with arms out, a wall of hushing faces above him, a dark painted edge around him.
- **Question + payoff:** How does a beggar the crowd is hushing stop anyone? Paid off at "But Bartimaeus shouted even louder... And Jesus stood still."

## 2. Best pair

**Pair 1 — "Why Jesus Stopped for a Blind Beggar" + THEY SAID | "BE QUIET"**

- Short, plain words, and it carries the search words "Jesus" + "blind" without sounding like a lesson title.
- The title gives the surprise (He stopped); the thumbnail gives the fight (everyone said no). Neither repeats the other, and the film's last line answers the "why".

Close second: Pair 2. It has the stronger hook, but weaker search words and a heavier first line.

## 3. Biggest risk

The film shows *that* Jesus stopped on His way to die, but never states a reason in words, so a viewer who wants a spoken "because..." may feel the "Why" is only half answered.
