   **THUMB PICTURE:** A hushing finger beside the shouting beggar; behind them, Jesus has stopped.  
   **Question + payoff:** What happens when Jesus stops for someone the crowd hushes? He calls the beggar and heals him.

3. **TITLE:** Blind Bartimaeus Would Not Give Up  
   **THUMB WORDS:** WOULD JESUS | STOP?  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** The beggar shouts with arms out, surrounded by dark painted edges and people hushing him.

   **THUMB PICTURE:** Close-up of the man’s closed eyes and tear, with Jesus leaning toward him to listen.  
   **Question + payoff:** What does Jesus ask before healing him? He asks what the man wants, and hears his request for sight.

   **THUMB WORDS:** JESUS | CALLED HIM  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** The red cloak flies across the frame as Bartimaeus rises toward Jesus.  
   **Question + payoff:** What happens when he answers that call? He asks to see, and Jesus makes him well.

6. **TITLE:** He Could See. Now What? | Bible  
   **THUMB WORDS:** FREE TO | WALK AWAY  
   **THUMB PICTURE:** His open eyes shine with tears; beyond him, Jesus faces the road toward sunset.  
   **Question + payoff:** Where will he go with his new freedom? He chooses to follow Jesus toward Jerusalem.

**BEST PAIR: 1 — “Jesus Was on His Way to Die | Bible” / “FOR ONE | BLIND MAN”**  
The huge journey and the single beggar create a clear, human question: why stop?  
The film answers through the healing and its final line.

**Biggest risk:** The title may make viewers expect a film mainly about Jesus’ death.
