The new words (170 words, about 65 s)

BARTIMAEUS: "Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
Many in the crowd told the blind beggar to be quiet. Would Jesus even stop?
His name was Bartimaeus, begging by the road near Jericho. "Son of David" meant God's promised King.
But he shouted even louder.
BARTIMAEUS: "Thou Son of David, have mercy on me."
(pause)
"And Jesus stood still." A whole crowd was moving. He stopped for one man, and called for him.
Bartimaeus threw off his cloak and came. Jesus asked him what he wanted:
JESUS: "What wilt thou that I should do unto thee?"
BARTIMAEUS: "Lord, that I might receive my sight."
JESUS: "Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole."
He trusted Jesus, and Jesus made him well. At once he could see.
He was free to go. But he followed Jesus, on the road to Jerusalem. There Jesus would die for our sins, the wrong we have done. On the third day, He would rise to life. On His way to the cross, Jesus stood still for one man.
Yellow = changed.

Why it changed

• 5 of 6: the end felt stuck on, so now the stop and the cross are one idea.
• 4 of 6: "whole" was unclear, so now "Jesus made him well".
• Bible fact: Jesus had others call him, and "many" (not all) said be quiet.

Still weak

• Old English. 4 reviewers want modern speech. I kept it (house rule: KJV word for word). The narrator explains each line.
• About 65 s. A bit over 60.
• 2 reviewers: say early that Jesus was walking to His death. I left this out to keep the hook short.
