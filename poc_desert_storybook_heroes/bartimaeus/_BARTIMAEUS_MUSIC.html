Bartimaeus music: new vs old

NEW: tune (my pick)

Felt piano + cello. One simple tune.

OLD: walking beat

Oud + drum, more texture than tune.
Listen for (NEW):
0-25 s: piano plays the tune 3 times. It never finishes.
26-29 s: music stops. "And Jesus stood still."
49 s: he sees. The cello sings the whole tune, home at last.
61 s: low and quiet at the cross.
76-79 s: last soft chord to the end.
