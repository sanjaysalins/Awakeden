OUTPUT FORMAT (use these headings, keep it tight, under 800 words):
1. SCORE IDEA - one sentence, then the instruments and the motif in plain words.
2. WHY IT FITS a warm desert-storybook painted look AND this story - 2-3 bullets.
3. SCORE CUE SHEET - a table: time range | what the music does | why (tie to the words/pictures).
4. SPLIT - the split second for the two parts and why.
5. MUSIC PROMPTS - the exact ElevenLabs free-text prompts for part 1 and part 2, ready to paste, with "around N seconds in" cues (cue times counted from the START OF THAT PART).
6. MIX NOTES - music level vs voice, where it lifts, where it drops out, the 3 s hold.
7. RISKS - the 3 ways this could go wrong, and how to check each by measurement or by ear.
