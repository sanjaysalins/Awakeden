1. SCORE IDEA

One hummable line, "Mercy," finally finishes only when the film says Jesus stood still for one man. Felt piano carries it, a small warm string group holds the road, and a solo cello sings it at sight and at sunset. A few quiet oud notes and one distant ney breath add Jericho colour. The tune is five notes: D, F, A, a hanging E, then home to D. Until the end we hear only D–F–A–E, a question that will not sit down.

2. WHY IT FITS

- The gouache desert is warm, close, and human. Felt hammers and a cello match that. The oud and ney are a light spice on the road, the way ochre sits beside olive.
- The story is one cry in a moving crowd. A five-note question is small enough for one beggar and clear enough to remember after one listen.
- Sight makes the tune brighter. The cross makes it low. The empty tomb gives the home note once. The sunset sings the whole sentence.
