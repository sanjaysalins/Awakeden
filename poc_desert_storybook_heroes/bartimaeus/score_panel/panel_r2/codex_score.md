## 1. SCORE IDEA

One small, unanswered tune becomes a complete song when Bartimaeus sees—and returns as Jesus’ personal act of mercy at sunset.

Felt piano leads; warm chamber strings support; one soft solo cello sings. A few dry oud notes suggest the road. No percussion, choir or continuous drone.

The motif: **E–G–A–B … C–B–A–G**. Four notes climb and wait; the answering four descend home. Initially shaded toward E minor, finally resting in G major. Approximately 68 bpm, with breathing space.

## 2. WHY IT FITS a warm desert-storybook painted look AND this story

- Felt hammers, bowed wood and occasional plucked oud feel handmade, like the painted surfaces; a small ensemble preserves the picture-book intimacy.
- The unanswered melody carries his longing. Its completion belongs to restored sight; its final return connects that mercy with the cross and resurrection.
- Jesus stopping receives the boldest musical gesture: silence.
