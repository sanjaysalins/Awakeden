# Bartimaeus score - Fable seat (blind)

## 1. SCORE IDEA
A five-note "mercy" tune that is asked as an unfinished question by felt piano, stops dead when Jesus stops, and is finally sung whole by a solo cello when the blind man sees.

- **Instruments:** felt piano (lead), solo cello (the singer), small warm string section (pad, then swell), one oud (soft plucked road pulse), one ney (two short breaths only).
- **Key / pace:** D major, 4/4, 72 BPM, a gentle walking pace.
- **Motif, "the Question":** five notes climbing, D-E-F#-A-B, the rhythm of "have mer-cy on me". It hangs on the B and never comes home.
- **"The Answer":** the same climb, then it steps back down, A-F#-E-D, and lands home. Question + Answer = the whole hummable tune. We hold the Answer back until 49 s.

## 2. WHY IT FITS
- Felt piano and a singing cello are soft, hand-made sounds, like gouache. Oud and ney give sand and sun without turning it into a "Bible epic".
- The story IS a question ("Would He stop for a beggar?"). The tune asks it three times and gets no answer until the eyes open. Viewers feel the answer before they think it.
- The walking pulse is the road. It stops only when Jesus stops, so the music does what the words say.

## 6. MIX NOTES
- Voice is king. Music sits about 16-18 dB under the voice whenever anyone speaks.
- Dialogue 38.8-48.9: pull 3 dB lower still.
- Lifts: 49-54 s (to about 10 dB under voice) and 69.4-76 s (12 dB under).
- Drops out: 25.6-26.6 full silence; 26.6-33.7 barely there; 68.4-69.4 a held breath.

- Duck by measured voice timings, not a generic sidechain setting.

## 7. RISKS
1. **Cues land at the wrong second** (the lift misses 49 s). Check: plot loudness per second for each part against the cue sheet; slide or trim the part by up to 1 s, or regenerate.
2. **No real tune, or part 2's tune differs from part 1's.** Check by ear: hum the Question after one listen of part 1, then see if the cello at 49 s finishes that same climb. If not, regenerate part 2 only.
3. **Music is not silent/thin where it must be, or masks the voice.** Check: measure level at 25.6-26.6 and 26.6-33.7 (force with a volume automation if needed); measure voice-minus-music gap in every spoken line, timeline sweep, not just the average.
