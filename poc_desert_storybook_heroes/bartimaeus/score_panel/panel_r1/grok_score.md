1. SCORE IDEA
A single four-note “mercy cry” motif (rising minor third, then a held plea) that freezes into silence when Jesus stops, then returns as clear sight and footsteps up the road. Instruments: dry frame drum and soft hand percussion for the road; oud and low duduk for Bartimaeus’s cry; sparse plucked lyre when Jesus speaks; one warm cello line that only fully blooms when his eyes open; no choir, no trailer brass.

2. WHY IT FITS
- Ochre/sand palette wants dry wood, skin drums, and reed — instruments that sound like dust and heat, not a Hollywood orchestra.
- The motif is the cry itself: short enough a child remembers it, tied to “have mercy,” then flipped into peace when sight comes.
- Storybook gouache needs space and soft edges; the score leaves air around every voice and paints one clear arc: plea → hush → stop → sight → follow → cross → still for one man.

4. SPLIT
Split at **26.58 s** (start of “And Jesus stood still”). Part 1 = cry, road, second shout, and the silence before the stop (0–26.58). Part 2 = stillness through sight, follow, cross, rise, bookend (26.58–79.17 ≈ 52.6 s). Why: the story’s hinge is the freeze; joining on the silence/near-silence avoids a seam in loud music and keeps each generation shorter so ElevenLabs does not insert dead air mid-phrase.

6. MIX NOTES
Music always under voice: aim −18 to −14 LUFS relative to dialogue peaks; never mask Bartimaeus’s shouts or Jesus’s low line. Lift slightly (still under) at 0–3 s, 20.8–25.6 s, 48.9–56 s, and 69.4–76 s. Drop nearly out at 25.6–26.6 and 26.6–33.7 (“And Jesus stood still”), and again 68.4–69.4. In the 3 s hold (76.2–79.17): motif echo + soft drone only, no new melody, fade to dust before black.

7. RISKS
1. Dead air or loop mush in a long gen — check by waveform: Part 1 and Part 2 must show continuous energy except the two planned near-silences; if a flat zero appears mid-phrase, regenerate that part.
2. Motif buried or too trailer-loud — A/B at 26.6–33.7 and 48.9–54.8 with voice soloed: if “stood still” or “he could see” is hard to hear, lower music 2–3 dB.
3. Join click at 26.58 — listen on headphones at the splice with 0.5 s crossfade on the silence; if a bump remains, nudge split ±100 ms into the quietest sample.
