## 1. SCORE IDEA

Mercy interrupts the road: an unfinished musical cry becomes a settled walking song when Bartimaeus sees and follows.

**Instruments:** gut-strung lyre, low breathy reed flute, occasional fingertip frame drum; intimate, woody, softly imperfect.

**Motif:** three notes—low, reaching upward, stepping slightly down, still waiting: **D–G–F**. At sight, add the final **D**, coming home. The same melody accompanies his following, the cross, and the sunset.

## 2. WHY IT FITS a warm desert-storybook painted look AND this story

- Plucked strings and breath have the grain of painted paper; small sounds leave room for dusty light and human faces.
- A restrained walking pulse connects Jericho to Jerusalem. Its disappearance makes Jesus stopping the film’s strongest musical event.
- Healing completes the beggar’s melody. Carrying it toward the cross keeps mercy and sacrifice within one story.
