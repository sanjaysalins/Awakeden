## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
A viewer I will call Amara, 24, in Lagos, on a phone, never read a Bible. She stops at the card "WHO WROTE THE MOCKERY?" (curious, but the card has no context) and at line 1 (a man dying; people shout). She likely finishes: it is short and the story is clear. She shares only if the "same words, thousand years apart" idea hits her (line 5). NOT served: someone who wants to know why Jesus died, who David is, or what "forgive" has to do with mocking. Also not served: a skeptic who needs proof.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"Jesus stayed on the cross by choice, and you can turn to Him." Lines 7 and 11 carry it. It was only half clear. I understood "He could have come down but stayed" (7). But "turn to the One who stayed" (11) is vague: turn how? Why do I need to? Nothing in the film says what He stayed FOR (my sins? me?). The ancient-song link (3-5, 9-10) is a second takeaway that competes with it.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Jesus, God's Son, dying on a cross. A king David wrote a song long ago. Crowds mock both. What: the mockers' words match the song. Why: I guess it shows the song predicted this, and Jesus chose to stay and forgave them.
Lost or misled:
- Line 3 "People laugh at him too. He writes it into a song." Who laughs at David? Why? Unclear.
- Line 5 "People passing by shout one more thing." Fine, but "passing by" is odd.
- Line 7 "no one takes His life from Him. He gives His life Himself." Where did that come from? Nobody said it in the film. Sounds like a claim with no source.
- Line 9 "That same song heard the insults a thousand years early." A song cannot hear. Confusing. "It also says what comes next" - what comes next after what?
- Line 10 "ends of the world," "remember and turn unto the LORD" - old English. "Turn unto" and "LORD" in capitals are strange to me.
- Line 2/4 "deliver" is hard; the card DELIVER = RESCUE helped, but only once.
- Line 8 "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do." Forgive who? For what? Why does a dying man pray for those mocking him? It appears suddenly.
Words that fail in translation: "deliver," "reviled," "scorn" (not used), "turn unto," "ends of the world," "mock."

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. What I was TOLD and could not follow:
- Line 3 "a thousand years" - I cannot check it; I take it on trust.
- Line 7 "Jesus had said that no one takes His life from Him" - told as fact, no verse shown; I cannot follow where or when.
- Line 9 "people from every nation will turn to God" - I cannot see how that fits the Psalm line; I just accept.
- Why the Psalm is about Jesus at all: the film says "almost the same words" but I cannot tell if David meant Jesus or himself.
- Who "the LORD" is versus "God" versus "Father": same person? Unexplained.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would anchor one simple idea and show it, not stack four (mockery match, choice to stay, forgiveness, nations). It would say in plain words why staying matters ("He stayed so you could be forgiven"). The ONE highest-value change: state in plain words, before line 11, WHAT His staying did for me, so "turn to Him" has a reason. Score: 6/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 0 (card "WHO WROTE THE MOCKERY?"), answered partly at line 5 (same words, a thousand years apart). A second gap opens at line 7 ("He could have come down... so He stayed"), paid off weakly at 11. Score: 6/10. Worth the wait, but the card's question is answered with "David", never stated as "David wrote it" clearly.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 11: replace with "He stayed for you. You can turn to Him."
2. Line 9: replace with "David's song also says what comes after: people from every land will turn to God."
3. Line 3: replace "People laugh at him too. He writes it into a song." with "People laughed at David too. He wrote about it in a song."
