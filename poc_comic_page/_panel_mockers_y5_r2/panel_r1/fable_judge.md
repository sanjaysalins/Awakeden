## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Named viewer: a 24-year-old scroller in Manila, English second language, knows "Jesus died on a cross," has never heard of Psalm 22. She stops on the card "WHOSE WORDS ARE THESE?" and the sneering face. She finishes because line 2 and line 4 are a puzzle that snaps shut at line 5 (~25 s). The sharer is the lifelong Bible reader who already loves Psalm 22 and sees it shown plainly. NOT served: the newcomer who gets the match at line 5 and is never told why it matters — the film changes subject to "come down" at line 6 and the Psalm is never mentioned again.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"They dared Him to come down and prove He was God's Son; He stayed on purpose, to give His life for the very people mocking Him." Carried by line 9. Clear — yes, for the second half. But the film has two spines: the opening card promises a Psalm 22 answer (lines 2-5) and the landing delivers a "stayed" answer (lines 6-10). The title question is never answered out loud.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Jesus on a cross, religious leaders and passers-by mocking, King David 1,000 years before, a Roman soldier. What: the mockers' words match words David wrote down; they dare Him down; He stays and prays for them; the soldier believes. Why: He stayed to give His life for them.
Lost at line 5: "Almost the same words, a thousand years apart." — then nothing. A newcomer asks "so?" and the film has already moved on. Line 1 "priests and elders": "elders" reads as "old people" in many languages. Line 7 "He gives it Himself." is odd English; ESL ears can hear "gives it to Himself." Line 8 is tagged JESUS on screen but read by the narrator, while the mockers get their own voice — the one speaker who does not get a voice is Jesus. "Deliver" is handled well by the DELIVER = RESCUE card. "Know not" (line 8) is archaic but short and survives.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- L1 priests and elders mock — RIGHT (Matt 27:41 context). "shout" — mild stretch; text says "said."
- L2 — RIGHT, KJV verbatim, honest truncation.
- L3 David, Psalm 22, enemies laughed — RIGHT (Ps 22:7 "laugh me to scorn").
- L4 — RIGHT, verbatim Ps 22:8a.
- L5 "almost the same words" — RIGHT.
- L6 — RIGHT, verbatim, correctly given to passers-by (27:39-40).
- L7 "no one takes His life… He gives it Himself. So He stayed." — RIGHT, John 10:18; "so He stayed" is a fair inference.
- L8 — RIGHT, verbatim Luke 23:34.
- L9 "to give His life for them" — RIGHT by inference (Matt 20:28 "ransom for many" + Luke 23:34); not verbatim anywhere, but not overclaimed.
- L10 "A Roman soldier… watching Jesus" — RIGHT (the centurion; "soldier" is a fair simplification). The earthquake trigger is dropped; darkness is shown. Fine.
Nothing WRONG. Nothing UNSUPPORTED.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would close the loop it opened. The mockers demanded "let God rescue him," and the film never says why God did not. Matt 20:28 (supplied) is the answer sitting unused: He came to give His life as a ransom — a rescue price. One sentence joins both spines: the rescue they demanded for Him is the rescue He was paying for them. That turns the DELIVER = RESCUE card from a vocabulary note into the punchline. ONE highest-value change: add that sentence at line 9. Score: 6/10 — plain words, every KJV quote paraphrased, an earned "stayed" landing; but two threads, one dropped.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at the card + line 1 "Listen." Pays off at line 5 (~25 s) — a match with no meaning attached. A second gap opens at line 6 ("come down") and pays off at lines 9-10; that one lands. Score 6/10. The first wait is short but under-rewarded; the second is worth it.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 5 → "Almost the same words, a thousand years apart. Their words were already in the Bible before they said them."
2. Line 9 → "They said, come down. He did not come down. He died there. They said, let God rescue him. He stayed and paid the rescue price Himself — His life, for them."
3. Line 1 → "Jesus, who says He is God's Son, is dying on a cross. The religious leaders mock Him. Listen."
