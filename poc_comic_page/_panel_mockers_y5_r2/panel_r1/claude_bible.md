**Note:** The lens text and the evidence pack don't match. The KJV I was given covers Exodus 1-19 and Numbers 13-14, but the film is Matt 27, Psalm 22 and Luke 23. I checked every claim against the KJV supplied inside the pack. The Exodus and Numbers verses are not relevant to this film.

## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Maria, 34, a churchgoer who half-knows the crucifixion story. She stops at "WHOSE WORDS ARE THESE?", finishes for the David-and-Jesus twist, and shares it with her small group. It doesn't serve a viewer who has never heard of Jesus or who wants a deeper proof of prophecy. Nothing says what a "psalm" is or why a 1,000-year-old song matters.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"The mockers at the cross repeated David's words, yet Jesus stayed on purpose." Lines 5, 7 and 9 carry it. It is mostly clear, but the film never says outright that this is prophecy. A newcomer can read it as coincidence.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Jesus, the mockers, David, a soldier. What: the same words appear 1,000 years apart. Why: it was planned, and He chose to stay.
- I got lost at line 4. A "david" voice says "He trusted on the LORD…", and a listener may think David is the one trusting. The speakers are David's enemies.
- "Deliver" is handled by the card. "Reviled", "ransom" and "wagging" are not used, which is good.
- "A song, Psalm 22" is fine, but "psalm" is a church word.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- L1 "The priests and elders shout at Him": RIGHT. Matt 27:41 says this, but it is outside the supplied extract.
- L2 "He trusted in God; let him deliver him now": RIGHT, Matt 27:43. It is trimmed after "now", dropping "if he will have him: for he said, I am the Son of God".
- L3 "Enemies laughed at him too": RIGHT, Ps 22:7 "laugh me to scorn".
- L3 "a thousand years": UNSUPPORTED in the supplied text. It is a fair approximation, but the KJV doesn't say it.
- L3 "he wrote down what they said": RIGHT in substance. The psalm's heading ("A Psalm of David") isn't supplied.
- L4 "He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him": RIGHT, verbatim, Ps 22:8 (trimmed).
- L5 "Almost the same words": RIGHT.
- L5 "People passing by also shout": OVERCLAIMED. Matt 27:39 says "reviled him, wagging their heads", not shouting. The head-wagging also matches Ps 22:7 "shake the head", and the film misses that link.
- L6 "If thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross": RIGHT, Matt 27:40.
- L7 "no one takes His life from Him. He gives it Himself": RIGHT, John 10:18.
- L7 "So He stayed": OVERCLAIMED. It is a fair inference from John 10:18 and Matt 20:28, but the supplied text never gives this as His reason for staying. It is stated as flat fact.
- L8 "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do": RIGHT, Luke 23:34. "Also prayed" is fine.
- L9 "He stayed to give His life for them": mostly RIGHT, Matt 20:28. That verse says "for many", so "for them" narrows it a little.
- L10 "A Roman soldier… said": OVERCLAIMED. Matt 27:54 says "the centurion, and they that were with him… saying", so it is plural. "Roman" is inferred, not stated. The quote itself, "Truly this was the Son of God", is RIGHT.
- L10 also omits why they feared: "the earthquake, and those things that were done".

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
A top explainer would say it out loud: "They didn't know it, but they were repeating David's song." The film never says prophecy or fulfilled. The hook then pays off cleanly. **Score: 6.5/10.** The structure and the twist are strong. The "so what" is implied rather than stated, and the ending is rushed.

## CURIOSITY GAP
It opens at the card ("WHOSE WORDS ARE THESE?") and L2. It pays off at L4-L5. **7/10.** Worth the wait, but the payoff is a quiet "almost the same" and not a clear answer. The L10 soldier echo of "Son of God" is a good second payoff.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **L5:** "Almost the same words, a thousand years apart. They did not know it. They were saying David's song." Then: "People passing by shook their heads and mocked."
2. **L10:** "The officer in charge, and the soldiers with him, saw the earthquake. They were afraid and said: 'Truly this was the Son of God.'"
3. **L7:** "So He stayed. By His own choice." This replaces the bare "So He stayed". It stops the line sounding like a bare claim and matches John 10:18.
