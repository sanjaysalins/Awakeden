## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)

Religious leaders challenge a dying man to prove His identity; their words resemble an older song; He accepts death and prays for forgiveness (lines 1–9).

“Enemies laughed at him too” makes David’s specific experience sound established, although the supplied psalm does not establish that biography (line 3). “He gives it Himself” is an awkward explanation of voluntarily surrendering His life (line 7). “Deliver,” “thou,” and “LORD” add decoding work (lines 2, 4, 6). The rescue card helps, but arrives after viewers first need it (lines 2–3).
