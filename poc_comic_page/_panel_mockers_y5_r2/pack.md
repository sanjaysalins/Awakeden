# FILM (vertical Bible short, ~73 s, motion-comic: painted stills with moving camera, speech balloons, narrator voice + 2 quote voices)
Opening card on screen (0.2-2.8 s): "WHOSE WORDS ARE THESE?"
Card at ~12 s: "DELIVER = RESCUE"
On-screen: speech balloons show each quoted Bible line with its reference tag (MATT 27:43, PSALM 22:8, MATT 27:40, JESUS LUKE 23:34, A SOLDIER MATT 27:54).
Visuals in order: cross at dusk with leaders pointing up; leader sneering close; old king David writing by lamp; psalm scroll by lamp; passers-by shaking heads; crowd pointing up at the cross; Jesus on the cross, calm; Jesus looking down over the crowd; darkness over the cross; people under the cross shadow.

# SPOKEN SCRIPT (numbered)
1 [narrator] Jesus, who says He is God's Son, is dying on a cross. The priests and elders shout at Him. Listen.
2 [mocker] "He trusted in God; let him deliver him now."
3 [narrator] In plain words: he trusted God, so let God rescue him. Now go back a thousand years, to King David. Enemies laughed at him too. In a song, Psalm 22, he wrote down what they said.
4 [david] "He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him."
5 [narrator] Almost the same words, a thousand years apart. People passing by also shout.
6 [mocker] "If thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross."
7 [narrator] If you are God's Son, come down. Jesus had said that no one takes His life from Him. He gives it Himself. So He stayed. And on the cross, He also prayed:
8 [narrator] "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do."
9 [narrator] They said, come down. He did not come down. He died there. He stayed to give His life for them.
10 [narrator] A Roman soldier, who stood watching Jesus, said: "Truly this was the Son of God."
