# EVIDENCE PACK: a 76-second YouTube Short, 'The Mockers' Words'. Viewers: global, many non-native English, mixed Bible knowledge.

## Narration (the whole film, spoken):
[narrator] Jesus, who says He is God's Son, is dying on a cross. The religious leaders shout at Him. Listen.
[mocker] "He trusted in God; let him deliver him now" [Matthew 27:43]
[narrator] In plain words: he trusted God, so let God rescue him. Now go back a thousand years, to King David. Enemies laughed at him too. In a song, Psalm 22, he wrote down what they said.
[david] "He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him" [Psalm 22:8]
[narrator] Almost the same words, a thousand years apart. Their words were already in the Bible before they said them. People passing by also shout.
[mocker] "If thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross" [Matthew 27:40]
[narrator] If you are God's Son, come down. Jesus had said that no one takes His life from Him. He gives it Himself. He stayed. And on the cross, He also prayed:
[narrator] "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do." [Luke 23:34]
[narrator] They said, come down. He did not come down. He died there. He came to give His life for many.
[narrator] A Roman officer, and the men with him, felt the earthquake and said: "Truly this was the Son of God." [Matthew 27:54]
