# FILM-ONLY PACK (what the viewer hears and sees)
Format: a 78-second vertical (9:16) Bible short for YouTube Shorts / TikTok, narrated, comic-style pictures with big on-screen question card at the start. Audience: worldwide.
First on-screen card (first 2.6 seconds): WHO SCRIPTED THE MOCKERY?
Title shown on upload: The Mockers' Words (Psalm 22:7-8)
Pictures (described, not seen by you): a crowd as dark silhouettes, an old king writing by lamplight, an open scroll, a man mocking and pointing up, a cross at dusk, a calm figure on the cross, many people walking toward a hill at dawn.
On-screen text: the quoted Bible lines appear in speech balloons as they are spoken; one card shows DELIVER = RESCUE.
Runtime: 78 seconds.

## EVERYTHING THE VIEWER HEARS (voices: narrator; david for the Psalm; mocker for the Matthew taunts)
1. The crowd mocking Jesus at the cross was reading from a script — they just didn't know it.
2. A thousand years before, Psalm twenty-two recorded how the Messiah would be mocked — the gestures, and the words.
3. "...they shake the head, saying, He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him: let him deliver him..." At the cross, Matthew's gospel records it — the passers-by wagging their heads, the rulers sneering nearly line for line: "He trusted in God; let him deliver him now, if he will have him: for he said, I am the Son of God." They thought they were inventing the cruelty.
4. They were reciting prophecy — unwilling witnesses that this was the One.
5. And they threw one more taunt at Him: "save thyself.
6. If thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross." But He could have come down.
7. He showed He was the Son not by escaping the cross, but by staying on it — bearing the scorn He could have silenced, for the very people throwing it.
8. The man they mocked is the One the whole song was about.
9. They told the King to come down — and He never did.
10. He stayed under scorn — theirs, and ours — to win the scorners.
11. And they were reading from a script they never finished: the song ends with all the ends of the world turning to Him.
12. Turn, and come to the One who would not come down.
