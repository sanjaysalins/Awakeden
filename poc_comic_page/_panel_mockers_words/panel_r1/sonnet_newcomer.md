## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Named viewer: Amara, 24, in Lagos, on a phone, curious, has heard "Jesus" but never read the Bible. She stops at the question card "WHO SCRIPTED THE MOCKERY?" and the line 1 hook ("reading from a script"). She finishes if she can follow. A Bible reader or churchgoer shares it for the "script they never finished" idea (11). NOT served: a newcomer who needs to know who Jesus is, what a cross is, what "Messiah" and "prophecy" mean (2, 4). Also not served: anyone who wants calm pacing; the quote blocks in 3 are dense.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"The people who mocked Jesus were unknowingly repeating words written 1,000 years earlier, and He stayed on the cross anyway, so turn to Him." Carried by lines 1, 4, 9, 12. Partly clear. I get "script" and "stayed". I do not get why staying matters or what "turn" means.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: a crowd mocking a man, Jesus, on a cross. What: an old song predicted their words. Why: so I should "come to" him. My guess only.
Lost or misled:
- Line 2 "the Messiah": never explained. Is Messiah = Jesus? I guessed so.
- Line 3: very long, two quote layers (Psalm then Matthew), hard to hear on a phone. "deliver him" is only explained by the DELIVER = RESCUE card. Good, but it comes late.
- Line 3 "Matthew's gospel": what is a gospel? Who is Matthew?
- Line 5-6: "save thyself" and "thou" are old English. Line 5 opens a quote but has no closing, so it sounds cut off.
- Line 6 "But He could have come down." Why could He? Unexplained.
- Line 7 "bearing the scorn He could have silenced": abstract.
- Line 8 "the whole song": which song? I only learn it is a Psalm from the title.
- Line 10 "scorners": rare word.
- Line 11 "all the ends of the world turning to Him": "turning" is vague.
- Line 12 "come to the One": come where? Do what?
Fail in translation: script, wagging, sneering, unwilling witnesses, scorn, scorners, turn.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
A top explainer would give one plain setup in the first 10 seconds: "Jesus, a man many call God's Son, was killed on a cross. Watching crowds mocked him." Then show one matched pair, old line next to new line, not two long quotes. Cut line 3 down hard. ONE highest-value change: replace the long line 3 with a short side-by-side of one phrase ("he trusted... let him deliver him") and say who Jesus is once. Score: 5/10. Strong hook, weak on-ramp for a newcomer.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1 (and the card). Pays off at lines 3-4, when the match is shown. Score 6/10. Worth the wait, but 3 is too dense to feel the click on a phone. The second gap, "a script they never finished" (11), is better and pays off in 12, but I do not feel what "turn" asks of me.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 2: replace "how the Messiah would be mocked" with "how God's promised Savior would be mocked".
2. Line 5-6: replace with: "Then they shouted: 'Save yourself! If you are God's Son, come down from the cross.'" Plain words, one closed quote.
3. Line 12: replace "Turn, and come to the One who would not come down." with "Trust the One who stayed on the cross for you."
