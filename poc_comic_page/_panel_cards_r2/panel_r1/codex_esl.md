## VERDICT (REVISE)

Several glosses work. The weakest wording confuses a word’s general meaning with its meaning in the verse. The pack omits the narrated passages, so some verse placements remain inferred.

## LINE-BY-LINE

- **5 — CHANGE:** `TARRY = STAY`. In “Tarry ye here, and watch with me,” Jesus asks them to remain there; “wait” suggests waiting for something to happen.
- **5 — CHANGE hook:** `COULD YOU STAY AWAKE?` “TOO WEAK TO PRAY?” can suggest physical weakness prevents prayer; “could ye not watch with me one hour?” concerns keeping watch.
- **7 — CHANGE, if the phrase is Psalm 31:5:** `REDEEMED = RESCUED`. In “thou hast redeemed me, O LORD God of truth,” “bought back” suggests a literal purchase and obscures deliverance; the missing narrated passage prevents an unconditional ruling.
- **11 — CHANGE hook:** `EVER FEEL ABANDONED BY GOD?` “Why hast thou forsaken me?” concerns abandonment; “forgotten” substitutes a different idea.
- **12 — CHANGE:** `POTSHERD = PIECE OF A BROKEN POT`. In “My strength is dried up like a potsherd,” “broken pot piece” is an awkward noun stack for learners.
- **17 — CHANGE hook:** `WHAT WAS / THE SIGN OF JONAH?` In “there shall no sign be given to it, but the sign of the prophet Jonas,” the restriction concerns that generation; “ONE SIGN ONLY” sounds universal.
- **19 — CHANGE hook:** `TOO LATE / TO BE SAVED?` Beside “To day shalt thou be with me in paradise,” “TOO LATE FOR GOD?” can mean God missed his opportunity.
- **20 — CHANGE:** `BEHOLD THY SON = HERE IS YOUR SON`. In “Woman, behold thy son!” the whole phrase presents a relationship; isolated “LOOK” risks reducing it to an instruction to glance.

## BIGGEST RISK

**REDEEMED = BOUGHT BACK** makes an explanatory image look like an exact contextual equivalent. Second-language readers may understand a financial transaction where the verse describes rescue. Verify the actual narrated phrase before retaining it.

The pack also gives no verse placement for several cards. Titles alone cannot establish contextual accuracy.

## WHAT IS GOOD (keep)

These glosses are clear and accurate on the corresponding phrases:

- **2–3:** `BEHELD = LOOKED AT` — “when he beheld the serpent of brass.”
- **4:** `EVEN SO = IN THE SAME WAY`; `WHOSOEVER = ANYONE WHO` — “even so must the Son of man be lifted up”; “whosoever believeth in him.”
- **6:** `RAIMENT = CLOTHES` — “they parted his raiment.”
- **7:** `COMMEND = ENTRUST` — “into thy hands I commend my spirit.”
- **9:** `GAVE UP THE GHOST = DIED` — “he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost.”
- **11:** `FORSAKEN = ABANDONED` — “why hast thou forsaken me?”
- **12:** `CLEAVETH = STICKS` — “my tongue cleaveth to my jaws.”
- **17:** `JONAS = JONAH` — “the prophet Jonas.”
- **18:** `SEPULCHRE = TOMB` — “they entered into the sepulchre.”
- **19:** `VERILY = TRULY` — “Verily I say unto thee.”
