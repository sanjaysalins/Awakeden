## VERDICT (REVISE)

REVISE. Three best: 1, 6, 15. Three weakest: 7, 13, 16.

Several openers match the passage and can ship. The weak set invents an event, aims a feeling at the wrong person, or fights the plain-word card on the same line. Shame and fear are doing the stopping.

## LINE-BY-LINE

4. CHANGE → LIFTED UP LIKE THE SNAKE — "Even so" only works after the serpent film; a cold scroll has no comparison.
5. CHANGE → ONE HOUR. THEY SLEPT. — The scene is the disciples asleep while he prays. The card scolds the viewer.
7. CHANGE → HE ENTRUSTED HIS SPIRIT — This line glosses COMMEND as ENTRUST. "AFRAID TO DIE?" is a fear hook on a trust cry.
11. CHANGE → MY GOD, WHY LEAVE ME? — This line glosses FORSAKEN as ABANDONED. "Forgotten" is a softer, different claim.
13. CHANGE → A RED CORD MARKED WHO LIVED — Joshua spares Rahab’s house. The book never says the cord survived the rubble.
16. CHANGE → A CRUSHER WAS PROMISED — The piece is the promise inside the curse. "CHRIST WON" states a finished fight, then asks what is left.
18. CHANGE → THE BODY WAS NOT THERE — "The Empty Tomb" is a label a thumb has already learned to skip.
19. CHANGE → PARADISE. THAT SAME DAY. — The sting is "today." "TOO LATE FOR GOD?" is a shame teaser and aims the lateness at God.
20. CHANGE → HE GAVE HIS MOTHER A SON — He provides for Mary. "YOUR PAIN" swaps her for the viewer.

## BIGGEST RISK

The feelings hooks will be the ones that travel: afraid, forgotten, too weak, too late, your pain. They stop a scroll and teach a different scene from the KJV line. The pack already contradicts itself. Line 7 cannot be both "entrust" and "afraid to die." Line 11 cannot be both "abandoned" and "forgotten." Line 13 adds a survival Joshua does not record. Line 16 announces a win inside a film titled "Promised."

Also fine as they stand: 2 Look and Live, 8 He Was Asleep in the Storm, 12 WHY DID JESUS SAY I THIRST? John 19:28 is a real "why," and the potsherd and cleaveth cards are the dry-mouth text. Leave this set alone.

Copy from the best three. 1 gives two facts, then the real question. 6 lets the prayer be the shock. 15 uses the verse’s own body-parts and can be read in a second.
