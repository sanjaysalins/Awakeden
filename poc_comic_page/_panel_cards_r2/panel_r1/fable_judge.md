## VERDICT: REVISE

Most lines hold. But the batch has one systematic hole (the Genesis 3:15 trio carries no plain-word cards at all), one false friend left uncarded in a title word, two plain-side glosses that are not plain, and three hooks that are generic or off-text. None is fatal; all are cheap to fix before burn-in.

## LINE-BY-LINE (not OK)

- **1** CHANGE (only if the narration quotes Lev 16:21-22): add `FIT MAN = A MAN CHOSEN FOR IT`; `INIQUITIES = SINS` — "fit man" is a false friend (modern = physically fit). "(none)" here is unverified, not verified.
- **5** CHANGE: add `WATCH = STAY AWAKE`; change `TARRY = WAIT` to `TARRY = STAY` — "watch" is the title word and a global viewer hears "watch a video"; "tarry ye here" = "stay here", not "wait here".
- **7** CHANGE hook to `HIS LAST BREATH / WAS A PRAYER` — "AFRAID TO DIE?" is a fear hook that could front any death piece; the text gives a better one. CHANGE `COMMEND = ENTRUST` to `COMMEND = HAND OVER` — "entrust" is as hard as "commend" for an ESL viewer; "hand over" echoes "into thy hands".
- **8** CHANGE (only if Mark 4:38 is quoted): add `CAREST THOU NOT = DON'T YOU CARE` — same unverified "(none)" problem as 1.
- **10** CHANGE: add `BEHELD = LOOKED AT` if Num 21:9 is spoken — same passage as 2 and 3, which both carry it; the pack itself proves the inconsistency.
- **11** CHANGE hook to `DID GOD / ABANDON JESUS?` — the hook says "forgotten", the card says "FORSAKEN = ABANDONED"; two different words for one idea inside 60s, and "forgotten" is the weaker, less accurate one.
- **12** CHANGE `CLEAVETH = STICKS` to `CLEAVETH = STICKS TO` — on a card, bare "STICKS" reads as a noun (wooden sticks). CHANGE `BROKEN POT PIECE` to `PIECE OF BROKEN POT` — natural word order. Add quote marks: `SAY "I THIRST"?`.
- **14** CHANGE: add `ENMITY = HATRED`; `SEED = CHILD` — Gen 3:15 has the two most opaque words in the whole batch and gets no card.
- **15** CHANGE: add `BRUISE = CRUSH` — modern "bruise" sounds minor; the piece's whole point is a blow to the head vs a blow to the heel.
- **16** CHANGE hook to `CHRIST WON. / WHY ISN'T IT OVER?` — "WHAT'S STILL TO COME?" is vague; the real gap (Rom 16:20 "shortly") is why Satan is still here. Also: the pack shows a curly apostrophe (U+2019) in "WHAT’S" — confirm the burn-in font has that glyph or use a straight one, or it renders as a box.
- **20** CHANGE hook to `DYING. / HE STILL SAW HIS MOTHER.` — "DOES JESUS SEE YOUR PAIN?" is the only hook in the set not drawn from its own text; it could front any piece. CHANGE `BEHOLD = LOOK` to `BEHOLD = HERE IS` — "Woman, look your son" is broken English; "Woman, here is your son" is the plain meaning.

### Top 5 changes
1. Card the Gen 3:15 trio (14, 15, 16): ENMITY, SEED, BRUISE.
2. #5: `WATCH = STAY AWAKE` — the biggest false friend, and it is in the title.
3. #7: `COMMEND = HAND OVER`; hook from the text, not from fear.
4. #20: text-grounded hook; `BEHOLD = HERE IS`.
5. #11 hook/card word alignment; #12 `STICKS TO`.

## BIGGEST RISK

The plain side of a plain-word card must itself be plain, and must read correctly as a two-word fragment on screen. ENTRUST, bare STICKS, and LOOK (for "behold thy son") each fail one of those tests. Second risk: cluster-01-cross runs five pieces back to back and four of the five hooks end in "?" — a subscriber watching the cluster will feel the template. The replacements for 7 and 20 (statements) break the run.

## WHAT IS GOOD (keep)

- Hooks that come straight from the text: `HE PRAYED FOR HIS KILLERS`, `WHAT WAS / FINISHED?`, `TOO LATE FOR GOD?`, `A PROMISE / INSIDE THE CURSE?`, `WHOSE HEAD? / WHOSE HEEL?`, `ONE SIGN ONLY. / WHICH ONE?` — short, honest, each opens a gap the piece can pay off.
- Glosses that are exactly right: `GAVE UP THE GHOST = DIED` (kills the spooky-ghost misread), `REDEEMED = BOUGHT BACK`, `WHOSOEVER = ANYONE WHO`, `JONAS = JONAH`, `SEPULCHRE = TOMB`, `VERILY = TRULY`, `RAIMENT = CLOTHES`, `FORSAKEN = ABANDONED`.
- Discipline of at most two plain-word cards per 60s. Hold that line when adding the Gen 3:15 cards — pick the two that the narration actually leans on.
