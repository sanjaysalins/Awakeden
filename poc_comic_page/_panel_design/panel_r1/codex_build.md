## VERDICT (PASS / REVISE / FAIL) and score 0-10

**FAIL — 3/10.** ffmpeg + PIL can render these effects. The proposal does not establish that they compose reliably, fit the timetable, or repeat at the stated cost. Its own example would fail its own gates.

The four-day estimate bundles a timeline engine, text layout, transitions, asset preparation and validators without defining their shared timing contract. Episode 2 becomes the integration test after paid assets arrive.

## BIGGEST RISK (quote the line)

> “Every new episode fills in episode.json. The build reads it. Nothing else to hand-edit.”

The file cannot yet express the promised film consistently. It mixes word times, display times and single timestamps without precedence rules. It omits chained-balloon sequencing and moving mouth anchors.

Concrete failures:

- The first Jesus balloon contains **11 words**, exceeding eight.
- The seven-word hook receives **1.8s**, versus its own **2.95s** minimum.
- The five-word thought needs **2.45s**, longer than its entire two-second panel.
- KNOCK references `p1b` at 9.1s; that panel ends at 2.6s.
- Literal whole-verse equality rejects legitimate balloon fragments. Fragment matching alone permits omissions, duplication and reordering.
- The mandatory gospel break-out conflicts with “no break-out on a sacred panel” unless a permitted destination is specified.

These are unresolved contracts, not polishing tasks.

## WHAT TO CUT OR SIMPLIFY

Cut Page Turn and figure Break-Out from the first build. Their masking, perspective and compositing work buys no evidence of repeatability.

Start with Ink Wake, hard cuts and anchored zooms. Limit Panel Walk to a fixed two-panel template.

Drop the compulsory “real clip” requirement. Camera-only footage, line-boil and a breathing lamp can satisfy pixel-change tests without delivering meaningful action. Requiring purchased clips everywhere adds expense without proving the desired effect.

Stop calling the build “$0”: that describes cash expenditure, while concealing implementation and per-episode preparation time.

## WHAT IS MISSING

A canonical timeline with frame-rate rounding, transition overlaps, audio ownership and computed text occupancy.

A layout contract covering phone safe areas, balloon overflow, tails and face occlusion. A fixed mouth anchor cannot reliably follow a moving clip.

Defined gate measurements. “~5%” colour change needs a colour space and protected sampling regions; otherwise intentional grading trips it. Pixel change is defeated by grain. The recipe permits two text objects; the design permits one layer. Neither defines whether verse tags count.

A consistent budget: $9–10 clip spend, ~$11 animation, and $22–30 totals have no reconciled ledger.

The order also contradicts itself: review precedes locking, but follows building the machine.
