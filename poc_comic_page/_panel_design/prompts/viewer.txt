 1 THE ONE IDEA 
 The comic is being drawn while you watch. When the ink dries, the panel wakes up. Every move, every word, lands on the voice. 
 Pencil, then ink, then colour, then alive. Fast. One big panel at a time. The reviewers' favourite thing in the old film (pencil to ink) becomes the whole language, not a one-off.
 Three candidates. Ranked.
 1 A · WET INK easy-medium
 pencil → ink → colour → alive
 What it is. Each panel is drawn in under half a second, then the real clip plays. A pen-scratch sound sells it. 
 Cost. $0. Pencil pass + a diagonal "ink front" over the clip's first frame (we already have blue-line 's wobbled front). About 1–2 build days. 
 Risk. If it is slow it becomes a tic and the film drags again. Rule: 0.5s max, 0.8s only for Jesus.
 2 B · BREAK THE GUTTER medium
 light, then the figure, cross the border
 What it is. Panel borders are real. At the turn, light spills across the gutter, then the figure steps over the border. 
 Cost. $0 but needs a cutout per break-out (rembg; one already exists). About 2 build days. 
 Risk. A cutout from a still over a moving clip can drift. Best as ONE move at the pivot, not the whole language.
 3 C · PAGE CAMERA easy
 the camera walks down a real page
 What it is. One big printed page. The camera is the reader's eye, moving panel to panel. 
 Cost. $0, one build day (crop + pan over a composed page). 
 Risk. Every motion-comic channel does this. It is close to what scored 4.75/10 for "alive". Keep it as a move, not the idea.
 Pick: A. Wet Ink is the signature. B becomes one move (the pivot). C becomes one move (the walk).
 Where this agrees with the specs, and where it departs
 Keeps MOTIONCOMIC_SPEC: camera-only clips, ink held (MC-R2). Scripture gets its own look (MC-R4). "Clear a hole" before the verse (MC-R5). No SFX on sacred beats. 
 Keeps COMIC DNA: fonts Bangers / Kalam / Permanent Marker. Christ dignified, one face. Dots masked off faces, now capped at 4%. 
 Keeps panel_animator names: blue-line front, line-boil, impact-burst, typography-panel, ink-transition, print-grade last. 
 Departs from COMIC_PAGE_PIPELINE rev 2.1 "no speech bubbles". The owner now asks for dialogue. Balloons return, with a verbatim gate and an "invented" flag. 
 Departs from DNA "Christ speech never in a balloon". It IS a balloon now, but a distinct one: gold rule + verse tag. Never an ordinary balloon. 
 Departs from DNA "Remotion engine". This build is ffmpeg + PIL, $0, the tools we have. 
 Departs from 1–4 panels per page. One big panel at a time. A grid only as a rare page reveal.
 2 THE MOVES 
 Eight moves. Each reads in one second on a phone. At rest: one big panel, always a real clip.
 1 · INK WAKE easy-med
 Use: how most panels enter. Hook at 0.5s. Jesus at 0.8s, the only slow one. Beat: any new panel. Build: pencil pass (exists) + wobbled ink front ( blue-line ) + the clip.
 2 · GUTTER CUT easy
 Use: the normal cut. Two black frames (the gutter) and a tiny page nudge. Beat: every 3–4s, so something new is always on screen. Build: ffmpeg concat + 2 black frames + 1 offset frame.
 3 · PANEL WALK easy
 Use: the camera travels across a real page from one panel to the next. Beat: cause → effect ("the record" → "keeps you at the door"). Build: crop-pan over a 2-panel composite ( grid-choreography primitives).
 4 · BREAK-OUT medium
 Use: once per episode. Light crosses the gutter first, then the figure breaks the border. Beat: the gospel pivot. Build: rembg cutout + layered PIL composite over a held frame.
 5 · ZOOM-THROUGH easy-med
 Use: push into a detail in one panel, and it becomes the next panel. Beat: "your record" → the scroll. Build: zoompan to an anchor + 0.3s crossfade.
 6 · CARD SLAM easy
 AFRAID GOD WILL TURN YOU AWAY?
 OLD WORD IN NO WISE = NEVER
 Use: the question card at 0.8s. The OLD = PLAIN decode after any hard KJV word. Beat: hook, decode, landing line. Build: typography-panel + a new card style. White + gold, Bangers.
 7 · PAGE TURN medium
 Use: once. The whole page leaves. The next act is underneath. Beat: the act turn (fear → His words). Build: PIL perspective warp per frame. Doable, not free of effort. Could ship later.
 8 · SFX HIT easy
 KNOCK
 Use: a real sound only (knock, creak, wind). Max 2 per episode. Never on Jesus or the cross. Beat: the hand on the door. Build: impact-burst + Bangers lettering, timed to the sound file.
 At rest a panel is a real Kling/Seedance clip. On top, $0 layers only: line-boil , a breathing lamp ( candle-only ), the ink front. No still is ever left half-drawn on screen: the pencil stage always completes.
 3 DIALOGUE SYSTEM 
 Five text shapes. One on screen at a time. Shape = who is speaking.
 Rules
 Jesus
 His own KJV words. Verbatim. Never changed. 
 Ivory fill. Ink rule + inner gold rule. Verse tag under the corner. 
 Long verse → chained balloons, in order, each ≤ 8 words. 
 His own voice. The narrator never reads His line. 
 A hard old word gets the decode card right after.
 The human
 Spoken: white fill, ink rule. Thought: grey dashed cloud, italic. 
 Invented lines: max 2 per episode, max 6 words. 
 Each one is flagged "invented": true in the file, with a reason. Doctrine review sees the list. 
 An invented line may only say what the narration already says. No new claims.
 Narrator, SFX, cards
 Narrator = the parchment band. Top-left. Bold plain sans. 
 SFX = Bangers, angled, stroked. Real sounds only. 
 Cards = white + gold. The hook question. The OLD = PLAIN decode. The landing. 
 No halftone dots over any text. Ever.
 How a balloon pops
 The balloon pops from its tail on the first spoken word (from alignment.json ). 0.18s, tiny overshoot. It stays while the words are said, then leaves 0.4s after the last word. The next text waits 0.2s.

 Max words: 8 per balloon. 6 for a thought. 7 for a card line. 
 Min time on screen: 1.2s + 0.25s per word. 
 Tail: aims at the mouth anchor stored in the file. Speaker off-panel → tail points at the frame edge nearest them. 
 One text layer: a balloon replaces the caption. Never both.

 6 THE RISKS
 Could look cheap
 Too many reveals. The ink wake becomes a tic. Rule: 0.5s, and not on every cut. 
 A CSS wipe is smooth. An ffmpeg wipe can band. Use the wobbled front at 30fps, not a straight line. 
 The thought cloud can look childish. Keep it small, grey, dashed. 
 Halftone over faces. Mask faces, cap at 4%, or drop the dots.
 Hard or impossible in ffmpeg
 A true 3D page turn. Needs PIL per-frame warp. Medium effort, so it ships last. 
 A figure really stepping across panels. The break-out here is a still cutout or light. Honest limit. 
 A cutout over a moving clip drifts. Break-out only on a held frame, ≤ 0.8s.
 Needs paid animation
 Any acting: Jesus turning, the man stepping through the door. 
 Lips moving for dialogue. We do NOT do this. Balloons + voice carry it. Lip-sync on ink art reads wrong.
 Doctrine and faces
 Invented lines must stay inside what the narration already says. Flagged, ≤ 2, panel-reviewed. 
 Jesus must be ONE man in every panel, including the landing. That is a stills problem (ref chaining), not a motion problem. Still the top risk. 
 A wrong word in His balloon is a FAIL, not a note.
