## VERDICT: REVISE

Sound set, no doctrine error inside any card. But one hook contradicts the KJV line the narrator then reads (#15), one hook is not a sentence (#16), two hooks are flat (#12, #14), four hooks share one "TOO ___?" template, and the plain-word cards spend attention on guessable words (YE, LOOKETH, TAKETH) while skipping the words that actually block a newcomer (WATCH, ENMITY, SEED, CAST LOTS).

Top 5 changes, in order:
1. #15 hook -> "HEEL OR HEAD: / WHICH BRUISE KILLS?" KJV says "bruise" twice; "crushed" is another Bible's word.
2. Card the blocking words: #5 WATCH = STAY AWAKE; #14/#15 ENMITY = HATE, SEED = CHILD; #6 CAST LOTS = THREW DICE. Cut YE, LOOKETH, TAKETH (visible stem, teach nothing).
3. #16 hook -> "CHRIST WON. / SO WHY THE WAIT?"
4. Break the "TOO ___?" template (#5, #6, #17, #19; three of them post together in the cross cluster): re-cut #6 and #17, keep #5 and #19.
5. #7 COMMEND = HAND OVER (ENTRUST is not plain); re-cut #12 and #14 hooks (below).

## LINE-BY-LINE

1. CHANGE: if Lev 16 is quoted, add INIQUITIES = WRONGS; a Leviticus piece with zero cards is the odd one out.
2. CUT "LOOKETH = LOOKS": the stem is visible, the card teaches nothing; keep BEHELD = LOOKED AT.
3. CUT "BRASS = BRONZE": brass is living English; one metal for another is a title patch, not a plain word. Keep BEHELD.
5. CHANGE: add WATCH = STAY AWAKE; moderns hear "watch" as "look at", and both title and hook lean on it.
6. CHANGE hook -> "FORGIVEN WHILE / THEY KILLED HIM?" (breaks the TOO template, sharper gap); add CAST LOTS = THREW DICE if spoken.
7. CHANGE "COMMEND = ENTRUST" -> "COMMEND = HAND OVER": entrust is a register-up word, not a plain one.
8. CUT "YE = YOU": the best-known KJV word; if "carest thou not" is spoken, card that instead: CAREST THOU NOT = DON'T YOU CARE.
10. CHANGE: same passage as #2/#3 yet zero cards; add BEHELD = LOOKED AT if Num 21:9 is quoted.
12. CHANGE hook -> "WHY WOULD GOD / BE THIRSTY?": the current hook restates the title as a question, no gap. If kept, put quote marks round "I THIRST".
13. CHANGE "TAKETH = TAKES" -> "SCARLET = RED" (if spoken): title says "red", KJV says "scarlet"; scarlet is the opaque word, taketh is guessable.
14. CHANGE hook -> "A PROMISE / INSIDE THE CURSE?": "their" has no referent at second 2 and "sentence" reads as grammar to ESL ears; add ENMITY = HATE, SEED = CHILD.
15. CHANGE hook -> "HEEL OR HEAD: / WHICH BRUISE KILLS?": KJV reads "bruise... bruise"; "crushed" is NIV, so the narrator will contradict the card. Add ENMITY/SEED as #14.
16. CHANGE hook -> "CHRIST WON. / SO WHY THE WAIT?": "why still future?" has no verb and "if" doubts the win; add SHORTLY = SOON if Rom 16:20 is quoted.
17. CHANGE hook -> "ONLY ONE SIGN. / WHICH ONE?": fourth "TOO ___?" in the set, and the text's own gap (Matt 12:39) is sharper.
20. CHANGE hook -> "DOES JESUS SEE YOUR PAIN?": "HE" has no referent at second 2 for a newcomer.

## BIGGEST RISK

#15. The hook promises "crushed", then the narrator reads "bruise" twice. A Bible reader sees a card outranking the text; a newcomer hears two stories in ten seconds. Trust in every later card drops.

Second: the "TOO ___?" formula. #5, #6, #19 go out together in the cross cluster; a viewer who meets two in a row sees a template, and a templated question stops being a question.

## WHAT IS GOOD (keep)

- REDEEMED = BOUGHT BACK (#7), SEPULCHRE = TOMB (#18), JONAS = JONAH (#17), CLEAVETH = STICKS and POTSHERD = BROKEN POT (#12), GAVE UP THE GHOST = DIED (#9), WHOSOEVER = ANYONE WHO and EVEN SO = IN THE SAME WAY (#4), TARRY = WAIT (#5), VERILY = TRULY (#19), RAIMENT = CLOTHES (#6), FORSAKEN = ABANDONED (#11): correct, short, and each removes a real misreading (ghost, cleave, Jonas especially).
- Hooks #5 (TOO WEAK TO PRAY?), #9 (WHAT WAS FINISHED?), #11 (EVER FEEL GOD LEFT YOU?), #19 (TOO LATE FOR GOD?): plain words, real gap, and the text pays each one.
- The rule "no hook card when a title card already exists" is right; two cards in two seconds is noise.
