## VERDICT (REVISE)

Most swaps are sound, but several are doctrinally loose or lose a load-bearing meaning, and a few hooks overreach.

## LINE-BY-LINE

**2 – CHANGE.** LOOKETH = LOOKS is fine. BEHELD = LOOKED AT is weak. In Num 21:9, "beheld" means looked with attention and trust. The whole piece is about faith-look. Use **BEHELD = LOOKED (AND LIVED)** or cut the card. Mere "looked at" can read as a glance.

**3 – CHANGE.** BRASS = BRONZE is OK as a modern-English swap. The Hebrew nechoshet is copper or bronze, so "brass" is a loose term. Same flat BEHELD issue as line 2: use **BEHELD = LOOKED**, and keep the card the same as in line 2.

**4 – CHANGE.** EVEN SO = IN THE SAME WAY is true to John 3:14. WHOSOEVER = ANYONE WHO is true. But this is the pivot of the gospel, and "must" carries the divine necessity (dei). Add or swap to **MUST = HAD TO BE (GOD'S PLAN)**. A card that only reflows two easy words and skips "must" misses the point. Keep both existing cards if there are only two slots.

**5 – CHANGE.** TARRY = WAIT is true for Matt 26:38 ("tarry ye here"). But the title is "Could Ye Not Watch." The hard word is **WATCH = STAY AWAKE**. Modern "watch" reads as "view." Use **WATCH = STAY AWAKE**. Hook: **TOO WEAK TO PRAY?** is fine, but it implies the verse blames weakness. Matt 26:41 says "the flesh is weak" and also commends a willing spirit. Acceptable. Soften to **TOO WEAK TO PRAY? (HE KNEW)**, or keep as is.

**6 – CHANGE.** RAIMENT = CLOTHES is true, but the hook is the problem. TOO GUILTY FOR FORGIVENESS? frames the verse as a answer to the viewer's guilt. In Luke 23:34 the forgiveness request is for "them," the executioners, and "they know not what they do." Without that it risks implying unconditional forgiveness for all. Use **CAN GOD FORGIVE THIS?** or **WHO DID HE FORGIVE?** The raiment card is the least important thing in this verse; **cut it** and use a card for "KNOW NOT" or none.

**7 – CHANGE.** REDEEMED = BOUGHT BACK is acceptable for Ps 31:5 (padah, ransomed). COMMEND = ENTRUST is true. The hook AFRAID / TO DIE? is OK, but it needs care: Luke 23:46 is a trust statement. Use **AFRAID TO DIE? HE TRUSTED.** or keep it. Note that "redeemed" in Ps 31:5 is not a purchase from a seller, so "bought back" imports a transaction. Prefer **REDEEMED = RESCUED**.

**8 – OK.** YE = YOU is trivial and safe. Mild waste of a card, but not wrong.

**9 – CHANGE.** GAVE UP THE GHOST = DIED is true. However, "it is finished" is the key. The hook WHAT WAS FINISHED? is good. Keep. Consider **GAVE UP THE GHOST = DIED (BY CHOICE)** only if the narration says so; otherwise leave it.

**11 – CHANGE.** FORSAKEN = ABANDONED is true to azab. But the hook EVER FEEL GOD LEFT YOU? is the risk. It implies the verse says God does leave believers, or validates the feeling as fact. Ps 22:1 is a cry, and Christ's own forsakenness was real for Him. Use **EVER FEEL FORGOTTEN BY GOD?** or **WHAT DID HIS CRY MEAN?** Better: **WHY DID JESUS CRY THIS?**

**12 – CHANGE.** POTSHERD = BROKEN POT is true (Ps 22:15, "broken piece of pottery"). CLEAVETH = STICKS is true. Hook WHY DID JESUS SAY I THIRST? is fine for John 19:28, but Ps 22:15 is the verse for the cards, so make sure the piece names John 19. Keep.

**13 – OK.** TAKETH = TAKES is fine but trivial.

**14 – CHANGE.** WHAT CAME BEFORE THEIR SENTENCE? is vague and legally misleading. Genesis 3:15 is spoken to the serpent, in the curse. Use **THE PROMISE INSIDE THE CURSE?**

**15 – CHANGE.** WHO GETS BRUISED? WHO GETS CRUSHED? is good, but "crushed" is KJV "bruise" for both, so the hook adds a distinction the verse blurs. Use **WHO GETS HURT? WHO GETS DEFEATED?**

**16 – CHANGE.** IF CHRIST WON, WHY STILL FUTURE? is a doctrinal trap. It assumes Gen 3:15 is still future, but the verse is promise, and it was fulfilled in Christ, with the final end still ahead (Rom 16:20). Use **CHRIST WON. WHY IS THE FIGHT NOT OVER?**

**17 – CHANGE.** JONAS = JONAH is true for Matt 12:39. Hook FALLEN TOO FAR FOR GOD? is OK but not what the sign is about. The sign is the resurrection. Use **WHAT IS THE ONE SIGN?**

**18 – OK.** SEPULCHRE = TOMB.

**19 – CHANGE.** VERILY = TRULY is acceptable, but Jesus' "Verily" is a solemn "Amen." Use **VERILY = I TELL YOU THE TRUTH**. TOO LATE FOR GOD? is an acceptable hook.

**20 – CHANGE.** BEHOLD = LOOK is flat. In John 19:26–27 it is a charge, not a sightseeing request. Use **BEHOLD = SEE (HERE IS)**. Hook DOES HE SEE YOUR PAIN? implies the verse speaks to the viewer's pain; reword to **WHO DID JESUS CARE FOR AT THE CROSS?**

## BIGGEST RISK

Lines 6, 11 and 16: the hooks turn the verse into a promise or a theological claim it does not make.

## WHAT IS GOOD (keep)

- WHOSOEVER = ANYONE WHO (4)
- SEPULCHRE = TOMB (18)
- POTSHERD and CLEAVETH cards (12)
- FORSAKEN = ABANDONED (11)
- WHAT WAS FINISHED? (9)
- TOO LATE FOR GOD? (19)
