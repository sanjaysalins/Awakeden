## 1. FIRST IMPRESSION
Hat: first-time viewer, phone, English is my second language.

I see an old man in a hood with a scroll, touching a big closed door at night (f_0.3.png). Big words: "AFRAID GOD WILL TURN YOU AWAY?" The picture is strong. A hand on a door means "I want to go in but I am scared." I get that without words. Yes, I would stop for a second. The question speaks to me.

Later a man in a white robe with long hair and a beard appears. He opens his arms (f_61.7.png). I do not know who he is. I think he is a kind teacher or a holy man. He is not named in any frame I saw. The 4 and 5 verse text ("JOHN 6:37") means nothing to me.

Overall: warm, safe, calm, serious. It feels like a church book or a school story, not like a fun feed video.

## 2. SCORES 0-10
- Scroll-stopping in 1 second: 7. The big hand and door with the question (f_0.3.png) is clear and fast. Dark brown/blue is a bit dull next to bright feeds.
- Reverent and trustworthy: 8. Calm, gentle, no tricks.
- Global appeal (any culture, ESL): 6. The door story reads anywhere. But "John 6:37", "All that the Father giveth me" (f_21.0.png) is old English and confusing. The white-robe man is a Western-looking Jesus; fine for many, foreign to some.
- Consistent shot to shot: 8. Same man, same door, same colours. Jesus face varies a little (f_21.0 vs f_52.0 vs f_61.7; the last one looks more cartoon).
- Feels alive (motion): 5. From the frames it looks like still pictures. f_44.5.png and f_46.0.png look almost the same, so little changes.
- Readable on a phone: 6. Big white/yellow title is great. The small caption boxes (f_9.0.png, f_30.0.png) are in a thin small font on beige, and the many small panels (f_30.0, f_44.5) have tiny details.

## 3. WEAKEST THREE
1. f_21.0.png, f_30.0.png, f_44.5.png: three or four small panels at once. On a phone I do not know where to look. The scroll with seal and the empty scroll panel do not say anything to me. Confusing.
2. f_21.0.png: bottom caption "All that the Father giveth me shall come to me;" in old English, dark red on beige. Hard for ESL, and "Father" is unclear (whose father?).
3. f_1.8.png and f_30.0.png: pale beige pencil-sketch panel next to a rich coloured panel. It looks unfinished or like a mistake, and the caption box ("Somewhere in you is the quiet fear") covers the picture. Also the dotted texture over faces (f_61.7.png) makes skin look dirty/plastic on a small screen.

## 4. VS THE FEED
Different, and in a good way. Most Bible shorts I might see are glossy realistic AI or 3D-cartoon. This has a hand-made ink look, so it feels more human and less "AI slop". But it is less bright and less exciting than anime or 3D. It feels slower, more like a printed book. Good for trust, less good for thumbs that move fast.

## 5. BEST ALTERNATIVE
In _contact_strip.png, the first (black-outline cartoon, bright gold door glow) and the third (bold clean cartoon colour, round 1) are easiest to read at a glance. In _contact_strip_round2.png, picture 3 (soft glowing arch, Jesus glowing) is the clearest and warmest. The watercolour (round 1, #4) feels gentle but weak on a phone. The black and white ink ones (round 2, #4 and #5) look serious but cold and old. I think the chosen look is already about as good as these. Round 2 #1 (bright light burst behind Jesus) would stop the thumb faster for the same cost class, but less reverent.
