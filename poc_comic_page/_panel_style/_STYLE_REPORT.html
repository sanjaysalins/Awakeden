1. The big find: a green cast (4 of 4 seats)

I measured it. It is real, and I found the cause. The "print-grade halftone" step turns beige paper olive-green. Paper colour with it off: 196, 181, 141. With it on: 159, 169, 104.

This tool is shared. Other pieces that used halftone may have the same green. Not yet checked.
