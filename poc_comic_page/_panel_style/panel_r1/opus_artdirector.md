# Seat: short-video art director (Opus)

## 1. FIRST IMPRESSION
A well-drawn, warm "graphic novel Bible" piece aimed at faith viewers 25-55. The inking is solid and the night-blue/lamp-gold idea is right. But my gut in the feed: **it reads murky**. A sick olive-green cast sits over the whole film (the paper margins in f_0.3, f_25.5, f_36.5 are pea-green, not parchment), and the first frame is a tiny man seen from far above. I would slow down for the big hook text, not for the picture. Half-stop, not full stop.

## 2. SCORES
- **Scroll-stopping in 1 s: 4/10.** The hook text works; the image (small distant figure + faded pencil half) does not.
- **Reverent and trustworthy: 7/10.** Calm, dignified, no gore. The final grinning, arms-up Jesus (f_58.5) tips toward clip-art.
- **Global appeal (any culture, ESL): 7/10.** Comic language is universal, and "IN NO WISE = NEVER" (f_25.5) is a great ESL bridge. Small italic serif captions hurt.
- **Consistent shot to shot: 5/10.** The old man holds up; Jesus changes face and render each time: painterly-photoreal (f_16.0), soft (f_25.5), cartoon grin (f_58.5).
- **Feels alive (motion): 4/10.** f_0.3 vs f_1.5 and f_44.5 vs f_52.0 are almost the same picture. That is about 7 s of near-still image in the payoff stretch.
- **Readable on a phone: 6/10.** The big bold cards are good. The cream caption boxes in thin italic serif (f_5.5, f_36.5) are too small and low-contrast at phone size.

## 3. WEAKEST THREE VISUAL THINGS
1. **The olive/yellow-green grade (all frames, worst in f_0.3, f_25.5, f_36.5).** It turns "night blue + warm gold" into swampy khaki. Gold needs neutral or cool surroundings to glow. Here everything is gold, so nothing glows.
2. **The hook frame f_0.3.** Bird's-eye wide shot, a figure about 5% of the frame, and an unfinished olive pencil panel under the text. There is no face, no eyes and no hand in the first second, so nothing human to stop a thumb.
3. **Jesus is not one character, and he is often a pasted cut-out.** Compare f_16.0, f_25.5 (glow outline floats over the panel border like a sticker) and f_58.5 (wide grin, cartoon proportions, left hand cut by the frame). The landing image is the least reverent and least "drawn" frame in the film. Also f_21.0 stacks three text layers (subtitle, JOHN 6:37, KJV strip) on top of art: too busy.

## 4. VS THE FEED
Better than the sea of glossy AI oil-painting Jesus and stiff 3D. It looks authored, and comic panels are native to phone scrolling. Worse than the top cinematic-realism and anime shorts on **energy**: those cut every 1-2 s and push in on faces. This film breathes slowly on wide shots. Different, and it could be a real lane, but right now the colour grade makes it look dated rather than intentional.

## 5. BEST ALTERNATIVE
Keep this comic family; do not switch to watercolour (strip 1, #4) or the B&W engravings (strip 2, #4-5), which read too quiet in the feed. The best one in the strips is **strip 2, #1 (cool blue stone + warm burst through the door)**: same ink, but true blue-vs-gold contrast and a clear light source. That is what this film should look like after a regrade. The goldseam cross proof (goldseam_cross_final.png) proves the line can hold a real deep blue. The film lost it.

## 6. ONE BIGGEST CHANGE + small ones
- **BIGGEST: regrade the whole film to neutral parchment + true night blue, gold only on the lamp, the door light and Jesus.** Kill the green cast. *Costs nothing (colour grade).*
- **Open on a close-up**: the hand on the door (it exists in f_5.5 bottom panel) or the man's face, fully inked, in frame 1. The wide rooftop shot comes second. *Costs nothing (re-edit), unless a new face close-up is wanted (costs money, one image).*
- **Captions**: swap the thin italic serif for a bold hand-lettered comic sans-style face at about 1.5x size, dark ink on bright paper. Drop one of the three text layers in f_21.0. *Costs nothing.*
- **Fix the landing**: re-render f_58.5 with the locked Jesus face from f_16.0/f_21.0, a gentle open-hand welcome (not a grin), the whole figure inside the arch. Push-in or a light bloom so the last 5 s move. *Costs money (one new still + one clip).*

## 7. KEEP
Bold black ink line. The pencil-to-ink reveal (a real signature, just never on frame 1). The old man's design. The scroll/record motif (f_30.0, f_44.5). Big two-colour hook cards (white + gold), and "IN NO WISE = NEVER" as a pattern for every archaic phrase. The AWAKEDEN mark, small, top-left.
