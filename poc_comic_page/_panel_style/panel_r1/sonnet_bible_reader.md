## 1. FIRST IMPRESSION
A night-blue and gold painted comic: an old man with a scroll stands at a locked door, afraid. Then Jesus appears and says "I will in no wise cast out" (John 6:37). It is for believers and for the quietly guilty. I would stop on f_0.3.png: the lit door and the big question "AFRAID GOD WILL TURN YOU AWAY?" is a clear hook. My gut: it is warm and trustworthy, a lot better than the usual AI Bible slop. But Jesus looks too much like a modern model. The last frame (f_58.5/f_61.7) is a grinning, arms-wide "welcome!" pose, and that makes me wince.

## 2. SCORES 0-10
- Scroll-stopping in 1 second: **7**. The glowing door and the huge question work (f_0.3.png). The olive-green half-empty sketch panel below looks unfinished.
- Reverent and trustworthy: **7**. The John 6:37 card is calm and respectful (f_21.0.png, f_25.5.png). The final grinning pose and the glow outline lower it.
- Global appeal (any culture, ESL): **8**. Door, scroll and light are universal. Big white-and-gold caps are easy to read.
- Consistency shot to shot: **8**. The same Jesus, same old man and same palette throughout. The green sketch panels and the glowing cut-out outline vary a little.
- Feels alive (motion): **6**. The pencil-to-ink reveal is a good idea, but the pairs f_52.0/f_58.5 and f_0.3/f_1.5 are almost identical, so there is little movement.
- Readable on a phone: **7**. The big caps are excellent. The small italic caption strips (f_16.0.png) are small, and the John 6:37 verse card (f_21.0.png) has tiny red type, with three text layers at once.

## 3. WEAKEST THREE
1. **f_58.5.png / f_61.7.png (Jesus, arms wide, grinning).** Too cheerful and too cartoonish, like a product ad. Church viewers may find it flippant. The yellow glow outline looks like a sticker cut-out. Compare the calmer faces in f_16.0.png and f_25.5.png.
2. **f_25.5.png, f_30.0.png (unfinished sketch panels).** The pale green wireframe panels and the muddy olive-green wash read as "not finished" and dull the night-blue and gold. The "THUD!" burst in f_36.5.png is a comic gag in a solemn moment.
3. **f_21.0.png (text clutter).** The caption, subtitle, verse card and a "JOHN 6:37" label all fight for space on top of the art, and the verse is tiny. Also f_44.5.png: the colour grade turns skin and walls sickly yellow-green.

## 4. VS THE FEED
Different and better than oil-painting AI (which is generic and glossy) and Pixar 3D (which is cute but cheap on Jesus). It is closer to a graphic novel, which feels more honest. It is not as dramatic as cinematic realism. The pencil reveal and panel slams are fresh for the Bible genre. The main risk is that Jesus and the final pose look like stock "Christian clip-art".

## 5. BEST ALTERNATIVE
This style is already the strongest overall. Within the strips, the dark ink-and-gold look in round 2 (3rd and 4th pictures) has more gravity, and the 2nd in round 1 has better sunset colour. The goldseam_cross_final.png frame shows the best reverence: a heavy ink line, a calm face and no grin. Use that restraint everywhere. I would not switch to watercolour (round 1, 4th picture): it is pretty but too soft for a phone.

## 6. BIGGEST CHANGE
**Big:** Redraw the final Jesus (f_58.5) as a quiet, steady, open-handed figure at the door, with a soft smile like the one in f_25.5.png, no wide grin and no cut-out glow. *Costs money (new picture).*
Small:
1. Fix the colour grade so the sketch panels and skin lose the green cast, with a true warm paper and deeper blue. *Free.*
2. Remove "THUD!" and any extra panels that sit blank, and give the verse card more room and larger text. *Free.*
3. Add more slow motion (a candle flicker, a drifting push-in) in the near-still frames. *Costs money, or free if done as a camera move.*

## 7. KEEP
The door-and-light metaphor, the night-blue and gold palette, the bold black ink line, the huge white/gold caps hook, the pencil-to-ink reveal, one consistent Jesus and old man, and the John 6:37 quote card.
