# Fable seat — visual style judgement (blind, whole-world audience)

## 1. FIRST IMPRESSION
A painted-comic Bible short, bold ink, night scene, a man afraid at a door, Jesus answers. For anyone, not just churchgoers. Would I stop? The hook card on f_0.3 ("AFRAID GOD WILL / TURN YOU AWAY?") would hold me for a second; the picture under it would not. The whole film sits under a muddy olive-khaki wash. The brief says "night blue and warm gold" and the proofs (goldseam_cross_final.png, goldseam_resurrection_heroshot.png) actually deliver that: clean blue, cream, warm rim. The film does not. Blue + gold has been mixed into green. Jesus' white robe reads khaki (f_16.0). The "paper" between panels is sage green, not paper. On a bright phone feed this reads dim, old and small.

## 2. SCORES
- Scroll-stopping in 1s: **5** — the text card works, the dim olive courtyard with a tiny figure does not (f_0.3).
- Reverent and trustworthy: **7** — sober ink faces, respectful; the yellow sticker-glow around Jesus (f_58.5) and the red "THUD!" (f_36.5) chip at it.
- Global appeal (any culture, ESL): **6** — comic grammar is universal and "IN NO WISE = NEVER" (f_25.5) is exactly right; italic-serif captions and the nailed-scroll panel (f_36.5) need Bible knowledge to read.
- Consistent shot to shot: **5** — Jesus has a dark-haired rendered face in f_16.0 and a lighter, rounder cartoon face in f_58.5; palette drifts night-olive → mustard daylight (f_44.5) → sunset (f_36.5 top-right).
- Feels alive: **3** — same two panels from f_0.3 to f_9.0 (9 s); f_44.5 and f_52.0 are near-identical (7.5 s). Only the pencil→ink reveal and slow push-ins move.
- Readable on phone: **5** — hook/verse cards strong; captions are small italic serif that jump position (top-left f_0.3, middle f_5.5, bottom f_9.0, right f_16.0); f_21.0 stacks verse card + caption strip + logo at once.

## 3. WEAKEST THREE
1. **f_44.5 / f_52.0** — the emotional turn (Jesus' hand on the man's shoulder) and Jesus' head is cropped off the top of the frame. No face, no caption, and the frame does not change for 7.5 s.
2. **Olive cast on every frame** — compare f_16.0 to goldseam_cross_final.png. Whites are khaki, blues are green, paper is green. The chosen look is not what shipped.
3. **f_58.5 / f_61.7** — the landing hero is a different, more "Sunday-school" Jesus than f_16.0, with a cut-out yellow outline that reads as an AI sticker. The most important frame is the least consistent one.
Also: f_21.0 text pile-up; f_36.5 red "THUD!" clashes in tone and a scroll tied to a nail is unreadable to a newcomer.

## 4. VS THE FEED
Oil-painting AI is now wallpaper on Bible feeds; bold ink reads as "made," not generated — an advantage if the ink is crisp and the colour is bright. Live-action (The Chosen clips) wins on faces; we cannot compete there, so we must win on graphic punch. 3D/Pixar and anime are brighter, cut faster, text bigger — our film is dimmer and 3x slower. Cinematic realism is blue/orange; ours looks like a dim copy of it. Motion-graphics: our hook cards already borrow this well. Verdict: **different and potentially better lane, currently executed dimmer and slower than every rival.**

## 5. BEST ALTERNATIVE
**This look is the right one** — but the proofs, not the film. goldseam_cross_final.png is the target: deep blue night, cream paper, black ink, gold only on light sources. Runner-ups if the lane were ever changed: round-2 D (black-and-white noir ink, single flame) is the most scroll-stopping and most "comic," but cold for a grace landing; round-1 E (teal/orange flat graphic) has the highest feed contrast. Neither beats a correctly graded goldseam.

## 6. CHANGES
**Biggest: re-grade the whole film to the proofs** — pull the green out, blue shadows, cream page, clean white robe, gold held for lamp/rim. *Costs nothing.*
1. No page held longer than ~4 s without a new visual event; at the key beat let one panel take over full-screen (as f_58.5 does). Re-crop existing art. *Costs nothing.*
2. One text layer at a time; captions in the same bold sans as the hook cards, ~1.3x bigger, fixed lower-third. *Costs nothing.*
3. Re-crop f_44.5 so Jesus' face is in frame (*free*); re-render the f_58.5 hero with the f_16.0 Jesus reference and no glow outline (*costs money: one still + one animation*).

## 7. KEEP
- Hook/verse cards: white + gold condensed sans on dark bars (f_0.3, f_25.5, f_58.5). Best thing in the film.
- Pencil→ink reveal (f_16.0 → f_21.0). Unique, visibly hand-made.
- "IN NO WISE = NEVER" explainer card.
- Bold black ink line + gold rim-light as in the proofs.
- Closing shape: one full-bleed panel, Jesus arms open in the arch, then hold.
- The oil-lamp-in-niche motif.
