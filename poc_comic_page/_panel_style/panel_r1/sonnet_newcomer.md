# Newcomer seat (never opened a Bible, English is my 2nd language, phone, Asia/Africa/LatAm)

## 1. FIRST IMPRESSION
It looks like a graphic novel. A tired old man with a scroll stands at a wooden door at night. A kind man in white appears. Big text asks "AFRAID GOD WILL TURN YOU AWAY?" (f_0.3.png). I would pause, because the question is about me. The picture alone says: "a worried man outside a door, a gentle man helps him." It is warm and safe. It is not very new, though. I have seen many "Jesus in white robe" images. I do not know who the white-robe man is until the text says "Jesus" (f_16.0.png). I would stay about 3 seconds, not more, unless the voice pulls me in.

## 2. SCORES
- Scroll-stopping in 1 second: 6. The pencil sketch plus a big question is odd and curious. The painted top panel is dim and green-yellow.
- Reverent and trustworthy: 7. Calm, kind face. The glowing outline on Jesus (f_52.0.png, f_58.5.png) looks a bit like a game character.
- Global appeal (any culture, ESL): 5. Jesus is a white-looking European man. The old man looks Italian. Many viewers will feel "this is a western religion." The text "in no wise = NEVER" (f_25.5.png) helps, but "cometh", "giveth" are hard English.
- Consistent shot to shot: 8. Same man, same door, same colours. Jesus drifts a little (face is younger in f_58.5 than f_21.0).
- Feels alive (motion): 6. The sketch turning into ink is a nice idea. I cannot judge real motion from stills. f_44.5 and f_52.0 look almost the same, so it feels static there.
- Readable on a phone: 6. Big white/yellow caps are very clear. The italic paper-strip captions are small and thin (f_5.5, f_16.0). Some captions sit over faces or busy art (f_16.0 over Jesus's arm).

## 3. WEAKEST THREE
1. **f_16.0.png, f_21.0.png**: Three panels, a lot to look at, tiny italic captions and a blank sketch box. On a small phone I do not know where to look. The verse card at the bottom (f_21.0) is tiny and low, and also overlaps the big subtitle. Too many text layers at once.
2. **f_30.0.png**: "THUD!" with a scroll nailed to wood. I do not understand it. It is the cross idea, but a newcomer sees a scroll on a post. Very confusing. Also the blank pale panel at lower right looks like a mistake.
3. **Overall colour (f_0.3, f_9.0, f_36.5)**: the whole film has a murky green-yellow cast. Paper panels look sickly olive, not the "night blue and warm gold" promised. It looks dull on a bright phone screen and old.

Foreign or confusing: the scroll with a wax seal (what is it? "your record" is only in words); the door (is it a house, a prison?); the rope belt; "cast out"; the "AWAKEDEN" logo with a red half looks like a news brand.

## 4. VS THE FEED
Better than most oil-painting AI Bible shorts, which are glossy and generic. More readable than live-action reenactments. Less cute and less shareable than Pixar/3D or anime, which get more clicks in my region. The comic panel idea with a sketch reveal is different and good. The hero Jesus (f_52.0, f_61.7) is close to a "religious poster" look. Just different, slightly better, not a clear winner.

## 5. BEST ALTERNATIVE
From _contact_strip.png: the first (thick-line cartoon, left) and third (clean cartoon) are brighter, and faces are easier to read from far. In _contact_strip_round2.png the second style (blue stones, warm gold arch, clear faces) is the strongest: it has clear blue-and-gold colour, expressions you can read in 1 second, and no mud. The watercolour one (4th in strip 1) feels gentle but will look weak on a phone. The black-and-white ones are too serious. So: keep the chosen comic look, but push it to the round2 #2 colour (clean blue plus bright gold). The goldseam_resurrection_heroshot.png is the best single picture: sun glow, open hands, warm sky, and the face is more varied, a little more world-friendly. The cross one (goldseam_cross_final.png) is strong but heavy for a first viewer.

## 6. BIGGEST STYLE CHANGE
**Biggest:** fix the colour. Remove the green-yellow cast, get true deep blue night and clean warm gold, and bright paper cream instead of olive. (Costs nothing if it is a colour grade on the edit.)
Small:
1. Cut the on-screen text layers to ONE at a time, and make captions larger, upright, high-contrast, away from faces (free).
2. Replace the "scroll nailed with THUD!" beat with a plain picture a stranger understands (a cross, or a hand letting the scroll fall) (costs money: new picture).
3. Make the people less one-culture: a darker-skinned, more Middle-Eastern Jesus and neighbours, or at least more variety in the crowd (costs money).

## 7. KEEP
- The pencil-to-ink reveal. It is the signature and the curiosity hook.
- The big white and yellow hook text ("AFRAID GOD WILL TURN YOU AWAY?", "IN NO WISE = NEVER"). It is clear to ESL viewers.
- The door and lamp story. One simple object, easy to follow.
- The final open-arms Jesus under the arch (f_58.5, f_61.7). A warm, clear ending.
- Same man in every panel.
