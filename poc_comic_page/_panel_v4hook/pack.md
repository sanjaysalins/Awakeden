# FILM PACK: vertical short (9:16), 61.8 seconds, painted comic style, with narration, sound effects and music.
Format: YouTube Short / TikTok / Reels. One narrator voice. Still comic panels that slowly move, with burned-in captions. Frames from the finished film are in the "frames" folder next to this pack (file name = second in the film).

## SPOKEN LINES (the whole narration, numbered; voice starts at second 0)
1. Somewhere in you is the quiet fear that if you actually came to God, He'd look at your record and turn you away.
2. That fear keeps you at the door, rehearsing whether you're allowed in. Jesus answers it before you can ask.
3. But listen to His own words: "All that the Father giveth me shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out." In no wise. Not maybe. Not if you clean up first.
4. You think your case might be the exception - too far gone, too late, too much. But him that cometh has no fine print. The only way to be cast out is to never come.
5. So come. Bring the record you're ashamed of - He already read it, and still said come. The door you've been rehearsing at was never locked.
