## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR

Priya, 26, in Bengaluru. English from school. She watches with auto-captions, knows the name Jesus, and feels too ashamed to pray. She stops for the first card, "AFRAID GOD WILL TURN YOU AWAY?" She finishes only if she reads "IN NO WISE = NEVER" and the last card. She shares it to a sister who will not walk into church.

She leaves at line 3, if "giveth / cometh / in no wise" arrives as noise. Not served: a newcomer with no Christian frame; a Muslim colleague (line 1 fears God, line 2 answers with Jesus, line 3 adds "the Father"); anyone whose app turns "in no wise" into "not wise."

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY

"If I come to Jesus, he will not reject me." Line 5 and the last card carry it. The ending is clear. The middle hides the same point: line 3 says it in English almost nobody learns in class.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE

Who: a man outside a shut door with a scroll. Jesus stands in a lit arch, arms open. What: the one who comes will not be thrown out. Why: Jesus said so. I can retell that from the last picture plus the last card.

I got lost at line 2, "rehearsing whether you're allowed in." Rehearsing means practicing a play. Line 4, "has no fine print": many places have no such thing, and translation gives "small letters." "Too far gone" has nowhere to go in another language. "The only way to be cast out is to never come" sounds like a contradiction. How are you thrown out if you never went in?

"Record" (lines 1 and 5) also means a police file or a song. The scroll helps, until a seal makes it look unread. Line 5 says "He already read it." A sealed scroll says the opposite.

Universal: shame, a shut door, open arms. Insider: the scroll as a moral file, KJV grammar, and "come" meaning come to Jesus. Line 1 fears God. The proof speaks as Jesus about the Father. Three names, no plain link.

Words that fail: "in no wise" → "never"; "him that cometh" → "anyone who comes"; "fine print" → "hidden rule"; "rehearsing" → "waiting and worrying"; "turn you away" → "say no to you"; "clean up first" → "become good first."

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE

Line 3's quote matches KJV John 6:37. RIGHT. "In no wise" means "not in any way." The card "IN NO WISE = NEVER" is RIGHT.

"Not if you clean up first" (line 3): that verse says nothing about moral cleanup. OVERCLAIMED.

"has no fine print" (line 4): OVERCLAIMED. The same chapter says no one comes unless the Father draws him (John 6:44).

"The only way to be cast out is to never come" (line 4): OVERCLAIMED. The verse says the one who comes will not be cast out. It does not say everyone else is cast out. "Cast out" means thrown out, not "left outside."

"He already read it, and still said come" (line 5): OVERCLAIMED. This verse does not say he read your record, and it does not say "Come."

"The door you've been rehearsing at was never locked" (line 5): UNSUPPORTED. The verse has no door. The shut door on screen makes "never locked" hard to trust.

The last card, "HE WILL NEVER TURN YOU AWAY," drops the verse's limit, "who comes." The spoken film does say "come" (line 5). The card alone is a mild OVERCLAIM.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE

Say the promise in today's words at the quote. Keep the old sentence on screen, small, as the source. Highest-value change: in line 3, speak "Anyone who comes to me, I will never turn away." Score: 6/10. The hook and the last card are already plain. The proof, in the middle, is the hardest English in the film.

## CURIOSITY GAP

Opens at line 1. The clear payoff is line 5, not line 3. Line 3 is the answer in a lock. Score: 6/10. Worth the wait only if you already feel the fear. From line 3 to line 5 the film says one idea twice, first in harder words.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES

Line 2: "That fear keeps you at the door, rehearsing whether you're allowed in." → "That fear keeps you outside the door, not sure you are allowed in."

Line 3: "In no wise. Not maybe. Not if you clean up first." → "That means never. Not maybe. Not after you become good first."

Line 4: "But him that cometh has no fine print. The only way to be cast out is to never come." → "But anyone who comes is kept. There is no hidden rule. He turns no one away who comes."
