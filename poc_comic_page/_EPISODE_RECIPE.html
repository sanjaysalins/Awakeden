The big idea
Today we fixed problems after the film was built. That is slow and costs money.
The recipe moves every check before the money is spent, and turns each lesson into a rule or a gate.

1. Rules that come from this episode

RuleWhat went wrong here
Hook first. Frame 1 = a face or hand + a question card. Voice at 0s. No masthead.1.3s logo open. 5 of 5 seats flagged it.
Plain words. No old word carries the key line. If a quote is old English, show a card: "OLD WORD = PLAIN WORD"."in no wise", "cometh", "rehearsing", "fine print": 5 of 5.
Say who Jesus is once, early, in plain words.4 of 5 seats lost here.
No claim beyond the verse. Every bridge line names the verse that backs it."only way to be cast out is to never come": 4 of 5.
Pictures must not fight the words.A bolt on the door, then "never locked".
One big panel at a time. Grids only as a rare reveal. No empty cells. Something new every 4 seconds.8 seconds of near-still. Alive score stuck at 4.75.
One face for Jesus, chained to every shot, landing too. Calm welcome. No glow sticker.Landing Jesus was off-model: 3 of 4.
One text layer at a time. Big bold captions. Cards for hook, decode, ask.3 text layers at the key line.
Colour is measured, not eyeballed.A shared tool turned paper green. 4 of 4 saw it. Nobody caught it for weeks.

5. Honest targets

  • Panel score of 7 or more on words AND on style, before animation spend. Today's film is 6.4.
  • The bigger lever for viewers is the hook and the plain words, not the art style.
  • Seats are AI. Odds are ranges with wide error bars. Not promises.
  • Cost per episode, from our own record: about $22 to $30 (Piece 1 was $28 to $30). Not yet verified for the new flow.
