## 1 WHAT I SAW
A poor man in a hood stands at a big dark door at night. He has a scroll. He looks sad and scared. He holds his hand out but the door is shut. Then a kind man with long hair and a beard speaks. Words appear in boxes: "All that the Father giveth me shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out." Last card: "IN NO WISE = NEVER". Idea: the man at the door is afraid. The kind man says, if you come to me, I will never send you away. I do not know who the kind man is, but the story says he is someone important, maybe God's son. I guess the first man could be me.

## 2 DOES IT FEEL LIKE A COMIC COMING ALIVE? 7/10
The pencil sketch turning into colour is a nice idea and the lamp, door and hand make a clear story. The look is a real comic style. I cannot judge motion, the sweep of light, or the floating balloons. The jump from the door man to the kind man in one second is a bit sudden (frame 1.0 to 1.6).

## 3 READABILITY
Big font, dark on cream, high contrast. Good on a phone. But "giveth", "cometh", "shall" are hard for me. I stop and translate. "In no wise" is very strange English. The four stacked boxes cover the chest, not the face, good. The last card "IN NO WISE = NEVER" helps me a lot: now I understand. Needed earlier. "Father" is unclear: whose father?

## 4 REVERENCE
Fine. He looks calm, kind, glowing. Words are in clean boxes, not shouting. Good.

## 5 WHAT WOULD LOSE VIEWERS
- Frame 1.6: "All that the Father giveth me" appears with no hint who "me" is. I may think the narrator speaks.
- Old words "giveth / cometh / in no wise" (frames 4.8 to 8.8) feel like homework. I may swipe.
- Frames 8.8 and 10.0 are nearly the same picture for a long time; the film feels still.

## 6 THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Show the plain meaning "NEVER sent away" sooner, e.g. under the balloons while the old words show.
2. Add a tiny name tag "Jesus" at first balloon.
3. Let the scared man appear small once more at the end (open door, or him looking up) so the answer matches the fear.

## 7 OVERALL 7/10
Pretty, calm, clear feeling. Old words are the main wall for me; the last card saves it.
