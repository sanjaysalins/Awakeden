# Blind seat: art director / retention judge (Fable)

## 1 WHAT I SAW (who is speaking, what is the idea)
A narrator speaks throughout. Panel 1 (f_0.3, f_1.0): a ragged hooded man at a night door under a lamp, scroll in hand - the man afraid to come. Panel 2: one portrait of Jesus; His words (John 6:37) arrive as four gold-ruled balloons, one phrase each. A bold card "IN NO WISE = NEVER" translates the old phrase. Idea: the fearful man is answered by Jesus's own promise - come, and you will never be thrown out.

## 2 DOES IT FEEL LIKE A COMIC COMING ALIVE? 5/10
The pencil-to-ink reveal (f_0.3, f_1.6) is real comic grammar and the strongest thing here. But from f_2.5 to f_10.0 - about 7.5 of 10.4 s - it is ONE portrait with a push-in while balloons stack. On stills that reads as a captioned poster, not a page turning: no cut, no angle change, no return to the man being answered. Cannot judge: push, drift, light band, balloon float, reveal timing.

## 3 READABILITY on a phone, second-language reader
Good. Balloons are big, high-contrast, 6 words or fewer, clear of the face. "giveth / cometh" are guessable; "in no wise" is the one opaque phrase and the card solves it well. Snags: f_1.6 shows the first balloon over a half-pencil drawing - looks like a glitch. The card's heavy sans is a different voice from the hand lettering; fine as a narrator note, but the two styles sit 2 cm apart.

## 4 REVERENCE
Fine. Jesus's words are set apart in their own gold balloons, face calm, nothing exaggerated. Two soft notes: no verse reference anywhere on screen; and the blocky "= NEVER" card sits on His chest like a meme caption - not wrong, slightly cheap next to the balloons.

## 5 WHAT WOULD LOSE VIEWERS
f_6.0 -> f_7.5 -> f_8.8 -> f_10.0: the same portrait for 4+ s with nothing new but a push and a card. Feed scrollers swipe on sameness. Also Jesus looks off-frame left, away from the viewer AND away from the man (frame-right in f_1.0) - the promise is spoken to nobody. And the f_1.6 balloon-on-pencil moment.

## 6 THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Finish the Jesus reveal before the first balloon lands (start reveal ~0.4 s earlier). Fixes f_1.6.
2. Mirror the Jesus panel so His eyeline goes toward the man's side / the viewer. $0.
3. Add a small gold-lettered "John 6:37" under the last balloon or on the card.
Bigger, not small: cut back to the man's face on "cast out" / the card - the one being answered should be seen hearing it.

## 7 OVERALL 6/10
