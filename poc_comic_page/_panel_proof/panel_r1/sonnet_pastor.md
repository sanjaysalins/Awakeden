## 1 WHAT I SAW (who is speaking, what is the idea)
A hooded, ragged man with a scroll stands at a closed, barred door at night, hand half-raised, afraid to knock. Then Jesus, close portrait, speaks John 6:37 in four gold-ruled balloons. Final card: "IN NO WISE = NEVER". Idea: the one who comes will never be turned away.

## 2 DOES IT FEEL LIKE A COMIC COMING ALIVE? 7/10
The pencil-to-ink wipe (f_0.3, f_1.6) is a real "page being drawn" moment, and the door scene is atmospheric. The Jesus panels are one static portrait with stacked balloons, so it reads more as a captioned illustration than a comic. I cannot judge the push-in, light sweep or balloon float from stills. The jump from the door scene to Jesus has no visible link in the frames (no panel border, no gutter).

## 3 READABILITY on a phone, for a second-language reader
Balloon text is large, dark on cream, high contrast. Good. The handwriting font is clear. "giveth", "cometh" are archaic but short. The final card is bold and very clear. Balloons sit over the chest, away from the face. The stack of four gets heavy by f_6.0 and covers much of the lower half.

## 4 REVERENCE: how Jesus's words are shown. Fine, or a problem?
Mostly fine. The quote is shown nearly verbatim and reverently, with no sound-effect gimmicks. Two concerns. (a) The balloons read "and him that cometh to me / I will in no wise cast out", which drops "him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out" as spoken, but the on-screen text breaks the sentence differently from the narrator and loses the clause "him that cometh to me" pairing; the KJV is "him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out", so it is verbatim, but the line split makes "I will" start its own balloon. Acceptable. (b) "IN NO WISE = NEVER" is a gloss placed over the face and robe of Christ, styled like a meme or sticker. Helpful for second-language readers, but the math-sign style sits lightly against Christ's own words. Also the drawn Jesus is a conventional handsome portrait; fine, not a problem for me.

## 5 WHAT WOULD LOSE VIEWERS
f_6.0 to f_8.8: the same static frame for about four seconds with the stacked balloons; little changes. Also f_0.3: the half-sketched wipe could confuse for half a second.

## 6 THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Style the "= NEVER" card in softer serif or gold on the balloon style, not a bold meme block.
2. Shorten the stack: fade older balloons as new ones arrive so the face is not covered.
3. Show a brief transition cue (door-to-Jesus), e.g. a gutter or light through the door.

## 7 OVERALL 7/10
Faithful, clear, reverent in the main. The gloss card is the only gimmicky note.
