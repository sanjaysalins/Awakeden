## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
A church-group member who knows the cross story but has never noticed that the mockery echoes Psalm 22. They stop at the question card, finish for the "a thousand years" reveal, and share it as "look at this." A pure newcomer is only partly served, because nothing says why Psalm 22 matters.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"The mockers at the cross repeated words written a thousand years earlier, and Jesus stayed anyway." Lines 5 and 9 carry it. The film makes the echo clear. It never says what the echo means: prophecy, or coincidence?

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Jesus on the cross, with people mocking him. What: their words match David's song. Why: unstated. The card "WHO WROTE THE INSULTS?" misleads, because the film never answers it. The answer is not David (line 3, "He writes their words into a song"). The mockers are quoting what he wrote, or the reverse, and the film leaves that ambiguous. "Deliver" is the right word to gloss, and the "DELIVER = RESCUE" card does it. "Reviled" and "wagging" are avoided, which is good. "He stayed" (lines 7 and 9) is simple and strong.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
The KJV block at the end of my lens prompt is Exodus and Numbers, which is a different film. I checked against the film-specific KJV text supplied in the evidence pack.
- Line 1 "The leaders shout at Him": RIGHT. Matt 27:41-43 has chief priests, scribes and elders mocking. "Shout" is mild paraphrase.
- Line 2 "He trusted in God; let him deliver him now": RIGHT. It is Matt 27:43, trimmed.
- Line 3 "a thousand years… David… Psalm 22": UNSUPPORTED by the pack text. It is historically sound, and Ps 22:7 supports "laugh me to scorn." The title "of David" is not in the pack.
- Line 4 "He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him": RIGHT. It is Ps 22:8 verbatim.
- Line 5 "Almost the same words" and "People passing by also shout": RIGHT. Matt 27:39 says "reviled… wagging their heads."
- Line 6 "If thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross": RIGHT. It is Matt 27:40.
- Line 7 "He could have come down": UNSUPPORTED. The pack has no verse for it. It is true (Matt 26:53), but nothing supplied backs it.
- Line 7 "no one takes His life… He gives His life Himself": RIGHT. This paraphrases John 10:18. Saying Jesus "had said" it is fine.
- Line 8 "Father, forgive them…": RIGHT. It is Luke 23:34. The balloon tag reads "JESUS," but the narrator voices the line. That is a minor mismatch.
- Line 9 "He died there": RIGHT.
- Line 10 "A Roman soldier, watching Him die": RIGHT, with a small stretch. Matt 27:54 says the centurion and those with him were "watching Jesus" and saw the earthquake. The quote is verbatim.

Nothing is WRONG. One OVERCLAIM risk: the framing implies that Psalm 22 was about Jesus. The film never states that, and it never says whether David's own situation is the primary one.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
A top explainer would state the claim in one line: "David wrote this about his own suffering. Jesus' mockers used the same words, and the Gospel writers noticed." That makes the echo meaningful and honest. It would also drop the opening question, or answer it. Score: 6/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
It opens at the card ("WHO WROTE THE INSULTS?") and at line 1. The first payoff is line 5 ("almost the same words"). The real payoff, why it matters, arrives only partly at lines 9 and 10. Score: 6/10. It is worth the wait for a Bible reader. A newcomer gets a pattern but no meaning.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 3: "Now go back a thousand years, to a king named David. People laugh at David too. He writes their words into a song, Psalm 22." → "Now go back a thousand years, to King David. Enemies laughed at him too, and he wrote it in a song, Psalm 22."
2. Line 5: "Almost the same words, a thousand years apart. People passing by also shout." → "Almost the same words, a thousand years apart. The Gospel writers noticed. People passing by also shout."
3. Line 7: "He could have come down." → "He could have come down. He told them He could ask for angels." (Matt 26:53). Also change the opening card to "WHOSE WORDS ARE THESE?"
