# Judge, no hat — "Who Wrote the Insults?" (73 s)

## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Daniel, 24, raised in church, scrolls Shorts, has heard the cross story but never read Psalm 22. He stops at the card "WHO WROTE THE INSULTS?" because it sounds like a trick question; he finishes because line 3's "go back a thousand years" promises a reveal and line 5 delivers it. The sharer is the lifelong Bible reader who gets the chill at line 4. NOT served: the total newcomer — line 1 assumes "Son of God" and "cross" need no setup, line 3 assumes "a king named David" means something, and no line says why any of it matters to him. Also the fast scroller: the payoff lands near 30 s of a 73 s film.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"The insults shouted at Jesus were written down a thousand years before — and He chose to stay anyway." Line 5 carries the first half, line 9 the second. The first half is clear. The second is unfinished: lines 7 and 9 say He stayed and could have come down, but never for what or for whom. The card "DELIVER = RESCUE" (12 s) sets up a word the film never uses again. A viewer can say back "He stayed," not "why."

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Jesus dying, mocked by leaders; David, a mocked king 1,000 years earlier. What: the cross insults match David's psalm; told to come down, Jesus stayed and prayed for them; a soldier called Him God's Son. Why: missing — line 7 "He gives His life Himself" says He chose it, not what for.
Lost: line 3 "He writes their words into a song" — with David on screen and line 4 in David's own voice, a newcomer can hear David doing the insulting. Line 1 "The leaders" — a newcomer may assume Romans. Words: "deliver" is saved by the card (good); "If thou be" is saved by line 7's paraphrase (good); "Son of God" is said four times with no plain gloss — the one phrase a newcomer must already know.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- L1 "The leaders shout at Him" — RIGHT in substance (Matt 27:41: priests, scribes, elders); vague, not wrong.
- L2 — RIGHT, verbatim Matt 27:43a; dropping "if he will have him" keeps the sense.
- L3 "People laugh at David too ... Psalm 22" — RIGHT (Ps 22:7; titled "A Psalm of David"); "a thousand years" RIGHT as a round figure. Visual "old king David by lamp" — UNSUPPORTED detail, harmless.
- L4 — RIGHT, verbatim Ps 22:8a.
- L5 "Almost the same words" — RIGHT; the "almost" is honest.
- L6 — RIGHT, verbatim Matt 27:40.
- L7 "He could have come down ... gives His life Himself" — RIGHT, fair inference and paraphrase of John 10:18; the only quote with no on-screen reference tag.
- L8 — RIGHT, verbatim Luke 23:34; "also prayed" wisely claims no sequence (Luke places it before the mocking).
- L10 "A Roman soldier ... said" — RIGHT in substance; Matt 27:54 is the centurion and those with him, after the earthquake.
- Card "WHO WROTE THE INSULTS?" — mildly OVERCLAIMED: David wrote down insults aimed at him; the leaders echoed them unknowingly. Line 5 is more honest than the title.
Nothing WRONG. The gap is omission: the purpose of the death is never stated.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would close the loop the film itself opened: they asked God to rescue Him (line 2, the card); He refused rescue so He could rescue them (Matt 20:28 "a ransom for many"; line 8 already shows Him praying for the mockers). One plain sentence gives the newcomer the why, gives the Bible reader the full Psalm 22 arc, and pays off the card's own word. Also: answer the hook out loud at line 5; give Jesus' one line (8) its own voice — the mocker and David get voices, the Son of God is read by the narrator; cut the repeat of "He stayed" (lines 7 and 9). The ONE change: the rescue sentence at line 9. Score: 6/10 — accurate and honest, but no why, and the hook is never answered in words.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at the card (0.2-2.8 s) and line 1 "Listen."; pays off at line 5. 7/10 — real and honest, but the viewer must assemble the answer himself. A second gap opens at line 7 ("He could have come down") and never pays off. The first wait is worth it; the second is the one that would make people share.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 5 → "Almost the same words, a thousand years apart. The insult was written down before it was ever shouted."
2. Line 9 → "They said, come down. He did not come down. He stayed — so that He could rescue them." (Matt 20:28; pays off DELIVER = RESCUE.)
3. Line 1 → "Jesus, the Son of God, is dying on a cross. The priests and the elders shout at Him. Listen." (Matt 27:41.)
