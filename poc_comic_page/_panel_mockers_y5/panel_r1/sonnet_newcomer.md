"Jesus was mocked with the same words an old king wrote long ago, and He stayed on the cross anyway." Lines 7 and 9 carry it ("He could have come down... He stayed"). Mostly clear. But I only half understand WHY staying matters. I am told He "gives His life Himself" (7) but not what for.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would say plainly, in one sentence, what the link means: the old song and the mocking match, so the story was told in advance. Right now the film shows the match but never says what it proves. Highest-value change: add one line after 5 stating the point. Also explain "Son of God" once, early. Score: 6/10.
