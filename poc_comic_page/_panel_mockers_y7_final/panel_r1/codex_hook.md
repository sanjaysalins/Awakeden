## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE (who / what / why in my own words; where I got lost or misled, quote the line; words that fail in translation)

People ridicule a dying man’s claim to be God’s Son; their taunt resembles an older biblical song; he stays on the cross and asks forgiveness for them (lines 1–9).

“Listen” gives me an immediate task (line 1). “Their words were already in the Bible before they said them” gives me the discovery, but leaves its significance implicit: prediction, literary connection, or coincidence (line 5)?

“Deliver” receives a useful gloss (line 3). “Trusted on,” “thou,” and “taketh” complicate comprehension across languages, although the spoken paraphrases help (lines 4, 6–7). “Give His life for many” remains abstract without explaining what his death accomplishes (line 9).
