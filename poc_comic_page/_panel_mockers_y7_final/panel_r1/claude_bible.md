## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Maria, 34, church small-group member who scrolls Shorts. She stops at "WHOSE WORDS ARE THESE?". She finishes because the mocker's line echoes David's. She shares it with her group. Not served: a total skeptic. Line 3 jumps to "a thousand years" and Psalm 22 with no setup. A child is also not served, because the script moves too fast.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"The mockers at the cross repeated words David wrote 1,000 years earlier, and Jesus stayed and forgave them." Lines 5 and 9 carry it. The film does make it clear, but it has two takeaways: the prophecy echo (3–5) and the stay-and-forgive (7–9). Line 10 adds a third.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
In my own words: Jesus is dying. People mock Him with words that were already in an old song. He stays and forgives. A soldier says He is God's Son.
- Line 1, "religious leaders shout": the verse does not say shout. See section 4.
- The card "DELIVER = RESCUE" is useful.
- Line 7, "no one takes His life from Him": this is an unsourced paraphrase. A newcomer will not know who said it or when.
- Line 10, "the earthquake": a newcomer has no earthquake setup.
- "Son of God" is never explained.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- **L1 "Jesus, who says He is God's Son, is dying on a cross."** RIGHT. Matt 27:43 has "I am the Son of God."
- **L1 "The religious leaders shout at Him."** OVERCLAIMED. The pack's Matt 27:39-44 extract has no "shout." The passers-by "revile" (v39), and the verse as supplied has no speaker for v43. The Matthew 27:41 chief priests and scribes are outside the extract, so I can't verify them from the pack. Their speech is "mocking" in the KJV, not shouting.
- **L2 "He trusted in God; let him deliver him now"** RIGHT. It is a truncation of v43 and is verbatim as far as it goes. The "if he will have him" clause is omitted without an ellipsis.
- **L3 "he trusted God, so let God rescue him."** RIGHT as a gloss.
- **L3 "a thousand years, to King David."** RIGHT as an approximation. The pack doesn't state the date, so it is UNSUPPORTED by the pack and fine by common chronology (about 1000 BC).
- **L3 "Enemies laughed at him too."** RIGHT. Ps 22:7, "laugh me to scorn."
- **L3 "In a song, Psalm 22, he wrote down what they said."** OVERCLAIMED. The text says the psalmist reports what his enemies say. It does not say David wrote it down after hearing it. Also, the pack has no David attribution for the psalm.
- **L4 "He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him"** RIGHT. It is verbatim Ps 22:8.
- **L5 "Almost the same words."** RIGHT. Matt 27:43 and Ps 22:8 are close.
- **L5 "Their words were already in the Bible before they said them."** RIGHT as a statement, though the phrasing is strong. It implies the mockers were quoting the psalm. The text doesn't say they knew it. Safer is "echoed".
- **L5 "People passing by also shout."** OVERCLAIMED. Matt 27:39 says "reviled... wagging their heads" and "saying". It does not say shout.
- **L6** RIGHT. It is verbatim Matt 27:40.
- **L7 "Jesus had said that no one takes His life from Him. He gives it Himself."** RIGHT. John 10:18 supports it. It is unsourced in the film.
- **L7 "He stayed."** RIGHT as inference.
- **L8** RIGHT. It is verbatim Luke 23:34.
- **L9 "He died there. He came to give His life for many."** RIGHT. Matt 20:28 supports it, but it is unsourced on screen.
- **L9 "They said, come down."** RIGHT. See Matt 27:40.
- **L10 "A Roman officer, and the men with him, felt the earthquake and said..."** RIGHT. Matt 27:54 says "centurion." "Roman" is not in the verse, and "felt" is a mild gloss of "saw the earthquake."
- **L10 "Truly this was the Son of God."** RIGHT. It is verbatim.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would tell one story, with the echo as the climax. The highest-value change is to cut lines 7–10 down to the forgive line, or to put on-screen source tags on L7 and L9 so the claims can be checked. It is a 6/10 on that bar. The accuracy is good, but the structure has two endings.

## CURIOSITY GAP
It opens at the card and line 1 ("Whose words are these?"). It pays off at line 5 ("Almost the same words"). Score 7/10. It is worth the wait. However, the card's "whose" is answered only implicitly.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. **L1:** replace "The religious leaders shout at Him." with "People mock Him. Listen."
2. **L5:** replace "People passing by also shout." with "People passing by mock Him too."
3. **L3:** replace "he wrote down what they said" with "he wrote about enemies who said almost these exact words."
