## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Named viewer: Priya, 26, Mumbai, English her second language, scrolls Shorts, knows "Jesus died on a cross" as a fact and nothing more. She stops on the card "WHOSE WORDS ARE THESE?" plus the sneering faces (lines 1-2). She finishes only if line 5 lands — it is the film's one surprise. The lifelong Bible reader finishes and shares: line 5 is a prophecy-proof clip he can forward. NOT served: Priya is never told WHY the match matters; the film states the fact (line 5) and changes subject. Also short-changed: anyone wanting Jesus' own voice — the mockers get an actor, Jesus (line 8) gets the narrator.

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"The men mocking Jesus were saying words David wrote a thousand years earlier — the cross was planned, and Jesus chose to stay." Line 5 carries the first half, lines 7 and 9 the second. Clear? Half. The fact is clear; the meaning is never spoken. After line 5 the film pivots ("People passing by also shout") and runs two more threads — He stayed (7-9), the officer (10) — without tying either back to the hook. A newcomer leaves with "Jesus chose to die" and the Psalm as trivia.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: Jesus dying; leaders and passers-by mocking; King David 1000 years earlier; a Roman officer. What: the taunt repeats David's song; Jesus stays and dies; the officer calls Him God's Son. Why: not said.
Lost at line 3: "he wrote down what they said" — I had just heard the leaders at the cross; "they" sounds like them, not David's enemies. Line 5: "Their words were already in the Bible" — whose, the mockers' or David's? Line 7: "He gives it Himself" — "it" is three words back; odd English. Line 9: "for many" — many what? Ransom shorthand that does not travel. Line 10: "felt the earthquake" — no earthquake was shown or mentioned before this. Good: the "DELIVER = RESCUE" card, and lines 3 and 7 paraphrasing the KJV in plain words.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- L1 leaders mock Him — RIGHT (Matt 27:41-43: chief priests, scribes, elders). "Shout" is dramatization; the text says "said."
- L2 Matt 27:43 — RIGHT, verbatim, fairly cut.
- L3 David, ~1000 years, Psalm 22 is David's, enemies laughed — RIGHT (Ps 22 title; 22:7).
- L4 Ps 22:8 — RIGHT, verbatim.
- L5 near-identical words, already written — RIGHT. The film wisely does not claim the mockers quoted knowingly. "In the Bible" is shorthand (the Psalm existed; "the Bible" did not) — acceptable.
- L6 Matt 27:40, passers-by — RIGHT.
- L7 John 10:18 paraphrase — RIGHT.
- L8 Luke 23:34 — RIGHT, verbatim. Balloon tag says JESUS; the voice is the narrator.
- L9 "give His life for many" — RIGHT (Matt 20:28).
- L10 Matt 27:54 — RIGHT; "felt" for "saw" is fine; drops "they feared greatly."
No WRONG, no OVERCLAIMED. The film UNDER-claims: it holds the evidence of line 5 and never states the conclusion.

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would make one loop, not three. Hook: whose words? Turn: David's, written a thousand years before — so the cross was the plan. Close: the mockers said IF you are the Son of God (line 6); a Roman outsider said TRULY (line 10). Same question, two answers, 76 seconds. Give Jesus His own voice at line 8. ONE highest-value change: one sentence after line 5 saying what the match means. Score: 6/10 — accurate and clean, but a proof with no verdict.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at the card and line 2; pays off at line 5 (~25 s). 6/10. Worth the wait, yes — but it closes a third of the way in. Lines 6-10 run on a second, unannounced question ("will He come down?") that pays at line 9 and never rejoins the first.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
- Line 5: "Almost the same words, a thousand years apart. Their taunt was already written down, a thousand years before they said it. The cross was no accident. Others walking past shout too."
- Line 9: "They said, come down. He did not come down. He died there. He came to give His life as a ransom — the price that sets many free."
- Line 10: "They had said IF. A Roman officer, an outsider, felt the ground shake and said:" — then the quote unchanged.
