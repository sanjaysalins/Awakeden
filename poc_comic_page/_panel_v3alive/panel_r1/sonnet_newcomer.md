## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Mei, 34, Vietnamese factory worker, scrolling on her phone at night, second-language English. She stops at the dark door and the old man with the scroll (line 1 fear is relatable). She may finish if she feels the man is "me". She shares only if a friend carries guilt. NOT served: anyone who does not know who "Jesus" or "God" or "the Father" is. The film never says who Jesus is (line 2).

## 2. MAIN TAKEAWAY
"If you come to this Jesus, he will not send you away, even with your bad past." Carried by line 3 ("him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out") and line 5. Fairly clear in the last 15 seconds; muddy before that, because line 3 is hard to follow.

## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who: a tired man at a door with a paper of his bad deeds. What: a man called Jesus says "come in, I won't throw you out." Why: so I stop being afraid of being rejected.
Where I got lost:
- Line 2 "Jesus answers it" - I do not know who Jesus is. The picture shows a calm man, so I guess he is a kind teacher or a god.
- Line 1/2 "God", "the Father", "me" in line 3: is the Father the same as God? Is Jesus the Father's son? Not explained.
- Line 3 "giveth", "cometh", "In no wise" - words that fail in translation. "In no wise" is also the title and I cannot parse it. A translator app gives nonsense.
- Line 2 "rehearsing" is hard. "Cast out" (title, 3, 4) sounds like throwing out of a country.
- Line 4 "fine print" is an idiom.
- Line 5 "He already read it" - how? Who told Him? Unexplained.
- "Come" to where? Church? Pray? Nothing says what to physically do.
- The scroll: I understood it as "his bad record" only because line 1 says "record". Good. The seal frames (23s) are unexplained.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
I cannot judge accuracy. What I was TOLD but could not follow: who Jesus is; who "the Father" is; "John 6:37" (a name? a book? a chapter?); "Awakeden"; line 3's whole quote grammar; "the exception" (4); how Jesus "read" my record (5).

## 5. BEST VERSION POSSIBLE
The best explainer would give one plain sentence early: "Jesus, who many people believe is God's Son." Then quote one short phrase, then say it in plain words. The visuals are strong and consistent (same man, same door; Jesus's open arms at the end is a clear picture of "welcome"). The ONE highest-value change: add a one-line "who is Jesus" and replace the archaic quote with plain English. Score 6/10.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1 (will He turn me away?). Pays off at line 3/5 (no, the door was never locked). Score 6/10. Worth the wait, but the answer is buried in line 3 hard words; the payoff lands only through the pictures (open door, arms open).

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 2: "Jesus answers it before you can ask." -> "Jesus, the man many call God's Son, answers it before you ask."
2. Line 3: after the quote add plain words: "In no wise" -> "Never. He will never turn you away."
3. Line 4: "no fine print" -> "no hidden rules".

## 6. MOST VIEWERS
Change the HOOK/TITLE: the 1.2s title "IN NO WISE CAST OUT" is unreadable meaning for non-natives and the first frames are a still door with a logo. Start on line 1 voice over the man at the door, and title it "Afraid God will say no?". KEEP: the night door, the lone man with the scroll, the warm gold light, caption boxes (easy to read on mute), the arms-open ending.
