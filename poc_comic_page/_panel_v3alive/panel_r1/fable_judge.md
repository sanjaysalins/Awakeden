## 1. WHO IS THIS FOR
Maria, 29, São Paulo, grew up in church, has not prayed in years, scrolling at 1am. She stops at line 1 because it names her feeling ("the quiet fear... turn you away"), not a Bible fact. She finishes if she survives the first 3s (see 6). The sharer is her believing friend, sending it with "this is for you." ## 3. GLOBAL AUDIENCE
Who/what/why: a man at night at a door, holding a scroll of his wrongs, afraid to knock. Jesus says anyone who comes is never turned away. So come.
Lost or misled:
- Line 3 "in no wise": the whole payoff hangs on an archaic idiom. ESL ears hear "not wise." The plain word "never" is never spoken. The title repeats it.
- Line 3 "giveth... cometh": half the verse is heavy and unexplained.
- Line 4 "fine print", lines 2/5 "rehearsing", line 4 "too far gone": idioms.
- Line 5 "was never locked" vs the pictures: a wooden bar sits across the door in f_1.2, f_23.5, f_36.5, f_44.5. The viewer sees a locked door for 45s, then is told it never was. (The bar is on the man's side, so the art secretly agrees, but nobody decodes that in a Short.) The final "door" (f_57.5, f_63.2) is a different stone arch with no door at all.
- f_44.5 scroll nailed to a post: a Col 2:14 echo for readers, a puzzle for newcomers; nothing spoken explains it.
- Captions start mid-sentence in lowercase ("that if you actually came to God," f_5.5) and are small for a phone.

## 4. BIBLICALLY ACCURATE
- Line 3 quote: John 6:37 KJV verbatim. RIGHT.
- Line 3 "Not maybe": Greek emphatic double negative. RIGHT. "Not if you clean up first": no condition in the verse; v.35/40 equate coming with believing. RIGHT.
- Line 4 "no fine print". RIGHT.
- Line 4 "The only way to be cast out is to never come": the contrapositive, not stated in 6:37, but John 5:40 and 3:36 carry it. RIGHT (supported elsewhere).
- Line 4 answering "too late" with an absolute: OVERCLAIMED (small). Jesus teaches a door that does shut (Luke 13:25; Matt 25:10). True for this viewer now (2 Cor 6:2); say "not too late today," not "never."
- Line 5 "He already read it, and still said come": Ps 139, Heb 4:13, Matt 11:28, Luke 5:32. RIGHT.
- Line 5 "never locked": figurative (John 10:9, Rev 3:20). RIGHT as a figure, same Luke 13:25 caveat.
- Line 2 "answers it before you can ask": in John 6 Jesus is answering unbelief (v.36), not a fearful seeker. Fair application, not the scene's context. RIGHT as application.

## CURIOSITY GAP
Opens at line 1 ("turn you away": will He?). Pays off at line 3; the sharper payoff is line 4's reversal. Score 6/10. Worth the wait, but the cover sticker "JOHN 6:37" (f_1.2) gives the answer away to any Bible reader at 1s, and 39 of 63 seconds come after the payoff as restatement, not deepening.

## THREE SMALLEST FIXES
1. Line 3: "In no wise. Not maybe. Not if you clean up first." -> "In no wise. That means never. Not maybe. Not if you clean up first."
2. Line 4: "But him that cometh has no fine print." -> "But 'him that cometh' means anyone who comes. No conditions. If you can hear this, it is not too late."
3. Line 5: "The door you've been rehearsing at was never locked." -> "The door you've been waiting at was never locked from the inside."
