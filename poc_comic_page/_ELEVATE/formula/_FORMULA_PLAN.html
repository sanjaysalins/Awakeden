Make the Thief film a repeatable formula

The film people loved:

1. What made it work (the DNA)

Length38 s. 8 lines. ~85 words.
Hook (0-3 s)A puzzle: "Jesus promised paradise to a criminal. Why?"
TwistTwo pairs from the Bible's own words: mocker vs asker. Someday vs Today.
VoicesNarrator + the person + Jesus.
LandingShort echo ("Not someday. Today.") + a soft ask. 3 s hold.
LookBold ink comic. Full-screen clips. Faces close.

2. The big problem for many languages

The English words are baked into the picture.

A Spanish viewer turns on Spanish subtitles and sees two sets of words. The English pill also sits where subtitles go.

Fix: make a word-free master film. Put words on a separate layer we can swap per language. Keep the bottom quarter clear for subtitles.

3. Doodles + motion graphics (no words needed)

Every doodle must make sense without reading. Ideas from this film:

  • Ink circle draws round the thief's face as he speaks.
  • Ink arrow from his cross to Jesus.
  • Calendar page far away ("someday") then a sunrise snaps close ("today").
  • Split screen: mocker turns away | thief turns in.
  • Ink line underlines Jesus' words.

Rule: 3-5 doodles per film. One idea each. Not on every shot.

4. Languages: how

  • Bible quotes: use an old free Bible in that language (e.g. Spanish Reina-Valera 1909, French Louis Segond 1910). Never machine-translate Scripture.
  • SRT lines: short (max ~32 letters), at least 1.2 s each. Spanish/German run ~25% longer.
  • Verse chips: numbers only ("23:43") work everywhere.
  • Later, paid: dubbed voice per language. Own ask + price first.
