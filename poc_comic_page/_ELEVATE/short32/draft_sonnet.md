# Two Thieves, 32s (Sonnet draft)

[narrator] Two criminals. Same hill. Same Jesus beside them. One got a promise.

[thief] "If thou be Christ, save thyself and us."

[narrator] That man wanted rescue from the cross. The other man knew he deserved his own.

[narrator] Then he asked one thing.

[penitent] "Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom."

[narrator] He asked for someday. Jesus answered with a different word.

[jesus] "To day shalt thou be with me in paradise."

[narrator] He asked to be remembered. Jesus said: with me. Today.

## Why it works
- Hook: "Two criminals. Same hill. Same Jesus beside them. One got a promise." Short, plain, a puzzle (why only one?) in the first 2 seconds. No Bible knowledge needed.
- Point: one man asks for rescue, the other asks only to be remembered. All KJV quotes are exact fragments from Luke 23:39-43.
- Landing is earned from his own words: he asked "remember me" (someday); Jesus answers "To day ... with me." The landing is "with me. Today." No stock closer, no pressure, no invented detail.
