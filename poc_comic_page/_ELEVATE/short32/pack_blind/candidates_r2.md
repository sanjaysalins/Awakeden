# Two ~32 second narrations of one story. Each is spoken aloud by the voice named in [brackets].

## Script P
[narrator] Jesus promised paradise to a thief. Why?
[narrator] Two criminals were dying on crosses beside Him.
[thief] "If thou be Christ, save thyself and us."
[narrator] One wanted off the cross. The other asked for very little.
[penitent] "Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom."
[jesus] "To day shalt thou be with me in paradise."
[narrator] He asked to be remembered someday. Jesus gave him today. Not just remembered. With Him. You can ask Him to remember you too.

## Script Q
[narrator] Two criminals were dying on crosses beside Jesus. Both spoke to Him.
[thief] "If thou be Christ, save thyself and us."
[narrator] One wanted off the cross. The other asked for very little.
[penitent] "Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom."
[narrator] He asked to be remembered. Someday.
[jesus] "To day shalt thou be with me in paradise."
[narrator] Not someday. Today. Not just remembered. With Him. You can ask Him to remember you too.
