# Three 32-second narrations. Same story. Each is spoken aloud by the voice named in [brackets].

## Script 1
[narrator] Two criminals were dying beside Jesus. Both asked Him for something.
[thief] "If thou be Christ, save thyself and us."
[narrator] One wanted off the cross. The other said Jesus had done nothing wrong.
[penitent] "Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom."
[narrator] He asked to be remembered. Someday.
[jesus] "To day shalt thou be with me in paradise."
[narrator] Not someday. Today. Not just remembered. With Him. You can ask Him the same.

## Script 2
[narrator] Jesus promised paradise to a thief. Why?
[thief] "If thou be Christ, save thyself and us."
[narrator] One man mocked. The other asked for very little.
[penitent] "Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom."
[jesus] "To day shalt thou be with me in paradise."
[narrator] He asked to be remembered someday. Jesus gave him today. And with Him. You can ask Him the same.

## Script 3
[narrator] Two criminals were dying beside Jesus. Both asked Him for something.
[thief] "If thou be Christ, save thyself and us."
[narrator] One wanted off the cross. The other defended Jesus.
[penitent] "This man hath done nothing amiss."
[narrator] Then he asked for very little.
[penitent] "Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom."
[jesus] "To day shalt thou be with me in paradise."
[narrator] He asked to be remembered. Jesus said, "with me." You can ask Him the same.
