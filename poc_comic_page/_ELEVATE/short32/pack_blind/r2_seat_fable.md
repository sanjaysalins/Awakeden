## Script P

**1. Newcomer (no Bible, English 2nd language)**
I understood: a man dying on a cross next to Jesus asked Jesus to remember him, and Jesus said he would be with Him in paradise that same day — and I can ask the same.
Lines I did not fully get:
- "If thou be Christ, save thyself and us." — old words ("thou", "thyself"); I may not know "Christ" means Jesus. The narrator's next line ("One wanted off the cross") rescues it.
- "when thou comest into thy kingdom" — "kingdom" sounds like a country. Not fatal; the narrator says "remembered someday" right after.
- The hook asks "Why?" — at the end I am not told a reason in plain words. I can guess it (he asked, Jesus gave), but the word "why" promised an answer I never heard.

**2. Bible reader (Luke 23:39-43 KJV)**
- v39, v42, v43 quoted verbatim. v43 drops "Verily I say unto thee" — a trim, not a change. Fine.
- "thief" — Luke says "malefactors"; "thief" comes from Matthew 27:38 / Mark 15:27. Harmonized, acceptable.
- vv40-41 are skipped: the man's own confession ("we indeed justly... this man hath done nothing amiss"). So the repentance half of the story is missing. Omission for time, not an error.
- "The other asked for very little." — mild under-sell. In request-size it is small; in faith it is huge (he calls a dying man "Lord" with a kingdom). Framing, not error.
- "Jesus gave him today" — follows KJV punctuation ("To day shalt thou be"). Correct reading, no overreach.
- No doctrinal error. No pressure. CTA matches v42 exactly.

## Rank

Tied on points (50 / 50). Tie-break: the first 2 seconds are the bigger lever on a 32s short, and a newcomer gets the frame (Jesus / thief / paradise) before the old-word quote arrives.

**1st: P** — better stop, newcomer pre-decoded.
**2nd: Q** — better craft in the middle, weak open.

**Best line (either script):** Q's `[narrator] He asked to be remembered. Someday.` — one word, placed before the answer, makes Jesus's "To day" hit.
**Worst line:** Q's opener `Two criminals were dying on crosses beside Jesus.` — a flat scene-set as the first words; nothing to stop a thumb.

**One-line fix for P (my pick):** move the "Someday." beat before Jesus speaks and close on "Today" — i.e. after the penitent's line insert `[narrator] He asked to be remembered. Someday.`, then Jesus's line, then `[narrator] Not someday. Today. Not just remembered. With Him. You can ask Him to remember you too.` Same word count; the hook's "Why?" is now answered by the reversal itself.

Still weak in both (for the fixer, not blocking): vv40-41 repentance is absent, so the man reads as "polite" rather than "repentant." If one more second exists, a 3-word narrator beat ("He owned his guilt.") before his ask would close it.
