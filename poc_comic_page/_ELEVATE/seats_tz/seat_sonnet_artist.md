## Film notes
**A**: Real comic grammar. Mixed full-bleed and 2-3 panel grids with ragged ink borders. Parchment caption boxes with red keyword accents ("FIVE", "WAGES", "PERSONALLY", "NO ACCIDENT"). Scripture shown as a tagged red label (ZECHARIAH 11:12b) plus quote. Cut rhythm 26/min, longest hold 5.8s. Picture variety is high: nail in palm, scroll, Zechariah throwing coins, potter hands, Judas, blurred motion frame, Christ face, cross, open-hand ending. Flaws: captions sometimes cover art (middle of coin pile, Judas); two blank grey/lavender panels (frames 15 and 17) look unfinished, and "EVERY COIN FOUND" in an empty box is weak; some panel text is tiny. Nail-in-hand image at the start is a strong stop, though it is not the narration's opening.

**B**: Clean, consistent, single full-bleed images, one "500 YEARS EARLY" gold hook title (good idea, only once). Grey rounded caption bar sits mid-frame on every frame, covering art. Many near-duplicate images (coins x4, desert x5, cross crops). Fastest cuts (29/min, 3.5s max hold), so it feels busy but the pictures repeat. Looks like subtitles on a slideshow, not a comic. No panel grammar.

**C**: Same pictures and same caption bar as B but no hook title, slower (16/min, 7.2s hold), and long runs of the same desert and coin frames. Least variety and least reason to stay.

## Top 3 reasons
1. A looks like a comic page (panels, borders, lettered boxes); B and C look like captioned stills.
2. A has the most different pictures per minute. B and C repeat coins, desert and cross.
3. B and C bury the art under a grey bar in the same spot every frame. A varies caption position and highlights key words in red.

## The ONE difference that drove scores
Lettering and panel style (comic caption boxes with red emphasis plus panel layouts) over a plain grey subtitle bar. Cut rhythm and the hook card are secondary; B's hook card helps its "stop" a little but does not save it.

## Odds
Simulated artist read, not real viewers. Rank A first about 65% (error bars wide). B vs C order is about 60/40.

## Still weak in A
Blank panels, captions over faces/art, tiny text.
