Barabbas: proposed words

Who is it for? Anyone scrolling, with no Bible knowledge, anywhere in the world.

Take-away? A guilty man went free because Jesus was handed over in his place, and he did nothing to earn it.

Curiosity gap? "A guilty man walked free. Why?" It is answered at the end.

Global audience? Short plain words, "the Son of God" explained, no blame on any people group. Simulated readers only.

Both cold readers picked Script 1 (scores 7 and 8 out of 10, against 6 and 7 for Script 2). I added the two fixes they both asked for.

Narrator A guilty man walked free. Why?

Narrator At the feast, Pilate the governor could free one prisoner. One was Barabbas, a robber. The other was Jesus, the Son of God.

Narrator Pilate knew the religious leaders handed Jesus over out of envy.

Pilate "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"

Crowd "Barabbas."

Pilate "Why, what evil hath he done?"

Crowd "Let him be crucified."

Narrator So Pilate set Barabbas free, and handed Jesus over to be crucified.

(pause)

Narrator Barabbas did nothing to earn it. Jesus went to the cross in his place.

84 words. About 36 seconds.

Still weak

  • The readers are simulated, not real viewers.
  • "In his place" is the usual reading. The verse only says one was freed and the other handed over.
  • Pilate's wife and his hand-washing are left out for time.

Locking these words costs nothing and spends nothing.
