# Two short narrations. Same story. Each is spoken aloud; voices are in [brackets].

## Script 1
[narrator] "Barabbas" means "son of the father."
[narrator] He was a robber in prison. Some old copies of Matthew even call him Jesus Barabbas.
[narrator] Pilate offered to free one man.
[pilate] "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"
[crowd] "Barabbas."
[pilate] "Why, what evil hath he done?"
[crowd] "Let him be crucified."
[narrator] Jesus called God "Abba." It means Father.
[narrator] It is the word inside the robber's name.
[narrator] So the robber went free. The true Son of the Father did not.
(pause)
[narrator] Barabbas was born with that name. Jesus died to give it to us.
