# Barabbas — draft (Fable seat, blind)

Matthew 27:15-26 (KJV), John 18:40. Vertical Short, spoken aloud.

## (a) Script

[narrator] Barabbas was a robber. He walked free. Why?
[narrator] At the feast, Pilate released one prisoner. The crowd chose.
[pilate] Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?
[narrator] Pilate knew the leaders acted from envy. He knew Jesus was innocent.
[crowd] Barabbas!
[pilate] Why, what evil hath he done?
[crowd] Let him be crucified!
[narrator] Pilate released Barabbas. And handed Jesus over to be crucified.
[narrator] The guilty man walked free. The innocent man took his place.
[narrator] (pause) That is the cross of Jesus. Barabbas just lived it first.

## (b) Count + runtime

- Words: 80 (voice tags and the pause note not counted)
- Runtime at 2.4 words/s: 33.3 s spoken + ~1 s pause before the landing = ~34 s

## (c) KJV fragments used (verbatim)

- John 18:40 — "Barabbas was a robber"
- Matt 27:17 — "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"
- Matt 27:21 — "Barabbas"
- Matt 27:23 — "Why, what evil hath he done?"
- Matt 27:22 — "Let him be crucified"
- Matt 27:26 — told in narrator voice, not quoted: "released ... Barabbas" / "to be crucified"
- Facts carried in plain words, not quoted: envy (27:18), Jesus innocent (27:19, 27:23-24)
- Not used anywhere: Matt 27:25. No people group named; only "the crowd", "the leaders", Pilate.

## (d) Why the hook and landing work

1. Hook: in the first 2.5 s a newcomer learns who (Barabbas), what (a robber walked free) and the why is the open gap — a robber going free makes no sense until the end.
2. The gap pays off from the story alone: Pilate had one release, the crowd chose the robber, so the innocent man went to the cross — "took his place" is exactly what the text shows (Barabbas released, Jesus delivered), no more.
3. Landing is grace-anchored and earned: Barabbas did nothing to deserve it and nothing is asked of the viewer — "Barabbas just lived it first" turns the whole story into the picture of the cross and ends on Jesus, in plain words, with no stock closer and no fear or pressure.
