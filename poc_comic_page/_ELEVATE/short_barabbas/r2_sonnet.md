# Barabbas, Round 2 (sonnet)

## Script
[narrator] A robber sits in chains. He is waiting to hear if he will die.
[narrator] His name is Barabbas.
[narrator] Outside, a crowd must choose.
[pilate] Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?
[crowd] Barabbas!
[crowd] Let him be crucified.
[narrator] The door opens. The robber walks out free.
[narrator] Jesus is led away to the cross instead.
(pause)
[narrator] That is the cross: the just for the unjust.
[narrator] The robber waited for death. Jesus took it.

## Word count / runtime
Words: 76. Runtime at 2.4 words/s: about 31.7 s, plus the pause, about 33-34 s.

## KJV fragments used
- Matt 27:17 "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"
- Matt 27:22 "Let him be crucified."
- Matt 27:21 "Barabbas" (crowd answer)
- John 18:40 "a robber" (Barabbas was a robber)
- 1 Peter 3:18 "the just for the unjust"
- Matt 27:26 (Barabbas released, Jesus delivered to be crucified), paraphrased

## Why it works
- Hook: it starts inside a cell with a person at stake. A newcomer learns who (a robber), what (waiting to hear if he dies), and why to keep watching (what happens to him).
- The gap opens with "will he die?" and pays off in the last line: he lived, and Jesus took it.
- Landing: the reversal is felt in the order of lines, with a free man walking out and Jesus led away, then a pause. Only then is the cross named in plain words. There is no pressure, only a gift.
