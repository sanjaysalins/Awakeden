# Two short narrations. Same story. Each is spoken aloud; voices are in [brackets].

## Script 1
[narrator] A guilty man walked free. Why?
[narrator] Pilate had two prisoners. One was Barabbas, a robber. The other was Jesus.
[narrator] Pilate knew the leaders handed Jesus over out of envy.
[pilate] "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"
[crowd] "Barabbas."
[pilate] "Why, what evil hath he done?"
[crowd] "Let him be crucified."
[narrator] So Pilate set Barabbas free, and handed Jesus over to be crucified.
(pause)
[narrator] Barabbas did nothing to earn it. He walked out free. Jesus went to the cross in his place.

## Script 2
[narrator] Barabbas was a robber. Guilty. And today, he walks free. Why?
[narrator] At the feast, Pilate freed one prisoner. The crowd chose.
[pilate] "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"
[crowd] "Barabbas!"
[pilate] "Why, what evil hath he done?"
[crowd] "Let him be crucified."
[narrator] Pilate knew it was envy. He knew Jesus had done no wrong.
[narrator] Then he released Barabbas, and handed Jesus over to be crucified.
(pause)
[narrator] Barabbas did not earn it. Someone else took the cross.
