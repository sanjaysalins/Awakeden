# Barabbas R3 (name angle) - Sonnet

## (a) Script
[narrator] Barabbas. His name means "son of the father."
[narrator] Some old copies of Matthew even call him Jesus Barabbas.
[pilate] "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"
[crowd] "Barabbas."
[narrator] Two men. Both a son of a father. Pilate knew it was envy.
[pilate] "Why, what evil hath he done?"
[crowd] "Let him be crucified."
[narrator] Barabbas was a robber. Jesus was the Son of the Father.
(pause)
[narrator] Pilate released Barabbas.
[narrator] They freed a son of a father. They sent away the Son of the Father.

## (b) Count
About 78 words, about 32.5 s at 2.4 words/s (the pause adds about 1 s).

## (c) KJV fragments used
- Matt 27:17 "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"
- Matt 27:21 "Barabbas."
- Matt 27:23 "Why, what evil hath he done?"
- Matt 27:22 "Let him be crucified."
- John 18:40 "Barabbas was a robber."
- Matt 27:18 (Pilate knew it was envy), Matt 27:26 (released Barabbas; paraphrased)
- Name meaning (bar abba) and the "Jesus Barabbas" reading are flagged as "some old copies", not KJV.

## (d) Why it works
- Hook: a stranger's name, then what it means, in 2.5 s. The newcomer gets who (Barabbas) and the gap: why does a name matter?
- The "Jesus Barabbas" line makes the choice feel strange: two men, same pair of words.
- Landing: no sermon, no call to act. The name repeats and flips on the last line. Pause first, then the reversal does the work.
