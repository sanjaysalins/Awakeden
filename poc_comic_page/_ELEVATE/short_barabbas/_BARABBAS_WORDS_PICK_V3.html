Barabbas: a new angle (v3)

Who is it for? Anyone scrolling, anywhere, with no Bible knowledge.

Take-away? The guilty man carried the name "son of the father". Jesus, the true Son, died so we could share that name.

Curiosity gap? What does his name mean, and why does it matter? Answered at the end.

Global audience? Short plain words, no group blamed. Simulated readers only.

Narrator "Barabbas" means "son of the father."

Narrator He was a robber in prison.

Narrator Pilate, the governor, offered to free one man. He knew the leaders were jealous of Jesus.

Pilate "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"

Crowd "Barabbas."

Pilate "Why, what evil hath he done?"

Crowd "Let him be crucified."

Narrator Listen: Bar-ABBA. Jesus called God "Abba." It means Father.

Narrator So the robber went free. The true Son of the Father did not.

(pause)

Narrator Barabbas was born with that name. Jesus died to give it to us.
