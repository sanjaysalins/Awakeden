Barabbas: new words (v2)

Who is it for? Anyone scrolling, anywhere, with no Bible knowledge.

Take-away? The cross is the innocent for the guilty. Barabbas is the picture of it.

Curiosity gap? "His cell door opens. He is free. Why?" Answered at the end.

Global audience? Short plain words, no group blamed. Simulated readers only.

Narrator A robber named Barabbas sits in chains, waiting for his verdict.

Narrator Then his cell door opens. He is free. Why?

Narrator Outside, a crowd must choose.

Pilate "Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?"

Crowd "Barabbas!"

Pilate "Why, what evil hath he done?"

Crowd "Let him be crucified."

Narrator Pilate knew the leaders were jealous of Jesus.

Narrator The robber walks out free. Jesus is led away to the cross.

(pause)

Narrator That is the cross: "the just for the unjust."

Narrator The robber was guilty. Jesus took the cross instead.

81 words, about 35 seconds.

What changed after the 2 new readers

  • Dropped "That door is open for you too". Both readers called it a stock closer.
  • Replaced "waited for death", which the text does not say.
  • "Leaders acted out of envy" became "the leaders were jealous of Jesus". It is plainer.

Still weak

  • Scores are about 6 to 7 out of 10. The readers are simulated, not real viewers.
  • No call to the viewer at the end. That is on purpose, since both readers found the old one stock.
  • The picture of the cell door only works with the film's pictures.

Locking these words costs nothing.
