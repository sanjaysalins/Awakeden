## 1. CAMERA — three new moves for the ORDINARY register

(Passion stays LEVEL, never hero. Glory stays ONE low-angle. Untouched.)
What the user loved in panel_d: a low three-quarter angle with converging arch lines —
energy from the CAMERA, not the body. That becomes a system:

| Move | What it is | Reach for it WHEN |
|---|---|---|
| **The Leaning Frame** | Dutch tilt, 5–15° | the words carry fear or doubt ("that fear keeps you at the door") — the horizon tips the way the soul tips. Max ONE per page. |
| **The Burden View** | high angle, looking down on the subject | the subject feels small, judged, alone — the fear's own view of the man. Bonus: ground fills the frame, killing dead sky for free. |
| **The Doorframe Eye** | extreme foreground occlusion — shoot past an object (door edge, latch, arch, scroll) filling 20–40% of frame in shadow | something stands between the subject and what they want — the camera peeks the way the Seeker peeks. Object-based, so no face-count risk. |

**The rule that keeps it reverent:** these moves bend around the SEEKER's world only.
**Christ is never tilted, never looked down on** — He is framed level (ordinary/passion)
or low-angle (glory, once). The camera is unstable around fear, still in His presence.
Level framing is kept for when truth is stated plainly (Jesus speaks = level = trustworthy).

## 2. DENSITY — the "no dead space" amendment

Confirmed by eye: p1a's right half is a flat blue wash; empty skies and bare ground recur.
The fix is a THREE-PLANE rule, written into the style block (not clutter — quiet incident):

- Every panel names a **foreground** element (near-camera, soft shadow, anchors an edge),
  a **midground** subject, and a **background** with drawn content (architecture, rooflines,
  painted night clouds + stars, lamp-glow haze).
- Skies are never flat: banked cloud masses edged in moonlight, scattered stars —
  still and windless, but PAINTED. (Passion beats keep the §5e blank still-darkness sky — senior.)
- Walls/ground carry mute period objects: hung oil lamps, worn steps, drifted leaves,
  moths at the light — **objects, never extra faces** (≤3-faces and crowd rules hold).
- Value guard: all added incident sits DARKER and SOFTER than the subject —
  the brightest value and sharpest edge always belong to the subject.

This clause is baked verbatim into every prompt below (the "Every region..." sentences).

## 3. PALETTE TEST — the seam mechanic in two rival colors

Gold stays the incumbent (it is the theology: gold = His glory). Two REAL contenders,
each tested on the same two scenes — a night exterior (p1a) and the glory beat (p5c) —
because a palette that wins the night must not kill the glory. Gold baselines already exist = $0.

- **MOONLIGHT SEAM** — cool silver-blue rim; nocturne indigo/slate/bone palette; amber
  survives only INSIDE doorways/windows (cold night vs small warm promise). My honest
  read: the only real threat to gold — night pieces will look stunning — but it demotes
  the seam from His glory to weather. High bar to switch.
- **EMBER SEAM** — warm copper-rose rim; burnt umber, wine-dark shadow, dusk-rose sky;
  banked-ember light. Intimate, human, mercy-toned. Risk: sits close enough to gold
  to be a near-miss — judged at arm's length per the bake-off rule.

full three-plane density, AND the **Witness Edge debuts** (foreground shoulder —
its scriptural home, Luke 23:35). One render, gold seam. If the grammar survives
Golgotha with a crowd, it survives anything in the catalogue.
